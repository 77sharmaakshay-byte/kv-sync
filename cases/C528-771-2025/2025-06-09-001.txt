Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Pankaj Tangwan, learned counsel for the
petitioners.
2. Mr. Deepak Bisht, learned Deputy Advocate General 
along with Mr. Prabhat Kandpal, learned Brief Holder for

3. The present C528 petition has been filed with the 
prayer to quash the charge sheet, cognizance order dated 
18.04.2024 and 29.04.2025 (Annexure No.3 to the 
accompanying affidavit) passed by Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Dehradun and the proceedings of Criminal Case 
No.3444 of 2024, “State Vs. Abhishek Saini and another” 
under Sections 323, 504, 506 and 452 of IPC pending in the 
Court of Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Dehradun.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner would submit that 
the FIR has been lodged on 28.11.2023 on false and 
frivolous allegations after one month from the date of 
incident i.e. 26.10.2023; that, the FIR has been lodged with 
ill motive only for making pressure on the petitioners for 
taking divorce; that, the present dispute is a matrimonial 
dispute and there are chances of amicable settlement 
between the parties.
5. Issue notice to respondent no.2 through ordinary 
process and registered post, acknowledgement due as well 
as by email and WhatsApp, if available.
6. Steps to be taken within three weeks. 
7. Learned counsel for the State seeks and is granted 
time to file counter affidavit.
8. List this case after service of notice for hearing on 
admission. 
9. Till the next date of listing, proceedings of Criminal 
Case No.3444 of 2024, under Sections 323, 504, 506 and 
452 of IPC pending in the Court of Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Dehradun, shall remain stayed.
