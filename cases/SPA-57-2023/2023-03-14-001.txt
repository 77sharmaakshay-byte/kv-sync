One of the grounds on which the writ 
petition preferred by the appellants has been 
dismissed is that the petitioners have not 
impleaded the affected private parties whose 
selection would be affected in case the 
petitioners were to succeed. 
Learned counsel for the appellants
submits that there are about 1000 persons, 
who have been selected, and, therefore, it 
was not possible to implead all of them as 
party respondents. 
We are not satisfied with this 
explanation as there is provision in law to implead the affected persons in 
representative capacity, where the number 
of such persons is very large or 
indeterminate. 
Mr. Rawat states that he shall provide 
the select list to the counsel for the 
appellants within three days. 
Let the counsel for the appellants collect 
the same from the office of Mr. Rawat during
this period. Thereafter, the appellants shall 
take steps to implead 10 of the selectees, 
who are claimed to be juniors, than the 
appellants in representative capacity. 
List the matter on 03.05.2023. 
Stay Application (IA No. 01 of 2023)
We are not inclined to stay the 
operation of the impugned order. 
This application is, accordingly, 
dismissed.
