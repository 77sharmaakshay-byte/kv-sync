4. This writ petition has been filed by the petitioner for 
mandamus commanding the respondent-Selecting Body to 
revise the Provisional Recommendation List dated 27.05.2026, 
annexure no.6 to the writ petition.
5. Petitioner applied for the post of Instructor (Welder 
Trade) pursuant to advertisement issued by the respondent￾Selecting Body. Petitioner participated in the selection process 
but his name was not recommended for appointment. 
6. According to learned counsel for the petitioner, the 
petitioner belongs to OBC Category and he scored 60.75
marks. He admittedly applied as an OBC candidate. The 
petitioner submits that the candidate belonging to Open 
(Converted from horizontal General Women Category) who 
scored lesser marks i.e., 51.25% has been recommended.
7. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner that
if the petitioner scores higher marks than that of candidate 
belonging to Open Category, he should migrate to the Open 
Category and therefore his name should have been
recommended.
8. Per contra, learned counsel for the respondent-Selecting 
Body submits that recommendation has already been made 
and secondly the seat meant for General Women Category 2
Candidate in the absence of Women candidate of General 
Category would migrate to the Open Category, therefore the 
Open Category candidate was recommended and the OBC 
candidate even scoring higher marks than that of General 
Category shall not be migrated to Open Category.
9. Issue notice to respondent no.4 returnable within four 
weeks.
10. Steps to be taken within three days.
11. List this case on 25.08.2026.
12. Counter affidavit(s) be filed by the respondent(s) within 
six weeks.
13. Respondent-State is directed to keep one post of 
Instructor (Welder Trade) vacant in the Government I.T.I. till 
the next date of listing.
