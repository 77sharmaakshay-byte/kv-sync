Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. T.P.S. Takuli, counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Yogesh Pacholia, counsel for the 
respondent.
3. This petition is filed by the petitioner/accused 
to quash the impugned order dated 12.09.2024 
passed by learned Civil Judge (Junior 
Division)/Judicial Magistrate, Ranikhet, District 
Almora in Criminal Case No. 225 of 2022 
‘Himanshu Bisht Vs. Manoj Bisht’.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the petitioner had moved an application 
paper no.65ka/1 & 65ka/8 under Section 91 Cr.P.C.
to summon the bank officers and staff of Nainital 
Bank, State Bank of India Ranikhet Branch, IDBI 
Bank, H.D.F.C. Bank, Ranikhet Branch as witnesses 
along with bank details of the 
respondent/complainant from the year 2016 to 2021,
as a huge amount was alleged to be given to the
petitioner by the respondent/complainant, however, 
no source of income for such huge amount was 
shown by the respondent/complainant; that, said 
application was rejected by the trial court vide order 
dated 12.09.2024, hence, this writ petition.
5. He would further submit that the 
respondent/complainant has filed the Criminal Case 
No. 225 of 2022 before the learned Civil Judge 
(J.D.)/Judicial Magistrate, Ranikhet, District Almora 
under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instrument Act 
for dishonour of the cheques allegedly given by the 
petitioner/accused to the complainant/respondent in 
the form of five cheques of `8 lacs each in total `40 
lacs; that, the respondent/complainant was examined 
in the trial court and he admits that he used to give 
the loan to the petitioner/accused by cash as he use 
to keep bulk money in cash and he was having 2-3 
lacs cash in his house, however, he has not disclosed 
the bank transaction as well as income tax on 
aforesaid alleged amount.6. Counsel for the petitioner would further 
submit that at that relevant point of time i.e. October, 
2016, the Central Government had imposed 
demonetization, therefore, it is highly improbable 
that the respondent/complainant has paid such huge 
amount in cash to the petitioner; that, the 
respondent/complainant has not produced the bank 
accounts detail along with the complaint under 
Section 138 Negotiable Instruments Act, therefore, 
in order to bring all the truth on record, the 
application (paper no.62ka/1 & 62ka/8) was moved 
by the petitioner.
7. Counsel for the respondent/complainant would 
submit that the respondent/complainant was ditched 
by the petitioner/accused on whom he had repose 
immense confidence and trust; that, no defence was 
made out in the cross examination of the witnesses, 
therefore, the trial court rightly rejected the 
application of the petitioner.
8. In view of the above, this Court is of the opinion 
that the matter needs deliberation.
9. Admit the petition.
10. Counsel for the respondent seeks and is granted 
time to file counter affidavit.
11. List this case on 11.03.2025.
12. Having considered the submission of counsel 
for the parties, as an interim measure, further 
proceedings of the Criminal Case No. 225 of 2022 
‘Himanshu Bisht Vs. Manoj Bisht’ pending in the 
court of learned Civil Judge (Junior 
Division)/Judicial Magistrate, Ranikhet, District 
Almora shall remains stayed till further orders.
13. Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
(Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.)
