Mr. Vinay Kumar with Ms. Sudha Tamta, learned 
Counsel for the petitioners. 
2. Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Addl. CSC for the State.
3. Mr. Gaurav Nagpal, learned Counsel i/b Mr. Ramji 
Srivastava, learned Counsel for respondent no.3-Selecting 
Body.
4. Petitioners participated in the selection process 
initiated by respondent no.3-selecting body pursuant to 
advertisement dated 11.03.2024 for recruitment on the post 
of Nursing Officer in Govt. Medical Colleges in the State of 
Uttarakhand. Petitioners were selected and recommended 
by respondent-Board to respondent no.2 for the purpose of 
appointment. However, petitioners were not issued 
appointment letters by respondent-State due to which they
approached this Court by filing WPSS No.762 of 2025 
(Sohan Lal and others v. State and others). The said writ 
petition was disposed of by Co-ordinate Bench of this Court 
vide judgment and order dated 11.12.2025 giving liberty to 
the respondent-State to enquire into the complaint against 
the selection of petitioners within a period of six weeks from 
the date of production of certified copy of the order of writ 
Court. It was also observed that if the enquiry is not 
concluded within six weeks, respondent-State shall issue 
appointment letters to petitioners. 5. The respondent-State neither concluded the enquiry 
within the prescribed period nor did it issue any 
appointment letter to petitioners, due to which, contempt 
petition being CLCON No.23 of 2026 (Sohan Lal and others 
v. Sachin Kurve and others) is filed by petitioners. Being 
furious by filing of said contempt petition, respondent-State 
cancelled the selection of petitioners vide impugned order 
dated 10.07.2026 which was issued by respondent no.2. 
Although, petitioners have impugned the order dated 
10.07.2026 but they could not annex copy of such order 
since it was never supplied to petitioners and straightway, it 
was sent to respondent no.3.
6. It is contended by petitioners that the advertisement 
itself provided that regular employees of central government 
or public sector undertakings of central government, 
working against substantive posts, in the State of 
Uttarakhand whose services cannot be transferred outside 
the State of Uttarakhand, would be eligible to participate in 
the selection process pursuant to advertisement dated 
11.03.2024. 
7. Learned Counsel for petitioners contends that 
pursuant to the aforesaid clause, petitioners submitted their 
candidature and they were selected, and now, only on the 
ground that petitioners are not the residents of State of 
Uttarakhand, they are denied appointment. 
8. Per contra, learned State Counsel submits that 
petitioners were initially registered with Rajasthan Nursing 
Council, though it is admitted to him that before 
advertisement, petitioners’ registration was transferred to 
the State of Uttarakhand. He further submits that condition 
of candidate being domicile of the State of Uttarakhand has 
also been included in the resolution passed by the Nursing Council but the fact of matter is that the resolution too is 
subsequent to the advertisement as well as selection.
9. Having heard learned Counsel for parties and having 
perused the writ petition, this Court is of the view that it is 
a clear high-handedness on the part of respondents to deny 
appointment to petitioners. 
10. The respondents may file counter affidavit within six 
weeks.
11. List on 17.11.2026. 
12. In the meantime, the effect and operation of impugned 
order dated 10.07.2026 issued by respondent no.2-Director, 
Department of Medical Education, Uttarakhad, Dehradun, 
shall remain stayed qua petitioners only. It is further 
directed that three vacancies on the post of Nursing Officer 
(Male) pursuant to advertisement dated 11.03.2024 shall be 
kept vacant during pendency of present writ petition. 
13. Interim Relief Application (IA/1/2026) stands disposed 
of.
