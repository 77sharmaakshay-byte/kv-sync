Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Vinay Kumar, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Petitioner, who is a Gram Panchayat Vikas Adhikari 
has challenged the order dated 16.06.2026, annexure-11 
to the writ petition, whereby petitioner was directed to 
deposit a sum of Rs.4,82,046.00/- within a period of 
fifteen days in the account of Gram Panchayat Ringalti, 
Talla, Block Nainidanda and submit a receipt thereof to 
the DPRO, Pauri Garhwal.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that, before passing the impugned order, no show-cause 
notice was issued to the petitioner, nor was he 
confronted with the imputations on the basis of which 
the said recovery has been made.
5. Learned State Counsel prays for and is granted a 
week’s time to get instructions in the matter particularly 
on the aspect as to whether the show cause notice was 
issued to the petitioner or not.
