Mr. Jitendra Chaudhary, learned Senior 
Counsel assisted by Mr. Priyanshu Gairola 
and Mr. Jai Krishna Pandey, learned counsel 
for the petitioner. 
2. Mr. Rajat Mittal, learned counsel with 
Mr. Ajay Joshi and Mr. Ashish Bansal, 
learned counsel for respondent no.1.
3. Mr. K.N. Joshi, learned Deputy 
Advocate General for the State.
4. The Coordinate Bench released the 
matter and now on urgent mention Mr. 
Jitendra Chaudhary, learned Senior Counsel 
apprise that now the matter is placed before 
this bench as per the latest OM and there is 
an urgency in the matter since there is 
apprehension that by virtue of the order 
impugned, the petitioner may be 
dispossessed at any time. 
5. The respondents herein on the basis of 
the sale deed dated 28.08.2001 initiate the 
mutation proceeding by moving an 
application which was allowed on 23.05.2026 
against which a Revenue Appeal was filed by 
the petitioner i.e. Revenue Appeal 
No.14/2025-2026 and the Collector by it’s 
order dated 04.08.2026 admit the appeal and 
stayed the order of Tehsildar dated 23.05.2026 passed in mutation proceeding 
with the rider that no third party interest 
shall be created on disputed land and the 
parties shall maintain status quo. 
Being aggrieved, the respondent no.1 
preferred the Revision before the Board of 
Revenue and it is alleged that in para 5 he 
miscoded the order of the Collector dated 
04.08.2026 and further in para 6 a wrong 
assertion was made that no opportunity was 
given to him by the Subordinate Court. In 
this Revision the Board of Revenue passed an 
order on 29.10.2026 and issued notice to the 
other side and stayed the order of Collector 
dated 04.08.2026.
6. Now being aggrieved with the order 
passed by the Board of Revenue dated 
04.08.2026 the petitioner herein preferred the 
instant petition under Article 227 of the 
Constitution of India. 
7. The order of the Board of Revenue 
dated 29.10.2026 is being assailed on two 
grounds:-
(i) The revision preferred before the Board 
of Revenue is not maintainable since the 
same is preferred against the interim order 
passed by the Collector in Revenue Appeal 
on 04.08.2026 under Section 219 of Land 
Revenue Act. 
(ii) The revision was filed with 
misrepresentation of fact since in the memo 
of revision the order of Collector is miscoded 
by showing revisionist himself as respondent 
no.1 though before the Collector he was 
respondent no.4 and in para 6 a false 
statement has been given that no such 
opportunity was given to the revisionist though as a matter of fact he was the 
caveator and was also heard by the Collector 
while passing the order on 04.08.2026. Apart 
from this, learned Senior Counsel submits 
that the order of the Board of Revenue, 
which is impugned in this petition whereby 
the order of Collector is stayed, no reason has 
been recorded though as a matter of fact the 
order of the Collector is not prejudicial to the 
respondents since the parties were directed 
to maintain the status quo and will not create 
any third party interest. 
8. On the other side Mr. Rajat Mittal, 
learned counsel for respondent no.1 submits 
that the subsequent part of the order of 
Collector was added later on in the absence 
of respondent no.1 and though no specific 
averment has been made in para 6 in this 
regard but intention of respondent no.1 was 
that the subsequent part of the order of the 
Collector was added later on in his absence. 
9. To examine whether the subsequent 
part was added later on or not in the order 
passed by the Collector on 04.08.2026 which 
is really surprising, therefore, this Court 
want to go through with the original record. 
10. Let the Registry may sent the 
requisition to summon the complete order 
sheet of Revenue Appeal No.13 of 2025-26 
pending in the court of Collector.
11. In the meantime Mr. Rajat Mittal, 
learned counsel may also filed his 
objection/counter affidavit.
12. Put up this matter on 20.11.2026 
immediately after fresh. 
13. In the meantime, the parties to the 
proceeding pending before the Collector as well as before the Board of Revenue are 
restrain from creating any third party interest 
and shall maintain status quo in respect of 
the land in question. 
14. Since the order is dictated in the open 
court, therefore, it is the duty of the learned 
counsel for the petitioner to apprise about 
this order.
