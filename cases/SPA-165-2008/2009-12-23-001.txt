Special Appeal No.165 of 2008
 It is not a matter of dispute, that the appellant came to 
be re-appointed as a Sweeper-cum-Chowkidar in the Satyamitranand 2
Giri Intermediate College, Haripur Kalan, Raiwala on account of an 
order issued by the Principal of said college. Based on the statement 
of the said Principal, Writ Petition (S/S) No.2557 of 2001, pending 
before this Court, was disposed of as having been rendered 
infructuous, thereby depriving the appellant of the relief sought by 
him from this Court. 
 Pleadings in the instant special appeal do not disclose, 
whether after the appointment of the appellant as Sweeper-cum￾Chowkidar at the hands of the Principal of the college his services 
were ever dispensed with by the managing committee. Accordingly, 
an affidavit be filed at the hands of respondent No.3 to depict, 
whether the alleged wrongful appointment made by the Principal of 
the college was ever corrected by dispensing with the services of the 
appellant. 
 Additionally, it would be relevant to ascertain, whether 
or not any action was taken for the alleged wrongful appointment 
made by the Principal of the college by the managing committee. In 
case, the answer to the aforesaid query is affirmative, the outcome 
thereof, shall also be incorporated in the affidavit disclosing the facts 
pertaining to the aforesaid query. 
 The affidavit shall be filed by respondent No.3 within 
two weeks from today with a copy in advance to the learned counsel 
for the appellant. 
 List for further consideration on 08.02.2010.
