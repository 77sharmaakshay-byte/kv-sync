Delay condonation application is allowed. 
Delay in filing review application is condoned. 
The learned Single Judge found that the 
petitioner had been appointed under the dying-in￾harness rules but was made to work on a daily 
rated basis. The writ court accordingly disposed 
of the writ petition directing the respondents to 
pay regular salary to the petitioner while relying 
upon a decision of the Division Bench in the case 
of Ravi Karan Singh Vs. State of U.P. and 
others, (1999) 3 UPLBEC 2263, wherein it was 
held that the appointments made under the dying￾in-harness rules are substantive in nature. 
A review application has been filed 
indicating that the petitioner was appointed as a 
daily wager and he was given seasonal 
appointment. No proof of this averment has been 
filed either in the review application nor is found 
in the counter affidavit. Further, there is no 
averment that the petitioner was never appointed 
under the dying-in-harness rules. 
In view of the aforesaid, the review 
application fails and is dismissed.
