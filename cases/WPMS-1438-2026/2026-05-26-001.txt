COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 1438 of 2026
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Sahil Mullick, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. The petitioner is aggrieved with the 
award of the Labour Court dated 31.03.2026 
passed in Adjudication Case No. 23 of 2024,
which was allowed by the Labour Court. 
3. The learned counsel for the petitioner 
argued that the application moved by the 
respondent/workman under Section 33C (2) of 
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 for computation 
of retrenchment compensation and for the 
salary of two months and notice period is not 
maintainable since against the order of 
retrenchment, a reference was made to the 
Labour Court on 16.08.2024 and the Labour 
Court by award dated 05.07.2024 reject his 
claim and the reference was answered in 
favour of the employer. He submits that earlier 
award of the Labour Court dated 05.07.2024 
was not assailed by the respondent and now 
after four years of the retrenchment he filed an 
application under section 33C (2) for 
computation of retrenchment compensation
and for the salary of two months notice which 
is not maintainable. He also submits that even 
the application moved by respondent
/workman under Section 33C (2) is also barred by the res judicata. 
4. All these aspects requirs scrutiny.
5. Issue notice to the respondent.
6. Steps to be taken within a week.
7. List this matter on 08.07.2026.
8. In the meantime, the award of the 
Labour Court dated 31.03.2026 passed in 
Adjudication Case No. 23 of 2024 Khem Chand
Vs. Sara Sae Pvt. Ltd. remain stayed provided 
the petitioner deposit a sum of Rs. 75,000/-in 
the Registry within two weeks.
