COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 208 of 2021
With connected petitions
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Parikshit Saini, learned counsel 
for the petitioners.
2. Mr. Tarun Mohan, learned Brief
Holder for the State.
3. The petitioners are serving as an 
Assistant Teachers in primary institution 
and for certain period they asked for 
medical leave which were forwarded by 
the head master of the institution to the 
Block Education Officer, but no entry was 
made in the service record of the 
petitioners and, thereafter since there was 
no entry of medical leave in the service 
record of the petitioners the recovery was 
ordered which was challenged by the 
petitioners in separate petitions wherein 
on 27.05.2020 and 29.05.2020 the 
recovery was stayed, the copy of which 
are enclosed in the petition. 
4. Thereafter, one person namely
Padam Kumar preferred a writ petition in 
the form of PIL with the following reliefs:-
“(i) issue a writ, order or direction in the nature 
 of mandamus commanding the respondents to 
 conduct and immediate probe or enquiry from 
 any appropriate authority and take necessary 
 action in accordance with law. 
(ii) issue a writ, order or direction in the nature 
of mandamus commanding the respondents to 
conduct the disciplinary proceedings and take 
necessary action against the private 
respondents with regard to the rules and law 
applicable in the instant matter.
(iii) issue a writ, order or direction in the nature 
of mandamus commanding the respondents to 
take affirmative actions of punitive nature 
against the private respondents after assessing 
the conduct of the private respondents.”5. The Writ Petition (PIL) No. 42 of
2020 was disposed of finally with the 
direction to the respondents to consider 
the representations in accordance with 
law. The representation which was 
directed to be decided are also enclosed as 
Annexure-5 to the petition. Thereafter, by 
the order impugned the Chief Education 
Officer, Haridwar, passed an order dated 
15.12.2020 whereby he revived the earlier 
order of recovery, which, in fact, was 
already stayed by the High Court and 
ordered to recover the amount in six equal 
installments, and it is further mentioned in 
the order that such recovery shall be 
subject to the final outcome of the 
pending petitions. All those pending writ 
petitions are clubbed with this petition. In 
para-9 a specific averment has been made 
that the action of the Chief Education 
Officer while passing the order impugned 
is nothing but a contemptuous act, since, 
in flagrant violation of the interim order 
passed by this Court, the order of recovery 
has been revived. 
6. In this petition, the recovery is 
already stayed and on 27.01.2021, the 
respondents were directed to file counter 
affidavit. It is very strange that up till 
date, the counter affidavit has not been 
filed. 
7. Prima facie, I find force on the
submissions of learned counsel for the 
petitioners that order impugned has been 
passed by the Chief Education Officer, 
Haridwar, in flagrant violation of the 
interim order dated 27.05.2020, passed in 
WPSS No. 507 of 2020 and order dated 
29.05.2020 passed in bunch of petitions 
i.e. WPSS No. 509 of 2020, 510 of 2020, 
511 of 2020, 513 of 2020, 515 of 2020, 
516 of 2020 and 518 of 2020.
8. Mr. Parikshit Saini, learned counsel for the petitioners apprise to this Court 
that the then Chief Education Officer has 
been re-employed after retirement just 
two months back. If it is so let the said 
officer shall remain present in the Court 
through V.C. to explain why in flagrant
violation of the interim order passed by 
this Court he has passed the order 
impugned.
9. List this case on 06.01.2026.
 
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
