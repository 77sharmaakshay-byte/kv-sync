 The appeal has been filed against 
the judgment of the District Judge, 
Dehradun, dismissing the objections 
moved by the appellants before the 
court below under Section 34 of the 
Arbitration Act. 
 No money has been paid in 
compliance of the Award rendered by 
the Three-Arbitrators, nonetheless, 
the work of Uttarakhand Jal Vidyut 
Nigam Ltd. was completed by the 
Contractor and the appellant is also 
producing electricity out of the 
establishment. 
 Despite availing ample time, more 
than what was granted by this Court, 
no defect has been removed. So, it is 
hereby made clear that the opposite 
parties may get the execution 
proceeded in the concerned Court. It 
is hereby made clear that there is no 
stay granted by this Court hampering 
the course of execution of Award. 
 List this matter for further orders 
after two weeks, when the Court may 
contemplate to dismiss the appeal for 
non-compliance of orders of this 
Court.
