C482 No. 509 of 2023 
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Vikas Kumar Guglani, Advocate, for the 
applicant. 
Mr. Atul Kumar Sah, Deputy A.G., with Mrs. 
Mamta Joshi, Brief Holder, for the State of 
Uttarakhand. 
As per the FIR, which was got registered by 
the complainant/respondent No.2, herein, she has 
submitted, that she was in close intimate 
relationship with the present applicant for a quite 
long time, as admittedly they have got in contact 
with each other through social media. It is admitted 
by the complainant/respondent No.2, that she had 
travelled from Delhi to Rudrapur on 1st November, 
2020, and thereafter, she stayed in a hotel called as 
‘Chilana Tower’. From there, the applicant took 
her to his sister’s home at Sarda Colony, Rudrapur,
on 3rd November, 2020. 
The complainant/respondent No.2, further 
admits the fact, that later on, she was kept in the 
home of family friend of the applicant, where they 
stayed together and established physical 
relationship. 
It is not even that, she has alleged in the FIR, 
that on a demand being raised by the applicant 
before marrying, she had even gone to Delhi to her parental home to bring the money, as it was alleged 
to be demanded by the present applicant. 
Owing to the aforesaid backdrop, the 
registration of the FIR, being FIR No. 28 of 2021 
dated 17th January, 2021, for the offence under 
Sections 376, 323, 504, 506 of the IPC, could not 
be apparently said to be made out, because it was a 
consensual relationship, and the complainant had 
been moving around with the present applicant for 
quite a long time at different places. 
In that eventuality, let notices be issued to 
respondent No.2. 
Steps to be taken by the applicant within a 
period of one week from today. 
Respondent No.1, who is represented by the 
Deputy A.G, prays for and is granted three weeks’ 
time to file the counter affidavit. 
List thereafter. 
Till the next date of listing, the further 
proceedings of Criminal Case No.3088 of 2021, 
State Vs. Mukesh Kohli, pending consideration 
before the Court of Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar, would be 
kept in abeyance.
