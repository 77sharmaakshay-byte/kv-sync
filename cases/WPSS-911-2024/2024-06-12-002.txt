Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Ramji Shrivastava and Mr. Nishant Krishna Adhikari, 
learned counsel for the petitioners. 
2. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Standing Counsel for the State of

3. Mr. Manoj Kumar and Mr. Saurav Adhikari, learned Standing 
Counsel for the Union of India/respondent no.2.
4. Petitioners are the contractual employees of respondent no.5-
Eklavya Model Residential School (EMRS), Jogla, P.O. Kalsi Gate, 
Kalsi, District Dehradun. An online advertisement was issued by 
respondent no.3/National Education Society for Tribal Students 
(NESTS) for supplying the vacancies of teaching and non-teaching 
staff of EMRS throughout India including EMRS in State of 
Uttarakhand. The petitioners are working with the respondent no.5/ 
Eklavya Model Residential School (EMRS) for the last two to thirteen 
years and so. 
5. Feeling aggrieved with the online advertisement, some of the 
Teaching and Non-Teaching Staff of the EMRS moved to this Court 
by filing Writ Petition (S/S) Nos.1414, 1427, 1680 and 1681 of 2023. 
6. This Court after hearing both the parties reached to this 
conclusion that prima facie, respondent no.3-National Education 
Society for Tribal Students (NESTS) has got no legal right to notify 
the vacancies with regard to Eklavya Model Residential School 
(EMRS) in the State of Uttarakhand as per the new guidelines issued 
for EMRS. As per Clause 9.3(e) and (f) of the aforesaid new 2
guidelines, the power for recruitment of teaching and non-teaching 
staff of the schools was given to State Level Society i.e. EMRS 
Society-respondent no.3. Consequently, the selection process pursuant 
to the online advertisement was stayed by this Court vide interim 
orders dated 17.08.2023 and 12.09.2023 passed in those writ petitions. 
7. Feeling aggrieved by the interim orders passed by this Court, 
the respondent no.3-NESTS took these matters to the Division Bench 
of this Court by filing of Special Appeal Nos. 331, 334, 342 and 344 
of 2023. The aforesaid special appeals have been disposed of by the 
Division Bench on the basis of a concessional statement made by the 
appellant-NESTS therein along with the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, 
Government of India. The portion of the undertaking given by the 
appellant and the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, quoted in the judgment 
and order of Division Bench is reproduced below…….
“The appellants filed their counter-affidavits, along with an 
application to seek vacation of the stay granted by this Court 
against the recruitment process. The stand taken by the 
appellants in their counter-affidavit, inter alia, is that the 
recruitment process in question, is not being made in respect 
of posts held by the respondents- writ petitioners. In 
Paragraph No.14 of the counter-affidavit, the stand taken by 
the appellants is as follows:-
“14. That the contents of para 25 of the writ petition 
to make the matter abundantly clear it is submitted that 
presently NESTS would not post any teacher against the 
posts occupied by these 28 contractual teachers in 
Uttarakhand. As of now, NESTS has not published the 
posts held by contractual teachers as they are engaged 
by state. The contract of teaches is with state society. 
NESTS has nothing to do with their continuation or 
discontinuation. However, our recruitment is being 
conducted in such a way as not to disturb the current 
contractual teachers”.3
8. From the perusal of the undertaking given by the appellant￾NESTS and the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Uttarakhand, it is revealed
beyond doubt that the recruitment process, which was in question, was 
not being made in respect of the post(s) held by the respondent￾petitioners in special appeals. Now, after having given such an
undertaking by filing the counter affidavit, respondent nos. 2 and 3 
have now turned around and they are in haste to fill up the vacancies 
held by the petitioners for the last so many years, though on 
contractual basis. 
9. Since it has been held by this Court while hearing the connected 
writ petitions filed by some of the petitioners that the central society
(NESTS) has got no right to issue an advertisement for supplying the 
vacancies in the Eklavya Model Residential School (EMRS), the said 
exercise is totally illegal and bereft of any merit for the reason that 
since the very inception as per new guidelines, the advertisement 
cannot be issued by the Central Society. It is in the exclusive domain 
and competence of State Level Society i.e. respondent no.4
10. It is contended by the learned counsel for the petitioners that 
pursuant to the earlier impugned online advertisement, the selection 
process has been concluded and the respondents now are in haste of 
giving joining to the selected persons on the post held by the 
petitioners. 
11. Feeling aggrieved, the petitioners are before this Court. 
12. Per contra, learned counsel for the respondents submits that the 
term of the petitioners has not been extended beyond 30.05.2024, and 
therefore, the petitioners have no right to continue.
13. Having considered the rival submissions made by learned 
counsel for the parties and having gone through the record of the writ 
petition, this Court is of the view that once the undertaking has been 4
given by the NESTS-respondent no.3 and Ministry of Tribal Affairs￾respondent no.2 before the Division Bench of this Court while 
deciding the special appeals that no selection process would be held 
for the post on which the petitioners are working, this exercise which 
is being done by respondents is illegal and contemptuous too.
14. Issue notice to respondent nos.3, 4 & 5, returnable within six 
weeks. 
15. Steps to be taken within one week. 
16. Respondents may file counter affidavit within six weeks. 
17. Rejoinder affidavit may file within two weeks thereafter. 
18. List these matters on 18.09.2024.
19. In the meantime, the respondents are restrained from giving 
joining to any of the persons selected on the post(s) as held by the 
petitioners in the respondent no.5-Institution.
20. Let a certified copy of this order be supplied to the learned 
counsel for the parties, today itself, as per rules.
