Mr. Shobhit Joshi, learned proxy counsel for Mr.

The submission of the learned counsel for the 
appellant is that the learned Single Judge failed to 
appreciate that the direction for demarcation of the 
petitioner’s land, falling in Plot No.177, consumed in 
laying the road had already been issued while deciding 
the earlier Writ Petition No.3687 of 2019, decided on 
10.12.2019. 
He further submits that when the writ petition 
was preferred, on account of the activity relating to 
construction of road being undertaken, petitioner’s two 
other plots No.203 and 206 were also taken 
possession of by the respondents. However, 
acquisition proceedings in respect neither of these 
parcels have been initiated till date. 
Our attention has been drawn to the counter￾affidavit filed by the respondent in the writ 
proceedings. Alongwith the counter-affidavit, a copy of 
the forwarding letter dated 28.02.2021, along with the 
measurement report, was annexed as Annexure No.1. 
A perusal of this measurement report, prepared by the respondents, shows that, admittedly, land to 
the extent of 0.093 hectare, falling in Khasra No.177 
has been consumed in laying a road. Therefore, the 
respondents are bound to take steps to acquire the 
land, and pay compensation therefor to the petitioner￾appellant herein. The respondents have not initiated 
the proceedings for acquisition of land under the Right 
to Fair Compensation and Transparency in Land 
Acquisition, Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013, 
till date. 
In the aforesaid circumstances, we direct the 
respondents to carry out measurement at site to 
examine the appellant’s claim that his land falling in 
Khasra Nos.203 and 206 has also been consumed in 
laying the road. 
The report be placed before this Court before the 
next date. 
List this case on 04.08.2022. 
 (R.C. Khulbe, J.) (Vipin Sanghi, C.J.)
