COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
I.A. No. 2 of 2023 (Modification Application)
In 
WPMS No.1355 of 2022
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J.
Mr. Navneet Kaushik, Advocate for 
the petitioner. 
Mr. Tanuj Semwal, Advocate, 
holding brief of Mr. Parikshit Saini, 
Advocate for respondent no. 4.
2. Petitioner has challenged an award 
passed by learned Labour Court, 
Haridwar on 01.02.2021. By the 
impugned award, respondent no.4-
workman was directed to be re-instated 
with back wages.
3. This Court vide order dated 
25.08.2022 had stayed recovery 
pursuant to the impugned award, 
provided petitioner complies with the 
provision contained in Section 17-B of 
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
4. It is contended by learned counsel 
for the petitioner that last drawn wages 
are being paid to the workman in terms 
of interim order dated 25.08.2022. This, 
however, has been disputed by learned
counsel for respondent no.4 as, 
according to him, respondent no. 4 is not 
being regularly paid wages as per 
provision contained in Section 17-B of 
Industrial Disputes Act, 1947.
5. Petitioner has prayed for 
modification of interim order dated 
25.08.2022.6. It is contended by learned counsel 
for the petitioner that petitioner is ready 
to take respondent no.4 back in service, 
during pendency of the present writ 
petition and he is also ready to pay 
regular wages to respondent no.4 in lieu 
of the services rendered by him as 
Driver. 
7. In view of the undertaking given by 
petitioner to pay regular wages to 
respondent no.4-workman, the interim 
order dated 25.08.2022 is modified and 
it is provided that petitioner shall take 
respondent no.4 back in service and 
assign duties of Driver to him. Petitioner 
shall pay the wages as were being paid 
to respondent no.4 at the time of his 
termination. It is made clear that wages 
shall be paid to respondent no.4 on 5th
day of each succeeding month. 
Modification Application stands disposed 
of.
8. List before appropriate bench as per 
roster.
