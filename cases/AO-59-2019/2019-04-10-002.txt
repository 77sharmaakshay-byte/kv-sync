AO No. 59 of 2019
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. I.P. Kohli, Advocate for the appellant.
Mr. Mohd. Azim, Advocate for the respondent 
Nos. 1 to 6.
This Appeal from Order arises out of an award 
dated 14.11.2018 as passed by MACT/1st Additional 
District Judge, Udham Singh Nagar in MACT No. 55 
of 2015, Smt. Munabbari and others v. The New 
India Assurance Company Limited and others, by 
virtue of which a sum of Rs. 32,09,762/- (Rupees 
Thirty Two Lakh, Nine Thousand Seven Hundred 
Sixty Two only) has been awarded as a compensation 
to the claimants on account of a death of the husband 
of respondent No. 1. The deceased has left behind 
himself 4 children, who are exclusively depending 
upon the widow mother i.e. respondent No. 1. 
This Court, by an interim order dated 
13.02.2019 had directed to deposit 50% of the 
awarded amount before the concerned Tribunal. It is 
stated that in compliance thereto, the amount has 
already been deposited.
Today, the matter is taken up on the Money 
Withdrawal Application (CLMA No. 4670/2019) 
filed by the claimants which is being opposed by the 
appellant on the ground that the liability to pay the 
compensation cannot be harnessed upon the 
Insurance Company exclusively for the reason being 
that as per the site plan, the trailer was wrongly 
parked and the deceased, who was riding in the 
motorcycle had colluded against the rear part of the 
trailer. Hence, the liability, ought to be fasten upon to 
respondent No. 7 i.e. the owner of the trailer.
Be that as it may, this question of fixation of 
liability would always be a question to be decided on 
merits after the contest being put in by respondent 
Nos. 7, who has already been served as per the office 
report dated 09.04.2019.
This issue with regard to the fixation of 
liability, which would be an issue of ultimate determination while adjudicating the Appeal, this 
itself cannot deprive the claimants to have a partial 
disbursement of the claimed amount, deposited by 
the appellant pursuant to an interim order. As the 
mother (respondent No. 1) has got no source of 
earning, as such this Withdrawal Application is 
allowed, only to the extent that the amount as 
deposited by the appellant in compliance of the 
interim order dated 13.02.2019, to the extent, 50% of 
the deposited amount would be released to the 
claimant but this release will not be construed as if it 
is the fixation of the liability on the appellant which 
would be the subject matter of ultimate adjudication 
of this appeal finally.
Put up this matter in the week commencing 
27.05.2019.
