7. On the previous date, the learned 
Attorney General argued that these petitioners 
have no locus to challenge the selection 
process, and, furthermore, also addressed in 
order to establish that the NESTS is empowered 
to proceed with the selection by referring the 
guidelines issued on 13.11.2020 as well as the 
Memorandum of Understanding dated 
26.08.2020.
8. Today, Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, learned 
Senior counsel, who appears for the petitioners 
argued in order to give response to the 
arguments of learned Attorney General, and 
submits that, the NESTS came into existence 
first time in the year 2019, and, thereafter, the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, Government of India
framed the guidelines in the year 2020 and as 
per sub Clause (e) of Clause 9.3, only the 
State/EMRS society is empowered to ensure 
recruitment of Teaching and Non-Teaching staff 
for the school based on the norms, guidelines 
and the recruitment rules prescribed by the 
Ministry from time to time in a time bound 
manner. 
9. He also brings to the notice of this Court 
the letter of Ministry of Finance dated 
12.01.2023, which was addressed to the 
Ministry of Tribal Affairs, whereby different 
posts were created and sanctioned in each of 
the EMRS Society, which are 740 in numbers
and also different temporary posts were created 
and sanctioned for the NESTS.
10. Mr. C.D. Bahuguna further submits that 
in this particular case, the advertisement was 
issued in the month of July 2023 and by 
referring this advertisement, Mr. Bahuguna 
submits that by virtue of Clause J & K under 
the Head note of Other Instructions, the NESTS
takes the task of recruitment which is contrary 
to the guidelines of 2020 framed by the 
Ministry of Tribal Affairs.
11. He also argued that the said 
advertisement was immediately challenged in 
the instant petition and the selection were 
stayed by this Court on 17.08.2023, and, 
thereafter, an MoU was entered on 22.08.2023 
in between the NESTS and the EMRS Society of 
Uttarakhand, which was signed by the Director,
Tribal Development Department of Government 
of Uttarakhand, on behalf of the Second Party 
and the Commissioner NESTS on behalf of the 
First Party. He submits that the Director is not 
competent to sign on behalf of the Second Party 
in view of the bye-laws of the EMRS Society 
since in Clause 8 of the bye-laws, the Director 
of the Tribal Welfare Department is not 
inducted even as a member of the Managing 
Committee and, in fact, the Principal Secretary 
of Social Welfare Department is the Ex-Officio 
Chairman and the District Social Welfare Officer is the Ex-Officio Secretary.
12. He further submits that entering with an 
MoU by the NESTS with the EMRS Society on 
22.08.2023 is nothing but contrary to the 
guidelines of 2020 which does not empower the 
NESTS to go with recruitment process and this 
power vests with the EMRS Society, and, 
furthermore, the guidelines does not empower 
the NESTS as well as to the EMRS entered with 
an MoU.
13. He submits that this MoU was entered in 
between the NESTS and the EMRS is nothing 
but in order to frustrate the instant petition 
which was filed before MoU.
14. Mr. C.D. Bahuguna argued that the MoU 
was entered with an ulterior purpose and 
motive and if the power was with the NESTS as 
per the guidelines, then there was no need to 
entered with an MoU.
15. He also submits by referring page No.556 
of the paper book, which is a DO Letter dated 
12.02.2020 of the Ministry of the Tribal Affairs 
address to all the Principal Secretaries of Tribal 
Welfare/Development Department of each of 
the States, wherein there is a reference of the 
draft rules for Teaching and Non-Teaching 
staffs under EMRS.
16. Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, by referring this 
letter, also pointed out that in this letter, it is 
clarified by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs that 
the State Government have to carry out the 
recruitment process for filling up various 
vacancies across the EMRSs of their States by 
following these RRs Rules.
17. Mr. C.D.Bahuguna further submits that 
even by virtue of D.O Letter dated 12.02.2020, 
the stand of the Ministry of Tribal Affairs,
Government of India is very clear that the 
power for recruitment vests with only the 
EMRSs of each of the States and not with the 
NESTS, which is in consonance with the 
guidelines of 2020.
18. Mr. C.D. Bahuguna by referring page 
No.544 of paper book submits that in the
previous selection of 2021 i.e. EMRS-Teaching Staff Selection Examination (ETSSE)-2021, 
EMRs conducted the recruitment process and 
in reference to which he refers clause 2.1,
which read as under:-
a. The recruitment of the teaching staff 
(Principal, Vice Principal, PGT and TGT) for 
EMRSs shall be state specific i.e. as per the 
State, where the schools are located, in 
accordance with the certificate of the State can 
apply.
b. National Testing Agency (NTA) is to 
conduct National Level EMRS-Teaching Staff 
Selection Exam.
c. Following the reservation roster of the 
respective State/UTs, NTA will provide State￾wise, post-wise, category-wise merit list to 
NESTS, MoTA.
d. State/UTs will conduct personality 
test/interviews and finalize the selection list of 
candidates.
e. States/UTs level interview panel will be 
constituted by the States/UTs that shall consist 
of various domain experts from the States and 
members from the NESTS/MoTA.
f. Appointment of teaching staff will be 
completed by the States/UTs following State￾specific reservation roster.
g. The State/ UT EMRS Society shall be 
responsible for final selection, implementation of 
reservation roster and posting of teachers in the 
State/UT.
h. The Selection of Principal, Vice Principal 
and PGT shall be by merit in which Computer￾Based Test (CBT) carries 160 marks and the 
personality tests/ interview carries 40 marks. 
However, for TGTs there won’t be a personality 
test/ interview and the merit shall be 
determined solely by the computer-based test of 
180 marks
19. Finally, Mr. Bahuguna concludes his 
arguments by submitting that even as per the 
guidelines of 2020, the power of recruitment 
was never given to the NESTS and this power 
always vests with the EMRS of the concerned 
State. He submits that only by virtue of MoU entered in between EMRS Society of 
Uttarakhand and the NESTS, the attempts are 
being made by the NESTS that this power of 
recruitment vests with the NESTS, but as a 
matter of fact, by virtue of the guidelines of 
2020, framed by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs of 
Government of India never empowered the 
NESTS to take the task of recruitment of each 
of the EMRS Societies of the State and submits 
that the power of recruitment always vests with 
the EMRS Society of the concerned State.
