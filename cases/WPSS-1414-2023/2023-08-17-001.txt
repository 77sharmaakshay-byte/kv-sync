COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.1414 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, learned Senior Advocate 
assisted by Mr. Ravindra S. Rawat, learned counsel 
for the petitioners.
2. Mr. V.K. Kapurwan, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State/respondent nos.1 & 2.
3. Mr. T.S. Bisht, learned Deputy Advocate 
General for the State/respondent no.4.
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
5. On the last date, this Court vide order dated 
11.08.2023 directed learned counsel for 
respondent nos.1 & 2 to seek instructions in the 
matter. 
6. Today, learned counsel for respondent nos.1 
& 2 submits that in view of the guidelines of EMRS, 
annexure-17 to the writ petition, Clause 9.1 (k) 
provides the recruitment of the teachers for the 
State can be entrusted to independent agency, if 
need be arise. 
7. Apart from this, it is also submitted by him 
that there may be some guidelines which entrust
the Central Society to advertise the post for 
recruitment of teaching and non-teaching staff in 
the Eklavya Model Residential School. But. at 
present, he is unable to show any such 
documentation on the subject despite having been 
given sufficient time to seek clear instructions. 
8. In nutshell, the case of the petitioner, as 
canvassed in the writ petition is that petitioners 
are working as TGT Teachers in Eklavya Adarsh 
Awaisya Vidhyalaya Kalsi, District Dehradun for the 
last five to seven years. By the impugned 
notification i.e. annexure-18 to the writ petition, 
the Central Society-respondent no.1 has issued an 2
advertisement for supplying the vacancies of the 
TGT Teachers in these schools and an online 
application form was issued for EMRS Staff 
Selection Exam-2023. 
9. Learned Senior Advocate for the petitioners 
submits that respondent no.1-Central Society has 
no right and locus to issue such an advertisement 
for filling up the posts of the teaching and non￾teaching staffs in the Eklavya Model Residential 
Schools across the country. In order to 
substantiate his argument, he submits on the 
strength of Clause 9.3 (e) and (f) of new 
guidelines of EMRS (annexure-17 to the writ 
petition) that the power for recruitment of 
teaching and non-teaching staffs of the school was 
given to the State Level Society i.e. EMRS Society￾respondent no.3.
10. Learned counsel for respondent nos.1 & 2 
prays and is granted three weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit.
11. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the 
petitioners to file rejoinder affidavit.
12. Let fresh steps be taken for service upon 
respondent nos.3 & 5 within a week.
13. Having heard the rival contentions of parties 
and on perusal of the guidelines relied upon by the 
learned Senior Advocate for the petitioners, I find 
force in the argument advanced by him. In this 
view of the matter, till the next date of listing, the 
selection process, pursuant to the online 
advertisement, annexure-18 to the writ petition is 
hereby stayed. 
14. List this matter on 17.10.2023.
15. Interim relief application (IA No.1/2023) 
stands disposed of accordingly.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
