3. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing

4. Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned counsel for 
respondent Nos.3 and 5.
5. Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, learned Senior 
Counsel for the petitioners argued that the 
National Education Society for Tribal 
Students (“for short NESTS”) is not
competent and authorized to initiate the 
selection process to fill up the post and in
reference to this, he placed reliance to 
Clause 4(h) and 4(i) of the Policy of 
Government of India of 2010. He submits 
that Policy of 2010 was further followed by 
another guidelines issued on 13.11.2020 
and as per Clause 9.3 (e) and (f), only the 
Society established by the State is 
competent to proceed with the recruitment 
process. He also submits that even by 
virtue of the Rules as framed, only the Society established by the State is 
competent enough to carry out the 
recruitment process for filling up vacancies 
across the EMRSs of the respective States, 
therefore, the commencement of 
recruitment process by the NESTS is 
wholly without jurisdiction. In addition to 
this Mr. Bahuguna submits that as per the 
undertaking, as given before the Division 
Bench, there is a categorical assertion of 
the NESTS that the recruitment process is 
being commenced to fill up the vacancies,
which are not occupied by the petitioners,
who are appointed on contractual basis. In 
addition to this, he submits that the 
advertisement was issued by the NESTS in 
July 2023, but in order to frustrate this 
petition an MoU was entered into between 
the NESTS and EMRS Society of 
Uttarakhand on 20.08.2023, that too after 
the issuance of the advertisement and by 
this MoU, the NESTS, took the task of 
recruitment process, to fill up vacancies 
communicated by State/UT Societies. 
Finally he submits that on or before the 
said MoU dated 22.05.2023, NESTS has no 
power to carry out recruitment process.
6. On the other side Mr. R. 
Venkataramani, learned Attorney General 
submits that in respect of the selections 
carried out by the NESTS, the petitioners 
have no locus standi and furthermore the 
contractual employees, who are the 
petitioners, herein, have been 
discontinued only due to expiry of the 
period their contractual appointment.
7. Be that as it may, in view of 
statement as given before the Division 
Bench, in Special Appeal No. 331 of 2023 
that the recruitment process in question is not being made in respect of posts held by 
the contractual employees and the NESTS 
has nothing to do with their continuation 
or discontinuation, however, the 
recruitment is being conducted in such a 
way as not to disturb the current 
contractual Teachers the interim order 
passed on 12.11.2024 is modified as 
under:-
(i) The respondents are permitted 
to appoint and give joining to the selected 
candidates, who have been declared to be 
selected pursuant to the selection process 
held by NESTS.
(ii) The petitioners, who are the 
contractual employees shall not be 
discontinued, since they are claiming their 
regularization and if they are discontinued,
it will affect their claim for regularisation.
(iii) The appointment made 
pursuant to the selection carried out by 
the NESTS and the continuation of the 
contractual Teachers shall be subject to 
the final outcome of all these petitions. 
8. Put up these matters for final 
disposal on 10.03.2026.
