Sri Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Shashi Kant Shandilya, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State- respondent no.1.
3. Mr. Ashish Joshi, learned counsel for respondent 
no.2 through video conferencing.
4. Mr. Rajendra Arya, proxy counsel for Mr. Neeraj 
Garg, learned counsel for respondent no.3.
5. By the instant writ petition, petitioner is 
challenging the advertisement dated 13.09.2024, 
issued by respondent no.2, whereby the post, which 
the petitioner is occupying since last more than ten 
years, i.e. Private Secretary, has been advertised. 
Petitioner is praying for regularization of his service, 
pursuant to the judgment passed by the Division 
Bench of this Court in Writ Petition (PIL) No.116 of 
2018, dated 12.11.2018.
6. It is further submitted that the said judgment 
was assailed before the Hon’ble Supreme Court in Civil 
Appeal Nos.7181-7317 of 2021, and the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court dismissed the civil appeal vide its 
judgment dated 15.10.2024.
7. Learned counsels for the respondents pray for 
and are granted four weeks’ time to file counter￾affidavit.
8. Two weeks’ time, thereafter, is granted to learned counsel for the petitioner to file rejoinder￾affidavit.
9. List on 30.12.2024.
10. Taking into consideration that the petitioner is 
working since last more than ten years on the post of 
Private Secretary, and has now become over age, the 
respondent- University is directed to proceed with the 
selection process pursuant to the impugned 
advertisement, however, result for the post, which the 
petitioner is occupying, shall not be declared without 
leave of the Court and the petitioner shall be permitted 
to continue to work on the post, which he is holding.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
