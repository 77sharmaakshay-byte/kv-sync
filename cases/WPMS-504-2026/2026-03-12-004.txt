5. This writ petition has been filed by the petitioner under 
Article 226 of the Constitution of India on the brief premise 
that the land which was earlier acquired by the respondent, 
National Highways Division, P.W.D., District Rudraprayag, for 
the widening of Highway No. 107 (New No. 109) on the 
Gaurikund to Rudraprayag stretch, is not being utilized by 
respondent no. 4 for the purpose for which it was acquired,
therefore, the petitioner seeks that the said land be returned to 
the petitioner for her use.
6. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner that 
the petitioner is a widow and a landless woman.
7. Learned counsel for the respondent(s) prays for and is 
granted four weeks’ time for filing counter affidavit(s).
8. List this case on 20.04.2026.
9. In the meantime, notice dated 14.01.2026 issued by 
respondent no.4 shall remain stayed.
