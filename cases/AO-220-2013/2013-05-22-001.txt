Heard.
This appeal is directed against the award
dated 28.02.2013, passed by Motor Accident
Claims Tribunal/Additional District Judge
Vikas Nagar, District Dehradun, in Motor
Accident Claim Case No. 205 of 2006 whereby
the Tribunal has awarded ` 20,81,872/- as
compensation, to the claimants. It is further
directed by the Tribunal that the amount shall
be paid by the insurance company which can be
recovered from the appellant Surendra Singh
Sahani (owner of the vehicle). It is found by the
Tribunal that the vehicle was plying without
valid permit. 
Admit the appeal.
Summon the record of the Tribunal.
Issue notice to respondents.
Having heard learned counsel for the
appellant, and after going through the
impugned award, as an interim measure, it is
directed that if the appellant deposits ` 5 lacs
out of the sum awarded by the Tribunal, before
it, within a period of 30 days, from today, the
recovery from him by the Tribunal shall remain
stayed till the next date of listing. (Stay
Application No. 4945 of 2013 stands disposed
of). 
List after service of notice on the
respondents alongwith the connected appeal, if
any. 
 (P.C.Pant,J.)
