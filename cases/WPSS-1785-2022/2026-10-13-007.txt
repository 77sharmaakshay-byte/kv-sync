4. On perusal of the order impugned, it reveals that
order rejecting the claim of the petitioner for
compassionate appointment cannot be sustained on
two counts. Firstly, offer of appointment to the other
family members of the deceased employee in the same
establishment is not regular rather it is contractual one
and secondly, grant of family pension to the wife of the
deceased employee cannot curtail her right to get
appointment on compassionate ground. Thus, prima
facie, this Court is of the view that rejection of the
candidature of the petitioner for compassionate
appointment is unjust and improper.
5. List on 28.10.2026.
6. On the date, Mr. Lalit Miglani, learned counsel
for the respondents shall address why on these two
grounds the respondents reject the claim of the
petitioner.
