Sri Vipin Sanghi, C.J.
Sri Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Ms. Priyanka Agarwal, learned counsel for the 
appellant.
Issue notice.
Ms. Charanjeet Kaur, learned counsel appears and 
accepts notice on behalf of respondent no. 1. 
Let notice issue to respondent no. 2, returnable on 
16.08.2023, by all permitted modes, including 
electronically. 
All the parties shall file their respective written 
submissions on the question of law, which arises for 
consideration, namely, whether the appellant can claim 
interest for the period, from when the possession of land 
was taken (which is after the date of the award) till the 
date of actual payment, in proceedings under Section 
3G(5) of the National Highways Act, 1956, or such a relief 
can be sought in proceedings under Article 226 of the 
Constitution of India, since the remedy under Section 
3G(5) is not available for claiming interest for the 
aforesaid period. 
The written submissions be filed, along with the 
relevant case law(s) within four weeks. 
List on 16.08.2023.
