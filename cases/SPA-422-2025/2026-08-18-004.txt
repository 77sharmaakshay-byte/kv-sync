2. Mr. Parikshit Saini, learned counsel for the

3. The present intra-court appeal is directed 
against the order of learned Single Judge dated 
06.09.2024, in WPSS No. 01 of 2024, whereby 
the review application filed by the appellants for 
review of the order passed in the writ petition 
has been rejected, as well as the judgment and 
order dated 06.08.2018, in the writ petition.
4. Learned Single Judge has disposed of the 
writ petition in terms of the judgment in a 
previous writ petition, bearing WPMS No. 1820 
of 2017, dated 06.08.2018, after recording the 
statement of learned State counsel that the facts 
of the case were identical.
5. Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned Deputy Advocate General, appearing for the appellants, 
submits that the present case is not identical to 
the earlier case. In support of his submission he 
has invited our attention towards the order 
dated 28.02.2014, passed by State Government, 
whereby 16 institutions were taken on grant-in￾aid and, in respect of 08 institutions, the posts 
were also sanctioned. In respect of the 
institutions from Sl. No. 11 to 16 wherein the
name of the respondent-institution figures at Sl.
No. 13, it was provided vide Clause 3, that 
separate orders would be passed for sanction of 
the posts in these institutions after receipt of the 
proposal. It is submitted that in the case of the 
respondent-institution there was only in￾principle approval to the grant-in-aid but since
posts were not sanctioned, therefore, the case of 
the respondent-institution was not covered by 
the judgment rendered in the previous case of 
Manavta Higher Primary School, Gandhi Nagar, 
Bindukhatta, District Nainital.
6. Mr. Parikshit Saini, learned counsel 
appearing for the respondent-institution has 
placed for our perusal an order passed on
28.02.2014 by which the department had 
sanctioned 07 posts in the respondent￾institution. Thus, his submission is that the plea
on basis of which distinction is being drawn, is 
factually not correct.7. Learned State counsel seeks time to 
ascertain whether the contention of learned 
counsel for the respondent that in respect of 
respondent-institution posts were sanctioned on 
28.02.2014 i.e. the date on which in-principle 
approval of grant-in-aid was granted, is correct 
or not.
8. Accordingly, adjourned.
