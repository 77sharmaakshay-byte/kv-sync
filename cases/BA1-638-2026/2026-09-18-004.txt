Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Vikas Kumar Guglani and Mr. Shariq 
Khurshid, learned legal aid counsel for the appellant.
2. Mr. Himanshu Sain, learned A.G.A. for the State.
3. The instant jail appeal is preferred on behalf of 
the appellant ‘Karan’ S/o Late Shri Jai Singh against 
the judgment and order passed by the Trial Court 
dated 04.07.2025 passed in Special Session Trial No. 
487 of 2022, whereby, the appellant herein is convicted 
for the offence punishable under section 363 of IPC 
with the maximum sentence of 7 years imprisonment 
with the fine of Rs. 10,000/- and in default of payment 
of fine he has to further undergo one month additional 
imprisonment though he has been acquitted for the 
offences punishable under sections 366, 376(2) of IPC
and section 5/6 of POCSO Act.
4. On the previous date the Coordinate Bench 
summoned the entire record, which has now been 
received and the paper book is also prepared. As per 
the order sheet the appeal has yet not been admitted 
and it is a regular appeal against the order of 
conviction.
5. Admit.
6. Heard on bail application.
7. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
appellant that the present appellant was arrested in 
the said crime on 18.05.2022 and since then he is 
languishing in jail and therefore he aleady served 4 years 3 months 27 days as on 16.09.2026, therefore, he 
be released on bail.
8. On the other side, Mr. Sain, learned A.G.A., have 
not disputed that the appellant already served half of 
the maximum sentence as awarded by the Trial Court.
9. After hearing the submissions as above and 
taking into consideration that the appellant already 
served more than half of the sentence as awarded by 
the Trial Court, therefore, this court is of the view that 
the appellant herein.
10. Accordingly, without expressing any opinion on 
the merit of the case, the bail application is allowed.
11. Let the appellant ‘Karan’ be released on bail on 
furnishing his personal bond and two reliable sureties 
each of the like amount to the satisfaction of the court 
concerned during the pendency of instant jail appeal.
12. List this appeal for final hearing in the month of 
December, 2026.
