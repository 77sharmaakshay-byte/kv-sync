WPSS No.1845 of 2012
Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J. 
Mr. K.P. Upadhyaya, Senior Advocate assisted 
by Mr. Hemant Pant, Advocate, for the petitioners. 
 Mr. Suyash Pant, Brief Holder, for the State. 
 Impleadment application (CLMA No.14092 of 
2019) is allowed. 
 Petitioners are directed to implead “Uttarakhand 
Public Service Commission, Haridwar through its 
Secretary” as respondent no.71 within a period of one 
week. 
 A Coordinate Bench of this Court vide order 
dated 16.03.2017 had directed the petitioners to 
implead all the affected persons as respondents in the 
matter. 
 Learned Senior Counsel for the petitioners 
submits that affected persons in this case would be 
around 3,000. 
 According to the petitioners, this would not be 
possible for the petitioners to make them party by 
individual notices and therefore the learned Senior 
Counsel for the petitioners prays that the petitioners 
may be allowed to serve the affected persons by way of 
a general notice/publication stating about the pendency 
of the writ petition before this Court. 
 Purely in the interest of justice, the petitioners are 
allowed to make a publication in two newspapers 
having a wide circulation in the State of Uttarakhand. 
 Let the same be done within a period of three 
weeks. 
 List this matter after three weeks in the daily 
cause list. 
 
 
 (Sudhanshu Dhulia, J.)
