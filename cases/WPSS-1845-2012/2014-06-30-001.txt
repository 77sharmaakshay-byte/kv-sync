IN THE HIGH COURT OF UTTARAKHAND AT NAINITAL
Writ Petition No.1845 (SS) of 2012 
Mukesh Bahuguna & others …. Petitioners
Versus 
State of Uttarakhand and others …Respondents 
 
Present: Mr. Manoj Tewari, Senior Advocate assisted by Mr. Alok Mahra, 
Advocate, present for the petitioner. 
Mr. N.P. Shah, learned Standing Counsel present for the 
State/respondent nos. 1 & 2. 
Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J. (Oral)
1. In this matter a pure question of law is involved 
which relates to inter se seniority between the Lecturers in 
Government Colleges. The question is whether seniority 
has to be counted from the date of promotions or from the 
date on which such vacancy arose. 
2. The petitioners were appointed as lecturer in 
Government Inter Colleges in the year 2005-06, whereas 
the private respondents who are now shown to be senior to 
the petitioners and who were working as Assistant Teacher 
(L.T. Grade) were promoted as Lecturer in the year 2010-
11. They have been shown senior to the petitioners as 
according to the State-respondents the post on which they 
were promoted fell vacant prior to 2005-06 i.e. prior to the 
date when the petitioners were actually appointed as 
Lecturer. Therefore, the question is as to what should be 
the actual date for determination of seniority in such a 
case. 
3. Notices were served to a large number of such 
lecturers. The petitioners, in any case, impleaded thirty one 
such lecturers to whom notices were issued through 
registered post, acknowledgement of which stands received, 
but no one has put in appearance on behalf of the private 2
respondents. The State, has however filed its counter 
affidavit stating that the private respondents were given 
seniority over the petitioners as the post of lecturers on 
which they were promoted fell vacant prior to the time 
when the petitioners were given appointment. 
4. The petitioners now contend that promotion to 
the post of Principal in Higher Secondary School is under 
consideration and promotions will be made as per the 
seniority, which is presently under challenge. 
5. In view of the fact as stated by the learned Senior 
Advocate Mr. Manoj Tiwari before this Court as well as the 
fact that in spite of service, the private respondents have 
failed to put in their appearance, as an interim measure, it 
is hereby directed that the respondents/State not to give 
effect to the seniority list, which is presently under 
challenge before this Court in the present writ petition, in 
any future promotions. 
6. List this matter after three weeks in daily cause 
list. Private respondents may file their counter affidavits in 
the meanwhile. 
7. Stay application (CLMA) No.14108 of 2012 
stands disposed of. 
 (Sudhanshu Dhulia, J.)
