In this writ petition, the dispute is with 
regard to inter se seniority between the directly 
appointed Lecturer in the Government Inter 
College and those who were appointed on 
promotion. The writ petition has been filed by 
those Lecturers who were directly appointed. A 
large number of private respondents who were 
appointed on promotion basis have already 
been made party. 
Now, an impeadment application no. 
14109 of 2021 has been moved on behalf of 
the respondents who are appearing in the case 
with the averments therein that almost 3000 
lecturers (between serial number 5154 to 8792 
in the seniority list except those who have 
already retired) would be affected by the 
judgment that may be passed in the instant 
writ petition. Therefore, they all be impleaded 
as party in the instant writ petition
Heard on impleadment application.
Learned counsel for the private 
respondent nos.3 to 63 would submit that the 
affected persons should be made party and 
they should be afforded an opportunity of 
hearing before any order is passed in the 
instant petition. 
On the other hand, learned counsel for 
the petitioners would submit that earlier also, 
a similar kind of impleadment application was 
moved, which was allowed, but, subsequently, 
the Court on 15.10.2019 directed the 
petitioner to make a publication in two 
newspapers having a wide circulation in the 
State of Uttarakhand so as to serve the affected persons by way of a general notice 
about the pendency of the writ petition before 
this Court. It is argued that the notice was 
duly published and the Court on 06.03.2020 
had already held that the service is sufficient. 
In fact, the notice was published in a 
newspaper on 04.03.2019 which is on record 
alongwith the affidavit of service that has been 
filed by the petitioner. By way of this notice, it 
was informed as hereunder;
“All such regularly promoted lecturers 
(promoted in the year 2010 and 2011) who 
may adversely be affected by the challenge in 
the writ petition are hereby called to appear in 
this Court in person or through counsel duly 
instructed by them on or before 13.11.2019 at 
10:00 o’clock forenoon to answer the petition, 
failing which the said petition shall be heard 
and determined in their absence.
As stated, service by way of this 
publication was held to be sufficient by this 
Court on 06.03.2020. Now, after this 
publication, no question survives for 
impleading any party by the appearing parties. 
Undoubtedly, if any of the affected party comes 
forward to get himself impleaded, that 
application may perhaps be considered, but, 
the application by the private respondent, as 
has been filed, cannot be entertained in view of 
what was ordered by this Court on 15.10.2019 
and what was held on 06.03.2020 after 
publication of the notice in the newspapers. 
Therefore, the impleadment application has no 
merit and it deserves to be dismissed.
The impleadement application is 
dismissed.
Pleadings have already been exchanged.
List this matter in the week commencing 
20.09.2021 for final hearing.
