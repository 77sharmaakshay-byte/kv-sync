This case relates to inter se seniority 
of Lecturers. It is between two groups, one, 
those who were promoted from Assistant 
Teacher, LT Grade and those, who were 
directly appointed.
It is the case of the petitioners that 
pursuant to the requisition and an 
advertisement of the year 2003, they 
appeared in the examination for appointment 
to the post of Lecturer and were appointed 
between 10.10.2005 and 20.11.2006. Till 
then, there was no dispute. It so happened 
that in the year 2010 and 2011, the 
Department sent a requisition to the 
Uttarakhand Public Service Commission for 
promotional exercise to the post of Lecturer 
and promotion orders were issued on 
06.08.2010 and 20.07.2011. It was with 
regard to the requisition sent on 30.03.2010 
and 25.04.2011 respectively.
The grievance of the petitioners begin 
from the point, when it is stated that the 
persons appointed by promotion were given 
seniority from some year prior to the
appointment of the petitioners, thereby 
placing the petitioners below such Lecturers, 
who were promoted in the year 2010 and 
2010.
It is the case of respondent no.2 on 
behalf of the State that the Assistant Teachers, who were working as the Assistant 
Teachers, L.T. Grade were temporarily 
promoted on the post of Lecturers pursuant 
to the applicable Rules and prior to that they 
were already working, as Lecturer on adhoc 
basis, pursuant to their adhoc promotion 
made between 2001 and 2007.
During the course of argument, 
learned counsel for the petitioners raised the 
following arguments:-
(i) All the Lecturers, who were 
appointed by way of promotion on 
06.08.2010 and 22.07.2011 were 
not earlier working as adhoc 
Lecturers. Some of them were 
working as adhoc Lecturers, but 
some of them were appointed for 
the first time as Lecturers;
(ii) Some of the Lecturers, who were 
promoted in the year 2010 and 
2011, even did not have the 
requisite educational qualification 
of post graduation in the 
particular subject when they were 
promoted as adhod Lecturer
and/or;
(iii) The period for which, the adhoc
Lecturer had worked cannot be 
counted for the purpose of their 
seniority because there 
appointment as an adhoc Lecturer 
was not as per Rule 16 of the 
1992 applicable Rules.
When asked, learned State counsel is 
not in a position to tell these things. There is 
no specific denial of the averments as made 
in para 22 of the writ petition with regard to 
the status of the adhoc Lecturers. Therefore, 
the Court directs the respondent no.2 to file a 
counter affidavit giving the following details:-
(i) How many Lecturers were 
appointed by way of promotion in the year 2010 and 2011. Alongwith 
it a breakup should given as to 
how many of them had already
been working as adhoc Lecturers?
(ii) How such adhoc Lecturers were 
appointed as adhoc Lecturers? 
What procedure was adopted? If 
more than one procedure was 
adopted, the procedure that was 
adopted with regard to the number 
of candidates should be revealed 
and;
(iii)Whether all such Lecturers, who 
had been working as adhoc 
Lecturer prior to their appointment 
as Lecturer in the years 2010 and 
2011 had the requisite 
qualification of Post Graduation 
when they were appointed as 
adhoc Lecturer?
Let a short affidavit be filed by the 
State within next four weeks to explain their 
point.
