4. Claim of the petitioner is pending since 
2019, which is the subject matter of the 
instant petition and it reveals from the 
records that every time either the State is 
shifting its burden to the Commission or the 
Commission is shifting its burden to the 
State Government.
5. On the previous date, it has been 
apprised by the Commission that the entire 
record of the selection has been sent to the 
Government, in such an eventuality, since 
the entire record was sent to the Government 
by the Commission, this Court on 
01.05.2025 passed the following order:-
5. In the meantime, the Secretary to the 
Government look into the matter and 
take final decision for appointment of the 
petitioner in view of the letter of the 
Secretary dated 09.08.2019, wherein 
the Commission was asked to send 
additional list against the vacant post, 
which is shown in the chart at sl. No. 13 
(Annexure 10 to the petition) wherein 
there is a reference one post of Lecturer 
(Home Science) reserved for OBC 
woman. In addition to this, the Secretary 
may also examine annexure 12 wherein 
at sl. No. 13 there is a reference of Home 
Science (Women Branch) and in a note it is mentioned that against one post, 
reserved for OBC under 25%, no 
candidate is available.
6. The contention of the petitioner is that 
the petitioner is next candidate under 
OBC category.
7. Let Secretary-respondent no. 1 may 
take decision after takingconsideration 
these two documents and apprise this 
court on the next date failing which this 
Court will initiate further proceedings on 
an application moved by the petitioner 
under Article 215 of the Constitution of 
India.”

On perusal of Para No.3 it reveals that 
the Secretary to the Government has not 
examined the entire record and despite court’s order dated 01.05.2025 again the 
matter has been referred to the Commission. 
It appears that the Commission as well as the 
Department concerned are repeatedly 
misleading to the Court by shifting the 
burden.
This appears to be a wilful disobedience
of the orders passed by this Court in this
petition, therefore, in such an eventuality, 
since an application has been moved under 
Section 215 of the Constitution of India for 
initiating a contempt proceeding, this Court 
is now issuing the notices to the Secretary to 
the Government as well as to the Secretary of
the Commission to show cause as to why the 
contempt proceeding be not drawn against 
them.
