4. Pursuant to the order dated 
22.05.2025, this Court after observing that 
the respondents wilfully flouted the direction 
of this Court, the notices were issued to the 
Secretary as well as to the Secretary of the 
Commission to show cause as to why the 
contempt proceeding be not drawn against 
them.
Against this order, the Commission 
preferred a Special Appeal, however, the 
special appeal was disposed of with a liberty 
to the Commission to raise their grievances 
before this Court.
Since this Court has already observed 
in the order that there is wilful disobedience 
on the part of the State as well as the 
Commission, therefore, in such an 
eventuality, one week’s time is granted to 
both the respondents to file their response.
5. Put up this matter on 03.07.2025.
