COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.455 of 2020
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, learned counsel
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. P.S. Bisht and Mr. R.S. Bisht, 
learned Additional Chief Standing 
Counsel for the State.
3. Mr. Ashish Joshi, learned counsel 
for the Commission-UKPSC through V.C.
4. Brief facts of the case are that the 
petitioner participated in the recruitment 
process held by the Uttarakhand Public 
Service Commission under OBC/(UF) 
Category for the post of Lecturer (Home￾Science). The two candidates, namely, 
Krishna Jayara and petitioner both
belongs to OBC/UF category placed at 
serial Nos.7 and 15 in the List containing 
marks of each candidates prepared by the 
Commission (Annexure No.2) at page 
No.43.
The grievance of the petitioner is 
that the candidate placed at serial No.7-
Krishna Jayara, in the list, did not turn 
up, despite this, the petitioner was not 
recommended for selection though she 
was next on merit under OBC/UF 
Category. Subsequently, bunch of 
petitions were filed before this Court and 
the Coordinate Bench of this Court 
passed judgment on 08.10.2018, the copy 
of which is annexed at Annexure No.8. In 
this judgment, this Court directed 
UKPSC, Haridwar, to forward the list of 
waiting list candidates to the Government 
without any delay and the respondents 
were also directed to give appointment 
from the waiting list candidates within 
two months. Thereafter, despite several 
reminders, the Recruiting Body-UKPSC 
have not sent any list. Consequently, the 
Secretary to the Government, by letter 
dated 04.06.2019, request to the Commission to send the additional select 
list (waiting list) of both the branches i.e.
General Branch and Women Branch 
subject/category wise. This letter is 
annexed as Annexure No.10 page 79, 
wherein, for Women Branch in the subject 
of Home Science, one vacancy was shown
for OBC (UR) and one vacancy was shown 
for General and in total two posts were 
shown, against which, the Commission 
was requested to send the waiting list.
5. Thereafter, the Director Secondary 
Education also send another letter dated 
10.06.2019, wherein list of those 
candidates, who have not turned up to 
join, is also enclosed and under Women 
Branch, the name of Krishna Jayara is 
shown at Serial No.30. Thereafter, in 
compliance of both the letters i.e. letter 
dated 04.06.2019 sent by the Secretary 
and letter dated 11.6.2019 sent by the 
Director Secondary Education, i.e. 
Annexure Nos.10 and 11 respectively, the 
Commission forwarded the waiting list,
which is annexed as Annexure No.12 at 
page 86.
In Women Branch for Home 
Science, only the name of one candidate, 
namely, Premlata, was sent under 
General/UF Category, however, by 
putting note under Clause-13, it is 
mentioned that against one post, no 
candidate under OBC/UF Category is 
available under 25%
Thereafter, the Government, being 
not satisfied with the waiting list sent by 
the Commission, sent another letter on 
09.08.2019 (Annexure-13) which was 
further followed by another letter dated 
27.11.2019 (Annexure-14), despite this, 
the Commission sit over the matter and 
has not sent any further list.
6. Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, learned counsel 
for the petitioner submits that once the 
Secretary to the Government clearly 
mentioned in its letter dated 04.06.2019 
by giving description of two posts one for General Category and other one for OBC
(UF) Category, deliberately the 
Commission has not sent the name of 
candidate against remaining one vacancy 
under OBC (UF) Category. In reference to 
the reasoning, as given under the head of 
Note, he submits the reasoning as given
are wholly unwarranted, particularly 
when earlier name of two candidates 
under OBC/UF Category were shown and 
since one candidate has not turn up,
therefore, the petitioner next on merit,
should be recommended for selection
under OBC (UF) Category.
7. Mr. Ashish Joshi, learned counsel 
for the Commission vehemently opposed 
the arguments as advanced by learned 
counsel for the petitioner and submits 
that the Commission is bound by the limit 
of 25% as per Rule 15. The objections, as 
raised by Mr. Ashish Joshi, learned 
counsel for the Commission, is out rightly 
rejected by observing that one vacancy 
was earmarked for OBC/UF Category, 
which is clearly evident from the letter of 
the Secretary dated 04.06.2019 and it is
also undisputed fact that the candidate 
next above the petitioner, did not turn up, 
therefore, against one vacancy which was 
earmarked for OBC/UF Category, the 
Petitioner should be recommended.
8. Mr. P.S. Bisht, learned Additional 
Chief Standing Counsel for the State fairly 
pointed out that the Secretary to the 
Government also subsequently by letter 
dated 27.11.2019 clearly pointed out that 
the limit of 25% have no bearing since the 
issue has been dealt with by High Court, 
therefore, the Commission is under legal 
obligation to send the list recommending 
the name of the candidate.
9. Mr. P.S. Bisht further pointed out 
that in this letter, the Secretary clearly 
pointed out that the vacancies are still 
vacant, however, despite clear cut 
clarification of the Government in its 
letter dated 27.11.2019, the Commissionhas not recommended the name of the 
petitioner though she is the only 
candidate to be selected under OBC/UF 
Category.
10. Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, learned counsel 
for the petitioner pointed out that as per 
the advertisement only one post of 
Lecturer (Home Science) for OBC/UF 
Women Category was advertised and the 
petitioner is the only left out to be 
recommended for selection, but despite 
the fact that the vacancy is available 
under OBC/UF category and furthermore 
despite repeated request of the 
Government the name of the petitioner
has not been recommended.
11. I found force on the submission as 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
12. Put up this matter on 10.12.2024 as 
a first case. On that day, the Secretary to 
the Commission shall join the proceeding 
through V.C.
