2. The present Criminal Revision has been 
preferred by the revisionist assailing the 
judgment and order dated 21.04.2026 passed by 
learned Principal Judge, Family Court, Udham 
Singh Nagar in Case No.133 of 2020, whereby 
maintenance at the rate of ₹15,000/- per month
has been awarded in favour of respondent no.1-
wife and ₹10,000/- per month has been awarded 
in favour of respondent no.2-son under the 
provisions of Section 125 of the Code of Criminal 
Procedure, 1973.
3. Learned counsel for the revisionist submits 
that the marriage between the revisionist and 
respondent no.1 was solemnized on 14.04.2012. 
It is contended that, on the date of the alleged 
marriage, respondent no.1 was already legally 
married to one Ashok Kumar Chawla and the said 
marriage was subsisting, as no decree of divorce 
had been obtained prior to her marriage with the 
revisionist. He further submits that this material 
fact was deliberately concealed by respondent 
no.1 while instituting the proceedings under 
Section 125 Cr.P.C. It is argued that, during her 
cross-examination conducted on 17.01.2026, 
respondent no.1 admitted that no legal divorce 
had been obtained from her earlier husband before solemnizing the marriage with the 
revisionist. On the strength of the aforesaid 
admission, learned counsel submits that the 
alleged marriage between the revisionist and 
respondent no.1 is void in the eyes of law and, 
consequently, respondent no.1 is not entitled to 
claim maintenance under Section 125 Cr.P.C. It 
is further submitted that learned Family Court 
failed to appreciate this vital aspect while passing 
the impugned order.
4. Having heard learned counsel for the 
revisionist and upon considering the submissions 
advanced, this Court is of the prima facie view 
that the matter requires consideration. 
Accordingly, till the next date of listing, the 
operation and effect of the impugned judgment 
and order dated 21.04.2026, insofar as it relates 
to the award of maintenance of ₹15,000/- per 
month in favour of respondent no.1-wife, shall 
remain stayed. However, the revisionist shall 
continue to pay maintenance at the rate of 
₹10,000/- per month to respondent no.2-son, as 
directed by learned Family Court, without default, 
subject to the final outcome of the present 
criminal revision.
5. Stay application (I.A. No. 1 of 2026) stands 
disposed.
6. Issue notices to respondent no.1, returnable 
at an early date.
7. List after service report is received. Counter 
affidavit, if any, be filed in the meantime.
