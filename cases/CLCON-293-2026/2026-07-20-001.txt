2. This contempt petition has been filed under Section 12

directed the respondents-defendants not to create any third 
party interest in respect of the property in-question.

that the respondents-contemnors are in full knowledge of

petitioner along with an application in the trial court.

matter of the case-property, was sold by the respondents￾contemnors through a registered sale deed on 12.02.2026 in

6. This Court prima facie finds the submission of learned 
counsel for the petitioner to be correct.
