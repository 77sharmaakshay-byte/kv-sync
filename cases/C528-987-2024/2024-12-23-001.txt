COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C528 No.987 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Raunak Pant, Advocate for the applicants.
2. By means of the present C528 application, the applicant 
has put to challenge the summoning/cognizance order dated 
06.11.2024 passed by learned Judicial Magistrate/Civil Judge
(J.D.), Vikasnagar, District Dehradun in Criminal Complaint 
Case No.249 of 2024, Shri. Ashok Mehta Vs. Shri Ajit 
Gulabchand & others, under Sections 420 and 406 of IPC, 
pending in the court of learned Judicial Magistrate/Civil Judge 
(J.D.), Vikasnagar, District Dehradun, qua, the applicant.
3. Applicants are the Chairman, General Manager and 
Retd. Project Manager of M/s Hindustan Construction Pvt. Ltd.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the applicants that 
a contract was given M/s Mehta Construction Company for 
construction of various works in Chamera Hydro Electric 
Power Plant situated in Himachal Pradesh.
5. M/s Mehta Construction Company executed the work 
from the years 2007 to 2013, but in the year 2013 a non 
performance letter was issued by the applicant. For almost ten 
years M/s Mehta Construction Company did not raise any 
dispute and finally in the year 2024 a complaint was filed 
against the applicants before learned Judicial Magistrate/Civil 
Judge (J.D.), Vikasnagar, District Dehradun.
6. It is further contended by learned counsel for the 
applicants that the dispute is with regard to a contract matter 
which contained the arbitration clause therein.
7. If there is a dispute with regard to the contract, it could 
have been referred to the Arbitrator by M/s Mehta Construction 
Company, but the criminal complaint case was filed against the 2
applicants in order to exert pressure upon them.
8. Learned counsel for the applicant further submits that it 
is nothing but a kind of pressure tactics upon the applicant.
9. Having considered the submission of learned counsel for 
the applicants and on perusal of the record, this Court is 
inclined to interfere in the matter. Matter needs deliberation.
10. Issue notice to respondent, returnable within six weeks.
11. Steps to be taken within seven days.
12. List this case on 02.04.2025.
13. In the meantime, proceedings of Criminal Complaint 
Case No.249 of 2024, Shri Ashok Mehta Vs. Shri Ajit 
Gulabchand & others, under Sections 420 and 406 of IPC, 
pending in the court of learned Judicial Magistrate/Civil Judge 
(J.D.), Vikasnagar, District Dehradun, qua, the applicant, shall 
remain stayed.
14. Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2024) stands disposed-off 
accordingly.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
