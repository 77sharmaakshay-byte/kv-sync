COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.1769 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Pankaj Tangwan, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned Standing Counsel for the 
State.
3. Mr. C.K. Sharma, learned counsel for the 
respondent/Commission.
4. Petitioner is aggrieved by the order dated 
13.09.2023 (Annexure no.6 to the writ petition), 
whereby name of the petitioner was not included in the 
provisional recommended list of candidates for the 
post of ‘Forester’.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted 
that pursuant to the advertisement issued by the 
respondent-Commission, he applied for the post of 
Forester; being dependent of freedom fighter, but due 
to non-availability of the Certificate of Dependent of 
Freedom Fighter, the same could not be uploaded. 
Subsequently, the respondent-Commission has itself 
issued a notification on 25.06.2021, whereby the 
candidates were given an opportunity to rectify the 
deficiency in their application form, on the payment of 
₹30/-
6. It is further contended by the petitioner that he 
deposited a fee of ₹30/- and submitted the Certificate 
of Dependent of Freedom Fighter, issued to him, on 
09.07.2020 (Annexure no.1 to the writ petition). But 
name of the petitioner was not found placed in the 
provisional list of successful candidates, who have
been recommended for appointment. Feeling aggrieved 
petitioner moved this Court by filing this writ petition.
7. On 20.09.2023, learned counsel for the 
respondent/Commission was asked to get instructions 
in the matter.2
8. Today, on instructions, learned counsel for the 
respondent/Commission submits that he is ready with 
the written instruction, which has been passed on to 
this Court. According to the learned counsel for the 
Commission the last date of submission of the form 
was 10.02.2020, but the Certificate of Dependent of 
Freedom Fighter issued to the petitioner was of 
09.07.2020 and it is only for that reason, name of the 
petitioner was not recommended for appointment.
However, it is not in dispute that the petitioner has 
secured more marks than the recommended candidates.
9. Learned counsel for the respondent-Commission 
prays for and is granted six weeks’ time to file counter 
affidavit.
10. Further two weeks’ time is granted to the 
learned counsel for the petitioner for filing rejoinder 
affidavit.
11. Issue notice to respondent nos. 4 and 5.
12. Steps to be taken within three days.
13. List this case on 14.12.2023.
14. Registry is directed to show the name of Mr. 
C.K. Sharma, learned counsel for the respondent￾Commission, in the next cause list.
15. In the meantime, one seat on the post of Forester 
in the category of dependent of Freedom Fighter, 
General, shall be kept vacant.
16. Interim Relief Application (IA No.1 of 2023) 
stands disposed-off accordingly.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
