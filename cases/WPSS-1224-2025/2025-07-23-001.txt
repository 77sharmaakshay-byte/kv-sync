 Heard on Stay Application (IA) No.1 of 
2025.
 It is the claim of the petitioner that he 
applied for the post of Assistant Teacher (L.T.) 
Computer Education in response to the 
advertisement issued by the respondent 
no.2/Uttarakhand Subordinate Services 
Selection Commission, Dehradun (“the 
Commission”). He qualified the Written 
Examination. He was awaiting for the 
documents verification, but he never received 
any information. Suddenly, he came to know 
that his candidature has been rejected on the 
ground that he did not appear for documents 
verification on 10.06.2025. It is the case of 
the petitioner that the notice was never 
published. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that in a similarly situated case, this Court 
has been pleased to pass an interim order 
yesterday. 
 This fact is admitted to the learned 
counsel for the Commission that this Court 
under the similar circumstances has passed 
an interim order directing the respondents to 
keep one post vacant.
 Having considered, as an interim measure, 
till the next date of listing this Court directs that 
one post of the Assistant Teacher (L.T.) Computer 
Education, on which, the petitioner applied, shall 
be kept vacant. 
 Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
