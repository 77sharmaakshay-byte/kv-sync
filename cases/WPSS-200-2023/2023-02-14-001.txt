Mr. Ramji Shrivastava, learned counsel

The petitioners in this case complains
that from the year 2011 to 2020, there was no 
determination of vacancy in terms of Rule 14 
of the Uttarakhand Subordinate Nursing
(Non-Gazetted) Service Rule 1979 and only 
in the year 2020 the vacancies were 
determined and advertisement was made, but 
the recruitment did not take place. The 
petitioners were over-aged by 2020 also. So
this Court is of the, prima facie, view that 
there has been a denial of opportunity to 
petitioners as there is no determination of 
vacancies between the years 2011 to 2020.
The petitioners have been denied 
participation in the selection process also on 
the ground that they have completed High 
School and Intermediate schooling from 
outside the State. Hence, rule nisi.
The respondents may file counter 
affidavit within four weeks. 
The matter be listed on 17.04.2023. 
In the interim, the application form of 
the petitioners for the selection of Nursing 
Officer shall be accepted by the respondent
no.3 and, if they are found eligible then they
will be allowed to participate in the selection 
process. However, their selection, if any, shall be subject to the result of the writ 
application and the petitioners shall not claim 
any equity at the time of the final disposal of 
the writ application.
Interim relief application (IA 
No.1/2023) stands disposed of.
