With

Hon’ble Prafulla C. Pant, J.
Mr. D.C.S. Rawat, Advocate, present for 
the appellant. 
Heard. 
This appeal is directed against award 
dated 23.05.2013, passed by Motor Accident 
Claims Tribunal/District Judge, Almora, in 
Motor Accident Claim Case No. 17 of 2012, 
whereby the Tribunal has awarded ` 4,96,658/- 
as amount of compensation to the claimant. 
Learned counsel for the appellant 
submitted that the deceased, being owner and 
driver of the private vehicle was not insured 
under the cover policy. 
Admit the appeal. 
Summon the record of the Tribunal. 
Issue notice to the respondent. 
Having heard learned counsel for the 
appellant, and after going through the 
impugned award, as an interim measure, it is 
directed that if the appellant deposits 
`2,50,000/- (adjusting the statutory deposit 
made before this Court), within a period of 45 
days from today, before the Tribunal, out of the 
compensation awarded by it, recovery in 
respect of the remaining amount shall remain 
stayed, until further orders of this Court. (Stay 
Application No. 8160 of 2013, stands disposed 
of). 
List after service of notice on the 
respondent. 
 (Prafulla C. Pant, J.) 
07.08.2013
