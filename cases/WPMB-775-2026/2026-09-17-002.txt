17.09.2026 UKHC010163892026
WPMB No. 775 of 2026
Arvind Singh & anr ---- petitioners
Vs
State of Uttarakhand & ors ---- respondents
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Gupta, C.J.
Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
1. Mr. Parikshit Saini, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. Rahul Verma, learned Additional 
Advocate General with Mr. Ranjan Ghildiyal, 
learned Standing Counsel for the State / 
respondents.
3. The contention of learned counsel for the 
petitioners is that the caste certificate of 
petitioner No. 2 has been cancelled by a 
committee headed by District Magistrate, 
Haridwar along with Additional District 
Magistrate (Administration), Haridwar; Sub 
Divisional Magistrate, Roorkee and District Social 
Welfare Officer as its members. It is urged that 
the constitution of the said committee is not in 
accordance with the direction in paragraph 13(4) 
of the judgment of the Supreme Court in Kumari
Madhuri Patil and another Vs Additional 
Commissioner Tribal Development and others, 
(1994) 6 SCC 241. It is accordingly submitted 
that the order is without jurisdiction. In support 
of the contention, learned counsel for the petitioners has placed reliance on the judgment 
of a Single Judge bench of this Court dated 
29.08.2023 in WPMS No. 1681 of 2020, Sagar 
Giri Vs State Bank of India, and the Division 
Bench judgment in Ram Bahadur Chettri Vs 
Sagar Giri and others, 2023 SCC OnLine Utt
1134, by which the judgment of the Single 
Judge was upheld. He further contends that the 
enquiry by the Vigilance Cell was one of the 
mandatory requirement as per the directions 
issued by the Supreme Court in Madhuri Patil’s
case, whereas in the present case, no such 
enquiry was got conducted.
4. The matter requires consideration. 
5. Let counter-affidavit be filed by the 
respondents within four weeks.
6. List immediately thereafter.
7. Having regard to the submissions made it 
is hereby provided that the effect and operation 
of the order dated 31.07.2026 passed by District 
Level Castes Screening Committee shall remain 
in abeyance till the next date of listing.
(Subhash Upadhyay, J.) (Manoj Kumar Gupta, C.J.)
