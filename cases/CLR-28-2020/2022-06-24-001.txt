Mr. Rajendra Dobhal, Senior Advocate for

Mr. B.S. Adhikari, Advocate for the

This is an admitted Civil Revision under 
Section 25 of the Provincial Small Cause Courts
Act, 1887, which is listed today on the Substitution 
Application No. IA/1476/2022, filed by the 
revisionists to bring on record the heirs of 
deceased revisionist No.1, who is said to have died 
on 23.01.2022.
In fact, looking to the heirs of parties to 
the present civil revision, no formal substitution as 
such is required; only the word ‘deceased’ is to be 
incorporated after the name of revisionist no.1.
In that view of the matter, the 
substitution application is allowed.
Learned counsel for the revisionist is 
directed to carry out the necessary amendment in 
the cause title of the revision within 48 hours’ 
time.
At the time when the revision was 
admitted, there was stay order, granted on 
17.02.2020, which is sought to be extended by way 
of filing Stay Extension Application No.
IA/1477/2022.
The Stay Extension Application too would 
stand disposed of.
List this revision under the head of 
‘hearing’, on 22.08.2022.
The interim order dated 17.02.2020
would remain extended till the next date of listing.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
