Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. S.K. Shandilya, learned counsel for 
the applicant.
2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned A.G.A. and Mr. 
Nikhil Bisht, learned Brief Holder for the 
State.
3. By means of the present application, the 
applicant seeks anticipatory bail in 
connection with Case Crime/F.I.R. No. 192 of 
2026 registered at Police Station-Rajpur, 
District Dehradun for the offences punishable 
under Sections 318(4), 336(3), 338, 340(2) 
and 61(2) of B.N.S. 
4. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that the present F.I.R. has been 
lodged by the informant alleging that a forged 
power of attorney in respect of certain land 
was prepared by showing late Hariraj Singh, 
father-in-law of the complainant, as its 
executant. It is submitted that, admittedly, 
Hariraj Singh had expired in the year 2013, 
whereas the alleged power of attorney is 
stated to have been executed on 12.05.2026, 
i.e., much after his death. 
5. Learned counsel for the applicant would 
further submit that the applicant is innocent 
and has falsely been implicated in the present matter; that, the applicant had merely signed 
the alleged power of attorney as a witness and 
was neither its executant nor a beneficiary 
thereof; that, the applicant had never met late 
Hariraj Singh during his lifetime and had no 
personal knowledge regarding his identity, 
whereabouts or date of death; that, the 
applicant was acquainted with co-accused 
Deepak Aggarwal, in whose favour the alleged 
power of attorney was prepared.
6. He would further submit that relying 
upon the representation made by the said co￾accused and the identity documents, 
including the Aadhaar Card and other 
documents, produced before him in respect of 
the person who was represented to be Hariraj 
Singh, the applicant bona fide believed the 
identity of the executant to be genuine and 
signed the document only in the capacity of a 
witness; that, the applicant had no knowledge 
or reason to believe that the person presented 
as Hariraj Singh was not the actual Hariraj 
Singh or that Hariraj Singh had already 
expired in the year 2013; and that, therefore, 
there was no dishonest or fraudulent 
intention, knowledge, or conscious 
participation on the part of the applicant in 
the alleged forgery or fabrication of the said 
power of attorney.
5. Learned State counsel would vehemently 
oppose the submissions made by learned 
counsel for the applicant, however, he seeks 
three weeks’ time to file counter affidavit. 
6. In the interest of justice, four weeks’ 
time is granted to learned State counsel to file 
counter affidavit.
7. As an interim measure, without 
expressing any opinion on the merits of the 
case, in an event of arrest during 
investigation, the applicant shall be released 
on an interim anticipatory bail subject to 
furnishing of personal bond with two reliable 
sureties of the like amount, to the satisfaction 
of the arresting Officer subject to the following conditions:- 
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation and shall appear before the 
Investigating Officer as and when 
required;
(ii) The applicant shall not directly or 
indirectly make any inducement, threat or 
promise to any person acquainted with 
the facts of the case so as to dissuade 
such person from disclosing such facts to 
the Court or to any police officer;
(iii) The applicant shall not leave the 
country without the prior permission of 
the concerned Court, if so required, 
during the pendency of the proceedings.
9. It is made clear that, in case the 
applicant fails to cooperate with the 
investigation or violates any of the aforesaid 
conditions, it shall be open to the State to 
bring the said fact to the notice of this Court 
and seek appropriate orders, including 
cancellation of the interim anticipatory bail. 
10. List this matter after four weeks.
(Alok Mahra, J.)
