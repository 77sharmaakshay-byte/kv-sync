In these contempt petitions the 
petitioners/applicants have sought an enforcement 
of the judgment dated 08.12.2016, as was rendered 
in Writ Petition (S/S) No. 1730 of 2014 ‘Praveen 
Kumar Nautiyal and Others vs. State of 
Uttarakhand and Another’, which was 
subsequently affirmed by the Division Bench in 
Special Appeal No. 80 of 2017 ‘State of 
Uttarakhand and Another vs. Manoj Kumar and 
Others’ by the judgment of 04.09.2017.
These contempt petitions have been argued 
on number of occasions and thereafter with the 
change of office of respondent no. 1; the 
impleadment application was preferred by the 
petitioner, along with the exemption applications, 
which are shown to be pending considerations in 
the present contempt petitions. In all the contempt 
petitions in which the impleadment application is 
shown to be pending, in fact, in some of the 
contempt petitions the impleadment applications 
has already been allowed; except in few where it is 
still shown to be pending consideration.
The impleadment application thus shown
pending in some of the connected Contempt 
Petition would stand allowed. The petitioners 
would carry out the necessary impleadment in the 
cause title of the contempt petition within a period 
of ten days from today.The Miscellaneous Application No. 11421 
of 2020 filed by the newly impleaded respondent 
in Contempt Petition No. 607 of 2017, 
Miscellaneous Application No. 11422 of 2020 
filed in Contempt Petition No. 609 of 2017 and 
Miscellaneous Application No. 11423 of 2020 in 
Contempt Petition No. 738 of 2018 would stand 
allowed. The response thus filed therein is taken 
on record.
List these contempt petitions next week for 
consideration on its own merit along with the 
contempt petition, which is listed at Item No. 8 
today. The Contempt Petition No. 609 of 2017 
‘Manmohan Singh Bighana and Others vs. Mrs. 
Bhupender Kaur and Another’ would be treated as 
to be the leading contempt petition.
List next week.
