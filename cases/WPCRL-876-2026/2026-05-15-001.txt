COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPCRL No. 876 of 2026
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Lalit Sharma, learned counsel with 
Ms. Suraiya Naaz, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Ms. Sweta Dobhal, learned Brief Holder 
for the State.
3. Petitioners are husband and wife and 
preferred this petition for quashing of FIR dated 
21.04.2026 bearing FIR No. 0044 of 2026 
registered at P.S. Selaqui, Dehradun wherein 
both of them have been implicated for the 
offences punishable under Sections 318(4), 
336(3), 338, 340(2) read with Section 61(2) of BNS 
2023.
4. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
petitioners that in fact petitioners are the victim 
since respondent/complainant cheated them by 
embezzling the amount in the firm in which 
respondent/complainant was inducted as a 
Director. He submits that with regard to the 
embezzlement, application under Section 175(3) 
of BNSS 2023 was moved and the same was 
allowed by the learned Judicial Magistrate, Vikas 
Nagar on 11.05.2026. He submits that the 
allegations, as alleged, are completely false and 
even otherwise the dispute appears to be civil in 
nature.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioners also 
submits that the respondent/complainant also 
filed a complaint against the petitioners and only 
thereafter lodged the impugned FIR.
6. Issue notice to respondent no. 3.
7. Steps to be taken within a week.
8. Let the respondents may file counter 
affidavit within four weeks. Two weeks thereafter is granted to the petitioners to file 
rejoinder affidavit.
9. List on 03.07.2026.
10. In the meantime, let the investigation 
pursuant to the impugned FIR may go on, 
however, the petitioners shall not be arrested 
provided they cooperate with the investigation.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
