dated before the Coordinate Bench on 1st September, 
2023, the factum of death of respondent No.4, 8 and 9

brought on record by the appellant’s counsel.

September, 2023, had granted time to the appellant to 
file the substitution application. The same has not 
been filed within the period of limitation as 
prescribed in the order dated 1st September, 2023. 
The Appeal from Orders would stand 
dismissed qua respondent No. 4, 8 and 9 respectively 
as in AO No.199 of 2017, AO No.198 of 2017 and 
AO No. 203 of 2017. 
The Appeals would continue to proceed as 
against the other respondents.
