Mr. Prashant Khanna, learned counsel for

2. Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned Brief Holder for

3. Mr. Ashish Joshi and Mr. Harendra Belwal, 
learned counsels for respondent no.4.
4. The grievance as canvassed by the 
petitioners in the writ petition is that petitioners 
are working as daily wagers with the 
respondent no.3 since 2008 to 2018 and their 
remuneration are being paid by the respondent 
no.3 .
5. Now, the petitioners want that the salary 
should be paid to them by respondent no.4 as 
according to the report submitted by City 
Magistrate/ Chairman, Yaatayaat Nagar 
Anushravan Samiti, Haldwani, the petitioners 
were the employees of the Nagar Nigam 
Haldwani and earlier the remuneration of the 
petitioner no.1 was being paid from 
Nagarpalika, Haldwani and accordingly, now 
they want their remuneration paid from the 
respondent.
6. Learned counsel for the respondents 
prayed for and is granted six weeks’ time for 
filing counter affidavit. Two weeks’ time 
thereafter is granted for filing rejoinder 
affidavit.
7. List on 09.11.2023, mentioning the name 
of Mr. Ashish Joshi, learned counsel for 
respondent no. 4 in the cause list.
8. In the meantime, the respondents shall 
not take any coercive action against the 
petitioners.
