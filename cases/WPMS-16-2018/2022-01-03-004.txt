These are three connected matters. In Writ 
Petition (M/S) No.3283 of 2017 the counsel for the 
petitioner seeks an adjournment on account of the 
non-availability of the arguing counsel. But,
looking to the order sheet, this Court is hesitant to 
grant the adjournment. 
But in the wider interest of justice it is being 
adjourned by way of last opportunity and the writ 
petition is directed to be listed in the week 
commencing 28.2.2022. 
In the connected matter, that is Writ Petition 
(M/S) No.16 of 2018, the Coordinate Bench of this 
Court by an order of 15.01.2021, directed the 
respondents to decide the representation of the 
petitioner. 
It has been informed by the counsel for the 
petitioner that the said representation has already 
been rejected. 
Mr. M.C. Pant, counsel for the petitioner in 
WPMS No.16 of 2018, may file his amendment 
application if so advised challenging the order of 
rejection of his representation. 
List these writ petitions in the week 
commencing 28.02.2022.
