Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Mohd. Matloob, learned counsel for the
applicant.
2. Mr. Himanshu Sain, learned AGA for the State.
3. Mr. Rajat Mittal, learned counsel for the
respondent no. 2.
4. The instant petition is moved by the applicants –
Girish Chandra Dalakoti and Vipin Panwar,
challenging the proceedings of Special Sessions Trial
No. 05 of 2026, arising out of Case Crime No. 271 of
2024 (State Vs. Girish Chand Dalakoti and others)
pending in the court of Special Judge (SC / ST) Act,
Dehradun wherein applicants have been summoned to
face the trial for the offences punishable under Section
351 (3), 352 of BNS, 2023 read with Section 3 (1) (r), 3
(1) (s) and 3 (1) (y) of the SC / ST (Prevention of
Atrocities) Act, 1989. 
5. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the
applicants that applicant is authorised to do puja by
one Jayanand Bharti and thereafter, there was some
dispute in between the applicants and management of
Trust and on the instance of some Trustees, respondent
no. 2 lodged the FIR. He further submits that even
otherwise, no material was collected by the I.O. against
the applicant while submitting the charge sheet.
6. Per contra, Mr. Rajat Mittal, learned counsel for
the respondent no. 2 submits that present applicant
was not authorised to do puja in the temple. 
7. Let the respondents may file counter affidavitwithin three weeks. One week, thereafter is granted to
the applicants to file rejoinder affidavit. 
8. List this case in the week commencing
23.11.2026.
9. In the meantime, further proceedings of Special
Sessions Trial No. 05 of 2026 arising out of Case Crime
No. 271 of 2024 (State Vs. Girish Chand Dalakoti and
others) pending in the court of Special Judge (SC / ST)
Act, Dehradun shall remain stayed.
