Mr. Saurabh Kumar Pandey, Advocate for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. Jai Prakash, A.G.A. for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition, 
petitioner seeks quashing of FIR No. 156 of 2026, 
under Sections 318(4), 336(3), 338, 340(2) of 
BNS, registered at Police Station- Basant Vihar, 
District Dehradun.
4. In the FIR, it is averred that forged gift deed 
was executed of the land belonging to the late 
father of the complainant. In the said gift deed, 
some other person had impersonated himself to 
be the father of the complainant. The said gift 
deed was executed on 20.12.2025 whereas father 
of the complainant died on 24.12.2024.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that only petitioner has been named as an 
accused in the FIR; that this property was 
purchased by the petitioner on the advice of the 
property dealer; that the petitioner never knew 
the father of the complainant nor any other party 
and he paid consideration of more than Rs. 1 
Crore to the executants through cash and bank 
transactions. On this basis, it is submitted that 
he himself has been cheated by the said persons.6. On the last occasion, this Court directed 
that detail investigation be carried out in respect 
of other person, who appeared before the 
Registrar and impersonated himself as father of 
the complainant. On instructions, learned State 
Counsel submits that investigation in that regard 
is underway.
7. Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the record.
8. Be that as it may, since the petitioner is a 
bonafide purchaser of the property and only to 
save some stamp duty, on the advice of property 
dealer, the gift deed was executed in place of sale 
deed, therefore, this Court finds some substance 
in the submission made by learned counsel for 
the petitioner and having considered the 
submissions, this Court is of the view that this 
matter definitely requires deliberation.
9. Issue notice to respondent no. 3, returnable 
within four weeks. Steps to be taken within a 
week.
10. List after service report is received.
11. Meanwhile, counter-affidavit be filed by the 
respondents.
12. Till the next date of listing, as an interim 
measure, petitioner shall not be arrested in 
relation to the aforesaid FIR, provided, he co￾operates with the investigation.
13. Stay Application made therefor stands 
disposed of accordingly.
