CLMA No.6225 of 2020 (Money Withdrawal Appl.)
In 
A.O. No. 28 of 2020
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. I.P. Kohli, Advocate for the 
appellant.
Mr. K.K. Harbola, Advocate for 
respondent nos. 1 to 3.
Heard learned counsel for the parties
through video conferencing. 
 This money withdrawal application 
has been filed by the wife of the deceased 
for withdrawal of the amount of 
compensation deposited by the appellant -
Insurance Company before learned 
Tribunal. 
 Learned Tribunal had awarded 
`5,84,360/- as compensation to the 
claimants of the deceased.
 Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that, pursuant to the order 
passed by this Court, entire awarded 
amount has been deposited with 
concerned Tribunal. 
 Having regard to the facts and 
circumstances of the case, money 
withdrawal application is allowed. 
Learned Tribunal is directed to release a 
sum of `3,00,000/- in favour of
claimant/respondent no. 1 (wife) of the 
deceased, without asking any security. 
The remaining amount shall be invested in 
an interest bearing scheme of a 
nationalized bank.
 (Manoj K. Tiwari, J.) Aswal 20.08.2020
