WPSS No.444 of 2021
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra and Mr. K.K. 
Harbola, Advocates for the petitioner.
Mr. P.S. Bisht, Additional Chief 
Standing Counsel with Sachin Mohan 
Singh Mehta, Brief Holder for the 
State/respondents.
Challenge in the writ petition is an 
advertisement for filling the post on 
contract basis in the National Health 
Mission, Almora. The grievance of the 
petitioner is that the condition No.2 in the 
said advertisement gives 30 marks 
precedents to such workers, who worked 
on contract basis under the scheme in 
District Almora only.
The learned counsel for the 
petitioner would submit that this is 
discriminatory and violative of Article 14 
of the Constitution of India. 
The matter was listed yesterday 
when it was adjourned for today, so as to 
enable the learned State counsel to get 
instructions in the matter. 
Today, learned State counsel would 
like to file a counter affidavit. The issue is 
much short, which relates to condition 
No.2 of the impugned advertisement and 
may be formulated as hereunder:-
“Can in such employment 
precedence in terms of 30 marks can be 
given on the basis of past working 
experience in a particular District.”
State may like to file a short counter 
affidavit within a week from today. Till 
then, further proceedings in pursuance to 
this advertisement shall remain in abeyance.
List this matter for final hearing on 
30.03.2021.
Interim Relief Application No.1 of 2021 
stands disposed of accordingly.
