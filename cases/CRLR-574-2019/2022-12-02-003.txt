CRLR No. 574 of 2019
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Ms. Abhilash Belwal, Advocate for the 
revisionist.
The challenge in this revision is made 
to the order dated 21.05.2019, passed in 
Misc. Criminal Case No. 295 of 2014, 
Sangeeta Vs. Rajesh Pal, by the Court of 
Judge, Family Court, Udham Singh Nagar. 
By it, the application under Section 125 of 
the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 filed 
by the private respondent was allowed and 
the revisionist was directed to pay Rs. 
5000/- per month as maintenance to the 
private respondent.
The revision is delayed. It is 
submitted that the revisionist is a poor 
man. He resides Bareilly. He had to make 
arrangements for filing the revision. In that 
process, the delay occurred.
Notices were issued to private 
respondent. 
Registry reported that the notice has 
been served.
Having heard, Delay Condonation 
Application No. 2996 of 2019 is allowed. 
Delay in filing the revision is condoned. 
List this matter on 09.12.2022 for 
hearing on admission.
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
