2. Present Criminal Writ Petition has been 
filed seeking to quash and set aside the 
order dated 14.12.2024, passed by the 
learned Chairman, National Lok Adalat, 
Pithoragarh/Civil Judge (Senior 
Division)/Judicial Magistrate, Pithoragarh, 
in Criminal Case No. 1032 of 2023, State of 
Uttarakhand vs. Rahul Singh.
3. In the Writ Petition, it is pleaded that 
the petitioner had filed a complaint against 
respondent no. 3, alleging that respondent 
no. 3 had induced him to pay a sum of ₹
4,00,000/- on the assurance that he would 
secure a job for the petitioner's son in the 
Merchant Navy. Subsequently, it was 
revealed that respondent no. 3 had similarly 
cheated several other persons. An F.I.R. 
dated 28.12.2022, being F.I.R. No. 0292 of 
2022, was registered under Section 420 of 
the Indian Penal Code, 1860, on a similar 
complaint filed by Mahendra Singh. No 
F.I.R. was registered on the petitioner's 
complaint. During the investigation of the 
aforesaid F.I.R., the petitioner and other 
persons who had allegedly been cheated 
were made as victim-witnesses. During the 
trial, the petitioner also deposed as PW-3 and supported the prosecution's case. 
However, during the pendency of the trial, 
the matter was compounded by the 
impugned order. Learned counsel for the 
petitioner submits that the application for 
compounding was filed by concealing 
material facts and that, as the petitioner was 
also a victim of the alleged offence, the 
matter ought not to have been compounded 
without his consent. 
4. This Court finds some substance in the 
submissions advanced by learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
5. Issue notice to respondent no. 3. Steps 
for service shall be taken within one week.
6. Learned counsel for the respondents 
may file their counter affidavit.
7. List this case on 02.11.2026.
