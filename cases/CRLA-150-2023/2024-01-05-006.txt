05.01.2024 Short Term Bail Appln. 04 of 2023 
In 
CRLA No. 150 of 2023 
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, A.C.J.
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J. 
1. Ms. Irum Zeba and Mr. Rafat Munir Ali, 
learned counsel for the appellant. 
2. Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Deputy Advocate 
General for the State. 
3. This is an appeal filed by the appellant / 
applicant under Section 374(2) of Cr.P.C. 
against the impugned judgment and order dated 
02.03.2023, passed by learned Additional 
Sessions Judge, Laksar, District Haridwar, in 
Sessions Trial No. 217 of 2018, whereby the 
appellant has been convicted for the offences 
punishable under Section 147/149, 148/149, 
323/149 and 302 / 149, 504 of IPC, and was 
sentenced to imprisonment for life. 
4. Appellant / applicant has applied for short 
term bail on the ground of marriage of his two 
children. 
5. Learned counsel for the appellant / 
applicant points out that marriage of appellant’s 
daughter is scheduled to be held on 07.01.2024, 
while marriage of appellant’s son is scheduled 
for 10.01.2024. The invitation card, in support 
of said contention, has been enclosed with the 
affidavit filed in support of short term bail 
application. 
6. Learned State Counsel was given time to 
get necessary instructions in the matter. Mr. 
Virk, learned Deputy Advocate General, on 
instructions, makes a statement that the 
marriage of both children of the appellant is scheduled to be held on 7th and 10th of January 
2024, respectively. . 
7. We, accordingly, allow the application 
for short term bail. 
8. Let the appellant / applicant Shamsher 
Ali be released on short term bail for a period 
of two weeks, which shall be reckoned from 
the date of his release from jail on his 
executing a personal bond and furnishing
two reliable sureties, each of like amount, to 
the satisfaction of the court concerned. 
9. On expiry of two weeks, appellant / 
applicant shall surrender before the trial 
court, who in turn, shall send him to jail. 
10. List the appeal on 28.03.2024. 
11. Let a certified copy of this order be 
supplied to learned counsel for the parties 
today itself, as per Rules. 
(Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.) (Manoj Kumar Tiwari, A.C.J.)
