Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J. (Oral)
Since all these criminal appeals arise from one 
and the same judgment and order passed by the court of 
Additional Sessions Judge, Laksar, District Haridwar, they are 
heard together. 
2. The instant appeals have been preferred against 
judgment and order dated 02.03.2023, passed in Sessions Trial 
No.217 of 2018, State Vs. Shamsher Ali and Others, by the court 
of Additional Sessions Judge, Laksar, District Haridwar. By it, the 
appellants has been convicted and sentenced under Sections
147/149, 148/149, 323/149, 302/149 and 504 IPC. 
3. Heard.
4. These appeals have already been admitted.3
5. List in due course for final hearing. 
6. Heard on Bail Applications.
7. According to the prosecution case, on 29.05.2018, 
at 9:30, when Anil, the nephew of the informant, along with 
Sachin, Rajesh and Isham Singh were moving in a bullock cart, 
when from the opposite direction, the appellant Firoz alias Firozu 
came on a motorcycle, who abused Anil. Thereafter, the appellant 
Firoz alias Firozu called some other boys from the village, and 
they gave beating to the nephew of the informant Anil. Anil, 
thereafter, called some other persons, and they approached and 
questioned the appellants as to why did they beat Anil. It is the 
case in the FIR that the appellants abused them and attacked 
them. In that process, the appellant Samsher Ali and Jamshed Ali 
hit the deceased Yogesh on his chest with a fishing hook, due to 
which he died. 
8. Learned counsel for the appellants Jamshed Ali 
and Shamsher Ali submits that there are great contradictions in 
the statements of the witnesses; PW2, Anil, has not supported the 
prosecution case; PW1, Mangeram, the author of the FIR, has also 
not supported the prosecution case. He would refer to the 
statements of PW3, Suresh Pal, PW4, Sachin and PW5, Sandeep 
Kumar to argue that there are great contradictions in their 
statements as to who had attacked whom and who informed PW5, 
Sandeep Kumar about the incident. With regard to the witnesses, 
PW7, Netrapal and PW8, Ritesh, it is argued that they are 
witnesses of inquest in the chargesheet, but they have given 
eyewitness account of the incident, which they did not tell to the 
Investigating Officer. Therefore, their statements are not reliable. 4
It is also argued that, in fact, according to the PW4, Sachin, the 
fatal blow was given by the appellant Shamsher Ali, whereas, 
according to PW5, Sandeep Kumar, the fatal blow was given by 
the appellants Jamshed Ali and Shamsher Ali; the recovery from 
the appellant Shamsher Ali is not established. It is from an open 
place without any disclosure statement, and the recovered article 
is not connected with the crime; the appellants Jamshed Ali and 
Shamsher Ali were on bail during trial. 
9. Learned counsel for the appellants Israel, 
Kareemuddin, Santar Ali and Firoz alias Firozu submits that the 
appellants were on bail during trial; there is no specific act 
assigned to them; even according to the prosecution case, they did 
not give the fatal blow to the deceased Yogesh.
10. Learned State Counsel submits that all the eye￾witnesses have supported the prosecution case.
11. It is a stage of bail post conviction. Much of the 
discussion is not expected of. Arguments are being appreciated 
with the caveat that any observation made in this order shall have 
no bearing at any subsequent stage of the proceedings. 
12. Admittedly, out from a small dispute, the situation 
had arisen to a level that a person died. Initially, according to the 
prosecution case, when PW2, Anil, along with others was moving 
in a bullock cart, some dispute arose between them and the 
appellant Firoz alias Firozu, and, subsequently, the incident took 
place. Various parts of statements have been referred to, which do 
not require deeper scrutiny at this stage. PW4, Sachin, has stated 
that he had witnessed the incident, and it is appellant Shamsher 
Ali, who hit the deceased on his chest by a fishing hook. Reference 
has been made to the statement of PW5, Sandeep Kumar, to argue 5
that, in fact, his presence is doubtful because according to him, 
he was called at the place of incident by PW4, Sachin, whereas, 
PW4, Sachin, has denied of having called PW5, Sandeep Kumar, 
at the place of incident. 
13. Having considered the entirety of facts, we are of 
the view that the appellant Shamsher Ali is not entitled to bail. 
Accordingly, his bail application deserves to be rejected. 
14. The bail application of the appellant Shamsher Ali 
is rejected.
15. Insofar as other applicants, namely, applicants￾Jamshed Ali, Israel, Kareemuddin, Santar Ali and Firoz alias 
Firozu are concerned, we are of the view that it is a case in which 
the execution of sentence should be suspended and these
appellants be enlarged on bail.
16. The bail applications of the appellants Jamshed 
Ali, Israel, Kareemuddin, Santar Ali and Firoz alias Firozu are
allowed. The sentence appealed against is suspended during the 
pendency of the appeals qua these appellants.
17. Let the appellants- Jamshed Ali, Israel, 
Kareemuddin, Santar Ali and Firoz alias Firozu be released on bail 
during the pendency of the appeals on their executing a personal 
bond and furnishing two reliable sureties, each of the like 
amount, by each one of them, to the satisfaction of the court 
concerned.
