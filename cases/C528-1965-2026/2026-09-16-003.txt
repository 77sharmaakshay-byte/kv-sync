1. Mr. Vipul Sharma, learned counsel 
with Mr. Maneesh Bisht, learned counsel for 
the applicants.
2. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned AGA for 
the State.
3. The instant petition is moved by the 
applicants challenging the proceedings of 
complaint case no. 1 of 2024, Kesar Jahan 
vs. Sahdab and others, wherein the 
applicants have been summoned to face the 
trial for the offences punishable under 
Sections 147, 323, 354 and 506 IPC.
4. It is argued by the learned counsel for 
the applicant that earlier respondent no. 3 
moved an application under Section 156(3) 
Cr.P.C. wherein concerned Magistrate called 
report from the concerned police station and
in compliance thereof report was submitted 
which reveals that respondent no. 3 is 
habitual to implicate the persons. He 
submits that despite police report, the 
Magistrate concerned treat the application 
moved under Section 156(3) Cr.P.C. as a 
complaint case and summoned the 
applicants. He submits that the concerned 
Magistrate committed illegality while 
summoning the applicants without 
substance and material. 5. All these aspects require scrutiny.
6. Respondent no. 3 joined the proceeding 
through VC and submits that she will 
engage her counsel to pursue the 
proceeding.
7. List this matter on 12.10.2026.
8. In the meantime, respondents may file 
their response.
9. For an interim measure, further 
proceeding of Complaint Case No. 1 of 2024, 
Kesar Jahan vs. Sahdab and others, 
pending in the court of learned Judicial 
Magistrate, Sitarganj, District U.S. Nagar 
shall remain stayed.
