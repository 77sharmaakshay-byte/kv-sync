Mr. Mohit Kumar Kashyap, learned

2. Mr. Mehul Joshi, learned counsel for

3. There is a delay of 119 days in filing the 
present criminal revision.
4. Learned counsel for the respondent 
would submit that an urgency application has 
been filed seeking early hearing of the matter. 
It is further submitted that no objection has 
been filed on behalf of the respondent to the 
delay condonation application and that the 
respondent has no objection to the delay of 
119 days being condoned.
5. Having considered the explanation 
furnished for the delay and the submissions 
made by learned counsel for the parties, this 
Court is of the view that the cause shown is 
sufficient. Accordingly, the delay of 119 days 
in filing the present criminal revision is 
hereby condoned. The delay condonation 
application stands allowed.6. Learned counsel for the parties would 
jointly submit that the dispute arises out of 
matrimonial discord and that, having regard 
to the nature of the dispute, there is a 
possibility of an amicable settlement between 
the parties, therefore, they pray that the 
parties may be afforded an opportunity to 
explore the possibility of settlement through 
mediation.
7. List the matter on 07.09.2026. The 
parties shall remain personally present before 
this Court on 07.09.2026 at 10:30 A.M. to 
explore the possibility of an amicable 
resolution of the dispute.
8. It is, however, made clear that in case 
either of the parties is unable to remain 
present on the date fixed due to any 
unavoidable circumstance, such party shall 
intimate the other party in advance.
