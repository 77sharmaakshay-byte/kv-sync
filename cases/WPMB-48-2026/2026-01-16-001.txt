6. The petitioners are seeking a writ of mandamus 
directing respondents to permit the petitioners to 
appear in the fourth year examination of the BAMS 
course pertaining to the Academic Session of 2020-21.
7. Admittedly, the petitioners have earlier filed Writ 
Petition (M/S) No.409 of 2023 before this Court and it 
was on basis of interim order dated 07.06.2023, 
passed in the said writ petition, that they were 
permitted to appear in the first year examination of BAMS course.
8. Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the 
petitioners submits that the written examination of 
fourth year BAMS course would commence from 
27.01.2026. In such a situation, the petitioners had 
been advised to file the present writ petition.
9. As the cause of action is the same, therefore, we 
are of the opinion that the instant writ petition should 
be heard along with pending writ petition.
10. Accordingly, we direct the instant writ petition to 
be listed on 23.01.2026 before the Vacation Bench 
along with Writ Petition (M/S) No.409 of 2023.
