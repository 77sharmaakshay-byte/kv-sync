COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRJA No. 76 of 2023
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. B.M. Pingal, counsel for the appellant/applicant.
2. Mr. V.K. Jemini, Deputy A.G. for the State.
3. A Short Term Bail Application (IA 1 of 2024) has been 
filed by the appellant/applicant with the prayer that father of the 
appellant/applicant has expired on 16.01.2024, therefore, the 
appellant/applicant be granted short term bail for a period of 30 
days to perform the last rites and rituals of his father.
4. On 23.1.2024, this Court had directed the State counsel 
to verify about death of the father of the appellant/applicant.
5. Today, on instructions, State counsel would submit that 
the father of the appellant/applicant has expired on 16.01.2024, 
therefore, short term bail for a period of 15 days is sufficient to 
perform the same.
6. In view of the above, the short term bail application is 
allowed.
7. Let the appellant/applicant be released on short term bail 
for a period of 15 days on furnishing bail bond with two sureties 
in the amount of `30,000/- and personal bond of the like amount 
to the satisfaction of the court concerned. The period of 15 days 
shall be reckoned from the date of his release from jail.
8. On expiry of 15 days, appellant/applicant shall surrender 
before the concerned trial court, who in turn, shall send him to 
jail.
9. List this case on 18.03.2024.
