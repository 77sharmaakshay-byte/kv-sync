Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Prem Kaushal, learned counsel for the 
applicant.
2. Mr. Himanshu Sain, learned AGA for the State. 
3. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that 
tomorrow is fixed for pronouncement of judgment, 
therefore, trial court may be directed to defer the 
pronouncement of judgment for another date. 
4. In this case, both the applicant and victim were 
of tender age and they were in love, which the victim 
admits in her statement recorded under Section 183 
BNSS, 2023 and during her examination in chief before 
the trial court. Though the consent of minor victim is 
irrelevant at this stage, but it goes to the root of the 
matter. Victim herself admits in her statement that she 
knew applicant since last 2 years. 
5. This aspect requires scrutiny. 
6. In such view of the matter, list this case on 
15.07.2026 so that State Counsel may get complete 
instructions or file objection in order to address the
issue involved. The trial court is directed to defer the 
pronouncement of judgment till 17.07.2026.
