COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
ABA No.1040 of 2023
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Pawan Mishra, Advocate for the 
applicant.
Mr. K.S. Bora, D.A.G. for the State.
Applicant seeks anticipatory bail in 
FIR No.235 of 2023, under Sections 420, 
467, 468 and 471 IPC, Police Station 
Raiwala, District- Dehradun. 
Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record. 
According to the FIR, the applicant 
has created some fraud at Gauri Shankar 
Mandir Trust Registers and he is in the 
process of selling the property. 
It is the case of the applicant that the 
informant in the case is trying to grab the 
property of trust; he had earlier field a 
litigation in the revenue court, which he 
lost. The trust is registered. 
Certain documents have been filed by 
the applicant. 
State to file objections. 
List this matter on 08.01.2024.
Having considered the submissions, 
as an interim measure, till the next date of 
listing, in the eventuality of arrest, the 
applicant shall be released on bail subject 
to his furnishing personal bond to the 
satisfaction of the arresting officer, subject 
to the following:-
(i) The applicant shall not approach 
in any manner, whatsoever, the victim or 
any other witness in the case.(ii) He shall cooperate with the 
investigation.
(iii) He shall not leave the country 
without prior permission of the concerned 
court.
(iv) He shall deposit his passport 
with the Investigating Officer (“IO”). The 
passport may only be returned by the order 
of the court concerned. In case the 
applicant does not have passport, he shall 
give an undertaking to that effect to the IO. 
(v) The applicant shall also give an 
undertaking on (i), (ii) & (iii) above.
 (Ravindra Maithani J.)
