COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS 
 WPMS No. 938 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Siddhartha Singh, learned counsel for the 
petitioners. 
2. None appears for respondent no. 3. 
3. Mr. Rajesh Pandey, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State/respondent no. 4. 
4. By the instant writ petition, the petitioner is 
challenging the order dated 21.12.2023 passed by the 
Deputy Director Consolidation, Haridwar in 
Revision No. 19 of 2019-20, Revision No. 20 of 2019-
20 and Revision No. 21 of 2019-20.
5. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the 
petitioners that the matter was remanded by this 
court by the judgment and order dated 25.06.2013 
passed in WPMS No. 419 of 2007 (Old No. 18542 of 
1999) ‘Matloob and Others vs. Assistant Director of 
Consolidation and Others’. The matter was 
remanded back to the Revisional Court in respect of 
the land of Chak No. 59.
6. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the 
petitioners that by the order impugned the land of 
Khasra No. 467, which was in possession of the 
petitioners since long back was allotted to the 
respondent, and in place of this land the plot of 
Khasra No. 383-M alloted, which is, in fact, is khala, in 
support of which amended chart is also brought on 
record as Annexure-1, which is part of the order 
impugned.
7. Learned Standing Counsel Mr. Rajesh Pande 
for the State fairly submits that the land shown as 
khala does not come within the purview of definition 
of “Holdings” as provided in clause (i) and (ii) of 
sub-section (2) of Section 3 of U.P. Consolidation of 
Holdings Act, 1953.
8. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the 
petitioners that the said order impugned has been passed without giving an opportunity of hearing to 
the petitioners; however, after passing of this order 
an application was moved, but the same was rejected 
by observing that on the date when the matter was 
heard, the parties were present.
9. I perused the order impugned, which was 
passed pursuant to the remand order passed by this 
court on 25.06.2013 and it appears that the Deputy 
Director, Consolidation passed the order impugned 
in a very casual manner without taking into 
consideration that the land shown as khala does not 
come within the purview of holdings. Order appears 
to be a very cryptic order passed without application 
of mind.
10. Admit.
11. Issue notice to the respondent nos. 1, 1/1, 1/2, 
1/3 and 2, 2/1, 2/2, 2/3 and 2/4 as well as 
respondent no. 3 through registered post, returnable 
within three weeks, acknowledgment due. 
Petitioners shall take steps within a week.
12. Let the respondents may file their counter 
affidavit within a period of four weeks.
13. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the 
petitioners for filing rejoinder affidavit.
14. List this matter on 11.06.2024.
15. Until further order of this court the order 
impugned dated 21.12.2023 passed by the Deputy 
Director Consolidation in Revision No. 19 of 2019-20, 
Revision No. 20 of 2019-20 and Revision No. 21 of 
2019-20 shall remain stayed.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
