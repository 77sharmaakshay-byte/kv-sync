COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 543 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned Additional C.S.C. 
for the State.
3. Since, this court is of the view that the transfer 
should not take place on the basis of the classification 
of Sugam and Durgam since there is no any such 
provision of law to determine what would be the 
criteria for determination of Sugam and Durgam area.
4. On the previous date in one such writ petition i.e. 
WPSS No. 533 of 2025 the Secretary appeared through 
V.C. in order to assist this court and the Secretary 
himself apprise to this court that the department is also 
facing the difficulty in computation of the marks on the 
basis of classification of Sugam and Durgam. 
Consequently, the Secretary was requested to discuss 
the matter with the Chief Secretary to resolve all these 
issues and if possible this session be declared as a zero 
session for transfer.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner Mr. Bhagwat 
Mehra in pre-lunch session apprise to this court that on 
21.04.2025 the Secretary passed a common order 
transferring 17 incumbents to the different places and 
gives the impression that this transfer order was passed 
by the Secretary in a very hurried manner in order to 
frustrate the petition i.e. WPSS No.533 of 2025.
6. On this, Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned 
Additional C.S.C. was directed to get instruction in the 
matter from the concerned Secretary.
7. On post-lunch session learned Advocate General 
also appears through V.C. along with Mr. Rajeev Singh 
Bisht and they inform to this court that this order of 
transfer dated 21.04.2025 was passed by the Secretary 
keeping in mind the order passed by this court on 
09.04.2025 in WPSS No. 533 of 2025.8. He also submits that all these 17 persons, who 
have been transferred by the order dated 21.04.2025 
relating to the earlier transfer session, but in this 
particular session no such transfer order has been 
issued.
9. It appears that learned counsel for the petitioner 
gives a wrong impression to this court that the 
Secretary passed a common order on 21.04.2025 by 
ignoring the order passed by this court in WPSS No. 
533 of 2025.
10. I also peruse the order dated 21.04.2025 passed 
by the Secretary and, on perusal of it, it appears that all 
the 17 persons have been transferred either on their 
serious illness or on the ground that the spouse are in 
Government service.
11. Thus, this court is of the view this order is 
perfectly correct though it is not under challenge.
12. Let the respondents may file counter affidavit 
within four weeks.
13. Two weeks’ time thereafter is granted to the 
petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
14. List this matter on 17.06.2025.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
