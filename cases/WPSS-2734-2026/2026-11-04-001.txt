4. It is very surprising that by furnishing 
the resignation the petitioner also 
highlighted the incident regarding which he 
has lodged the FIR but instead of examining 
the reasons for furnishing the resignation 
the petitioner was removed from services 
merely on the ground that his tenure is 
completed. The question is why the 
respondent being employer have not 
examined his grievance which he has 
highlighted in his resignation. 
The respondents being Employer are 
under legal obligation to examine the issue 
which the petitioner has highlighted. 
Surprisingly, instead of examining the issue, 
the petitioner has been removed. Though, no 
legal right accrues in favour of the petitioner 
since he is a contractual employee but this 
fact cannot be ignored that he has rendered 
service in the department since 2014 and 
now at present he is about 37 years old.
5. Let the respondent being employer of the petitioner should examine his grievance 
which he has highlighted in the resignation
letter.
6. The Engineer-in-Chief shall examine 
the grievance of the petitioner and apprise to 
this Court on the next date.
7. Put up this matter on 30.11.2026.
