WPSS No.1532 of 2026
Hon'ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. DCS Rawat with Mr. Jai Krishna Pandey, 
learned Counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned B.H. for the State.
3. Petitioner has challenged his transfer order dated 
03.06.2026 whereby petitioner was transferred from 
District Industries Center, Uttarkashi to District 
Industries Center, Almora as Senior Administrative 
Officer. 
4. Petitioner contends that he was working in 
Uttarkashi only for last 11 months where he joined on 
14.06.2025 on his request transfer. The petitioner 
further contends that the name of petitioner was not 
mentioned in the eligibility list sent along with Letter 
No.284 dated 30.04.2026 which was prepared by the 
Department for transferring those employees who were 
eligible for being transferred. However, despite that, the 
Committee, under Section 16 of the Uttarakhand 
Annual Transfer for Public Servants Act, 2017, met on 
02.06.2026, out of blue moon, mentioned the name of 
petitioner in the list for transfer from inaccessible area 
to accessible area.
5. Having considered the submissions of petitioner 
and having perused the documents annexed with the 
writ petition, particularly eligibility list as well as the list 
prepared by the Committee and moreover on the ground 
that the petitioner spent only about 11 months on his 
request transfer in Uttarkashi, this Court is inclined to 
interfere in the matter.6. Accordingly, it is directed that till the next date of 
listing, effect and operation of impugned transfer order 
dated 03.07.2006 shall remain stayed qua the 
petitioner. 
7. The respondents may file their counter affidavit 
within four weeks.
8. List on 24.08.2026.
9. Stay Application (IA/1/2026) stands disposed of. 
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
