2. The Co-ordinate Bench, vide order dated 
19.05.2026, directed the Sub-Divisional 
Magistrate, Laksar to conduct an enquiry and 
submit a report to this Court, inasmuch as 
two persons were claiming to be the original 
petitioner, namely, Ali Mohammad S/o 
Jaharu alias Jahur. One of them is the 
present petitioner, whereas the other person, 
who appeared before the Court on 
19.05.2026, also claimed himself to be the 
original Ali Mohammad S/o Jaharu alias 
Jahur. Accordingly, the Co-ordinate Bench 
directed the SDM, Laksar to conduct an 
enquiry, including a visit to Village Aithal, 
Tehsil Laksar, and submit a report before this 
Court.
3. Today, learned State Counsel, on the 
basis of the written report received from the 
Sub-Divisional Magistrate, Laksar, submits 
that the present petitioner is the original Ali 
Mohammad S/o Jaharu alias Jahur. It is 
further submitted that the person who 
appeared before this Court on 19.05.2026 
claiming himself to be the original petitioner 
has been identified as Ali Mohammad S/o 
Abdula and not Ali Mohammad S/o Jaharu alias Jahur, the present petitioner. 
4. Learned State Counsel is directed to 
place on record the enquiry report of the Sub￾Divisional Magistrate, Laksar, along with an 
affidavit before the next date of listing. 
5. Issue notice to the remaining 
respondents. Steps shall be taken within one 
week. 
6. List this matter after receipt of the 
service report. 
7. Till the next date of listing, as an interim 
measure, the petitioner shall not be arrested, 
provided he cooperates with the investigation 
and appears before the Investigating Officer as 
and when required. 
8. The Interim Relief Application stands 
disposed of accordingly.
