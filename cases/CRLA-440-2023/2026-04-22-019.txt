Instant appeal is preferred against the judgment and order 
dated 15.06.2023, passed in Session Trial No.04 of 2019, State Vs. 
Devendra Singh @ Bau and others, by the court of Sessions Judge, 
Nainital. By it, the appellant has been convicted under Section
302/120B of IPC and Section 25 of Arms Act and sentenced 
accordingly. The appellant seeks bail in this appeal.
2. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the 
record.
3. After attending the court proceedings on 1st September 
2019, when PW2, Diwan Singh, came out of Ramnagar Court along 
with his brother, Virendra Manral, at a distance of 20 feet from the 
court’s gate, according to the FIR, the appellant shot Virendra 2
Manral dead. The FIR also records the presence of other persons 
and specifies the roles of the other accused.
4. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that it is a case 
fit for bail. Had PW2, Diwan Singh, been present at the place of the 
incident, he would have taken the deceased to the hospital; 
whereas, as per the record, Sanjay Negi, who is a suspect in the FIR, 
had taken the deceased to the hospital. It is also argued that the 
presence of Sonu Kandpal was found at some other place, although 
he has been assigned the role of exhortation. It is further submitted 
that PW5, Chandan Singh Mehta, has introduced a tea stall where 
the deceased ran after the firing; however, he submits that the said 
tea stall has clearly not been stated by any other witness.
5. Learned counsel for the appellant further submits that the 
medical evidence does not support the prosecution case; that, 
according to the medical evidence, the bullet entered from the chest 
and routed the stomach, whereas, according to the prosecution 
witnesses, the shot was fired from behind, which creates total doubt 
in the prosecution case.
6. On the other hand, learned State Counsel submits that 
there are four eyewitnesses to the incident. PW2, Diwan Singh, and 
PW3, Kuldeep Singh Manral are relatives, whereas PW4, Anil 
Dhuliya, and PW5, Chandan Singh Mehta, are independent 
witnesses. They have stated how the appellant shot the deceased. 
He refers to the statement of PW2, Diwan Singh, in para 44, where 
he has stated that the shot was fired at the deceased and hit him 
below the chest. It is argued that this corroborates the medical 
evidence. In fact, in para 7 of his statement, PW2, Diwan Singh, has 3
also stated that the shot was fired and it hit the deceased below his 
chest, due to which he sustained injuries.
7. Having considered the entirety of the facts and the 
statements of witnesses, this Court is of the view that there is no 
ground to enlarge the appellant on bail. Accordingly, bail application 
deserves to be rejected.
8. The bail application is rejected. 
9. List this case in due course along with connected matters.
