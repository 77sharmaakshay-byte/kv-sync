07.11.2024 FA No. 124 of 2015 
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
On 29.12.2023, at the request of 
Mr. Yogesh Pant, Advocate, the case was 
adjourned for final hearing. On 
25.07.2024, there was no representation 
on behalf of the appellant. Therefore, 
interim order dated 20.11.2015 was 
vacated. 
2. The appellant has filed a Recall 
Application (IA No.13407 of 2024) to 
recall the order dated 25.07.2024. 
3. Heard Mr. Yogesh Pant, learned 
counsel for the appellant, Mr. Arvind 
Vashistha, learned Senior Advocate 
assisted by Mr. Kartikey Kulashri, learned 
counsel for the respondent nos. 1 to 3 and 
Mr. Pradeep Kumar Chauhan, learned 
counsel for the respondent nos. 4 to 6. 
4. Mr. Yogesh Pant, Advocate, submits 
that he is ready to argue the matter on 
merit. 
5. The present matter is fixed for final 
hearing. 
6. In the facts and circumstances of the 
case, the Recall Application (IA No. 13407 of 2024) is allowed. The said order dated 
25.07.2024 is recalled. 
7. List on 18th and 19th December, 2024 
for final hearing. 
8. Both the parties have requested time 
to file their written submissions. 
9. Mr. Yogesh Pant, Advocate, submits 
that the appellant shall file his written 
submissions by 10.12.2024. 
10. The respondents may file their 
written submissions by 16.12.2024. 
11. Interim order dated 20.11.2015 is 
extended till the next date of listing.
