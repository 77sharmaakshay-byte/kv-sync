FA No. 124 of 2015
Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate for 
the appellant. 
Ms. Lata Negi, Advocate holding 
brief of Mr. P.K. Chauhan, Advocate for 
the caveator/applicant. 
Having heard learned counsel for 
the plaintiff/appellant, it transpires that 
the controversy is that plaintiff allegedly 
borrowed Rupees Twenty Lakh from 
defendant/respondent no.3 Mr. Surendra 
Singh and as a collateral security, the 
borrower was constraint, as being asked, 
to execute a deed in the form of sale-deed 
without delivering any possession. 
It is also pertinent to mention that 
the property, in question, is already 
mortgaged with some bank and in such 
mortgage deed, Mr. Surendra Singh was 
a witness as well as guarantor. Knowing 
all these facts, Mr. Surendra Singh asked 
Mr. Vijay Kushwaha to execute this deed 
in his favour. 
That apart, Mr. Vijay Kushwaha 
refunded the amount of debt and in 
return, Mr. Surendra Singh 
acknowledged the receipt of that refund 
on dated 07.04.2007. 
So, in the back drop of these facts, 
an O.S. No.317 of 2007 was instituted by 
Mr. Vijay Kushwaha for cancellation of 
such alleged sale deed and this suit was 
resisted by all the defendants, including 
Mr. Surendra Singh. 
Learned Trial Judge dismissed 
such suit by the impugned judgment 
dated 15.09.2015 without analysing and 
appreciating this legal controversy, as 
has been indicated above. Ms. Lata Negi, Advocate appears 
on behalf of the caveator and seeks 
adjournment for Monday, but the Court 
does not agree with her submission for 
the reason that it was highlighted that 
the caveator is none other but he is 
subsequent purchaser of the property, 
during pending litigation. He was neither 
party in the Suit nor in this appeal. Any 
person is not at liberty to hamper the 
proceedings of this Court by way of 
moving the simple caveat and seeks 
adjournment in such a manner. 
If the caveator feels himself to be 
the stakeholder in the matter, then he 
will always be at liberty to move an 
application seeking impleadment in this 
appeal. 
Appeal is hereby admitted. 
Issue notice to the respondents. 
Steps be taken within a week. 
Call the Lower Court records. 
List this appeal in due course. 
Meanwhile, the status quo, 
regarding the possession of the property, 
in question, shall not be disturbed, in 
any manner, whatsoever, including 
creating any third party interest in the 
shape and nature of the disputed 
property. 
Stay application (CLMA No.13186 
of 2015) stands disposed of accordingly.
