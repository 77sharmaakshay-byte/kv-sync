Mr. P.K. Chauhan, Advocate, for

This impleadment application 
(CLMA 13403/2015) has been moved by 
Pradeep Bansal, Satyaveer Singh and 
Anju Singh. They all are the subsequent 
purchasers of the property, in question, 
which they had purchased on 16.12.2013 
from Smt. Babli and Smt. Neetu. 
It is pertinent to mention that 
this sale deed was executed during the 
pendency of A.O. No. 85/2010, which 
was filed challenging the stay vacation 
order dated 5.9.2009 passed by the 
learned Trial Judge. 
The Court feels that these 
applicants are necessary parties and 
should be impleaded in the matter. Filing 
of objections will not serve any useful 
purpose and will simply make delay in 
disposal of the appeal. 
So, impleadment application is 
allowed. 
Let the amended memo of the 
parties be filed within a week. 
List thereafter.
