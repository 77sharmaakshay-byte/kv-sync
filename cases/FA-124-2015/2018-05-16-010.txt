There is a delay of 165 days in filing the 
restoration application. The reasons given in 
the delay condonation application is that the 
factum of dismissal of appeal for default could 
not be communicated to the appellant in time, 
which resulted in delay in filing the 
restoration application. 
Having regard to the facts mentioned in 
the delay condonation application, delay in 
filing the restoration application is hereby 
condoned, subject to the payment of cost of 
`5,000/-, which shall be deposited in the 
‘Advocate Welfare Fund' within two weeks 
from today. 
Accordingly, the delay condonation 
application is allowed. 
Also heard on restoration application. 
Reasons stated in the restoration 
application appear to be correct. 
Accordingly, restoration application is 
allowed. 
Let the appeal be restored to its original 
number along with interim order, if any. 
List this matter in the month of July, 
2018 in the weekly cause list.
