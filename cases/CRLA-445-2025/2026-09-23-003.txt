Mr. Akram Parvez, learned legal aid counsel 
for the appellant. 
2. Mr. Siddhartha Bisht, learned AGA for the 
State. 
3. The defect pointed out by the Registry is 
ignored.
4. The appellant proposes to file an appeal 
against the judgment and order dated 
07/08.01.2025, passed in Special Sessions Trial 
No.145 of 2023, State vs. Bachan Lal @ Bachnu, 
by the court of learned Special Judge (POCSO)/ 
Addl. Sessions Judge, Dehradun. By it, the 
appellant has been convicted under Sections 
376(3), 354, 506 of IPC and Sections 3(a)/4, 7/8 of 
the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences 
Act, 2012 and sentenced accordingly. There is a 
delay of 136 days in filing the appeal.
5. Heard learned counsel for the parties on the 
delay condonation application (IA No.01 of 2025).
6. Learned Legal Aid Counsel for the appellant submits that the appellant could not engage any 
lawyer. He could not take any legal assistance; 
however, by the intervention of the High Court 
Legal Services Committee, the appeal could be 
preferred, and in the process, delay has occurred.
7. It is not disputed by the learned State 
counsel.
8. There are good grounds to condone the delay 
in filing the appeal. The delay in filing the appeal is 
hereby condoned and the Delay Condonation 
Application (IA No.1 of 2025) is allowed.
9. Also heard on the Exemption Applications
(IA No.02 of 2025 and IA No.5 of 2026). 
10. Learned legal aid counsel for the appellant 
has filed exemption applications to dispense with 
filing of affidavits.
11. The Exemption Applications are allowed. 
12. Misc. application (IA No.4 of 2026) stands 
disposed of, accordingly. 
13. Admit.
14. Call for LCR.
15. Once the LCR is received, let the paper book 
be prepared and provided to the learned counsel for 
the parties, as per Rules.
16. Let the State file objection to the Bail 
Application (IA No.3 of 2025) within four weeks.
17. List this matter on 03.11.2026.
