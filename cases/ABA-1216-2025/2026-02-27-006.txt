This Application for anticipatory bail has been filed 
in Case Crime No.404 of 2019, registered at Police Station 
SIDCUL, District Haridwar under Sections 409, 420, 467, 
468 and Section 471 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860
2. Heard Mrs. Neetu Singh, learned counsel for the 
applicant and Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned Assistant 
Government Advocate for the respondent. 
3. Mrs. Neetu Singh, Advocate, submitted that the 
applicant was Secretary of Swami Vivekanand Education 
Trust. The institute-in-question was being run by the said 
Trust. Ashwani Kumar Kamboj, the co-accused of similar 
role, has already been granted anticipatory bail by this Court 
in Anticipatory Bail Application No. 22 of 2024. She further 
submitted that the applicant shall appear before this Court 
on 20.03.2026. 2
4. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, Assistant Government 
Advocate, has sought two weeks’ time to file objection to the 
anticipatory bail application. 
5. Time is granted.
6. Having heard the learned counsel for the parties 
and in the facts and circumstances of the case, as an ad￾interim measure, it is directed that in the event of arrest of 
the applicant Sushant Garg, he shall be released on interim 
bail on executing a personal bond of Rs.30,000/- and two 
reliable sureties, each of the like amount, to the satisfaction 
of the Arresting Officer, subject to the condition that he shall 
not directly or indirectly make any inducement, threat or 
promise to any person acquainted with the facts of this case.
7. List on 20.03.2026 for hearing on the Anticipatory 
Bail Application.
