Ms. Pushpa Joshi, Senior Advocate assisted by 
Ms. Chetna Latwal, Advocate for the appellant.
Mr. J.S. Virk, Deputy Advocate General with 
Mr. R.K. Joshi, Brief Holder for the State.
Miscellaneous application (IA No.5003/2020) 
is allowed. Counter affidavit filed on behalf of the 
State is taken on record.
The appellant has been convicted under 
Sections 376 and 306 IPC and has been sentenced to 
undergo rigorous life imprisonment with a fine of 
Rs.10,000/- (Rupees Ten Thousand only) under 
Section 376 IPC, and to undergo ten years of rigorous 
imprisonment with a fine of Rs.5,000/- (Rupees Five 
Thousand only) under Section 306 IPC, with default 
stipulation. 
The appellant was 19 years of age at the time 
of the incident and as of now he is 22 years of age.
A plea for expeditious disposal of the matter is 
being made by the learned Senior Counsel for the 
appellant.
Under the facts and circumstances of the case, 
we are of the view that the matter needs early 
hearing.
List this case in the first week of December, 
2020 for final hearing.
Urgency application (IA No.9579/2020) stands 
disposed accordingly.
