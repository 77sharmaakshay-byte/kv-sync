Heard learned counsels.
2. This application is filed seeking for suspension 
of sentence and bail.
3. Ms. Pushpa Joshi, learned senior counsel for the 
appellant contends that the trial court has committed an 
error in convicting the accused. The incident took place on 
21.10.2017 of an offence under Section 376 IPC, and 
thereafter she (victim) committed suicide on 23.10.2017. It 
is, therefore, pleaded that except her complaint lodged on 
the date of the alleged offence under Section 376 IPC, no 
other statement of her’s was recorded. This itself leads to a 
doubt on the entire prosecution case. It is further pleaded 
that the appellant is presently aged about 22 years and 
keeping in mind his tender age, he requires to be enlarged 
on bail. That he has been in custody since the date of the 
offence.2
3. The same is disputed by the learned government 
advocate. He contends that a heinous offence of rape has 
been committed on the girl. She has lodged her complaint. 
She has narrated the entire incident to the police. Unable 
to bear the trauma, she has committed suicide on 
23.10.2017. There is sufficient material to sustain the 
order of the trial court. So far as the age is concerned, that 
can only be a consideration and cannot be a sole ground to 
grant bail. Hence he pleads that the application be 
dismissed. 
4. On hearing learned counsels, we do not find any 
ground to enlarge the appellant on bail. The complaint has 
been lodged by the victim herself on 21.10.2017 narrating 
the entire manner in which the incident has occurred. 
Unable to bear the trauma she has committed suicide two 
days later. The trial court having considered the evidence 
and the entire material has rightly convicted the accused. 
Prima facie, we do not find any error committed by the trial 
court that warrants interference by us at the present stage. 
5. So far as the age is concerned, it is relevant to 
notice that the appellant was aged about 19 years on the 
date the offence took place. Indeed it is quite shocking that 
a boy aged 19 years would go to the extent of committing
an offence under Section 376 IPC. Hence, we are of the 
view that so far as the contention on the age is concerned,
the same requires to be held against the appellant and not 
in his favour.
6. Under these circumstances, we do not find any 
ground to enlarge the appellant on bail. Consequently, bail 
application no. 620 of 2020 is dismissed. 3
