 The respondent No.2 is the legally wedded wife 
of the revisionist. According to the revisionist, on an 
application moved by the respondent No.2/wife under 
Section 23 (1) of the Protection of Women from Domestic 
Violence Act, the learned Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Kashipur, Udham Singh Nagar has fixed an 
amount of Rs.7,000/- per month as interim maintenance to 
be paid by the revisionist/husband to the respondent 
No.2/wife, vide order dated 03.11.2016. The appeal filed 
by the revisionist against the order dated 03.11.2016 
before the Additional Sessions Judge, Kashipur, District 
Udham Singh Nagar has been dismissed vide order dated 
31.08.2017. Hence, the present criminal revision. 
 Learned counsel for the revisionist submits that 
income of the revisionist is only Rs.7936/- and the interim 
maintenance awarded by the learned Additional Chief 
Judicial Magistrate, Kashipur, District Udham Singh 
Nagar is on a higher side. 
 Considering the overall facts and materials 
available before this Court, as an interim measure, the 
interim maintenance is reduced from Rs.7,000/- per month 
to Rs.6,000/- per month, provided the revisionist gives an 
amount of Rs.50,000/- to the respondent No.2 within a 
period of 15 days from today i.e. on or before 26.01.2018, 
as part of accumulated arrears. 
 List this matter on 12.02.2018 in the daily cause 
list.
