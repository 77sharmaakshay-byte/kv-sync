Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Ravi Shankar Kandpal, learned counsel (Legal 
Aid) for the appellant.
2. Mr. Rakesh Joshi, learned Brief Holder for the 
State.
3. This is a criminal appeal filed through Legal Aid 
Counsel. Appellant has challenged his conviction vide 
judgment and order dated 30.05.2023, passed by 
Additional Sessions Judge, Kotdwar, District Pauri 
Garhwal in Sessions Trial No.06 of 2021, whereby he 
has been convicted for offence punishable under Section 
302 IPC and sentenced to undergo life imprisonment 
with a fine of Rs.10,000/- with default stipulation of one 
year additional imprisonment. He has further been 
convicted for offence punishable under Section 201 IPC 
and sentenced to undergo five years’ imprisonment with 
a fine of Rs.1,000/- with default stipulation of six 
months’ additional imprisonment. Both the sentences 
were directed to run concurrently. 
4. Learned State Counsel submits that since the 
appeal is not filed from jail, but it has been filed through 
Legal Aid Counsel, therefore, it should have been 
registered as regular criminal appeal and not a jail 
appeal.
5. Registry is directed to examine this aspect and 
register the appeal under the appropriate head within 24 
hours.
6. List on 02.07.2024 as fresh.
