As the matter is taken up, it is 
informed by learned counsel for the 
petitioners that the report of UKSSSC has 
already been received. But, he would 
submit that attempts have not been 
sincerely made to segregate the candidates 
who used unfair means in the examination 
and who did not.
The report is before the Court. The 
Court requests learned counsel for the 
respondent no.5, the Commission to 
elaborate as to how the process was 
undertaken by the UKSSSC to examine the 
feasibility of segregation of the candidates, 
who used unfair means in the examination 
and who did not.
He would submit that, in fact, the 
Expert Committee had done it. He referred 
to a report.
There are two experts namely, Dr. 
Neeraj Tiwari, Professor and Head, 
Department of Statistics, Soban Singh 
Jena University, Almora Uttarakhand and 
Dr. Raman Nautiyal, Scientist-E, ICFRE 
(Retd.), who have helped the respondent 
no.5, the Commission in filing the report.
The Court wanted to know as to 
whether those experts could join the proceedings through Video Conferencing to 
assist the Court as to what was the 
method, they adopted for ensuring as to 
whether segregation could be done or not?
Learned counsel for the Commission 
would submit that a request may be made 
to the experts so that they may join the 
proceedings at 2:00 PM today through 
video conferencing.
Let the request be made. If they could 
join to assist the Court, the matter may 
proceed further.
