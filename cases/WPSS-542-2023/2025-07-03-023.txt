Arguments heard, but not concluded.
In the instant matter, a public 
examination has been cancelled on the
ground of unfair means that is put to 
challenge. One of the basis of cancellation 
of the examination was that the police has 
lodged an FIR and in the matter under 
investigation, certain offenders were 
identified. The charge sheet records that 
the investigation was still on when the 
charge sheet was submitted.
Let State file a short affidavit 
revealing as to whether, investigation in 
the matter has been completed or as to 
whether after filing of initial charge sheet, 
any supplementary charge sheet along 
with other material has been filed? If so, all 
those documents may also be placed. This 
affidavit may be filed within three weeks.
At the same time, the Court requests 
the respondent no.4 to submit a short 
affidavit revealing therein as to whether 
they assisted the Investigating Officer of 
the case so as to identify the accused in 
that case and if so, how was it done? The 
respondent no.4 may also indicate as to 
whether by reading the log or 620 successful candidates, is it possible to 
segregate the candidates who might have 
used unfair means.
Let it be filed within three weeks.
List thereafter.
