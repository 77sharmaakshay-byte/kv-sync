Heard.
List this petition along with WPSS 
No. 288 of 2023. Counter affidavits be 
filed by respondents in the meantime.
Petitioner has challenged the 
decision taken by Selecting Body to 
cancel the entire selection. From the 
material on record, it is apparent that 
there was a complaint received that 
unfair means were used by examinee on 
a massive scale and the agency hired for 
holding online examination has also been 
blacklisted, as there were complaints 
received against the said company and 
the owner of said company have been arrested, who are still in jail.
In such view of the matter, this 
Court does not find any good ground to 
stay the selection process, which has 
now begun, in terms of the 
advertisement, which is impugned in this 
writ petition. 
Interim Relief Application (I.A. No. 
1 of 2023) is rejected.
