In this bunch of petitions, it is 
submitted by learned counsel for the 
petitioners that they appeared in the 
examination. They were successful, but the 
whole examination process has been 
cancelled without any reasons. It is being 
argued that, in fact, if any irregularity was 
done by some of the candidates, that was 
easily traceable and, in fact, it has been 
traced by the Special Task Force. Therefore, 
what is the contention of the petitioners is 
instead of cancelling the whole of the 
examination, the respondent authority 
could have segregated those candidates, 
who have used unfair means or did any illegality, while maintaining the selection of 
the petitioners. It is also being submitted 
that the next exam has also been notified 
for 11.06.2023. If the matter is not heard, 
or the interim order is not passed, the 
rights of the petitioners may be blurred. 
It is such an important matter. It is 
submitted that State has yet not filed its 
counter affidavit.
Let State file its counter affidavit till 
05.06.2023 in all the cases, failing which 
the Court would be compelled to call the 
Secretary of the concerned department to 
assist the Court in open Court on 
06.06.2023. 
On behalf of the respondent no.3, a 
statement is given that counter affidavit has 
been filed in only two of the cases. 
Let the respondent no.3 file counter 
affidavit in all the cases by 05.06.2023. 
Rejoinder affidavit, if any, may be filed 
thereafter on 06.06.2023 and 07.06.2023.
List this matter on 08.06.2023, for 
final hearing.
