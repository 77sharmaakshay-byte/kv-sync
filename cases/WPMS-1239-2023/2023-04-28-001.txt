WPMS No.1239 of 2023
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Hari Mohan Bhatia, Advocate for 
the petitioner.
Mr. H.M. Raturi, D.A.G. for the

Mr. D.S. Mehta, Advocate for the 
respondent no.4/Workers Union.
Heard.
An agreement, which was entered in 
between the Workers Union and the 
Management dated 28.10.2021, is an issue 
in this petition, which, according to the 
petition, the Management has acted 
partially and have given benefit to 16 
workers. It is being argued that the 
remaining 71 workers are not agreeable to 
the Clause 3.5 of the agreement. They 
wanted the matter to be referred to the 
authorities. Therefore, they have not been 
given the benefits. But, despite that, the 
recovery has been issued under Section 6-
H(1) of the U.P. Industrial Dispute Act, 
1947.
Learned counsel for the respondent 
no.4/Workers Union would submit that, 
in fact, Clause 3.5 of the agreement had 
already been deleted at the time of 
agreement. It is not in existence. It was 
the part of the agreement, but it has been 
deleted. In fact, Annexure 5 to the petition 
is the copy of the agreement filed by the 
petitioner himself, in which Clause 3.5 of 
the agreement has been deleted and 
signed by 7 persons. 
Who are those 7 persons? Are not they from Management? And if, there is 
an agreement in which Clause 3.5 is 
intact, why it has not been filed by the 
petitioner?
When requested, learned counsel for 
the petitioner seeks time to file the 
agreement dated 28.10.2021 entered 
between the Management and the 
Workers Union, in which Clause 3.5 is 
still there, signed by the parties 
concerned. 
Let it be so filed along with a short 
affidavit. 
List this matter on 02.05.2023. 
 (Ravindra Maithani J.)
