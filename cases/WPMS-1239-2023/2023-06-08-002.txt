Heard on Stay Application No. 1 of 
2023.
It is a case of recovery of certain 
amount from the petition, which the 
petitioner is bound under the agreement.
It is argued on behalf of the 
petitioner that, in fact, the agreement is 
not registered. But, learned counsel 
appearing for the respondent no.4 would 
submit that the petitioner had given of 
benefit of the agreement to some of the 
workers.
At it, learned counsel for the 
petitioner would submit that the 
payment is made to such workers, who 
had agreed existence of Clause 3.5 of the 
agreement.
The Court refrains to make any 
observation with regard to Clause 3.5 of 
the agreement.A scanned copy of the agreement 
has been filed by the petitioner alongwith 
the supplementary affidavit, which 
would fall for scrutiny at the subsequent 
stage.
Having considered, this Court is of 
the view that this application shall be 
decided after inviting objections from the 
petitioner.
Let it be so filed within a week.
List this matter on 19.06.2023 after 
fresh cases.
