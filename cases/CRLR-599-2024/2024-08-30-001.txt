COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLR No.599 of 2024
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
 Mr. I.D. Paliwal, Advocate for the 
revisionist.
 Mr. C.S. Dumka, AGA for the State.
 The challenge in this revision is made 
to the judgment and order dated 
25.06.2024, passed in Smt. Laxmi Devi 
and another vs. Ramkrishna Nautiyal and 
others, by the court of District and 
Sessions Judge, Tehri Garhwal, by which,
the son of the respondent no.2, Smt. 
Laxmi Devi has been granted `8,000/- per 
month maintenance from the date of filing 
of the application i.e. from 03.12.2018 till 
30.06.2023, when he attained majority. 
 Learned counsel for the revisionist has 
though in the memo of revision stated that 
by the impugned order maintenance has 
been granted to the respondent no.2, but 
he has not made him party. 
 Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would submit that during the course of 
the day, an amended memo of party will 
be filed showing the son of the revisionist 
as one of the respondents in the revision. 
 Let it be so done.
 It is argued that the trial court has 
denied maintenance to the son of the 
revisionist, but the appellant court took a 
view that the son of the revisionist be paid 
`8,000/- per month, as maintenance till 
he attains majority. 
 Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would submit that, in fact, earlier `8,000/- per month, as interim 
maintenance was awarded by the trial 
court which was challenged by the 
revisionist in Criminal Revision No.178 of 
2021 before this Court and by the order 
dated 16.11.2022, this Court has reduced 
the amount of interim maintenance to 
`4,000/- per month. 
 Having considered, this Court is of the 
view that this matter requires deliberation. 
 Admit.
 Issue notice to the private respondent, 
returnable within six weeks. Steps to be 
taken within a week. 
 List on 26.11.2024.
 Having considered, till the next date of 
listing, the revisionist shall pay the arrears 
of maintenance @ `4,000/- per month to 
the son of the revisionist, till he attains
majority. 
 Stay Application (IA) No.1 of 2024, 
stands disposed of. 
 
(Ravindra Maithani, J.)
