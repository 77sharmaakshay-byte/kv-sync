3. Whenever the crops are ripe for harvest, the 
petitioners shall harvest the crop. The date of harvest 
shall be intimated to the local Tehsildar, who or his 
representative shall be present during the harvest and 
weigh the crop and keep the record. The harvested crops 
shall be weighed at the spot in the presence of the 
Tehsildar or his representative and a statement of the 
weighment shall be prepared in duplicate and retained 
by the petitioner and the Tehsildar and be produced on 
the next date of hearing. The harvested crop shall be 
stocked safely pending further orders of this Court. 
4. Liberty is given to the parties to mention the case 
after reopening post Diwali Vacations. 
5. Interim order granted earlier also to continue till 
the next date of listing.
