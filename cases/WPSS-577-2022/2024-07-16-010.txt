Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
1. Mr. K.K. Harbola, learned counsel holding 
brief of Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for 
the petitioner/applicant.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hariya, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State.
3. Mr. Ramji Srivastava, learned counsel for 
respondent no.3-Uttarakhand Medical Service 
Selection Board. 
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
5. Today, the matter is listed on Amendment 
Application No.05 of 2023. The objection has 
already been filed on the said amendment 
application.
6. Having gone through the amendment 
application as well as objection to the 
amendment application filed on behalf of 
respondent no.3, this Court finds that 
amendment application deserves to be allowed 
for the reason that by way of amendment 
application, applicant is only seeking to 
challenge new advertisement, issued by 
respondent no.3.
7. The amendment application is, therefor, 
allowed.
8. Let the necessary amendment be 
incorporated within a period of one week from 
today by taking steps as per law, by way of 
filing amended copy of writ petition. 
9. Learned counsel for the respondents may 
file their counter affidavits to the amended writ 
petition.
10. List this petition on 21.09.2024.
