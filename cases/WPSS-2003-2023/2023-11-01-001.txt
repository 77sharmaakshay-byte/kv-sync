Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J. (Oral)
By means of this writ petition, petitioner has 
challenged the order dated 20.10.2023, annexure-14 to 
the writ petition issued by respondent no.4, whereby the 
candidature of the petitioner was rejected for the post of 
Operation Theatre Technician.
2. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
3. It is the case of the petitioner that an 
advertisement was issued by respondent no.4 on 
13.08.2021 inviting the application for various posts 
including the post of Operation Theatre Technician. 
4. According to learned counsel for the petitioner, 
as per the terms and conditions of the advertisement, 
candidate apart from fulfilling the other educational 
qualifications was required to be registered with the 
Uttarakhand State Medical Faculty before the last date of 
submission of the application form. 
5. Since the petitioner was not registered with the 
Uttarakhand State Medical Faculty on the last date of 
submission of the application form i.e. 15.09.2021, which 
was subsequently extended till 27.01.2022, therefore, the
candidature of the petitioner was rejected. 2
6. It is submitted by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that petitioner is qualified in all respect except 
petitioner was not registered with the Uttarakhand State 
Medical Faculty. But, subsequently, respondent-State 
took a policy decision that the candidates who have been 
registered with the U.P. State Medical Faculty can be 
registered with the Uttarakhand State Medical Faculty 
and for that purpose, one year was granted to such 
candidates. Consequently, the last date of submission of 
application form was also extended by respondent no.4 
till 27.01.2022. He further submits that before 
27.01.2022, petitioner had submitted his application to 
the Uttarakhand State Medical Faculty for registration 
surrendering his all requisite documents of qualifications. 
Thus, petitioner shall be treated eligible as he was, 
subsequent to the last date i.e. 27.01.2022 got 
registration with Uttarakhand State Medical Faculty. 
7. Per contra, learned counsel for respondent no.4 
submits that even after the extension of period of one year 
for registration with the Uttarakhand State Medical 
Faculty, petitioner was not eligible and till the last date of 
submission of application form i.e. on 27.01.2022, he was 
not eligible, therefore, rejection of the candidature of the 
petitioner suffers from no vices. He also relied upon the 
judgment passed by this Court in Writ Petition (S/S) 
No.1901 of 2023, Nidhi Sharma vs. Uttarakhand Medical 
Service Selection Board & others dated 10.10.2023, 
wherein this Court has held that the eligibility of a
candidate shall be looked into only after the last date of 
submission of the application form.
8. To this, learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that facts of the case cited by respondent no.4 3
are quite different with that of the facts of the present 
case, inasmuch as, in the case in hand, the window for 
making the application for registration in the 
Uttarakhand State Medical Faculty was opened only on 
06.01.2022, therefore, petitioner after making preparation 
submitted his application form on 15.01.2022 to the 
Uttarakhand State Medical Faculty and the said Faculty 
sent the documents of the petitioner with N.O.C. to the 
U.P. State Medical Faculty. 
9. On the strength of the aforesaid facts, it is 
argued by learned counsel for the petitioner that 
petitioner is not at all at fault for not getting his 
registration certificate prior to the last date of submission 
of application form and there is no dispute to this fact 
that at present, petitioner is having the registration 
certificate duly registered with the Uttarakhand State 
Medical Faculty, which was registered on the basis of 
earlier registration certificate issued to the petitioner by 
the U.P. State Medical Faculty in the year 2014. 
10. In order to buttress his argument, he placed 
reliance upon the judgment of Hon’ble Apex Court in the 
case of Kumari Laxmi Saroj & others vs. State of U.P. & 
others, 2023 (2) UPLBEC 874. In the said judgment, it 
has been held that where the candidates are not at fault 
for not being registered prior to the last date of 
submission of application form, they will not made to 
suffer without there being no fault on their part. 
11. Having heard the learned counsel for the 
parties and from perusal of both the judgments supplied 
by the learned counsel for the parties, this Court feels 
that the facts of this case are different to the facts of the 4
case Nidhi Sharma (supra). Here in the case in hand, 
petitioner was incapacitated for not applying for the 
registration of the Uttarakhand State Medical Faculty for 
the reason that the window was only opened on 
06.01.2022 and before that the said application could not 
have been made by the petitioner for registration. In this 
view of the matter, this Court inclined to entertain the 
writ petition. 
12. Learned counsel for the respondent(s) prays for 
and is granted six weeks’ time to file counter affidavit(s).
13. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the 
petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
14. List this matter on 09.01.2024.
15. However, it is provided that one post of 
‘Operation Theatre Technician’ in open category shall be 
kept vacant for the petitioner, during the pendency of the 
present writ petition. 
16. Interim relief application (IA No.1/2023) stands 
disposed of accordingly.
