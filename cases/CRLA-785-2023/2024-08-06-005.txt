4. As per case of the prosecution on 04.07.2016 
information was given by informant-Manju Devi in Police 
Station Khatima, District Udham Singh Nagar stating 
therein that her husband-Suraj Chand went out of his home 2
at 08:00 AM; he made a call from his telephone at 
afternoon and told the informant that he was not coming 
for lunch. In the evening at 08:00 PM he again called to the 
informant not to prepare dinner for him at home and he 
would come home in the morning. But when he did not 
come to his home in the morning he was called over his 
telephone, but his telephone was switched-off and no 
information regarding his whereabouts were received by 
his family members.
5. On the basis of the aforesaid FIR a chick FIR 
No.163 of 2016 under Section 365 IPC was registered at 
Police Station Khatima, District Udham Singh Nagar. 
Subsequently on pointing out of Lalit Singh Jyala, Pappu 
Singh Rana and Ishwari Singh, dead body of the husband 
of the informant, Suraj Chand was recovered from a gutter,
who met homicidal death. After investigation charge-sheet 
was submitted against all the accused persons including the 
appellant/applicant. After trial the appellant/applicant has 
been convicted as stated above.
6. Learned counsel for the appellant/applicant 
strenuously submitted that there is no evidence against her 
except the call detail of the mobile of accused Lalit Singh 
Jyala No.9897845303 to the appellant/applicant’s 
No.7830129272. Lalit Jyala/co-accused, since deceased 
was the real brother of Munni Devi @ Maya Devi and 
therefore according to learned counsel for the 
appellant/applicant, it is not strange that the C.D.R.
reflected talks between them.
7. It is further submitted by learned counsel for the 
appellant/applicant that she is a lady and in view of Section 
437 of Cr.P.C. 1973 (Section 480 of Bharatiya Nagarik 
Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (B.N.S.S.) the appellant/applicant 3
is entitled to be released on bail, during pendency of this 
appeal. She is in jail since 05.10.2023.
8. Per contra, learned counsel for the State could not 
show any other evidence against the appellant/applicant 
from the entire brief, but he submitted that Suraj Chand 
was kidnapped and then brutally murdered by the 
appellant/applicant along with other co-accused persons.
9. Having considered rival contention of the parties 
and without expressing any opinion on the final merits of 
the appeal, we are inclined to release the 
appellant/applicant-Munni Devi on bail, during pendency 
of this appeal.
10. Accordingly, the bail application is allowed. Let 
applicant – Munni Devi be released on bail, on her
executing a personal bond and furnishing two reliable 
sureties, each of the like amount to the satisfaction of the 
Court concerned. 
11. List this case in due course.
