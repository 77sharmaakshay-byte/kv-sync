IN THE HIGH COURT OF UTTARAKHAND 
AT NAINITAL
Anticipatory Bail Application No. 692 of 2025
Reena Bisht. …….Applicant.
Versus
State of Uttarakhand. ….…Respondent. 
Present:
Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for the applicant.
Mr. G.S. Sandhu, learned Addl. Advocate General with Mr. Pradeep Lohani, learned Brief Holder for 
the State.
Mr. Neeraj Pandey, learned counsel for the complainant.
Hon’ble Mr. Justice Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. The present applicant is praying for anticipatory bail in 
relation to FIR dated 21.05.2025 bearing FIR No. 0049 of 2025, 
Police Station – Shyampur, District – Haridwar lodged by one
Smt. Geetanjali Giri, wife of Mahant Rohit Giri, implicating 
present applicant along with her husband Mahant Rohit Giri, 
Meena Chauhan, Sameer, Tej Prakhash Bisht, Raj Kumar Mishra, 
Ritik, Vijay Mohan Panwar, Sonu and Sonu Kashyap @ Mogli for 
the offences punishable under Section 109 (1), 303 (1), 308 (2), 308 
(3), read with Section 61 (2) (b) of BNS, 2023. During ongoing 
investigation, Section 308 (3) of BNS, 2023 is converted to Section 
308 (7) of BNS, 2023.
2. Brief facts of the case are that complainant Smt. Geetanjali 
Giri married with Mahant Rohit Giri on 07.03.2006, from which 
one male child was born, who is aged about 19 years old as on 
date. Due to some matrimonial dispute in between both of them, 
a petition under Section 13 B of the Hindu Marriage Act was filed
on 07.03.2022 seeking divorce by way of mutual consent wherein 
it is contended that both of them are living separately since 
21.05.2018. In this petition, first motion was completed but 
second motion was failed. Thereafter, on 30.09.2023, Mahant 2
Rohit Giri filed another divorce petition under Section 13 (1) (i-b) 
of the Hindu Marriage Act seeking divorce on the ground of 
desertion. In paragraph 4 and 5 of the petition, date of desertion 
is mentioned as 21.05.2018 and this is the same date, which was 
mentioned in the petition filed Section 13 B of the Hindu 
Marriage Act. 
3. It is argued by the learned counsel for the applicant Mr. 
Aditya Singh that on 22.08.2024 in the aforesaid divorce petition 
the concerned court ordered to proceed ex parte and for recalling 
the order of ex parte, the complainant – Smt. Geetanjali filed an 
application along with affidavit under Order 9 Rule 7 read with 
Section 151 CPC on 18.01.2025. 
4. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that in both the 
petitions filed under Section 13 B and under Section 13 (1) (i-b) of 
the Hindu Marriage Act, address of the complainant is the same
and in both the divorce petitions the date of desertion is same i.e. 
21.05.2018 and the address of the complainant and Mahant Rohit 
Giri are also different which itself reveals that both of them are 
living separately since 21.05.2018. 
5. Learned counsel for the applicant further argued that so far 
as present applicant is concerned she is in live-in-relation with 
Mahant Rohit Giri for the last more than one year and from this 
living relation, one female child was born on 14.01.2025 and both 
of them the applicant and Mahant Rohit Giri are residing together 
in the same house along with minor child. 
6. Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for the applicant, 
submits that Mahant Rohit Giri of Maa Chandi Devi Temple with 
whom the applicant was in live in relation was arrested by 
Punjab Police from Neel Parvat, Haridwar where the temple is 3
located, in a molestation case filed by a woman from Ludhiyana 
(Punjab) in which a non bailable warrant was issued. 
7. Learned counsel for the applicant further submits that after 
seven days of arrest of Mahant Rohit Giri on 21.05.2025 Smt. 
Geetanajali lodged a first information report implicating 
applicant and Mahant Rohit Giri with others raising various 
allegations and one of the allegation is that her husband Mahant 
Giri informed that the applicant Reena Bisht made a video of 
physical relationship and he is praying money otherwise, the 
video would be made viral. It is further alleged that on hearing 
the arrest of Mahant Rohit Giri, complainant reached at the house 
of Mahant Rohit Giri and during this period, the applicant tried 
to run over her son by her car. Learned counsel further argued 
that all the allegations, as alleged in the FIR, are completely false, 
baseless and after thought which is evident from the fact that 
though Mahant Rohit Giri was arrested on 14.05.2025 but in the 
FIR, Mahant Rohit Giri is also implicated for the alleged incident 
of 14.05.2025 and not only this after 7 days of the alleged incident 
FIR was lodged. He further submits that the allegation, as alleged 
in the FIR, against the applicant is highly improbable, since at 
that time, applicant was having 4 month old daughter and no 
prudent person can presume that such an incident can be caused 
by the present applicant. 
8. On the other side, Mr. Neeraj Pandey, who put his 
appearance on behalf of the complainant submits that since 
Mahant Rohit Giri resigned on 13.05.2025, one day prior to his 
arrest wherein he proposed to appoint his son to perform the 
duties of Mahant, consequently, after the arrest of Mahant Rohit 
Giri, his son namely Shri Bhawani Nand Giri, who is 19 years old 
resume the charge of Mahant.4
9. Mr. Neeraj Pandey, learned counsel for the complainant 
further argued that all the arguments advanced by Mr. Aditya 
Singh are thoroughly misconceived. Even the date of desertion as 
mentioned in the petition are not admitted and in fact, in the 
divorce petition written statements was filed wherein 
complainant has categorically stated that she is living separately 
from Mahant Rohit Giri since 22.05.2023 and this is absolutely 
incorrect to say that she is living separately since 21.05.2018. 
Apart from this, he further submits that living relation of the 
present applicant with Mahant Rohit Giri is not permissible in 
view of Section 380 (2) of the UCC Act, 2024. 
10. Apart from this, Mr. Neeraj Pandey, learned counsel for the 
complainant, submits that present applicant had removed the 
CCTV camera installed in the premises of temple.
11. On this, Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for the 
applicant, has seriously object by submitting that since the 
applicant was having 4 month old daughter and Mahant Rohit 
Giri was arrested on 14.05.2025, therefore, it is highly improbable 
for her to remove the CCTV camera and it’s footage and 
furthermore, the allegations, as alleged in the FIR, are completely 
baseless, which is evident from the fact that in reference of 
incident of 14.05.2025, the FIR has been lodged after 7 days. He 
further submits that no such resignation as per his information 
was given by Mahant Rohit Giri and it is manufactured one. 
12. Yesterday, when the matter was listed, Mr. Aditya Singh, 
learned counsel for the applicant pointed out that on the date of 
arrest of Mahant Rohit Giri, on the charge of molestation by 
Punjab police the complainant entered in the premises of temple 
by projecting herself as a Trustee of the Trust and she made an 
attempt to take over the management of the temple.5
13. This Court took very serious note of serious charge of 
molestation by a woman against Mahant Rohit Giri of the Mahant 
of Maa Chandi Devi Temple, which is one of the most ancient 
temple in the northern India, which is said to have been founded 
in 8th Century by Adi Guru Shankaracharya, one of the top priest 
of the Hindu Religion, which obviously hurt the sentiments of 
lakh of devotees. This Court instruct to Mr. G.S. Sandhu, Addl. 
Advocate General to ask the District Magistrate and SSP, 
Haridwar to join the proceedings through V.C. and in compliance 
thereof, both the officers joined the proceedings through V.C. and 
this Court direct them to monitor the entire situation and to 
maintain law and order in the entire premises of temple and get 
instructions from the Ministry of Tourism and Religious Affairs
what preventive measure can be taken in the premises of temple 
since thousands of devotees visits everyday in the temple for 
Darshan as well as to perform pooja. 
14. Today, District Magistrate as well as SSP, Haridwar joined 
the proceedings through V.C. and apprise this Court that there is 
a Committee called as Badrinath Kedarnath Temple Committee
(in short BKTC) and this Committee is managing affairs of
various temples in the State including Badrinath and Kedarnath 
temples. 
15. After hearing learned counsel for the parties and taking into 
consideration the allegation, as alleged in the FIR, prima facie, 
this Court is of the view that the trustees of the temple are 
creating noxious atmosphere in the temple and there is complete 
mismanagement in the trust and it cannot be ruled out that there 
may be misappropriation of donation. Apart from this since there 
are serious allegations of molestation against Mahant Rohit Giri
of which this Court already take serious note of it. Apart from 6
this, there are also serious allegations, as alleged in the FIR, which 
requires thorough investigation for which SSP Haridwar is 
directed to depute a senior officer not below the rank of Dy. S.P. 
to investigate the matter and inquire about the conduct of the 
applicant as well as of the complainant and also against the 
persons against whom FIR has been lodged. SSP, Haridwar is 
also directed to monitor the entire investigation and apprise this 
Court about the status of investigation on the next date. 
16. Apart from this, taking into consideration that Maa Chandi 
Devi Temple is an ancient temple of northern India and 
thousands of devotees visit everyday for pooja and Darshan in 
the temple, therefore, till the investigation is completed, this 
Court is primarily of the view that some preventive measures 
requires to be taken.
17. Mr. Ravi Babulkar, learned counsel for the Badrinath 
Kedarnath Temple Committee (in short BKTC) on instructions, 
submits that there is Badrinath Kedarnath Temple Committee 
Act, 1939 and under this Act, the Committee controls the affairs 
of various temples and as such, Badrinath Kedarnath Temple 
Committee (in short BKTC) can supervise the affairs of the 
existing Managing Trust of the Maa Chandi Devi Temple.
18. In such view of the matter, till the investigation is 
completed, the existing Trust shall continue but the existing Trust 
will act under the direct control and supervision of Badrinath 
Kedarnath Temple Committee (in short BKTC) and for that 
purpose, the District Administration is directed to provide full 
assistance to the Badrinath Kedarnath Temple Committee(in 
short BKTC). Apart from this Badrinath Kedarnath Temple 
Committee (in short BKTC) is directed to appoint “Receiver” 
immediately. Furthermore, since the investigation is going on in 7
such an eventuality, applicant and complainant are restrained 
from interfering in day to day affairs of the Managing Trust of 
Maa Chandi Devi Temple. 
19. Since applicant is praying for anticipatory bail, therefore, 
keeping in view the fact that since applicant is having four month 
old daughter, this Court is of the view that till the investigation is 
going on, applicant deserves for interim protection. In such an 
eventuality, let the investigation may go on, but in the event of 
arrest of the applicant – Reena Bisht, she shall be released on 
interim bail on executing a personal bond of Rs. 30,000/- and two 
reliable sureties, of the like amount, to the satisfaction of the 
Investigating Officer/ Arresting Officer, subject to the following 
conditions:
(i) Applicant as well as complainant shall cooperate 
with the investigating agency and make themselves
available for interrogation by the concerned 
Investigating Officer as and when required; 
(ii) Applicant shall not directly or indirectly make any 
inducement, threat or promise to any person acquainted 
with the facts of this case and; 
(iii) Applicant shall not leave the country without 
previous permission of this Court. 
(iv) SSP, Haridwar is also directed to monitor the 
investigation as well as maintain law and order in the 
entire premises of Maa Chandi Devi Temple and he 
shall ensure that no one put any harm to any devotee, 
who visits the temple for pooja.8
(v) District Administration is directed to give full 
assistance to officials of the Badrinath Kedarnath 
Temple Committee (in short BKTC). 
20. Respondents may file counter affidavit within four weeks. 
Two weeks, thereafter, is granted to the applicant to file rejoinder 
affidavit.
21. List on 13.08.2025.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
