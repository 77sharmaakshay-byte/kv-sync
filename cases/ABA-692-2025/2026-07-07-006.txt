6. When this anticipatory bail application 
came up before this Court on 25.06.2026, this 
Court take very serious note of the serious 
charges of molestation against the Mahant Rohit 
Giri, Mahant of math Chandi Devi temple and for 
an interim measure till the investigation is 
completed, the existing trust was permitted to 
continue under the direct control and supervision 
of the Badrinath Kedarnath Temple Committee 
(in short BKTC), a statutory body, governed 
under the 1939 Act with the further direction that District Administration shall provide full 
assistance to the said Committee and for an 
interim measure, the applicant was granted 
interim protection by granting interim bail 
subject to certain conditions. 
7. Aforesaid order was assailed before the 
Apex Court by the present Trustee/Mahant 
Bhawani Nandan Giri in Special Leave to Appeal 
(Criminal) No.11285 of 2025 and the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court on 19.08.2025 remand the matter 
without disturbing the order dated 25.06.2025 
with the direction to decide the issue of 
permanent management of the Trust by focusing 
on the best interest of the “math” and its 
“devotees”. The Collector was also directed to 
submit a report. The BKTC was also directed to 
submit up to date report to the High Court.
8. While remanding the matter, the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court also requested to the High Court 
to implead the persons, who may not be the 
party before the High Court and have been heard 
and also requested to take up the matter on 
priority and decide the issue, so that an 
arrangement/regular arrangement is put into 
place rather than an ad-hoc arrangement. Thus 
the mandate of the remand order of the Supreme 
Court was to decide the issue for 
permanent/regular arrangement on the best 
interest of the “math and its devotees”.
