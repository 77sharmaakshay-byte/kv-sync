COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
ABA No. 692 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. None appears for the applicant.
2. Mr. Sandeep Sharma, learned A.G.A. for the 
State.
3. In compliance to the previous order the Collector 
as well as BKTC submitted its report and the same are 
opened, court perused the same. Let both the report be 
treated part of the record.
4. The Collector’s report is marked as ‘report-A’ 
and the report of the BKTC is marked as ‘report-B’. 
From the Collector’s report it reveals that the 
Collector’s report is not as per the direction of the 
Hon’ble Apex Court dated 19.08.2025, whereby, the 
Collector was directed to focus on the best interest for 
the math and its devotees. It appears from the report of 
the Collector that the Collector only submitted the 
report with regard to the present situation of temple 
and its premises including the facilities but what would 
be the best in the interest of math and its devotees this 
is completely missing, therefore, the Collector is 
directed to submit a report strictly as per the direction 
of the Hon’ble Apex Court focusing on the interest for 
the math and its devotees.
5. Put up this matter on 27.03.2026.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
