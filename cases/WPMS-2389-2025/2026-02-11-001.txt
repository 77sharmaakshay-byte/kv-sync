WPMS No.2389 of 2025
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The present writ petition has been filed 
challenging the Award dated 17.03.2025, 
passed by learned Presiding Officer, Labour 
Court, Dehradun in Adjudication Case No.
18 of 2012, “Prantiya Mahamantri, Bijali 
Karmachari Sangh, Dehradun vs. Managing 
Director, Uttarakhand Power Corporation 
Limited and Others”.
2. Learned Presiding Officer has held that 
the contractual workers are entitled to the 
minimum of the regular pay scale payable 
to regular employees working on the same 
post.
3. Mr. D.S. Patni, learned Senior 
Advocate assisted by Mr. Bagwat Mehra, 
learned counsel for the petitioners.
4. Mr. M.C. Pant, learned counsel for the 
respondent no.1 through video 
conferencing.
5. Mr. Neeraj Garg, learned counsel for 
the respondent no.2.
6. Learned Senior Advocate appearing for 
the petitioners submitted that the 
contractual workers were engaged through 
outsourcing agency. They are not within the 
definition of “workmen”. Therefore, they are not entitled to the minimum of the regular 
pay scale. He has relied upon a judgment of 
the Hon’ble Supreme Court dated 
16.12.2025 in Civil Appeal arising out of SLP 
(Civil) No.17711 of 2019.
7. Mr. M.C. Pant, Advocate, for the 
respondent no.1 submitted that the 
contractual workers are performing the 
same duties as regular employees. They fall 
under the definition of “workmen”. 
Therefore, they are entitled to the minimum 
of the regular pay scale, based on the 
principle of “equal pay for equal work”.
8. Learned counsel for the respondents 
have sought two weeks’ time to file counter 
affidavit in the present fresh matter.
9. In the facts and circumstances of the 
case, time is granted.
10. List on 10.03.2026 after fresh cases. 
The interim relief application shall be 
considered on the date fixed.
(Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
