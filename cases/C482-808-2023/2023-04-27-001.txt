COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
27.04.2023 C482 No. 808 of 2023
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Siddharth Bankoti, Advocate, for 
the applicants.
Mr. Tumul Kumar Nainwal, Brief 
Holder, for the State.
In an incident, which had chanced on 
15.09.2016, a minor scuffle took place between 
the family members, which has resulted into 
the death of the father of the applicant No. 1 as 
he fell down. Consequently the FIR No. 456,
dated 16.09.2016, was registered by the 
present applicant for the involvement of the 
applicants for the offences under Sections 304, 
323, 34, 452 & 506 of IPC, which was tried by 
the Court of Sub Divisional Judicial Magistrate,
Safidon (Jind), and it ultimately resulted into 
conviction of the respondent for the offence 
under Section 323 of IPC, by a judgment 
rendered on 21.09.2022.
Arising out of the same incident dated 
15.09.2016, the FIR was also registered by 
respondent No. 2, being FIR No. 78 of 2021, as 
filed on 07.03.2021, for the offences under 
Sections 323, 498-A, 504 & 506 of IPC and 
Section 3/4 of Dowry Prohibition Act.
Apart from above proceedings, there 
had been other matrimonial proceedings going 
on inter se between the parties, i.e., the 
proceedings under Section 13 of the Hindu 
Marriage Act, instituted by the present 
applicant No. 1, for seeking dissolution of 
marriage and the second proceeding for 
seeking custody of the child, as preferred under 
the provisions of Guardians and Wards Act, 1890, that was dismissed as against the present 
applicant.
Owing to the fact that now the 
misunderstanding between the parties have 
reached to such an extent that there is no 
possibility of settlement, and coupled with the 
fact, as it has been argued by the learned 
counsel for the applicants, that the first FIR was 
registered at a belated stage, the entire 
proceedings drawn by way of Criminal Case No. 
65 of 2022, State Vs. Manjeet @ Sonu and 
another, is nothing but as a cause of vengeance 
due to judgment of conviction rendered in the 
proceeding which was initiated by the present 
applicant.
Issue notice to respondent No. 2.
Respondent No. 1 is represented by the 
learned Government Advocate. Steps to serve 
respondent No. 2 would be taken by the 
applicant within a period of one week from 
today.
List after service of notice on the 
respondent.
Till the next date of listing, further 
proceedings of Criminal Case No. 65 of 2022, 
State Vs. Manjeet @ Sonu & Another, pending 
consideration before the Court of Judicial 
Magistrate, Sitarganj, District Udham Singh 
Nagar, would be kept in abeyance.
Interim Relief Application No. 
IA/1/2023 stands disposed of accordingly.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
