COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
SA No. 92 of 2019
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Kailash Chandra Tewari, Advocate 
for the appellants.
Mr. Kurban Ali, Advocate for the 
respondent.
A Co-ordinate Bench of this High Court 
by an order dated 15.07.2019, while admitting 
the second appeal had directed the parties to 
maintain status-quo. The appellant has filed an 
application under Order 39 Rule 2-A of CPC 
alleging thereof, that plaintiff-respondents have 
violated the interim order of 15.07.2019.
To this application, the plaintiff –
respondents have filed their objection. 
The counsel for the appellants submits 
that in order to substantiate his stand taken in 
the application under Order 39 Rule 2-A of 
CPC, about the status of the property, which 
was prevailing, on the date of the passing of the 
interim order on 15.07.2019, is a fact which is 
required to be established by evidence. 
In order to substantiate his stand taken 
under Order 39 Rule 2-A of CPC, the counsel 
for the appellant prays for, that he may be 
permitted to file an application alongwith 
supporting documents to establish violation of 
the interim order dated 15.07.2019.He is permitted to do so within a period 
of two weeks from today.
List this application under Order 39 Rule 
2-A, for its consideration as soon as the 
application is filed within the aforesaid period.
Interim order dated 15.07.2019 is 
continued to operate till the next date of listing.
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
