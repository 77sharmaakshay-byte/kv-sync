Admit the appeal on the following 
substantial questions of law:-
(a) Whether the appellants being the 
grandson and daughter-in-law of the 
respondent have right of residence in the 
ancestral property in dispute and a suit 
for injunction against them is 
maintainable?

appreciate the uncontroverted testimony 
of the appellants and Smt. Lalita Devi 
that the appellants and Smt. Lalita Devi 
are living in 4 rooms that were given to 
Smt. Lalita Devi by the respondent?
Summon the lower court record.
List this matter after receipt of lower 
court record.
Meanwhile, both the parties shall 
maintain status quo.
