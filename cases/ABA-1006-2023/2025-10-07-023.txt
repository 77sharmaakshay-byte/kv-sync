COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
ABA No.1006 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Aditya Singh and Mr. Amanjot 
Singh Chadha, learned counsel for the 
applicant.
2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned A.G.A. with 
Ms. Sweta Badola Dobhal, learned Brief 
Holder for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. Applicant was involved in connection 
with FIR No.0032 dated 11.03.2017 
which was registered at P.S. Pant Nagar, 
District Udham Singh Nagar, for the 
offences u/s 420, 465, 466, 468, 471, 
474, 120-B, 34 IPC and Sections 8/9 of 
the Prevention of Corruption Act. He 
moved ABA No.1006 of 2023, which was 
disposed of by reason of judgment and 
order dated 14.12.2023 with certain 
conditions, one of which was Condition 
No.(iii) which provided that he shall not 
leave the country without the previous 
permission of this Court.
4. Applicant has now moved 
miscellaneous application No.6 of 2025
for permitting him to travel United States 
of America for a period of one month. It 
is stated in Para 6 of the said application 
that applicant is a Green Card Holder of 
USA and in order to retain the said 
status, he is required to travel to the said 
Country once in 180 days and he is an 
Indian Passport Holder bearing 2
No.C3444285 issued by Regional 
Passport Office, Dehradun having validity 
till 08.10.2034.
5. Pursuant to the time given by this 
Court, State has filed its objection along 
with delay condonation application. Delay 
in filing the objection is condoned. 
Objection is taken on record. Delay 
Condonation Application (IA/7/2025) 
made therefor, is allowed.
6. Learned State Counsel objected the 
misc. application for giving permission to 
the applicant to visit USA only on the 
ground that his visit is affecting the trial. 
7. Applicant was earlier allowed to visit
USA for a period of two months 
commenced from his date of departure
vide this Court’s order dated 27.02.2025. 
However, it was also directed that he 
shall immediately report his arrival to this 
Court. The applicant departed to USA 
pursuant to the order dated 27.02.2025
on 25.03.2025 and returned to India on 
19.05.2025, as it is reflected from 
annexure No.A.5 to the Misc. Application.
8. Since, the applicant has obeyed this 
Court’s order and returned to India and 
intimated about his arrival to the Court 
by filing an application (IA/5/2025), this 
Court has no reason to deny permission 
to applicant to visit USA for a period of 
one month as prayed by him. Further, 
the objection raised by learned State 
Counsel is misconceived. The appearance 
of the applicant-accused is required only 3
at the time of recording of statement 
under Section 313 Cr.P.C. Therefore, this 
Court is of the view that by merely 
allowing the application of the applicant 
for permitting him to travel USA is not 
coming in the way of proper proceedings 
of the trial.
9. Accordingly, miscellaneous 
application (IA/6/2025) is allowed. The
applicant is permitted to visit United 
States of America for a period of one 
month commencing from the date of his 
departure, subject to purchase and 
availability of air tickets. He shall 
immediately report his arrival in India to 
this Court. 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
