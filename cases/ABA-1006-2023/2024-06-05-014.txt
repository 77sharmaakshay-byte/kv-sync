COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
ABA No.1006 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for applicant. 
2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned AGA for the State.
3. Heard.
4. Applicant was involved in connection with FIR 
No.0032 dated 11.03.2017 which was registered at P.S. Pant 
Nagar, District Udham Singh Nagar, for the offences u/s 
420, 465, 466, 468, 471, 474, 120-B, 34 IPC and Sections 
8/9 of the Prevention of Corruption Act. He moved ABA 
No.1006 of 2023 which was disposed of by reason of 
judgment dated 14.12.2023 with certain conditions, one of 
which was Condition No.(iii) which provided that he shall 
not leave the country without the previous permission of this 
Court. 
5. Applicant has now moved the application (MCRC 1 
of 2024) in the said anticipatory bail application requesting 
to relax the aforesaid condition and to permit him to visit 
USA for a period of two months. The application is 
supported by affidavit. It is stated in paragraph no.5 of 
application that applicant is a Green Card Holder of USA 
and in order to retain the said status, he is required to travel 
once in 180 days to the said country to maintain his status;
and he is an Indian Passport Holder bearing No.M7135700 
issued by Regional Passport Office, Dehradun having 
validity till 01.03.2025. 
6. Pursuant to the time given by this Court, the State has 
filed its objection to the said application wherein it is mainly 2
stated that his presence is required in the criminal 
prosecution launched against him, as referred hereinabove. 
7. I have considered the rival submissions of parties and 
perused the record as well. In order to procure the 
attendance of applicant in this criminal prosecution, as 
referred above, this Court felt it necessary that the applicant 
should submit details of his property before this Court and 
an undertaking should be filed that he would not alienate or 
dispose of his property during his absence from India and 
that he would report to this Court once he returns back.
8. Pursuant to the aforesaid directions, applicant filed 
supplementary affidavit before this Court on 21.05.2024, 
along with which, copy of Khatauni of his property of Fasli 
Year 1398-1403 was filed; today on 05.06.2024, an 
undertaking is also furnished before this Court by the 
applicant, copy whereof has duly been served upon the 
respondent –State. 
9. In such view of matter, Misc. Application (MCRC/1/ 
2024) is allowed. Condition No.(iii) imposed vide order 
dated 14.12.2023 is relaxed to the extent that the applicant is 
permitted to visit United States of America for a period of 
two months commencing from the date of his departure 
which according to learned counsel for applicant is in the 
month of July, 2024, subject to purchase and availability of 
air tickets. He shall immediately report his arrival in India 
to this Court. 
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
