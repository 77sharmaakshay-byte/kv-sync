COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
ABA No.1006 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for 
the applicant.
2. Mr. Bhaskar Chandra Joshi, learned 
A.G.A. with Mr. Vipul Painuli, learned Brief 
Holder for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. The applicant has moved miscellaneous 
application No.4 of 2024 for permitting him 
to travel United States of America for a 
period of two months or any such further 
order as this Hon’ble Court may deem fit 
and proper.
4. The respondent-State was given 
opportunity twice to file objection to the said 
application, but, it has not filed any 
objection so far, therefore, this Court has no 
option but to proceed for disposal of the 
aforementioned application, which is 
pending since long.
5. Applicant was involved in connection 
with FIR No.0032 dated 11.03.2017 which 
was registered at P.S. Pant Nagar, District 
Udham Singh Nagar, for the offences u/s 
420, 465, 466, 468, 471, 474, 120-B, 34 
IPC and Sections 8/9 of the Prevention of 
Corruption Act. He moved ABA No.1006 of 
2023, which was disposed of by reason of 
judgment dated 14.12.2023 with certain 
conditions, one of which was Condition 
No.(iii) which provided that he shall not 
leave the country without the previous 
permission of this Court.
6. Applicant has moved miscellaneous 2
application (MCRC/1/2024) for permitting 
him to visit USA for a period of two months. 
Said application was granted by this Court 
by a reason of order dated 05.06.2024 and 
he was allowed visiting USA for a period of 
two months commenced from his date of 
departure. However, it was also directed
that he shall immediately report his arrival 
to this Court. The applicant departed to USA 
pursuant to the order dated 05.06.2024 on 
02.07.2024 and returned to India on 
28.07.2024, as it is reflected from annexure 
No.1 to the earlier application 
(MCRC/3/2024).
7. The applicant in the present application 
made the same prayer. Since, the applicant 
has obeyed this Court’s order and returned 
to India and intimated about his arrival to 
the Court by filing an application 
(MCRC/3/2024), this Court has no reason to
deny permission to applicant to visit USA for 
a period of two months as prayed by him.
8. Accordingly, miscellaneous application 
(IA/4/2024) is allowed. Condition No.(iii) 
imposed vide order dated 14.12.2023 is 
relaxed to the extent that the applicant is 
permitted to visit United States of America 
for a period of two months commencing 
from the date of his departure which 
according to learned counsel for applicant, is 
in the month of March, 2025, subject to 
purchase and availability of air tickets. He 
shall immediately report his arrival in India 
to this Court. 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
