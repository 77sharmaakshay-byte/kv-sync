3. Heard. 
4. Applicant is before this Court seeking 
anticipatory bail in connection with FIR No.0032 dated 
11.03.2017 which was registered at P.S. Pant Nagar, 
District Udham Singh Nagar, for the offences u/s 420, 
465, 466, 468, 471, 474, 120-B, 34 IPC and Sections 
8/9 of the Prevention of Corruption Act (S.T. No.4 of 
2020, State v. Balwant Singh and others pending 
before the Court of Sessions/Special Judge, P.C. Act, 
Nainital). 
5. It is the contention of learned counsel for the 
applicant that the said FIR was lodged against as many 
as 113 accused persons in respect of same land scam, 
which is commonly known as ‘N.H. Ghotala in 
Uttarakhand’. It is further submitted by him that most 
of the accused persons have been enlarged on bail by 
this Court, and many of them have been given 
anticipatory bail by this Court. He further submitted 
that this is a case of parity. 
6. Per contra, learned State counsel does not 
dispute this fact of the case being of parity with those 
who have already been released on bail or anticipatory 
bail. Some orders passed by this Court are also 
annexed by the applicant as Annexure No.7 in the 
paper book of this anticipatory bail application. 2
7. In the facts and circumstances of the case, the 
applicant- Prabhsharan Singh is directed to be released 
on Anticipatory Bail, in the event of his arrest, on 
executing a personal bond of Rs.30,000/- and 
furnishing two reliable sureties, each in the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the SHO concerned, on 
the following conditions:- 
(i) Applicant shall attend the court proceedings 
regularly and will not seek any unnecessary 
adjournment; 
(ii) Applicant shall not directly or indirectly make any 
inducement, threat or promise to any person 
acquainted with the facts of this case. 
(iii) Applicant shall not leave the country without the 
previous permission of this Court. 
8. The anticipatory bail application stands disposed 
of accordingly.
