Mr. Aakash Sanyal, learned counsel 
holding brief of Mr. Aditya Singh, learned 
counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Saurabh Bhardwaj, learned Brief 
Holder for State of Uttarakhand/respondent.
3. Applicant was involved in connection with 
FIR No.0032 dated 11.03.2017 which was 
registered at P.S. Pant Nagar, District Udham 
Singh Nagar, for the offences u/s 420, 465, 
466, 468, 471, 474, 120-B, 34 IPC and 
Sections 8/9 of the Prevention of Corruption 
Act. He moved ABA No.1006 of 2023, which 
was disposed of by reason of judgment and 
order dated 14.12.2023 with certain 
conditions, one of which was Condition No.(iii) 
which provided that he shall not leave the 
country without the previous permission of this 
Court.
4. Applicant has moved miscellaneous 
application No.10 of 2026 for permitting him 
to travel United States of America for a period 
of two months. It is stated in Para 6 of the 
said application that applicant is a Green Card 
Holder of USA and in order to retain the said 
status, he is required to travel to the said 
Country once in 180 days and he is an Indian 
Passport Holder bearing No.C3444285 issued 2
by Regional Passport Office, Dehradun having 
validity till 08.10.2034.
5. There is no objection from the side of 
learned State Counsel.
6. Applicant was earlier allowed to visit USA 
for a period of one month commenced from 
his date of departure vide this Court’s order 
dated 07.10.2025. However, it was also 
directed that he shall immediately report his 
arrival to this Court. The applicant departed to 
USA pursuant to the order dated 07.10.2025
on 05.11.2025 and returned to India on 
02.12.2025, as it is reflected from annexure 
No.A.4 to the Misc. Application.
7. Since, the applicant has obeyed this 
Court’s order and returned to India and 
intimated about his arrival to the Court by 
filing an application (IA/8/2026), this Court 
has no reason to deny permission to applicant 
to visit USA for a period of two months as 
prayed by him. 
8. Accordingly, miscellaneous application 
(IA/10/2026) is allowed. The applicant is 
permitted to visit United States of America for 
a period of two months commencing from the 
date of his departure, subject to purchase and 
availability of air tickets. He shall immediately 
report his arrival in India to this Court. 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
