The petitioner claims that he is a 
person with disability; he is not being
considered for promotion to the post of 
Deputy Director, Education under the 
disability reservation quota.
Heard.
Admit.
Learned C.S.C. takes notice on behalf 
of the State.
Respondents may file counter affidavit 
within four weeks. 
Within two weeks thereafter, rejoinder 
affidavit, if any, may be filed.
List on 30.12.2025.
Heard on Interim Relief Application (IA 
No. 1 of 2025).
By means of interim relief application, 
the petitioner seeks direction to the 
respondents to consider the case of the 
petitioner for promotion to the post of 
Deputy Director, Education under the quota
of person with disabilities.
If the interim relief sought by the 
petitioner is granted, it would amount to 
grant of final relief, at the stage of admission itself. Such a relief cannot be granted to the 
petitioner at this stage. 
Consequently, the interim relief 
application deserves to be rejected.
The interim relief application is 
rejected.
