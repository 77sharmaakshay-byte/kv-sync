4. Petitioner participated in a selection 
for appointment as Assistant Teacher, 
Government Primary School in District 
Pauri Garhwal. According to him, he 
scored 76.67 quality point marks in the 
selection, which entitles him for 
appointment against a post in Science 
(OBC) category, however, petitioner was 
denied appointment, and a candidate 
with lower quality point marks has been 
offered appointment.
5. Learned State Counsel was asked to 
get instructions. Mr. S.C. Chaudhary, 
learned State Counsel submits that 
appointment was not offered to the 
petitioner, as he was relying upon two 
Permanent Resident Certificates, one 
issued from Uttarakhand and another 
issued from State of U.P., therefore, an 
inquiry has been ordered and till 
conclusion of the inquiry, one post has 
been kept reserved, as per the 
instructions received by him from the 
office of District Education Officer 
concerned. The written instructions 2026:UHC:1758
produced in Court is taken on record. 
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the writ petition may be 
disposed of in terms of the statement
made by learned State Counsel.
7. The writ petition is, accordingly, 
disposed of in terms of statement made 
by learned State Counsel, however, in 
the ongoing inquiry, petitioner shall also 
be given opportunity of hearing. Till 
conclusion of inquiry, one post of 
Assistant Teacher shall be kept vacant.
