4. It is contended by learned counsel for 
the respondents that, as per Uttarakhand 
Horticulture and Food Processing 
Subordinate Group-III Service Rules, 2009, 
the persons serving as Mali would be 
promoted as Supervisor Group–III, only 
after facing a selection process, therefore, 
according to them, the respondents, who 
were serving as Peon, cannot be treated 
differently from Mali. 
5. The stand taken by the State Counsel 
is that since respondents, who are serving 
as Peon, can be promoted as Junior Clerk 
only after facing a selection and the criteria 
for promotion is merit, therefore, as per 
Government Order dated 06.11.2013, respondents cannot get pay scale admissible 
for the post of Junior Clerk, as first ACP. 
6. Learned State Counsel is directed to 
get clear instructions, as to whether the 
2009 Rules held the field till 2021, when 
new rules were enforced, as per the 
statement made by the State Counsel. 
Learned State Counsel shall also get 
instructions as to whether 2009 Rules, which 
held the field till 2021, provided for selection 
for promotion to the post Supervisor Group￾III or not.
7. Learned State Counsel shall also get 
all the relevant rules, including 2009 and 
2021 Rules.
