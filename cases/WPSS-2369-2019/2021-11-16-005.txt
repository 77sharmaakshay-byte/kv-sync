This writ petition was dismissed for want of 
prosecution by an order of 12.11.2021. The 
restoration application has been marked as 
defective on two grounds, that each pages have not 
been verified and secondly the copy of the order 
dated 12.11.2021 has not been filed. 
Looking to the grounds, taken in the 
restoration application, the defect as pointed out by 
the Registry, would stand over ruled; because the 
order of 12.11.2021 is available on record, in the 
order sheet of the records of the writ petition and 
considering the ground taken by the petitioner for 
his absence on 12.11.2021 and being satisfied with 
the same, coupled with the fact that the same has 
not been seriously opposed by the respondents, the 
Restoration Application No.15577 of 2021 would 
stand allowed. The order dated 12.11.2021, would 
stand recalled and the writ petition is restored to its 
original number. 
The petitioner has preferred an Amendment 
Application No.15576 of 2021 seeking for
amending the additional pleading, grounds, as well 
as the relief clause. 
When this matter was taken up by the 
Coordinate Bench earlier vide its order of 
08.10.2021, respondents were granted time to file 
objection to the amendment application, but no 
objection has been filed by the respondents so far. In that eventuality and considering the nature 
of the amendment which has been sought for, since 
it only adds to pre existing pleadings and it is not a 
plea, which is in contradiction to the principal case, 
the same would stand allowed.
The counsel for the petitioner may carry the 
necessary amendment in the writ petition within a 
period of ten days from today. 
The respondents, is so advised may file their 
counter affidavit to the amended writ petition
within a period of two weeks thereafter. 
One Mr. Vipin Gusain, claiming himself to 
be a direct recruit to the post, had filed the 
Intervention Application No.15575 of 2021.
The counsel for the petitioner, if so advised, 
may file his objection to the intervention 
application within the time period as provided in 
the aforesaid part of today’s order.
List thereafter.
The Registry is directed to list the matter next 
showing the name of Mr. Lalit Samant, Advocate, 
as the counsel for respondent no.4.
