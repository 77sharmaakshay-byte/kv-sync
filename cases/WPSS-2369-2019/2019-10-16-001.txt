 Heard. 
 As prayed, four weeks’ time is granted to 
learned Government pleader to file counter 
affidavit. 
 List thereafter. 
 Also heard on interim relief application. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the petitioner has made 
representation on 20.12.2013 for redressal of 
his grievance but the authority 
concerned/Registrar General of this Court has 
not taken any decision as yet. 
 The Registrar General of this Court shall 
take decision on the representation of the 
petitioner by passing a speaking and reasoned 
order before the next date of listing and shall 
enclose the copy of the decision taken by him 
alongwith the counter affidavit. 
 Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties, as an interim measure, it is directed 
that till the next date of listing, one post in 
District Haridwar for which the petitioner is 
claiming shall not be filled up by the 
respondents. 
 Interim relief application stands disposed 
of accordingly.
