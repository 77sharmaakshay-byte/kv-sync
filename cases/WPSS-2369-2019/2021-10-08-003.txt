JUDGMENT 
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J. (Oral)
The petitioner was appointed on 01.08.2003. 
He is working on the Group ‘D’ post and presently, he is 
working in the Family Court, Haridwar as Process Server.
2. It is the claim of the petitioner that he is 
entitled to the Group ‘C’ post. But, instead of offering 
promotion to the petitioner, an advertisement dated 
12.09.2019 has been issued for filling up the vacancy in 
the Group ‘C’ post by the respondent no.4. The petitioner 
has challenged the advertisement dated 12.09.2019 with 
the related reliefs so that the Group ‘C’ post in the Family 2
Court, Haridwar be filled up by way of promotion. 
Initially, when the matter was taken up on 16.10.2019, 
the Court by way of interim measure directed that one 
post be kept vacant. The following order was passed on 
that date:-
“ Mr. P.K. Chauhan, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. Virendra Singh Rawat, Brief 
Holder for the State. 
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate for 
respondent no.2. 
Mr. N.S. Pundir, Advocate for 
respondent no.4. 
Heard. 
As prayed, four weeks’ time is granted 
to learned Government pleader to file 
counter affidavit. 
List thereafter. 
Also heard on interim relief 
application. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that the petitioner has made 
representation on 20.12.2013 for redressal 
of his grievance but the authority 
concerned/Registrar General of this Court 
has not taken any decision as yet. 
The Registrar General of this Court 
shall take decision on the representation of 
the petitioner by passing a speaking and 
reasoned order before the next date of 
listing and shall enclose the copy of the 
decision taken by him alongwith the 
counter affidavit. 
Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties, as an interim measure, it is 
directed that till the next date of listing, 
one post in District Haridwar for which the 
petitioner is claiming shall not be filled up 
by the respondents. 
Interim relief application stands 
disposed of accordingly.”3
3. The respondent no.2 filed counter affidavit 
along with it, an interim order vacation application has 
been filed.
4. It is the case of the respondent no.2 that the 
services in the Family Court are Governed by the 
Uttarakhand Subordinate Civil Courts Ministerial
Establishment Rules, 2007 (for short, “2007 Rules”). 
Under the 2007 Rules, there is no provision for 
appointment by promotion in the Group ‘C’ post in the 
Family Court, therefore, according to the respondent 
no.2, the interim order need to be vacated.
5. Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the record. 
6. Learned counsel for the respondent no.2 would 
submit that the interim order dated 16.10.2019 has been 
passed under the wrong presentation of the facts because 
the services of the petitioner are covered by the 2007 
Rules, which do not provide appointment by promotion, 
in Group ‘C’ post in the Family Court establishment. The 
general rules for promotion, as applicable to other 
establishment, are not applicable in the instant case. The 
representation which was filed in the year 2013 by the 4
petitioner has already been dismissed by an order dated 
14.06.2021. Hence, it is submitted that the interim order 
need to be vacated.
7. On the other hand, learned counsel for the 
petitioner would submit that the petitioner has a right to 
claim for promotion in Group ‘C’ post.
8. Learned counsel for the petitioner raised the 
following points in his submissions:-
(i) As per the advertisement dated 
12.09.2019, three posts were advertised for 
the Family Court establishments of Haridwar, 
Roorkee and Laksar Therefore, at least one 
vacancy has to be filled up by way of 
promotion. 
(ii) Under Rule 20 Sub-Rule (10) of the 2007 
Rules, the appointment in Group ‘C’ post in 
the Family Court could be made by way of 
promotion. 
(iii) Earlier the petitioner had claimed 
promotion on Group ‘C’ post and filed Writ 
Petition (S/S) No.1606 of 2014 (for short, “the 5
first petition”), in which, on 09.10.2014, an 
interim order was passed. Subsequently, the 
first petition was decided on 07.04.2017, when 
it was argued on behalf of the petitioner also 
that the case is squarely covered by the 
Judgement of this Court in Writ Petition (S/S) 
No. 586 of 2014 (for short, “the connected writ 
petition”). 
9. It is submitted that in the first petition, 
directions were issued to the respondents to consider the 
case of the petitioner in view of the order/judgment in the 
connected writ petition. The representation was 
submitted by the petitioner on 20.12.2013, but, it was not 
decided and in the meantime another advertisement was 
issued by the respondents.
10. Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that in the connected writ petition, the provisions 
of the Uttarakhand Subordinate Offices Clerical Grade 
Employees (Direct Recruitment) Rules, 2004 (for short, 
“2004 Rules”), as amended in 2013, were considered to 
uphold that the quota for promotion in Group ‘C’ posts
would be 45% in the Judgeship also. Therefore, it is 
submitted that by virtue of the judgment in the first 6
petition and connected writ petition, the petitioner is 
entitled to be considered for his appointment by way of 
promotion in the Group ‘C’ post in the Family Court,
Haridwar. 
11. There is no dispute that the services of the 
petitioner are governed by the 2007 Rules. Rule 3 of the 
2007 Rules prescribed for cadre of the service. According 
to it, “the ministerial service shall consist of the 
following classes and categories of officials employed in 
each Judgeship and Family Court in Uttarakhand.”
Under Rule 3 of the 2007 Rules, there are two separate 
establishments categorized namely:-
(i) Civil Court and;
(ii) Family Court.
12. Under the caption of Family Court, the lowest 
rank post is Typist-cum-Copyist, which is given at serial 
number (a) and its source of recruitment, according to the 
2007 Rules, is by direct recruitment alone. There are four 
other posts in higher category in the Family Court 
establishment, as per the 2007 Rules, on which, 
appointments can be made either by promotion or 
deputation or direct recruitment. The petitioner’s 
claim is on the post of Typist-cum-Copyist, 7
which is the lowest rank post in the Family Court 
establishment, but, as stated, as per the Rules, it could
be filled up only by direct recruitment.
13. Learned counsel for the petitioner has placed 
reliance on Rule 20 of the 2007 Rules. The Rule 20 of the 
2007 Rules, provides for promotion and according to Sub￾Rule (1) of it, “the higher post in the Judgeship of 
Family Court shall be reserved for clerks in that 
Judgeship or Family Court and promotion to higher 
posts shall be made from amongst them”. As stated,
under Rule 3 of the 2007 Rules, there are four posts, 
which are above the Typist-cum-Copyist post. Sub-Rule 
(10) of Rule 20 of the 2007 Rules, which has been referred 
to by the learned counsel for the petitioner states as 
hereunder:-
“20 (10) The appointment by way of 
promotion from the eligible candidates of 
group ‘D’ within the prescribed quota 
shall be made by the appointing authority 
in accordance with the conditions and 
procedure prescribed by rules and 
Government orders issued from time to 
time in the lowest pay scale of Group ‘C’ ”8
14. A bare perusal of Sub-Rule (10) of Rule 20 
reveals that it is, in fact, attracted only when appointment 
is made by way of promotion. In the instant case, the 
appointment is sought on Typist-cum-Copyist, which 
according to Rule 3 of the 2007 Rules, is not to be filled 
up by way of promotion, therefore, in the instant case, the 
provisions of Rule 20 generally and Rule 20 Sub-Rule (10)
specifically are not applicable.
15. Reference has been made to the first petition 
which, in fact, was decided on 07.04.2017, when 
directions were given to the respondents to consider the 
case of the petitioner in terms of the judgment in 
connected writ petition. The judgment in the connected 
writ petition i.e. Writ Petition (S/S) No.586 of 2014 is 
before the Court as Annexure No.1. It deals with 
promotion of Group ‘D’ employees in the District 
Judgeship. In the connected writ petition, the issue was 
with regard to quota that may be applicable to such 
promotions in Group ‘C’ posts in the District Judgeship.
In the connected writ petition the Court interpreted the 
provisions of the 2004 Rules, which were subsequently
amended in 2013 and the Court held that the quota for
such promotional post will be 45%.9
16. The Civil Court establishment and Family 
Court establishment are two distinct categories under 
Rule 3 of the 2007 Rules. In the Civil Court 
establishment, the posts of Copyist, Junior Clerk, 
Assistant Accounts Clerk, etc. are to be filled up by way of 
direct recruitment as well as by selection from amongst 
regular Group ‘D’ employees. The ratio of law, which was 
laid down in the connected writ petition was applicable to 
such post alone. In the connected writ petition and in the 
first petition, the provisions of Rule 3 of the 2007 Rules in 
their application to the Family Court establishment has 
not been discussed. The issue, as to whether in the 
Family Court appointment can be made in Group ‘C’ post 
by way of promotion has not been considered in the first 
petition, therefore, at the strength of the order, passed in 
the first petition, petitioner cannot claim that he is 
entitled for his appointment in the Group ‘C’ post by way 
of promotion. 
17. An amendment application has also been filed 
by the petitioner for challenging the 2007 Rules. The 
amendment application is still pending. The petitioner 
purposes to seek a declaration by way of amendment 
application that the Rule 3, of the 2007 Rules is ultra 10
vires. But, until it is done, it is admitted that appointment 
in the lowest rank post in the Family Court establishment 
cannot be made by way of promotion. 
18. The petitioner is admittedly working in the 
Group ‘D’ post. The interim order has been passed, based 
on the averments made by the petitioner in the petition, 
wherein, he has stated that he is entitled for promotion.
As discussed, the 2007 Rules does not permit such 
promotion.
19. In view of the above, this Court is of the view 
that the interim order dated 16.10.2019 deserves to be 
vacated.
20. The interim order dated 16.10.2019, is vacated.

the amendment application.
