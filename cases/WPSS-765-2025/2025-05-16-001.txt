Heard on Interim Relief Application, IA 
No.1 of 2025
It is the case of the petitioner that he 
has been denied pension on the ground that 
he is not covered by the old pension scheme. 
By the interim relief application, the 
petitioner seeks directions to the respondents 
to determine and pay the pension benefit to 
the petitioner. 
Since it is the main prayer of the 
petitioner, it may not be granted at this 
interim stage. Hence, the interim relief 
application is rejected, accordingly.
