Mr. Navneet Kaushik, Advocate, for

Mr. Tumul Kumar Nainwal, Brief 
Holder, for the State.
The respondent No. 3, was in a close 
relationship with the respondent No. 2 i.e. 
the complainant herein, from the time when 
they were pursuing their studies. 
Gradually, their affinity increased 
with one another and they got married on 
14.03.2017, but owing to certain disputes
pertaining to transfer of shares of the 
company, the dispute arose inter se 
between them, and on account of 
misunderstanding, the present applicant 
filed a divorce petition, being HMA No. 589 
of 2021, Anmol Kumar Goyal Vs. Shikha 
Rana under Section 13(1)(ia) of Hindu 
Marriage Act, 1955 on 02.08.2021, which is 
said to be still pending consideration.
In those proceedings, the notices 
were issued, and prior to the date fixed for 
appearance in the proceedings under 
Section 13 of the Hindu Marriage Act, the 
FIR, being FIR No. 185 was registered on 
14.05.2022 by complainant respondent No. 
2.
The argument of the learned 
counsel for the applicant is that apart from 
the fact that the dispute is matrimonial in nature, he further submits that since it is 
difficult to continue with the matrimony, he 
has already instituted a proceeding under 
Section 13 of the Hindu Marriage Act. He 
contends that the registration of the present 
FIR on 14.05.2022 was nothing but as an 
avengefull act on the part of the respondent 
to mitigate the proceedings under Section 
13 of the Hindu Marriage Act. Hence, the FIR 
has been registered.
Issue notice to respondent No. 2.
Respondent No. 1 is represented by the 
Government Advocate. Respondents may 
file counter affidavit within a period of three 
weeks from today.
Steps to serve respondent No. 2 
would be taken by the applicant within a 
period of one week from today.
List after service of notice on the 
respondent.
Till the next date of listing, 
summoning order dated 10.04.2023, as 
issued in Criminal Case No. 379 of 2023, 
State Vs. Anmol Kumar Goyal and others, 
would not be given effect to.
Stay Application No. IA/1/2023 
stands disposed of accordingly.
