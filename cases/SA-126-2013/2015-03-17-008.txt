 Mr. Siddhartha SIngh, Advocate for the appellants. 
 Mr. Alok Mehra, Advocate for the respondents. 
 Respondent no.1-Gopal Ram, in fact, died during 
pendency of first appeal but somehow, the fact of his death 
could not come to the notice of plaintiff/appellants. So, his 
heirs were not impleaded. Gopal Ram is the brother of 
respondent nos.2, 3 and 4. After the death of respondent no.4 
Tika Ram, his heirs were substituted. 
 I think that for effective adjudication, the heirs of Gopal 
Ram should also be substituted. 
 The second appeal is always continuation of original suit 
as well as of the first appeal. 
 Thus, the substitution application (CLMA 13071/14) is 
allowed. Let the substitution be made and the heirs of Gopal 
Ram be impleaded within three days. Thereafter, the service 
shall be effected upon the newly added heirs wherefor the 
steps shall be taken within next one week. 
 Objection filed on behalf of respondent no.1 against the 
substitution application is rejected. 
 At the same time, delay condonation application in 
setting aside abatement (CLMA 13069/14) as well as the 
setting aside application (CLMA 13070/14) are, accordingly, 
set aside. 
 List after service report.
