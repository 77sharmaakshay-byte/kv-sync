Present second appeal has been preferred 
by the plaintiffs-appellants being aggrieved 
against the judgment and decree dated 
11.07.2013 passed by Additional District Judge / 
Special Judge, C.B.I., Nainital in Civil Appeal 
No. 55 / 2008, whereby the judgment and decree 
dated 04.11.2008 passed by learned Civil Judge 
(JD), Haldwani, District Nainital was affirmed. 
Admit the second appeal on the following 
substantial questions of law: 
“(i) As to whether both the courts 
below have erred in law in dismissing 
the suit on the ground of limitation 
ignoring/condoning the time spent in 
revenue courts seeking the 
declaration of being Bhumidhar with 
transferable rights incidentally 
challenging the disputed sale deed 
and ignoring the provisions of Order 
7 Rule 6 CPC read with Article 58, 59 
of the Limitation Act? 
(ii) As to whether a power of 
attorney holder entrusted with the 
management of the property can 
execute the sale deed of the property 
in absence of any specific rights of 
transfer being mentioned in the power 
of the attorney? It is provided, as an interim measure, 
that the parties shall maintain status quo over 
the suit property till further orders of this 
Court. Stay application is disposed of. 
 Lower Court Record be summoned. List 
after the Lower Court Record is received.
