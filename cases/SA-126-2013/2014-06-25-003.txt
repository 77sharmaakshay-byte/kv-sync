S.A. No. 126 of 2013 
Hon’ble U.C. Dhyani, J. Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate for the 
appellants. 
 None is present for the respondents 
despite service of notice. Affidavit of service 
has been filed on behalf of the appellants to 
indicate that the respondents are duly served. 
 Respondent no. 4 died on 14.10.2013. 
An intimation to this effect was given to the 
Court on 25.11.2013. The Court permitted the 
appellants to take steps within three weeks, but 
the steps were not taken in time. Thereafter, 
winter vacations followed. Steps were taken 
when the Court reopened after winter break, 
hence, applications are filed by the appellants 
for setting aside abatement, for condoning the 
delay in filing the substitution application and 
for substituting the legal heirs of deceased 
respondent no. 4. 
 All such applications are allowed turn￾by-turn. 
 Abatement of the appeal is set aside. 
Setting aside abatement application no. 1991 of 
2014 is allowed. 
 Being satisfied with the sufficiency of 
reasons furnished by appellant no. 4 in his 
affidavit, delay in filing the substitution 
application is condoned. Delay condonation 
application no. 1902 of 2014 is allowed. 
Consequently, substitution application (CLMA 
no. 1992 of 2014) is also allowed. 
 The appellants are permitted to add the 
word ‘dead’ after the name of respondent no. 4 
and incorporate the names of his legal heirs / 
legal representatives in the memo of appeal. 
 Let amended memo of the parties be 
filed within a week. 
 List on 04.07.2014, for admission. 
 
(U.C. Dhyani, J.)
