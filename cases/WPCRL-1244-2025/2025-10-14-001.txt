Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Gaurav Singh, learned counsel for the 
Petitioner. 
2. Mr. Amit Bhatt, learned Government 
Advocate for the State.
3. Present writ petition has been filed by the 
petitioner under Article 226 of the Constitution of 
India for quashing the First Information Report 
dated 31.08.2025 registered as Case Crime No.421 
of 2025 for the offence punishable under Section 
111, 318(4), 336(3), 338, 340(2), 351(3), 352, 61(2) 
of the Bhartiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023, Police 
Station Gangnahar, District Haridwar qua the 
petitioner. 
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner at the 
outset makes a submission that firstly the petitioner 
comes before this Court on the basis of parity 
regarding the co-accused Monika to whom the 
Coordinate Bench of this Court had granted 
protection vide order dated 25.09.2025; secondly on 
an inquiry of this Court in the light of the FIR 
where the petitioner was in any manner beneficiary 
to the said proceedings of the said property and 
transaction involved, learned counsel for the 
petitioner makes a submission that there is none as 
per.
5. Learned State counsel further refutes to the 
submission advanced by learned counsel for the 
petitioner and further makes a statement that there 
are no grounds for parity. 
6. After hearing learned counsel for the parties, 
this Court is of the considered view that the parity at 
the first phase does exist in the light of order passed 
by the Coordinate Bench of this Court vide order 
dated 25.09.2025 and primafacie in the light of the 
FIR at this juncture, petitioner does not seem to be the beneficiary and mere a witness to the said 
transaction against which the present matter has 
been surfaced. 
7. List on 26.11.2025. 
8. As an interim measure, till the next date of 
listing no coercive measures shall be taken against 
the petitioner pursuant to the First Information 
Report dated 31.08.2025 registered as Case Crime 
No.421 of 2025 for the offence punishable under 
Section 111, 318(4), 336(3), 338, 340(2), 351(3), 
352, 61(2) of the Bhartiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 
2023, Police Station Gangnahar, District Haridwar 
qua the petitioner provided he cooperates with the 
investigating agency.
9. Stay Application No.01 of 2025 stands 
disposed of.
