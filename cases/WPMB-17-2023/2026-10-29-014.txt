1. Mr. Himanshu Pal, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. Manoj Kumar, learned Standing Counsel for 
AIIMS, Rishikesh. 
3. Petitioner responded to a Notice Inviting Tender 
issued by AIIMS, Rishikesh for the work of dietary 
services. His bid was found to be the second lowest, 
but since the one with lowest bid was declared 
technically disqualified, therefore, petitioner’s bid 
became the lowest. 
4. Learned counsel for petitioner submits that, after 
generation of contract through Government e￾Marketplace (GeM), petitioner went to the competent 
authority in AIIMS, Rishikesh to submit bank 
guarantee, however, bank guarantee was not accepted 
from him, and five days thereafter, petitioner received 
an email dated 22.03.2023, whereby petitioner was 
informed that the buyer has cancelled its contract 
GEMC-511687731462992 because EPBG or transaction 
charges have not been paid by him within the 
stipulated time. Thus, feeling aggrieved, petitioner 
filed this Writ Petition challenging the cancellation order 
dated 22.03.2023.5. A counter affidavit is filed by Gaurav Badola, 
Administrative Officer, AIIMS, Rishikesh. However, in 
paragraph no. 4 of the counter affidavit, it is mentioned 
that the contract was cancelled due to substantial 
change in scope of work and inadvertent omission of 
some clauses. Paragraph no. 4 of the said counter 
affidavit is extracted below :
“4. That GeM bid was proposed for cancellation by 
Buyer, AIIMS Rishikesh due to required substantial change 
in scope of work and inadvertent omission of some clauses 
(e.g; Guidelines for Modern Kitchen and Dietary Services, 
2022, issued by Department of Health and Family Welfare, 
Government of India) through GeM portal on 13.02.2023 
and the same was accepted by seller i.e; petitioner on
14.02.2023. Therefore it was a mutual decision to scrap 
this tender. Hence, respondents were not legally bound to 
accept the PBG against the tender which stood cancelled. 
Copy of the relevant GeM Document regarding acceptance 
of cancellation of tender by petitioner dated 14.02.2023 is 
enclosed as Annexure # SCA-1.”
6. Thus, the reason assigned for cancelling the 
contract, as indicated in the email dated 22.03.2023, 
does not match with the reason assigned in paragraph 
no. 4 of the counter affidavit. This indicates that there 
is something more to it than what meets the eye.
7. We, therefore, call upon the Director, AIIMS, 
Rishikesh to file his personal affidavit, explaining the 
matter. Such affidavit shall be filed on or before 
17.11.2026. 
8. List on 18.11.2026.
9. In case such affidavit is not filed within stipulated 
time, then the Director, AIIMS, Rishikesh shall appear 
in person, along with entire record of the case, before 
this Court on 19.11.2026.
