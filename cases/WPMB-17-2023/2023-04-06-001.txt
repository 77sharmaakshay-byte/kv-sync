Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Mr. Himanshu Pal, learned counsel for the

Issue notice. 
Mr. Lalit Sharma, learned Standing Counsel for 
the Union of India, appears and accepts notice on 
behalf of all the respondents. 
The petitioner has preferred the present Writ 
Petition to seek the following reliefs :-
“i) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding/ directing the respondent nos. 1, 
2 & 3 to adhere to the contract dated 13.02.2023 and 
proceed further as per the contract.
ii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding/ directing the respondent nos. 1, 
2 & 3 to accept the bank guarantee in lieu of letter dated 
06.03.20239 (A11).
iii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
Certiorari, quashing the impugned order dated 22.03.2023 
(A14), whereby the contract awarded in the favour of 
petitioner was cancelled.
iv) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding/ directing the CBI/CVC to conduct 
an enquiry regarding malpractices in grant of government 
contracts/tenders to favorable bidders/ contractors.”
The petitioner participated in the tender invited 
by the respondent-AIIMS, Rishikesh for providing 
dietary services at AIIMS, Rishikesh. The petitioner 
participated in the said tender, and was adjudged as L2 
bidder. Since the L1 bidder did not accept the contract, 
it was awarded to the petitioner on 13.02.2023. The 
contract was executed with the petitioner on 13.02.2023. On 06.03.2023 the respondents asked 
the petitioner to furnish a bank guarantee within 15 
days, of the amount of Rs. 21.68 lacs. The petitioner 
states that the petitioner procured the bank guarantee 
from its bank, and offered the same to the 
respondents. However, the same was not accepted. 
The petitioner also sent representations. Eventually, 
the respondents have taken the stand that, since the 
petitioner had not submitted the bank guarantee, the 
contract had been cancelled. The bank guarantee had 
been procured by the petitioner before the expiry of 15 
days on 18.03.2023, whereas the 15 days’ period 
granted to the petitioner were to expire on 21.03.2023.
Therefore, prima facie, it does not make any 
sense, as to why the petitioner would procure the bank 
guarantee, and not furnish the same. The petitioner 
had also sent a representation to the respondents on 
22.03.2023, stating that he had prepared the bank 
guarantee, and offered the same in time. 
Prima facie, it appears to us that the action of the 
respondents is arbitrary. The respondents are directed 
to file their counter affidavit(s) within four weeks. 
The respondents shall maintain status quo in the 
meantime. 
List on 17.05.2023.
