COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 2183 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. Atul Bahuguna, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand/
respondent nos.1 to 4.
3. By means of this writ petition, petitioner 
has sought the indulgence of this Court for 
quashing the impugned result dated 
23.11.2023 (Annexure No.11 to the writ
petition) issued by respondent no.3 for 
selection on the post of Home Guard (Women) 
pursuant to the advertisement dated 
02.08.2023 and other reliefs to appoint her on 
the post of Home Guard in Women Platoon, 
Pithoragarh.
4. It is the case of the petitioner that 
pursuant to the advertisement issued by 
respondent no.3, she has submitted her 
candidature for the post of Home Guard 
(Women) in Women Platoon, District 
Pithoragarh under OBC category. As per the 
scheme of the examination, she scored 43 
marks which is bifurcated i.e. 28 for physical, 
10 for graduation and 05 for sports quota 
(State level player). Despite scoring 43 marks, 
she was not selected for training and the 
private respondent no.6, who scored 41 marks, 
was selected and sent for training.
5. The ground for petitioner being not 
sending in training, according to respondent￾State was that OBC certificated issued in her 
favour was not valid for the reason that the 
said OBC certificate was issued on 29.08.2017, 
which is only valid for a period of three years.
The grounds for not selecting the petitioner 2
appears to be prima facie against the law 
enunciated by the Coordinate Bench of this 
Court as well as by the Hon’ble Apex Court, 
wherein, it is held that the status of a person 
which is acquired to him by birth cannot be 
changed, and, therefore, there is no point in 
saying that the OBC certificate, which was 
issued to the petitioner would expire after a 
period of three years. The point involves in the 
writ petition is no longer res integra.
6. Issue notice to respondent nos.5 and 6, 
returnable within six weeks.
7. Steps to be taken within 03 days.
8. Learned State Counsel prays for and is 
granted six weeks’ time for filing counter 
affidavit. Further, two weeks’ time is granted 
for filing rejoinder affidavit.
9. List on 28.02.2024.
10. In the meantime, respondent no.3 is 
directed to send the petitioner for training for 
the post of Home Guard (Women) provisionally, 
if the petitioner obtained higher marks than 
the private respondents, which shall be subject 
to the final outcome of the present writ petition 
and the result of the training shall be declared 
after the permission of this Court.
11. Let a certified copy of this order be 
supplied to learned counsel for the parties
today itself on payment of usual charges.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
