Heard on Interim Relief Application, IA 
No.1 of 2025
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the respondent no.3/the 
Uttarakhand Medical Service Selection Board 
(“the Board”), had invited application for 
appointment of X-Ray Technician in 
hospitals. The petitioner also applied for it, 
but his candidature was not considered on 
the ground that he did not have the 
permanent registration certificate with the 
Uttarakhand Para Medical Council. 
It is the case of the petitioner that, in 
fact, he had applied for the registration with Uttarakhand Para Medical Council on 
18.12.2023, which is prior to the cut off date 
and in similar matters, the respondent no.3 
had considered the receipt for registration as 
an eligibility for applying to the position. 
By means of the interim relief 
application, the petitioner seeks directions 
that till the disposal of the writ petition, one 
position of the X-Ray Technician under EWS 
category may be kept vacant. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner also 
submits that 3 persons were appointed; the 
last candidate has not joined and he does not 
intend to join the post of X-Ray Technician. 
Having considered, as an interim 
measure, till the next date of listing, the 
respondents shall not appoint any other 
person under EWS category for the position 
of X-Ray Technician. It shall have no effect on 
the persons, who have already been 
appointed/joined. 
Interim relief application stands 
disposed of, accordingly.
