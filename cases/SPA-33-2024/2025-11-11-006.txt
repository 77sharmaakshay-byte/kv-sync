Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned Chief Standing 
Counsel along with Mr. Naveen Tewari, learned Brief 
Holder for the State of Uttarakhand/Appellants.
2. Ms. Neeti Rana, learned counsel for 
respondents.
3. Heard learned C.S.C. and learned counsel for 
the respondents.
4. Perused the order in detail.
5. The order, prima facie, appears to be in the 
teeth of the law laid down by the Constitutional 
Bench of the Hon’ble Apex Court in the case of 
Secretary, State of Karnataka and Others Vs. 
Umadevi and Others (2006) 4 SCC 1, more 
particularly, in paragraph no.54 the Constitutional 
Bench has held as under:-
“54. It is also clarified that those 
decisions which run counter to the principle 
settled in this decision, or in which directions 
running counter to what we have held herein, 
will stand denuded of their status as 
precedents.”
6. In that view, there shall be a stay of the 
operation of the direction issued, subject to the 
condition that the services of the respondents shall 
be confirmed in accordance with the Regularization 
Rules, 2013.
7. Compliance to be reported in two weeks.
8. List this case on 25.11.2025.
