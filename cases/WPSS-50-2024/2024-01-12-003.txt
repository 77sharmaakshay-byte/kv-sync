4. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
5. By means of this writ petition the petitioner 
has challenged the order dated 01.01.2024 whereby 
the representation of the petitioner was rejected and 
further the final result dated 01.01.2024 issued by 
the Public Service Commission, qua the name of 
the private respondent for the post of Date Entry 
Operator in Uttarakhand Public Service 
Commission with consequential relief of holding 
the petitioner eligible and to revise the final result. 
6. It is the case of the petitioner that pursuant to 
the advertisement issued by the 
respondent/Commission dated 13.01.2023 inviting 
applications for the post of Data Entry Operator in 
Uttarakhand Lok Sewa Aayog, the petitioner 
submitted his candidature. The petitioner appeared 
in the written examination as well as in typing test 
and according to him he was fully eligible for 
appointment on the post of Data Entry Operator in 
Uttarakhand Lok Sewa Aayog. The 
respondent/Commission by the impugned order has
rejected the candidature of the petitioner holding 
him ineligible for the post for lacking essential 
educational qualification.6. The contention of the learned counsel for the 
Commission is that the petitioner is not holding the 
essential educational qualification as prescribed 
under the advertisement.

the attention of this Court to Clause 4 of the 
advertisement wherein the essential qualification 
has been given. The same is quoted hereinbelow:-

8. It is the contention of the learned counsel for 
the petitioner that the petitioner is in both ways
eligible for the post as he has passed metric 
examination and he is in possession of professional 
certificate issued by N.C.V.T. of the Government of 
India in Data Entry Operation and further he is 
graduate and he is holding a computer application 
certificate/diploma from recognized institution of 
one year. 
9. The attention of this Court is drawn by the 
petitioner on a certificate, which is supplied by the 
petitioner by filing supplementary affidavit. On 
perusal of the National Trade Certificate, which is 
issued by the Director General of Training, the 
petitioner is holding one year certificate for 
Computer Operator and Programme Assistant.10. Per contra, learned counsel for the 
Commission has submitted that the candidature of 
the petitioner has rightly been rejected by the 
Commission and the certificate which he claims to 
be a qualification of one-year certificate in requisite 
qualification, is still required scrutiny of this Court.
11. Learned counsel for respondent no.1 
/Commission prays for and is granted six weeks 
time to file counter affidavit. Two weeks time 
thereafter is granted to the learned counsel for the 
petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
12. Issue notice to respondent no.3. 
13. List this petition on 21.05.2024. 
14. In the meanwhile, it is directed that during 
the pendency of this appeal, one post of Data Entry 
Operator of OBC category shall be kept vacant. 
15. Interim Relief Appl. No.01 of 2024 stands 
disposed of.
