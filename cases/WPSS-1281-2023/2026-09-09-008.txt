Restoration Application (MCC No.7 of 2026)
3. Today the matter is listed on a restoration application by 
which the petitioner wants to recall the order dated 
27.03.2026 whereby the writ petition was dismissed for non￾prosecution.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner that 
since the earlier counsel could not appear in the Court, hence 
the petition was dismissed for non-prosecution.
5. Petitioner wants to pursue the writ petition sincerely and 
it is due to non appearance of the Advocate the writ petition 
was dismissed for non-prosecution.
6. Learned counsel for the respondent does not want to file 
objection as the restoration application is well within time.
7. In such view of the matter, restoration application is 
allowed. The order dated 27.03.2026 is hereby recalled and 
writ petition is directed to be restored to its original number.
