SA No. 116 of 2015 
Hon’ble U.C.Dhyani, J. 
 Mr. T. A. Khan, Senior Advocate, assisted 
by Mr. Aditya Kumar Arya, Advocate, present 
for the appellant. 
 Present Second Appeal has been preferred 
by the plaintiff-appellant being aggrieved against 
the judgment and decree dated 24.07.2015, 
passed by learned 1st Additional District Judge, 
Haldwani, District Nainital, in Civil Appeal No. 
11 of 2012, whereby judgment and decree dated 
23.03.2012 passed by Civil Judge (J.D.), 
Haldwani in Civil Suit No. 64 of 2008 in favour 
of the appellant/plaintiff has been set aside and 
the suit of the appellant/plaintiff has bee 
dismissed. 
 Heard. 
 Admit the appeal on following substantial 
questions of law: 
 1. Whether the learned First Appellate 
Court committed illegality by not appreciating 
the report of Advocate Commissioner ? 
 2. Whether any land can be identified 
without meets and bounds and without actual 
measurement of the boundaries? 
 Issue notice to the sole respondent. Steps be 
taken within a week. 
 List after the notice is served upon her. 
 Lower Court Record may be summoned in 
the meanwhile. 
 After hearing learned counsel for the 
appellant and after considering the grounds 
taken up in the Second Appeal, it is provided, as 
an interim measure that, status quo as on today, 
shall be maintained by the parties on the suit 
property till further orders. 
 (Interim stay application No. 8852 of 2015 
stands disposed of)
