1. Mr. B.D. Pande and Mr. Akshay Pradhan, learned 
counsel for the petitioner. 
2. Mr. Y.C. Tiwari, learned Standing Counsel for the 
State of Uttarakhand/ respondent nos. 1 & 2. 
3. Mr. Bhagwat Mehta, learned counsel for the 
Nagar Palika Parishad/ respondent no. 3. 
4. Two issues arise for consideration in the instant 
Writ Petition. The first one is, whether the State 
Government, or Nagar Palika Parishad, can debar any 
person from participating in the tender process on the 
ground of person concerned not being a domicile of the 
State. The second one is, whether any restriction can 
be imposed on a contractor based on the category, 
under which one is registered, for debarring him from 
participating in tender of lesser value.
5. These questions are under consideration in WPMB 
No. 433/2025, which is cognizable by Division Bench, 
WPMS No. 2710/2021, which is cognizable by Single 
Judge Bench, and WPMB No. 385/2025, which is also 
cognizable by Division Bench. 
6. The respondent-Nagar Palika Parishad has 
already awarded contract in favour of different 
contractors, in pursuance of the impugned tender 
notice. Their names shall be brought on record by 
learned counsel appearing on behalf of Nagar Palika Parishad within 24 hours to facilitate their impleadment 
in the present Writ Petition by the petitioner. 
7. Issue notice to the successful tenderers by 
speed-post. Steps to be taken within a week. 
8. The respondents are granted four weeks’ time to 
file counter affidavit. 
9. List thereafter, along with WPMB No. 433/2025, 
WPMB No. 385/2025 & WPMS No. 2710/2021, after 
obtaining orders from the Chief Justice, on the 
administrative side, for WPMS No. 2710/2021 being 
placed before the Division Bench. 
10. The award of the contracts shall be subject to the 
decision of the Writ Petition(s).
