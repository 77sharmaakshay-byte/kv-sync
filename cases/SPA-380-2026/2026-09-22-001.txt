1. Sri Shubhang Dobhal, learned counsel for the appellant. 
2. Sri Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for Nagar Palika Parishad, 
Pithoragarh/respondent Nos. 1 & 2. 
3. Sri J.C. Pande, learned Standing Counsel for the State of 
Uttarakhand. 
4. The admitted facts, as evident from the respective stands of the 
parties, is that on basis of a decision of Nagar Palika Parishad, Pithoragarh, 
under a PPP arrangement, the appellant was permitted to repair / construct 
an existing toilet at Siltham Church Road and construct a shop over it 
through his own investment and in return, he was to get rights over the 
shop. A formal lease agreement in respect of the shop, constructed by the 
petitioner over the toilet, was entered into between the parties on 
19.07.2011, whereunder Rs. 1,000/- per month was payable by the 
appellant-writ petitioner as lease rent and the period of lease was 15 years 
starting from 19.07.2011. 
5. The case of the appellant-writ petitioner is that the toilet and the 
shop were, subsequently, demolished during road widening. The 
appellant-writ petitioner, thereafter, applied for allotment of an alternative 
shop, but which was declined by the Executive Officer, Nagar Palika 
Parishad, Pithoragarh by order dated 19.02.2022 and it was left open to the 
appellant-writ petitioner to participate in the allotment process whenever any application in that behalf is invited by Nagar Palika Parishad, 
Pithoragarh as per the Procurement Rules, 2017. 
6. In Paragraph-16 of the counter-affidavit filed on behalf of Nagar 
Palika Parishad, Pithoragarh, it is admitted that the lease agreement was 
executed between the parties on 19.07.2011. The stand taken is that the 
constructions were encroachments, consequently, the same were 
demolished on 04.10.2013 by GREF/ BRO. It is stated that as per 
Condition No. 7, the appellant-writ petitioner should have got the shop 
insured, but since he failed to get it insured, therefore, he has to face the 
consequences. 
7. As noted above, it is not disputed that the appellant-writ petitioner 
invested in repairing the existing toilet and in constructing a shop over it, 
on basis of the representation made by Nagar Palika Parishad that the 
ownership of the structure was with it. Consequently, in case the same 
was ultimately found to be an encroachment, as is the stand taken in 
Paragraph-16 of the counter-affidavit, Nagar Palika Parishad should either 
allot an alternative shop to the appellant-writ petitioner, or pay 
compensation to the appellant-writ petitioner for the losses suffered by 
him. 
8. Sri Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for Nagar Palika Parishad, 
seeks time to obtain instructions in this regard. 
9. Accordingly, he is granted two weeks’ time to complete his 
instructions and file affidavit of the Executive Officer, Nagar Palika 
Parishad, Pithoragarh, disclosing its stand in respect of the above. 
10. List after two weeks.
