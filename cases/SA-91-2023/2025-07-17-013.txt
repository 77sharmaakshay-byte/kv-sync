2. Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate for the

3. This second appeal has been preferred by the 
appellant against the judgment/decree dated 
31.05.2023 passed by VII Additional District Judge, 
Dehradun in Civil Appeal No.118 of 2022 “Smt. 
Ekadashi Devi vs. Shailendra Nath Kaushik and 
others” and the judgment/decree dated 18.05.2022 
passed by II Additional Civil Judge, Junior Division, 
Dehradun in O.S. No.193 of 2017.
4. Original Suit No.193/2016 was filed by the 
respondents/plaintiffs against the appellant
/defendant on 11.07.2016 for ejectment and 
Original Suit No.98/2017 was filed by the 
appellant/defendant against the respondents on 
18.04.2017 for cancellation of sale deed and 
permanent injunction; by order dated 22.09.2017 
passed in O.S. No.193/2016 both the suits were 
consolidated and the O.S.No.193/2016 was made 
the leading case; learned Trial Court, vide 
judgment/decree dated 18.05.2022, partly allowed 
the O.S. No.193/2016 filed by the respondents and 
directed the appellant to handover the possession of 
the suit property within 60 days to the respondents, 
while O.S. no.98/2017 filed by the appellant was 
dismissed. 
5. Against the judgment/decree dated 
18.05.2022 passed by the Trial Court, appellant 
filed Civil Appeal No.118/2022; respondents filed objections in the appeal contending that against two 
decrees, two appeals were to be filed; in reply to 
the objection filed by the respondents, the appellant 
filed the application no.24C2 with the prayer that 
the Civil Appeal No.118/2022 be treated as a single 
appeal against the judgment/decree in both the 
suits; on the pleadings of parties, learned first 
appellate court formulated three points of 
determination, which are extracted as under:-
(i) Whether by filing a single appeal against two 
Decree and Judgment is permissible in law? Or the 
appeal of the appellant/plaintiff is barred by the 
principles of res judicata?
(ii) Whether the learned Trial Court was just in 
holding that the plaintiff/appellant had failed to 
prove her case?
(iii) Whether the appellant has any case on merit?
6. The First Appellate Court determined the point 
no.1 and dismissed the appeal on the ground of res 
judicata and held that as point no.1 has been 
determined against the appellant, as such point 
nos.2 and 3 require no determination. 
7. Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
8. Admit the appeal on the following question of 
law:-
A. Whether the first appellate court ought to 
have alerted the appellant by making him 
aware of the requirement of law of filing 
two separate appeals against two 
judgments/decrees and gave him 
opportunity to file separate memorandum 
of appeal, more so when the application
paper no.24C2 was kept pending and was 
ultimately rejected at the time of final 
adjudication of appeal.
B. Whether dismissal of application paper 
no.24C2 at the time of final adjudication of appeal, has resulted in infringement of 
rights of the appellant, as the appeal was 
to be considered and decided on question 
of facts and law. 
9. As an interim measure, till the next date of 
listing, parties are directed to maintain status quo in 
respect of the suit property as well as not to create 
third party interest thereon. (Stay Application IA 
No.1/2023 is disposed of).
10. List on 21.08.2025.
