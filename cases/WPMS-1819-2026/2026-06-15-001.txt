5. This writ petition is filed by Rice Miller 
Welfare & Development Society, which is 
reportedly registered under Societies 
Registration Act, 1860.
6. Learned counsel for the respondent no. 
6 submits that there are large number of 
persons who would be benefited by order, if 
any, passed in this writ petition; however, 
petitioner has not enclosed the list of 
members of petitioner’s society. It is further 
contended that each of the members is liable 
to pay separate court fee, as individual relief 
would be claimed by each of the member.
7. In such view of the matter, petitioner is directed to furnish list of members by filing a 
supplementary affidavit within one week. 
Petitioner shall thereafter pay separate court 
fee in respect of each of the members within 
one week thereafter. Certificate of 
registration of society shall also be brought 
on record along with supplementary 
affidavit. 
8. List this case on 30.06.2026.
