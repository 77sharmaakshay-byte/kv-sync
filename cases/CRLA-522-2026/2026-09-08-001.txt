2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned AGA for

3. The instant criminal appeal is preferred 
by the appellant-Anjali against the judgment 
and order dated 18.08.2026 passed by the 
trial court in Session Trial Court No.02 of 
2024 State vs. Ganga Devi & others wherein 
she is convicted for the offences punishable 
under Sections 313, 504, 506, 318 of IPC with 
the sentence of 5 years simple imprisonment 
with fine of Rs.10,000/-.
4. As per officer report appeal is well 
within time.
5. Admit.
6. Summon the trial court record.
7. Heard on bail application.
8. It is argued by learned counsel for the 
appellant that infact in this case, the victim 
was raped by a juvenile and only charge 
against the appellant is that she facilitate the 
drug to the victim for her abortion. Apart 
from this, it is also submitted that the 
appellant was on bail during trial and never misuse the same.
9. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned AGA 
have not disputed that the victim was raped 
by a juvenile, who is also convicted and only 
the charge against the appellant was to 
provide the medicine at the time of abortion 
and also admits that the appellant was on 
bail during trial and never misused the same. 
10. After hearing the submission of learned 
counsel for the parties and taking into 
consideration that the limited role is assigned 
to the appellant and she was on bail during 
trial and never misused the same, this Court 
is of the view that appellant deserves for bail. 
11. Accordingly, without expressing any 
opinion on the merit of the case, the bail 
application is allowed.
12. Let the appellant-Anjali be released on 
bail on her executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two reliable sureties of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the court 
concerned during the pendency of this appeal.
13. Fine shall also remain suspended. 
14. List this criminal appeal in the month of 
November, 2026. 
15. Bail application stands disposed of.
