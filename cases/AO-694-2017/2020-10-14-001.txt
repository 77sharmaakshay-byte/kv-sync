A.O. No. 694 of 2017
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. D.C.S. Rawat, Advocate for the 
appellant.
Mr. Devesh Upreti, Advocate for 
respondent nos. 1 to 3.
Ms. Mamta Bisht, Advocate for the 
respondent/owner.
Heard learned counsel for the parties 
through video conferencing.
After considering the submission of 
learned counsel for the appellant, it is 
directed that, till the next date of listing, 
effect and operation of the impugned award 
dated 28.09.2017 passed by the learned 
Motor Accident Claims Tribunal/District 
Judge, Champawat in M.A.C.P. No. 16 of 
2015 shall remain stayed, provided appellant 
deposits entire awarded amount, after 
adjusting statutory amount deposited by the 
appellant in the Registry of this Court, before
the Tribunal concerned within a period of 
four weeks from today. 
Learned Tribunal is directed to release 
50% of the awarded amount to be deposited 
by the appellant before the Tribunal 
concerned, proportionately in favour of 
claimants/respondent nos. 1 to 3 without 
asking any security. The remaining amount 
shall be invested in an interest bearing 
scheme of a nationalized bank.
Stay application (CLMA No. 16591 of 
2017) stands disposed of.
Consequently, urgency application (IA 
NO. 9863 of 2020) also stands disposed of.
