This is Civil Misc. Application No. 6252 of 2013
for release of the amount deposited by the appellant in2
compliance of order dated 21.08.2012.
(2) Heard.
(3) Brief facts of the case are that a dispute arose
between the appellant (State of Uttarkhand) and the
respondent M/s Hydel Construction Pvt. Ltd. which
was referred to the Arbitral Tribunal. The Arbitral
Tribunal gave its award on 06.02.2011. The present
appellant filed an application under section 34 of
Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, before the
District Judge, Dehradun. After hearing the parties the
application filed under section 34 of said act was
dismissed vide impugned order dated 06.01.2012, and
this appeal was filed on behalf of the State, which was
admitted vide this court's order dated 21.08.2012. This
Court at the time of admitting the appeal directed the
appellant to deposit the entire amount directed to be
paid vide the award passed by the Arbitral Tribunal,
within a period of eight weeks.
(4) Learned counsel for the respondent submitted that
the project of Maneri Bhali phase II after its completion
has already started the generation of electricity, and the
respondents are being deprived of their payment.
(5) Having considered submissions of learned counsel3
for the appellant, and learned counsel for the
respondent, the Misc. Application 6252 of 2013 is
disposed of, with the direction that 50 % amount of the
deposited by the appellant shall be released in favour of
the respondent. Rest of the 50 % amount is directed to
be deposited in the Fixed Deposits to prevent the loss
of interest of said sum.
(6) List this appeal for final hearing in due course.
