WPSS No.1327 of 2022
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Abhilash Nainwal, Advocate for the

Mr. P.C. Bisht, Additional Chief Standing 
Counsel for the State.
The petitioners have sought a writ of 
mandamus to prepare the seniority list in the cadre,
to which the petitioners belonged and to grant them 
promotion on the basis of the revised seniority list 
thus to be prepared. 
The said relief is being objected by the Addl. 
C.S.C., on the ground that the issue would be 
amenable to the Public Services Tribunal, but this 
Court is of the view that in the light of the language 
used under Section 4 of the Act, the Tribunal will 
only be having jurisdiction to try a matter, when a 
party, who approached the Public Services 
Tribunal, is aggrieved by an order, since the 
tribunal has got no such power to issue a writ of 
mandamus, the bar of jurisdiction of Tribunal will 
not come into play in filing the writ petition. 
The Addl. C.S.C. may file counter affidavit 
to the writ petition within three weeks.
List thereafter.
