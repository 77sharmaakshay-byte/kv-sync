5. All these First Appeals are admitted only for 
determination of limited issue giving employment in lieu
of compensation of Rs. 5 lakhs. As per Entry 4 of 
Schedule 2 of Act No. 30 of 2013. Entry 4 of Schedule 2 
provides that the appropriate Government shall ensure 
that the affected families are provided with the options 
i.e. Option (a), (b) and (c). As per Section 3(e)(v) in 
relation to the acquisition of land the Central 
Government is the appropriate Government.
Section 31 under Chapter V of Act No. 30 of 2013, 
which pertains to Rehabilitation and Resettlement award 
makes a provisions for Rehabilitation and Resettlement 
award for the affected families by the Collector. Sub￾section (2) of Section 31 of Act No. 30 of 2013 provides 
that the Rehabilitation and Resettlement award shall 
include the details of mandatory employment to be 
provided to the members of the affected families.
6. On 28.04.2018 the Collector issue an award by 
observing that there will be a separate award for 
Rehabilitation and Resettlement as per Schedule 2 of 
Schedule 3. The award under Schedule 2 and 3 are not on 
record. Let the appellant may file it by the next date.
7. These First Appeals were admitted on 16.09.2025 
for considering the limited issue for implementation of 
Entry 4 of Schedule 2 of Act No. 30 of 2013. Since, all 
these appeals are admitted, therefore, following points 
for determination are being formulated:i. Whether the options provided in terms of Entry 4 
of Schedule 2 of Act No. 30 of 2013 is prerogative 
of the claimant to choose one of them?
ii. Whether the appropriate Government for the 
implementation of the options in terms of Entry 4 
of Schedule 2 of Act No. 30 of 2013 is the Central 
Government or the RVNL and at what stage the 
job should be offered to the affected families?
8. As per definition clause i.e. Section 3(e)(v) of Act 
No. 30 of 2013 prima-facie it appears that the appropriate 
Government is the Central Government, therefore for 
proper adjudication to these First Appeals the Central 
Government, the Ministry of Railway Govt. of India is 
permitted to be arrayed as one of the respondent for 
which learned counsel for the appellant is given liberty to 
move an appropriate application.
9. Learned counsel for the appellant is directed to 
implead concerned Ministry as one of the respondent 
during course of the day.
10. In the meantime, the appellant RVNL is directed to 
send the requisition to the appropriate Government for 
the purposes of implementation of the options given in 
terms of Entry 4 Schedule 2 of Act No. 30 of 2013.
11. Till then the execution proceeding, if any, shall 
remain in abeyance.
12. It is also further made clear that those affected 
families who have opted for the amount of compensation 
in lieu of employment are permitted to withdraw the 
amount of compensation and if such an application is 
moved by any affected family or their member, the 
Collector shall release the amount with interest without 
any delay.
13. In compliance to the previous order Mr. Ajit Singh 
Yadav, the Chief Project Manager of RVNL is present in 
court in order to assist. For the time being, the personal 
appearance of the official concerned is exempted.
14. Put up all these appeals on 14.10.2025.
