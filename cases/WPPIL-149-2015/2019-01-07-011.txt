Hon’ble R.C. Khulbe, J.
Mr. R.P. Nautiyal, learned Senior Counsel 
assisted by Mr. Prashant Khanna, learned counsel 
for the petitioner. 
Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned Additional Chief 
Standing Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand￾respondent nos. 1 to 3. 
While it is asserted in the affidavit filed by the 
Chief Engineer and HOD, Minor Irrigation 
Department, Dehradun that the pipeline, through 
which water was being supplied, was earlier 
damaged because of natural calamities; and a part of 
the pipeline was broken by the family members of 
the petitioner himself to let water into their fields, it 
is also stated that water is now being supplied to the 
subject Village. 
This submission is vehemently denied by Mr. 
R.P. Nautiyal, learned Senior Counsel appearing on 
behalf of the petitioner. 
The only way in which this assertion of the 
Chief Engineer can be verified is if the District 
Judge, Dehradun is requested to depute a Judicial 
Officer to visit the site and ascertain whether water, 
for irrigation and drinking purposes, is being made
available to the residents of the subject village. 
A copy of this order shall be forthwith 
transmitted to the District Judge, Dehradun who is 
requested to submit his report by 05.02.2019. 
The petitioner and the Chief Engineer shall be 
put on notice by the officer deputed to the site, and 
the inspection shall be caused in their presence or in 
the presence of their representatives. 
Post this case on 11.02.2019
