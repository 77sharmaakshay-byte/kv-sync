 An extremely evasive and vague supplementary counter affidavit is 
filed, wherein no reference is made as to whether or not the petitioner’s 
village is now being supplied with drinking water. 
2. The reliefs sought for in this writ petition are for a mandamus 
directing the respondent authorities to install a water tank and pipelines 
as per the legal resolution dated 28.10.2009 of Mundhol Village 
Panchayat, Block Chakrata, District Dehradun; and for a mandamus, 
directing the State of Uttarakhand, to take necessary action against the 
concerned persons who misused the Government order dated 
31.03.2005, and misappropriated Government funds. 
3. Two schemes appear to have been prepared for supplying drinking 
water to Mundhol Village Panchayat and, while serious irregularities are 
said to have taken place in implementation of the second scheme, the 
Principal Secretary has stated, in his letter, that there could not have been 
any financial irregularities since implementation of the scheme has 
resulted in Government money being saved in the process. In the 
supplementary counter affidavit, all that is stated is that the scheme has 
been successfully implemented. However, enclosed with the very same 
supplementary counter affidavit, is a letter dated 19th December, 2012 
sent by the Executive Engineer to the Chief Engineer, Minor Irrigation 
Department, Uttarakhand Dehradun, along with the report dated 
18.09.2012, which discloses that the drinking pipeline, supplying water 
to the subject village, has been damaged. If successful implementation 
of the scheme is mere laying of a pipeline without water being actually 2
supplied to the villagers, then the action of the authorities must be 
faulted. What is even more disconcerting is that the Joint Secretary, who 
filed this supplementary counter affidavit, has not even chosen to state 
whether or not drinking water is being supplied to the villagers 
concerned. 
4. We consider it appropriate, in such circumstances, to direct the 
Joint Secretary to be personally present in Court on the next date of 
hearing along with the entire records to enable us to ascertain, whether or 
not drinking water is now being supplied to the petitioners’ village. 
5. Sri S.S. Chauhan, learned Deputy Advocate General, is present in 
Court and undertakes to inform the Joint Secretary of his obligation 
under this order.
