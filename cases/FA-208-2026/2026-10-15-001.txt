Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J. (Oral)
The challenge in this appeal is made to the order dated 
01.08.2026, passed in Guardians and Wards No.3 of 2025 (in short 
“the case”), Mrs. Jyoti Bisht Vs. Mr. Dheeraj Singh and Other, by the 
court of Additional Judge Family Court Haldwani. By which, the 
court held that it had no jurisdiction to hear the case and, 
accordingly, the matter was returned to the appellant under Order 
VII Rule 10 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, with the liberty to 
file the case before a court of competent jurisdiction.
2. By the impugned order itself, the court had undone the act 
that it had done at an interim stage by giving custody of the child to 
the appellant for 16 days. That order was undone.
3. Heard learned counsel for the appellant and perused the 
file.
4. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that once the 
court had already decided that it had no jurisdiction, there could not 
have been any other further directions.2

5. This argument has no basis. It is not disputed that the 
court had no jurisdiction while passing the interim order dated 
01.08.2026. The court did not pass any further order after holding 
that it had no jurisdiction. What the court had already done, that
was undone? During the pendency of the suit, during the summer 
vacation, 16 days’ custody of the child was given to the appellant. It 
was restored.
6. In view of the fact that the court had no jurisdiction, it was 
held that the interim order may not be continued and it was 
accordingly modified, and the appellant was directed to hand over 
the custody of the child to the father of the child. There is nothing 
illegal in this order; hence, there is no reason to interfere.
7. Accordingly, the instant first appeal is dismissed in limine.
