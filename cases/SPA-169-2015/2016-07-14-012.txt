4. This matter pertains to a practicing lawyers 
and their clerk, who are facing criminal investigation, 
subsequent to the order dated 20.04.2015 passed by the 
learned Single Judge of this Court. This appeal has been 
filed by the appellants against the said order. This Court 
did not interfere with the investigation but had stayed the 
arrest of the appellant (Ms. Khushboo Chaurasia). 
Meanwhile, after investigation, the Police had filed its 
charge sheet in the matter under Sections 193 /196 /197 
/199 /200 /205 /209 /465 /466 /420 /120B of IPC before 
the court concerned. Consequently, the court has taken 
cognizance on the charge sheet and summoned the 
accused persons, which was in turn challenge by each of 
the concerned persons by way of invoking the inherent 
jurisdiction of this Court under Section 482 of Cr.P.C, 
against the summoning order, and the cognizance taken by 
the court concerned. 2 
5. Meanwhile, numerous affidavits have been 
filed by parties across the table, without such affidavits 
called for by this Court, in the Registry saying by the other 
side showing their intention to bring an end to this matter.
