Special Appeal No. 169 of 2015

Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J.
Hon’ble U.C. Dhyani, J.
 This special appeal before us is filed by a 
practicing lady Advocate of this Court, namely, Ms. 
Khushboo Chaurasia. She has challenged the order dated 
20.04.2015 passed by learned Single Judge of this Court in 
a review application in WPMS No. 713 of 2015. The review 
application was for recalling the order dated 26.03.2015. 
 Brief facts of the case are as follows: 
 A writ petition (WPMS No. 713 of 2015) was filed 
before this Court by one Mr. Harimohan Gulati & four 
others with the following prayer:- 
“i) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus directing the respondents to register the 
sale deed in respect of property in question if it is not 
covered by the subject matter of writ petition (PIL) No. 
98 of 2011. 
ii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus directing the respondent authorities to 
enquire into the matter, and if the property does not 
belong to Bharat ji Mandir Trust, register the sale 
deed in respect of the property in question. 2 
iii) Issue any other suitable order or direction which 
this Hon’ble court may deem fit and proper in the 
circumstances of the case. 
 iv) Award costs of writ petition to the petitioners.” 
 The above prayer was being sought as earlier a 
Division Bench of this Court in a PIL had restrained the 
authorities from registering sale deeds relating to 
properties of Bharat ji Mandir – a trust. The orders passed 
in Writ Petition (PIL) No. 98 of 2011, are as follows:- 
“Mr. Anil Joshi and Mr. Virendra Kaparwan, 
Advocates for the petitioners 
Mr. J.P. Joshi, Chief Standing Counsel for the 
State/respondent Nos. 1 to 4. 
Admit. 
Let notice of this writ petition be served upon 
respondent No.5. 
Let the writ petition be listed after service is 
effected upon respondent No.5. 
Until expressly permitted by this Court, Sub￾Registrar, Rishikesh, Dehradun is restrained from 
registering any deed of conveyance pertaining to 
transfer of any land belonging to Shri Bharat Ji 
Mandir Trust. The Sub-Registrar, Rishikesh, 
Dehradun is directed to produce copies of all 
documents registered in his office, whereby and 
under, any one, either purporting to act on his behalf 
or on behalf of Shri Bharat Ji Mandir Trust, has 
transferred any property belonging to Shri Bharat Ji 
Mandir Trust to any person. The Sub-Registrar, 
Rishikesh, Dehradun is further directed to produce all 3 
such documents pertaining to all such land 
mentioned in Wajib-ul-arz. 
This order will not stand in the way of 
investigation and other actions those are being taken 
or contemplated by the State Government.” 
 Thereafter it appears that learned Single Judge 
of this Court had passed the following, in another writ 
petition being WPSS No. 2392 of 2012, which also pertains 
to properties at Rishikesh:- 
 “Mr. J.P. Joshi, Advocate for the petitioner. 
Mr. Paresh Tripathi, Additional Chief Standing 
Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand/respondents. 
Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
The case of the petitioner is that inspite of 
presentation of the documents for registration, the 
Registrar concerned has refused to register the 
document as per the judgment passed by the Division 
Bench of this Court in Public Interest Litigation No.98 
of 2011. According to the counsel for the petitioner, 
the property of the petitioner has nothing to do with 
the property covered by the Division Bench Judgment 
of this Court. The Division Bench order cannot stand 
in the way for Registration of the document presented 
by the petitioner. 
In view of the submission of learned counsel for 
the petitioner, I direct the concerned authority to 
inquire into the fact whether the land owned by the 
petitioner that is sought to be sold by executing a 
document is in any way factually covered by the 
property which is a subject matter of Division Bench. 4 
For this purpose, he will serve notice upon the 
petitioner and consider any material which may be 
produced before him. If it is found that the petitioner’s 
property has nothing to do with the property covered 
by the Division Bench judgment, obviously he will 
take further action for registration of the document. 
Entire exercise shall be completed within a period of 
three weeks’ from the date of communication of this 
order. 
The writ petition is disposed of. 
Stay application no.12251 of 2012 and Urgency 
application no.2036 of 2013 stand disposed of.” 
 Consequently, a writ petition (WPMS No. 713 of 
2015), seeking the same relief (as above), was decided by 
the learned Single Judge of this Court vide order dated 
26.03.2015. Following is the order:- 
“Mr. Khushboo Chaurasia, Advocate for 
petitioner. 
Mr. Gajendra Tripathi, Brief Holder for State 
of Uttarakhand / respondents. 
Learned counsel for the parties submitted 
that present case is squarely covered by the 
judgment dated 23.05.2013, passed by this Court 
in WPMS No. 2392 of 2012, therefore, same may 
be disposed of in the same terms, however, 
Collector may be directed to inquire into the fact 
whether the land owned by the petitioner that is 
sought to be sold by executing a document is in 
any way factually covered by the property which is 
subject matter of Division Bench. 5 
 Ordered accordingly. 
CLMA No. 3192 of 2015 also stands disposed of 
accordingly.” 
 All the same, immediately therafter a review 
petition was filed before this Court by one Govind Prasad 
Bahuguna wherein it was stated that writ petition (WPMS 
No. 713 of 2015) filed before this Court was inter alia, by 
one petitioner, namely, Rajkumar Gulati (petitioner No. 3) 
S/o Mehar Chand Gulati, who was already dead at the 
time of filing of the writ petition. There was another 
allegation as well. Thereafter the Court directed the 
remaining petitioners to be present, in person, before this 
Court. They present themselves before the Court through 
their Senior Counsel Mr. Sharad Sharma, who categorically 
made statement that none of them had authorized either 
Mr. Lalit Mohan Mishra - Advocate or Ms. Khushboo 
Chaurasia – Advocate to represent their case before this 
Court and it is purely a case of fraud and 
misrepresentation. The Court, therefore, recalled its order 
dated 26.03.2015 dismissed the writ petition, and 
thereafter had ordered for lodging of an FIR inter alia
against the present appellant, who is a practicing lawyer 
before this Court, as well as against another person, 
namely, Mr. Lalit Mohan Mishra, who is a local Advocate of 
Rishikesh. The operative portion of the order dated 
20.04.2015 passed in WPMS No. 713 of 2015 (in MCC No. 
215 of 2015) reads as under:- 
“In view of the statements as recorded 
hereinbefore, it is prima facie established that present 
petition has been filed with fictitious and 6 
manufactured signatures. Now the question remains, 
as to how and who has manufactured the fictitious 
and forged signatures on all the documents or 
petitioners themselves have signed the papers. 
One thing is clear from the statements recorded 
hereinabove that by playing fraud on the Court order 
under review has been obtained. Therefore, in my 
considered opinion, an F.I.R. must be lodged and 
matter must be thoroughly investigated by the police 
to find out as to who has fabricated and 
manufactured the papers, signatures and who is 
instrumental in filing the present petition with 
ulterior motive to play fraud on the Court and legal 
system to obtain order under review. 
In view of the discussion made hereinabove, 
order under review dated 26.03.2015 is hereby 
recalled. Since petitioners are denying having filed 
this petition, therefore, petition is dismissed. 
Registrar (Judicial) of this Court is directed to 
lodge an F.I.R. against Khushboo Chaurasia, Lalit 
Mohan Mishra, petitioners no.1, 2, 4 and 5 and 
review applicant and against the unknown persons 
under Sections 120-B, 193, 196, 197, 199, 200, 205, 
209 and 420 I.P.C. After registration of the F.I.R. with 
P.S. Mallital, Nainital, learned D.G.P. shall constitute 
the special investigating team headed by the officer 
not below the rank of Senior Superintendent of Police. 
Investigating Agency shall obtain specimen 
handwriting of all the accused and thereafter shall 
send the same to the F.S.L for the comparison with 
the signatures available on the record of this petition. 7 
Investigating Agency, shall be at liberty to obtain the 
specimen handwriting of others, who, according to 
Investigating Agency, may be involved in the crime 
Investigating Agency, shall also investigate who is 
guilty of abetting the offence or hatching the 
conspiracy. 
Paper book of the present petition shall be kept 
in sealed cover and photocopy thereof shall be kept on 
record and original papers, if required by the 
Investigating Agency for the purpose of investigation 
or examination by the F.S.L, shall be sent either to 
the Investigating Agency or to the F.S.L in the seal 
cover, as the case may be. 
Let copy of this order be forwarded to the 
learned D.G.P., Uttarakhand for information and 
compliance.” 
 We have heard the learned Senior Counsels – 
M.S. Pal, Mr. Manoj Tiwari, Ms. Pushpa Joshi, who are 
representing the appellant as well as learned Senior 
Counsel Mr. Sharad Sharma for respondent Nos. 1 to 5, 
Mr. Subhash Upadhyay, learned C.S.C. for the State and 
Mr. Sushil Vashisth, learned counsel for respondent No. 9. 
We are conscious of the fact that the orders passed by the 
learned Single Judge were under an extraordinary 
circumstances. However, on consideration of the fact that 
the appellant before this Court is a practicing lady 
advocate of less than two years of practice and is 
inexperienced, and since the learned Single Judge himself 
has directed a Senior Police Officer, not below the rank of 8 
S.S.P., to investigate the matter, we think it prudent to 
make an interference, though limited. 
 Let the investigation, as directed by the learned 
Single Judge of this Court go on but the order of the 
learned Single Judge will not operate against the appellant 
i.e. Ms. Khushboo Chaurasia. Respondents are restrained 
from either registering an F.I.R. against her or taking any 
coercive action against her. We, however, make it 
absolutely clear that all other directions given by the 
learned Single Judge in his order shall be implemented. 
The investigation shall proceed, as directed by the learned 
Single Judge of this Court. We further direct that if during 
the investigation, the Investigating Officer finds 
involvement of the present appellant in any manner which 
has criminal implications, he shall apprise this Court. We 
make it absolutely clear that our order would not amount 
to any interference with the investigation of the 
Investigating Officer, who has got absolute freedom to 
investigate on all aspects of the matter, save to the limited 
extent referred above. 
 All the respondents, who are representing before 
us through their counsels, shall file their counter affidavits 
within a period of three weeks. 
 List this appeal on 25.05.2015.
