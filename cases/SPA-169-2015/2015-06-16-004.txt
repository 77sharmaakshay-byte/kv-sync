By the Court (Oral)
1. This special appeal has been filed by the 
appellant against the order of the learned Single Judge of 
this Court dated 20th April, 2015. In the order of the 
learned Single Judge, interalia, due to certain alleged 
conduct of a practicing lawyer before this Court, it has 
been ordered that a First Information Report be lodged and 
the investigation be conducted by an officer not below the 
rank of Senior Superintendent of Police. In the order dated 
20th April, 2015 itself the learned Single Judge had directed 
the Registrar General of this Court to communicate the 
order to the Director General of Police, Uttarakhand. There 
is a letter dated 24th April, 2015 on record of the Registrar 
(Judicial) which confirms that the copy of the order dated 
20th April, 2015 of the learned Single Judge had been 
communicated vide letter no. 
3949/UHC/M.S.Section/Nainital/dated: 25th April, 2015 to 
the Director General of Police, Uttarakhand, Dehradun. 2
2. A statement has also been made by the learned 
Chief Standing Counsel Mr. Subhash Upadhyay that the 
order of the learned Single Judge has been communicated 
to the Director General of Police. This Court, though vide 
its order dated 01.05.2015 had not interfered with the 
investigation, as directed by the learned Single Judge, but 
as far as the arrest of the appellant was concerned, it was 
observed that no coercive measure be taken. Investigation 
was, however, to go on and the matter was fixed for 
25.05.2015. Thereafter, the matter was fixed for today and 
the Investigating Officer was directed to be present before 
this Court. 
3. In compliance of the order dated 25.05.2015, the 
learned Government Advocate Mr. K.S. Rautela has 
informed this Court, the Investigating Officer Mr. Tilak Ram 
Verma, who is an Inspector rank officer and is the 
Investigating Officer in the matter, is present before this 
Court. 
4. Clearly the investigation is not being conducted 
in accordance with the directions of the learned Single 
Judge, as contained in the order dated 20th April, 2015, 
which has been duly communicated to the Director General 
of Police, Uttarakhand. The order of the learned Single 
Judge dated 20th April, 2015 reads as under:- 
 “One thing is clear from the 
statements recorded hereinabove that by playing 
fraud on the Court order under review has been 
obtained. Therefore, in my considered opinion, 
an F.I.R. must be lodged and matter must be 
thoroughly investigated by the police to find out 
as to who has fabricated and manufactured the 
papers, signatures and who is instrumental in 
filing the present petition with ulterior motive to 
play fraud on the Court and legal system to 
obtain order under review. 
In view of the discussion made hereinabove, 
order under review dated 26.03.2015 is hereby 
recalled. Since petitioners are denying having 3
filed this petition, therefore, petition is 
dismissed. 
Registrar (Judicial) of this Court is directed 
to lodge an F.I.R. against Khushboo Chaurasia, 
Lalit Mohan Mishra, petitioners no.1, 2, 4 and 5 
and review applicant and against the unknown 
persons under Sections 120-B, 193, 196, 197, 
199, 200, 205, 209 and 420 I.P.C. After 
registration of the F.I.R. with P.S. Mallital, 
Nainital, learned D.G.P. shall constitute the 
special investigating team headed by the officer 
not below the rank of Senior Superintendent of 
Police. Investigating Agency shall obtain 
specimen handwriting of all the accused and 
thereafter shall send the same to the F.S.L for 
the comparison with the signatures available on 
the record of this petition. Investigating Agency, 
shall be at liberty to obtain the specimen 
handwriting of others, who, according to 
Investigating Agency, may be involved in the 
crime Investigating Agency, shall also investigate 
who is guilty of abetting the offence or hatching 
the conspiracy. 
Paper book of the present petition shall be 
kept in sealed cover and photocopy thereof shall 
be kept on record and original papers, if required 
by the Investigating Agency for the purpose of 
investigation or examination by the F.S.L, shall 
be sent either to the Investigating Agency or to 
the F.S.L in the seal cover, as the case may be. 
Let copy of this order be forwarded to the 
learned D.G.P., Uttarakhand for information 
and compliance.” 
 (emphasis provided). 
5. We fail to understand as to how a top police 
officer of the State, such as the Director General of Police 
has not been able to understand the simple terms of the 
order by which the Investigation had to be conducted by an 
officer not below the rank of Senior Superintendent of 
Police? The Director General of Police is hereby given notice 
to explain as to why the order dated 20th April, 2015 passed 
by the learned Single Judge of this Court has not been 
complied with. The reply should be given to this Court 
within three weeks from today, which shall be in form of 4
his personal affidavit. In case there has been a 
miscommunication he is free to explain. 
6. In order to save himself, the Chief Secretary of 
the State shall now comply with the order of the learned 
Single Judge, subject to the restrictions made by this Court 
vide order dated 1st May, 2015. He shall appoint an 
Investigating Officer in the matter who is not below the 
rank of Senior Superintendent of Police, as directed by the 
learned Single Judge of this Court vide order dated 20th
April, 2015 and conduct the investigation in the matter and 
submit a report before this Court within a period of six 
weeks from today. 
7. Registrar General is hereby directed to 
communicate this order to the Chief Secretary. He shall 
also made available copies of the order dated 20.04.2015 
and 01.05.2015, reference of which have been made above. 
8. Apart from the above this order shall also be 
communicated to the Director General of Police of the 
State. 
9. List this matter on 29th July, 2015 in a daily 
cause list. 
 (U.C. Dhyani, J.) (Sudhanshu Dhulia, J.)
