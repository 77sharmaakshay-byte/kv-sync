2. The present applicant is seeking 
anticipatory bail in connection with 
F.I.R./Case Crime No. 0082 of 2026, under 
Sections 305(a), 331(4), 317(2) and 3(5) of 
the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, 
registered at Police Station Selaqui, District 
Dehradun.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that the applicant was not named 
in the F.I.R. and has been implicated solely 
on the basis of the alleged confessional 
statements of the co-accused, wherein it has 
been stated that the applicant was 
accompanying them at the time of 
commission of the alleged theft. He further 
submits that, apart from the said 
statements of the co-accused, there is no 
other material or evidence available against 
the applicant.
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
5. This Court finds some substance in the 
submissions advanced by learned counsel 
for the applicant. Having regard to the facts and circumstances of the case, this Court is 
of the view that the aforesaid aspects require 
deeper scrutiny.
6. Let the respondent-State file its 
counter-affidavit within three weeks.
7. List thereafter.
8. In the meantime, as an interim 
measure, without expressing any opinion on 
the merits of the case, in the event of arrest, 
the applicant shall be released on interim 
anticipatory bail on furnishing a personal 
bond with two reliable sureties, each in the 
like amount, to the satisfaction of the 
Arresting Officer/Trial Court, subject to the 
following conditions:
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation and shall make himself 
available for interrogation as and when 
required by the Investigating Officer.
(ii) The applicant shall not, directly or 
indirectly, approach, influence, threaten 
or induce any witness in any manner 
whatsoever.
