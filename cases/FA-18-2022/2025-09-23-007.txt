5. All these bunch of First Appeals are confined to a 
limited issue of giving employment to the members of the 
affected families by virtue of Entry 4 of Schedule 2 Act 
No. 30 of 2013.
6. As per Entry 4 of Schedule 2 of Right to Fair 
Compensation and Transparency in Land Acquisition, 
Rehabilitation and Resettlement Act, 2013, it is 
mandatory upon the appropriate Government to ensure 
that the affected families are provided three options i.e. 
Option (a), Option (b) and Option (c). The reference court 
by the order impugned observed that each of the options 
are binding upon the appropriate Government and to 
choose a particular option is completely a prerogative of 
the affected family.
7. Mr. V.K. Kaparuwan, learned counsel for the 
appellant argued that the Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd., in short, 
RVNL, is only an Executing Agency of the Central 
Government and here in respect of the project the 
appropriate Government is the Central Government, 
therefore, the options as per Entry 4 of Schedule 2 can 
only be implemented by the appropriate Government i.e. 
the Central Government and not by the Executing 
Agency. He submits that the project is a time bound 
project and after implementation of the project it will be 
handed over to the concerned Ministry for its operational
and maintenance and, therefore, by virtue of Entry 4 of 
Schedule 2 r/w Section 31 of Act No. 30 of 2013 only the 
appropriate Government, the Central Government, can implement options and here in this case since the affected 
families opt for employment, therefore, such employment 
can only be offered by the appropriate Government i.e. 
the Central Government and not by the Executing 
Agency the RVNL.
8. I found force on the submission of learned counsel 
for the appellant that even if the affected families opted 
for job even then job can only be offered by the 
appropriate Government i.e. the Central Government that 
too on completion and handing over the project.
9. Learned counsel for the respondents submits that 
most of the affected families have opted for job but there 
are some affected families, who opt for compensation in 
lieu of employment.
10. Let the learned counsel for the respondents may 
get instructions from their client.
11. List this matter on 25.09.2025 on top of Board along 
with FA Nos. 31, 32 and 229 of 2022 and the Chief Project 
Manager (CPM) may join the proceeding through V.C. 
after getting necessary instructions from the appropriate 
Government with regard to implementation of option of 
providing job to the affected families.
