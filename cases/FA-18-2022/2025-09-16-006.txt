FA No. 12 of 2022, FA No. 15 of 2022, FA No. 16 of 2022, FA No. 17 of 2022, FA No. 18 of

Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. V.K. Kaparuwan, learned counsel for the 
appellant.
2. Mr. I.P. Kohli, learned Standing Counsel for the 
State.
3. Mr. Ajay Kumar, learned counsel for the 
respondent through V.C.
4. Mr. Tarun Lakhera and Mr. Shivanand Bhatt, 
learned counsel for the respondents in the connected 
appeals.
5. All these first appeals have been preferred against 
the impugned award passed by the Land Acquisition 
Rehabilitation and Resettlement Authority (LARRA), 
Uttarakhand, whereby the appellant herein Rail Vikas 
Nigam Ltd. was directed to give 12% per annum simple 
interest on the awarded amount and to provide the 
employment to one of the dependants of the family 
whose land was acquired.
6. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that so 
far as the quantum of compensation and the direction for 
giving 12% per annum interest on the awarded amount is 
concerned RVNL the present appellant are not pressing 
appeal and to that extent appeal be permitted to be 
withdrawn; however, instant appeals are being pressed
only in respect of the limited issue of giving employment 
to one of the dependants of the family whose land was 
acquired in lieu of Rs. 5 lakhs the additional 
compensation.
7. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that the 
possession has already been handed over to the Rail 
Vikas Nigam Ltd. and since the appellant is not pressing 
the appeal in respect of the quantum of compensation 
and the amount towards the interest and the instant 
appeals are being pressed only for the limited issue, 
therefore, for the limited issue the appeals be admitted by 
condoning the delay.8. Learned counsel for the respondents have not 
raised any objection on the delay condonation 
application.
9. Learned Standing Counsel Mr. I.P. Kohli and Mr. 
Tarun Lakhera, learned counsel for the claimants submits 
that they have no objection if the appellant are not 
pressing the appeal in respect of the quantum of 
compensation and the interest.
10. Accordingly, the instant first appeals are dismissed 
as withdrawn so far it relates to the quantum of 
compensation and interest component. All these appeals 
are admitted only to a limited issue of giving 
employment to the dependant of the family whose land 
were acquired.
11. Learned counsel for the appellant Mr. V.K. 
Kaparuwan also submits since the instant first appeals 
are only admitted to the limited issue of giving 
employment and it is purely legal issue, therefore, there 
is no need to summon the record.
12. In FA 23 of 2022 the substitution application moved 
by the learned counsel for the appellant is allowed.
13. Let the amended memo of parties be filed within a 
week.
14. It has been apprised by the learned counsel for the 
parties that some of the claimants have received the 
amount of compensation but some of them have not 
received.
15. In such view of the matter, the Collector, Pauri 
Garhwal is directed to do the needful for disbursement of 
the amount of compensation along with interest. 
16. It is made clear that while disbursing the amount 
towards the interest the Collector shall ensure that the 
interest will be payable at the rate of 12% per annum only 
till the date of depositing the amount of compensation.
17. It is made clear that receiving the amount of 
compensation and the interest by any of the claimants 
will not affect their right to get the employment which 
infact is the subject matter of the instant first appeals.
18. Let the copy of this order be handed over to Mr. 
I.P. Kohli, learned Standing Counsel for necessary 
compliance.
19. List all these appeal on 22.09.2025 for final hearing.
