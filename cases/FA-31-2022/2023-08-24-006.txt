COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
FA No. 31 of 2022
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. V. K. Kaparuwan, counsel for the 
appellant/Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd.
Mr. Anil Kumar Joshi, counsel for the 
respondent nos. 2, 3 & 4.
2. The appeal is preferred against the 
impugned award dated 31.12.2019 passed by 
the Land Acquisition, Rehabilitation and 
Resettlement Authority (LARRA), 
Uttarakhand, whereby the Authority had 
directed the appellant/Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd. 
to give 12% p.a. simple interest on the 
awarded amount and to provide the 
employment to the respondents.
He would submit that the present appeal 
as against the direction for giving 12% p.a. 
interest on the awarded amount has been 
withdrawn and now the appeal is as against the 
direction to give employment to respondents, 
therefore, the possession of the acquired land 
should be handed over to the appellant/Rail 
Vikas Nigam Ltd.
3. Counsel for the appellant/Rail Vikas 
Nigam Ltd. would further submit that the 
application was moved before the District 
Magistrate, Garhwal on 15.12.2022 (at page 
no. 141 of the appeal) thereby requesting the 
District Magistrate, Garhwal to take the 
possession of the acquired land and hand over 
the same to the appellant/Rail Vikas Nigam 
Ltd. for construction of the railway line but the 
District Magistrate is refusing to hand over the 
said possession. 
4. The counsel for the respondent nos. 2, 3 & 4 would submit that he has no objection as 
the appellant/Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd. has 
withdrawn its appeal against granting of 12% 
p.a. interest and there was no appeal against 
the amount originally awarded; that, this 
appeal is now left in respect of the direction 
only for giving employment to the 
respondents, therefore, the possession of the 
acquired land may be transferred to the 
appellant/Rail Vikas Nigam Ltd.
5. Counsel for the appellant would submit 
at Bar that the whole awarded amount along 
with 12% p.a. interest has already been 
deposited with the Collector, Pauri Garhwal. 
6. Counsel for the respondent nos. 2, 3 & 4
requests that the awarded amount so deposited 
by the appellant with the Collector, Pauri 
Garhwal may be released in favour of the 
respondents.
7. In view of the above, Misc. application 
(IA No. 06 of 2023) would stand allowed. 
8. List this matter on 11.10.2023.
9. The Collector, Pauri Garhwal is directed 
to do the needful for disbursement of awarded 
amount with interest and for transfer of land to 
appellant. 
10. Urgency application (IA No. 7 of 2023)
stands disposed of.
