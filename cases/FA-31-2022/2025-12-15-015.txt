FA No. 31 of 2022, FA No. 18 of 2022, FA No. 32 of 2022, FA No. 229 of 2022
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. V.K. Kaparuwan, learned counsel for the 
appellant.
2. Mr. Siddhartha Sah, learned counsel for the 
private respondents.
3. Mr. Lalit Sharma, learned Deputy Solicitor General 
for India/Indian Railway.
4. All these appeals have been filed by the Rail Vikas 
Nigam Ltd. against the order of reference, whereby, the 
reference court directed to the appellant to offer an 
employment in lieu of the additional compensation of Rs. 
5 lakhs. On the previous date the State was directed to 
classify the cases in which the claimants have received an 
additional amount of compensation i.e. Rs. 5 lakhs and to 
segregate those cases in which the claimants are seeking 
employment in lieu of the additional compensation.
5. Mr. I.P. Kohli, who represents the State, examined
and classified and submits that except in four cases in 
other appeals the claimants already accepted the 
additional amount of compensation of Rs. 5 lakhs.
6. Once the claimants accepted the amount towards 
additional compensation of Rs. 5 lakhs in lieu of 
employment, therefore, they cannot claim further to get 
an employment, however this issue are involved in other 
set of appeals which are now delinked.
7. In such view of the matter, since as per the 
mandate of the Act two options have been given by the 
Corporation either to accept the additional amount of 
compensation of Rs. 5 lakhs or to get employment, 
therefore, this is completely a prerogative of the 
complainant to opt for one offer.
8. Mr. V.K. Kaparuwan apprise to this court that 
infact RVNL is an executing agency of the Ministry of 
Railways and after completion they have to handover 
entire project to the Ministry of Railways for its 
maintenance and functioning and as such employment 
can be offered by the Ministry of Railways.
9. In such view of the matter since Mr. Lalit Sharma have not received any instructions, in such view of the 
matter, last opportunity is given to get the instructions or 
to file an affidavit positively within ten days.
