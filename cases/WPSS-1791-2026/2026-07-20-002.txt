3. Mr. Sagar Malara, learned counsel 
holding brief of Mr. Yogesh Pacholia, learned
counsel for respondent No.5-N.C.T.E.
4. By means of present writ petition, 
petitioner has challenged the communication 
dated 25.04.2026 issued by respondent no.2 
and order dated 06.05.2026 issued by 
respondent no.3, whereby the services of the 
petitioner were terminated.
5. It is contended by learned counsel for 
the petitioner that petitioner was appointed 
as an Assistant Teacher on 07.06.2022. 
Petitioner was in possession of Bachelor 
Degree in Arts as well as B.Ed. qualification. 
The petitioner secured 40.67% marks in 
graduation.
6. The services of petitioner were 
terminated on the ground that his marks is 
below 45% in graduation.
7. It is contended by learned counsel for 
petitioner that in view of the notification 
issued by respondent no.5-NCTE dated 13.11.2019, for the candidates who have 
taken admission in B.Ed. course prior to 
29.07.2011, the condition of securing 45% 
marks in graduation will not be applicable.
8. It is further contended by him that the 
petitioner did graduation and B.Ed. course in 
the year 2006 and 2009 respectively and 
thus, the condition of having 45% marks in 
graduation would not be attracted in the 
case of the petitioner.
9. Having perused the writ petition and 
having appreciated the contention raised by 
learned counsel for the paties, this Court 
finds substance in the submission made by 
learned Counsel for the petitioner.
10. In such view of the matter, in the 
peculiar facts and circumstances of the case, 
petitioner deserves protection from this 
Court.
11. In view of the above, it is directed that 
till the next date of listing, effect and 
operation of the impugned communication/
orders dated 25.04.2026 and 06.05.2026 
passed by respondent nos.2 and 3 
respectively, shall remain stayed.
12. Respondents may file their counter 
affidavit(s) in the matter within six weeks. 
13. Put up on 21.09.2026 showing the name 
of Mr. Yogesh Pacholia, learned counsel for 
respondent No.5-N.C.T.E.
14. Interim relief application (IA/1/2026) 
stands disposed of.
