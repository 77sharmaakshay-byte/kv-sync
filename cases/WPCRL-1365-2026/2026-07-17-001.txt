2. By means of the present Criminal Writ 
Petition, the petitioner seeks quashing of the 
First Information Report dated 28.06.2026, 
registered as F.I.R./Case Crime No. 404 of 
2026, under Sections 120-B, 420, 467, 468 
and 471 of the I.P.C., at Police Station Patel 
Nagar, District Dehradun. 
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner
submits that the petitioner was appointed as 
a Special Educator for specially-abled 
students studying in the said Institute. It is 
further submitted that the petitioner's 
husband was serving as the Director of the 
Institute and tendered his resignation on
02.06.2026 on account of alleged harassment 
and victimization at the hands of the 
complainant and other persons associated 
with the Institute. It is also submitted that 
although the petitioner's husband had lodged 
a complaint against the complainant and 
others, no First Information Report was 
registered thereon. According to the learned 
counsel for the petitioner, the present F.I.R. 
has been lodged only to pressurize the 
petitioner and her husband, and the 
complainant happens to be the Chief 
Managing Director of the said Institute. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner further submits that the allegations contained in the 
complaint primarily relate to the alleged 
financial misconduct committed by the 
petitioner's husband during the period when 
he was working as the Principal of the 
Institute from the year 2022 till June, 2026. It 
is submitted that even if the allegations 
contained in the F.I.R. are accepted at their 
face value, no specific role or overt act has 
been attributed to the petitioner so as to 
constitute the offences alleged against her. 
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the material available on record.
5. Having considered the submissions 
advanced by the learned counsel for the 
parties and upon perusal of the material 
available on record, this Court is of the prima 
facie opinion that the matter requires 
consideration. 
6. Issue notice to respondent no. 3. Steps 
be taken within one week. 
7. Counter affidavit, if any, be filed by the 
respondents within four weeks. 
8. List this case after receipt of the service 
report. 
9. Till the next date of listing, as an interim 
measure, the petitioner shall not be arrested 
in connection with the aforesaid F.I.R., 
provided she cooperates with the investigation 
and appears before the Investigating Officer as 
and when required.
10. The Stay Application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
