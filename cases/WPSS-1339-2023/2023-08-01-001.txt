Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Ms. Snigdha Tiwari, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. Atul Bahuguna, learned counsel for respondent

3. Mr. Ajay Singh Bisht, learned counsel for respondent

4. Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
5. By means of this writ petition, petitioners have prayed 
for their regularization pursuant to the Office Order dated 
04.02.2022 issued by the Indian Council for Agricultural 
Research on the ground that they are still working and their 
services are still required by the respondent-Department.
6. The contention of the petitioners is that they have 
been working with respondent no.2-Vivekanand Parvatiya 
Krishi Anusandhan Sansthan, Almora, Institute of Indian 
Council for Agricultural Research, since 1986 to 1996, as 
Daily Wagers. 
7. It is further contended that pursuant to the Office 
Order dated 04.02.2022, eight institutes, under the Indian 
Council for Agricultural Research, have regularized the 
services of the daily wage employees after creating the posts
in the institutions where they have been working. However, 
since long, no such exercise was undertaken by the 
respondent-department.
8. It is also submitted that on the aforesaid facts, the 
representation(s) submitted by the petitioners has been 
rejected by respondent no.2 vide order dated 20.06.2023, 
annexure-9 to the writ petition, mainly on the ground that the 
petitioners have not submitted the proof of their longstanding 
services as daily wager in the respondent-department. 2
9. It is further submitted that the grounds raised by 
respondent no.3 for rejecting their representations for 
regularization was totally uncalled for, as the petitioners have 
been working with the respondent no.2 for a long span of 
time, and it is the respondents’ duty to keep the documents of 
the petitioners especially for the reason that they are hardly 
literate and belong to the lower strata of the society. 
10. Learned counsel for respondent no.2 pointed out that 
the petitioners have not been made the proper party, rather the 
Indian Council for Agricultural Research (ICAR), is the legal 
person, where-under, the respondent no.2-Vivekanand 
Parvatiya Krishi Anusandhan Sansthan, Almora, was 
working. According to the learned counsel, the respondent 
no.2 is also an autonomous society.
11. In this view of the matter, petitioners are directed to 
bring on record Indian Council for Agricultural Research 
(ICAR) as respondent no.3 within a period of seven days’ 
from today.
12. Learned counsel for respondent no.2 seeks and is 
granted six weeks’ time to file counter affidavit. 
13. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the 
petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
14. List this matter on 12.10.2023.
15. In the meantime, it is provided that none of the 
petitioners shall be thrown out of service only for the reason 
that they have come up before this Court for redressal of their 
grievance(s).
