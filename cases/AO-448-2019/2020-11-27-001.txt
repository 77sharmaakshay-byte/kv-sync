There is delay of 60 days in filing the Appeal.
Reasons furnished in the affidavit filed in 
support of delay condonation application are 
sufficient to condone the delay. 
Accordingly, the delay condonation 
application (CLMA No. 14373 of 2019) is allowed 
and the delay in filing the Appeal is condoned. 
Heard learned counsel for the parties 
through video conferencing. 
Admit.
Summon the lower Court’s record.
List this matter after six weeks. 
After considering the submission of learned 
counsel for the appellant, it is directed that, till 
the next date of listing, effect and operation of the 
impugned award dated 20.05.2019 passed by the 
learned Motor Accident Claims Tribunal/IInd 
Additional District Judge, Kashipur in M.A.C.P. 
No. 231 of 2015 shall remain stayed, provided 
appellant deposits a sum of ` 4,00,000/- out of 
the awarded amount before the Tribunal 
concerned within a period of four weeks from 
today. 
Stay application (CLMA No. 14372 of 2019) 
stands disposed of.
Learned Tribunal is directed to release a 
sum of ` 2,00,000/-, out of the sum to be 
deposited before the Tribunal concerned, 
proportionately in favour of claimants, without 
asking any security. The remaining amount shall 
be invested in an interest bearing scheme of a 
nationalized bank.
Urgency application (I.A. No. 11591 of 2020) 
is, accordingly, disposed of.
