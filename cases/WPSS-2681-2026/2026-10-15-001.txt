1. Mr. S.S. Yadav, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. G.S. Negi, learned Addl. C.S.C. for the State.
3. The petitioners are working as Sweepers since 
2010 and 2017 respectively and praying for
regularization.
4. As per Annexure-1 the post of Sweeper is 
sanctioned to be filled up only by way of contractual 
appointment but the question is if the petitioners are 
working since long back then what would be the status 
as on date there is no clarity on this. It is also pointed 
out that in other establishments of the Government the 
appointment are being made on the post of Sweeper 
on regular basis, therefore, atleast the petitioners are 
entitled to get the minimum of payscale attached to the 
post of Sweeper.
5. Let the State may clarify the position and apprise 
what policy has been framed to absorb those who are 
working in a post identified as Class-IV though Class￾IV as on date is now declared as dying cadre, but the 
existing employees should be given same status.
6. Put up this matter on 31.10.2026.
7. In the meantime, the petitioners shall not be 
disturbed.
