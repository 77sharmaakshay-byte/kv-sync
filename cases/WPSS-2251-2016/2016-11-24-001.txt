Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J. 
1. Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate, present for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. N.S. Pundir, Brief Holder, present for the 
State of Uttarakhand/respondent Nos.1 to 3. 
3. For a private college, namely, Inter College 
Indrapuri, District Pauri Garhwal, which is being run by 
the Committee of Management and which is totally under 
grant-in-aid, selection for the post of Lecturers were 
initiated in the year 2012, in which the petitioner’s name 
figured in the top seven candidates, who were to be 
interviewed. Thereafter, for certain reasons, the interview 
could not take place and the same interview has been 
initiated after a period of four years i.e. in the year 2016, in 
which the petitioner’s name does not figure on the basis of 
different calculation of merit. This is what, presently being 
challenged by the petitioner before this Court. 
4. Apart from the anomaly relating to the 
individual i.e. the present petitioner, what is worrying is as 
to why in an education institution, a post which is lying 
vacant at least since 2012, has not been filled till 2016 and 
what were the compelling reasons for this. Moreover, in the 
year 2016, a fresh recruitment process is liable to be 
initiated, inasmuch as, that would widen the net of the 
authorities in picking up candidates between 2012 to 2016. 
There would be many other eligible candidates vying up for 
the post of Lecturer. 2 
5. In view thereof, further selection process to 
the post of Lecturer is hereby stayed. 
6. Issue notice to respondent No.4. Steps to be 
taken within 24 hours. 
7. Considering the urgency in the matter, two 
weeks’ time is granted to the respondents to file counter 
affidavit. 
8. List this matter on 08.12.2016 in the daily 
cause list.
