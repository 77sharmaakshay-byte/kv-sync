Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Harsh Vardhan Dhanik and Mr. Imran Ali 
Khan, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Dr. Kartikey Hari Gupta, learned counsel for the 
respondent.
3. The re-transfer of the petitioner is sought to be 
justified by the department on the ground that the person,
who was transferred to the erstwhile post of the petitioner 
at Purola, had requested for cancellation on the ground 
that she is suffering from cancer.
4. The fact remains that the petitioner was transferred 
from Purola to Haldwani and the transfer of Smt. Pooja 
Rawal, is not a consequential transfer.
5. The petitioner was transferred to a vacant post from 
Purola to Haldwani and, on account of the vacancy there, 
Smt. Pooja Rawal was transferred to Purola. She sought 
cancellation on the ground of her illness, for which 
sufficient records are not before this Court.
6. Be that as it may, the justification for re-transferring 
back the petitioner appears to be flawed. There is 
absolutely no nexus or reason for the petitioner to be 
transferred back merely because Smt. Pooja Rawal has
been transferred back from Purola to Dehradun, as the 
petitioner is occupying the vacant post. In fact, the 
impugned order would show that one Ms. Savitri Giri, In￾charge, Divisional Manager has been given additional 
charge of Logging Manager, West Haldwani, to which 
post the petitioner was initially transferred.
7. There being no nexus between the posts for the transfer of the petitioner from Purola to Haldwani and that 
of Smt. Pooja Rawal to Purola, the justification does not 
appeal to this Court.
8. Hence, there shall be a stay of the impugned 
proceedings. Petitioner shall be permitted to resume duties 
as In-charge Divisional Logging Manager, West 
Haldwani.
9. List this case on 24.07.2025.
