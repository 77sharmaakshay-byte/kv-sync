WPSS No.1345 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, learned Senior Advocate 
assisted by Mr. Chandra Shekhar Dalakoti, learned 
counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State.
3. By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has 
challenged the orders dated 15.07.2024 and 24.06.2024, 
passed by respondent nos.4 and 5, whereby service of 
the petitioner has been terminated only on the ground 
that the petitioner possess B.Ed qualification and is 
teaching from Class Ist to Vth (primary classes).
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that the judgment and order passed by 
Hon’ble Apex Court in the case of Devesh Sharma Vs. 
Union of India reported in 2023 Live Law (SC) 633, 
wherein the Hon’ble Apex Court found and concluded 
that the candidates, who are holding B.Ed qualification 
are not eligible to impart education and also to be 
appointed as Teacher for primary classes i.e., Class Ist
to Vth.
5. The impugned termination order is the fall out of 
the aforesaid judgment, but the respondent-Authorities 
while passing the impugned termination order has 
failed to notice the fact that subsequently in the 
Review/Clarification Application No.77741/2024 and 2
batch of the order dated 11.08.2023 passed in Devesh 
Sharma (supra), vide order dated 08.04.2024 a caveat 
has been made by the Hon’ble Supreme Court in the 
aforesaid order saying that those who are already in 
service will not be ousted for the reason that they are 
having B.Ed. qualification rather they are permitted to 
complete a bridge course within one year from the date 
of passing of the judgment on aforesaid 
review/clarification applications in Devesh Sharma
(supra) to retain their service.
6. Having considered rival submission of the parties 
prima facie the termination orders appears to be 
contrary to the law declared by the Hon’ble Apex 
Court, therefore the impugned order is hereby stayed.
7. The respondent may file counter affidavit within 
six weeks.
8. Two weeks’ time thereafter is granted to the 
petitioner for filing rejoinder affidavit.
9. List this case on 03.12.2024.
