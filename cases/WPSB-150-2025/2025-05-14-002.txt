14.05.2025 WPSB No. 150 of 2025
Hon’ble G. Narendar, C.J.
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
1. Mr. Shobhit Saharia, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. P.C. Bisht, learned Additional Chief Standing 
Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand/ respondents. 
3. The matter was listed yesterday, and it was 
adjourned to today to enable the learned Additional 
Chief Standing Counsel to secure instructions. 
4. The facts are peculiar. Petitioner was suspended, 
on being arrested, and sent to judicial custody, 
pursuant to a successful trap. Thereafter, the 
suspension was revoked by order dated 03.05.2024. 
Thereafter, it appears that, on change of the 
Disciplinary Authority, again an order, revoking the 
suspension order came to be passed, and, 
consequently, another order of placing him under 
suspension came to be passed, on the very set of facts, 
i.e. a trap case. 
5. We had directed the learned Additional Chief 
Standing Counsel to secure instructions, as to whether 
any disciplinary proceedings have been initiated, or 
whether any show-cause notice has been issued to the 
petitioner. 
6. On a query, the learned Additional Chief Standing 
Counsel submits that he has no instructions. 
7. In the absence of any new facts, the question 
that stares this Court, is whether the Disciplinary 
Authority has the power to review its own order, in the absence of any statutory review power. The order,
placing the petitioner under suspension, appears to be 
without jurisdiction. 
8. In that view of the matter, there shall be a stay
of the impugned order dated 19.11.2024, placing the 
petitioner under suspension. 
9. List this case on 30.07.2025.
 (Alok Mahra, J.) (G. Narendar, C.J.)
