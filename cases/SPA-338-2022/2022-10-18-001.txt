 Since there is a dispute whether or not the 
land of the appellants falling in Khata No. 26 of 
Village Bagi is in occupation of respondent No. 6, 
we direct the Tehsildar concerned to make an on￾spot inspection after identifying the land falling in 
Khata No. 26 and reporting as to who is in 
occupation of the said land. It should specifically 
be stated whether respondent No. 6, i.e. THDC or 
anybody claiming through THDC is in occupation of 
the said land. The inspection should be conducted 
on 02.11.2022 at 11:00 AM. 
 The parties shall remain present during the 
inspection and shall participate in the proceedings. 
The Inspection Report shall be prepared and signed on the spot. They shall also be free to record
observation or objection, if any. 
 The Tehsildar shall take assistance of other 
Experts for the purpose of identification and 
measurement of the land falling in Khata No. 26. 
 Let the report be sent to this Court before the 
next date.
