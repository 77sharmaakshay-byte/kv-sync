CRMA No. 1588/2016 (Bail Application) 
In 
CRLA No. 214 of 2016 
Hon'ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
 
 Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate for the 
appellant. 
 Mr. Sandeep Tandon, Deputy Advocate 
General for the State of Uttarakhand. 
 Heard. 
 It is the contention of learned counsel for the 
appellant that the appellant is facing trial in 
respect of offences punishable under Sections 376 
of I.P.C and Section 4 of the Protection of Children 
from sexual offences Act, 2012, registered at P.S. 
Ranipokhari, District Dehradun. It is also 
contended that all the prosecution witnesses are 
interested witnesses and therefore, their testimony 
cannot be relied. There is no independent witness 
of the alleged incident. The conviction against the 
appellant is recorded solely relying upon the 
statements of interested witnesses, which itself is 
bad in the eyes of law. Lastly, it is contended that 
in her statement recorded under section 164 
Cr.P.C, the victim clearly stated on oath that no 
act was committed by the appellant which comes 
within the ambit of section 376 of I.P.C. 
 Considering the entire conspectus of the 
things, without expressing any opinion as to the 
final merits of the case, this Court is of the view 
that applicant deserves bail. 
 The bail application is allowed. 
 Let applicant Anant Raj be released on bail, 
during the pendency of present criminal appeal, 
on his executing a personal bond and furnishing 
two sureties, each of the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the Special Judge concerned. 
 
 (Lok Pal Singh J.)
