Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Naman Kamboj, learned counsel 
for petitioner through V.C.
2. Mr. Ahutosh Thakral, learned counsel 
for the respondents-Bank.
3. A preliminary objection has been 
raised by the learned counsel appearing 
for the Bank that the Punjab National 
Bank (a Nationalised Bank) is not 
amenable to the writ jurisdiction as the 
same does not fall within the definition of 
“State” under Article 12 of the 
Constitution of India.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
was asked to explain this matter before 
this Court vide order dated 11.08.2026.
5. Today, the learned counsel for the 
petitioner relying upon the judgment 
passed by the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi 
in the case of Kul Bhushan Chopra vs. 
The Punjab National Bank C.W. No. 436 
of 1978 decided on September 22, 1978,
has submitted that the Nationalised Bank falls within the word “authority” under 
Article 12 of Constitution of India.
6. Learned counsel for the respondents 
submits that the writ petition would also 
not be maintainable for the reason that 
the order impugned in the present writ 
petition is regarding termination of the 
petitioner as well as the Appellate order 
which can be challenged before the 
Labour Court as the petitioner is class IV 
employee and he is a Workman under 
Section 2 (s) of the Industrial Disputes Act 
1947. 
7. Learned counsel for the respondents 
prays for and is granted six weeks’ time to 
file counter affidavit.
8. Two weeks’ time thereafter is granted 
to petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
Issue of maintainability of writ petition on 
both counts is still left open. 
9. List this case on 04.11.2026.
