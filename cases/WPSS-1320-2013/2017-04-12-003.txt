WPSS No. 1320 of 2013
Hon’ble Rajiv Sharma, J.
 Ms. Prabha Naithani, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. B.P.S. Mer, Brief Holder for the State. 
Petitioner was working as Clerk in the 
District Court, Nainital. He was transferred on his 
request from Nainital to Dehradun on 21.11.2008 
with a rider that he would rank below the clerks 
who were already serving in District Court, 
Dehradun. 
Thereafter, in the administrative exigency, 
petitioner has been transferred from District 
Dehradun to District Champawat on 31.07.2009. 
Surprisingly, in gradation list dated 
03.07.2013, the petitioner has been shown junior 
to the respondents and was placed at Sl. No.6 
whereas he ought to have been placed at Sl. No.1. 
in the judgeship Champawat. 
The petitioner sought voluntary transfer from 
Nainital to Dehradun with a caveat that he may be 
placed below to the employees who were working in 
the Dehradun Judgeship in Class III posts. 
However, when the petitioner has been 
transferred from Dehradun to Champawat vide 
order dated 31.07.2009, petitioner has not given 
any undertaking. 
In the Champawat Judgeship, the petitioner 
ought to have been assigned seniority on the basis 
of his length of services. Petitioner joined his 
services on 02.01.2002 and the persons appointed 
after him have been placed above him. 
Accordingly, the writ petition is allowed. 
Impugned gradation list dated 03.07.2013 is 
quashed and set-aside. Respondents are directed 
to prepare a fresh gradation list in view of the 
discussion and observation made hereinabove and 
assign the seniority of the petitioner from the 
initial date of his joining.
