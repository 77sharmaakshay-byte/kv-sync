It has been argued by the learned counsel 
for the petitioner that this writ petition was 
initially allowed on 12.04.2017 and the matter 
was taken up in Special Appeal No. 167/2017 
‘Devendra Singh Mehra vs. Jogender Kumar & 
Others’. The Special Appeal thus filed was 
allowed on 27.11.2018 and the matter was 
remanded back with the direction to the 
respondents in the writ petition to file the 
counter affidavit, which has not been filed 
earlier, thereafter neither filed their counter 
affidavit, nor they have filed their authority in 
the writ petition on behalf of the private 
respondents. 
Hence, put up this case in the week 
commencing 07.01.2019. 
In the meantime, the learned counsel for 
the petitioner would give a written intimation 
about today’s order to the counsel for the 
respondent, who had appeared in the Special 
Appeal for the purposes of taking cognizance to 
the effect that the matter is fixed for 
07.01.2019.
