Mr. D.C.S. Rawat, Mr. Hemant, MR. 
Aditya Kumar and Mr. A.S. Sirhoi, learned

2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned A.G.A. for the 
State.
3. Present C-528 application has been filed 
seeking quashing of the impugned order 
dated 17.10.2025 passed by learned Civil 
Judge, Judicial Magistrate (I) Bhikiyasain, 
District Almora in Complaint Case No.09 of 
2026 under Section 318 (4) B.N.S. as well as 
entire proceedings of the aforesaid criminal 
case.
4. Learned counsel for the applicants 
would submit that the applicants are 
Directors of Solar Pulse Energy Pvt. Ltd., a 
company engaged in the installation, 
maintenance and operation of solar power 
plants; that respondent no.2 is also a partner 
in the said business along with the 
applicants; and that a commercial 
relationship existed between the parties. He 
would further submit that a bare perusal of 
the F.I.R. would reveal that the dispute, if 
any, arising between the applicants and 
respondent no.2 is purely contractual in 
nature.
5. It is further submitted that, as per the 
agreement entered into between the 
applicants and respondent no.2/
complainant, there exists an arbitration 
clause, and the applicants have already 
issued a notice for invoking the said arbitration clause. He would further submit 
that prior to the registration of the impugned 
F.I.R., an F.I.R. No. 0031 of 2024 under 
Sections 309(4), 351(2) and 352 of the B.N.S. 
had already been registered at Police Station 
Deghat on the complaint of the applicants 
against respondent no.2 and his associates 
for acts including illegal interference with the 
solar plant operations, criminal intimidation 
and obstruction of work; that, the present 
F.I.R. is nothing but a counterblast to the 
said F.I.R., and therefore the entire criminal 
proceedings are unsustainable in the eyes of 
law.
6. Issue notices to respondent no.2, 
returnable within six week.
7. Steps to be taken within one week.
8. Objections, if any, be filed by the 
respondents in the meantime.
9. As an interim measure, it is provided 
that further proceedings of Complaint Case 
No.09 of 2026 pending in the court of learned 
Civil Judge, Judicial Magistrate (I) 
Bhikiyasain, District Almora shall remain 
stayed till the next date of listing.
10. Stay application (IA 1 of 2026) stands 
disposed of.
