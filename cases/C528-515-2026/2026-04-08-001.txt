Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Raj Kumar Singh, learned

2. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned Deputy 
A.G. along with Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned 
A.G.A. for the State.
3. Present C-528 application has been 
filed seeking quashing of the charge￾sheet and the cognizance/summoning 
order dated 21.12.2024 passed by the 
learned 3rd Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Dehradun in Criminal Case 
No. 9591 of 2024, arising out of offences 
punishable under Sections 324, 342, 
352, 427, 504 and 506 of I.P.C., along 
with the entire criminal proceedings of 
the aforesaid case.
4. Learned counsel for the applicants 
would submit that the applicants are 
innocent and have been falsely 
implicated in the present case. It is 
contended that the impugned 
proceedings are nothing but a 
counterblast to the F.I.R. earlier lodged 
by the applicants against respondent 
no.2/complainant; that, on 19.02.2024, 
respondent no.2 had misbehaved with 
them, abused them in filthy language 
and further insulted them by using 
caste-based derogatory remarks; that,
when the applicants objected to such 
conduct, respondent no.2 allegedly 
picked up a quarrel with applicant no.2 and inflicted injuries upon him by 
stabbing with a knife, as a result of 
which applicant no.2 sustained serious 
injuries and had to be admitted to 
hospital; that, thereafter, applicant no.1 
lodged an F.I.R. against respondent no.2. 
In the said case, after investigation, the 
Investigating Officer submitted a charge￾sheet against respondent no.2 for the 
offences punishable under Sections 504, 
506 and 307 I.P.C., whereupon the 
learned trial court took cognizance and 
summoned respondent no.2; that,
respondent no.2 was arrested in the said 
case and was subsequently enlarged on 
bail by this Court. 
5. Learned counsel would further 
submit that, in order to pressurize the 
applicants and to shield himself from the 
consequences of the aforesaid criminal 
case, respondent no.2 has concocted a 
false and fabricated story and lodged the 
present F.I.R. on 02.02.2024 against the 
applicants, alleging that the applicants 
assaulted him and also molested his 
minor daughter and wife. However, 
during the course of investigation, the 
Investigating Officer, after examining the 
CCTV footage, found that no such 
incident of molestation with the daughter 
or wife of respondent no.2 had taken 
place; that, despite such findings, the 
Investigating Officer, in a casual manner, 
proceeded to submit the charge-sheet 
against the applicants merely on the 
basis of certain medical documents, 
which are stated to be only photocopies 
and not the original medical records, 
therefore, the entire criminal proceedings are unsustainable in the eyes of law and 
are liable to be quashed. 
6. Having considered the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
applicants, this Court finds that the 
matter requires consideration. 
7. Issue notice to respondent no.2, 
returnable within six weeks. 
8. Steps to be taken within one week. 
9. List after service of notice upon 
respondent no.2. 
10. Objections, if any, be filed by the 
State/respondent. 
11. Meanwhile, as an interim measure, 
it is directed that further proceedings of 
Criminal Case No. 9591 of 2024, pending 
before the learned 3rd Additional Chief 
Judicial Magistrate, Dehradun, shall 
remain stayed till the next date of listing.
12. Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
