Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Bhagwat Mehra and Mr. K.K. Harbola, 
learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. The petitioner was selected and appointed to the 
post of Assistant Teacher in Primary School against the 
post, which was advertised pursuant to the Rules of 
2012. In total 15 posts were earmarked for the 
scheduled caste category candidates and the petitioner 
was selected and appointed under the Scheduled Caste 
category.
4. Subsequently, by the order impugned his 
appointment has been cancelled on the ground there 
was some mistake in computing the marks on her
credentials. The impugned order is challenged on the
ground that the in total 15 posts were earmarked for 
the SC category candidates but only 10 have been 
selected and 5 remaining posts are still vacant.
5. By placing reliance to Annexure-16 he submits 
that the last candidate under the SC category, who was 
selected and appointed obtained 64.01 marks and the 
petitioner’s marks is 63.14 and as per the Rules there is 
no benchmark, therefore, in such an eventuality, they 
have to fill up all the remaining 5 posts.
6. I found force on submission of learned counsel 
for the petitioner if the 15 posts were earmarked for 
the SC category why 5 posts were left out and secondly 
when there is no benchmark under the Rules then why 
they kept remaining 5 posts as vacant when the 
candidate under the same category are available.7. Let the State may get instructions in this regard.
8. Put up this matter on 18.11.2026.
