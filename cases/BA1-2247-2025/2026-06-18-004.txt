Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Pavan Kumar Nath, learned counsel for 
the applicant.
2. Mr. B.C. Joshi, learned A.G.A. with Mr. Vijay 
Khanduri, Brief Holder for the State.
3. Present applicant Chandan Singh @ Chandan, 
S/o late Mohan Singh is praying for regular bail in 
reference to FIR dated 28.06.2025, bearing FIR No. 
240 of 2025, P.S. Ramnagar District Nainital which 
was lodged against unknown person for the offence 
punishable under Section 103(1) of BNS 2023.
4. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
applicant that the applicant is innocent and has been 
falsely implicated which is evident from the fact that 
applicant is not named in the FIR since FIR was 
against unknown person. He submits that the
applicant has been implicated on suspicion and the 
entire case of the prosecution is based on 
circumstantial evidence and there is no evidence 
connecting the applicant to be involved in the crime. 
He further submits that there was no intention or 
motive on the part of the applicant in commission of 
the alleged crime and he is languishing in jail since 
29.06.2025. He further submits that after commission 
of crime, even as per the prosecution, the applicant 
was lying on the cot where body of the deceased was 
found and if he actually committed the alleged 
offence, then he fled away from the place of 
occurrence. He further submits that investigation is 
faulty and no recovery is shown from the possession 
of the applicant. 5. On the other side prosecution filed a detail 
objection by submitting that there are all credible 
evidence against the applicant and during 
investigation statement of the star witnesses, namely, 
Balwant Singh Negi (informant), Jagdish Chandra 
Pathak, Arun Kumar Saini, S.I. Surbhi Rana, S.I Tara 
Singh Rana were also recorded. The Doctor were also 
examined and there are certain CCTV footages in 
which the applicant was seen entering into the place 
of incident and the applicant was arrested from the 
spot having blood stained hands and clothes, in 
support of which recovery memo is also placed on 
record.
6. On such submission learned counsel for the 
applicant submits that there is no scientific evidence
and the shoes of the applicants sent for forensic 
analysis but without FSL report charge sheet has 
been filed in a very hurried manner.
7. Two aspects are relevant : first; if actually the 
applicant committed the offence why he has not fled 
from the place of occurrence and secondly why 
without FSL report charge sheet has been filed. As 
per the mandate of provision of law on completion of 
investigation charge sheet should be filed and if in 
absence of FSL charge sheet has been filed then 
whether such charge sheet can be treated to be a 
charge sheet.
8. Though all these aspects are the subject matter 
of the trial but the fact remains why the applicant 
after commission of crime was lying on the same cot 
where the body of the deceased was found. This is a 
very crucial aspect to be considered. 
9. The prosecution come up with a case that 
applicant was in a drunken condition and caused the 
incident in the same condition and that may be the 
reason that he has not fled from the place of the 
incident.
10. The I.O. is present in Court and submits that 
the FSL report is still awaited.
11. Put up this matter on 08.07.2026. On that day 
the State counsel shall get status of the FSL report 
and if it is completed, place the same before this 
Court.
