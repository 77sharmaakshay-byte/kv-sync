Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Vipul Sharma, learned counsel for the
petitioner. 
2. Mr. Yogesh Tiwari, learned Standing

3. Mr. Sudhir Kumar, learned counsel for 
respondent no.5.
4. Mr. Teerath Pal Singh, Addl. Chief 
Executive Officer, Smart City, Dehradun (the then 
Assistant Collector, Bazpur) and Mr. Rakesh 
Tiwari, Assistant Collector, Bazpur are present in 
person. 
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner/auction
purchaser would submit that the petitioner is the 
auction purchaser of the land in question in 
pursuance of an order passed by the Debt 
Recovery Tribunal, Delhi; that, the Court receiver 
was appointed and a direction was given to the 
Collector to provide assistance to the Court 
receiver for transfer of possession to the 
petitioner/auction purchaser; that, the Court 
receiver filed a report to the effect that hardly any 
assistance was provided by the Assistant Collector, 
Bazpur at that time rather he told the receiver that 
there is an influential person named Bubby and it 
is difficult to get the possession delivered to the 
petitioner/auction purchaser. 
6. Mr. Teerath Pal Singh, Addl. Chief 
Executive Officer, Smart City, Dehradun, who was 
the Assistant Collector, Bazpur on the relevant 
date and time would submit that revenue case 
No.22/16 of 2013-14 was filed by respondent no.5 
against the respondent no.4 in respect of the same 
land wherein status quo order had been passed on 
08.08.2014 by his predecessors. 7. To this, learned counsel for the 
petitioner/auction purchaser would submit that as 
per the Section 18 of the Recovery of Debts and 
Bankruptcy Act, 1993 “no court or other authority 
shall have, or be entitled to exercise, any 
jurisdiction, powers or authority in relation to the 
matters specified in section 17”. 
8. He would further submit that Section 34 of 
this Act also says that the provisions of this Act 
shall have effect notwithstanding anything 
inconsistent therewith contained in any other law, 
therefore, the proceedings of Case No.22/16 of 
2013-14 were not maintainable, therefore, if at all 
there was any status quo order that was of no legal 
consequence in view of the decree passed by the 
Debt Recovery Tribunal. 
9. Learned counsel for respondent no.5 would 
submit that Sections 18 and 34 of the Recovery of 
Debts and Bankruptcy Act, 1993 are not 
applicable in the present case. He prays for and is 
granted time to place law on this point. 
10. Mr. Teerathpal Singh, the then Assistant 
Collector, Bazpur is directed to file additional 
counter affidavit stating all the details in respect of 
the request made by the petitioner/auction 
purchaser or the receiver and the action taken on 
that request before the next date of listing. 
11. Put up on 21.03.2024.
