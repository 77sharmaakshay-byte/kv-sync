Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Nalin Saun, learned counsel for the Revisionist.
2. Proposed Revisionist was convicted under Section 138 of 
Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 and was sentenced to undergo 
simple imprisonment for a period of one year along with a fine of 
Rs.7,10,000/- vide judgment dated 22.10.2024, passed by the 
court of learned Judicial Magistrate, New Tehri, District Tehri 
Garhwal. An appeal (Criminal Appeal No.75 of 2024) was filed by 
the Revisionist. The said appeal has been dismissed vide judgment 
dated 31.10.2025, passed by the court of learned Additional 
Sessions Judge, Tehri Garhwal. Hence, the present criminal 
revision.
3. Heard learned counsel for the Revisionist on the
Exemption Application.
4. Admit.
5. Exemption application has been filed by the Revisionist 
with a prayer to exempt him to surrender before the court below.
6. Considering the overall facts and circumstances of the 
case, exemption application is allowed subject to the condition that 
the Revisionist shall deposit 30% of the fine imposed by the court 
of learned Judicial Magistrate, New Tehri, District Tehri Garhwal 
with the court concerned. 
7. Issue notice to the Respondent. Steps to be taken within a 
week. Counter affidavit to be filed within a period of three weeks.
8. List this matter on 08.01.2026.
