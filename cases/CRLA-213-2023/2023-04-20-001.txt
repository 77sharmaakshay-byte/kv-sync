This Criminal Appeal has been 
preferred against the judgment of 
conviction, whereby the appellant has been 
convicted for the offence under Section 306 
of IPC. The appeal has been preferred along 
with the Delay Condonation Application No. 
IA/1/2023, seeking condonation of 66 days 
delay.
Having heard the learned counsel 
for the parties on the Delay Condonation 
Application and having gone through the 
contents of the same; being satisfied with 
the reasons given for seeking condonation 
of delay, the Delay Condonation Application 
would stand allowed and the delay, which 
has chanced in filing the Appeal, would 
hereby stand condoned. 
With the consent of the learned 
counsel for the parties, the Appeal is heard 
on admission.
Admit the Appeal.
Summon the lower Court record.
List the Bail Application No. 
IA/2/2023, after the receipt of the records 
of the Court below.During the intervening period, the 
learned Government Advocate may file 
objection to the Bail Application.
