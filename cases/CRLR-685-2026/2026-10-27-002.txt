Mr. Tushar Chauhan, Advocate for the 
revisionist.
2. Mr. S.S. Chauhan, Deputy Advocate 
General for the State.
3. There is a time barred criminal revision.
4. The revisionist has filed the delay 
condonation application and seeks 
condonation of delay of 349 days in preferring 
the criminal revision. 
5. The ground for the delay has been stated 
in the affidavit accompanying the delay 
condonation application. In the said affidavit, 
it has been stated that there is delay of 349 
days in filing the revision which is neither 
deliberate nor wilful. It is further stated in 
the affidavit that it was only upon gaining 
knowledge of the execution proceedings 
initiated against him that the revisionist 
became aware of the serious legal 
consequences arising from the impugned 
order. Thereafter, the revisionist upon 
realizing the gravity of the matter and the 
necessity of challenging the said order has 
approached this Court seeking appropriate 
relief in accordance with law.
6. It is further mentioned in the affidavit that owing to the precarious financial 
condition of the revisionist he was not in a 
position to arrange the requisite professional 
fees for engaging an Advocate and
consequently was unable to challenge the said 
order immediately. Immediately upon 
arranging the requisite amount, the 
revisionist approached the learned counsel 
and took necessary steps for challenging the 
said order without any further undue delay. 
The delay, if any, in approaching this Court 
was thus neither intentional nor deliberate.
6. Notices were issued on delay 
condonation application and the office report 
dated 28.09.2026, states that as per the 
service report of learned C.J.M., Haridwar, 
respondent nos.2 to 4 personally served.
7. Despite of service, none has appeared on 
behalf of respondent nos.2 to 4.
8. The cause shown in the affidavit in 
support of the delay condonation application 
is sufficient and the delay of 349 days in 
preferring the revision deserves to be 
condoned. The delay condonation application 
(IA No.1 of 2026) is allowed. The revision 
shall be treated within time.
