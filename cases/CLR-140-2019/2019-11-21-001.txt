Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J. 
Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate for the revisionist. 
Mr. Neeraj Garg, Advocate for the respondent.
Heard.
Admit.
Summon the lower court record. 
No notices are required to be sent to the 
respondent as respondent is appearing through caveator. 
This is a regular revision under Section 25 of the 
Provincial Small Cause Courts Act, which is to be 
treated in permanence to the first appeal. 
In that view of the matter, there would be a stay of 
the impugned judgment subject to the condition that the 
revisionist pays the entire decreetal amount and 
continues to pay all damages as imposed by the 
impugned order.
Stay application no.15564 of 2019 stands disposed 
of.
