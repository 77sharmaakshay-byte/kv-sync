Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.

counsel for the revisionist and Mr. Neeraj 
Garg, learned counsel for the 
respondents.

revisionist submitted that the revisionist 
had deposited the entire decreetal

list this case on 21.02.2022.
Urgency Application (IA No.15565 
of 2021) stands disposed of accordingly.
