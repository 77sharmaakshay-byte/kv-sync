Hon’ble R.C. Khulbe, J.

Ms. Mamta Joshi, learned B.H. for the State.
Mr. Sandeep Kothari, learned counsel for the 
private respondent.
As per the impugned order dated 06.03.2020 
passed by the Judge, Family Court, Vikasnagar, 
Dehradun, the revisionist was directed to pay 
Rs.8,000/- per month as maintenance.
It is the submission of learned counsel for the 
respondent that the revisionist has failed to pay the 
maintenance allowance as awarded by the Judge, 
Family Court.
List on 13.01.2022 along with connected 
matter.
On that day, both the parties will remain 
present before the Court for mediation.
In the meantime, the revisionist will deposit 
the entire maintenance amount @ Rs.5,000/- per 
month by way of demand draft before the Court.
On the next date of hearing the revisionist 
husband will pay Rs.10,000/- to the respondent wife 
as travelling expenses.
