WPSS No.1473 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Anil Kumar Joshi, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Sachin Mohan Singh Mehta, learned Brief 
Holder for the State.
3. The petitioner has challenged the order dated 
31.05.2023 approved on 03.06.2023, which is annexed as 
Annexure no.4 to the writ petition, whereby respondent 
no.5 – Smt. Usha Sarkar was given appointment on the 
post of ‘Anganwadi Karyakarti’ for Anganwadi Centre, 
Gurugram-8, Tehsil - Sitarganj, District Udham Singh 
Nagar.
4. The main challenge of the petitioner as thrown in 
the writ petition is that the candidature of the petitioner has 
wrongly been rejected by the respondent and she has been 
deprived of participating in the selection process and 
consequently, she has been denied appointment/promotion 
on the post of ‘Anganwadi Karyakarti’ at the aforesaid
centre.
5. It has been pointed out by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that even the form submitted by respondent no.5 
for the aforesaid selection process was beyond the last date 
of filing of the application and therefore, it is urged that 
undue favour has been given to respondent no.5.
6. Learned counsel for the State prays for and is 
granted six weeks’ time for filing counter affidavit.
7. Further two weeks’ time is granted to the learned 
counsel for the petitioner for filing rejoinder affidavit.2
8. List this case on 31.10.2023.
