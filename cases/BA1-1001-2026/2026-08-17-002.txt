Mrs. Pushpa Joshi, learned Senior

2. Mr. B.C. Joshi, learned AGA for the 
State.
3. The instant bail application is moved 
on behalf of the applicant-Tinku, S/o 
Samay Singh seeking bail in reference to 
first information report dated 02.02.2026 
bearing Case Crime No.0065 of 2026, P.S. 
Bhagwanpur, District Haridwar wherein he 
is charge sheeted alongwith the other co￾accused for the offences punishable under 
Section 103(1), 109(1), 115(2), 190, 191(2),
191(3), 324(4), 326(cha), 333 and 3(5) of BNS, 
2023. 
4. Now in this bail application, a short 
term bail application is moved on the 
ground that twin female child were born on 
17.08.2025 and one already passed away on 
14.03.2026 and the jail authorities took the 
applicant in custody for last rites of his 
daughter and now the second child is
constantly suffering from respiratory 
distress including severe anemia Lower 
respiratory tract infection and sepsis since she was born due to pre mature delivery 
and needs constant medical attention, 
therefore, the present applicant be granted 
short term bail.
5. On the previous date the State was 
directed to verify the document as enclosed 
alongwith the short term bail application 
about the medical prescription of the new 
born baby and Mr. B.C. Joshi, learned AGA 
got instruction about such medical 
prescription and submits that the new born 
baby is immediately require medical 
attention.
6. In view of the reasons, as given in the 
short term bail application and taking into 
consideration that a new born baby is 
suffering from several medical problems 
and at this juncture the presence of the 
applicant is require to give better treatment 
to the new born baby, therefore, this Court 
is of the view that the applicant deserves 
for short term bail for six weeks to be 
computed from the date of his release. 
7. Accordingly, I.A. No.01 of 2026 
whereby the applicant is seeking short term 
bail, is allowed. 
8. Let the applicant-Tinku be released on 
short term bail for the period of six weeks 
to be reckoned from the date of his actual 
release on his executing a personal bond 
and furnishing two reliable sureties of the 
like amount to the satisfaction of the court 
concerned.
9. On expiry of the period of six weeks, 
the applicant shall surrender before the 
Superintendent of the jail concerned.10. So far as the regular bail is concerned, 
the respondent may file objection within 
three weeks. 
11. List this bail application after four 
weeks.
