COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
D-55 WPMS No.1679 of 2018
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
 Mr. Aditya Singh, Advocate for the petitioner. 
 Mr. Narain Dutt, Standing Counsel with Mr. 
Tarun Mohan, Brief Holder for the State. 
 Mr. T.A. Khan, Senior Advocate assisted by 
Mr. Vinay Kumar, Advocate for respondent 
nos.9, 10 and 11.
 At the very outset, learned Senior Counsel 
appearing for the respondent nos.9, 10 and 11 
submits that instant petition is arising out from 
the proceedings under Section 145 of the Code 
of Criminal Procedure, 1973. He submits that 
with regard to the same land earlier in the year 
1986-87, the petitioner had filed a suit under 
Section 209 of the U.P. Zamindari Abolition and 
Land Reforms Act, 1950 and that suit is still 
pending before the Assistant Collector First 
Class. He submits that with regard to the same 
property, C-482 No.632 of 2015 and WPMS No.
1523 of 2023 are also pending before this 
Court. He submits that in the interest of justice 
it would be expedient if all these petitions are 
decided together.
 Learned counsel for the petitioner raises 
doubt with regard to the identity of land. 
According to him, it is not possible that the land 
involved in the instant petition and the other 
petitions, as referred by the learned Senior 
Counsel for the respondent nos.9, 10 and 11
are one and the same. 
 Be it as it may, let Registry seek directions 
for listing all these petitions before one and the 
same Bench. 
 
(Ravindra Maithani, J.)
