COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
C482 No. 1236 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Aditya Singh and Mr. Naman 
Kamboj, learned counsel for the 
applicants.
2. Mr. V.S. Pal, learned A.G.A. for the 
State.
3. By the instant C482 application, the 
present applicant is challenging the 
proceedings of Criminal Case No. 40 of 
2023, arising out of Case Crime No. 27 of 
2021, State vs. Mohd. Firoz & another, 
pending in the court of Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Champawat, wherein the 
present applicant has been summoned to 
face the trial for the offences punishable 
under Section 66C and 66D of the 
Information Technology Act as well as for 
the offence punishable under Section 420 
IPC.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that the applicant has been falsely 
implicated and submits that the 
investigating agency in a mechanical 
manner submitted the charge sheet. He 
further submits that the investigating 
agency without examining that who is 
infact the actual modus operandi of the 
offence implicated the applicant because 
the product of the applicant’s company, ie. 
Online application “Pagar Book” which is 
widely and publically available on internet. 
He further submits that the product of the 
company is subscribed by third party and 
the platform of the company has been misused by the third party.
5. The present applicant is a young 
entrepreneur who is serving as a Director 
in Gyankaar Technologies Private Limited, 
a registered company under the 
Companies Act, 2013 and the said 
company has developed a mobile 
application, namely, “Pagar Book” which 
manages/provides solution to take staff 
attendance, manages staff salaries and 
payments. The said online application by 
way of taking subscription is being widely 
used by the public, particularly by the 
employer to transfer the salaries in the 
account of the employees.
6. Today, the applicant is also appears
through V.C. and this Court interact with 
him. In the year 2012, he passed 12th
examination with 87% marks and 
thereafter he qualified JEE Examination 
and completed B.Tech Engineering 
(Physics) from IIT Bombay.
7. Prima facie, I found substance on the 
submissions of the learned counsel for the 
applicant, however, all these aspects 
required to be considered after inviting 
counter affidavit from the respondents.
8. Issue notice to respondent no. 2 
returnable within three weeks.
9. Learned counsel for the applicant 
shall take steps to serve respondent no. 2 
within a week.
10. Respondents may file counter 
affidavit within four weeks. Two weeks 
thereafter is granted to the applicant to 
file rejoinder affidavit.
11. List this case on 27.08.2024.
12. Until further orders of this Court, 
further proceedings of Criminal Case No. 
40 of 2023, arising out of Case Crime No. 
27 of 2021, State vs. Mohd. Firoz & anr., 
pending in the court of Chief Judicial Magistrate, Champawat, District 
Champawat shall remain stayed qua the 
present applicant only.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
