COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 525 of 2017
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Nikhil Singhal, counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Arvind Vasistha, Senior Advocate assisted by Mr. 
Pankaj Kumar Sharma, counsel for the respondent no.1.
3. Counsel for the petitioner/plaintiff would submit that 
the S.C.C. Suit No. 12 of 2002 for arrears of rent, eviction 
and mense profit was filed by the petitioner/plaintiff, which 
was dismissed by Judge, S.C.C./Civil Judge (S.D.), Haridwar 
vide judgment and order dated 30.05.2011 inter alia on the 
ground that the provisions of U.P. Act No. 13 of 1972 are 
applicable over the property in question; that, the 
petitioner/plaintiff preferred Revision No. 21 of 2011 against 
the said judgment and order, which was also dismissed by 
the revisional court vide judgment and order dated 
26.10.2016. Hence, this petition. 
He would further submit that the revisional court 
without appreciating the facts and evidence on record, has 
dismissed the revision in a cursory manner; that, in a similar 
petition filed against the another tenant of adjoining shop 
situated in the same building, the decree of eviction has been 
passed, and same has been affirmed by the Hon’ble Apex 
Court.
4. In view of the above, admit the petition.
5. Senior counsel for the respondent/tenant would submit 
that he does not want to file counter affidavit/reply to the 
petition.
6. List this case on 30.07.2024 for final hearing.
(Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.)
