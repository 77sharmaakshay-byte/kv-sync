COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.2965 of 2023
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. B.S. Adhikari, Advocate for the 
petitioners.
Mr. Devendra Bohra, Standing 
Counsel for the State.
It is the claim of the petitioners that 
they had given certain land to the Tehri 
Hydro Development Corporation, but now, 
State Government is intending to allot 
certain land to the displaced persons, and, 
while doing that exercise, State authorities 
are encroaching upon the land, which is 
under the possession of the petitioners. 
Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that, in fact, in the year 
2020, a joint survey was conducted and 
lands have been demarcated, as given in 
Annexure No.8.
Let learned State Counsel get 
instructions as to what is the basis of the 
allotment. Are State authorities following 
the joint survey conducted in the year 
2020, which is Annexure No.8? And if not, 
how had they demarcated their own lands? 
List this matter on 20.10.2023, just 
after fresh cases. 
 (Ravindra Maithani J.)
