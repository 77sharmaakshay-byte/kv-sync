4. In the previous order dated 
26.03.2025 when both the parties were 
disputing the site plan/map furnished by 
them, then the District Magistrate, 
Haridwar was directed to examine both 
the maps with the assistance of Revenue 
Officials, who are well versed with the 
demarcation after physical inspection of 
the spot and submit the report which map 
is correct. Simultaneously, the District 
Magistrate was further directed that if he 
finds that both the maps are not correct 
then a correct map/site plan be prepared 
by giving reasons.
5. Now, in compliance thereof, District 
Magistrate, Haridwar filed his affidavit 
bringing on record the inspection report 
which was carried out in the presence of 
the officials of the BHEL as well as revenue 
department.
6. In this report members of the 
inspection team pointed out that map 
submitted by the BHEL is not correct. 
7. Mr. Yogesh Tiwari, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State pointed out earlier 
also the objection was filed on behalf of respondent no. 4 in which in para 11 a 
categorical statement has been made that 
in the site plan/ map everything which is 
existed on the spot are clearly shown and 
in order to check the site plan the Court 
can verify it by any agency. He submits 
that on the basis of this report the location 
of pump and sewerage line is correctly 
shown which is outside the area of the 
land which is earmarked for the displaced 
persons. He further submits that the 
location of the pump house and sewerage 
has been shown by respondent no. 4 in its 
site plan is outside the area which is 
earmarked for displaced persons. He 
submits that the attempt of giving 
reference of incorrect map is nothing but 
an attempt to mislead the Court.
8. Mr. V.K. Kohli, learned Senior 
Advocate for the petitioner again disputing 
the map furnished by the District 
Magistrate.
9. Mr. B.S. Adhikari, who is assisting 
counsel for the petitioner pointed out that 
while carrying out the inspection by the 
team deputed by the District Magistrate, 
strong objection was raised by the 
representative of the petitioner.
