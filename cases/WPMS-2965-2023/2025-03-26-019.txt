COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 2965 of 2023
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. V. K. Kohli, learned Sr. Advocate assisted by 
Mr. B.S. Adhikari, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Yogesh Tiwari, learned Standing Counsel for 
the State.
3. Mr. Rajendra Dobhal, learned Sr. Advocate 
assisted by Mr. Siddharath Sah, learned counsel for the 
private respondents. 
4. Mr. V.K. Kohli, learned Sr. Advocate for the 
BHEL submits that the land, which was subject matter 
of WPMS No. 1309 of 2010, is entirely different from 
the land, which is subject matter of the present writ 
petition. 
5. Two maps / site plans have furnished before this 
Court, one by BHEL, which is at page 531 of the paper 
book and next one is Annexure No. 1 to the affidavit 
filed by respondent no. 4 dated 20.06.2024, page 570 of 
the paper book. 
6. Respondents are disputing the map / site plan of 
the BHEL and the BHEL is also disputing the map /
site plan furnished by the State. 
7. Mr. V. K. Kohli, learned Sr. Advocate for the 
BHEL submits that in the land, which is earmarked for 
the allotment of displaced persons, there is pump 
house and sewer line of the BHEL. 
8. On the other side, Mr. Yogesh Tiwari, learned 
Standing Counsel for the State submits that as per the 
map / site plan prepared by the Assistant Engineer, 
Sub Division – III, Dehradun, (Infrastructure 
Rehabilitation Division, New Tehri), the pump house 
and the sewer line of the BHEL is completely outside 
the area which is earmarked for the displaced persons. 
9. Since both the parties are disputing the maps / 
site plans of each other, in such an eventuality, it is 
directed that District Magistrate, Haridwar with the 
assistance of Revenue Officials, who are well versed 
with the demarcation, shall examine both the maps / 
site plans, after physical inspection of the site and 
submit the report which map is correct. If the District Magistrate finds that both the maps are not correct, 
then a correct map / site plan be prepared. The District 
Magistrate, Haridwar shall also give reasons. The 
entire exercise shall be completed by the District 
Magistrate, Haridwar within three weeks and submit 
the report by way of an affidavit. 
10. List on 29.04.2025.
11. Interim order is extended till the next date of 
listing. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
