Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Ms. Rajni Rangwal and Mr. Ankush 
Singhal, Advocates for the petitioner.
2. Mr. V.S. Pal, A.G.A. for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition, 
petitioners seek quashing of FIR No. 89 of 2026, 
under Sections 22 and 8 of NDPS Act, 
registered at Police Station-Kelakhera, District 
Udham Singh Nagar.
4. As per the averments made in the FIR, one 
Nirmal Singh was arrested from the open space 
and from his possession, two strips of 8
capsules each and half strip of 3 capsules, total 
19 capsules of tramadole were recovered; the 
total weight of these capsules was 7315 m.g.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that the petitioner is owner of the medical shop 
and license and registration is also issued in his 
name. Learned counsel for the petitioner further 
submits that neither the person is arrested from 
his shop nor any search or seizure was 
conducted in his shop; that there is no proof 
that the petitioner sold those tablets to the 
person namely Nirmal Singh, who was arrested 
from the open space. Despite this fact, the petitioner has been made an accused and 
further his shop has also been sealed.
6. Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the record.
7. State was directed to seek instruction as 
to on what basis, medical shop of the petitioner 
has been sealed. Today, on instruction, learned 
State Counsel could not provide any 
satisfactory reason that under what provision, 
the shop of the petitioner was sealed.
8. This Court finds some substance in the 
submission made by learned counsel for the 
petitioner and having considered the 
submissions, this Court is of the view that this 
matter definitely requires deliberation.
9. Let counter-affidavit be filed by the 
respondents within three weeks.
10. List thereafter.
11. Till the next date of listing, as an interim 
measure, petitioner shall not be arrested in 
relation to the aforesaid FIR, provided, he co￾operates with the investigation.
12. Stay Application made therefor stands 
disposed of accordingly.
