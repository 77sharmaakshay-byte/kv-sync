2. By means of the present Writ Petition, 
the petitioner has sought quashing of First 
Information Report No. 39 of 2026, dated 
01.03.2026, registered at Police Station 
Basant Vihar, District Dehradun, for the 
offences punishable under Sections 316(2) 
and 318(4) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 
2023.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the complainant has 
mentioned the name of the petitioner in the 
FIR as one of the Directors of the company, 
namely, M/s Aadiguru Travel India Pvt. Ltd., 
Noida/Delhi. It is further submitted that the 
FIR was lodged by the complainant on 
28.02.2026, whereas the petitioner had 
already resigned from the position of 
Director of the said company on 31.10.2025. 
Her resignation was accepted and became 
effective from 01.11.2025. Thus, according 
to learned counsel for the petitioner, the 
petitioner was no longer a Director of the 
company prior to the alleged incident.
4. Learned State Counsel submits that, as 
mentioned in the FIR, the ticket in question 
was booked by the complainant.
5. Be that as it may.6. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
further submits that the other co-accused 
have already been granted interim protection 
by this Court.
7. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
8. This Court finds some substance in the 
submissions advanced by learned counsel 
for the petitioner. Having considered the 
submissions made and the material 
available on record, this Court is of the view 
that the matter requires consideration.
9. Let counter affidavit be filed by the 
respondents within three weeks.
10. Issue notice to respondent No. 3. Steps 
be taken within a week.
11. Connect with Writ Petition (Criminal) 
No. 815 of 2026.
12. Till the next date of listing, as an 
interim measure, the petitioner shall not be 
arrested in connection with the aforesaid 
FIR, provided she cooperates with the 
investigation.
13. The Stay Application stands disposed 
of accordingly.
