3. Admittedly the deceased employee served 
in the Department for 35 years though he was not 
regularized though was under the zone of 
consideration as he was shortlisted for the 
regularization, but unfortunately he died. Now the 
claim of the petitioner for compassionate 
appointed is rejected simply for the reason that the 
deceased employee was not regularized.
4. The rejection of claim of the petitioner for 
seeking compassionate appointment is wholly 
unjustified, particularly when, the deceased 
employee served in the Department for 35 years’ 
though he shortlisted for regularization and could 
not be regularized since before that he died, 
therefore, on this account alone the claim of the 
petitioner cannot be rejected. 
5. In such view of the matter, the respondents 
are directed to review the entire issue and apprise 
on the next date, failing which the concerned 
Authority shall join the proceedings through VC.
6. List this matter on 05.11.2026.
