COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPSB No.576 of 2024
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J.
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
1. Mr. K.P. Upadhyay, learned Senior 
Counsel assisted by Mr. Anil Kumar 
Joshi, learned counsel for petitioners.
2. Mr. Pooran Singh Bisht, learned 
Addl. C.S.C. for State.
3. Mr. M.C. Kandpal, learned Senior 
Counsel assisted by Mr. S.S. Chauhan, 
learned counsel for respondent nos.2 &
3. Petitioners are Diploma 
holder/Assistant Engineers serving in 
Uttarakhand Pey Jal Nigam. They have 
challenged Office Memo dated 
14.05.2024 issued by Chief Engineer, 
Head Quarter. By the said Office Memo, 
request made by Junior 
Engineers/Additional Assistant Engineers 
was accepted and a committee was 
constituted to look into the claim of 
Junior Engineers/Additional Assistant 
Engineers for registration of their B.Tech 
degree, obtained from the institution of 
Civil Engineers (Ludhiana) Punjab,
without prior permission of the 
competent authority in the Nigam.
4. Petitioners contend that for 
upgrading their qualification, persons 
appointed as Junior Engineer, have to 
seek permission from competent authority and if permission is not 
obtained while taking admission in an 
institution, such upgraded 
qualification/B.Tech degree cannot be 
considered for promotion against degree 
holder quota post of Assistant Engineer.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
submits that a person, having Diploma in 
Engineering, can be appointed as Junior 
Engineer in the Nigam and after 
completing requisite length of service as 
Junior Engineer, he can be considered for 
promotion to the post of Assistant 
Engineer on the criteria of seniority 
subject to rejection of unfit. He further 
submits that any one, who has obtained 
B.Tech degree after appointment as 
Junior Engineer, can be considered for 
promotion as Assistant Engineer against 
degree holder quota post provided he 
has obtained permission to pursue 
B.Tech Course from the competent 
authority. He further submits that under 
the Rules, separate 8.33% quota has 
been earmarked for promotion of degree 
holder Junior Engineers but only such 
Junior Engineers, who have obtained 
B.Tech degree with permission of 
departmental authority, alone can be 
considered for promotion against said 
quota, in view of provision contained in 
Regulation 5(II) of the Uttarakhand Pey 
Jal Sansadhan Vikas Evam Nirman Nigam 
Abhiyanta Sewa Niyamawali, 2011. He 
also relies upon circular dated 25.09.1984 issued by Chief Personnel 
Officer, U.P. Jal Nigam, Lucknow, in 
which it is provided that a Junior 
Engineer, if takes admission in Bachelor 
of Engineering Course or appears in 
AMIE Examination, without permission of 
appointing authority, shall be liable for 
disciplinary action and he will not be 
entitled to benefit of upgraded 
qualification, if acquired without 
permission from competent authority. He 
also refers to circular issued by 
Uttarakhand Pey Jal Nigam on 
05.08.2008 and submits that similar 
provision is made in the said circular 
also. 
6. Learned counsel for petitioner, thus, 
contends that impugned decision taken 
by Office Memo dated 14.05.2024 is 
contrary not only to the Service 
Regulations, but also to the circulars 
issued by U.P. Jal Nigam, and also 
Uttarakhand Pey Jal Nigam from time to 
time. He further submits that impugned 
decision, if not interfered with, will cause 
heart burning and prejudice to such 
Junior Engineers, who did not pursue 
B.Tech Course for want of permission 
from competent authority.
7. Per contra, Mr. S.S. Chauhan, 
learned counsel, appearing for Pey Jal 
Nigam submits that service Regulations 
were enforced only in 2011 and earlier 
there was no statutory provision, which 
prohibited consideration of B.Tech degree obtained by Junior Engineers 
during service without prior permission 
of the competent authority. He further 
submits that circular issued by 
Uttarakhand Pey Jal Nigam in 2008 was 
in respect of Degree obtained from 
Rajasthan Vidhyapeeth and it has no 
application to B.Tech degree obtained 
from other institutions. Matte requires 
scrutiny. 
8. Let notices be issued to respondent 
nos.5 to 14 returnable within four weeks.
9. Steps to be taken by 13.09.2024.
10. List this petition on 05.11.2024.
11. In the meantime, promotiona 
exercise may go on, however result of 
Junior Engineers/Additional Assistant 
Engineers, who obtained 
B.Tech/equivalent qualification without 
permission of the competent authority, 
shall be kept in a sealed cover and will 
not be declared without leave of this 
Court.
