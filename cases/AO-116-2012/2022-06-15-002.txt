Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Siddhartha Bisht, Advocate, for the 
appellant. 
In this Appeal from Order, the Coordinate 
Bench of this Court, while admitting the Appeal, 
had passed an interim order on 27th March, 2012, 
which is being sought to be extended through IA 
No.2463 of 2022, filed by the appellant on the 
ground that in view of the judgment of the Hon’ble 
Apex Court as reported in (2018) 16 SCC 299, 
Asian Refreshing of Road Agency Pvt. Ltd. Vs. 
C.B.I., the Tribunal is treating that the interim order 
would be deemed to have expired after the expiry of 
six months’ period from the date of the grant of the 
interim order. 
In the instant case, the respondents were 
noticed, but there is an office report only to the 
effect that respondent Nos. 1, 2 and 8 had been 
served with the notices. There is no report with 
regard to the service of notice qua the respondent 
Nos. 3 to 7. 
While extending the interim order to be made 
operational till the next date of listing, the Registry 
is directed to furnish a fresh report of service so far 
it relates to respondent Nos. 3 to 7. 
List this Appeal from Order, as soon as the 
service report is supply by the Registry. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
