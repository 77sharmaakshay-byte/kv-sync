Hon’ble V.K. Bist, J.
 Mr D.S. Patni, Advocate for the appellant. 
 Heard. 
 Admit the appeal. 
 Summon the lower court record. 
 Issue notice to the respondents returnable 
at an early date. 
 List after lower court record is received. 
(Stay Application No.2461 of 2012)
 Heard learned counsel for the appellant. 
 After considering the submission of 
learned counsel for the appellant, it is directed 
that execution of the award dated 23.01.2012 
passed by the Motor Accident Claim 
Tribunal/Additional District Judge/III Fast Track 
Court, Haridwar in M.A.C.P. No.10 of 2008, 
shall remain stayed provided the appellant 
deposits Rs.5,09,600/-within a period of six 
weeks from today. The amount already deposited 
under the statutory provisions shall be adjusted. 
 Registry is directed to remit the entire sum, 
deposited under the statutory provision, to the 
Tribunal concerned within two weeks from 
today. The amount so remitted by this Court as 
well as the amount deposited by the appellant in 
compliance of this order, shall be invested by the 
Tribunal in a fixed deposit so that it could earn 
interest and would be subject to further orders of 
this Court. 
 Stay application stands disposed of.
