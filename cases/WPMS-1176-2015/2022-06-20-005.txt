Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate for the

Mr. Sudhir Kumar, Advocate for the

Heard learned counsel for the 
parties. 
Learned counsel for the respondent 
prays for two more weeks’ time for filing 
objection to the delay condonation 
application as well as restoration 
application. 
Since respondent was granted time 
for filing objection to the aforesaid 
applications vide order dated 
09.12.2020, therefore, this Court is not 
inclined to grant any further time for this 
purpose. 
There is a delay of 552 days in filing 
restoration application.
Having heard, though I am inclined 
to allow the delay condonation, but it 
cannot be an unconditional order.
Subject to payment of cost of ` 1,000/- by the petitioner to 
Uttarakhand High Court Lawyers Welfare 
Fund, to be deposited within two weeks 
from today, the delay condonation 
application (CLMA No. 10475 of 2020) 
will stand allowed.
Also heard learned counsel for the 
parties on the restoration application
(MCC No. 642 of 2020). 
For the reasons mentioned in the 
restoration application, the application is 
disposed of and the order dated 
30.04.2019 passed by this Court is 
hereby recalled and the writ petition is 
restored to its original number. 
As prayed, six weeks’ & no more 
time is granted to learned counsel for 
the respondent for filing counter affidavit 
to the writ petition. 
List this matter on 04.08.2022.
