Mr. G.D. Joshi, learned Counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Addl. CSC for the State.
3. Petitioner applied for the post of Assistant Teacher, 
Primary School pursuant to advertisement dated 07.11.2025 
issued by respondent no.3-District Education Officer (Primary 
Education) Uttarkashi. Petitioner was selected and offered 
appointment by respondent no.3 on 26.01.2026. Petitioner was 
called upon to join the school as Assistant Teacher within a 
period of 15 days from the date of issuance of appointment 
order. However, due to illness, petitioner could not join and he 
sought extension of joining time given to him. Respondent no.3 
acceded to the request of petitioner for a further period of 7 days 
but due to continuance of illness in petitioner’s health, he could 
not join the school within the stipulated time. Petitioner 
approached respondent no.3 on 30.03.2026 along with medical 
documents with the request to permit him to join. 
4. It is submitted by learned Counsel for petitioner that 
instead of taking action on the application moved by petitioner 
for extension of joining time, matter was referred by respondent 
no.3 to respondent nos.1 and 2 and respondent no.3 is awaiting 
further orders from respondent nos.1 and 2.
4. Learned State Counsel is directed to seek instructions in 
the matter within three days. It is expected that respondents 
shall give joining to the petitioner at the earliest possible.
5. List on 17.08.2026 as fresh.
