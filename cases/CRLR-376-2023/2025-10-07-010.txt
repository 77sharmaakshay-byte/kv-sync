Mr. Ramji Shrivastava, learned counsel for the Revisionist. 
2. Mr. Vipul Painuly, learned A.G.A. for the State of Uttarakhand. 
3. Mr. Arvind Vashistha, learned Senior Advocate assisted by Mr. 
Hemant Singh Mahra, learned counsel for Respondent No. 2.
4. As per the order sheet dated 18.02.2025, the Coordinate Bench had
observed and recorded certain doubts regarding the basis on which the 
presence of a person named Azhar Abbas could be determined from the call 
detail records. It was further observed that the Investigating Officer had 
exonerated the said person solely on the basis of CCTV footage and call 
detail records.
5. On the next order sheet dated 20.02.2025, the Coordinate Bench had 
granted time to the learned State Counsel to furnish information in response 
to the queries raised in the order dated 18.02.2025, particularly as to how the 
CCTV footage and call detail records could form the basis for determining 
the presence of a person at the place of incident.
6. Learned State Counsel, however, submits that due to some 
misunderstanding, the wrong file was placed before the Court and, therefore, 
seeks further time to furnish the requisite information.
7. List this case on 14.10.2025.
8. Interim order, if any, shall remain extended till the next date of 
listing.
