4. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would submit that the present criminal 
revision has been preferred against the 
order dated 07.08.2026 passed by the 
learned Principal Judge, Family Court, 
Haridwar in Case No. 420 of 2024, 
instituted under Section 144(1) of the 
B.N.S.S. read with Section 7(f) of the 
Family Courts Act, whereby the learned 
Principal Judge, Family Court, Haridwar 
has allowed the application moved by 
respondent nos. 2 and 3 and directed the 
revisionist to pay interim maintenance of 
₹25,000/- per month to respondent no. 2 
and ₹15,000/- per month to respondent 
no. 3, who are the minor daughter and 
son, respectively, of the parties; that, ahus, a total amount of ₹40,000/- per month has 
been directed to be paid as interim 
maintenance from the date of filing of the 
application. It has further been directed 
that the entire arrears of maintenance 
shall be paid to the mother of respondent 
nos. 2 and 3 within a period of one month, 
and the matter has been fixed for 
29.09.2026.
5. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would further submit that, prior to passing 
of the impugned order, the court below had 
fixed interim maintenance at the rate of 
₹25,000/- per month, which amount has 
regularly been paid by the revisionist to the 
respondents. It is further submitted that, 
by the impugned order, the amount of 
interim maintenance has been enhanced 
substantially and the revisionist has also 
been directed to clear the entire arrears, 
which are stated to be more than 
₹8,00,000/-, within a period of one month.
6. She would submit that the revisionist 
is ready and willing to clear the entire 
arrears, but, having regard to his financial 
circumstances, some reasonable time may 
be granted for payment thereof. It is 
submitted that the revisionist is ready to 
deposit/pay a sum of ₹1,00,000/- towards 
the arrears on the date fixed before the 
court below and undertakes to pay the 
remaining arrears in reasonable 
instalments and the revisionist will 
regularly pay the enhanced amount of 
interim maintenance as directed by the 
court below.
7. Learned counsel appearing for 
respondent no. 2 would submit that the 
learned court below has rightly determined 
the amount of interim maintenance and 
that the revisionist is liable to comply with the impugned order. However, without 
prejudice to the rights and contentions of 
respondent no. 2, he submits that, if any 
indulgence is granted, the revisionist may 
be directed to pay a sum of ₹2,00,000/-
instead of ₹1,00,000/- towards the arrears 
on the date fixed, and the balance arrears 
may thereafter be permitted to be paid in 
instalments.
8. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the material available on 
record.
9. It is not in dispute that the arrears of 
interim maintenance payable pursuant to 
the impugned order are stated to be more 
than ₹8,00,000/-. It is also submitted on 
behalf of the revisionist that he is ready 
and willing to discharge the entire liability 
towards the arrears of interim 
maintenance. The grievance of the 
revisionist is confined to the time granted 
by the learned court below for payment of 
the entire arrears. The revisionist has 
expressed his willingness to pay a sum of 
₹1,00,000/- on the date fixed before the 
court below, i.e. 29.09.2026, and to pay 
the remaining arrears in instalments, 
besides regularly paying the enhanced 
interim maintenance of ₹40,000/- per 
month.
10. Having regard to the facts and 
circumstances of the case, and without 
expressing any opinion on the merits of the 
revision, some indulgence can be granted 
to the revisionist for clearing the 
accumulated arrears, subject to strict 
compliance with the following conditions:
(i) The revisionist shall pay/deposit a sum 
of ₹1,00,000/- towards the arrears of 
interim maintenance before the learned 
court below on or before 29.09.2026; (ii) The revisionist shall regularly pay the 
enhanced interim maintenance of 
₹40,000/- per month, as directed by the 
learned court below, on or before the 7th 
day of every English calendar month; (iii) 
The balance amount of arrears shall be 
paid by the revisionist in such instalments 
as determined by the learned court below 
on the date fixed, keeping in view the 
financial circumstances of the revisionist 
and the interest and welfare of the minor 
respondents.
11. Subject to the aforesaid conditions 
and the revisionist making payment 
strictly in terms thereof, the effect and 
operation of the impugned order dated 
07.08.2026, passed by the learned 
Principal Judge, Family Court, Haridwar, 
to the extent it directs payment of the 
entire arrears within a period of one 
month, shall remain stayed until further 
orders. It is, however, made clear that the 
revisionist shall continue to remain liable 
to pay the enhanced monthly interim 
maintenance of ₹40,000/- regularly and 
punctually in terms of the aforesaid order.
