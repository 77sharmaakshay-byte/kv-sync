Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Ahrar Baig, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. Pradeep Lohani, learned A.G.A. for 
the State.
3. Mr. Mohd. Matlub, learned counsel for 
respondent no.4.
4. By means of the present writ petition, 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution of 
India, the petitioners have prayed for issuance 
of a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
certiorari for quashing the First Information 
Report/Case Crime No. 0481 of 2026 dated 
17.09.2026, registered under Sections 308(6), 
351(1), 352 of the B.N.S., at Police Station 
Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar. The 
petitioners have further prayed for issuance of 
a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding the respondent 
authorities not to arrest him pursuant to the 
aforesaid F.I.R.
5. Learned counsel appearing for the 
petitioners would submit that the dispute in 
question arises out of matrimonial discord 
between the parties; that, petitioner no.1 is 
the married sister-in-law (Nanad), petitioner nos.2 and 3 are the father-in-law and mother￾in-law respectively, petitioner no.4 is the 
brother-in-law (Devar) and petitioner nos.5 
and 6 are the unmarried sisters-in-law 
(Nanad) of respondent no.4/complainant.
6. He would further submit that the 
petitioners are innocent and have been falsely
implicated in the instant case; that, even if 
the allegations contained in the impugned 
F.I.R. are taken at their face value and 
accepted in their entirety, the allegations, 
prima facie, relate to the matrimonial 
relationship between respondent 
no.4/complainant and her husband and do 
not disclose the commission of any offence by 
the present petitioners. He would further 
submit that the principal allegations are 
directed against petitioner no.1 and, by 
making general and omnibus allegations, the 
other family members have also been 
implicated in the present case.
7. He would further submit that the 
Hon’ble Supreme Court has repeatedly 
deprecated the practice of implicating the 
entire family of the husband in matrimonial 
disputes on the basis of general and omnibus 
allegations, without specific allegations 
constituting the ingredients of the alleged 
offences against each individual accused. He 
would further submit that the allegations 
made in the impugned F.I.R. are false, 
frivolous and vague and that petitioner no.1 is 
living separately with her husband, namely, 
Shahrukh. On these grounds, learned counsel 
for the petitioners submits that the impugned 
F.I.R. is liable to be quashed.
8. Learned counsel appearing for 
respondent no.4 would vehemently oppose the 
submissions advanced by learned counsel for the petitioners. However, he seeks some time 
to file a detailed counter affidavit.
9. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties and having perused the material 
available on record, it appears that the 
dispute arises out of matrimonial discord 
between the parties. Since learned counsel for 
respondent no.4 seeks time to file a counter 
affidavit, this Court considers it appropriate to 
grant an opportunity to the respondents to 
place their stand on record.
10. In the interest of justice, four weeks' 
time is granted to learned counsel for the 
State/respondents to file counter affidavit. 
Rejoinder affidavit, if any, may be filed within 
two weeks thereafter.
11. List thereafter.
12. In the aforesaid facts and circumstances, 
and without expressing any opinion on the 
merits of the case, as an interim measure, it is 
directed that, till the next date of listing, the 
petitioners shall not be arrested in connection 
with 0481 of 2026 registered at Police Station 
Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar, 
subject to the condition that the petitioners 
shall cooperate with the investigation.
11. Interim relief application stands disposed 
of accordingly.
