Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. B.M. Pingal, Advocate for the

Mr. P.C. Bisht, Addl. C.S.C. for the State of 
Uttarakhand.
On account of the fact, that while admitting 
the writ petition, the Coordinate Bench of this Court 
directed the respondents to file counter affidavit. 
But ever since 26.12.2020, no counter affidavit was 
filed and when the matter was taken up on 
28.10.2021, it was informed by the Government 
Advocate that the Member Secretary/District 
Assistant Registrar, Cooperative Societies, Tehri 
Garhwal, to whom the correspondence was already 
made from the office of Chief Standing Counsel 
had not yet responded and provided the narratives.
Hence, by the previous order dated 28.10.2021 the 
Member Secretary/District Assistant Registrar, 
Cooperative Societies, Tehri Garhwal was directed 
to appear in person.
Mr. Subhash Chandra Gahtori, District 
Assistant Registrar, Cooperative Societies, Tehri 
Garhwal, is present in person in the Court today.
The learned Additional Chief Standing 
Counsel submits that in fact the present Officer, 
who is now holding the office of the District 
Assistant Registrar Cooperative Societies; in fact 
was not in the helm of affairs of this office, at the 
time when the counter was called by the earlier 
order of 22.12.2020. But, however in furtherance of 
an order of 28.10.2021 respondent no.5, has filed 
the counter affidavit, which is listed today for 
orders. Counter affidavit filed there I.A. No.11259 
of 2021 alongwith the Misc. Appl. No.11258 of 
2021 would stand allowed. The counter affidavit is 
taken on record.
Learned counsel for the petitioner prays for 
and is granted three weeks time to file rejoinder affidavit.
List thereafter. 
Personal appearance of the Member 
Secretary/District Assistant Registrar, Cooperative 
Societies, Tehri Garhwal District Assistant 
Registrar would remain exempted unless required 
in future. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.) 15.11.2021
