Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Akshay Pradhan, learned counsel for 
the revisionist.
2. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned D.A.G. 
alongwith Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned A.G.A. for

3. On the last occasion, the Coordinate 
Bench of this Court directed the revisionist to 
file an affidavit to the effect that as and when 
respondent no. 2 is released from jail, then 
the custody of the minor child would be 
handed over to respondent no. 2 by the 
revisionist.
4. Learned counsel for the revisionist would 
submit that further one week’s time be 
granted to him to file the affidavit in this 
regard. Time so prayed is granted to him.
5. Today, respondent no. 2 is present 
through video conferencing from the office of 
DCPU, Haridwar. The Court interacted with 
her and she has expressed her desire that she 
be permitted to meet her minor child every 
fortnight.
6. Considering the fact that child is only 
one and a half years old and needs care of his 
mother also, therefore, this Court thinks it 
appropriate and directs that the revisionist 
shall bring the child to the office of DCPU, 
Haridwar every fortnight. Superintendant, 
Sub-Jail, Roorkee is directed to ensure that 
respondent no. 2 (Smt. Rachna) be brought to 
the office of DCPU, Haridwar every fortnight.7. The dates of the visits would be 
intimated by the Chairman, DCPU, Haridwar 
to the Superintendant, Jail Authority as well 
as to the revisionist.
8. List this matter on 29.07.2026.
9. Interim order is extended till the next 
date of listing.
