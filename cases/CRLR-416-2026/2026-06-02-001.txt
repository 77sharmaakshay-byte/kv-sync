CRLR No. 416 of 2026
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Akshay Pradhan, learned counsel 
for the revisionist.
2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A for 
the State/respondent no.1.
3. The revisionist is challenging the order 
dated 13.05.2026 and 16.05.2026 passed by 
learned IInd Additional Civil 
Judge(J.D.)/Judicial Magistrate Roorkee, 
District Haridwar whereby child of one year 
and three months old is ordered to be 
admitted to prison with his mother. It is 
submitted by learned counsel for the 
revisionist that the revisionist being 
grandfather of the child is taking due care of 
child, therefore, it is not in the interest of 
child to admit him to be in prison with 
mother.
4. Let the revisionist along with the child 
and his wife may appear before the 
Chairman of the Child Welfare Committee 
Haridwar, tomorrow at 11.00 a.m., who will 
interact with the revisionist and his wife in 
order to assess the mind of the child then 
apprise to this Court that whether admitting 
the child in prison with the mother is in the 
interest of the child or not.
5. Thereafter, the Chairman shall join 
proceeding through V.C. in the presence of 
revisionist and his wife and child. The 
concerned Jail Authority shall also facilitate the respondent no. 2 - Smt. Rachna to join 
proceeding through V.C. at 1:00 p.m.
6. List tomorrow at 01.00 p.m. 
7. Till then, Mr. Tumul Nainwal learned 
AGA shall inform to the concerned police 
officials not to act upon the order impugned. 
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
