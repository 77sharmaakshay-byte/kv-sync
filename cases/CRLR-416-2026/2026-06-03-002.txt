COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLR No.416 of 2026
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Akshay Pradhan, learned counsel 
for the revisionist. 
2. Mr. Tumul Nailwal, learned AGA for the 
State. 
3. The Chairman of the Child Welfare 
Committee, Haridwar joined the proceedings 
through video conferencing along with the 
revisionist and the child as well as the 
mother who is in prison. He apprised to this 
Court that the mother of the child is 
agreeable that she may be permitted to 
meet or interact through video conferencing 
with the child every week and after being 
released on bail, permanent custody of the 
child be given to her.
4. When specific query was made from 
the revisionist, he strictly refused to hand 
over the custody of the child to the mother 
after being released on bail. Such an 
attitude of the revisionist cannot be 
accepted since the respondent Smt. Rachna 
is mother of the child, therefore, the child 
cannot be deprived to get love and affection 
from his mother.
5. At this juncture, the respondents 
submit that till such time she is in prison, 
she may be permitted to interact with the child through video conferencing but 
simultaneously the revisionist has to give an 
undertaking that as and when the revisionist 
shall be released on bail, the custody of the 
child be handed over to the respondent 
mother.
6. Let an affidavit be filed by the 
revisionist positively by 09.06.2026.
7. Put up on 10.06.2026 as fresh.
8. On 10.06.2026, all of them again with 
the Chairman of the Child Welfare 
Committee, Haridwar shall join the 
proceeding through video conferencing.
9. Till then, the revisionist shall not be 
compelled to give the custody of his child to 
the mother, who is in prison. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.) 03.06.2026 Shiv/
