4. Learned counsel appearing for 
respondent no.2 would submit that, on 
03.06.2026, before the Coordinate Bench 
of this Court, the revisionist had 
undertaken that, as and when 
respondent no.2, being the mother of the 
minor child, was released from custody, 
he would hand over the custody of the 
minor child to respondent no.2. He would 
further submit that respondent no.2 has 
since been released on bail on 
29.07.2026; however, despite the 
aforesaid undertaking, the revisionist has 
not yet handed over the custody of the 
minor child to respondent no.2.
5. Per contra, learned counsel appearing for the revisionist would 
submit that the revisionist is ready and 
willing to comply with the undertaking 
given before the Court. He would further 
seek two weeks’ time to obtain 
instructions and to ascertain whether the 
custody of the minor child has been 
handed over to respondent no.2 and 
necessary efforts shall be made by him to 
ensure compliance with the undertaking 
given before the Court.
6. Having regard to the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
parties and in the interest of justice, two 
weeks’ time, as prayed for by learned 
counsel for the revisionist, is granted to 
obtain instructions and take necessary 
steps in terms of the undertaking given 
before the Court.
7. List this case on 15.10.2026.
