Ms. Divya Jain, learned counsel for the applicant, in

for the C.B.I.
5. The Co-ordinate Bench of this Court, vide order 
dated 29.07.2026, granted interim bail to the applicant for 
a period of one month, directing the applicant-Ramnath 
Gupta and applicant-Urmila Bisht to surrender before the 
court concerned, failing which warrant would be issued 
against them.
6. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that the 
interim bail granted to the applicants was extended from 
time to time and that the applicants are still enjoying the 
benefit of interim bail.
7. Learned Senior Advocate appearing for the CBI 
submits that, in the meantime, the matter was referred to 2
the CBI for further investigation and even after further 
investigation, charge-sheet has been submitted against 
eighteen persons, which does not include the applicants. 
However, the charge-sheet narrates that the investigation 
in respect of the other accused persons/suspects is still 
continuing.
8. In the opinion of this Court, in a crime of such 
magnitude having serious implications, the interim bail 
cannot be allowed to remain pending for such a long 
period.
9. In view of the aforesaid, the interim bail granted to 
the applicants is not extended, and the applicants shall 
surrender before the court concerned immediately.
10. List these cases on 09.12.2026.
