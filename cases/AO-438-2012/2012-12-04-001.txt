 Heard learned counsel for the appellant as well as learned 
counsel for the Insurance Company, Mr. B.K.Gupta, Advocate on the 
application under Order 41, Rule 27 C.P.C. 
 By means of this application (No. 9179 of 2012), the 
appellant has filed Form No. 54 issued by the Licensing Authority 
concerned (paper no. 39 on the memo of appeal) and has prayed that 
this document may be taken on record as additional evidence. 
 Learned counsel for the appellant has contended that the 
proposed document was not available to the appellant when the claim 
petition was being heard by the Motor Accident Claims 
Tribunal/District Judge, Pauri Garhwal. Learned counsel further 
submitted that for a just decision of Issue No.4 framed by the learned 
Tribunal, the appellant may be permitted to prove the said document in 
appeal. 
 Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the Insurance 
Company has vehemently contended that the proposed document by 
itself cannot be read in evidence unless it is duly proved by the 
appellant and that it is not an original public document. Learned 
counsel further submitted that in case this Court is of the view that this 
document be taken on record to be proved by the appellant, then 
opportunity of rebuttal may also be granted to the Insurance Company. 
 I have gone through the material on record. 2
 The learned Tribunal had framed Issue No.4 as under:- 
 “Whether the driver of the said vehicle had valid and 
effective driving licence” 
 The learned Tribunal while deciding Issue No.4 has 
observed that the owner of the vehicle has not filed any oral or 
documentary evidence to show that the driver of the vehicle was 
having a valid and effective driving licence. Consequently, the learned 
Tribunal has held that it is not proved that the driver was having a 
valid and effective driving licence at the time of accident. 
 Having heard the submissions of the learned counsel for 
the parties and the finding recorded by the learned Tribunal on Issue 
No.4 and to meet the ends of justice, this Court is of the view that for a 
just decision of this appeal, the document sought to be taken on record 
by the appellant be taken on record subject to payment of adequate 
cost and the appellant/owner of the vehicle may be given an 
opportunity to prove this document before the learned Tribunal. It 
would be in the fitness of things that the finding as recorded by the 
learned Tribunal on Issue No. 4 be set aside and the Issue No.4 be 
remitted to the learned Tribunal for decision afresh. The learned 
Tribunal shall permit the owner of the vehicle/appellant herein to 
prove this document by leading evidence and the Insurance Company 
shall have a right of rebuttal by leading its evidence, if any, before the 
learned Tribunal. 
 Accordingly, the application (No. 9179 of 2012) filed 
under Order 41, Rule 27 C.P.C. is allowed subject to payment of costs 
of Rs. 3,000/-(three thousand). The cost be paid within a period of two 
weeks to the State Legal Services Authority for being used for 
mediation purpose. On payment of cost, the document (paper no. 39 
on the record of memo of appeal) filed by the appellant is taken on 
record. The finding recorded by the learned Tribunal on Issue No. 4 is 
set aside and the Issue No. 4 is remitted to the learned Tribunal to 
record its finding afresh after affording reasonable opportunity of 
leading evidence to the appellant/owner of the vehicle and the 3
insurance company shall have right to rebut the same by documentary 
as well as oral evidence. The learned Tribunal shall record its finding 
on Issue No.4 and shall send the original record along with its finding 
on Issue No.4 to this Court within a period of three months from the 
date of receipt of the original record. The registry is directed to send 
the original record to the learned Tribunal along with copy of this 
order for compliance. 
 List this appeal along with A.O. 264 of 2012 and AO No. 
141 of 2012 after the finding on Issue No.4 along with the original 
record is received, for orders.
