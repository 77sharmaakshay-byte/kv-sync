17.04.2026 WPMS No.966 of 2026
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
In Original Suit No.6 of 2018, 
“Swami Omkaranand Saraswati 
Charitable Trust vs. Shri Darshan 
Mahavidyalaya Trust and Another”, the 
plaintiff- respondent had filed an 
Application under Order XXVI Rule 9 of 
the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908. The 
said Application was rejected by the trial 
court. A Civil Revision (No.1 of 2026, 
“Swami Omkaranand Saraswati 
Charitable Trust vs. Shri Darshan 
Mahavidyalaya Trust and Another”) was 
filed by the plaintiff- respondent. 
Learned Additional District Judge, Tehri 
Garhwal has allowed the revision vide 
impugned judgment dated 07.04.2026.
2. Heard Mr. Vikas Bahuguna, learned 
counsel for the petitioners.
3. Mr. Vikas Bahuguna, Advocate, has 
contended that the original suit has 
been filed for specific performance of 
contract and for perpetual injunction. 
Issues have already been framed. The 
issues raised in the original suit are to be adjudicated upon considering the 
validity of the agreement to sell, the 
intention of the parties and the law of 
limitation. Therefore, conducting a local 
investigation in the original suit is 
neither requisite nor proper.
4. Admit.
5. Notice is being issued to the 
respondent. Steps to be taken within a 
week.
6. List on 21.05.2026. Till then, the 
effect and operation of the impugned 
judgment dated 07.04.2026 are stayed. 
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
