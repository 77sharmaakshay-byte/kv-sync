AO No.499 of 2014
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Deepak Rawat, Advocate for the appellant.
2. Mr. T.K. Arya, Advocate holding brief of Mr. Devesh 
Upreti, Advocate for the respondents-claimants.
3. Learned counsel for the appellant mainly contends on
the merit of the appeal that in a claim petition which has 
been made by the respondents-claimants under Section 
163A of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, the compensation 
should be decided on the basis of the structured formula
as appended to the Second Schedule to Section 163A of 
the Motor Vehicles Act; the learned Tribunal has 
committed an illegality while assessing the compensation 
on the basis of income above Rs.40,000/- per annum that 
is against the schedule. To buttress his submission, he 
supplied the following case laws:-
(i) Deepal Girishbhai Soni and others vs. United 
Indian Insurance Co. Ltd., Baroda, (2004) 5 SCC 385;
(ii) United India Insurance Company Ltd. vs. Sunil 
Kumar and another, (2019) 12 SCC 398.
4. Learned counsel for the respondents prays for and is 
granted two weeks’ time to prepare to rebutt the 
arguments as advanced by the learned counsel for the 
appellant.
5. List this case on 23.09.2025.
