Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Gaurav Singh, learned counsel for 
the petitioners.
2. Mr. Pradeep Lohani, learned A.G.A. 
for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution 
of India, the petitioners have prayed for 
issuance of a writ in the nature of 
certiorari for quashing the First 
Information Report/Case Crime No. 142 of 
2026 dated 02.06.2026, registered under 
Sections 318(4), 336(3), 338, 340(2) and 
64(2) of B.N.S., 2023 at Police Station 
Bahadrabad, District Haridwar. The 
petitioners have further sought issuance of 
a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding the respondent 
authorities not to arrest the petitioner 
pursuant to the aforesaid F.I.R.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that, according to the 
allegations contained in the impugned F.I.R., the petitioners, along with other co￾accused persons, are alleged to have 
committed fraud and misappropriation in 
respect of the properties and bank deposits 
of respondent no.3/complainant, allegedly 
in connivance with certain employees of 
the office of the Sub-Registrar and the 
concerned bank.
5. Learned counsel would, however, 
submit that the petitioners are innocent 
and have falsely been implicated in the 
present case; that, petitioner no.1 and 
respondent no.3/complainant were in a 
live-in relationship and that respondent 
no.3 himself acknowledged and 
represented petitioner no.1 as his wife in 
various documents, copies whereof have 
been annexed with the present writ 
petition; that, respondent no.3 had also 
opened and operated a joint bank account 
with petitioner no.1 and had consistently 
accorded her the status of his wife, as 
reflected from the documents brought on 
record.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would further submit that respondent no.3 
voluntarily executed a registered gift deed 
in favour of petitioner no.1, whereby he 
transferred/relinquished his share in the 
property in her favour. It is further 
contended that the allegations regarding 
the alleged forgery of two sale deeds are 
contrary to the material available on 
record, inasmuch as respondent no.3 
himself executed a registered sale deed in 
respect of the property situated at Tehsil Roorkee, District Haridwar, in favour of 
petitioner no.2 before the office of the Sub￾Registrar, Roorkee, District Haridwar.
7. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would further submit that respondent no.3 
had also transferred his half share in a flat 
situated at Ghaziabad in favour of 
petitioner no.1 by executing a duly 
registered gift deed before the office of the 
Sub-Registrar, Ghaziabad. It is submitted 
that neither the aforesaid registered 
instruments nor the said gift deed has, till 
date, been cancelled, set aside or declared 
invalid by any competent court of law; 
that, a bare perusal of the allegations 
contained in the impugned F.I.R. reveals 
that the dispute essentially arises out of 
transactions of a civil nature, which have 
been given a criminal colour by lodging the 
present F.I.R.; that, the allegations levelled 
in the impugned F.I.R. are wholly 
unfounded and, even if taken at their face 
value, do not prima facie disclose the 
commission of the offences alleged against 
the petitioners, therefore, the impugned 
F.I.R. is liable to be quashed.
8. Having heard learned counsel for the 
petitioners and upon perusal of the 
material available on record, this Court 
finds that the controversy between the 
parties primarily pertains to property 
transactions and the validity of registered 
instruments, which have not been 
cancelled or declared invalid by any 
competent court of law. Whether the 
allegations disclose the commission of cognizable offences against the petitioners 
requires consideration in the light of the 
material collected during investigation. 
Accordingly, the matter requires 
consideration.
9. Issue notice to respondent no.3, 
returnable at an early date.
10. Steps to be taken within one week.
11. List after service report is received.
12. Meanwhile, counter-affidavit be filed 
by the respondents within four weeks.
13. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties, till the next date of listing, as an 
interim measure, the petitioners shall not 
be arrested in connection with the 
F.I.R./Case Crime No.142 of 2026 
registered at Police Station Bahadrabad, 
District Haridwar, provided the petitioners 
cooperate with the investigation.
14. Stay Application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
