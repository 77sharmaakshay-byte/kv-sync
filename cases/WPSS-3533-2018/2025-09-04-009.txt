COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
Review Appl. No. 15967 of 2021
In
WPSS No. 3533 of 2018
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
(Through V.C.)
1. Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, learned Senior 
Advocate assisted by Mr. Chandra Shekhar 
Dalakoti, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, Mr. I.P. Kohli 
learned Standing Counsel and Mr. 
Hargovind Pant, learned Brief Holder for 
the State.
3. Review application being MCC No. 
15967 of 2021 has been filed by the 
State for review of the judgment and 
order dated 13.01.2021 by which the 
Coordinate Bench allowed the writ 
petition in terms of the judgment of 
Hon’ble Apex Court in the case of Netram 
Sahu vs. Chattisgarh and another, 
(2018) 5 SCC 430. Now, the review has 
been sought on the ground that in the 
counter affidavit, filed by respondent no. 
3, there is no such averment that the 
case of the petitioner is covered by the 
aforesaid judgment, even, in respect of 
the observation given in para 10 of the 
judgment under review there was no 
such statement in the counter affidavit.
4. Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, learned Senior 
Advocate fairly admits this fact that 
there is no such averment in the counter 
affidavit of respondent no. 3.
5. In such view of the matter since 
there is no such averment in the counter affidavit reference of which has been 
given in para 9 and 10 of the judgment 
under review, therefore, judgment under 
review is reviewed and recalled. The writ 
petition is restored and the matter is 
again posted for hearing on 29.10.2025.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
