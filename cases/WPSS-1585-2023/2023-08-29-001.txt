5. By means of this writ petition, the 
petitioner has challenged the order dated 
28.03.2023, by which the representation of 
the petitioner filed pursuant to the order 
passed by this Court in Special Appeal No. 
481 of 2014, Vinay Upadhyay Vs. State of 
Uttarakhand, dated 02.01.2023, has been 
rejected. 
6. It is submitted by learned counsel for 
the petitioner that the representation has 
been decided by the respondent in a very 
cursory manner without giving any heed to 
the grievance of the petitioner and 
therefore, the same is highly arbitrary and 
against the law. 
7. Learned State Counsel prayed for and 
is granted eight weeks’ time for counter 
affidavit. Two weeks’ time thereafter is 
granted for filing rejoinder affidavit.
