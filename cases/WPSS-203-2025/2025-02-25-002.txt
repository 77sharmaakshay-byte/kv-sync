 Heard.
 It is the case of the petitioner that he 
had applied for his appointment for the 
post of Assistant Teacher (LT) in EWS 
Orphan Category. There were 05 seats 
reserved for this category. But, the 
respondents have recommended for one 
appointment under the Orphan Category 
and the name of the petitioner has not 
been considered.
 Learned counsel for the respondent 
no.2 would submit that the petitioner had 
applied under EWS Orphan Category. 
Under General Category 04 seats were 
reserved for Orphan Category, but no 
candidate has applied in that Category, 
therefore, they were given to General 
candidates. He admits that EWS is a 
Vertical Reservation Category. 
 The petitioner has admittedly applied 
under EWS Orphan Category. 
 Learned counsel for the respondent 
no.2 would submit that there is no such 
sub-category under EWS as Orphan
Category. 
 The question that will fall for discussion is that, if the petitioner had 
secured qualifying marks, why the 
petitioner could not have been considered 
under General Category reserved for 
Orphans?
 Admit.
 Learned Chief Standing Counsel takes 
notices on behalf of respondent nos.1, 3 
and 4.
 Mr. B.D. Pandey, Advocate takes notice 
on behalf of respondent no.2.
 Counter affidavit may be filed within 
four weeks.
 Two weeks thereafter, rejoinder 
affidavit, if any, may be filed.

 Interim Relief Application (IA) No.01 of
