Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Anil K Bisht, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. N.S. Pundir, D.A.G. for the State.
The Director of Elementary Education, 
Uttarakhand, joined the proceedings 
through video conferencing.
He submits that in the counselling 
held in Dehradun for appointment of the 
petitioner in the District Champawat, some 
suspicion was raised with regard to the 
documents that were submitted by the 
petitioner. Therefore, they were sent for 
verification and it was revealed that the 
petitioner had obtained one degree from 
Uttar Pradesh based on his permanent 
resident certificate of Pilibhit. He had 
obtained another permanent resident 
certificate of State of Uttarakhand. In view 
of this anomaly, the verification is still 
pending. He submits that the petitioner had 
also appeared for counselling in District 
Almora, and he was offered the 
appointment in Almora on 06.11.2024, and 
he was asked to give an undertaking that in 
case any plea with regard to document is 
found to be false, it would be his 
responsibility, but it is told by the Director 
of Elementary Education, Uttarakhand, 
that the petitioner had not given any 
undertaking and had not joined in Almora.
The Court wanted to know as to what 
is the mode of communicating the offer of 
appointment to the petitioner in District 
Almora? He seeks time to verify it.
List this matter on 11.08.2025, just after fresh cases.
Learned State Counsel may get 
instructions on the mode by which the 
appointment offer was given to the 
petitioner for his appointment in District 
Almora. 
The Director of Elementary Education, 
Uttarakhand, is not required to join the 
proceedings personally anymore.
 (Ravindra Maithani J.)
