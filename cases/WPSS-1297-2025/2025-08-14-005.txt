In this matter, Director, Elementary 
Education had appeared in the Court and 
had submitted that the petitioner was 
offered an appointment on the post of 
Assistant Teacher, in District Almora.
On 07.08.2025, learned State counsel 
was required to get instruction as to by 
which mode such appointment was offered.
Learned State counsel was not in a 
position to tell the Court as to whether any 
appointment was ever offered to the 
petitioner or not and if so, what was its
mode.
This Court leaves it at it. This issue 
may be considered at a subsequent stage.
Heard
Learned C.S.C. takes notice on behalf 
of the respondents.
Respondents may file counter 
affidavit within four weeks. Two weeks 
thereafter, rejoinder affidavit, if any, may 
be filed.
