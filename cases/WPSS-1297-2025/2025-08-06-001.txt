Mr. N.S. Pundir, D.A.G. for the State.
It is the case of the petitioner that in 
the year 2024, District Education Officer 
(Elementary Education) of all the Districts 
in the State of Uttarakhand had invited 
applications for appointment to the post of 
Assistant Teacher, Basic School. She 
appeared in the fifth round of counselling 
for District Champawat, which was held in 
Dehradun on 20.12.2024, but since then, 
she has not been given any communication 
by the Director of Elementary Education, 
Uttarakhand. The petitioner made a 
representation on 02.06.2025 to the 
District Education Officer (Elementary 
Education), Champawat, which is Annexure 
NO.5 to the writ petition, but no response is 
given to her.
Let the Director of Elementary 
Education, Uttarakhand, join the 
proceedings of this Court tomorrow at 2:15 
PM. He should explain the Court as to why 
despite counselling having been done on 
20.12.2024, the petitioner has not been 
informed as to what happened in the 
matter; why is this silence and inaction; 
and who is responsible for it. 
Let a copy of this order be also 
forwarded to the Secretary, Elementary 
Education, State of Uttarakhand. 
Learned State Counsel is requested to 
communicate this order to the concerned 
authorities and also brief them as to what is the issue pending before this Court.
List this matter on 07.08.2025, at 
2:15 PM.
