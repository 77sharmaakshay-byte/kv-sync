5. This is the writ petition filed by the 
petitioner against the order dated 18.03.2024 
passed by Prescribed Authority/A.D.M. (Finance 
and Revenue), Udham Singh Nagar and the order 
dated 03.05.2024 passed by Consolidation 
Officer, Kichha, Udham Singh Nagar.
6. Learned counsel for respondent no.7 would 
raise preliminary objection to the maintainability 
of the writ petition on the ground that against the 
impugned orders petitioner has a remedy of 
appeal under Section 12 of the U.P. 
Consolidation of Holdings Act, 1953.
7. Learned senior counsel appearing for the 
petitioner and learned counsel for respondent 
no.6 would fairly admit that the impugned orders 
are appealable u/s 12 of the U.P. Consolidation of 
Holdings Act, 1953 but said remedy is not 
efficacious as the authorities at the helm of 
affairs are hand in gloves with respondent no.7. 
8. In view of the above, let counter affidavit 
be filed by the appearing respondents within six weeks.
9. List on 12.09.2024 for admission/orders.
10. Till then, parties shall maintain status quo
over the property in dispute.
