Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Ms. Ananya Jain, learned counsel holding 
the brief of Mr. Sandeep Kothari, learned 
counsel for the appellant.
2. Mr. Deepak Bhardwaj, learned Brief Holder 
for the State.
3. The instant appeal was already admitted on 
25.03.2026 and the trial court record was 
summoned, now received and paper book is also 
prepared.
4. Heard on bail application IA No. 1 of 2026.
5. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
appellant that the appellant is convicted by the 
trial court for the offence punishable under 
Section 7 read with Section 8 of the POCSO Act 
read with Section 75 of BNS 2023 with the 
maximum sentence of 3 years rigorous 
imprisonment and fine of Rs. 5000/-.
6. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
appellant that the appellant was arrested in this 
case on 05.04.2025 and since then he is in jail and, 
as such, already served about 1 years and 5
months, therefore be released on bail.
7. On the other side Mr. Deepak Bhardwaj, 
learned Brief Holder for the State have not 
disputed that the appellant already served more 
than 1 year and 5 months of the sentence and the maximum sentence as awarded by the Trial 
Court is 3 years.
8. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties and taking into consideration that the 
appellant has already served more than 1 year 
and 5 month, this Court is of the view that the 
appellant deserves for bail during the pendency 
of the instant appeal.
9. Accordingly, without expressing any 
opinion on the merit of the case the bail 
application is allowed.
10. Let the appellant Pankaj Singh be released 
on bail, during the pendency of the instant 
criminal appeal, on is executing a personal bond 
and furnishing two reliable sureties of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the court concerned.
11. List this case in the month of November 
2026 for final hearing.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
