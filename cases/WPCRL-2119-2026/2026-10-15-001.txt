Mr. Raj Kumar Singh, learned Counsel for petitioners. 
2. Mr. Vijay Khanduri, learned B.H. for State.
3. Petitioners have impugned the FIR No.503 of 2026 
dated 02.10.2026 registered u/s 2(b)(xiii)/3 of the Uttar 
Pradesh Gangsters and Anti-Social Activities (Prevention) 
Act, 1986 (hereinafter to be referred to as the ‘Act of 1986’) 
registered with P.S. Rishikesh, District Dehradun.
4. According to petitioners, FIR was lodged against them 
on the basis of eight criminal cases registered with Police 
Stations Rishikesh, Raiwala and Rani Pokhari under 
different sections of BNS/IPC. It is contended that out of 
those eight cases, only four matters are registered against 
present petitioners. It is further submitted that in view of 
the judgment rendered by Hon’ble Supreme Court in the 
case of ‘Shiv Pratap Singh @ Chinu v. State of U.P. and 
others’ reported in 2026 LiveLaw (SC) 836, petitioner cannot 
be proceeded under the provisions of the Act of 1986. He 
further submits that the Act of 1986 has been adopted by 
the State of Uttarakhand, therefore, the proceedings under 
the Act of 1986 would be otiose in view of aforesaid 
judgment of Apex Court.
5. The allegations levelled against the petitioners and one 
Somendra Chauhan are of cheating the people on the 
pretext of sending them abroad. 6. Having seen the seriousness of offence alleged against 
the petitioners and their involvement in the crime, the Court 
is of the opinion that at this stage, petitioners are not 
entitled for any protection from this Court. 
7. Learned State Counsel is granted six weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit in the matter.
8. List on 07.12.2026.
