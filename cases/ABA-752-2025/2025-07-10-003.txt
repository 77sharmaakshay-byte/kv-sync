COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
ABA No. 752 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Alok Kumar, learned counsel for the 
applicant.
2. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned AGA for the State.
3. Mr. Sagar Kothari, learned counsel for the 
complainant.
4. Sub-Registrar join the proceeding through V.C.
5. The present applicant ‘Jagmohan Singh Chauhan’ 
S/o Shri Chand Chauhan is praying for anticipatory 
bail in relation to First Information Report dated 
28.05.2025 bearing FIR No. 0258 of 2025 P.S. Rishikesh, 
District Dehradun, wherein the present applicant along 
with Makan Singh Negi, Usmaan, Subodh Pokhriyal 
have been implicated for the offences punishable under 
Section 61(2) and 318(4) of the BNS, 2023.
6. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that in 
the FIR the allegations pertain to a registered sale deed 
dated 24.12.2024, that the same is forged one, which 
was executed through a power of attorney holder, 
which was already revoked.
7. He submits that in this sale deed, he is the only 
witness. Apart from this, learned counsel for the 
applicant submits the allegations as alleged prima facie
appears to be a civil dispute, and, furthermore, the sale 
deed in question is registered on and the law is very 
well settled that if a particular document is registered 
one then it should be presumed to be genuine until and 
unless the same is declared to be forged one.
8. He also submits that instead of availing civil 
remedy for seeking declaration that the sale deed is 
forged one, the complainant lodged the FIR, which is 
against the mandate of law declared by the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court in the case of Lalita Kumari (2013) SCC 
OnLine, SC 999 since before registration of FIR, no 
preliminary enquiry was done.
9. He also submits that lodging of the FIR is nothing but appears to be an attempt to by-pass the civil 
remedy available to the complainant.
10. Pre lunch session, the Sub-Registrar asked to join
proceeding through V.C. and he joined that the sale 
deed is strictly as per the provision of Transfer of 
Property Act.
11. All these aspects required deep scrutiny.
12. Let the counter-affidavit be filed by the State 
within four weeks.
13. Two weeks’ time, thereafter, is also granted to the 
counsel for the applicant to file rejoinder-affidavit.
14. List this matter on 28.08.2025.
15. In the meantime, the investigation may go on, 
however, for an interim measure, it is directed that in 
the event of arrest of the applicant, “Jagmohan Singh 
Chauhan”, he shall be released on interim bail on 
executing a personal bond and two reliable sureties 
each of the like amount to the satisfaction of the 
Investigating Officer/Arresting Officer, subject to the 
following conditions:
(i) Applicant shall cooperate with the Investigating 
Agency and he shall make himself available for 
interrogation by a Investigating Officer as and 
when required;
(ii) Applicant shall not directly or indirectly make 
any inducement, threat or promise to any person 
acquainted with the facts of this case.
(iii) Applicant shall not leave the country without 
previous permission of this Court.
