Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Alok Kumar, learned counsel for the

2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. with Mr.

3. Mr. Sagar Kothari, learned counsel for the 
complainant.
4. The applicant is praying for anticipatory bail in 
relation to First Information Report dated 28.05.2025 
bearing FIR No. 258 of 2025 P.S. Rishikesh, District 
Dehradun, in reference to a sale deed registered on 
24.12.2024. Mr. Sagar Kothari, who put appearance on 
behalf of complainant, pointed out that the total 
consideration as shown in the sale deed is Rs. 
12,40,50,000/-; and the mode of payment is through 
four cheques and two cheques of an amount of Rs. 10 
lakhs each total Rs. 20 lakhs dated 18.12.2024, which is 
a date prior to the registration of the sale deed.
A further reference of rest of the payment is by 
way of two cheques of Cheque No. 150532 of S.B.I., 
Racecourse, Dehradun and Cheque No. 150533 of S.B.I., 
Racecourse, Dehradun, and both the cheques are of 
dated 18.12.2025, which is the date after registration of 
the sale deed. 
5. It is also alleged in the FIR that the property, 
which was sold out by the aforesaid sale deed was 
already sold to the complainant namely Rajesh Agarwal 
in the year 2008 by Inderjeet Singh and the allegation is 
that the subsequent sale deed was executed in 
connivance with Jagmohan Singh, Makan Singh and 
Usman on the basis of cancelled power of attorney 
dated 31.08.2024.
6. The question is how by giving reference of post 
dated cheque, which has yet not been encashed the sale 
deed was executed and registered.
7. Let the Sub Registrar, Rishikesh, join the proceeding through V.C. and explain it at 2:00 PM.
