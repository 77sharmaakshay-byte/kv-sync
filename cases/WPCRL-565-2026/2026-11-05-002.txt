2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. with

Holder for the State of Uttarakhand/
respondent Nos.1 to 3.
3. Petitioner has filed this writ petition 
seeking a direction to respondent-police 
authorities to take prompt and suitable 
action on the basis of complaint dated 
21.08.2025 submitted by petitioner and 
conduct a fair and impartial investigation in 
accordance with law.
4. Learned State Counsel on instruction 
submits that on the basis of complaint filed 
by petitioner, preliminary inquiry was 
conducted by the police authority in the 
Police Station Kaladhungi, District Nainital 
and during the preliminary inquiry,
according to the police, petitioner could not 
submit any concrete document to suggest 
that some cognizable offence is made out 
against the private respondents and 
accordingly, the closure report was filed.
5. Petitioner has annexed notarized 
agreement dated 10.10.2024 with regard to 
purchase of bhumidhari land in Village 
Dhamola Kaladhungi, Tehsil Kaladhungi, 
District Nainital, which belongs to 
respondent No.6-Dori Lal Tamta S/o 
Mahesh Ram Tamta, R/o village Dhamola, Tehsil and P.S. Kaladhungi, District Nainital.
6. It is reflected from the said agreement 
that it was decided that land shall be sold 
to petitioner on sale consideration of 
₹36,50,000/- and out of the said amount, a 
sum of ₹11,31,000/- was to be paid by 
petitioner to respondent No.6-Dori Lal 
Tamta on the same date and it was also 
agreed that rest of the amount will be paid 
in 10 months and the sale deed will be 
registered.
7. Petitioner, who is present before this 
Court, submits that he and his wife both are 
Teacher and the amount of ₹11,31,000/-
was withdrawn by petitioner and his wife 
from their account maintained in Bank of 
Baroda, Branch Bhimtal, District Nainital
and was paid to respondent No.6 Dori Lal in 
cash.
8. On asking of this Court, petitioner 
contends that he had given the details of 
bank statements to the investigating 
officer, even thereafter, the closure report 
was submitted after doing preliminary 
inquiry in a very cursory manner for reason 
best known to the police.
9. In this view of the matter, Station 
House Officer (SHO), Kaladhungi/ 
Investigating Officer, who conducted the 
preliminary inquiry, shall remain present 
before this Court on 16.11.2026 along with 
documents of preliminary inquiry.
10. Put up on 16.11.2026 as fresh.
