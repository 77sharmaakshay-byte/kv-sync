 Heard on Delay Condonation Application 
(CLMA) No.12730 of 2017.
 The appellant proposes to challenge the 
judgment and order dated 29.04.2017, passed 
in Motor Accident Claim Petition No.48 of 2016, 
Shri Abhishek Kukreti Vs. United India 
Insurance Company Limited and another, by the 
court of Motor Accident Claims Tribunal/First 
Additional District Judge, Rishikesh, District Dehradun, by which, the appellant has been 
awarded Rs.26,04,583/- compensation. It is 
delayed. 
 A delay condonation application has been 
filed.
 It is the case of the appellant that he came to 
know about the judgment long after its delivery. 
After it, he arranged for financial resources so 
that appeal may timely be filed and in that 
process delay occurred. It is not deliberate. 
 Learned counsel for the respondent no.1 has 
objected to the delay condonation application. 
 It is not objected to by learned Senior Counsel 
appearing for the respondent no.2.
 The delay in filing the appeal is condoned.
 The delay condonation application is allowed. 
 Heard.
 Admit.
 Lower court record is already on record.
 List for final hearing on 03.09.2026.
