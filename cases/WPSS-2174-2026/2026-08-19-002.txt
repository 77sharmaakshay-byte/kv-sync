3. Pursuant to order dated 14.08.2026, 
instruction was received and produced 
by learned State Counsel. In the 
instruction, there is no clarity with 
regard to the fact of mutual transfer 
rather it is more about the transfer, 
which may be effected under Section 
17(2)(e) which provides that the transfer 
can only be effected in the cadre and no 
interdistrict or interdivisional transfer are 
made. 
4. Learned counsel for petitioner submits that petitioner-Vandana Dhami
has applied for mutual transfer along 
with petitioner-Viday Singh (in WPSS 
No.2174 of 2026). Petitioner-Vandana 
Dhami is working as Assistant Teacher in
Government Primary School, Mathgaon,
Block Development Jakholi, District
Rudraprayag, Garhwal Division, while
petitioner-Viday Singh is working as 
Assistant Teacher in Government 
Primary School, Chausala, Block 
Development Berinag, District 
Pithoragarh, Kumaon Division, as 
Assistant Teacher.
5. In such view of the matter, in order 
to clarify the position as to whether 
mutual transfer can be effected by the 
department or not, counter affidavit is 
required.
6. State is directed to file counter 
affidavit within two weeks. 
7. Put up on 08.09.2026.
