Mr. M.C. Pant, Advocate for the

Mr. Shubham Bhardwaj, Advocate 
holding brief of Mr. Shobhit Saharia, 
Advocate for the respondents. 
The petitioner claims that he 
superannuated after serving with the 
District Legal Services Authority, District 
Court, Haridwar, on 31.12.2022, but till 
date, his retiral dues has not been paid. 
In the instant matter, notices have 
already been served on the State Legal 
Services Authority and District Legal 
Services Authority, Haridwar.
Today, learned counsel for the 
respondents submits that they have 
received instructions. Now they will file 
counter affidavit.
It is really strange that legal services 
authority may delay in filing counter 
affidavit for a long in a matter like retiral 
dues.
Let Secretary, State Legal Services 
Authority, examine the matter and ensure 
that admissible dues are paid to the 
petitioner before the next date fixed.
In the meanwhile, counter affidavit 
may be filed by the respondents within 
three weeks. 
A week thereafter, rejoinder affidavit, 
if any, may be filed.
List for final hearing on 20.08.2025.Let a copy of this order be sent to the 
Secretary, State Legal Services Authority.
Urgency Application, IA No.5 of 2025, 
stands disposed of, accordingly.
