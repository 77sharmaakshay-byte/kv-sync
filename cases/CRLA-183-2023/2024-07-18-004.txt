(2) Having been convicted, inter 
alia, for the offence under Section 302 
IPC vide judgment and order dated 
16.3.2023, appellant is serving life 
imprisonment. He has sought short term 
bail on the ground that he has to sell 
part of his agricultural land. Learned 
Counsel for the appellant submits that 
appellant’s father is a co-accused in this 
case and he is also serving life 
imprisonment. He further submits that 
appellant’s mother is all alone in the 
house and she is unable to enter into 
financial transaction regarding sale of 
the land. He submits that short term bail 
for two weeks be granted to appellant. 
(3) Learned State Counsel submits 
that he has no objection if appellant is 
granted short term bail on the ground 
mentioned in the application. However, 
he submits that the appellant be 
released on short term bail in police custody. 
(4) Mr. Siddhartha Sah, learned 
Counsel appearing for the complainant, 
however, opposed grant of short term 
bail and submitted that if appellant is 
released on short term bail, there will be 
great threat to the life of complainant. 
(5) Having regard to the facts and 
reasons mentioned in the short term bail 
application (IA/4/2024), we allow the 
same and direct that the 
appellant/applicant Gurbej Singh alias
Gupej Singh be released on short term 
bail, in police custody, for a period of 
one week from the date of his release, 
subject to his executing a personal bond 
and furnishing two verified sureties, each 
in the like amount, to the satisfaction of 
the Court concerned and after taking an 
undertaking that he shall be produced by 
the police before the Chief Judicial 
Magistrate concerned immediately after 
expiry of the period of short term bail.
