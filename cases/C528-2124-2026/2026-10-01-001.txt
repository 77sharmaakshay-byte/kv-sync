Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Anup Kumar Jha, (through V.C.) learned
counsel for the applicant.
2. The instant petition is moved under Section 528
of BNSS, 2023 by the applicant Chanchal Rani, who is a
senior citizen and 80 years old, for challenging the
proceeding of Criminal Case No. 447 of 2026, arising
out of Case Crime No. 381 of 2024 (State Vs. Chanchal
Rani).
3. It is argued by the learned counsel for the
applicant that applicant sold a piece of land to the
informant in the year 2018 but the informant has not
paid the full and final payment, therefore, the
applicant filed an objection in the mutation proceeding
and now being prejudiced with the objection filed in
the mutation proceeding, the informant lodged this
FIR. He further submits that proceeding has been
instituted by the informant with malafide intent and is
liable to be quashed. 
4. All these aspects require scrutiny.
5. Ms. Meenakshi Sharma, learned AGA for the
accepts notice on behalf of the respondent no. 1.
6. Issue notice to respondent no. 2.
7. Steps within a week.
8. Let counter affidavit be filed within four weeks.
Two weeks, thereafter, is granted to the applicant to
file rejoinder affidavit. 
9. List this case in the week commencing
23.11.2026.10. Till further orders, further proceeding Criminal
Case No. 447 of 2026, arising out of Case Crime No.
381 of 2024 (State Vs. Chanchal Rani) pending in the
court of Addl. Chief Judicial Magistrate, Kashipur,
Udham Singh Nagar, shall remain stayed.
