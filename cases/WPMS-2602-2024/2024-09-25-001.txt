COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.2602 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. M.S. Tyagi, learned Senior Advocate assisted by 
Mr. Siddhartha Sah, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Yogesh Chandra Tiwari, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State.
3. By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has 
challenged a Government Letter dated 21.06.2024 and an order 
dated 11.07.2024, passed by learned Assistant Collector, Ist
Class, Doiwala, Dehradun, whereby the order of exchange of 
the property in-question has been set aside as well as the order 
dated 08.07.2024 issued by learned SDM, Doiwala, Dehradun.
4. It is case of the petitioner that the petitioner is a tenure 
holder of Village-Mazri Grant, Dehradun Tehsil-Doiwala. 
Being tenure holder of the said village, he entered into an 
exchange with due permission from respondent-State with 
Gram Sabha in respect of the property in-question and 
accordingly the exchange order was passed on 03.03.2022.
5. Two villagers of Village-Mazri Grant, feeling aggrieved 
by the order of exchange filed a Revision No.36 of 2021-22 in 
the Board of Revenue and that revision was subsequently 
dismissed by the said Board on 27.04.2022.
6. It is submitted by learned Senior Advocate that despite 
dismissal of the revision, a review was filed by the revisionist
before the Board of Revenue, which is numbered as 32 of 
2022-23, which is still pending consideration. But suddenly the 
learned Assistant Collector, Ist Class, Doiwala, Dehradun
strangely without any legal provision has withdrawn the order 
of exchange by impugned order dated 11.07.2024 (annexure 
no.31).2
7. Now, it is feeling aggrieved the aforesaid illegal exercise 
of power by the learned Assistant Collector, Ist Class, Doiwala, 
Dehradun, the petitioner is before this Court.
8. Learned State counsel prays for and is granted six 
weeks’ time for filing counter affidavit.
9. Two weeks’ time thereafter is granted to learned counsel 
for the petitioner for filing rejoinder affidavit.
10. List this case on 18.12.2024.
11. In the meantime, petitioner shall not be evicted from the 
property in-question.
12. Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2024) stands disposed-off 
accordingly.
