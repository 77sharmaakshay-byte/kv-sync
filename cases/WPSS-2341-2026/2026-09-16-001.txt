Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned Counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. Ganga S. Negi, learned Addl. C.S.C. for the State.
3. Petitioner is working with respondent-Department as a 
Head Mistress in Govt. Primary School. Pursuant to 
government order dated 10.06.2016, petitioner was posted 
in Govt. Model Primary School, Padampur Sukhron, Block 
Dugadda, District Pauri Garhwal. Petitioner joined there on 
04.10.2016. Petitioner was transferred by order dated
09.06.2023 from aforesaid school to Govt. Primary School 
Bhatwara, Block Bironkhal, District Pauri Garhwal. 
Petitioner challenged the said transfer order by filing WPSS 
No.909 of 2023 (Kalpana Tiwari v. State and others) on the 
ground that since petitioner is posted in a government 
model school, she cannot be transferred from that school to 
any other primary school without assessment of her 
quarterly internal evaluation and annual result evaluation 
by the Deputy Education Officer, or on her own 
choice/request, as contemplated in sub-clause (h) of Clause 
3 of the aforesaid government order dated 10.06.2016. The 
said transfer order was initially stayed and subsequently, 
the writ petition was disposed of directing respondent no.2-
Director (Elementary Education) to reconsider the order of 
transfer of petitioner in light of applicable policy under the aforesaid government order. Respondent no.2 has passed 
the impugned order dated 27.11.2025 (Annexure no.14) 
wherein it has been stated that petitioner, being a 
government servant, is amenable to the provisions of the 
Uttarakhand Annual Transfer for Public Servants Act, 2017, 
and therefore, she can very well be transferred from model 
school to any other school, however, the impugned order is 
silent in respect of government order dated 10.06.2016. 
Petitioner has now challenged the said order by filing the 
present petition. 
4. It is contended by petitioner similar controversy is 
before the Co-ordinate Bench of this Court in WPSS 
No.1403 of 2026 (Manju Rani Sharma v. State and others), 
wherein the Co-ordinate Bench of this Court had been 
gracious enough to pass the order of ‘status quo’.
5. Let counter affidavit be filed by the State within four 
weeks.
6. Till the next date of listing, effect and operation of 
order dated 27.11.2025 as well as the impugned order dated 
01.04.2026 (Annexure Nos.14 and 18 respectively) shall 
remain stayed.
7. List on 20.11.2026.
8. Interim Relief Application (IA/1/2026) stands disposed 
of.
