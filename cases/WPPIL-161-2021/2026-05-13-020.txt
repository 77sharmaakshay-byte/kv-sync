13.05.2026 WPPIL No. 161 of 2021
Ravi Shankar Joshi ---Petitioner
 Versus
State of Uttarakhand and another --Respondents
Hon'ble Manoj Kumar Gupta, C.J.
Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
1. Sri Ravi Shankar Joshi, petitioner, in person.
2. Sri P.S. Rawat, learned Chief Standing Counsel for the State 
of Uttarakhand. 
3. On 01.04.2026, we recorded the statement of learned 
Advocate General that the Selection Committee was to meet on 3rd
April, 2026 and in the said meeting, it would constitute a Search 
Committee for suggesting names of deserving persons to the 
Selection Committee. 
4. Considering the said stand of the State, the matter was 
adjourned to 8th April, 2026 with direction to the State to bring on 
record the development(s) that would take place in the meantime, 
along with affidavit of a responsible officer. 
5. On 08.04.2026, the matter was again adjourned for four 
weeks. 
6. Today, when the matter is taken up, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State of Uttarakhand has placed before us a communication 
received from the Secretary, Uttarakhand Administration, dated 
12.05.2026 addressed to the Chief Standing Counsel. In Paragraph-3 
of the said communication, it is stated that a meeting was convened 
on 29.04.2026 for constituting a Search Committee. The said 
meeting could not be attended by the leader of the opposition because 
of unavoidable reasons. In the said meeting, a decision was taken to 
constitute a Search Committee comprising of five persons. It is not stated in the communication as to what happened after 29.04.2026 
and whether the Search Committee has been constituted so far or not. 
The affidavit, as was required to be filed by order dated 01.04.2026, 
has also not been filed. 
7. We grant 24 hours’ time to respondent No. 1 to clarify the 
present position by filing affidavit of respondent No. 1, failing which 
he shall remain present before this Court on 15.05.2026 at 11:00 
A.M.
8. List on 15.05.2026 at 11:00 A.M.
