IA/7/2023
3. By this application, the respondents seek 
extension of time for compliance of our order dated 
27.06.2023. 
4. We have considered the said application. The 
respondents have asked for another six months’ time 
to report compliance of our aforesaid order. In our 
view, the same is not justified, and the respondents 
should complete the process of appointing the 
Lokayukta within three months from today. No further 
extension shall be granted. 
5. The application stands disposed of accordingly. 
IA/5/2023
6. By this application, the respondents seek 
modification of our order dated 27.06.2023. 
7. We are not inclined to modify our order, with 
regard to payment of salary to the staff claimed to 
have been deployed in the office of the Lokayukta. We 
fail to understand, as to what the said staff comprising 
of 17 persons, is doing in the Office of the Lokayukta, 
which is completely defunct. The payment of salary to 
such persons, from the office of the Lokayukta, is a complete wasteful expenditure of public money. We, 
therefore, reject the request made. However, it shall 
be open to the respondents to post all such employees 
in such other departments, wherever their work is 
required on deputation, or otherwise, and they may be 
paid salaries from the departments, where they serve. 
8. List on 05.03.2024.
