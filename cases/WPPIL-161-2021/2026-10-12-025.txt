Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, A.C.J.
Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J. 
1. Mr. Suhaas Ratna Joshi, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Puran Singh Rawat, learned Chief
Standing Counsel with Mr. Gajendra Tripathi, 
learned Standing Counsel and Mr. Akshit 
Gururani, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. A co-ordinate bench vide order dated 
16.06.2026, extended the interim order dated 
07.11.2023, for a further period of six months. 
We are informed that the ministerial staff of the 
Office of Lokayukta has not been paid salary 
since last several months. The employees 
cannot be made to suffer because of delay on 
the part of the State in holding selection for 
appointment to the post of Lokayukta.
4. In such view of the matter, we extent the 
interim order dated 07.11.2023 till end of 
current financial year and permit the State to 
release salary of regular employees serving in 
the Office of Lokayukta out of the budget 
already allocated. State shall also be at liberty to pay pending dues towards electricity, 
telephone and water supply etc. to the 
concerned agency for the current financial year.
5. State Counsel submits that the rules have 
now been notified on 25th August 2026, known 
as “The Uttarakhand Lokayukta Search 
Committee, Determination of Functions Rules,
2026”. He seeks two weeks’ time to get further 
necessary instructions in the matter.
6. On his request, list the matter on 
04.11.2026.
