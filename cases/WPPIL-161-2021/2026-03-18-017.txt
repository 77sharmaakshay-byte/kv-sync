Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
1. Mr. Karthik Jayashankar, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. Gajendra Tripathi, learned Standing Counsel for 
the State.
3. On 27.06.2023, the Court noted with concern that the 
mandate of law for appointing a Lokayukta in the State was 
not being followed, whereas heavy expenditure was being 
incurred towards the Establishment of Lokayukta. Since 
thereafter, repeated orders have been passed and every 
time the State is seeking time to apprise the Court of the 
status of the proceedings relating to appointment of 
Lokayukta.
4. It is pertinent to note here that State in its affidavit 
dated 25.06.2025 stated that the process of appointment of
Lokayukta is on verge of completion; however, so far the 
process has not been completed. 
5. Learned State Counsel prays for further time to 
apprise the Court of the present status of the selection 
process.
6. While recording our displeasure, we grant two weeks’ 
further time to the State Counsel to apprise the Court of the 
present status. 
7. List on 01.04.2026.
