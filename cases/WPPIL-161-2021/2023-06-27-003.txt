ORDER : (per Sri Vipin Sanghi, C.J.)
An affidavit has been filed by Shri Lalit Mohan 
Rayal, Additional Secretary, Department of Good 
Governance, Eradication of Corruption and Public 
Services, Government of Uttarakhand, in terms of our
order dated 17.03.2023. 
2. The affidavit discloses that, initially, the Uttar 
Pradesh Lokayukta and Up-Lokayukta Act, 1975 was 
made applicable to the State of Uttarakhand, on its 
creation, and that Hon’ble (Retd.) Justice S.H.A. Raza 2
was appointed as the 1st Lokayukta, and upon his 
superannuation on 23.10.2008, Hon’ble (Retd.) Justice 
M.M. Ghildiyal was appointed as the Lokayukta on 
01.11.2008. Vide notification dated 24.09.2013, the 
Uttarakhand Lokayukta Act, 2011 was brought into force
and, in terms of Section 36(1) of the said Act, the Uttar 
Pradesh Lokayukta and Up-Lokayukta Act, 1975 stood 
repealed, insofar as, its application in State of 
Uttarakhand is concerned. Consequently, the term of 
Hon’ble (Retd.) Justice M.M Ghildiyal, as Lokayukta,
came to an end. Since then, there is no Lokayukta 
appointed in the State of Uttarakhand. 
3. The affidavit further discloses that the 
Uttarakhand Lokayukta Act, 2014 was enacted on 
26.02.2014, and, with that, the Uttarakhand Lokayukta 
Act, 2011 was repealed. The said Act was further 
amended vide notification dated 23.12.2014, and the 
Uttarakhand Lokayukta (Amendment) Act, 2014 was 
brought into force, which, inter alia, stated that the said 
Act shall come into force from the date of appointment 
of the Lokayukta. Since no Lokayukta has been 
appointed under the said Act, the Act of 2014 has not 
come into force. This is despite the fact that the Search 
Committee, constituted under the said Act, has made 3
recommendations, but they have not been acted upon. 
The affidavit further discloses that a Bill was presented 
to the State Legislature for enactment of the 
Uttarakhand Lokayukta Act, 2017, but the said Bill has 
not been passed till date. Pertinently, it was informed to 
the Supreme Court, as early as on 10.01.2018, that a 
new enactment, pertaining to appointment of Lokayukta,
has been introduced in the State Legislative Assembly, 
when the Supreme Court was hearing WPPIL (Civil) No. 
57/2016, Ashwani Kumar (Vice Chairman) v. State 
of Uttarakhand. That assurance, given to the Supreme 
Court, has remained unimplemented till date.
4. Section 63 of the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 
2013 mandates that “every State shall establish a body 
to be known as the Lokayukta for the State, if not so 
established, constituted or appointed, by a law made by 
the State Legislature, to deal with complaints relating to 
corruption against certain public functionaries, within a 
period of one year from the date of commencement of 
this Act”. 
5. The purport of the aforesaid Section 63 is 
that, even if the State Legislature does not enact a law 4
for appointment of a Lokayukta, such Lokayukta shall be 
appointed under the provisions of the Lokpal and 
Lokayuktas Act, 2013. Therefore, in our view, it is no 
excuse for the State Government to state that, as there 
is no State Legislation in respect of appointment of a 
Lokayukta, the State Government is under no obligation 
to make appointment of the Lokayukta under the Lokpal 
and Lokayuktas Act, 2013. 
6. We, therefore, direct the State Government to 
forthwith proceed to make appointment of a Lokayukta 
in the State, positively within the next eight weeks
under the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act, 2013. 
7. The affidavit also discloses the shocking state 
of affairs with regard to the expenditure incurred in the 
Office of the Lokayukta. It is disclosed that till 
31.03.2023, in respect of the Office of the Lokayukta, 
the budget allocated was Rs. 36,95,40,563/-, out of 
which the Lokayukta office has utilized a sum of Rs.
29,73,99,044/-. The detailed break-up is given in 
Annexure B-2 of the affidavit, which reads as follows :-5
8. It is further disclosed that the services of 09 
employees, out of 24, of the Lokayukta Office, are 
temporarily being used in the Uttarakhand Real Estate 
Regulatory Authority (RERA) and the Uttarakhand Real 
Estate Appellate Tribunal (REAT). There is no disclosure 
as to what is the fate of the remaining 15 employees 
appointed in the Office of the Lokayukta, who have been 
drawing salaries. It is a matter of grave concern that 
the public exchequer is being burdened with such heavy 6
expenditures, even though the Office of the Lokayukta in 
the State is defunct, since the year 2013. 
9. We, therefore, direct the State not to incur
even a single rupee towards the Office of the Lokayukta 
till the appointment of the Lokayukta has been made. 
No further expenditure shall be incurred in the name of 
the Office of the Lokayukta by the State henceforth. 
10. The State shall file a further affidavit,
disclosing the names and designations of all the 24 
employees appointed in the Office of the Lokayukta, as 
well as the salaries drawn by them, and the manner in 
which their services are being utilized. An affidavit in 
this regard shall be filed within three weeks.
