Sri Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Karthik Jayashankar, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned Chief Standing Counsel 
assisted by Mr. Gajendra Tripathi, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State- respondents. 
Modification Application (IA No.09 of 2023)
3. Co-ordinate Bench of this Court had passed 
interim order on 27.06.2023, directing the State not to 
incur any expenditure towards the Office of Lokayukta 
till the appointment of the Lokayukta is made. 
4. State has moved an application seeking 
modification of the said order. 
5. Learned Chief Standing Counsel submits that 
there are as many as 16 regular employees serving in 
the establishment of Lokayukta, who have not been 
paid any salary since July, 2023. He further points out 
that one employee, namely, Gopal Singh Bisht, retired 
on 31.10.2023, and before his retirement, there were 
17 employees serving in the office of Lokayukta. He 
further submits that pension and other retiral dues to 
Gopal Singh Bisht could also not be released because 
of the restraint order. 
6. It is further submitted that the work of 
renovation of office building of Lokayukta is 
undergoing, and due to restraint order passed by this 
Court, renovation work of the office building of Lokayukta is hampered. He further submits that bills 
regarding electricity, telephone, water supply etc., and 
the bills regarding fuel and maintenance of official 
vehicles are also pending. 
7. Having regard to the problem faced by the 
regular employees working in the establishment of 
Lokayukta, and also with a view to ensure that 
functioning of the office of Lokayukta is not hampered, 
we allow the modification application, and permit the 
State to make payment of salary to regular 
employees, out of the budget already allocated. State 
shall also be at liberty to pay pending dues towards 
electricity, telephone and water supply etc. to the 
concerned agencies, during the current financial year, 
i.e. upto 31.03.2024. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.) (Manoj Kumar Tiwari, A.C.J.)
