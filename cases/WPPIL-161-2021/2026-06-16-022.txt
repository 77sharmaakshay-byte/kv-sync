3. Learned State Counsel states that the Selection 
Committee, in the meeting held on 04.06.2026, has 
constituted a Search Committee. An Office Memorandum 
has also been issued on 13.06.2026, notifying the 
constitution of the Search Committee, and requiring it to 
prepare a panel, and make the same available to the 
Chairman of the Selection Committee. 
4. Learned State Counsel, therefore, seeks time so that 
recommendations are obtained from the Search Committee, 
and thereafter further action is taken in the matter. 
5. Having regard to the fact that Search Committee has 
already been constituted, we defer the hearing of the 
instant writ petition for six weeks, so that in the meantime, 
the Search Committee prepares a panel and forward the 
same to the Selection Committee.
6. Learned State Counsel shall file a status report 
regarding further development on the next date.
