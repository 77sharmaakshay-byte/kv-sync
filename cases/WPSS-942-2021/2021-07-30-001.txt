Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.

Mr. Subhash Upadhyay, Advocate, for

Mr. P.C. Bisht, Additional CSC, for the 
State of Uttarakhand. 
The Registry has pointed out that the 
writ petition suffers from certain defects. 
However, looking to the nature of the defects,
and the undertaking, which has been given by 
the learned counsel for the petitioner, he 
undertakes that the defects would be removed
by him within a period of one week from today.
The relief sought in the writ petition by 
the petitioner, is as against the decision dated 
22.08.2019, as it had been passed by the 
Committee with regards to the claim of the pay 
scale, which the petitioner intended or claims to 
be entitled to be awarded.
This writ petition could not be decided 
except, without calling the counter affidavit in 
the matter.
In an eventuality and that, subject to the 
condition the petitioner removes the defects as 
pointed by the Registry within a period of one 
week, the respondents may file counter 
affidavit to the writ petition within a period of 
four weeks.
List this writ petition after the receipt of 
the counter affidavit.
