3. By means of present writ petition, petitioner 
has challenged his termination order dated 
29.07.2025 passed by respondent no.5-District 
Education Officer (Primary Education), Haridwar 
(Annexure No. 6 to the writ petition) and other 
ancillary reliefs.
4. Facts of the case are that pursuant to the 
appointment letter dated 24.12.2008 issued by 
respondent no.5, petitioner joined his duties as an 
Assistant Teacher on 06.01.2009. By order dated 
29.05.2025, petitioner was placed under 
suspension pursuant to a verification exercise 
conducted by the respondents relating to the 
educational certificates and service records. After a 
due inquiry, charge-sheet dated 24.07.2025 was 
served upon the petitioner, whereby, petitioner was 
asked to produce his written statement along with 
evidence within a period of 7 days.
5. The petitioner replied the charge-sheet 
though it was filed subsequent to the period 
granted to him for filing the reply. The disciplinary 
proceedings commenced which culminated into the 
termination of services of petitioner i.e. imposing 
major penalty of dismissal of service against the petitioner. 
6. Ordinarily, as against the order of dismissal 
from service which is a major penalty, under the 
Uttarakhand Government Servant (Discipline and 
Appeal) Rules, 2003 (as amended by Amendment 
Rules, 2010), remedy of filing an appeal is 
provided under Rule 11 and therefore, the writ 
petition is not maintainable.
7. Learned Senior Advocate appearing for the 
petitioner submitted that by the dismissal order, a 
direction was issued to recover the amount of 
salary which has been received by petitioner, and a 
further direction was issued to lodge the FIR 
against the petitioner, therefore, the writ petition is 
liable to be entertained.
8. Learned Senior Advocate appearing for the 
petitioner further submits that the appellate 
authority cannot pass an order for restraining the 
respondent authority to lodge the FIR and staying 
the recovery which is directed by the impugned 
order; he also submits that the directions referred 
above in the order impugned is beyond the scope 
of Rule 3 of Uttarakhand Government Servant 
(Discipline and Appeal) Rules, 2003 (as amended 
by Amendment Rules, 2010).
9. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
10. There is some substance in the submission 
made by learned counsel for the petitioner. The
matter requires some deliberation for which a 
counter affidavit is needed.
11. Learned counsel for the State prays for and is 
granted four weeks’ time to file counter affidavit.
12. List this case on 08.09.2026.
13. Till the next date of listing, the effect and 
operation of impugned order is stayed only to the 
extent whereby the recovery has been directed to 
be made and a direction for registration of the FIR against the petitioner has been given.
14. Stay Application (IA/1/2026) stands disposed 
of.
