On the last date, this Court had 
required that the appellant be produced 
before the expert doctor so as to ascertain the 
mental condition of the appellant.
Today, learned State Counsel submits 
that the appellant was produced before the 
expert doctor at All India Institute of Medical 
Sciences, Rishikesh, and they have given a 
report. The report has been tendered for the 
perusal of the Court.
Let it be taken on record.
The findings are as follows:-
“1. The patient is suffering from Schizophrenia- currently in significantly 
improved condition, on daily medication.
2. The patient requires continued 
treatment and regular follow-ups for 
maintenance of improvement.
3. During his current in-patient stay, the 
patient was able to communicate verbally 
without any apparent difficulty and without 
requiring any support, and at present he is fit 
to stand trial.”
The Court proceeded to hear the 
arguments.
Learned Amicus Curiae submits that 
the matter may be adjourned.
List this matter on 28.09.2026.
