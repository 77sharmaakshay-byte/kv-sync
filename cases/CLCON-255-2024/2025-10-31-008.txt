 It is very strange that the respondents are 
trying to ignore the Court’s order. The Court 
has directed the competent authority to take 
a decision on the question of regularization of 
the petitioner. But, what is being argued on 
behalf of the respondents that the Board of 
Governors have once considered the matter 
and deferred it till the Rules are notified. 
Apparently and admittedly, today there are 
Rules. A decision is to be taken in view of the 
existing Rules. Apparently, the respondents 
failed to take a decision in accordance with 
the existing Rules. Does not it prima facie 
discloses that it is a wilful disobedience of the 
Court’s order? 
 When it is so discussed, the learned State 
Counsel still seeks time to get instruction. 
 List on 13.011.2025. 
 Urgency Application (IA) No.5 of 2025, 
stands disposed of accordingly.
