None for the appellant. 
Mrs. Seema Sah, Advocate, for the

This First Appeal was dismissed for want of 
prosecution on 8th April, 2022. The said order has 
been sought to be recalled by filing a Restoration 
Application No. 5416 of 2022, along with the Delay 
Condonation Application No. 5417 of 2022, 
seeking condonation of 56 days’ of delay. 
After having gone through the contents of the 
Delay Condonation Application and being satisfied 
with the reasons given therein, the delay which has 
chanced in filing the Restoration Application, 
would stand condoned. 
Accordingly, the Restoration Application 
would too stand allowed. 
The order dated 8th April, 2022, dismissing 
the First Appeal for want of prosecution is hereby 
recalled and the First Appeal is restored to its 
original number.
