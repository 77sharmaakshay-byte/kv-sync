Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. I.D. Paliwal, learned counsel for 
the appellant.
Mrs. Seema Sirohi, learned counsel 
for the respondents.
This is an admitted appeal, which is 
listed on an urgency application preferred 
by respondent no.1.
Owing to the fact, that respondent is 
a senior citizen of about 80 years of age,
hence, he has sought an expeditious 
disposal. 
Learned counsel for the appellant is 
not ready to argue the case due to lack of 
preparation.
The urgency application would stand 
allowed and in the light of the provisions 
contained under Order 41 Rule 11 of the 
CPC, the appeal itself is directed to be 
listed in the week commencing 
27.11.2023 with an understanding 
between the counsel for the parties that 
the appeal would be argued finally.
