COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPCRL No. 1148 of 2026
Samadh alias Samu Abdul alias Aman ... Petitioner
Vs. 
State of Uttarakhand and Others ... Respondents. 
Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.,
 Mr. Akshay Pradhan, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. Vijay Pal, learned A.G.A. for the State of 
Uttarakhand. 
3. By means of the present writ petition, the 
petitioner seeks quashing of the First Information 
Report dated 24.03.2026, registered as FIR No. 0050 of 
2026, under Sections 318(4), 351(2) and 352 of the 
Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, at Police Station 
Jhabrera, District Haridwar.
4. As per the allegations contained in the impugned 
FIR, an agreement for the sale of land was executed 
between the complainant and one Kulwinder Singh. The 
total sale consideration of the property was fixed at ₹10 
crores. It is alleged that after receiving a sum of 
₹9,60,00,000/- from the complainant, Kulwinder Singh 
failed to execute the sale deed in favour of the 
complainant.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that, as 
per the allegations contained in the FIR, the only role 
attributed to the present petitioner is that of a 
middleman/broker in the transaction. No allegation has 
been levelled against the petitioner regarding receipt of 
any part of the sale consideration or any wrongful 
financial gain. 
6. Learned State Counsel, on instructions, fairly 
submits that the role attributed to the petitioner in the 
FIR is confined to that of a broker.
7. Learned State Counsel, on the basis of written 
instructions received from the Investigating Officer, 
further submits that no material has surfaced during 
the investigation to indicate that the petitioner derivedany financial benefit from the alleged transaction.
8. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused 
the material available on record.
9. Having considered the submissions advanced by 
the learned counsel for the parties and upon perusal of 
the material available on record, this Court is of the 
prima facie opinion that the matter requires 
consideration.
10. Issue notice to respondent No. 3, returnable 
within four weeks.
11. List this case after receipt of the service report 
along with WPCRL No. 576 of 2026.
12. Counter affidavit, if any, be filed by the 
respondents within the aforesaid period.
13. Till the next date of listing, as an interim measure, 
the petitioner shall not be arrested in connection with 
FIR No. 0050 of 2026 dated 24.03.2026, registered 
under Sections 318(4), 351(2) and 352 of the Bharatiya 
Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, at Police Station Jhabrera, District 
Haridwar, provided that he cooperates with the 
investigation and appears before the Investigating 
Officer as and when required.
14. The Stay Application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
 (Alok Mahra, J.)
