2. Mr. Dharmendra Barthwal, learned counsel for the

3. By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has 
challenged the condition given in Para no.13(M)(c)(vi) of the 
Advertisement No.CRPD/SCO/2026-27/02 dated 31.05.2026, 
annexure no.2 to the writ petition, whereby it has been 
prescribed that the candidate shall not sign in English capital
letters.
4. The case of petitioner is that petitioner pursuant to the 
aforesaid advertisement submitted an application form for 
appointment on the post of Assistant Manager (Law) with the 
respondent-Bank. The admit card was issued to the petitioner 
by the respondent, but he was not permitted to participate in 
the examination only on the ground that the petitioner put 
signature in capital letters. 
5. Learned counsel for the respondents may file counter 
affidavit within four weeks.
6. List this case on 30.10.2026.
7. In the meantime, any appointment made pursuant to the 
aforesaid advertisement on the post of Assistant Manager 
(Law) shall be subject to final outcome of the writ petition.
