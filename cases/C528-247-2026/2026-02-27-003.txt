Mr. Karan Anand, learned counsel

2. Mr. K.S. Bora, learned Deputy

3. Mr. Mahendra Singh Rawat, 
learned counsel for respondent no.2.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that the F.I.R. was lodged by 
respondent no.2 alleging that the 
parties came in contact through a 
social media platform and, thereafter, 
the applicant allegedly subjected the 
complainant to financial and physical 
exploitation; that, subsequently the 
complainant came to know that the 
applicant was a married person. After 
investigation, the Investigating Officer 
submitted a charge-sheet and the 
learned Magistrate has taken 
cognizance thereon. It is contended 
that the allegations, even if taken at 
their face value, do not constitute the 
alleged offences and the entire criminal 
proceedings are liable to be quashed.
5. Learned counsel for respondent 
no.2 opposes the prayer and seeks time 
to file counter affidavit/objections.6. Learned counsel for the applicant 
further submits that the matter before 
the court below is fixed for 10.03.2026 
and bailable warrants have been issued 
against the applicant. He, therefore, 
prays that liberty be granted to move 
an appropriate application for 
adjournment before the court 
concerned.
7. Considering the aforesaid, the 
applicant is permitted to move an 
application seeking adjournment before 
the court below. In the event such 
application is filed, the court concerned 
shall consider and decide the same in 
accordance with law, till the next date 
of listing before this Court.
8. List this matter after two weeks.
