Having heard learned counsel for 
the petitioner, it transpires that he got 
booked a 4 BHK residential flat with 
respondent no.3 M/s Disha Hospitality 
Pvt. Ltd., who is the 
contractor/constructing agency of the 
number of flats in the area and 
pursuant to such, a letter dated 
07.11.2013 was issued by the 
respondent no.3, categorically informing 
that his flat (C-5-1) has been booked on 
5th Floor, Block (C) for the total cost of 
Rs.114.75 lakh (including 5 KVA power 
backup etc.). 
A payment plan was also 
proposed and accordingly following that 
plan, the petitioner has paid Rs.68.50 
lakh uptil now. 
But suddenly, the respondent 
no.3 has started to show that flat of the 
petitioner was booked not on the 5th
Floor but on the 5th level. 
This is being done so because 
the respondents do not want to count 
the basement as well as ground floor 
and they do not want to count the independent floors. If it is permitted to 
happen, then, the petitioner shall be 
allotted the flat not at the 5th Floor and 
they are going to cancel the booking of 
the petitioner on 31.01.2017. 
I think the respondent no.3 
must be stopped from doing so. 
Accordingly, it is hereby directed 
that the respondents will file their 
counter affidavit within a period of four 
weeks in this Court and they shall not 
take any step for cancellation of booking 
of the flat of the petitioner in any 
manner whatsoever, until the matter is 
finally adjudicated by this Court. 
 Urgency application (IA No.412 
of 2017) and interim relief application 
(CLMA No.773 of 2017) stand disposed 
of.
