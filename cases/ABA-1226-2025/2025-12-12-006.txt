12.12.2025 ABA No.1226 of 2025
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
This is an Anticipatory Bail 
Application. 
2. Heard Mr. Deep Chandra Joshi, 
learned counsel for applicants and Mrs. 
Sweta Badola Dobhal, learned Brief 
Holder for respondent.
3. Mr. Deep Chandra Joshi, Advocate, 
submitted that the applicants were 
granted regular bail in Case Crime 
No.575 of 2025 under Sections 115(2), 
190, 191(2), 191(3), 351(3) and Section 
352 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 
2023, registered at Police Station Pathri, 
District Haridwar. Now, Section 109 of 
the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 has 
been added. Non-bailable warrants have 
been issued against the applicants. He 
further submitted on instruction that the 
applicants undertake that upon the 
cancellation of the non-bailable warrants, 
issued against the applicants, they shall 
continue to appear before the concerned 
Court. 
4. Mrs. Sweta Badola Dobhal, learned Brief Holder, has sought two weeks’ time 
to file objection to the anticipatory bail 
application. 
5. Time is granted. 
6. The execution of the non-bailable 
warrants, issued against the applicants, 
are kept-in-abeyance till the next date of 
listing. 
7. List on 10.01.2026.
