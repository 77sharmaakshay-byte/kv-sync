5. Against the order dated 03.01.2026 passed in this 
petition as well as in the connected petition the 
respondent herein Panna Vinay Shah preferred a 
Special Appeal No. 18 of 2026 and the Division Bench of this court by an order dated 09.01.2026 requests this 
court to consider and dispose of the issue regarding 
maintainability of the writ petition as well as locus 
standi of the writ petitioner.
6. Against the order dated 09.01.2026 the petitioner 
herein approached to the Hon’ble Apex Court and 
preferred Special Leave to Appeal No. 9113 of 2026 
and the Hon’ble Apex Court on 17.03.2026 dispose of 
the Special Leave Petition with the direction that the 
respondent no. 1 shall move proper affidavit raising all 
the grounds on which he is objecting to the 
maintainability and locus standi of writ petitioner and 
thereafter the learned Single Judge shall dispose of the 
same expeditiously and objectively and by a reasoned 
order without being influenced by any observation 
made by the Division Bench of the High Court.
7. Now, in compliance of the order of the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court the respondent no. 1 filed an 
additional affidavit along with IA 6 of 2026 and in 
response to this the writ petitioner filed IA 7 of 2026 
along with an affidavit, wherein, the writ petitioner 
pray for rejecting the preliminary objection of 
respondent no. 7. This IA 7 of 2026 was filed on 
01.08.2026.
8. On the same date another IA 08 of 2026 was 
moved in WPMS No. 1167 of 2024 seeking certain 
amendments for the purposes of praying for additional 
relief. 
9. I gone through with the amendment application 
IA 8 of 2026 but the question is whether this IA is 
maintainable at this juncture particularly after the 
order of the Hon’ble Apex Court dated 17.03.2026 
passed in Special Leave to Appeal No. 9113 of 2026. As 
per Supreme Court’s order First this court have to 
decide the issue on the maintainability and locus, 
therefore, at this juncture, the writ petition cannot be 
permitted to amend the prayer, first he has to address 
on the issue of maintainability and locus and only 
thereafter he may be permitted to file any amendment 
application whatsoever.
10. In such view of the matter, learned counsel for 
the applicant though earlier was adamant to press this 
application but now he makeup his mind to withdraw 
this application at this stage.
11. Accordingly, IA 8 of 2026 is dismissed as 
withdrawn at this stage.
12. So far as C528 No. 1129 of 2025 is concerned, letthis matter be also clubbed with other petitions so that
on the next date this court may also examine what 
would be the impact of the proceeding of Criminal 
Case No. 0080 of 2018 with the subject matter of 
WPMS No. 1167 of 2024 and the connected matter.
13. Learned counsel for the applicant in C528 No. 
1129 of 2025 shall handover the copy of the entire 
record to Mr. Bhupesh Kandpal.

already filed the objection and the petitioner also filed 
their response, therefore, no fresh affidavit be filed by 
either of the parties for deciding the issue on the 
maintainability and locus.
