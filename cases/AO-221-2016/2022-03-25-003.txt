These are two appeal from orders challenging 
the award of 01.02.2006, it had been as rendered in 
M.A.C.P. Case No.210 of 2012 Smt. Sudeshna & 
others vs. Mohd. Farookh & others. One of the 
appeal from order being Appeal From Order 
No.221 of 2016, has already been admitted. 
In the admitted appeal from order, the 
appellant has filed an application, being I.A. No.01 
of 2022 for deletion of appellant no.4, as he has met 
with the sad demise. 
The said application would stand allowed. 
The name of the appellant no.4, is directed to 
be deleted from the array of parties, by making 
necessary amendment in the cause title of the 
appeal from order. 
In the connected Appeal From Order i.e. 
A.O. No.233 of 2016, there is a pending delay 
condonation application, which has been preferred 
by the Insurance Company, against the same award,
where there happens to be four days delay in filing 
the appeal. 
After having considered the reasons given 
therein and being satisfied with the reasons for 
delay, the delay of four days which has chanced in 
filing the Appeal From Order No233 of 2016,
would stand condoned.
With the consent of the parties, Appeal 
From Order No.233 of 2016, is taken up for 
hearing at admission stage.
“Admit” the appeal from order no.233 of 
2016.Since the records of the court below, have 
already been summoned in the connected appeal 
from order and the parties are already represented, 
no fresh notices are required to be sent.
List this appeal from order under the head of 
hearing in the week commencing 11.04.2022. 
Place the copy of this order in the connected 
A.O. No.233 of 2016, also.
