WPMS No. 82 of 2021
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. C.K. Sharma, Advocate for the 
petitioner.
Mr. Ganga Singh Negi, Addl. CSC for 
the State/respondents.
The learned counsel for the petitioner 
contends that the land of the petitioner 
had been taken over by the respondents 
for the purpose of construction of a Dam 
from Balawali to Khanpur.
He submits that despite of taking 
over of the land, he had not been provided 
with the compensation, which would 
violate the provisions contained under 
Article 14 to be read with Article 300A of 
the Constitution of India.
Before venturing into the merits of 
the matter and to deciding the respective 
rights, let the counter affidavit be filed by 
the respondents. They may file the same 
within a period of three weeks from today.
List this writ petition thereafter.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
