COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 82 of 2021
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. C.K. Sharma, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya and Mr. Devesh 
Ghildiyal, learned Standing Counsel for 
the State.
3. In compliance to the previous 
order, Engineer-in-Chief joined the 
proceeding through V.C. and apprise this 
Court that even as on date the land of 
the petitioner, which was acquired, is still 
recorded as a agricultural land. He also 
submits that determination of the 
compensation, as reflected from the 
chart appended at page 101 is based 
upon the letter of the then District 
Magistrate Mr. Harbansh Singh Chugh 
dated 03.01.2016 and pursuant to the 
said letter, the land of the petitioner,
which was acquired, was treated to be a 
non agricultural land and thereafter 
compensation was determined. He 
further apprise to this Court that even if 
the land is located adjacent to road then 
the nature of the land shall not be 
converted rather compensation, as per 
the nature of the land, which has been 
determined to be increased by 10% and 
according to this calculation, roughly the 
amount of compensation comes to Rs. 
2,11,000/-. He further submits that the 
amount of the compensation as reflected 
from page 101 is based upon the letter of the then District Magistrate by 
treating the land as non agricultural
land, though, as a matter of fact the land 
is recorded as a agricultural land.
4. In support of this, the report of the 
Assistant Consolidation Officer, Laksar 
dated 05.05.2025 is also placed on 
record and on perusal of which it reveals 
that land in question is still recorded as
agricultural land.
5. To examine how the compensation 
has been determined as Rs. 31,68,000/-
which is reflected from the chart, as 
appended at page 101, signed by then 
officials of the Irrigation Department 
which was recommended by the 
Executive Engineer by letter dated 
02.03.2019 whether it is based upon the 
letter of the District Magistrate or not let 
the letter of the then District Magistrate, 
Haridwar 03.01.2016 be placed before 
this Court by way of an affidavit and in 
the meantime, the tentative calculation
of the actual amount of compensation 
taking into consideration that there is a 
road adjacent to land in question be also 
carried out. This Court shall also 
examine whether the District Magistrate 
is competent to issue such circular. 
6. In addition to this, in the affidavit 
the respondents shall also disclose that 
how many persons were given the 
amount of compensation by treating 
their agricultural land to be a non 
agricultural land on the basis of the 
letter of the District Magistrate dated 
03.01.2016.
7. The District Magistrate, Haridwar is 
also directed to file an affidavit giving 
details of the claimants who were given 
the compensation by treating their land 
from agriculture to non agriculture on the basis of the aforesaid letter of the 
then District Magistrate.
8. Put up this matter on 16.05.2025 
on top of the board.
9. Let a copy of this order be supplied 
to Mr. Hairiya and Mr. Ghildiyal, learned 
Standing Counsel so that they may 
communicate this order to the concerned 
District Magistrate in order to enable him 
to file an affidavit.
10. Personal appearance of the officials 
are exempted.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
