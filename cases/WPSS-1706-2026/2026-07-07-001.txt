Hon'ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Sandeep Tiwari, learned counsel for 
petitioners.
2. Mr. K.N. Joshi and Mr. N.S. Pundir, 
learned D.A.G. with Mr. Maneesh Bisht, learned 
Brief Holder for the State of Uttarakhand/
respondents.
3. These writ petitions have been filed by 
petitioners, Village Panchayat Development 
Officers (VPDOs), challenging the transfer 
order dated 27.06.2026 issued by respondent 
No.3, by which, they have been transferred 
from one Block to another.
4. The main contention of learned counsel 
for petitioners is that the transfer has been 
effected without following the provisions of the 
Uttarakhand Annual Transfer For Public 
Servants Act, 2017, (in short ‘the Transfer Act’). 
5. He further submits that transfer of 
petitioners does not fall in any category, which 
has been contemplated under Section 6 of the 
Transfer Act. The transfer order impugned only 
reflected that the transfer has been made in 
order to boost up the administration and 
further on overstaying of petitioners in their 
respective place of posting for the last more 
than 05 years.
6. Prima-facie, this Court finds substance in 
the contention raised by learned counsel for 
petitioners. Section 6 of the Transfer Act 
contemplates the following transfers:-
(a) Compulsory transfer from accessible area 
to remote area;
(b) Compulsory transfer from remote area to 
accessible area; and 
(c) Transfer on the basis of request.
7. The transfer order impugned does not fall 
in any of the above category. If it is a transfer 
under category (a) and (b) as stated above, it 
is not clear as to whether respondents have 
prepared any eligibility list for effecting the 
transfer under category (a) and (b).
8. In such view of the matter, learned State 
Counsel is directed to seek instruction in the 
matter within two days.
9. Put up as fresh on 13.07.2026.
10. Till then, petitioners shall not be relieved 
from their place of posting.
