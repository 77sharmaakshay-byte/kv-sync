On 23.11.2023, this Court had kept 
the proceedings of Criminal Case No. 8629 
of 2022, State Vs. Neeraj Laushali, under 
Sections 376, 323, 506 IPC, pending in the 
court of Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Dehradun (“the case”) in abeyance. This 
order was further extended on 19.02.2024.
Now an Application IA No. 2 of 2025 
has been filed by the petitioner on the 
ground that despite stay, non bailable 
warrants have been issued against the 
petitioner.
This Court makes it clear that till the 
next date of listing, the proceedings of the 
case shall remain in abeyance.
In case, any coercive process is 
admissive, in between their effect shall also 
remain in abeyance.
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
seeks time to file rejoinder affidavit.
Let it be so filed within three weeks.
List on 22.07.2025 for final hearing.
IA No. 2 of 2025 stands disposed of 
accordingly.
