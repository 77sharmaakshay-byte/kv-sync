The challenge in this petition is 
made to the charge sheet dated 
07.09.2022, cognizance order dated 
17.12.2022 in Criminal Case No. 8629 of 
2022, State Vs. Neeraj Laushali under 
Sections 376, 323, 506 IPC , pending in 
the court of Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Dehradun (“the case”). By which the 
petitioner has been summoned to answer 
the accusations under Section 323, 376, 
506 IPC.
The case is based on a report lodged 
by the respondent no.2, the informant. 
According to the FIR, the petitioner 
and the informant came close to each 
other in the year 2018, when they were 
into the modelling business. 
Subsequently, under the pretext of 
marriage, according to the FIR, the 
applicant established physical relations 
with the victim. But, he did not 
solemnized the marriage, instead the 
parents of the petitioner demanded Rs.10 
lakh and a car. Thereafter, the petitioner, 
according to the FIR, threatened the 
informant.
It is case of the petitioner that the 
relationship was consensual. The 
petitioner and the informant both are
modelling partner. They are major. It is 
not a case of rape.Learned counsel for the informant 
would submit that, prima facie, the case 
is made out. The factual dispute cannot 
be decided in this petition.
What would be required for 
deliberation is as to whether it is a case 
of misconception of fact or as to whether 
the consent was free?
Having considered, this Court is of 
the view that this matter requires 
deliberations.
Learned C.S.C. takes notice on 
behalf of the State/respondent no.1
Mr. Kaushal Sah Jagati, Advocate 
takes notice on behalf of the respondent 
no.2.
Respondents may file counter 
affidavit within three weeks. Two weeks 
thereafter, rejoinder affidavit, if any, may 
be filed.
List this matter on 19.02.2024.
Having considered, till the next date 
of listing, further proceedings of the court 
below shall remain in abeyance.
