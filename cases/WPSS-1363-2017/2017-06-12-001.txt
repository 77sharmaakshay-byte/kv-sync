 
 
WPSS No. 1363 of 2017
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate for 
the petitioner. 
Mr. Mahender Singh Rawat, 
Advocate, holding brief of Mr. D.S. Patni, 
Advocate for the respondent. 
Heard learned counsel for the 
parties. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the petitioner retired as 
Administrative Officer on 31.01.2017 
from Uttarakhand Payjal Nigam, 
Haldwani, District Nainital. His grievance 
is that although his retiral dues have 
been sanctioned by the Competent 
Authority/MD, but the amount of retiral 
dues has not been released so far. 
Learned counsel for the respondent 
submits that due to poor financial health 
of Uttarakhand Payjal Nigam, some delay 
has been caused in releasing the 
outstanding dues of the petitioner and he 
further submits that as and when funds 
are released by the State Government, 
outstanding dues of the petitioner would 
be paid to him. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
has placed reliance upon the judgment 
rendered by Division Bench of this Court 
in Writ Petition (S/B) No. 494 of 2015 
and submits that this writ petition may 
be disposed of in terms of the said 
judgment. Mr. M.S. Rawat, Advocate, 
holding brief of Mr. D.S. Patni, Advocate, 
appearing for the respondent has no 
objection, if the writ petition is disposed 
of in terms of the judgment rendered by 
Division Bench of this Court. 
Accordingly, the writ petition is 
disposed of in terms of the judgment rendered by Division Bench of this Court 
in Writ Petition (S/B) No. 494 of 2015. 
Interim Relief Application No. 6405 
of 2017 stands disposed of. 
 (Manoj K. Tiwari,J.)
