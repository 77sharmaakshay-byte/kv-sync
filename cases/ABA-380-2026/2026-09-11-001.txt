Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Anchit Khokher, learned counsel for 
the applicant.
2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned A.G.A. along 
with Mr. Nikhil Bisht, learned Brief Holder for 
the State.
3. By means of the present application, 
the applicants seek anticipatory bail in 
connection with F.I.R./Case Crime No. 224 
of 2026 dated 10.08.2019, registered at 
Police Station Bazpur, District Udham Singh 
Nagar, for the offences punishable under 
Sections 306 and 506 I.P.C.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would submit that the impugned F.I.R. has 
been lodged by the father of the deceased, 
alleging, inter alia, that the deceased was 
married to the co-accused in the year 2014 
and, subsequent to the marriage, the 
deceased was continuously subjected to 
mental and physical harassment by the 
family members of his wife. It is further 
alleged that the deceased was pressurized to 
live separately from his parents and that a 
sum of ₹3,00,000/- belonging to the deceased was wrongfully retained by the 
accused persons and when the deceased 
demanded return of the said amount, he 
was allegedly threatened with dire 
consequences. It is further alleged that, on 
account of the aforesaid harassment, the 
deceased was driven to commit suicide by 
hanging himself at his matrimonial house on 
02.08.2019.
5. He would further submit that, upon 
completion of the investigation, the 
Investigating Officer has submitted the 
charge-sheet against the applicant and other 
co-accused persons; that, the applicant is 
the uncle of the wife of the deceased and has 
falsely been implicated in the present case 
on account of his relationship with the co￾accused. He would submit that the applicant 
resides separately from the family of his 
niece and her in-laws and has no close 
association or even regular interaction with 
them.
6. It is further submitted that the 
allegation against the applicant is sought to 
be supported primarily on the basis of an 
alleged suicide note, stated to have been 
found written on the thigh of the deceased. 
He would contend that there is no specific or 
proximate overt act attributed to the 
applicant, nor is there any allegation of any 
positive act of instigation, intentional aid or 
abetment on his part which could constitute 
the offence punishable under Sections 306 
and 506 of the I.P.C. 
7. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would further submit that the applicant is 
suffering from serious medical ailments and 
is in a highly debilitated condition; that, he has been diagnosed with leprosy and is also 
suffering from severe skin rashes and 
persistent itching, and his medical condition 
has recently deteriorated. It is submitted 
that, having regard to his fragile health 
condition and the nature of the ailments 
from which he is suffering, his custodial 
detention may seriously prejudice his 
health. He would further submit that the 
charge-sheet has already been filed before 
the competent court and, therefore, no 
custodial interrogation of the applicant is 
required, therefore, the applicant may be 
granted interim protection.
8. Per contra, learned State Counsel 
would oppose the prayer for anticipatory bail 
and submit that the allegations levelled 
against the applicant are serious in nature. 
However, learned State Counsel seeks some 
time to place the complete instructions/
material on record and to file a detailed 
counter affidavit.
9. In the interest of justice, four weeks' 
time is granted to learned State Counsel to 
file a detailed counter affidavit.
10. List this case on 12.10.2026.
11. Having considered the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the parties 
and upon perusal of the material available 
on record, this Court finds that the charge￾sheet has already been submitted before the 
competent court. The applicant has also 
placed on record his medical condition, 
which requires due consideration. Further, 
having regard to the nature of the 
allegations, the role attributed to the 
applicant, the fact that the investigation has 
culminated in filing of the charge-sheet, and without expressing any opinion on the 
merits of the case, this Court is of the prima 
facie view that the applicant has made out a 
case for grant of interim protection.
12. Accordingly, in the event of arrest, the 
applicant shall be released on interim 
anticipatory bail till the next date of listing, 
on his furnishing a personal bond and two 
reliable sureties, each in the like amount, to 
the satisfaction of the arresting officer/court 
concerned, subject to the following 
conditions:
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation and shall appear before the 
Investigating Officer as and when required;
(ii) The applicant shall not directly or 
indirectly make any inducement, threat or 
promise to any person acquainted with the 
facts of the case so as to dissuade such 
person from disclosing such facts to the 
Court or to the Investigating Agency;
(iii) The applicant shall not leave the 
territory of India without prior permission 
of the Court concerned;
(iv) The applicant shall not misuse the 
liberty granted by this order and shall 
maintain peace and good conduct during 
the period of interim protection;
(v) In case of breach of any of the aforesaid 
conditions, it shall be open to the 
State/prosecution to seek modification or 
cancellation of interim anticipatory bail in 
accordance with law.
