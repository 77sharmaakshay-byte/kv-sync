COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.2152 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for 
the petitioners.
2. Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned Brief Holder for 
the State.
3. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
4. The writ petition is filed by the 
petitioners seeking indulgence of this Court 
for quashing the notification dated 
22.11.2023 as well as the notification dated 
17.07.2020, annexure-14 and 6 to the writ 
petition, respectively and further a direction 
to the respondent no.3 to complete the 
promotion exercise to the post of Assistant 
Teacher, L.T. Grade, initiated pursuant to the 
advertisement dated 20.06.2019 on the basis 
of the Statutory Rules, as prevalent on 
19.06.2019.
5. On 30.11.2023, learned State Counsel 
was directed to seek instructions in the 
matter. Today, learned State counsel submits
written instructions dated 01.12.223, on the 
basis of which, it is submitted that the 
promotion exercise would be conducted in 
accordance with the amended Rules dated 
23.12.2019. 
6. These instructions run counter to the 
judgment and order passed by this Court 
wherein it is has been categorically held in 
Writ Petition (S/S) No.366 of 2023, Smt. 
Geeta Verma vs. Secretary School Education, 2
Uttarkhand & others that the promotional 
exercise which was initiated on the basis of 
the advertisement dated 20.06.2019 shall be 
conducted strictly in accordance with the 
Rules, which were in existence on that date. 
The instructions which have been provided to 
learned State Counsel are in uttered 
disobedience of the judgment and order 
passed by this Court, therefore, respondent
no.3 i.e. Additional Director of Secondary 
Education, Nainital should be cautious enough 
before sending such instructions. 
7. Learned State Counsel prays for and is 
granted four weeks’ time to file counter 
affidavit.
8. Since issue involved in this writ petition 
is no longer res integra, therefore, 
respondents are directed to permit the 
petitioners to participate in the counseling to 
be held on 02.12.2023 for the post of 
Assistant Teacher, L.T. Grade (Hindi). 
9. List this matter on 09.01.2024.
10. Interim relief application (IA No.1/2023) 
stands disposed of accordingly.
11. Let a certified copy of this order be 
issued to learned counsel for the parties, 
today itself, on payment of usual charges.
 
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
