05.07.2024 C482 No.1206 of 2024
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The present Application under 
Section 482 of the Code of Criminal 
Procedure, 1973 has been filed to quash 
the entire proceedings of Criminal Case 
No.1217 of 2023, “State vs. Yogendra 
Singh Kamboj and Others”, pending 
before the Court of Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Tehri Garhwal under Sections 
409, 420, 467, 468, 471 and Section 
120B of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 
arising out of FIR No.26 of 2020, 
registered at police station Tehri, District 
Tehri Garhwal.
2. Heard Mr. Lalit Sharma, learned 
counsel for the applicant and Mr. Rakesh 
Negi, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Mr. Lalit Sharma, Advocate, 
contended that the Institute in-question 
namely, “Swami Vivekanand Polytechnic 
College”, was run by a Trust, namely, 
“Swami Vivekanand Educational Trust”. 
Applicant is one of the trustee as well as 
the chairman of the said Trust. He was 
not named in the First Information Report. After completion of the 
investigation, charge-sheet has been 
filed against two accused. Charge-sheet 
has not been filed against the applicant 
as he has not been found to have 
committed any offence.
4. Learned counsel appearing for the 
State has sought four weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit.
5. List on 03.09.2024.
6. Till then, the further proceedings of 
Criminal Case No.1217 of 2023, “State 
vs. Yogendra Singh Kamboj and Others”, 
pending before the Court of Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Tehri Garhwal, are stayed 
qua the applicant – Ashwani Kumar 
Kamboj.
