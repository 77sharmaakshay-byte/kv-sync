COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
AO No.486 of 2017
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J (Through V.C.)
1. Mr. Naresh Pant, learned counsel for 
the appellant.
2. Mr. Bhuwan Bhatt, learned counsel for 
the respondent.
3. The service is duly affected upon 
respondent No.3-Owner of the vehicle, despite 
this, no one put appearance on his behalf.
4. The counsel for both the parties apprise 
to this Court that even before the MACT, the 
owner of vehicle have not contested the case 
and that is the reason the matter was 
proceeded ex-parte against him, but the 
liability has been fastened upon the 
Insurance Company.
5. Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that at the time of the accident, the 
Driver of the vehicle was not having valid 
Driving License, therefore, in view of the 
judgment of Hon’ble Apex Court in the Case 
of Gohar Mohammad versus Uttar Pradesh 
State Road Transport Corporation and 
Others, (2023) 4 SCC, 381, the liability can 
only be imposed upon the owner of the 
vehicle.
6. In the present proceeding, the owner of 
the vehicle is deliberately avoiding to 
participate, therefore, in such an eventuality 
there is no option except to issue a Bailable 
Warrant against the respondent No.3.
7. The CJM concerned is directed to 
execute the Bailable Warrant.
8. List this matter on 07.10.2025.
9. The Registry is directed to communicate 
about this order to the CJM concerned. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
