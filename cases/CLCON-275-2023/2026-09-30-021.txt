3. The present contempt petition has 
been filed by the petitioner for willful, 
disobedience of the judgment and order 
dated 08.08.2019 passed by the Division 
Bench of this Court.
4. It is contended by learned Senior 
Advocate for the petitioner that order dated 
08.08.2019 passed by the Division Bench of 
this Court has not been complied with, 
whereby the respondents were directed to 
revise the seniority list of Deputy Collectors 
in terms of the observations made in the 
said order and to extend the benefit of their 
regular appointment w.e.f. 08.04.2010 
treated as relating back to the period from 
01.10.2007.
5. Against the said order, four special 
leave petitions were filed before the Apex 
Court including by the respondents in the 
writ petition, who were direct recruits. All those SLPs were dismissed and the State 
Government was directed to issue a revised 
seniority list of Deputy Collectors within a 
period of three months from the date of 
judgment i.e. 29.07.2026.
6. Today, learned CSC has supplied 
instructions to the Court date 29.09.2026, 
which are taken on record. On the basis of 
those instructions, it was contended that the 
State is sincerely making efforts to comply
with the order passed by the writ Court. It is 
also submitted that three months’ time will
come to an end on 29.10.2026.
7. Since the respondents have lost before 
the Apex Court, in such view of the matter, 
it is expected by the respondents￾contemnors to comply with the order dated 
08.08.2019 passed by the Division Bench in 
its letter and spirit in between.
8. List this matter on 30.10.2026.
