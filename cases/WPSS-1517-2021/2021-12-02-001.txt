Learned counsel for the petitioner is directed to 
inform the Court, that as a consequence of the 
affirmation of the judgment of 7th March, 2006, which 
was rendered in Writ Petition (S/S) No. 687 of 2005, 
Balak Ram Vs. State of Uttaranchal and others, the 
Coordinate Bench of this Court had directed the 
respondents to consider the grant of relaxation in the 
upper age limit, to the petitioner, to the extent of his 
period of service rendered on daily wages, after its 
affirmation by the Division Bench and later on by the 
Apex Court vide its judgement of 28th October, 2013, as 
rendered in SLP (Civil) No.(s) 13557 of 2012, what 
orders have been passed by the respondents for the 
purposes of the grant of relaxation to the petitioner for 
inclusion of the services of the petitioner for the grant 
of service benefits, including the benefit of 
regularization. 
Necessary information would be supplied by the 
petitioner within a period of one week from today.
