D/2 C-447 No.34 of 2025
With

Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
 Mr. Ram Bharose Kala (through video 
conferencing) and Mr. Pankaj Semwal, 
Advocates for the applicants.
 Mr. Virendra Singh Rawat, AGA for the 
State. 
 Heard.
 In the year 2020, the respondent no.2 
filed a complaint against the applicants in 
the court of Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Rudraprayag, which is basis of Criminal 
Case No.42 of 2020 (New No.365 of 2020), 
Smt. Santoshi Rana Vs. Sunit Rana and 
others, under Sections 323, 498-A, 504, 
506 IPC and Sections 3, 4 of the Dowry 
Prohibition Act, 1961 (“the case”). The 
applicants seeks transfer of the case on the 
ground that now the respondent no.2 is 
staying at Kotdwar. 
 When this matter was taken up, learned 
State Counsel invited the Court’s attention 
that in another case, the respondent no.2 
was provided legal aid at the State 
expenses and the transfer petition has 
recently been filed on behalf of the 
respondent no.2. Thereafter, Mr. Prince 
Chauhan, Advocate appeared and he 
submits that the respondent no.2 has filed 
a transfer application for transferring her 
suit from Kotdwar to Rudraprayag, as she 
is staying in Rudraprayag, in which, the 
Coordinate Bench has granted stay of the 
proceedings at Kotdwar and notices have 
been issued (in C-447 Nos.30 of 2025 and 
31 of 2025).  Instant petition has been filed on the 
ground that the respondent no.2/Santoshi 
Rana is staying in Kotdwar, therefore, the 
case pending in the court of Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Rudraprayag may be 
transferred to the court at Kotdwar. 
 Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that, in fact, in a proceedings 
under Section 127 of the Code of Criminal 
Procedure, 1973, the respondent no.2 was 
cross-examined on 12.08.2024 in the court 
at Rudraprayag. She admitted that she was 
staying in a rented accommodation at 
Kotdwar. 
 Today, Mr. Prince Chauhan, Advocate 
submits that earlier respondent no.2 was 
staying in a rented accommodation at 
Kotdwar and now, she is not there. She 
has returned to her father’s house at 
Rudraprayag. 
 Let respondent no.2 file objections to the 
stay application.
 List along with C-447 Nos.30 of 2025 
and 31 of 2025. 
 
 
(Ravindra Maithani, J.)
