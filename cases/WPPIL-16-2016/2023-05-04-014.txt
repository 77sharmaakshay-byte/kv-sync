10. Mr. Aman Rab, learned counsel appears for 
the State Environmental Impact Assessment 
Authority (SEIAA). He states that the order dated 
21.04.2023 was communicated to SEIAA only on 1st
May, 2023. He submits that SEIAA will require four 
weeks’ time to comply with the said order. 
11. Mr. Rawat, learned Senior Counsel, who 
appears for the State of Uttarakhand, seeks further 
six weeks’ time to comply with our earlier direction 
with regard to constitution / expansion of an 
independent Complaint Redressal Committee. 
12. We, accordingly, grant time to SEIAA and the 
State, as prayed for. 
13. The petitioner has filed this Recall Application 
(IA No. 19 of 2023) to say that it is not the SEIAA,
but the NMCG, which can take a decision on,
whether, or not, to permit mining in any stretch of 
river Ganga. 
14. Let response to this Application be filed by 
the National Mission for Clean Ganga, the Ministry 
of Environment, Forests and Climate Change, and 
by the State Government within four weeks. 
15. Rejoinder, if any, be filed before the next 
date. 
16. The compliance of our directions, aforesaid, shall be made without prejudice to the rights and 
contentions of the parties, and the pendency of the 
present Application shall not come in the way of the 
compliance of the said order. 
17. The petitioner is directed to implead “the 
State Environmental Impact Assessment Authority 
(SEIAA)” as a party respondent, and to provide a 
copy of the complete paper book to learned counsel 
for SEIAA within two days. 
18. List on 14.07.2023, along with the connected 
matters. 
19. Interim order to continue till the next date.
