Hon’ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
Mr. Navneet Kaushik, Advocate for 
the petitioner. 
Mr. Yogesh Pandey, Addl. C.S.C. for 
the State. 
Heard. 
Present writ petition has been for 
quashing the impugned order dated 
04.02.2019 passed by respondent no. 1 
[District Magistrate (appropriate 
Authority) Pre-Natal Diagnostic 
Techniques Udham Singh Nagar]. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that petitioners are not 
willing to continue their ultrasound clinic 
in the name and style Brij Lal Hospital & 
C.T. Scan Centre, Naginal Palace, 
Sitarganj District Udham Singh Nagar 
and are ready to donate the machines to 
the State Government. 
The grievance of the petitioner is 
only to the extent with the observation 
made by the District Appropriate 
Authority/District Magistrate (PC-NDT) 
Udham Singh Nagar in the order dated 
04.02.2019 that the complaint case be 
lodged against the petitioners. 
Perusal of the impugned order 
would reveal that though order of 
cancellation has been passed after 
affording opportunity of hearing to the 
petitioners but the fact remains that a 
show cause notice was not issued to the 
petitioners as to why complaint has not 
been lodged against the petitioner. Thus, 
the last portion of the impugned order is 
without jurisdiction. As prayed, three weeks’ time is 
granted to the learned counsel for the 
respondents to file the counter affidavit. 
List thereafter. In the meanwhile, 
the effect and operation of the impugned 
order dated 04.02.2019 qua lodging the 
complaint against the petitioners shall 
remain stayed. 
 (Lok Pal Singh, J.) 
Parul 20.02.2019
