CLMA 6093/2016 (For delay condonation application in time barred AO)
IA 6095/2025 (For Money Withdrawal Application)
In
AO No. 329 of 2016
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. I.P. Kohli, learned counsel for the appellant.
2. Mr. Rakshit Srivastava, learned counsel for the 
respondents.
3. Money withdrawal application has been moved 
by the claimants for withdrawing the amount towards 
compensation.
4. As per the office report there is delay of 44 days. 
The objections were invited on 29.06.2016 and the Trial 
Court record was also summoned and simultaneously 
the appellant was directed to deposit 50 percent of the 
total awarded amount and subject to this condition the 
recovery in pursuance to the impugned judgment was 
also stayed.
5. Delay is not opposed. Accordingly the delay 
condonation application is allowed and the delay is 
condoned.
6. Admit the appeal.
7. Now, the money withdrawal application has 
been moved by the claimants praying for withdrawal
the amount.
8. Learned counsel Mr. I.P. Kohli submits that the 
appellant complied with the order and 50 percent 
amount has already been deposited before the Motor
Accident Claim Tribunal. The money withdrawal 
application has been moved on the ground that the 
claimant is still under treatment.
9. Money withdrawal application is allowed.
10. Rs. 10 lakhs out of the deposit made pursuant to 
the order dated 29.06.2016 is permitted to the 
withdrawn by the claimants. For the purposes of 
withdrawal he shall move an application before the 
concerned Tribunal and on such application the 
concerned Tribunal shall release the amount forthwith.11. List this matter for final hearing in the month of 
March, 2026.
