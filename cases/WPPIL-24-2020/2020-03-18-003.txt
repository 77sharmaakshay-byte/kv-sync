1. We had, in our order dated 2.3.2020, noted the submissions of Dr. 
K.H. Gupta, learned Counsel for the petitioner, that none of the establishments, 
which slaughter animals in the Doon Valley, have either been granted a ‘No 
Objection Certificate’ from the Ministry of Environment & Forests, Govt. of 
India, nor have any efforts been made by the State Government to ascertain 
whether each one of them discharge effluents of 500 kiloliters per day or more. 
The third respondent was directed to forthwith cause an inspection of all 
establishments in the Doon Valley where animals are slaughtered, and submit a 
report to this Court as to how many of these industries have been established 
after receipt of a ‘No Objection Certificate’ from the Ministry of Environment 
& Forests, Govt. of India, and how many of them discharge effluents of 500 
kiloliters per day or more. 
2. Pursuant to the aforesaid directions, the third respondent has now filed 
his report, wherein he has stated that he had directed the Regional Office, 
Uttarakhand Pollution Control Board to submit its report; the report dated 
06.03.2020 stated that there was a single slaughter house in Bhandari Bagh and, 
in that slaughter house, no slaughtering is being carried out; apart from the 
aforesaid, no slaughter house is established in Dehradun area; and he had 
himself visited the slaughter house on 11.3.2020 situate at Bhandari Bagh and 
had found that a notice was pasted on the main gate to the effect that the 
slaughter house was closed until further orders. 2
3. If as is now contended by the District Magistrate, the only slaughter 
house in the Doon Valley has been closed, it defies comprehension how animals 
continue to be slaughtered and sold in the city of Dehradun, the entire area of 
which falls within the Doon valley. The petitioner has, along with the writ 
affidavit, enclosed photographs of truckloads of livestock being brought into the 
city of Dehradun. He has also filed photographs of the market where 
chicken/fish and meat are being sold. 
4. While a feeble attempt is made by Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned Additional 
Chief Standing Counsel for the State, to submit that what is brought into the 
city of Dehradun is only frozen meat imported from outside the State, the 
respondents should then have no objection to the grant of relief sought for in the 
writ petition, which is for a mandamus to the State of Uttarakhand and other 
officials not to permit entry of livestock/chicken/goat /sheep/buffalo/fish into 
the State of Uttarakhand. 
5. It is evident that the report of the District Magistrate, Dehradun is 
evasive and gives rise to the suspicion that attempts are being made to gloss 
over slaughter of animals in the Doon Valley contrary to the Notifications 
issued by the Government of India, reference to which has been made in our 
earlier order dated 02.03.2020. The third respondent shall be present in the 
Court on 23.03.2020 to explain how hen/goat/sheep/buffalo/fish are brought 
into the Doon Valley and sold thereat, either as livestock or as meat, if, as is 
now contended before us, the only slaughter house in the city of Dehradun has 
been closed. 
6. Post on 23.03.2010 at 10:15 AM in the daily list, for the presence of 
the District Magistrate, Dehradun. 
7. Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned Additional Chief Standing Counsel, is
