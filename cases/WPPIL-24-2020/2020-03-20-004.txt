Civil Misc. Application No.4491 of 2020 is 
filed seeking exemption from personal appearance 
of the applicant on 23.03.2020. In the affidavit, 
filed in support of the application, it is submitted 
that the District Magistrate is the Ex-officio 
Chairman of the District Disaster Management 
Authority; in view of the Novel Corona Virus 
Epidemic in the State, the Incident Response Team 
has been constituted under the Disaster 
Management Act, 2005. One person has been 
found to be positive in District Dehradun. The 
deponent, being the Ex-officio Chairman of the 
District Disaster Management Authority is 
responsible for the Incident Response Team; and 
his personal appearance may, therefore, be 
dispensed with. 
We had, in our order dated 18.03.2020, 
found the report submitted by the District 
Magistrate, Dehradun evasive, and as giving rise 
to the suspicion that attempts were being made to 
gloss over the slaughter of animals in the Doon 
Valley, contrary to the Notifications issued by the 
Government of India. It is in such circumstances, 
that we had directed the District Magistrate, 
Dehradun to appear before this Court on 
23.03.2020. 
While, we see no reason to dispense with his 
appearance, in the light of what we had observed 
in our order dated 18.03.2020, the present situation 
warrants restraint. The coronavirus epidemic in the 
State, with one case of a coronavirus patient being 
found positive, has been referred to in the 
affidavit. Two more cases have been identified 
yesterday as coronavirus positive in Dehradun. We, therefore, defer appearance of the 
District Magistrate by a further period of four 
weeks. But for the fact that the situation is 
alarming, we may not have been inclined to show 
any such indulgence. 
List on 24.04.2020, on which date the 
District Magistrate, Dehradun shall be personally 
present in Court at 10:15 a.m.
