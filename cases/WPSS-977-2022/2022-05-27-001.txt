WPSS No. 977 of 2022
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J.
Mr. Atul Bahuguna, Advocate for the 
petitioners. 
Mr. V.S. Rawat, Brief Holder for the 
State of Uttarakhand.
Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
Notice has been accepted by Mr. 
Dharmendra Barthwal, Advocate on behalf 
of respondent nos. 2 & 3, however, there is 
no representation on their behalf.
Let counter affidavit be filed within 
four weeks. Two weeks’ time, thereafter, is 
allowed for filing rejoinder affidavit. 
List this matter after six weeks.
It is contended by learned counsel for 
the petitioners that, despite scoring more 
than 50% marks in aggregate in terms of 
regulations, petitioners have not been 
recommended to be appointed on the post 
of Assistant Accountant.
Considering the submission advanced 
by learned counsel for the parties, it is 
directed that, till the next date of listing, 
promotion quota vacancies available on the 
post of Assistant Accountant, shall not be 
filled. 
Interim relief application (I.A. No. 1 of 
2022) stands disposed of. 
Petitioners’ counsel shall inform Mr. 
Dharemendra Barthwal about this order in 
writing, within 24 hours.
