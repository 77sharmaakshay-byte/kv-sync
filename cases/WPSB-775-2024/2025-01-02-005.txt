Standing Counsel, for the respondent no. 4.
(2) Learned Counsel for the 
petitioners submits that petitioner no. 1 and 
2 were appointed, after selection, as 
Computer Literate Steno and Divisional 
Assistant respectively in the year 2002 and 
in view of their work and conduct, they 
were appointed on higher post of Assistant 
Director and they have been serving 
continuously under Uttarakhand State Aids 
Control Society for the last more than two 
decades. Petitioners are aggrieved by 
termination of their service. 
(3) Mr. Rahul Consul, learned 
Counsel appearing for the respondent no. 2 
and 3, submits that petitioners were 
appointed on contract, which was extended 
from time to time. He submits that posts, 
on which petitioners were serving, have 
now been abolished by National Aids 
Control Organisation, therefore petitioners’
appointment was discontinued. 
(4) Learned Counsel for the 
petitioners submits that in other States, 
persons holding similar designations are 
continuing and services of as many as 354 
persons, serving under State Aids Control Society in Manipur, have also been 
regularised. He thus submits that the act of 
respondent no. 2 in terminating services of
petitioners is arbitrary and illegal. 
(5) Since petitioners have put in 
continuous service of more than two 
decades and there is no adverse material 
against the petitoiners, therefore, having 
regard to the long services rendered with 
Uttarakhand State Aids Control Society by 
petitioners, we provide that till the next 
date of listing, petitioners shall be permitted 
to serve on the same position as before and
they shall also be paid the remuneration, as 
they were getting earlier.
(6) Counter affidavit filed on behalf of 
respondent no. 2 and 3 are taken on 
record. Application (IA/2/2024) made 
therefor stands disposed of. Other 
respondents may file counter affidavit 
within four weeks. Rejoinder affidavit to 
counter affidavit of respondent no. 2 and 3, 
if any, be filed within four weeks.
(7) List on 17.3.2025.
(8) Interim relief application 
(IA/1/2024) stands disposed of.
