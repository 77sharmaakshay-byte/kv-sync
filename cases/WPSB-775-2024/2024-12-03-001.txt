COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 2284 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Vikas Pandey, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. The grievance of the petitioners is that petitioners 
were engaged on contractual basis in the year 2002, 
however, in the year 2023 they have been discontinued 
on the ground that posts against which they were 
working on contractual basis are not sanctioned. In 
paragraph 15 and 16 of the writ petition, learned 
counsel for the petitioner has given reference of other 
States wherein similarly situated incumbents have been 
either regularised or absorbed. He further made a 
reference of the order passed by the Telangana High 
Court in identical matter wherein the Telangana High 
Court directed the Telangana Government to continue 
those incumbents, who were decided to be disengaged 
by the Telangana Government. He further submits that 
in different States, similarly situated incumbents were 
adjusted in other posts under the same project. He 
further submits that the project is still continuing.
3. Mr. R.S. Bisht, learned Standing Counsel for the 
State accepts notice on behalf of respondent no. 1.
4. Mr. Rahul Consul, learned counsel, accepts notice 
on behalf of respondents no. 2 and 3 through V.C.. 
5. Issue notice to respondent no. 4 by registered 
post acknowledgement due.
6. Steps be taken with a week.
7. Mr. Rahul Consul, learned counsel for the 
respondents no. 2 and 3, shall get instructions about the 
averments made in paragraph 15 and 16 of the writ 
petition, as well as shall apprise this Court why same 
benefit should not be given to the petitioners, as given 
by the High Court of Telangana. 
8. List on 12.12.2024, as fresh.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
