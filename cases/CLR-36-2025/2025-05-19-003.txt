COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
19.05.2025 CLR No.36 of 2025
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The proposed revisionist – plaintiff filed 
an Original Suit (O.S. No.227 of 2021) 
against the respondent no.1 and respondent 
no.2, his brother and wife of the brother, for 
possession. An Application under Order I 
Rule 10 read with Section 151 of the Code of 
Civil Procedure, 1908 was filed by the 
respondent nos.3 to 6 herein. The said 
Application has been allowed vide impugned 
order dated 25.01.2025, passed by learned 
Civil Judge (Senior Division), Haridwar. 
2. Heard Mr. Atul Kumar Bansal, learned 
counsel for the proposed revisionist.
3. Mr. Atul Kumar Bansal, Advocate, 
contended that the suit property was 
purchased by the mother of the plaintiff and 
defendant no.1 through a sale-deed from 
Sushil Kumar and Sunil Kumar. Sunil Kumar 
is respondent no.3 and respondent no.5 and 
respondent no.6 are the legal 
representatives of Late Sushil Kumar. A 
registered gift deed dated 29.03.2010 was 
executed by the mother of the plaintiff in his 
favour. The case of the respondent nos.3 to 6 is that the mother of the plaintiff and 
defendant no.1 purchased the suit property 
from Sushil Kumar and Sunil Kumar through 
a registered sale-deed dated 05.09.2007, 
but the consideration was not paid by Late 
Smt. Aruna Rani, the mother of the plaintiff 
and defendant no.1.
4. Mr. Atul Kumar Bansal, Advocate, 
further submitted that the entire 
consideration was paid by Late Smt. Aruna 
Rani to Sushil Kumar and Sunil Kumar.
5. Admit.
6. Notice is issued to the respondents. 
Steps to be taken within three days by all 
the permissible modes.
7. List on 19.06.2025.
8. Till then, the further proceedings of 
Original Suit No.227 of 2021 are stayed.
9. Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2025) 
stands disposed of accordingly. 
(Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
