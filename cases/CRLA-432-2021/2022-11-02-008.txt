Mr. Naman Kamboj, learned counsel for

Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Deputy Advocate 
General for the State.
The appellant was granted extension of 
short term bail application. He was granted 
short term bail on 07.09.2022 in IA No. 5 of 
2022 for 45 days, which is expiring today.
It is submitted by the learned counsel 
for the appellant that the appellant was 
operated on 28.10.2022 and because of the
complications of the operation, he has been 
advised to take further bed rest. However, no 
such document has been attached to the 
application.
Hence, keeping the short term bail 
extension application pending, we give 
liberty to the appellant to file appropriate 
documents through an affidavit within seven 
days with the stipulation that the short term 
bail, as granted to the appellant, shall be 
extended by another seven days i.e. till 
09.11.2022.
The matter be listed on 09.11.2022.
Urgent copy of this order be supplied to 
the learned counsel for the parties, as per 
Rules.
