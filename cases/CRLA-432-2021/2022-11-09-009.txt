This application has been filed by the 
applicant – Amit Parley for extension of the 
short term bail. 
It is also noted that earlier occasion, the 
applicant has been granted short term bail on 
07.09.2022 by this Court on the ground that 
he was suffering from AC joint injury and he 
was recommended for treatment by surgical 
intervention. Thereafter, the surgical 
intervention was carried out on 28.10.2022.
Thereafter, on the submissions made by 
the learned counsel for the appellant, on the 
last date i.e. on 02.11.2022, seven days 
extension was granted to him with liberty to 
the appellant to file appropriate documents 
through an affidavit to extend the short term 
bail application granted by us to the applicant 
– Amit Parley. 
Today, in the Court, the learned counsel 
for the appellant files supplementary affidavit, which is taken on record and the 
Registry is directed to register the same as an 
interim application. 
We have also perused the documents, 
especially the document appearing at Page 
No. 33 of the supplementary affidavit, which 
has been issued by the doctor on 07.11.2022
advising the appellant take rest, for post￾operation recovery, up to 24.11.2022.
In that view of the matter, we are 
inclined to allow the application for 
extension.
Interim bail, granted by us on 
07.09.2022, which was extended by us for 
another seven days on 02.11.2022 is hereby 
extended till 28.11.2022 on the same bail 
bond executed by the appellant before the 
learned 4th Additional Sessions Judge, 
Dehradun. 
The application (IA No. 07 of 2022) for 
extension of short term bail is allowed. 
Let the applicant – Amit Parley 
surrender before the Court on 29.11.2022. To 
that extent, the time extension application is 
allowed and this order may be read as part of 
the original order passed by us on 
07.09.2022.
List this matter on 28.02.2023 for final 
disposal.
 
(Alok Kumar Verma, J.) (Sanjaya Kumar Mishra, J.)
