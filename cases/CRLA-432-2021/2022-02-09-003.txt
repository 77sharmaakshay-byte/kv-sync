Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Naman Kamboj, Advocate 
for the appellant.
Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, A.G.A for 
the State.
This matter is heard through 
Video Conferencing.
Appellant Amit Parley has been 
convicted and sentenced in Sessions 
Trial No. 144 of 2018, by the Court of 
4th Additional Sessions Judge, 
Dehradun. His appeal is pending. He 
has moved a short term bail 
application no. 2 of 2022 on the 
ground that the marriage of his 
cousin sister is to be held on 
18.02.2022. For the purposes of 
“bhat” ceremony, his presence 
necessary because there is no mail 
family member in his family.
Learned State counsel, under 
instruction, would submit that the 
marriage is settled on that date.
Without considering on merits, 
purely on the ground of marriage of 
the cousin sister of the appellant, the 
appellant may be enlarged on short 
term bail for six days subject to his 
furnishing a personal bond and two 
reliable sureties of the like amount, to 
the satisfaction of the court 
concerned.
After expiry of the period of 
short term bail, the appellant shall 
surrender before the court and an 
information shall be forwarded to this 
Court.The short term bail application
is allowed accordingly.
