20.11.2024 Short Term Bail Application No. IA/12/2024
In

Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, A.C.J.
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Naman Kamboj, learned counsel for the 
appellant and Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Deputy 
Advocate General for the State of Uttarakhand.
2. The appellant has challenged the judgment 
and order dated 02.09.2021 passed by 4th
Additional Sessions Judge, Dehradun, District 
Dehradun in Sessions Trial No. 144 of 2018, 
whereby he has been convicted for offences 
punishable under Sections 302, 201 and 120B IPC 
and sentenced to life imprisonment.
3. Today, the matter is listed on Short Term 
Bail Application (IA/12/2024). Appellant has 
sought short term bail on the ground that the 
marriage of his cousin sister is scheduled to be 
solemnized on 22.11.2024. It is contended that 
there is no male member in the family of his 
cousin and appellant’s presence is necessary for 
performing ‘Bhaat’ ceremony, which is customary 
ritual in western Uttar Pradesh, where appellant’s 
family resides.
4. Learned State counsel was asked to get 
instructions. On instructions, he submits that there 
is marriage of appellant’s cousin sister on 
22.11.2024 and he also endorses the statement 
made by the appellant that there is no male 
member in the family of his cousin sister who can 
perform ‘Bhaat’ ceremony.
5. Having regard to the aforesaid facts, we are 
inclined to grant short term bail for a period of one 
week to the appellant.
6. Accordingly, the short term bail application 
is allowed.7. Let the appellant-Amit Parley be enlarged on 
short term bail only for a period of one week from 
the date of his release, on his executing personal 
bond with two reliable sureties, each of the like 
amount, to the satisfaction of the Court 
concerned. 
8. Needless to say that immediately after 
expiry of one week, appellant shall surrender 
before the Superintendent of the Jail concerned. 
9. List this appeal in due course.
10. Let a certified copy of this order be issued 
today itself.
(Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.) (Manoj Kumar Tiwari, A.C.J.)
