Mr. M. S. Bhandari, learned counsel for the

2. Ms. Manisha Rana Singh, learned DAG for

3. Heard on the bail application (IA No.14 of 
2026). 
4. Learned counsel for the appellant submits 
that, as per the medical prescriptions enclosed 
with the short term bail application, the mother of 
the appellant has been suffering from pneumonia.
5. Learned State Counsel, on instructions, 
submits that at the relevant time, the mother of the 
appellant was admitted to the hospital, where she 
remained for four days, and has now been 
discharged.
6. In fact, the short term bail application has 
been moved by the appellant on the ground that 
the mother of the appellant was admitted to the 
hospital on 15.04.2026. Now, since the mother of 
the appellant has already been discharged from the 
hospital and, as per learned State Counsel, she is 
back at home, there is no reason to grant shortterm bail. Accordingly, the short term bail 
application is rejected.
7. List this case in due course.
