Heard on Interim Relief Application 
No. 3 of 2025
It is the case of the petitioner that 
pursuant to an advertisement issued by the 
respondent no.2, Secretary, Uttarakhand 
Subordinate Service Selection Commission, he 
applied to the post of Assistant Store Keeper. 
He was declared successful. His name was 
recommended for appointment, but the 
respondent no.1 is not permitting to join.
Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the petitioner has already been 
selected. His name was recommended for 
appointment, but the respondent no.1 is not 
offering him the appointment letter on the 
ground that he does not possess requisite 
qualification.
Learned State counsel would submit that 
the appointing authority has to verify the 
document and upon verification it was found 
that the petitioner is short of necessary 
qualification.
Though, on behalf of the petitioner it is 
argued that the petitioner is qualified. His 
certificate is valid and recognized.Having considered, as an interim 
measure, till the next date of listing, one 
position in the respondent no.2 establishment 
for the post on which the petitioner is 
recommended shall be kept vacant.
Interim Relief Application No. 3 of 2025 
stands disposed of accordingly.
