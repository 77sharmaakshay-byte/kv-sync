In this case, the impugned 
judgment has been assailed on the 
ground of fake driving license of 
Mohd. Taufiq, who was also the 
owner of the offending truck at the 
time of accident and he was driving 
the same. Mohd. Taufiq presented his 
written statement before the Tribunal 
and has also cross-examined DW1, 
the official of the ARTO office, but he 
himself did not produce any evidence 
in this regard nor could present in 
the witness box. 
I think the service upon 
respondent no. 2 Mohd. Taufiq 
should be made effectively wherefor 
steps shall be taken within a week. 
List after service is effected.
