2. The applicant has preferred the present 
anticipatory bail application apprehending arrest 
in connection with F.I.R./Case Crime No.167 of 
2025, registered under Sections 318(4), 351(2) 
added during investigation Section 336(3), 340(2) 
and 338 of B.N.S., 2023, registered at Police 
Station Raipur, District Dehradun.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant submits 
that the sole allegation against the applicant, as 
emerging from the entire F.I.R., is that the 
applicant had issued a cheque for a sum of 
₹18,50,000/- in favour of the complainant, which, 
upon presentation, was dishonoured. It is further 
submitted that, in respect of the dishonour of the 
said cheque, the complainant has already 
instituted proceedings under Section 138 of the 
Negotiable Instruments Act, which are pending 
before the learned Judicial Magistrate-II, 
Dehradun, and have been registered as 
Complaint Case No.3854 of 2025. Learned 
counsel for the applicant further submits that the 
applicant is ready and willing to deposit a sum of 
₹18,50,000/- before the learned Judicial 
Magistrate-II, Dehradun, in the aforesaid complaint case, within a period of 45 days from 
today.
4. Learned State Counsel prays for and is 
granted four weeks’ time to file counter affidavit.
5. List this matter on 05.11.2026.
6. In the meantime, as an interim measure, 
and without expressing any opinion on the merits 
of the case, it is directed that, in the event of 
arrest of the applicant during investigation, he 
shall be released on interim anticipatory bail on 
furnishing a personal bond and two reliable 
sureties, each in the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the arresting officer/Court 
concerned, subject to the applicant depositing a 
sum of ₹18,50,000/- before the learned Judicial 
Magistrate-II, Dehradun, in Complaint Case 
No.3854 of 2025, within a period of 45 days from 
today, and subject further to the following 
conditions:
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation; 
(ii) The applicant shall not approach any witness 
in any manner, whatsoever. 
(iii) In the event the applicant fails to deposit the 
aforesaid amount of ₹18,50,000/- within the 
stipulated period of 45 days from today, the 
interim protection granted by this order shall 
stand vacated automatically, without any further 
order of this Court.
7. Supplementary affidavit is taken on record. 
The Urgency application (I.A. No.1 of 2026) 
stands disposed of.
