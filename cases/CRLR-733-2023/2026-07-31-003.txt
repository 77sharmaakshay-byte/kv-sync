Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Digvijay Singh Bisht, learned 
counsel for the revisionist.
2. Mr. Jai Prakash Kandpal, learned 
Brief Holder for the State.
3. Mr. Ajay Singh Bisht, learned counsel 
for respondent no.2.
4. Learned counsel appearing for 
respondent no.2 submits that, owing to 
certain personal reasons, he seeks 
permission to withdraw his Vakalatnama 
from the present criminal revision.
5. Respondent no.2 is present in person 
before the Court. She submits that the 
revisionist has not paid the maintenance 
amount awarded in her favour and, on 
account of her financial constraints, she is 
not in a position to engage a private 
counsel to contest the present revision. 
She, therefore, prays that a legal aid 
counsel be appointed to contest her case.
6. Having regard to the submissions 
made by respondent no.2 and considering her inability to engage counsel, this Court 
deems it appropriate to provide legal 
representation to her through the Legal 
Services Authorities. Accordingly, Ms. Indu 
Sharma, learned counsel, is appointed as 
Legal Aid Counsel to represent respondent 
no.2 and to assist the Court in the present 
matter.
7. Mr. Ajay Singh Bisht, learned counsel, 
who has sought withdrawal from the case, 
shall hand over the complete paper book of 
the revision along with all relevant 
pleadings and documents available with 
him, to Ms. Indu Sharma, learned Legal 
Aid Counsel.
8. List this case on 07.08.2026.
9. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
shall obtain instructions, in the meantime, 
regarding the amount of maintenance, if 
any, paid by the revisionist to respondent 
no. 2 and the amount of maintenance, if 
any, that remains outstanding and payable 
by him.
10. Registry is directed to delete the name 
of Mr. Ajay Singh Bisht, learned counsel on 
behalf of respondent no.2 in the next cause 
list by replacing the name of Ms. Indu 
Sharma, learned legal aid counsel.
