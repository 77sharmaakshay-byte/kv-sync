10.10.2023 CRLR No. 733 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Proposed Criminal Revision has been 
filed challenging the order dated 
05.09.2023, passed by learned Judge, 
Family Court, District Nainital in 
Miscellaneous Criminal Case No. 110 of 
2023, by which, learned Judge has directed 
the revisionist to pay Rs. 5,000/- per month 
to his wife, respondent no. 2/applicant for 
her interim maintenance. 
2. Heard Mr. Nitin Tewari, learned 
counsel for the revisionist and Mrs. Manisha 
Rana Singh, learned A.G.A. for the State. 
3. Mr. Nitin Tewari, Advocate, submits 
that the revisionist was married to the 
respondent no. 2 -applicant on 08.12.2022.
After the marriage took place, he came to 
know that the respondent no. 2 was already 
married and she had married the revisionist 
without getting a divorce from her first 
husband Girish Chaudhary. 
4. Issue notice to respondent no. 2 
before Admission.
5. Steps to be taken within 48 hrs. 
6. List on 02.11.2023.
