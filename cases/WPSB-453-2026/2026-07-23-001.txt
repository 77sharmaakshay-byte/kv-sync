3. The petitioner has challenged the vires of Rule 
13(ii) of The Uttarakhand Government Servants
Salary (First Amendment) Rules, 2025 whereby in 
supersession of the earlier provision regarding grant 
of an additional increment at the time of grant of 
Selection Grade and promotion pay-scale, it has now 
been provided that the increment would not be 
payable. 
4. The submission of learned counsel for the 
petitioner is that under the earlier rules a vested 
right to receive one increment had accrued in favour 
of the petitioner and which could not be taken away 
by means of the amendment in question. He further 
submits that the consequential order passed by the 
department on 09.06.2026 for recovery of the 
amount already received by the petitioner, on the 
basis of the amended statutory provision is also wholly illegal.
5. The matter requires consideration.
6. Let counter affidavit be filed by the respondents 
within four weeks.
7. List immediately thereafter. 
8. Meanwhile, the effect and operation of the 
order dated 09.06.2026 shall remain in abeyance 
and no recovery shall be made from petitioner in 
pursuance of the said order, however, it shall be 
open to the respondents to refix the salary but the 
same shall be subject to the final outcome of the 
writ petition.
