COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 685 of 2023
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Heard Mr. P.C.Petshali, learned 
counsel for the petitioner, and Mr. V.K. 
Kaparwan, learned Central Government 
Counsel for all the respondents at 
length.
2. By the instant petition, the petitioner 
is praying for a direction in the nature of 
mandamus directing the respondents to 
take a decision upon the petitioner’s 
request to change his category of service 
to the post of ‘Fitter’ from the post of 
‘Track Maintainer’ as forwarded in the 
letter dated 11.05.2022 issued by the 
respondent no.4.
3. During the course of arguments, 
the arguing counsel for the petitioner 
has neither shown the appointment 
letter of the post of ‘Track Maintainer’ 
nor shown any document to demonstrate
that actually, he is discharging the
duties of the Fitter. Even the petitioner’s 
counsel could not show any relevant 
rules.
4. The counsel for the petitioner prays 
for a week’s time to file supplementary 
affidavit for bringing on record the
appointment letter of the post of ‘Track 
Maintainer’ as well as the documents in 
order to demonstrate that he is 
discharging the duties of the ‘Fitter’including the relevant rules. He may do 
so within a week. 
5. List after a week. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
