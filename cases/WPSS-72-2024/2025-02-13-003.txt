COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 72 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Dinesh Gahatori, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State.
3. The petitioner applied for recruitment to the 
post of Panch Karm Assistant pursuant to the 
advertisement issued on 23.12.2024, however he 
apprehends that his candidature will not be 
considered on the ground that he become over age, 
though he is having all requisite qualification, which 
he has obtained in the year 2018.
4. Though, according to him he was already 
having that qualification in the year 2012 but since 
the diploma as required was not recognized by the 
State Government, therefore, that diploma was not 
taken into consideration.
5. Be that as it may, undisputedly, as on today,
the petitioner is having all requisite qualification and 
he has applied, therefore, the respondents are 
directed to consider the candidature of the petitioner 
by ignoring the fact that the petitioner has become 
over age, however, his result shall be open only after 
final outcome of the petition.
6. Apart from this, it has also been informed to 
this court that there are also other candidates, who 
are having qualification, but now become overage, in 
such an eventuality, the selecting body-respondent 
no. 2 is directed to issue a corrigendum to this effect 
so that those candidates, who are having requisite 
qualification but become overage may also apply and 
if such a candidate apply their candidature may also 
be considered and their result be kept in the sealed 
envelope.
7. In the meantime, let the respondents may file counter affidavit within four weeks.
8. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the 
petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
9. List this matter after six weeks.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
