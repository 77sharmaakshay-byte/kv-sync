COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 72 of 2024
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J
1. Mr. Dinesh Gahtori, learned counsel for 
the petitioners.
2. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand. 
3. Learned State Counsel submits that 
due to interim order passed in this writ 
petition, candidates selected for the post of 
Panchkarm Assistant have not been 
appointed so far. He submits that 254 
vacancies on the post of Panchkarm 
Assistant were notified by Director, AYUSH 
and even after declaration of result of the 
selection, fate of successful candidates is 
still hanging in a balance. Thus, he submits 
that State be permitted to make
appointment on the post of Panchkarm 
Assistant.
4. Having regard to the submission made 
by learned State Counsel, interim order 
dated 13.02.2025 is modified and it is 
provided that the selected candidates shall 
be offered appointment and they shall be 
permitted to join duties, however two posts 
shall be kept reserved for the petitioners. 
5. List this case on 28.10.2025.
6. Consequently, miscellaneous 
application (IA No. 4 of 2025) stands 
disposed of.
