COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 2883 of 2025
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J
1. Mr. Bhuwan Bhatt, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. M.C. Pant, learned counsel for the 
respondent –Bank.
3. Mr. Suyash Pant, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand.
4. Petitioner earlier filed Writ Petition 
(M/S) No. 467 of 2024 challenging the 
recovery proceedings initiated against him 
by Central Bank of India. The said writ 
petition was disposed of with liberty to 
petitioner to submit proposal for one time 
settlement of the outstanding dues, within 
one week. Petitioner was directed to deposit 
₹5.00 lakh with his proposal to show his 
bona fide. 
5. According to petitioner, pursuant to the 
said direction, petitioner got a bank draft of 
₹5.00 lakh prepared from Union Bank of 
India on 27.02.2024; the said bank draft 
was furnished before Branch Manager, 
Central Bank of India, Roorkee, along with 
proposal of one time settlement, on 
28.02.2024. Since the proposal as well as 
bank draft was not accepted by Bank 
Authorities, then petitioner approached 
District Magistrate on 29.02.2024 and the 
District Magistrate issued a letter on 
02.03.2024, directing the Branch Manager 
concerned to accept the proposal and the 
bank draft from the petitioner; however, 
Branch Manager did not accept the proposal 
and bank draft, and now a notice has been issued on 09.09.2025, directing the 
petitioner to hand over possession of the 
mortgaged property. Thus, feeling 
aggrieved, petitioner has approached this 
Court, challenging the said notice. 
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that respondent-Bank has wilfully 
violated the earlier order passed in WPMS 
No. 467 of 2024; petitioner was willing to 
liquidate the loan by adopting the process of 
one time settlement and the Bank 
Authorities, however, are not interested in 
settlement of the dispute and now they are 
trying to take benefit of their own wrong, by 
issuing a possession notice. 
7. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
further submits that Contempt Petition No. 
62 of 2024 has also been filed against the 
Bank, which is pending before coordinate 
Bench. 
8. List this case on 30.10.2025 along with 
CLCON No. 62 of 2024.
9. In the meantime, respondents may file 
counter affidavit(s), if any.
10. Till the next date of listing, effect and 
operation of the impugned possession 
notice dated 09.09.2025 shall remain 
stayed. 
11. Stay application (IA No. 1 of 2025) 
stands disposed of.
