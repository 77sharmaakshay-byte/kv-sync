 This is restoration application MCC 
No.625 of 2019 moved by the petitioner to 
recall the order dated 06.03.2019 whereby the 
appeal was dismissed for want of prosecution. 
 There is a delay of 89 days in filing the 
restoration application. Delay is not inordinate 
has been explained sufficiently. In such 
circumstances, delay is condoned. Delay 
condonation application stands allowed. 
 In view of the reasons indicated, 
restoration application is allowed. Order dated 
06.03.2019 is hereby recalled. Appeal from 
order is restored to its original number. 
 Summon the lower court record. 
 List thereafter for final hearing.
