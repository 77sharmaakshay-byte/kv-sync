4. The writ petition has been filed by the petitioner on 
the premise that the grade pay of Rs.4,200/- has already 
been sanctioned in favour of the petitioner way back on 
27.05.2025 with effect from 01.01.2006 by the State of 
U.P., and the grade pay of Rs. 4,600/- has been 
sanctioned by the State of Uttarakhand vide order dated 
03.08.2024 with effect from 01.08.2006. The documents 
to this effect have also been annexed by the petitioner as 
Annexure No.1 to the supplementary affidavit 
(sanctioned by the State of U.P.) and Annexure No.11 to 
the writ petition (sanctioned by the State of 
Uttarakhand). The arrears of grade pay have not been 
paid to the petitioner even till today, despite the order 
already issued by respondent no. 2.
5. Petitioner contends that no payment of arrears has 
been made in favour of the petitioner. He further 2
contends that specific orders have also been made by the 
State of Uttarakhand in respect of the payment of arrears 
vide order dated 11.08.2024, annexed as annexure-5 to 
the writ petition.
6. Per contra, learned counsel for the State of 
Uttarakhand submits that, towards the arrears of grade 
pay of Rs.4,600/-, the arrears with effect from 
01.08.2006 to 30.09.2010, amounting to Rs. 31,365/-, 
have already been sanctioned for payment to the 
petitioner vide order dated 24.12.2025.
7. But the petitioner says that this amount has not 
been paid to the petitioner.
8. Respondent(s) shall file counter affidavit(s) within a 
period of three weeks showing cause as to why, despite 
the sanction of the Grade Pay of Rs. 4,200/- and Rs. 
4,600/-, arrears have not been paid to the petitioner, or 
to pay the same by the next date of listing.
9. List on 11.08.2026.
