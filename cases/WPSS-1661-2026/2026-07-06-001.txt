4. Petitioner has filed the present writ 
petition seeking Mandamus commanding 
respondents to provide petitioner Grade 
Pay of ₹4200/- w.e.f. 01.01.2006 and 
Grade Pay ₹4600/- w.e.f. 01.08.2006 
and prayed for further ancillary reliefs.
5. It is contended by learned counsel 
for petitioner that petitioner was granted 
Grade Pay of ₹4200/- vide order dated 
03.05.2024 passed by respondent No.2-
Commissioner w.e.f. 01.01.2006 and 
₹4600/- was also granted to him vide 
subsequent order dated 03.08.2024 
w.e.f. 01.08.2006.
6. It is further contended by him that 
earlier order dated 03.05.2024 was reviewed and it is stated that the Grade 
Pay of ₹4200 will be paid to petitioner 
once it is paid by State of U.P.
7. It is further contended by him that 
despite grant of Grade Pay of ₹4600/- to 
the petitioner w.e.f. 01.08.2006, it was 
not paid to him so far by the 
respondent-department.
8. Learned State Counsel is directed to 
seek instruction in the matter, as to why 
the payment of the Grade Pay of 
₹4600/- w.e.f. 01.08.2006 is not made
to petitioner pursuant to order dated 
03.08.2024, within a week.
