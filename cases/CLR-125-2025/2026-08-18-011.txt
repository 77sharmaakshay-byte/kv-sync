11. Now, filing of the frequent applications by the revisionist 
as well as by the respondent it reveals that after disposal of the 
revision the parties are unnecessarily wasting time of the court 
by filing frequent applications raising disputed question of the 
fact again and again though this court is of the firm opinion that 
the revisionist has also not approached with clean hands and he 
is not fair with the court and he suppressed about two 
alternative commercial premises, wherein, the two workshops 
are being run by him, still want to retain the shop in question
which is under his tenancy.
12. Therefore, in such an eventuality, when both the parties 
are raising the disputed question of fact though the revision was
already disposed of finally, this court has no option except to 
recall the initial order dated 12.11.2025.
13. Accordingly, the initial order dated 12.11.2025 is recalled 
and the matter is released and to be listed before the another
