COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CLR No. 125 of 2025
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Pradeep Kumar Chauhan, learned 
counsel for the revisionist.
2. Mr. Nikhil Singhal, learned counsel 
for the respondent.
3. The instant revision has been 
preferred under Section 25 of the 
Provincial Small Causes Courts Act, 1887 
against the judgment and order dated 
04.11.2025, passed by District and 
Sessions Judge, Haridwar in SCC Suit No. 
41 of 2024, titled as “Manish Gupta vs. 
Ashok Sharma” whereby the application 
moved under Order 7 Rule 11 read with 
Section 151 C.P.C. has been rejected.
4. Mr. Pradeep Kumar Chauhan, learned 
counsel for the revisionist, on instructions, 
apprise to this Court that the revisionist is 
ready to vacate the property in question, 
however, he prays that two years time 
may be given to the revisionist to vacate 
the property in question.
5. On the other side, Mr. Nikhil Singhal, 
learned counsel for the respondent have 
accepted the proposal of Mr. P.K.Chauhan.
6. In view of submissions as made 
above and particularly taking into 
consideration that the revisionist is ready 
to vacate the property in question within 
two years, the instant revision is disposed 
of finally with a direction to the revisionist 
to vacate the property in question on or 
before 31.10.2027.
7. In reference to the aforesaid 
undertaking the revisionist shall file an 
affidavit before the District and Sessions 
Judge Haridwar within two weeks 
positively from today.
8. It is further made clear that if the 
revisionist breach any undertaking given 
above the respondent shall apprise to this 2025:UHC:9993
Court by moving an application so that the 
appropriate steps shall be taken against 
the revisionist.
