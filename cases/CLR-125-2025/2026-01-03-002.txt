1. Mr. P.K. Chauhan, learned counsel 
for the revisionist through V.C.
2. Mr. Nikhil Singhal, learned counsel 
for the respondent.
3. Clarification application MCC No. 2 
of 2025 has been moved by the 
respondent/landlord by submitting that 
actual rent of the shop in question is Rs. 
50,000/- per month which is evident 
from the account statement which 
pertains to the deposit of rent of two 
months, i.e., May and June 2023. Apart 
from this, Mr. Nikhil Singhal, learned 
counsel submits that the revisionist 
already owned two commercial shops in 
the same locality, the photographs of 
which is enclosed at page 93 and 95 of 
the clarification application since mobile 
no. of the revisionist is shown in the sign 
board. He also placed before this Court 
the photocopy of the sale deed of the 
mother of the revisionist wherein 
revisionist constructed commercial shop.
5. Mr. Pradeep Chauhan, learned 
counsel who appears for the revisionist 
denied all these facts and submits that
commercial building as shown in the
photographs at page no. 93 and 95 does 
not belong to the revisionist.
6. In such view of the matter, 
concerned Tehsildar is directed to go on 
the spot to verify whether the photographs as shown at page 93 and 95 
belongs to the revisionist Ashok Sharma 
or not and also verify the sale deed 
dated 24.11.2021 and submit its report 
positively by 06.01.2026.
7. Mr. Nikhil Singhal shall provide 
photographs as well as copy of sale deed 
to Mr. Pradeep Hairiya today itself so 
that Mr. Hairiya communicate the 
concerned Tehsildar today itself. 
8. Put up this matter on 07.01.2026.
