6. As it appears from this report the plot measuring 
1825 sq.ft., which was purchased for residential 
purposes a motor workshop in the name of M/s S.S.D. 
Motors is being run, which is in the name of Aadesh 
Sharma, the brother of the revisionist. The photographs 
is also enclosed along with this report. 
7. Mr. Nikhil Singhal placed before this court one of 
the document i.e. Paper No. 18C/2, wherein, the 
brother of the revisionist Aadesh Sharma is shown as 
an employee in Shri S.S.D. Motors. 
8. It appears that the revisionist is making a false 
statement by giving impression that the S.S.D. Motors 
workshop is being run by his brother Aadesh Sharma, 
whereas Paper No. 8C/2 reveals that his brother is 
working as an employee in this motor workshop. Not 
only this, written statement filed in the Trial Court also 
reveals that misleading statement has been given that 
Plot measuring 1825 sq.ft. was purchased only for 
residential purposes though as per the report of 
Tehsildar it is being used for commercial purposes. 
Giving a false statement amounts to perjury with the 
court. The revisionist, who is present in court, is 
directed to file his response why he may not be 
prosecuted for giving a false statement in court within 
48 hours.
9. Put up this matter on 11.06.2026 on top of Board.
