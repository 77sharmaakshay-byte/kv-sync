The present Contempt Petition is listed 
on number of applications. The first 
application, which is listed today is recall 
application being MCC No. 1364/2018, 
whereby, the applicant has sought recall of the 
order dated 13.09.2018 passed by the 
Coordinate Bench of this Court, by virtue of 
which the Contempt Petition has been disposed 
of. 
The reason for recall as pleaded in the 
application is that the manner in which the 
disposal of the Contempt Petition was made it 
deserved to be decided on merits. 
Considering the grounds taken in the 
recall application, as well as, the reason 
assigned in the recall application filed in 
Contempt Petition No. 567 of 2017, and the 
order passed on it today this Court is satisfied 
that the Contempt Petition requires a 
consideration on its merit. Hence, recall 
application is allowed. The order dated 
13.09.2018 is recalled and the Contempt 
Petition is restored to its original number. 
Urgency Application listed today would 
too stands disposed of with the direction that 
this Contempt Petition may be listed along with 
WPSS No. 182/2018 in the second week of 
January, 2019.
