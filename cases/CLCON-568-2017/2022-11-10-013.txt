Shri Sanjaya Kumar Mishra, J.
Mr. Vinay Kumar, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
Mr. M. C. Pandey, learned Additional Advocate 
General with Mr. T. S. Phartiyal, learned Deputy 
Advocate General for the respondents.
In the connected contempt application, the 
impleadment/amendment application has already been 
allowed.
The learned counsel for the petitioners submits 
that they could not file the impleadment application 
because of some unavoidable reasons. The Court 
exercises its power suo-moto and directs that the 
applicants namely “Mr. Ravi Nath Raman, Principal 
Secretary (Education), District Dehradun and
Vandana Garbiyal, Director Elementary 
Education, Directorate of Education Uttarakhand 
Dehradun District- Dehradun” shall be impleaded 
as respondent nos. 3 and 4 and the original 
incumbent shall continue to remain party to the 
contempt application. 
The petitioner is directed to file requisites and 
amended cause title page of the contempt petition 
within seven days. 
Thereafter, notice be issued to the newly 
added respondents/contemnors through registered 
post/speed post, acknowledgement due, returnable 
within four weeks. 
List this matter on 12.01.2023.
