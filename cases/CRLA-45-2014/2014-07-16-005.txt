Applicants-appellants were convicted for the 
offences punishable under Section 364A IPC read with 
Sec. 120B IPC, Sec. 201 IPC, Sec. 302 IPC read with 
Sec. 149 IPC by learned II Addl. Sessions Judge, 
Haridwar, in Sessions Trial no. 93 of 2008 and were 
sentenced accordingly. 
 Whereas learned counsel for the complainant 
alongwith learned Dy. Advocate General vehemently 
opposed the bail, learned counsel for the applicants￾appellants contended that the applicants were not 
named in the FIR/ missing report, neither was there any 
demand for ransom, nor was there any motive to  3
commit the alleged crime, it is a case of circumstantial 
evidence in which the chain of circumstance is not 
complete. 
 It is further submitted that whereas some of the 
convicts-appellants were acquitted with regard to 
alleged recovery of firearms, but were convicted 
holding that the murder was committed with the help of 
said firearms. The owner of the farm, from where the 
dead body of the victim was allegedly recovered, was 
not examined, police did not get dead body of the 
victim identified by his wife or children, main witness 
(PW17) did not support the prosecution case. 
Prosecution failed to prove that the mobile phone on 
which co-accused Vijay Sethi alias Kali and deceased 
made phone calls to the complainant, belonged to 
PW17. 
 It is a case of circumstantial evidence, in which 
the applicants-appellants were on bail during the trial. 
Cause of death of victim was ante mortem firearm 
injuries. The spade was recovered at the instance of 
one of the applicants after 72 days. Dead body was 
recovered from a field at the instance of five accused￾appellants. On 19.09.2007, the victim was abducted; on 
20.09.2007, the FIR was lodged; on 27.09.2007, dead 
body of the victim was recovered. 
  4
 Considering the facts and attenuating 
circumstances, a case for bail is made out in favour of 
the applicants-appellants. 
 The bail applications are allowed. Let the 
applicants-appellants be released on bail, during the 
pendency of present criminal appeal, on their each 
executing a personal bond and furnishing two sureties 
each of like amount to the satisfaction of Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Haridwar in connection with Sessions Trial 
no. 93 of 2008. 
 Let certified copies of this order be also kept in 
connected criminal appeals.
