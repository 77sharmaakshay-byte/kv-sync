Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Ms. Vandana Singh Mehra, learned 
counsel for the revisionist.
2. Mr. Sandeep Sharma, learned A.G.A. 
for the State.
3. Mr. Avidit Noliyal, learned counsel for 
the respondent.
4. On the previous date the ITR of the 
revisionist in order to ascertain his annual 
turnover was handed over to Mr. H.M. 
Bhatia, learned counsel for Income Tax 
Department and he has gone through with 
the ITRs with the assistance of officials of 
Income Tax Department and apprise to 
this Court that from the assessment year 
of 2024-2025 the revisionist has not 
submitted his ITR. Apart from this, Mr. 
Bhatia submits that as per the CIBIL 
report for the period from 20.08.2023 to 
22.09.2025 the revisionist availed various 
loan facilities of an amount of more than 
Rs. 1 crore. After came to know all these 
details, it appears that the revisionist has 
not come up with the clean hands and he 
deliberately suppressed about his income. 
5. No doubt the dispute appears to the 
matrimonial one and this Court is 
repeatedly making an attempt to settle 
their disputes.
6. The respondent/wife is present in 
Court since last two days but the 
revisionist is not present. Not only this, 
the daughter of the revisionist who is 
living with the wife also joined the 
proceeding through V.C. Apart from this,
on the previous date, the revisionist undertakes to give laptop to his daughter 
but it has been apprise to this Court that 
the assurance as given by the revisionist 
has not been fulfilled. It appears that 
revisionist is not interested to settle the 
dispute and is also not sincere to secure 
the future of his minor daughter. The 
respondent-wife also apprise to this Court 
that revisionist filed divorce in Solapur, 
Maharashtra, if it is so, then in that 
eventuality, the revisionist is directed not 
to pursue the divorce petition till the 
matter is decided by this Court. In this 
regard, the respondent/wife shall brought 
to the notice of the concerned court of 
Solapur about this order so that the 
concerned court may aware about this 
matter. 
8. The instant revision though has been 
preferred by the husband against the 
order granting maintenance to the 
respondent/wife and the Coordinate Bench 
already stayed the order impugned but 
taking into consideration that the dispute 
is matrimonial and efforts are being made 
for their amicable settlement and on the 
previous date both the parties were 
directed to appear before the Counsellor 
and Counsellor submitted its report and I 
perused the same and this Court is of the 
view that whatever be the dispute in 
between the revisionist and his wife it 
should not affect the future of their
daughter who is innocent and have no 
concern with their dispute.
9. On the previous date the revisionist 
undertakes to give laptop to the daughter 
but now when this fact came to knowledge 
of this Court that the revisionist already 
initiated divorce proceeding then in such 
an eventuality to examine what 
maintenance the respondent/wife is 
entitled and how much the revisionist shall 
pay, the Court wants to know what is the actual income of the petitioner. 
10. Since the revisionist is not giving 
correct figure of his income and the 
revisionist has not submitted its ITR since 
2024-25, in such an eventuality, the 
matter is being posted after one month so 
that Mr. H.M. Bhatia, learned counsel for
Income Tax Department from his own 
level may get appropriate report from 
Principal Commissioner of Income Tax, 
Pune-4, Maharashtra who is having 
jurisdiction of ITO Ward 2(3) Solapur 
where the revisionist has filed his ITR for 
the previous financial year.
11. List on 24.04.2026 on top of the 
board.
12. Registry is directed to supply copy of 
this order to Mr. H.M. Bhatia.
13. Since the matter is posted after one 
month, the revisionist and respondent no. 
1 make all possible efforts to settle their 
disputes amicably so that it may not 
adversely affect the future of minor 
daughter.
