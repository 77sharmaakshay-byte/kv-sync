2. Mr. N.S. Pundir, learned DAG, for the State 
of Uttarakhand/1 to 4.
3. The petitioner along with four others, 
namely, Hameeda Khatoon, Sri Jan Alam, Smt. 
Kahkasha Khan Naz and Saziya, participate in the 
selection for appointment to the post of Assistant 
Teacher in Primary School under the OBC 
category, claiming the benefit of horizontal 
reservation for “hearing disability”, wherein, a 
clarification was sought from the Director and the 
Director by letter dated 08th February, 2026 
clarified that since all these candidates had given 
“Temporary Disability Certificate”, therefore, by 
virtue of Clause 3(c) of Government Order dated 
24th February, 2026, they are not entitled to get the 
benefit of horizontal reservation. Subsequent 
thereto, the two incumbents, namely, Smt. 
Kahkasha Khan Naz and Sri Arvind Kumar
preferred a writ petition before this Court and the 
petition was disposed of by giving liberty to make 
a representation, and if such a representation is 
made, the same may be decided, and now while 
deciding their representation, both of them offered
appointment since by that time they provide the 
“Permanent Disability Certificate”. 
4. The case of the petitioner is on equal footing 
and learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
earlier the petitioner was granted “Temporary 
Disability Certificate” but subsequent thereto a “Permanent Disability Certificate” was given on 
10th February, 2026, which was submitted to the 
District Education Officer by a representation 
dated 11th February, 2026 but he has not been 
offered the appointment. 
5. Prima facie it reveals that the petitioner’s
“Permanent Disability Certificate” was not taken 
into consideration though two incumbents, 
namely, Smt. Kahkasha Khan Naz and Sri Arvind 
Kumar, who furnished their “Permanent Disability 
Certificate” offered appointment by order dated
21st October, 2026, the copy of which is also 
supplied to the learned Dy.AG for the State.
6. Let Mr. Pundir, Dy.AG may get instructions 
from the Director as well as from the District 
Education Officer, why the petitioner has not been 
given appointment, particularly when, the two 
incumbents, namely, Smt. Kahkasha Khan Naz 
and Sri Arvind Kumar, offered appointment based 
on the subsequent “Permanent Disability 
Certificate”.
7. List this matter on 29.10.2026.
