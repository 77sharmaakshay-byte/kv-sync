AO No.77 of 2013 
Hon’ble B.S. Verma, J.
Heard Mr. P.C. Maulekhi, Advocate for the 
appellant and Mr. Karan Anand, Advocate for the 
respondent no.1/claimant. 
Admit the appeal. 
Issue notices to respondent nos.2 to 7. 
Summon the lower court record. 
List thereafter. 
Also heard on the stay application no.1139 2013. 
Having considered the submission of the learned 
counsel for the appellant, as an interim measure, it is 
directed that if the appellant deposits a sum of ` 7.50 
Lakhs (Seven Lakh Fifty Thousand) (minus statutory 
deposit) with the MACT concerned within a period of 
eight weeks from today, the execution of the impugned 
award shall remain stayed. On being depositing the 
amount, the respondent/claimant would be at liberty to 
withdraw 50% of the awarded amount without 
furnishing any security. The rest of the amount shall be 
kept in some fixed deposit interest earning scheme in a 
nationalized bank. (Stay application is disposed of).
