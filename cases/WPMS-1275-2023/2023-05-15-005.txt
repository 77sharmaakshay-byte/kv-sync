Upon hearing the learned Counsel for the parties, the Court made the 
following ORDER: (Sri Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
1. By the instant writ petition, the petitioner is challenging the 
order dated 20.10.2022 passed by the Deputy Director
Consolidation/Collector, Haridwar, in Revision No. 42/2017-18 
“Prabhu Avtar Singh & Others vs. Suresh Agarwal & Others”. 
2. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that Shri Yakub 
Ali Khan is the recorded tenure holder of the land in dispute i.e. 
Khasra No. 53-M admeasuring 15-7-0 Pukhta situated at village 
Pipalliya, Pargana Rudrapur, Tehsil Kichcha, Distirct Nainital 
(Now District Udham Singh Nagar), who executed a sale deed on 
11.07.1983 in favour of Brajpal Singh. Later on, Shri Brajpal Singh 
executed a sale deed on 28.9.1984 in favour of Shri Suresh 
Agarwal and Shri Deepak Agarwal and transferred his 1/2 share, 
and pursuant to the said sale deed both got the possession of the 
land in dispute and started a sugarcane crusher on the said land, 2
which continued till the year 1992. Thereafter, pursuant to the 
said sale deed dated 28.09.1984 a mutation application was filed; 
however, during the pendency of the mutation application one 
Shri Manvir Singh in connivance with Shri Yakub Ali Khan filed 
a declaratory suit under Section 9 of the Consolidation of 
Holdings Act. The said suit was decreed on 9.9.1996 in favour of 
Shri Manvir Singh. Thereafter, in the year 2001 Shri Manvir Singh 
executed a sale deed in favour of Shri Amrik Singh S/o Shri 
Sadhu Singh and Shri Prabhu Avtar Singh S/o Shri Avtar Singh.
3. It is further contended by the petitioner that the petitioner 
when came to know about the ex-parte order dated 9.9.1996 then 
he filed a restoration application on 4.1.2000, which was 
dismissed by the Consolidation Officer by an order dated 
02.05.2006. It is further contended by the petitioner that the 
petitioner was not impleaded as the party respondent in the said 
proceedings and no opportunity of hearing was given to him. 
Feeling aggrieved by the order dated 9.9.1996 and 2.5.2006, two 
separate appeals i.e. Appeal No. 283 and Appeal No. 284 were 
preferred before the Settlement Officer Consolidation, Udham 
Singh Nagar, the memo of which are appended to the writ 
petition as Annexure Nos. 6 and 7. Subsequently, the appeal so 
preferred were transferred to the Court of Settlement Officer 
Consolidation Roorkee, District Haridwar pursuant to the order 
dated 16.12.2015 by Board of Revenue. Both the appeals were 
allowed by the Settlement Officer Consolidation, Roorkee, 
Haridwar, by an order dated 23.01.2018 and both the orders 
dated 9.9.1996 and 2.5.2006 were set aside and the matter was 
remitted back to the Consolidation Officer to decide the case on 
its own merits after hearing both the parties and also directed to 
decide the claim of Shri Manvir Singh about the land in dispute 
based on his possession.3
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner further submits that 
another Appeal No. 285 “Deepak Agarwal vs. Mohammad Asim 
Khan and Others” was also preferred against an order dated 
16.08.2013, whereby, a mutation order was passed in favour of 
Shri Manvir Singh on the basis of adverse possession. The said 
appeal was preferred under Section 11(1) of the Consolidation of 
Holdings Act, which was allowed by an order dated 23.01.2018, 
and by this order, the mutation order was passed in favour of 
Shri Deepak Agarwal (now deceased) i.e. husband of the 
petitioner.
5. Assailing the order dated 23.01.2018 passed by the learned 
Settlement Officer Consolidation, two revisions were filed i.e. 
Revision No. 42 of 2018 and Revision No. 43 of 2018 along with 
the stay applications and the stay applications were rejected by 
an order dated 22.03.2018 observing that since by the impugned 
order the matter was remanded back to the Consolidation Officer, 
therefore, no prejudice is caused to the petitioner. Subsequently, 
two writ petitions were filed i.e. WPMS No. 1399 of 2018 and 
WPMS No. 1401 of 2018 “Prabhu Avtar Singh vs. Suresh Agarwal 
and Others”, and this Court vide order dated 18.05.2018 disposed 
of the writ petition with a direction to the Deputy Director of 
Consolidation, Haridwar, to decide Revision Nos. 42 and 43 of 
2017-18 independently without being influenced by any order. 
This order is enclosed in the writ petition as Annexure-11. 
Thereafter, the Deputy Director Consolidation by an order dated 
20.10.2022 allowed the revisions and set aside the order dated 
23.01.2018 passed by the Settlement Officer Consolidation.
Aggrieved by this order dated 20.10.2022, the present petition is 
preferred.4
6. The contention of the petitioner is that by the order 
impugned the order passed on 23.01.2018 is set-aside and the 
matter is remanded. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that Deputy Director Consolidation did not consider the 
objections filed by the petitioner and contended that, in fact, the 
title of the land in question vested in the name of Shri Yakub Ali
Khan, who sold the same in favour of Shri Brajpal and, thereafter 
Shri Brajpal sold one part of the land to the predecessor of the 
petitioner and second part to Shri Suresh Agarwal vide registered 
sale deeds. So far as the contesting respondents are concerned, 
the petitioner submits that he received the title over the property 
in dispute by way of adverse possession, which he has claimed 
by preferring a suit under Section 9 of Consolidation of Holdings 
Act for declaration of his rights as Bhumidhar over the property in 
question.
7. The petitioner has brought on record two affidavits of Shri 
Yakub Ali Khan and Shri Brajpal Singh dated 21.04.1987 and 
21.04.1987 in order to establish that as per these affidavits it is 
admitted that the land was sold to Shri Deepak Agarwal. 
8. I have gone through with the order dated 23.01.2018, which 
was set-aside by the order impugned dated 20.10.2022 and I have 
also perused the sale deed by which Shri Brajpal Singh sold the 
land in question to the predecessor of the petitioner in one part 
and Shri Suresh Agarwal in the second part.5
9. The issue as raised in the present petition requires 
deliberations for which the response of respondent nos. 5 to 19 is 
required.
10. Issue notice to all the private respondents i.e. respondent 
nos. 5 to 19.
11. Petitioner will take steps for effecting service upon the said 
respondents by regular mode of service. In addition to this, let 
notice be also served to the aforesaid respondents through 
Consolidation Officer concerned.
12. Steps to be taken within a week.
13. List on 16.06.2023.
14. In the meantime, parties are directed to maintain status quo 
over the land in dispute and shall not create any third party 
interest qua the land situated at Khasra no. 53-M admeasuring 
15-7-0 Pukhta situated at village Pipalliya, Pargana Rudrapur, 
Tehsil Kichcha, District Nainital (Now District Udham Singh 
Nagar).
15. Stay application (IA No. 1 of 2023) stands disposed of.
