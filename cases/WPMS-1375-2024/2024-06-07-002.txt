COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 1375 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Mahesh C. Pant, learned counsel 
for the petitioners.
2. By the present writ petition, the 
petitioner is challenging the order dated 
23.02.2024, passed by the Board of 
Revenue whereby the Board of Revenue 
rejected the application of the respondents
(petitioners herein) filed under Order 6 
Rule 17 read with Section 151 of CPC on 
the ground that the said application is not 
supported with an application under 
Section 5 of the Limitation Act.
3. Being aggrieved with the order 
impugned passed by the Board of Revenue 
whereby directions have been issued to 
move another application under Order 6 
Rule 17 read with Section 151 of CPC along 
with a delay condonation application for 
seeking amendment. The learned counsel 
for the petitioners submits that no direction 
can be issued for filing an application under
Order 6 Rule 17 read with Section 151 of 
the CPC for seeking amendment. The 
learned counsel submits that once the 
Board of Revenue rejected the application 
by observing that the application is not 
filed along with delay condonation 
application the Board of Revenue
simultaneously cannot direct the revisionist 
to move another application under Order 6 
Rule 17 read with Section 151 of CPC 
along with delay condonation application.
4. I find substance on the submissions of the learned counsel for the petitioners
that the court cannot sit in the advisory 
capacity, and, therefore, the Board of 
Revenue cannot issue direction to the 
revisionist to move an application under 
Order 6 Rule 17 read with Section 151 
CPC. This is for the revisionist either to 
move or not to move such an application.
5. Issue notice to the respondents 
returnable within three weeks.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioners
shall take steps to serve the respondents
within a week.
7. List this case on 10.07.2024.
8. In the meantime, the direction issued 
by the Board of Revenue by order dated 
23.02.2024 in para 7 shall remain stayed.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
