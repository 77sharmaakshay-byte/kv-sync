Ganpat and another .…… Appellants

Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. H.M. Raturi, Advocate for the 
appellants.
Mr. Deepak Sharma, Advocate for the

Mr. Sandeep Kothari, Advocate for the 
applicant in Impleadment Application No. 1993 
of 2025.
On 21.03.2016, when the appeal was 
admitted, the Court directed the parties to 
maintain status quo and not to create any third 
party interest in the property in question in 
any manner whatsoever.
It appears that thereafter the respondent 
Basant Singh, S/o Sri Jairam, R/o Baluwala, 
Pargana Pachwa Doon, District Dehradun 
transferred the property by way of executing 
sale deed on 28.08.2023 in favour of Sri 
Sukhpal Singh and Sri Shailendra Singh for 
consideration of Rs.46 Lakh. It is, thereafter, 
on 21.09.2023, the appellants moved an 
application under Order 39 Rule 2A read with 
151 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 for 
resetting the contempt proceeding against the 
respondent. 
The respondent was required to file 
objection, which he is evading, as he has yet 
not filed any objection. Meanwhile, the 
purchasers Sri Sukhpal Singh and Sri 
Shailendra Singh have also been directed by 
the Court not to transfer the property in favour 
of any other persons. Sri Sukhpal Singh and 
Sri Shailendra Singh are before the Court now. 
Notices were issued to them.Since Sri Sukhpal Singh and Sri 
Shailendra Singh have been issued notices and 
they have been directed not to alienate their 
property in suit. They need to be impleaded in 
the second appeal.
Let they be impleaded as respondent nos. 
2 and 3. They have already been represented.
The appellant shall file an amended memo 
of appeal.
It appears that the respondent has 
willfully disobeyed the order of the Court dated 
21.03.2016, when he was directed not to 
transfer the property in suit.
Now there are two courses open against 
him under Order 39 Rule 2A of the Code, one 
by way of attaching his property and second by 
sending him to civil prison.
First and foremost, the Court would 
explore the possibility of attaching his 
property, if any.
Learned counsel for the appellants 
submits that he does not have the details of the 
property of the respondent Basant Singh.
Let a copy of this order be forwarded to 
the Collector, Dehradun, who shall give details 
of the property registered in the name of the 
respondent Basant Singh. The Collector, 
Dehradun shall submit a report to this Court 
on or before 23.07.2026.
List on 23.07.2026 for final hearing after 
fresh cases.
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
