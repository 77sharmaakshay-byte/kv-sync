COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
SA No.40 of 2016
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Present Mr. Sudhir Kumar, counsel for the 
appellants. 
2. Present Mr. Deepak Sharma, counsel for the 
respondent through V.C.
3. On the last date of hearing, notice was issued 
to the respondent/defendant to appear in person as 
the application (IA No. 1992 of 2023) was moved 
by the appellants/plaintiffs; that, despite the
interim order dated 21.03.2016 of the Coordinate 
Bench to maintain status quo at the spot and not to 
create any third party interest over the property in 
question, the respondent/defendant executed a 
sale-deed of the property in question on
28.08.2023 in favour of persons, namely, Sukhpal 
Singh S/o Mannu Singh R/o Badamawala, Tehsil 
Vikas Nagar, Dehradun and Shailendra Singh S/o 
Mange Singh R/o 352 Basantpur, Mahavir 
Hospital Lane Vikasnagar, Dehradun, but 
respondent/defendant is not present in person as 
directed. 
4. Learned counsel for the respondent/defendant 
would submit that the respondent/defendant is an 
old age person and physically not able to move,
therefore, he could not appear before the Court 
however, no document of illness is placed on 
record. 
5. The illness or old age does not give any 
license to flout the order of the Court. Learned 
counsel for the respondent/defendant is not able to 
submit whether the sale-deed of the property in 
question was executed on 28.08.2023 by the 
respondent/defendant in favour of Sukhpal Singh 
S/o Mannu Singh R/o Badamawala, Tehsil Vikas Nagar, Dehradun and Shailendra Singh S/o Mange 
Singh R/o 352 Basantpur, Mahavir Hospital Lane 
Vikasnagar, Dehradun in consideration of Rs. 
46,00,000/- notwithstanding the interim order 
dated 21.03.2016 passed by the Coordinate 
Bench, as stated above.. 
6. In the above facts and circumstances, it 
appears to this Court that respondent/defendant is
whiling away the time with ulterior motive. 
7. Issue notice to the defendant/respondent to 
explain as to why the property of the 
defendant/respondent should not be attached and 
why he should not be detained in civil 
imprisonment for a term of three months,
returnable within two weeks from today. 
8. Issue notice to Sukhpal Singh S/o Mannu 
Singh R/o Badamawala, Tehsil Vikas Nagar, 
Dehradun and Shailendra Singh S/o Mange Singh 
R/o 352 Basantpur, Mahavir Hospital Lane 
Vikasnagar, Dehradun to place the facts before the 
Court. 
9. Meanwhile, Sukhpal Singh and Shailendra 
Sailendra Singh are restrained from creating any 
third party interest, alienating the property and
transferring the possession of the suit property in 
favour of any other person if any sale-deed as 
alleged was executed on 28.08.2023 in their favour 
by the respondent/defendant. 
10. List this matter on 3rd November, 2023. 
 
(Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.)
