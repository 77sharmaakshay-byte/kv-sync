Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
Mr. Sudhir Kumar, Advocate for

Mr. Tapan Singh, Advocate for the

Having heard the rival 
contentions, it transpires that initially, an 
O.S. No.395 of 2006 was filed seeking 
cancellation of the Sale Deed dated 30th
January, 2006. Such Suit was decreed on 
27.09.2012 by the Trial Judge in addition 
to an injunction order was also issued to 
the defendant not to create interfere in 
the possession and title of the plaintiff 
pertaining to the property, in dispute. 
The judgment of Trial Judge was 
appealed and First Appellate Court, vide 
its order dated 15.02.2016, was 
dismissed the Suit. 
It has been argued that the First 
Appellate Court has decided the Issue 
No.1 holding that the Civil Court did not 
have jurisdiction to adjudicate this 
matter because questioned property was 
purely agricultural one, so, litigation 
pending before the Civil Court is barred 
under Section 331 of Zamindari Abolition 
Act. 
After deciding the Issue No.1, 
First Appellate Court went further ahead 
and decided the Issue No.2 holding that 
the questioned Sale Deed was executed in 
exchange of due consideration. 
It has been argued that once it 
was held by the First Appellate Court 
expressing the lack of jurisdiction of the 
Civil Court to decide such a controversy, 
then it must not have expressed its own 
view regarding validity of the Sale Deed, 
as indicated above. On this point, the second appeal 
is admitted for hearing. 
Following substantial questions 
of law is formulated:- 
“(1). Whether the learned lower 
Appellate Court was justified in holding 
that the Civil Court has no jurisdiction to 
try and decide the suit without 
considering that the names of the 
plaintiffs-appellants was recorded in the 
revenue records on the date of suit? 
(2). Whether the learned lower 
Appellate Court was justified in holding 
that the impugned sale deed had been 
executed for a valuable consideration? 
(3). Whether the learned lower 
Appellate Court was justified in 
dismissing the suit of the plaintiffs￾appellants after holding that the Civil 
Court has no jurisdiction to hear and 
decide the suit?” 
Meanwhile, both the parties 
shall maintain the status-quo at the 
spot and they will not create any third 
party interest on the property, in 
question, in any manner, whatsoever. 
Stay application (CLMA 
1991/2016) stands disposed of 
accordingly.
