Hon’ble Siddhartha Sah, J.
Mr. H.M. Raturi, learned counsel (through 
V.C.) and Mr. Ghanshyam Joshi, learned counsel for 
the appellants. 
2. Mr. Sandeep Kothari, learned counsel for the 
applicant in impleadment application (IA No.1993 
of 2025). 
3. Perusal of the order sheet dated 21.03.2016 in 
the present case reveals that this second appeal was 
admitted on the said date and an interim order was 
also passed to the effect that, in the meantime, both 
the parties shall maintain status-quo at the spot and 
they will not create any third party interest in the 
property, in question, in any manner, whatsoever.
4. The order dated 21.03.2016 further reveals 
that the respondent Basant Singh, was duly 
represented by Mr. Tapan Singh, Advocate, at the 
time of passing of the said order. In fact, Mr. Tapan 
Singh, Advocate, appeared in the appeal on various 
dates, inter alia, 27.07.2016, 26.10.2016, 14.07.2017 
(through brief holder), 17.08.2017, 15.09.2017, and 
08.12.2017.
5. Perusal of the order dated 17.10.2023 would 
reveal that an order was passed for issuing notice to 
the defendant/respondent to explain as to why the 
property of the defendant/respondent should not be 
attached and why he should not be detained in civil 
imprisonment for a term of three months.
6. The office note dated 25.10.2023 reveals that, 
in compliance of the order dated 17.10.2023, an
administrative notice was issued to the 
defendant/respondent. The service report dated 
19.02.2023 reveals that the respondent Basant 
Singh, was served personally. Despite service of 
notice, there is nothing on record to suggest that the respondent Basant Singh, has filed any explanation 
to the said show-cause notice so far.
7. In view of the above, a show-cause notice is 
issued to the respondent Basant Singh, as to why he 
should not be detained in civil imprisonment for 
committing contempt of the order dated 21.03.2016 
passed by this Court. The said notice be served upon 
the respondent Basant Singh, through the Additional 
District Judge, Vikasnagar, Dehradun.
8. The Registry is directed to do the needful.
9. List on 28.05.2026.
