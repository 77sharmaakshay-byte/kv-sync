3. This is Misc. Application (IA No.1992 of 
2023) filed by the appellant praying therein to 
summon the respondent for his appearance in person

passed by this Court.

vide order dated 21.03.2016 the Co-ordinate Bench 
of this Court had directed the parties to maintain the 
status quo at the spot and not to create any third 
party interest on the property in question; that, 
despite the order dated 21.03.2016 the 
respondent/defendant executed the sale deed of the 
property in question on 28.08.2023 in favour of Shri 
Shukhpal Singh S/o Mannu Singh R/o Badamawala, 
Tehsil Vikasnagar, Dehradun and Shri Shailendra 
Singh S/o Mange Singh R/o 352 Basantpur, 
Mahavir Hospital Lane Vikasnagar, Dehradun for
consideration of ₹46,00,000/-. Hence, this 
application under Order 39 Rule 2-A r/w 151 C.P.C. 
for initiating the contempt proceedings against the 
respondent/defendant. 
5. Issue notice to the respondent/defendant of 
IA No.1992 of 2023 directing him to appear in 
person on 17.10.2023 and to file reply to this 
application before the Court within one week of 
receiving the notice.
