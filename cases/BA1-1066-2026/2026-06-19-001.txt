Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Mukesh Kumar Kaparuwan, learned 
counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. for the 
State.
3. Present applicant Sumit Kumar, S/o Mukesh 
Kumar Rai praying for bail in reference to FIR 
dated 18.02.2026, bearing FIR No. 0025 of 2026 P.S. 
Dineshpur District Udham Singh Nagar wherein 
Surbhi Saha, Sujata Saha, Disha Saha and Aman 
along with two other unknown persons have been 
implicated for the offences punishable under 
Section 103(1) and 109 of BNS 2023. On completion 
of investigation the present applicant has been 
charge sheeted for the offences punishable under 
Section 103(1), 109(1), 191(2) and 3(5) BNS 2023.
4. Along with the bail application short term 
bail application IA No. 1 of 2026 has been moved 
by the present applicant on the ground that
marriage of his brother, namely Deepak Rai is 
scheduled to be held on 30.06.2026 and 01.07.2026 
at Madhepur, Madhuvani, Bihar. The marriage 
invitation card is also enclosed as Annexure no. 1. 
He submits that even otherwise applicant is not 
named in the FIR, however, during investigation 
his name has been disclosed on the confessional 
statement of other co-accused and have now been 
charge sheeted along with other co-accused 
persons for the offences punishable under Section 
103(1) and 109(1), 191(2), 3(5) BNS 2023.5. On the previous date, the State Counsel was 
directed to verify about the marriage of the 
applicant’s brother. Today, Mr. Tumul Nainwal, 
learned A.G.A. for the State apprise to this Court 
that the official concerned has verified about 
marriage of brother of the applicant and the 
marriage is scheduled to be held as mentioned in 
the marriage invitation card. 
6. On short term bail, as prayed for, by the 
present applicant, Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned 
AGA submits that some stringent condition may be 
imposed while granting short term bail.
7. After hearing the arguments of the learned 
counsel for the parties and taking into 
consideration that the marriage of the brother’s 
applicant is scheduled to be held on 30.06.2026 and 
01.07.2026, let the applicant be admitted to short 
term bail for a period of three weeks to be 
computed from the date of release. 
8. Accordingly, the short term bail application
is allowed. 
9. Let the applicant ‘Sumit Kumar’ be released 
on short term bail for a period of three weeks, to be 
reckoned from the date of release, on his executing 
a personal bond and furnishing two reliable 
sureties of the like amount to the satisfaction of the 
court concerned.
10. Needles to say on completion of period of 
three weeks, to be reckoned from the date of 
release, the applicant shall surrender before the 
court concerned failing which warrant shall be 
issued against him.
11. List the regular bail application on 
22.07.2026.
12. In the meantime, State Counsel may file 
objection to the regular bail application.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
