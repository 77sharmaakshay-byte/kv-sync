The appellant has preferred this 
appeal from jail challenging the judgment 
and order dated 25.10.2023, passed in 
Sessions Trial No.25 of 2018, State v. 
Ramdas and others, by the court of 2nd
Additional District and Sessions 
Judge/FTSC, Roorkee, District Haridwar. It 
is delayed. Delay Condonation Application, 
IA No.1 of 2026, has been received from jail.
It is the case of the appellant that 
earlier a lawyer was appointed, who had 
assured that appeal had been filed, but it 
had not been filed. Hence the delay.
The delay condonation application is 
not objected to by learned State Counsel. 
Having considered, we are of the view 
that there are good grounds for 
condonation of delay. Accordingly, the 
Delay Condonation Application deserves to 
be allowed.
The delay condonation application is 
allowed. 
The delay in preferring the appeal is 
condoned. 
Heard on admission. 
Admit. 
Let call for the lower court record. Let paper book be prepared and 
provided to learned counsel for the parties, 
as per rule.
List this matter on 12.05.2026.
