This application is filed for an out of turn 
hearing. The writ petitions relating to the years 
2014, and prior thereto, have been identified for 
expeditious disposal and are listed before this Court 
every Wednesday and Thursday afternoons. Since 
several writ petitions of 2014 and prior thereto are 
still pending on the file of this Court, it may be 
inappropriate for us to take up the writ petition,
relating to the year 2016, for an out of turn hearing.
Suffice it, in such circumstances, to expedite 
the hearing of the writ petition. 
The Urgency Application (IA) No.10429 of 
2019 stands disposed of accordingly.
