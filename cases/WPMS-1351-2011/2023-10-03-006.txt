Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Yogesh Chandra Tiwari, learned 
Standing Counsel for the petitioners.
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, learned counsel 
along with Mr. Kshitij Sah, learned counsel for 
the respondents.
Ms. Deepmala has been sought to be 
brought on record by virtue of Impleadment 
Application No.IA/6729 of 2023, as filed by her,
on the ground, that she is the purchaser of the 
disputed property from respondent no.8, by 
virtue of a registered sale deed dated 20.07.2022. 
The factum of execution of the sale deed is not 
denied by the petitioners’ counsel. Hence, 
Deepmala has stepped into the shoes of 
respondent no.8 and would be the necessary 
party in the light of provisions contained under 
Order 22 Rule 10 of the CPC.
The Impleadment Application would stand 
allowed.
The learned counsel for the petitioners is 
directed to make the necessary amendments in 
the cause title of the writ-petition within a period 
of ten days from today.
List as soon as the impleadment is carried.Within the aforesaid period, the newly 
impleaded respondent is permitted to file counter 
affidavit.
