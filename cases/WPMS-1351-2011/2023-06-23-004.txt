COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.1351 of 2011
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Yogesh Tiwari, Standing Counsel, 
for the State of Uttarakhand/petitioner.
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate, for the 
respondent. 
On the same issue, which is subject 
matter of the present writ petition with regards 
to the proceedings emanating from the 
provisions contained under section 9 (A) (2) of 
the Consolidation of Holdings Act, there had 
been two writ petitions being WPMS No.2222 
of 2010, as well as the present writ petition. 
In WPMS No.2222 of 2010, it is 
contended by the applicant to the impleadment 
application, that he was a party to the 
proceedings, having being arrayed as 
respondent no.7. Hence, in that eventuality, 
both the writ petition should have been decided 
together, in that eventuality he might not have 
been the necessary party to the present writ 
petition.
But since the WPMS No.2222 of 2010, 
has been dismissed for want of prosecution on 
14.07.2022, and no restoration has been sought 
till date by the petitioner, he becomes a 
necessary party to be impleaded in the 
connected writ petition i.e. present writ petition WPMS No.1351 of 2011, because any orders is 
passed in the proceedings of the writ, his rights 
may be affected.
Considering the grounds taken in the 
impleadment application IA No.6728 of 2022. 
The same would stand allowed. Learned 
counsel for the petitioner is directed to implead 
the applicant as respondent no.8, to the present 
writ petition. 
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
