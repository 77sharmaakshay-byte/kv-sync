COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
UKHC010176842026
WPMS No.2524 of 2026
Prithviraj Chauhan College 
of Nursing and another ………Petitioners
Vs.
State of Uttarakhand and others ……Respondents
 
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. D.S. Patni, learned Senior Advocate assisted by 
Mr. Tribhuwan Phartiyal, learned counsel for the petitioners. 
2. Mr. D.S. Bohra, learned D.A.G. for the State.
3. Mr. Tarun Pande, learned counsel for respondent no.5.
4. Petitioners have challenged the order dated 06.08.2026, 
passed by respondent no.1 whereby the Essentiality Certificate 
dated 28.11.2025 was cancelled with immediate effect.
5. It is contended by learned Senior Advocate appearing for 
the petitioners that on the basis of a complaint made by one 
Mahesh Singh, the inspection of petitioner no.1 – Prithviraj 
Chauhan College of Nursing, Barkhera Rajput, Kashipur, 
District-Udham Singh Nagar (hereinafter referred to as 
“College”) was carried out on 22.11.2025; and, on the basis of 
the report submitted by the Committee, the impugned order 
has been passed.
6. It is submitted by learned Senior Advocate for the 
petitioners that the Inspection Committee has made 
recommendation in favour of petitioner no.1-College subject to 
certain terms and conditions of an affidavit being filed by the 
petitioner no.1-College.
7. Learned Senior Advocate for the petitioners submits that 
despite the inspection report ruled in favour of the petitioners,
the Essentiality Certificate issued in favour of petitioners on 
28.11.2025 has been cancelled by the impugned order.2
8. It is further submitted by him that 90 students are 
pursuing course of GNM and B.Sc. Nursing and if Essentiality 
Certificate is cancelled abruptly in the manner in which it has 
been done, the future of students would be at stake.
9. Per contra, learned counsel for the State submits that 
petitioner no.1-College was heard by respondent no.1 before 
passing the order and therefore there is no illegality in the 
impugned order.
10. Learned Senior Advocate for the petitioners has further 
submitted that earlier order of cancellation of the Essentiality 
Certificate was challenged before this Court by filing WPMS 
No.1283 of 2026, Prithviraj Chauhan College of Nursing and 
others Vs State of Uttarakhand, which was decided by this 
Court vide judgment and order dated 08.05.2026; the Court 
while allowing the writ petition and quashing the order 
impugned therein, directed that respondent no.1 therein would 
be free to conduct inspection or to pursue the complaint made 
by one - Mahesh Singh after giving a copy of the complaint and 
affording opportunity of hearing to the petitioner.
11. Learned Senior Advocate for the petitioners further
submits that respondent no.1 has not conducted inspection in 
accordance with the order passed by this Court on the earlier 
occasion; and it appears that the respondent no.1 was bent 
upon to cancel the Essentiality Certificate.
12. Having heard the learned counsel for the parties and 
having perused the documents on record, especially the 
inspection report dated 22.12.2025, this Court is of the 
opinion that controversy would be resolved if three Member 
Advocate Commission is sent for inspection of the College and 
a report is called accordingly. 
13. Accordingly a three member commission is constituted 3
consisting of:-
a. Mr. Vinay Shankar Pandey, Secretary, Medical Health 
and Family Welfare.
b. Mr. B.N. Molakhi, learned Deputy Advocate General for
the State.
c. Mr. R.C. Tamta, learned Advocate of the Court.
14. The Committee so constituted shall visit and conduct the 
inspection of the College on 06.11.2026 on Sunday and would 
submit its report before this Court on or before 25.11.2026.
15. For the purpose of conducting aforesaid inspection, the 
petitioners shall pay a sum of ₹25,000/- each to Mr. B.N. 
Molakhi, learned D.A.G. and Mr. R.C. Tamta, learned 
Advocate, members of the Commission.
16. List this case on 25.11.2026.
17. In the meantime, effect and operation of the impugned 
order dated 06.08.2026 shall remain stayed. 
18. Registry is directed to communicate the order to the 
members of the Commission within 24 hours.
 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
