Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Harshpal Sekhon, learned counsel 
for the applicants.
2. Ms. Meenakshi Sharma, learned AGA 
for the State.
3. The instant petition is preferred under 
Section 528 of BNSS 2023 by the applicants 
who are in-laws and brother-in-law (devar) 
of respondent no. 2 for challenging the 
proceeding of criminal case no. 595 of 2025 
titled as State vs. Surendra Kaur and ors. 
wherein the applicants have been 
summoned to face the trial for the offences 
punishable under Sections 498A, 323, 506 
IPC read with Section 3/4 of the Dowry 
Prohibition Act.
4. Brief facts of the case are that son of 
applicant no. 1 and 2 married with 
respondent no. 2 on 13.03.2022 and for 
more than one year they lived together and 
in the meantime a job was offered from New 
Zealand to the husband of respondent no. 2 
then he left for New Zealand on 18.03.2023 
and thereafter dispute were arose in 
between respondent no. 2 and her husband. 
Consequently, respondent no. 2 lodged the
FIR wherein the charge sheet is filed.
5. It is contended that respondent no. 2 
and her family members did not turn up for the amicable settlement though on the 
request of the applicants their son came
back from New Zealand but he was arrested 
from the airport though on the next day he 
was released but his passport has not been 
released. It is submitted that the applicants 
are still ready for the amicable settlement 
with respondent no. 2 and her family 
members.
6. Issue notice to respondent no. 2.
7. Steps to be taken by 23.09.2026.
8. Put up this matter on 07.10.2026.
9. On that day son of applicant no. 1 and 
2 and respondent no.2 shall appear before 
the Counsellor at 11:00 am then the matter 
be placed before this Court on the next day, 
i.e., on 08.10.2026.
10. Till the next date of listing, further 
proceeding of criminal case no. 595 of 2025 
titled as State vs. Surendra Kaur and ors. 
pending in the court of learned Judicial 
Magistrate, Kashipur, District U.S. Nagar, 
arising out of case crime no. 356 of 2024 
shall remain stayed.
