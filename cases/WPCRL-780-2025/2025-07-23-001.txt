4. This writ petition has been filed by the petitioner seeking 
the indulgence of this Court directing respondent nos.1 to 3 to 
transfer the investigation of FIR No.29 of 2024 dated 
24.01.2024, under Section 306 IPC registered at Police Station 
Nehru Colony, District Dehradun to some independent 
investigation agency particularly C.B.C.I.D. or C.B.I. from 
respondent no.4 and to direct respondent nos.5 or 6 to 
investigate the matter, in accordance with law.
5. Facts of the case as disclosed from this writ petition is 
that petitioner’s son – Mukul Bhatt was found dead on the 
railway track near Hanuman Temple, Mohkampur on 
26.11.2023. The FIR was lodged by the petitioner under 
Section 306 of IPC against respondent nos.7, 8 and others.
6. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioner that 
the Police is not conducting the investigation effectively with 
promptitude, therefore, she needs investigation to be 
transferred as prayed.
7. Learned counsel for the State may submit progress 
report of the investigation within three weeks.
8. List this case on 25.08.2025 as fresh.
