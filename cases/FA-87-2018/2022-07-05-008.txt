The plaintiffs of Suit No.46 of 2011 Smt. 
Kumud and Another vs. Mahant Govind Das and 
another, stood decided alongwith yet another suit, 
being Suit No.01 of 2014 Shri Rakesh Saxaria vs. 
Smt. Kumud and Others, by a composite judgment 
and decree as rendered by the court of Civil Judge 
(Senior Division), Kotdwar, Pauri Garhwal, the 
present first appeal has been preferred against the 
said impugned judgment dated 20.02.2018.
When the appeal came up for consideration 
before this Court, for hearing at admission stage, 
this Court itself on 13.08.2018, had admitted the 
appeal without passing any interim order on the 
application under Order 41 Rule 5 C.P.C. The 
appeal remained pending without there being any 
injunction, as such against the parties to the 
proceedings of appeal. 
Later on when the appeal, was considered on 
21.11.2019 and after hearing the respective 
contentions of the counsel for the parties, this Court 
passed an interim order to the following effect:-
“Considering the nature of the decree 
and being a regular first appeal, this Court is 
of the view that there has had to be an interim 
order in order to curb the future multiplicity 
of litigation and as such the parties to the first 
appeal are restrained from dealing with the 
property in any manner whatsoever during 
the pendency of the first appeal.Registry is directed to prepare the 
paper book.”
By an order of 21.11.2019, if its 
interpretation is given to its logical composite
inferences, which could be drawn from it, it was 
that the parties were restrained from dealing with 
the property in any manner.
The two expressions “dealing with the 
property”, would always mean a transfer or an 
exchange or creation of interest by execution of any 
deed of conveyance, which falls to be within an 
ambit of transfer and the reason is justified, because 
of the intention of the Court dated 21.11.2019, was 
to avoid any future multiplicity of the litigation in 
future.
The plaintiffs contending that the interim 
order dated 21.11.2019, has been violated had filed 
an application invoking the provisions contained 
under Order 39 Rule 2-A, to be read with Section 
151 of the C.P.C. and for the purposes of sustaining 
the application under Order 39 Rule 2-A of C.P.C., 
the appellant had substantiated their stand would 
based upon the contentions which has been raised 
in para 4, wherein, it has been submitted that the 
respondents had started raising construction and 
hence had flouted the order dated 21.11.2019. 
This application which has been filed by the 
plaintiff/appellant, has been objected by the 
respondents by filing an objection to it, contending 
thereof that the basic intention of the order of 
21.11.2019, was limited to the extent that no third 
party interest was expected to be created, which 
may amount to multiplying the litigation by 
creation of the third party rights and since it has 
been categorically denied in the objection, that no 
sale deed has been executed ever thereafter the 
order of 21.11.2019, the interim order of 
21.11.2019 has not been violative in any manner 
whatsoever.
In response to it, the learned counsel for the 
appellant has drawn the attention of this Court to 
the pleadings which has been raised in para 6 of the 
objection, which makes reference to certain sale 
deeds, which has been executed by the respondents,but the reference to those sale deeds, if they are 
taken into consideration, they all relate to the sale 
deeds prior to 21.09.2019, when there did not 
operate any interim order in the pending first 
appeal. 
Since at that relevant point of time, there was 
no restraint as such from sale, the sale deed referred 
to in para 6 cannot not be extracted to be read as to 
be in violation of interim order of 21.11.2019. 
Hence, this Court is of the view that since the 
basic spirit and intention of the interim order of 
21.11.2019, was confined to creation of third party 
interest and which has not been violated by the 
respondents, the application under Order 39 Rule 2-
A of C.P.C. merely on the pretext that respondents 
are raising construction, which was not restrained 
by the order of 21.11.2019, the application under 
Order 39 Rule 2-A of C.P.C., apparently no 
contempt as such has been made out against the 
respondents. Hence, the application is rejected. 
This order of rejecting the application under 
Order 39 of C.P.C. will be without prejudice to the 
rights of the applicants if any under law to seek an 
appropriate recourse in accordance with law.
