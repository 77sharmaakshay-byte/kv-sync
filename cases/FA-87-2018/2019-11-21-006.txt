FA No.87 of 2018
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J. 
Mr. Nikhil Singhal, Advocate for the appellant.
Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate for the respondents. 
This is an admitted first appeal preferred by the 
defendant appellant against the grant of decree for 
declaration as well as injunction in favour of the 
plaintiff respondent.
Considering the nature of the decree and being a 
regular first appeal, this Court is of the view that there 
has had to be an interim order in order to curb the future 
multiplicity of litigation and as such the parties to the 
first appeal are restrained from dealing with the 
property in any manner whatsoever during the 
pendency of the first appeal. 
Registry is directed to prepare the paper book. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.) 21.11.2019
