Heard on Interim Relief Application, 
IA No.1 of 2025
The petitioner is working in Nagar 
Palika. He is not covered under the Old 
Pension Scheme. By means of the instant 
petition, the petitioner claims that he 
should be covered by the Old Pension 
Scheme. By means of interim relief 
application, the petitioner claims that till 
the petition is decided, the petitioner 
should not be treated under the New 
Pension Scheme, instead, he should be covered under the Old Pension Scheme. 
Such interim relief may not be 
granted at this stage. Hence, the interim 
relief application is rejected.
