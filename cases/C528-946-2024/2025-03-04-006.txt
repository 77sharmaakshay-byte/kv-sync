3. By means of this Criminal Misc. 
Application filed under Section 528 of BNSS, 
applicants have challenged the summoning 
order dated 22.11.2024 passed by Chief 
Judicial Magistrate, Pauri Garhwal in 
Criminal Case No. 792 of 2024, State Vs. 
Siddhartha Bagri and Others, charge-sheet 
dated 02.05.2024, under Section 304-A of 
IPC and the entire criminal proceedings 
arising out therefrom.
4. Also heard on the Stay Application (IA 
No.1 of 2024).
5. Applicant No. 1 is the Director and 
Chief Technical Officer in Pravaig Dynamics 
Private Limited and Applicant No. 2 is 
Director and Chief Executive Officer of the 
same company.
6. As per the case of the applicants, test and trial to run Interceptor Safari: AWD EV 
SPV (special purpose vehicle) for which 
there was an understanding between M/s 
Aska Equipment Pvt. Ltd., Forest 
Department, Government of Uttarakhand 
and the applicants. It is contended that Aska 
Equipment Pvt. Ltd is the distributor of the 
said vehicle. The first trial was conducted by 
the Forest Officers at Chila Range in the 
morning of 08.01.2024 and thereafter again, 
the Mechanical Engineer/Driver took a total 
of 10(01+09) forest officers/employees of 
ASKA Company in the vehicle for trial on 
Cheela Road, though, he was fully aware 
that the said vehicle has a total capacity of 
only 08 (07+01) passengers, but, despite 
that he permitted 10 passengers to sit on 
the said vehicle. It has also come in the 
counter-affidavit that during investigation, it 
was revealed from the cameras installed in 
the said vehicle that the Mechanical 
Engineer/Driver himself was telling the 
passengers sitting in the vehicle that the 
said vehicle attains the speed of 100 km in 4 
seconds and he himself increased the speed 
of the vehicle, which unfortunately met with 
an accident, as a result of which, 6 persons 
died.
7. From the above, it is clear that rash 
and negligence has been committed by the 
driver and applicants have no role in it.
8. After perusing the records and having 
heard learned counsel for the parties, this 
Court, prima facie, is of the opinion and is 
satisfied that in the interest of justice, 
further proceedings of Case No. 792 of 
2024, State Vs. Siddhartha Bagri and Otherspending before the court of Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Passuri Garhwal shall remain 
stayed qua the applicants till the next date 
of listing. (Stay application is disposed of).
