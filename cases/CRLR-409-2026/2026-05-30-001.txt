COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLR NO. 409 of 2026 
\Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Tushar Chauhan, learned counsel for 
the revisionist and Mr. Himanshu Sain, learned 
A.G.A. for the State. 
2. The instant criminal revision is barred by 
delay of 29 days preferred against the order of 
learned Additional Principal Judge, Family Court, 
Dehradun dated 27.01.2026, passed in Criminal 
Case No. 390 of 2021, Smt. Neetu Vs. Shri 
Sanjay Vohra, wherein, maintenance of 
Rs.10,000/- has been granted to the respondent 
wife. 
3. The delay is well explained and the same 
is condoned. 
4. Admit. 
• 5. Taking into consideration that the 
dispute in between the revisionist and the 
respondent no.2 appears to be 
matrimonial one and both of them 
married in the year 2002 and out of their 
marriage, on child was born who is now, 
in such view of the matter, this Court is of 
the view that efforts may be made for 
their amicable settlement. 
6. Issue notice to the respondent no.2. Let 
the counsel for the revisionist take steps to 
serve the respondent no.2 by all modes 
including whatsapp and e-mail so that on the 
next date, the respondent no.2 may join the 
proceedings either through video conferencing or to remain present in the Court. The 
revisionist shall also remain present on the next 
date fixed. 
7. List on 06.07.2026. 
8. In the meantime, the order impugned 
passed by the Additional Principal Judge, Family 
Court, Dehradun dated 27.01.2026 is stayed 
provided the revisionist regularly pay 
maintenance of Rs.6,000/- p.m. to the wife. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.) 30.05.2026 Shiv/
