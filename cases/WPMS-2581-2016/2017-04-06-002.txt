An amendment application being CLMA 
no. 2555 of 2017 has been filed on behalf of the 
petitioners for substituting the words and figures 
“Article 226” in place of the words and figures 
“Article 227”. The same is supported by an 
affidavit. 
In the counter affidavit filed on behalf of 
the respondents, a plea has been taken that “ a 
registrar is not a court” and therefore, if it is so, 
the present writ petition is not legally 
maintainable under Article 227 of the 
Constitution of India and there was an efficacious 
alternative remedy for the petitioners to 
challenge the impugned order under Article 226 
of the Constitution of India, therefore the present 
writ petition is not legally maintainable and liable 
to be dismissed on this ground alone”. 
Thus, the amendment application is in 
consonance with the objections filed by the 
respondents. 
Amendment Application is accordingly 
allowed. 
 The amendment be incorporated during 
the course of the day. Thereafter, the writ petition 
be placed before the appropriate Court having 
jurisdiction.
