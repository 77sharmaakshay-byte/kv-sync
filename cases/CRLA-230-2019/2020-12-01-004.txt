The exemption application (CRMA No. 
4255/2020) is allowed.
This is an application, (CRMA No. 
4254/2020) seeking for a short term bail. It is 
contended that the mother of the 
appellant/applicant passed away on 25.11.2020. 
Her cremation was completed by the brother of 
the appellant/applicant. 
Now, in terms of the religion, various 
ceremonies are required to be done, which will 
take at least one month to complete. Hence, he 
requests that a short term bail be granted.
The same is disputed by the learned 
advocate general for the State.
On hearing learned counsels, we are of the 
considered view that keeping in mind the death of 
the mother of the appellant/applicant, and to 
attend the ceremonies of her last rites is 
necessary for the appellant/applicant, he should 
be released on a short term bail. Hence, the 
short-term bail application (CRMA No. 4254 of 
2020) is allowed.
The appellant/applicant is directed to be 
released on bail for a period of one month from 
the date of his release on he furnishing a personal 
bond of Rs. one Lakh with two sureties to the like 
sum, to the satisfaction of the trial court
concerned.
