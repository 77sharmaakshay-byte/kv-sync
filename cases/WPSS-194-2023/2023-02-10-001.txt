COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
10.02.2023 WPSS No. 194 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Heard Mr. B.S. Negi, learned 
counsel for the petitioners, Mr. P.C. Bisht, 
learned Additional Chief Standing Counsel 
with Mr. M.S. Bisht, learned Brief Holder for 
the State and Mr. Nishant Krishna Adhikari, 
Advocate holding brief of Mr. Ramji 
Srivastava, learned counsel for respondent 
no.2. 
Admittedly, in a similar matter 
(WPSB No. 169 of 2021), on 16.04.2021,
this Court has directed the respondents to 
entertain and consider the applications of 
the petitioners of the said Writ Petition and 
to permit them to participate in the
selection process for the post of Staff Nurse 
provisionally. The State has also been 
directed to consider to grant the age 
relaxation to the petitioners in the light of 
the Uttarakhand Public Service (Relaxation 
of age limit for the recruitment) Rules, 
2003.
Learned counsel for the 
respondents requested four weeks’ time to 
file counter affidavit.
It has been stated by the parties 
that in the similar matter i.e. in WPSS No. 
170 of 2023 and batch, the Coordinate 
Bench has passed an order on 31.01.2023, “…..this Court is of the view that the 
present petitioners would be permitted to 
participate in the process of selection 
though provisionally subject to the 
condition, that so far as the aspect of their 
age is concerned if it falls for consideration 
within the parameters of the provisions of 
the Rules of 2003. So far as the aspect 
pertaining to the qualification is concerned, 
the same would be governed by the interim 
protection granted by the Division Bench on 
16.04.2021.”
Learned counsel appearing for the 
petitioners requested to grant interim relief
in view of the said order, passed in the 
WPSS No. 170 of 2023 and batch.
Learned counsel appearing for 
respondent no.2 fairly conceded that the 
present matter and the matter of WPSS 
No.170 of 2023 and batch are similar in 
nature.
Interim relief to the petitioners is 
being granted to the petitioners under the 
condition mentioned in the order dated 
31.01.2023, passed in WPSS No.170 of 
2023 and batch.
Interim Relief Application (No.02 of 
2023) is disposed of accordingly. 
It is clarified that this interim order 
shall be subject to final outcome of the 
present writ petition, and/or subject to 
further order(s), which may be passed in this petition.
List this case along with WPSS No. 
170 of 2023.
(Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
