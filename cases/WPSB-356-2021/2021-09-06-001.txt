Heard. 
Petitioners are challenging the 
judgment rendered by Uttarakhand Public 
Service Tribunal in Claim Petition 
No.23/NB/DB/2018. Said judgment is being 
challenged by the petitioners on various 
grounds. Learned Tribunal has held that 
persons who were senior to the petitioners 
in the cadre of Inspector (Group III), even 
though promoted later, shall be treated as 
senior to the petitioners on the post of 
Inspector (Group II). 
Explanation to Rule 6 of The 
Uttaranchal Government Servants Seniority 
Rules 2002, which applies to cases where 
appointment is to be made on a post only by 
promotion, reads as under:- 
“Explanation-A person senior in the 
feeding cadre shall even though promoted 
after the promotion of a person junior to 
him in the feeding cadre shall, in the cadre to which they are promoted, regain the 
seniority as it was in the feeding cadre.” 
Shri Amar Murti Shukla, learned 
counsel appearing for the petitioners fairly 
concedes that the judgment rendered by 
learned Tribunal qua persons appointed as 
Inspector Group II by promotion cannot be 
faulted as it is in consonance with the 
aforesaid provision contained in Seniority 
Rules, 2020. He submits that petitioners are 
limiting their challenge to the seniority given 
to direct recruits above the petitioners. 
Issue notices to private respondents, 
returnable within six weeks. 
List after receipt of service report.
