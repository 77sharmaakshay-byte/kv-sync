Heard on Stay Application No. 1 of 
2025
It is the case of the petitioner that the 
recovery is being made from him in defiance to 
the principles of law, as laid down by the 
Hon’ble Supreme Court in the case of State of 
Punjab and others Vs. Rafiq Masih, (2015) 4 
SCC 334.
To it, learned counsel for the respondent 
nos. 2 to 4 would submit that the case is not 
covered by the judgment of the Supreme Court 
in the case of Rafiq Masih (supra) because the 
petitioner is Class-II employee and also he has 
given an undertaking that in case of excess
payment, it may be recovered from him.
The petitioner seeks stay of the recovery.
This Court is of the view that without 
permitting the respondents to file objection, 
the stay application may not decided.
Respondents may file objection to the 
stay application.
