This Revision has been preferred by the 
wife against the rejection of her application under 
Section 125 Cr.P.C., which has been rendered by 
the Principal Judge Family Court, Dehradun on 
16th October, 2018, in Case No. 354 of 2015, 
Smt. Rekha Negi Vs. Gambhir Singh Negi. 
Challenge has been given to the order 
dated 16th October, 2018, rejecting the application 
under Section 125 Cr.P.C., which has been filed 
belatedly with delay of 28 days as reported by the 
Registry. 
After having gone through the reasons 
given in the Delay Condonation Application and 
being satisfied with the same, coupled with the 
fact that the said prayer for condoning the delay, 
since not being seriously opposed by the 
respondents, the Delay Condonation Application 
is allowed and the delay, which has chanced in 
filing the Revision would stand condoned. 
With the consent of the parties, the 
Revision is being heard on admission stage itself. 
Since it is against the rejection of the application 
under Section 125 Cr.P.C, the Revision is 
directed to be admitted. 
Summon the lower court’s record. 
The respondent No.2, who has appeared 
through his counsel Mr. D.C.S. Rawat, if he is so 
advised, may file his objection within a week. 
List thereafter.
