Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Heard Mr. Siddhartha Singh, the learned 
counsel for the revisionists and Mr. Aditya Singh, 
the learned counsel for the respondent Nos. 1 to 
5 through video conferencing.
After some arguments, the learned counsel 
for the respondents submits that there are some 
formal defects in the Modification Application 
No.10899 of 2018 and requests for permission to 
withdraw the same with permission to file a fresh 
modification application.
The learned counsel for the revisionists
opposed the submission of the learned counsel 
for the respondents.
Perused and gone through the record.
In the interest of justice, on the cost of 
Rs.5,000/-, the prayer of the learned counsel for 
the respondents is allowed. The learned counsel 
for the respondents is permitted to file a fresh 
Modification Application in accordance with law.
The learned counsel for the revisionists
submits that the cost may be deposited in 
Advocate Welfare Fund.
The learned counsel for the respondents 
accepts this submission.
On the request of the learned counsel for 
the parties, post for arguments on merits on 
05.08.2020.
