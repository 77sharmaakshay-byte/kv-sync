The petitioners to these writ petitions have 
put a challenge to the condition no.9, of the 
Advertisement dated 03.01.2023, as amended on 
11.01.2023, which has been issued by the 
Uttarakhand Medical Service Selection Board, 
Dehradun, by virtue of which, the respondents
are said to have imposed, the condition that a
candidate, who would be eligible to participate in 
the aforesaid selection process, would be eligible 
subject to the condition, that he or she has 
acquired his basic qualification of High School 
and Intermediate from the Institution situated 
within the State.
It has been further argued by the learned 
counsel for the petitioners, that this condition as 
imposed, therein, would be in violation to the 
Articles 14 and 21 of the Constitution of India, 
and he further submits that the propriety of the 
Rule 8 of the “Uttarakhand Chikitsa Shiksa 
Vibhag (Medical College) Nursing Cadre (Non￾Gazetted) Service Rules, 2020”, whereby this 
particular stipulation was provided, therein, 
creating a distinction and restrictions in
participation of the candidates, who have
acquired their qualification of High School and 
Intermediate, from other Institutions than those 
situated within the State of Uttarakhand, was an 
aspect, which was considered by the Division 
Bench, in which there operates an interim order dated 16.04.2021.
On the contrary, it has been argued by the 
learned counsel appearing for the respondent no.2
Mr. Ramji Srivastava, that the aforesaid 
stipulations provided under clause 9 it runs in 
accordance with the amendment, which has been 
made by the Gazette Notification 
No.164/XXX/2/19 01 (17) 2012, dated 
28.06.2019, whereby the aforesaid amendment
did provided a condition, that the candidate 
applying should have acquired his basic
schooling qualification from any Institution 
situated within the State of Uttarakhand.
But so far as the reference made to the said 
amendment is concerned, it relates to the post,
which are required to be filled in for Group C 
posts, which are within the purview of the
selection by Public Service Commission. The 
issue would be as to whether, when the matter 
stands covered by a specific rule, which governs 
the appointment of the Nursing Officers, or it will 
be governed by the subsequent amendment made
in 2019, as referred to by the learned counsel for
the respondent no.2, is a debate, which is to be 
decided by this Court, after exchange of the 
pleadings.
Respondents may file their counter 
affidavit within a period of four weeks. 
So far as the petitioners of these writ 
petitions are concerned, they are provisionally 
permitted to take up their examination in 
pursuance to the advertisement dated 03.01.2023, 
as it stood amended on 11.01.2023, their 
participation in the selection process would be 
exclusively provisional in nature. It will not 
entitle them to raise a claim of appointment. 
However, their selection process would abide 
with the decision to be taken in these writ 
petitions. 
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
