COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C-482 No.476 of 2020
Hon’ble Siddhartha Sah, J.
Heard Mr. Anil Kumar Joshi, learned counsel for the 
applicant and Mr. V. S. Rawat, learned A.G.A. for the State.
2. By means of the present Criminal Misc. Application under 
Section 482 of Cr.P.C., the applicant has sought quashing the 
impugned charge-sheet No.42A/2016 dated 15.07.2016 under 
Sections 420/464(a)/467/468/471 and 120-B of IPC and 
summoning order dated 23.11.2016 passed by the learned 
Judicial Magistrate, Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar filed 
in FIR No.247 of 2012, Police Station Kichha, District Udham 
Singh Nagar.
3. Learned counsel for the applicants would submit that the 
present Criminal Miscellaneous Application emanates from FIR 
No.247 of 2012, registered under Sections 420, 464(a), 467, 468, 
471 and 120-B of the IPC, with the allegations that, by way of 
criminal impersonation, a bank account bearing No.8771 was 
opened in the Nainital Bank, Kichha Branch, on 02.02.1997 in 
the name of one Shri Chandra Jain, who had already died on 
12.12.1991. It is alleged that the main accused, Dhiraj Singh 
Raghuwanshi, by forging documents and cheating, got himself 
added as a co-account holder. The said account was subsequently 
closed by Dhiraj Singh Raghuwanshi and another person who 
impersonated himself as Shri Chandra Jain.
4. It is further alleged in the FIR that the Senior Officials of 
the Bank did not take any action and, in fact, they tried to cover up the entire fabrication and forgery.
5. The investigation carried out pursuant to the aforesaid FIR 
culminated in the filing of a charge-sheet dated 15.09.2016 
against Devendra Pratap Singh, Rakesh Kumar Gupta and 
Pramod Kumar under Sections 420, 464(a), 467, 468, 471 and 
120-B of the IPC.
6. Pursuant to the aforesaid charge-sheet, the learned Judicial 
Magistrate, Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar, vide order 
dated 23.11.2016, took cognizance against the accused persons 
under Sections 420, 464(a), 467, 468, 471 and 120-B of the IPC, 
and the case was registered as Criminal Case No. 6323 of 2016, 
titled “State of Uttarakhand vs. Devendra Pratap Singh and 
Others.”
7. Assailing the aforesaid charge-sheet and cognizance order 
dated 23.11.2016 passed by the learned Judicial Magistrate, 
Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar, learned counsel for the 
applicant, at the very outset, would submit that the applicant was 
working as Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Nainital
Bank Limited between 04.07.2011 to 21.01.2013 and thereafter, 
repatriated to his parent Bank i.e. Bank of Baroda and now has 
been superannuated from service. The applicant was an 
Executive of Bank of Baroda and has been sent on deputation to 
discharge the duties of Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of 
the Nainital Bank Limited and has joined the Nainital Bank on 
04.07.2011 after approval of Reserve Bank of India and before 
that he was working in Bank of Baroda. 
8. Learned counsel for the applicant would submit that, as 
per the bank records, at the time of opening Account No. 5431, 
Mr. P. C. Mohindra was the Branch Manager. He had been 
absconding from the Bank since 1990 and, as a consequence, was removed from the services of the Bank.
9. Learned counsel for the applicant would further submit 
that there is no vicarious liability inasmuch as the opening of a 
bank account falls within the domain of the concerned official at 
the branch level. There is no direct nexus between such acts and 
the functions discharged by the Chairman of the Bank, who is 
posted at the Head Office and has no connection with the day-to￾day affairs of the branch.
10. Per contra, learned State counsel would submit that the 
FIR was registered against the applicant and, after due 
investigation, a charge-sheet has been submitted against him 
under Sections 420, 464(a), 467, 468, 471 and 120-B of the IPC. 
As such, while exercising jurisdiction under Section 482 of the 
Cr.P.C., this Court cannot enter into factual arena.
11. After considering the rival submissions made on behalf of 
the learned counsel for the parties and after going through the 
record, the present Criminal Misc. Application requires scrutiny. 
12. Admit. 
13. Issue notices to the respondent no.3 through the Chief 
Judicial Magistrate, District Ghaziabad. 
14. Steps to be taken within a week. 
15. List this matter on 20.08.2026.
16. Interim order dated 01.07.2024 to continue till further 
orders.
