This is a bunch of three Appeals from Orders. 
Since, the issue involved in each of them is the same, 
hence, these are being taken up together.
A) AO No.479 of 2022 has been preferred as against the 
award of 02.05.2022 which was rendered in MACP Case 2
No.134 of 2016, whereby an award of Rs.8,40,000/- has 
been fastened upon the appellant, which is under challenge.
1. The Appeal from Order is accompanied with 
IA/2/2022, being application for condonation of delay, 
seeking condonation of 145 days of delay, which has 
chanced in filing the appeal. 
2. The same is being opposed by respondent no.1 
and 2/ claimants by filing an objection to it by way of 
IA/3/2023. The solitary ground, taken by the respondents 
while opposing the delay condonation application, is that the 
delay has not been satisfactorily explained in precision. As 
such, the delay condonation application ought to be 
rejected.
3. But, after having heard the learned counsel for 
the parties, on the aspect of delay, the reason for delay, 
which has been attributed, has been because of the fact, 
that when the copy of the award was received by the 
Divisional Office of the Appellant, certain procedural 
formalities were required to be undertaken for getting an 
appropriate sanction / permission for filing of the appeal 
before the High Court.
4. Apart from that, it is contended, that before any 
opinion could be drawn to prefer an appeal, a legal opinion
was also required to be solicited, which had consumed some 
time. Thus, they contend that the delay deserves to be 
condoned.
5. In the objection thus preferred by the 
respondents, they have stressed upon the implications of 
the provisions contained under Section 173 of the M.V. Act 
and has contended, that the rationality given in the 
application for seeking condonation of delay is not 
satisfactory, on the ground, that since the appellant itself 
being an expert body, being conscious of the fact of the 3
provisions contained under Section 173(1) of the M.V. Act, 
they ought to have completed the formalities within the time 
frame work to file the appeal within time.
6. Being conscious of the said fact, this Court cannot 
be ignorant of the fact that a statutory body created under 
an Act, has had to undergo certain procedural formalities for 
the purpose of preferring an appeal, the officer concerned in 
the department cannot file an appeal on his own wisdom 
until and unless a legal opinion is solicited and the same is 
thereafter ratified by the superior officer, who is competent 
to take a decision to direct to prefer an appeal.
7. Thus, the reason for objection, which has been 
raised, is not acceptable to this Court, coupled with the fact, 
that there has been consistent decision rendered by the 
Hon’ble Apex Court, that a pragmatic view has to be taken 
by the Court while considering the delay condonation 
application, so that the lis may be adjudicated on merit.
8. Apart from it, the prospective of delay becomes 
vital only, when with the lapse of time certain vital rights 
had accrued to the decree holder. If with the condonation of 
delay the vital rights accruing because of the judgment is 
not being affected adversely, the same has to be rationally 
considered for its condonation. The very fact, that the 
claimants have filed a money withdrawal application, even 
prior to condonation of delay, it is a tacit acceptance of the 
condonation of delay.
9. Owing to the aforesaid, the delay condonation 
application No.IA/2/2022 would stand allowed and the delay 
of 145 days in filing the appeal is condoned. Accordingly, 
IA/3/2023, the objection to the delay condonation,
application too would stand disposed of.
10. With the consent of the learned counsel for the 
parties, the Appeal from Order is taken up for admission.4
11. Admit the appeal.
12. Summon the LCR.
13. No notices are required to be sent to the 
respondents as they are already represented by the 
respective counsels.
14. The execution of the impugned award dated 
02.05.2022 would be kept in abeyance, subject to the 
condition that, if the appellant deposits the entire awarded 
amount before the learned MACT, within a period of six 
weeks from today, if not already deposited in compliance of 
the earlier order, passed by the coordinate bench.
15. As soon as the aforesaid deposit is made, it is 
thereafter, the money withdrawal application IA/5/2023
