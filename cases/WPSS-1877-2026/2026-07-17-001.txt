2. Mr. Maneesh Bisht, learned Brief Holder for the 
State.
3. Petitioner has challenged the order dated 
20.06.2026 whereby representation/application of 
petitioner dated 11.05.2026 was disposed of saying 
that the petitioner has wrongly been sanctioned the 
benefit of Assured Career Progression (ACP) Scheme. 
4. From perusal of the record, in particular order 
dated 28.04.2026 annexed as Annexure No.4 to the 
writ petition, it appears that petitioner was appointed 
as Junior Accounts Clerk on 19.03.1999; he was given 
first promotion on 31.12.2004 of Senior Accounts 
Clerk and second promotion was given to him on 
17.05.2008 to the post of Assistant Accountant and 
third promotion on 08.08.2011 on the post of 
Accountant. 
5. Since petitioner had been granted three 
promotions after his direct recruitment in the cadre, it 
was decided that petitioner was not entitled to get 
benefit of any ACP which was wrongly sanctioned in favour of petitioner, and accordingly, it was directed 
that pay of petitioner is to be re-fixed. Due to 
aforesaid order dated 28.04.2026, pay of petitioner 
was re-fixed and after re-fixation, petitioner was 
directed to make re-payment of the excess payment 
made to him due to wrong sanction of A.C.P.
6. Learned Counsel for the respondents may file 
counter affidavit within four weeks.
7. List thereafter along with WPSS No.1473 of 2026 
(Deepak Chandra Arya v. State).
