Heard on interim relief application. 
It is the case of the petitioner that she 
responded to an advertisement issued by 
the UKSSSC for the position of Draftsman 
in the Irrigation Department; now the result 
has been declared; the petitioner belongs to 
the OBC category; she has secured more 
marks than the general category (women), 
therefore, the petitioner ought to have been given offer of appointment; but, by not 
doing so, the rules of reservation as laid 
down in the cases of Saurav Yadav v. State 
of Uttar Pradesh, AIR 2021 SC 233 and 
Sadhana Singh Dangi and others v. Pinki 
Asati and others, (2022) 12 SCC 401, have 
been violated.
By means of the interim relief 
application, the petitioner seeks direction 
that operation of the result may be stayed.
The selected candidates have also 
been made parties. They have been issued 
notices. 
Having considered, as an interim 
measure, it is directed that any action that 
has been taken pursuant to the result 
dated 25.06.2025 as issued by the UKSSSC 
and/or the appointment of the private 
respondents shall remain subject to final 
outcome of the instant writ petition.
Interim relief application stands
disposed of accordingly.
