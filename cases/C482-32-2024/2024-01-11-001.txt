COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C482 No.32 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Rajeev Pathak, learned counsel 
for the applicant.
2. Mr. M.A. Khan, learned A.G.A. for 
the State.
3. By the instant application 
preferred under Section 482 Cr.P.C., the 
applicant is challenging the proceedings
of Criminal Case No.1448 of 2023 (Case 
Crime No.0261 of 2020), State Vs. 
Mansoor Ahmad, for the offences 
punishable under Sections 420, 467, 
468 & 471 I.P.C at Police Station 
Kiccha, District Udham Singh Nagar, 
pending in the Court of Learned Judicial 
Magistrate/ Civil Judge Kichchha, 
District Udham Singh Nagar along with 
the cognizance and summoning order 
dated 17.01.2023. In the said 
proceedings, the applicant is facing trial 
in respect of the offences punishable 
under Sections 420, 467, 468 and 471 
IPC.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that the present applicant was 
appointed as an Assistant Teacher Urdu 
in Government Primary School in the 
year 1995. 
5. Subsequently, a verification report 
was asked from the Regional Secretary 
Secondary Education Council U.P. and 
the result of the applicant was cancelled 
and the marksheet was not issued from 
the Council.6. Learned counsel for the applicant 
also submits that, in fact, the applicant
passed “Adeeb-E-Kamil”, which is 
equivalent to B.A. Urdu and this
qualification was taken into 
consideration while giving the 
appointment to the applicant.
7. However, in respect of the said 
mark sheet a first information report 
was lodged on 15.09.2020, in respect of 
the marksheet issued by the M B Inter 
College, Haldwani, Nainital, which was 
challenged by the writ petitioner in writ 
petition bearing Writ Petition (Criminal)
No.1519 of 2020, wherein this Court 
granted interim protection that no 
coercive steps shall be taken against the 
petitioner. The said Criminal Writ 
petition is still pending.
8. Learned counsel for the applicant 
further submits that the appointment 
was given to the petitioner on the basis 
of the examination, which he has passed 
i.e. Adeeb-E-Kamil and Moallim-E-Urdu,
and not on the basis of the mark-sheet 
of intermediate examination. In a 
reference to this, a statement has been 
given in para 10.
9. Learned counsel for the applicant 
also submits that since on the basis of 
alleged mark-sheet, which according to 
the prosecution is forged one, the 
petitioner was not given appointment
and he has not taken any benefit from 
the said marksheet, the initiation of 
criminal proceedings against the present 
applicant is an abuse of process of law.
10. All these aspects are required to 
be considered after inviting counter-affidavit from the State.
11. Mr. Saurabh Pandey, learned Brief 
Holder, accepts notice on behalf of 
respondents prays for and is granted 
four weeks’ time to file counter-affidavit. 
Two week’s time, thereafter, is also 
granted to learned counsel for the 
applicant for filing rejoinder-affidavit.
12. List after six weeks.
13. In the meantime, further 
proceeding of Criminal Case No.1448 of 
2023 (Case Crime No.0261 of 2020) 
State Vs. Mansoor Ahmad for the 
offences punishable under Section 420, 
467, 468 & 471 I.P.C at Police Station 
Kiccha, District Udham Singh Nagar, 
pending in the Court of Learned Judicial 
Magistrate/ Civil Judge Kichchha, 
District Udham Singh Nagar shall 
remain stayed.
