COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
24.02.2022 CLR No.17 of 2013
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Heard Mr. Siddhartha Singh, learned 
counsel for the revisionist and Mr. Nikhil 
Singhal, learned counsel for the respondent 
nos. 1 to 3.
Heard on the amendment application, 
filed by the revisionist. 
The learned counsel for the revisionist 
submitted that during the pendency of the 
present revision, the respondent no.4, 
namely, Smt. Pushpa Sharma has expired on 
29.12.2021 and this information was provided 
by the learned counsel for the respondents on 
31.12.2021. 
The learned counsel for the revisionist 
further submitted that the respondent no.1 is 
son of the deceased-respondent no.4 and 
respondent nos. 2 & 3 are daughters of the 
deceased-respondent no.4, who are the only 
legal heirs of the deceased of the respondent 
no.4. and as such, substitution is not called 
for and the word “deceased” needs to be 
written after the name and parentage of 
deceased respondent no.4 in the cause title of 
the revision.
The learned counsel for the respondent 
has no objection. 
The said amendment application (IA 
No.6181 of 2022) is allowed. 
The learned counsel for the revisionist 
requested one week’s time to file an amended
array of parties. 
Post this case on 24.03.2022.
