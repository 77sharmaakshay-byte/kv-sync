Mr. Amar Shukla, Advocate for the

Mr. Narain Dutt, Standing Counsel 
for the State/respondent nos. 1 to 6.
The petitioner claims his retiral dues.
It is the case of the petitioner that he 
was superannuated on 31.05.2024. Now, 
his retiral dues could not be processed 
because the respondents have raised an 
objection that in the year 2017, the 
petitioner’s salary was wrongly fixed in 
accordance with 7th Pay Commission. What 
is argued is that, in fact, the petitioner had 
opted to be governed as per 6th Pay 
Commission because his ACP was due in 
the month of March, 2017 and accordingly, 
his pension was rightly calculated. But 
now, misplaced objection has been raised.
State was required to file counter 
affidavit, but it has not been filed.
Let State file its counter affidavit 
within three weeks. Two weeks thereafter, 
rejoinder affidavit, if any, may be filed.
List on 20.05.2025 for final hearing.
The Court makes it abundantly clear 
that the respondent no.1 shall by then pay 
admitted retiral dues to the petitioner. The 
scope of the petition may be confined to 
the issue which is in dispute.
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
