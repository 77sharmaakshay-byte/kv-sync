COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.1680 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Ramji Shrivastava and Mr. Nishant Krishna 
Adhikari, learned counsel for the petitioners.
2. Mr. V.K. Kapruwan, learned Standing Counsel 
for the Union of India takes notices for respondent nos. 
1 and 2.
3. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Brief Holder for the 
State for respondent no.4.
4. Learned Standing counsel for the Union of India
submits that Clause 9.1(k) of the Eklavya Model 
Residential Schools (EMRS) Guideline (Annexure 
No.14 to the writ petition) provides that recruitment of 
teachers for States can be entrusted to an independent 
agency, if need arises. 
5. In nutshell, the case of petitioners, as canvassed 
in the writ petition, is that petitioners are working as 
PGT in Eklavya Adarsh Awasiya VidhyalayaI, Kalsi,
District Dehradun for last few years. By the impugned 
notification i.e. annexure-15 to the writ petition, the 
National Education Society-respondent no.1 has issued 
an advertisement for filling up the vacancies of PGT in 
these schools.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that 
respondent no.1-National Education Society has no 
right or locus to issue such an advertisement for filling 
up the posts of teaching and non-teaching staff in 2
EMRS across the country. To substantiate his 
argument, he on the strength of sub-clauses (e) & (f) to 
Clause 9.3 of new guidelines of EMRS submits that
the power for recruitment of teaching and non-teaching 
staff of these schools was given to the State Level 
Society i.e. EMRS Society-respondent no.3 herein.
7. Learned counsel for respondent nos.1, 2 and 4
pray for and are granted three weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit.
8. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the 
petitioners to file rejoinder affidavit.
9. Issue notices to respondent nos. 3 and 5. Steps to
be taken for service upon respondent nos.3 & 5 within 
a week.
10. Having heard the rival contentions of parties and 
on perusal of the guidelines relied upon by the learned 
counsel for the petitioners, I find substantial force in 
the arguments advanced by him. In such view of the 
matter, till the next date of listing, the selection 
process, pursuant to the impugned advertisement, 
annexure-15 to the writ petition, is hereby stayed. 
11. List this matter on 17.10.2023.
12. Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2023) stands 
disposed of accordingly.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
