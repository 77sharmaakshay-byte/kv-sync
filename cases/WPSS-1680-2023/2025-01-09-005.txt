7. These are three set of petitions; (a) one 
set of petitions has been filed by thecontractual employees in which they are 
claiming regularization. (b) In second set 
of petitions, the petitioners who are the 
contractual employees, are challenging the 
selection process on the ground that 
NESTS has no power for initiating the 
selection process.
8. In these petitions, there is serious 
objection on behalf of the respondents 
that these petitions are not maintainable, 
since, all these petitioners have 
participated in the selection process. 
Learned Attorney General submits that it 
is a settled principle of law after 
participating in the selection process , the 
selection process cannot be challenged 
subsequently.
9. The another set of petitions are those 
which have been filed by those petitioners 
who have been selected in the selection 
process initiated pursuant to the 
advertisement issued by the NESTS and 
their relief is that though they have been 
given appointment however they are not 
given joining.
10. In the first category of petitions, in
which the petitioners who are contractual 
employees claiming regularization, let the 
respondents may file fresh affidavit within 
four weeks to deal with the issue of 
regularization.
11. In the second category of petitions in 
which the petitioners are challenging the 
selection process despite the fact that they 
participated in the selection process, Mr. 
C.D. Bahuguna, seeks time to address on 
the issue.
12. In third category of petitions, which 
has been filed by the regularly selected 
candidates, in favour of whom appointment letters have been issued, 
since this Court already passed an interim 
order on 12.11.2024 to permit all these 
petitioners to joint without disturbing the 
contractual employees who are before this 
Court, in reference to this Mr. Vashisth, 
learned Senior Advocate submits that he 
will seek instructions in the matter.
13. This Court hope and trust that by 
that the interim order dated 12.11.2024
will be complied with.
