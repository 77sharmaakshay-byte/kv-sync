this Appeal.

for condonation of delay are 
satisfactory. Hence, delay condonation 
application is allowed and the delay in

List this matter on 07.05.2018. 
Considering the submission of 
the learned counsel for the parties, it

observed that, in case, any 
construction is raised over the 
property in question that will be 
subject to final outcome of this appeal.
