COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 208 of 2021 and other connected petitions
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Parikshit Saini, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Sunil Khera, learned Deputy Advocate General 
with Mr. Tarun Mohan, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Order impugned dated 15.12.2020 has been passed 
apparently in flagrant violation of two interim orders 
passed by this Court on 27.05.2020 and 29.05.2020. It 
appears that by the order impugned dated 15.12.2020, the 
Chief Education Officer has revived the order of recovery, 
which was already stayed by the High Court. Not only this, 
what the Chief Education Officer has done, he directed his 
subordinates to ensure recovery in six instalments and such 
recovery shall be subject to the final outcome of the writ 
petitions, in which recovery was stayed.
4. Yesterday, this matter was listed and after gone 
through the record, this Court was primarily of the view 
that in flagrant violation of the interim orders passed by this 
Court impugned order has been passed. A specific 
averment has been made in the petition that the Chief 
Education Officer in a contemptuous manner passed the 
order impugned. 
5. The then Chief Education Officer Mr. Anand 
Bhardwaj, who is present in person today, submits that 
there is a clerical mistake while dictating the order
impugned.
6. After gone through with the record, this Court is of 
the view that the justification with regard to clerical mistake 
is apparently false and misleading one. 
7. Mr. Bhardwaj further submits that though impugned 
order was passed but no recovery was made. This also 
appears to be a misleading statement since subsequent 
order of recovery was also stayed by this Court. 
8. In such view of the matter, this Court have no option 
except to initiates contempt proceedings against Mr. Anand 
Bhardwaj, the then Chief Education Officer, who may file 
his response by tomorrow. 
9. List on 08.01.2026.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
