WPSS No. 543 of 2020
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Parikshit Saini, Advocate for the petitioner.
Mr. Vikas Pande, Standing Counsel for the 
State.
This matter is heard through Video 
Conferencing.
Notices on behalf of respondent nos. 1 to 4 have 
been accepted by the learned Standing Counsel, he 
prays for and is granted four weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit.
The petitioner who was appointed as an 
Assistant Teacher, Primary Education, had proceeded
on medical leave for a certain period as specified in the 
writ petition. In all, she has availed 36 days medical
leave.
By the impugned order under challenge in the 
present writ petition i.e. dated 12.05.2020, the 
respondent No. 4, has directed the recovery of the 
amount from the petitioner. 
The contention of the petitioner is that since the 
impugned order of directing to recover the amount for 
the medical leave period availed by the petitioner to 
the tune of Rs. 41,779/- since being punitive in nature, 
it could not have been imposed without conducting an 
enquiry or providing an opportunity of hearing to the 
petitioner. 
He further submits that an identical issue had 
crept in before the coordinate Bench of this Court with 
leading WPSS No. 509 of 2020, along with other
bunch of writ petitions and the coordinate Bench of 
this Court on 29.05.2020, had stayed the recovery until 
further orders.
Since the matter happens to be identical and the 
action taken is in violation of principle of natural 
justice, there would be a stay of the impugned order 
under challenge in the present writ petition and no 
recovery in pursuance to it, would be made from the 
petitioner.
List this writ petition, along with Writ Petition 
(S/S) No. 509 of 2020, after four weeks.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
