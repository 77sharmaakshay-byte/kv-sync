3. The claim of the appellant for being granted 
compassionate appointment consequent to death of his 
father has been denied solely on the ground that 
formal order for regularization was not passed by the 
time he died.
4. It is noteworthy that on 05.01.2022 an office 
memorandum was issued enclosing therewith the list 
of daily wagers who had been working since before 
10.04.1996 and in which the name of Late Shri Vishnu 
Dutt Joshi, father of the appellant, figured at Serial 
No.151.
5. The father of the appellant died on 08.01.2022 
and consequently formal order for regularization could 
not be passed. For said reason only, the learned Single 
Judge has dismissed the writ petition.
6. The employee concerned had indisputably 
worked in the department since 01.04.1993 and his name had also figured in the list prepared by the 
department for regularization. Therefore, the claim of 
the appellant has to be considered after examining 
whether his father was entitled for regularization and, 
if so, then merely because formal order for 
regularization could not be passed by the time he died, 
the appellant cannot be non-suited.
7. Accordingly, we direct the competent authority to 
examine the case of Late Shri Vishnu Dutt Joshi for 
regularization in reference to the date on which the 
office memorandum was issued i.e. 05.01.2022 and
apprise the Court of the same. The respondents shall 
also disclose whether Man Singh who is at Serial 
No.152 just below Late Shri Vishnu Dutt Joshi or any 
other employee below him had been regularized. 
8. The said aspect shall also be kept in mind by the
competent authority while considering the case of Late 
Shri Vishnu Dutt Joshi for regularization.
9. Learned State Counsel shall obtain written 
instructions in respect of the stand of the department 
on the said aspect within two weeks.
10. List on 01.09.2026.
