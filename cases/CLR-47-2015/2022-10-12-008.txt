12.10.2022 CLR No.47 of 2015
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Heard Mr. Sudhir Kumar and Mr. Kurban
Ali, learned counsel for the revisionist, Mr. 
Bhuwan Bhatt, learned counsel for the 
respondent no.4 and Mr. Rakesh Thapliyal, 
learned Senior Advocate assisted by Mr. 
Dharmendra Barthwal, learned counsel for the 
respondent no.5 on the Delay Condonation 
Application (IA No.5282 of 2022) and the 
Restoration Application (MCC No.5281 of 2022).
Mr. Kurban Ali, learned counsel for the 
revisionist requested to permit him to withdraw 
his vakalatnama.
Permission is accorded. 
The learned counsel for the respondents 
opposed the said application orally.
Perused and gone through the record.
In the interest of justice, the delay 
condonation application is allowed. The delay of 
45 days’ in filing the restoration application is 
condoned. The restoration application is 
allowed. The order dated 12.07.2022 is recalled.
The Civil Revision (No.47 of 2015) is 
restored to its original number.
List this case on 04.11.2022 along with 
CLR No.20 of 2015 for final hearing.
