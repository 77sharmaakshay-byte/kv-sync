Hon’ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
Mr. J.S. Bisht, Advocate with Ms.

Mr. S.S. Adhikari, A.G.A with Mr.

Heard. 
The appellant has been convicted 
for the offences punishable under section 
3(1) of Gangster and Prevention of Anti 
Social Activities Act and has been 
sentenced accordingly. 
Admit the appeal. 
Summon the lower court record. 
List after lower court record is 
received. 
Also heard on the bail application. 
It is the contention of the learned 
counsel for the appellant that appellant 
was on bail during trial and never 
misused the same. 
Considering the entire conspectus 
of the case, this Court is of the view that 
appellant deserves bail during the 
pendency of the appeal. 
The bail application is allowed. Let 
applicant Arvind Sharma, be released on 
bail on executing personal bonds and 
furnishing two sureties each of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the Special 
Judge (Gangster Act), Nainital. 
The Magistrate concerned shall 
obtain photographs of the appellant/ 
accused and their identity proof on the 
personal bond and while taking sureties, 
the Magistrate shall also ensure that the 
latest photographs of the sureties along 
with their proper identity proof be affixed. The Magistrate concerned shall 
also obtain duplicate copy of the papers 
regarding personal bonds and sureties 
and will send one set of the same to this 
Court so that same may be placed on 
record. 
 (Lok Pal Singh, J.) 
Parul 05.01.2018
