ORDER : (per Mr. Manoj Kumar Gupta C. J.)
1. The seventh respondent by transfer on 
attachment basis vide order dated 05.02.2026 has been 
attached at District Haridwar as District Supply Officer. 
2. The case of the appellant is that earlier when 
he was District Supply Officer, Haridwar, he committed 
grave irregularities in issuance of Antodaya Ration Cards. 
The Deputy Commissioner (Food and Civil Supplies), 
Garhwal held inquiry and after seeking response from the 
seventh respondent, vide his report dated 04.07.2023,
recommended for disciplinary action being taken against 
him and two Supply Inspectors, namely, Smt. Babita and 
Shri Toynath Sharma. On 11.08.2023, the seventh 
respondent was transferred to Almora on administrative 
1grounds.
3. The department held inquiry against the 
marketing inspectors but no inquiry was held against 
respondent no.7, despite, the recommendation made by 
the Deputy Commissioner (Food and Civil Supplies), 
Garhwal for holding disciplinary inquiry. He is now again 
being posted by attachment at District Haridwar.
4. It is submitted that before learned Single 
Judge, the State Government actively concealed the fact 
that in fact it had held no inquiry against the seventh 
respondent so far, as directed by the Deputy 
Commissioner, Foods.
5. Learned State Counsel was required to obtain 
instructions in this behalf by the previous order dated 
15.06.2026.
6. In compliance of the said direction, learned 
State Counsel has placed on record the instructions 
dated 16.06.2026 received from the Commissioner (Food
and Civil Supplies) in which again it has not been clearly 
stated as to whether any inquiry was held against the 
seventh respondent in compliance of the order of Deputy 
Commissioner (Food and Civil Supplies). 
7. On the contrary in paragraph no.1 of the 
instructions, it is stated that on the basis of report of 
2Deputy Commissioner (Food and Civil Supplies), Garhwal 
dated 04.07.2023 no financial irregularities have come to 
light, which is clearly contrary to the observations and 
findings in the report of the Deputy Commissioner (Food 
and Civil Supplies) dated 04.07.2023.
8. It further appears that by a subsequent order 
dated 10.06.2026 respondent no.7 has now been 
permanently transferred as DSO, Haridwar.
9. As there were serious adverse observations 
against the seventh respondent in the inquiry held by 
Deputy Commissioner (Food and Civil Supplies) and,
despite specific recommendations for holding inquiry 
against respondent no.7, without holding any such 
inquiry, he has again been posted at Haridwar, we stay 
the operation of the order of attachment dated 
05.02.2026 as well as any subsequent order, which may 
have the effect of posting him at Haridwar.
10. List in the week commencing 27.07.2026.
11. Meanwhile, the respondents shall file counter 
affidavit.
