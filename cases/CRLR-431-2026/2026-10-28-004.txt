Mr. Bhuwan Bhatt, learned counsel for the 
revisionists. 
2. Mr. Vaibhav Singh Chauhan, learned 
counsel for the respondent. 
3. In pursuance of the earlier order passed in 
this Court, the amended delay condonation 
application has been filed. The same is taken on 
record. 
4. Delay Condonation Application (IA No.1 of 
2026) has been filed on behalf of the revisionists 
seeking condonation of delay of 579 days in 
preferring the present criminal revision. The 
grounds on which the condonation of delay has 
been sought are that the revisionist no.1 had filed 
an appeal before the Sessions Judge, Haridwar
bearing Criminal Appeal No.80 of 2022 titled as 
Pradhuman Agarwal v. Ambrish Kumar Rastogi. 
However, during the pendency of the said 
criminal appeal, the revisionist no.1 met with sad demise on 28.06.2023 and the said criminal 
appeal was not contested on behalf of the 
revisionist no.1 because the Revisionist No.2 was 
not aware about these proceedings, and as such, 
it was dismissed vide order dated 07.08.2024.
5. The respondent initiated proceedings of 
execution on 17.07.2025, and notice was issued 
in the address of the property situated at 
Kankhal, District Haridwar. The notices of the 
said execution of the property served upon the 
deponent through WhatsApp application, 
thereafter the deponent immediately contacted 
his counsel in Haridwar and got the knowledge 
about the judgments dated 21.12.2021 and 
07.08.2024.
6. Thus, the condonation of delay of 579 days 
in filing the present criminal revision has 
occurred and that is the ground on which the 
condonation of delay has been sought. 
7. The learned counsel for the respondent does 
not have any objection to the said delay 
condonation application. 
8. In such view of the matter, the delay shown 
is sufficient and the delay condonation 
application deserves to be allowed and is 
accordingly allowed. The criminal revision shall 
be treated to be within time. 
9. List on 05.11.2026 for admission.
