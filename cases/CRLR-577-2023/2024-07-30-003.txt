CRLR No.577 of 2023
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Syed Nadim, Advocate for the
revisionist.
Mr. Vipul Painuly, Brief Holder for the 
State.
The challenge in this revision is made 
to the order dated 26.05.2023 passed in 
Miscellaneous Criminal Case No.426 of 
2022, Smt. Sidara & others vs. Amir, 
under Section 125 of the Code of Criminal 
Procedure, 1973, by the Judge, Family 
Court, Haldwani, District Nainital. By it, 
revisionist has been directed to pay 
Rs.8,000/- per month (Rs.3,000/-to the 
respondent no.2-wife and Rs.25,00/- each 
to the respondent no.3-son and 
respondent no.4-daughter, respectively) 
as maintenance.
Heard.
Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would submit that the revisionist is a 
junior lawyer. He gets Rs.10,000/- per 
month from the consultancy firm where he 
works. He is not in a position to pay 
Rs.8,000/- to the respondent nos.2 to 4. 
Having heard, this Court is of the 
view that this matter requires deliberation.
Issue notice to the private 
respondents returnable within six weeks. 
Steps to be taken within a week.
List on 15.10.2024.
