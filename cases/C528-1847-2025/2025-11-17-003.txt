C528 No.1847 of 2025
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Abhishek Singh Rawat and Ms. Babita 
Jalal, learned counsel for applicants. 
2. Mr. Akshay Latwal, learned A.G.A. with Ms. 
Sweta Badola Dobhal and Mr. Prabhat Kandpal, 
learned Brief Holders for State of Uttarakhand/
respondent No.1.
3. By means of the present C528 application, 
applicants have challenged the charge-sheet 
dated 25.06.2025 pursuant to FIR No.0180 of 
2024 dated 07.07.2024 u/s 66C of the 
Information & Technology Act, 2002 (amended 
up to date) and u/s 504 and 506 IPC, registered 
with P.S. Kotwali Kotdwar, District Pauri Garhwal, 
as well as the entire proceedings of Criminal 
Case No.1488 of 2025 State Vs. Sagar Tiwari and 
Ors., pending in the Court of learned Additional 
Chief Judicial Magistrate, Kotdwar, District Pauri 
Garhwal, u/s 66C of the Information &
Technology Act, 2002 (amended up to date) and 
u/s 504, 506 and 120B IPC.
4. In short the case of the prosecution is that 
informant Saloni Tiwari/respondent No.4 has 
registered a case against the applicants stating 
therein that applicant No.2-Akash Tiwari on the 
asking of applicant No.1-Sagar Tiwari has 
created a fake instagram id and used that id for 
threatening the informant. It is further stated 
that Kabir Dass-respondent No.5 is a person who 
provided id and password to Akash Tiwari and 
they thereby threatened the informant￾respondent No.4 and used abusive language 
against her.
5. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
applicants that applicant No.1-Sagar Tiwari and 
respondent No.4-Saloni Tiwari are husband and 
wife and there is some dispute pending between them in Court of law. Applicant No.2-Akash 
Tiwari is real brother of applicant No.1-Sagar 
Tiwari, who lives in Wainfleet Skegness, United 
Kingdom.
6. It is further contended by learned counsel 
for the applicants that this FIR has been lodged 
by respondent No.4-Saloni Tiwari, wife of 
applicant No.1-Sagar Tiwari, in counterblast, as 
matrimonial dispute is pending between them.
7. Per contra, learned State Counsel submits 
that there are allegations against both the 
applicants of threatening the informant.
8. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties, prima facie this Court is satisfied with 
the submissions made by learned counsel for the 
applicants. Since applicant No.1-Sagar Tiwari and 
respondent No.4-Saloni Tiwari are husband and 
wife, and there appears to be some matrimonial 
dispute between them, therefore, the 
proceedings of the above criminal case needs to 
be stayed at this stage.
9. Issue notice to respondent Nos.2 to 4, 
returnable within four weeks.
10. Steps be taken within a week.
11. Put up on 24.02.2026.
12. In the meantime, proceedings of Criminal 
Case No.1488 of 2025 State Vs. Sagar Tiwari and 
Ors., pending in the Court of learned Additional 
Chief Judicial Magistrate, Kotdwar, District Pauri 
Garhwal, shall remain stayed.
13. Stay Application (IA/1/2025) stands 
disposed of.
