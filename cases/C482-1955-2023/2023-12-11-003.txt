COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C-482 No. 1955 of 2023
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Present Mr. Aditya Singh, counsel for the 
applicant.
Present Mr. Deepak Bisht, D.A.G. for the 
State/respondent no. 1.
Present Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, counsel
accepts notice on behalf of the respondent 
no.2. He seeks and is granted time to file his 
Vakalatnama.
2. Counsel for the applicant would submit 
that present C-482 application is arising out of 
the judgment and order dated 06.11.2019 
passed by 4th Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Dehradun in Misc. Case No. 115
of 2017, whereby the said court passed the 
judgment on the basis of the agreement arrived 
at between the applicant and respondent no. 2
in the mediation proceedings and the said 
agreement was made part of the judgment.
He would further submit that the terms of 
the agreement were not exactly what was 
agreed upon between the parties on which 
basis, the order dated 06.11.2019 has been 
passed, therefore, the applicant moved an 
application for review before the 2nd
Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Dehradun; that, the said court did not find any 
favour with the applicant and dismissed the 
review application vide order dated 
21.12.2021. Thereafter, the applicant preferred 
an appeal, which was also dismissed vide 
judgment and order dated 15.09.2023 by the 
6th Additional District & Sessions Judge, 
Dehradun, hence, the present C-482 application. 
He would further submit that the said 
settlement/agreement was drafted by both the 
counsel for the parties, however, the terms 
were not exactly what was agreed between the 
parties. 
He would further submit that even as per 
the agreement, the Case No. 2789 of 2018 
pending before 4th Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, which was filed by respondent 
no.2/wife under Sections 494 & 495 of IPC, 
has not been withdrawn by her.
3. Per contra, counsel for the respondent 
no.2 would submit that the applicant/husband 
is well educated person and a Government 
employee and the agreement arrived at 
between the parties was in the language of 
Hindi, therefore, the averments made by the 
applicant that the terms were not exactly what 
was agreed between the parties, is concocted 
and frivolous; that, the applicant wants to 
withdraw himself from the terms of the 
agreement. 
He would further submit that as per 
terms of the agreement settled between 
applicant and respondent no.2 in the 
mediation, the payment against two cheques in 
the amount of 9 lacs and 4 lacs is stopped by 
the applicant; that, the offences for which the 
Case No. 2789 of 2018, cannot be withdrawn, 
as the offences punishable under Sections 494 
& 495 I.P.C., are non-compoundable offence. 
He would further submit that Petition 
No. 810 of 2018 filed under Section 13 of 
Hindu Marriage Act, for dissolution of 
marriage by decree of divorce is also to be 
withdrawn by the applicant as per the terms of 
the contract. 
4. Counsel for the applicant would submit that if the respondent no. 2 moves an 
appropriate application for withdrawing the 
Case No. 2789 of 2018 under Sections 494 & 
495 of IPC and ready to cooperate for 
dissolution of marriage by decree of divorce 
with mutual consent under the scheme of 
Hindu Marriage Act, then the applicant would 
abide by the terms of the agreement arrived at 
in the impugned judgment.
5. To this, counsel for the respondent no. 2 
seeks time to file counter affidavit/reply.
6. List this matter on 08.01.2024.
7. Meanwhile, counsel for the applicant 
shall remove the defects as pointed out by the 
Registry.
