Mr. Anil Anthwal, learned counsel for the

3. Heard on Delay Condonation Application.
4. There is delay of 202 days in filing the recall 
application. The delay has been sufficiently 
explained and is hereby condoned. Accordingly, 
delay condonation application is allowed. 
5. The recall application is accordingly taken up 
for consideration. 
6. The submission of learned counsel for the 
appellant is that the order of this Court, dismissing 
the appeal, but with liberty to the appellant to 
challenge the letter of the University dated
25.05.2018, on the basis of which the services of 
the appellant was terminated, would not serve any purpose as the order of learned Single Judge, under 
challenge, against the termination order thereby 
stands upheld. It is submitted that the learned 
Single Judge has decided the bunch of writ petitions 
by a common order and judgment dated 03.10.2024 
and against which separate special appeals were 
filed by aggrieved persons. 
7. It is pointed out that one of such appeals being 
Special Appeal No.52 of 2025 filed by Smt. Kakuli
Mandal has been allowed by the Coordinate Division 
Bench vide judgment dated 31.07.2025 and the said 
judgment has also been upheld by the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court by dismissing the Special Leave 
Petition vide order dated 16.04.2026. 
8. The contention appears to have force, as in 
case the appeal is not heard and decided on merits, 
the order of learned Single Judge, dismissing the 
challenge to the order of termination of services of 
the appellant, would attain finality and any separate 
challenge made by the appellant in pursuance of 
liberty granted by this Court before the learned 
Single Judge would not serve any purpose. 
Accordingly, recall application is allowed. Order 
dated 31.07.2025 is hereby recalled. 
9. The special appeal shall be listed for hearing 
before the appropriate Bench as per Roster. 
(Ashish Naithani, J.) (Manoj Kumar Gupta, C.J.)
