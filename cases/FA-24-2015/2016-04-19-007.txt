It is pertinent to note that no 
written objections have been filed by the 
respondents against the application for 
leave to appeal. 
Having heard the rival contentions, 
it transpires that Original Suit 
No.244/2002 was filed by the plaintiffs Mr. 
Vijay Singh and Mr. Gulab Singh, in whose 
favour, the impugned judgment has been 
passed, against Dr. A.K. Srivastava, who is 
only the Director of the Institution, 
namely, Academy of Management Studies, 
Dehradun, being run under the 
administrative control of applicant 
Dayanand Shiksha Sansthan. 
The injunction order was passed 
against Dr. A.K. Srivastava because he 
remains physically present at the spot, but 
it cannot be gainsaid that by this 
injunction order, the ultimate sufferer is 
the applicant Institution, which was not 
impleaded as the defendant in the array of 
parties in Original Suit No.244/2002. 
So, in view of what has been set 
forth above, I grant leave to appeal with a 
rider that under the garb of this appeal, 
the appellant Institution shall not put any 
submission seeking remand of the matter 
for trial afresh just on the question that 
the suit was bad on account of non-joinder 
of necessary party because it is a legal 
question and can well be adjudicated by 
this Court in this appeal.  Appeal is admitted for hearing. 
Summon the LCR. 
List along with connected appeal 
soon after LCR is received. 
During pendency of this appeal, 
parties are directed not to create any third 
party interest or encumbrance on the 
property, in question, in any manner, 
whatsoever and they will also not change its 
nature. 
Application for leave to appeal 
(CLMA 1590/2015) and interim relief 
application (CLMA 1592/2015) stand 
disposed of accordingly.
