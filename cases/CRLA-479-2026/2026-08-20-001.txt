Mr. Akhil Kumar Sah, learned counsel 
for the appellant.
2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned AGA for 
the State.
3. The instant criminal appeal is preferred 
by the appellant-Jacky Sagar, S/o Shri Hari 
Singh Sagar against the judgment and order 
passed by the learned Additional Session 
Judge, Ramnagar, District Nainital dated 
15.07.2026 in Session Trial No.01 of 2025 
arising out of Case Crime No.126 of 2023 
whereby the appellant is convicted for the 
offence punishable under Section 495 of IPC 
with the sentence of three years rigorous 
imprisonment and fine of Rs.50,000/-
4. The appeal is well within time. 
5. Admit.
6. Summon the trial court record.
7. Heard on bail application. 
8. It is submitted by learned counsel for 
the appellant that the appellant was on bail 
during trial and after conviction the trial 
court grant him interim bail for one month so 
that during that period he may file the appeal 
but the appeal could not be filed within that 
period and there is only two days in preferring the appeal. He submits that when 
appellant was on bail he never misused the 
same. 
9. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned AGA 
have not disputed on such submission and 
now taking into consideration that appellant 
was on bail during trial and after conviction 
he was granted interim bail, this Court is of 
the view that the interim bail granted by the 
trial court be made absolute.
10. Accordingly, without expressing any 
opinion on the merit of the case, the bail 
application is allowed and the interim bail 
granted by the trial court is made absolute 
and the appellant shall remain on bail during 
the pendency of the instant bail application. 
11. Fine shall also remain suspended. 
12. List this criminal appeal in the month of 
November 2026.
