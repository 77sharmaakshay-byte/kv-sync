COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C-528 No. 597 of 2026
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Ms. Geetanjali Dhami, learned counsel
for the applicant.
2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned A.G.A. for the
State.
3. The present criminal misc. application 
has been filed by the applicant with a prayer 
to quash and set-aside the chargesheet, 
summoning order as well as the entire 
proceedings of Special Sessions Trial No. 38 of 
2022, State Vs. Deepanshu Kunwar and 
Others, pending in the court of learned 
Special Sessions Judge (POCSO), Almora.
4. In this case, an FIR was lodged on the 
report of the Child Welfare Committee, 
wherein, it was alleged that a Journalist 
belonging to a private company has taken 
interview of the victim and has broadcasted it 
in its news channel and thereafter that news 
was circulated in the social media. During 
investigation, no offence under Section 228 of 
IPC was found against the applicant by the 
Investigating Officer, but, despite that, he has 
been chargesheeted under Section 23 and 74 
of Juvenile Justice Act.
5. Learned counsel for the applicant would 
submit that applicant has falsely been 
implicated in the present case and he has not 
forwarded the said news of the victim. During 
investigation, charges of Section 228 of IPC 
were dropped against the applicant; applicant 
is pursuing his studies and is preparing for 
competitive examination.6. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
7. This Court finds some substance in the 
submission made by learned counsel for the 
applicants and having heard the submission, 
under the facts and circumstances of the case, 
this Court is of the view that this matter 
definitely requires deliberation.
8. Issue notice to respondent no. 2, 
returnable within three weeks. Steps to be 
taken within a week.
9. List after service report is received.
10. In the meantime, counter-affidavit(s), if 
any, be filed by the respondents.
11. After perusing the records and having 
heard learned counsel for the parties and also 
considering the fact that civil suits are 
pending between the parties, this Court, 
prima facie, is of the opinion and is satisfied 
that in the interest of justice, further 
proceedings of Special Sessions Trial No. 38 of 
2022, State Vs. Deepanshu Kunwar and 
Others, pending in the court of learned 
Special Sessions Judge (POCSO), Almora, 
shall remain stayed qua the applicant till the 
next date of listing.
 (Alok Mahra J.) 29.04.2026
