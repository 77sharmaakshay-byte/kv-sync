Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. S.R. Singh and Mr. V.B.S. Negi, learned 
senior counsel assisted by Mr. M.C. Pant and Ms 
Azmeen Wason, learned counsel for the petitoiner.
2. Mr. Rahul Verma, learned Deputy Advocate 
General with Mr. P.S. Bisht, learned Chief Standing 
Counsel for the respondent/contemnor.
3. Learned State counsel placed before this court 
the written instructions of the Government and 
apprise to this court that pursuant to the decision of 
the Cabinet now the cut-off date is changed from 
12.11.2018 to 15.10.2024 and the eligible outsource 
employees will be entitled to get equal pay for equal 
work w.e.f. 01.03.2026. He also submits that pursuant 
to the decision taken by the Cabinet in some of the 
departments the outsourced employees filled up 
contractual form to get the status of contractual 
employees and all this exercise is being done to cover 
all outsource employees under the purview of 
Regularization Rules of 2013 by giving status of the 
contractual employees. He submits that this is the first 
step for giving the contractual status and thereafter in 
the second step they will be considered under the 
Regularization Rules of 2013. He placed before this 
court the instructions of the Secretary dated 01.07.2026 
as well as the Government Order, which are placed on 
record.
4. Mr. S.R. Singh, learned senior counsel submits 
that compelling the outsourced employees to fill-up 
the contractual form is contrary to the direction of thewrit court. He submits that pursuant to earlier office 
memorandum dated 02.01.2017 some of outsourced
employees were also regularized under the 
Regularization Rules of 2013 as amended time to time, 
therefore, the same treatment should be given to the 
remaining outsourced employees. Mr. S.R. Singh also 
suggests that the State Government can proceed to 
amend the Rules of 2013 in order to include the 
outsourced employees under the Regularization Rules 
of 2013.
5. Mr. P.S. Bisht, learned C.S.C. submits that on this 
aspect he will discuss with the Secretary and then 
apprise to this court on the next date.
6. List this matter on 09.07.2026 as a first case.
7. Copy of this order be handed over to Mr. P.S. 
Bisht, learned C.S.C., by tomorrow.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
