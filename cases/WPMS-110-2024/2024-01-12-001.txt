2. The petitioner has challenged the order 
dated 16.10.2023, whereby the petitioner, who 
is the first appellate authority under the Right to 
Information Act, in the Bar Council of 
Uttarakhand, is directed to give an explanation 
on the points mentioned in paragraph 6 of the 
aforesaid order, and directed the Public 
Information Officer and the Public Information 
Officer, Bar Council of Uttarakhand, has been 
directed to ensure to supply of the information 
to the appellant within three days, and further a 
direction has also been issued in paragraph 11 
of the impugned judgment, whereby the 
Secretary Law and Justice, Department, 
Government of Uttarakhand, is directed to take 
cognizance of the irregularities going on in the 
Bar Council of Uttarakhand.
3. From the perusal of the aforesaid 
judgment and order, it appears that certain 
information was sought by the 
respondent/complainant Smt. Rita Suri, from 
the Bar Council of Uttarakhand. According to 
the petitioner, the information sought by the respondent/complainant was supplied to the 
complainant Rita Suri by the Public 
Information Officer.
4. However, it is admitted that there is some 
delay in the supply of this information. Being 
dissatisfied with the supply of the information
and incomplete information, the 
respondent/complainant preferred an appeal 
before the petitioner/first appellate authority in 
the Bar Council of Uttarakhand, the 
respondent/complainant moved before the 
Information Commission by filing the 
complaint no.14629. 
5. While filing the objection to the said 
complaint, it has been mentioned by the 
petitioner that the information required by the 
petitioner are again being made available to the 
respondent/complainant on 02.12.2022. Still the 
learned Tribunal issued notice to the petitioner 
to give show-cause. The petitioner has appeared 
before the State Information Commission, 
Dehradun, and placed his point of view, but still 
the impugned order has been passed, and the 
aforesaid direction has been issued.
6. Issue notice to the respondent nos.1, 2 
and 4, returnable within six weeks. Steps to be 
taken within a week.
7. List on 18.06.2024.
8. In the meantime, the effect and operation 
of the impugned order dated 16.10.2023, is hereby stayed.
