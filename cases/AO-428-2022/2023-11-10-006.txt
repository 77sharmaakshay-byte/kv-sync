AO No428 of 2022
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. M.S. Bhandari, learned counsel 
for the appellant.
Mr. Pulak Agarwal, learned counsel 
for respondent no.2.
This appeal from order has been 
preferred under Section 173 of the M.V. 
Act, as against the award of 07.07.2022,
whereby the liability of payment of 
compensation as assessed to be 
Rs.15,35,000/- along with interest 
payable on it @ 7% has been fastened 
upon the appellant.
The appeal from order is 
accompanied with delay condonation 
application No.IA/2/2022 seeking 
condonation of 51 days of delay.
The Co-ordinate Bench, had issued 
notices to the respondents by an order of 
16.12.2022 and, as per the office report, 
respondent no.1 is shown to have been 
served personally.
Respondent no.2 is represented by 
Mr. Pulak Agarwal, learned counsel.
Respondent no.2 may not be having 
any serious objection as such to the delay 
condonation application.Since respondent no.1, despite of 
service of notice, has not put in 
appearance and the delay condonation 
application remains unopposed by 
respondent no.1, the same would stand 
allowed. The delay of 51 days, which has 
chanced in filing the appeal from order,
would hereby stand condoned.
With the consent of the present 
counsels, the appeal from order is heard 
for admission.
Admit the appeal
Summon the LCR.
Issue notice to respondent no.1.
Steps to serve the respondent will be 
taken within seven working days.
Subject to the condition that the 
appellant deposits an amount of Rs.10 
lakh within a period of six weeks from 
today before tribunal, the execution of the 
decreed Award dated 07.07.2022 would be 
kept in abeyance.
List after service of notice on the 
respondent.
Accordingly, Urgency Application 
No.IA/7/2023 would stand disposed of.
