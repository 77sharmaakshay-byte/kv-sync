petitioner.
2. There is no representation for the respondents.
3. By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has 
challenged the orders dated (4685) dated 11.11.2023 and 
(16637-44) dated 11.11.2023 (Annexure nos.9 and 10), 
whereby pay of the petitioner has been re-fixed and
accordingly reduced and a recovery has been directed to 
be made from salary of the petitioner.
4. It is case of the petitioner that the petitioner is 
posted as Sub-Inspector with the respondent-Department 
and his pay was fixed on the basic-pay of ₹46,200/-
Level-6 on 01.07.2016, but now by the impugned order 
the pay was re-fixed to ₹44,900/- Level-6 vide order 
dated 11.11.2023.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that 
pay of the petitioner was reduced after taking recourse to 
the provisions of CCS (Revised Pay Rules), 2016, but 
according to the petitioner the said Rules are not 
applicable to him as the same has been clarified by a letter 
issued by D.O.P.T. on 27.07.2017 and further on 
20.09.2018 that they cannot reduce the salary of the 
petitioner and no recovery could have been effected.
6. It is further submitted by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that the petitioner is a Sub-Inspector with the 
respondent-Department and there is no such allegation 2
that any fraud or misrepresentation was played by the 
petitioner in allegedly wrong fixation of his salary.
7. Learned counsel for the petitioner has placed his 
reliance on a judgment of Hon’ble Apex Court in the case 
of State of Punjab Vs. Rafiq Masih (2014) 8 SCC 883 
wherein it is stated that “where the employee is not 
instrumental for the access payment being made to him 
and no fraud and mis-representation has been played by 
the employee, and if he is a low paid employee such 
recovery shall not be made from such employee”, 
however the petitioner is a Class-B Employee, but there is 
no allegation against him of any complicity with the 
wrong fixation of the salary rather it is fault on the part of 
the respondent who wrongly fixed salary of the petitioner.
8. Learned counsel for the respondents is directed to 
file its counter affidavit within a period of six weeks.
9. List this case on 19.03.2024.
10. In the meantime, no recovery shall be made from 
the petitioner pursuant to the impugned order dated 
(16637-44) dated 11.11.2023.
11. Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2023) stands 
disposed-off accordingly.
