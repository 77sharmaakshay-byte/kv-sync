Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Gaurav Singh, learned counsel for the 
applicant.
2. By the instant petition preferred under section 528 
of BNSS, 2023, the present applicant is challenging the 
proceeding of Complaint Case No. 364 of 2025 ‘Hori Lal 
Kontya vs. Sudheer Kumar Thakur’, which is a 
proceeding under section 138 of N.I. Act, 1881 on the 
ground that the allegations as alleged in the complaint 
particularly para-2 clearly reveals that the cheque in 
question relates to time barred debt of 2014.
3. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
applicant that the cheque issued for a time barred debt 
does not constitute a legally enforceable liability under 
section 138 of N.I. Act, therefore, the proceeding is 
completely an abuse of process of law.
4. This aspect requires scrutiny.
5. Issue notice to the respondent.
6. Steps within a week.
7. Let the respondent may file counter affidavit 
within three weeks.
8. One week thereafter is granted to the applicant to 
file rejoinder affidavit.
9. List on 02.11.2026.
10. In the meantime, further proceeding of Complaint 
Case No. 364 of 2025 ‘Hori Lal Kontya vs. Sudheer 
Kumar Thakur’ pending in the court of learned Judicial 
Magistrate-III, Haridwar, shall remain stayed.
