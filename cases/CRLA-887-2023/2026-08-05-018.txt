The instant appeal has been preferred against 
judgment and order dated 28.11.2023/01.12.2023, passed in 
Sessions Trial No.89 of 2012, State Vs. Gauransh Uniyal and 
Others, by the court of 6th Additional Sessions Judge, Dehradun. 
By it, the appellants have been convicted under Section 302 read 
with Section 34 IPC, and sentenced accordingly. The appellant￾Gaurav Uniyal has filed Second Short Term Bail Application, IA 
No.6 of 2026, on the ground that the condition of his eyes is 
deteriorating.
2. Heard on Second Short Term Bail Application, IA 
No.6 of 2026
3. Learned counsel for the appellants submits that 
the appellant Gaurav Uniyal was granted short term bail on the 
ground that the condition of his eyes was deteriorating; he is 
suffering from high myopia in both eyes; he is at the verge of 
becoming blind. He was to undergo refractive surgery for his eyes 
at PGI, Chandigarh. The appellant visited PGI Chandigarh, where 
some further tests have been advised and the next date for 2
consultation is 06.08.2026. It is argued that for another four 
weeks, the appellant may be granted short term bail. 
4. Fact remains that on the condition of the eyes of 
the appellant Gaurav Uniyal, he was granted short term bail. 
Some prescriptions have been filed along with the short term bail 
application.
5. Having considered, without adverting to the merits 
of the case, purely on the humanitarian ground, this Court is of 
the view that the appellant-Gaurav Uniyal may be enlarged on 
short term bail for a period of four weeks from the date of his 
release.
6. Let the appellant-Gaurav Uniyal be released on 
short term bail for a period of four weeks from the date of his 
actual release, on his executing a personal bond and furnishing 
two reliable sureties, each of the like amount, to the satisfaction of 
the court concerned.
7. It is further directed that on expiry of the period of 
short term bail, the appellant-Gaurav Uniyal shall surrender 
before the court concerned and intimation to that effect shall also 
be given to this Court.
8. The short term bail application stands disposed of, 
accordingly. 
9. List this matter on 16.09.2026, for hearing on the 
bail application, IA No.1 of 2023.
