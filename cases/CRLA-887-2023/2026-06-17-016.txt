2. Instant appeal is preferred against the judgment and 
order dated 28.11.2023/01.12.2023, passed in Session Trial 
No.89 of 2012, State vs. Gauransh Uniyal and others, by the 
court of learned 6th Additional Sessions Judge, Dehradun. 
By it, the appellants have been convicted under Section 302 
read with Section 34 of IPC. 
3. Heard the learned counsel for the parties on the Short 
Term Bail Application (IA No.04 of 2026). 
4. The appellant Gaurav Uniyal seeks short-term bail on 
the ground that he is suffering from high myopia in both 
eyes; the condition of his eyes is deteriorating day by day. 
He is on the verge of becoming blind. He needs to undergo 
refractive surgery for his eyes at PGI Chandigarh.
5. Learned counsel for the appellant would submit that 
the appellant is suffering from myopia and has to undergo 
refractive surgery at PGI Chandigarh so as to restore his 
vision, which is deteriorating day by day.
6. Learned State Counsel, on instructions, would submit that, in fact, the appellant is suffering from myopia and the 
visual prognosis is guarded in view of pathological myopia. 
It is admitted that the appellant has been consulted by 
doctors for his low vision.
7. Having considered, purely on humanitarian ground, 
the short term bail is granted to the appellant for a period of 
two weeks from the date of his release, subject to his 
executing a personal bond and furnishing two reliable 
sureties, each of the like amount, to the satisfaction of the 
concerned court.
8. It is further directed that on expiry of the duration of 
short term bail, the appellant shall surrender before the court 
concerned.
9. List this case on 28.07.2026. 
10. Short term bail application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
