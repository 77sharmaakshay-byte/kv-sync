The short term bail has been sought 
by the appellant on the ground that the 
appellant is suffering with some eye related 
problems; his vision has been quite
deteriorated; at present, he is suffering from 
High Myopia and he has been advised for 
refractive surgery in December, 2025.
Learned State Counsel was required 
to get instructions. Today, under 
instructions, learned State Counsel submits
that the appellant is undergoing treatment 
at AIIMS, Rishikesh.
Let State file a short affidavit revealing 
as to on which dates the appellant was 
taken to hospital and as to whether it is 
true that the appellant was advised for 
refractive surgery, and if it is so, when was
the procedure done and if it is not done, 
when it is proposed.
List this matter on 10.06.2026.
