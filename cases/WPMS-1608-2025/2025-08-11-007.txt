The petitioner has brought to the 
notice of the Court that order dated 
27.06.2025, passed by this Court has been 
willfully disobeyed by the respondents 
because the Court had clarified that the 
delegates of the member cooperative 
society may cast the vote but the delegates 
should be elected representatives of the 
member cooperative society.
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that, in fact, the delegates who are 
even unelected have been permitted to cast 
their vote.
Let respondent nos. 3 and 4 file 
response to IA No. 3 of 2025 filed by the 
petitioner within three weeks.
