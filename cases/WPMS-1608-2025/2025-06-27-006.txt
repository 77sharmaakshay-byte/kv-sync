Heard on Stay Application No. 2 of 2025
8. The challenge in this petition is made to a communication 
dated 15.05.2025, issued by the respondent no.2, the Commissioner, 
Cane Development and Sugar Industries, by which, constituencies for 
Cane Development Societies election has been notified under Section 
29 (3) of the Uttarakhand Cooperative Societies Act, 2003 (“the Act”) as 
well as Rule 450 (8) of the Uttarakhand Cooperative Societies Rules, 
2004 (“the 2004 Rules”).
9. Learned counsel for the respondent no.4 as well as the 
Administrator of the Society submit the petitioner is not a member of 
the Cooperative Society. He has not locus standi to file the petitioner. He 
would refer to an information supplied to him under the Right To 
Information Act, 2005 in support of it. It is taken on record.
10. In answer to it, learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that the petitioner is member of the Society. His father’s name of 
Jagveer @ Jaipal and he has documents to show it. Learned counsel for 
the petitioner placed certain documents for the perusal of the Court 
which is from UP Ganna Portal-Board to argue that, in fact, the 
petitioner’s father name is at some places written as Jaipal. It has two 
more documents with it. The last document, it is submitted is the 
document given by the sugar mill to reveal that the petitioner has given 
cane in the sugar mill in which the name of his father is written as
Jaipal @ Jagveer. These documents are also taken on record. 
10. Learned counsel for the petitioner would submit that the 
Cane Development Societies are always primary societies. Another 
society may not be its member. He would submit that in Cooperative 
Cane Development Society, Libberhedi, District Haridwar (“the Society”), 3
many cooperative societies have been inducted as its member, who have 
nothing to do with the cane development. It may influence the election 
of the society, which in turn may not be in the interest of cane growers 
for whose benefit, the society has been formed. Secondly, it is argued 
that even if it is accepted that any cooperative society can be its 
member, the right to vote cannot be given to any nominated member of 
any member cooperative society. It can be given to a delegate, who 
under Rule 82 of the 2004 Rules, may be an elected member of the 
member cooperative society.
12. Learned counsel appearing for the respondents as well as 
the Administrator of the Society submits that the Cooperative Societies 
may be member of the society as per its Byelaws. It is also submitted 
that in view of Clause 34 of the Byelaws, one delegate of any member 
Cooperative Society may also cast vote. 
13. The only question is with regard to the nature of delegates
of the member Cooperative Societies. What is being argued on behalf of 
the petitioner is that the delegates should be elected.
14. Learned counsel for the respondent no.4 would submit that 
it may be either nominated or elected member of the member 
Cooperative Society.
15. Learned counsel for the Administrator of the Society 
submits that according to the Scheme of the Act, a delegate may only be 
elected.
16. Reference has been made to Sections 20 and 21 of the Act. 
Section 20 first proviso provides that no nominal or associated member 
shall have right to vote. Whereas, Section 21 of the Act provides that 
every individual member, every delegate and every nominee shall 4
exercise his vote in the affairs of the Cooperative Society in person and 
no member, delegate or nominee shall be permitted to vote by proxy. A 
plain reading of Section 21 of the Act makes it clear that the status of 
delegate and nominee is different. Who is delegate and who is nominee, 
it is two distinct status.
17. Rules 82 to 85 of the 2004 Rules deals with election of 
delegates. According to Rule 82 A of the Rules, the process of election of 
delegates of the cooperative societies shall be the same as that of the 
election of the member of the Committee of Management, which means
that the delegates are to be elected. It is what is argued by the petitioner 
and what is admitted by learned counsel for the Administrator of the 
Society.
18. By means of the stay application, the petitioner has sought 
stay the effect and operation of the information dated 15.05.2025. Such 
a stay may not be granted because the information is with regard to 
various other societies also.
19. Insofar as, the Cooperative Cane Development Society,
Libbarhedi District Haridwar is concerned, this Court clarifies that the 
delegates of the member societies may cast the vote, but the delegates 
should be elected representatives of the member cooperative societies. 
20. The Stay Application stands disposed of accordingly.
