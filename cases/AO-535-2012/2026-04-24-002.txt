D/12 Delay Condonation Application (CLMA) No.11955 of 2012
In
AO No.535 of 2012
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J. Mr. Rajendra Singh Arya, Advocate holding 
brief of Mr. Neeraj Garg, Advocate for the 
appellant.
 Mr. Bindesh Kumar Gupta, Advocate for 
respondent no.1/National Insurance Co. Ltd.
 The appellant proposes to challenge the 
judgment and award dated 29.05.2010, passed in 
Motor Accident Claim Case No. 100 of 2008, 
Master Saurabh Gupta Vs. National Insurance Co. 
Ltd. and others by the Motor Accident Claim 
Tribunal/District Judge, Dehradun. 
 It is delayed. A delay condonation application 
has been filed.
 Heard on delay condonation application. 
 Learned counsel for the appellant submits that 
since the appellant could not get timely advised, 
therefore, delay occurred. 
 The objections have been filed on behalf of the 
respondent no.1/National Insurance Co. Ltd. that 
this would not be the ground to condone the 
delay. 
 The fact remains that this appeal was filed in 
the year 2012. As an Institution, we have also 
delayed disposal of the delay condonation 
application on time. 
 Having considered, the grounds of delay are 
good enough to condone the delay. Therefore, 
delay in filing the appeal is condoned. 
 Delay condonation application is allowed.
 In order to appreciate the arguments on 
admission, call for the lower court record. 
 Once lower court record is received, paper-book 
be prepared and supplied to the learned counsel 
for the parties, as per Rules. 
 List on 18.06.2026.
