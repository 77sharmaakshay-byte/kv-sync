This is second appeal arising out of 
matrimonial discord. The husband seeks 
divorce, which has not been held.
Learned counsel for the appellant submits 
that the parties are staying separate for more 
than nine years now. It is a case of irretrievable 
break down of marriage and there has been no 
cohabitation for approximately nine years. 
Hence, it amounts to cruelty.
Before any decision is taken, this Court is 
of the view that one more effort should be made 
for settlement that may arrive between the 
parties.
List on 21.08.2026 at 10:30 a.m.
On that date, parties shall appear in 
person before this Court.
