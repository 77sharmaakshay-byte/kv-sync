Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
 Mr. R.S. Sammal, Senior Advocate

 Mr. Akshay Joshi, Advocate for the

 The appellant filed a suit for divorce 
against the respondent, which was 
registered as Matrimonial Suit No.110 
of 2021, Rajendra Singh Airi Vs. Smt. 
Nanda Devi, in the court of Civil Judge 
(Sr. Div.), Pithoragarh (“the case”). But, 
the case was dismissed on 30.09.2023, 
which was challenged by the appellant 
in Civil Appeal No.7 of 2023, Rajendra 
Singh Airi Vs. Smt. Nanda Devi, in the 
court of District Judge, Pithoragarh 
(“the appeal”). The appeal was 
dismissed on 22.12.2023.
 Heard on admission.
 Learned counsel for the appellant 
would submit that the appellant and 
respondent, both are staying separate 
since 2017. There is continuous 
separation and the marriage has 
become fiction. 
 Learned counsel for the appellant 
would submit that in view of the law as 
laid down by the Hon’ble Supreme 
Court in the case of Rakesh Raman Vs. 
Kavita, AIR 2023 SC 2144, this mass 
separation amounts to cruelty. It is 
submitted that under the admitted 
facts, this law has not been considered and this is the substantial question of 
law in this second appeal. 
 The appeal is admitted on the 
following substantial questions of law:-
(i) Whether the courts below 
committed an error and failed 
to exercise jurisdiction as long 
period of separation coupled 
with the absence of 
cohabitation amounts to 
cruelty, which is a valid 
ground for divorce under 
Section 13 of the Hindu 
Marriage Act, 1955.
 Call for the lower court record.
 List for final hearing on 16.07.2026.
