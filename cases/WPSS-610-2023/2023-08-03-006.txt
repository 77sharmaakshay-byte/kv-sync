WPSS No. 610 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. P.C. Bisht, learned Additional Chief 
Standing Counsel for the State.
3. In this case, though the counter 
affidavit has been filed by respondent no.4-
District Magistrate, Bageshwar, to which a 
rejoinder affidavit has already been filed by 
the petitioner, but from the perusal of the 
writ petition and the impugned order, it 
appears that a counter affidavit is also 
required to be filed by the Board of 
Revenue-respondent no.2 as well as the 
Commissioner, under whose direction the 
name of the petitioner was passed-over for 
the training of 17th batch of the Revenue 
Inspector/Kanoongo.
4. Heard the learned counsel for the 
petitioner on the interim relief application.
5. It is submitted by the learned counsel 
for the petitioner that he is working as a 
Patwari/Revenue Sub-Inspector since 2005 
and is presently posted as Patwari Kshetra 
Khankar, Tehsil Kafligair, District 
Bageshwar.
6. From perusal of the writ petition, it is reflected that the impugned order, by which 
name of the petitioner was withheld for the 
training of 17th Batch of the Revenue 
Inspector, has not been challenged and 
simply a prayer of mandamus has been 
made by the writ-petitioner.
7. In this view of the matter, at this 
stage, no interim relief could be granted to 
the petitioner.
8. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
seeks two days’ time for amending the writ 
petition.
9. Permission is granted.
10. List this case on 07.08.2023, after 
fresh cases.
11. In the meantime, an amendment 
application may be moved by the petitioner.
