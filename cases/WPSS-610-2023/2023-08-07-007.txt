WPSS No.610 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel along with 
Mr. K.K. Harbola, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. P.C. Bisht, learned Additional C.S.C. for the 
State.
3. The amendment application (IA No.3/2023) has 
been moved on behalf of the petitioner and by this 
amendment application, the prayer clause (i-a) is prayed 
for to be included in the prayer clause of the writ 
petition. 
4. Learned State Counsel has no objection, if the 
amendment application is allowed.
5. The amendment application is allowed. 
6. Let necessary amendment be incorporated during 
the course of the day.
7. The amended writ petition has been filed by the 
petitioner. The same is taken on record. 
8. By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has 
challenged the letters dated 20.10.2022, 08.12.2022 & 
01.04.2023 annexure-11,12 & 13, respectively to the writ 
petition, whereby the respondent has passed over the 
name of the petitioner for the training of 17th batch of 
Revenue Inspector to be held w.e.f. 01.08.2023.
9. Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that 
petitioner was appointed as Patwari, Revenue Sub￾Inspector in the year 2005 and is presently posted as 
Patwari Circle, Khankar, Teshil Kafilgair, District 2
Bageshwar. He also submitted that though he is senior 
and has served more than 18 years of service as Revenue 
Sub-Inspector, but his name has been passed over for the 
training of 17th batch of Revenue Inspector as stated 
above. 
10. From the order impugned, it is reflected that the 
name of the petitioner was withheld for the reason that a 
first information report was lodged against him under 
Section 302 IPC, wherein after investigation, the charge￾sheet has been submitted under Section 306 of IPC in the 
court of Chief Judicial Magistrate, Bageshwar. It appears 
that due to this reason, petitioner’s name was passed
over. 
11. It is submitted by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that proceedings of the aforesaid Criminal 
Case No.490 of 2021, State vs. Tara Dutt Pathak & 
others has been stayed by this Court. 
12. Although, the reasons have been cited by the 
respondent-authorities for not sending the name of the 
petitioner for training, but in view of the fact that the 
proceedings of aforesaid criminal trial has already been 
stayed by this Court, this Court find it appropriate to 
direct the petitioner to be sent for the said training. 
13. By mere registration of a crime, it cannot be said 
that the petitioner has been convicted or is found guilty 
of the alleged offence, therefore, in the opinion of this 
Court only on account of filing of first information report 
and charge-sheet, petitioner cannot be denied the right of 
his consideration for promotion on the post of Revenue 
Inspector for which the training is sine qua non as per the 
provisions of U.P. Subordinate Revenue Executive 3
Bhulekh Nirikshak-Hill Patties of Kumaon and Garhwal 
Division Service Rules, 1983.
14. Per contra, learned State Counsel supported the 
order passed by the authorities saying that some
imputations were there against the petitioner and 
criminal case, though stayed, but it is still pending. 
15. To this proposition that mere filing of FIR and 
charge-sheet, can a person be withheld from sending for 
training? There is no answer. 
16. Though, the counter affidavit has already been 
filed by respondent no.4, but in order to decide the writ 
petition, the counter affidavit is to be filed by respondent 
nos.1, 2 & 3. Learned counsel for the respondent nos.1, 2 
& 3 prays for and is granted six weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit(s).
17. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the 
petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
18. List this matter on 13.10.2023.
19. Having considered the rival contentions, it is 
provided that the impugned order, by which the name of 
the petitioner has been withheld for sending him for 
training of 17th Batch of Revenue Inspector, is hereby 
stayed. The concerned respondent is directed to permit 
the petitioner to undergo training of the 17th Batch of 
Revenue Inspector. The training, so obtained, by the 
petitioner shall be provisional and subject to the final 
outcome of the writ petition.
