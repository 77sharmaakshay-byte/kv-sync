WPSS No.2335 of 2022
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. M.S. Dhapola, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State.
3. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
4. Petitioner was engaged by the respondent￾department as In-charge in Khatima Dakshini 
Dirghakar Multipurpose Cooperative Society Ltd. in 
the year 2018. But, suddenly, he was disengaged by 
the respondent no.3 vide order dated 17.05.2022. 
Thereafter, petitioner moved representation and the 
respondent no.3 vide order dated 15.09.2022 
rejected the same. It is feeling aggrieved by the said 
order, petitioner is before this Court.
5. Learned State Counsel submits that since the 
petitioner’s services have been dispensed with, it is 
not possible to re-engage him in the service without 
there being any vacancy or proposal initiated for 
filling up that vacancy. 
6. Be that as it may, since the petitioner is 
working with the respondent-department and he has 
been disengaged, in the event of there being vacancy 
on the post of In-charge, petitioner can be 
re-engaged against that vacancy. 
7. Learned State Counsel is directed to find out as
to whether there is vacancy in the aforesaid centre on 
the post of In-charge or not.
