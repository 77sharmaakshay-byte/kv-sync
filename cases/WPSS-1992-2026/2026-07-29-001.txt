for respondent No.5-N.C.T.E.
4. By means of present writ petition, 
petitioner has challenged the communication 
dated 25.04.2026 issued by respondent no.2 
(Annexure No. 22 to the writ petition) as well 
as the order dated 06.05.2026 issued by 
respondent no.3, whereby the services of the 
petitioner were terminated.
5. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that petitioner was appointed as an 
Assistant Teacher on 28.05.2022. Petitioner 
was in possession of Bachelor Degree in Arts as 
well as B.Ed. qualification. The petitioner 
secured 42% marks in graduation.
6. The services of petitioner were terminated 
on the ground that his marks is below 45% in 
graduation. 7. It is contended by learned counsel for 
petitioner that in view of the notification issued 
by respondent no.5-NCTE dated 13.11.2019, 
for the candidates who have taken admission in 
B.Ed. course prior to 29.07.2011, the condition 
of securing 45% marks in graduation will not 
be applicable.
8. It is further contended by him that the 
petitioner did graduation and B.Ed. course in 
the year 1998 and 1999 respectively and thus, 
the condition of having 45% marks in 
graduation would not be attracted in the case 
of the petitioner, as he had taken admission in 
B.Ed. Course prior to 29.07.2011.
9. Having perused the writ petition and 
having appreciated the contention raised by 
learned counsel for the parties, this Court finds 
substance in the submission made by learned 
Counsel for the petitioner.
10. In such view of the matter, in the peculiar 
facts and circumstances of the case, petitioner 
deserves protection from this Court.
11. In view of the above, it is directed that till 
the next date of listing, effect and operation of 
the impugned orders dated 25.04.2026 and 
06.05.2026 passed by respondent nos.2 and 3 
respectively, shall remain stayed.
12. Respondents may file their counter 
affidavit(s) in the matter within six weeks. 
13. Put up on 12.10.2026.
14. Interim relief application (IA/1/2026) 
stands disposed of.
