Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Neeraj Singh Kaira along with Ms. 
Sarita Bisht, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Pradeep Lohani, learned A.G.A. 
for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution 
of India, the petitioner has prayed for 
issuance of a writ in the nature of 
certiorari for quashing the First 
Information Report/Case Crime No. 0222 
of 2026 dated 21.09.2026, registered 
under Sections 11(1), 3,5,11,3 of the 
Uttarakhand Protection of Cow Progeny 
Act, 2007, at Police Station Kaliyar Sharif, 
District Haridwar. The petitioner has 
further sought issuance of a writ, order or 
direction in the nature of mandamus 
commanding the respondent authorities 
not to arrest the petitioner pursuant to the 
aforesaid F.I.R.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would submit that the applicant is innocent and has falsely been implicated in 
the present case; that, there is no 
allegation against the applicant of having 
slaughtered any cow or cow progeny. It is 
contended that the prosecution case, 
insofar as the applicant is concerned, is 
primarily based upon the alleged 
confessional statement of a co-accused, 
who is stated to have been apprehended 
from the spot.
5. He would further submit that, unlike 
the co-accused, the applicant was not 
arrested from the spot and was, in fact, 
admitted in Chandra Nursing Home, 
Roorkee, District Haridwar, on 20.09.2026 
at about 11:50 A.M. and remained under 
treatment therein until his discharge in the 
evening of 23.09.2026. Thus, according to 
learned counsel, the alleged incident is 
stated by the prosecution to have taken 
place on 21.09.2026 after 10:00 P.M., i.e., 
during the period when the applicant was 
admittedly undergoing treatment in the 
said hospital, therefore, the applicant 
could not have been present at the place of 
occurrence or participated in the alleged 
incident and that the medical record of the 
applicant constitutes a circumstance 
requiring consideration at the stage of bail.
6. Learned State counsel would 
vehemently oppose the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
applicant. However, he seeks three weeks’ 
time to file a counter affidavit and also 
verify the aforesaid facts.
7. Having considered the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the parties and the fact that, as per the 
documents relied upon by the applicant, 
he was admitted in Chandra Nursing 
Home, Roorkee, District Haridwar, on 
20.09.2026 at about 11:50 A.M. and was 
discharged only in the evening of 
23.09.2026, whereas the alleged incident is 
stated to have occurred on 21.09.2026 
after 10:00 P.M., the aforesaid 
circumstance requires verification from the 
original medical record and other material 
collected during investigation. At this 
stage, without expressing any opinion on 
the merits of the case, this Court is of the 
view that the matter requires consideration 
after the State is afforded an opportunity to 
place the relevant material on record.
8. Accordingly, three weeks’ time, as 
prayed for, is granted to the learned State 
counsel to file counter affidavit. The 
applicant shall be at liberty to place on 
record the relevant medical documents in 
support of his contention.
9. List after three weeks.
10. Till the next date of listing, as an 
interim measure, the petitioner shall not 
be arrested in connection with the 
F.I.R./Case Crime No. 0222 of 2026 
registered at Police Station Kaliyar Sharif, 
District Haridwar provided the petitioner 
cooperates with the investigation.
11. Stay Application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
