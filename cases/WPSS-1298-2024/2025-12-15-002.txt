2. Petitioner was given compassionate 
appointment as Constable in Provincial Arms 
Constabulary (PAC) in the year 2012. 
3. Director General, Uttarakhand Police 
issued a circular on 10.01.2017, permitting 
compassionate appointment in Civil Police and 
also in Local Intelligence Unit (LIU), as earlier 
such appointments were permissible only in 
PAC. 
4. In this writ petition, petitioner contends 
that since compassionate appointment is now 
permitted in other branches, including civil 
police, therefore he be given appointment in 
civil police in place of PAC. 
5. Learned State Counsel submits that once 
petitioner accepted offer of appointment in PAC 
with eyes wide open and that offer was made as 
per then prevailing policy, then petitioner cannot 
now seek appointment elsewhere merely 
because the policy has undergone some change. 
6. This Court finds substance in the said 2025:UHC:11192
submission.
7. Compassionate appointment once offered 
and accepted, cannot be a ground for seeking 
similar appointment in some other branch.
8. Learned counsel for petitioner, however, 
submits that after the circular dated 10.01.2017, 
issued by Director General, certain persons,
who were appointed in PAC, were transferred to 
Civil Police. He submits that petitioner be 
permitted to make representation. 
9. The writ petition is, accordingly, disposed 
of by permitting petitioner to make 
representation. Representation, if any, made 
shall be dealt with, as per law, within six 
months.
