4. In pursuance to the order dated 16.04.2025, the Vice 
Chairman, Development Authority, Tehri Garhwal, has 
submitted a report, which clearly indicates a massive 
construction being put up. The photographs clearly reveal 
that the appellant is trying to carry out the work in full 
swing. 
5. As observed by us, permitting the further construction 
by the appellant would amount to creating rights, where 
none existed.
6. In that view, the Development Authority shall ensure 
that, pending disposal of the writ petition, no further 
construction is permitted. The appellant to place on record 
the sanction plan and list this case for disposal on 
14.05.2025.
7. In view of the directions issued to the Development 
Authority, Registry to print the name of Mr. Rahul Consul as 
counsel assisting the Court.
