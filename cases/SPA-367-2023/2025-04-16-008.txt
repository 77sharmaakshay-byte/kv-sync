16.04.2025 SPA No. 367 of 2023
Hon’ble G. Narendar, C.J.
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
1. Mr. B.P. Nautiyal, learned Senior Counsel assisted 
by Mr. Mohd. Matlub, learned counsel for the appellant.
2. Mr. Sandeep Kothari, learned counsel for 
respondent nos. 1 & 2. 
3. Mr. P.C. Bisht, learned Additional Chief Standing 
Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand. 
4. Issue Court notice to Sri Sunder Lal Semwal, 
presently discharging duties as Chief Development 
Officer, District Uttarkashi. Notice be served through 
the Office of the District Magistrate, Uttarkashi. 
5. Heard the learned Senior Counsel for the 
appellant, and learned Standing Counsel for the Garhwal 
Mandal Vikas Nigam Ltd. 
6. The brief facts, in a nutshell, are that one Sunil 
Kumar was granted leasehold rights on 26.06.1994. 
Immediately thereafter, he sub-leased the rights in 
favour of the appellant on 30.06.1994. The 
Government, upon coming to know about the same, 
initiated proceedings, and cancelled the lease deed, on 
the ground that the conditions of the allotment of 
leasehold rights have been violated, and that despite a 
prohibition against sub-leasing, it was sub-leased by the 
appellant. Against this cancellation of the leasehold 
rights, an Appeal was preferred before the 
Commissioner, Garhwal Division, which came to be dismissed. Thereafter, the appellant approached this 
Court by filing Writ Petition (M/S) No. 1896/2006. The 
said Writ Petition was dismissed vide order dated 
15.06.2016, as withdrawn.
7. Thereafter, it appears the ingenuity of the 
appellant seems to have got the better of the system, or 
the ingenuity was put into practice, probably with the 
collusion of the system, and the same cannot be ruled 
out, and is a matter, which we will look into at the time 
of final hearing. 
8. The learned counsel for the respondents submits 
that the appellant has started putting up a huge 
construction, despite the appellant’s case having been 
disposed of before the learned Single Judge. 
9. The appellant having never been in possession, 
and his rights as a leaseholder, having been 
extinguished, and the extinguishment of his leasehold 
rights having attained finality, it is nothing short of 
fraud on power, when the Authority presumes otherwise 
and contrary to facts that have already been 
adjudicated and attained finality.
10. In that view, if further construction is permitted, it 
would virtually amount to presenting a fait accompli to 
this Court, more so when the Authorities have issued a 
sanctioned plan, without even, as much as, a reference 
to the status of the litigation that had preceded. 
11. In that view, we are of the opinion that permitting 
further construction would lead to the appellant pleading 
equities at the time of hearing. In that view of the 
matter, there shall be no further construction, pending disposal of the present Special Appeal. 
12. The Development Authority, Tehri Garhwal, 
headed by its Vice Chairman, shall forthwith inspect the 
building and submit a report, regarding the status of the 
construction. Report to be submitted to this Court by 
next date of hearing. 
13. List this case on 25.04.2025.
