4. As per the case set up by the original writ￾petitioner it made application for allotment of 18

5. According to the original writ-petitioner, 
possession of the subject land was handed over

writ-petitioner that it had spent Rs.4.70 lakhs in 
constructing a boundary wall. However, there is 
no material on record to establish either of the 
aforesaid two facts. In the circumstances aforesaid, we direct the original writ-petitioner 
to produce the original record of the Corporation 
in respect of the allotment. The Corporation 
shall also clarify whether the remaining 13 Nali 
of land is in its possession or not and, if in 
possession, what use it is being put to by the 
Corporation. The photographs of the site shall 
also be filed.
6. Appellant, if so advised, may also file 
affidavit in respect of the aforesaid aspects.
