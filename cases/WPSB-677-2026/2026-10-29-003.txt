1. Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. Rajeev Bisht, learned Deputy 
Advocate General with Mr. M.S. Bisht, learned 
Brief Holder for the State.
3. According to the petitioner, he was 
appointed as Principal in a Government aided 
Intermediate College in District Almora and the 
said college was subsequently provincialized 
on 02.08.2011. The grievance raised by the 
petitioner is that other similarly situate
persons have been formally absorbed in 
Government service, however, such absorption 
order has not been passed in respect of the 
petitioner.
4. By means of this writ petition, the 
petitioner has sought the following reliefs :
i) To issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus commanding the 
respondents particularly respondent No. 1 to 
issue absorption order regarding the 
petitioner, as per Rule-3(4) of the Uttar 
Pradesh Provincialized Education 
Institutions (Absorption of Employees in 
Government Service) Rules, 1992.
ii) To issue a writ, order or direction in the UKHC010187332026
2026:UHC:9841-DB
nature of mandamus commanding the 
respondents to grant all consequential 
benefits.”
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that after provincialization an order of 
appointment was issued in favour of the 
petitioner on 22.02.2013. 
6. Learned State Counsel was asked to get 
instructions. On instructions, he submits that 
the Director, Secondary Education has referred 
the papers of the petitioners to the State 
Government for taking appropriate decision 
and the decision of the State Government is 
awaited.
7. Having regard to the facts of the case, 
we dispose of the writ petition with a direction 
to the Secretary, Department of School 
Education, Government of Uttarakhand to 
examine the matter and pass appropriate 
order qua the petitioner, as per law, within 
four months from date of presentation of 
certified copy of the order.
