These are the bunch of three writ petitions. 
In one of the Writ Petitions, being WPMS No.16 
of 2018, the petitioner has filed the amendment 
application seeking an amendment in the 
pleadings of the writ petition. The same is not 
seriously opposed by the respondents’ counsel. 
Hence, the same would stand allowed. The 
petitioner is permitted to carry out the necessary
amendment within a period of one week from 
today, and as soon as the amendment is carried 
by the petitioner, it will be open for the 
respondents to file the counter affidavit to the 
amended pleadings within a period of two weeks 
thereafter.
List these writ petitions after the expiry of 
the aforesaid period.
In the connected matter i.e. in WPMS 
Nos.516 of 2018, IA No.13518 of 2019, filed by 
the respondents along with the supplementary 
counter affidavit, the same would stand allowed. 
The supplementary counter affidavit is taken on 
record.
