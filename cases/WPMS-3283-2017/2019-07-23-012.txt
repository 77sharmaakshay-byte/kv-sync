 The Uttarakhand Power Corporation Ltd. 
and Power Transmission Corporation of 
Uttarakhand Ltd. shall file an affidavit stating 
therein that when the advertisement was 
issued for filling up the vacant posts in the 
department and why the provisions giving 
relaxation to the employees, working in the 
Department have not been made in view of the 
dictum of Hon’ble Apex Court in the case of 
Union of India Vs. Central Administrative 
Tribunal and Others passed in Civil Appeal No. 
175-176 of 2019 decided 08.01.2019. 
 In the said affidavit, the Corporation shall 
also mention about the persons who are 
already working in the Department, the 
department are willing to give relaxation in view 
of the aforesaid dictum? 
 The said affidavit be filed by 05.08.2019. 
 List this matter on 06.08.2019.
