WPMS No.2680 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
1. Mr. Deepak Joshi, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Addl. C.S.C. 
for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. Mr. Mohit Kumar, learned counsel for 
the respondent no.2.
4. By means of this writ petition filed 
under Article 227 of the Constitution of 
India, the petitioner has put a challenge to 
the judgment and order dated 30.08.2024 
passed by Appellate Tribunal/Additional 
District Magistrate, Udham Singh Nagar in 
Tribunal Appeal No.52/03 of 2023-24 
Madanpal Singh vs. Sanjeev Kumar & 
others whereby the appeal filed by 
respondent no.2 was allowed and 
petitioner was directed to vacate the house 
in which she was residing, within a period 
of one month from the date of order i.e. 
30.08.2024.
5. It is submitted by learned counsel for 
the petitioner that petitioner is daughter￾in-law (Bahu) of respondent no.2 and 
respondent no.3 is husband of the 
petitioner. A Misc. Appl. No.36 of 2022, 
Madanpal Singh vs. Sanjeev Kumar was 
filed by respondent no.2 before the 
Prescribed Authority/S.D.M., Kashipur 
under Section 5 read with Section 23 of the 
Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and 
Senior Citizens Act, 2007 (hereinafter 
referred to as the Act). The said application of respondent no.2 was rejected by learned 
Prescribed Authority by reason of the 
judgment and order dated 26.04.2023.
6. It is feeling aggrieved by the said 
order, respondent no.2 preferred Appeal 
No.52/03 of 2023-2024, as stated above, 
before the Appellate Tribunal i.e. District 
Magistrate, Udham Singh Nagar. The said 
appeal was allowed by reason of the 
impugned judgment and order. 
7. It is submitted by learned counsel for 
the petitioner/daughter-in-law that the 
house in question is the matrimonial 
house of the petitioner as she was married 
to respondent no.3 and their marriage is 
still subsisting.
8. While learned counsel for respondent 
no.2 submitted that in view of the fact that 
petitioner as well as respondent no.3 are 
quarrelling, both of them have been evicted 
by respondent no.2 and, further that he 
has constructed the house and petitioner 
and her husband has no right to stay in 
the house against the wish of the 
respondent no.2.
9. Having considered the submission 
made by learned counsel for parties and on 
perusal of the judgment and order 
impugned, this Court is of the view that 
matter requires deliberation. 
10. Learned counsel for the respondents 
prays for and is allowed three weeks’ time 
to file counter affidavit.
11. List on 27.11.2024.
12. In the meanwhile the execution of the 
impugned order dated 30.08.2024 passed 
in Tribunal Appeal No.52/03 of 2023-24 
Madanpal Singh vs. Sanjeev Kumar & 
others, shall remain stayed. 12. Stay Appl. No.01 of 2024 stands 
disposed of.
