In this appeal, the appellant was 
granted bail on 15.05.2024, when this 
Court ordered as follows:-
 “10. Having considered the submissions 
of learned counsel for the parties but 
without expressing any opinion about the 
final merits of the case, in the opinion of 
this Court, the appellant/convict deserves to 
be enlarged on bail during the pendency of 
present criminal appeal.
 11. Accordingly, bail application is 
allowed. Let the appellant/convict be 
released on furnishing bail bond with two 
sureties in the amount of Rs.50,000/- and 
personal bond of the like amount to the 
satisfaction of the Court concerned.”
 Now, a Modification Application No. 2 
of 2024 has been filed that the appellant 
has to get his retiral dues, but in the 
absence of the words “suspension of 
sentence” in the order granting bail, he is 
not getting those benefits.
 CBI has filed its objections. According 
to it, the application is not legally tenable 
because while granting bail, the 
suspension of sentence was not explicitly 
granted by the Court.
 In appeal, bail may be effective only if 
the execution of sentence appealed 
against is suspended. Therefore, this 
Court observes that by granting bail, the 
sentence appealed against remains in suspension. 
 Modification application is disposed 
of accordingly.
