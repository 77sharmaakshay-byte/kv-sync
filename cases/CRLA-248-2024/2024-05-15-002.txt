15.05.2024 CRLA No.248 of 2024
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Ms. Manisha Bhandari and Mr. Druv 
Chandra, learned counsel for the appellant.
2. Mr. Lalit Sharma, learned counsel for the 
CBI. 
3. Heard. 
4. Learned counsel for the appellant/convict 
would submit that this is an appeal filed by the 
appellant/convict against the judgment and 
order dated 15.04.2024 passed by Special Judge,
CBI Dehradun in CBI Case No.09 of 2010, 
whereby the appellant/convict has been 
convicted under Section 66 of Information 
Technology Act 2000 and has been sentenced 
accordingly.
5. Admit.
6. Summon the LCR.
7. Also heard on the bail application (IA 
No.1 of 2024).
8. Learned counsel for the appellant/convict 
would submit that the appellant/convict was on 
bail during trial and he has never misused the 
liberty of bail granted to him. 
9. Learned counsel for the CBI does not 
dispute the above submissions.
10. Having considered the submissions of 
learned counsel for the parties but without 
expressing any opinion about the final merits of 
the case, in the opinion of this Court, the 
appellant/convict deserves to be enlarged on 
bail during the pendency of present criminal 
appeal. 11. Accordingly, bail application is allowed. 
Let the appellant/convict be released on 
furnishing bail bond with two sureties in the 
amount of ₹50,000/- and personal bond of the 
like amount to the satisfaction of the Court 
concerned.
12. List on 24.10.2024. 
(Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.)
