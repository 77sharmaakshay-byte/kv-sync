COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C 482 No. 595 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Ravi Bisht, learned counsel for the 
applicants.
2. By the instant C482 application, applicants are 
praying for quashing of the proceedings of Criminal 
Complaint Case No. 5293 of 2019 arising out of case 
crime no. 119 of 2019 wherein the applicants have been 
summoned for the offences punishable under Section 
420, 406 IPC and under Section 4/ 5 of the Prize Chits 
& Money Circulation Scheme (Banning) Act, 1978. 
Applicants were earlier granted bail on 09.12.2020 but 
subsequently, it was cancelled on 19.02.2020 on the 
ground that applicants had not returned the money to 
the complainant. Subsequently, non bailable warrant 
was issued on 26.02.2024 against the applicants. 
3. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the 
applicants that now, parties have settled their dispute 
amicably and they have made a written agreement on 
21.02.2024. Applicant paid the entire amount through 
three bank drafts and after receiving the amount, the 
complainant made an application before the CJM, 
Dehradun for compounding of the offence wherein 
20.04.2024 is fixed. The said application dated 
20.03.2024 is placed on record. 
4. Since parties have entered into compromise and 
entire amount has been paid to the complainant and for 
compounding of the offence an application has been 
moved before the CJM, therefore, at this juncture notice 
is required to be issued. 
5. Mr. Saurabh Pandey and Ms. Meenakshi Sharma, 
learned Brief Holders for the State accepts notice on 
behalf of respondent no. 1.
6. Issue notice to respondent no. 2. Learned counsel 
for the applicant shall take steps within a week by 
registered post returnable within three weeks. 
7. Respondents may file counter affidavit within 
four weeks. 8. List on 07.05.2024.
9. In the meantime, further proceedings of Criminal 
Complaint Case No. 5293 of 2019 for the offences 
punishable under Section 420, 406 IPC and under 
Section 4/ 5 of the Prize Chits & Money Circulation 
Scheme (Banning) Act, 1978 pending in the court of 
Chief Judicial Magistrate, Dehradun, shall remain 
stayed. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
