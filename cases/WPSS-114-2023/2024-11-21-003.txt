COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 114 of 2023
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Vinay Kumar, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Satyendra Singh Lingwal, learned counsel for 
the respondent through V.C.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
pursuant to the direction issued by this court in the 
earlier round of litigation the claim for the petitioner 
for seeking compassionate appointment was 
considered by the University authority and the same 
was recommended however, at that time since there 
was no Vice Chancellor the matter was referred to the 
Chancellor and thereafter a recommendation was made 
to give the appointment to the petitioner on contractual 
basis.
4. He submits that the recommendation for giving 
contractual appointment under the Dying in Harness 
Rules is impermissible.
5. Learned counsel Mr. Satyendra Singh Linwal, 
who appears for the respondent, seeks and is granted a 
week’s time to get complete instruction in the matter to 
apprise this court why the petitioner is not being given 
regular appointment on compassionate ground.
6. List this matter on 06.12.2024.
