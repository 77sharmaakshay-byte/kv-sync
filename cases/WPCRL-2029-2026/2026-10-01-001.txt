Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Ms. Unnati Pant and Ms. Tabassum, 
learned counsel for the petitioner. 
2. Mr. Akshay Latwal, learned A.G.A. for 
the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition, 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution of 
India, the petitioner has prayed for issuance 
of a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
certiorari quashing the First Information 
Report/Case Crime No. 0481 of 2026 dated 
17.09.2026, registered under Sections 308(6), 
351(1) and 352 of the Bharatiya Nyaya 
Sanhita, 2023 (B.N.S.), at Police Station 
Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar. The 
petitioner has further prayed for issuance of a 
writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding the respondent 
authorities not to arrest him in pursuance of 
the aforesaid F.I.R.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the dispute in question arises 
out of matrimonial discord between the 
parties and that the petitioner is the husband 
of Irum, against whom major allegations have 
been levelled in the F.I.R.
5. Learned counsel further submits that 
the petitioner is innocent and has been falsely 
implicated in the present case. He contends 
that even if the allegations contained in the 
impugned F.I.R. are taken at their face value and accepted in their entirety, they primarily 
relate to the matrimonial relationship between 
respondent no. 3/complainant and her 
husband and do not disclose the commission 
of any offence by the present petitioner.
6. Learned counsel further submits that 
the Hon'ble Supreme Court has repeatedly 
deprecated the practice of implicating the 
entire family of the husband in matrimonial 
disputes on the basis of general and omnibus 
allegations, without specific allegations 
against each individual accused disclosing the 
essential ingredients of the alleged offences.
7. Having heard learned counsel for the 
petitioner and learned State Counsel, and 
upon perusal of the material available on 
record, it appears that the dispute arises out 
of matrimonial discord between the parties.
8. In the interest of justice, four weeks' 
time is granted to learned State Counsel to file 
a counter affidavit. Rejoinder affidavit, if any, 
may be filed within two weeks thereafter.
9. Issue notice to respondent no. 3. Steps 
to be taken within a week. 
10. List thereafter and connect with WPCRL 
No. 1953 of 2026.
11. In the facts and circumstances of the 
case, and without expressing any opinion on 
the merits of the matter, as an interim 
measure, it is directed that the petitioner shall 
not be arrested in connection with the 
aforesaid F.I.R. till the next date of listing, 
subject to the condition that he shall 
cooperate with the investigation.
12. The interim relief application stands 
disposed of accordingly.
(Alok Mahra, J.)
