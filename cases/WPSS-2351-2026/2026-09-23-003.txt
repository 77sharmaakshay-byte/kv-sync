Mr. Sandeep Tiwari, learned counsel for

2. Mr. K.N. Joshi, learned Deputy Advocate

3. Mr. Bhuwan Bhatt, learned counsel for 
respondent no.8.
4. By means of the present writ petition, 
petitioner has challenged the order dated 
20.06.2026, whereby the petitioner was 
removed from service.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
contends that a complaint has been made by 
one of the Directors of the Cooperative Societies 
and, on the said complaint, the Assistant 
Registrar, Cooperative, has directed that the 
matter be inquired again by a Two-Member 
Committee. He further contends that, since the 
matter has been directed for re-inquiry, the 
removal order should have been quashed or, at 
least, kept in abeyance by the Assistant 
Registrar, Cooperative, but the same has not 
been done.
6. Co-ordinate Bench of this Court, vide order dated 15.09.2026, directed the respondents to 
get instructions in the matter as to why, after
the order of removal, the inquiry has been 
conducted and under which provision of law.
7. Today, Mr. Bhuwan Bhatt, learned counsel 
for respondent no. 8, has informed this Court 
that the order passed by the Assistant 
Registrar, Cooperative, whereby the 
Two-Member Committee was constituted for 
re-inquiry into the matter, has now been 
withdrawn. Thus, the removal order stands 
against the petitioner.
8. Respondent(s) may file the counter 
affidavit(s) in the matter within three weeks.
9. List on 16.10.2026.
