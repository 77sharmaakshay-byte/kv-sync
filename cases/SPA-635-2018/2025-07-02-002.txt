3. This intra-court appeal is directed against 
final order dated 25.07.2018, passed by learned 
Single Judge in WPSS No. 4 of 2015. By the 
said judgment, claim made by appellant for 
regularisation under Dying in Harness Rules, 
1974 was rejected.
4. Learned counsel for the appellant submits 
that appellant’s husband, who was serving as 
Chaukidar on daily wages in Forest Department 
since 1984, passed away on 19.11.2011;
thereafter appellant was engaged on daily wages
as Chaukidar; and now after serving more than 
13 years as daily wager, appellant is entitled for 
regularisation. 
5. Learned State Counsel, however, submits 
that appellant had claimed benefit of 
regularisation by invoking Dying in Harness 
Rules, which are not meant for this purpose. He 2025:UHC:5639-DB
submits that appellant can claim regularisation 
under the Special Rules framed by the State 
Government for that purpose.
6. Although learned Single Judge has given 
valid reasons for denying the relief, as claimed 
by appellant, however, having regard to the fact 
that appellant has served for more than 13 years 
as daily wager in Forest Department, we dispose 
of this Appeal by permitting her to make 
representation to the Competent Authority, 
claiming regularisation under the applicable 
Rules. If, she makes such representation within 
three weeks from today, the Competent 
Authority shall examine her claim for 
regularisation as per law, within four months 
thereafter.
