3. The grievance of the petitioner is 
with regard to post retiral benefits 
including amount towards GPF, family 
pension etc., which has been withheld by 
the respondent-University on the ground 
that the petitioner has not vacated the 
accommodation which was allotted to her. 
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the allotted accommodation 
has already been vacated despite this 
benefits are not being given. 
5. Earlier the University was 
represented by Mr. Bhupesh Kandpal, and, 
subsequently by Mr. Shailendra Nauriyal, 
and both the counsel are not present due 
to their family problems.

immediately after fresh. On that day, the 
State after consultation with the University 
may apprise to this Court why post retiral 
benefits including GPS as well as family 
pension along with benefit relating to the 
ACP have not been given to the petitioner.
