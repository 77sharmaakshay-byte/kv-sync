WPSS No. 2019 of 2023
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Kishore Rai, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned 
Standing Counsel for the State.
3. Mr. Sandeep Kothari, learned 
counsel who appears for the University 
submits that the Registrar has not been 
impleaded as respondent.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
seeks three days time to implead the 
Registrar, Uttarakhand Ayurvedic 
University, Dehradun as respondent no. 
3.
3. The grievance of the petitioner is 
that the petitioner retired from service 
on 31.05.2022 but her post retiral 
benefits has not been given.
4. Today, Registrar, Uttarakhand 
Ayurvedic University also joined the 
proceeding through V.C. and informed
that the petitioner retired on 
31.05.2024, however, the 
accommodation which was allotted to her 
was sub-let to the third person by the 
petitioner without informing to the 
University which was vacated on 
31.08.2024. Thereafter the University 
directed the petitioner to deposit the 
penal rent of an amount of Rs. 
1,69,272/-.
5. Now the question is whether the 
petitioner is liable to pay penal rent or 
not to examine this issue the version of the respondents are necessary.
6. Let respondents may file counter 
affidavit within four weeks. Two weeks 
thereafter is granted to the petitioner to 
file rejoinder affidavit.
7. List in the last week of March 2025.
8. In the meantime, after deduction of 
penal rent from the amount of gratuity of 
the petitioner rest of the retiral benefits 
be released in the favour of the 
petitioner. It is made clear that 
deduction of penal rent from the amount 
of gratuity shall be subject to the final 
outcome of the writ petition.
