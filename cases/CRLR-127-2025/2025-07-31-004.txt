COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRLR No.127 of 2025
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Narendra Bali, learned counsel for the revisionist.
2. Ms. Sukhwani Singh, learned counsel holding brief of 
Mr. Parikshit Saini, learned counsel for the respondent.
3. The revisionist was released on bail without 
complying the condition imposed by a Co-ordinate Bench of 
this Court wherein he has to deposit the fine imposed upon 
him i.e. 18,30,000/-. 
4. Surprisingly, the revisionist failed to deposit the 
amount of fine as stated above and still he has been 
released on bail by the learned trial court.
5. This Court vide order dated 15.07.2025 has been
pleased to pass the following order:-
“6. If the fine is not deposited within the time given by this 
Court to the revisionist today, the bail granted to him earlier, 
shall stand rejected and he would be taken into custody for 
serving out the sentence.”
6. In view of the order dated 15.07.2025, the bail 
granted to the revisionist already stands cancelled. 
Accordingly, no further order for cancellation of bail is 
required. Thus, the bail cancellation application (IA 
No.2/2025) stands disposed of.
7. Learned trial court is, therefore, directed to issue 
NBW against the revisionist to serve out the sentence.
8. List on 16.09.2025.
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
