CRLR No.127 of 2025
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Narendra Bali, Advocate for the revisionist.
2. Mr. Parikshit Saini, Advocate for the respondent.
3. Today, the matter is listed on IA No.2 of 2025 i.e. 
the Bail Cancellation Application moved by the 
respondent.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
respondent, Mr. Parikshit Saini that the bail was granted
to the revisionist by this Court vide order dated 
16.05.2025 which was a conditional order and before 
being released on bail, the revisionist was required to
deposit the amount of fine, as imposed by the learned 
Trial Court. But, without depositing the entire amount of 
fine, excluding the amount of 20% deposited by the 
revisionist during the pendency of the appeal, the 
revisionist was released on bail by the learned Magistrate. 
It appears that the learned Magistrate has misinterpreted 
the order passed by this Court, and without depositing the 
amount of fine as stated above, the revisionist was
released on bail.
5. Learned counsel for the revisionist submits that he 
would deposit the entire amount of fine as imposed by the 
learned Trial Court, excluding the 20% as deposited in the 
appellate court, within 15 days i.e. on or before 
31.07.2025. 
6. If the fine is not deposited within the time given by 
this Court to the revisionist today, the bail granted to him 2
earlier, shall stand rejected and he would be taken into 
custody for serving out the sentence.
7. List this case On 31.07.2025.
