COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLR No. 127 of 2025
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Narendra Bali, learned counsel for the Revisionist. 
2. Ms. Sukhwani Singh, learned counsel holding brief of Mr. 
Parikshit Saini, learned counsel for the Respondent. 
3. Today, the matter is listed on the Urgency Application moved 
on behalf of the Revisionist.
4. Learned counsel for the Revisionist submits that, as per the 
order of this Court, an amount of ₹18,30,000/- was required to be 
deposited; however, the same could not be deposited within the 
stipulated time. It is further submitted that when the Revisionist
subsequently attempted to deposit the said amount, the learned Court 
below refused to accept the same.
5. Learned counsel for the Respondent, however, objects to the 
aforesaid submission and submits at the outset that the Revisionist has 
defaulted on several occasions and is in the habit of non-compliance. 
It is further contended that despite ample opportunities having been 
granted, the Revisionist has failed to comply with the orders of the 
Court.
6. In rejoinder, learned counsel for the Revisionist prays that three 
days’ time be granted for deposit of the said amount.
7. Learned counsel for the Respondent, in response, submits that 
if any indulgence is granted, strict conditions be imposed and a 
specific time limit be fixed.
8. In view of the above submissions, three working days’ time is 
accorded to the Revisionist to comply with the order of this Court.
9. List this case on 08.01.2026.
 (Ashish Naithani, J.)
