COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
16.05.2025 CRLR No.127 of 2025
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Narendra Bali, learned counsel for the 
revisionist.
2. Present criminal revision is preferred against 
the judgment and order dated 03.02.2025 passed by 
learned Fourth Additional Sessions Judge, 
Haridwar in Criminal Appeal No.34 of 2024, 
“Krishnaveer Vs. State of Uttarakhand & Another”
as well as impugned judgment and order dated 
20.01.2024 passed by the learned Judicial 
Magistrate-I, Haridwar in Complaint Case (Old) 
No.3367 of 2015, Complaint Case (New) No.1339 
of 2022 “Sudesh Kumar Saini Vs. Krishnaveer”
punishable under Section 138 of Negotiable 
Instrument Act whereby the revisionist was 
convicted and sentenced to undergo three months 
simple imprisonment along with fine of 
₹18,30,000/- and in default of payment of fine to 
undergo further one month simple imprisonment 
additionally.
3. Learned counsel for the revisionist/convict 
would submit that judgment and orders of the 
Courts below suffer from perversity; that, the 
impugned judgment and orders of the Courts below 
are incorrect, improper and illegal and thus, 
deserve to be set-aside.
4. Issue notice to the respondent through 
ordinary process and registered post, 
acknowledgement due as well as by email and 
WhatsApp, if available.
5. Steps to be taken within two weeks. 
6. Heard on the bail and suspension of 
sentence. 
7. Learned counsel for the revisionist/convict 
would submit that the revisionist/convict is ready to 
deposit the entire amount of fine; that, the 
revisionist/convict is a law abiding person, he did 
not commit any offence and has been implicated in this case with mala fide intention for taking undue 
advantage and he cannot defend himself by 
remaining in jail; that, the revisionist /convict may 
be exempted from surrendering and released on 
bail as the revision may take considerable time for 
its conclusion due to heavy pendency of cases.
8. Per contra, Counsel for the State opposed the 
bail application, however, submitted that if the 
revisionist /convict is ready to deposit the entire 
fine amount then bail application of the 
revisionist/convict may be considered.
9. Having considered the entirety of the facts 
and without expressing any final opinion on the 
merits of the case, the revisionist/convict is
exempted from surrendering and admitted to bail.
10. Let the revisionist/convict be released on 
bail, during the pendency of present criminal 
revision, on furnishing bail bond with two sureties 
in the amount of ₹50,000/- and personal bond of 
the like amount to the satisfaction of the learned 
Trial Court, subject to payment of the entire 
amount of fine by the revisionist/convict within one 
month from today.
The amount of fine so deposited in the Court 
be deposited in a fresh account of the case as 
opened in the MACT cases. 
11. Application for bail and suspension of 
sentence stands disposed of accordingly.
12. List this case on 08.08.2025 for hearing on 
admission.
(Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.)
