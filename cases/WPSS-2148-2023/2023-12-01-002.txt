4. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
5. Petitioner participated in a selection process in the 
promotional post of Sub-Inspector (Civil Police). The 
selection process was conducted by respondent no.4, who 
after conclusion of the process has sent the select/merit list 
to the respondent-department.
6. It is the contention of learned counsel for the 
petitioner that in the said list, petitioner secured 248 marks 
and the private respondents have also secured 248 marks, 
but the private respondents have been given appointment 
on the basis of their marks while the petitioner was left 
over despite the fact that the petitioner is senior in service 
as well as in age to the private respondents. 
7. On 29.11.2023, respondent no.4 was directed to 
seek instruction as to why such action has been taken by 
them for appointing the private respondents ignoring the 
seniority of the petitioner in age and in service.
8. Today, learned counsel for the respondent no.4-
Commission, on instructions, fairly submits that, as per 
the selection process, only selection has been done by the 
Commission and after preparation of the merit list, the 
same was forwarded to the respondent-appointing 
authority for the purpose of giving appointment. So, there 
is no cause of action available to the petitioner to maintain 
the writ petition against the Commission. 
9. Learned State Counsel submits written instructions 2
dated 01.12.2023 and submits that in view of the 
provisions of Rule 5(5) of the Uttarakhand (Outside the 
purview of the Public Service Commission) Group-C post 
and Direct Recruitment Rules, 2008, the private 
respondents were given promotion as against the 
petitioner, as in the case of similar marks secured by the 
candidates, the person who secured higher marks in the 
written test will have preference against those, who 
secured lesser marks in the written examination. The 
private respondents secured higher marks than the 
petitioner.

attention of this Court to Section 1(3) (one) of the Rules,

Rules of 2008 would not be attracted for the selection in
the Police Department.

13. Learned State Counsel has drawn the attention of 
this Court to letter dated 16.02.2022, issued by Additional 
Secretary of the Government to the Director General of 
Police, Uttarakhand, whereby the Director General of 
Police was directed to take resort to the Rule 5(5) of the 
aforesaid Rules, 2008, wherein, in the merit list, there are 
candidates, who secured equal marks. 
14. Having heard the learned counsel for the parties 
and perusal of the Rules, as referred by learned counsel 
for the petitioner as well as written instructions given by 
learned State Counsel, it is debatable that the aforesaid 
Rules are applicable or not to the selection/promotion of 
the Police Department and the Rules can be pressed into 
service simply by issuance of the office circular, which 
has no statutory force. 
15. Issue notice to the private respondents, returnable 
within six weeks.
16. Steps to be taken within a week.
17. Learned counsel for the respondent(s) prays for and 
is granted six weeks’ time to file counter affidavit(s).
18. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the 
petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
19. List this matter on 06.03.2024.
20. In the meantime, it is directed that one post of Sub￾Inspector (Civil Police) shall be kept vacant for the 
petitioner.
