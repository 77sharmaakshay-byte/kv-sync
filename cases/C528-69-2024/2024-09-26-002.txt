Heard.
The petitioner seeks quashing of the 
Charge Sheet No.1 of 2022 dated 09.12.2022, 
as well as cognizance order dated 14.12.2023, 
passed in Special Sessions Trial No. 1068 of 
2023, under Section 505 (2) IPC read with 3 (1) 
(u) of the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled 
Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989, by 
the court of Special Sessions Judge, Udham 
Singh Nagar.
According to the FIR, the applicant 
authored a book on “Political History of 
Uttarakhand” and in that book, he made 
derogatory remarks on particular Scheduled 
Tribes community, which has hurt the 
members of the community. It is this FIR, in 
which after investigation, charge sheet was 
submitted and summoning order has been
passed which is impugned in this case.
Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that no, prima facie, case is made out. 
He would submit that the petitioner is 
historian. He authored the book and in that 
book, he quoted some parts from “Himalayan 
Gazetteer of the United Provinces of Agra and 
Oudh” authored by H. R. Nevill, published in 
the year 1904. It is argued that, in fact, the 
Investigating Officer (“IO”) has confirmed it in 
the charge sheet. Despite that, charge sheet 
has been filed.
Perusal of the charge sheet, in fact, 
reveals that according to IO also alleged 
derogatory remark has been copied from aGazetteer authored by H.R. Nevill in the year 
1904.
Having heard, this Court is of the view 
that this matter requires deliberation.
Respondent no.2 is also represented.
Respondents may file counter affidavit 
within four weeks. Two weeks thereafter, 
rejoinder affidavit, if any, may be filed.
List on 25.11.2024.
Till the next date of listing proceedings of 
the court below shall remain in abeyance.
Interim Relief Application No. 1 of 2024 
and Urgency Application No. 3 of 2024 stand 
disposed of accordingly.
