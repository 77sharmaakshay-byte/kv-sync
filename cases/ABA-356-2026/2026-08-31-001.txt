Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Gaurav Singh, counsel for the 
applicant through Video Conferencing.
2. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned Addl. 
Advocate General along with Mr. S.C. 
Dumka, learned A.G.A. and Mr. Nikhil Bisht, 
learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. By means of the present application, 
the applicants seek anticipatory bail in 
connection with F.I.R. No. 267 of 2026, 
registered at Police Station Bhagwanpur, 
District Haridwar, for the offences 
punishable under Sections 3/5/11 of the 
Uttarakhand Cow Progeny Act, 2007.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would submit that the impugned F.I.R. has 
been lodged on the allegation that, while the 
police personnel were on patrolling duty, 
they received information from an informer 
that the present applicants, along with 
certain other persons, had allegedly 
slaughtered cow progeny in a mango 
orchard. It is further alleged that, acting 
upon the said information, the police 
conducted a raid at the alleged spot and 
recovered approximately 50 kilograms of suspected bovine meat along with certain 
equipment allegedly used in the commission 
of the offence. The recovered material was 
thereafter inspected by the Veterinary 
Officer and samples thereof were taken, 
pursuant to which the present F.I.R. came 
to be registered.
5. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would further submit that the applicant is 
innocent and has falsely been implicated in 
the present case; that, the applicants were 
neither present at the alleged spot at the 
time of the raid nor was any incriminating 
article recovered from his possession; that, 
the motorcycle allegedly recovered from the 
spot does not stand registered in the name 
of either of the present applicant; that, the 
implication of the applicant rests solely upon 
the information allegedly furnished by the 
police informer, who stated that the 
applicant, along with co-accused Kaleem 
and certain unknown persons, were involved 
in the alleged slaughter of cow progeny; that, 
except for the said information, there is no 
independent material or evidence, at this 
stage, connecting the applicants with the 
commission of the alleged offence, therefore, 
the custodial interrogation of the applicant 
is not warranted and he is entitled to the 
protection of anticipatory bail.
6. Per contra, learned State Counsel 
would oppose the anticipatory bail 
application and submits that a substantial 
quantity of alleged bovine meat was 
recovered during the raid conducted by the 
police and that the investigation is still in 
progress. However, learned State Counsel 
seeks time to file a detailed counter affidavit.
7. In the interest of justice, four weeks' 
time is granted to learned State Counsel to 
file a detailed counter affidavit.
8. List this case after four weeks.9. Having considered the submissions and 
the material on record, and having regard to 
the nature of allegations and the stage of 
investigation, this Court is prima facie of the 
view that the applicant has made out a case 
for interim protection. The applicant was not 
apprehended from the spot, no incriminating 
recovery has been made from his 
possession, and their alleged involvement 
appears to be based primarily on 
information furnished by a police informer. 
Accordingly, without expressing any opinion 
on the merits of the case, this Court 
considers it appropriate to grant interim 
protection to the applicant till the next date 
of listing.
10. Accordingly, it is directed that the 
investigation shall proceed independently, 
fairly and strictly in accordance with law 
and shall not be influenced by any 
observation made in this order. However, in 
the event of arrest of the applicant in 
connection with the aforesaid Case 
Crime/F.I.R., he shall be released on interim 
anticipatory bail till the next date of listing 
on his furnishing a personal bond along with 
two reliable sureties, each in the like 
amount, to the satisfaction of the Arresting 
Officer/Investigating Officer, subject to the 
following conditions:
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with 
the investigation and shall appear before 
the Investigating Officer as and when 
required;
(ii) The applicant shall not directly or 
indirectly make any inducement, threat 
or promise to any person acquainted 
with the facts of the case so as to 
dissuade such person from disclosing 
such facts to the Court or to the 
Investigating Agency;
(iii) The applicant shall not leave the territory of India without prior 
permission of the Court concerned;
(iv) The applicant shall not misuse the 
liberty granted by this order and shall 
maintain peace and good conduct 
during the period of interim protection;
(v) In case of breach of any of the 
aforesaid conditions, it shall be open to 
the State/prosecution to seek 
modification or cancellation of interim 
anticipatory bail in accordance with law.
