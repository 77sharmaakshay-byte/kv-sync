Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
1. Heard Mr. Dharmendra Barthwal, learned 
counsel for the petitioner and Mr. Ganga Singh 
Negi, learned Additional CSC for the State.
2. This Court on 28.01.2026, passed an order 
by which the petitioner was granted one more 
opportunity to produce the original certificates 
and the relevant documents before the 
respondent no.4. The petitioner was directed to 
appear before the respondent no.4 on 30.01.2026 
at 11:00 a.m. along with original certificates and 
other relevant documents, whereupon the 
respondents were directed to consider the 
petitioner’s candidature in accordance with law 
and if found in order and eligible, her name shall 
be included in the further selection process as per 
her merit and Recruitment Rules. 
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that she appeared before the said Authority. 
However, the respondent no.4 has not considered 
the case of the petitioner, who is much higher in 
the merit list, as compared to the 16 candidates 
who are lower in the merit. This Court vide order 
dated 29.05.2026 directed the District Education 
Officer (Elementary Education), Uttarkashi to show cause as to why the case of the petitioner 
was not considered despite the fact that she was 
more meritorious than the persons, who were 
given appointment. The District Education Officer 
has filed an affidavit on 09.06.2026, wherein he 
has admitted the fact that the appointment letter 
was signed on 26.01.2026. He submits that the 
selection process was completed on 12.01.2026. 
However, on the same affidavit, he admits that 
the candidates who were given time to submit 
their original certificates, their selection was kept 
in provisional category and appointment letters 
were not issued to certain candidates. The 
District Selection Committee which held its 
meeting on 06.03.2026, decided that opportunity 
be given to the concerned persons and 15 days 
time was granted to provide the records relating
to their residence certificate. It is further stated 
that till date the records were not provided by 
them and appointment letters were not issued to 
the said candidates. The averments made in para 
11 of the said affidavit read as under:-
“That the candidates who were given time had 
already been selected by the selection committee on 
12.01.2026 and when the records were verified 
again at the office level, it was found that the 
concerned candidates had submitted their original 
certificates on 12.01.2026, the date of verification of 
original certificates, but the certificates regarding 
their own or their guardian's residence outside 
Uttarakhand for livelihood/studies while undergoing 
D.El.Ed training from Uttar Pradesh were not made 
available by the concerned. candidates, due to which 
their selection was kept in the provisional category 
and appointment orders were not issued, because in 
the meeting of the District Selection Committee held 
on 06.03.2026, it was decided by the Selection 
Committee to provide an opportunity to the concerned 
persons and give them 15 days time to provide the 
records related to their residence, but even till date 
the records were not provided by them and 
appointment letters were not issued to them. True/correct typed version/copy of letter dated 
6.3.2026 is being filed herewith and marked as 
Annexure No.2 to this affidavit.”
4. Thus, it is an admitted fact that the 
selection process had not come to an end and 
Selection Committee had provided 15 days time 
to the said candidates to provide the records.
5. On the contrary, the case of the petitioner is 
that she had submitted her documents on 
30.01.2026. Despite the fact that 16 candidates 
who are lower in the merit to the petitioner have 
been given appointment, the case of the petitioner 
has not been considered. As there are still three 
posts vacant, as the said candidates have failed to 
appear within 15 days as directed by the District 
Selection Committee on 06.03.2026 and 16 
persons who are less meritorious to the petitioner 
have been given appointment, the District 
Education Officer (Elementary Education), 
Uttarkashi shall consider the case of the 
petitioner for appointment and shall submit an 
affidavit to the said effect on the decision been 
taken. District Education Officer (Elementary 
Education), Uttarkashi shall also clarify that why 
the 16 persons, who were lower in the merit than 
the petitioner, have been given appointment
without considering the case of the petitioner.
6. List on 11.09.2026 after fresh.
