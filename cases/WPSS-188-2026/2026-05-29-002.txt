filed by the petitioner is taken on record.
 
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that vide order dated 28.01.2026, the 
petitioner was granted one more opportunity 
to produce the original certificates and 
relevant documents before the respondent no. 
4. The petitioner was permitted to appear on 
30.01.2026 at 11:00 A.M. along with original 
certificates and relevant documents, 
whereupon the said respondents were directed 
to consider the petitioner’s candidature, in 
accordance with law, and if found in order of 
eligibility, the name of the petitioner was to be 
included in the further selection process as 
per her Merits and Recruitment Rules. 
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner
submits that the respondent no. 4 instead of considering the case of the petitioner, who 
was much higher in merit had given 
appointment to the candidates who are lower 
in merit than the petitioner and has not 
considered the case of the petitioner. 
6. Learned State counsel shall seek 
instructions from the District Education 
Officer (Elementary Education), Uttarkashi as 
to why the case of the petitioner was not 
considered despite the fact that she was more 
meritorious than the persons, who were given 
appointment. The said respondent shall also 
clarify as to when the selection process was 
concluded and when the appointment letters 
were issued to the candidates said to be below 
in merit than the petitioner.
