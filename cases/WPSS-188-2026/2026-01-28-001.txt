Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
Mr. D.S.Patni, learned Senior Counsel, 
assisted by Mr. Dharmendra Barthwal, learned 
counsel for the petitioner. 
2. Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State. 
3. The petitioner has filed the instant Writ 
Petition with the following prayers:
“i) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
certiorari quashing the oral rejection of the petitioner (as 
done by the Counselling Committee for the respondent 
no. 4 during the counselling held by it on 12.01.2026) 
from the office of the respondent no. 4 and quashing the 
name insofar as the rejection of the petitioner’s 
candidature is concerned. 
ii) Issue a writ, order or direction the nature of 
mandamus directing the respondents to forthwith 
consider the petitioner’s candidature for her selection in 
the selection process initiated by means of 
advertisement dated 07.11.2025 afresh and 
accordingly verify petitioner’s documents as per the 
original advertisement as well as the documents
required as per amended instructions issued by
respondent no. 4 on 11.01.2026 and to appoint her to 
the post of Assistant Teacher (primary) in the Aft 
Category (General Female Seat) with all consequential 
benefits, including back wages and seniority. (contained 
as annexure no.2 to the writ petition.)
iii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus thereby restraining the respondents from 
filing at least one vacancy (of Assistant Teachers 
(Primary) Category-General (Arts) and to reserve one 
post of assistant Teacher (Primary) Category- General 
(Arts) for the petitioner till the pendency of the instant 
writ petition.”
4. Counsel for the petitioner submits that in 
pursuance to the advertisement issued on 
07.11.2025 by the respondent no. 4, the 
petitioner being eligible applied for the post of 
Assistant Teacher (Primary) and her name was 
included in the merit list published by the 2026:UHC:656
respondents. Counsel for the petitioner submits 
that petitioner appeared for documents 
verification on 12.01.2026 along with downloaded 
mark-sheet and certificate of D.El.Ed, as per the 
Uttarakhand Teachers Recruitment Rules and the 
advertisement dated 07.11.2025, however on 
11.01.2026 a list of amended instructions were 
issued and on the basis of the said Counselling
Committee rejected the petitioner’s candidature
on the ground that the additional documents were 
not produced by her. Learned counsel for the 
petitioner submits that she submitted a 
representation on 16.01.2026 with the prayer that 
the new additional documents as required are 
available however no heed was paid on the said 
representation. He also refers to an order dated 
23.01.2026 passed by the Coordinate Bench of 
this Court in WPSS No. 145 of 2026, wherein in 
similar circumstances an opportunity was 
granted to the petitioner to produce the original 
certificates and the relevant documents before the 
respondents. 
5. In the interest of justice, the petitioner is 
granted one more opportunity to produce the 
original certificates and the relevant documents 
before the respondent no.4. The petitioner shall 
appear before the respondent no.4 on 30.01.2026 
at 11:00 A.M. along with original certificates and 
the other relevant documents, whereupon the 
respondents shall consider the petitioner’s 
candidature in accordance with law and if found 
in order and eligible, her name shall be included 
in the further selection process as per her merit 
and Recruitment Rules. 
6. The writ petition stands ordered 
accordingly.
