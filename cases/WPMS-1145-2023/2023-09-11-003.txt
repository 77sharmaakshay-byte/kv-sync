The instant writ petition was 
dismissed in non prosecution on 
17.08.2023. Now a Restoration 
Application MCC No.2 of 2023 has been 
filed on the ground that the learned 
counsel for the petitioner could not 
appear at the time when the matter was 
taken up. 
It is not objected to by learned State 
Counsel. 
Having considered, this Court is of 
the view that there are good grounds to 
restore the petition. 
The restoration application is 
allowed.
The order dated 17.08.2023 is 
recalled.
Learned counsel for the respondents 
seeks time to file counter affidavit. 
Let it be filed within four weeks.
Three weeks thereafter, rejoinder 
affidavit, if any, be filed. 
List this matter for final hearing on 
24.11.2023.
