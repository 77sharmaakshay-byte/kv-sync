The matter was admitted on 18.09.2024. 
2. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that on 
filing of the suit for partition, being Suit No.5 of 2018, the 
court had issued notices and the parties have put in 
appearance and have filed their respective statements and, 
thereafter, nothing much has progressed. The pendency of 
a suit for partition, for nearly 07 years is in the teeth of
1Rule 3 of the Uttarakhand Case Flow Management (For 
Subordinate Courts) Rules, 2009, which clearly stipulate 
that suits of such nature are required to be heard and 
disposed of within two years from the date of appearance 
or deemed appearance of defendant-respondent-opposite 
party. The relevant rule reads as under :-
“3. Categorisation of suits/ Cases/ Appeals/

(1) The Presiding Officer of the court shall categories

(14) money suits based solely on

3. In that view, we direct the Court of Assistant 
Collector, Ist Class, Haridwar to hear and dispose of Suit 
No. 5 of 2018 titled as ‘Biram Singh and Another vs. 
Rajveer and Others’ pending on his file, as expeditiously as 
possible but at any rate within an outer limit of four months 
from the date of receipt of a certified copy of this order.
4. The parties shall appear before the Court of 
Assistant Collector, Ist Class, Haridwar on 05.05.2025 at 
03:00 P.M. and shall not be permitted any adjournment 
except in the case of any grave situation or serious health 
crisis leading to hospitalization. We would request the 
court of Assistant Collector, Ist Class, Haridwar to attempt 
to hear and dispose of the suit on day to day basis.
