Mr. S.R.S. Gill, learned counsel for the 
applicants.
2. Mr. Pradeep Lohani, learned A.G.A. for 
the State.
3. Learned counsel for the applicants 
would submit that there are three applicants 
in the present case and, as per the gang 
chart, applicant no. 1 has been shown as 
the leader of the gang, with three criminal 
cases registered against him, whereas 
applicant nos. 2 and 3 have been shown as 
members of the gang, with one criminal case 
registered against them. He would further 
submit that, out of the three cases relied 
upon in the gang chart, F.I.R. No. 117 of 
2022 has already been quashed on the basis 
of compromise vide order dated 17.04.2026, 
whereas, in F.I.R. Nos. 94 of 2016 and 89 of 
2016, closure reports have been submitted 
before the competent court and the same 
have been accepted in respect of applicant 
no. 1. 
4. He would further submit that F.I.R. No. 117 of 2022, which is also shown against 
applicant nos. 2 and 3, has likewise been 
quashed on the basis of compromise vide 
order dated 17.04.2026. Thus, learned 
counsel submits that, since the criminal 
cases forming the basis of the gang chart 
have either been quashed or have 
culminated in the acceptance of closure 
reports, the applicants have been wrongly 
implicated as members of an alleged gang 
and, therefore, the impugned criminal 
proceedings are liable to be quashed.
5. Learned State counsel prays for and is 
granted one week's time to obtain 
instructions in the matter.
6. List this case on 20.11.2026 as fresh.
