COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
16.10.2024 C-528 No. 658 of 2024
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. U. K. Uniyal, learned Senior 
Counsel assisted by Mr. Harshit Sanwal, 
counsel for the petitioners.
2. Mr. G. S. Sandhu, learned Additional 
Advocate General assisted by Ms. Mamta 
Joshi, learned Brief Holder for the State. 
3. Learned Senior Counsel for the 
petitioners would submit that this petition is
filed by the petitioners to quash the entire 
proceedings of Sessions Trial No. 112 of 2024 
arising out from Case Crime No. 294 of 2017, 
the charge sheet no. 56/18(A) dated 
20.05.2018 under Sections 376, 342, 506, 370 
& 120-B of IPC as well as the summoning 
order dated 14.03.2019 and committal order 
dated 03.09.2024 registered at P.S. Dalanwala, 
Dehradun. 
4. He would further submit that an F.I.R. 
No. 296 of 2017 was registered by the 
complainant/respondent no. 2/Reena Munda 
against the petitioners for committing repeated 
rape upon her, when she was working as 
domestic worker in the house of the 
petitioners; that, the charge sheet has been 
filed against the petitioners; that, when the 
matter was further investigated, then 
subsequently, three more charge sheets have 
also been filed in the matter. 
5. He would further submit that in those 
charge sheets several other persons were also 
made accused for running a racket of 
blackmailing the innocent persons by levelling 
the charge of rape upon them; that, in charge sheet dated 30.01.2024 (annexed as Annexure 
No. 20 of the petition), it has unequivocally 
come in the concluding paragraph that in 
further investigation, the offence of rape upon 
complainant/respondent no. 2 by the 
petitioners could not be confirmed and that 
allegation was levelled upon them only for 
blackmailing.
6. Learned State counsel would submit that 
it is inexplicable that how come subsequent 
charge-sheets could be filed without 
cancelling the previous charge-sheets and he 
fairly concedes that in the charge sheet dated 
30.01.2024, it is specifically stated that the 
offence of rape is not confirmed against the 
petitioners, however, prays for time to file 
counter affidavit.
7. In view of the above, this Court is of the 
considered view that the matter needs 
deliberation.
8. Admit the petition.
9. List this matter on 27.02.2025.
10. Till further orders, the proceedings of 
Sessions Trial No. 112 of 2024 ‘State vs. 
Amarjeet Sethi & Ors’ pending before the 
court of Additional District & Sessions 
Judge/FTSC (POCSO), Dehradun under 
Sections 376, 370, 342, 506 & 120-B of IPC 
registered at P.S. Dalanwala, Dehradun shall 
remain stayed.
11. Interim Relief Application (IA No. 01 of 
2024) stands disposed of.
12. Counter affidavit be filed in the 
meantime. 
 (Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.)
