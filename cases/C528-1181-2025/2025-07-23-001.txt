COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
C-528 No. 1181 of 2025
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Anand Kumar Pandey, learned counsel for the 
Applicant. 
2. The present Application under Section 528 of the 
Bharatiya Nagrik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 has been filed by 
the Applicant-Nain Singh Mahara S/o Trilok Singh Mahara 
against the Respondent-Kiran Mahara, who happens to be 
the wife of the Applicant-Nain Singh Mahara. The matter has 
surfaced due to the matrimonial discord between the 
parties.
3. The Applicant comes with a prayer to quash the 
impugned order dated 11.06.2025, passed by the learned 
Chief Judicial Magistrate, Champawat, in Misc. Case No. 163 
of 2025, “Kiran Mahara vs. Nain Singh Mahara”. He has also 
prayed for a Stay Application (IA No. 1 of 2025, Paper No. 
22), whereby it is prayed that the effect and operation of the 
impugned order dated 11.06.2025, passed by the learned 
Chief Judicial Magistrate, Champawat, in Misc. Case No. 163 
of 2025, “Kiran Mahara vs. Nain Singh Mahara,” be stayed.
4. Admit.
5. Issue notice to the Respondent.
6. Steps to be taken within one week.
7. List this case on 16.09.2025.
8. Objections, if any, be filed in the meantime.
9. The grounds urged in the stay application are found to 
be sufficient.
10. As an interim measure, it is directed that the effect 
and operation of the impugned order dated 11.06.2025, 
passed by the learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, Champawat, 
in Misc. Case No. 163 of 2025, “Kiran Mahara vs. Nain Singh 
Mahara,” shall remain stayed qua the present Applicant only till the next date of listing.
11. The Stay Application stands disposed of accordingly.
