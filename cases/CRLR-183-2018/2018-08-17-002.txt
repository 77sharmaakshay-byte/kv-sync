This revision is directed against 
the judgment and order dated 
11.01.2017 passed by Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Pithoragarh, whereby the 
application filed by respondent no.2 
u/s 125 of Cr.P.C. has been allowed 
ex-parte and the revisionist-husband 
has been directed to pay Rs.5,000/- 
per month to the respondent no.2-wife 
and Rs.2,000/- per month to his minor 
daughter from the date of order, by the 
7th of each month. 
There is a delay of 434 days in 
filing the revision, wherefor learned 
counsel for the revisionist has filed the 
delay condonation application. 
Heard and perused the affidavit 
filed in support of the delay 
Condonation application. 
Cause shown is sufficient. Delay 
in filing the revision is condoned. Delay 
Condonation application is allowed. 
Admit. 
Learned counsel for the parties 
have made statement that there are 
chances of amicable settlement between the parties. 
In view of the above, parties to 
appear on 06.09.2018 for mediation. 
Noting their presence, they will be 
referred for mediation on the same day. 
List this case on 06.09.2018.
