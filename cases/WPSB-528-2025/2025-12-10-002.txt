9. Heard on Interim Relief Application (IA No. 1

10. It is a case of the petitioner that she is 
working as Senior Nursing Officer, Sub District 
Hospital, Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar, 
but she has been promoted and posted at District 
Hospital, Pithoragarh. 
11. According to the petitioner, she could not be 
promoted and transferred at a remote area, in view 
of Section 7(d) read with Section 18(2) of 
Uttarakhand Annual Transfer for Public Servant Act, 
2017, because the petitioner has a son, who suffers 
from 65% disability, as has been certified by the 
competent authority. He is suffering from
Osteogenesis Imperfecta and is unable to walk. 
12. The matter was taken up yesterday. Learned 
counsel for the State, on instructions, submits that, in fact, Pithoragarh is a remote area. He submits that 
there is no vacancy in the accessible area. 
13. Having considered, as an interim measure, till 
the next date listing, the effect and operation of the 
impugned order dated 28.10.2025, shall remain in 
abeyance. 
14. The interim relief application stands disposed 
of accordingly.
