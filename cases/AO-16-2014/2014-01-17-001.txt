CLMA No. 485/14 (Stay Application):
 Also heard on the stay application. 
 Having heard the learned counsel for the 
appellant and upon examining the record, it is 
directed that, further execution of the award 
dated 28.10.2013 passed by Motor Accident 
Claims Tribunal/ 3rd Addl. District Judge, 
Haridwar, in Motor Accident Claim Petition no. 
169 of 2011, shall remain stayed, provided the 
appellant deposits a sum of ` 2,50,000/- (Rs. 
Two lac fifty thousand) before the Tribunal 
concerned within a period of six weeks from 
today. The sum already deposited under the 
statutory provisions shall be adjusted. 
 Registry is directed to remit the entire 
sum, deposited by the appellant, to the Tribunal 
concerned. The amount deposited by the 
appellant in compliance of this order, shall be 
invested by the Tribunal in a Fixed Deposit 
Scheme so that it could earn interest and would 
be subject to further orders of this Court. 
 Stay application stands disposed of.
