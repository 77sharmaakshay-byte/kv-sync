4. Petitioners who are working as T.G.T./
P.G.T. Teachers in the Eklavya Model 
Residential School have been relieved from 
their services, as their contractual 
engagement has not been extended.
5. It is contended by the learned counsel 
for the petitioner that the respondents are 
bent upon to dispense with the services of 
the petitioners and earlier also, an attempt 
was made when online advertisement was 
issued in the month of July, 2023 by Central 
Society to fill up the vacancies on the post of 
teaching and non-teaching staff of the said 
EMR School.
6. The petitioners, feeling aggrieved, 
challenged the aforesaid advertisement, by 
filing writ petition Nos.1680 of 2023 (S/S) 
and 1427 of 2023(S/S) before this Court and 
this Court has been pleased to grant interim 
order by staying the selection process 
pursuant to the aforesaid advertisement.2
7. Now, despite making a clear stand 
before a Division Bench of this Court in
Special Appeal Nos. 334 of 2023 and 344 of 
2023 preferred against the interim order 
passed in aforesaid writ petitions, that the 
petitioners shall not be touched, but, the 
respondents have adopted another mode to 
throw away the petitioners from the Society 
and consequently, the impugned order dated 
30.05.2024 has been passed.
8. Learned counsel for the respondents 
pray for and are granted two weeks’ time to 
file counter affidavits.
9. Issue notice to respondent Nos.4 & 5.
10. Steps to be taken within 03 days.
11. List immediately after fresh cases on 
28.06.2024.
