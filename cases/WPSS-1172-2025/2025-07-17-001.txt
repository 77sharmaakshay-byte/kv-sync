Heard.
It is the case of the petitioners that 
they appeared for recruitment to the post of 
Assistant Agriculture Officer, Grade-3. 
Their names were not reflected in the final 
list of selected candidates, but still, 47 
posts remained unfilled due to non-joining 
or other reasons of the selected candidates, 
but the respondent no.4, the Uttarakhand 
Public Services Commission, has yet not 
published the waiting list, which is 
mandate of Selection list and waiting list of 
candidates selected on the basis of 
competitive examinations and other 
selections conducted by the 
commission/selection institutions in 
Uttarakhand State Services, Rules, 2023 
(“the 2023 Rules”), particularly Rule 6 has 
been referred to.
Learned counsel for the respondent 
no.4 submits that he was not given 
advanced notice. Today he has taken 
notice. He seeks time to get instructions in 
the matter. 
List this matter on 25.07.2025.
On that date, learned counsel for the 
respondent no.4 shall place for the perusal of the Court the waiting list, as prepared by 
the respondent no.4, pursuant to Rule 6 of 
the 2023 Rules, and if it is not prepared, he 
would assign reason on that date.
Let the name of learned counsel for 
the respondent no.4 be correctly reflected in 
the cause list. 
 (Ravindra Maithani J.)
