WPSS No. 3726 of 2018 
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
 Mr. M.C. Kandpal, Senior Advocate, assisted by 
Mr. Chitrarth Kandpal, Advocate for the petitioner. 
 Mr. V.S. Rawat, Brief Holder for the State of 
Uttarakhand/ respondents. 
 Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
 Petitioner’s husband was a teacher in a 
Government Primary School, who died in harness on 
05.03.2015. In view of the financial crisis suffered by the 
family on account of death of sole breadwinner, petitioner 
applied for compassionate appointment. According to the 
petitioner, she possesses Bachelors degree alongwith B.Ed. 
qualification and she has also qualified C.T.E.T. & 
U.T.E.T., therefore, she is eligible to be appointed as 
teacher in a Government Primary school. Grievance of the 
petitioner is that her claim for compassionate appointment 
has not been considered. 
 Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the 
petitioner submits that the respondents are pondering as to 
whether petitioner can be appointed as L.T. Grade Teacher 
or as a Primary School Teacher. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
petitioner is ready and willing to serve as Primary School 
Teacher, therefore, petitioner’s claim be considered for 
appointment as Primary School Teacher. 
 Having regard to the facts and circumstances of the 
case, this Court thinks that ends of justice would be met, if 
Competent authority is directed to examine the claim of the 
petitioner for compassionate appointment and take 
appropriate decision thereupon, in accordance with law, 
within some stipulated time frame. 
 Accordingly, writ petition is disposed of with 
liberty to the petitioner to make representation to Director, 
Elementary Education, Uttarakhand within two weeks from 
today. If such representation is made, Director, Elementary 
Education, Uttarakhand shall examine petitioner’s claim 
and take appropriate decision, in accordance with law, 
within a period of six weeks from the date of receipt of 
such representation along with certified copy of this order. 
 Pending application(s), if any, also stand disposed 
of.
