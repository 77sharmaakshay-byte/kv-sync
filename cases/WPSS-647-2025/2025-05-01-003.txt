 Heard on Stay Application (IA) No.1 of 
2025.
 It is the case of the petitioner that she 
has applied for the post of Medical Social 
Worker Examination 2023 in the OBC 
Category. But, at the stage of document 
verification, she has not been considered 
on the ground that her OBC Certificate is 
not valid. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that the petitioner has obtained OBC Certificate on 18.07.2019 
and the last date for filling up of the 
application for the aforesaid post was 
05.07.2023. He would submit that in the 
OBC Certificate, which was issued to the 
petitioner in the year 2019, there was no 
clause that it is valid for three years only. 
Even otherwise, it is submitted that now,
the petitioner has valid OBC Certificate 
and she may not be debarred on this 
ground in view of the judgment passed in 
the case of Ram Kumar Gijroya Vs. Delhi 
Subordinate Services Selection Board and 
another, (2016)4 SCC, 754; Pushpa Vs. 
Government, NCT of Delhi and others, 
2009 SCC OnLine Del 281 as well as in 
the case of State of Uttarakhand and 
others Vs. Shailendra Singh, Special, 
Supreme Today AI, 2018 0 Supreme(UK) 
278.
 Having considered, as an interim 
measure, till the next date of listing, one 
post under the OBC Category shall be 
kept vacant.
