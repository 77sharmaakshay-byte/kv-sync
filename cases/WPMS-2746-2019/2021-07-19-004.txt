COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.2746 of 2019
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
(Via video conferencing).
Mr. K.P. Upadhaya, Senior Advocate, for 
the petitioner. 
Mr. Bhupinder Singh Bisht, Advocate, 
for the respondents. 
Though, when this writ petition was 
entertained, the Coordinate Bench of this Court,
has passed a conditional interim order, whereby 
the coercive measures to recover the amount as 
a consequence of the order passed under 
Section 14B and 7Q of the Act, was kept in 
abeyance, subject to the condition of deposit of
an amount of Rs.10,00,000/-.
The petitioner contends that in 
compliance of the interim order dated 
13.09.2019, he has already deposited an amount 
of Rs.10,00,000/-, but despite of the compliance 
of the interim order, the respondents since were 
still proceeding to recover an amount, and 
therefore, he has filed a fresh stay application
on 02.06.2021, submitting thereof that the 
recovery as a consequence thereto may be kept 
in abeyance, as it runs contrary to the interim 
order, already passed earlier by this Court. To this application, there is no objection filed by 
the respondents so far.
Learned counsel for the respondent 
Mr. Bhupinder Singh Bisht, prays for and is 
granted ten days’ time to file the objection to 
the Interim Application No.12589 of 2021.
Put up this writ petition for consideration 
of the stay application on 30.07.2021, and also 
for hearing at admission stage. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
