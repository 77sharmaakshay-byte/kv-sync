COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 2325 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Anil K Bisht, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned Additional C.S.C. 
for the State/respondent nos. 1 to 4.
3. By the instant petition the petitioner is praying 
for quashing of the order dated 20.11.2024 passed by 
the Chief Development Officer, Pauri Garhwal as well 
as consequential order dated 23.11.2024 passed by the 
District Social Welfare Officer, Pauri Garhwal, 
whereby, the approval of the appointment of the 
petitioner dated 02.06.20211 has been cancelled.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
the petitioner was appointed as an Assistant Teacher in 
Jayanand Bhartiya Kanya Vidhyalay Panchpuri, 
Bironkhal, Pauri Garhwal, which was approved by the 
then District Social Welfare Officer, Pauri Garhwal on 
02.06.2011; however, subsequently, on a complaint 
made by one Akhter Hasnain Rijwi, Advocate, by the 
order impugned the approval as accorded on 
02.06.2011 has been cancelled on the ground that the 
degree of Shiksha Visharad is not equivalent to B.Ed.
5. It is also submitted by the learned counsel for the 
petitioner that during service the petitioner also 
obtained the minimum qualification of D.El.Ed. in the 
year 2019 with due permission from the department, 
copy of which is also enclosed at page-40 (Annexure-8).
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
the petitioner is continuing since 2011 and even as on 
today he is having all requisite qualification as 
prescribed for the post; therefore, at this stage, there is 
no justification on the part of the respondents to 
recall/cancel the approval, which was accorded in the 
year 2011.
7. Learned counsel for the State have not disputed 
this fact that as on today the petitioner is having 
requisite qualification; however, the question whether the Shiksha Visharad is equivalent to B.Ed. or not, for 
that purposes the respondents may file their counter 
affidavit.
8. Admittedly, as on date the petitioner is having all 
the requisite qualification and admittedly the petitioner 
was appointed in the year 2011, which was duly 
approved; therefore, the discontinuation of the 
petitioner at this stage by recalling the approval 
accorded in the year of 2011 merely on the ground that 
the Shiksha Visharad is not equivalent to B.Ed. is 
appears to be unjustified and unsustainable.
9. Learned State counsel accepts notice for 
respondent nos. 1 to 4.
10. Issue notice to respondent no. 5. Steps within a 
week.
11. Let the respondents may file counter affidavit 
within four weeks.
12. Two weeks thereafter is granted to the petitioner 
to file rejoinder affidavit.
13. List this matter in the first week of March, 2025.
14. Till the next date of listing the effect and 
operation of the impugned order dated 20.11.2024 
(Annexure-1) passed by the Chief Development Officer, 
Pauri Garhwal as well as consequential order dated 
23.11.2024 passed by the District Social Welfare, Pauri 
Garhwal (Annexure-2) shall remain stayed. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
