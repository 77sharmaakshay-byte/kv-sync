COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 2335 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. S.K. Mandal, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Jayvardhan Kandpal, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State.
3. By the instant writ petition, the petitioner is 
challenging the office order dated 29.11.2024, whereby, 
one incumbent namely Gaurav Mehra (respondent no. 
5), a regular incumbent appointed on the post of Junior 
Engineer (Civil) has been giving posting in Nagar 
Palika Parishad, Bhimtal, Nainital with the additional 
charge of Nagar Palika Parishad, Kaladhungi, Nainital.
4. The grievance of the petitioner is that due to the 
appointment/posting of Gaurav Mehra the petitioner
has been ousted, who is serving as a daily wager on the 
post of Junior Engineer (Civil) since 09.03.2000 and 
submit that if the order impugned is given effect to 
then it will frustrate the pending writ petition i.e. WPSS 
No. 3224 of 2018 in which he has prayed for 
regularisation.
5. Learned Standing Counsel for the State Mr. 
Jayvardhan Kandpal on written instructions submits 
that, in fact, the petitioner has been replaced by a 
regular incumbent Gaurav Mehra; therefore, there is no 
illegality in the order impugned.
6. This is undisputed fact that the petitioner is 
working since long back in Nagar Palika Parishad, 
Bhimtal, Nainital and if the order impugned given 
effect to certainly he will be ousted from service and it 
will also frustrate the pending writ petition of the 
petitioner, which is pending for adjudication of the 
issue of regularization.
7. Apart from this, as it appears from the order 
impugned Mr. Gaurav Mehra has been given 
additional charge of the Nagar Palika Parishad, 
Kaladhungi, Nainital; therefore, in such an eventuality, 
and keeping in view of the fact that the pending writ petition of the petitioner for seeking regularization may 
not frustrate, the respondent no. 5 should be given
posting at Nagar Palika Parishad, Kaladhungi of which 
he already is assigned additional charge. Apart from 
this, there is no justification on the part of respondents 
to give dual charge to respondent no. 5 particularly 
when petitioner is serving in Nagar Palika Parishad, 
Bhimtal, since last more than twenty four years.
8. Learned Standing Counsel Mr. Jayvardhan 
Kandpal submits that in fact the initial induction of the 
petitioner is bad since the petitioner was inducted 
without approval of the Government by the then 
Chairman of the Nagar Palika Parishad and that too 
against the post, which was not sanctioned.
9. Let the respondent may file counter affidavit 
within four weeks.
10. Issue notices to respondent nos. 4 and 5. Steps 
within a week.
11. List this matter on 25.02.2025 along with WPSS 
No. 3224 of 2018.
12. For an interim measure, till the next date of 
listing the impugned order dated 29.11.2024 
(Annexure-1) shall remain stayed so far it relates to 
appointment/posting of Gaurav Mehra in Nagar Palika 
Parishad, Bhimtal, Nainital.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
