 Heard on Amendment Application (IA) 
No.4 of 2024.
 By means of the amendment 
application, the petitioner proposes to add 
two respondents and also add some 
paragraphs in the writ petition. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that the proposed 
respondents are tenants of the petitioner; 
they are creating hindrances in the 
smooth running of the petitioner Samiti. 
Therefore, these facts are needed to be 
incorporated in the writ petition and for 
that purpose those persons are to be 
impleaded.
 It is not objected to by learned State 
counsel. 
 Amendment Application (IA) No.04 of 
2024 is allowed. 
 Let amended petition be filed within a 
week.  Thereafter, issue notices to the newly 
added respondents. They may also file 
their counter affidavits within four weeks.
 Two weeks thereafter, rejoinder 
affidavit, if any, may be filed.
 List thereafter for final hearing. 
 In the present file, counter affidavit of

The file has not been kept properly. This 
Court is facing this problem on multiple 
occasions. 
 Let a copy of this order be sent to the 
Registrar (Judicial) of this Court to 
examine, as to who is negligent and not 
properly maintaining the file and take 
such remedial steps, so that the file may 
be properly maintained.
