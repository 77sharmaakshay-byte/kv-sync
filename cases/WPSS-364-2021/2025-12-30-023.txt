COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 364 of 2021
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Mukesh Kumar Kaparuwan, learned counsel 
for the petitioners.
2. Mr. Sandeep Kothari, learned counsel for 
THDC/respondent no. 2.
3. Mr. Yogesh Pacholia, learned counsel for the 
AICTE/respondent no. 3.
4. The petitioners B.Tech degree, which they have 
obtained from the institution of Surveyors are not being 
taken into consideration by the respondent THDC on the 
ground that the petitioners completed B.Tech course 
through distance mode.
5. The AICTE filed their short counter affidavit 
contending therein that the AICTE in 52nd meeting of 
emergent council held on 03.08.2017 has granted 
recognition to the courses conducted by professional 
bodies/institutes such as institute of surveyors and 
certificate awarded by them are in accordance with the 
office memorandum issued by MHRD on 06.12.2022 are 
recognized for all purposes including higher education 
and employment, which were duly recognized by MHRD 
with permanent recognition upto 31.05.2013, therefore all 
the students who were enrolled with these institutions as 
per MHRD OM with permanent recognition upto 
31.05.2013 stands recognized and public notice was also 
issued on 31.05.2017 in this regard.
6. Now, the question is if the AICTE recognized the 
course then how the THDC is not taking into 
consideration the B.Tech degree obtained by the 
petitioners from the institution of surveyors.
7. Let Mr. Sandeep Kothari may get the instructions 
from the Director (HR) within three days, failing which 
the Director (HR) shall join the proceeding through V.C. 
on 06.01.2026.
8. List this matter on 06.01.2026.
