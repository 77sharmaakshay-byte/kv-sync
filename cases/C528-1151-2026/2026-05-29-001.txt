2. An F.I.R. came to be lodged by the 
complainant alleging therein that the 
father of the applicant had purchased a 
piece of land by means of a registered 
sale deed dated 12.06.1987, purportedly 
registered in Zild No.2668. It is alleged 
that the said sale deed is not traceable in 
the records pertaining to Zild No.2668 
and, therefore, the sale deed in question 
is forged and fabricated. On the basis of 
the aforesaid allegations, it is further 
alleged that the applicant is in illegal 
possession of the property in dispute on 
the strength of the said forged 
document.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that the dispute between the 
parties is essentially civil in nature. It is 
submitted that the complainant has 
already instituted Original Suit No.335 of 
2019 before the competent Civil Court 
seeking cancellation of the aforesaid sale 
deed, which is still pending adjudication. 
It is further contended that the 
genuineness, validity and legality of the 
sale deed are matters directly involved in 
the said civil proceedings and are yet to 
be adjudicated upon by the Civil Court. 
Learned counsel submits that unless the 
competent Civil Court records a finding 
with regard to the alleged fabrication of 
the sale deed, the criminal proceedings initiated on the same set of allegations 
are premature. It is also argued that the 
present criminal proceedings have been 
instituted only to give a criminal colour 
to a purely civil dispute relating to title 
and possession of immovable property 
and to exert undue pressure upon the 
applicant.
4. Issue notice to respondent no.2, 
returnable at an early date.
5. List the matter after receipt of the 
service report. 
6. Considering the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
applicant, it is directed that, till the next 
date of listing, further proceedings of 
Criminal Case No.4726 of 2025, pending 
before the Court of learned Judicial 
Magistrate-III, Dehradun, shall remain 
stayed, qua the applicant.
7. Stay application (I.A. No.1 of 2026) 
stands disposed of.
