 This Miscellaneous Application has been filed praying for release 
of the amount deposited by the appellant in compliance of the order dated 
21.08.2012 in favour of the respondent. 
2. Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
3. Brief facts of the case are that certain disputes arose between the 
appellant State of Uttarakhand and the respondent M/s Hydel 
Construction Pvt. Ltd., which were referred to the Arbitral Tribunal. The 
Arbitral Tribunal gave its award on 08.04.2007. The present appellant 
filed an Application under Section 34 of the Arbitration and Conciliation 
Act before the District Judge, Dehradun. After hearing the parties, the 
Application filed under Section 34 was dismissed by the learned District 
Judge, Dehradun, vide order dated 06.01.2012. Hence, the present appeal 
was filed on behalf of the State, which was admitted on 21.08.2012. This 
Court, at the time of admitting the appeal, directed the appellant State to 
deposit the entire amount directed to be paid vide the award passed by the 
Arbitral Tribunal, within a period of eight weeks. 2
4. The learned Senior Advocate appearing on behalf of the respondent 
contended that the project of Maneri Bhali Phase II, after its completion, 
has already started generation of electricity and the respondent is being 
deprived of its payment. 
5. Having considered the submissions of the learned counsel for the 
appellant and the learned counsel for the respondent, Miscellaneous 
Application Nos. 12796 of 2013 is disposed of with a direction that 25% 
of the amount deposited by the appellant shall be released in favour of the 
respondent without furnishing any security and 25% of the amount 
deposited by the appellant shall be released in favour of the respondent on 
furnishing a bank guarantee. Rest of the amount deposited by the 
appellant be deposited in a Fixed Deposit scheme to prevent loss of 
interest on the said sum. 
6. List this appeal for final hearing in the week commencing 24th
February, 2014.
