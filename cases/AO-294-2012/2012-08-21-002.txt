 This appeal from order has been preferred by the 
appellant against the judgment and order dated 06.01.2012 
passed by the District Judge, Dehradun in Arbitration Case 
No.64 of 2007 State of Uttarakhand Vs. M/s. Hydel 
Construction Pvt. Ltd. Whereby the learned District Judge 2
has rejected the objection U/S 34 of Arbitration & 
Conciliation Act of the appellant against the arbitral award 
dated 08.04.2007 passed by the Arbitral Tribunal in respect 
of the claims regarding out of extra item of ues of steel fibre 
in shortcrete by the respondent herein arising from Principal 
agreement No.2/SE/1980-81 dated 30.03.1981 . 
 Learned Dy. Advocate General appearing on 
behalf of State/appellant has contended that respondent 
wrote a letter dated 25.11.2003 which is annexure No.6 to 
the appeal, to the petitioner-department wherein it is 
mentioned in the para 2 that “As regards our rates for supply 
and placing of the scanfibre with shortcrete, the same shall be 
as per the actual cost of the material at site 10% of the cost at 
site as labour and equipment charges and 20% overheads 
and profit on the same”. It is also requested that sanction 
may kindly be granted for the same. This letter was never 
cancelled nor withdrawn by the respondent. As above the 
rate of Rs.128.17 per Kg. was calculated by the respondent 
whereas the petitioner/department has rated the same at 
Rs.119. per Kg. This fact is not disputed that appellant￾department has accepted the supply and use of shortcrete 
as mentioned in the letter. 
 By the perusal of the objections, which have been 
filed as annexure No.7 to the appeal whereby it is clearly 
mentioned in the para No.5 that the work was treated as 
extra items. Ultimately the work continues and this work 
was decided to be “extra item” for which the rates were to be 
analysed. 
 A dispute arose between the parties regarding 
rates of extra item of “use of Scanfibre Steel in shortcrete” 
instead of the wire mesh which was being used earlier which 
was referred to the Hon’ble Arbitral Tribunal. 
 Learned Tribunal, after hearing the parties, 
considering the objections as well as the opinion of Expert 
Sri B.B.L. Mittal, Chief Engineer/Director, State Engineer’s 3
Academy, Uttaranchal and also perusal of the documents 
available on the record, came to the conclusion that 
respondent is entitle for the rate of Rs.220/- per kg. for 
scanfibre steel in short create for entire period. Of course, 
initially the work was assigned to the respondent on 
experimental basis at the rate of Rs. 95/- per kg. and final 
rate was to be negotiated between the parties. 
 Aggrieved by the said award passed by the 
learned Tribunal, State filed the objection before District 
Judge, Dehradun under Section 34 of the Arbitration & 
Conciliation Act, 1996 which was dismissed vide impugned 
order dated 06.01.2012 in Arbitration Case No.64 of 2007. 
Learned District Judge has also committed any manifest 
error of law in rejecting the objection of the appellant. 
 Since the award is a money decree. In view of the 
Order 41 Rule 1(3) of CPC makes provision that 
“where the appeal is against a decree for 
payment of money, the appellant shall, 
within such time as the Appellate Court 
may allow, deposit the amount disputed in 
the appeal or furnish such security in 
respect thereof as the Court may think fit”. 
 The Order 41 Rule 5 (3)(a) of CPC provides that 
substantial loss may result to the party applying for stay of 
execution unless the order is made. 
 In view of the above, as an interim measure, it is 
directed that the execution of the award shall remain stayed, 
if appellant deposits entire decree amount under award with 
the Registry of this Court within a period of two months from 
today. (Stay application stands disposed of).
