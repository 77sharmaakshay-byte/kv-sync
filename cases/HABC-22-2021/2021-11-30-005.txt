JUDGMENT 
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J. (Oral)
This is a habeas corpus petition. Petitioner 
claims that she is in live-in relationship with Mr. Vikas 
Singh Yadav S/o Mr. Harish Chandra Yadav, who has 
been confined in Vikalp Nasha Mukti Evam Punarwas 
Kendra, near State Bank, Delhi Road, Pakbara, District 
Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh. According to the petitioner, 
Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav has been illegally detained by the 
respondent no.6-Center, at the behest of the respondent 
no.5, who is father-in-law of Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav. 
2. On 25.11.2021, this Court directed the 
respondent no.4 to retrieve the corpus of Mr. Vikas Singh 
Yadav and produce him before this Court. Mr. Vikas 2
Singh Yadav is present before this Court as identified by 
Mr. B.C. Joshi, Sub-Inspector, Police Station Bhimtal, 
District Naintial. He is also accompanied by Mr. Mukesh 
Rajora, Sub-Inspector, Police Station Pakbara, District 
Moradabad. Director of the Vikalp Nasha Mukti Evam 
Punarwas Kendra, Mr. Mohd. Parvez is also present 
before this Court.
3. The Court questioned Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav, 
he would submit as hereunder:-
(i) I was practicing Advocate in the High Court of 
Uttarakhand, Nainital. My in-laws and my 
family members lodged a missing report at 
Police Station Majhola, District Moradabad. On 
03.09.2021, my in-laws took me from Bhimtal 
and to Moradabad. On the same night, my in￾laws pressurized me that I should stay in 
Moradabad. They snatched my mobile phone, 
my ID Cards, my ID of Nainital High Court, 
they also took `4,500/- from me. 
(ii) On the next day, they handed me over to Mr. 
Parvez. I was handed over at Reliance Petrol 
Pump, Delhi Road, Moradabad. Thereafter, Mr. 3
Mohd. Parvez along with the two persons, took 
me in their Nasha Mukti Center. On the same 
day, they asked me to sign on a file. For the 
last three months, I am confined in that 
Center. I do not get any sun light. I have been 
facing physical and mental torture. I have been 
living in a very unhygienic condition in the 
center. In the center, in an area of about 2000 
square feet, they had confined about 90 
persons in the last month and this month, 
there are 60 persons; they are kept like 
animals. I was 85 Kilograms, when I entered 
the Center, today perhaps I have reduced to 65 
Kilograms.
(iii) In their Center, there is a Sanjivini Booti, if 
anyone has any problem with any person, 
either with regard to property or someone 
wants to evade the police, or with regard to 
their family matters, the person may be sent to 
this Center. All the problems are resolved. The 
Center people would not reveal the information 
about the inmates, those who are confined in 
the Center. In fact, in my case also, the 4
petitioner was searching for me and initially the 
Center people denied my admission in the 
Center. I was never medically examined. I never 
saw any doctor in the Center. In the Center,
they do not provide adequate food. In the 
Center, they also give special punishment and 
for that purpose they would lower the pants 
and would beat with chappal and danda. For 
that purpose they had identified the places 
which are not covered by CCTV cameras. There 
was an inmate named Robin Massih. I was told 
that he was fine, when he was taken to the 
Center, but on 24th or 26th of November, 2021, 
his condition was deteriorated. The Center 
people contemplating his imminent death, got 
him admitted in the hospital, where he died a 
day thereafter. I was told that Robin Massih’s 
sister got him admitted there because she 
wanted the share in the property, as Robin 
Massih was unmarried. 
(iv) The Center keeps people there for a longer 
time. In fact there are persons, who have been 
detained in the Center for more than 13 5
months now. I can even name them. A Shabaz 
is there for the last 13 months. He is a resident 
of Baheri, perhaps it is in District Bareilly. One 
Mohd. Sajman is confined in the Center for the 
last 09 months. He is resident of District 
Bijnor. One Mr. Asad Khan from District 
Sambhal has been in the Center for almost 11 
years now. He was released for one month or 
two months and again called at the Center. 
4. Mr. Mohd. Parvez, the Director of Vikalp Nasha 
Mukti Evam Punarwas Kendra, the respondent no.6 is 
present before the Court. The Court wanted to know from 
him, as to how they are running the Center. The Court 
also wanted to know from him, as to what is the basis of 
confining Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav in the Center?
Particularly, as to whether he has ever been medically 
examined by any doctor to certify that he is a person to be 
kept in the Center. 
5. According to Mr. Mohd. Parvez, Mr. Vikas 
Singh Yadav was never examined by any doctor before he 
was taken in the Center. According to him, his family 
members handed Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav to him, 6
therefore, they took him in the Center. It was told that 
Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav was a Smack addict. 
6. On the further query of the Court, as to 
whether Mr. Mohd. Parvez, the Director of the Center, has 
ever got Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav examined from the doctor 
to ascertain, as to whether he is mentally ill or to confirm 
he is a person fit to be confined in the Center? He replied 
that such medical examination was never done. In fact, 
Mr. Mohd. Parvez has placed a purported prescription 
given by a doctor, Rekha Dhariwal, Pakbara Clinic, 
District Moradabad to suggest that the doctor had once
examined Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav. This prescription be 
kept on record. It is in two pages. It records that Mr. 
Vikas Singh Yadav is a Smack addict. 
7. The question is how a doctor at the first 
instance can diagnose that a person is Smack addict?
What procedure had been done? What investigation had 
been carried out? How was it ascertained by the doctor 
that a person is addict? There is no reply to it. 
8. Mr. Mohd. Parvez has also placed before the 
Court a Registration No.67/SMHA/2021-22 dated 
29.06.2021, which is a Provisional Registration given 7
under Section 65(2) or Section 66(10) of the Mental 
Healthcare Act, 2017 (for short, “the Act”), to run Apna 
Vikalp Nasha Mukti Avam Puranavas Kendra and this 
certificate records that number of beds should be 20. The 
Court wanted to know from Mr. Mohd. Parvez, as to how 
against 20 beds, they are taking 60 people or 90 people. 
To it, Mohd. Parvez replied that the persons, who cannot 
pay money, they are also detained in excess to 20.
9. During the course of hearing, Mr. Pratiroop 
Pandey, learned AGA for the State of Uttarakhand, would 
suggests that a person cannot be confined in mental 
health establishment merely on the recommendations of 
the family members. It is barred under Section 3 of the 
Act. He has referred to Section 3(5) of the Act, which 
reads as hereunder:-
“3. Determination of mental illness.-
(1) …………………………………………………………………
(2) …………………………………………………………………
(3) …………………………………………………………………
(4) …………………………………………………………………
(5) The determination of a person's mental illness shall 
alone not imply or be taken to mean that the person is 
of unsound mind unless he has been declared as such 
by a competent court.”
10. Learned State counsel also submits that there 
have been various guidelines of the Court that regulate 
the entry of persons in the mental health establishment. 8
11. Mr. Lalit Miglani, learned AGA for the State of 
Uttarakhand would submit that the State of affairs, as 
disclosed by Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav are very serious and 
in this matter, an FIR should be immediately lodged and 
the inmates should be rescued. 
12. Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the petitioner should be freed to move 
wherever he wants to move. He would submit that under 
Section 55 of the Act, the State should have supervised
the mental health establishment, which according to the 
learned counsel for the petitioner, it appears has not been 
done. 
13. During the course of hearing, a woman 
introduced herself Smt. Premlata Yadav wife of Mr. Vikas 
Singh Yadav intervened. She has stated that Mr. Vikas 
Singh Yadav’s activities are quite strange. He was staying 
with a woman in Mehra Gaon, District Nainital. They 
found 2 ml. syringes from his possession. His behavior 
towards with the family members was bad. They thought 
that he is taking drugs or the woman with whom he is 
staying, is giving him drugs. Therefore, Vikas Singh Yadav 
has been handed over to Mr. Mohd. Parvez. He needs 9
treatment. He is not fit. There are dark patches below his 
eyes; which were not there earlier. 
14. The Court is only concerned now with the 
safety of Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav. He may not be detained. 
At present he shall not be sent to the respondent no.6 
Center. But, his health condition (especially mental 
health) needs to be examined before he is freed. In case 
doctors advise that Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav needs special 
care, the matter may then be considered accordingly. 
Therefore, before proceeding further in the matter this 
Court shall now separately direct examination of Mr. 
Vikas Singh Yadav by doctors. Once report is received,
the matter shall be taken up at 04:00 PM today. 
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
