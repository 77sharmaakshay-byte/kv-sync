At the very outset, learned counsel 
appearing for the State seeks 
adjournment on the ground that Chief

Learned Amicus Curiae would 
submit that various information with 
regard to the mentally ill people is not
still available, despite response affidavit 
of the State. He would submit that 
information is required to be collected 
from the District Magistrates of the 
respective Districts. 
Learned Amicus Curiae may 
communicate with the District 
Magistrates and collect the information 
in the format. Apart from other, the
information may also include as to how 
the mentally ill persons are being 
identified on regular basis and what is 
the system for providing them 
medicines as per protocol. 
Learned Amicus Curiae would 
submit the collected information with 
his comments, in the light of the 
response affidavit, filed by the State, till last week of October, 2022, after 
sharing it with the State Government 
and other respondents.
