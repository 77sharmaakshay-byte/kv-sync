5. Section 65(1) of the Mental 
Healthcare Act, 2017 ordains that no 
person or organisation shall establish or 
run a mental health establishment 
unless it has been registered with the 
Authority under the provisions of this 
Act. ‘Authority’, contemplated in Section 
65(1) of the Act, is to regulate the 
functioning of mental health 
establishments and various other 
matters. Section 65(6) of the said Act 
provides that the Authority shall, within 
a period of eighteen months from the 
commencement of the Act, by 
notification, specify the minimum 
standards for different categories of 
mental health establishments, and
period of eighteen months, mentioned in 
Section 65(6) of the Act, is to be 
reckoned from 08.01.2018, when the Mental Healthcare Act was enforced. 
6. Section 121 of the Act confers Rule 
making power of Central and State 
Governments. Section 122 of the Act 
lays down the power of Central Authority 
to make Regulation; while, Section 123 
provides such powers upon the State 
Authority.
7. We are informed that the State of 
Uttarakhand is yet to notify the Rules in 
exercise of Rule-making power under 
Section 121 of the Act.
8. Learned counsel for petitioner 
submits that there is no regulatory 
framework in existence in Uttarakhand 
to regulate the standard and functioning 
of mental health establishments, due to 
which, persons with mental health are 
facing various problems and there are 
complaint of malpractices, which are 
surfacing every now and then.
9. Learned State Counsel, on 
instructions, submits that the Rules 
under Section 121 of the Act shall be 
notified, positively within ten days.
10. We, therefore, post this case on 
04.09.2023. 
11. In the meantime, if the Rules are 
not notified, the Secretary, Medical 
Health & Family Welfare, Uttarakhand 
shall appear in person before this Court, 
on the next date fixed.
