Detailed order has been 
dictated and the matter is to be taken 
up at 04:00 PM today itself. In the 
meanwhile, to ascertain the mental 
condition of Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav, 
the following order is being passed.
Apparently the way Mr. Vikas 
Singh Yadav has addressed the 
Court, it cannot be said that he is 
suffering with any mental illness, but 
this Court cannot also certify that he 
does not require any examination by 
any doctor. Therefore, before 
proceeding further, this Court 
considers it necessary that Mr. Vikas 
Singh Yadav be produced for 
examination before the CMS, B.D. 
Pandey District Hospital, Mallital, 
Nainital. 
The Court requests the CMS, 
B.D. Pandey District Hospital, 
Mallital, Nainital to constitute a team 
of doctors, so as to ascertain the 
mental condition of Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav. For this purpose, if required, 
Mr. Vikas Singh Yadav may be 
admitted in the hospital for such 
days, as the doctors may consider 
necessary.
Let a copy of this part of the 
order alone be immediately sent to 
the CMS, B.D. Pandey District 
Hospital, Mallital, Nainital through 
Mr. B.C. Joshi, Sub-Inspector, Police 
Station Bhimtal, District Nainital. 
A preliminary report may be 
placed before this Court at 03:45 PM 
today itself. 
List this matter at 04:00 PM.
