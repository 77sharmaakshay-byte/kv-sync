Mr. Pankaj Singh, Advocate for the

This matter is being heard through 
video conferencing. 
On the last date, the matter was taken 
up on 22.07.2022. On that date, an 
affidavit, filed on behalf of the State, was 
taken on record, but the Court noted that 
on page 6 of that affidavit, a verification 
part was blank. The Court, while naming 
the officer who filed the affidavit, also 
observed “how Ms. Jasvinder Kaur, Under 
Secretary, has verified the affidavit?”
Today, learned Advocate General 
appears for the State would submit that, in 
fact, this is factually wrong, because, Ms. 
Jasvinder Kaur, Under Secretary did not 
verify the affidavit, instead, she only 
identified the deposer in the affidavit dated 
18.07.2022.
The affidavit dated 18.07.2022, filed in 
this case, reveals that Ms. Jasvinder Kaur, 
Under Secretary, has identified the deposer 
and the affidavit has been verified by Mr. 
Chitresh Kumar Mittal, Oath 
Commissioner, High Court of Uttarakhand 
(Secretariat Dehradun). 
The affidavit, which was verified by 
Mr. Chitresh Kumar Mittal, Oath 
Commissioner, was not as per law, its
verification paragraph is blank, therefore, 
the Court requests the Registry to consider 
deleting the name of Chitresh Kumar Mittal 
from the panel of Oath Commissioner.
The order dated 22.07.2022, which 
suggested removal of the name of Ms. Jasvinder Kaur, Oath Secretary, form the 
panel of Oath Commissioner, is modified 
accordingly.
Learned Advocate General also 
suggests that Government has also 
contacted Hans Foundation, a non￾governmental organization, which is 
working in the field to get information and 
to suggest input so as to better implement 
the provisions of the Mental Health Care 
Act, 2017.
Learned Amicus Curiae may also file 
response and suggest ways and means 
pursuant to the affidavit now filed on behalf 
of the State, which is taken on record today.
List this matter on 01.09.2022, for 
further hearing.
In the meanwhile, learned Amicus 
Curiae may also get information from Hans 
Foundation, with regard to implementation 
of the Act.
