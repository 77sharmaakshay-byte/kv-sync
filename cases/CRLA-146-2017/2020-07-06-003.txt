Application no. 1903 of 2020 is 
filed by the appellant seeking a short 
term bail. The reason assigned in the 
application is that appellant’s parents 
are aged about 65-70 years and he 
has 5-7 bighas of land, which has not 
been cultivated, therefore, bail may be 
granted. 
The same is objected by the 
learned assistant government 
advocate. 
On hearing learned counsels, 
we do not find any merit in the 
application. 
Age of old parents is not a 
criteria to enlarge a convict on bail. 
Similarly, non cultivation of land also 
cannot be considered a ground on 
which a convict can be enlarged on 
bail. Hence, for all these reasons, the 
application is dismissed. 
Post for hearing in the usual 
course.
