List thereafter.
Also heard on Interim Relief 
Application.
The case of the petitioner is that an 
advertisement was issued on 05.10.2016, but 
the selection process could not be completed in 
pursuant to the advertisement dated 
05.10.2016. Thereafter, the State Government 
amended the regulations on 04.01.2017. 
Pursuant to the amended regulation, a fresh 
notification was published on 08.03.2017. The 
petitioner has applied against the new 
advertisement and he had been selected and 
now he was posted as a Lecturer (Biology) in 
Inter College, Khirket, District Almora.
By the impugned order dated 
02.09.2020, the services of the petitioner have 
been terminated without afforded opportunity 
of hearing. Petitioner was a regular Lecturer 
(Biology) in the said institution.
The effect and operation of the 
impugned order dated 02.09.2020 shall 
remain stayed until further orders of this 
Court. The respondents are directed to permit 
the petitioner to continue on the post of 
Lecturer (Biology).
Interim Relief Application (CLMA 
No.7251 of 2020) is disposed of accordingly.
