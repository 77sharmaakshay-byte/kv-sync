3. Mr. M.K. Ray, learned counsel 
appearing for respondent no.3.
4. By means of the present writ petition, 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution 
of India, the petitioner has prayed for 
issuance of a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of certiorari for quashing the First 
Information Report/Case Crime No. 0152 
of 2026, dated 26.09.2026, registered at 
Police Station Kotwali, Pithoragarh, District 
Pithoragarh, for the offences punishable 
under Section 352 of the B.N.S. and 
Sections 3(1)(v), 3(1)(/k) and 3(1)(u) of the 
Scheduled Castes and the Scheduled 
Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, 1989.
5. Learned counsel appearing for the petitioner would submit that the petitioner 
is an ex-Army personnel and has falsely 
been implicated in the present matter. He 
would further submit that the impugned 
F.I.R. has been lodged solely on the basis 
of a photocopy/screenshot of an alleged 
Facebook post and that, even if the 
contents of the said post are taken at their 
face value, the same do not contain any 
abusive or derogatory reference to any 
member of a Scheduled Caste or 
Scheduled Tribe by referring to his/her 
caste name.
6. He would further submit that the 
Investigating Officer has issued a notice to 
the petitioner under Section 35(3) of the 
B.N.S.S., requiring him to appear before 
the concerned police station on 
06.10.2026, however, on the said date, the 
petitioner was at Nainital and, therefore, 
could not appear before the Investigating 
Officer. It is further submitted that the 
petitioner is ready and willing to appear 
before the Investigating Officer and to 
cooperate with the investigation. He, 
therefore, prays that the petitioner may be 
granted protection from arrest, subject to 
his cooperation with the investigation.
7. Learned counsel appearing for 
respondent no.3 would vehemently oppose
the submissions advanced by learned 
counsel for the petitioner. He prays for and 
is granted three weeks' time to file his 
vakalatnama and counter affidavit.
8. List this case on 21.11.2026.
9. Learned counsel for the petitioner shall supply copy of the writ petition to 
learned counsel for respondent no.3 during 
the course of the day.
10. Learned State Counsel shall also file 
counter affidavit, if any, in the meantime.
11. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties and having regard to the 
submissions advanced before this Court, 
without expressing any opinion on the 
merits of the case, the petitioner is directed 
to appear before the concerned 
Investigating Officer on 08.10.2026 and 
shall comply with the notice issued under 
Section 35(3) of the B.N.S.S.
12. The petitioner shall not be arrested till 
08.10.2026, subject to his appearing 
before the Investigating Officer on the said 
date and cooperating with the 
investigation.
