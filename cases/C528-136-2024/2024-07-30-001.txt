COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
C528 No. 136 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Abhishek Verma, learned counsel 
for the applicants.
2. By the instant criminal miscellaneous 
application preferred under Section 528 of 
BNSS 2023, the applicants are challenging 
the proceedings of Complaint Case No. 3194 
of 2020 filed by the respondent/complainant 
in which the applicants have been 
summoned for the offences punishable 
under Sections 498A, 323 and 504 IPC read 
with Section 4 of Dowry Prohibition Act.
3. The brief facts of the case are that 
applicant no. 1 married with respondent/ 
complainant on 10.02.2019 and from this 
wedlock one child was born. In para 9 it is 
stated that there are possibilities of 
amicable settlement and prior to that so 
many attempts were made but applicant no. 
1 could not succeed. 
4. After taking into consideration the 
averments made in para 9 of the petition,
this Court is of the view that efforts may be 
made for their amicable settlement.
5. Issue notice to respondent.
6. Learned counsel for the applicants 
shall take steps to serve respondent within 
three days by both mode including 
electronic mode as well as through the 
counsel representing the respondent in the 
trial court.
7. List this case on 20.08.2024.
8. On that day applicant no. 1 and 
respondent shall remain present in Court for the purpose of their amicable settlement.
9. Till the next date of listing, further 
proceedings of Complaint Case No. 3194 of 
2020, pending in the court of Judicial 
Magistrate/1st Addl. Civil Judge (J.D) 
Roorkee District Haridwar for the offences 
punishable under Sections 498A, 323 and 
504 IPC read with Section 4 of Dowry 
Prohibition Act shall remain stayed.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
