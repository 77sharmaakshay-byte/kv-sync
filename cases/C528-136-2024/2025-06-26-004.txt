Mr. Abhishek Verma, learned counsel for the Applicants. 
2. Mr. Tapan Singh, learned counsel for the Respondent. 
3. As per the order sheet dated 05.05.2025, passed by the 
learned Coordinate Bench, the parties were directed to appear 
before the District Legal Services Authority, District Haridwar, on 
14.05.2025 at the specified time. Further directions were issued 
for the District Legal Services Authority, District Haridwar to 
appoint a Mediator for the conciliation proceedings between the 
parties. It was also directed that the Mediator would submit a 
report to this Court through the Secretary, District Legal Services 
Authority, Haridwar. The matter was accordingly listed for today 
along with the report of the Mediator.

Applicants never appeared before the Mediator at the District 
Legal Services Authority, Haridwar, and consequently, the 
mediation proceedings could not take place.
5. It appears, based on the conduct of the parties, that the 
mediation process cannot proceed and is deemed to have failed.
6. List this case on 18.07.2025.
7. In the meantime, if so desired, rejoinder affidavit on behalf 
of the Applicants may be filed.
8. Interim order, if any, is extended till the next date of listing.
