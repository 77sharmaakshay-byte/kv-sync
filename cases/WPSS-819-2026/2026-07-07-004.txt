2. Mr. K.N. Joshi, learned Dy.A.G. for the State.
3. In view of the grounds stated, Delay Condonation 
Application (IA/2/2026) is disposed of. Counter 
affidavit filed by respondent nos.2 and 3 is taken on 
record. 
4. Petitioner was engaged on contract through 
UPNL as lady Bandi Rakshak in District Jail, Almora. 
5. The services of petitioner were disengaged by 
respondents for the reason that petitioner herself had 
refused to serve as Bandi Rakshak due to which 
another lady was called from Home guards who 
discharged the duties of lady Bandi Rakshak and now 
the situation is that the said post has already been 
filled up by way of regular selection.
5. It is contended by learned State Counsel that at 
present, there is no vacancy on the post of lady Bandi 
Rakshak in District Jail Almora.
6. Learned Counsel for the petitioner contends that 
petitioner is a single mother who has the burden of 
raising a child, and therefore, she may be 
accommodated in any other nearby place like Nainital 
and Haldwani. 
7. Learned State Counsel is directed to seek clear instructions by next week as to whether there exists 
any vacancy of lady Bandi Rakshak in District Jail, 
Nainital or Sub-Jail Haldwani where petitioner can be 
accommodated.
