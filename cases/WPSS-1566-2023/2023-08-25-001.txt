Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for 
the petitioners. 
2. Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned Brief Holder for 
the State. 
3. By means of this writ petition, petitioners 
have challenged the order dated 18.09.2018 
issued by respondent no.2 and also the 
impugned order dated 19.12.2018/20.12.2018 
passed by respondent no.3, annexure nos.10 
& 12, respectively to the writ petition, by 
which the respondent no.3 has cancelled the 
promotional pay scale (Grade Pay Rs.5400/-) 
granted to the petitioners, and also directed 
for recovery of excess payment made to the 
petitioners.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
submitted that the promotional scale (Grade 
Pay Rs.5400/-) was granted to the petitioners 
in accordance with prevailing government 
orders and after due recommendation of the 
Selection Committee, in which there was no 
misrepresentation or fraud committed by the 
petitioners. 
5. Learned State Counsel prays for and is 
granted eight weeks’ time to file counter 
affidavit. 
6. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to 
the petitioners to file rejoinder affidavit. 
7. List this matter on 20.11.2023. 
8. Following the ratio of the judgment passed 2
by the Hon’ble Apex Court in State of Punjab 
and Others vs. Rafiq Masih, reported in 2015 
(4) SCC 334, the respondents are directed not 
to make any recovery from the petitioners 
during pendency of this writ petition. 
9. Interim relief application (IA No.1/2023) 
stands disposed of accordingly.
