Application (IA/8/2026)
4. The present application has been filed seeking 
direction to the Registry to accept the bank draft of 
₹88,41,971.16 submitted by the appellant in pursuance 
of order of the Hon’ble Supreme Court dated 27.01.2026 
in petitions for Special Leave to Appeal (c) No(s). 31060-
31061/2025. The relevant part of the said order is as 
follows:
“8. Out of the sale consideration received by the 
sale of entire crops, 50% of it shall be utilized by 
the petitioners to meet out the expenses and the 
balance 50% shall be deposited by the petitioners 
with the Registrar General of the High Court of 
Uttarakhand.
9. The amount so deposited with the Registrar 
General of the High Court, shall be invested in a 
short term fixed deposit with a Nationalized Bank.
10. The petitioners shall furnish the accounts 
regarding the utilization of the 50% of the sale 
consideration to the High Court of Uttarakhand in 
the pending matter within a period of six weeks.”
5. It is stated that the total amount received by sale 
of paddy is ₹1,53,77,607.32. The sugarcane has been supplied to two different sugar mills. The details of which 
is as follows:
S.No. Name of Company Total Amount
(in Rs.)
1. Kichha Sugar Company Ltd. 1,32,72,283.35
2. JGN Sugar and Biofuels (P)
Ltd. Sitargunj
19,01,109.3
6. The remaining sugarcane crop has been sold 
through auction for a sum of ₹4,08,000. Thus, according 
to the appellants, the total sale proceeds of sugarcane 
was ₹1,55,81,392.65. 
7. It is stated that out of the said amount only 
₹23,06,335 has been received and an amount of 
₹1,32,72,282.35 is still outstanding against Kichha Sugar 
Mill which is a Government Sugar Mill. Accordingly, the 
appellants has furnished bank draft of ₹88,41,971.16
which according to the appellants corresponds to 50% of 
the amount received by the appellant so far.
8. It is stated that as and when remaining amount is 
recovered from Kichha Sugar Mill, 50% of the said 
amount would again be deposited in compliance of the 
order of the Hon’ble Supreme Court.
9. As regards adequacy of the amount, we refrain 
from making any observation in view of the fact that the 
matter is still pending before the Hon’ble Supreme Court. 
However, since the appellants have produced bank draft 
along with application, therefore, we direct the Registrar 
General to accept the bank draft, subject to any order 
passed by the Hon’ble Supreme Court in this behalf. The 
amount, as directed, be deposited in a short-term fixed 
deposit with a nationalised bank and parties be informed 
about the same.
10. The application (IA/8/2026) stands disposed of.
11. List in due course along with connected matter.
