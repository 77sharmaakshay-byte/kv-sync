1. Sri Mohd. Shafy, learned counsel for the appellants in SPA No.

2. Sri Gajendra Tripathi, learned Standing Counsel and Sri M.S. 
Bisht, learned Brief Holder for the State of Uttarakhand. 
3. Under the order of Supreme Court dated 27.01.2026 passed in 
Special Leave to Appeal (C) No(s). 31060-31061/2025, the appellants 
were required to deposit 50 percent of the amount realised from the sale of 
paddy and sugarcane before this Court. In this regard, an Application 
bearing No. 8 of 2026 was filed by the appellants and whereby, we 
permitted the appellants to deposit Rs. 88,41,971.16 by bank draft before 
this Court representing 50 percent amount received by the appellants by 
that time. In the said order itself, it was recorded that an amount of Rs. 
1,32,72,282.35 was outstanding against Kichha Sugar Mill and further that 
the appellants had undertaken to deposit 50 percent of the said amount 
before this Court as soon as the said amount is paid to the appellants. 
4. In the present application (IA No. 9 of 2026), it is stated that 
Kichha Sugar Mill has now paid Rs. 1,32,54,259.07 to the appellants after 
deducting a sum of Rs. 18,024.28 under various heads like insurance, 
stationery, road fund, ledger deduction, building deduction and education 
fund, etc. The appellants accordingly want to deposit Rs. 66,27,129.53 
being 50 percent of the amount received by them from Kichha Sugar Mill. 
5. The application (IA No. 9 of 2026) is allowed without commenting on the adequacy of the amount being deposited as the matter is still sub
judice before the Supreme Court. 
6. Registry is directed to accept the bank draft of the aforesaid 
amount from the appellants. The same shall be invested in a short-term 
deposit in a nationalised bank and the parties shall be informed about the 
same accordingly. 
7. The Urgency Application (IA No. 10 of 2026) also stands disposed 
of.
