13.02.2026 WPMS No. 329 of 2026 
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J. 
An Original Suit (O.S. No. 1 of 2023) was 
filed by the respondent for perpetual injunction. 
An Application for temporary injunction was 
filed by the plaintiff. On 06.11.2025, learned 
Civil Judge (Senior Division), Vikasnagar, 
District Dehradun directed the parties to 
maintain status quo over the suit property. 
Aggrieved by the said order, the plaintiff filed a 
Miscellaneous Civil Appeal (No.13 of 2025). 
Learned Additional District Judge, Vikasnagar, 
Dehradun by the impugned order dated 
15.01.2026, has allowed the appeal and 
restrained the petitioners-defendants from 
taking possession of the suit property and 
evicting the respondent-plaintiff. Hence, the 
present petition under Article 227 of the 
Constitution of India. 
2. Heard Mr. Abhishek Verma, learned 
counsel for the petitioner and Mr. Ajay Joshi, 
learned counsel for the caveator / respondent. 
3. On the request of both the parties, list on 
19.02.2026.
