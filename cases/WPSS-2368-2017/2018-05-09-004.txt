2. Mr. Sharad Jindal makes a statement 
that the different papers for typing test were 
prepared as there were only three computers. He 
submits that he is not aware as to how many 
candidates participated in the typing test. On this, 
Mr. C.K. Sharma, Advocate interrupted and 
prompted the Manager about the actual position. 
The conduct of Mr. C.K. Sharma, Advocate is 
against the professional ethics. He prompted him 
that as if manager Mr. Sharad Jindal is not aware 
as to how many candidates appeared, but he 
prompted him that there were 13 candidates. Then 
the Manager stated that there were 13 candidates 
and only three computers. So separate papers were 
prepared for the reasons that the candidates who 
firstly appeared in the typing test may not disclose 
to other candidates who are appearing later for the 
typing test. No prudent person can believe on the 
statement of Mr. Sharad Jindal as for a single post 
when 13 candidates are appearing, a candidate who 
appeared first in the typing test will disclose to 
other candidates because it will directly affect the 
chance of her own selection. It appears to this Court 
that Mr. Sharad Jindal has made false statement 
before this Court to protect the appointment of 
respondent no.7. 2
3. The interview tabulation is produced in 
original shape by the Chief Education Officer, 
Nainital, and the same is taken on record and kept 
in sealed cover. From the perusal of the marks 
awarded in Column No.7 to selected candidate Smt. 
Neha Sharma initially the same appears to be 10 
and subsequently it was made 20 by Smt. Hari Sati, 
Principal of the G.P.P. Kanya Inter College, 
Ramnager, District Nainital. 
4. The Present Chief Education Officer 
submits that at that time he was not there and 
approval of Smt. Neha Sharma was made by Mr. 
Raghunath Aggarwal, the then Chief Education 
Officer. Mr. Vijay Chandra Pandey the then 
Principal submits that at the time of conducting the 
typing test he was present in the examination hall 
and 13 candidates appeared for the typing test. He 
submits that for 13 candidates 5 paper sets were 
prepared. The typing papers were randomly given to 
the candidates. Mohan Chander Pandey, the expert 
from the Private Audhyogik Prasikshan Sansthan, 
Ram Nagar, District Nainital was called as an 
expert. 
5. While the Court was making query from 
Mr. Vijay Chandra Pandey, Mr. Sharad Jindal made 
efforts to improve the statement of Mr. Vijay 
Chandra Pandey. The expert was called from the 
Private Audhyogik Prasikshan Sansthan, Ram 
Nagar, District Nainital. Mr. Vijay Chandra Pandey, 
further submits that there is a Government 
Audhyogik Sansthan in Ram Nagar. But it again 
creates a doubt on the functioning of respondents in 
appointment of respondent no.7 Smt. Neha Sharma 
that instead of calling expert for typing test from the 
Government Audhyogik Prashikshan Sansthan, the 
expert from the private body was called, so that the 
respondents in connivance with each other may 
succeed in appointing a candidate of their choice. It 
is not a private institution where appointment of 
respondent no.7 was made on the post of clerk, the 
salary is to be paid from the State funds and this 
Court is of the firm opinion that it is a clear case of 
giving appointment to respondent No. 7 Smt. Neha 
Sharma by extending undue benefit to her and 
ignoring the other candidates. 3

7. As per the advertisement, the 
qualification for the post of Assistant Clerk was 
intermediate, Computer-M.S. Office, speed of 25 
words per minute in Hindi Typing. The petitioner 
Smt. Anju Agarwal is having the qualification of six 
months computer application course and one year 
course in Hindi Stenography and Typing and 
experience of 3 years as Computer Operator-cum￾Office Assistant. Whereas the selected candidate 
Smt. Neha Sharma is not having the requisite 
qualification in view of the advertisement issued by 
the respondents. The appointment of respondent 
no.7 has been made in clandestine manner 
ignoring the requisite qualification. From the 
perusal of the entire record produced by the 
Manager of the Institution and Chief Education 
Officer, Nainital, prima facie, it would reveal that 
the respondent nos. 1 to 6 have made illegal 
appointment of respondent no.7. 
8. From the perusal of the interview 
tabulation whereupon there is overwriting and by 
overwriting 10 marks is made as 20 marks. Prima 
facie, it appears to this court that all the members 
of the Selection Committee manipulated the 4
documents just to give undue benefits of 
appointment to respondent no.7.
