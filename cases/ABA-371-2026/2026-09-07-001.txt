Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Gaurav Singh, learned counsel for 
the applicants through Video Conferencing.
2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned A.G.A. along 
with Mr. Nikhil Bisht, learned Brief Holder for 
the State.
3. By means of the present application, 
the applicants seek anticipatory bail in 
connection with F.I.R./Case Crime No. 335 
of 2026, registered at Police Station Kotwali 
Manglour, District Haridwar, for the offences 
punishable under Sections 3/5/11 of the 
Uttarakhand Cow Progeny Act, 2007.
4. Learned counsel for the applicants
would submit that the impugned F.I.R. has 
been lodged on the allegation that, while the 
police personnel were on patrolling duty, 
they received information from an informer 
that the present applicants, along with 
certain other persons, had allegedly 
slaughtered cow progeny in a mango 
orchard. It is further alleged that, acting 
upon the said information, the police 
conducted a raid at the alleged spot and 
recovered approximately 300 kilograms of 
beef along with certain equipment allegedly 
used in the commission of the offence. The 
recovered material was thereafter inspected 
by the Veterinary Officer and samples thereof were taken, pursuant to which the 
present F.I.R. came to be registered.
5. Learned counsel for the applicants
would further submit that the applicants are 
innocent and have falsely been implicated in 
the present case; that, the applicants were 
neither present at the alleged spot at the 
time of the raid nor were any incriminating 
article recovered from their possession; that, 
the implication of the applicants rests solely 
upon the information allegedly furnished by 
the police informer, who stated the names of 
the present applicants; that, except for the 
said information, there is no independent 
material or evidence, at this stage, 
connecting the applicants with the 
commission of the alleged offence, therefore, 
the custodial interrogation of the applicants
are not warranted and they are entitled to 
the protection of anticipatory bail.
6. Per contra, learned State Counsel 
would oppose the anticipatory bail 
application and submits that a substantial 
quantity of alleged beef was recovered 
during the raid conducted by the police and 
that the investigation is still in progress. 
However, learned State Counsel seeks time 
to file a detailed counter affidavit.
7. In the interest of justice, four weeks' 
time is granted to learned State Counsel to 
file a detailed counter affidavit.
8. List this case on 28.09.2026.
9. Having considered the submissions and 
the material on record, and having regard to 
the nature of allegations and the stage of 
investigation, this Court is prima facie of the 
view that the applicants have made out a 
case for interim protection. The applicants
were not apprehended from the spot, no 
incriminating recovery have been made from 
their possession, and their alleged 
involvement appears to be based primarily 
on information furnished by a police 
informer. Accordingly, without expressing any opinion on the merits of the case, this 
Court considers it appropriate to grant 
interim protection to the applicants till the 
next date of listing.
10. Accordingly, it is directed that the 
investigation in the aforesaid Case 
Crime/F.I.R. shall proceed independently, 
impartially and strictly in accordance with 
law, without being influenced by any 
observation made herein. However, in the 
event of arrest of the applicants in 
connection with the aforesaid Case 
Crime/F.I.R., they shall be released on 
interim anticipatory bail till the next date of 
listing, upon their furnishing a personal 
bond along with two reliable sureties, each 
in the like amount, to the satisfaction of the 
Arresting Officer/Investigating Officer, 
subject to the following conditions:
(i) The applicants shall cooperate with the 
investigation and shall appear before the 
Investigating Officer as and when required;
(ii) The applicants shall not directly or 
indirectly make any inducement, threat or 
promise to any person acquainted with the 
facts of the case so as to dissuade such 
person from disclosing such facts to the 
Court or to the Investigating Agency;
(iii) The applicants shall not leave the 
territory of India without prior permission 
of the Court concerned;
(iv) The applicants shall not misuse the 
liberty granted by this order and shall 
maintain peace and good conduct during 
the period of interim protection;
(v) In case of breach of any of the aforesaid 
conditions, it shall be open to the 
State/prosecution to seek modification or 
cancellation of interim anticipatory bail in 
accordance with law.
