Heard on interim relief application.
The challenge is made to the order 
dated 13.03.2023, passed in ZA Appeal 
No. 03(22-23) under Section 331 of the 
U.P. Zamindari and Land Reforms Act, 
1950, by the Commission, Kumaon 
Division, Nainital, by which the matter has 
been remanded for re-hearing.
Learned Senior Counsel for the 
petitioner would submit that earlier the 
partition suit between the parties has 
already been decided; preliminary decree has been formed; therefore, the earlier suit 
was consolidated with the instant matter 
and case decided; there is no illegality in 
the order passed by the Assistant 
Collector, 1st Class, Bazpur, District 
Udham Sing Nagar while deciding the 
partition suit on 07.10.2020 (Revenue 
Case No. 22/02 of 2019-20, Baldev Singh 
v. Ratandeep Singh and others). It is 
argued that the order of remand is not in 
accordance with law.
Having considered, as an interim 
measure, it is directed that till the next of 
listing the operation of the impugned order 
shall remain in abeyance.
Interim Relief Application (IA no. 1 of 
2025) stands disposed of accordingly.
