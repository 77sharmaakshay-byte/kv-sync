18.07.2022 CLR No.47 of 2022 
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
This proposed Civil Revision has been 
filed against the order dated 02.05.2022, 
passed by Civil Judge (Senior Division), 
Dehradun in Miscellaneous Case No.182 of 
2019, “Prabhatesh Pratap Singh vs. Prabhan 
Pratap Singh and Others”, by which, the 
application of the third party, i.e. the 
respondent no.1, filed under Order 1 Rule 
10 read with Section 151 of the Code of 
Civil Procedure, 1908, has been allowed.
Heard Mr. Neeraj Garg, learned 
counsel for the revisionist.
The learned counsel for the revisionist￾plaintiff submitted that a preliminary decree 
in the partition suit was passed. Thereafter, 
the matter for final decree is pending before 
the concerned court. The third party, i.e. 
the respondent no.1 herein, moved an 
application to implead him on the basis of a 
Will dated 01.03.2012. The said application 
has been allowed. Hence, this revision.
The learned counsel for the revisionist 
submitted that the respondent no.1 is 
neither a necessary party nor a proper party 
and his presence is not required for 
effective adjudication of the question,involved in the matter, and, in the matter of 
final decree, the said application on the said 
ground cannot be allowed.
Admit.
Issue notice to the respondents.
Steps to be taken within 24 hours.
List this case on 01.08.2022.
Meanwhile, in case, the revisionist 
moves an application for adjournment, the 
concerned court shall consider the same till 
01.08.2022.
