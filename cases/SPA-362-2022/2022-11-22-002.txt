Issue notice. Learned counsels appear and 
accept notice on behalf of the respondents. 
The submission of learned counsel for the 
appellant is that respondent no.1- writ petitioner did 
not have locus standi to file the writ petition, wherein 
the impugned order has been passed. 
Learned counsel for respondent no.1 has placed 
reliance on Rule 3(1) of the Uttar Pradesh Kshettra 
Panchayats and Zila Panchayats (Removal of 
Pramukhs, Up-Pramukhs, Adhyakshas and 
Upadhyakshas) Enquiry Rules, 1997, which lays down 
the ‘procedure relating to complaints’, and begin with 
the words “3(1) Any person making a complaint……….”
 The submission is that any person is entitled to 
make a complaint, and it is not necessary that the 
complainant should be a resident of the relevant 
Kshettra Pachayat or Zila Panchayat. 
On the other hand, the submission of learned 
counsel for the appellant is that Section 194 of the 
Uttarakhand Panchayati Raj Act, 2016 has repealed 
the parent enactment, under which the aforesaid Rules 
have been framed, and there is no saving clause. Section 194 of the Uttarakhand Panchayati Raj Act, 
2016 reads as follows:- 
“194. Repeal.- (1) The Uttar Pradesh Panchayat Raj 
Act, 1947 and Uttar Pradesh Kshettra Panchayat and 
Zila Panchayat Act, 1961 (as applicable in Uttrakhand 
State) is hereby repealed. 
(2) Notwithstanding such repeal, anything done or 
any action taken under the said enactment shall be 
deemed to have been done or taken under the 
corresponding provisions of this Act.”
Learned counsel for respondent no.1 submits 
that this Court has held that despite repealing of the 
aforesaid enactments, framed by the legislature of 
Uttar Pradesh, the aforesaid Rules continue to apply in 
the State of Uttarakhand. He wishes to produce 
judgment to this effect. 
At his request, list this case on 07.12.2022.
