2. By means of the present writ petition, 
the petitioner seeks quashing of the First
Information Report dated 16.06.2026, 
registered as FIR No. 0219 of 2026, under 
Section 108 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 
2023, at Police Station Kiccha, District 
Udham Singh Nagar.
3. As per the allegations contained in the 
impugned F.I.R., the same has been lodged 
by the wife of the deceased alleging that the 
petitioner, along with certain other persons, 
had been continuously harassing and 
threatening the deceased to sell his property 
to them, failing which they would eliminate 
his entire family. It is alleged that, owing to 
the continuous threats extended by the 
petitioner and the co-accused, the deceased 
remained under constant mental stress, fear 
and intimidation. On the evening of 
05.06.2026, the deceased suddenly felt 
uneasy and was taken to a local hospital. At 
about 10:30 p.m., he disclosed to the 
attending doctor that he had consumed 
poison. As his condition deteriorated, he was 
referred to a hospital at Bareilly, where he 
ultimately succumbed to the poisoning 
during the intervening night of 05/06.06.2026 at about 12:00–12:30 a.m.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the deceased died on 
05.06.2026, whereas the impugned F.I.R. 
came to be lodged only on 16.06.2026, after 
an unexplained delay of more than eleven 
days. It is further submitted that a 
registered Agreement to Sell had already 
been executed between the petitioner and 
the deceased on 08.08.2025 for a total sale 
consideration of Rs.1.4 crore, out of which 
an amount of Rs.80,00,000/- had been paid 
to the deceased through cheques. The 
balance sale consideration was agreed to be 
paid at the time of execution of the sale 
deed, and the parties had stipulated that the 
sale deed could be executed on or before 
20.12.2027. It is, therefore, contended that 
there was no occasion or necessity for the 
petitioner to pressurize or threaten the 
deceased. Learned counsel further submits 
that immediately after the incident, on 
06.06.2026, the cousin sister of the 
deceased submitted an application before 
the Station House Officer, Police Station 
Kiccha, alleging that it was the complainant 
and her brother who had been harassing the 
deceased with the intention of grabbing his 
property. According to the petitioner, the 
said contemporaneous complaint completely 
demolishes the prosecution case and 
indicates that the impugned F.I.R. has been 
lodged as an afterthought.
5. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the material available on 
record.
6. Having considered the submissions 
advanced by the learned counsel for the 
parties and upon perusal of the material 
available on record, this Court is of the 
prima facie opinion that the matter requires 
consideration.
7. Issue notice to respondent No. 4, 
returnable within four weeks.
8. List this case after receipt of the service 
report.9. Counter affidavit, if any, be filed by the 
respondents.
10. Till the next date of listing, as an 
interim measure, the petitioner shall not be 
arrested in connection with the aforesaid 
F.I.R., provided that he cooperates with the 
investigation and appears before the 
Investigating Officer as and when required.
11. The Stay Application stands disposed 
of accordingly.
