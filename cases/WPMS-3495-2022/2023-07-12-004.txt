Ms. Tanya Kapoor, Advocate for the

Mr. V.S.Rawat, Brief Holder for the 
State.
Instant petition was dismissed for non 
prosecution on 04.05.2023.
Now restoration application has been 
filed on the ground that due to personal 
reasons learned counsel for the petitioner 
could not appear because his mother was 
unwell.
The Restoration application is 
delayed by six days. A Delay Condonation 
Application has also been filed. The reason 
for the delay is that the counsel for the 
petitioner could not attend the Court due to 
personal reasons.
Having considered, this court is of the 
view that the grounds for delay are 
sufficient. The delay condonation 
application is allowed. Delay in filing the
Restoration Application (MCC No. 02 of 
2023) is condoned.
Having considered, the restoration 
application is allowed. Order dated 04.05.2023 
is recalled. The petition is restored to its 
number.
List this case on 03.11.2023 for final 
hearing.
