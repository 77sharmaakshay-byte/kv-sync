All the facts, which have been sought 
to be pressed by the respondents, by virtue of 
the Recall Application No. 15681/2021, were 
the facts, which were available on record at the 
time when the coordinate Bench of this Court 
had passed an interim order of payment of 
minimum wages to the petitioner on 
25.11.2020. 
It is not reflected that the applicant to 
the Recall Application had argued these 
grounds before the coordinate Bench of this 
Court. Hence, I do not find any reason to recall 
the interim order, as sought for by the Recall 
Application. 
Consequently, the Recall Application 
lacks merits and the same is hereby dismissed.
List this writ petition in the week 
commencing 12.04.2021 for final hearing.
