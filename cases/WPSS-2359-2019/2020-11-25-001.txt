WPSS No.2359 of 2019 
Hon’ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
 
 Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
 Mr. Sandeep Kothari, Advocate for the 
respondents. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner presses 
for interim order in the matter on the ground 
that in all the other connected writ petitions, 
there is interim order of the Court directing the 
respondents to pay minimum of pay scale to the 
petitioners. He submits that the controversy 
involved in these writ petitions is similar and 
prayed to grant similar interim order in his case 
also. 
 Though, the interim relief application filed 
by the petitioner was dismissed by this Court, 
vide order dated 29.11.2019, but as similarly 
situated persons have been granted minimum 
of pay scale by the interim orders of this Court 
passed in connected writ petitions, petitioner 
deserves similar relief. 
 Thereafter, as an interim measure, it is 
directed that the respondents shall pay 
minimum of pay scale to the petitioner. 
 Pleadings have been exchanged. 
 List for final disposal in the first week of 
January 2021, along with connected matter.
