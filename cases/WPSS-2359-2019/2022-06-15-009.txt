The petitioners of this bunch of writ 
petitions are claiming their respective 
regularisation under the Regularisation Rules of 
2013, the effect of which, has already been kept 
in abeyance in the proceedings which are sub 
judice before the Division Bench. The Division 
Bench is yet to decide the matter, about the 
validity of the Regularisation Rules of 2013.
In that eventuality, where by a judicial 
order the Regularisation Rules have been kept 
in abeyance, this bunch of writ petitions, where 
the respective petitioners have claimed their 
regularisation, cannot be considered till the 
matter is decided by the Division Bench. 
Hence, put up this bunch of writ 
petitions, as soon as the Division Bench decides 
the matter on its own merit, pertaining to 
validity of the Regularisation Rules of 2013.
