WPMS No. 886 of 2026
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. M.C. Pant and Ms. Anupriya 
Kukreti, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Ghanshayam Joshi, learned 
Additional Chief Standing Counsel for 
respondent no.1, 2, 4 and 5.
3. The instant petition has been moved by 
the petitioners against the award of the labour 
court dated 27.01.2025 passed in Adjudication 
No. 04 of 2024 (Smt. Puneeta Rani vs. 
Management of Sri Gurunanak Public Mahila 
Inter College and another) whereby the 
reference was decided in favour of the 
respondent/workman with the directions to 
reinstate her with all consequential benefits. 
The petitioners herein are challenging the 
award in part to the extent of consequential 
benefits and for the rest of the award there is no 
challenge since in compliance of the award 
offer has already been given pursuant to the 
officer letter dated 26.03.2026 to the 
respondent/workman to join the institution on 
the post of peon on temporary basis, copy of 
which is enclosed as Annexure No.15
4. It is argued by Mr. M.C. Pant that there 
is a complete ban on recruitment to Class IV 
posts and the Labour Court directed to 
reinstate with the all consequential benefits on 
the same status when she has at the time of 
induction in the institution but the respondent 
workman is claiming the benefit at par with the 
regular employee which is the beyond the 
scope of award.
5. Mr. Ghanshyam Joshi, learned 
Additional Chief Standing Counsel accepts 
notice on behalf of respondent 1,2, 4 and 5.
6. Issue notice to respondent no.3.
7. Steps be taken within a week.
8. Let the respondents may file the counter affidavit within three weeks. One week’s 
thereafter is granted to the petitioner to file
rejoinder affidavit.
9. List this case on 26.05.2026.
10. In the meantime, subject to the strict 
compliance of the impugned award dated 
27.01.2025 passed in Adjudication Case No. 04 
of 2024 the execution of order of Deputy 
Labour Commissioner dated 01.04.2026 
(Annexure-2) is stayed.
