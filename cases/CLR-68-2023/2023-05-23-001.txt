COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
23.05.2023 CLR No.68 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
A Counter Claim was filed by the 
revisionist-defendant no.5 in Original Suit 
No.123 of 2013, filed by respondent-plaintiff. 
According to the defendant no.5, he had 
constructed a house on the property-in￾dispute in the year 1975 and he is residing 
with his family in the said house. Therefore, 
the said property is no longer being used as 
an agricultural property. Defendant no.5 had 
moved an application before the Trial Court to 
frame an additional issue of Section 331 A of 
the Uttar Pradesh Zamindari Abolition and 
Land Reforms Act, 1950 (as applicable in the 
State of Uttarakhand). The said application 
has been dismissed by Trial Court vide 
impugned order dated 27.04.2023. 
2. Heard Mr. Naman Kamboj, learned 
counsel for revisionist-defendant no.5. 
3. Learned counsel for revisionist 
contended that in the facts and circumstances 
of the case, the Trial Court is bound to refer 
the question to Revenue Court after framing 
the issue of Section 331A of the said Act, 
1950. 
4. Admit. 
5. Issue notice to respondent no.1. 
6. Steps to be taken within three days. 
7. List on 20.06.2023. 
8. Till then, further proceedings of Original 
Suit No.123 of 2013 are stayed.
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
