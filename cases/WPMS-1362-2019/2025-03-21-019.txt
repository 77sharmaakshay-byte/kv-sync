5. Today the matter is listed on an Amendment Application (IA 
No.12020 of 2023), whereby the petitioner wants to add para no.18-A￾after para no.18, Ground No.F after Ground No.G and prayer No.II-A
after prayer no.III.
6. Vide order dated 22.10.2024, this Court has called for objection 
from the respondent nos.4 and 5 on the amendment application. Objections 
have been filed by respondent no.4 only and it is only objected that the 
amendment should have been incorporated earlier in the writ petition, but 
not at a belated stage. 
7. This writ petition was filed in the year 2019 while amendment is 
being sought in the year 2023.
8. Having considered the amendment application and the reason for 
the amendment as well as the objections filed by respondent no.4, this 
Court is of the view that the amendment sought to be incorporated are not 
touching the merits of the case at all and are only consequential.
9. Accordingly amendment application is allowed.
10. Let necessary amendment be incorporated by the petitioner within a 
period of 7 days by supplying amended memo of writ petition as per 
Rules.
11. After the steps have been taken, the respondents may file their 
counter affidavit to the amended part of the writ petition.
12. List this case on 28.04.2025.
