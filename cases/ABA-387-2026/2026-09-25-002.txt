2. The present applicant is seeking 
anticipatory bail in connection with FIR No. 
350 of 2025, under Sections 64(2)(m) and 
351(2) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 
and under Sections 5(1)/6 of the Protection 
of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012, 
registered at Police Station Jhabrera, 
District Haridwar.
3. As per the prosecution story, the victim 
was residing with the complainant, who is 
her grandfather, and the applicant 
committed sexual assault upon the victim 
continuously for the last one year prior to 
the lodging of the FIR. It has further been 
mentioned in the FIR that, on the night of 
12.12.2025, the applicant entered the house 
and sexually assaulted the victim, and when 
the complainant woke up, he fled from the 
scene.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that the applicant has been falsely 
implicated in the present case and that, 
even if the contents of the FIR are taken to 
be true on their face value, the incident as 
narrated in the FIR is highly improbable. He 
has further submitted that the complainant is in the habit of blackmailing, as he has 
lodged another complaint against some 
other persons alleging the same offences. He 
has further submitted that the applicant 
lodged an FIR on 10.09.2026, wherein the 
complainant, on phone, demanded 
₹50,00,000/- from the father of the 
applicant in lieu of taking back the said 
complaint. The father of the applicant 
recorded the said conversation and the same 
video has been handed over to the 
Investigating Officer.
5. Learned counsel for the applicant has 
further submitted that the charge-sheet has 
been filed and, pursuant to the lodging of 
the FIR, the applicant was granted interim 
protection during the investigation and has 
cooperated with the investigation. He has 
further submitted that there is no 
requirement for custodial interrogation after 
filing of the charge-sheet.
6. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
7. This Court finds some substance in the 
submissions advanced by learned counsel 
for the applicant. Having regard to the facts 
and circumstances of the case, this Court is 
of the view that the aforesaid aspects require 
deeper scrutiny.
8. Let the respondent-State file its 
counter affidavit within three weeks.
9. List thereafter.
10. In the meantime, as an interim 
measure, without expressing any opinion on 
the merits of the case, in the event of arrest, 
the applicant shall be released on interim 
anticipatory bail on furnishing a personal 
bond with two reliable sureties, each in the 
like amount, to the satisfaction of the 
Arresting Officer/Trial Court, subject to the 
following conditions:
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation and shall make himself 
available for interrogation as and when 
required by the Investigating Officer.
(ii) The applicant shall not, directly or indirectly, approach, influence, threaten 
or induce any witness in any manner 
whatsoever.
