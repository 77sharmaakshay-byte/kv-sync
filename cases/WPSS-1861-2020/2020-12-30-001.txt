WPSS No.1861 of 2020 
Hon’ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
 
Mr. T.C. Pandey, Advocate for the petitioner. 
 Mr. V.S. Rawat and Mrs. Indu Sharma, Brief 
Holder for the State. 
 By means of present writ petition, petitioner 
has sought a writ of certiorari to quash the letter 
dated 27.08.2020 issued by the respondent no.3 
(annexure no.1) whereby recovery of excess 
payment of ` 33,975/- has been initiated against 
the petitioner. 
 Heard. 
 Admit. 
 Counter affidavit to be filed within six weeks. 
 List thereafter. 
 Also heard on the stay application. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that by the order of Court dated 
06.07.2017 passed in WPSS No.1544 of 2015, 
which also stood confirmed by the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court in SLP preferred by the State 
Government, the services rendered by the 
petitioner as work charge employee in the work 
charge establishment was considered as 
qualifying service for the purpose of grant of 
pension and accordingly the pension of 
petitioner was fixed and retiral benefits were 
paid to the petitioner. He would submit that now 
by the impugned order the respondents have 
revised the family pension of the petitioner and 
has issued a recovery against the petitioner. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner further 
submits that there is no mention to recover the 
amount from the petitioner in the government 
order dated 04.02.2020, which has been made 
the basis for passing of the impugned recovery 
order. 
 Having considered the submissions of 
learned counsel for the petitioner, as an interim 
measure, it is directed that no recovery shall be 
made from the petitioner pursuant to impugned order/letter dated 27.08.2020, till the next date 
of listing. 
 Stay application CLMA No.11598 of 2020 is 
disposed of.
