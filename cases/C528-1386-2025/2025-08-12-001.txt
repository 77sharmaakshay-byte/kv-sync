COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
C-528 No. 1386 of 2025
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Avidit Noliyal, learned counsel for the Applicants. 
2. Mr. Rakesh Negi, learned Brief Holder for the State of 
Uttarakhand. 
3. The present Criminal Misc. Application under Section 
528 of the Bharatiya Nagrik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, has 
been filed by the Applicants with the prayer to quash the 
cognizance/summoning order dated 09.07.2025, passed by 
the learned Additional Civil Judge (J.D.)/Judicial Magistrate, 
Khatima, District Udham Singh Nagar, in Criminal Case No. 
81 of 2025, “State vs. Bheem Singh and Others”, under 
Sections 85, 115(2), 351(2), 352 of the B.N.S., 2023, and 
Section 3/4 of the Dowry Prohibition Act. The case is 
registered at Police Station Jhankaiya, District Udham Singh 
Nagar, and involves:
Against Applicant No. 1: Sections 85, 115(2), 351(2), 
352 of B.N.S., 2023, and Section 3/4 of the Dowry
Prohibition Act.
Against Applicant Nos. 2 and 3: Section 85 of B.N.S., 
2023, and Section 3/4 of the Dowry Prohibition Act.
Against Applicant Nos. 3, 4, and 5: Sections 85 and 
352 of B.N.S., 2023, and Section 3/4 of the Dowry 
Prohibition Act.
4. The Applicants have also filed an Interim Relief 
Application (IA No. 01 of 2025, Paper No. 40), supported by 
an affidavit, with a prayer to stay the effect, operation, and 
enforcement of the summoning order dated 09.07.2025, 
and the entire proceedings of the aforesaid Criminal Case 
No. 81 of 2025, “State vs. Bheem Singh and Others”.
5. Learned counsel for the Applicants submits that the 
present matter arises from matrimonial discord, as the marriage between Respondent No. 2 (wife) – Sugandhi is 
just about three years old. The learned counsel further 
submits that there are positive chances that the parties may 
arrive at an amicable settlement.
6. Admit.
7. Issue notice to Respondent No. 2.
8. Steps to be taken within one week.
9. List this case on 07.10.2025.
10. In the meantime, objections, if any, may be filed.
11. As an interim measure, it is directed that, till the next 
date of listing, the effect, operation, and enforcement of the 
summoning order dated 09.07.2025, passed by the learned 
Additional Civil Judge (J.D.)/Judicial Magistrate, Khatima, 
District Udham Singh Nagar, in Criminal Case No. 81 of 
2025, and the entire proceedings of the said Criminal Case 
No. 81 of 2025, “State vs. Bheem Singh and Others”, shall 
remain stayed qua the present Applicants.
12. The Interim Relief Application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
