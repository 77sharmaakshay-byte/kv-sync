COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 3478 of 1996, WPMS No. 22651 of 1997, WPMS No. 808 of 2002
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. D.S. Patni, learned senior counsel assisted by 
Mr. Karmanya Pandey, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Yogesh Chandra Tiwari, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State.
3. All these writ petitions have been nominated to 
this Bench on 16.01.2026 though prior to this a Second 
Appeal No. 66 of 1987 was came up before this Bench 
and on several dates matters were heard at length and 
after hearing at length the judgment was reserved on 
30.12.2025. After reserving the judgment in the Second 
Appeal No. 66 of 1987 these petitions have been 
nominated since the subject matter of these writ 
petitions are identical to the subject matter of Second 
Appeal No. 66 of 1987.
4. At this juncture it is also relevant to mention here 
that infact the subject matter of all these writ petitions 
as well as second appeal was remanded by the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court by common judgment and order dated 
18.05.2023 passed in Civil Appeal No. 4983 of 2019, 
Civil Appeal No. 4988 of 2009, Civil Appeal No. 4984 of 
2009, Civil Appeal No. 4985 of 2009, Civil Appeal No. 
4983 of 2009 and Civil Appeal No. 4989-4992 of 2009 to 
decide afresh and while remanding the earlier 
judgments of this court were also quashed. In fact after 
remand all these petitions including the Second Appeal 
No. 66 of 1987 should be listed together but were listed 
before different courts and this fact was not brought to 
the knowledge during the hearing of second appeal 
that all matters should be heard together.
5. Now, since all these writ petitions have been 
nominated to this Bench and judgment was already 
reserved in the second appeal, therefore, taking into 
consideration that the matter was remanded by the 
Hon’ble Apex Court, let the record of Second Appeal 
No. 66 of 1987 in which the judgment was reserved by 
this Bench on 30.12.2025 be also clubbed with thesepetitions.
6. Mr. D.S. Patni, learned senior counsel submits 
that the matter be listed after Holi vacation on which 
Mr. Yogesh Chandra Tiwari, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State, has no objection.
7. Consequently, all these matters are directed to be 
listed for final hearing on 16.03.2026 along with the 
record of Second Appeal No. 66 of 1987, wherein, the 
judgment was reserved on 30.12.2025.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
