2. Mr. Himanshu Sain, learned AGA, for the State

3. The instant criminal appeal has been preferred 
by the appellant – Mohammad Furkan S/o 
Mohammad Khalid (convict) against the judgment 
and order passed by the learned Additional District 
and Sessions Judge/Special Judge (NDPS Act)
Vikasnagar, Dehradun dated 29.07.2026 in Special
Sessions Trial No.170 of 2021, “State Vs. Mohammad 
Furkan” whereby the appellant has been convicted
for the offences punishable under Sections 8/20 of 
the NDPS Act, with the sentence of ten years 
rigorous imprisonment and fine of Rs.1,00,000/-. 
4. As per the office report, the appeal is well 
within time.
5. Admit. 
6. Summon the trial court record.
7. Heard on the bail application.
8. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
appellant that the appellant is 60 years old and has
no criminal antecedents and was on bail during trial 
and never misused the same. He further submits that 
the conviction is bad because at the time of search 
and seizure, though the inventory was prepared, but 
it was not certified by the Magistrate in the manner 
as provided under the Rules of 2022.9. On the other side, learned AGA for the State 
have not disputed that the appellant was on bail 
during trial and have no criminal antecedents.
10. On the previous date, learned State Counsel 
was directed to get instructions, whether the 
appellant during the trial misused the bail or not and 
today, on instructions, the learned AGA submits that 
the appellant never misused the bail. 
11. After hearing the submissions of the learned
counsel for the parties and taking into consideration 
that the appellant was on bail during trial and never 
misused the same and the age of the appellant is 60 
years, this Court is of the view that the appellant 
deserves for bail during the pendency of the instant
criminal appeal.
12. Accordingly without expressing any opinion on 
the merits of the case, the IA No.01 of 2026 is 
allowed. 
13. Let the appellant – “Mohammad Furkan”, be 
released on bail, on his executing a personal bond 
and furnishing two reliable sureties, each of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the Court concerned 
during the pendency of the appeal. The fine shall also 
remain suspended. 
14. List this appeal in the week commencing 
23.11.2026.
