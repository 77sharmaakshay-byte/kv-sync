COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.1284 of 2018
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Amar Shukla, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Ashutosh Thakral, learned counsel for the respondent.
3. At the outset, learned counsel for the petitioner contends 
that at this juncture what remains is only the matter of “back 
wages”. As per records, despite the assurance being given before 
this Hon’ble Court that respondent no.2 (workman) will be 
reinstated, and the salary will be paid accordingly. As per order 
dated 08.05.2018 still the respondent no.2 was suspended in the 
year 2021, and before his final termination in the year 2022, he 
continued to have received the pay under suspension. 
4. Learned counsel for the respondent draws attention to the 
order of this Court dated 08.05.2018, whereby learned counsel for 
the petitioner had given a statement that petitioner is ready and 
willing to re-instate the workman (respondent no.2), and the 
assurance was also made to this Court that necessary orders 
regarding reinstatement would be issued within ten days’ from 
today i.e. date of order 08.05.2018. Further it has been mentioned 
that he also assures the Court that respondent no.2/workman would 
be permitted to render the services in the factory of the petitioner.
5. To the above assurance, both the learned counsels for the 
parties have accented to the assurance of the arrangement so 
mentioned above, but as per contention of the learned counsel for 
the petitioner the respondent no.2/workman despite the pendency of the present matter has misbehaved and due to his misconduct he 
was primarily suspended and thereupon his services were 
terminated. Whereas learned counsel for the respondent refer to 
third paragraph of order dated 08.05.2018, wherein it is mentioned 
that “he further assures the Court that current salary would be 
regularly paid to the workman during pendency of the writ 
petition.”
6. Thus, paragraph 3 of the said order implies that till the 
matter being sub judice before this Court, the said order would 
work as interim order i.e. that the services of the respondent 
no.2/workman would be made as it is and secondly as per 
averment of learned counsel for the petitioner in such 
circumstances as the matter being sub judice before this Court still 
the respondent no.2/workman being suspended and ultimately 
terminated, did the workman respondent no.2 approach the labour 
court or did he apprise this Court of such condition because there 
is question still surfaces that during the period of suspension as per 
learned counsel for the petitioner, the respondent no.2 workman 
received the salary of so-called suspension.
7. Both the learned counsels are requested to apprise this 
Court the above queries.
8. List this matter on 19th June, 2025.
9. Urgency application (IA No.9173 of 2025) stands disposed 
of.
