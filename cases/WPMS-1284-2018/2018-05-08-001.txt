WPMS No. 1284 of 2018 
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. Gopal Narain, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
This is employer’s petition under 
Article 227 of the Constitution against 
award given by learned Labour Court, 
Haridwar. By the said award, learned 
Labour Court has declared termination of 
services of respondent no.2 w.e.f. 
28.02.2013 to be unjust & illegal and 
further provided the respondent no.2 is 
entitled to reinstatement with back 
wages. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
very fairly submits that petitioner is ready 
and willing to reinstate the workman 
(respondent no.2) and he assures the 
court that necessary order regarding his 
reinstatement would be issued within ten 
days from today. He also assures the 
court that the respondent/workman 
would be permitted to render services in 
the factory of the petitioner. 
He further assures the Court that 
the current salary would be regularly paid 
to the workman during pendency of the 
writ petition. 
In view of the aforesaid statement, 
effect and operation of the impugned 
order dated 03.02.2018 passed by 
learned Labour Court Haridwar, shall 
remain stayed till the next date of listing. 
It is made clear that in case of 
failure on the part of the petitioner to 
honour the aforesaid assurance given to 
this Court, this interim order shall 
automatically stand vacated without 
reference to the court, within four weeks’ 
from today. 
 (Manoj K. Tiwari, J.)
