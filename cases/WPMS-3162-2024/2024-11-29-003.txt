COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 3162 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Mahesh Chandra Pant, learned 
counsel for the petitioner and Mr. Devesh Bora, 
learned Brief Holder for the State.
2. By the instant writ petition, petitioner 
is praying for quashing of the letter of intent dated 
05.09.2024 (Annexure-1 to the writ petition) issued 
in favour of the respondent no. 5.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that for the purposes of grant of mining 
lease, the petitioner submitted his application on 
01.08.2019, whereas, the respondent no. 5 
submitted the application on 16.05.2023. It is 
submitted by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that as per Rule 9 of the Uttarakhand Minor
Minerals (Concession) Rules, 2023, which were 
framed by invoking the power conferred by Section 
15 of Act No. 57 of 1957, the first preference will go 
to the person, who applied first, but, here, despite 
the fact that petitioner applied in the year 2019, 
the letter of intent have been issued to the 
respondent no. 5, who applied in the year 2023. He 
submits that the issuance of the “letter of intent” in 
favour of respondent no. 5 is in complete violation of Rule 9 of the Uttarakhand Minor Minerals 
(Concession) Rules, 2023. Learned counsel further 
submits that before grant of LOI, the District 
Magistrate, Bageshwar by letter dated 10.07.2024 
forwarded the application of both the petitioner 
and respondent no. 5 to the Director by mentioning 
therein that the NOC of the land owners furnished 
by both the applicants are mostly of the same 
person, however, this aspect have not been dealt 
with by the Director and without application of 
mind and further without taking into consideration 
that the petitioner applied much earlier to the 
respondent no. 5, the LOI has been issued in favour 
of the respondent no. 5. 
4. On the previous date, learned State 
Counsel was directed to get instruction in the 
matter. Today, on instruction, he submits that the 
petitioner has not given the NOC in respect of 60% 
of land areas of land owners. This is also one of the 
pre-condition as per rule 5(10) of Uttarakhand 
Minor Minerals (Concession) Rules, 2023.
5. The submission as advanced by learned 
State Counsel is misconceived and unacceptable 
since there is no such reference either in the letter 
of District Magistrate dated 10.07.2024 or in the 
“letter of intent”, therefore, the submissions are 
outrightly rejected.6. Issue notice to respondent no. 5. Steps 
to be taken within a week.
7. Let the counter-affidavit be filed by the 
respondents within three weeks.
8. Two weeks thereafter, rejoinder 
affidavit may be filed.
9. List this matter on 08.01.2025.
10. In the meantime, till the next date of 
listing, the impugned LOI dated 05.09.2024 issued 
in favour of respondent no. 5 shall remain stayed.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
