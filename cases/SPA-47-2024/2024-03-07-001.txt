accepts notice on behalf of the respondents.
4. There is a delay of 20 days in filing the present 
Special Appeal. For the reasons stated in the 
application, the delay is condoned. Delay Condonation 
Application (IA/1/2024) is allowed. 
5. In the present case, the employee Jaya Rani 
Srivastava expired on 12.06.2019, and as per her 
nomination, her brother and nephew were the legal 
heirs, whom she nominated in the year 2011, i.e. on 
06.06.2011, as per her nomination. Her mother Smt. 
Savitri Devi Srivastava expired on 01.01.2022, as per 
the Death Certificate placed on record by the 
appellants-writ petitioners (Annexure No. 10).
6. Now the retiral dues have been denied to the 
appellants-writ petitioners, on the ground that they 
should get the Succession Certificate. Once the 
nomination was made in their favour in the year 2011, 
there is no reason for them to get the Succession 
Certificate, when Jaya Rani Srivastava was alive, and 
she expired after eight years of making the 
nomination. 
7. Counsel for the University will file an additional affidavit to show why this nomination made in the year 
2011 is being doubted by the University, when the 
employee was alive and she was in good health.
8. List on 01.05.2024.
