3. Petitioner participated in a selection 
for appointment as Assistant Teacher in a 
Government Primary School. Since he was 
not selected, therefore, he has approached 
this Court, seeking the following reliefs:-
“a) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
certiorari for quashing the select list dated 01-06-2026, 
issued by respondent no 3. for post of Assistant Teacher 
(Primary) in District Pithoragarh (Annexure No. 9 to the writ 
petition) ( pg 37-41)
b) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
Mandamus commanding and directing the 
Respondent/Appointing Authority to consider the 
candidature of the petitioner for appointment to the post of 
Assistant Teacher (Primary/Science Group) in Government 
Primary Schools in District Pithoragarh under the General 
Category, against the vacancies earmarked for the said 
category, pursuant to the advertisement dated 07.11.2025 
(Annexure No.6 to the writ petition), strictly in accordance 
with the petitioner's merit pg. 26-30).”UKHC010100702026

4. Learned State Counsel submits that 
as per clause (x) (i) of the advertisement, 
only persons who are permanent 
resident/domicile of Uttarakhand were 
eligible to participate in the selection; even 
though petitioner is having a permanent 
resident certificate from Sub Divisional 
Magistrate, Jaspur, Udham Singh Nagar 
in State of Uttarakhand, however, it was 
revealed that petitioner has also obtained 
a permanent resident certificate from 
Tehsil Dhampur, District Bijnor in State of 
Uttar Pradesh. Learned State Counsel 
thus submits that one cannot be 
permanent resident of two different States 
and petitioner suppressed this material 
information in his application form and 
presented himself to be a domicile of 
Uttarakhand. He submits that many 
candidates, who are having permanent 
resident certificate of some other State, 
besides State of Uttarakhand were found 
participating in the selection and the 
competent authority has taken a decision 
not to include name of such persons in 
the select list.
5. In view of the statement made by 
learned State Counsel, based on 
instructions, received by him from District UKHC010100702026
2026:UHC:7611
Education Officer (Elementary), 
Pithoragarh dated 12.06.2026, this Court 
do not find any reason to interfere in the 
matter. Every person has to meet the 
conditions of eligibility, as mentioned in 
the advertisement. No one can be 
permanent resident of two different States 
and this Court finds substance in the 
submission made by learned State 
Counsel that petitioner played fraud upon 
the competent authority in State of 
Uttarakhand for getting another 
permanent resident certificate, when he 
was already having such certificate from 
competent authority in District Bijnor, 
State of Uttar Pradesh.
6. Thus, the writ petition fails and is 
dismissed. Interim order, if any, stands 
vacated.
