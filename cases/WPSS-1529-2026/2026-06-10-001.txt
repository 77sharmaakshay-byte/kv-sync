WPSS No.1529 of 2026
Hon'ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Pradeep Kumar Chauhan, learned Counsel for 
the petitioner through V.C.
2. Mr. K.N. Joshi, learned Dy.A.G. for the State.
3. Learned counsel for petitioner contends that 
petitioner participated in the selection process for the 
post of Assistant Teacher, Government Primary School 
in District Pithoragarh. The selection was to be made 
on the basis of quality points secured by candidates. 
The petitioner submits that he belongs to general 
category and the total quality points to the credit of 
petitioner were 81.50. The petitioner further submits 
that a candidate of same category having 77.42 quality 
points was selected ignoring the petitioner who was on 
the higher pedestal than him. 
4. Learned State Counsel seeks 10 days’ time to seek 
instructions in the matter.
5. List on 30.06.2026.
6. In view of the facts and circumstances of the case, 
it is directed that one post of Assistant Teacher, 
Government Primary School (Science Group) under the 
General Category be kept vacant in District Pithoragarh.
7. Interim relief application (IA/1/2026) stands 
disposed of.
