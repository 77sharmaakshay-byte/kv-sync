IN THE HIGH COURT OF UTTARAKHAND AT NAINITAL
Second Appeal No. 125 of 2017 
Bijendra Singh Khatri …. Appellant 
Versus 
Hitendra Singh Khatri & Others 
 …. Respondent 
Mr. Bhupesh Kandpal, Advocate for the appellant. 
Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
Having heard the learned counsel of the 
appellant, it transpires that original suit no. 324/2004 
(Bijendra vs. Hitendra) was filed by the appellant herein for 
the cancellation of sale deed as well as injunction seeking 
restrainment of the defendants not to interfere in the 
possession. 
Preliminary issue was formed which was decided 
by the learned Trial Court vide order dated 24.8.2013 
wherein it was held that since the land was agriculture in 
nature hence, it was barred by the provisions of Section 
331 U.P. Zamindari Abolition and Land Reforms Act, 1950 
(herein after called as the Act), whereagainst the First 
Appeal 127/2013 was preferred. The learned District Judge 
affirmed the finding of the learned Trial Judge vide
impugned judgment dated 27.7.2017 whereagainst this 
second appeal has been preferred. 2
It has been argued on behalf of the appellant 
that the plaintiff and defendant no. 1 are the real brothers. 
Defendant Shri Hitendra Singh Khatri forged an 
unregistered Will of the mother Smt. Gaura Devi who died 
in 1978 and after almost 26 years of the death with the 
connivance of the Revenue Authorities, he got his name 
mutated. Such mutation never came into the notice of the 
plaintiff Shri Bijendra Singh Khatri (appellant herein). On 
the strength of getting his name mutated, he executed 
several sale deeds in favour of the respondent nos. 2 to 9, 
which could not have been done. 
Learned counsel has relied upon the law down in 
Horil vs. Keshav & Anr reported in 2012 (1) UAD 345
wherein it was held that the revenue courts neither 
equipped nor competent to adjudicate on allegations of 
fraud-courts really skilled and experienced to try such 
issue are the Courts constituted under the Code of Civil 
Procedure. 
That apart, the Hon’ble Apex Court expressed 
the view pertaining to the scope of Section-9 of the CPC 
wherein it was held that Civil Court has inherent 
jurisdiction to try all types of civil disputes unless its 
jurisdiction is barred expressly or by necessary implication, 
by any statutory provision and conferred on any other 
tribunal or authority. 
It was further argued that the property in 
question is no more agricultural in nature at all but all 
around there are dwelling residential buildings and the 
houses have been constructed by so many persons. 
Considering all these arguments, I admit this 
second appeal on the following substantial questions of 
law: 3
1. As to whether the Learned Appellate 
Court ought to have allowed the appeal and 
erred in law as provisions of Section 331 UPZA 
and LR Act are not applicable as the land is 
within the Cantonment Area. 
2. As to whether the learned Appellate 
Court ought to have allowed the appeal and 
erred in law as the Cancellation of documents 
can be done by the Civil Court only. 
Meanwhile, both the parties are hereby directed 
to maintain the status quo at the spot. They shall not 
create any third party interest on any part of the property 
in question, nor shall change the property in question in 
any manner, whatsoever. 
Issue notices to all the respondents. 
Steps be taken today itself. 
List this matter soon after the service is affected. 
Let the certified copy of this order be supplied to 
the learned counsel of the appellant, today itself, on 
payment of usual charges. 
 (Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.)
