Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Amish Tewari, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned Additional Chief 
Standing Counsel with Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned 
Standing Counsel for the State.
3. The undisputed facts of the case are that petitioner 
was working with the respondent-Industry Department 
since 1987 as an ad-hoc Assistant Manager, Industry. The 
petitioner was regularized on the said post on 05.07.2002 
and superannuated on 08.05.2017.
4. The grievance which has been canvassed by the 
petitioner before this Court is that he has not been paid any 
pension though his services were regularized in the year 
2002.
5. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that the petitioner is entitled to get admissible pension 
from respondent-State and denying pension to the 
petitioner would hit his right of life, enshrined under 
Article 21 of the Constitution of India.
6. Having considered the submission made by learned 
counsel for the petitioner and having gone through the 
record the undisputed facts which culled out from the 
pleadings are that the petitioner was regularized on 
05.07.2002 and retired on 08.05.2017. Thus, he fulfills the 
criteria of pensionable service of ten years.
7. In this view of the matter, the respondents are 
directed to pay the pension which is undisputedly 
admissible to him, during pendency of the writ petition, 
ignoring the fact that ad-hoc service of the petitioner is yet 
to be given credence to.
8. List this case on 20.06.2024.
