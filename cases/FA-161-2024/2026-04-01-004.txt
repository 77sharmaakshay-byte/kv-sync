Mr. Dheeraj Joshi, learned counsel holding brief of Mr.

2. Mr. Mayank Joshi, learned counsel for the respondent.
3. There is a delay of 625 days in filing the appeal.
4. Objections (IA No.2/2025) are taken on record. Misc. 
application stands disposed of.
5. Learned counsel appearing for the respondent 
opposed the prayer for condonation made on behalf of the 
appellant. He submits that delay has not been properly 
explained. 
6. Having regard to the facts and circumstances of the 
case and also in the interest of justice, delay of 625 days is 
condoned, subject to the cost of Rs.5,000/- payable to the 
respondent within ten days.
7. Accordingly, delay condonation application (IA 
No.1/2024) stands allowed.
8. Registry is directed to release the amount deposited 
by appellant in terms of order dated 03.10.2024 in faovur 
of the respondent upon her making an application. 
9. List on 23.04.2026.
