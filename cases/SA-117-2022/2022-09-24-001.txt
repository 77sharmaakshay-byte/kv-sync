SA No.117 of 2022
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Sudhir Kumar, Advocate for the 
appellant.
This is the second appeal against 
judgment and decree dated 27.08.2022
passed in Civil Appeal No.168 of 2018, 
Smt. Bhavee Gupta alias Mati Gupta Vs. 
Geeta Prasad, passed by the court of 
District Judge, Dehradun. 
Heard learned counsel for the 
appellant.
Learned counsel for the appellant 
would submit that though substantial 
question of law has been formulated in 
the grounds appeal but he restricts the 
substantial question of law only to the 
point no.1 and 3 raise therein, which are 
as follows:
1. Whether the learned lower Appellate 
Court has committed a mistake of law in 
passing the impugned judgment and 
decree without considering the fact that the 
defendants-respondents had not 
challenged the findings recorded by the 
learned trial Court against them while 
deciding their counter claim and those 
findings had become final in absence of 
any challenge to them and had the force of 
res-judicata?
3. Whether the learned lower 
Appellate Court has committed an error of 
law in not considering that the land of the 
defendants-respondents is situated in a 
different plot number and had different 
boundaries than the land of the plaintiff￾appellant. As such the defendants￾respondents had no right to interfere in the 
possession of the plaintiff-appellant?
Admit the appeal on the substantial 
question of law as detailed above.Issue notices to the respondent no.1 
and 2.
Steps to be taken within a week.
Call for the LCR.
Heard on Stay Application IA No.1 of 
2022.
Learned counsel for the appellant 
would submit that initially when the suit 
was filed in the trial court, an interim 
injunction was granted. Subsequent to it, 
the suit was decreed but now since in the 
appeal, the judgment and the decree has 
been upset, the appellant apprehends that
his rights may be interfered with.
Having considered, this Court is of 
the view that till the next date of listing, 
the operation of the judgment and decree 
shall remain in abeyance. 
 (Ravindra Maithani J.) 24.09.2022
