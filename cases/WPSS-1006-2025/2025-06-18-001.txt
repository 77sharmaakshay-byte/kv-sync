Heard.
It is the case of the petitioners that on 
05.04.2023, process for recruitment of 
Special Teachers (Primary Education) in 
District Champawat was initiated and16 
posts were advertised. The petitioners 
participated in the process and they were 
selected. Counselling/Document 
Verification was also done on 11.07.2023, 
but, thereafter, due to a litigation, the 
appointments could not be offered. 
The litigation in terms of a writ 
petition, which was then filed, had already 
been dismissed, but the petitioners have 
not been offered any appointment, instead 
now, by virtue of the impugned government 
order, the eligibility criteria for the 
completed recruitment process has been 
amended.
When requested to explain, learned 
State Counsel seeks time to get instructions 
in the matter.
List this matter on 24.06.2025.
