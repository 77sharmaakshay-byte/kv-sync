Mr. Yogesh Kumar Pacholia, learned counsel for

2. Mr. K.N. Joshi, learned Deputy Advocate 
General along with Mr. Maneesh Bisht, learned Brief 
Holder for the State.
3. Issue notice to the respondent no.4, returnable 
within four weeks.
4. Steps to be taken within three days.
5. Respondents may file the counter affidavit 
within four weeks.
Heard on stay application (IA No.1/2026).
6. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
petitioners that, though the petitioners have been 
working as contractual employees with the 
respondent-department for the last 29 years, they 
had earlier worked at ATI, Nainital, and have been 
working with respondent no. 4 since 2002 as Hostel 
Assistant and Cook, respectively. Petitioners pray 
that they be paid the minimum of the pay scale 
applicable to Group-D employees of respondent no. 4 
during the pendency of the present writ petition.
7. Since petitioners have put in substantially long service with the respondent-department, therefore, it 
can be assumed that the services of the petitioners
are required by respondent no.4 and are perennial in 
nature. Therefore, it is unfair on the part of 
respondent no. 4 to pay only Rs.11,000/- per month 
to the petitioners on the ground that they are 
outsourced employees.
8. In view of the above, it is provided that 
respondent no.4 shall pay minimum of the pay scale 
of the Group-D employees to the petitioners during 
the pendency of the present writ petition, and no 
adverse order shall be passed against them only on 
account of the fact that they have approached this 
Court by way of the present writ petition.
9. List on 16.11.2026.
10. Stay application (IA No.1/2026) stands 
disposed of accordingly.
