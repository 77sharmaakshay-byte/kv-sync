Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Ms. Prabha Naithani, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Devendra Pant, learned S.C. for the State.
3. This writ petition has been filed seeking a direction 
to the respondent to reconstruct/repair the existing 
pathway (kachha rasta) of about 330 meters situated in 
Kandoli Beet No.7, Dehradun.
4. Vide order dated 07.04.2026 State counsel was 
directed to get instructions in the matter.
5. Today instructions have been produced before this 
Court, which are taken on record, and on the basis of the 
said instructions learned counsel for the State submits 
that there is no pathway shown in the working plan of the 
forest area and no pathway falls within the reserve forest 
area. The M.L.A. Fund which has been issued to the 
Forest Division has, therefore, returned back to the 
District Development Officer.
6. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that instructions appears to be incorrect as the 
Department itself showed the pathway in the said area 
and it is only after submission of the inspection report the 
money was released for reconstruction/repair of the said 
pathway from the M.L.A. Fund to the District 
Development Officer.2
7. In such view of the matter, State counsel is directed 
to file a counter affidavit within four weeks.
8. List this case on 21.05.2026.
