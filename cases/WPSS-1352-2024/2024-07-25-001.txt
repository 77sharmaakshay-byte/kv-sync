4. Pursuant to the orders dated 
19.09.1991 and 12.07.1994 passed by 
the Hon’ble High Court of Allahabad at 
Lucknow Bench, petitioners were 
regularized vide order dated 
23.12.2002.
5. The issue falls for consideration 
before the Court is that as to whether 
the petitioners be considered 
regularized from 19.09.1991 or from 
the date of their engagement as daily 
wager whichever is later.
6. The respondent-State of 
Uttarakhand vide Government Order 
dated 15.02.2023 has granted all the 
service benefits to the petitioners like 
Time Scale, ACP/MACP, from the date of
their regular appointment, but, now, by 
the impugned order dated 20.06.2024
(Annexure No.13 to the writ petition), 
the said benefit which was extended to 
the petitioners, has been withdrawn.2
7. From the perusal of the impugned 
order, it is reflected that no reasons 
whatsoever is given in the said order 
and the benefit which has been 
extended to the petitioners, has been 
withdrawn, without issuing show-cause 
to the petitioners, which entail financial 
implications. 
8. It is the case of the petitioners that
they have been paid all the aforesaid 
benefits vide Government Order dated 
15.02.2023(Annexure No.12 to the writ 
petition).
9. In this view of the matter, 
respondents are directed to file counter 
affidavits within a period of four weeks.
10. Two weeks’ time, thereafter, is 
granted to petitioner to file rejoinder 
affidavit.
11. List on 23.10.2024.
12. Till next date of listing, the effect 
and operation of order dated 
20.06.2024 shall remain stayed.
