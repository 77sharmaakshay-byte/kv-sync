2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, A.G.A. for the State.
3. The instant criminal revision is preferred 
against the judgment and order dated 
13.05.2026, passed by the court of learned 
1st Additional District and Sessions Judge, 
District Haridwar in Criminal Appeal No. 49 of 
2025, Harendra Singh Vs. State of 
Uttarakhand and Others, by which, the 
learned court below has affirmed the 
judgment and conviction order dated 
19.03.2025 passed by the court of learned 
Judicial Magistrate-III, Haridwar in Complaint 
Case No. 173 of 2023, convicting and 
sentencing the revisionist for a period of three 
months simple imprisonment alongwith a fine 
of Rs. 10 Lakhs under Section 138 of 
Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881.
4. Heard.
5. Admit.
6. Issue notice to respondent no. 2, 
returnable at an early date. Steps to be taken within a week.
7. List after service report is received.
8. Meanwhile, LCR be summoned.
9. Heard on Stay Application No. 1 of 2026.
10. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
submits that another complaint was also filed 
by the complainant in respect of dishonour of 
cheque amounting to Rs. 1,98,000/- and that 
complaint was dismissed by the learned 
Magistrate by holding that the complainant 
could not prove his financial capacity. On this 
basis, learned counsel for the revisionist 
submits that both the courts below have not 
considered the fact that complainant had no 
financial capacity to advance loan of Rs. 7 
Lakhs to the revisionist, in respect of which 
the cheque was issued to him, during the 
same financial year.
11. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
further submits that the revisionist has 
deposited 20% of the fine amount before the 
Appellate Court and he is ready and willing to 
deposit the remaining 80% of the amount 
before the court of learned Judicial 
Magistrate-III, Haridwar within a period of six 
weeks from today. It is further submitted that 
the learned Magistrate while awarding the 
sentence, had added Rs. 2 Lakhs as fine in 
respect of which earlier complaint was 
dismissed.
12. Subject to revisionist paying 80% of the 
amount, in respect of the impugned cheque 
dishonoured i.e. Rs. 7 Lakhs, within a period 
of six weeks from today, further execution of 
recovery warrant issued against the 
revisionist, shall remain stayed till the next 
date of listing. However, it is made clear that if 
the revisionist does not pay the said amount within six weeks from today, then this interim 
order shall be automatically vacated.
13. Learned counsel for the revisionist has 
also filed an exemption application for 
exemption to surrender before the trial court 
pursuant to the impugned judgment and 
order dated 13.05.2026 passed by the court of 
learned 1st Additional District and Sessions 
Judge, District Haridwar in Criminal Appeal 
No. 49 of 2025.
14. Considering the grounds as taken in the 
affidavit filed in support of the exemption 
application, the Exemption Application is 
allowed. The revisionist is exempted from 
surrendering before the trial court pursuant 
to the impugned judgment and order dated 
13.05.2026 passed by the court of learned 1st
Additional District and Sessions Judge, 
District Haridwar in Criminal Appeal No. 49 of 
2025.
