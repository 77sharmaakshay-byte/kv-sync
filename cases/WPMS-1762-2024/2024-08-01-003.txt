Mr. Suyash Pant and Mr. J.S. Bisht,

Uttarakhand. 
2. Petitioners were earlier issued 
O.B.C. Certificate by Tehsildar, Sitarganj, 
District Udham Singh Nagar, on 
29.12.2020, however, when petitioner 
no.1 subsequently applied for O.B.C. 
Certificate on 23.05.2024, his application 
has been rejected on the ground that 
evidence furnished by him, with his 
application, is incomplete. 
3. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
submits that petitioner no.1 has been 
appointed as Constable (G.D.) in Assam 
Rifles against a post reserved for O.B.C. 
Category and if Caste Certificate is not 
issued in his favour, then his selection 
for the post of Constable (G.D.) would be 
cancelled by the Competent Authority. 
4. Learned State Counsel was asked to 
get instructions as to whether petitioner 
no.1 comes within creamy layer or not. 
Today, learned State Counsel, on 
instructions, submits that petitioner no.1 
does not come within creamy layer. 
5. Since petitioner no.1 was given an 
O.B.C. Certificate on 29.12.2020, 
therefore, this Court is, prima facie,satisfied that petitioner no.1 belongs to 
O.B.C.
6. As prayed, four weeks’ time is 
granted to learned State Counsel to file 
counter affidavit. Two weeks’ time, 
thereafter, is allowed for filing rejoinder 
affidavit. 
7. List on 14.10.2024. 
8. In the meantime, Tehsildar, 
Sitarganj is directed to issue provisional 
caste certificate to petitioner no.1 on or 
before 05.08.2024 and issuance of such 
certificate shall be subject to final 
outcome of the writ petition. 
9. Interim relief application (I.A. No.1 
of 2024) stands disposed of.
10. Let a certified copy of this order be 
issued today itself.
