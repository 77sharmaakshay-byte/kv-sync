Hon’ble B.S.Verma, J.
(Miscellaneous Application No. 9347 of 2011) 
 Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the record. 
 By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has sought a writ in 
the nature of mandamus commanding the respondents to consider and appoint 
the petitioner on suitable post in the Department on compassionate ground 
along with all consequential benefits. 
 The grievance of the petitioner is that his mother Smt. Mohani 
Devi was an employee of education department and was serving on the post of 
Head Mistress, Government Primary School Bhatora, P.O. Binta, Block 
Dwarahat, District Almora, who died in harness on 22-4-1985. The petitioner 
claims appointment in the education department on compassionate ground of 
in service death of his mother. Some correspondence was made between the 
petitioner and the department in that regard and the representation was lastly 
made by the petitioner on 7-7-2011, which is pending for disposal before the 
Director of Education, Uttarakhand, Dehradun-respondent no.2. 
 In the above facts and circumstances, the respondent no. 2 is 
directed to take a decision on the representation dated 7-7-2011 (Annexure No. 
9 to the writ petition) made by the petition in accordance with law, 
expeditiously within a period of eight weeks from the date of production of a 
certified copy of this order. 
 With the above direction, the writ petition is disposed of finally. 
 All pending applications also stand disposed of. 
 (B.S.Verma,J.)
