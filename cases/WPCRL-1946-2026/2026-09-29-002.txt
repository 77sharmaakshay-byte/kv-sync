2. By means of the present Writ Petition, 
the petitioner has sought quashing of the 
First Information Report dated 16.09.2026, 
being FIR No. 364 of 2026, registered at 
Police Station Gangnahar, District Haridwar, 
for the offences punishable under Sections 
109(1), 352 and 353(3) of the Bharatiya 
Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.
3. The petitioner submits that his mother 
has lodged an F.I.R. against the complainant 
and other co-accused in respect of the same 
incident. He further submits that no injury 
was caused in the incident. According to the 
petitioner, the complainant fired three shots 
at him, but he somehow managed to get into 
his car and escape. In self-defence, the 
petitioner also fired a shot, which hit the 
complainant's car. It is further submitted 
that, about ten days prior to the incident, 
the complainant had assaulted the 
petitioner and damaged his car, in respect of 
which an N.C.R. was lodged on 27.08.2026. 
The petitioner further submits that the 
complainant's intention can be gathered 
from the WhatsApp chats exchanged 
between the complainant and another co￾accused, namely, Krishna. In the said chats, the complainant allegedly threatened 
Krishna not to support the petitioner and 
acknowledged that the petitioner had 
somehow managed to escape on that 
occasion, but would not be so fortunate the 
next time.
4. Be that as it may.
5. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
6. This Court finds some substance in the 
submissions advanced by learned counsel 
for the petitioner. Having considered the 
submissions made and the material 
available on record, this Court is of the view 
that the matter requires consideration.
7. Let a counter affidavit be filed by the 
respondents within three weeks.
8. List thereafter and connect this matter 
with WPCRL No. 1989 of 2026.
9. Till the next date of listing, as an 
interim measure, the petitioner shall not be 
arrested in connection with the aforesaid 
F.I.R., provided that he cooperates with the 
investigation.
10. The Stay Application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
