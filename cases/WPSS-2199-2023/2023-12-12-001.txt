COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.2199 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Ms. Priyanka Agrawal, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Atul Bahuguna, learned Standing Counsel for 
the State.
3. By means of this writ petitioner, the petitioner has 
challenged the order dated 02.03.2023, whereby 
respondent no.2 has directed to recover a sum of 
₹1,74,112/- in 72 equal monthly installments from the 
petitioner.
4. It is case of the petitioner that the petitioner was 
appointed on 02.12.2002 with respondent and this fact has 
been further substantiated by the order dated 05.02.2009, 
wherein the date of appointment of the petitioner was also 
shown as 02.12.2012, but now the recovery is being 
effected from the petitioner on the ground that instead of 
02.12.2012 the petitioner was appointed on 01.03.2003
and excess payment was made to him by wrong grant of 
1st A.C.P. 
5. According to the learned counsel for the petitioner 
the 1st A.C.P. was given to the petitioner, on the basis of 
his appointment on 02.12.2002, after completion of ten 
years of service and now after lapse of a considerable 
period of eleven years, the recovery being effected from
the petitioner on the ground that the 1st A.C.P. should 
have been paid to the petitioner, on the basis of his date of 
appointment on 01.03.2003.2
6. From perusal of record it appears that there is no 
fault and misrepresentation on the part of the petitioner 
for grant of 1st A.C.P. to him and therefore the petitioner 
cannot be blamed for the same.
7. Learned counsel for the petitioner has placed his 
reliance on a judgment of Hon’ble Apex Court in the case 
of State of Punjab Vs. Rafiq Masih (2014) 8 SCC 883 
wherein it is stated that “such kind of action on the part of 
the State has been deprecated on the ground that without 
there being any fault on the part of the employees the 
recovery order are being passed.”
8. Learned counsel for the State prays for and is 
granted six weeks’ time for filing counter affidavit.
9. Further two weeks’ time is granted to learned 
counsel for the petitioner for filing rejoinder affidavit.
10. List this case on 14.03.2024.
11. In the meantime, no recovery shall be made 
henceforth from the salary of the petitioner, till the next 
date of listing.
12. Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2023) stands 
disposed-off accordingly.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
