3. The petitioner submitted a bid for a 
contract with the title name: Construction of 
Jawarnaidi to Bakswar Motor Road, under State 
Sector Constituency Jageshwar, District 
Almora. The bid of the petitioner has been 
declared as non-responsive on the ground that
the affidavit filed by him in respect of 
availability of a Pocklain Excavator was of a 
date prior to the date on which the bids were 
invited. The other ground is that the stamp 
paper for the said affidavit was also of an earlier 
date.
4. In the Standard Documents for
Procurement of Works, the requirement is that 
the bidder should demonstrate that he had
available either in his ownership or on lease 
hold basis, certain critical equipments for the 
work. In order to show compliance of the said requirement, the petitioner had submitted the
affidavit of Puran Singh, dated 11.07.2025. 
Puran Singh is the owner of Hydraulic Track 
Excavator (Pocklain) and by the said affidavit,
he stated that he had agreed to lease out his 
machine in favour of the petitioner-firm on a 
monthly rent of Rs. 1,50,000/-. 
5. There is no requirement under the 
Standard Documents for Procurement of Works
that the stamp paper or the affidavit should of a 
date anterior to the date, on which, the bids 
were invited. Moreover, for observing
compliance of the aforesaid conditions, prima 
facie, in our opinion, the only requirement was 
to examine whether requisite machinery was 
available with the bidder, in his ownership or 
on lease hold basis. The said condition was duly 
fulfilled by the petitioner by submission of the 
affidavit of Puran Singh. 
6. It has been submitted by the petitioner in 
the supplementary affidavit that his bid was for
Rs.2,73,65,423.10/-, whereas the bid of 
respondent no.4, who has been declared as L-1, 
was for Rs.3,34,43,988.75/-. The petitioner has 
also filed a recent affidavit of Puran Singh, in 
which, he has again stated that he would be 
hiring his machine to the petitioner for the work 
in-question at a monthly rent of Rs. 1,95,000/-. 
7. The value of the bid of the petitioner is 
almost 20% less than the bid of the private 
respondent. 
8. The petitioner has thus made out a prima facie case in its favour. 
9. Let notices be issued to the respondent 
nos. 4 & 5 by speed post. Steps be taken within 
a week. 
10. List in the week commencing 05.10.2026. 
11. The respondents are directed to file 
counter affidavit by the next date. 
12. Meanwhile, the State-respondents are 
restrained from settling the contract in-question 
in favour of any person.
