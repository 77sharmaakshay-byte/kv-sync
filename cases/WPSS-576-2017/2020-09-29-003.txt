 Review application (MCC 431 of 2020) 
alongwith Delay condonation application (CLMA 
7805 of 2020) has been filed seeking review of 
the order dated 11.04.2017. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that he may be granted some time to file 
objection to the delay condonation application. 
 To this, learned Standing Counsel for the 
State of U.P. would submit that respondent 
department is apprehending that the petitioner 
will pursue the contempt petition against him. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner makes 
statement at Bar that he will not pursue the 
contempt proceedings against the review 
applicant till filing of his objections to the delay 
condonation application. 
 In view of the above statement, two weeks’ 
time is granted to learned counsel for the 
petitioner to file objection to the delay 
condonation application as well as review 
application. 
 Till then, it is directed that the petitioner 
will not pursue the contempt proceedings 
against the review applicant. 
 List this matter on 15.10.2020 for hearing 
of the review application. 
 (Lok Pal Singh, J.)
