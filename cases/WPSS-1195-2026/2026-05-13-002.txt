2. Petitioner has moved this writ petition before this 
Court, seeking a writ of mandamus commanding 
respondent No. 1 to consider petitioner’s candidature 
for promotion on the post of Junior Engineer (Civil) 
under the 5% departmental promotion quota under 
Rule 5(b) of the Uttarakhand Public Works Department 
Subordinate Engineering (Junior Engineer, Civil, 
Technical, Electrical and Mechanical) Service Rules, 
2007, as amended.
3. This Court, vide order dated 05.05.2026 called for 
instructions from the State.
4. Today, the instructions are supplied to this Court, 
which are taken on record.
5. On the basis of instructions, learned State 
Counsel submits that since the petitioner possesses a 
three years’ Diploma in Civil Engineering from Janardan 
Rai Nagar Rajasthan Vidyapeeth, a Deemed-to-be 
University, obtained in the year 2016, the said 
qualification was not recognized in the State of 
Uttarakhand at the time when the petitioner obtained the diploma.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
the said qualification is recognized for the reason that 
University is a Deemed-to-be University and is 
recognized by University Grants Commission.
7. In view of the conflicting statement made by the 
parties, a counter affidavit is needed. Respondents may 
file counter affidavit within three weeks.
8. List on 11.06.2026.
9. In the meantime, the petitioner is permitted to 
participate in the promotional process. The result of 
the petitioner shall be kept in a sealed cover and shall 
not be opened without leave of the Court.
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
