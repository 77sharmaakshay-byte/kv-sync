Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, learned counsel for 
the applicant. 
2. By means of the present transfer 
application, the applicant seeks transfer of 
Domestic Violence Case No. 969 of 
2026,’Smt. Sampatti Devi and Another vs. 
Smt. Anupama and Another’ pending before 
the Ist Additional Civil Judge (Junior 
Division), Dehradun to Judicial Magistrate, 
Uttarkashi or any competent court of law 
situated at District Court, Uttarkashi. 
3. Learned counsel for the applicant has 
canvassed before the court that she is 
residing at village Bhetiyara, District 
Uttarkashi and she has to take care of her 
minor daughter along with her old widow 
mother. He also submits that the husband 
of the applicant is currently Lecturer in GIC, Lambgaon, Block Pratapnagar, District Tehri 
Garhwal. The difficulty as expressed in the 
present transfer application is that the court 
at Dehradun is about 150 km away from the 
residential place where she is presently 
residing with her minor daughter and 
commuting to and from to her present place 
of posting at village Deen Gaon, Block 
Pratapnagar, District Tehri Garhwal and the 
said distance could not be covered by her in 
one day on the date fixed if she would have 
to attend the said court then she will have to 
necessarily reach there one day before the 
date fixed and in some case on next day also 
which will cause heavy loss of time and 
money to the applicant. 
4. On account of the aforesaid grounds, 
the applicant has sought transfer of the said 
case. It is the wife's convenience which has 
to be seen and in the present facts and 
circumstances of the case, an interim order 
is called for.
5. Heard. 
6. Admit. 
7. Issue notice to the respondent. Steps to 
be taken by all the permissible modes within 
one week. 
8. As an interim measure, it is provided 
that till the next date of listing, the further 
proceedings of Domestic Violence Case No. 
969 of 2026, “Smt. Sampatti Devi and Another vs. Smt. Anupama and Another”, 
pending in the court of Ist Additional Civil 
Judge (Junior Division), Dehradun shall 
remain stayed. 
9. List on 25.09.2026.
10. Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2026) 
stands disposed of accordingly.
