Versus
State of Uttarakhand & others … Respondents
Mr. Pankaj Miglani, Advocate for the petitioner. 
Mr. Paresh Tripathi, CSC, for the State. 
Mr. Rakesh Thapliyal, Advocate, for respondent no.4. 
Mr. Lalit Sharma with Mr. Sanjay Bhatt, Advocates, for the Union of India.

Hon’ble Alok Singh, J. 
Dated: March 17, 2017
Hon. Rajiv Sharma, J. (Oral)
 The affidavits filed today in the Court by the 
respondents are taken on record. 
 In sequel to the directions issued by this Court 
on 2.12.2016 in WPPIL No.140 of 2015, Dr. Pravin 
Kumar, Director National Mission for Clean Ganga, Mr. 
Kumar Ajitabh, Project Officer (Legal), National Mission 
for Clean Ganga, Dr. Manoranjan Hota, Advisor, Ministry 
of Environment, Forest & Climate Change, Mr. Avinash 
Akolkar, Member Secretary, Central Pollution Control 
Board and Mr. Vinod Singhal, Member Secretary, 
Uttarakhand Environment Protection & Control Board, 
are present in person before the Court along with the 
records to assist the Court. 
 Mr. Praveen Kumar has undertaken before the 
Court that the Inter-State Council under Article 263 of 
the Constitution of India for the riparian States through 
which River Ganga flows down to the States of Uttar 2
Pradesh, Jharkhand, Bihar and West Bengal, would be 
constituted exclusively for rejuvenation of Ganga within a 
period of four weeks from today. 
 Now, as far as Direction Nos.‘B’ and ‘F’ are 
concerned, it is stated in the affidavit filed by the Union 
of India that 13 Projects with a total project cost of 
Rs.662.28 crores have been sanctioned including for 
Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs) as well as for 
Interceptions of Drains as per the judgment dated 
2.12.2016. A Hybrid Annuity Mode Based Investment 
Plan has been approved by the Cabinet in January, 2016. 
 Now, as far as the direction to frame a law 
exclusively for River Ganga is concerned, it is stated in 
the affidavit that the Ministry of Water Resource, River 
Development & Ganga Rejuvenation has constituted a 
committee under the Chairmanship of an eminent jurist 
Justice Sri Girdhar Malviya, former Judge of High Court 
of Allahabad for preparation of a draft Act on Ganga vide 
office memorandum dated 21.7.2016. The scheme/detail 
of the bill are under discussion/consideration as per 
Annexure No.III. 
 Accordingly, Sri Justice Girdhar Malviya is 
requested through the Union of India to prepare the draft 
Act for its earlier promulgation to save River Ganga. 
 It has also been brought to the notice of the 
Court by Mr. Praveen Kumar that out of 23 projects sent 
by the State Government, 16 Projects have been 
sanctioned and 7 are under pipeline. He also submits 
that 75 Projects have been undertaken for providing 3
crematoria as well as for beautification of Ghats. He 
further submits that 28 projects have started and for the 
remaining, the tender process is on and the same would 
be completed within three months. 
 The B.H.E.L. through its General Manager has 
undertaken to complete the Sewage Treatment Plant 
within a period of nine months. 
 Respondent no.2 –Uttarakhand Environment 
Protection & Pollution Control Board (hereinafter to be 
referred as ‘the State Board’) was directed to take action 
against 180 industries to whom show cause notices were 
issued including 106 industries to whom Closure Notices 
were issued for the year 2015-16. Accordingly, few 
industries have been closed down. Closure Notices 
issued to majority of the units have been withdrawn. The 
Court should have been apprised of this development. 
The Court shows its serious displeasure in the manner in 
which the respondent no.2 i.e. the State Board has acted. 
It was for the District Magistrate, Haridwar to ensure due 
compliance of the Direction Nos. ‘F’, ‘G’ and ‘H’. The 
State Government was directed to take action against 
respondent no.2 u/s 62 of the Act of 1974 by serving a 
notice. The notice has been issued to the State Board 
u/s 62 of the Act. The reply is awaited. The State 
Government is directed to take stern action against the 
State Board. 
 Respondent no.7 has also apprised the Court 
that the directions have already been issued u/s 18(1)(b) 
of the Water (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act, 1974 
on 14.3.2017 regarding prevention and treatment of 
sewage discharging into river Ganga and its Tributaries 4
for taking stern action against the officers/ officials of the 
State Board. Respondent no.7 –Central Pollution Control 
Board is directed to take prompt action against the 
respondent no.2-the State Board. The Central 
Government is directed to order the Central Pollution 
Control Board to perform the functions of the State 
Board in case of non-compliance of the statutory 
provisions as well as for not enforcing sufficient 
measures to protect river Ganga within three months. 
 Now, as far as Direction No.‘L’ is concerned, 
the State Board has issued notice to 5 Ashrams for not 
complying the directions as per Annexure No.14 of the 
reply. 
 Vide Direction No.‘M’, this Court had directed 
that no industry/ hotel/commercial establishment/ 
educational institution shall discharge untreated 
sewage/industrial effluents in River Ganga without its 
treatment. It is stated in the affidavit that the public 
notice has been issued in daily newspapers ‘Amar Ujala’ 
and ‘Dainik Jagran’ with an appeal that industries/ 
hotels/commercial establishments/educational institutes 
operating in the State of Uttarakhand shall ensure not to 
discharge untreated sewage/industrial effluent in River 
Ganga without its treatment. 
 District Magistrate, Haridwar is directed that 
all the industries/ hotels/commercial establishments/ 
educational institutions, discharging sewage/ industrial 
effluent directly in river Ganga be closed down within a 
week. The drains opening into the river Ganga are also 
ordered to be sealed and closed within one week. 5
 Now, as far as direction No.‘N’ is concerned, 
the State Government has directed the Member Secretary 
of the State Board to conduct a survey of highly/grossly 
polluting industries located on the banks of river Ganga. 
These industries, after the report of the State Board, be 
re-located. 
 The Court had directed that no person shall 
litter/defecate/urinate, in open, within a radius of 500 
meters on both sides of river Ganga. The State 
Government, in compliance, has promulgated “Anti 
Littering and Anti Spitting Act, 2016”. The Court 
appreciates the promulgation of the Act to save river 
Ganga. 
 Now, as far as the ban on plastic is concerned, 
necessary notifications have been issued by the State 
Government on 13.1.2016 and 29.12.2016. These 
notifications be implemented in letter and spirit. The 
District Magistrates, appearing in the Court, have 
apprised the Court that there are certain Units which are 
manufacturing the plastic carry bags in the State of 
Uttarakhand. The State Government is directed to act 
against these industries in accordance with law and to 
ensure to make the State of Uttarakhand a plastic free 
State. 
 The State Government has already issued the 
notification dated 7.3.2017 prohibiting the people not to 
use soap, oil and shampoo while taking a bath in river 
Ganga. Bathing of cattle in river Ganga is also 
prohibited. 6
 The State Government has already issued an 
order to ensure that there is no begging in the State of 
Uttarakhand. The State Government is recommended/ 
suggested to make suitable laws prohibiting begging in 
the State. 
 Now, as far as the provision for providing 
Skimmers is concerned, the State Government has taken 
up the issue and Mr. Paresh Tripathi, learned C.S.C. has 
assured that sufficient numbers of Skimmers would be 
provided at Haridwar and Rishikesh within a period of 
three months from today. 
 Pursuant to the directions issued vide 
Direction ‘V’, the State Government has issued a public 
notice in the daily newspaper ‘Amar Ujala’ on 25.2.2017 
and in ‘Dainik Jagran’ on 8.3.2017, not to permit the new 
industries based on Sugarcane, Pulp/Paper Industries, 
Distilleries, Textile Industries etc. The State Board and 
the SEIAA have been directed not to issue any CCA 
under Water Act & Environmental clearance respectively 
to new industries/commercial ventures employing more 
than 50 guests/Ashrams in compliance to the order 
passed by this Court. 
 It has come in the affidavit that 12 Clusters of 
Bio-digester Toilets have been approved, out of which 9 
have been constructed comprising 61 Male WCs, 56 
female WCs and 9WCs for different abled persons. The 
State Government is directed to provide at least 500 
Clusters of Bio-Digester Toilets in Municipal Corporation, 
Haridwar as well as Municipal Council, Rishikesh. 7
 The State Government has also issued a 
notification declaring the ‘River Conservation Zone’ vide 
Notification dated 28.2.2017 under the Uttarakhand 
Flood Plain Zoning Act, 2012. No construction shall be 
permitted within the “River Conservation Zone”. 
 Mr. Rakesh Thapliyal, learned Assistant 
Solicitor General, submits that the Comptroller & Auditor 
General of India (CAG) has undertaken the audit of the 
Projects from Uttarakhand to West Bengal. The CAG is 
directed to undertake audit on monthly basis to ensure 
that the funds are properly/judiciously utilized for 
rejuvenation of river Ganga. 
 It is made clear that while setting up the new 
Sewage Treatment Plants (STPs), the norms laid down for 
the State of Uttarakhand and by the Central Pollution 
Control Board by the Ministry of Environment, Forest & 
Clean Ganga should be followed, more particularly with 
regard to Fecal Colly, in letter and spirit. 
 The Courts places on record its appreciation 
for the sincere efforts made by the Union of India as well 
as by the State of Uttarakhand in implementing the 
judgment dated 02.12.2016 for rejuvenation of river 
Ganga. 
 The District Magistrates are directed to ensure 
that all the Hydro-Electric Projects operating in the State 
of Uttarakhand shall set up Sewage Treatment Plants 
within three months from today. 
 All the elected/non-elected representatives, 
officers/officials of the local bodies are directed to obey 
the orders passed by the District Magistrate towards the 8
implementation of this judgment. Non-compliance of 
these directions may call for taking action against them 
under the contempt jurisdiction. 
 The Court places on record its appreciation for 
the sincere efforts made by the Chief Secretary of the 
State of Uttarakhand, Dr. Pravin Kumar, Director 
National Mission for Clean Ganga, Mr. Kumar Ajitabh, 
Project Officer (Legal), National Mission for Clean Ganga, 
Dr. Manoranjan Hota, Advisor, Ministry of Environment, 
Forest & Climate Change and Mr. Avinash Akolkar, 
Member Secretary, Central Pollution Control Board, to 
implement the judgment. 
 Copy of this order shall be placed on their 
personal files. 
 Personal appearance of the District 
Magistrates as well as for the above-named officers is 
dispensed with. However, Dr. Pravin Kumar, Director, 
National Mission for Clean Ganga is requested to present 
in the Court on 30.03.2017 to assist the Court. 
Applications seeking exemption of the Officers, are 
allowed accordingly. 
(Alok Singh, J.) (Rajiv Sharma, J.)
