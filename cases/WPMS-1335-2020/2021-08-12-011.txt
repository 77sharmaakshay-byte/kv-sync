COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.1335 of 2020
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
(Via video conferencing).
Mr. Siddhartha Ranka, Advocate, with 
Mr. Yogesh Kumar Pacholia, Advocate, for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. M.C. Pandey, Additional Advocate 
General, for the State of Uttarakhand. 
After passing of the interim order, which 
was passed by this Court 12.01.2021, imposing 
certain conditions on the petitioner. The 
petitioner contends that he has complied with 
those conditions, and as a consequence thereto,
there had been the de-sealing of the premises 
and certain raw materials has already been 
released, but the finished product has not been 
released, and subsequent consequential order 
has been passed by the respondent, which has 
been placed on record, by virtue of the 
supplementary affidavit, where a denial of the
release of the finished product was made. 
Hence, the application has been filed seeking an 
appropriate directions for the respondents for 
release of the finished product, to this 
application, learned Additional Advocate 
General for the State, prays for and is granted two weeks’ time to file objection to it. 
Besides this, the petitioner is also 
directed to file an appropriate amendment 
application, putting a challenge to the 
subsequent order of 21.05.2021, whereby there 
had been a partial denial of the benefits 
accruing from the interim order of 12.01.2021.
Put up this writ petition in the week 
commencing 13.09.2021. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
