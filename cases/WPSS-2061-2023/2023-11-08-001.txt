4. It is the case of the petitioner that petitioner is the 
widow of Late Rodha Ram Sharma, who retired on 
31.01.1987, from Vigilance Department, Sector Dehradun 
from erstwhile State of Uttar Pradesh. Husband of the 
petitioner expired on 13.03.2020.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that 
pension of the petitioner has been revised, but due to 
mistake on the part of respondent, the letter of revision of 
the pension of the petitioner’s husband instead of being sent 
to respondent no.5, the said letter was dispatched to 
respondent no.4. Consequently, petitioner is not getting her 
revised pension by respondent no.3.
6. Learned counsel for the State of U.P. is directed 
either to show-cause or to pay revised pension to the 
petitioner within a period of three weeks’ from today.
7. List this case on 12.12.2023.
