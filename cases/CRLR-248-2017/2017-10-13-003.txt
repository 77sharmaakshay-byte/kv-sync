There is 342 days delay in filing the 
revision. 
Reasons furnishing in the affidavit 
filed in support of the delay condonation 
application is sufficient to allow the 
delay condonation application. 
Consequently, the delay condonation 
application is allowed and the delay in 
filing the revision is condoned. 
Heard learned counsel for the 
parties. 
Considering the submission 
advanced by the learned counsel for the 
parties, it is directed that, till the next 
date of listing, effect & operation of the 
order dated 16.06.2016 passed by 
learned Judge, Family Court Nainital, 
Camp Court Haldwani in Criminal Misc. 
Case no. 301 of 2015, shall remain 
stayed, provided the revisionist pays a 
sum of ` 2,500/- per month as 
maintenance to his wife on or before 10th
of every month during the pendency of 
this criminal revision. It is further directed that in pursuance of impugned 
judgment, no coercive steps shall be 
taken against the revisionist, provided 
revisionist deposits a sum of 
` 10,000/-, as arrear before the learned 
trial Court by 30.10.2017. 
In the meantime, trial Court is 
directed to decide the application moved 
by the revisionist under Section 126 (2) 
Cr.P.C. expeditiously, preferably within 
two months from today. 
Stay application as well as Misc. 
application stand disposed of. 
Urgency application is, accordingly, 
disposed of. 
Let a certified copy of this order be 
issued today itself.
