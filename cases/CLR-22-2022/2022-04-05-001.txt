05.04.2022 CLR No.22 of 2022
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
This proposed revision has been 
filed under Section 25 of the Provincial 
Small Cause Courts Act, 1887, against 
the judgment and decree dated 
03.08.2019 along with an application 
for condonation of delay.
Heard Mr. P.C. Maulekhi, learned 
counsel for the applicant.
The learned counsel for the 
applicant submitted that the matter is 
very urgent and requested to consider 
the Stay application. He further 
submitted that the lower court record 
should be summoned. 
Having heard the learned counsel, 
it seems appropriate to issue notice to 
the respondent on the delay 
condonation application before passing 
any order on the Stay application or 
before passing any order to summon 
the original record. 
Issue notice to the respondent. 
Steps to be taken forthwith. 
List this case on 11.05.2022.
Let a certified copy of this order be 
given to the learned counsel for the 
applicant today itself on payment of 
usual charges.
