Admit.
No notice is required to be sent to 
the respondent, as he is already represented 
by Mr. Sanjeev Kumar Sah, Advocate. 
Since the decree in question,
happens to be a decree, which has been 
rendered in a proceeding under Section 15 
of the Provincial Small Cause Courts Act, 
1887, as rendered in SCC Case No. 19 of 
2017, Suresh Chandra Vs. Diwan Singh Bora, 
which takes the shape of regular proceeding 
which has been put to challenge in the 
present SCC Revision under Section 25 of 
the Provincial Small Cause Courts Act. 
There would be an interim order to 
the effect, that the execution of the 
impugned decree dated 03.08.2019, would 
be kept in abeyance, subject to the 
following conditions:-
(i) The revisionist deposits the entire 
decreetal amount, as settled by the 
impugned decree, before the Judge, Small 
Causes, Haldwani, Nainital, within a period 
of six weeks from today.
(ii) The interim order would be 
effective subject to the condition, that revisionist continues to remit the monthly 
rent, as determined by the impugned order 
i.e. Rs. 2250/- per month, by paying the 
same to the respondent by 10th of each 
month.
Non-compliance of any of the above 
conditions would automatically result into 
vacating the interim order granted by this 
Court vide its today’s order and the decree 
would be put to execution by the 
respondent.
Let a certified copy of this order be 
supplied to the parties within 48 hours’ 
time, on payment of usual charges.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
