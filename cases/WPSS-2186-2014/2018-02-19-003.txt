The husband of the petitioner was 
initially appointed on 18.11.1967 at 
Hawalbagh Block. He was posted at 
Government Primary School, Kunhil and 
Government Primary School, Kunidhar in 
Salt Block. Thereafter, he was promoted to 
the post of Assistant Teacher from Primary 
School to Junior High School. He had given 
his joining at Block Dhauladevi. Thereafter, 
husband of the petitioner had worked at 
Junior High School, Totam, Block Salt, 
District Almora and he had also worked in 
Junior High School, Kanmungakichar, Block 
Syalde w.e.f. 16.02.1983. 
The case of the respondents precisely 
is that the husband of petitioner remained 
absent w.e.f. 18.05.1984. A notice was issued 
in daily newspaper “Amar Ujala” asking the 
husband of the petitioner to assume his 
duties. 
It is an admitted fact that the husband 
of the petitioner was the permanent 
Government employee. The services of the 
husband of the petitioner could be 
terminated only in accordance with law on 
the basis Rules framed under Article 309 of 
the Constitution of India. 
If it is the case of the respondents that 
the husband of the petitioner remained 
absent willfully, the regular enquiry was 
required to be instituted against him in 
accordance with Rules. 
2018:UHC:10840The mode adopted by the respondent 
vide letter 17.06.1996 whereby the services of 
the husband of the petitioner has been 
terminated, is not heard of. There is negation 
of the Rule of Law. The services of the 
permanent employee have been terminated in 
very arbitrary manner. 
The petitioner moved an application 
seeking family pension. The same has been 
denied to her on the ground that the 
husband of the petitioner has not completed 
20 years of services and the employee whose 
services are terminated is not entitled to 
pension and members of the family are not 
also entitled to family pension. The other 
ground taken in the reply is that there is 
delay of 30 years in seeking family pension. 
Needless to say that the pension is the 
continuous right. The widow has right to get 
pension on every first day of the month. The 
termination of the services of husband of the 
petitioner vide order dated 17.06.1996 is 
declared nullity. He would be deemed to be in 
continuous service for all intents and 
purposes till the date of his retirement. 
Accordingly, the writ petition is 
allowed. Respondents are directed to release 
and pay family pension to the petitioner 
within a period of ten weeks from today 
alongwith arrears with interest @ 6% per 
annum. 
Pending application, if any, stands 
vacated.
