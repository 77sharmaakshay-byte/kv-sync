This Court while considering the fact that 
the dispute happens to be a matrimonial dispute, 
had issued notices to the respondent no.2, for 
making an effort for mediation.
There are reports dated 03.04.2023 and 
12.05.2023, that respondent no.2, has been served 
with the notices. Firstly through her mother, and 
the second service has been made personally, but 
even today she has not put in appearance in order 
to participate in the mediation proceedings.
Hence, this C482 application is directed to 
be listed after three weeks.
Interim order dated 18.04.2022, would 
continue to operate till the next date of listing.
The applicant would be called upon to 
participate in the mediation process, subject to the 
condition the respondent no.2, puts an appearance.
