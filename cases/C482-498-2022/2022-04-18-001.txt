Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Bhuvnesh Joshi, Advocate for the

Mr. Lalit Miglani, A.G.A. with Ms. 
Sonika Khulbe, Brief Holder for the State.
The challenge in this petition is made 
to the proceedings of Criminal Case No. 
1257 of 2021, which involves offence 
under Section 498A IPC as well. 
It is argued by the learned counsel for
the petitioners that based on the similar 
allegations, a complaint was filed by the 
private respondent in a court at Aligarh, in 
which on 10.11.2021, the petitioners have 
been summoned to answer the accusation 
under Sections 498A & 323 IPC and 
Section 3/4 of the Dowry Prohibition Act, 
1961. 
It appears that, in fact, the complaint 
in Aligarh court was filed in the month of 
September, 2019, in which, cognizance was 
taken on 10.11.2021. In the instant case, 
FIR was lodged on 17.01.2020, which 
records an incident of 07.01.2020. 
Does it mean that after filing the 
complaint at the court at Aligarh, the 
private respondent started living with the 
petitioners? But, the summoning order 
passed by the court at Aligarh does not 
reveal it.
Having considered, this Court is of 
the view that this matter definitely requires 
deliberation. 
Issue notice to the respondent no. 2, 
returnable within four weeks. Steps to be 
taken within a week.
List thereafter. 
Till then, the proceedings of Criminal 
Case No. 1257 of 2021, State v. Praveen Kaushik and others, under Section 498A 
IPC and Section 3/4 of the Dowry 
Prohibition Act, 1961, pending in the court 
of Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Haridwar shall remain in abeyance.
