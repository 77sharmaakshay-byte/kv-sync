Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The proposed First Appeal has been filed 
along with an Application under Section 5 of 
the Limitation Act, 1963 to condone the delay 
of 295 days.
2. Mr. I.P. Kohli, learned counsel for the 
applicants – proposed appellants and Mr. 
Neeraj Garg, learned counsel for the 
respondents.
3. Heard on the Delay Condonation 
Application (IA No.01 of 2023). 
4. Perused and gone through the record.
5. Having regard to the totality of the facts 
and circumstances of the case and the cause 
shown by the applicants, through the 
affidavit, this Court is inclined to hold that the 
cause shown by the applicants for condoning 
the delay in filing the First Appeal, is a 
sufficient cause within the meaning of Section 
5 of the Limitation Act, 1963. Therefore, the 
application filed for delay condonation 
deserves to be allowed. The Delay 
Condonation Application (IA No.01 of 2023) is 
allowed. The delay of 295 days’ is condoned.
6. The First Appeal is admitted.7. On the joint request of learned counsel 
for both the parties, list on 22.05.2024 for 
hearing on the Stay Application (IA No.02 of 
2023).
