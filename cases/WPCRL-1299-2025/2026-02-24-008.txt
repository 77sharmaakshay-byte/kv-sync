COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPCRL No. 1299 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Suhaas Ratna Joshi and Ms. Bhavya Sharma, 
learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Deputy A.G. with Mr. 
Bhaskar Chandra Joshi, learned A.G.A. and Mrs. Sweta 
Badola Dobhal, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. The statutory committee refused to release the 
petitioner simply for the reason that he is a convict of a 
heinous crime of murder of a minor child for ransom.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner argued that the 
reasoning of the committee is completely contrary to 
Rule 5 of 2022 policy i.e. Uttarakhand State (For 
Sentence Pardon/Premature Release of Convicted 
Prisoners Punished with Sentence of Imprisonment for 
Life by the Court) Permanent Policy, 2022 as notified 
on 25.11.2022.
5. In support of his argument he gives reference of 
the judgment passed by the Division Bench in the case 
of Suo Moto PIL: In the matter of Premature release of 
the convicts vs. Principal Secretary, Home, 
Government of Uttarakhand and Others i.e. WPPIL 
No. 50 of 2024 dated 27.09.2024. On perusal of 
paragraph 18, which contains the list of 34 inmates 
including the petitioner mentioned at S.No. 11 reveals 
that out of 34 inmates only one case of Sundar Singh 
S/o Har Singh, which is seen at S.No. 25 fall under the 
prohibited category in terms of Rule 5(iv) of 2022 
Policy.
6. In this judgment of Division Bench comes to the 
conclusion that the committee has rightly not 
recommended Sundar Singh for premature release and 
then further observed that since 33 inmates do not fall 
under the prohibited category as per Rule 5 of 2022 
Policy, hence, they are eligible for premature release. 
Finally the Division Bench directed the committee to 
consider the matter.
7. The question is once the Division Bench clearly 
observed that 33 inmates including the petitioner does not fall under the prohibited category and found to be 
eligible for premature release how the committee took 
a contrary view by taking into consideration that the 
petitioner was involved in a heinous crime. Prima-facie 
the reasoning as given by the committee is contrary to 
the judgment passed by the Division Bench of this 
court as well as contrary to Rule 5(iv) of 2022 Policy.
8. Mr. Virk pointed out that the provisions has been 
incorporated under the Jail Manual, which is in 
consonance with 2022 policy i.e. Uttarakhand Jail 
Manual, 2023.
9. Let the committee take a fresh decision strictly in 
the light of the observation of the Division Bench as 
well in terms of Rule 5 of the Policy as well as Jail 
Manual of 2023 and then apprise to this court by 
27.02.2026.
10. List this matter on 27.02.2026.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
