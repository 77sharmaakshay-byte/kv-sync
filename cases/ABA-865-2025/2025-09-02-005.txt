COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
02.09.2025 ABA No.865 of 2025
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The applicant no.1 Sahil Ahmad has 
filed an Application (Miscellaneous 
Application No.01 of 2025) for 
permission to go to Canada for 
employment. He has passport and he 
has been granted visa to travel to 
Canada. 
2. Heard Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, 
learned counsel (through video 
conferencing) for applicants and Mr. 
Tumul Nainwal, learned Assistant 
Government Advocate for respondent. 
3. The Investigating Officer was 
requested to be present through video 
conferencing to clarify whether the 
applicant Sahil Ahmad is involved in any 
other case or not, and objection to the 
Application, if any. 
4. Mr. Prahalad Bisht, the 
Investigating Officer, is present through 
video conferencing. He has submitted 
that a charge-sheet has already been 
filed in FIR No.124 of 2022, but 
statement of the applicant Sahil Ahmad and his interrogation are required in the 
present matter. He further submitted 
that he will take the statements of the 
applicant and complete the interrogation 
by 04.09.2025. 
5. Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, Advocate, 
on instructions, submitted that the 
applicant Sahil Ahmad shall appear 
before the Investigating Officer for his 
statement and interrogations. 
6. Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, Advocate, 
submitted that the applicant has been 
acquitted of the offence under Sections 
323, 354, 354C, 376 read with Section 
511, 377, 379A and Section 506 of the 
Indian Penal Code, 1860 in the matter of 
FIR No.203 of 2021, registered at Police 
Station Yamunanagar, Haryana and the 
proceedings arising from the First 
Information Report No.124 of 2022 are 
stayed in C482 No.2475 of 2023. 
7. Applicant no.1 is directed to clarify 
the following facts through his affidavit:-
(i) Whether any appeal is pending 
against his acquittal?
(ii) Whether permission has been 
obtained in the said stayed case. 
8. Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, Advocate, has sought time to clarify the said 
circumstances and to file an affidavit of 
the father of the applicant no.1.
9. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned 
Assistant Government Advocate, has also 
asked two days’ time to ascertain if any 
other cases are registered/pending 
against the applicant no.1.
10. Time is granted. 
11. On the request of learned counsel 
for the parties, list on 08.09.2025 as a 
first case. 
12. The Investigating Officer is directed 
to appear through video conferencing 
before this Court on 08.09.2025 at 
10:30 a.m.
