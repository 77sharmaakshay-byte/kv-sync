The present Application has been filed by the 
applicants seeking anticipatory bail in Case Crime 
No.268 of 2025, registered at Police Station Bazpur, 
District Udham Singh Nagar under Sections 406, 420, 
464, 465, 467, 468 and Section 471 of the Indian Penal 
Code, 1860.
2. Heard Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, learned 
counsel for the applicants and Mr. Tumul Nainwal, 
learned Assistant Government Advocate for the 
respondent.
3. Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, Advocate submitted 
that the applicant no.1 is the husband of the informant 
and the applicant no.2 is the father of the applicant 
no.1. An Application was filed by the informant against 
1the applicant no.1 under Section 12 of the Protection of 
Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005. A copy of 
Income Tax Return of the informant was filed by the 
applicant no.1 in the said case. The present FIR has 
been lodged by the informant on 02.07.2025 alleging 
that the said Income Tax Return is false and forged. 
Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, Advocate further submitted 
that the said allegations are totally false.
4. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, Assistant Government 
Advocate, on instructions, has sought three weeks’ 
time to file objection to the anticipatory bail application.
5. Time is granted. 
6. Having heard the learned counsel for the 
parties and in the facts and circumstances of the case, 
as an ad-interim measure, it is directed that in the 
event of arrest of the applicants Sahil Ahmad and Tahir 
Ahmad, they shall be released on interim bail on 
executing a personal bond of Rs.30,000/- and two 
reliable sureties, each of the like amount, by each one 
of them, to the satisfaction of the Investigating 
Officer/Arresting Officer, subject to the following 
conditions:-
(i) Applicants shall cooperate with the 
Investigating Agency and they shall make 
themselves available for interrogation by a 
police officer as and when required; 
(ii) Applicants shall not directly or indirectly 
make any inducement, threat or promise to 
2any person acquainted with the facts of this 
case.
(iii) Applicants shall not leave the country without 
the previous permission of this Court. 
7. List on 03.09.2025 for hearing on the 
anticipatory bail application.
