Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma,J.
Apprehending their arrest, the applicant no.1 Sahil 
Ahmad and applicant no.2 Tahir Ahmad have filed the 
present application for anticipatory bail in Case Crime 
No.268 of 2025, registered at Police Station Bajpur, District 
Udham Singh Nagar under Sections 406, 420, 464, 465, 
467, 468 and Section 471 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860. 
2. The applicants have been granted interim bail on 
the following conditions:-
(i) Applicants shall cooperate with the Investigating 
Agency and they shall make themselves available 
for interrogation by a police officer as and when 
required; 2
(ii) Applicants shall not directly or indirectly make any 
inducement, threat or promise to any person 
acquainted with the facts of this case;
(iii) Applicants shall not leave the country without the 
previous permission of this Court. 
3. The present Application (Miscellaneous Application 
No.01 of 2025) has been filed by the applicant no.1 Sahil 
Ahmad for permission to go to Canada for employment. He 
has filed a latter of engagement, issued by Infosys Company 
Ltd., Bengaluru, Karnataka (head office). His assignment in 
Canada is till 26.11.2026. 
4. Heard Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, learned counsel for 
the applicants and Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned Assistant 
Government Advocate for respondent. 
5. Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, Advocate, appearing for 
the applicant no.1 Sahil Ahmad submitted that the applicant 
has a valid passport and he has been granted Visa to travel 
to Canada and necessary documents in regard to the travel 
to Canada have already been handed over to the 
investigating officer on 29.08.2025. His statements have 
already been recorded by the investigating officer. The 
applicant no.2 Tahir Ahmad, the co-accused and the father 
of the applicant no.1 Sahil Ahmad, has filed his affidavit 
dated 03.09.2025. He undertook in his said affidavit that he 
will appear before the concerned investigating officer in case 
if any document or statement is required in absence of his 3
son and he also undertook to cooperate with the 
investigation. Applicant no.1 Sahil Ahmad has also 
undertaken through his affidavit dated 29.08.2025 that in 
case of any requirement of documents, his father shall 
furnish the same. Applicant no.1 Sahil Ahmad further 
undertook to not extend his stay without previous 
permission of this Court and the applicant is ready to abide 
by any condition if imposed by this Court. 
6. Mr. Prahalad Bisht, the Investigating Officer, is 
present through video conferencing. He submitted that he 
has recorded the statements of the applicant Sahil Ahmad 
and interrogations of the applicant have been concluded.
7. Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, Advocate, submitted that 
the applicant has been acquitted of the offence under 
Sections 323, 354, 354C, 376 read with Section 511, 377, 
379A and Section 506 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 in the 
matter of FIR No.203 of 2021, registered at Police Station 
Yamunanagar, Haryana and no appeal is pending against his 
acquittal. He further submitted that the proceedings arising 
from the First Information Report No.124 of 2022 are stayed 
in C482 No.2475 of 2023 and one case under the provisions
of the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 
2005 is pending before the Court of Judicial Magistrate, 
Bajpur, District Udham Singh Nagar. 4
8. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned Assistant Government 
Advocate appearing for the State submitted on instructions
that no other case is registered/pending against the 
applicant Sahil Ahmad. 
9. Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, Advocate, contended that 
if the applicant is not permitted to travel Canada for his 
employment, he will lose his job and the same will cause 
irreparable injury and hardship to the applicant. 
10. The right of an accused to travel abroad is not 
absolute and is subject to restrictions to ensure justice is not 
evaded. 
11. Keeping in view the facts and circumstances, the 
applicant Sahil Ahmad is allowed to go Canada for his 
employment till 26.11.2026, subject to the following 
conditions:-
(i) Applicant shall furnish his personal bond and two 
sureties in the sum of Rs.40,000/- each to the 
satisfaction of the trial court for ensuring his 
return and appearance before this Court and the 
trial court; 
(ii) Applicant shall not visit any other country during 
the said period without previous permission of this 
Court, if it requires, or trial court;
(iii) Applicant shall return and appear before the 
concerned court and would not stay further in 5
Canada without previous permission of the 
concerned court; 
(iv) During the period of the applicant being abroad, 
he shall be permitted to appear through his 
counsel and he would not be entitled to raise any 
objection that the evidence had been taken or 
other proceedings were completed in his absence;
(v) Applicant shall be present in-person as and when 
required by this Court or by the trial court;
(vi) Applicant shall disclose about his mobile cellular
number and his e-mail ID and full address in 
Canada;
(vii) Before going to Canada, the applicant shall inform 
all the Courts/authority where his cases/
proceedings are pending and if any order is 
passed by those Courts/authority, it will comply 
with those orders;
(viii) Applicant shall abide by his other undertakings. 
12. The present Application (Miscellaneous Application 
No.01 of 2025) is disposed of in the aforesaid terms. 
13. List on 25.09.2025 for hearing on the Anticipatory 
Bail Application.6
14. Let a certified copy of this order be supplied to the 
learned counsel for the parties, today itself, as per rules.
