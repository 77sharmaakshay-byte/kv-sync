The petitioner seeks quashing of 
Promotion order dated 19.10.2022, with 
other related reliefs.
Heard.
Learned Senior Advocate for the 
petitioner would submit that impleading 
the petitioner, certain writ petitions 
(WPSS Nos. 1199, 1174 and 1182 of 
2022) (“the writ petitions”) have been filed 
in this Court, which were decided on 
31.08.2022, with the directions to the 
concerned respondent to declare their 
result and if the petitioners are found 
suitable/eligible for promotion, then issue 
such orders.
It is the case of the petitioner that, in 
fact, the petitioner was eligible. He ought 
to have been made a party in those writ 
petitions. When the petitioner came to 
know about it, he filed a Review 
Application (MCC No.2 of 2023) to the writ 
petitions, which was rejected on 
28.04.2023. In fact, it is a kind of 
connected litigation.
Having heard, this Court is of the 
view that this matter requires 
deliberations.Admit.
State takes notice for the respondent 
nos. 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, and 7.
Issue notices to the respondent no.5.
Let State file counter affidavit within 
four weeks.
Two weeks thereafter, rejoinder 
affidavit, if any, may be filed.
List this matter on 19.08.2023.
Heard on Interim Relief Application 
IA No.1 of 2023.
The interim application shall be 
decided after hearing the respondents.
