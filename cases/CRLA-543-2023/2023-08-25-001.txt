2. This is an Appeal against conviction 
filed under Section 374 (2) Cr.P.C. The 
appellant has challenged the judgment & 
order dated 20.06.2023 passed by learned 
Special Sessions Judge (POCSO), District 
Pithoragarh in Special Sessions Trial No. 14
of 2021, whereby appellant has been 
convicted under Section 363, 366-A, 
376(2)(i)(3) I.P.C. and Section 3(1)(xii) of 
Scheduled Castes and Schedule Tribes 
(Prevention of Atrocities) Act and was 
awarded maximum punishment of 20 years 
rigorous imprisonment with fine of 
` 25,000/-. The fine was also awarded 
under the other sections, in which appellant 
was convicted. The appellant has sought his 
release on bail.
3. As per office report, there is delay of 3
days in filing this Appeal.
4. Learned State Counsel fairly submits 
that he do not oppose the prayer for 
condonation of delay and submits that the 
delay may be condoned. 
5. Accordingly, the delay condonation 
application is allowed and the delay in filing the Appeal is condoned. 
6. Admit.
