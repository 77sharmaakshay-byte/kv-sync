UKHC010169072026
WPSS No.2478 of 2026
Saroj Kumari Gupta ………Petitioner
Vs.
State of Uttarakhand and others ………Respondents
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Vinod Tiwari and Mr. Vinay Bhushan Pathak, 
learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned B.H. for the State.
3. Petitioner is aggrieved by illegal recovery of ₹8,51,106/-
from her retiral dues without any valid reason and without 
giving any notice to her on the ground of excess payment.
4. The petitioner is a Class III employee who retired from 
the post of Senior Nursing Officer on 31.03.2024.
5. The aforesaid amount was deducted from her retiral dues 
on the ground that some excess payment was made to her 
during her service.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner contends that the said 
recovery cannot sustain in view of the judgment rendered by 
the Apex Court in the case of State of Punjab Versus Rafiq 
Masih, reported in 2015 (4) SCC 334.
7. Since the amount has already been recovered from the 
petitioner no case is made out for interim order at this stage.
8. Learned counsel for the State may file counter affidavit 
within three weeks.
9. List this case on 30.10.2026.
