COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRLR No. 364 of 2026
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Gaurav Singh, learned counsel for the 
revisionist.
2. The instant revision has been moved by the 
revisionist Gyan Prakash Dikshit, who married with 
respondent no. 1 on 30.11.2014 and from their marriage 
there are two children, one is the son, other one is the 
daughter, against the order of Principal Judge, Family 
Court, whereby, the interim maintenance of Rs. 
14,000/- has been granted.
3. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
revisionist that the respondent-wife is the teacher in a 
private institution and getting salary of Rs. 15,000/-
and the revisionist and his parents as well as the 
respondents are residing in the same house and already 
paying all expenses of his children. 
4. As per office report, the revision is well within 
time.
5. Admit.
6. Put up this matter on 04.06.2026.
7. On that day both the parties may join the 
proceeding through V.C. along with the children so 
that the efforts may be made for their amicable 
settlement.
8. In the meantime, the order impugned passed by 
the Principal Judge, Family Court, dated 24.02.2026 
passed in Misc. Case No. 248 of 2025 shall remain 
stayed provided the revisionist shall regularly pay a 
sum of Rs. 8,000/- per month.
