COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.692 of 2023
With
Stay Application IA No.1 of 2023
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Shobhit Saharia, Advocate for 
the petitioners.
Mr. P.S. Bisht, Additional C.S.C. for 
the State.
The challenge in this petitions is 
made to order dated 19/20.04.202 
(Annexure No.1) and consequential 
orders, which require reassessment of 
Seniority of the respondent nos. 5, 6, 7, 
and 8.
Heard.
Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that, in fact, the private 
respondents were appointed in the year 
2006-2007. Earlier, for the year 2015-16, 
a final seniority list was prepared, which 
is Annexure-11, against which a writ 
petition (WPSS No.3067 of 2018) was filed 
in this Court, which was decided on 
04.09.2018, with the direction to the 
District Magistrate to consider the
representations and decide them 
accordingly. The representations were 
filed and rejected. Again, for the year 
2021-22, Seniority List was prepared, 
which, after inviting objections, attained 
finality. But, it is argued that without any 
reasons, the chapter of Seniority has been 
reopened by the impugned orders. 
Reference has been made to the Seniority 
Lists prepared for the year 2015-16 and 
2021-22, the objections and 
representations, which were considered by the competent authority and the order of 
this Court passed in WPSS No.3067 of 
2018.
Having heard, this Court is of the 
view that this matter requires 
deliberations.
Admit.
State takes notice for the respondent 
nos. 1 to 4.
Issue notices to the respondent nos. 
5, 6, 7, and 8.
Let State file counter affidavit within 
four weeks.
Two weeks thereafter, rejoinder 
affidavit, if any, may be filed.
List this matter on 04.07.2023.
Heard on Stay Application IA No.1 of 
2023.
It is the positive case of the 
petitioners that the Seniority had already 
attained finality twice, i.e., for the year 
2015-16 and 2021-22. In fact, it is argued 
that when the Seniority List for the year 
2015-16 was prepared, a writ petition was 
filed. Thereafter, pursuant to the order 
passed in the writ petition, the 
representations were considered by the 
competent authority and finality was 
given to the Seniority List. 
Not only this, it is also the case of 
the petitioners that for the Year 2021-22 
also, tentative lists were prepared. 
Objections were invited, which had also 
attained finality, but, despite that, chapter 
has been reopened, which may prejudice 
the interest of the petitioners, because if 
the seniority is reopened and the private 
respondents are given seniority above the petitioners, it may diminish their chances 
of promotion.
Having considered, till the next date 
of listing, the operation of the impugned 
order dated 19/20.04.2023 (Annexure 
No.1) shall remain in abeyance.
Stay Application, IA No.1 of 2023, 
stands disposed of, accordingly.
 (Ravindra Maithani J.)
