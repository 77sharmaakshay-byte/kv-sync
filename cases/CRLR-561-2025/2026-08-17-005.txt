Mr. Tusharika Sharma, learned

2. Mr. Shailendra Singh Chauhan,

Uniyal, learned Brief Holder for the State. 
3. Mr. Saurav Adhikari, learned counsel 
for respondent nos.2 & 3.
4. Learned counsel for respondent nos. 2 
and 3 submits that respondent nos. 2 and 
3 have filed a Withdrawal Application (I.A. 
No. 4 of 2026), seeking permission to 
withdraw the amount of ₹25,000/-
deposited by the revisionist pursuant to 
the order dated 03.09.2025 passed by the 
Coordinate Bench of this Court.
5. Perusal of the order dated 03.09.2025 
indicates that the operation and effect of 
the impugned judgment and order passed 
by the learned Family Court, whereby the 
revisionist was directed to pay interim maintenance at the rate of ₹8,000/- per 
month to respondent nos. 2 and 3 under 
Section 125 Cr.P.C., was stayed subject to 
the condition that the revisionist would 
deposit a sum of ₹25,000/- before the 
Court.
6. In view of the aforesaid order and 
considering the nature of the deposit, the 
Withdrawal Application (I.A. No. 4 of 2026) 
filed by respondent nos. 2 and 3 is allowed. 
Respondent nos. 2 and 3 shall be entitled 
to withdraw the amount of ₹25,000/-
deposited by the revisionist pursuant to 
the order dated 03.09.2025. Such 
withdrawal shall be subject to the final 
outcome of the present revision and shall 
abide by any further order that may be 
passed by this Court.
7. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
prays for and is granted two weeks’ time to 
objections to the memo of revision.
8. List this case on 10.09.2026.
9. Interim order dated 03.09.2025 to 
continue till the next date of listing.
