COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.527 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Harshvardhan Dhanik, learned counsel 
for the petitioners.
2. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State.
3. Mr. Ramji Srivastava, learned counsel for 
the respondent-Selecting Body.
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
5. The writ petition has been filed by the 
petitioners seeking indulgence of this Court to 
quash condition no.9 of the advertisement dated 
11.03.2024 issued by Uttarakhand Medical 
Service Selection Board, Dehradun. 
6. The similar controversy arose before a 
Co-ordinate Bench of this Court wherein the 
selfsame condition no.9 of the advertisement was 
challenged. In that petition the Co-ordinate Bench 
provisionally permitted the petitioners therein to 
participate in the selection process with the 
observation that their selection process would 
abide by the final decision in those petitions. 
7. Learned counsel for the petitioners claim 
the same relief in this petition as well, to which, 
learned State Counsel as well as learned counsel 
appearing for the Selecting Body have also 
conceded. 
8. Learned counsel for the respondent(s) prays
for and is granted six weeks time to file their 
counter affidavit(s).
9. Two weeks time is granted to the learned 
counsel for the petitioner(s) to file rejoinder 
affidavit(s).
10. In the meantime, it is provided as an interim measure, that the selecting body shall 
consider the candidature of the petitioners for the 
posts of Nursing Officer in Government Medical 
College pursuant to impugned advertisement 
dated 11.03.2024 provisionally and their 
application forms shall be accepted on producing 
e-copy/certified copy of this order. They shall be 
permitted to participate in the examination for 
selection on the post of Nursing Officer 
Government Medical College as per 
advertisement dated 11.03.2024; their selection 
shall be subject to the order passed by this Court
and their result shall not be declared. 
11. List on 25.06.2024.
12. Let certified copy of this order be issued to 
the learned counsel for the petitioner, today itself, 
on payment of usual charges. 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
