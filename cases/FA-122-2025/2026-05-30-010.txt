FA No.122 of 2025
Hon’ble Siddhartha Sah, J.
Mr. Sagar Kothari, Advocate for the 
appellants.
Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate for the 
respondent.
The first appeal is preferred against the 
judgment and decree dated 20.07.2023 passed 
by learned 9th Additional Senior Civil Judge, 
Dehradun in Original Suit No.99/2017, Sanjeev 
Rathi vs. Lokesh Lohia and another, whereby the 
suit filed by the plaintiff/respondent has been 
decreed.
The appeal is delayed by 615 days.
Pressing the delay condonation application, 
learned counsel for the appellants would submit 
that apart from the Civil Suit, there was one 
criminal proceedings in between the parties. The 
appellants were convicted by the trial court and 
he had preferred the appeal before the appellate 
court. There were compromise talks between the 
parties which could not fructify. Instead of filing 
the first appeal, the appellant got a wrong legal
advice to file an application under Order 41 Rule 
5 (2) of the CPC for the stay of the judgment of 
the trial court before the court of learned District 
Judge, Dehradun which kept on pending for one 
year and which was withdrawn on 23.05.2025. 
Even the plaintiff was not satisfied with the 
decree and he too has preferred a first appeal 
which is connected with this first appeal. 
Therefore, there is a delay and he submits that 
the delay is bona fide. Since the rights of the 
parties have to be adjudicated and thus the delay 
that has chanced in filing the appeal becondoned.
Per contra, learned counsel for the 
respondent-Mr. Piyush Garg would contend that 
the contentions as raised by the learned counsel 
for the appellants is just to cover the gross delay 
that has chanced in filing the appeal. He would 
contend that no talks were ever held between the 
parties. The judgment was passed on 
20.07.2023 and he filed an application under 
Order 41 Rule 5(2) of the CPC on 29.04.2024
which shows that the conduct of the appellants 
has been highly irresponsible and only ruse to 
buy time. He further submits that the 
submissions on behalf of the appellants are 
totally false. Since the appellants had also filed 
the criminal revision in Janunary, 2025 and the 
present appeal has been filed in June, 2025 that 
too without court fee and the court fee has been 
tendered only after one year and the ground that 
is being stated by the appellants that the 
application was filed under wrong advice is not 
tenable because the counsel whom he says had
adviced to file an appeal had filed his 
Vakalatnama on 28.11.2024. Thus he would 
contend that the appellant was very well aware 
that he filed an application under Order 41 Rule 
5(2) of the CPC and thus contends that the 
appellant has been highly irresponsible and there 
has been gross negligence and does not warrant 
any leniency for the condonation of the delay.
Having heard learned counsel for the parties 
and after going through the record, it is apparent 
that the appellant has not been as responsible as 
need to be for agitating his case and could have 
approached this Court within time.
Learned counsel for the respondent would 
also be correct in submitting that the conduct of 
the appellant does not entitle to any leniency. 
However, fact remains that the decree for recovery of money is pending execution and the 
respective rights of the parties have to be 
adjudicated in this appeal. Though the appellant 
has been highly negligent and also irresponsible 
in agitating his cause, but since the rights of the 
parties have to be finally adjudicated in the 
appeal, hence in such view of the matter, the 
delay that has chanced and the cause that has 
been shown is found to be sufficient under the 
peculiar facts and circumstances of the case.
Purely in the interest of justice so that the 
technicalities of law may not come in the way for 
adjudication of the respective rights of the parties
the Delay Condonation Application (IA No.1 of 
2025) is thus allowed. 
However, subject to cost of Rs.50,000/-, half 
of which to be deposited in the High Court Bar 
Association Advocate Welfare Fund within a 
period of three weeks from today and half of the 
said amount to be paid to the respondent within 
the said period.
Subject to the above, the delay is being
condoned and the appeal shall be treated to be 
within time. 
Admit the appeal.
Summon the Trial Court Record.
List this case on 30.07.2026.
Since the appeal has been admitted, hence 
in view of Order 41 Rule 5 CPC, subject to 
deposit of entire decretal amount before this 
Court within a period of four weeks, the effect 
and operation of the impugned judgment and 
decree dated 20.07.2023 shall remain stayed till 
the next date of listing. The amount be kept in an 
interest bearing FDR.
