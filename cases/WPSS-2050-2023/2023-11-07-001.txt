 Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, learned counsel

2. Mr. Sushil Vashisth, learned S.C. with 
Mr. SMS Mehta, learned B.H. for the State. 
3. In this petition, petitioner has 
challenged order dated 14.08.2023 whereby 
services of petitioner, as Nursing Officer, has 
been terminated. 
4. It is the contention of petitioner that 
before passing the impugned order, no 
enquiry whatsoever has been conducted by 
the respondents. Attention of the Court was 
drawn by learned counsel for the petitioner 
to Annexure No.12 to the petition which is 
the office memo dated 07.01.2022, whereby, 
an Enquiry Officer, namely, Dr. Bharti Rana, 
Director, Medical Health & Family Welfare, 
Garhwal Region, Pauri was nominated by the 
orders of Director General, Medical Health & 
Family Welfare, and it has been specifically 
mentioned that the principles of natural 
justice must be adhered to while conducting 
enquiry against the petitioner. However, 
without following the observations made in 
order dated 07.01.2022, the impugned 
termination order qua petitioner has directly 
been passed by the selfsame authority. 2
5. Learned State counsel seeks and is 
allowed six weeks’ time for filing the counter 
affidavit. It should specifically be mentioned 
in such affidavit whether any enquiry was 
undertaken pursuant to order dated 
07.01.2022. 
6. The petitioner may choose to file his 
rejoinder affidavit within two weeks 
thereafter. 
7. List on 16.02.2024.
