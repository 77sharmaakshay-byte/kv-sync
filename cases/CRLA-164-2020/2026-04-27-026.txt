(2) Having been convicted for the 
offence under Section 302 read with 
Section 34 IPC, vide judgment dated 
31.10.2019, appellant is serving life 
imprisonment. Today, the matter is listed 
on short term bail application 
(IA/4606/2026). 
(3) Learned Counsel for the 
appellant submits that the appellant 
seeks temporary release to attend the 
marriage of his nephew.
(4) Having considered the 
submission, this Court finds that the 
ground urged, namely, marriage of 
nephew, does not constitute a compelling 
or exceptional circumstance warranting 
grant of short term bail, particularly in the 
case of a convict undergoing sentence for 
a grave offence under Section 302 IPC. 
The nature and seriousness of the 
offence, coupled with the antecedents of 
the appellant, who is stated to be a 
hardened criminal, do not justify exercise of discretion in his favour.
(5) Short term bail application is 
accordingly rejected.
