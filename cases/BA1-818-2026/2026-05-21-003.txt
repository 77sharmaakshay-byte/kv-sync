Vishal ……Applicant

Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Gaurav Singh, learned counsel for the Applicant.
2. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, Mr. Devendra Singh, learned 
AGAs, for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. In the High Court of Uttarakhand, the present case 
arises from Bail Application filed under Section 483 of 
Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, seeking regular 
bail for the Applicant – Vishal, who has been accused in 
FIR/Case Crime No.21 of 2026, dated 10.01.2026, under 
Sections 103 (1) and 3 (5) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 
2023, at Police Station Kotwali Haridwar, District Haridwar.
4. Learned counsel for the Applicant submits that the 
Applicant has filed the present short term bail application on 
the ground that Applicant is pursuing BA. L.L.B, course from 
South Point Law College, Village Joshi Chauhan, District 
Sonipat, District Haryana, and the Applicant has successfully 
passed 7th Semesters, and at present studying in 8th Semester 
and the examinations of 8th Semester is scheduled from 
16.05.2026 to 11.06.2026. In this regard, the learned counsel 
for the Applicant has annexed the examination date sheet 
along with short term bail application. 
5. Considering the overall facts and circumstances of the 
case, this Court is inclined to grant the short term bail for a 
period of twenty four days’ to the Applicant. Hence, the short 
term bail application of the Applicant is allowed. The Applicant is directed to be released on bail for a period of 
twenty-four days from the date of his actual release, subject to 
the furnishing of his personal bond and two reliable sureties 
each of the like amount to the satisfaction of the Magistrate 
concerned. 
6. It is rest assured, that as soon as the aforesaid time
period of the short term bail expires, the Applicant would be 
surrendering himself before the Jail Superintendent, of the 
concerned jail. 
7. List this matter on 15.06.2026.
 (Ashish Naithani, J.)
