6. Petitioner institution gave admission to 26 
students, NEET qualified without any counselling, 
though as per directions of the Central Government 
admission should be done only after counselling.
7. Learned counsel for the University Mr. Sandeep 
Kothari submits that since the admission to these 26 
students were given without counselling, therefore, 
these 26 students cannot be enrolled in the University 
and they cannot be permitted to participate in the 
examination. 
8. In response to this, learned counsel for the 
petitioner argued that though 26 students were given 
admission without any counselling, but they are 
studying since last 2 years, therefore, keeping in view 
the future prospects of 26 students, they may be 
enrolled provisionally in the University and permitted 
provisionally to participate in the examination. 
9. Now, the question is - if the admissions of 26 
students are without counselling whether they can be 
enrolled in the University and be permitted to appear 
in the examination. This aspect requires to be 
scrutinized. Further taking into consideration the fact 
that the students are NEET qualified and studying 
since last two years, in such an eventuality, if they are 
not enrolled in University and they are not 
provisionally permitted to appear in the examination 
certainly it will affect the future prospect of the 26 
students. 10. Mr. Sandeep Kothari, learned counsel for the 
University submits that only the institution approached 
this Court and if there is genuine grievance then that 
can be looked into in a petition filed by those 26 
students. 
11. Here the only issue is with regard to the 
enrolment of 26 students, who are NEET qualified, 
admitted in the institution petitioner without 
counselling. 
12. List on 23.04.2025.
13. By that time, learned counsel for the University 
may get instructions in the matter.
