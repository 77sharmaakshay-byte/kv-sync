7. Since all these petitions raise 
identical issues concerning the 
dispute relating to admission to the 
B.A.M.S. course for academic sessions 
2021-2022, 2022-2023, 2023-2024 
and 2024-2025 and 2025-2026, they 
are being heard together.
8. Learned counsel appearing for the 
petitioners submits that the present writ 
petitions have been filed seeking a direction to respondent no. 2 to enroll 
the students admitted by the petitioners’ 
college and to permit such students to 
appear in their practical as well as 
semester examinations of the B.A.M.S. 
course.
9. Learned counsel further submits 
that admission to the B.A.M.S. course 
was made subject to qualification in the 
National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test 
(NEET) pursuant to the Central 
Government Notification/Regulation 
dated 07.12.2018, whereby NEET was 
prescribed as a mandatory eligibility 
criterion for admission to the said 
course. It is submitted that NEET was 
introduced for the first time for the 
academic session 2018–2019, which led 
to the filing of several writ petitions 
across the country challenging the 
imposition of NEET as an essential 
qualification for admission to the 
B.A.M.S. course. The controversy 
ultimately reached the Hon’ble Supreme 
Court, which, in Association of 
Managements of Homeopathic Medical 
Colleges of Maharashtra v. Union of 
India & Ors., (2019) 20 SCC 511, held 
that for the academic session 2018–
2019, qualification in NEET was not an 
essential requirement for admission to 
the B.A.M.S. course. Thereafter, in 
subsequent civil appeals, the Hon’ble 
Apex Court relaxed the condition of 
mandatory NEET qualification and 
permitted students, who were otherwise 
eligible, to continue their studies for the 
academic sessions 2019–2020 and 2020–
2021. It is submitted that while most 
such students have already completed 
the course, a few are still pursuing their 
studies.10. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
further submits that the students 
involved in the present writ petitions 
were earlier permitted to appear in the 
examinations pursuant to interim orders
passed by this Hon’ble Court. It is 
further submitted that the students have 
qualified the NEET examination and 
possess the minimum prescribed 
eligibility and requisite percentage as 
mandated under the applicable 
regulations.
11. Learned counsel further submits 
that the next practical and semester 
examinations are scheduled to be held 
on 27.01.2026. Therefore, a prayer is 
made that the students of the petitioners’ 
college be permitted to appear in the said 
examinations.
12. In view of the aforesaid facts and 
circumstances, and purely in the interest 
of justice, the rest of students admitted 
during the Academic Sessions 2021-
2022, 2022-2023 and 2023-2024 and 
2025-2026, who have qualified the NEET 
examination and fulfill the minimum 
prescribed eligibility and percentage, are 
provisionally permitted to appear in the 
examination scheduled to be held on 
27.01.2026, subject to the final decision
in the present writ petitions.
13. List the case in due course.
