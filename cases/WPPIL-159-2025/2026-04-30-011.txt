6. The present writ petitions relate to students 
who were admitted to BAMS Course in different 
institutions in the academic year 2018-19. This 
Court, vide its order dated 26.02.2020 in WPMS 
No.2911/2018, filed by an association of private 
unaided educational institutions in Uttarakhand in 
the name of Association of Combined Entrance 
Examination, permitted the University to consider 
the case of 474 students of the said academic year 
for enrollment, subject to payment of ₹ 50.00 lakhs 
as penalty by the petitioner association. 
7. It is stated by learned counsel for the 
petitioners that the association had duly complied with the said condition and had deposited ₹ 50.00 
lakhs with the respondent University. In the 
aforesaid backdrop, it is urged that the students who 
have been admitted are entitled to appear in the 
examination and their result be declared but time 
and again the University is creating hindrance in the 
same.
8. Mr. Sandeep Kothari, learned counsel 
appearing on behalf of the respondent university 
does not dispute that ₹ 50.00 lakhs was deposited
by the association with the University in terms of 
order dated 26.02.2020 in WPMS No.2911/2018. He, 
however, submits that the petitioners are not
amongst the 474 students, who were permitted to 
be enrolled by this Court. He states that he will 
provide list of 474 students, who were permitted to 
be enrolled, to learned counsel for the petitioners 
within two days. 
9. Accordingly, he is permitted to provide the list 
to learned counsel for the petitioners, so that they 
may complete their instructions on the said aspect. 
10. Let these petitions be delinked from the 
bunch and be listed separately on 07.05.2026.
