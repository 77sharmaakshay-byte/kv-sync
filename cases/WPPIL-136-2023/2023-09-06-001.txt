COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS 
06.09.2023 WPPIL No. 136 of 2023 
Hon’ble Vipin Sanghi, C.J.
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Navnish Negi, learned counsel for 
the petitioner. 
2. The Uttarakhand State Legal Services 
Authority has preferred the present writ 
petition, in public interest, highlighting the 
serious situation existing in relation to the 
Raaji tribes residing in the State of 
Uttarakhand. 
3. The District Legal Services Authority has 
conducted a survey of the said tribes by 
visiting the several villages occupied by 
them, and compiled the data which has been 
placed on record. 
4. The said data shows that the population 
of the Raaji tribes has sharply declined over 
the years. The factors relevant to determine
their development indicate that the State has 
not taken enough steps to provide even basic 
facilities in the villages where the said tribe
resides. The distribution of the population in 
the villages occupied by the Raaji tribes showthat there are hardly any survivors beyond 
the age of 55 years. The life expectancy 
amongst the members of the said tribe is, 
therefore, very low. 
5. Mr. Negi points out that even Primary 
Health Centers are situated at distances of 
about 25 Kms. from the villages where the 
said tribes reside, and there are hardly any 
educational facilities available to them. 
6. Mr. Negi has also drawn attention of the 
Court to the Scheduled Tribes and Other 
Traditional Forest Dwellers (Recognition of 
Forest Rights) Act, 2006 and, in particular, to 
Section 3(2) thereof to submit that there can 
be no excuse for the State Government not 
to take developmental activities for setting
up dispensaries, hospitals, schools etc. in the 
villages occupied by the Raaji tribe. 
7. Mr. V.K. Kaparuwan, learned Standing
Counsel for the Union of India, accepts notice 
on behalf of respondent No. 1. 
8. Ms. Puja Banga, learned Brief Holder for 
the State, accepts notice on behalf of 
respondent Nos. 2 to 7. 
9. We direct the respondent No. 1, as well 
as respondent Nos. 2 and 3 to urgently look into the situation aforesaid, and to take 
remedial steps without waiting for any 
further orders. 
10. The respondents should file their 
affidavits disclosing the steps that they have 
taken, and they propose to take, with 
timeline to remedy the situation aforesaid. 
11. List the matter on 06.11.2023.
