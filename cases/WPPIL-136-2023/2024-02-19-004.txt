COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
19.02.2024 WPPIL No. 136 of 2023
Hon’ble Ritu Bahri, C.J.
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Navnish Negi, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. V.K. Kapruwan, learned Standing Counsel for 
the Union of India/ respondent no. 1. 
3. Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned Chief Standing Counsel 
assisted by Mr. Gajendra Tripathi, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand/ respondent nos. 
2, 3, 5, 6 & 7. 
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
5. Mr. Geeta Ram Nautiyal, Incharge Director, 
Social Welfare Department, Uttarakhand and Mr. 
Sanjay Tolia, Director, Tribal Welfare Department, 
Uttarakhand are present in Court today. On a specific 
query from the Court, they have informed the Court 
that plots have been allotted to 83 Tribals for 
constructing 134 houses, and budget under the 
Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojna had already been 
sanctioned in the year 2021-22. A specific query has 
been put to the respondents, as to whether electricity
and water connection have been given in the area, 
where plots have been allotted. 
6. At this stage, counsel appearing for the 
Uttarakhand State Legal Services Authority submits 
that they will also, through their paralegals, conduct a 
survey, and inform this Court that apart from 83 Tribals, how many other Tribals are still landless.
7. Counsel for the respondents, representing State 
seeks time to file affidavit. Respondents will file a 
better affidavit giving details, whether benefit of social 
welfare schemes is being extended to these Tribals. 
Especially with regard to mid-day meals, an order has 
been given to the respondents, whereby in the entire 
State of Haryana, one bottle milk is being given since 
COVID-19 to all children as mid-day meal. In the 
affidavit, the respondents will also give details of other 
mid-day meals. The affidavit shall be filed within two 
weeks. 
8. List on 06.03.2024.
9. Copy of this order be given to the counsel for the 
State. 
10. Personal presence of Director, Social Welfare 
Department and Director, Tribal Welfare Department is 
exempted. 
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.) (Ritu Bahri, C.J.)
