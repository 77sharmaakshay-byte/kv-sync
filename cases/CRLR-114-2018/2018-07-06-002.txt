Stay vacation application No. 1287 of 
2018 has been filed by the respondent nos. 2 
& 3 to vacate the interim order dated 
26.04.2018 passed by this Court, whereby 
this Court has directed the revisionist to pay 
50% of the arrear amount of the monthly 
maintenance to respondent no.2 by 
15.05.2018 and remaining 50% of arrear was 
directed to be paid by 15.06.2018. 
It is the contention of learned counsel 
for the respondent nos. 2 & 3 that the interim 
order dated 26.04.2018 has not been 
complied with. 
Per contra, learned counsel for the 
revisionist would submit that the amount of 
Rs. 4,500/- has been deposited, 
From the perusal of the order dated 
23.03.2018 passed by the trial court, it would 
reveal that the learned trial court has directed 
the revisionist to pay monthly maintenance @ 
Rs. 5,00/- and Rs. 4,500/- to the opposite 
party Nos. 2 and 3 respectively from the date 
of order. 
It appears that the order dated 
26.04.2018 passed by this Court has not been 
complied with by the revisionist. Thus the 
interim order passed by this Court is vacated. 
Stay vacation application no. 1287 of 
2018 stands disposed of. 
As prayed, three weeks’ time is granted 
to learned counsel for the revisionist to file 
rejoinder affidavit. 
List thereafter. 
 (Lok Pal Singh, J.)
