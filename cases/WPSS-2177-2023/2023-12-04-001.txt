Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. D.S. Patni, learned senior counsel assisted by 
Mr. Dharmendra Barthwal, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned Additional Chief 
Standing Counsel for the State.
3. By means of this writ petition, petitioner has 
challenged order dated 12.05.2020 (Annexure no.6), 
passed by respondent no.2, whereby grade-pay of the 
petitioner has been reduced from ₹4200/- to 2400/-,
retrospectively.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that 
the petitioner, who was working with the respondent￾Department as a Collection Ameen has retired on 
31.12.2019. After his retirement, reduction in grade￾pay of the petitioner has been made which resulted into 
reduction of his pension as well. 
5. Petitioner preferred a Writ Petition (S/S) 
No.1406 of 2021 before this Court for a direction to 
respondent to pay the regular pension and other retiral 
dues including gratuity to the petitioner and other 
ancillary relief, which was decided by Co-ordinate 
Bench of this Court dated 30.10.2021 with a direction 
to respondent-Commissioner to consider and pass 
appropriate order on the representation of the 
petitioner, as expeditiously as possible, but not later 2
than two months from the date of production of 
certified copy of this order dated 30.10.2021, but 
respondent-Commissioner has not taken any action 
pursuant to the aforesaid judgment and order rendered 
by the Co-ordinate Bench of this Court.
6. The petitioner being a retired person could not 
file any contempt petition against respondent no.1, but 
the order impugned in the present writ petition was 
made available to the petitioner on 29.11.2023 on his 
making an application under Right to Information Act, 
2005.
7. Learned counsel for the State prays for and is 
granted six weeks’ time for filing counter affidavit.
8. Further two weeks’ time is granted to learned 
counsel for the petitioner for filing rejoinder affidavit.
9. List this case on 28.02.2024.
