the trial court for the offence under Section 302 read with

2. Mr. Ajay Joshi (through video conferencing), 
learned counsel holding brief of Mr. Deep Prakash Bhatt, 
learned counsel for the appellant and Mr. Pramod Tiwari, 
learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Heard on the Short Term Bail Application (IA 
No.3 of 2025).
4. The short term bail application has been filed on 
the ground that the appellant has to appear for the 2
upcoming examination conducting by Uttarakhand 
Subordinate Selection Commission on 19.01.2025. The 
said examination is to be held in the Government Girls 
Inter College, Fazilpur Mahrolla near DIET Rampur Road, 
Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar. Appellant has filed 
a copy of the Admit Card, issued by the said Commission.
5. Having heard learned counsel for the parties, 
this Court is of the view, purely on humanitarian ground, 
that the appellant may be granted short term bail for a 
period of three days from the date of his release.
6. Appellant Jiwan Singh be released on short term 
bail on executing his personal bond of Rs.30,000-00 and 
furnishing two reliable sureties, each in the like amount, to 
the satisfaction of the Court concerned for a period of 
three days' from the date of his release with the following 
conditions:-
(i) Appellant shall not directly or indirectly make 
any Inducement, threat or promise to any 
person acquainted with the facts of this case; 
and,
(ii) Appellant shall not leave the country without 
the previous permission of this Court; and,
(iii) Appellant shall surrender at once before the 
concerned Court on expiry of the term of this 
short term bail, who will remand him to judicial 
custody.
7. The Short Term Bail Application (IA No.03 of 
2025) is disposed of accordingly.
