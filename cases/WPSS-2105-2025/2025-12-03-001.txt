Mr. Jitendra Chaudhary, learned

2. Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, Addl. C.S.C.

3. The petitioner has challenged the order 
dated 28.10.2025, whereby her candidature 
for the post of Aganwari Karyakarti has been 
cancelled on the ground that in view of 
Government Order dated 18.12.2024, two 
members of the same family cannot be 
appointed in one Aganwari Kendra.
4. In the present case, the mother of the 
petitioner is serving as Supervisor in the 
Aganwari Kendra concerned, whereas the 
petitioner herself had been selected as 
Aganwari Karyakarti.
5. It is submitted on behalf of the 
petitioner that she is a divorcee and, 
therefore, she cannot be treated as a member 
of her parental family for the purposes of 
applicability of the aforesaid Government 
Order.
6. Learned State counsel prays for and is 
granted four weeks’ time to file a counter 
affidavit.
7. List this case on 08.01.2026.
8. Considering the facts of the case, it is 
directed that till the next date of listing, the 
post of Aganwari Karyakarti in Aganwari 
Centre Chakarpur, Tehsil & Block Gadarpur, 
District Udham Singh Nagar shall not be filled 
up.
