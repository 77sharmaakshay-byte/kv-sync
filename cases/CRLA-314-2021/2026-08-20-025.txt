Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J. (Oral)
Instant appeal is preferred against the judgment and 
order dated 04.09.2021/06.09.2021, passed in Special Sessions 
Trial No.114 of 2017, State Vs. Sandeep Singh, by the court of 
FTC/Additional Sessions Judge/Special Judge POCSO, Udham 
Singh Nagar. By it, the appellant has been convicted under 
Sections 376(2), 363, 366 IPC and sentenced accordingly. The 
appellant Sandeep Singh has filed a short term bail application on 
the ground that the daughter of the appellant has to undergo a 
surgery.
2. Heard on short term bail application.
3. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that the 
appellant’s daughter, who is a minor, has to undergo a surgery. 
Earlier also she had undergone a surgery, which has failed; now, 
repeat surgery for bone grafting is to be done; the doctor has 
prescribed to it; there is no one in the family to take care of the 
family of the appellant; his wife is back at home with minor 
children. Therefore, the appellant may be granted short term bail.
4. Learned State Counsel admits that the appellant’s wife 
is staying at home along with her minor children and one of the 2
children has to undergo a surgery. She would submit that the 
surgery is not scheduled.
5. When the Court required learned counsel for the 
appellant, as to why the date has not been fixed and as to why 
short term bail may be granted for the purpose of surgery? He 
submits that there is none in the family of the appellant even to 
take the date for surgery. 
6. Having considered, without adverting to the merits of 
the case, purely on the humanitarian ground, this Court is of the 
view that the appellant may be enlarged on short term bail for a 
period of six weeks from the date of his release. 
7. Let the appellant be released on short term bail for a 
period of six weeks from the date of his actual release, on his 
executing a personal bond and furnishing two reliable sureties, 
each of the like amount, to the satisfaction of the court concerned. 
8. It is further directed that, on expiry of the period of 
short term bail, the appellant shall surrender before the court 
concerned and intimation to that effect shall also be given to this 
Court. 
9. The short term bail application stands disposed of.
10. List on 29.10.2026.
