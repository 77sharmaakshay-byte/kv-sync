Instant appeal has been preferred against the 
judgment and orders dated 04.09.2021/06.09.2021 
passed in Special Sessions Trial No. 114 of 2017, by the 
Court of FTC/Additional Sessions Judge/Special Judge, 
POCSO, Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar.By the 
impugned judgment and order, the appellants has been 
convicted under Sections 363, 366, 376 (2) IPC and 
Section 6 of the Protection of Children From Sexual 
Offences Act, 2012 and sentenced thereunder. 
2. Heard on short term bail application.
3. Learned counsel for the appellant would 
submit that the appellant has one year six months old
daughter, who is with his wife. His parents are aged. His 2
daughter has got a fracture on her leg and the Doctor has 
referred her to a higher centre.
4. Learned State counsel, under instructions 
would submit that the factual aspects, as narrated by the 
learned counsel for the appellant are corrected.
5. Without adverting to the merits, purely on 
humanitarian ground, this Court is of the view that the 
appellant may be enlarged on short term bail for a 
period of two weeks.
6. Let the appellant be released on short term 
bail during the pendency of this appeal for a period of 
two weeks from the date of his release, subject to his 
furnishing a personal bond and two reliable sureties of 
the like amount, to the satisfaction of the court 
concerned.
7. After expiry of the period of short term bail, 
the appellant shall surrender before the court 
concerned and an information shall be forwarded to this 
Court.3
8. The short term bail application is allowed 
accordingly.
9. List this matter on 05.12.2023.
