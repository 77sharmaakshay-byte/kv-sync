COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
I.A. No. 03 of 2021
In
CRLA No. 314 of 2021
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. S.C. Bhatt, Advocate for the 
appellant/applicant.
Mr. Siddhartha Bisht, Brief Holder 
for the State.
The appellant/applicant is in judicial 
custody, post conviction under Sections 376 
(2), 363 and 366 IPC. He has sought short 
term bail on the grounds that his cousin is 
to marry on 26.11.2021; the family is a 
joint family and his wife is also to deliver a 
child in the third week of November, 2021. 
There are emotional moments for the 
appellant.
Heard learned counsel for the 
applicant as well as learned State Counsel 
and perused the record.
Learned counsel for the applicant 
would submit that the applicant is the eldest 
son in the joint family; his cousin is to 
marry on 26.11.2021; his wife is also to 
deliver a child in the third week of 
November, 2021. These are emotional 
moments for him, therefore, he may be 
granted short term bail.
Learned counsel for the applicant has 
also submitted that the factum has been 
verified by Sub Inspector Babita Tamta of 
the concerned police station. 
On 18.11.2021, the State was given 
time to verify the contents of the short term 
bail application. 
Today, the learned State Counsel 
states that he has not received any 
instructions as yet. 
This is very very sorry state of 
affairs. Having considered, this Court is of 
the view that purely on humanitarian 
ground, the applicant may be granted short 
term bail for a period of one week, from the 
date of his release.
Short term bail application (I.A. No. 
03 of 2021) is allowed.
The applicant shall be enlarged on 
short term bail only for a period of one 
week from the date of his release, on his 
executing a personal bond and furnishing 
two reliable sureties, each of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the court 
concerned. It is further directed that, on 
expiry of the period of short term bail, the 
applicant shall surrender before the court 
concerned and intimation to this effect shall 
also be given to this Court. 
List on 01.12.2021.
Let a certified copy of this order be 
issued today itself on payment of usual 
charges.
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
