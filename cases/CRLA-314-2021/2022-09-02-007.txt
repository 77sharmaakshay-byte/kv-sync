Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J. (Oral)
Instant appeal is preferred against the judgment 
and order dated 04.09.2021/06.09.2021, passed in Special 
Sessions Trial No. 114 of 2017, by the court of FTC/Additional 
Sessions Judge/Special Judge, POCSO, Rudrapur, District 
Udham Singh Nagar. By the impugned judgment and order, 
the appellant has been convicted under Sections 363, 366, 
376 (2) IPC and Section 6 of the Protection of Children from 
Sexual Offences Act, 2012 (for short, “the Act”) and sentenced 
under Sections 363, 366, 376 (2) IPC. 
2. In this appeal, the appellant has filed a short term 
bail application, on the ground that his father is unwell, who 
has an appointment at AIIMS, Rishikesh on 05.09.2022. There 
is no other person in the family, who could take his father to 
the hospital, therefore, short term bail for a short period may 
be granted.2
3. Learned Senior Advocate appearing for the 
appellant would submit that appellant is the only son of his 
father; his father is unwell and the appellant has to take his
father for medical examination at AIIMS, Rishikesh. 
4. Along with the short term bail application, a
discharge slip of Civil Hospital, Khatima, which records the 
Diagnosis Gastritis (Pain Abdomen) referred to a higher centre. 
It does not reveal the investigations. An affidavit in support of 
the short term bail application, has been filed by a person, 
namely, Jaswant Singh, who is alleged to be the relative of the 
appellant and doing pairvi. The father of the appellant is 
stated to be 65 years of age.
5. Having considered all the attending factors, this 
Court is of the view that there is no sufficient ground to grant 
short term bail. Accordingly, the short term bail application 
deserves to be rejected.
6. Accordingly, the short term bail application (IA No. 
5 of 2022) is rejected.
