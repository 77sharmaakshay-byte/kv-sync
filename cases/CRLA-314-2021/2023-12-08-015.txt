COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRLA No.314 of 2021
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Present Mr. Pramod Kumar, counsel 
holding brief of Mr. Nandan Arya, counsel for 
the appellant.
2. Present Ms. Manisha Rana Singh, AGA 
along with Mr. Pramod Tiwari, Brief Holder 
for the State. 
3. Heard on the time extension application 
(IA No.9 of 2023). 
4. Counsel for the appellant would submit 
that the applicant/accused was granted short 
term bail application as his daughter was to 
undergo surgery for fracture in her right leg; 
that, the period of short term bail has expired 
on 07.12.2023 and he has to surrender today, 
however, the medical necessity is still there. 
5. Per contra, counsel for the State would 
submit that by order dated 15.09.2023 short 
term bail was granted to the applicant/accused 
for a period of two weeks; that, on 06.10.2023 
it was extended for a further period of six 
weeks; that, on 10.11.2023 it was again 
extended for three weeks. She would further 
submit that Annexure No.1 is the discharge 
summary obtained from the Swastik Hospital, 
according to which, daughter of the 
applicant/accused was discharged on 
03.12.2023 itself. 
6. Counsel for the applicant/accused could 
not submit whether some further treatment is 
advised to the daughter of the applicant/accused. 
7. Having considered the submissions made 
by counsel for the respective parties, this Court 
does not find any good ground to extend the 
short term bail granted to the 
applicant/accused. Time Extension Application 
is, accordingly, dismissed. 
(Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.)
