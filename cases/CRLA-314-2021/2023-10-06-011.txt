Mr. Nandan Arya, Advocate for the

Mr. V.S. Rawat, Brief Holder for the

The appellant was granted short 
term bail on 15.09.2023 on the ground of 
the illness of his daughter.
Now Time Extension Application No. 
7 of 2023 has been filed on the ground 
that the daughter of the appellant was 
taken to Dr. Susheela Tiwari, Government 
Hospital, Haldwani, but due to non 
availability of the expert surgeon, the 
procedure could not be conducted. The 
daughter of the appellant has been 
referred to AIIMS, Delhi. 
The short term bail granted to the 
appellant is extended to another six
weeks.
The time extension application 
stands disposed of accordingly.
Let a copy of this order be supplied 
to learned counsel for the parties, today 
itself, on payment of usual charges.
