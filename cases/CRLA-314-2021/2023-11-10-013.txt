In

Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Nandan Arya and Mr. 
Manoj Bhatt, Advocates for the 
applicant.
Mr. C.S. Dumka, A.G.A. for the 
State. 
The applicant sought extension 
of short term bail. In fact, on 
15.09.2023, he was granted short 
term bail on the ground that his 
daughter has got a fractured on her 
leg and the doctor has referred her 
for a higher centre. It is about two 
months. 
Today, learned counsel for the 
applicant would submit that the 
surgery has yet not been done 
because in between the father of the 
applicant has expired. 
On 06.10.2023, the short term 
bail was extended. On that date, it 
was told that due to non-availability 
of Surgeon, the surgery could not be 
done. This is the last extension. 
Three weeks’ time more is extended 
to the applicant after expiry of the 
time. 
Accordingly, the time extension 
application is allowed.
