The instant appeal has been preferred against 
judgment and order dated 23.07.2026, passed in Special Sessions 
Trial No.47 of 2024, State Vs. Sunil Singh, by the court of Special
Sessions Judge/Special Judge (NDPS Act), District Pithoragarh. 
By it, the appellant has been convicted under Sections 8 read with 
20(b)(ii)(c) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 
1985 (“the Act”), and sentenced accordingly. 
2. Heard.
3. This appeal has already been admitted.
4. List in due course for final hearing. 
5. Heard on the Bail Application (IA) No.1 of 20262
6. According to the prosecution case, on 08.08.2024, 
charas was recovered from the possession of the appellant.
7. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that the 
entire prosecution case is false; there is non-compliance of Section 
50 of the Act; on the one hand, according to the police, the 
accused denied for being searched before the Magistrate or the 
Gazetted Officer, but the Incharge Nayab Tehsildar was called at 
the spot; the inventory report bears the FIR number, which was 
allegedly prepared at the spot, whereas, the FIR was lodged much 
thereafter. 
8. Learned State Counsel admits that the inventory 
report was prepared at the spot, which bears the FIR number.
9. The Court wanted to know from learned State 
Counsel as to how the FIR number was recorded in the inventory 
report, when the FIR was admittedly lodged much thereafter? He 
has no answer to it.
10. Having considered this and other attending 
factors, we are of the view that it is a case in which the execution 
of sentence should be suspended and the appellant be enlarged 
on bail.
11. The bail application is allowed.
12. The sentence appealed against is suspended 
during the pendency of the appeal.3
13. Let the appellant be released on bail during the 
pendency of the appeal on his executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two reliable sureties, each of the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the court concerned.
