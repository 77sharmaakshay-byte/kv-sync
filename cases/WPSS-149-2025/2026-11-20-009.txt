1. Ms. Neeti Rana, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Narain Dutt, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State.
3. The petitioner was suspended twice
firstly on 26.07.2013 and then on 
09.03.2015. Pursuant to earlier suspension 
order dated 26.07.2013, charge sheet was 
filed on 13.12.2013 and nothing was found 
against the petitioner and, ultimately, he
was reinstated on 14.03.2014.
Thereafter, again the petitioner was 
placed under suspension wherein charge 
sheet was filed on 01.05.2015 and thereafter 
on 01.05.2018 the petitioner was terminated 
from service against which he preferred an 
appeal which was rejected on 28.11.2018 
and now this petition is filed.
4. It is argued that with same set of 
charge earlier the petitioner was placed 
under suspension and nothing was found 
and he was reinstated but with same set of 
charge again he was placed under 
suspension and now terminated.
5. There is no record with regard to the 
earlier suspension order and the charge 
sheet, therefore, it is not possible at this 
juncture to examine whether on the same set of charge the petitioner was placed under 
suspension. Let Mr. Narain Dutt, learned 
Standing Counsel may produce the entire 
record on the next date.
6. Put up this matter on 30.11.2026.
