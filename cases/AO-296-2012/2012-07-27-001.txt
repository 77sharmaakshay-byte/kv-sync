MCC No. 598/12 (Recall Application)
IN
AO No. 296/2012
Hon’ble V.K. Bist, J.
 Mr. Vinay Kumar, Standing Counsel for 
the applicant/State. 
 Mr. Hari Mohan Bhatia, Advocate for 
respondents. 
 Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
 On 17.07.2012, 48 hours time was granted 
to the counsel for the State to remove the 
defects pointed out by the Registry. It was also 
observed in the order that in case, defects are 
not removed within the stipulated time, the 
appeal shall stand dismissed without reference 
to the Bench. In compliance of that order, the 
defects were not removed within the stipulated 
time and the appeal stood dismissed without 
reference of the Bench. 
 Now instant application has been moved 
on behalf of the applicant/State seeking recall 
of the order dated 17.07.2012 on the ground 
that the matter relates to Uttarkashi and decree 
was to be obtained from District Court, 
Dehradun due to which efforts could not be 
made in order to remove the defects. 
 I have perused the order sheet. The 
appeal was filed on 28th June, 2012, thereafter 
time upto 5th July, 2012 was given for removing 
the defects and time was again extended upto 
10.07.2012. When the matter came on board on 
17.01.2012 again time was granted for removing 
the defects. It appears that the applicant/ 
appellant is not, at all, serious in the matter. In 
these circumstance, I was inclined to dismiss 
the recall application, but considering the 
veracity of the matter and in the interest of 
justice, I allow the recall application subject to 
payment of the cost of ` 2,000/-(Rs. Two 
thousand) to be paid to the High Court Legal Services Committee within a period of one week 
from today. Counsel for the applicant/appellant 
shall produce a copy of receipt in the Registry 
within ten days from today. 
 In case, cost so imposed, is not paid, this 
order shall stand recalled and incase, cost is 
made absolute within the timeframe, the 
appellant is granted time upto 3rd August, 2012 
to remove the defects. Further, in case cost is 
paid and defects are removed, the appeal be 
listed for orders on 4th August, 2012. 
 (V.K. Bist, J.) 
 27.07.2012
