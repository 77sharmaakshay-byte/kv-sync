COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 1088 of 2021
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Shobhit Saharia, learned 
counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Atul Bahuguna, learned 
Standing Counsel for the respondents.
3. The petitioner is aggrieved by the 
order dated 07.05.2021 passed by the 
Commandant as well as the order dated 
08.06.2021 passed by the Appellate 
Authority.
4. By invoking Section 74(1) of the 
Sashastra Seema Bal, 2007 (hereinafter 
referred to as the Act) on 18.11.2020, 
court of inquiry was ordered against the 
petitioner and thereafter after invoking 
Section 74 (2) of the Act, he was 
declared “Deserter”, and, subsequently, 
two show cause notices were given to 
the petitioner but no reply was filed, 
thereafter the petitioner was dismissed 
from service pursuant to order dated 
07.05.2021 by invoking the power 
vested under Section 11(2) of the SSB 
Act 2017 read with Rule 179 of the 
Sashastra Seema Bal Rules 2009.
5. Mr. Shobhit Saharia, learned 
counsel for the petitioner submits that on 
18.11.2020, court of inquiry was ordered 
but without concluding the court of 
inquiry by invoking Section 74(2) of the 
Act, the petitioner was declared as 
deserter which is not permissible. It is 
argued that once the court of inquiry is order it should come to some conclusion 
whether the petitioner was absent or 
not.
6. Mr. Atul Bahuguna, learned 
Standing Counsel for the respondents 
seeks three days’ time to produce the 
outcome of the court of inquiry which 
was ordered on 18.11.2020.
7. Put up this matter on 20.11.2024 
after fresh.
