Hon’ble Vipin Sanghi, C.J.
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J. 
Mr. Tarun Mohan, learned counsel for 
the appellant. 
Mr. Pradeep Joshi, learned Additional 
Chief Standing Counsel for the State / 
respondent Nos. 2 and 3. 
Mr. Vinay Kumar, learned counsel for 
respondent No. 4. 
We have heard learned counsels at 
some length. 
In our view, it is essential to implead All 
India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) 
as a party respondent to clarify whether the 
diploma obtained by the appellant from the 
India Air Force could be considered as 
equivalent qualification for the purpose of 
recruitment to the post of Regional Inspector 
(Transport). 
Let the appellant take steps to file the 
amended memo of parties, and for issuance 
of notice to AICTE, returnable on 
10.05.2023. 
In the meantime, we direct the 
respondent State to file its affidavit clearly 
stating whether the qualification of the appellant is considered as equivalent 
qualification for the purpose of the aforesaid 
recruitment. 
The affidavit be filed by the State within 
three weeks. 
List the matter on 10.05.2023. 
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.) (Vipin Sanghi, C.J.)
