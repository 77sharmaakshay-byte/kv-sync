Learned counsel for respondent No. 1 
submits that the appeal is barred by 
limitation. This submission is premised on 
the fact that the impugned order was passed
in the writ petition on 10.06.2022. 
The appellant had preferred a review 
application, which was dismissed on 
21.07.2022, where after the present appeal 
has been preferred. 
In the aforesaid circumstances, even if 
there is a technical delay in filing of the 
present appeal, we are inclined to condone 
the same.
