2. This intra-court appeal is directed against the judgment 
and order dated 10.06.2022 rendered by learned Single Judge 
in WPSS No.1523 of 2018. By the said judgment, appellant's 
claim for appointment to the post of Regional Inspector
(Technical) in Transport Department was rejected. The appellant 
has also challenged the order dated 21.07.2022, whereby the 
application for recall of the judgment was dismissed. Paragraph 
nos.15 to 20 of said judgment are relevant, which are 
reproduced below:-
“16. In the educational qualification, as extracted above, the 
eligibility for holding a Diploma in Engineering, is provided under 
sub clause (kha) of sub clause (1) of clause 4, which provides that 
a technical educational qualification or education of diploma, 
would be treated to be eligible qualification, which has been 
granted by the State Technical Education Board. Since the 
diploma, which the petitioner contends that he was having was 
issued by the India Air Force, it cannot be treated as to be in 
parlance, to satisfy the qualification of sub clause (kha) of sub 
clause (1) of clause 4 of the advertisement. Hence, rejection of 
the candidature of the petitioner, since he has not supplied the 
mark-sheet of the diploma, coupled with the fact, that the UKHC010120632022

17. The reason behind it is that apart from the fact that 
educational qualification, as contained in List 3 of Schedule 7 of 
the Constitution of India, the technical education and the 
constitution of the State Technical Education Board, as 
referred to under clause (kha) of sub clause (1) of Clause 4 of the 
advertisement, has had to be read in the context of the 
Uttaranchal Board of Technical Education Act of 2003. This 
Act was enacted in order to provide for establishment and 
constitution of Technical Board in Uttaranchal for the matters 
connected therewith or incidental thereto. 
18. On the relevant consideration, for the purpose of deciding the 
present controversy, petitioner contends that he is qualified as per 
clause 4 of the advertisement, is not acceptable for the reason 
being that the Act of 2003 under Section 2(a) defines the 
technical education which reads as under:-
“2. Definitions.- In this Act, unless the context otherwise 
requires:-
(a) “Technical Education” means the 
technical/professional education imparted by various 
technical institutions and managing the teaching and 
training upto diploma (2 to 3 years), degree and post 
graduate level (3 to 4 years) duration in various disciplines 
viz. Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Chemical Engineering, 
Electronics, Instrumentation, Information Technology, 
Architecture, Computer Science & Engineering, Pharmacy, 
Textile Designing, Interior Design and Decoration, Office 
Management and other diversified professional/engineering 
courses approved by AICTE and recognized by the 
Uttaranchal Board of Technical Education and concerned 
University.”
19. It simplicitor lays that the technical professional education, 
would be the technical education, which is imparted by the 
institutions recognised by the Board, and that too which is the 
course conducted for 2 to 3 years and that too and since under 
the Act under sub Section (a) of Section 2, the technical education 
has had to be of 3 years from a recognised Institution and the 
qualification prescribed in the advertisement itself when it 
provided for 3 years diploma, obviously the petitioner will not fall 
within the zone of consideration admittedly for the reason:-
(i) That the diploma obtained by the petitioner, was not 
from a recognised Institution and the diploma, which he 
placed reliance was a diploma, which was not for a period of 
3 years of qualification, but rather the petitioner had 
obtained the said diploma from the Indian Air Force, after

20. Apart from it, clause 4 of the advertisement, as already 
discussed provide the diploma qualification to be from State 
Technical Education Board, the diploma issued by the Air Force,

3. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that appellant 
was employed as Sergeant in Motor Unit of Indian Air Force,
who retired after serving for 20 years, on 08.05.2017. He 
submits that Indian Air Force granted Diploma in Mechanical 
Engineering to the appellant and qualification needed for 
appointment as Regional Inspector (Technical) in Transport 
Department is also a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering, 
therefore, appellant could not be non-suited for want of 
requisite educational qualification.
4. Ms. Urmila Mehta, learned counsel holding brief for Mr. 
Yogesh Kumar Pacholia, learned counsel for AICTE, however, 
submits that the Diploma possessed by appellant, issued by 
Indian Air Force, is not recognized by All India Council for 
Technical Education.
5. Mr. Pankaj Miglani, learned counsel appearing for the 
Selecting Body / Uttarakhand Public Service Commission
submits that, as per advertisement, only a candidate who 
possesses Diploma in Mechanical or Automobile Engineering,
issued by State Technical Education Board, or any qualification UKHC010120632022
2026:UHC:10773-DB
recognized as equivalent thereto, alone is eligible for 
appointment. He submits that since the qualification possessed 
by the appellant is not recognized as equivalent to Diploma in 
Mechanical Engineering / Automobile Engineering by the State 
Government, and moreover that Diploma is not recognized by 
AICTE, therefore, the appellant was rightly not considered for 
appointment. Thus, he submits that the view taken by learned 
Single Judge cannot be faulted.
6. We find substance in the submissions made by learned 
counsel for the respondents. 
7. Law is well settled that one must possess the qualification 
mentioned in the advertisement for appointment to a post. 
Admittedly, the Diploma possessed by the appellant, which was 
issued by Indian Air Force, does not confirm to the requirement 
mentioned in the advertisement, as it was not issued by the 
State Technical Education Board, nor such Diploma possessed 
by the appellant was recognized as equivalent by the State 
Government to a Diploma issued by the State Technical 
Education Board. Even otherwise also, All India Council for 
Technical Education (AICTE) is the regulatory body for technical 
courses in India and, any diploma in engineering course, which 
is not recognised by AICTE, cannot by treated to be valid for 
appointment. Thus, the view taken by learned Single Judge 
cannot be faulted. The writ petition fails and is accordingly 
dismissed.
