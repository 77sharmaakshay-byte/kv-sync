I.A. No.16777 of 2022 (Delay Condonation Appl.)
MCC No. 16776 of 2022 (Restoration Appl.)
In 
WPSS No.2283 of 2016
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J.
Mr. Tapan Singh, Advocate for the
petitioner.
There is no representation for the 
respondent. 
There is delay of 1173 days in filing 
the restoration application. 
Reasons furnished in the affidavit filed 
in support of delay condonation application 
are sufficient to condone the delay. 
Consequently, the delay condonation 
application is allowed and the delay in filing 
the restoration application is condoned.
Having heard, though I am inclined to 
allow the restoration application, but it 
cannot be an unconditional order.
Subject to payment of cost of 
` 2,000/- by the petitioner to Uttarakhand 
High Court Lawyers Welfare Fund, to be 
deposited within two weeks from today, the 
restoration application will stand allowed 
and the order dated 13.08.2019 passed by 
this Court is recalled and the writ petition is 
restored to its original number.
List this matter on 18.04.2023.
