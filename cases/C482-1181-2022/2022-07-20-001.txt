20.07.2022 C 482 No.1181 of 2022
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J. t
This is an application under Section 
482 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 
1973 (hereinafter referred to as “the 
Code”) to set-aside the impugned order 
dated 12.04.2022, passed by the First 
Judicial Magistrate, Dehradun in Criminal 
Case No. 4893 of 2017, “Chanchal 
Kumar Vs. Satish Kannojiya”, under 
Section 138 of the Negotiable 
Instruments Act, 1881, by which, the 
application, filed by the present 
applicant-accused under Section 91 of 
the Code with request to summon the 
account statement of the father of the 
complainant of State Stock Holding 
Corporation of India, has been 
dismissed. 
Heard Mr. Aditya Singh, learned 
counsel for the applicant.
The learned counsel for the 
applicant submitted that according to the 
complainant himself, he works as Stock 
Vendor using his father’s licence and in 
his cross examination, he stated that he 
had lent the disputed money to the 
present applicant from the amount, 
collected by the sale of the stamp papers 
and at the stage of defence, the present 
applicant-accused moved the said 
application under Section 91 of the Code, 
which was dismissed by the learned trial 
court. 
Issue notice to the respondent no. 
2.
Steps to be taken within four days.
List this matter on 18.08.2022.Till then, the further proceedings of 
the said Criminal Case No. 4893 of 2017 
are stayed. 
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.) 20.07.2022
