4. Mr. Arvind Vashisth, learned Senior 
Advocate, submits that in the present writ 
petition, an order has been passed by a 
Co-ordinate Bench on 24.03.2026, wherein 
Mr. D.K. Sharma, learned Senior Advocate 
appearing for the petitioners, on instructions, 
undertook that petitioners are ready to hand 
over the keys of the property which they barged 
into after getting protection order from this 
Court.
5. Learned Senior Advocate appearing for 
respondent no.8 submits that, in the writ 
petition, a categorical averment has been made 
by petitioners that they are not in possession of the property and had sought relief of possession 
from this Court in the writ petition. However, 
only protection for persons was granted. He also 
submits that, despite the undertaking given in 
the month of March 2026, for the last seven 
months, the keys have not been handed over for 
one pretext and the other.
6. In view of the above, list on 29.10.2026, on 
which date, petitioners shall remain physically 
present along with the keys of the property so 
that the order dated 24.03.2026 can be 
complied with.
