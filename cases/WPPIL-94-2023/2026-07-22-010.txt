for the State. 
4. The specific case of the petitioner is 
that the proposed site for setting up of Solid 
Waste Management Plant and disposal 
facility, which is also proposed to be used as 
landfill facility, would be within prohibited 
distance from river Ratnal, a tributary of 
river Malan. However, in the counter affidavit 
filed by the respondents, they have not 
given any clarification with regard to river Ratnal. Only averments have been made in 
respect of river Malan.
5. Learned State Counsel as well as 
learned counsel for the Nagar Nigam pray 
for and are granted three weeks’ time to 
clarify the said aspect by filing their 
affidavits.
6. We further direct the District 
Magistrate, Pauri Garhwal-respondent no.3 
to constitute a team of revenue officials to 
inspect the site and find out the distance of 
the site from river Ratnal. The survey report 
shall be placed before us on the next date of 
hearing through the State Counsel. 
7. List after three weeks.
