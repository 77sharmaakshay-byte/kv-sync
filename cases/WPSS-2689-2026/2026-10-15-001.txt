3. By the instant petition, the petitioners 
are praying for the following relief:-
(i) Issue a writ order in the nature of 
certiorari quashing the 28.04.2018 passed 
by respondent no. 2 (contained as Annexure 
No. 2 page no. 36-42 to this writ petition).
(ii) Issue writ order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing respondents 
not to make any recovery from the 
petitioners in pursuance to the 
order/communication dated 28.04.2028 as 
directed by this Hon’ble court in WPSS No. 
990 of 2018 vide judgment dated 
06.11.2023 (contained as Annexure No. 3, 
page no. 43-47 to this writ petition).
4. It is submitted by the learned counsel 
for the petitioners that the issue as raised is 
squarely covered by the judgment passed by 
the Coordinate Bench dated 06.11.2026, in 
WPSS No. 990 of 2018, Manoj Kumar vs. 
State of Uttarakhand and others, whereby 
bunch of petitions have been allowed.
5. Let the State may get instructions 
whether the issue as raised is covered by the aforesaid judgment or not.
6. Put up this matter on 03.11.2026.
7. In the meantime, no recovery shall be 
made.
