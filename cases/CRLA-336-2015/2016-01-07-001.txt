Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J. (Oral) 
We have heard Mr. Ramji Srivastava, learned 
counsel on behalf of both the convicts / applicants. 
Accused/applicants Shivraj and Tinku @ Vinod are 
seeking bail during the course of the hearing of the 
appeal. Although, application on behalf of accused Tinku 
@ Vinod has been moved by another counsel Mr. Karan 
Anand but we feel that allegation of assailing the deceased 
victim has been stated in the first information report for 
the same incident, hence, we are disposing of both these 
bail applications by hearing learned counsel Mr. Ramji 
Srivastava alone. 
The incident occurred a day before Deepawali 
festival in November, 2010, when accused / applicants 
alongwith two others were indulged in playing cards near 
the house of informant Aakash. They were asked not to 
play cards by the informant, his father Suresh and uncle 
Rakesh as well as Akshay S/o Rakesh. Having been 
asked as stated above, accused persons enraged and 
began to abuse the informant and others. It has been 2
alleged that applicant Shivraj, having khunkari in his 
hand, while applicant Tinku @ Vinod, having sword in his 
hand, assaulted the victims which made them suffer 
serious injuries and, ultimately, injured Rakesh Kumar 
succumbed to such injuries. 
Mr. Ramji Srivastava, learned counsel, on behalf of 
the accused/applicants, has drawn the attention of this 
Court towards the sharp discrepancies in the medical 
examination conduced by doctors who attended the 
injured persons in the hospital as well as the injuries 
which were noticed on the body of the same deceased in 
the autopsy. It has been argued that PW4 Dr. V.S. Tolia 
conducted the post mortem and has found as many as five 
incised wounds on the scapula region as well as in the 
right elbow of the arm. While, in the medical report, PW6 
Dr. S.K. Jah has noticed only one lacerated wound in the 
elbow of the right arm and no other injuries he has 
adverted in his such report. It has been argued that, in 
any case, all these injuries were not enough to infer the 
intention of the assailants to cause the death of the victim. 
Nonetheless, there is a sharp contraction in the reports of 
doctor duo. It has been alleged that one of the applicant 
Shivraj is in caption ever since the date of his arrest since 
November, 2010 till date and applicant Tinku @ Vinod, 
another convict, was on bail during the course of trial. 
Looking to the above facts and circumstances of the 
case, we feel that bail applications should be allowed. 
The convicts /applicants are granted bail on furnishing 
their personal bonds and two two sureties each of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of learned Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Dehradun. 
CRMA Nos. 2073 of 2015 and 1689 of 2015 stand 
allowed accordingly.
