Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. D.C.S. Rawat, learned 
counsel for the revisionist.
2. Present criminal revision has 
been preferred against the judgment 
and order dated 04.05.2026 passed by 
the learned Additional Principal Judge, 
Family Court, Haldwani, District 
Nainital in Misc. Criminal Case No. 
160 of 2025, whereby the learned 
Family Court allowed the interim 
maintenance application filed by the 
respondent and directed the revisionist 
to pay interim maintenance of 
₹35,000/- per month to the 
respondent from the date of filing of 
the maintenance application, i.e., 
12.04.2025.
3. Learned counsel for the 
revisionist would submit that, after 
two months of filing of the application 
under Section 144 B.N.S.S., the 
respondent was appointed as an 
Ayurvedic Medical Officer and has 
been serving on the said post 
thereafter, but the said fact was 
deliberately concealed from the Court. 
He would further submit that the 
revisionist filed objections to the 
maintenance application on the said 
ground; that, thereafter, the 
respondent filed her affidavit to this effect; however, the court below, 
without considering the fact that the 
respondent is gainfully employed and 
capable of maintaining herself, passed 
the impugned order granting interim 
maintenance in her favour.
4. Heard learned counsel for the 
revisionist.
5. Admit.
6. Issue notice to the respondent, 
returnable at an early date.
7. Steps to be taken within one 
week.
8. Lower Court Record be 
summoned.
9. List after service of notice is 
received.
10. Meanwhile, as an interim 
measure, the effect and operation of 
the impugned order dated 04.05.2026 
passed by the learned Additional 
Principal Judge, Family Court, 
Haldwani, District Nainital in Misc. 
Criminal Case No. 160 of 2025 shall 
remain stayed till the next date of 
listing.
11. Stay application stands disposed 
of accordingly.
