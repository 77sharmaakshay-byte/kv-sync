6. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
7. By means of this writ petition, petitioner has
challenged the orders dated 19.08.2016, 07.05.2019, 
12.01.2022 and 01.08.2024, annexure-16, 18, 20 & 28, 
respectively to the writ petition. 
8. The dispute is with regard to the property which was 
devolved upon the Vakeela, widow of Late Gafoor, after 
his death, who according to the learned Senior Advocate 
for the petitioner died issueless. The petitioner claims 
inheritance over the property of Vakeela, being her second 
husband and brother of her husband Gafoor.
9. However, learned counsel for respondent no.1/1 
made an objection to the submission made by learned 
Senior Advocate for the petitioner saying that the property 
is devolved to the Vakeela under the provisions of Section 
172 of the Uttar Pradesh Zamindari Abolition and Land 2
Reforms Act, 1950 (for short “The Act, 1950”), therefore, 
when she died, the property will be reverted back in 
accordance with the provisions of Section 171 of the Act, 
1950 and the petitioner, who is second husband of the 
Vakeela, is nowhere in the list of decedents. 
10. Since the issue involved in the writ petition requires 
deliberation, therefore, the counter affidavit is required. 
11. Respondents are directed to file counter affidavits 
within a period of six weeks.
12. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the 
petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
13. List on 09.01.2025.
14. In the meantime, parties shall maintained status 
quo, qua the possession of the property, as prevailing 
today, and they shall not create any third party interest.
