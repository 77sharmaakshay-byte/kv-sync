COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.2790 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Vinay Kumar, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned C.S.C. with 
Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Additional C.S.C.
for the State of Uttarakhand/respondents.
3. By means of this writ petition, the 
petitioner has challenged the 
communication dated 10.09.2024 (Ann. 
No.7), whereby, the respondent￾Administrator is going to conduct the 
election of the Uttarakhand Matra Shishu 
Evam Parivar Kalyan, Mahila Karamchari 
Sangh, District Executive, District 
Dehradun.
4. It is contended by the learned counsel 
for the petitioner that the earlier tenure of 
the Association has come to an end on 
16.11.2023 and no election could have 
been held thereafter.
5. It is further contended by him that 
under the bye-laws of the Association, no 
power has been assigned to any 
Administrator to conduct such election, 
therefore, the circulation of programme to 
conduct the election on 15.10.2024 is 
nothing but it exceeds the jurisdiction of 
the Administrator.
6. Per contra, learned C.S.C. for the 
State submitted that the Administrator was 
appointed by a Coordinate Bench of this 
Court vide judgment and order dated 2
13.12.2023 passed in WPMS No.1398 of 
2023 Renu Raturi Vs. State of Uttarakhand 
and Others. The said order speaks in no 
ascertain term that the Administrator shall 
perform all the functions of the 
Association, and, therefore, election is 
rightly being held by the Administrator 
appointed by this Court.
7. Put up on 12.11.2024 along with 
WPMS No.1398 of 2023.
8. In the meantime, the respondents
may conduct the election of Executive 
Body of District Dehradun of the 
Uttarakhand Matra Shishu Evam Parivar 
Kalyan, Mahila Karamchari Sangh, as per 
the schedule. But, the result of the election 
shall not be declared without leave of this 
Court.
9. Let a certified copy of this order be 
supplied to learned counsel for the parties 
during course of the day, as per Rules.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
