Heard. 
The appellant proposes to challenge the 
award dated 22.10.2012, passed in MACP 
No.54 of 2008, Smt. Roshni Devi and Others 
v. Ramswaroop Agarwal, by the court of 
Motor Accident Claim Tribunal/District 
Judge, Pauri Garhwal. By it, the appellants 
have been awarded compensation. It is for 
enhancement.
It is brought to the notice of the Court 
that the respondent no.2 has passed away.
Learned counsel for the appellants 
submits that since the respondent no.2 was 
the owner of the vehicle and the liability is on 
the Insurance Company, he may be 
permitted to deleted the respondent no.2 
from the array of parties. 
Let respondent no.2 be deleted from the 
array of parties. 
Heard on Delay Condonation 
Application, CLMA No.5634 of 2012
It is the case of the appellants that they 
could not get legal advice on time, and in 
that process, delay occurred.
It has been objected to by learned 
counsel for the respondent no.1.
In fact, much delay has been caused by 
not entertaining the delay condonation 
application on time. Almost after 14 years, the delay condonation application is finally 
heard. Therefore, having considered, this 
Court is of the view that the delay in 
preferring the appeal deserves to be 
allowed. 
The delay condonation application is 
allowed. 
The delay in preferring the appeal is 
condoned. 
Heard on admission. 
Admit. 
Let call for the lower court record. 
List this matter for final hearing on 
17.07.2026.
