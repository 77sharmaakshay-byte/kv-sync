3. The instant petition is preferred under Section 
528 of BNSS, 2023 by the applicant – Smt. Renu and 
her minor daughter, challenging the order dated 
10.07.2026 passed by the learned Additional Chief 
Judicial Magistrate, Roorkee, District Haridwar in 
Miscellaneous Case No.226 of 2024, “Anuj Vs. Smt. 
Renu and another”, in a proceeding under Section 25 
(2) of the Protection of Women from Domestic 
Violence Act, 2005 arising out of Miscellaneous Case 
No.717 of 2014, “”Smt. Renu and another Vs. Anuj and 
others”, as well as the proceeding of Miscellaneous 
Application No.226 of 2024, pending in the court of 
learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Roorkee, 
District Haridwar.
4. The brief facts of the case are that the applicant 
no.1 married with the respondent no.2 on 25.04.2012 
and out of the said wedlock, a female child was born 
on 12.04.2013, who is at present about 13 years old 
living with the applicant. It is contended by the 
learned counsel for the applicant that after the 
marriage, the respondent no.2 and his family members 
harass the applicant no.1 on account of dowry.
5. Subsequently, the applicant no.1 initiated the 
proceedings under Section 12 of the Protection of 
Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005, wherein, 
the learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Roorkee by order dated 26.09.2018 pass an order of 
protection and as well as for residence under Sections 
18 and 19 of the Act and directed the respondent no.2 
to pay Rs.500/- each i.e. Rs.1,000/- per month and 
other benefits. Being aggrieved, the respondent no.2 
preferred a Criminal Appeal No.212 of 2018 before the Session Court, which was partly allowed on 
09.08.2021, whereby the order under challenge was 
modified by directing the respondent no.2 to pay 
Rs.5,000/- per month as house rent in lieu of the 
residence order and Rs.2,000/- each i.e. Rs.4,000/- per 
month. 
6. In an another proceeding under Section 125 of 
CrPC, the interim maintenance was also granted, 
which was challenged in Criminal Revision No.56 of 
2017, by the applicant for enhancement and the High 
Court by order dated 07.09.2018, enhanced the amount 
of maintenance and the same was assailed by the 
respondent no.2 before the Hon’ble Apex Court, 
which was also dismissed vide order dated 07.02.2019.
7. It is contended that though the order granting 
maintenance attains finality, the respondent no.2 has 
not clear the arrears due to the applicant. 
Consequently, she has no other option except to file 
the execution application under Section 28 of the 
Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 
2005, the reference of which has been given in 
paragraph 7 of the affidavit. It is contended that 
instead of complying with the order granting 
maintenance and clearing outstanding arrears though 
the respondent-husband has sufficient resources he 
has also filed Criminal Revision No.215 of 2021, 
against the order of the learned Additional Chief 
Judicial Magistrate dated 26.09.2018 and 09.08.2021, 
which is still pending and now the respondent no.2 
filed an application for early disposal of the 
Miscellaneous Application No.226 of 2024, on a plea 
that he is suffering from ill health and remained in jail 
for four months, and the said application is allowed by 
the order impugned for early disposal of 
Miscellaneous Application No.226 of 2024, though he 
has suppressed the material facts while moving this 
application.
8. Though, this Court does not find any merit in 
this petition since by the order impugned only an 
order has been passed to expedite the Miscellaneous 
Application No.226 of 2024, which itself reveals that
only in order to avoid the proceedings this petition is 
filed, However, as apprised by learned counsel for the 
applicant that for almost identical issue a criminal revision is also filed which is pending, therefore, it 
may be clubbed with the same so that matter be 
decided together, and till then the proceeding pending 
before the learned Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate be stayed.
9. After gone through with the record, the dispute 
appears to be a matrimonial one, therefore, in such an 
eventuality this Court is of the view that let the efforts 
be made for their amicable settlement.
10. List this matter along with Criminal Revision 
No.215 of 2021 on 30.09.2026 immediately after fresh.
11. In the meantime, learned counsel for the 
applicant inform to the other side through the counsel 
who is appearing in the court below.
12. Till then, the proceedings of Miscellaneous 
Application No.226 of 2024, pending in the court of 
learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Roorkee, 
District Haridwar, shall remain stayed.
