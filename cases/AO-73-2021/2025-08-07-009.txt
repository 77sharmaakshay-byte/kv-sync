COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
AO No.73 of 2021
Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
Mr. Pulak Aggarwal, learned counsel for 
the appellant.
2. Mr. Pradeep Chamyal, learned counsel for 
respondent nos.1 to 3/claimants.
3. Earlier money withdrawal application on 
behalf of respondent nos.1 to 3 was filed and 
vide order dated 13.08.2021 Co-ordinate 
Bench had directed the MACT to release 25% 
of the total awarded amount i.e. 68,07,005/- in 
favour of respondent nos.1 to 3/claimants.
4. Now, respondent nos.1 to 3/ claimants 
have filed Money Withdrawal Application 
(IA/3/2024) praying for release of the 
remaining amount.
5. It is stated in the accompanying affidavit
to the money withdrawal application filed by 
respondent nos.1 to 3/claimants that 
respondent nos.1, 2 and 3 are wife, daughter 
and son of the deceased respectively; 
respondent no.1 is more than 54 years old lady 
and she is suffering from various old age 
diseases and her regular treatment is going on; 
the claimants/ respondents are suffering from 
financial crises, who are unable to maintain 
themselves and they are in dire need of money 
for their livelihood, hence, they may be permitted to withdraw the entire amount 
deposited before the learned Tribunal. 
6. No objection has been filed to the Money 
Withdrawal Application, however, learned 
counsel for the appellant orally opposes the 
money withdrawal application.
7. Considering the facts and circumstances 
of the case that have been brought on record, 
Money Withdrawal Application is allowed and 
additional 25% out of the total remaining 
amount i.e. the amount remaining after 
withdrawal of earlier 25% amount from 
₹68,07,005/- deposited by the appellant before 
the Tribunal is ordered to be released in favour 
of respondent nos.1 to 3/ claimants, as per 
their respective share, within three weeks from 
the date of presentation of the certified copy of 
this order.
8. List on 17.10.2025.
9. Misc. Application (IA/5/2025) stands 
disposed of.
 (Subhash Upadhyay,J.)
