COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
 
AO No.73 of 2021
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. H.C. Pande, Advocate for the appellant.
Instant appeal is preferred against the 
judgment and award dated 22.01.2021, passed in 
Motor Accident Claim No.288 of 2017, Smt. Swarn 
Kaur and others vs. Devendra Pal Singh and another, 
by the Motor Accident Claims Tribunal/Additional 
District Judge, Khatima, District Udham Singh 
Nagar.
Heard.
Admit.
Issue notices to the respondents. 
Summon the LCR.
Heard on Stay Application (IA) No.1 of 2021.
Learned counsel for the appellant would 
submit that it is a case filed under collusion; the 
vehicle which according to the claimants hits the 
deceased has falsely been planted to get the 
insurance; FIR was lodged after 27 days; there is no 
medical prescriptions which may reveal that the 
death was by accident, even the postmortem does not 
reveal that it was a case of road accident. 
Be it as it may, this Court does not see any 
reason to stay the operation of the judgment and 
award. Appellant shall deposit the amount, which 
shall be invested in a recurring term deposit in some 
nationalized bank. The amount shall not be released 
without prior permission of this Court. 
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.) 09.04.2021
