COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
IA No.02 of 2021 (Money Withdrawal Application)
In
AO No.73 of 2021
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
(Via video conferencing).
Mr. H.C. Pande, Advocate, for the 
appellant.
Mr. Pradeep Chamyal, Advocate, for the 
respondent. 
As a result of the accident, which has 
chanced on 13.08.2017, the husband of the 
respondent No.1, Late Mr. Jaswant Singh, has 
met with the sad demise. On the culmination of 
the proceeding of MACP Case No.288 of 2017, 
which was rendered by an award dated 
22.01.2021, the total amount of compensation, 
which has been determined to be paid was on 
the basis of the salary drawn by the 
deceased/Jaswant Singh, who was of 53 years 
of age, at that relevant point of time, working as 
an Assistant Teacher. The compensation was 
assessed to be made payable to the tune of 
Rs.68,07,005/-.
This Court while admitting the appeal 
from order on 09.04.2021, had directed the 
appellant to deposit the entire amount, before 
the MACT concerned, and the same has been deposited in compliance thereto, along with an 
interest.
Today the matter is listed on the money 
withdrawal application.
Considering the fact that the widow and 
the children i.e. respondent Nos.1, 2 and 3 
herein, in order to sustain themselves in life
after the death of the only breadwinner, it 
would be in the interest of justice that 25% of 
the total awarded amount i.e. Rs.68,07,005/-, is 
permitted to be withdrawn by the respondent 
No.1, herein.
Accordingly, the money withdrawal 
application would stand disposed of with a 
direction to the MACT Tribunal/Additional 
District Judge, Khatima, District Udham Singh 
Nagar, before whom the money stands 
deposited, to remit the 25% of the awarded 
amount to the respondent No.1 to 3, herein, 
within a period of six weeks from the date of 
the presentation of the certified copy of this 
judgment. 
Accordingly, the money withdrawal 
application stands disposed of.
List this appeal from order in the last 
week of September, 2021.
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
