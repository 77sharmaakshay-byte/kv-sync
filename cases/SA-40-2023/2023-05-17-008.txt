SA No.40 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
An Original Suit (No.354 of 1998) was filed 
by respondent – plaintiff for perpetual injunction 
to restrain the appellants – defendants from 
installing a gate on her way. Right of way was 
granted by a sale deed dated 04.05.1993. 
Thereafter a correction deed dated 13.06.2006 
was executed. During the pendency of the said 
original suit, according to the plaint, defendants 
had installed a gate on the said way, therefore, 
while amending the plaint, relief of mandatory 
injunction was also sought by the respondent –
plaintiff. The said Original Suit was decreed. First 
Appeal, filed under Section 96 of the Code of Civil 
Procedure, 1908, has been dismissed. Hence, 
proposed Second Appeal has been filed. 
2. Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for the 
appellants.
3. Mr. B.P. Nautiyal, learned Senior Advocate 
(through video conferencing) assisted by Mr. 
Mohd. Matlub, learned counsel for the respondent.
4. Mr. Aditya Singh, Advocate, contended that 
the correction deed dated 13.06.2006 has not 
been proved in terms of Section 91 of the Indian 
Evidence Act, 1872.
5. On the other hand, learned Senior Advocate 
has submitted that contents of correction deed need not be proved in terms of Section 91 of the 
Indian Evidence Act, 1872.
6. After considering the submissions of learned 
counsel for the parties and in the facts and 
circumstances of this case, it seems appropriate to 
decide the rival contentions of both the parties 
after framing substantial question of law. 
Therefore, present Second Appeal is admitted on 
the following substantial question of law:-
“whether the learned courts below erred in 
law in not appreciating that bar under 
Section 92 of the Indian Evidence Act would 
only apply once document is proved in terms 
of Section 91 of the Indian Evidence Act?”.
7. List on 26.07.2023 for final hearing. 
8. Meanwhile, the effect and operation of the 
judgment and decree dated 24.12.2022, passed 
by learned IVth Additional District Judge, Dehradun 
in Civil Appeal No.71 of 2011 are stayed.
