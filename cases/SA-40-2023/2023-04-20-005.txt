COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
20.04.2023 SA No.40 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Respondent – plaintiff had filed an 
Original Suit No. 354 of 1998 for perpetual 
injunction to prevent the appellants –
defendants from installing a gate on her 
way. The right of way was granted by 
correction sale-deed. During the pendency 
of original suit, according to the respondent 
– plaintiff, defendants installed a gate on 
the said way, therefore, while amending the 
plaint, relief of mandatory injunction was 
also sought by the respondent – plaintiff. 
The original suit was decreed and first 
appeal has been dismissed. Hence the 
proposed Second Appeal.
2. Heard Mr. Aditya Singh, learned 
counsel for the appellants and Mr. B.P. 
Nautiyal, learned Senior Advocate assisted 
by Mr. Manokam Nautiyal, learned counsel 
for the respondent.
3. Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for 
the appellants submits that the said 
correction deed has not been proved.
4. On the other hand, learned Senior 
Advocate submitted that the said correction 
deed has been duly proved by the plaintiff.
5. In the facts and circumstances of the case, it would be appropriate to summon 
the original record before passing any order 
on Admission.
6. Summon the original record.
List on 08.05.2023, as a fresh case. 
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
