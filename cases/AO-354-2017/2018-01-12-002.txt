 This is money withdrawal 
application CLMA No.615 of 2018 moved 
by the claimant with a prayer to permit 
the applicant/claimant to withdraw the 
total amount deposited by the appellant 
before the Tribunal. 
 The Court finds that the Coordinate 
Bench, vide order dated 21.06.2017, had 
directed the appellant to deposit 50% of 
the total awarded amount, adjusting the 
compulsory statutory amount. Statutory 
amount has also been deposited by the 
appellant in this appeal. 
 Let the statutory amount be 
remitted to the Tribunal concerned. 
 For the reasons stated, money 
withdrawal application is allowed. 
 The amount deposited by the 
appellant shall be released by the 
Tribunal in favour of 
respondent/claimant.
