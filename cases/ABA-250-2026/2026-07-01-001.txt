Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Rajat Mittal, Advocate for the applicant.
2. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned D.A.G. alongwith 
Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned A.G.A. for the State.
3. The present applicant is praying for 
anticipatory bail in connection with FIR No. 0277 
of 2026, under Section 108 of BNS, registered at 
Police Station–Kotwali Haridwar, District–
Haridwar.
4. In this case, an FIR was lodged by father of 
the deceased in which it was alleged that the 
applicant and the other co-accused were running 
a Committee and have confiscated the jewellery 
and Rs. 15 Lakhs of the deceased and were not 
returning back the same to her and when she 
asked for returning back her jewellery and 
money, they threatened the deceased to face dire 
consequences, therefore, the deceased was left 
with no other option but to consume poison, due 
to which, she subsequently died.
5. Learned counsel for the applicant would 
submit that even if the averments of the FIR are 
taken to be true on its face value, then also, no 
act of instigating the deceased to commit suicide 
was assigned to the present applicant as the 
chain of events, as per the FIR, would reveal that deceased was allegedly harassed by the accused 
persons on 19.05.2026 and the deceased 
consumed poison on 21.05.2026; that she was 
first taken to the Government Hospital and 
thereafter to a Nursing Home at Jwalapur, where, 
she ultimately died. It is submitted by learned 
counsel for the applicant that during this period, 
neither she gave any statement to the Police or to 
the Hospital Authorities, nor any complaint was 
made in this regard.
6. Learned State Counsel, on the other hand, 
vehemently opposed the anticipatory bail 
application.
7. Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the record.
8. This Court finds some substance in the 
submission made by learned counsel for the 
applicant and having considered the 
submissions, this Court is of the view that all 
these aspects require deep scrutiny.
9. Let the respondent-State may file counter 
affidavit within four weeks. Two weeks, thereafter, 
is granted to the applicant to file rejoinder 
affidavit. 
10. List this matter on 03.08.2026.
11. In the meantime, as an interim measure, 
without expressing any opinion on the merit of 
the case, the applicant shall be released on an 
interim anticipatory bail, subject to furnishing a 
personal bond with two reliable sureties, each of 
the like amount, to the satisfaction of the 
Arresting Officer/Trial Court subject to the 
following conditions:-
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation;
(ii) The applicant shall not approach any 
witness in any manner, whatsoever.
