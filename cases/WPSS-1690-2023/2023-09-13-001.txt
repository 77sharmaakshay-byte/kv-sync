Mr. Harendra Belwal, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Sachin Mohan Singh Mehta, learned 
Brief Holder for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. The petitioner sought indulgence of this 
Court by issuance of a direction to the respondents 
to treat the services of the petitioner as regular 
Anurakshak/Beldar with the respondents￾department from the date of his initial appointment 
i.e. 29.03.2007 under the Dying in Harness Rules.
4. It is the contention of the petitioner that his 
father, who was a work-charge Beldar with the 
respondents department, died on 08.12.2006 and he
was appointed as Beldar on the daily rated basis 
under the Dying in Harness Rules in place of his 
father on 29.03.2007.
5. It is the further contention of the petitioner 
that while giving appointment to the petitioner on 
compassionate ground instead of giving regular 
appointment, he was appointed as a daily rated 
employee and he was regularise on the post of 
Beldar vide order dated 24.03.2018. Now, he seeks 
to treat him a regular employee w.e.f. his initial 
date of engagement i.e. 29.03.2007.
6. Learned State Counsel prays for and is 
granted six weeks’ time to file counter affidavit. 
Two weeks’ time thereafter is granted for filing 
rejoinder affidavit.
