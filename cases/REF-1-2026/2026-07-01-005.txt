This criminal reference is second time 
before this Court. Earlier, the 
appellant-condemned prisoner, was 
sentenced to death by the court of Additional 
Sessions Judge, Tehri Garhwal in Sessions 
Trial No.2 of 2015 on 21.08.2021. That 
reference was registered as Reference No.2 of 
2021 and the appeal filed by the appellant
was registered as Criminal Appeal No.441 of 
2021. That criminal reference, in the earlier 
round, was decided on 10.05.2022, when the 
impugned judgment and order was set aside 
and the sessions court was directed to first 
make an enquiry and try the issue of mental 
illness of the appellant first and proceed in 
accordance with Section 329 of the Code of 
Criminal Procedure, 1973 (“the Code”).
After remand by this Court, by the 
impugned judgment dated 24.02.2026, again,
the appellant has been sentenced to death. 
In the reference, this Court has 
requested Mr. Arvind Vashistha, SeniorAdvocate to assist the Court.
Argument began with regard to the 
compliance of Section 329 of Code. It was 
argued that even post remand of the case by 
this Court on 10.05.2022, the procedure as 
stipulated under Section 329 of the Code has 
not been complied with. 
In fact, perusal of the order reveals that 
in the case, 22.01.2016 was fixed for 
examination of the appellant under Section 
313 of the Code, but on that date, the court 
noted that the appellant is not answering the 
court’s query, therefore, he was sent for his 
medical treatment and on that date, the 
Court noted that “the court will take decision 
later on for recording the statements under 
Section 313 of the Cr.P.C. only after knowing 
the mental and health condition of the 
accused-Sanjay Singh”. 
It appears from the record that on 
12.02.2021, when the appellant again 
appeared in the court and requested that he 
should be released. On that date, the court 
noted the questions that were asked to the 
appellant and his response and found that he 
is rational in answering the questions and in 
fact, he was aware of the date when he was 
arrested. Therefore, the court further got a 
report from panel of doctors from AIIMS, 
Rishikesh. A report dated 20.03.2021 of 
AIIMS, Rishikesh was received by the Court. 
Thereafter, on 31.03.2021, the Court noted 
that as per report of the doctors of AIIMS, the 
appellant is fit for trial. He was able to 
understand the court’s proceedings and 
resumption of trial was done. 
In fact, this Court in the earlier round of 
reference on 10.05.2022 had recorded in para 
17 that “in such a situation, the court should 
have followed the procedure laid down under 
Section 329 of the Code read with Section 
105 of the Mental Healthcare Act. As the 
learned Trial Judge has not followed the procedure described, the entire trial is 
vitiated, and hence, the appeal has to be 
allowed.”
The factual narration as even 
hereinbefore reveals that on 22.01.2016, the 
trial of the case was postponed due to mental 
health reason of the appellant. On 
12.02.2021, the appellant was questioned. 
He sought his release. He was referred to a
panel of doctors, AIIMS, Rishikesh. A panel
of doctors AIIMS, Rishikesh submitted their 
report on 20.03.2021 and thereafter, the trial 
resumed on 31.03.2022. Is not it a procedure 
under Section 329 of the Code? What is its
effect?
