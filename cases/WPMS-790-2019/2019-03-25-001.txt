WPMS No.790 of 2019 
Hon’ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
 
 Mr. Sarvesh Agarwal, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
 This writ petition has been filed to set 
aside the impugned order dated 02.03.2019 
passed by learned Permanent Lok Adalat, 
Udham Singh Nagar in case no.24/2018 
Smt. Vinita Kumar vs. Dr. Meenu Sood. 
 Admit. 
 Issue notice to the respondent, fixing 
06.05.2019. 
 Steps to be taken within a week. 
 Also heard on the stay application. 
 It is the contention of learned counsel 
for the petitioner that the impugned award 
passed by the court below is illegal 
inasmuch as the matter was not 
maintainable before the Permanent Lok 
Adalat. It is also contended that the court 
below has not passed its judgment on the 
basis of compromise, rather it has decided 
the case on merit. 
 Having considered the submissions of 
learned counsel for the petitioner, as an 
interim measure, it is directed that the 
effect and operation of the impugned order 
dated 02.03.2019 shall remain stayed till 
the next date of listing. (Stay application 
CLMA No.3725 of 2019 is disposed of). 
 
(Lok Pal Singh, J.)
