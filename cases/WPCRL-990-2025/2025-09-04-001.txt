3. The present writ petition under Article 226 of the 
Constitution of India is filed by the Petitioners for 
quashing the impugned F.I.R. dated 29.08.2025 registered 
as Case Crime No.587 of 2025, for the offence punishable 
under Sections 115(2), 324(2), 333, 351(2) & 352 of BNS,
at P.S. Kotwali Haridwar, District Haridwar and further to 
direct the respondent no.2 to follow the guidelines of the 
Hon’ble Supreme Court as laid down in the case of 
Satender Kumar Antil vs. Central Bureau of Investigation, 
(2022) 10 SCR 351 during the investigation of the present 
matter and to direct the respondent no.2 not to adopt any 
coercive steps against the petitioners and not arrest them in 
pursuance of the aforementioned F.I.R.

5. Admit. 
6. Issue notice to the Respondent no.3, returnable 
within two weeks. Steps to be taken within one week.
7. Objections, if any, to be filed by the Respondents 
within a period of three weeks.
8. List this matter on 16.10.2025.
9. In the interregnum, it is directed that the 
investigation shall proceed but petitioners shall not be arrested by the police in connection with the impugned 
F.I.R. dated 29.08.2025 registered as Case Crime No.587 
of 2025, for the offence punishable under Sections 115(2), 
324(2), 333, 351(2) & 352 of BNS, at P.S. Kotwali 
Haridwar, District Haridwar. However, they will continue 
to cooperate with the Investigating Officer of the case and 
shall remain present, as and when required for production 
of documents and for recording of their statements.

disposed of accordingly.
