COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.1325 of 2021
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate, for the 
petitioner.
Mr. P.C. Bisht, Additional CSC, with 
Ms. Indu Sharma, Brief Holder, for the State of 
Uttarakhand/1 and 2.
The brief facts of the case are that the 
petitioners contend, that the respondents have
proceeded with to issue an advertisement on 
02.03.2019, for filling of the post of veterinary 
pharmacists. The total posts, which were
proposed and shown to be advertised, to be 
filled in, in pursuance to the said selection 
process was declared as 18 posts, which were 
advertised, and which didn’t included any post 
which could be made available for the 
Scheduled Tribes candidates. 
The grievance of the petitioners is that 
after the culmination of the selection process, 
the result was declared on 29.09.2021, and in 
the result, thus declared by the respondent 
No.2, as against the total 18 posts, which were
originally advertised, total 53 appointments 
have been proposed to be made in pursuance to 
the conclusion of the said selection process.
The petitioners contends that the 
proposed recruitment of the candidates in 
excess to the post advertised, runs contrary to 
the two judgments, on which the learned 
counsel for the petitioners place reliance i.e. the 
judgments rendered by the Division Bench of 
this Court on 10.05.2012, and that of the 
Hon’ble Apex Court rendered on 05.10.2016, 
holding thereof that the recruitment could not 
be made on an excess post, to the post than
which has been advertised by the respondents.
In that eventuality, the respondents are 
directed to file their counter affidavit. Let notices be issued to the respondent Nos.3 to 36.
Petitioners would take steps within a period of 
ten days’ from today.
By way of an interim measure, it is 
directed that it will be open for the respondents 
to issue regular appointment letters to the 18 
selected candidates, who are at the top of the 
merit list as it has been published on
29.09.2021, and in an eventuality, if they 
propose to issue any appointment letter, to any 
other candidates, thus, selected and included in 
the select list of 29.09.2021, in excess to the 
post advertised, they would specifically 
mentioned in their letter of appointment, that 
their appointment would abide by the decision 
in the writ petition.
List this writ petition after service of the 
notice on the private respondents.
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
