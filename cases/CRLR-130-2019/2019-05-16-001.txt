Hon’ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
Mr. Aditya Singh, Advocate for the

 Mr. Sandeep Tandon, Deputy Advocate 
General for the State. 
 This criminal revision has been preferred 
against the judgment and order dated 19.02.2019 
passed by Additional Sessions Judge, Kotdwar, 
District Pauri Garhwal in criminal appeal no.14 of 
2018 whereby the revisionist has been convicted 
under Section 138 of Negotiable Instruments Act 
and has been sentenced to undergo simple 
imprisonment for a period of three months along 
with a fine of Rs.40,00,000/-. 
 Admit. 
 Summon the lower court record. 
 Stay application filed by the revisionist is 
considered as bail application. 
 It is the submission of learned counsel for 
the revisionist that the revisionist was on bail 
during trial as well as during the pendency of 
appeal and he has never misused the liberty of bial 
granted to him. 
 Having considered the submission of learned 
counsel for the revisionist, without expressing any 
opinion as to the final merits of the case, in my 
view, revisionist should be enlarged on bail during 
the pendency of present criminal revision. 
 Let the revisionist Rajeev Gaur be released on 
bail on his executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two sureties each of like amount to the 
satisfaction of court concerned. 
 Bail application/stay application stands 
disposed of accordingly. 
 Let a certified copy of the order be issued to 
learned counsel for the revisionist today itself on 
payment of usual charges. 
 
 (Lok Pal Singh, J.) 
 16.05.2019
