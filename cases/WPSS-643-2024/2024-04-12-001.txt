Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for the petitioners.

3. Mr. C.K. Sharma, learned counsel for respondent no.3-

4. The present writ petition is filed by the petitioners
challenging the condition contained in Clause No.9 of the 
advertisement dated 14.03.2024 issued by respondent no.3-
Commission (Annexure No.5), whereby the cut-off date for 
consideration of the age to bring a candidate within the 
eligibility has been fixed as 01.07.2024 in place of 01.07.2023.
5. It is the contention of the learned counsel for the 
petitioners that this fixation of the cut-off date i.e. 01.07.2024 
runs contrary to the service Rules governing the services of the 
petitioners known as the Uttarakhand Sub-ordinate Education 
(Trained Graduate Category) Service Rules, 2014.
6. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioners that 
in the said Rule, the recruitment year is defined as calendar 
year starting w.e.f. 01 July of the recruitment year to the 30th 
June of the next year. In this view of the matter, the cut-off 
date shall be fixed on 01.07.2023.
7. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioners that 
the vacancies relate back to the recruitment year 2023-24, 
therefore, the cut-off date should have been fixed for 
01.07.2023 in view of the provisions of the Uttarakhand Sub￾ordinate Education (Trained Graduate Category) Service 
Rules, 2014.2
8. I have considered the submissions made by the learned 
counsel for the parties and in the opinion of this Court, this 
matter requires scrutiny and deliberation which can be done 
only after the filing of the counter affidavits.
9. Six weeks’ time is granted to respondents to file counter 
affidavits. Further, two weeks’ time is granted to petitioners to 
file rejoinder affidavit, if any.
10. In order to maintain the equity and to do justice, as an 
interim measure, it is provided that the respondent￾Commission will accept the application form of the petitioners
manually/physically for the recruitment on the post of 
Assistant Teacher (L.T.) Grade pursuant to the advertisement 
dated 14.03.2024 and to permit them to participate in the 
selection process, which shall be subject to the final outcome 
of the writ petition.
11. List this case on 16.08.2024.
12. Let certified copy of this order be supplied, to learned 
counsel for the parties, today itself, on payment of usual 
charges.
 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
