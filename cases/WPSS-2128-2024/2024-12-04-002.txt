1. Petitioners are working as Junior Engineers with the 
respondent Department through outsourcing agency and most of 
them are working since long back and are now seeking 
regularization / absorption in view of the judgment passed by 
the Division Bench of this Court in WPPIL No. 116 of 2018 
decided on 12.11.2018, which was affirmed by the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court. 
2. On the previous date, certain questions were framed by this 
Court to examine the issue of engagement of employees in 
different departments through outsourcing agency. The 
questions, as framed by this Court in paragraph 6 of order dated 
13.11.2024 are being extracted herein below:
 “6. Let the respondents may get complete instructions on 
the following issues:-
i. Whether against substantive/ sanctioned posts, 
appointments/ recruitments can be made through outsourcing 
agency? 2
ii. Whether the posts, which are within the purview of the 
“Uttarakhand Public Service Commission” (a Constitutional 
recruiting body), can be filled up through outsourcing agency? 
iii. Whether the posts which are within the purview of 
“Uttarakhand Public Service Commission” can be filled up 
through outsourcing agency, if ‘yes’, then whether such 
recruitment/ appointments through outsourcing agency were 
made after due consultation with the “Uttarakhand Public 
Service Commission? 
iv. Whether Class II posts can also be filled up through 
outsourcing agency? 
v. Whether any criteria/ procedure has been prescribed/ 
followed for recruitment through outsourcing agencies? 
vi. Whether selections/ appointments/ recruitments were 
made through outsourcing agencies on the recommendation of the 
Selection Committee of subject experts? 
vii. Whether any statutory rules have been framed for 
empanelling outsourcing agencies? How many outsourcing 
agencies have been empanelled by the State Government, and 
what criteria they have adopted for empanelling the outsourcing 
agencies? Place the list of empanelled outsourcing agencies. 
viii. Whether a substantive/ sanctioned post can be filled up 
through outsourcing agency by ignoring the statutory rules, 
which govern the recruitment?
ix. Who is the controlling and supervising authority over 
the empanelled outsourcing agencies?” 
3. Subsequently, thereafter, some more questions were framed
and one of the questions was whether without sending the 3
requisition to the recruiting body for regular selection 
appointment can be made through outsourcing agency. 
4. Learned Counsels for the State, on written instructions, give 
answer of most of the questions in “No”. What they have stated
that in fact, outsourcing appointments were completely 
prohibited but if the requisition is sent for regular selection and 
there is delay in the regular selection process, then in such an 
eventuality, for the time being, for the purposes of smooth 
functioning of the Department, instructions were issued to 
engage through outsourcing agency. In reference to this, one 
Circular of the Chief Secretary dated 29.10.2021 is also placed on 
record along with written instructions, which was addressed to 
the all the Principal Secretaries, Secretaries, Secretaries Incharge 
as well as Head of the Department and the Commissioners and 
District Magistrates of the State wherein certain instructions and 
guidelines were issued for engaging candidates on daily wage 
basis, contractual, work charge, consolidated pay, part time, ad 
hoc through outsource agency. This Circular was issued in 
continuation of earlier Government Order dated 27.04.2018 and 
14.06.2018 wherein some more Clauses were substituted.

5. Apart from this, learned State Counsel inform to this Court 
that the posts of Junior Engineer against which petitioners were 
appointed are in fact within the purview of the Public Service 
Commission and therefore, the posts, which are within the 
purview of the Public Service Commission, cannot be filled up 
through outsourcing agency. 
6. Now, the question, if the posts, which are within the 
purview of the Public Service Commission, cannot be filled 
through outsourcing agency, then why appointments were made 
through outsourcing agency. 6
7. Apart from this, one more question was with regard to the 
procedure of empanelment of outsource agencies and in reply 
thereto, Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned Addl. CSC submits that 
the is Government Order dated 11.08.2023 prescribes the 
procedure for empanelling the outsourcing agency. 
8. The entire issue is required to be examined which certainly 
affects the future of large number of candidates, who are eligible,
and qualified and are in queue for waiting their appointments. If 
the Government Order provides that only after sending the 
requisition for recruitment on regular basis and on an eventuality 
of delay of selection process, for the time being appointment can 
be made through outsourcing agency, then the question is how
without sending requisition large number of appointments were 
made through outsource agencies. 
9. Learned State Counsel also informs to this Court that 
learned Advocate General had also sent a proposal to the 
Government for framing Scheme as one time measure for 
regularizing the out sourced employees, who are working since 
last more than 10 years. 
10. Let affidavit be filed by the Secretary, Karmik, Government 
of Uttarakhand, to answer the queries framed in earlier order
within three weeks positively along with present queries within 
three weeks. He shall also disclose whether any decision has been 
taken on the proposal sent by the learned Advocate General. 
11. Put up this matter on 30.12.2024.
