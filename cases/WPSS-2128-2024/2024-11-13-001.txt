4. All the petitioners in this writ petition have been 
appointed through outsourcing agency on the post of 
Junior Assistant in Zila Panchayat, Chamoli, and 
working since last more than ten to thirteen years. 
They are seeking regularization in view of the 
judgment passed by the Division Bench of this Court in 
Writ Petition (PIL) No.116 of 2018, dated 12.11.2018, 
which was also affirmed by the Hon’ble Supreme 
Court. 
5. For deciding the issue, as raised, it is necessary 
to ask certain instructions from the State. 
6. Let the respondents may get complete 
instructions on the following issues:- 
“i. Whether against substantive/ sanctioned posts, 
appointments/ recruitments can be made through 
outsourcing agency? 
ii. Whether the posts, which are within the purview 
of the “Uttarakhand Public Service Commission” (a 
Constitutional recruiting body), can be filled up through outsourcing agency? 
iii. Whether the posts which are within the purview of 
“Uttarakhand Public Service Commission” can be 
filled up through outsourcing agency, if ‘yes’, then 
whether such recruitment/ appointments through 
outsourcing agency were made after due 
consultation with the “Uttarakhand Public Service 
Commission? 
iv. Whether Class II posts can also be filled up 
through outsourcing agency? 
v. Whether any criteria/ procedure has been 
prescribed/ followed for recruitment through 
outsourcing agencies? 
vi. Whether selections/ appointments/ recruitments 
were made through outsourcing agencies on the 
recommendation of the Selection Committee of 
subject experts? 
vii. Whether any statutory rules have been framed 
for empanelling outsourcing agencies? How many 
outsourcing agencies have been empanelled by the 
State Government, and what criteria they have 
adopted for empanelling the outsourcing agencies? 
Place the list of empanelled outsourcing agencies. 
viii. Whether a substantive/ sanctioned post can be 
filled up through outsourcing agency by ignoring the 
statutory rules, which govern the recruitment? 
ix. Who is the controlling and supervising authority 
over the empanelled outsourcing agencies?” 
7. List this matter on 04.12.2024. 
8. Keep this matter as part-heard. 
9. On the next date of listing, Mr. Pooran Singh 
Bisht, Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned Additional Chief 
Standing Counsels and Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing Counsel for the State will assist this Court.
