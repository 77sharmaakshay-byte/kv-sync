2. The petitioners have challenged the 
provisional recommendation made by the 
respondent Commission dated 11.11.2023 
(Annexure-7 to the writ petition) made pursuant to 
the advertisement dated 05.10.2021.
3. It is contention of the learned counsel for the 
petitioners that the petitioners’ name configure in 
the recommendation dated 25.10.2023, but, 
suddenly in the subsequent recommendation list, 
the name of the petitioners were deleted.

petitioner, the reason for removal of the name of the 
petitioners from the recommendation list was that 
the petitioners were not having the three years’ 
industrial experience, as required as per the terms 
and conditions of the advertisement.

returnable within six weeks. Steps to be taken 
within a week.
6. The learned counsel for the respondents pray

may be filed by the petitioners.
