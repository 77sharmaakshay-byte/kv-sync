CLMA No. 11900/2018 (Time Extension Application) 
In 
WPMS No. 1748 of 2017 
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. N.S. Pundir, Advocate for the petitioner. 
Mr. Sanjay Bhatt, Advocate for the 
respondent. 
The petitioner had filed this application 
seeking extension of time to comply the order dated 
20.07.2017, whereby this Court has directed the 
petitioner to deposit the amount of salary involved 
in the impugned order. The applicant annexes the 
copy of the draft drawn on Punjab National Bank 
being draft no. ULM 936880 for a sum of Rs. 
15,28,862/-. 
In these circumstances, the time extension 
application is allowed. 
The petitioner is directed to deposit the 
aforesaid draft in original, the copy of which is 
annexed with the time extension application, before 
the Registrar General of this Court within a period 
of three days from today. 
Subject to the above observations, the time 
extension application stands disposed of. 
The interim order already granted will 
continue to operate till the next date of listing. 
 
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
