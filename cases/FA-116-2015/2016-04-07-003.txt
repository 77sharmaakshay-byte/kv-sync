Having heard learned Counsel 
for the parties, it transpires that 
reference, at the instance of the 
appellants, was made by the Collector 
to the District Judge, Nainital for 
enhancement of the compensation. 
Such matter was heard by the Ist 
Additional District Judge, Nainital, 
who rejected the reference suit on 
merits vide her impugned judgment 
and order dated 7.8.2015. Feeling 
disgruntled, the appellants have 
preferred this appeal. 
Admit. 
Summon the lower court record. 
List in due course. 
I think that no useful purpose 
shall be served by staying the 
operation of the impugned judgment 
until the matter is adjudicated finally. 
Hence, the stay application (CLMA 
1 7 015) is r 2 06/2 ejected.
