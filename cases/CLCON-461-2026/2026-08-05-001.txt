2. The petitioner alleges willful and

consequential benefits of the order has not 
been given to the petitioner.

with partly while the benefit has not been 
given to petitioner.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner
submits that petitioner was inflicted with the 
punishment of withholding two increments 
with cumulative effect, which order was 
challenged by the petitioner by filing the writ petition in WPSS No. 678 of 2018, Ramesh

Commission and other. The said writ petition 
was allowed vide aforesaid order 20.11.2025 
and the writ court directed the respondents 
to give all the consequential benefits, 
including fixation of salary, increment, etc. to 
the petitioners, within a period of two months
from the date of receipt of the order.

submits that the order was supplied to the 
respondent-contemnor on 22.12.2025 and in 
compliance of the said order, the order was 
passed, but while accepting the judgment, no 
consequential benefits has been given to the 
petitioner.
