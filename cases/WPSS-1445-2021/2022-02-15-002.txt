WPSS No.1445 of 2021
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. T.A. Khan, Senior Advocate, for the 
petitioner.
The petitioner has claimed for being 
granted appointment on compassionate ground. 
When the counter affidavit was called for 
by an order dated 18.11.2021, the respondents 
were not present, and no counter affidavit has 
been filed till date.
Even today, when the matter is taken up 
in the revised call, none appears for the 
respondents. However, in the interest of justice, 
ten days’ further time is granted to the 
respondents Counsel by way of the last 
opportunity to file counter affidavit in the 
matter.
The information of today’s order would 
be supplied by the learned Senior Counsel for 
the petitioner or his office, to the learned 
Counsel for the respondent within 48 hours.
List this writ petition as soon as the 
counter affidavit is filed by the respondents.
