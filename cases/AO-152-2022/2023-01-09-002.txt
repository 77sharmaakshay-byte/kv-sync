COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
09.01.2023 AO No.152 of 2022
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J. s
Heard Mr. Deepak Rawat, learned 
counsel for the appellant – Insurance 
Company and Mr. Bharat Singh, learned 
counsel for the respondent no.1 – claimant 
on the Money Withdrawal Application (IA 
No.02 of 2022).
Learned counsel for the appellant –
Insurance Company submitted that the 
Insurance Company has deposited 50% 
amount of the awarded amount. 
Learned Motor Accident Claims 
Tribunal/IVth Additional District Judge, 
Haridwar has passed the Award dated 
10.11.2021 in Claim Petition No.121 of 
2016, “Smt. Neelu vs. Siyanand and 
Others”, by which, the learned Tribunal has 
awarded a total sum of Rs.1,44,35,930/-
along with an interest at the rate of 6% per 
annum from the date of petition till its 
actual payment.
Having regard to the submissions of 
the learned counsel for the parties, the 
Tribunal concerned is directed to release 
Rs.5,00,000/- (Rupees five lakhs) out of the 
said deposited amount in favour of the 
respondent no.1 – claimant, namely, Smt. 
Neelu, in accordance with law.
The Money Withdrawal Application (IA 
No.02 of 2022) is disposed of accordingly.
List this case on 02.05.2023.
