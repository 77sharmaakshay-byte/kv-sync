COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLR No. 476 of 2025
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Siddhartha Jain, learned 
counsel for the revisionist.
2. Mr. Pushpendra Kumar and Ms. 
Neelima Mishra Joshi, learned counsel for 
the respondent.
3. The revisionist-husband, Mahipal 
Singh Chauhan and the respondent-wife 
Neeta Chauhan are present in Court.
4. On the previous date, learned 
counsel for the respondent apprise to this 
Court that the two sons of the respondent 
are not looking after the respondent￾mother. 
5. Taking into consideration this aspect 
the sons of the parties, were directed to 
join the proceedings, who are serving in 
Army, as a Major and other one is in the 
Bank. Mr. Madhur Chauhan, who is a
Major in Army joined the proceedings 
through V.C. and submits that he and his 
brother are taking all responsibilities to 
look after the mother and will also provide 
accommodation to her at Dehradun and he 
also advised to his mother that since both 
of them are living separately since more 
than 17 years, therefore, don’t compel to
their father to live together. Mr. Madhur 
Chauhan son of the parties undertakes 
that he will search for the accommodation for his mother.
6. Admittedly, both the revisionist and 
respondent are the senior citizens, living 
separately since last more than 17 years 
and therefore, prima facie, this Court is of 
the view that the marriage in between the 
parties appears to be a dead marriage, in
view of the several decision of the Hon’ble 
Apex Court.
7. List this case on 18.03.2026. By that 
time, their sons search an accommodation 
at Dehradun also to look after her and on 
the next date both the sons shall again 
join the proceedings through V.C.
8. Interim order dated 06.08.2025 shall 
continue. 
 
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
