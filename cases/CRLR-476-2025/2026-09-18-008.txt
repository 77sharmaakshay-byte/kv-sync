3. The present matter arises out of 
matrimonial discord between the parties. 
The revisionist and respondent are husband 
and wife respectively.
4. Both the parties are present in person 
before the Court.
5. Learned counsel appearing for the 
respondent/wife would submit that, 
pursuant to the order dated 23.04.2026, the 
respondent/wife has made efforts to identify 
suitable accommodation with a view to 
residing separately and has found a flat, the 
approximate cost whereof is ₹50 lacs.
6. Learned counsel appearing for the 
revisionist would submit that the revisionist 
is not financially capable of bearing the aforesaid amount and that his maximum 
capacity to make payment towards the 
accommodation of the respondent/wife is 
₹36 lacs. He would further submit that 
certain arrears of maintenance are also 
outstanding against the revisionist and are 
liable to be paid to the respondent/wife.
7. Learned counsel appearing for the 
respondent/wife would submit that the 
balance amount, after taking into 
consideration the amount of ₹36 lacs 
proposed to be paid by the revisionist, may 
be adjusted against and paid from the 
arrears of maintenance payable to the 
respondent/wife. He would further submit 
that the respondent/wife has identified and 
proposed to purchase the aforesaid flat for 
her residential benefit and has placed the 
relevant proposal before the Court. A copy of 
the said proposal has also been handed over 
to learned counsel for the revisionist in 
Court, which has been received by him.
8. On the joint request made by learned 
counsel for the parties, list this case on 
09.10.2026.
9. On the next date of listing, learned 
counsel for the respective parties shall 
apprise the Court of the subsequent 
developments with regard to the proposal 
and the arrangements for the
