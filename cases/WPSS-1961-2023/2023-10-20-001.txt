COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.1961 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Alok Dalakoti, learned counsel for the 
petitioner, through video conferencing.
2. Mr. Atul Bahuguna, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State.
3. By means of this writ petition, petitioner has 
challenged the order dated 16.09.2023 (Annexure 
no.1 to the writ petition), whereby services of the 
petitioner has been terminated w.e.f., 23.09.2023.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted 
that the petitioner was engaged as a Contractual 
G.I.S. Analyst with respondent-Uttarakhand Forest 
Training Academy, Haldwani, District Nainital, in 
the year 2008, for a period of one year. The said 
contractual appointment of the petitioner was 
further extended time to time and lastly w.e.f., 
05.04.2023 to 05.03.2024, on an honorarium of 
₹55,000/- per month, vide order dated 03.04.2023, 
on the recommendation of H.O.F.F. Dehradun, 
Uttarakhand dated 01.04.2023, but suddenly by the 
impugned order, which specify no reason, the 
aforesaid engagement of the petitioner as a 
contractual employee has been abruptly brought to 
an end, without any ostensible reasons.
5. It is submitted that such kind of exercise of 
power by the respondents is highly arbitrary and against the law. A person who has been working for 
the last 15 years cannot be treated in this manner.
6. Learned counsel for the State prays for and is 
granted six weeks’ time for filing counter affidavit.
7. Further two weeks’ time is granted to the 
learned counsel for the petitioner for filing rejoinder 
affidavit.
8. List this case on 26.12.2023.
9. Impugned order dated 16.09.2023 shall 
remain stayed till the next date of listing and the
respondents are restrained from taking any coercive 
actions against the petitioner, in-between.
10. Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2023) stands 
disposed-off accordingly.
