After hearing the learned counsel for the parties at some length, 
the learned counsel for the respondents invited the attention of the 
Court to an urgency application dated 27.11.2009. In the affidavit 
annexed to this application, it was alleged that the petitioner is again 
absent from duty w.e.f. 12.05.2009 onwards. This fact has been denied 
by the learned counsel for the petitioner. Consequently, the respondents 
are directed to produce the attendance register and payment register for 
the period from 02.01.2009 upto till date. 
List this matter for further hearing on 29th March, 2010.
