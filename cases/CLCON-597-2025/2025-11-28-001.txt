COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
6D CLCON No. 597 of 2025
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Imraj Singh Rautela, Advocate, 
holding brief of Mr. Prateek Tripathi, Advocate 
for the petitioners.
Mr. Devesh Ghildiyal, Brief Holder for the 
State.
The petitioners were reverted from the 
post of Sahayak Nideshak to Sahayak Lekha 
Parikshak Adhikari by an order dated 
19.03.2025 passed by the competent authority. 
That order was challenged by the petitioners in 
WPSB No. 85 of 2025, Birendra Singh Nabiyal 
and others Vs. State of Uttarakhand and others 
(“the petition”), which was allowed on 
30.07.2025 and the Court had set aside the 
order dated 19.03.2025 and directed the 
respondents to permit the petitioners to 
discharge duties on the post of Sahayak 
Nideshak/Lekha Prikshak Adhikari (Grade Pay 
Rs.5400/-, Level-10).
The petitioners have brought to the notice 
of the Court that order dated 30.07.2025, 
passed by the Court in the petition has been 
willfully disobeyed by the respondents and the 
petitioners have not been permitted to discharge 
their duties, as directed by this Court in its 
order dated 30.07.2025.
It is, prima facie, case of contempt.
Issue notice to the respondent no.1 to join 
the proceedings through video conferencing and 
to tell the Court as to why, he should not be 
proceeded for committing contempt of this 
Court and why charges may not be framed 
against him.
List on 19.12.2025.
