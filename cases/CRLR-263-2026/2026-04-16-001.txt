COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLR No. 263 of 2026
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Pankaj Singh Chauhan, learned 
counsel for the revisionist.
2. The instant criminal revision has been 
preferred by the revisionist against the Judgment 
and order dated 19.04.2026, passed by the 
learned Addl. Principal Judge, Family Court, 
Haldwani, District Nainital in Misc. Criminal 
Case No. 117 of 2021, Smt. Shivangi vs. Shri 
Prajjawal Kumar Rawat, whereby Rs. 12,000/-
per month as a maintenance has been granted to 
the respondent/wife under Section 125 Cr.P.C.
3. As per the office report the revision is well 
within time.
4. Admit.
5. Summon the trial court record.
6. Issue notice to the respondent.
4. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
revisionist that the revisionist make all possible 
efforts to live with the respondent/wife but 
without any reason she is living separately since 
06.04.2021, therefore, she does not deserve for 
any maintenance. Apart from this, he submits 
that the revisionist also filed a suit under Section 
9 of Hindu Marriage Act for restitution of 
conjugal rights in which the respondent/wife 
never put appearance. He submits that both of 
them married in the year 2019 and there is no 
issue from this marriage and since April 2021 
they are living separately. He also submits that 
maintenance as granted by the Family Court is 
also on higher side.
5. After hearing the arguments of the learned 
counsel for the revisionist and taking into 
consideration that there is no issue from this marriage and the respondent/wife is living 
separately since April 2021 and the revisionist 
also filed a suit for restitution of conjugal rights, 
this Court is of the view that at this stage 
amount towards maintenance awarded by the 
Family Court appears to be on higher side 
particularly when respondent/wife is living 
separately without any reason and had not put 
appearance in the suit filed by the revisionist for 
restitution of conjugal rights. Further taking into 
consideration that the dispute in between the 
parties appears to be matrimonial one and they 
are living separately since April 2021, this Court 
is of the view that efforts may be made for their 
amicable settlement.
6. In such view of the matter, the order 
impugned 19.04.2026, passed by the learned 
Addl. Principal Judge, Family Court, Haldwani, 
District Nainital in Misc. Criminal Case No. 117 
of 2021, Smt. Shivangi vs. Shri Prajjawal Kumar 
Rawa , is stayed provided that the revisionist 
shall regularly pay a sum of Rs. 7,000/- per 
month to the revisionist.
7. List after service of notice.
8. On the next date the parties shall remain 
present in Court so that they may be directed to 
appear before the Counsellor.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
