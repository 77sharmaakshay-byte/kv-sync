(4) This Court had directed the Housing 
Commissioner, Uttar Pradesh Avas Vikas 
Parishad and Uttarakhand Housing & 
Development Authority to file their 
personal affidavits within two weeks on 
certain issues. 
(5) Today the matter is listed on an 
application (IA No. 13816 of 2023) filed 
on behalf of respondent nos. 1 & 2, 
whereby further two weeks time has 
been sought for filing personal affidavit 
of Housing Commissioner, Uttar Pradesh 
Avas Vikas Parishad.
(6) Mr. B.S. Adhikari, learned counsel 
appearing for respondent nos. 1 & 2 
submits that the record, regarding 
Improvement Scheme formulated for 
U.P. Avas Vikas Parishad in respect of 
District Haridwar, is being scanned to 
ascertain, as to whether any declaration 
in terms of Section 51 of the U.P. Avas 
Evam Vikas Adhiniyam, 1965 was made or not. He assures the Court that 
personal affidavit of Housing 
Commissioner, U.P. Avas Vikas Parishad 
shall be filed within two weeks. 
(7) For the reasons indicated in the 
application, the same is allowed. Time 
granted vide order dated 12.10.2023 is 
extended for further two weeks. 
(8) List this case on 29.11.2023.
(9) In the meantime, personal affidavit 
of Housing Commissioner, Uttarakhand 
Housing & Development Authority shall 
also be filed. 
(10) It is made clear that no further 
extension of time shall be given to the 
respondents for the purpose.
