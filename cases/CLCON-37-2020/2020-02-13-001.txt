CLCON No. 37 of 2020
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Heard Mr. Ajay veer Pundir, Advocate 
for the petitioner.
It is contended by the petitioner that 
opposite party has willfully violated the 
interim order passed by this Court on 
12.12.2019. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that petitioner had deposited the 
entire amount of sale consideration of the 
plots with Awas Vikas Parishad in the year 
2009. He further submits that the amount of 
development charge was thereafter deposited 
by the petitioner in terms of the order of this 
Court in the month of December, 2019. He 
further submits that in case the writ petition 
filed by the petitioner is decided against him, 
then, petitioner undertakes to pay the 
amount of interest over the development 
charge to Avas Vikas Parishad. Thus, he 
submits that the opposite party is not 
justified in not executing sale deed in favour 
of the petitioner despite the order passed by 
this Court on 12.12.2019.
Issue notice to opposite party calling
upon him to file his response to the 
allegations made in the contempt petition.
List this matter after three weeks. 
 
 (Manoj K. Tiwari, J.) 
Navin 13.02.2020
