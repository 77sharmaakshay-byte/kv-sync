The brother of the petitioner died-in￾harness on 02.10.2013. Petitioner 
submitted an application seeking 
appointment on compassionate ground. 
However, surprisingly, till date no 
decision has been taken by the 
respondents on the application of the 
petitioner. 
Accordingly, the writ petition is 
disposed of with the direction to the 
respondents to consider the case of the 
petitioner for compassionate appointment 
within a period of ten weeks from today, 
as per the Rules of 1974 as amended 
from time to time. 
Pending application, if any, also 
stands disposed of accordingly.
