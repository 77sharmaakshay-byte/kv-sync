WPSS No. 4200 of 2018 
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. Narayan Singh Negi, Advocate for 
the respondent. 
Petitioner is not being permitted to 
appear in Uttarakhand Teachers Eligibility 
Test-I, as he has secured less than 50% marks 
in graduation. 
This matter now stands settled by the 
learned Single Judge of this Court in the case of 
Baldev Singh and others Vs. State of 
Uttarakhand and others reported in 2011 (2) 
UD 575, which has been upheld by the Division 
Bench of this Court in Special Appeal, being 
Special Appeal No. 286 of 2013, wherein, the 
ban of 45% has been held to be arbitrary. 
In view thereof, the respondents are 
directed to accept the application forms of the 
petitioner provisionally and permit the 
petitioner to appear in the Uttarakhand 
Teachers Eligibility Test-I. 
Counter affidavit be filed by the 
respondents within a period of three weeks. 
List this matter after three weeks in the 
daily cause list. 
 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
