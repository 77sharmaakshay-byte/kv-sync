2. Counsel for the petitioners would submit 
that the petitioners had filed a Suit No. 22 of 
1985-86 before the Assistant Collector 1st
Class, Haldwani, District Nainital under 
Section 229-B of U.P. Zamindari Abolition 
and Land Reforms Act, 1950 for declaring 
their Bhoomidhari rights over the suit property 
wherein the status quo order was passed on 
26.08.1986; that, this order was confirmed by 
the Assistant Collector, Haldwani; that, feeling 
aggrieved by the said order, the respondents
preferred the Appeal before the Additional 
Commissioner, Kumaon Division; which was 
allowed and the interim order of status quo 
was set-aside; that, against the said order, the 
petitioners preferred the Second Appeal before 
the Board of Revenue, which was dismissed.
He would further submit that the last 
order came in the year 2006 despite the fact, 
that the respondents did not file written 
statement since 2006 to 2010, the opportunity 
to file written statement was closed by the trial 
court against which, the respondents went 
through several stages and finally by the impugned order dated 08.05.2023 passed by 
the Board of Revenue Uttarakhand, Dehradun, 
an opportunity to file written statement was 
granted to the respondents, albeit, with the 
direction to the trial court to decide the suit 
within three months. Feeling aggrieved by the 
order dated 08.05.2023, the 
petitioners/plaintiffs preferred the present writ 
petition. 
3. Admit the petition. 
4. Pleadings have been exchanged. 
5. List this matter on 22.04.2024 for final 
hearing.
