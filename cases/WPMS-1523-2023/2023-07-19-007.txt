COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS 
 MCC No. 2/2023 (For Review Application) 
In 
WPMS No. 1523 of 2023 
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Siddhartha Singh, learned counsel for 
the petitioners. 
2. Mr. Piyush Garg, learned counsel for 
respondent no. 1. 
3. Mr. N.S. Pundir, learned Deputy Advocate 
General for the State/respondent nos. 2 and 4. 
4. The present review application (MCC No. 
2/2023) has been filed for reviewing the judgment 
and order dated 21.06.2023. In paragraph-7 of the 
affidavit filed in support of review application, it 
is contended that on 21.06.2023 the petitioners 
could not come personally and had not instructed 
the counsel to make such concession. 
5. It is further contended by the petitioner that 
he want to contest the petition on merits, as he is 
facing irreparable loss due to the concession got 
recorded by the counsel which was beyond any 
authority or any instruction. 
6. Today, the petitioner no. 1 Mr. Jodha Ram, 
who is aged about 94 years and petitioner no. 2/2 
Mr. Nand Lal S/o Late Kundan Ram are present 
in the court and they are duly identified by their 
counsel through their Aadhar Cards. 
7. Since, the order dated 21.06.2023 was a 
consenting order; however, today, the petitioner 
no. 1, who is aged about 94 years submits that he 
wants to contest this writ petition on merit, for 
which the counsel for the respondent has no 
objection. In view of this, review application is 
allowed. Order dated 21.06.2023 is recalled. 
8. The cheque of Rs. 10,000/-, which was ordered to be paid while passing the order dated 
21.06.2023, is returned by Mr. Jodha Ram to the 
counsel for the respondent. 
9. Learned counsel for the petitioners Mr. 
Siddhartha Singh submits that due to the conduct 
of his client, he wants to withdraw his 
vakalatnama.
10. In view of this, learned counsel for the 
petitioners Mr. Siddhartha Singh is permitted to 
withdraw his vakalatnama. The petitioner no. 1, 
who is present in the Court is permitted to engage 
some other counsel. 
11. List this matter after one week. 
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
