4. Counter affidavits have been filed by 
respondent no. 2 in both the applications 
under Section C-528 before the Court. The 
same are taken on record.
5. Registry is directed to paginate the 
same as per record.
6. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would submit that the applicant has been 
falsely implicated in the present case; that, 
the Investigating Officer has submitted the 
charge-sheet without conducting a fair and 
complete investigation and without 
collecting and considering all relevant 
material available on record; that, the 
charge-sheet records that the applicant 
was absconding and, therefore, the 
investigation against him was kept 
pending. However, the said observation is 
factually incorrect inasmuch as the 
applicant had approached this Court by 
filing Writ Petition (Criminal) No. 1119 of 
2024, wherein a Coordinate Bench of this Court, vide order dated 16.10.2024, 
granted interim protection to the 
applicant; that, despite being aware, or 
being expected to be aware, of the said 
order, the Investigating Officer proceeded 
to submit the charge-sheet showing the 
applicant as absconding and kept the 
investigation pending against him.
7. Learned counsel would further 
submit that, subsequent to the lodging of 
the F.I.R., an inquiry was conducted by 
the Additional Superintendent of Police, 
Pauri. However, the findings and report of 
the said inquiry have neither been 
considered nor made part of the 
investigation by the Investigating Officer. It 
is further submitted that the charge-sheet 
has been submitted only on the basis of 
the statements of the victim and the 
complainant, without examining or 
appreciating other material evidence 
available during the course of 
investigation.
8. Learned State Counsel prays for and 
is granted one week's time to obtain 
instructions and verify the aforesaid 
factual aspects, including whether the 
order dated 16.10.2024 passed in Writ 
Petition (Criminal) No. 1119 of 2024 was 
brought to the notice of the Investigating 
Officer, and whether the inquiry report 
prepared by the Additional Superintendent 
of Police, Pauri, was considered and made 
part of the investigation before submission 
of the charge-sheet.
9. List this case on 08.06.2026.
