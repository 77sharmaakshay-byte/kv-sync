COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
23.11.2022 CLR No.100 of 2022
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The present proposed Appeal has been 
filed under Section 25 of the Provincial Small 
Cause Courts Act, 1887 against the judgment 
and decree dated 13.09.2022, passed by the 
learned Judge, Small Cause Court/IInd 
Additional District Judge, Haldwani, District 
Nainital in SCC Suit No.8 of 2018, “Smt. 
Pratibha Chandra vs. Hargovind Singh and 
Another”, by which, the said SCC suit has 
been decreed. 
Heard Mr. P.C. Maulekhi, the learned 
counsel for the revisionists. 
The learned counsel appearing for the 
proposed revisionists submitted that the suit 
property was constructed prior to the year,
1985 and relevant documents have been filed 
before the Trial Court. He further submitted 
that the power of attorney holder has no right 
to adduce oral evidence on behalf of the 
landlady, whereas, the said attorney had no 
personal knowledge regarding the agreement. 
He further submitted that unregistered 
document cannot be read in evidence.
List this case on 30.11.2022 after fresh
cases. 
Copies of the said documents, filed 
before the Trial Court, may be filed, 
meanwhile. 
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
