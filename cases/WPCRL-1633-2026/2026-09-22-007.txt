4. By means of the present writ petition, 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution 
of India, the petitioners have prayed for 
issuance of a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of certiorari for quashing the First 
Information Report/Case Crime No. 0252 
of 2026 dated 02.06.2026, registered 
under Section 318(4), 351(2), 351 and 
61(2) of B.N.S. at Police Station Raipur, 
District Dehradun. The petitioners have as 
further prayed for issuance of a writ, order 
or direction in the nature of mandamus 
commanding the respondent authorities 
not to arrest him pursuant to the aforesaid F.I.R.
5. Learned counsel appearing for the 
petitioners would submit that the 
petitioners are innocent and have been 
falsely implicated in the present case; that, 
even if the allegations contained in the 
impugned F.I.R. are taken at their face 
value and accepted in their entirety, the 
essential ingredients of the offences alleged 
against the petitioners are not made out; 
that, the dispute in question is essentially 
civil in nature, which has been given a 
criminal colour by lodging the impugned 
F.I.R.; that four persons had purchased 
approximately 1.25 bighas of land from the 
petitioners and, after the completion of 
fencing and construction of the boundary 
wall, possession of the land had been 
handed over to the purchasers and 
mutation had also been effected in their 
favour, however, subsequently, the 
petitioners allegedly attempted to interfere 
with the said land, removed the fencing, 
extended threats to the complainant and 
fraudulently sold the same land to another 
person by preparing forged and fabricated 
documents.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would further submit that the impugned 
F.I.R. has been lodged in a casual and 
routine manner on the basis of a false, 
fabricated and concocted story, allegedly 
motivated by political considerations; that, 
the petitioners have executed sale deeds 
only in favour of the four persons 
specifically named in the F.I.R.; that, all four sale deeds were duly registered on 
05.11.2024 and that no other sale deed in 
respect of the land in question has ever 
been executed by the petitioners; that, 
possession of the land in question had 
already been delivered to the said four 
purchasers and mutation had also been 
carried out and accepted in their favour, 
therefore, the allegation that the 
petitioners subsequently cheated the 
complainant by fraudulently reselling the 
same land is inherently improbable and 
requires examination in the light of the 
registered sale deeds and the relevant 
revenue records.
7. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would further submit that the complainant 
is not a purchaser under any of the 
registered sale deeds in question and 
claims only to be a caretaker appointed by 
the alleged purchasers, without any 
independent title or ownership over the 
property. It is argued that the dispute 
arises out of a clerical error in the revenue 
records, which has been given a criminal 
colour. He would further submit that the 
allegations are primarily against Mr. 
Virendra Kothari, while the petitioners are 
merely alleged to have assisted him in 
obtaining possession of the land already 
sold by him, without any specific overt act, 
dishonest intention or active participation 
being attributed to them.
8. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties and upon perusal of the record, it 
prima facie appears that the dispute between the parties is civil in nature and 
requires further deliberation and a 
response from the respondents is 
necessary before proceeding further in the 
matter.
9. Accordingly, learned State Counsel is 
directed to file a detailed counter affidavit, 
specifically addressing the execution and 
registration of the sale deed dated 
03.08.2017, registered on 04.08.2017, the 
identity of the parties, possession and 
mutation status, and the alleged 
subsequent transactions, if any. The State 
shall also place on record the relevant 
revenue documents and other material 
collected during the investigation.
10. List this case on 15.10.2026.
11. Meanwhile, counter affidavit, if any, 
be filed by respondent no.3.
12. Till the next date of listing, as an 
interim measure, the petitioners shall not 
be arrested in connection with First 
Information Report/Case Crime No.0252 of 
2026 registered at Police Station Raipur, 
Dehradun District Dehradun, provided the 
petitioners cooperate with the 
investigation.
13. Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
