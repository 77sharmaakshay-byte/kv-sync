5. Vide order dated 22.08.2025, 
petitioner was directed to take steps upon 
respondent Nos.2 & 3, but the respondent 
Nos.2 & 3 are still unserved, as the 
undelivered envelope received back with 
the remark ‘despite several enquiry, 
recipient was not found’.
6. Learned senior counsel for the 
petitioner submits that despite all sincere 
efforts, the services could not have been 
effected upon respondent Nos.2 & 3, 
therefore, he made a request to this Court 
to permit him to issue notice and to serve 
respondent Nos.2 & 3 through publication 
in two daily newspapers having wide 
circulation in area.
7. The prayer, so made, is allowed.8. Accordingly, the notices to respondent 
Nos.2 & 3 shall be published by the 
petitioners in two daily newspapers having 
wide circulation in area where respondents 
Nos.2 & 3 are residing.
9. Steps for publication be taken within 
15 days.
10. Put up on 15.12.2025.
11. Interim order dated 21.12.2016 is 
extended till then.
