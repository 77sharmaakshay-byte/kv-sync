CLR No. 32 of 2020
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Yogesh Pant, Advocate for the 
respondent.
The learned counsel for the revisionist 
has filed a Time Extension Application (IA No. 
1643/2021), praying for an extension of time to 
comply with the order dated 09.02.2020.
After having considered the reasons, 
which have been given for non-compliance of 
the conditions, as given therein in the order 
dated 09.02.2020, which is on account of the 
ailment and subsequent death of the revisionist 
on 29.11.2020, the order could not be complied 
with.
Accordingly, the Time Extension 
Application is allowed. The learned counsel for 
the revisionist is granted further one month’s 
time to comply with the order dated 09.02.2020.
The Time Extension Application is 
disposed of accordingly.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
