In

Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J. 
Mr. B.D. Pande, Advocate, present 
for the appellant. 
Mr. I.P. Gairola, Advocate, present 
for the respondent. 
This is an application moved by 
the appellant/defendant accompanying 
with an affidavit alleging that the sole 
appellant died on 31st August, 2013. 
Application has been moved to 
substitute his wife and two sons and 
daughter who are his legal heirs. 
Learned counsel for the 
respondent has made a statement 
before this Court that he has no 
objection if the substitution application 
is allowed. 
Substitution application is allowed. 
Let necessary substitution be 
made within three weeks. 
List this matter after three weeks 
in daily cause list.
