Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Ms. Anupriya Kukreti, learned counsel holding brief 
of Mr. M.C. Pant, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Amar Murti Shukla, learned counsel for 
respondent no.2.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the 
services of the petitioner were terminated on the ground 
that he was charged with theft of coupons from the 
establishment. Subsequently, after trial before the 
criminal court, he was acquitted.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that the charges in the departmental proceedings and in 
the criminal trial were the selfsame, and therefore, the 
benefit of acquittal should be extended to the petitioner. 
She has placed reliance on a judgment of the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court, contending that the present matter is 
squarely covered by the ratio laid down in Ram Lal vs. 
State of Rajasthan & Others, Civil Appeal No. 7935 of 
2023, decided on 04.12.2023 by the Hon’ble Apex Court.
5. Learned counsel for the respondent also go through 
the aforesaid judgment and make submission before this 
Court.
