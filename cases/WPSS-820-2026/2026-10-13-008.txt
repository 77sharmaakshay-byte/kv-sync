Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
Mr. Shubhang Dobhal and Mr. Vinay 
Kumar, learned counsel for the petitioners. 
2. Mr. S.S. Chaudhary, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State.
3. Ms. Priyanka Agarwal, learned counsel 
for Selecting Body/Commission.
4. All the vacancies earmarked under the 
horizontal reservation were carry forward to 
the open category since no one was available 
under the horizontal reservation however 
these vacancies, which were carry forward to 
open category from horizontal reservation 
were treated to be under a different class and 
against these vacancies only general category 
candidates were permitted to compete by 
excluding the reserved category candidate, 
who belongs to SC/ST/OBC. 
5. Now these petitions have been filed by 
those reserved category candidates on the ground that they obtained higher marks to 
the candidates selected under the general 
category.
6. It is argued that since they obtained 
higher marks in comparison with the 
candidate of general candidate, therefore, 
they have a legal enforceable right to get 
their selection against the vacancies 
earmarked for the general category. 
7. I found force on the submission of 
learned counsel for the petitioners since it is 
settled law that the reserved category 
candidate can compete with the general 
category. 
8. On the other side Ms. Priyanka 
Agarwal, learned counsel for the Selecting 
Body/Commission submits that they 
followed the principal as laid down by the 
Government Orders, issued time to time and 
those vacancies, which were under the 
general horizontal reservation filled up with 
qualified candidates coming in the order of 
proficiency of the same category.
9. Put up tomorrow.
