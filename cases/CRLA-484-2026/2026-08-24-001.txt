1. Heard Sri Subhash Joshi, learned counsel for the appellant and Sri 
B.N. Molakhi, learned Deputy Advocate General for the State. 
2. The instant Appeal is directed against the judgment and order 
dated 14.08.2026 passed by learned Special Judge (NDPS Act) / Sessions 
Judge, Chapawat in Special Sessions Trial No. 17 of 2024 (FIR No. 01 of 
2024) titled ‘State vs. Diwan Giri’ under Section 8/20 of the Narcotic 
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, Police Station Paati, 
District Champawat, whereby the appellant has been convicted under 
Section 20(b)(ii)(C) of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances 
Act, 1985 and sentenced to undergo fifteen years’ rigorous imprisonment 
with fine of Rs. 1,50,000/-. In default of payment of fine, the appellant has 
further been directed to undergo one year simple imprisonment 
additionally. 
3. Appeal is admitted. 
4. Call for the Trial Court Record. 
5. Learned Deputy Advocate General appearing for the State prays 
for and is granted two weeks’ time to file objection to the Bail Application 
(IA No. 1 of 2026). 
6. List after two weeks before the appropriate Court.
