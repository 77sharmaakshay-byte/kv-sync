4. The contention is that the petitioner had submitted 
work experience certificate dated 08.04.2026 which 
evidences experience of execution of two works of value 
₹ 79,61,137.00 and ₹ 28,80,586.25, total value being is 
₹ 1,08,41,723.28. He submits that since the petitioner 
had worked as sub-contractor, therefore, as per the 
certificates issued at page 165 and 167, the petitioner 
would be entitled to benefit of 25% of the total value of 
the component relating to hill side cutting which is more 
than 1/3rd of the total value of hill cutting i.e. 
96,12,788.00 specified in the bill of quantity. Thus, the 
rejection of the bid of the petitioner in the technical 
round for not having the experience of hill side cutting is 
manifestly illegal. 
5. The matter requires consideration. 
6. Shri S.C. Chauhan, learned counsel for respondent 
nos.2 and 3 prays for time to obtain instructions.

nos.2 and 3 undertakes that in the meanwhile the 
contract shall not be finalised.
