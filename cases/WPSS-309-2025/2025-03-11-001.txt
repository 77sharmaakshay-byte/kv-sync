Present Writ Petition under Article 226 of the 
Constitution of India has been filed by the petitioner with 
the following prayers:-
“(i) Issue a Writ, Order or direction in the nature 
of certiorari to call for the record and quash the 
impugned order dated 03-03-2025 passed by 
the respondent no.2 (contained as Annexure 
No.11 to this writ petition), whereby recovery of 
Rs.9,64,767/- has been sanctioned from retiral 
dues of the petitioner. 
(ii) Issue a Writ, Order or direction in the nature 
of mandamus commanding the respondents to 
release entire dues without any recovery. 
(iii) Pass any other writ, order or direction, 
which this Hon’ble Court may deem fit and 
proper in the facts and circumstances of the 
case. 2
(iv) Award the cost of the writ petition to the 
Petitioners.”
2. The petitioner retired from his Group C post on 
03.05.2020. 
3. Heard Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, learned counsel 
with Mr. Suryakant Maithani, learned counsel for petitioner
and Mr. Bhupendra Koranga, learned Brief Holder for 
respondents.
4. Mr. Shubang Dobhal, Advocate, has relied upon 
a judgment of the Hon’ble Supreme Court, passed in 
“State of Punjab and Others Vs. Rafiq Masih (White 
Washer) and Others”, (2015) 4 SCC 334. 
5. Mr. Bhupendra Koranga, learned Brief Holder, 
has sought four weeks’ time to file counter affidavit. 
6. Time is granted. 
7. List on 23.05.2025. 
8. The effect and operation of the impugned 
recovery order dated 03.03.2025 (Annexure No.11 to the
writ petition) are kept-in-abeyance till further order. 
9. The Stay Application (IA No.01 of 2025) is 
disposed of accordingly.
