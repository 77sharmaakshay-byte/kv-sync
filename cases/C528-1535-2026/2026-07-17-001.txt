3. The instant petition has been preferred by the 
applicant – Bhupal Dutt Bhatt, under Section 528 of 
BNSS, 2023, for quashing of the proceedings of Sessions 
Trial No.14 of 2024, “State Vs. Bhupal Dutt Bhatt”,
arising out of FIR dated 24.07.2023, bearing FIR No.0042 
of 2023, wherein, the applicant is facing the trial for the 
offences punishable under Section 307 of IPC.
4. It is submitted by the learned senior counsel for 
the applicant that in fact the dispute is with regards to 
the appointment of the Priest of the temple, and there is 
a cross FIR also i.e. FIR No.0043 of 2023, lodged by the 
applicant, though in the said FIR, final report has been 
filed and in the FIR No.0042 of 2023, the chargesheet 
has been filed. He submits that the investigation of FIR 
No.0043 of 2023 was conducted in a very casual manner 
and without collecting any material evidence, the final 
report was submitted. He also pointed out that against 
the final report, the protest was filed, but the same has 
been dismissed and now it has been advice to challenge 
the order rejecting the protest petition. He submits that 
in this FIR No.0042 of 2023, the applicant was arrested 
and was subsequently enlarged on bail by this Court on 
06.09.2023, the copy of which is enclosed at page 80, 
and by referring this order, it is argued by the learned 
senior counsel for the applicant that primarily the 
dispute is with regards to the appointment of Priest in 
the temple.
5. All these aspects require deep scrutiny.6. Issue notice to the respondent no.2. Steps within
a week. Let the respondents may file their counter 
affidavit within three weeks. One week time is granted 
to the applicant to file rejoinder affidavit.
7. List this matter in the week commencing 
17.08.2026.
8. Till the next date of listing, the further 
proceedings of Sessions Trial No.14 of 2024, “State Vs. 
Bhupal Dutt Bhatt”, pending in the court of learned 
Sessions Judge, Pithoragarh, shall remain stayed.
