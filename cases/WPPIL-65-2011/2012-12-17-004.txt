W.P. (PIL) No.65 of 2011 
Hon’ble Barin Ghosh, C.J.
Hon’ble U.C. Dhyani, J.
 None appears for the petitioner. 
 Mr. Vinay Kumar, Standing Counsel for 
the State/respondent Nos.1 to 3. 
 Counter affidavit filed on behalf of 
respondent No.2 in Court is accepted on record. 
 From the counter affidavit, it appears that 
out of 27 ponds, 17 have been retrieved in terms 
of the order of this Court dated 28th September, 
2012. In respect of five, proceedings have been 
initiated before the Revenue Department for 
removal of encroachers therefrom. 
 In respect of the remaining 05 ponds, it is 
submitted that about eight years back, those were 
settled for establishment of a Madarasa. The 
District Magistrate, Hardwar has not been able to 
give full particulars in respect thereof and what 
steps, if any, are to be taken in that regard. 
 In such view of the matter, we are 
adjourning this matter for three weeks from today, 
in order to enable the District Magistrate 
concerned to file an appropriate affidavit 
indicating steps taken for retrieval of five water 
bodies, in respect whereof the District Magistrate 
has moved the Revenue Department and also to 
the State Government what course of action 
should be adopted for removal of the Madarsa 
from the remaining five water bodies 
 (U.C. Dhyani, J.) ( Barin Ghosh, C.J.)
