 Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, Senior Advocate

 Mr. Pradeep Joshi, Standing Counsel for the 
State of Uttarakhand. 
 Three photographs along with two letters; one 
dated 12th March, 2014 and the other dated 15th
March, 2014 have been furnished to the learned 
Amicus Curiae by the people of the locality, 
whereby and under, they have alleged that the 
Court’s order dated 2nd January, 2014 is being 
violated rampantly and, despite the same having 
been brought to the notice of the District Magistrate, 
the District Magistrate has not taken any action, 
although the District Magistrate was directed by this 
Court to ensure that no construction proceed any 
further in any manner whatsoever. We, 
accordingly, hand over these photographs along 
with the letters to the Standing Counsel, who is 
directed to bring the same to the notice of the 
District Magistrate and to file a response thereto on 
2nd April, 2014 when the matter shall be listed.
