 Heard learned counsels. 
 For the reasons assigned, the delay condonation application 
(CLMA No.19104) is allowed and the delay in filing the recall 
application (CLMA No.19103 of 2018) is condoned. 
 The office defects are overruled for the present. 
 The plea of the applicants is to recall the order dated 
13.06.2018 passed by the Division Bench of this Court. In terms of 
the said order, a direction was issued to the respondent-State 
Government to remove all the encroachments from the village 
ponds/johars, playgrounds and common grazing land within a 
period of six months from the date of the order. The said order was 
passed by placing reliance on the judgment of the Hon’ble Supreme 
Court in the case of Jagpal Singh & others vs. State of Punjab & 
others reported in (2011) 11 SCC 396. 2 
 The case of the applicants is that notices have been issued to 
them to vacate the land; that the land has been rightfully granted to 
them. 
 On considering the submissions, we do not find any ground to 
recall the order dated 13.06.2018. In terms of the said order, the 
learned Bench has directed the State Government to remove the 
encroachments from the village ponds/johars, playgrounds and 
common grazing land. Since the direction is for a specific class of 
land, therefore, we do not find any good ground to recall the order 
dated 13.06.2018. 
The recall application (CLMA No.19103 of 2018) is dismissed.
