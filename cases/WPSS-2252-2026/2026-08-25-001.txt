2. Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Deputy Advocate

3. By means of present writ petition, petitioner has 
challenged order dated 07.08.2026, whereby the 
respondent no.2- Joint Secretary Medical Health and 
Family Welfare has directed the respondent no.3 to 
transfer the petitioner from Dehradun to 
Pithoragarh; further the transfer order dated 
19.08.2026 which has been passed by the 
respondent no.3 pursuant to the aforesaid order 
dated 07.08.2026.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that the petitioner’s transfer is made after 
the time limit of the transfer as prescribed under 
Section 23 of the Uttarakhand Annual Transfer for 
Public Servants Act, 2017 (“the Act”) i.e. after 
30.06.2026. He further submits that the transfer 
has been made on the dictates of the Hon’ble 
Minister of the Department.
5. It is submitted by the learned Senior Counsel 
that the petitioner is the State General Secretary of the Uttaranchal Medical and Public Health 
Ministerial Association which is a recognized 
association by the Government as is reflected also 
from the election certificate (Annexure-6 to the writ 
petition) and being the elected representative of the 
recognized Association, the petitioner cannot be 
transferred within a period of two years from the date 
of his election; the petitioner was elected on 
15.11.2005, thus, he cannot be transferred within a 
period of two years from the date of election or till the 
period expired. Thus, he is protected under Section 
17 (2)(d) of the Transfer Act, 2017. 
6. Apart from that, it is also pointed out that the 
petitioner has rendered 10 years of service in 
inaccessible region (Durgam) in District Almora and 
District Dehradun, and for that reason, he should 
not have been transferred from Dehradun a remote 
District of Pithoragarh; more particularly, when the 
petitioner is posted in Dehradun in D.G. Office since 
2022. In this view of the matter, the case of the 
petitioner is also covered within the provision of 
Section 7(d)(ii) of the Transfer Act, 2017.
7. It is also submitted by learned senior counsel 
for the petitioner that the petitioner was posted as 
Head Assistant in the office of D.G., and no such 
post is available in Pithoragarh.
8. Per contra, it is submitted by learned counsel 
for the State that since there are various complaints
against the petitioner, therefore, the noting reflected
the name of the Hon’ble Minister directing his transfer. Prior to that, the process of transfer of 
petitioner was on.
9. It is further contended by learned State Counsel 
that being the Minister of the concerned Department,
the Minister can direct for such an order of transfer.
10. Having considered the rival submissions of the 
parties, this Court is of the view that since there is a 
transfer Act in the State of Uttarkhand therefore, no 
transfer can be made in violation of Provisions of the 
said Act. In the case in hand, there appears to be 
flagrant violation of the Act as mentioned 
hereinabove i.e. Section 17(2)(d) and 7 (d)(ii) and 
Section 23 of the Transfer Act, this Court is of the 
view that the petitioner deserves protection at this 
stage. 
11. So far as the contention made by the State 
Counsel regarding the complaint of petitioner is 
concerned, then the case of the petitioner is also 
covered with the provision of Section 18(4) of the 
Transfer Act, 2017 wherein, without confronting the 
petitioner to the complaint, no transfer order can be 
passed.
12. Respondents may file counter affidavit within 
four weeks.
13. List on 04.11.2026.
14. In the meanwhile, the impugned transfer orders 
dated 07.08.2026/19.08.2026 shall remain stayed.
