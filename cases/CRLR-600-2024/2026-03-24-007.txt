COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLR No. 600 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. V.B. S. Negi, learned Sr. Advocate assisted 
by Mr. Chetan Joshi, learned counsel for the 
revisionists.
2. Mr. Sandeep Sharma and Mr. Himanshu Sain, 
learned AGA for the State.
3. Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for the 
respondent no. 2. 
4. Learned Sr. Advocate for the revisionist has 
placed reliance on the judgment of Hon’ble Supreme 
Court in the case of Anant Prakash Sinha Vs. State of 
Haryana and another (2016) 6 SCC 105 particularly 
on paragraphs no. 2, 6 and 22 and by referring this 
judgment, learned Sr. Advocate submits that in view 
of the aforesaid judgment, it cannot be said that 
application moved by the complainant for alteration of 
charge is not maintainable. He further submits that in 
view of the aforesaid judgment, at least the trial court 
should examine the material produced by the 
complainant and apply its judicial mind before 
rejecting the application but in the present case the 
trial court has rejected the application in a very 
cursory and casual manner without examining the 
material.
5. Learned counsel for both the parties also placed 
reliance on an another judgment of Hon’ble Supreme 
Court in the case of P. Karikalakshmi Vs. Sri Ganesh 
and another (2017) 3 SCC 347, particularly, by placing 
reliance to paragraph 6 and 7 of the judgment. 
6. List on 07.04.2026 for further arguments. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
