COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRLR No. 110 of 2026
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mrs. Monika Pant, learned counsel for the 
revisionist.
2. The instant criminal revision has been preferred 
for enhancement of the maintenance granted by the 
Family Court. As per the office report, there is a delay 
of 31 days. The delay is sufficiently explained and as 
such the delay condonation application is allowed.
3. Admit.
4. Learned counsel for the revisionist informed to 
this court that the respondent-husband also filed a 
revision i.e. Criminal Revision No. 894 of 2025 in which 
notices have been issued.
5. Taking into consideration that the dispute is 
nothing but appears to be a matrimonial dispute, 
therefore, the efforts may be made for their amicable 
settlement. 
6. In such view of the matter, let the learned counsel 
for the revisionist may inform to the counsel, who is 
representing the respondent-husband in the aforesaid 
revision.
7. List along with CRLR No. 894 of 2025 on 
26.02.2026.
8. On that day both the parties shall join the 
proceeding either physically or through V.C.
9. Learned counsel for the revisionist shall supply 
the copy of the revision to Mr. Saurabh Kumar Pandey 
during the course of the day.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
