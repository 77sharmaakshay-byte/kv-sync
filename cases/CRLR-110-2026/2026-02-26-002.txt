4. These are the two revisions, one filed by the wife 
and other one by the husband. The Family Court fixed 
interim maintenance of Rs. 30,000/-. There is one issue 
from their marriage and the child is 2 ½ years old. Both 
of them married in the year 2013. The husband is 
serving as Lt. Colonel in the Army and getting monthly 
salary of more than Rs. 2.30 lakhs. The interim 
maintenance as fixed by the Trial Court though is not 
reasonable and it has to be increased but learned 
counsel for the revisionist-wife submits that even the 
interim maintenance as fixed by the Family Court has 
not been paid to the revisionist.
5. Mr. Saurabh Pandey, who appears on behalf of 
the husband, submits that let an attempt be made for 
their amicable settlement and both the parties may be 
asked to be present on the next date. He also 
undertakes on instructions that by that time the arrear 
towards interim maintenance will also be paid by the 
husband by crediting the amount in the account of the 
revisionist-wife by tomorrow. The revisionist-wife shall 
provide the account details to Mr. Saurabh Pandey 
today itself.
6. Put up this matter on 25.03.2026.
7. On that day both the parties shall remain present 
in court.
