2. Mr. Suryakant Maithani, learned counsel for

3. The instant appeal is preferred under 
Section 173 of the Motor Vehicle Act, 1988 by the 
Insurance Company against the award passed by 
the Motor Accident Claim Tribunal dated 
26.05.2026 in MACP No.40 of 2018, “Smt. Urmila 
Barthwal Vs. Shri Neeraj Singh Khadka” on the 
ground that the Tribunal committed an illegality
while allowing the claim petition since the vehicle 
in question was never been insured with the 
Insurance Company and the documents with 
regard to the insurance pertains to a different 
vehicle, and in paragraph 54 the Tribunal itself 
recorded its finding that the accident took place by 
the vehicle no.UK07 TC 2603 though the claimants’
case is that the accident was caused by vehicle 
no.UK07 TC 2676.
4. On the other side, Mr. Suryakant Maithani, 
learned counsel who appears for the claimants 
submits that prima facie the findings as recorded in 
paragraph 54 that the accident took place by the 
vehicle no.UK07 TC 2603 appears to be a clerical 
mistake, therefore, he may be given a liberty to 
move a proper correction application, and till then 
he will not press the execution application.
5. In such view of the matter, since the learned 
counsel for the claimants seeks liberty to move a correction application before the Tribunal and also 
gives an undertaking that he will not press the 
execution application, therefore, on such statement 
and undertaking the matter is posted on
04.11.2026.
6. Put up this matter on 04.11.2026.
