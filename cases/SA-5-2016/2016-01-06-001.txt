Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate 
for the appellant. 
Having heard the learned counsel 
on behalf of the plaintiff/appellants, it 
transpires that some findings of the 
First Appellate Court are in concurrence 
with the learned Trial Judge, while few 
others are dissenting making them non￾concurrent. It transpires to be the 
dispute among the real brothers and 
sisters, making the Will left by the 
father as a subject of controversy. So, 
looking to the nature of such litigation, 
this second appeal is hereby admitted 
for hearing, clubbing it with another 
Second Appeal No.06 of 2016, wherefor 
also, this order of the day shall apply 
equally. 
Issue notice to the contesting 
respondent nos.1 to 3. 
Steps be taken within two days. 
The following substantial 
questions of law are hereby formulated:- 
1 Whether there can be any oral 
family settlement between the 
father and his sons in regard to 
self acquired property of father.
2 Whether a suit for declaration 
on the basis of oral family 
partition is maintainable without 
even impleading the other co￾sharers.
3 Whether a Civil Court has got 
jurisdiction to entertain a suit in 
regard to declaration of rights 
pertaining to Bhumidhari land.4 Whether both the courts below 
have not erred by not framing an 
issue in regard to alleged Will 
dated 20.03.1996 which is the 
basis of defense of defandants 
no.1, 2, 3.
5 Whether a Will can be 
presumed to be a genuine 
document on the basis of evidence 
of attesting witness without 
complying with the provisions of 
Section 63 of Indian Succession 
Act.
Meanwhile, both the parties are 
hereby directed to maintain the status 
quo as regards the nature and 
possession of the whole property, in 
question. They shall also not create any 
third party interest, whatsoever, on 
such land. 
Call the Lower Court records. 
List soon after the expiry of the 
winter vacations. 
Stay application (CLMA No.120 of 
2016) stands disposed of accordingly.
