Mr. Shivam Gulati, learned counsel for 
the appellant. 
2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned AGA for 
the State.
3. The instant criminal appeal is preferred 
by the appellant-Jatin, S/o Dharam Das 
against the judgment and order dated 
17.08.2026 passed by the trial court whereby 
he is convicted for the offences punishable 
under Sections 323, 354, 354D, 506 of IPC and 
Section 11(iv)/12 of POCSO Act with the 
sentence of six months rigorous 
imprisonment with the fine of Rs.10,000/-.
4. As per officer report appeal is well 
within time.
5. Admit.
6. Summon the trial court record.
7. Heard on bail application.
8. It is submitted by learned counsel for 
the appellant that while convicting the 
appellant the trial court granted interim bail 
to the appellant and within the period as 
provided in the judgment, under challenge,
the instant appeal is now preferred. He 
submits that the appellant have no criminal 
antecedent and on sole testimony of the victim he has been convicted.
9. On the other side Mr. Tumul Nainwal, 
learned AGA have not disputed that the trial 
court while convicting the appellant also 
granted the interim bail.
10. After hearing the submissions of 
learned counsel for the parties and taking 
into consideration the maximum sentences as 
awarded by the trial court is six months 
rigorous imprisonment and the appellant 
was on bail during trial and never misused 
the same, therefore, this Court is of the view 
that the interim bail, granted by the trial 
court, be made absolute.
11. Consequently, interim bail granted by 
the Trial Court on 17.08.2026 is made 
absolute. Appellant shall remain on bail, 
during the pendency of this appeal, on the 
same bond and sureties and on the same 
terms and conditions.
12. List this appeal in the month of 
November, 2026. 
13. Bail application stands disposed of. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
