Mr. Abhishek Singh Rawat, learned counsel

2. Heard on delay condonation application.
3. Learned counsel for the revisionist makes a 
submission regarding the delay that the revisionist 
works in a Laundry and thereby was not in the 
knowledge of the present matter; secondly his 
mother is aged about 65 years old and makes a 
submission that the matter may be considered and 
the delay be condoned.
4. There is delay of 68 days in preferring the 
instant revision. The grounds shown in the affidavit 
filed in support the delay condonation application 
are sufficient to condone the delay. The delay is 
condoned. Accordingly, delay condonation 
application is allowed.
5. Admit.
6. Issue notice to the respondent returnable at an 
early date. 
7. Steps be taken within one week.
8. Let the objection be filed by the respondent 
within three weeks.
9. List on 17.12.2025. 
10. As an interim measure, till the next date of 
listing effect and operation of the judgment and 
order dated 07.05.2025 passed by learned 
Additional Principal Judge Family Court, Haldwani, 
Nainital in Misc. Criminal Case No.510 of 2023 
shall remain stayed subject to the condition that 
revisionist shall deposit 50% of the maintenance 
amount. 
11. Stay application stands disposed of.
