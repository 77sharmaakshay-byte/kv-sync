Mr. Balvinder Singh, learned counsel

2. Mr. Dinesh Chauhan and Mr. N.S.

Pramod Tiwari, learned Brief Holder for the 
State.
3. By means of the present writ petition 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution 
of India, the petitioner has prayed for 
issuance of a writ in the nature of 
certiorari for quashing the First 
Information Report/Case Crime No. 0037 
of 2026 dated 13.02.2026, registered 
under Sections 115(2), 351(2), 352, 85 of 
B.N.S., 2023 and under Section 3/4 of 
Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 at Police 
Station Bazpur, District Udham Singh 
Nagar. The petitioner has further sought 
issuance of a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus commanding the 
respondent authorities not to arrest the 
petitioner pursuant to the aforesaid F.I.R.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioners would submit that the impugned F.I.R. has 
been lodged by respondent 
no.3/complainant alleging, inter alia, that 
on 04.12.2022, the petitioners assaulted 
her with an intention to cause her death 
and that, prior thereto, they had also 
demanded dowry and, after subjecting her 
to cruelty, had driven her out of her 
matrimonial home along with her minor 
daughter and extended threats of dire 
consequences.
5. He would further submit that the 
allegations arise out of a matrimonial 
dispute and that the petitioners are the 
close relatives of the husband of 
respondent no.; that, petitioner no.1 is the 
husband, petitioner nos.2 and 3 are the 
father-in-law and mother-in-law 
respectively, petitioner no.4 is the sister-in￾law and petitioner no.5 is the maternal 
aunt of respondent no.3/complainant.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would further submit that the petitioners 
are innocent and have falsely been 
implicated in the present case on account 
of matrimonial discord; that, the very 
foundation of the prosecution story is 
rendered doubtful by the contemporaneous 
circumstances; that, according to the 
allegations contained in the F.I.R., the 
petitioners allegedly assaulted respondent 
no.3 on 04.12.2022, however, on the said 
date, respondent no.3 had appeared in an 
examination conducted by the Board of 
School Education Haryana, Bhiwani, and, 
as per the examination record, she was present at the examination centre, where 
the reporting/entry time was 12:50 P.M. It 
is, therefore, contended that her presence 
at the examination centre on the date and 
around the time of the alleged occurrence 
raises a serious and substantial doubt 
regarding the occurrence as narrated in 
the F.I.R. and warrants consideration of 
the defence version at the appropriate 
stage.
7. Learned counsel would further submit 
that respondent no.3 has made omnibus 
and sweeping allegations against the entire 
family of petitioner no.1, without 
attributing specific and distinct overt acts 
to each of the petitioners. In particular, 
petitioner no.5, who is the maternal aunt 
of petitioner no.1, is not residing with the 
petitioners and has no occasion to 
participate in the alleged matrimonial 
affairs or the alleged occurrence. The 
implication of such a relative, without any 
specific allegation demonstrating her 
individual involvement, further indicates 
that the F.I.R. has been lodged with an 
ulterior motive to implicate the entire 
family of the husband, therefore, the 
impugned F.I.R. is liable to be quashed.
8. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties and upon perusal of the material 
available on record, this Court is, prima 
facie, of the view that the allegations 
levelled in the impugned F.I.R. arise out of 
a matrimonial dispute and that respondent 
no.3 has made allegations against the 
entire family of petitioner no.1. It is also evident from the record that the alleged 
incident is stated to have taken place on 
04.12.2022, whereas the material relied 
upon by the petitioners indicates that 
respondent no.3 had appeared in an 
examination conducted by the Board of 
School Education Haryana, Bhiwani, on 
the same date and had reported at the 
examination centre at 12:50 P.M. The 
aforesaid circumstance, coupled with the 
allegation that petitioner no.5, being the 
maternal aunt of petitioner no.1, was 
residing separately and had no apparent 
occasion to participate in the matrimonial 
affairs of respondent no.3, requires 
consideration.
9. Issue notice to respondent no.3, 
returnable at an early date.
10. Steps to be taken within one week.
11. List after service report is received.
12. Counter affidavit/objections be filed 
by the State/respondent.
13. Till the next date of listing, as an 
interim measure, the petitioner shall not be 
arrested in connection with First 
Information Report/Case Crime No.0037 of 
2026 registered at Police Station Bazpur, 
District Udham Singh Nagar, provided the 
petitioners shall cooperate with the 
investigation.
13. Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
