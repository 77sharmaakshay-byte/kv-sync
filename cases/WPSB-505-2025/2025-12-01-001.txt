Heard on Interim Relief Application, IA 
No.1 of 2025.
It is the case of the petitioner that she 
appeared in the Civil Services Examination,
CSE, 2024, and secured All India Rank 
189th. Her preference was Indian Police 
Service, but it has been denied to her on the ground of less height. 
It is the case of the petitioner that 
Rule 8(3) of the Regulations Relating to the 
Medical Examination of Candidates for the 
Civil Services Examination, 2024 (“the 
Regulations”), has been wrongly read by the 
respondent-authorities by denying the 
benefit of the Regulations to the petitioner. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the Central Administrative 
Tribunal had directed that one post of IPS 
cadre arising from Civil Services 
Examination, 2024, be kept vacant, but 
since now the Original Application has 
already been decided, further interim order 
is required till the disposal of the case, one 
post of the Indian Police Service (IPS) in 
Civil Service Examination, 2024, be kept 
vacant. 
Having considered, as an interim 
measure, till the next date of listing, one 
post of IPS in Civil Service Examination, 
2024, shall be kept vacant. 
Interim relief application stands 
disposed of, accordingly.
