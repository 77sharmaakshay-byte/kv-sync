Hon’ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
Mr. Rajesh Joshi, Advocate for the

Mr. P.C. Bisht, Standing Counsel for the

Mr. Rakesh Thapliyal, Senior Advocate 
for respondent nos. 4 & 5.
Stay Vacation Application No. 4444, 
5294 and 5347 of 2020 have been filed by 
the respondents.
Admit the writ petition. 
Misc. application IA No. 7120 of 2020 is 
allowed. Rejoinder affidavit is taken on 
record.
Heard learned counsel for the parties on 
the stay vacation application.
Learned counsel for the petitioners
would submit that respondent nos. 4 & 5 
have rightly been promoted on the post of 
Superintendent (Gazetted) vide promotion 
order dated 22.05.2020.
On the instructions received
telephonically, Mr. Rakesh Thapliyal, 
learned Senior Counsel appearing on behalf 
of the respondent nos. 4 & 5 would submit 
that pursuant to promotion order dated 
22.05.2020, respondent nos. 4 & 5 have 
joined their duties on promotional post on 
23.05.2020. Petitioners have filed this 
petition seeking writ in the nature of 
certiorari to quash the promotion order 
dated 22.05.2020. Since pursuant to the 
promotion order the respondent nos. 4 & 5 
have joined their duties, therefore
petitioners have filed this petition before this 
Court on 11.06.2020. 
This Court without having considered 
the facts that the petitioners are coming 
belatedly before the Court stayed the effect 
and operation of the promotion order vide 
order dated 12.06.2020. It is settled 
proposition of law that interim order should not be in the nature of final relief. The relief 
sought by the petitioners is for quashing of 
the promotional order. There should be no
interim order on the stay of the promotion. 
Further more that the respondent nos. 4 & 5 
have already joined on the promotional post.
Accordingly, stay vacation applications 
are disposed of with the direction that the 
respondent nos.4 & 5, who have been 
promoted and joined on the post of 
Superintendent (Gazetted) shall continue to 
work on the said posts until further order of 
this Court. The order dated 12.06.2020 is 
modified to the extent as above.
List this case for final disposal in the 
month of October, 2020.
 (Lok Pal Singh, J.)
