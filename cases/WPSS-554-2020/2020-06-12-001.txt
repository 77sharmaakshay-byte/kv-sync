The petitioners have challenged the 
impugned order of promotion dated 22.05.2020, 
promoting respondent Nos. 4 and 5; on the post of 
Superintendent (Gazetted).
The argument of the learned counsel for the 
petitions is that, respondent Nos. 4 and 5, who 
were initially working as CT Grade Teacher, later 
on, on account of same being declared as a dying 
cadre, as per the Government Order dated 
21.09.2005, their services had been merged in the 
LT Grade, and as a consequence of the merger in 
the LT Grade, the respondent Nos. 4 and 5, they 
had been drawing a higher scale with a grade pay 
of Rs. 4600/-.
Learned counsel for the petitioners further 
argues that in accordance with the Rule 6 of the 
Rules, called as “The Uttarakhand Tribes 
Welfare Gazetted Officer Services Rules, 2019”.
Under Rule 6 of the said Rules of 2019, 50 percent 
posts of the Superintendent (Gazetted); have to be 
filled through promotion, on the basis of seniority, 
from amongst the psychologists, substantially 
appointed and who have completed 5 years of 
service.
The grievance of the petitioners is that once 
the respondent Nos. 4 and 5 had already been merged into LT Grade Teacher, and since they 
were drawing a higher grade pay of Grade Pay of 
Rs. 4600/-, they couldn’t have been promoted by 
the impugned order on the post of Superintendent 
(Gazetted), which is carrying a lower grade pay of 
Rs. 4200/-, because there cannot be a promotion of 
respondent Nos. 4 and 5 by down grading in their 
pay scale.
Learned counsel for the petitioners further 
submits that the respondents would not be entitled 
to be considered for promotion, because in 
accordance with Rule 6 of Rules, 2019, they do not 
fall to be within the feeding cadre, eligible for the 
promotion on the post of Superintendent 
(Gazetted), as per the said Rules of 2019.
In that view of the matter, apparently this 
Court is satisfied with the arguments, as extended 
by the learned counsel for the petitioners that the 
promotion granted to respondent Nos. 4 and 5 is by 
downgrading their pay scale, which cannot be done 
under the Rules of 2019, as well as the fact that 
they do not fall within the feeding cadre for 
promotion on the post of Superintendent 
(Gazetted), hence there would a stay of the order 
impugned dated 22.05.2020; till the next date of 
listing.
List this matter after three weeks.
Stay Application (CLMA No. 3770/2020) 
stands disposed of.
