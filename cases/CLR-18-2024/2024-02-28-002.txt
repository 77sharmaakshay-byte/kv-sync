28.02.2024 CLR No. 18 of 2024
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The proposed Civil Revision has been filed 
challenging the order dated 18.01.2024, passed 
by learned Judge, Family Court, Khatima, 
District Udham Singh Nagar in Civil Case No. 49 
of 2023, “Smt. Dalwinder Kaur and Another vs. 
Harjeet Singh”, filed by the respondent no. 1-
plaintiff under Section 19 of the Hindu 
Adoptions and Maintenance Act, 1956 against 
her father-in-law, by which, the Application,
filed by the revisionist-defendant under Order 1 
Rule 10 (2) of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 
to implead the father of the plaintiff-respondent 
no. 1 as a defendant, has been dismissed. 
2. Heard Mr. Deep Prakash Bhatt, learned 
counsel for the proposed revisionist. 
3. Mr. Deep Prakash Bhatt, Advocate, has 
relied upon a judgment of Hon’ble Kerala High 
Court, passed in “Deepa vs. Laly Mathew”, 
2003 (1) KLT 87; judgments of Hon’ble 
Allahabad High Court, passed in “K.S.M. 
Basheer Mohammad and Sons vs. Ram Bali 
Singh and Others”, 2001 (2) A.R.C. 470, 
and, in “Raj Kishore Mishra vs. Smt. Meena 
Mishra”, AIR 1995 ALL 70.”
4. Admit. 5. Issue notice to respondent no. 1.
6. Steps to be taken within a week. 
7. List on 30.04.2024. Till then, the further 
proceedings of the said Civil Suit No. 49 of 
2023 are stayed. 
8. Stay Application (IA No. 1 of 2024) is 
disposed of accordingly. 
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
