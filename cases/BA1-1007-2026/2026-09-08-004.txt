Mr. Akshay Pradhan, learned counsel for the

2. Mrs. Meenakshi Sharma, learned AGA, for the 
State of Uttarakhand.
3. The present applicant – Aamir, is praying for 
bail in reference to FIR dated 20.05.2026 bearing FIR
No.308 of 2026, at Police Station Patel Nagar, District 
Dehradun, wherein, he has been implicated for the 
offences punishable under Sections 115 (2), 351 (2) and 
352 of BNS, 2023 read with Sections 4 and 5 of the 
Immoral Traffic (Prevention) Act, 1956.
4. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
applicant that the applicant is languishing in jail since 
20.05.2026 and the applicant’s wife is pregnant and is
at the advance stage of more than eight months and 
there is no one to look after her, therefore, applicant be 
enlarged on interim bail.
5. On such submission, learned AGA, on 
instructions, submits that the wife of the applicant is 
pregnant and at the advance stage of more than eight 
months.
6. After hearing the submissions of the learned 
counsel for the applicant and taking into consideration 
that the applicant’s wife is pregnant and is at the 
advance stage of her pregnancy, this Court is of the 
view that, at this stage, instead of granting a regular 
bail to the applicant, let the applicant be admitted to 
interim bail for a period of one month.
7. In such view of the matter, the instant bail 
application is being kept pending to be posted after 
three weeks.
8. Let the applicant – Aamir, be released on bail to 
be reckoned from the date of release, on his executing 
a personal bond and furnishing two reliable sureties, 
each of the like amount to the satisfaction of the Court concerned. It is made clear that the applicant shall 
surrender before the court concerned on or before the 
expiry of tenure of interim term bail. 
9. List this matter on 05.10.2026.
