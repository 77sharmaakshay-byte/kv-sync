COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
02.02.2024 C482 No. 160 of 2024
Sri Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Vipul Sharma, learned counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Saurabh Kumar Pandey, learned Brief Holder for 
the State- respondent no.1.
3. By the instant application, preferred under Section 
482 of Cr.P.C., the applicant is challenging the proceedings 
of Criminal Case No.7200 of 2022, “State vs. Pankaj 
Punera”, wherein the present applicant is facing trial in 
respect of the offences punishable under Sections 323, 504, 
506 and 498-A IPC, which is pending in the Court of 
Additional Judicial Magistrate/ Vth Senior Civil Judge, 
Dehradun.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that the 
applicant married with respondent no.2 in the year 2015, 
and from this wedlock, a son was born in the year 2016. He 
submits that due to some differences, since 2021 they are 
living separately, and the present applicant filed divorce 
petition for seeking divorce.
5. The FIR was lodged by respondent no.2 on 
16.11.2021, and after investigation, charge-sheet was filed, 
and the cognizance was taken.
6. In Paragraph No.16, the applicant has given a 
categorical statement that the applicant is ready and willing 
to live with his wife- respondent no.2, and an attempt 
should be made for their amicable settlement through 
mediation.
7. Apparently, as per the allegations, as alleged in the 
FIR, the dispute is nothing, but a matrimonial dispute, and 
an attempt should be made for their amicable settlement 
through mediation.8. Issue notice to respondent no.2.
9. Learned counsel for the applicant shall take steps to 
serve respondent no.2, within a week, by registered post.
10. List this matter on 18.03.2024.
11. On that day, both, the applicant and respondent 
no.2, shall remain present in Court. The applicant shall 
inform respondent no.2, by any mode, about the date fixed 
in the matter.
12. Till then, the proceedings of Criminal Case No.7200 
of 2022, “State vs. Pankaj Punera”, for the offences 
punishable under Sections 323, 504, 506 and 498-A IPC, 
pending in the Court of learned Additional Judicial 
Magistrate/ Vth Senior Civil Judge, Dehradun, shall remain 
stayed.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
Vacation Judge
