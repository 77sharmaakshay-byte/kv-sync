Heard.
This appeal is directed against the award
dated 08.07.2013, passed by Motor Accident
Claims Tribunal/Additional District Judge,
Bageshwar, in Motor Accident Claim Case No.
03 of 2012 whereby the Tribunal has awarded `
6,89,000/- as amount of compensation to the
claimant/respondent No.1 Parwati Devi. 
Admit the appeal.
Summon the record of the Tribunal.
Issue notices to respondents.
Having heard learned counsel for the
appellant, and after going through the impugned
award, as an interim measure, it is directed that
if the appellant deposits ` 4 lakhs (adjusting the
statutory deposit made before this Court) before
the Tribunal, within a period of 45 days from
today, recovery in respect of remaining sum
shall remain stayed until further orders of this
court. (Stay Application No. 9098 of 2013
stands disposed of).
List after service on notice on the
respondents.
