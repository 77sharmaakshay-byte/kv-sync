BA1 NO. 976 of 2026
Hon'ble Ashish Naithani, J.,
 Mr. Mani Kumar, learned counsel for the Applicant. 
2. Mr. J.P. Kandpal, and Mr. Dinesh Chauhan, learned A.G.A. for 
the State of Uttarakhand. 
3. The present Bail Application has been moved on behalf of the 
Applicant, who is in judicial custody in connection with FIR No. 69 of 
2025 (Sessions Trial No. 176 of 2025), registered at Police Station 
Gadarpur, District Udham Singh Nagar, for the offences punishable 
under Sections 3(5) and 103(1) of Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 and 
under Section 3/25 of the Arms Act, 1959.
4. Learned counsel for the Applicant, while pressing for grant of 
bail, submits that the present matter is a case of parity as co-accused 
have been enlarged on bail; that though the present Applicant is roped 
in the present matter and being main accused he is alleged to have shot 
the fire resulting in the death of the deceased, the case is not so. It is 
further submitted that there are anomalies in the procedure of the 
investigation, mainly the grounds of arrest were communicated while 
his arrest.
5. Learned State Counsel however refutes the submissions so 
advanced by learned counsel for the Applicant contending that the 
present Applicant is the main accused and in his case a fire arm, 
namely a country made pistol (tamancha), was recovered from his 
possession. He further prays for and is granted ten days' time to file 
objections/counter affidavit to the Bail Application.
6. Let objections/counter affidavit be filed within ten days.
7. List this case on 19.06.2026. 
 (Ashish Naithani, J.)
