Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
Mrs. Pushpa Joshi, learned senior counsel 
assisted by Mr. Saurav Adhikari, learned 
counsel for the appellants.
2. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned AGA for 
the State of Uttarakhand.
3. Mr. Saurabh Kumar Pandey, learned 
counsel for the victim.
4. The instant appeals are preferred by the 
appellants against the order of their conviction 
and sentence passed by the Trial Court. These 
appeals are admitted on 06.01.2014 and all the 
appellants are on bail.
5. Now the compounding application (IA No.388 of 2026) is filed alongwith an affidavit of 
the victim with the averment that she does not 
want to pursue with the proceedings, since she 
is now well settled and married with another 
person and having four minor children and if 
the appeal is permitted to continue then it’s 
outcome will affect her matrimonial life.
6. On the other side, the appellant –
Shamsher, who is convicted under Section 376 
of IPC, also submits that he is now married with 
another lady having three children, and 
therefore, the order of the Trial Court be 
quashed since the victim herself is not intended
to pursue with the appeal, since it’s outcome 
will affect her matrimonial life and secondly the 
outcome of the appeal may likely affect the 
matrimonial life of the applicant – Shamsher,
also.
7. Learned senior counsel for the appellants
submits that all the appellants, herein, are 
present in Court except Masta (appellant of
CRLA No.370 of 2013) who is infact is no more. 
The victim also joined the proceedings through 
VC and they all are identified by their
respective counsels.
8. Learned AGA submits that the offence 
punishable under Section 376 of IPC is not 
compoundable and these appeals are admitted 
appeal preferred against conviction. On such 
submissions, learned senior counsel for the 
appellants placed reliance to one of the 
judgment of the Hon’ble Apex Court in the case 
of “Madhukar and others Vs. State of 
Maharashtra and another” reported in 2025 
SCC Online SC 1415, by placing reliance to 
paragraphs 6 and 7, and submits that even the 
offence punishable under Section 376 of IPC can 
be compounded. 
9. Let the State may file their objections 
keeping in view the mandate of the judgment of the Hon’ble Apex Court in the case “Madhukar 
and others Vs. State of Maharashtra and 
another” (supra) within two weeks.
10. List these matters on 05.10.2026.
11. On the next date, the presence of the 
parties are not required.
