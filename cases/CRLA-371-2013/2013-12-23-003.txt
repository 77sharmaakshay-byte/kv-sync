In
CRLA No. 371 of 2013
Hon’ble V.K. Bist,J.
Mrs. Pushpa Joshi, Senior Advocate,
assisted by Mr. Saurav Adhikari, Advocate,
present for the appellant.
Mr. M.A. Khan, A.G.A. present for the
State.
Heard.
This is an admitted appeal, and bail
application of Gulam Rasool (appellant no. 1)
is pressed by learned Senior Advocate.
It is contended by learned Senior
Advocate, for the appellant that appellant has
been sentenced under section 366/120B,
395/120B, 323/120B and 506/120B IPC. It is
further submitted that appellant was on bail
during trial.
Having considered submission of
learned counsel for the appellant, and after
going through the papers on record, this
Court is of the view that appellant deserves
bail.
The bail application is allowed. Let
appellant Gulam Rasool (appellant no. 1) be
released on bail, during the pendency of the
appeal, on same terms and condition on
which he was on bail during trial.
