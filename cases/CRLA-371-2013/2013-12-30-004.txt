I.A. No.4863 of 2013 (Urgency Application)
In 
CRLA No.371 of 2013
Hon’ble V.K.Bist, J.
Mrs. Pushpa Joshi, Senior Advocate 
assisted by Mr. Saurav Adhikari, Advocate 
for the appellants. 
Mr. R.K. Sah, A.G.A. with Mr. K.S. 
Rangarh, Brief Holder for the State. 
Heard learned counsel for the 
parties. 
It is contended by learned Senior 
Advocate for the appellants that appellant 
nos.2 to 5 have been sentenced under 
section 366/120B, 395/120B, 323/120B 
and 506/120B IPC. It is further submitted 
that appellant nos.2 to 5 were on bail 
during trial. 
Having considered submission of 
learned counsel for the appellants and 
after going through the papers on record, 
this Court is of the view that appellant 
nos.2 to 5 deserve bail. 
The urgency application is allowed. 
Let the appellants Zahoor (appellant no. 
2), Safardil (appellant no. 3), Dadi 
(appellant no. 4) and Vajeer (appellant 
no. 5) be released on bail, during the 
pendency of the appeal, on same terms 
and condition on which they were on bail 
during trial.
 (V.K. Bist, J.)
Arpan 30.12.2013
