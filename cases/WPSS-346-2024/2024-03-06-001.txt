3. Petitioners have sought the indulgence of 
this Court for quashing the order dated 
16.02.2024 passed by the respondent No.4, 
whereby, the name of the private respondents 
were recommended for promotion on the post 
of Administrative Officer in Pay-Scale 9300-
34,800 Grade Pay Rs.4600/- (7th Pay 
Commission Level-7).
4. The petitioners disputed the selection of 
the private respondents on the ground that 
petitioners are senior to the private 
respondents, as per the Final Seniority List 
dated 09.05.2023. The name of the petitioners 
are reflected at Serial No.6 to 9 in the said 
Seniority List, while the name of the private 
respondent Nos.5 to 9 are at Serial No.12 to 
16. 
5. The Final Seniority List is annexed as 
Annexure No.7 to the writ petition and from 
the perusal of the same, it is reflected that this 
is the amended final seniority list of 2021-22 of 
Ministerial Staff of the Collectorate, District 
Uttarkashi.2
6. It is Submitted by learned counsel for 
the petitioners that the earlier seniority list 
dated 19/20.04.2023 was kept in abeyance 
by this Court vide order dated 16.05.2023. 
As per record, the Final Seniority List dated 
09.05.2023 is not stayed. 
7. Issue notice to respondent Nos.5 to 9, 
returnable within six weeks.
8. Steps to be taken within a week.
9. Learned State Counsel is directed to file 
counter affidavit within a period of six weeks.
10. List on 11.06.2024 along with WPSS 
No.692 of 2023.
11. The promotion given to the private 
respondents (Respondent Nos.5 to 9) shall be 
subject to the final outcome of the present 
writ petition.
