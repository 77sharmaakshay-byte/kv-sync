Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. H.C. Pathak, learned counsel for the applicants.
2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned AGA along with Mr. Nikhil 
Bisht, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. By means of present C528 application, applicants 
have challenged the charge-sheet dated 09.04.2026, 
cognizance order dated 14.05.2026, annexure-10, passed 
by learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Nainital
in Criminal Case No.591 of 2026, State vs. Bhuwan 
Chandra Arya & others as well as the entire proceedings 
of the aforesaid criminal case.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the applicants 
that land in-question was purchased by one Sunil Kumar 
in the year 2006, who was allegedly employed as a 
domestic help with the applicants which fact is denied by 
them. The land was sold by the original tenure holders, 
Shri Tari Ram and Bhuwan Chand to the Sunil Kumar. 
The complaint has now been filed by respondent no.2, 
who claims ownership over the said land after so many 
years.
5. On the aforesaid averments, respondent no.2 filed 
an application under Section 156(3) Cr.P.C. before the 
Magistrate, who passed an order directing registration of 
an FIR and investigation of the matter, which culminated 
in filing of the charge-sheet, taking of cognizance, and 
passing of the impugned summoning order in the 
Criminal Case No.591 of 2026, State vs. Bhuwan 2
Chandra Arya & others. 
6. Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that 
they have no concern with the alleged sale deed, as they 
are neither the purchasers nor the sellers of the property, 
nor were they witnesses to the said sale deed. He further 
submits that no case of cheating, even prima facie, is 
made out against the applicants. 
7. Issue notice to the respondent no.2, returnable 
within six weeks.
8. Steps to be taken within a week.
9. List on 20.07.2026.
10. In the meantime, respondents may file the counter 
affidavit.
11. Till the next date of listing, further proceedings of
Criminal Case No.591 of 2026, State vs. Bhuwan 
Chandra Arya & others, pending in the court of learned 
Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Nainital shall 
remain stayed.
12. Stay application (IA No.1/2026) stands disposed of 
accordingly.
