Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Lalit Sharma and Ms. Suraiya Naaz, learned 
counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Himanshu Sain, learned A.G.A. for the State.
3. In all these petitions the present applicant is 
challenging the order passed by the learned Magistrate 
allowing the application moved by the different 
complainant under section 175(3) of BNS, 2023. The 
challenge is on the ground that the present applicant is 
the owner of the land, which were sold out to the 
complainant herein by way of registered sale deed and 
not only this even their names are also mutated in the 
revenue record but subsequently their possession 
could not be ascertained due to discrepancies in the
boundaries and before filing the application under 
section 175(3) of BNSS, 2023, the complaint was made, 
wherein, the FIR was not registered since the police 
was of the view that it is a case of demarcation but 
subsequent thereto the respondent-complainant 
moved an application under section 175(3) of BNSS, 
2023, wherein, the report of police was called, which is 
also placed on record from which it reveals that the 
Sub-Inspector was of the view that the matter is 
required to be enquired from the revenue department so that they may proceed for the demarcation. Despite 
such report learned Magistrate ordered for registration 
of FIR.
4. The question is if the Investigating Agency was 
of the view that it is a case of demarcation then on 
what basis the application moved by the complainant 
under section 175(3) was allowed by the Magistrate. 
This aspect requires scrutiny and prima-facie it reveals 
that if the case is of a demarcation whether it requires a 
criminal investigation, let the State may get the 
instructions whether the present applicant is actually 
the owner of all the piece of land, which were sold out 
to the complainant.
5. List this matter on 22.07.2026 along with C528 
No. 1498, 1499, 1511, 1516 and 1517 of 2026.
6. Till then the respondents are restrained from 
registering the FIR pursuant to the order impugned.
