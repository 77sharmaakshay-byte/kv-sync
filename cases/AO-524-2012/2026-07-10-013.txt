The United India Insurance Company Ltd. ....Appellant

Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
None is present for the parties.
In this appeal from order, arguments have 
already been heard and this matter is reserved 
for judgment. 
On behalf of the appellant, it has been 
argued that in the instant case, the deceased was 
the driver of the tanker owned by the 
Cantonment Board, therefore, he is not a third 
party in view of the law laid down by the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court in the case of New India 
Assurance Company Limited v. Sadanand Mukhi 
and others, (2009) 2 SCC 417. It is also argued 
that the claimants could have claimed 
compensation under the Employee’s 
Compensation Act, 1923 (“the Act”). 
This aspect requires more deliberations. 
What is the scope of Section 147 of the Motor 
Vehicles Act, 1988? What is the concept of the 
third party? And What is the applicability of the 
Employee’s Compensation Act, 1923, if any in 
such matters? Therefore judgment is not 
delivered and the matter shall be heard afresh on 
the above points. 
List this matter on 13.08.2026, for further 
hearing.
