Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. M.S. Tyagi, Senior Advocate assisted 
by Mr. Nagesh Aggarwal, Advocate for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate for the 
respondents.
3. Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the 
petitioners would submit that this is a matter 
arising out of the consolidation proceedings and
the petitioner has got right through the Will of 
the original owner.
4. Learned counsel for the respondents, on the 
other hand, would submit that the respondents 
are real sons of the original owner and they have 
got the Will executed by their father in their 
favour in respect of the land and the same is a 
registered Will, therefore, it could override the 
alleged later Will which is admittedly 
unregistered.
5. In considered view of this Court, this 
matter needs deliberation.
6. Admit the petition.
7. Learned counsel for the parties would 
submit that the pleadings have already been 
exchanged.
8. List for final hearing on 16.07.2024.
