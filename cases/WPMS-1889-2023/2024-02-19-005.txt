Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Nagesh Agarwal, Advocate for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate for the 
respondents.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the petitioner was bequeathed the 
property in question by the father of respondent 
no.1; that, petitioner was first impleaded in 
consolidation proceedings, however, subsequently 
on the application of the respondent no.1, he was 
deleted from the array of parties; that, the 
consolidation court has no power to delete 
necessary parties at the time of final of 
pronouncement of judgment; that, now the 
respondent no.1 wants to raise construction on the 
land in question thereby changing the nature of 
land. 
4. Per contra, learned counsel for respondent 
no.1 would submit there was no question of 
executing the Will by his father Amir Hasan in 
favour of present petitioner in the year 1926 as his 
father Amir Hasan had already expired in 1925.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
controverted and prayed for time to file certified 
copy of the Will duly attested by the petitioner, by 
way of affidavit before this Court along with the 
evidence, if led by the petitioner, before the lower 
courts to prove that Will.
6. Allowed.
