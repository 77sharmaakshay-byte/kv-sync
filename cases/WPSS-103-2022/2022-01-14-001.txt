The petitioner contends, that in the process of 
selection, despite of having secured higher marks of 
58.75, his candidature is not being considered and 
rather the vacancy, which has chanced on account 
of non-joining of a candidate, who were included 
under the first select list, as against the vacancy 
available to reservation available to the dependants 
of the freedom fighter, the respondent No.3 has 
been accommodated therein, who otherwise, 
according to the petitioner has secured only 57.75 
marks, which is lesser than to the petitioner’s 
marks. 
The petitioner submits that his claim stands 
substantiated, based on the judgment rendered by 
this Court, which was later on affirmed by the 
Division Bench also. 
In these peculiar facts and circumstances, while issuing notices to respondent No.3, the 
respondent No.1 and 2, may file their counter 
affidavit within a period of four weeks from today. 
Issue notices to the respondent No.3. 
Steps would be taken by the petitioner within 
a period of one week from today. 
By way of interim measure, it is directed that 
the petitioner, since he has secured higher marks, 
his candidature would be considered by the 
respondent No.2, as against the vacancy, which has 
fallen vacant and the decision on the same would be 
kept in a sealed envelope and would be only given 
effect to only after prior permission of this Court.
