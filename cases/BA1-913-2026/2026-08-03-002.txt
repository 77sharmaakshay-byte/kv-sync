2. Mr. Himanshu Sain, learned AGA for

3. In this particular case the applicant has 
been charge sheeted for the offences 
punishable under Section 302 read with 
Section 201 IPC and Section 392, 411 of IPC 
and 103(1), 309 (6), 238 (a) and 317 (2) of the 
BNS, 2023. 
4. It is argued by learned counsel for the 
applicant that except the last seen evidence 
on the basis of CCTV footages at a liquor 
shop the applicant has been charge sheeted. 
He submits that the dead body was 
recovered on 17.02.2026 at about 02:51 p.m. 
and after almost four days on pointing out of 
the present applicant blood stained jacket 
and the currency of Rs.20,000/- was also 
recovered from the place of the incident from 
where the dead body was recovered. He 
submits that the investigation appears to be 
faulty since the recovery of the blood stained 
cloth alongwith the recovery of money is 
shown to be recovered on the pointing out of 
the applicant after four days from the place 
where the dead body was found. He submits that the story of the prosecution about the 
recovery is completely doubtful and easily an 
inference can be drawn that the recovery of 
the blood stained cloth and money is 
completely planted one. Primafacie, the 
investigation appears to be faulty though 
there are the evidences of last seen at about 
07:30 a.m. in the morning and then 09:30 and 
the dead body was recovered at about 02:51 
p.m. on the same day. He also submits that in 
order to deprive the applicant to get default 
bail in a hurried manner the charge sheet has 
been filed without FSL report. 
5. Let the I.O. concerned may file his 
personal affidavit about such lacuna in the 
investigation, which has been pointed out by 
the learned counsel for the applicant, 
positively within a week failing which the 
I.O. shall join the proceeding through V.C.
6. Put up this matter on 11.08.2026.
