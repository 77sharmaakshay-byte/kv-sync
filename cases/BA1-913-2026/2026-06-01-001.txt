BA 1st No.913 of 2026
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. S.R.S. Gill, learned counsel for the Applicant.
2. Mr. S.S. Chauhan, learned DAG with Mr. Vikash Uniyal, 
learned Brief Holder, for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. In the High Court of Uttarakhand, the present case arises 
from Bail Application filed under Section 483 of Bharatiya 
Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, seeking regular bail for the 
Applicant – Vinayak Tyagi, who has been accused in FIR 
No.110 of 2026, under Sections 103 (1), 309 (6), 238 (a), 317 
(2) of BNS, at Police Station Manglore, District Haridwar. 
4. Learned counsel for the Applicant submits for the 
consideration of the bail that the matter is solely based on the 
circumstantial evidence as there are no eye witnesses. The main 
reliance regarding last seen theory is the circumstantial 
evidence, which may be based on the CCTV footage; regarding 
the CCTV footage, learned counsel for the Applicant submits 
that the reliance cannot be placed on CCTV footage as it is not 
relevant and cannot be connected with the said incident.
5. There is a representation on behalf of the Complainant –
Sri Bilal Ahmad, learned counsel through VC, who seeks 
permission to assist the Court.
6. Permission granted.
7. Two weeks’ time is granted to the State to file counter 
affidavit.
8. List this matter on 17.06.2026.
