Hon’ble G. Narendar, C.J.
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Harshit Sanwal, learned counsel holding brief of 
Mr. Pankaj Kapil, learned counsel for the petitioner. 
2. Mr. P. C. Bisht, learned Addl. C.S.C. for the State. 
3. The petitioner is serving as District Employment 
Officer and, by the impugned order, he has been transferred 
from District Uddham Singh Nagar to District Chamoli; 
that, before his transfer, options were invited, pursuant to 
which the petitioner submitted his options for nine places. 
The contention of the petitioner is that out of the options 
given by the petitioner, the post is still lying vacant in the 
options no. 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 given by the petitioner, but 
despite this fact, his options have not been considered and 
he has been transferred to a distant place at Chamoli. 
4. As per the instructions received by the learned 
counsel for the State, it is submitted that all the options 
submitted by the petitioner have been filled up and, hence, 
he was transferred to District Chamoli. 
5. At this, learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
the post is still lying vacant in the options no. 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 
and 9 given by the petitioner.
6. Be that as it may, the options. 2, 4, 5, 6, 8 and 9 given 
by the petitioner vide his detailed representation dated 
06.06.2025 be considered and the report to be submitted to 
the Court by the next date of hearing. 
7. List this case on 03.07.2025.
