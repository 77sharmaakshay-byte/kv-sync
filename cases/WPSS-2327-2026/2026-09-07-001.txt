3. Petitioner was transferred by order dated 
27.08.2026 from Government Primary School, Udhuwa, 
Block Bhimtal, District Nainital to Government Primary 
School, Indiranagar, Block Haldwani, District Nainital. 
Before the petitioner could join at the Government 
Primary School, Indiranagar, Block Haldwani, District 
Nainital, her transfer order dated 27.08.2026 was 
cancelled vide impugned transfer cancellation order 
dated 31.08.2026 issued by respondent no.4, annexure￾8 to the writ petition.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that petitioner has been transferred to the Government 
Primary School, Indiranagar, Block Haldwani, District 
Nainital, on her application moved under the spouse 
policy and the transfer order has been made by the 
competent authority after considering the case of the 
petitioner. He further submits that petitioner has already 
worked in the Government Primary School, Udhuwa, 
Block Bhimtal, a Durgam School, for the last 12 years 
and, since her husband was working as Assistant 
Teacher, L.T. Grade, and is posted at Government Higher Secondary School, Ranibagh, Block Bhimtal, District 
Nainital, she was transferred under the spouse policy. 
He also submits that cancellation of the transfer order is 
totally arbitrary, illegal, and cannot be sustained for the 
reason that the cancellation order has been passed on 
the premise that both husband and wife are working in 
the same Block. Petitioner submits that there is no such 
provision in the Transfer Act, 2017 that under the 
spouse policy transfer cannot be made outside the Block.
5. Per contra, learned State Counsel does not dispute 
that there is any such provision under the Transfer Act, 
2017, which prohibits spouse policy transfer from being 
made if the husband and wife are working in the same 
Block.
6. In such view of the matter, the impugned 
cancellation transfer order dated 31.08.2026 is hereby 
stayed. Respondents are directed to give immediate 
joining to the petitioner at the Government Primary 
School, Indiranagar, Block Haldwani.
7. Respondents may file the counter affidavit within 
six weeks.
8. List on 26.10.2026.
9. Stay application (IA No.1/2026) stands disposed of 
accordingly.
10. Let a certified copy of this order be issued to the 
petitioner, today itself, on payment of usual charges.
