JUDGMENT
BARIN GHOSH, C.J. (Oral)
Subject matter of the writ petition is termination/ 
cancellation of the compassionate appointment of the petitioner. 
The matter is such that the petitioner should have approached the 
Central Administrative Tribunal first. Petitioner could not directly 
approach this Court. We, accordingly, dismiss the writ petition 
with liberty to the petitioner to approach the Central 
Administrative Tribunal.
