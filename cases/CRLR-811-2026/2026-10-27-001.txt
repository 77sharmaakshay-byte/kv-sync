"Rajkumar and others Vs. State of Uttarakhand" and 
judgment and order of conviction dated 01.07.2022,

and sentenced to undergo 1 year R.1. and a fine of Rs. 
1,000/- each and in default of payment of fine further 
one month additional R.I. under Section 170 read with 
Section 34 of IPC, 3 months R. I. and a fine of Rs. 200/-
each in default of payment of fine further additional 1 months R.I. under Section 171 read with Section 34 of 
IPC, 5 years R.I. and a fine of Rs. 5,000/- each in default 
of payment of fine further additional 6 months R.I. 
under Section 420 read with Section 34 of IPC, 1 years 
R.I. and a fine of Rs. 1,000/- each in default of payment 
fine further additional 1 months R.I. under Section 468 
read with Section 34 of IPC, 1 years R.I. and a fine of 
Rs. 1,000/- each in default of payment further additional 
1 months R.1. under Section 471 read with Section 34 of 
IPC, 3 years R.I. and a fine of Rs. 1,000/- each in default 
of payment further additional 1 months R.I. under 
Section 472 read with Section 34 of IPC directing all the 
sentences of each of the revisionists shall run 
concurrently.
4. Vide Exemption Application (IA No.2 of 2026), 
the learned counsel for the revisionists has stated that 
the revisionists were on bail during trial as well as 
during the pendency of the appeal and they have never 
misused the same. On the said premise, learned counsel 
for the revisionists has sought exemption from 
surrender. 
5. On the other hand, learned Brief Holder for the 
State would submit that since the conviction is more 
than three years, hence, before considering the bail, the 
revisionists ought to surrender before the court 
concerned. 
6. The court finds favour with the submissions
advanced by the learned Brief Holder. 
7. Hence, the court is not inclined to allow the 
Exemption Application, and the Exemption Application is hereby rejected.
8. On this, learned counsel for the revisionists prays 
for and on his request, the revisionists are granted 48 
hours time to surrender before the court concerned. 
9. The learned Brief Holder prays for and is granted 
time to file objection to the Bail Application by or 
before 30th October, 2026. 
10. List on 31st October, 2026 for considering the Bail 
Application.
