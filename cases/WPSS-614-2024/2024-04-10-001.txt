4. The present writ petition is filed by the petitioners
challenging the condition containing in Clause no.9 of the 
advertisement dated 14.03.2024 issued by respondent no.3
(Annexure No.6 to the writ petition), whereby the cut-off 
date for consideration of the age to bring a candidate within 
the eligibility has been fixed as 01.07.2024 in place of 
01.07.2023.
5. It is the contention of the learned counsel for the 
petitioner that this fixation of the cut-off date i.e. 
01.07.2024 runs contrary to the service Rules governing the 
services of the petitioners known as the Uttarakhand Sub￾ordinate Education (Trained Graduate Category) Service 
Rules, 2014.
6. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that in the said Rule, the recruitment year is defined as 
calendar year starting w.e.f. 01 July of the recruitment year 
to the 30th June of the next year. In this view of the matter, 
the cut-off date shall be fixed on 01.07.2023.
7. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that the vacancies relate back to the recruitment year 2023-
24, therefore, the cut-off date should have been fixed for 
01.07.2023 in view of the provisions of the Uttarakhand 
Sub-ordinate Education (Trained Graduate Category) 
Service Rules, 2014.
8. I have considered the submissions made by the 
learned counsel for the parties and in the opinion of this 
Court, this matter requires scrutiny and deliberation which 2
can be done only after the filing of the counter affidavits.
9. Six weeks’ time is granted to respondents to file 
counter affidavits. Further, two weeks’ time is granted to 
petitioners to file rejoinder affidavit, if any.
10. In order to maintain the equity and to do justice, as 
an interim measure, it is provided that the respondent￾Commission will accept the application form of the 
petitioner manually/ physically for the recruitment on the 
post of Assistant Teacher (L.T.) Grade pursuant to the 
advertisement dated 14.03.2024 and to permit them to 
participate in the selection process, which shall be subject 
to the final outcome of the writ petition.
11. List on 08.08.2024.
12. Interim relief application (IA No.1/2024) stands 
disposed of.
