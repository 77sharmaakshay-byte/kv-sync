2. Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned S.C. for the State.
3. Petitioner have challenged the order dated 
08.07.2024 issued by respondent no.3-Principal, District 
Education and Training Institute (DIET), Udham Singh 
Nagar and further commanding the respondents to repay 
the illegally deducted/recovered amount of ₹3,67,445/-
along with interest @ 12 p.a.
4. It is case of the petitioner that the petitioner was 
working with the D.I.E.T. as a Lecturer who retired on 
29.02.2024 and after his retirement, a PPO was issued to 
the petitioner and an amount of ₹3,67,445/- was 
deducted from the amount of gratuity and retiral dues 
alleging excess payment made to the petitioner.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the 
said recovery is totally illegal and arbitrary as before 
making the recovery the petitioner has not been given any 
notice, thus same deserves to be set aside and the money 
be returned to the petitioner. 
6. Learned counsel for the State submits that since the 
money has already been recovered, therefore it is not a 
case of interim order.
7. Having heard learned counsel for the parties, this 2
Court is of the view that the controversy involved in the 
present writ petition cannot be gone into by this Court 
without counter affidavit.
8. Learned counsel for the State is directed to file 
counter affidavit within four weeks.
9. List this case on 29.09.2026.
