IN THE HIGH COURT OF UTTARAKHAND AT 
NAINITAL
Second Appeal No. 115 of 2015 
(Under Section 100 of C.P.C.)
N K and ishor Pandey ....….…… Plaintiff/Appellant
versus
Smt. Bhagwati Devi Bhojak ..………. Defendant/ Respondent 
 
Mr. T. A. Khan, Senior Advocate, assisted by Mr. Aditya Kumar Arya, 
Advocate, present for the appellant. 
U.C. Dhyani, J. (Oral)
 Heard learned counsel for the appellant on both 
the applications being CLMA No. 9319 of 2015 and CLMA 
No. 9620 of 2015. 
 Learned Senior Advocate argued that this Court, on 
05.08.2015 passed the following Order: 
 “After hearing learned counsel for the 
appellant and after considering the grounds taken 
up in the Second Appeal, it is provided, as an 
interim measure that, status quo as on today, shall 
be maintained by the parties on the suit property 
till further orders.” 
 It is submitted by the learned Senior Counsel 
for the appellant that in spite of the aforesaid order of 2 
status quo, respondent is defying the said order. He, 
therefore, wants that any Advocate Commissioner should 
go on the spot and submit his report as to whether the said 
order of this Hon’ble Court is being defied by the 
respondent or not? It is also submitted by the learned 
Senior Counsel for the appellant that the notice has been 
served upon the respondent. 
 Both the applications (CLMA No. 9319 of 2015 
and CLMA No. 9620 of 2015) are allowed. Mr. Sarvesh 
Aggarwal, Advocate, who is present in the Court in 
another case, is appointed as an Advocate Commissioner, 
who shall visit the spot and submit his report as to whether 
the respondent is defying the order of this Court dated 
05.08.2015, in spite of service of notice and knowledge of 
order or not? 
 It is the statement of learned Senior Advocate for 
the appellant that the respondent has already been served 
and has knowledge of the Order dated 05.08.2015. 
 Learned Advocate Commissioner shall be entitled 
for honorarium to the tune of Rs.10,000/- plus Travel 
Expenses, which shall be borne by the appellant. 
 A certified copy of this Order be handed over the 
learned Advocate Commissioner within 24 hours. 
 (U.C. Dhyani, J.)
 24.08.2015
