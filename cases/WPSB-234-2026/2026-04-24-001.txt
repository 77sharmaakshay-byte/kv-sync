3. The submission of the petitioner is that the impugned order 
dated 05.05.2025, whereby approval has been granted for recovery of 
a sum of Rs. 2,80,608/- from the gratuity of the petitioner, has been 
passed without any notice or opportunity of hearing to him. Further 
contention is that the first promotional pay-scale was rightly granted 
to the petitioner since 11.02.2001 after counting his ad-hoc services, 
but the impugned order has been passed without taking into 
consideration the ad-hoc services and, accordingly, the conclusion,
arrived at by the respondents that the first promotional pay-scale was 
wrongly granted since 11.02.2001, is wholly erroneous. 
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner places reliance on the 
judgment of the Hon’ble Supreme Court in State of Punjab & 
others vs. Rafiq Masih reported in (2014) 8 SCC 883. 
5. The matter requires consideration. 
6. Let counter-affidavit(s) be field by the respondents within 
three weeks. 
7. The petitioner will have a week thereafter to file rejoinder￾affidavit. 8. List after expiry of four weeks. 
9. Meanwhile, there shall be stay of the effect and operation of 
the order dated 05.05.2025, and any amount, deducted by the 
respondents from the gratuity of the petitioner, shall be refunded to 
the petitioner after obtaining adequate security, other than bank and 
cash guarantee.
