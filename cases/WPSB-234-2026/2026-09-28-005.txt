Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
1. Ms. Ketki Chaudhary, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. S.M.S. Mehta, learned Brief Holder for the State 
of Uttarakhand. 
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner states that the 
petitioner has not been refunded the gratuity amount, as 
was directed by this Court vide order dated 24.04.2026.
4. Learned State Counsel has placed on record the 
instructions received from the Director, Agriculture, 
according to which proposal was sent by the Directorate to 
the Government for allocating adequate budget. However, 
the budget has not been allocated so far. 
5. Learned State Counsel is granted time to obtain 
instructions in this regard. 
6. Learned State Counsel further states that all efforts 
would be made for making payment within three weeks. 
Accordingly, his statement is recorded. 
7. Let necessary allocation of budget be done within 
three weeks and the gratuity amount be refunded to the 
petitioner. 
8. List after three weeks. 
9. The petitioner may file rejoinder affidavit in the 
meantime.
