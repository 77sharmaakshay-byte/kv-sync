Surendra Singh …………Petitioner

Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Anshu Kumar, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. N.S. Pundir, learned D.A.G. for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition, the petitioner 
has sought a direction to respondent nos.2 and 3 to 
provide petitioner the benefit of Senior Administrative 
Officer, Salary Matrix (47,600-1,51,100) Scale-8 in 
compliance of order dated 24.07.2019 (26.07.2019 has 
wrongly been transcribed) and further to provide notional 
promotion and pensionary benefits for the said post.
4. The petitioner submitted that the petitioner was 
given promotion on 24.07.2019 on the post of Senior 
Administrative Officer, Salary Matrix (47,600-1,51,100) 
Scale-8 and he was not given benefit of the promotional
pay-scale as he retired on 31.07.2019 after the promotion 
has been given to him.
5. This Court vide order dated 10.07.2026 directed 
State counsel to seek instructions into the matter as to 
why the benefit of the promotion be not given to the 
petitioner.
6. Today, learned counsel for the respondent-State, 
based on instructions dated 20.07.2026 which are taken 
on record, submits that the petitioner retired on 2
31.07.2019 while the posting pursuant to promotional 
order dated 24.07.2019 was issued on 09.09.2019.
7. Since the petitioner has retired before the posting 
order, therefore he is not entitled to get the benefit of the 
promotional post.
8. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
though the petitioner retired on 31.07.2019, but still the 
petitioner has got a right to receive the benefit of notional 
promotional as he has been promoted on 24.07.2019 
(26.07.2019 has wrongly been transcribed).
9. Learned counsel for the petitioner relied upon the 
Government Order dated 11.06.2003, annexure no.4 to 
the writ petition, issued by State of Uttarakhand, Personal 
Section-2 and submits that even those persons who have 
been found place in the eligibility list for promotion are 
entitled to get the benefit of notional promotion even after 
death or retirement of such persons, therefore the 
petitioner is at a better footing as he was promoted 
though not posted, therefore, he is entitled to get the 
benefit of promotion which was given to him before his 
retirement.
10. There is some substance in the submission made by 
learned counsel for the petitioner.
11. Learned counsel for the State prays for and is 
granted six weeks’ time for filing counter affidavit.
12. List this case on 21.09.2026.
