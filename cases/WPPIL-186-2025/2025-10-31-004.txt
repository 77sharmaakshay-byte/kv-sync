Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Amicus Curiae for the

2. Mr. Rakesh Joshi, learned Brief Holder for the State 
of Uttarakhand.
3. Heard the learned Amicus Curiae Shri J.S. Virk.
4. Learned counsel Shri Karthik Jayashankar submits 
that he is instructed to appear on behalf of the proposed 
6th respondent M/s X Corporation.
5. The submission is placed on record.
6. Learned Amicus Curiae has preferred an application 
for impleading certain social media platforms, namely, M/s 
Meta Platform, X Corporation, WhatsApp Application 
Services Private Ltd. and Facebook India Online Services 
Private Limited. 
7. In the light of the submissions made by learned 
Counsel Shri Jayashankar, the X Corporation is directed to 
be arrayed as respondent no.5.
8. Issue notices to the proposed respondents M/s Meta 
Platform Inc. Instagram Designated Agent, M/s Whatsapp 
Application Services Private Ltd., Salarpuria and Facebook 
India Online Services Private Limited.
9. We have queried Mr. Jayashankar as to whether technology used by M/s X Corporation enables red￾flagging messages, which carry un-parliamentary, vulgar 
and provocative words. We have also queried as to 
whether M/s X Corporation has any mechanism to pull 
down such messages on its own accord and thirdly, 
whether the social media platforms ought to be made 
liable to compensate the victims of such online onslaughts.
10. The learned counsel Shri Jayashankar submits that 
he would secure instructions in this regard and prays for 
two weeks’ time.
11. Learned Amicus Curiae files a communication from 
the DGP whereby the investigation into the offences
complained of have been handed over to the SSP, STF 
Dehradun.
12. We direct the Amicus Curiae to array SSP, STF 
Dehradun as a party respondent.
13. Learned counsel Shri Jaishankar has also placed 
before the Court a note stating that two of the posts have 
been deleted by the user and one post has been withheld
by X. It is further stated that the details of the account 
holders have also been shared with the respondent police.
14. The SSP, STF, Dehradun is also permitted to 
communicate to the proposed respondents regarding the 
pendency of the petition and the next date of hearing.
15. List this case on 24.11.2025.
