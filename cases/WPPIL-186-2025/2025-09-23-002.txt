COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
23.09.2025 In Re: Suo Motu PIL No. Nil of 2025
Hon’ble G. Narendar, C.J.
Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J. 
1. Heard the Inspector General of Police 
(Cyber Crimes).
2. The IG (Cyber Crimes) expresses his
shock with the content of the posts.
3. In fact, one message clearly amounts to 
giving provocation to anybody to engage a 
hired killer. The other posts are utterly 
distasteful and are directly demeaning of the 
judgment of the Hon’ble Apex Court. What is 
ever more shocking is the social media 
platforms hosting and retaining such 
provocative, intimidative and most vituperative
content. One post states that the counsel who 
defended the accused and her family members 
should also be visited with the same action. 
The post are of such nature that immature 
minds will certainly get influenced by them and 
will heighten emotions in the society and breed
a sense of vengeance and enmity against the 
targeted persons. 4. One post says “shoot this one too, and 
along with it, ……., as well”. One post 
describes the judgment as ‘a joke’.
5. We direct the IG (Cyber Crimes) to 
forthwith initiate proceedings and direct the 
social media platforms to pull down such 
posts. 
6. The Registry is directed to register this as 
a Suo Motu PIL. 
7. Social media platforms like X, You Tube, 
Facebook, Instagram etc. be arrayed as party 
respondents.
8. We appoint the Deputy Advocate General 
for the State, Mr. J.S. Virk, as Amicus Curiae
to assist the Court.
9. List on 25.09.2025, at 04:00 P.M.
10. The IG (Cyber Crimes) and the SSP, 
Nainital to submit an action taken note by 
then.
 
 (Subhash Upadhyay, J.) (G. Narendar, C.J.)
