1. Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Amicus Curiae.
2. In compliance with our earlier order dated 
23.09.2025, the SSP, Nainital and the IG, Cyber 
Crimes are present before the Court. The SSP has filed 
into the Court the Action Taken Report. 
3. Learned DAG submits that, in compliance of the 
directions issued by this Court, two Personal Security 
Officers have been assigned for the protection of the 
person (whom we would designate as victim for the 
sake of the proceedings). The IG, Cyber Crimes has 
submitted a compliance report, wherein it is stated that 
03 primary posts have been identified, and that the 
said 03 posts have been re-tweeted multiple times, and 
are being widely shared; and that they are registered 
with Ticket Nos. LD-17194, LD-17197 & LD-17198; and 
that they have also identified 10 other objectionable 
comments; and that orders of this Court have also 
been forwarded to the social media platforms like 
Twitter/X, and that SSP STF, namely Navneet Singh & 
DySP Cyber, namely Ankush Mishra have also got in 
touch with the designated representatives of Twitter/X,
and the contact number is also provided; the 
instructions to pull down the posts have also been 
conveyed through phone calls and WhatsApp; and that 
the representative has acknowledge the receipt of the 
notices, and has also informed that the matter has 
been escalated to the parent company in the USA for 
necessary compliance; and that the compliance from 
the end of the social media platforms is awaited. The compliance report is taken on record. 
4. We hope that the social media platforms i.e. 
WhatsApp, Meta, Facebook, Twitter/X would abide by 
the directions, with the necessary urgency that the 
matter requires. The dignity of women, by itself, and 
in the context of our culture, is absolutely non￾negotiable. We hope that the spirit, with which this 
order is made, and the legal compulsions are 
considered in right earnest, and action initiated with 
speed. 
5. Copy of this order be forwarded to the IG, Cyber 
Crimes. 
6. Registry to print the name of Mr. J.S. Virk,
