5. Heard the learned Amicus Curiae Shri J.S. Virk; the 
learned Senior Counsel Shri Arvind Datar appearing on 
behalf of respondent no.5; learned Senior Counsel Dr. 
Aditya Sondhi appearing for respondent no.6 and learned 
Senior Counsel Shri Vivek Reddy for respondent no.7.
6. The learned Senior counsel Shri Arvind Datar would 
submit that respondent no.8 (Facebook) is not a 
necessary party as it does not run a social media 
platform and similarly the learned senior counsel Shri 
Vivek Reddy would submit that WhatsApp LLC. is the 
appropriate party and hence the array of parties needs to be looked in and corrected. The submissions are placed 
on record.
7. The learned counsel appearing for the respondents 
may communicate with the learned Amicus Curiae and 
convey the correct position. The learned Amicus Curiae is 
requested to look into the same and file appropriate 
applications either to implead the necessary parties or to 
delete unnecessary parties. 
8. That apart, the learned senior counsels would 
request two weeks’ time to file a statement.
9. This Court has requested the learned senior 
counsels to examine the possibility and position in law as 
to whether any mechanism can be developed by the 
social media entities to ensure that certain obnoxious/ 
descriptive, un-parliamentary, vulgar, abusive or 
demeaning words can be automatically/voluntarily 
filtered or blocked by the entity after the posts are made 
and secondly to ensure/enable a mechanism which would 
prevent re-tweeting or reposting of such offensive posts.
10. Learned senior counsels would submit that they 
would secure instructions and make appropriate 
submissions before this Court. Same is placed on record. 
11. List on 15.12.2025.
