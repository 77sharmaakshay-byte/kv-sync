4. In all these writ petitions, the 
petitioners are serving in different 
departments, who were engaged through 
UPNL.
5. According to the petitioners, they are 
working for last more than 10 years.
6. In Writ Petition (S/S) No. 1089 of 2023, 
the relief is being sought that the State may 
frame a scheme for the employees, who are 
serving through UPNL for last more than 10 years. 
7. Almost, in all the writ petitions, 
identical issues are being raised and main 
grievance of the petitioners in all these Writ 
Petitions is that they are serving for last more 
than 10 years through UPNL. 
8. The State has already filed, in some of 
the writ petitions, its counter affidavits, 
however, in Writ Petition (S/S) No. 1089 of 
2023, counter affidavit has not been filed. 
9. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya who represents the
State, seeks two weeks’ time to file a counter 
affidavit in Writ Petition (S/S) No. 1089 of 
2023, because in this Writ Petition, a relief is 
being sought for framing a scheme of 
absorption. 
10. Let the counter affidavit be filed in Writ 
Petition (S/S) No. 1089 of 2023, within a 
period of four weeks. 
11. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya further submits 
that on the next date, learned Advocate 
General will represent the State in these 
cases.
