COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
28.11.2024 AO No. 89 of 2022 
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The proposed Appeal under Section 
173 of the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 has 
been filed by the claimants to enhance the 
amount awarded by learned Motor 
Accident Claims Tribunal / IIIrd Additional 
District Judge, Dehradun in Motor Accident 
Claim Petition No.154 of 2009. 
2. The proposed Appeal has been filed 
along with an Application to condone the 
delay of 720 days. 
3. Heard Mr. Neeraj Garg, learned 
counsel for the proposed appellants, Mr. 
Siddhartha Bisht, learned counsel for the 
respondent no.2 and Mr. Bharat Tewari, 
learned counsel for the respondent no.3. 
4. Mr. Bharat Tewari, Advocate, has 
opposed the said Delay Condonation 
Application. 
5. The respondent no.3 has also 
challenged the said Award dated 
05.01.2018 in AO No. 108 of 2018.
6. Having regard to the totality of the 
facts and circumstances of the case and 
the cause shown by the appellants, this Court is inclined to hold that the cause 
shown by appellants is a sufficient cause 
to condone the delay.
7. Therefore, the Delay Condonation 
Application (IA No. 1 of 2022) is allowed. 
The delay is accordingly condoned. 
8. Admit. 
9. List on 04.04.2025 for final hearing 
with AO No. 108 of 2018.
