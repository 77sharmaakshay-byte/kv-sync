 This revision is preferred against the order 
dated 10.04.2019 passed by Judge Family Court 
Haridwar in Criminal Case No.164 of 2014, 
Smt. Sushma vs. Sh. Umakant Sharma whereby 
the concerned court allowed the application filed 
under Section 125 of Cr.P.C and the revisionist 
was directed to pay Rs. 20,000/- per month as 
maintenance. When the matter was taken up for 
hearing on 14.06.2019, the Coordinate Bench of 
this Court was directed to the revisionist to pay 
Rs. 15,000/- per month to the respondent-wife 
against the maintenance allowance as awarded 
by the concerned Judge Family Court but the 
revisionist has to fail the honour of the order of 
the Coordinate Bench passed on 14.06.2019;
accordingly the order dated 14.06.2019 is hereby 
vacated. 
 List the matter on 16.02.2022 for hearing 
the revision finally. 
 Stay Vacation application IA No. 1226 of 
2021 stands disposed of.
