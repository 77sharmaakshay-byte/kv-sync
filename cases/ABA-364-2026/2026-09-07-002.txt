Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Gaurav Singh, learned counsel for the 
applicant through video conferencing. 
Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned A.G.A. along with 
Mr. Nikhil Bisht, learned Brief Holder for the 
State of Uttarakhand. 
2. The applicant seeks anticipatory bail in 
connection with FIR No. 196 of 2026, registered 
at Police Station Bhagwanpur, District Haridwar, 
for the offences punishable under Sections 3/11 
of the Uttarakhand Protection of Cow Progeny 
Act, 2007, and Section 3/11(f) of the Prevention 
of Cruelty to Animals Act, 1960.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant submits 
that he has been falsely implicated in the 
present case, as there was no act of 
slaughtering, and the only allegation against the 
applicant is that two cows were found without 
any public tag inside the compound/gher of the 
applicants.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant further 
submits that the applicant is innocent and that 
the prosecution story is wholly concocted. He 
submits that the applicant is ready and willing 
to cooperate with the investigation and 
undertake to abide by any condition that may be 
imposed by this Court.
5. Learned State Counsel prays for and is 
granted three weeks' time to file a counter￾affidavit.
6. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties and upon perusal of the material 
available on record, this Court finds that the 
matter requires consideration.7. As an interim measure, without expressing 
any opinion on the merits of the case, it is 
directed that, in the event of arrest during the 
course of investigation, the applicant shall be 
released on interim anticipatory bail on 
furnishing a personal bond and two reliable 
sureties, each of the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the Arresting Officer, subject to 
the following conditions:
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation and shall appear before the 
Investigating Officer as and when required;
(ii) The applicant shall not directly or 
indirectly make any inducement, threat or 
promise to any person acquainted with the 
facts of the case so as to dissuade such 
person from disclosing such facts to the 
Court or to any police officer;
(iii) The applicant shall not leave the country 
without the prior permission of the 
concerned Court, if so required, during the 
pendency of the proceedings.
8. It is made clear that, in case the applicant 
fails to cooperate with the investigation or 
violates any of the aforesaid conditions, it shall 
be open to the State to bring the said fact to the 
notice of this Court and seek appropriate orders, 
including cancellation of the interim anticipatory 
bail.
9. List this case on 28.09.2026.
10. On the next date of listing, the State shall 
also produce the video shot by the raiding team 
at the spot.
