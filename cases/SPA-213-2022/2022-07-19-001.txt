 Mr. Devesh Upreti, learned counsel for

 Mr. Pradeep Joshi, learned Addl. Chief 
Standing Counsel for the State. 
 In response to our query, learned 
counsel for the appellant states that the 
appellant was expelled from the Society in 
question in the year 2013 itself. We have 
asked the learned counsel for the appellant 
whether the said expulsion was ever 
challenged by the appellant, and if so, what is 
the outcome of such challenge. 
 Learned counsel for the appellant seeks 
an adjournment to take instructions and 
apprise the court in respect of the aforesaid 
aspects. 
 We have made it clear to the learned 
counsel for the appellant that in case the 
expulsion has not become challenged, or has 
become final, the appellant would have no 
standing to pursue the remedy, as sought in 
the writ petition. 
 At the request of learned counsel for the 
appellant, list this matter on 25.08.2022.
