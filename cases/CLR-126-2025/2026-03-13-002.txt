COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
13.03.2026 CLR No.126 of 2025
Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
1. Mr. Amar Murti Shukla, learned counsel for the 
revisionist.
2. Mr. S.K. Mandal, learned counsel for the 
respondents.
3. Perusal of order-sheet reveals that the learned 
counsel for the revisionist on 13.11.2025 had given 
an undertaking before the Coordinate Bench that the 
revisionist will clear entire arrears within 45 days, out 
of which, ₹ 20 lakhs will be paid by him within a 
month. 
4. In view of undertaking given by counsel for the 
revisionist, the Coordinate Bench, vide order dated 
13.11.2025, while granting interim order, had 
directed the revisionist to pay a sum of ₹ 10 lakhs 
within 15 days and the remaining amount within a 
month. Thereafter, the case was listed on 
05.01.2026, on which date, the Court recorded that 
in compliance of previous order, the revisionist has 
paid a sum of ₹ 11,50,000/- towards arrear of rent 
and, on the request of the revisionist, further one 
month’s time was granted to the revisionist to pay 
the remaining arrears. 
5. Learned counsel for the respondents submits 
that the revisionist has not paid the remaining arrears 
till today and as such the orders dated 13.11.2025 
and 05.01.2026 has not been complied with by him,
in totality. 6. Learned counsel for the revisionist submits that 
his client has met with an accident and, therefore, he 
could not pay the remaining amount, however, he 
undertakes to pay the same within a period of two 
weeks from today.
7. List this case on 27.03.2026.
8. Interim order dated 05.01.2026 is extended till 
the next date of listing. 
 (Subhash Upadhyay, J.)
