Hon'ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
1. Mr. Amar Murti Shukla and Mr. 
Saurabh Kumar Pandey, learned counsel for 
the revisionist. 
2. Mr. S.K.Mandal, learned counsel for 
the respondents. 
3. Vide order dated 13.11.2025, a 
Coordinate Bench of this Court noted the 
undertaking of learned counsel for the 
revisionist that the revisionist will clear the 
entire dues within 45 days, and within a 
month the revisionist will pay Rs. 20 lakhs. 
The revisionist was directed to pay a sum of 
Rs. 10 lakhs within 15 days of the date of 
passing of the order and the remaining 
amount was to be paid within a month. 
4. As per own case of the revisionist, till 
date only an amount of Rs. 18 lakhs has 
been paid to the respondent. Counsel for the 
respondent submits that the arrears were to 
be cleared within 45 days, however, till date 
even Rs. 20 lakhs has not been paid. Apart 
from that, the monthly rent from December, 
2025 has not been paid.
5. Learned counsel for the revisionist is granted a week’s time to seek instructions 
from his client within which time, the 
arrears shall be cleared. 
6. In case, the said undertaking is not 
placed before the Court, the Court shall 
consider the stay vacation application filed 
by the respondent. 
7. List on 5th June, 2025.
