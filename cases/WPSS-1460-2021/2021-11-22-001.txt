COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.1460 of 2021
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Pankaj Tangwan, Advocate, for the 
petitioner.
Ms. Anjali Bhargava, Additional CSC, 
for the State of Uttarakhand.
Though the petitioner has been granted 
appointment on compassionate ground as a 
Class IV employee, but she since not being 
satisfied with the nature of the appointment 
granted on compassionate grounds, she has 
sought an appointment on Class III posts, based 
on the classification which she alleges to have 
been possessed by her.
Raising the said contention, the petitioner 
preferred a Writ Petition being WPSS No.1143 
of 2021, “Neha Aswal Vs. State of Uttarakhand 
& others”, which was disposed of by the 
coordinate Bench of this Court on 09.09.2021, 
directing the respondents to consider and decide 
the representation of the petitioner dated 
11.08.2021. The representation of the 
petitioner, thus preferred has been rejected by 
the impugned order on the ground that one of 
the essential qualification, for the Class III 
posts is that of having the knowledge of the 
typing in Hindi and English.
The petitioner in her application has 
never expressed that she possessed this 
qualification.
The matter requires scrutiny.
Respondents may file counter affidavit 
within a period of three weeks.
List thereafter. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
