This bunch of petitions has been filed by the convicts for 
their premature release. In fact, certain directions were issued 
in WPPIL No. 50 of 2024, wherein, in Para No.24, a table was 
also given, which reveals that, in fact, in 71 cases, this Court 
has directed for consideration of premature release, and in 45 cases, the report was awaited.
On 12.08.2026, this Court had requested learned State 
Counsel to submit the list of 71 and 45 inmates, along with 
their status with regard to whom directions were given by this 
Court in WPPIL No.50 of 2024 on 27.09.2024.
The reports are not being placed. It is causing avoidable 
delay in disposing of these writ petitions. 
In Para No.24 of the judgment dated 27.09.2024, in 
WPPIL No.50 of 2024, the Coordinate Bench of this Court has, 
inter alia, noted that, “...The decision of the Government has 
to be lawful and fair as per Article 14 of the Constitution.

The Court wanted to know as to what is the detail of 71 
and 45 inmates with regard to whom directions were given for 
their consideration of premature release, as well as with regard 
to whom the reports were awaited, respectively.
This status is not being submitted by learned State 
Counsel.
The Court directs the Principal Secretary, Home,
Government of Uttarakhand, to file an affidavit, categorically 
giving details of all those 71 and 45 inmates, who have been 
referred to in Para No.24 of the judgment dated 27.09.2024 by 
the Coordinate Bench of this Court in WPPIL No.50 of 2024.
The details shall include as to whether their matter has
