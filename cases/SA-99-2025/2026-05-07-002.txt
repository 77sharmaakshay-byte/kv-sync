6. The present Second Appeal has been preferred 
against the judgment and order dated 15.10.2024, 
passed by the learned District Judge, Pithoragarh in 
Civil Appeal No. 12 of 2024, titled Smt. Suranti 
Devi vs. Sri Rajendra Singh Firmaal & Others, 
whereby the learned Appellate Court dismissed the 
appeal preferred by the appellant/plaintiff. The 
present appeal is also directed against the judgment and order dated 18.07.2024, passed by the learned 
Civil Judge (Jr. Div.), Pithoragarh in Original Suit 
No. 166 of 2021, titled Smt. Suranti Devi vs. Sri 
Rajendra Singh Firmaal & Others, whereby the suit 
of the appellant/plaintiff was dismissed.
7. There is a delay of 212 days in filing the 
present appeal. Since there is delay in preferring the 
Second Appeal, the same has been accompanied by 
a delay condonation application being IA No. 1 of 
2025. 
8. The reasons assigned for the delay are that the 
applicant/appellant, due to her financial constraints, 
could not file the instant appeal against the aforesaid 
judgments and orders passed by both the learned 
Courts below. It is submitted that thereafter, the 
applicant/appellant contacted her counsel on 
30.07.2025 for assailing the impugned judgments 
and orders. Upon perusal of the documents, learned 
counsel informed the deponent that the prescribed 
period for filing the appeal had already expired; 
however, the appeal could still be filed along with a 
delay condonation application. The applicant was 
accordingly asked to come to Nainital thereafter so 
that the appeal along with the delay condonation 
application could be prepared and filed before this 
Hon’ble Court.
9. It is further submitted that there was no 
deliberate or wilful inaction on the part of the 
deponent/applicant in not filing the appeal within time, and the delay, if any, has occurred solely due 
to the reasons and circumstances stated above. It is 
further prayed that the delay in filing the present 
appeal be condoned in the interest of justice so that 
the appeal may be admitted and heard on merits; 
failing which, the applicant/appellant shall suffer 
irreparable hardship and injury.
10. It is also pertinent to note that although there 
is a delay of 212 days in filing the appeal before this 
Hon’ble Court, the same has occurred due to 
reasons beyond the control of the 
deponent/appellant, as stated in the preceding 
paragraphs.
11. There is no serious objection by learned 
counsel for the State. Since personal rights of the 
appellant are involved in the matter and the same 
require adjudication, this Court is of the view that, 
in the interest of justice and for attaining finality of 
the proceedings, the delay in preferring the present 
Second Appeal is hereby condoned.
12. Delay condonation application stands 
disposed of, accordingly. 
13. List for admission on 04.06.2026.
