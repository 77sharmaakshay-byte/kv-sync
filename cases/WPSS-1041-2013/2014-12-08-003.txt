Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J. (Oral)
Heard Mr. M.C. Kandpal, Senior Advocate assisted by

Sharma, Brief Holder present for the State/respondents. 
The husband of the petitioner was a seasonal 
employee in Forest Department. Admittedly, he was not a 
regular employee nor was he working on a regular post. He 
was killed in the year 2007. His wife (i.e. the petitioner) 
was given compassionate appointment, but on contractual 
basis. On earlier occasion, a writ petition bearing No. 1689 
of 2012 wherein the learned Single Judge of this Court 
vide judgment dated 01.12.2012 gave a liberty to the 
petitioner to file a representation before the respondents. 
Consequently, the representation of the petitioner was 
decided vide order dated on 24.12.2012 by the Divisional 
Forest Officer who has rejected the representation of the 
petitioner on the ground that her husband was not a 
regular employee, hence the petitioner cannot be 
appointed on a regular post. 
The rule 2(a) of U.P. Recruitment of Dependents of 
Government Servants Dying in Harness Rules, 1974 
clearly defines a “Government Servant” which reads as 
under :- 
“2(a) “Government servant” means a Government 
servant employed in connection with the affairs of 
Uttar Pradesh who –

 (i) was permanent in such employment; or 
(ii) though temporary had been regularly appointed 
in such employment; or 
(iii) though not regularly appointed, had put in 
three years’ continuous service in regular vacancy 
in such employment. 
Explanation- “Regularly appointed” means 
appointed in accordance with the procedure laid 
down for recruitment to the post or service, as the 
case may be; 
Admittedly, the services of the petitioner’s husband 
are not covered under the definition of a Government 
Servant. She has, however, been given appointment on 
contractual basis since 2008. 
In view thereof, the writ petition is disposed of with 
the direction that in case the rules so permit her 
regularization be considered. A mandamus is also issued 
to the respondents to give her minimum of pay scale as is 
being given to other employees. 
No order as to costs.
