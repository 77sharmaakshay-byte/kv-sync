COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 845 of 2023
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. I.D. Paliwal, learned counsel for the 
petitioner and Mr. Atul Bahuguna, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State and Mr. Pankaj Miglani, learned 
counsel (through Video Conferencing) for the 
Commission/respondent no. 3.
2. The grievance of the petitioner is that 
his candidature for appointment to the post,
which was advertised on 28.12.2021 has been 
rejected by the Commission on the ground that 
the petitioner did not fulfil the requirement of 
height and measurement of chest.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
argued that, in fact, the petitioner by birth is the 
resident of the District Almora, which is a hill 
district of State of Uttarakhand and therefore, he 
is entitled to a relaxation in height and chest.
4. While submitting the online form, the 
petitioner submitted the permanent resident 
certificate/domicile of the hill State, however, in 
the column whether he belongs to hill district or 
not, he had put a mark ‘No’.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the petitioner was permitted to appear in the physical test and since he has 
enclosed the hill certificate alongwith the 
application form, therefore, he qualified the 
physical test and he was also permitted to appear 
in the written examination, which is the next step 
of the selection.
6. Thereafter, the petitioner appeared in 
the written examination, which was held on
18.12.2022 and thereafter result was declared in 
which the petitioner was declared successful.
7. Thereafter, after completion of 
physical test and written examination, the 
petitioner was shortlisted for document 
verification and list was published on 09.02.2023, 
which is appended as Annexure-6, however, 
thereafter the Recruiting Body rejected the 
candidature of the petitioner on the ground that 
the petitioner could not fulfil the requirement of 
height and measurement of chest and being 
aggrieved with the rejection of the candidature of 
the petitioner, the instant petition has been 
preferred.
8. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the petitioner is the permanent 
resident of District Almora and the certificate to 
this extent was issued on 18.03.2019 and a certificate with regard to the Scheduled Castes 
was also issued to the petitioner on 17.03.2019, 
which are enclosed as Annexure-7. He argued 
that since the petitioner is the domicile of the 
State of Uttarakhand and the resident of District 
Almora, which is a hill district, therefore, he is 
entitled to get relaxation in height as well as in 
measurement of chest, which, in fact, was given 
to the petitioner at the time of physical test and 
that was the reason the petitioner was permitted 
in the written examination and after qualifying 
the physical test and written examination, the 
petitioner was shortlisted for document 
verification on 19.02.2023, therefore, after short￾listing the petitioner for document verification, 
there was no justification on the part of the 
Recruiting Body to reject the candidature of the 
petitioner.
9. On the previous date, the State was 
directed to place before this Court the 
requisition, which has been placed before this 
Court. Undisputedly, in the present case, the 
petitioner was given the benefit of height and 
measurement of chest since he belongs to a hill 
district and after qualifying the physical test, the 
petitioner was permitted to appear in the written examination, which he qualified and that is the 
reason that he has been shortlisted for document 
verification.
10.Now, after completing all formalities, 
rejecting the candidature of the petitioner merely 
on the ground that he has not given declaration
in his application form that he belongs to hill 
district. Once the petitioner has been given the 
benefit of height and chest by treating him to be 
a resident of hill district and permitted to appear 
in the written examination, which he has 
qualified and then short-listed for document 
verification, this Court is of the view that 
rejection of candidature at this stage by the 
Recruiting Body is wholly unwarranted.
11.Apart from this, once the petitioner 
has been shortlisted for document verification, 
then on this technical ground that he could not fill 
up the relevant column, candidature of the 
petitioner cannot be rejected. It has been 
informed to this Court that selection is now over 
since, the entire record including the select list 
has been sent by the Recruiting Body to the 
Government.
12. Mr. Pankaj Miglani, learned counsel for 
the respondent-Commission also informs to this Court that there are identical matters relating to 
those, who are also claiming the benefit of 
relaxation being the resident of the hill area.
13. Be that as it may, if such petitions are 
pending, the Government is well-versed with those 
petitions. Therefore, while examining the 
candidature of the petitioner, the Government is 
directed to examine the marks of other petitioners 
of the pending writ petitions, the details of which,
Mr. Pankaj Miglani will hand-over to Mr. Atul 
Bahuguna by tomorrow 12:00 p.m so that Mr. Atul 
Bahuguna may send the detail of this petition as 
well as the details of the other pending writ 
petitions and this entire exercise shall be 
completed within ten days from today and will 
place the result of the petitioners and other 
candidates, who are claiming the benefit under the 
same category in a sealed envelope.
14. Put up this matter on 07.01.2025, on 
top of the board.
