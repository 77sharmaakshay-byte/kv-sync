The petitioner applied for the 
recruitment in the post of Police 
Constable. It was on multiple steps. 
Heard. 
It is the case of the petitioner that 
initially physical examination was to be 
done, in order to measure the height, 
chest expansion, etc. Thereafter, written 
examination and after that physical. The 
petitioner qualified every stage of it, but 
finally, his candidature has been rejected 
on the ground that his height is less than 
the height required for the post. 
The question is, if the height of the 
petitioner was short to the standard 
height, why he was permitted to proceed 
further from the first physical test? 
Having considered, this Court is of 
the view that this matter requires 
deliberations. 
Admit.
Learned C.S.C. takes notice for the respondent no.1. 
Mr. C.K. Sharma, Advocate takes 
notice for the respondent no.2. 
Respondents may file counter 
affidavit within four weeks. 
Two weeks thereafter, rejoinder 
affidavit, if any, be filed. 
List this matter on 21.11.2023.
Heard on Interim Relief Application 
IA No.1 of 2023
By means of instant interim relief 
application, the petitioner seeks directions 
that one post of Civil Police (Male) may be 
kept vacant so that in case petitioner is 
successful, he may get fruit of the 
petition. 
Instead of passing such order, it is 
provided that the selection pursuant to 
advertisement dated 28.12.2021, of the 
respondent no.2, shall remain subject to 
final outcome of the instant petition. 
The interim relief application stands 
disposed of, accordingly.
