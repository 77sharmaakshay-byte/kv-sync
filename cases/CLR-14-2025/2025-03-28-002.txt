28.03.2025 CLR No.14 of 2025
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J. 
The challenge in the proposed Civil 
Revision is to an order dated 
26.11.2024, passed by learned 
Additional District Judge/Commercial 
Court (Kumaon Division), Haldwani in 
Original Suit No.156 of 2024, whereby 
the Application of the proposed 
revisionist-defendant, filed under Order 
VII Rule 10 of the Code of Civil 
Procedure, 1908, has been dismissed. 
2. Heard Dr. Farrukh Khan, learned 
counsel for proposed revisionist through 
video conferencing. 
3. Dr. Farrukh Khan, Advocate, 
submitted that the Offer Letter dated 
13.10.2021 of the proposed revisionist 
contains the Arbitration Clause. The said 
Offer Letter was accepted by the 
respondent-plaintiff in its Purchase Order 
dated 14.10.2021.
4. It has been mentioned in the 
impugned order that the proposed 
revisionist-defendant had earlier filed an 
Application under Section 8 of The Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, 
which was dismissed. 
5. Dr. Farrukh Khan, Advocate, 
submitted that an Appeal has been filed 
against the dismissal of the said 
Application, filed under Section 8 of The 
Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
6. In the facts and circumstances of 
the case, it would be appropriate to 
grant an opportunity of hearing to the 
respondent also, even passing any order 
on Admission. 
7. Notice is issued to the respondent.
8. Steps to be taken within five days’ 
by all the permissible modes including 
dasti, as prayed. 
9. List on 15.04.2025.
