Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Saurabh Kumar Pandey and Mr. 
Nalin Saun, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Addl. Advocate 
General along with Mr. Sunil Upadhyaya, 
learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that a Coordinate Bench of 
this Court, vide order dated 08.04.2026, had 
directed the State authorities to preserve the 
C.C.T.V. footage of Koti Colony Outpost, 
Police Station Lambgaon, pertaining to the 
relevant period and to place the same before 
this Court.
4. He would further submit that one Amit, 
learned counsel appearing for respondent 
no. 6 before the trial Court, has allegedly 
uploaded incriminating and objectionable 
material against the petitioner and his 
family members on his Facebook account. In 
support of the said submission, learned 
counsel has produced before the Court a 
printout of the alleged Facebook post. He 
would further place reliance upon the order 
dated 16.04.2026 passed by the Coordinate Bench, whereby all the parties were directed 
not to post any incriminating material on 
any social media platform. It is contended 
that, despite the aforesaid direction, the said 
advocate has continued to post such 
material on his Facebook account in 
violation of the order passed by this Court.
5. In compliance with the order dated 
08.04.2026, learned State Counsel, on 
instructions, fairly submits that no C.C.T.V. 
camera is installed at Koti Colony Outpost. 
However, he seeks one week's further time to 
ascertain and place on record the availability 
of the C.C.T.V. footage, if any, of Police 
Station Lambgaon for the relevant period. 
Insofar as the allegation regarding the 
publication of incriminating material on the 
Facebook account of the counsel appearing 
for respondent no. 6 is concerned, learned 
State Counsel also seeks time to verify the 
authenticity of the documents produced by 
the petitioner.
6. During the course of hearing, learned 
counsel for the petitioner handed over a 
copy of the printout of the alleged Facebook 
post to learned State Counsel in open Court 
for the purposes of verification.
7. Learned State Counsel is directed to 
verify the aforesaid allegations as well as the 
documents produced by the petitioner and 
place an appropriate report before this Court 
on or before the next date of listing.
8. List this matter on 30.07.2026 along 
with WPCRL No. 1613 of 2023.
9. WPCRL No. 1000 of 2021 and WPCRL 
No. 826 of 2026 shall stand de-linked from 
the present matter, as the said petitions 
have already been decided.
(Alok Mahra, J.)
