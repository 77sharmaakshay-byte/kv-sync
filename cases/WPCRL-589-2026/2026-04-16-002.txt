COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPCRL No. 589 of 2026
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Saurabh Kumar Pandey, learned 
counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. J. S. Virk, learned Addl. Advocate 
General and Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned AGA 
for the State. 
3. By the instant petition, petitioner is praying 
for the following reliefs:
 
 “i. To issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing the respondents to take 
immediate lawful action against the accused persons 
involved in threatening, harassing and circulating 
obscene video of petitioner's sister.
 ii. To issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing an independent 
investigation by senior officer/another agency into the 
illegal acts, omissions and misconduct of police officials 
of PS Lambgaon and PS Chamba District 
TehriGarhwal.
 iii. To issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing the respondents to 
produce and preserve CCTV footage of Koti Colony 
Outpost/PS Lambgaon for the period from evening of 
09.05.2025 to 10.05.2025 in accordance with Supreme 
Court guidelines and also to produce phone location 
and call record of concern police officials as well as 
petitioner from evening of 09.05.2025 to 10.05.2025
 iv. To issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing the Respondent No.3 
(State Police Complaint Authority) to decide the 
petitioner's complaint dated 06.11.2025 within a time 
bound period.”
4. In this petition, respondent nos. 4 to 11 are
police officials though they are impleaded as 
respondent party by their name and designation. 
5. Petitioner raised serious allegation against 
respondents no. 4 to 11 and he also approached to 
the State Human Right Commission and State 
Police Complaint Authority with complete electronic evidence and petitioner has also 
attacked on the police officials by submitting that 
when no action has been taken against the erring 
police officials, therefore, he was compelled to 
raise his voice on social media and since then, 
private respondents herein are biased against him, 
consequently, he has been falsely implicated in 
multiple FIRs only to counter to his complaint 
against the police officials. On 09.05.2025, he was 
taken into police custody and shown to be 
arrested on 10.05.2025 and during this period, he 
was subjected to physical and mental torture. 
6. On 08.04.2026, this Court directed the 
respondent nos. 5 and 6 to join the proceedings 
through V.C. and preserve the CCTV footage of 
Koti Colony Outpost / PS – Lambgaon, for the 
period from 09.05.2025 to 10.05.2025 and place the 
same before this Court on the next date. 
7. In compliance of the previous order, 
respondent no. 5 – Circle Officer, Tehri Garhwal, 
joined the proceeding through V.C. and 
respondent nos. 6 and 11 are also present in the 
Court. Respondent no. 5 – Circle Officer apprise to 
this Court about the past history of the petitioner 
and his family members.
8. After gone through with the petition and 
arguments of Mr. S.K. Pandey and past history of 
the petitioner and his family members, as apprised 
by respondent no. 5 – Circle Officer, this Court is 
of the view that matter requires deep scrutiny but 
before that the police officials should proceed for 
medical check up of the petitioner, as per 
procedure laid down in Chapter XIII of the Mental 
Health Care Act, 2017. 
9. Mr. Saurabh Kumar Pandey, learned 
counsel for the petitioner, submits that petitioner 
is at present residing in Dehradun and if it is so, 
then Mr. Pandey, shall give details where 
petitioner is residing to Mr. J.S. Virk, learned 
Addl. Advocate General for the State during the 
course of the day, so that police officials may take 
assistance of Dehradun police in order to proceed 
under Chapter XIII of the Mental Health Care Act, 
2017.
10. Mr. J. S. Virk, learned Addl. Advocate 
General for the State apprised to this Court that 
petitioner is repeatedly sending messages in social media platform targeting some police officials.
11. This Court takes serious cognizance on the 
petition filed by the petitioner, therefore, Mr. S.K. 
Pandey, learned counsel for the petitioner, 
directed to advice his client not to send any 
message in social media platform targeting police 
officials and if he has any grievance, he can 
apprise to this Court at any point of time. 
12. Till the next date of listing, no coercive 
action shall be taken against the petitioner. 
13. On the next date, Mr. J.S. Virk, learned 
Addl. Advocate General shall apprise to this Court 
about the mental condition of the petitioner. 
14. List on 23.04.2026 on top of the board.
15. For the time being personal appearance of 
the police officers are exempted. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
