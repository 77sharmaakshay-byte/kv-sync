 Heard. 
 This appeal is directed against award 
dated 28.02.2013, passed by Motor Accident 
Claims Tribunal/Additional District Judge, 
Vikas Nagar, Dehradun, in Motor Accident 
Claim Case No. 205 of 2006, whereby the 
Tribunal has awarded ` 20,81,872/- as 
compensation to the claimants. 
 Admit the appeal. 
 Summon the record of the Tribunal. 
 Issue notices to the respondents. 
 Having heard learned counsel for the 
appellant and after going through the impugned 
award, as an interim measure, it is directed that 
if the appellant New India Assurance Company 
Ltd. deposits 50% of the sum awarded by the 
the Tribunal (adjusting the statutory amount 
deposited before this Court), before it, within 
45 days from today, recovery in respect of 
remaining 50% of the sum awarded shall 
remain stayed, until further orders of this Court 
(Stay Application No. 3638 of 2013, stands 
disposed of). 
 List after service of notice on the 
respondents.
