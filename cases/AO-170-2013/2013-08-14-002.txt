Hon’ble Prafulla C. Pant, J.
Mr. P.C. Maulekhi, Advocate, present for

Mr. Vikas Bahuguna, Advocate, present
for the claimant/respondent no. 1 Chitra Rani.
This is Money Withdrawal Application
No. 8506 of 2013, moved by the claimant/
respondent no. 1 for release of the amount
deposited by the appellant in compliance of
this Court’s order dated 16.04.2013 (in
connection with MAC case no. 205 of 2006
decided on 28.02.2013, by Motor Accident
Claims Tribunal/ Additional District Judge,
Vikas Nagar, Dehradun)
Heard.
Having heard learned counsel for the
appellant, and learned counsel for the claimant/
respondent no. 1, Money Withdrawal
Application No. 8506 of 2013, is disposed of
with the direction that ` 2.5 lac out of the sum
deposited by the appellant vide this Court’s
order dated 16.04.2013, shall be released in
favour of the claimant/ respondent no. 1 Chitra
Rani. Rest of the sum deposited by the
appellant shall be invested in the fixed deposit
to avoid loss of interest.
List this admitted appeal for final hearing
in due course.
 (Prafulla C. Pant, J.)
