20.06.2023 WPPIL No. 136 of 2020 
With 
WPPIL No. 91 of 2019 
Sri Vipin Sanghi, C.J.
Sri Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. D.S. Mehta, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Amicus Curiae for the 
petitioner in WPPIL No.91 of 2019. 
3. Mr. S.S. Chauhan, learned Deputy Advocate 
General for the State. 
4. The matter has been directed to be listed before 
the Court today in the light of the communication 
dated 19.05.2023, received by this court from Mr. V.K. 
Singh, IPS (Retd.), Chairman, Prison Reform 
Committee, Uttarakhand. The said Committee was 
constituted by this Court vide order dated 08.12.2021. 
5. Let a copy of the said communication dated 
19.05.2023 be made available to Mr. D.S. Mehta, Mr. 
Virk, as well as Mr. S.S. Chauhan within one day. 
6. Mr. Chauhan assures that response to the said 
communication would be placed before this Court on 
the next date. 
7. The respondents have not filed their compliance 
report in respect of the Preliminary Recommendations 
on Urgent Prison Reforms in the State of Uttarakhand, 
submitted by one of the members of the aforesaid 
Committee, namely, Dr. Murali Karnam on 
02.06.2022. 8. Mr. Chauhan submits that the State has 
preferred a Special Leave Petition to assail the orders 
dated 08.12.2021 and 27.07.2022 before the Supreme 
Court. However, he is not aware of any further 
development in that regard. 
9. Even the second of the aforesaid orders was 
passed nearly 11 months ago. If the State was serious 
about the SLP, it would have the definite information 
before us about the status of the SLP. Mere filing of 
the SLP is no reason for the respondents to not to 
comply with our directions issued on 08.12.2021 and 
27.07.2022. 
10. We, therefore, direct the respondent-State to 
positively comply with these directions before the next 
date, unless the Supreme Court intervenes in the 
matter in the meantime. 
11. List on 27.07.2023. 
12. On the next date, the Secretary (Home), State 
of Uttarakhand, and the IG (Prisons) shall join the 
proceedings virtually. 
13. The virtual link should be communicated to the 
Members of the aforesaid Committee, so that they can 
join the proceedings. This order be also communicated 
to the Members of the Committee by the Registry. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.) (Vipin Sanghi, C.J.)
