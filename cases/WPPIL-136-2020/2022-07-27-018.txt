Delay in Recall Application (IA No.11682 of 2022)
The learned counsel for the petitioners does not 
fairly oppose the delay in filing the recall application. 
For the reasons stated in the affidavit filed in 
support of the delay condonation application, the delay 
condonation application is allowed, and the delay of 
194 days’ in preferring the recall application is, 
hereby, condoned. 
Recall Application (IA No.11681 of 2022)
By this application, the State seeks to recall the 
directions issued by this Court in the present writ 
petition on 08.12.2021, and in particular, in Paragraph 
No.31 (ix), whereby this Court constituted a 
Committee comprising of Mr. V.K. Singh, former IG 
(Prison), Telangana, as the Chairman; Prof. Vijay 
Raghavan, TISS Mumbai; and, Prof. Murali Karnam, 
NALSAR, Hyderabad. The Committee was required to 
inspect all the thirteen jails of the State of 
Uttarakhand, and make recommendations, firstly, with 
regard to the enhancement of the infrastructure of the 
Jails, and with regard to improving the amenities 
available in the Jails for the benefit of jail inmates. The Committee was also required to recommend the 
necessary laws, which need to be enacted or 
promulgated for the benefit of the jail inmates. The 
recommendations were required to be submitted 
within a period of three months. The State was 
granted six months’ time to comply with the 
recommendations. 
The premise on which the present application is 
being moved is that, in compliance of the order passed 
by the Supreme Court in Writ Petition (Civil) No.406 of 
2013, “Re-inhuman Conditions in 1382 Prisons”, the 
Government of India issued an order on 04.10.2018, 
constituting a “Committee on Prison Reforms”, led by a 
former Judge of the Supreme Court. 
The submission of the learned Standing Counsel 
appearing for the State is that the terms of reference 
of the said Committee cover the field in respect 
whereof this Court constituted the Committee while 
passing the order dated 08.12.2021. 
In response to our query, we are informed that 
the Committee constituted by this court, in fact, 
visited some of the jails in the State, and gave 
(Preliminary Recommendations on Urgent Prison 
Reforms in Uttarakhand). The Committee also raised 
its bills for transportation etc. Out of the bills raised, a 
sum of Rs.30,548/- still remains outstanding. There 
can be no justification for non-payment of the said 
bills. 
Therefore, we direct the State to, positively, 
clear the bills, so raised by the Committee, 
peremptorily in next two weeks. No further extension 
shall be granted. 
The Committee constituted under the orders of the Supreme Court is looking at the larger picture with 
regard to the conditions of jail all over the country. 
However, the Committee constituted by this Court was 
concerned with inspection of thirteen jails in the State 
of Uttarakhand, and in the light of the conditions 
prevailing therein, the Committee was required to 
submit its recommendations. 
In our view, the State cannot evade the 
Preliminary Recommendations on Urgent Prison 
Reforms in the State of Uttarakhand, by seeking recall 
of Paragraph No.31(ix) of the order dated 08.12.2021, 
passed by this Court. 
We, therefore, dismiss this application. We direct 
the State to file a report with regard to the 
implementations of Preliminary Recommendations on 
Urgent Prison Reforms in the State of Uttarakhand 
within next three weeks. The respondent-State should 
also disclose compliance with regard to other 
directions issued in the order dated 08.12.2021, in the 
affidavit that they shall file in three weeks. 
A copy of the Preliminary Recommendations on 
Urgent Prison Reforms in the State of Uttarakhand has 
been tendered by the learned Standing Counsel for the 
State in the Court, and the same is taken on record.
