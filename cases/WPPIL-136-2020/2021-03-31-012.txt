The Court made the following: 
ORDER: (per Hon’ble The Chief Justice Sri Raghvendra Singh Chauhan)
 
 In compliance of the order dated 03.03.2021, 
Mr. A.P. Anshuman, the Inspector General of Prison, 
Uttarakhand, has submitted an affidavit. The same is 
taken on record. 
2. Bare perusal of the affidavit clearly reveals 
that the affidavit is shorn off concrete details. It is 
absolutely vague in its content.  2
3. In para 3-F, Mr. Anshuman has mentioned the 
different facilities which are being provided in different 
jails. However, he has failed to mention, how many 
training halls and sheds are available in District Jail, 
Haridwar? Although, he claims that there is a skill 
development programme, the exact nature and the 
content of the programme has not been revealed. 
4. Similarly, in para 3-F(x), he claims that “CCTV 
cameras are proposed for every jails. In first phase, 
CCTV cameras are being installed in Dehradun, Haridwar 
jail and sub-jail Haldwani”. However, it is unclear as to 
when the first phase began? The present status of the 
first phase? When the second phase would begin? And 
the time frame required for ensuring that CCTV cameras 
are installed in all the jails. The critical information is 
conspicuously missing from the affidavit. 
5. Likewise, in para 4, Mr. Anshuman mentions 
that “reformative techniques are used”. He further 
mentions that “training is being provided to the inmates 
with regard to the stitching, carpentering, powerloom, 
nursery / gardening under the Kaushal Vikas Yojana”. 
However, he does not mention whether the Kaushal 
Vikas Yojana is being implemented in all, or some of the  3
jails? If the said Yojana is being implemented in some of 
the jails, then in which jail it is being implemented? He 
further mentions that “distance education has been 
imparted by IGNOU and NIOS”. However, the affidavit 
does not mention, whether the education being imparted 
by these two organizations is being imparted on a daily, 
weekly or monthly basis? And education in which 
subject is being imparted by these two organizations? 
6. He further claims that “training is being 
imparted by volunteers of Dev Sanskrit University and 
Shanti Kunj, Haridwar”. However, he does not mention 
as to what the training relates to? Whether it is the 
training in academic area, or in technical area, or 
vocational area? Moreover, there is no indication 
whether the volunteers of Dev Sanskriti University are 
the students, or the faculty members of the said 
University. 
7. He further mentions that “in District Jail, Tehri, 
the basic course of Art of Living is being conducted by 
live telecast”. But it is unclear as to whether the said 
live telecast is being conducted by Shri Shri Ravishankar 
himself, or by his devotees, or trained personnel.  4
Moreover, it is unclear whether the live telecast is being 
done on daily, weekly or monthly basis? 
8. He also claims that “the use of ‘ringals’ to the 
prisoners, contract is being made with THDC and CSR 
branch for making baskets, decoration items etc.” 
However, it is unclear, whether any MOU has been 
entered between the jail department and the THDC, or 
not? It is unclear as to what the abbreviation “CSR” 
stands for? It is further unclear, whether these 
decorative items made by the prisoners would be 
marketed through THDC, or through other agencies, 
government or non-government agencies? 
9. In his affidavit, he has also revealed that there 
is a large vacancy in the jail department. Although, the 
sanctioned strength of the department is 1030 posts, the 
working strength is 624. Thus, there is a vacancy of 407 
posts. However, he has not mentioned as to how the 
said vacancies are scheduled to be filled up? And the 
time period it would take to fill up these large vacancies. 
10. According to Mr. Anshuman, there is a grave 
problem of overcrowding, practically in most of the jails 
of the State. The District Jail in Dehradun has capacity 
of housing 580 inmates, but presently has 1421  5
inmates; District Jail, Haridwar has capacity of 840, 
whereas presently it houses 1328 prisoners; likewise, 
sub-jail, Haldwani, was constructed for housing merely 
382 prisoners. But presently, it houses 1756 prisoners. 
According to Mr. Anshuman, the total capacity of all the 
jails is for housing 3540 prisoners, whereas presently 
6608 prisoners are being housed. Thus, obviously the 
problem of overcrowding stares all of us in our face. 
Despite the problem of overcrowding, the Government 
has sanctioned construction of only three new jails in 
District Pithoragarh, Champawat and Udham Singh 
Nagar. However, when these three jails were 
sanctioned? What is the status of the construction of the 
three jails? What is the capacity of these three jails, is 
unclear. 
11. Even, while dealing with the compliance of 
directions issued by the Hon’ble Supreme Court, Mr. 
Anshuman merely mentioned “that a Committee has 
been constituted at the State level for proper 
implementation of Model Prison Manual”. However, 
neither the constitution of the Committee, nor the date 
of its constitution, nor the Government Order by which 
this Committee is constituted, has been submitted 
before this Court. He further claims that the prison  6
inmates calling system has been installed. However, it 
is unclear, whether the said system has been installed in 
all the jails of the State, or in only a few of them? 
12. Lastly, he claims that tender notice has been 
floated for construction of District Jail, Udham Singh 
Nagar. However, the status of this tender notice is 
unclear. Moreover, although he claims that “45 Type-II 
residential quarters have been constructed for the staff 
of sub-jail Haldwani”, it is unclear, whether the 
residential accommodations have been allotted, or not? 
Although he mentions that the construction work of 
residential quarters of District Jail, Nainital is in 
progress, but the status of such a progress is unclear. 
13. Surprisingly, while dealing with the inmate 
population, Mr. Anshuman does not reveal the number 
of under-trials, and number of convicted prisoners. 
Moreover, he does not reveal the basis of classification 
of the under-trials, and the convicted prisoners. 
14. Therefore, this Court directs Mr. A.P. 
Anshuman, the learned Inspector General of Prisons, to 
submit a more detailed and a more clear affidavit on the 
points mentioned here-in-above. He is directed to 
submit the said affidavit on or before 19.04.2021.  7
15. Let a certified copy of this order be supplied to 
the learned counsel for the parties, on payment of the 
prescribed charges, by tomorrow. 
16. List this case on 20.04.2021.
