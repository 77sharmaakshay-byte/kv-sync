COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.2289 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
1. Mr. I.D. Paliwal, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. Sandeep Kumar Verma, learned 
Brief Holder for the State.
3. By means of this writ petition, the 
petitioner has challenged the promotion 
order dated 28.06.2024 whereby respondent 
no.3 was promoted on the post of Lecturer
Computer Science and Engineering/PGDCA.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for 
the petitioner that both the petitioner as well 
as respondent no.3 responded to the same 
advertisement, which was issued by the 
respondents-Board on 22.02.2014 for 
supplying the vacancies on the post of 
Computer Programmer/Operator in 
Uttarakhand Technical Education, Board. 
Both the petitioner as well as respondent 
no.3 were selected pursuant to the aforesaid 
selection process however respondent no.3 
joined on the said post in the year 2014 
while petitioner joined on the said post on 
18.10.2018. The petitioner got joining only 
after interference of this Court in a Writ 
Petition (S/S) No.3323 of 2017. 
5. It is contended by learned counsel for 
the petitioner that in view of the terms and 
condition of the appointment letter 
contained in para 10, the seniority of the 
petitioner was to be determined on the basis 
of merit list. Since the petitioner was ahead 
of respondent no.3, and he was senior to her 
instead of respondent no.3 the petitioner should have been promoted as Lecturer. 
6. Issue notice to respondent no.3 
returnable within six weeks.
7. Learned counsel for the respondents 
may file their counter affidavits within six 
weeks.
8. List on 11.02.2025. 
9. In the meantime, it is provided that 
promotion of respondent no.3 shall be 
subject to the final decision of this writ 
petition.
