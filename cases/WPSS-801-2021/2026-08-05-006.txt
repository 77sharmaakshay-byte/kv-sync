3. Today, the matter is listed on the amendment 
application (IA No. 3/2026), whereby petitioner wants to 
incorporate certain amendments in the writ petition by 
inserting paragraph nos.13A to 13I along with annexures-7 & 
8 in the writ petition after paragraph no.13; prayer clause 
nos.i-a to i-d, as well as to incorporate respondent no.4-
Bilbar Singh Bhandari, S/o Dhan Singh Bhandari, Gram 
Sajwan Kanda, P.O. Devprayag, District Tehri Garhwal.
4. Learned State Counsel submits that since the 
amendment sought by petitioner is formal in nature, which 
crept in after rejection of the representation of the petitioner, 
therefore, no objection is required.
5. Having perused the amendment application as well as 
the affidavit filed in support thereof, this Court is of the view 
that the amendment application deserves to be allowed. 
Accordingly, the amendment application is allowed.
6. Let necessary amendment be incorporated by supplying 
an amended copy of the writ petition as well as amended 
memo of parties within a week.
