HON’BLE ASHISH NAITHANI, J.
Heard Mr. Abhijay Negi and Ms. Snigdha 
Tiwari, learned counsel for the petitioner, Mr. C.S. 
Rawat, learned Chief Standing Counsel assisted by 
Mr. Gajendra Tripathi, learned Standing Counsel for 
the State of Uttarakhand/respondents. 
2. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that, despite the petitioner having been exonerated 
of all charges as far back in October, 2024, yet till 
today, no posting has been given to the petitioner. 
3. Per contra, the learned C.S.C. would submit 
that the concerned Secretary has already addressed 
letter to the higher-ups regarding posting of the 
petitioner. 
4. It is settled law that no government employee 
can be rendered without post or work, except in 
accordance with law, i.e. by way of suspension or 
transfer. In the instant case, neither being available, 
the action of the respondent, in not giving a posting 
to the government employee, cannot be appreciated. We hope that before reopening of the Court after 
Holi holidays, the respondents would have addressed 
this issue, and ensure a posting to the petitioner. 
5. List this case on 20.03.2025.
