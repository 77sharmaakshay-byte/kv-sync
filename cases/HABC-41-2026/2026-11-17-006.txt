The petitioner has filed the instant habeas 
corpus petition so as to seek the corpus of Ms. 
Saloni, who, according to the petitioner, is his 
wife. Their marriage was registered on 
18.07.2026, at Roorkee, District Haridwar. They 
were staying together. According to the 
petitioner, the respondent nos. 4 to 9, in the 
absence of the petitioner, took the corpus along 
with them on 18.08.2026.
This case is not as simple as it looks. In 
fact, the petitioner and the corpus had filed 
WPCRL No.1444 of 2026, Saloni and Another v. 
State of Uttarakhand (“the first petition”) seeking 
protection on the ground that they are married 
and their marriage is registered, but the private 
respondent nos. 4 to 8 in the first petition have 
been threatening them, as they were opposed to 
the marriage.
Learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that the respondent nos. 4 to 8 in the instant 
habeas corpus petition and the first petition are 
same.
In the first petition, this Court had passed the following order:-
“5. The documents on record reveal that both 
the petitioners are major. In that view of the matter, 
and in view of the ruling of the Hon’ble Supreme 
Court in the case of Lata Singh Vs State of U.P. and 
another, reported in (2006) 5 SCC 475, the 
petitioners have made out a case for grant of 
protection. 
6. The Station House Officer, Police Station, 
Pirayan Kaliyar, District Haridwar is directed to 
assess the threat, if any, to the life and limb of the 
petitioners, and provide necessary protection, if it is 
found that there is a threat to the life and limb of the 
petitioners. The S.H.O. is further directed to summon 
the private respondents, and such other persons, 
who are inimically placed towards the marriage of 
the petitioners, and counsel them, in accordance 
with law.”
In the instant habeas corpus petition, this 
Court had interacted with the corpus on multiple 
occasions. On 12.10.2026, this Court had 
observed that the corpus is not freely stating 
before the Court. She is under tremendous 
psychological and mental pressure.
Today, the corpus has been produced 
before this Court by SI Amit Nautiyal, Police 
Station Kaliyar, District Haridwar, and FC 1244 
Mausam.
The Court interacted with the corpus today 
also. It appears that she is not out from 
pressure. She is not free even today.
If based on her statement the petition is 
closed today, perhaps, it would be not expedient 
in the interest of justice.
Both the petitioner and the corpus had 
sought protection by filing the first petition and 
the protection was given. In fact, the Station 
House Officer, Police Station Kaliyar, District 
Haridwar, was directed by this Court to provide 
protection and call the private respondent nos. 4 
to 8, and counsel them. Has he done so? And 
when was it done? Where are those General 
Diary entries? 
This Court is of the view that this matter 
requires little more exercise. 
Let the Station House Officer, Police 
Station Kaliyar, District Haridwar, submit a 
report as to what action has he taken pursuant to instruction given in Para No.6 of the judgment 
dated 23.07.2026, passed in the first petition, 
specifically he will state as to when did he call 
the private respondent nos. 4 to 8? When did he 
counsel them? Where is its record? Where are the 
General Diary entries? And if it is not done, why 
he should not be proceeded for contempt of this 
Court?
It is the case of the petitioner that despite 
protection order, on 18.08.2026, the corpus was 
taken away in the absence of the petitioner of 
which a report was given by the petitioner to the 
Station House Officer, Police Station Kaliyar, 
District Haridwar, but no action was taken on it. 
The Station House Officer, Police Station 
Kaliyar, District Haridwar, shall also report as to 
what action he has taken on the report that was 
given to him by the petitioner with regard to 
forcible removal of corpus from his home in his 
absence. He shall submit a detailed report.
Today also, the corpus, while crying, 
submits that she does not want to join the 
company of the petitioner. Does it reflect the 
tremendous pressure under which she is giving 
the statement? This Court refrains to make any 
comment on it, but as stated, it appears that the 
she is not giving the statement voluntarily or 
with her free will.
List this matter on 01.12.2026.
On that date, the corpus and the petitioner 
both shall remain personally present before this 
Court. 
On that date, the Station House Officer, 
Police Station Kaliyar, District Haridwar, shall 
submit its report.
(Siddhartha Sah J.) (Ravindra Maithani J.)
