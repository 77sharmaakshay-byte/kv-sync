The petitioner has filed the instant habeas 
corpus petition so as to seek the corpus of Ms. 
Saloni, who, according to the petitioner, is his 
wife. Their marriage was registered on 
18.07.2026, at Roorkee, District Haridwar. They 
were staying together. According to the 
petitioner, the respondent nos. 4 to 9, in the 
absence of the petitioner, took the corpus along 
with them on 18.08.2026.
On 02.09.2026, this Court had required 
the Station House Officer to produce the corpus
before this Court. On 09.09.2026, we interacted 
with the corpus in the chambers, when she 
stated that she is in love with the petitioner and 
wants to join his company. Now, in the Court, 
she changed her stand. 
Today also, the Court interacted with the 
corpus in the chambers. 
This Court is of the view that whatever the 
corpus is stating, it is not her free will. She is 
under tremendous psychological and mental 
pressure. The Court proposed the corpus as to whether she would like to stay in nari niketan for 
a while? She declined to it. She stated that she 
would like to go to her parents’ house.
As stated, the corpus is under tremendous 
pressure. She is not expressing her free will. 
Therefore, we are of the view that the matter 
needs to be adjourned. 
List this matter on 17.11.2026.
On that date, the corpus shall remain 
personally present before this Court.
