Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Saurabh Budhori, learned counsel for 
appellant/applicant.
2. Mr. Rakesh Joshi, learned A.G.A. with Mr. 
Manoj Chandra Bhatt, learned Brief Holder for 
the State of Uttarakhand/respondent.
3. Today, the matter is listed on Second Bail 
Application (IA/5/2026) moved by appellant/
applicant for his release on bail.
4. Learned counsel for appellant/applicant 
submits that appellant/applicant is under 
incarceration on his conviction vide judgment 
and order dated 05.06.2024 passed by learned 
Additional Sessions Judge, Khatima, District 
Udham Singh Nagar, in Sessions Trial No.214 
of 2021, State Vs. Anil Saxena, for the offence 
punishable under Section 304B IPC and 
sentenced for 07 years’ R.I. with fine of 
Rs.10,000/- with default stipulation of six 
months additional R.I.
5. He further submits that the cause of 
death of deceased was shown in the post￾mortem report due to haemorrhage and 
shock. He also submits that the appellant/ 
applicant is languishing in jail since 
05.06.2024 and his first bail application was 
rejected by this Court on 11.09.2025. 2
6. Learned State Counsel has filed its 
objection along with delay condonation 
application (IA/6/2026). For the reasons 
indicated in delay condonation application, 
delay in filing the objection is condoned. 
Objection filed by the State is taken on record. 
Delay condonation application (IA/6/2026) 
stands disposed of.
7. Learned State Counsel vehemently 
opposed the second bail application stating 
that there is no new ground taken by 
appellant/applicant in the second bail 
application, he has taken the same ground as 
taken in first bail application. 
8. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties, this Court is of the view that no new 
ground has been taken by the appellant/
applicant in second bail application and further
the allegations levelled against appellant/
applicant are grave and serious in nature. 
From perusal of the post-mortem report, it is 
found that there are three severe injuries on 
the head due to which haemorrhage and shock 
was occurred and the deceased died, thus, this 
Court is not inclined to grant bail the 
appellant/applicant at this stage.
9. Accordingly, second bail application (IA/
5/2026) is rejected.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
