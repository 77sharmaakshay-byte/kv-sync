petitioner and Sri M.A. Khan, learned brief holder for the
State. 
By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has sought
quashing of the first information report dated 18.6.2008
registered as Case Crime No. 79/2008 relating to offence
punishable under Section2/3 of U.P. Gangsters & Anti Social
Activities (Prevention) Act, 1986 with P.S. Pathri, Distt.
Hardwar. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner argued that there is a
single case shown against the petitioner in the gang chart and
that is not sufficient to show “activities” required for the
purposes of constituting the offence punishable u/s 2/3 of
U.P. Gangsters & Anti Social Activities (Prevention) Act, 1986.
However, having gone through the contents of the FIR and the
seriousness of the allegations, we are not inclined to interfere
in the present matter. 
Therefore, the writ petition is dismissed summarily. (Stay
Application No. 4186/08 also stands dismissed)
