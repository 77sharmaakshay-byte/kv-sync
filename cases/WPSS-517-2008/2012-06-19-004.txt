Hon’ble Tarun Agarwala, J.
Mr. Ajay Veer Pundir, the learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. N. S. Pundir, the learned brief holder for the State of 
Uttarakhand. 
An advertisement was published for filling up of 134 posts 
of Sub-Inspector and Platoon Commanders in Civil Police as well 
as in Provincial Armed Costabulary (P.A.C.). Pursuant to this, 
the petitioner applied and appeared in the written examination. 
Results were declared on 10th January, 2007 in which the name of 
the petitioner was not found and, being aggrieved, the present 
writ petition was filed. 
The counter affidavit indicates that the petitioner had 
applied under the O.B.C. category and the cut off marks for the 
O.B.C. candidates was 185, whereas the petitioner has obtained 
only 130.75 marks. This fact has not been denied by the 
petitioner since no rejoinder affidavit has been filed inspite of 
filing counter affidavit in August, 2008. 
In the light of the aforesaid, the court does not find any 
reason to interfere in the impugned order. The writ petition fails 
and is dismissed.
