COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
C-528 No. 489 of 2025
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Ankur Sharma, learned counsel for the Applicants. 
2. Mr. Akshay Latwal, learned A.G.A. for the State of 
Uttarakhand. 
3. On a hopeful note that the parties, especially Applicant 
No. 1 – Pankaj Nautiyal and Respondent No. 3-his wife, Smt. 
Priyanka Pant Nautiyal, may arrive at some kind of 
compromise, the parties were directed to appear in person 
today.
4. Today, after interacting with the parties, specifically 
beginning with Respondent No. 3- Priyanka Pant Nautiyal, and 
thereafter her husband/Applicant No. 1- Pankaj Nautiyal, the 
first impression gathered by the Court is that the parties do not 
wish to continue living under the same roof in matrimony. 
However, there appears to be a possibility of an amicable 
settlement, based on a condition put forth by Respondent No. 
3 (the wife), who states before the Court that she may agree 
to a settlement provided her personal belongings (Stree Dhan), 
jewellery, and the expenses incurred during the course of the 
marriage are compensated.
5. To this, the husband/Applicant No. 1 asserts and submits 
before the Court that he is willing to pay the marriage-related 
expenses and is also willing to return her jewellery and 
belongings (Stree Dhan).
6. In light of the above observations, the learned counsels 
for the parties are requested to assist the parties, especially 
the husband (Applicant herein) and the wife (Respondent No. 3 
herein) in reaching an amicable settlement. The extent of 
compensation shall be determined between Applicant No. 1 
and Respondent No. 3.
7. Learned counsels for the parties are requested to 
facilitate and guide the parties in this direction.8. List this case on 10.09.2025.
9. Interim order, if any, is extended till the next date of 
listing.
