COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPCRL No. 119 of 2026
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. D.K. Sharma, learned Senior Advocate (through video 
conferencing) assisted by Mr. Amar Murti Shukla, learned counsel 
for the Petitioners. 
2. Mr. Vipul Painuly, learned A.G.A. for the State of 
Uttarakhand.
3. The present Criminal Writ Petition, filed under Article 226 of 
the Constitution of India, has been instituted with the following 
prayers:
“(i) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus, commanding and directing the official 
Respondent Nos. 2 and 3 to provide adequate security and 
protection to the Petitioners, in accordance with law, by 
safeguarding their rights to life, personal liberty, dignity and 
property as guaranteed under Article 21 of the Constitution of 
India, in view of the serious threat perception to the life and 
liberty of the Petitioners at the hands of private Respondent 
Nos. 4 to 8 and their goons.
(ii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus, commanding and directing the official 
Respondent Nos. 2 and 3 to restore forthwith the possession 
of the Petitioners in respect of the property in question.”
4. The Petitioners have also filed an Interim Relief Application 
(I.A. No. 02 of 2026) praying for issuance of an ad-interim writ of 
mandamus commanding and directing the official Respondent Nos. 
2 and 3 to provide adequate security and protection to the 
Petitioners, in accordance with law, so as to safeguard their life, 
personal liberty, dignity and property as guaranteed under Article 21 
of the Constitution of India, in view of the serious threat perception 
to their life and liberty at the hands of private Respondent Nos. 4 to 
8 and their associates.5. The petition is admitted.
6. Issue notice to the private Respondents. Steps be taken within 
one week.
7. Counter affidavit, if any, may be filed by the Respondents
before the next date.
8. Considering the facts and circumstances of the case, as an 
interim measure, it is directed that the official Respondent Nos. 2 
and 3 shall ensure that no harm is caused to the Petitioners and shall 
provide necessary protection to the life, liberty and property of the 
petitioners, in accordance with law, till the next date of listing.
9. The Interim Relief Application and Urgency Application 
stand disposed of accordingly.
10. List this case on 13.04.2026.
