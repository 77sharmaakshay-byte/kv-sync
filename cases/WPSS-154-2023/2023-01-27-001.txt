COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 141 of 2023
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Ganesh Kandpal, Advocate for 
the petitioner.
Mr. Puran Singh Bisht, Additional 
C.S.C. along with Mr. S.S. Chaudhary, 
Brief Holder for the State.
As a consequence of an order of 
promotion dated 10.10.2022, the services 
of the petitioner stood transfered as a 
Head Mistress in Government Primary 
School, Rawat Nagar, Block Haldwani, 
District Nainital. But, for the reasons 
undisclosed, though she has been 
permitted to join, she has not been 
permitted to work. Hence, the writ 
petition.
The respondents, who were 
represented by the Standing Counsel, may 
file their counter affidavit within a period 
of three weeks from today. But, the 
pendency of this writ petition will not be 
creating any obstruction for the 
respondents from paying the monthly 
remuneration to the petitioner on the 
promoted post.
List as soon as the counter affidavit 
is filed by the Standing Counsel.
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.) 
 Vacation Judge
