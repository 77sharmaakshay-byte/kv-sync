COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C-528 No. 293 of 2026
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Ms. Anmol Sandhu, learned counsel, 
holding brief of Mr. Lalit Sharma, learned 
counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Akshay Latwal, learned A.G.A. for the 
State.
3. The present criminal misc. application has 
been filed by the applicant challenging the 
chargesheet dated 08.07.2025, summoning 
order dated 17.11.2025 as well as the entire 
proceeding of Criminal Case No. 2508 of 2025, 
State Vs. Ankit Singh, under Sections 64 and 69 
of BNS, pending in the court of Additional Chief 
Judicial Magistrate, Haldwani, District Nainital.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant would 
submit that FIR was lodged on 30.05.2025 by 
respondent no. 2, wherein, she has alleged that 
applicant and respondent no. 2 met through 
social media application – Instagram and 
thereafter got friendly with each other and on 
15.09.2024, the applicant came to Delhi and 
there on the false promise of marriage, physical 
relations were made between them and again 
respondent no. 2 came with the applicant to 
Lalkuan and thereafter to Haldwani and there 
also, physical relations were made between 
them. Thereafter, the applicant took her to his 
house where parents of the applicant were not 
agreeable to their marriage and a quarrel took 
place between the applicant and his parents. 
Thereafter, both applicant and respondent no. 2 
went back to Delhi. Soon thereafter, respondent 
no. 2 came to know that she has become 
pregnant and when she asked the applicant for marriage, he refused. 
5. Learned counsel for the applicant further 
draws the attention of this Court to the Police 
report in which respondent no. 2 admitted that 
she is already married and having a son aged 
about seven years and no divorce has been 
granted to her husband. Meaning thereby, she 
was legally married at the time when she first 
met the applicant and when the FIR was lodged.
6. Having heard the submission, this Court is 
of the view that this matter definitely requires 
deliberation.
7. Issue notice to respondent no. 2, 
returnable at an early date. Steps to be taken 
within a week.
8. List after service report is received.
9. In the meantime, counter-affidavit(s), if 
any, be filed by the respondents.
10. Having heard submission of learned 
counsel for the parties and also considering the 
fact that since respondent no. 2 is already 
legally married and no judicial separation order 
has been passed in her favour, therefore there 
remains no question of pretext of false marriage 
given by the applicant, this Court, prima facie, 
is of the opinion and is satisfied that in the 
interest of justice, further proceedings of
Criminal Case No. 2508 of 2025, State Vs. Ankit 
Singh, under Sections 64 and 69 of BNS, 
pending in the court of Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Haldwani, District Nainital, shall 
remain stayed qua the applicant till the next 
date of listing.
 (Alok Mahra J.) 26.02.2026
