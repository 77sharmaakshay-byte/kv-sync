2. The applicant has preferred the 
present criminal miscellaneous 
application assailing the order dated 
04.12.2025 passed by learned Special 
Judge (POCSO Act)/Sessions Judge, 
Tehri Garhwal, whereby the application 
seeking determination of juvenility was 
rejected.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that learned Sessions Judge has 
erred in law in rejecting the application 
without due consideration of the 
provisions of Section 7 of the Juvenile 
Justice Act. It is contended that the 
Court below has summarily dismissed 
the plea of juvenility solely on the basis 
of the statement of the victim recorded 
under Section 161 Cr.P.C., without 
conducting a proper inquiry as mandated 
under law. It is further submitted that 
the Juvenile Justice Act casts a statutory 
obligation upon the Court to determine 
the issue of juvenility whenever such a 
plea is raised, irrespective of the stage of 
the proceedings, and only upon such 
determination, the Court can proceed 
further in the matter.
4. Issue notice to respondent no.2, 
returnable at an early date.5. List the matter after receipt of the 
service report. 
6. Considering the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
applicant, it is directed that, till the next 
date of listing, further proceedings of 
S.S.T. No.43 of 2024, pending before the 
Court of learned Special Judge (POCSO 
Act)/Sessions Judge, Tehri Garhwal, shall 
remain stayed.
7. Stay application (I.A. No.1 of 2026) 
stands disposed of.
