Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Hon’ble Siddhartha Sah, J.
Mr. Rajendra Singh Negi, Advocate for the 
appellant.
2. Ms. Manisha Rana Singh, learned D.A.G. for the 
State.
3. The appellant proposes to file an appeal against 
the judgment and order dated 04.08.2025, passed in 
Special Sessions Trial No.27 of 2024, State vs. Baliram 
Bata Magar @ Guman Bata, by the court of learned 
Special Sessions Judge (NDPS Act)/District & Sessions 
Judge, District Champawat. By it, the appellant has been 
convicted under Sections 8/20 & 8/18 of the Narcotic 
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985 and 
sentenced accordingly. There is a delay of 142 days in 
filing the present appeal.
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties on the Delay 
Condonation Application (IA No.01 of 2026).
5. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that the 
appellant belongs to a poor strata of society and nobody 
from relatives of appellant come forward to do pairvi of 
his case; appellant is also not having any means for 
bearing expenditure to file the present criminal appeal
within the limitation period, therefore the aforesaid delay has occurred.
6. It is not objected to by the learned State counsel.
7. There are good grounds to condone the delay in 
filing the appeal. The delay in filing the appeal is hereby 
condoned and the Delay Condonation Application (IA 
No.1 of 2026) is allowed.
8. Heard. 
9. Admit.
10. Call for LCR.
11. Once the LCR is received, let the paper book be 
prepared and provided to the learned counsel for the 
parties, as per Rules.
12. List this matter on 13.07.2026.
13. Meanwhile, objections to the Bail Application (IA 
No.01 of 2026) be filed by the State counsel.
