COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLR No. 851 of 2023
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Ms. Garima Thapa, learned counsel for 
the revisionist.
2. Mr. B.C. Joshi, learned A.G.A for the 
State.
3. Mr. Amit Kapri, learned counsel for the 
respondent.
4. The respondent Jitendra Singh, the 
husband of revisionist no.1 married with 
another lady though divorce decree is 
already stayed by this Court. The revisionist 
no. 2 is the minor son. Earlier the 
respondent denied in open court about the 
second marriage and submits that all the 
photographs as shown by the revisionist are 
fake but subsequently post lunch session, he 
tendered apology and submitted that he was 
in depression and all these photographs with 
regard to the second marriage are genuine. 
He was directed to file the details of the 
assets including the ITR along with the 
account statement of the second wife. 
5. It appears that the respondent no. 2 is 
repeatedly misleading to the court and have 
not approach with clean hands since he is 
not disclosing about details of the assets.
Learned counsel for the revisionist submits 
that recently the respondent has transferred the property in the name of second wife on 
06.09.2023 and on 24.03.2026 the another 
property was sold out. Not only this, even 
prior to that one property was purchased by 
him at the cost of Rs. 40,000,00/-. The 
counsel for the revisionist also pointed out 
that as per the ITR the revisionist is doing 
some business but he is giving the 
impression to the court that he is working as 
an Assistant in a jewelry shop. She also 
pointed out that the jewelry shop is owned 
by respondent no.2.
6. This Court is of the view that the 
respondent no.2 is repeatedly misleading to 
the Court but before taking further steps 
this Court is of the view that let the S.H.O. 
concerned may get the details of all assets 
of the respondent no. 2 as well as his 
second wife and submit the report before 
this Court and he can take assistance of 
some income tax officer. The report shall be 
placed before this Court within two weeks.
7. List this case on 27.04.2026.
