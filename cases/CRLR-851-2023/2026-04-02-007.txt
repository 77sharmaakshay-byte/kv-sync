COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRLR No. 851 of 2023
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Ms. Garima Thapa, learned counsel for the 
revisionist. 
2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned AGA for the State. 
3. Mr. Amit Kapri, learned counsel for the 
respondent.
4. Parties are present along with their minor son. 
Revisionist wife apprise to this Court that respondent 
husband married with another lady and living with 
her. She has also shown some photographs and for half 
an hour, this Court interacted with the parties but 
respondent husband is repeatedly making statement 
that all these photographs are fake. Not only, he also 
submits that the photographs enclosed at page nos. 90, 
91, 92 and 93, are also fake. 
5. This Court post the matter at 04.00 p.m. so that he 
may go through with photographs. 
6. Now, after post lunch session, respondent 
husband submits that he was under depression pre 
lunch session and could not understand the query of 
the Court and all these photographs are genuine. This 
Court further interacted with him by putting certain 
more queries but what this Court observed that 
respondent husband is repeatedly misleading to the 
Court by giving false statement. This is really a very 
serious issue. 
7. Be that as it may, taking into consideration that 
there is a matrimonial dispute in between the parties 
and the respondent husband is now married with 
another lady, now the question is in such an 
eventuality what further steps can be taken to secure 
future of wife and son. 
8. Learned counsel for the revisionist submits that 
respondent husband married with another lady during 
the divorce proceeding and is running a jewellery shop 
and he has transferred a house in the name of lady with 
whom he married. He has another shop in Vikas 
Nagar. 
9. This Court is giving three days time to the respondent husband to give complete details of his 
properties including bank account, ITR of last five 
years and account statement of the lady with whom he 
married. 
10. On the next date, respondent husband shall 
remain present in the Court. 
11. List on 08.04.2026 as a first case.
