COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
22.11.2023 CRLR No. 851 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Revisionists have filed the present 
Revision to enhance the monthly allowance 
granted under Section 125 of the Code of 
Criminal Procedure, 1973 by the learned 
Judge, Family Court, Vikas Nagar, District 
Dehradun on 16.08.2023 in Miscellaneous 
Criminal Case No. 87 of 2021 and for 
maintenance allowance of the revisionist no. 
1-wife of respondent no. 2-opposite party. 
The learned Judge, Family Court, Vikas 
Nagar, District Dehradun has directed the 
revisionist no. 2 to pay Rs. 8,000/- per month 
to his minor son for his maintenance, while, 
the said Application, filed under Section 125 
of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 has 
been rejected in relation to revisionist no. 1-
wife of the respondent no. 2. 
2. Heard Mr. Rajat Mittal, learned counsel 
for the revisionists and Mr. Akshay Latwal, 
learned Brief Holder for the State. 
3. Mr. Rajat Mittal, Advocate, submits that 
the respondent no. 2, husband of revisionist 
no. 1 got solemnized second marriage. 
4. Admit. 
5. Issue notice to the respondent no. 2. 
6. Steps to be taken within a week. 
7. List on 29.12.2023.
