COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPCRL No.1139 of 2025
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Vinod Sharma with Mr. Kamlesh Budhlakoti and 
Mr. Krishna Sharma, Advocates for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Bhaskar Chandra Joshi, AGA with Mr. Vikas 
Uniyal, Brief Holder for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition, petitioner has 
put to challenge the FIR No.0421 of 2025, under Sections 
111, 318(4), 336(3), 338, 340(2), 351(3), 352, 61(2) of 
BNS, 2023 dated 31.08.2025 registered at P.S. Gang 
Nahar, Roorkee, District Haridwar.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the 
petitioner is a practicing lawyer in Roorkee, District 
Haridwar and the allegations from the perusal of the First 
Information Report is only to the extent that the petitioner 
failed to get the mutation done in favour of the purchaser
who is informant in the present FIR. 
5. Vide order dated 24.09.2025, this Court directed the 
learned State Counsel to bring on record any material 
against the petitioner.
6. Learned counsel for the State, on instructions,
submits that the petitioner belonged to the Praveen 
Balmiki Gang and in this gang, she used to terrorize the 
people and got the land belonging to the people registered 
in the name of different persons on very nominal 
consideration and also indulged in impersonation. The 
instructions which has been received by the learned 
counsel for the State is passed on to the Court and the 2
same is placed on record.
7. From perusal of the aforesaid instructions, it is not 
specifically clear as to what role the petitioner was given 
in this all transactions. It appears that the petitioner is 
linked with Praveen Balmiki only for the reason that she 
appears in the cases of Praveen Balmiki in the court of 
law.
8. Having considered the rival submissions of the 
parties, this Court is of the view that the petitioner 
requires protection at this stage.
9. Issue notice to the respondent no.3 returnable within 
six weeks. Steps to be taken within three days. The 
respondents may file the counter affidavit.
10. List this case on 28.11.2025.
11. In the meantime, no coercive measures shall be 
taken against the petitioner during the pendency of the 
present writ petition pursuant to the impugned FIR.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
