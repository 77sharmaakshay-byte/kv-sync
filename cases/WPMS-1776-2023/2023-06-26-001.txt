26.06.2023 WPMS No. 1776 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
In an Appeal (Civil Appeal No. 13 of 
2022), filed by the present petitioners￾appellants against the respondents, an 
Application under Order 41 Rule 27 of 
the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908 was 
filed by the petitioners-appellants. The 
said Application has been dismissed by 
learned Appellate Court vide impugned 
order dated 15.04.2023. 
2. Heard Mr. Neeraj Garg, learned 
counsel for the petitioners and Mr. Vinod 
Nautiyal, learned Deputy Advocate 
General along with Mr. Yogesh Pandey, 
learned Additional C.S.C. for respondent 
no. 11. 
3. Mr. Neeraj Garg, Advocate, has 
submitted that the Application for 
producing additional evidence in appeal 
has to be considered at the stage of final 
hearing of an appeal and not prior 
thereto. In support of his submissions, 
he has relied upon a judgment of the 
Hon’ble Supreme Court in case of 
“Union of India vs. Ibrahim Uddin”,(2012) 8 SCC 148”.
4. Issue notice to respondent nos. 1 to 
10 and respondent no. 12.
5. Steps to be taken within a week. 
6. List on 04.09.2023.
7. Till then, further proceedings of 
Civil Appeal No. 13 of 2022, pending 
before the Court of District Judge, 
Nainital, are stayed.
