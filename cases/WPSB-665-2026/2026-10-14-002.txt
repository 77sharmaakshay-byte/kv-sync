4. Petitioner has challenged Rule 8 of the 
Uttarakhand Employees State Insurance 
Scheme Pharmacist Cadre (Allopathic) Service 
Rules, 2021. 
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner drew 
our attention to an order dated 13.05.2025, 
passed by the Coordinate Bench in WPSB No. 
486 of 2021, in which, after considering the 
stand taken by the Joint Secretary, Labour 
Department, Government of Uttarakhand, the 
writ petition was disposed of with direction to 
the State Government to finalise the matter and issue necessary notification within four 
months. More than 16 months have gone by 
after passing of the aforesaid order in Writ 
Petition No. 486 of 2021, but no progress has 
been made in the matter. 
6. In such view of the matter, we direct the 
Secretary, Department of Labour to take a 
decision in terms of order passed in WPSB No. 
486 of 2021, positively within two weeks and 
report back to this Court on the next date of 
listing. 
7. List this case on 04.11.2026. 
8. In the event, the decision is not taken 
before the next date of listing, the Secretary, 
Labour Department, shall appear in person 
before this Court along with record of the 
case.
