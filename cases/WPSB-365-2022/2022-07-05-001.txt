05.07.2022 WPSB No. 365 of 2022 
Hon'ble Vipin Sanghi, C.J. 
Hon’ble R.C. Khulbe, J.
 Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
 Issue notice. 
 Mr. Pradeep Joshi, learned Additional Chief 
Standing Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand / 
respondents, appears and accepts notice. 
 The petitioner was dismissed from service upon his 
being found guilty of misconduct of remaining absent 
on different occasions totalling upto thirty days, apart 
from being found intoxicated on one occasion. 
 The Uttarakhand Public Services Tribunal has 
rejected the petitioner’s claim on the ground of 
limitation by holding that he has not preferred the 
same within the period of one year from the date of 
communication of the appellate order dismissing his 
departmental Appeal on 23.02.2018. At the same 
time, the Tribunal found the punishment imposed upon 
the petitioner to be disproportionate, and directed the 
respondents that they may consider the petitioner’s 
prayer for compulsory retirement or reduction to rank 
to a minimum pay-scale, since the petitioner had 
rendered twenty years of service. 
 The respondents have not considered the said 
observation of the Tribunal.  We direct the respondents to consider the said plea 
of the petitioner for reduction of the penalty, which 
prima facie appears to be rather disproportionate. 
 The respondents may take a decision on the 
aforesaid aspect within six weeks. However, in case 
they wish to contest this petition, they may file their 
counter-affidavit(s) within eight weeks. 
 List this case on 06.09.2022. 
 
 (R.C. Khulbe, J) (Vipin Sanghi, CJ)
