Since both these criminal appeals arise from one 
and the same judgment and order passed in Sessions Trial No.92
of 2021, State v. Aklesh Mohan Bahuguna and Others, they are 
heard together.
2. The instant appeals have been preferred against 
judgment and order dated 05.08.2026, passed in Sessions Trial 
No.92 of 2021, State v. Aklesh Mohan Bahuguna and Others, by 
the court of 1st Additional Sessions Judge, Rishikesh, District 
Dehradun. By it, the appellants have been convicted under 
Sections 302 read with 34 IPC, and sentenced accordingly. 
3. Heard.2
4. These appeals have already been admitted.
5. List in due course for final hearing. 
6. Heard on Bail Applications, IA No.1 of 2026
7. According to the FIR, Archana was married to the 
appellant Mukesh Mohan Bahuguna, who is the son of the 
appellant Smt. Vimla Devi. The in-laws of Archana had harassed 
her multiple times. On 28.05.2020, at 10:10 p.m., Archana 
ranged her brothers and others that she is being harassed by her 
in-laws. It is thereafter, the informant Vijay Sharma along with his 
brother Ajay Sharma and others went to the matrimonial house of 
Archana. There was some altercation between the informant and 
the family members of Archana, and in that altercation, the 
appellants exhorted the co-convict Aklesh to finish the informant 
and his companions. On it, the co-convict Aklesh opened fire, due 
to which Ajay Sharma fell on the ground and died. 
8. Learned counsel for the appellants submits that 
the appellants were on bail during trial; they have no direct role of 
killing; the only role of exhortation has been attributed to them.
9. Learned State Counsel admits this fact.
10. Having considered this and other attending 
factors, we are of the view that it is a case in which the execution 
of sentence should be suspended and the appellants be enlarged 
on bail.
11. The bail applications are allowed.3
12. The sentence appealed against is suspended 
during the pendency of the appeals.
13. Let the appellants be released on bail during the 
pendency of the appeals on their executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two reliable sureties, each of the like amount, by each 
one of them, to the satisfaction of the court concerned.
