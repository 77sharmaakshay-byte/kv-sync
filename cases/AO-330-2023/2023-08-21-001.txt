2. Counsel for the appellant would submit 
that the Tribunal has given the right to pay and 
recover to the Insurance Company, however, 
he placed reliance upon the judgment of 
Hon’ble Apex Court in the case of ‘Balu 
Krishna Chavan Vs. The Reliance General 
Insurance Company Ltd. & Ors. 2022 
LiveLaw (SC) 932’ as well as the judgment of 
this Court in the case of ‘United India 
Insurance Co. Ltd. vs. Smt. Tara Devi & Ors. 
2009(2) U.D., 541’ in which, it is held that in 
the cases of M.A.C.T, the Tribunal has no 
power to give the right to the Insurance 
Company of pay and recover of the 
compensation amount and the same lies with 
the Apex Court under Article 142 of the 
Constitution of India.
