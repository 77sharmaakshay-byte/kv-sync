Mr. Harsh Vardhan Dhanik, Advocate, for

This Appeal from Order under Section 
173 of the Motor Vehicles Act, has been 
preferred against the award dated 29.05.2023, as 
rendered in MACP Case No. 121 of 2019, Smt. 
Mohini and Another Vs. Ramesh Chandra Batra 
and others. 
While admitting the Appeal, the 
coordinate Bench of this Court, by an order dated 
21.08.2023, has directed the appellant to deposit 
50% of the awarded amount i.e. approximately 
Rs. 20.00 lakh. 
The respondents/claimants have filed a 
Money Withdrawal Application No. IA/2/2023.
Looking to the contingencies expressed 
by the claimants in the aforesaid Money 
Withdrawal Application, the same would stand 
partly allowed. The learned Motor Accident 
Claims Tribunal is directed to disburse an amount 
of Rs. 10.00 lakh to the claimants/applicants 
within a period of six weeks from the date of 
receipt of the certified copy of this order.
Accordingly, Money Withdrawal 
Application would stand disposed of.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
