COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 2485 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, learned Senior 
Advocate assisted by Mr. Chandra 
Shekhar Dalakoti, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. Sachin Mohan Singh Mehta, 
learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Pursuant to the previous order 
dated 02.01.2025, Mr. Sachin Mohan 
Singh Mehta, learned Brief Holder placed 
before this Court the written instructions 
of the Director, Secondary Education, 
Uttarakhand in which it is mentioned 
that the issue as raised in this petition is
not covered by the judgment passed by 
the Division Bench of this Court which 
was affirmed by the Hon’ble Supreme 
Court, the reference of which is given in 
previous order dated 02.01.2025.
4. On perusal of the written 
instructions it reveals that the Director, 
Secondary Education have not gone 
through with the judgment.
5. The short issue in this petition is 
that the petitioner was inducted as 
Lecturer in the year 1996, at that point 
of time requirement of B.Ed. was not the 
essential qualification, however, B.Ed. 
was made essential qualification in the 
year 2008. Thereafter the institution was 
provincialised in the year 2014, however, 
the petitioner is not permitted to work on 
the ground that the petitioner is not having B.Ed. qualification as per the 
Rules which was amended in the year 
2008.
6. Mr. Bahuguna, learned Senior 
Advocate for the petitioner submits that 
qualification of the incumbent has to be 
seen at the time of induction and any 
subsequent amendment will not 
invalidate his appointment. Mr. 
Bahuguan further submits that the issue 
of Bhuwan Chandra Papnai was identical 
which was not disputed by Mr. Mehta, 
learned Brief Holder for the State. 
7. Now, the question is how and on 
what basis the Director opined that the 
issue of the present case is entirely 
different to the issue of Mr. Bhuwan 
Chandra Papnai. In order to examine 
this, put up this matter on 09.01.2025. 
On that day, Director, Secondary 
Education, Uttarakhand shall join the 
proceedings through V.C. and justify 
about his written instructions which was 
sent to the Chief Standing Counsel.
 
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
