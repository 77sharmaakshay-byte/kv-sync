1. Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, learned counsel for the
petitioner.
2. Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Addl. Chief Standing
Counsel for the State.
3. Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Addl. Chief Standing
Counsel for the State has placed the written
instructions before this Court, which is taken on
record.
4. On perusal of the written instructions, this Court
is of the view that those decisions, which are made for
the benefit of public at large can be termed as a “Policy
Decision” and not in respect of an individual or cadre
in service. So far as Dearness Allowance is concerned
no discrimination can be made amongst daily wagers,
PRD volunteers, Home Guards or any regular
incumbent. This Court is of the view that D.A. should
be extended equally to all categories of employees,
irrespective of their status. 
5. Let an affidavit be filed by the Secretary,
Finance, within two weeks to explain how it is a policy
decision and why Dearness Allowances are not being
extended PRD volunteers.
6. List on 30.11.2026.
