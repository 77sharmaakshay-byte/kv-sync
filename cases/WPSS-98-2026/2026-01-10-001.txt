Mr. Vinay Kumar, learned counsel for

2. Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned Addl.

3. Mr. Shailendra Nauriyal, learned 
counsel for respondent no.5 through V.C.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that in pursuance to advertisement 
dated 14.03.2024 vacancies earmarked for 
the orphan category under the SC quota
were vacant and the respondents though 
have recommended the names of the persons 
in the open category and the OBC category 
against the said vacancies which remained 
unfilled under the orphan category, however,
the same has not been done in the case of 
SC category.
5. Learned counsel for the Uttarakhand 
Subordinate Service Selection Commission
(UKSSSC), however, submits that the 
vacancies which remained unfilled under the 
orphan category, persons have been 
recommended from the SC category also as 
has been done in the open and OBC 
category.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner, 
however, dispute the said submission and it 
is submitted that in case the two vacancies 
under the orphan category are vacant and no 
persons from the SC category has been 
recommended then one post in the said 
category may be kept vacant.7. In view of the above submission, as an 
interim measure, it is provided that one post 
earmarked in the orphan category shall be
kept vacant under the SC quota.
8. Counter affidavit, if any, be filed within 
four weeks.
9. List this case on 23.02.2026.
