Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Nikhil Kushwaha and Mr. Avneesh Kumar, 
learned counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned A.G.A. for the 
State.
3. The instant petition is moved under section 528 
of BNSS, 2023, by the present applicant ‘Amit Kumar’ 
S/o Hardwari along with the compounding 
application with the affidavit of the applicant and the 
respondent no. 2 for quashing of the proceeding of 
Criminal Case No. 1248 of 2026 ‘State vs. Amit Kumar’ 
pending in the court of learned Addl. Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar, 
wherein, the present applicant is summoned to face 
the trial for the offences punishable under sections 69, 
351(2) and 352 of BNS, 2023.
4. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the 
applicant that the compounding application is 
supported with the affidavit of the informant/victim￾the respondent no. 2, wherein, it is contended that 
they have resolved their dispute amicably and due to 
some misunderstanding the FIR was lodged and,
therefore, the proceeding be quashed since nothing 
survive in between them.
5. The statement as given in the compounding 
application particularly in para-2 & 3 are being 
reproduced herein as under:
“2. That now the dispute has been amicably 
settled by the applicant and respondent no. 2 by their 
mutual consent out of the court with the mediation 
of the respected persons of the locality, and it is settled between the parties that they will not initiate 
any case against each other and will not interfere 
with the affairs of each other in future.
3. That since the parties of the case have entered 
into compromise, therefore, respondent no.2 does 
not want to prosecute the applicant in the aforesaid 
case crime further more thus to continue the 
proceedings of the aforesaid criminal case would be 
futile exercise therefore, the impugned FIR No. 0511 
of 2025 dated 16-12-2025 Registered at Police Station 
Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar, Charge sheet 
dated 18-02-2026 in connection with the impugned 
FIR and the Cognizance/Summoning order dated 
09-06-2026 passed by learned Additional chief 
Judicial Magistrate, Kashipur, District Udham Singh 
Nagar under sections 69, 351(2) & 352 of B.N.S. in 
Misc. Criminal case no. 1248 of 2026, 'State of 
Uttarakhand V/s Amit Kumar' along with the entire 
proceedings of the aforesaid Misc. Criminal case, 
Consequent to / arising out of the impugned FIR are 
liable to be quashed/set-aside by this Hon'ble Court, 
to secure the ends of justice in terms of compromise 
took place between the applicant and respondent 
no.2 with their mutual consent out of the court and 
the applicants be permitted to compound the 
aforesaid case because to proceed with the criminal 
proceeding further against the present applicant 
would be a futile exercise and would be resulting to 
waste the valuable time the Court as the dispute 
between the parties has been settled amicably out of 
the court, failing which the parties shall suffer 
irreparable loss and injury, which cannot be 
compensated in any manner.”
6. Now, learned counsel for the respondent/ 
informant submits that the informant now wants to 
withdraw the affidavit filed in support of the 
compounding application since she is not agreeable 
for closing this proceeding and the matter be decided 
on its own merit.
7. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned A.G.A., who 
accepts notice for respondent no. 1, submits that after 
thorough investigation the charge-sheet was filed but 
as contended in the compounding application the 
informant earlier was ready to close the proceeding 
because of their settlement and now she is 
withdrawing her affidavit. Therefore, in such an 
eventuality, he may be permitted to file the objection 
since the matter is now required to be decided on its 
own merit.8. Let the respondent may file their counter 
affidavit within three weeks.
9. One week thereafter is granted to the applicant 
to file rejoinder affidavit.
10. List in the week commencing 26.10.2026.
11. In the meantime, further proceeding of Criminal 
Case No. 1248 of 2026 ‘State vs. Amit Kumar’ pending 
in the court of learned Addl. Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar arising out of 
Case Crime No. 0511 of 2025 is stayed.
