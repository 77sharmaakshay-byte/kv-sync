 Mr. N.S. Pundir, Deputy Advocate

 Heard. 
 Though the submission of the 
learned counsel for the petitioner is that 
the controversy involved in the present 
petition is squarely covered by the 
Judgment given by the full Bench of 
this Court in WPSB No. 284 of 2004, 
Madan Mohan Chaudhary Vs. State of 
Uttaranchal and others,’ reported in 
2011 (1) U.D. 6; but, this is denied by 
the learned State Counsel. 
 Learned Deputy Advocate General 
appearing for the State submitted that 
he may be given two weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit. 
 As prayed, two weeks’ and no more 
time is granted to the respondents to 
file counter affidavit. 
 List this matter on 16.07.2018, 
immediately after fresh cases.
