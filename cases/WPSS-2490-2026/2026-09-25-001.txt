Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Sumit Joshi and Mr. M.S. Bhandari, learned 
counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Maneesh Bisht, learned B.H. for the State.
3. Mr. Sagar Malara, learned counsel holding brief of 
Mr. Yogesh Kumar Pacholia, learned counsel for N.C.T.E.
4. By means of present writ petition, petitioner has 
challenged the impugned Termination Order dated 
07.05.2026 and impugned Relieving Order dated 12.05.2026 
issued by respondent nos.4 and 5, whereby services of the 
petitioner were terminated. 
5. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that petitioner was appointed as an Assistant Teacher 
(Primary). The petitioner was in possession of Bachelor 
Degree in Science (Botany, Chemistry and Zoology). The 
petitioner secured 45.61% marks in graduation. 
6. The services of the petitioner were terminated on the 
ground that the petitioner’s marks are below 50% in 
graduation. 
7. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that in view of the notification issued by respondent no.6-
NCTE dated 13.11.2019, for the candidates who have taken 
admission in B.Ed. course prior to 29.07.2011, the 
condition of securing 50% marks in graduation will not be 
applicable. 2
8. It is contended by learned counsel for petitioner that 
the petitioner did graduation and B.Ed. course prior to 2011
and thus, the condition of having 50% marks in graduation 
would not be attracted in case of the petitioner. 
9. Having perused the writ petition and having 
appreciated the contention raised by learned counsel for the 
parties, this Court finds substance in the submission made 
by learned counsel for the petitioner. 
10. In such view of the matter, in the peculiar facts and 
circumstances of the case, petitioner deserves protection 
from this Court. 
11. In view of the above, it is directed that till the next date 
of listing, effect and operation of the impugned 
communication/orders dated 07.05.2026 and 12.05.2026 
passed by respondent nos.4 and 5, shall remain stayed. 
12. The respondent(s) may file their counter affidavit(s) in 
the matter within six weeks. 
13. List this case on 27.11.2026.
