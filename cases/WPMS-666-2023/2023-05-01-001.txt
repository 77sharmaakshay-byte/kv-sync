Mr. Pulak Agarwal, Advocate, for

Mr. Vikramaditya Shah, Advocate, 
holding brief of Mr. Shankar Aggarwal, 
Advocate, for respondent No. 1.
The challenge given by the present 
petitioner in the writ petition is to the 
judgment dated 17.02.2022, as rendered by 
the Permanent Lok Adalat, Dehradun, in 
Case No. 3134 of 2021, Smt. Khushboo Vs 
H.D.F.C. Ergo General Insurance Co. and 
another.
The argument raised by the learned 
counsel for the petitioner is that the 
Permanent Lok Adalat, didn’t had the 
jurisdiction to decide the matter on merits, 
in view of the provisions contained under 
sub-Section (8) of Section 22C of the Act, 
because, until and unless there happens to 
be a prior settlement, the Permanent Lok 
Adalat, cannot venture into and decide the 
controversy on its own merit, which 
otherwise, is a responsibility to be 
discharged by the Court before whom the 
principal proceedings are drawn and 
pending consideration. But, still by virtue of 
the impugned judgment dated 17.12.2022, 
the Permanent Lok Adalat, Dehradun, has 
awarded an amount of Rs. 13,87,997/-.
The respondent, who has put in appearance through caveator may file his 
counter affidavit within the period of three 
weeks from today.
Let notice be issued to respondent 
No. 2.
Steps to serve respondent No. 2 
would be taken by the petitioner within a 
period of one week from today.
Till the next date of listing, the 
impugned judgment dated 17.12.2022 
would be kept in abeyance, subject to the 
condition that the petitioner deposits 50% 
of the awarded amount before the Registry 
of this Court within a period of six weeks 
from today.
List after service of notice on the 
respondent No. 2, or upon filing of counter 
affidavit.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
