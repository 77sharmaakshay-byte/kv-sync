 Heard. 
 Admit. 
 Issue notice to respondent nos.3, fixing

 Steps to be taken within three days. 
 This Court is not inclined to issue 
notice to respondent no.4 at this stage. 
 Also heard on the stay application. 
 It is contended that the respondent 
no.3 submitted her resignation which was 
accepted by the Committee of Management 
(present petitioner) and in regard thereto 
communication was sent to respondent 
no.2, who vide impugned order dated 
25.10.2019, revoked the resignation of 
respondent no.3. 
 It is settled position in law that once 
resignation has been accepted, the same 
cannot be revoked at any rate. That being 
the position, it is directed that the effect 
and operation of impugned order dated 
25.10.2019 shall remained stayed, until 
further orders of this Court. 
 Interim relief application CLMA 
No.16733 of 2019 is disposed of.
