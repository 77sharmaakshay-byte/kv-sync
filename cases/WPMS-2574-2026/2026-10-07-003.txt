2. Section 133-A of Uttarakhand Zamindari 
Abolition and Land Reforms Act, 1950 reads as 
under:
“133-A. Government lessees. – Every 
person to whom land has been let out by the 
State Government shall be called a Government 
lessee in respect of such land and shall, 
notwithstanding anything to the contrary 
contained in this Act, be entitled to hold the same 
in accordance with the terms and conditions of 
the lease relating thereto.”
3. Land allotted to the petitioner under 
Government Grants Act has been directed to be 
vested in the State by the impugned order on the 
ground that allottee transferred the holding in favour of some other person in violation of the 
terms of allotment order.
4. This Court directed the petitioner, vide 
order dated 16.09.2026 to bring on record the 
order of allotment made under Government 
Grants Act. Thereafter, on petitioner’s request, 
further time was given to petitioner for this 
purpose vide order dated 28.09.2026.
5. Today again, counsel for the petitioner 
seeks more time for bringing on record the order 
of allotment passed under Government Grants 
Act. On his request, two weeks and no more is 
given to petitioner for complying with the order 
dated 16.09.2026.
6. List this case on 02.11.2026.
7. In the event, petitioner fails to bring on 
record the allotment order, then an adverse 
inference shall be drawn against him and it shall 
be presumed that there was absolute prohibition 
on transfer of land by the allottee, to any other 
person.
