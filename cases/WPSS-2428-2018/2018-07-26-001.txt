 
WPSS No.2428 of 2018 
Hon’ble V.K. Bist, J.
Mr. P.C. Petshali, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. N.S. Pundir, Deputy Advocate 
General alongwith Mr. Devesh 
Ghildiyal, Brief Holder for the State of 
Uttarakhand. 
Heard learned counsel for the 
parties. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
relied on the judgment passed by 
coordinate Bench of this Court in 
WPSS No.1152/2016 & connected 
petitions, decided on 28.03.2017, by 
submitting that termination order 
could not be passed without providing 
opportunity of hearing. 
In my view, opportunity of 
hearing must be provided to the 
regular employee before terminating 
his services. I find that in this case, 
allegation is that, on the basis of 
forged documents appointment has 
obtained, therefore, I permit the 
petitioner to file supplementary 
affidavit, annexing therein the copy of 
documents on which petitioner got 
appointment. 
In case, it is found that 
petitioner’s documents are genuine, 
the writ petition will be allowed by 
imposing cost on the respondents, but 
at the same time, if it is found that 
documents of the petitioner are forged, in that event, cost shall be imposed on 
the petitioner also. 
As prayed, six weeks’ time is 
granted to the State counsel to file 
counter affidavit. 
List this matter after six weeks. 
 (V.K. Bist, J.)
