Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
 Mr. Nikhil Singhal, learned counsel for writ 
petitioner. 
2. Petitioner challenges judgment and decree 
dated 19.09.2017 (Annexure No.1) passed by 
learned Judge, SCC Court/Civil Judge (S.D.), 
Roorkee (Haridwar) in SCC Suit No.14 of 2013, 
Surendra Kr. v. Furkan Ahmed, as also judgment 
and order dated 20.10.2023 (Annexure No.2) 
passed by learned Second Addl. District Judge, 
Roorkee (Haridwar) in SCC Revision No.11 of 
2017, Furkan Ahmed v. Surendra Kumar. 
3. It is the contention of learned counsel for 
petitioner that while deciding the revision 
petition moved by petitioner-defendant, the 
Court below has committed a manifest illegality 
by treating the revision to have been filed u/s 
115 C.P.C., although it was filed u/s 25 of the 
Provincial Small Cause Courts Act, 1887. 
4. It is argued that since the revision u/s 25 
of the Provincial Small Cause Courts Act, 1887 
is immediately after trial, the view of the Apex 
Court as well as of various High Courts, on the 
matter, is that such revision would be heard, 
more or less, like an appeal, and the Court 
hearing SCC revision would, accordingly, be 
enjoined to re-appreciate and re-assess the 
evidence brought about by the parties. 2
5. I have gone through the impugned 
judgment and order passed by the Second Addl. 
District Judge, Roorkee. It is clear on its perusal 
that the revision has been treated as if filed u/s 
115 Cr.P.C. 
6. In such view of matter, issue notices to the 
un-served respondents returnable within six 
weeks. 
7. List on 03.04.2023. 
8. In the meantime, the execution pursuant 
to the judgment and decree dated 19.09.2017 
passed by the Judge, SCC/Civil Judge (S.D.), 
Roorkee, Haridwar in SCC Suit No.14 of 2013, 
Surendra Kumar v. Furkan Ahmed, shall remain 
stayed till the next date of listing. 
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
