3. By the instant petition, the applicant is 
challenging the proceeding under Section 138 
of N.I. Act only on the ground that the notice 
in terms of Section 138 of N.I. Act is beyond 
the period as stipulated under the Proviso (b)
of Section 138 of N.I. Act. In support of such 
submission he placed reliance of decision of 
Hon’ble Supreme Court in the case of M/s 
Rahul Builders vs. M/s Arihant Fertilizers & 
Chemical & Another 2008(2) SCC 321
wherein it is held that unless a notice is 
served in conformity with Proviso (b) of 
Section 138 of N.I. Act, the complainant 
would not be maintainable.
