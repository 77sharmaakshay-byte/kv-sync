2. Mr. Siddhartha Singh, learned counsel &

3. By the instant petition, the petitioners 
are praying for the following reliefs:-
“(i) A writ or order in the nature of certiorari to 
quash/set-aside the impugned judgment and 
order dated 03.02.2026 (Annexure No.1, Page 
No. 17 to 29) passed in revision no. 14/2024-
25 "Ashish Tiwari & others Vs. Shri Shambhu 
Suman & Others" by the learned Director of 
Consolidation, Uttarakhand, Dehradun.
(ii) Any other relief to the petitioners which this 
Hon'ble Court may deem fit and proper.
(iii) Award the cost of petition to the 
petitioners.”
4. After arguing at length, Mr. Siddhartha 
Singh, Advocate, submitted that the matter 
may be posted on 02.05.2026.
5. List on 02.05.2026 as a fresh case.
6. In the meantime, the effect and 
operation of the dated 03.02.2026, passed by 
Director of Consolidation, Dehradun in 
Revision No.14/2024-2025, “Ashish Tiwari 
and Others vs. Shri Shambhu Suman and 
Others” shall remain stayed.
