5. This Court vide order dated 01.05.2024 
directed the learned Standing Counsel for 
the State of U.P. to file counter affidavit.
6. Learned counsel for the State of U.P. 
fairly submits that despite writing a letter to 
the respondent i.e. D.M. Rampur, no one is 
responding and for that reason the counter 
affidavit could not be filed. 
7. The petitioner’s case as reflected from 
the writ petition is that he was posted in a 
regular establishment as Chainman (Class￾IV post in the Survey Department) and was 
posted at Kichha in the year 1994; petitioner 
was transferred from Garh-Mukteshwar to 
Kichha in the year 1994-95.
8. In the reliving order of 78 employees
who were transferred from Garh￾Mukteshwar to Kichha, the name of the 
petitioner Furkan Ahmed was not found. 
But the respondents no. 1 to 3 have notannexed the entire list of 78 employees who 
had been transferred from Garh￾Mukteshwar to Kichha. Instead of 
mentioning the names of employees who had 
joined at Kichha, name of 25 employees who 
have not joined, are mentioned. This 
information does not clarify the things. 
Accordingly, the State of Uttarakhand shall 
file the list of those persons who had joined 
pursuant to their transfer from Garh￾Mukteshwar to Kichha.
9. Apart from that, learned counsel for 
the State of U.P. as well as the State of 
Uttarakhand are directed to bring on record 
the list of those 78 employees who had been 
transferred in the year 1994-95.
10. It is expected that officers at the 
highest level of both the States will 
coordinate and submit this information 
before this Court within three weeks.
