In order to better elucidate the 
controversy involved in the writ petition,
because of the fact that petitioner having been 
appointed in the State is a fact which has been 
denied by the State’s Standing Counsel. So far 
as the status of initial appointment of the 
petitioner is concerned i.e. w.e.f. 01.01.1983, 
there is no material on record to show that the 
petitioner was initially and actually appointed 
on 01.01.1983, to which the learned counsel for 
the petitioner prays for that he may be granted 
three weeks’ time to place the letter of 
appointment of the petitioner on record, after 
procuring the same under the Right to 
Information Act and during this period the State 
of U.P. too would file the counter affidavit, 
bringing complete facts on record.
List this matter after three weeks.
As far as IA/15071/2021, filed by the 
respondents in reply to the rejoinder affidavit of 
the petitioner, the same is allowed. 
Supplementary counter affidavit is taken on 
record.
