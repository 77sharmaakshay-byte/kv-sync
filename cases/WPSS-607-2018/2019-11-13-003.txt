 By means of the writ petition, petitioner 
has prayed for the following reliefs: 
 (i) Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing the respondent nos. 
1,2,3 & 6 to provide the service benefits to the 
petitioner considering his status at this juncture. 
 (ii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus, directing the respondent 
nos. 1, 6 & 7 to produce the documents of final 
allocation for examination of the status of the 
petitioner service and its employer.” 
 Perusal of the record would reveal that 
petitioner has not filed any document in order to 
substantiate his claim that he is appointed in 
the Department. 
 In theses circumstances, petitioner is 
permitted to file supplementary affidavit in this 
regard within 15 days. 
 List thereafter. 
 No good ground is made out to grant any 
interim relief to the petitioner at this stage. 
Interim relief application is dismissed.
