 Heard.
 Learned counsel for the petitioners 
at the very outset submits that it is a 
case of recruitment. Therefore, State’s 
counter affidavit is necessary to be 
filed, which has yet not been filed. He 
also submits that still there are many 
vacancies, which could be filled up by 
the Uttarakhand Subordinate Service 
Selection Commission (“the 
Commission”), but the Commission is 
not filling it. 
 This is rebutted by the learned 
counsel for the Commission saying that 
there are no vacancies available under 
the category to which the petitioners belong to.
 Let State as well as the Commission 
file a short affidavit on the assertion as 
made today, within 10 days. 
 State in their counter affidavit 
should specifically indicates that what 
were the requisition, particularly which 
positions were identified under 
Physically Handicapped Category and 
what were the further classification in 
those categories.
 A week thereafter, response to it, 
may be filed by the petitioners.
