WPSS No.1021/2017 
Hon’ble Rajiv Sharma, J.
Mr. H.C. Joshi, Advocate, for the petitioners. 
Mr. K.N. Joshi, Dy. A.G., for the State. 
Petitioners had prayed for a direction 
commanding the respondents to make 
payment of dearness allowance to the 
petitioners from the date when it was stopped 
along with prevailing rate of interest. A 
further writ of mandamus was sought 
commanding the respondents to pay arrears 
of minimum of revised pay scale and grade 
pay as per recommendation of Sixth Pay 
Commission as being paid to similarly 
situated regularly appointed employees in the 
Department. 
By judgment dated 12.5.2017 passed by 
this Court, the petition was allowed and the 
petitioners were ordered to be granted the 
minimum of pay scale in terms of the 
judgment dated 23.3.2017 rendered by this 
Court in WPSS No.1721 of 2014 and 
analogous matters. 
However, inadvertently, the petitioners 
were not granted the benefit of Dearness 
Allowance in terms of judgment dated 
23.3.2017 passed in WPSS No.495 of 2014 
and analogous matters, though specifically 
prayed for. 
Accordingly, the modification application 
(MCC 534/17) is allowed. The petitioners are 
also held entitled to the payment of Dearness 
Allowance as per the ratio of the judgment 
rendered by this Court on 23.3.2017 in WPSS 
No.495 of 2014 and analogous matters, which 
shall be released in favour of the petitioners 
within six months from today. Judgment 
dated 12.5.2017 shall stand modified to the 
above extent only. 
 
(Rajiv Sharma, J.)
