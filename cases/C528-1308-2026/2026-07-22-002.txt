5. Applicant Smt. Amrit Pal Singh and her 
husband Anmol Pal Singh were called in 
person in order to settle their dispute since the 
dispute appears to be a matrimonial one. 
6. Both of them were married in the year 
2012 and there are two children one son and 
one daughter; and elder one is son; both are 
studying; and there is no source of income, 
however the applicant no.1 is residing in the 
house, owned by her father-in-law, having four 
rooms, which she is using for the residential 
purposes where she is living with her two 
minor children. Earlier an ex-parte divorce 
decree was passed and during the enforcement 
of decree, the respondent/husband got 
remarry with another lady. Subsequently a 
recall application was moved and the ex-parte
divorce decree was recalled and the matter is 
still pending for consideration before the concerned family court. In the meantime the 
applicant initiated proceeding under the 
Protection of Women From Domestic Violence 
Act, 2005 (for short D.V. Act.) and now the 
instant petition has been moved under Section 
528 BNS, 2023 challenging the order dated 
08.04.2026 passed in Criminal Appeal No.66 of 
2025. 
7. Both the parties are called for their 
amicable settlement but the face remain that 
the respondent/husband now re-marry with 
another lady so in such situation, it is very 
difficult for this Court to restore their 
matrimonial life and this is completely a 
discretion of the wife whether in such a 
situation when the respondent/husband 
remarry with another lady, she is comfortable 
on restoration of her matrimonial life with her 
husband and; she submits that it is not 
possible. Now the question is there are two 
minor children of the applicant from the 
marriage with the respondent/husband, 
therefore, in such an eventuality, this Court 
have to focus on the future of the two minor 
child. 
8. The detail of the assets of the 
respondent/husband is placed on record by 
learned counsel for the applicant. He also 
submits that the respondent/husband is 
running two factories and two residential 
colonies have also been developed by him in 
Rudrapur. Apart from this, 10 properties, he 
has gifted in the name of his brother and 
mother after 2023. There is no denial that both 
the children are from the marriage of the 
applicant with the respondent, therefore, the 
respondent being the father of two minor children, is under legal obligations under the 
law to ensure the future of two minor child. 
Furthermore, the divorce petition is pending 
for adjudication though earlier the divorce 
decree was passed ex-parte but as on date the 
divorce proceeding is still going on, therefore, 
as on date the applicant Smt. Amrit Pal Singh 
is legally wedded wife of the 
respondent/husband, therefore, since the 
applicant is the legally wedded wife of the 
respondent/husband he is further under the 
legal obligation to maintain his wife also. Since 
the details of certain assets has been placed on 
record by the applicant. 
9. Mr. Navneet Kaushik, learned counsel 
for the applicant submits that he will also file 
additional affidavit giving details of the other 
assets. 
10. Let the husband/respondent may file his 
affidavit with regard to the details of the assets 
and also proposed what steps he will take to 
secure the future of his minor children, within 
a week.
11. Put up this matter on 04.08.2026 on top of 
the board. On that day both the parties shall 
also remain present in Court.
