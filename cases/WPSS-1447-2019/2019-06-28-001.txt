 Heard. 
 By means of the present writ petition, 
petitioners have prayed for the following 
relief: 
“(i) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature 
of mandamus commanding the respondents to 
make payment of non-productivity link bonus 
(Ad-hoc Bonus) to the petitioners for the period 
of 2017-18 and 2018-19. 
 (ii) Issue a writ, order or direction quashing the 
recovery order dated 19th July 2018 passed by 
respondent no.1 and the recovery order dated 
12.06.2019 passed by respondent no.3.” 
 Admit. 
 Four weeks’ time is granted to learned 
counsel for the respondents to file counter 
affidavit. 
 List thereafter. 
 Also heard on stay application. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that the impugned order has 
been passed by the respondent for recovery 
of the amount, which was paid to the 
petitioners as bonus in the year 2015-16 and 
2016-17. He would further submit that there 
is no misrepresentation or fraud on the part 
of the petitioners, as such the recovery from 
the petitioners is illegal and cannot be 
sustained in the eyes of law. 
 He would place reliance upon a 
judgment of Hon’ble Apex Court rendered in 
the case of State of Punjab & Others Vs. 
Rafiq Masih (White Washer) reported in 
(2015) 4 SCC 334 and submit that 
petitioners have worked on Group C posts, 
thus, it would be impermissible as per the law laid down by their Lordships of the 
Hon’ble Supreme Court to recover the 
amount, which has already been paid to the 
them. 
 Having heard learned counsel for the 
petitioner and in view of the dictum of 
Hon’ble Apex Court in the case of Rafiq 
Masih (supra), it is directed that no recovery 
shall be made from the petitioners in 
pursuance of the impugned orders. 
 Stay application stands disposed of.
