Mr. Lalit Singh Sammal, learned counsel for the

2. Mr. Ashish Joshi, learned counsel for the 
respondents-Bank (appeared through V.C.).
3. Petitioner’s mother purchased five FDs, enumerated 
in paragraph 6 of the writ petition, from the respondent￾Bank Branch at Khatima, District U.S. Nagar. The 
petitioner’s mother died, and petitioner has obtained a 
succession certificate in respect of the aforesaid FDs from 
the Court of Civil Judge (S.D.), Khatima, District Udham 
Singh Nagar. Despite the succession certificate, the 
aforesaid FDs have not been released in favour of the 
petitioner.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that respondent-Bank is insisting upon the petitioner to 
obtain a succession certificate for the locker as well, and 
it is only thereafter, the aforesaid FDs be released in 
favour of the petitioner. He further contends that 
petitioner has already applied for a succession certificate 
in respect of the locker before the Court of District Judge, 
Rudrapur, Udham Singh Nagar. 
5. On the face of it, demand raised by the respondent￾Bank appears to be illegal. Once the succession certificate 
has been obtained by the petitioner in respect of the 
aforesaid FDs, the FDs cannot be withheld.

three days’ time to get instructions in the matter.
