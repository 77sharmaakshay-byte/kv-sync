COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRJA No. 1 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. V.S. Pal, learned A.G.A. for the State.
2. It is a criminal jail appeal preferred on behalf of 
the appellant, who has been convicted by the Special 
Session Judge (POCSO) Pithoragarh in Special Session 
Trial No. 15 of 2023 by judgment and order dated 
14.10.2024 by which he has been convicted for the
offences punishable under Sections 363, 366 and 376 
IPC. For the offence punishable under Section 363 the 
appellant has been convicted with 7 years R.I. and fine 
of Rs. 20,000/-; for the offence under Section 366 he has 
been convicted with sentence of 10 years R.I. and fine 
of Rs. 20,000/- and also for the offence punishable 
under Section 376 of IPC he has been convicted with 
sentence of 10 years R.I. and fine of Rs. 40,000/-.
3. As per the office report there is a delay of 24 days 
and since it is a jail appeal against the conviction, 
therefore, the learned A.G.A. V.S. Pal have not 
seriously opposed the delay, accordingly the delay is 
condoned.
4. Admit. 
5. Summon the Trial Court record.
6. Mr. Siddharth Manral is appointed as an Amicus 
Curiae on behalf of the appellant in order to assist this 
court. 
7. The Registry is directed to supply the paper book 
of this appeal to the learned Amicus Curiae Mr. 
Siddharth Manral.
8. List in the last week of February, 2025.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
