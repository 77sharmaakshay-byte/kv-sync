2. Learned counsel for the petitioners would 
submit that the defect raised by the Registry is 
that pages no.85 to 88 are not legible, which is 
the evidence of the complainant before the 
District Consumer Disputes Redressal Forum, 
Haridwar. He would further submit that in order 
to cure this defect, an application was given
before the Forum for supplying the certified copy
of said documents but the application was not 
accepted and the petitioner was orally told that 
the file is not available. He would submit that he 
is ready to file an affidavit to this effect.
3 Issue show cause notice to the Presiding 
Officer, District Consumer Disputes Redressal 
Forum, Haridwar to explain why the application
for supplying the certified copies is not being 
accepted.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioners would 
submit that this is the writ petition against the 
judgment of the National Consumer Disputes 
Redressal Commission dated 06.11.2023 (in 
short, “National Commission”) whereby the 
National Commission allowed the revision of the 
respondent/complainant and set aside the order of 
State Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission
(in short, “State Commission”) and also affirmed 
the order passed by District Consumer Disputes 
Redressal Forum (in short, “District Forum”) wherein the petitioners were directed to pay an 
amount of ` 6,20,648/- along with interest @ 6% 
per annum from the date of filing of the 
complaint till its realization. 
5. He would further submit that against the 
impugned order dated 06.11.2023 initially 
petitioners had filed a petition before the Hon’ble 
High Court of Delhi, however, vide order dated 
05.07.2024, same was withdrawn with liberty to 
file the petition before this Court since this High 
Court exercises its jurisdiction over the State 
Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission.
6. He would further submit that in the petition 
filed before the Hon’ble High Court of Delhi, 
said High Court was pleased to grant stay in 
favour of the petitioners subject to deposit of the 
principal amount of Rs.6,25,000/- within four 
weeks from today before the District Forum, 
Haridwar (Annexure-10). 
7. He would further submit that this amount 
was duly deposited by the petitioners within time, 
which fact was also recorded by the High Court 
of Delhi in para-6 of its order dated 05.07.2024 
(Annexre-11) that “the petitioner has already 
deposited the principal amount with the 
concerned District Forum” and thereafter in 
para-7 it was observed that “Such fact is not
disputed even by learned counsel for respondent 
and, therefore, it is made clear that for a period 
of six weeks from today, the execution petition 
shall remain stayed.”
8. Issue notices to the respondent through 
ordinary process and registered post, 
acknowledgement due as well as by email and 
WhatsApp, if available.
9. Steps to be taken within two weeks. 10. Put up on 07.11.2024. 
11. Till the next date of listing, the effect and 
operation of the impugned orders dated 
06.11.2023 and 29.05.2008 shall remain stayed. 
12. Interim relief application (IA No.1 of 2024) 
stands disposed of.
