COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 2041 of 2024
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J
1. Mr. Mayank Pandey, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Vijay Kumar, learned counsel for 
the respondent. 
3. In compliance of the order dated 
06.03.2024 passed by High Court of Delhi, 
petitioner has deposited a sum of ₹6.25 
lakh before the District Consumer Disputes 
Redressal Commission, Haridwar. 
Respondent has prayed to release the 
said amount in his favour on the ground 
that his daughter is suffering from 4th Stage 
Cancer and he needs money for her 
treatment. 
4. Mr. Mayank Pandey, learned counsel 
for the petitioner opposed the prayer for 
release of entire amount, however, he 
concedes for 50 percent of the amount be
released in favour of petitioner. 
5. For the reasons stated in the money 
withdrawal application, the same (IA No. 2 
of 2025) is partly allowed. 
6. District Consumer Disputes Redressal 
Commission, Haridwar is directed to release 
₹3.00 lakh in favour of respondent/applicant 
and the remaining amount shall be kept in 
interest bearing security.
