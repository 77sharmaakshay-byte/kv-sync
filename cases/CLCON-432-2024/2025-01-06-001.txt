2. By means of the present contempt petition filed under Section 12 
of the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971, it is alleged by the petitioners that 
the respondents have wilfully and deliberately committed the contempt 
of this Court’s order dated 20.06.2024, passed in WPSS No.1790 of 
2022, Arjun Singh Vs. Uttarakhand Transport Corporation and others, 
wherein the writ petition was decided in terms of judgment and order 
dated 14.06.2022 passed by Co-ordinate Bench of this Court in WPSS
