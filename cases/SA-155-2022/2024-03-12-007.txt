COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
12.03.2024 SA No.155 of 2022 
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The proposed Second Appeal has been 
filed along with an Application to condone the 
delay of 5749 days. 
2. Heard Mr. Vikas Kumar Guglani, learned 
counsel for the proposed appellants and Mr. 
R.P. Nautiyal, learned Senior Advocate, assisted 
by Ms. Garima Thapa, learned counsel for 
respondent no.1-Rajendra Singh on Delay 
Condonation Application (IA No.2 of 2022)
3. Notice has been served on respondent 
no.2. 
4. A cross Second Appeal has been filed by 
respondent no.1. The said Second Appeal (SA 
No.8 of 2005) is still pending. 
5. Mr. Vikas Kumar Guglani, Advocate, 
appearing for the proposed appellants,
submitted that the appellants’ father was 
contesting the Second Appeal No.8 of 2005 as a 
sole respondent. Respondent-Mangal Singh, 
father of the appellants, died on 16.02.2020. 
Thereafter, the present proposed appellants
were substituted in Second Appeal No.8 of 
2005. Then, it has come to the knowledge to 
the proposed appellants that the appellants are 
required to file a Second Appeal. 
6. Mr. R.P. Nautiyal, Senior Advocate, 
contended that the delay has not been properly 
explained. 7. The rules of the procedure are made to 
advance the cause of justice and not to defeat 
it.
8. Having regard to the totality of the facts 
and circumstances of the case, this Court is 
inclined to hold that the cause shown by the 
proposed appellants for condoning the delay in 
filing the Second Appeal, is a sufficient cause 
within the meaning of Section 5 of the 
Limitation Act, 1963.
9. Therefore, the application filed for delay 
condonation deserves to be condoned. The 
Delay Condonation Application (2 of 2022) is 
allowed. Delay is condoned. 
10. List along with SA No.8 of 2005 on 
21.03.2024 for argument on Admission. 
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
