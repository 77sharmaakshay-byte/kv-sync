COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
04.11.2022 MCC No. 1 of 2021 (Restoration Application) and
IA No. 2 of 2021 (Delay Condonation Application in 
Restoration Application)
in
AO No. 564 of 2015
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
An Appal (A.O. No.564 of 2015) was 
filed against the Award dated 
31.07.2015, passed by the learned Motor 
Accident Claims Tribunal, Dehradun, by 
which, the claim petition was dismissed. 
On 27.04.2019, none was present to 
press the said Appeal. Therefore, the said 
Appeal was dismissed for want of 
prosecution. 
Heard Mr. Syed Nadim, the learned 
counsel assisted by Mr. Bhupendra 
Prasad, learned counsel for the applicant￾appellant, Mr. M.K. Goyal, the learned 
counsel for the respondent no. 3-
Insurance Company, Mr. Prabhat Pande, 
the learned counsel for the respondent 
no. 4 and Mr. N.S. Pundir, learned 
counsel for the respondent no. 6 on Delay 
Condonation Application (No. 2 of 2021)
and the Restoration Application (No. 1 of 
2021).
Even availing sufficient opportunity 
to file objection to the Delay Condonation 
Application, no written objection has been 
filed by the Insurance Company. 
However, the learned counsel appearing 
for the Insurance Company has opposed 
the said Applications orally.
The learned counsel for the 
applicant-appellant submitted that the 
counsel for the appellant could not mark 
the date of the said Appeal, therefore, 
the said Appeal was dismissed in default 
of the appellant. The counsel of the 
applicant informed the applicant, but due 
to personal difficulty and thereafter, due 
to spread of Covid-19 Pandemic, the Restoration Application could not been 
filed within time. 
The Delay Condonation Application 
has been filed along with an affidavit of 
the applicant-appellant. The said affidavit 
is unrebutted. 
In the facts and circumstances of the 
case, this Court is inclined to hold that 
the cause shown by the applicant for 
condoning the delay is a sufficient cause
within the meaning of Section 5 of the 
Limitation Act, 1963.
Therefore, in the interest of justice, 
the application for Condonation of Delay 
deserves to be allowed, however, on the 
cost of Rs. 1,000/- (Rs. One thousand) to 
compensate the respondent no. 3. 
Accordingly, the delay in filing the said 
Restoration Application is condoned.
The Restoration Application (No. 1 of 
2021) is allowed.
The order dated 27.04.2019 is 
recalled.
The Appeal (No.564 of 2015) is re￾admitted on its original number. 
List this case on 12.12.2022 for final 
hearing.
(Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
