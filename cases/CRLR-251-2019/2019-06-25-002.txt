After having heard the learned counsel for 
the parties on the Delay Condonation Application 
No. 1195/2019, which has been preferred by the 
revisionists, wherein the revisionist has challenged 
the order dated 01.02.2019, as passed by the 
learned Family Judge, Haridwar in Criminal Case 
No. 280 of 2013, Smt. Divya Sharma and Others v. 
Satendra Kumar Sharma under Section 125 
Cr.P.C.
The present revision has been filed along 
with delay of 6 days and the Delay Condonation 
Application has also been filed by the revisionist in 
support thereof.
After considering the reasons, which have 
been given in the Delay Condonation Application 
and also considering the period of delay, this Court 
is of the view that the delay, which has chanced in 
preferring the present revision, deserves to be 
condoned. Coupled with the fact that as far as the 
question of delay is concerned and since the same 
is not being seriously opposed by the respondents, 
hence the Delay Condonation Application stands 
allowed and the delay which has chanced in filing 
the present criminal revision would stand 
condoned. 
The learned counsel for respondent No. 2 
prays for and is granted two weeks’ time to file his 
objection in the revision. 
Looking to the family condition, the learned 
counsel for the parties have expressed that there 
could be a possibility of an amicable settlement 
between the parties, as the two children are also being affected because of the dispute between the 
husband and wife. 
Hence, put up this matter on 13.07.2019 
before the Lok Adalat so that an effort for 
mediation may be made by the Mediators.
The learned counsel for the parties would 
ensure that they supplied the necessary information 
to their respective clients to ensure their presence 
on 13.07.2019 before the Lok Adalat.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
