27.02.2024 CLR No.11 of 2024 
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Present SCC Revision has been filed 
under Section 25 of the Provincial Small 
Cause Courts Act, 1887 against the 
judgment and decree dated 09.01.2024, 
passed by learned Judge, Small Cause
Court/IInd Additional District Judge, 
Roorkee, District Haridwar in SCC Suit 
No.15 of 2018, by which, the learned 
Judge has decreed the suit for eviction, 
outstanding rent and damages. 
2. Mr. Nagesh Aggarwal, learned 
counsel for revisionists. 
3. Mr. Deepak Sharma, learned 
counsel for respondent through video 
conferencing. 
4. Mr. Nagesh Aggarwal, Advocate, 
contended that the revisionist-defendant 
is the tenant of the respondent-plaintiff over the suit property on the rent of 
Rs.250/- per month. The respondent has 
claimed rent at the rate of Rs.2500/- per 
month. However, there is no evidence on 
record to support the said claim. 
5. Mr. Nagesh Aggarwal, Advocate, 
further submitted that the provisions of 
the Uttar Pradesh Urban Buildings 
(Regulation of Letting, Rent and Eviction) 
Act, 1972 (as applicable in the State of 
Uttarakhand) are applicable in the 
present matter.
6. Admit. 
7. List on 20.05.2024.
8. Heard on the Stay Application (IA 
No.01 of 2024). 
9. Till the next date of listing, the 
effect and operation of the impugned 
judgment and decree are stayed, subject 
to the condition that the revisionists 
deposit the outstanding rent/damages at the rate of Rs.250/- per month from 
01.06.2015 till 29.02.2024 within a 
period of four weeks’ from today and
continue to deposit the regular 
rent/damages at the rate of Rs.250/- per 
month by the seventh day of every 
month. 
10. It is made clear that in any event of 
failure to comply with any of the 
conditions, the interim order would 
automatically stands vacated. 
11. The Stay Application (IA No.01 of 
2024) is disposed of accordingly.
