4. In the rejoinder-affidavit filed by the petitioner in 
reply to the counter-affidavit of the Commission, it is stated 
that till the DPC met on 28.06.2022, no charge-sheet was 
served on the petitioner and the minutes of the DPC itself 
records that departmental action is proposed against the 
petitioner. It is further stated that charge-sheet was 
served on the petitioner at 04:15 PM on 28.06.2022, after 
the DPC meeting had already concluded in the forenoon.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner places reliance on 
the judgments of the Supreme Court in “Union of India 
and others vs. K.V. Jankiraman & others, (1991) 4 
SCC 109 and Bank of India and another vs. Degala 
Suryanarayana, (1999) 5 SCC 762” in contending that 
if, by the time the DPC met, charge-sheet had not been 
served, the sealed cover proceeding was wrongly adopted 
and the petitioners should now be promoted on the basis of
recommendation made by the DPC.6. Learned State Counsel seeks time to file counter￾affidavit on behalf of the State- respondents and reply to 
the case set-up by the petitioner in the rejoinder-affidavit 
filed by him in response to the counter-affidavit of the 
Commission.
7. As prayed, two weeks’ time is granted for the purpose 
to learned State Counsel.
8. List immediately thereafter.
9. Learned counsel for the petitioner shall provide a 
copy of the rejoinder-affidavit of the petitioner filed in 
response to the counter-affidavit of the Commission to 
learned State Counsel during course of the day.
