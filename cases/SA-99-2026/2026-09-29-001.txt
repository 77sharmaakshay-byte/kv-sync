Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Devang Dobhal, learned counsel for the 
applicant.
2. The instant second appeal has been preferred by 
the appellant/defendant ‘Bhagwati Prasad’ against the 
judgment and decree dated 27.07.2026 passed by the 
learned District Judge, Uttarkashi, in Civil Appeal No. 
20 of 2023 ‘Janardan Prasad vs. Bhagwati Prasad’.
3. Brief facts of the case are that a suit was filed by 
the respondent/plaintiff for permanent injunction as 
well as for demolition of boundary wall and shed 
falling in Khasra No. 1233 measuring 0.003 hectare. 
The suit was dismissed by the learned Civil Judge, 
Uttarkashi, by judgment and order dated 19.08.2023 on 
one of the ground that there is no compliance of Order 
VII Rule 3 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 1908, since 
the property is not identifiable and even the 
boundaries have not been specified in the plaint.
4. Being aggrieved with the judgment passed by 
the learned Civil Judge the plaintiff went in appeal 
before the learned District Judge by means of Civil 
Appeal No. 20 of 2023 but the finding of the trial court 
is reversed, which is reflected from para-20 of the 
judgment under challenge. Prima-facie on perusal of 
the plaint the suit property is not identifiable and the 
learned Civil Judge rightly dismissed the suit, 
however, the learned District Judge reversed its 
finding without scrutinizing the plaint. Hence, the 
appeal is admitted on the following substantial 
question of law:“Whether the learned Appellate Court was right 
in allowing the appeal by reversing the finding 
of learned Civil Judge while dismissing the suit 
that there is no compliance of Order VII Rule 3 of 
CPC since the suit property is not identifiable.”
5. Issue notice to the respondent.
6. Steps within a week.
7. Summon the lower court record.
8. List in the week commencing 26.10.2026.
9. In the meantime, the impugned judgment and 
decree passed by the learned District Judge, 
Uttarkashi, dated 27.07.2026 in Civil Appeal No. 20 of 
2023 ‘Janardan Prasad vs. Bhagwati Prasad’ shall 
remain stayed.
