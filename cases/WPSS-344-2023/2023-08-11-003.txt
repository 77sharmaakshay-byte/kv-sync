WPSS No. 344 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. B.S. Negi, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Shobhit Saharia, learned counsel 
for the respondents, appeared through
video conferencing.
3. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
4. The writ petition has been filed by the 
petitioner, challenging the order dated 
30.04.2022, whereby the respondent has 
accepted the application for voluntary 
retirement made by the petitioner.
5. It is the contention of the learned 
counsel for the petitioner that the said 
application was withdrawn before the 
acceptance by the respondent-department. 
But the respondents-department disputed 
this fact and come up with the case that it 
has not been withdrawn before the 
acceptance.
6. The respondents-department has been 
directed to file the counter affidavit. But the 
same has not been filed yet.
7. Learned counsel for the respondents 
seeks further three weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit. The prayer so made is 
granted.
8. Further two weeks’ time is granted for 
filing rejoinder affidavit.
IA No. 3 of 2023 (Interim Relief 
Application)
9. After filing the writ petition, an 
application has been moved by the 
petitioner for interim relief with these 
averments that after the acceptance of voluntary retirement application of the 
petitioner, respondents-department is 
pressing harsh upon the petitioner to vacate 
the premises which has been given to him 
and to hand over the vacant position of the 
premises to the respondents-department.
10. Learned counsel for the respondents 
has drawn the attention of this Court on the 
order dated 11.05.2023, whereby this much 
was directed to the petitioner that in case 
the vacant possession of the premises is not 
handed over to the respondents, the
proceedings under the provisions of Public 
Premises (Eviction of Unauthorized 
Occupants) Act 1971, will be initiated.
11. In this view of the matter, this Court 
does not find it proper to pass any interim
order in favour of the petitioner. Interim 
application is hereby rejected.
12. List on 09.10.2023.
