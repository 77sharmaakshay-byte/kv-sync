This is the third bail application.
Heard.
It is argued by learned counsel for the 
appellant that the remand is illegal. Hence, it 
is a ground for bail.
Learned counsel for the applicant also 
submits that earlier, the applicant had 
challenged the remand order, which did not 
find favour with the trial Court. Thereafter, 
the applicant filed C528 No.1316 of 2026, 
which was withdrawn by him with the liberty 
to pursue the pending bail application. It is 
argued that the bail application has now
been rejected. Hence, the applicant is here 
with the third bail application. 
He further submits that subsequently 
when the remand was extended, the 
applicant challenged that order in C528 
No.1400 of 2026, which is listed today before 
the Co-ordinate Bench. The issued involved 
in the instant bail application and C528 
No.1400 of 2026 is one and the same.Having heard, this Court is of the view 
that both the bail application and the C528 
No.1400 of 2026 should be heard and 
decided by one and the same Bench. 
Let Registry seek directions and list 
accordingly.
