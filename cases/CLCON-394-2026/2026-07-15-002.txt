Mr. Mohd. Suhail, learned counsel holding brief of Mr. 
Sandeep Kothari, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. This is a contempt petition filed under Section 12 of 
the Contempt of Courts Act, 1971. Petitioner alleges willful 
and deliberate contempt of this Court's order dated 
19.05.2026 passed in Special Appeal No.93 of 2026, Sainik 
School Ghorakhal and another vs. Sanjeev Kumar 
Chaudhary, whereby the appellants, i.e., Sainik School 
Ghorakhal, were directed to pass a formal order for the 
exchange of the said shops, allotting Shop No. 18 to the 
contempt petitioner and Shop No. 9 to Pushpa Shah. The 
order was passed on 19.05.2026. 
3. Since no time has been prescribed for compliance 
with the said order passed by this Court, the contempt 
petition is premature in nature.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that, in 
view of Rule 10-A, Chapter XXII of the Uttarakhand High 
Court Rules, 1952, wherein it is provided that where the 
time period is not mentioned in the order for its 
implementation/compliance, the order shall be 
implemented or complied with within two months from the 2
date of receipt of the order.
5. Rule 10-A of the Uttarakhand High Court Rules, 1952 
is quoted hereinbelow:-
“10-A. Time period for implementation of orders.-In the 
absence of any time period prescribed by the Court for 
implementation of the direction or order made or the 
rule absolute issued by the Court, the same shall be 
implemented within two months of the receipt of the 
order.”
6. Even with the aid of this Rule, petition appears to be 
pre-mature as two months will expire on 19.07.2026.
7. Put up on 24.07.2026 as fresh.
