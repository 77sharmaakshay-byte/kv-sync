Mr. Karan Singh Dugtal, learned counsel for the 
Revisionists.
2. Mr. S.S. Chauhan, learned DAG with Mr. Vikash 
Uniyal, learned Brief Holder, for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. In the present criminal revision filed under Section 
442 of the Bhartiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, the 
Revisionist has challenged the order dated 15.12.2025, 
passed by the court of learned 1st Additional Sessions 
Judge, Kashipur in Sessions Trial No.08 of 2025 “State Vs. 
Sachin Sharma”, by which the learned Trial Court has 
allowed the application filed by the prosecution seeking to 
frame charges against the Revisionist under Sections 3/21, 
4/22 and 5/23 of the Banning of Unregulated Deposits 
Schemes Act, 2019 along with BNS Section.
4. Learned counsel for the Revisionist makes a prayer 
that the Revisionist is now being roped in under the 
Banning of Unregulated Deposits Schemes Act, 2019, and 
is apprehending his arrest whereas his bail application had 
been considered and the Revisionist is on bail at present.
5. Admit.
6. Counter affidavit to be filed within a period of three 
weeks.
7. Till the next date of listing, the effect and operation 
of the impugned order dated 15.12.2025, passed by the 
court of learned 1st Additional Sessions Judge, Kashipur in 
Sessions Trial No.08 of 2025 “State Vs. Sachin Sharma”, 
shall remain stayed.8. However, it is made clear that the Revisionist shall 
not make the interim relief as a tool for prolonging the 
case. In default, the interim order shall stand vacated.
9. List this matter on 18.03.2026.
10. Stay application (IA No.1/2025) stands disposed of 
accordingly.
