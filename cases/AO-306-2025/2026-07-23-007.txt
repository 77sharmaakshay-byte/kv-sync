It is argued on behalf of the appellant in 
AO No.419 of 2025 that the deceased had an 
Income Tax Return on 19.11.2021, but his 
compensation has not been assessed based 
on that Income Tax Return, instead, based 
on the Minimum Wages Act, 1948, 
calculation has been made. It is also 
submitted that, in fact, the death took place 
on 14.11.2022.
Fact remains that in the claim petition, 
the claimants have shown the monthly 
income of the deceased as Rs. 25,000/-. 
When confronted as to why compensation
beyond Rs.25,000/- may be assessed, 
learned counsel for the appellant seeks time. 
List this matter on 13.08.2026, along 
with the connected appeal.
