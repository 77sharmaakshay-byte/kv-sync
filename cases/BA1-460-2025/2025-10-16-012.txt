COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
BA1 No. 460 & 1179 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Kailash Chandra and Ms. Gurbani Singh, 
learned counsel for the applicant. 
2. Mr. G. S. Sandhu, learned Addl. Advocate 
General with Mr. Deepak Bhardwaj, learned Brief 
Holder for the State. 
3. Present applicant is praying for regular bail in 
relation to FIR dated 18.10.2024 bearing FIR No. 0337 of 
2024, Police Station – Kankhal, District – Haridwar, 
which was registered at 23.35 hour by one Rudranand, 
son of Shri Shayamal Hari Giri implicating five persons
namely Yogi Ramgopal Nath, Ashok Kumar, Saurav, 
Lalit and Pradeep. The FIR reveals that it is a missing 
report but FIR has been registered for the offence 
punishable under Section 302, 201 read with Section 
120-B IPC. The FIR further discloses that information 
was received at police station on 18.10.2024 at 23.25 
hours. It further reveals that deceased left Haridwar on 
15th June to visit Rajasthan meaning thereby deceased 
was alive on 15th June. 
4. Charge sheet has been filed against Ashok 
Kumar, Ram Gopal Nath and Sanjeev Kumar Tyagi, 
(not named in the FIR) and other co-accused Saurav, 
and Pradeep are still absconding. During investigation, 
statement of co – accused Yogi Ram Gopal Nath were 
recorded by the I.O., who disclosed that in fact, 
deceased was murdered on 1st June. Present applicant 
along with other co-accused except Pradeep and 
Saurav were arrested on 18 / 19.10.2024. 
5. Learned counsel for the applicant argued that if 
the FIR was registered for missing report, then, how 
FIR was registered under Section 302 IPC.
6. Mr. G.S. Sandhu, learned Addl. Advocate 
General for the applicant may get instructions, if 
deceased was murdered on 1st June, then how, it is 
mentioned in the FIR that deceased left the Haridwar 
on 15th June and what evidence has been collected by 
the I.O. that deceased was murdered on 1st June. He 
may also get instructions why FIR has been registered under Section 302 IPC based on missing report. 
7. List on 03.11.2025. 
8. In the meantime, State may also file objection in 
BA1 No. 1179 of 2025.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
