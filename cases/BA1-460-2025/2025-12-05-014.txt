3. It is argued by the learned counsel 
for the applicant that the investigation 
conducted by the I.O. appears to be 
faulty since there is great suspicion on 
the conduct of the complainant 
Rudranand as well as other complainant 
Shobhit Kumar and this aspect has not 
been investigated at all. He also pointed 
out that deceased was having two mobile 
numbers but in the investigation only 
one mobile number was included but for 
the remaining mobile number no 
investigation was done.
4. Apart from this, complaint was filed 
for missing of the deceased but in the 
chick FIR Section 302 IPC is also 
mentioned. Furthermore, from the FIR 
itself the deceased left Haridwar to 
Rajasthan on 15.06.2024 and at least for 
one month mobile number of the 
deceased was in functioning but after 
one month his mobile was found to be 
switched off for four months. 
5. Prima facie, from the FIR, it reveals 
that that deceased left Haridwar on 
15.06.2024 then how prosecution comes to the conclusion that deceased in fact 
was murdered on 1st June that too in 
Haridwar.
6. All these aspect certainly goes to 
the root of the matter.
7. Put up this matter on 11.12.2025. 
On that day, the Investigating Officer 
shall remain present in Court. Further, 
the visiting register of the Ashram be 
also placed before this Court.
