Hon’ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
Mr. Prabhat Pande, Advocate for the 
appellant. 
Present appeal is preferred against 
the judgment and award dated 
21.07.2017 passed by Motor Accident 
Claim Tribunal/District Judge, Pauri 
Garhwal, MAC case no. 25 of 2016. 
There is 9 days’ delay in filing the 
appeal. Grounds for delay are that the 
after obtaining certified copy of the 
impugned judgment legal opinion was 
sought for, and after approval the appeal 
has immediately been filed. 
No fruitful purpose would be served 
to issue notice to the respondent against 
delay condonation application, as much 
as, delay of 9 days’ is not too much. The 
delay is not inordinate and has 
sufficiently been explained. 
Delay of 9 days’ in filing the appeal 
is condoned. Delay condonation 
application is allowed. 
Admit the appeal. 
Summon the lower court record. 
Issue notice to the respondents. 
List for hearing on interim relief 
application after lower court record is 
received. 
 
 (Lok Pal Singh, J.) 
Parul 09.11.2017
