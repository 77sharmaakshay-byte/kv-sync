This is an Appeal from Order which 
has been preferred by the Insurance 
Company against the award dated 21.7.2017 
as rendered in MACP Case No. 25/2016. On 
the Appeal being preferred, this court while 
condoning the delay of 9 days and admitting 
the Appeal vide its order dated 09.11.2017 
had not passed any interim order staying the 
effect and operation of the award in favour 
of the appellant. 
Today, the matter is being listed on 
money withdrawal application as has been 
preferred by the respondent on account of 
the fact that since this court has never 
directed the appellant to deposit any amount 
as awarded in favour of the respondent 
claiming that there is no occasion for the 
respondent to file money withdrawal 
application because the claimant has remedy 
under law to file an execution proceeding 
for executing the award as rendered by the 
Motor Accident Claim Tribunal. 
Accordingly, the money withdrawal 
application stands rejected.
