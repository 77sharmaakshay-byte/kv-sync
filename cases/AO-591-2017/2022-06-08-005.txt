Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. T.C. Pande, Advocate, holding brief of 
Mr. Prabhat Pande, Advocate for the appellant.
Mr. Vinoda Nand Barthwal, Advocate for the

The appellant has filed an urgency 
application, on the ground that in the absence of 
there being any interim order passed by this Court, 
the execution is being proceeded, though he has 
deposited the decreetal amount, before the court 
below under protest.
In view of the aforesaid statement made at 
the bar, the Urgency Application No.13928 of 2022 
would stand allowed. 
The appeal from order is directed to be listed 
under the head of hearing in the last week of July 
2022.
Since it is stated at bar, that the deposit has 
already been made by the appellant before the 
Motor Accident Claim Tribunal, no coercive 
measures as a consequence of execution 
proceedings, would be taken against the appellant 
till the next date of listing.
