4. The instant bail application is moved 
on behalf of the applicant Suleman, S/o 
Rasheed for seeking bail in connection with 
Case Crime No.178 of 2026 wherein he is 
charge sheeted alongwith the other co￾accused for the offences punishable under 
Section 109(1), 115(2), 191(2), 191(3), 351(2) 
and 352 of BNS, 2023, P.S. Manglaur, District 
Haridwar. 
5. It is argued by learned counsel for the 
applicant that applicant is innocent and has 
been falsely implicated and furthermore, the 
charge sheet is already filed and as per the 
prosecution, the main assailant is already 
enlarged on bail. Apart from this, he submits 
that applicant’s wife is in advance stage of 
her pregnancy of 36 weeks and there is no 
one to look after her, therefore, he may be 
enlarged on short term bail. 
6. On the other side Mr. G.S. Sandhu, 
learned Additional Advocate General have not disputed that the applicant’s wife is in 
the advance stage of 36 weeks pregnancy. 
7. After hearing the submissions as above, 
this Court is of the view that applicant 
deserves for short term bail for one month. 
8. Let the applicant-Suleman be released 
on short term bail for a period of one month 
to be reckoned from the date of his actual 
release on his executing a personal bond 
and two sureties of the like amount to the 
satisfaction of the court concerned.
9. On expiry of the period of one month, 
the applicant shall surrender before the 
Superintendent of the jail concerned.
10. Put up on 02.11.2026.
