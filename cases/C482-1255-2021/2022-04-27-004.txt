On 08.10.2021, an interim order 
was passed in this case, which was 
extended on 03.01.2022. But now, 
today, learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that despite an interim 
order passed by this Court, the court 
below has proceeded against the 
petitioner and issued bailable warrants.
Copy of the order dated 
26.03.2022 has also been filed.
The Court does not want to 
comment in the manner, in which 
bailable warrants have been issued 
against the petitioner.
Be it as it may, the proceedings of 
the Criminal Case No. 389 of 2020, 
State Vs. Tanuj Negi, pending in the 
court of Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Ramnagar, District Nainital 
shall remain stayed till further order of 
this court.
Let a copy of this order be sent by 
the Registry to the court concerned, 
today itself.
