5. It is submitted by learned counsel for 
the petitioners that the petitioners were 
inducted as a Junior Engineer (Civil) in the 
year 2016 after due selection process. He 
submits that pursuant to the order of 
appointment, the petitioners continued 
without any break. 
5. It is also submitted by learned counsel 
for the petitioners that the petitioners are 
entitled to get regularization pursuant to 
2013 Rules of Regularization as well as 
pursuant to the judgment passed by Division 
Bench of this Court on 22.02.2024, passed in 
bunch of the petitions leading one is a WPSB 
No.616 of 2018. 
6. It is further submitted by learned 
counsel for the petitioners that despite the 
representation made for regularization, no 
decision has been taken by the respondents. 
7. Learned counsel for the respondents 
prays for and is granted six weeks’ time to 
file counter-affidavit. Two weeks’ time, 
thereafter, is also granted to learned counsel 
for the petitioners to file rejoinder-affidavit. 
8. List this matter on 10.12.2024. 9. Till the next date of listing, the 
respondents are restrained from interfering 
in the functioning of the petitioners to the 
post of Junior Engineer (Civil). 
10. Interim relief application (IA/1/2024) 
stands disposed of accordingly.
