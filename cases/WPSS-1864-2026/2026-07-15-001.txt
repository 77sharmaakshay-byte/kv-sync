Saksham Chauhan ………Petitioner

Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Vinay Kumar, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. G.S. Negi, learned Addl. C.S.C. for the State.
3. Mr. Tilak Ram Sharma, learned counsel holding brief 
of Mr. Shailendra Nauriyal, learned counsel for respondent 
no.4.
4. The contention of learned counsel for the petitioner is 
that petitioner by virtue of issuance of Government Order 
dated 01.07.2026, annexure no.16 to the writ petition, has 
allowed such candidates to participate in the document 
verification who at the time of filing of their application form 
did not annexed the certificate of Uttarakhand Rajya 
Andolankari for claiming reservation for them.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that this 
cannot be done in view of the law laid down in the case of 
Tej Prakash Pathak & Ors. v. Rajasthan High Court & Ors., 
reported in (2025) 2 SCC 1, wherein the Apex Court has 
categorically held that eligibility criteria under advertisement 
cannot be changed during the selection process.
6. He has placed reliance on para no.65.2 of the aforesaid 
judgment, which is quoted hereunder:-
“65.2. Eligibility criteria for being placed in the select list, 
notified at the commencement of the recruitment process, cannot 
be changed midway through the recruitment process unless the 
extant Rules so permit, or the advertisement, which is not 
contrary to the extant Rules, so permit. Even if such change is 
permissible under the extant Rules or the advertisement, the 
change would have to meet the requirement of Article 14 of the 2
Constitution and satisfy the test of non-arbitrariness;
7. Learned counsel for the petitioner however submits 
that the last date of submission of the application form 
pursuant to the advertisement is 01.11.2024 and now at 
this juncture the respondent cannot be permitted to supply 
the certificate of Uttarakhand Rajya Andolankari by virtue of 
Order dated 01.07.2026.
8. The learned counsel for the State and Selecting Body 
prays for and are granted a week’s time to get instructions 
in the matter regarding the fact that under which law and 
Rules the aforesaid government order has been issued by 
the State Government.
9. List this case on 22.07.2026 as fresh.
10. Till the next date of listing, State is restrained from 
issuing appointment letter to the private respondents.
