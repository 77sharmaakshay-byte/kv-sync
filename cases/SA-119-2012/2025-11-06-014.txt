COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
SA No. 119 of 2012
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Arvind Vashisht, learned Sr. Advocate 
assisted by Mr. Siddhant Tiwari, learned counsel for 
the appellant.
2. Mr. I.P. Kohli, learned Standing Counsel for the 
State / respondents.
3. Today, arguments heard at length.
4. Mr. Arvind Vashisht, learned Sr. Advocate for 
the appellant has placed reliance on the judgment of 
Constitutional Bench of Apex Court in the case of 
Valjibhai Muljibhai Soneji and another Vs. State of 
Maharastra AIR 1963 SC 1890 and by referring this 
judgment, he submits that validity of the acquisition 
proceedings under the Land Acquisition Act can be 
examined by Civil Court and as such, suit filed by the 
plaintiffs – appellants is maintainable. 
5. On the previous date, Mr. I.P. Kohli, learned 
Standing Counsel for the State advanced his arguments 
by placing reliance on four judgments of Apex Court 
wherein it has been held that scheme of the Land 
Acquisition Act is complete code in itself and thereby 
the jurisdiction of the civil court to take cognizance of 
the cases arising under the Act, by necessary 
implication stood barred. The civil court thereby is 
devoid of jurisdiction to give declaration on the 
invalidity of the procedure as contemplated under the 
Act. The only right an aggrieved person has is to 
approach the constitutional court viz. High Court and 
Supreme Court under their plenary power under 
Articles 226 and 136 respectively with self imposed 
restrictions on their exercise of extraordinary power. 
6. For rest of the arguments, list on 10.11.2025 on 
top of the board.
