COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
SA No.119 of 2012
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Present Mr. Arvind Vashistha, senior counsel 
assisted by Ms. Nidhi Thapa, counsel for the 
appellant through V.C.
2. Present Mr. G.S. Negi, Addl. C.S.C. for the 
State. 
3. Heard on the amendment application (IA 
No.12157 of 2022).
4. Senior counsel for the appellant would 
submit that the additional substantial questions of 
law are needed to be framed which are as under:-
“(i) Whether acquisition of land under Land 
Acquisition Act 1894 can be presumed by mere 
admission/proof of notification under Section 4 
of Land Acquisition Act 1894 and without 
proving that any acquisition in pursuance to 
such notification ever culminated?
(iii) Whether bare pleading in the written 
statement, without there being any documentary 
or oral evidence supporting/proving pleadings in 
written statement are sufficient to dislodge 
plaintiff's case?”
5. Perusal of the file would reveal that these two 
additional substantial questions of law sought to be 
framed are very much included in already framed 
substantial question of law. Merely for re-framing 
the substantial question of law in different words 
shall not make any better case. The amendment 
application is misconceived same is dismissed 
accordingly.
6. Put up on 12.03.2024.
