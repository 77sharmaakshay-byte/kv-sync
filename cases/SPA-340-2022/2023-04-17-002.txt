ORDER: (per Sri Vipin Sanghi, C.J.) 
 The report has been filed by the Tehsildar, as per 
which the land in Khata No. 26 comprising of Khasra Nos. 
897, 901, 903, 904, 911, 912, 913, 917, 920, 922 and 930 
is recorded in the name of respondent-THDC India Private 
Limited. At the same time, the THDC India Private Limited 
is not in possession and even the appellants were not found 
in possession at the site. Upon inspection, it was found that 
the THDC India Private Limited has not undertaken any 
activities on the land, and the THDC India Private Limited 
has also not dealt with the land. 2 
2. The appellants are claiming compensation on the 
ground that the land was acquired for the purpose of THDC 
India Private Limited. Though the appellants have placed on 
record the Notification issued under Section 4, read with 
Section 17, of the Land Acquisition Act, 1894, the appellants 
have not placed on record any documents as regards the 
acquisition proceedings. 
3. Learned counsel for the appellants seeks time to 
produce certain documents in that regard. 
4. At his request, list on 12.09.2023. 
5. The appellants may file a Supplementary Affidavit 
within six weeks.
