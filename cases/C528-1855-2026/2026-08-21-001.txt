Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Lalit Sharma, learned counsel with Mr. 
Jai Krishna Pandey, learned counsel for the 
applicant.
2. Mr. B.S. Kathayat, learned counsel for the 
respondents. 
3. The instant petition is preferred under 
Section 528 of BNSS 2023 by the present applicant 
Devendra Nainwal being aggrieved with the order 
dated 25.07.2026 whereby the Principal Judge, 
Family Court, Haldwani directed to clear the entire 
dues towards interim maintenance on a monthly 
installment of Rs. 1 lakh on or before 10th of every 
month and by paying 50% of the entire dues on or 
before 25.08.2026.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant make 
innocuous prayer that the proceeding as initiated 
by the wife under section 125 Cr.P.C. has to be 
expedited pursuant to the Notification dated 
27.08.2009, i.e., The Uttarakhand Case Flow 
Management (For Subordinate Courts) Rules, 2009 
wherein the directions have been issued to all the 
Presiding Officers to make an endeavour to 
dispose of cases as mentioned in Track-1 within 
one year and cases as mentioned in Track-II and III 
within two years. He submits that present case 
relates to the maintenance, therefore, in view of the 
said Notification this case falls under the category 
of Track-1 and has to be expedited within one year. 
He further submits that there was an order earlier 
even then no endeavour has been made by the 
Trial Court to expedite the case. Apart from this, he submits that false allegations has been leveled 
by the respondent/wife against her father-in-law 
regarding which FIR has been lodged raising the 
allegation of rape which was found incorrect. By 
raising this issue learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that in fact the respondent/wife is not 
entitled to get maintenance in terms of provision of 
Section 125 Cr.P.C.
5. All these aspects require scrutiny but the fact 
remains that serious allegation has been alleged by 
the respondent/wife against father of the applicant 
with regard to the commission of rape and the 
allegation has been found to be false.
6. This is a petition under Section 528 of BNSS 
2023 and the mandate of Section 528 of BNSS 2023 
is itself reflected from the provisions of Section 528 
of BNSS which reads as under:- 528. Saving of inherent powers of High Court.
Nothing in this Sanhita shall be deemed to limit or affect the 
inherent powers of the High Court to make such orders as may 
be necessary to give effect to any order under this Sanhita, or to 
prevent abuse of the process of any Court or otherwise to secure 
the ends of justice.
7. No doubt proceeding under Section 125 
Cr.P.C. is pending before the Principal Judge, 
Family Court but the dispute in fact arising out of 
matrimonial dispute in between the applicant and 
the respondent. Both the parties married in the 
year 2003 and there are two childs from this 
marriage and both are living with the wife. At this 
juncture this aspect has to be examined why the 
present applicant being father of the child has been 
deprived of love and affection of his children. Let 
the affidavit be filed by the respondent/wife 
within two weeks.
8. List this case on 08.09.2026.
9. In the meantime, the impugned order dated 
25.07.2026 passed by the learned Addl. Principal 
Judge, Family Court Haldwani shall remain stayed, 
however, the applicant shall pay the monthly
maintenance regularly.
