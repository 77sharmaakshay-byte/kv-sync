5. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that though in the present writ 
petition, the award dated 04.01.2023 is 
challenged by the petitioner-Bank, but the 
award has also been challenged by the 
Registrar Co-operative Societies, Uttarakhand, 
Dehradun in WPMS No.2128 of 2023, Registrar 
Co-operative Societies vs. Rajendra Singh 
Kandiyal & others and in that writ petition, the 
later portion of the award, by which the 
respondent-Bank was directed to pay a sum of 
Rs.5,00,000/- to the workman, in the event of
workman has not been reinstated with the 
Bank due to non-availability of vacancy/post.
6. In this view of the matter, connect this 
petition with WPMS No.2128 of 2023.
