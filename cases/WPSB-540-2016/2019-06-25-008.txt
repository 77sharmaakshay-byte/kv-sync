Urgency Application No.8054 of 2019 
is filed seeking out of turn hearing. 
The present writ petition relates to the 
year 2016. Cases filed in the year 2014, 
and prior thereto, have been identified for 
expeditious disposal and are listed for 
hearing every Wednesday and Thursday 
afternoon.
Since the present writ petition relates 
to year 2016, it would be inappropriate to 
take up this case out of turn when cases,
relating to the year 2014 and prior thereto,
are still pending before this Court.
Leaving it open to the petitioner to 
move another application, in case the writ 
petition is not listed before 30th September, 
2019, the Urgency application stands 
disposed of.
