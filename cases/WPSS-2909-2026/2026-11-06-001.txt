4. By the instant petition, the petitioner who is 
working as an officiating Assistant Review Officer 
in State Election Commission is challenging the 
resolution passed by the Departmental Promotion
Committee dated 13.02.2026, whereby the 
Committee passed a resolution that against one 
post of ARO, which now fell vacant due to the 
promotion of one incumbent Sri Mohan Chand, 
will be filled up as per the roster of P(Promoton) –
D(Direct) – P(Promotion). 
5. The resolution is being challenged on the 
ground that the DPC is making an effort to 
misinterpret Rule 5 of the Uttarakhand State 
Election Commission (Headquarter) Assistant 
Review Officer Service Rules, 2016 as notified on 
11th July, 2016, since Rule 5 provides that the 
recruitment to the post in the service shall be made 
50% by way of direct recruitment and 50% by way 
of promotion amongst substantively appointed 
Typist/Data Entry Operator.
6. To clarify the position of the cadre structure, 
the Secretary of the Commission was asked to join 
the proceedings through VC and he place reliance 
to Rule 18 which read as under:-
“Rule 18:- If in any year of recruitment 
appointments are made both by direct recruitment and by promotion, a combined 
select list shall be prepared by taking the 
names of candidates from the relevant lists, 
in such manner that the prescribed 
percentage is maintained, the first name in 
the list being of the person appointed by 
promotion.” 
7. By placing reliance to Rule 18, he submits 
that to maintain the percentage in terms of Rule 5 
the Departmental Promotion Committee prepared
a roster i.e. P (Promotion)-D(Direct)-P(Promotion)-
D(Direct) etc. He submits that there are in total 
three post of ARO out of which two posts were 
filled up amongst promotee i.e. Mohan Chand and 
Ramesh Chand, and one remaining post was filled 
up through direct recruitment by one Mahesh 
Bijalwan. He further pointed out that the promotee 
Mohan Chand now promoted to the post of 
Review Officer and against this post the DPC 
resolved that the vacancy occupied by Sri Mohan 
Chand as promotee should be filled up by way of 
direct recruitment.
8. The analogy as applied by the DPC is 
completely against the mandate of law, 
particularly against the mandate of Rule 18 as 
aforesaid. 
9. What this Court has observed after 
examining the impact of Rule 18 where the 
recruitment are made both by direct recruitment as 
well as by promotion, a combined select list shall 
be prepared by taking the names of the candidates
from the relevant lists, in such manner that the 
prescribed percentage is maintained and the first 
name in the list being of the person appointed by 
promotion. The prescribed percentage is in terms 
of Rule 5 i.e. 50% by direct and 50% by promotion. 
10. There is no dispute that there are in total 
three vacancy, therefore, to maintain the 
percentage of quota, the third vacancy would be 
filled up according to the lien and mode of 
selection. In other words the post occupied by the promotee Sri Mohan Chand who now promoted to 
the post of Review Officer will be filled up only by 
way of promotion. 
11. To explain further if in future in such a 
contingency if a direct recruitee is promoted to the 
post of Review Officer then that post should be 
filled up by way of a direct. 
12. In my opinion since Rule 18, which refers 
the name of a candidate therefore Rule 18 should 
be interpreted in such a manner that a person who 
occupy a particular post by way of promotion 
should be filled up on his further promotion in the 
same manner in which a person who have a lien 
was appointed i.e. if by direct then only by way of 
direct recruitment and if by promotion then only 
by way of promotion. 
13. Thus, this Court is of the view that the 
resolution passed by the Departmental Promotion 
Committee appears to be against the mandate of 
Rule 18, however, this aspect requires scrutiny. Let 
the contesting respondent the respondent no.2 
may file their counter affidavit on or before 
25.11.2026. Three days’ thereafter is granted to the 
learned counsel for the petitioner to file rejoinder 
affidavit.
14. Put up this matter on 30.11.2026 for final 
disposal immediately after fresh.
15. Till the next date of listing, the resolution 
passed by the Departmental Promotion Committee 
dated 13.02.2026 (Annexure no.1) shall remain 
stayed.
16. Personal appearance of the official is
