COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
FA No. 210 of 2018
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. D. Barthwal, Advocate, for the 
appellants.
Mr. Yogesh Pant, Advocate, for the 
respondent.
The appellant No. 1 Dinesh Chandra 
Joshi is reported to have died on 09.10.2020, 
and the appellant has filed Substitution 
Applications (IA/19857/2021) to bring the heirs 
of deceased appellant No. 1 on record, which is 
reported to be delayed by 34 days.
He has also preferred an Application 
(IA/19858/2021) under Order 22 Rule 9 of CPC 
for setting aside abatement, as well as the Delay 
Condonation Application No. 19856/2021, 
which has chanced in filing the substitution 
application.
Having considered the reasons given in 
the Delay Condonation Application, this Court 
is of the view that the delay has been 
satisfactorily explained. Consequently, the 
delay would stand condoned; Delay 
Condonation Application would stand allowed 
and consequently, the Substitution Application 
as well as the Application for setting aside 
abatement would also stand allowed.
The learned counsel for the appellant is 
directed to carry out the necessary amendments 
in the cause title of the First Appeal, bringing 
on record of the heirs of deceased appellant No. 
1, within a period of 10 days from today.
List this First Appeal as soon as it 
matures for final hearing.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
