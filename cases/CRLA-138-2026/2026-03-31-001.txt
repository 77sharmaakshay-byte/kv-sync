The appellant proposes to challenge 
the judgment and order dated 10.12.2025, 
passed in Special Sessions Trial No.14 of 
2020, State Vs. Rajendra Singh @ Rajan 
and Others, by the court of Special 
Sessions Judge, District Chamoli. By it, the 
appellant has been convicted and sentenced
under Sections 5/6 of the Protection of 
Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012. It 
is delayed by 43 days. Delay Condonation 
Application, IA No.1 of 2026, has been filed.
Heard.
It is the case of the appellant that he 
belongs to a modest rural background and 
his family members were required to 
arrange financial resources for engaging 
legal counsel and preparing the appeal. In 
that process, delay occurred. 
The delay condonation application is 
not objected to by learned State Counsel. 
Having considered, we are of the view 
that there are good grounds for 
condonation of delay. Accordingly, the 
Delay Condonation Application deserves to 
be allowed.
The delay condonation application is 
allowed. 
The delay in preferring the appeal is condoned. 
Heard on admission. 
Admit. 
Let call for the LCR. 
Let paper book be prepared and 
provided to learned counsel for the parties, 
as per rule. 
Heard on Bail Application, IA No.2 of 
2026
List this matter on 04.05.2026, for 
hearing on the bail application.
In the meantime, State may file 
objections to the bail application.
