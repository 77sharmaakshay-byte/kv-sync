AO No. 289 of 2016
Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate for the 
appellant. 
Issue notices to the respondents 
calling their objections within three 
weeks against the delay condonation 
application (CLMA No.5215 of 2016). 
Steps be taken within a week. 
List in due course along with AO 
No.204 of 2016. 
Miscellaneous application has 
also been moved seeking exemption to 
file the certified copy of the award dated 
15.02.2016 since such copy has already 
been filed in the appeal filed by the 
Insurance Company. 
Miscellaneous application (CLMA 
No.5240 of 2016) is hereby allowed and 
exemption, as prayed for, is granted. 
The offending truck was insured 
with the New India Insurance Company. 
The written statement was filed but at 
the time of arguments none turned up 
on behalf of the owner/ driver of such 
vehicle. So, the validity of driving licence 
could not be explained before the 
Tribunal properly, with the result, the 
liability, has been fastened upon the 
Insurance Company with a right to 
recover the amount from the owner of 
the offending vehicle. 
In these aforesaid circumstances, 
I stay the recovery proceedings.
