CLMA No.5341 of 2020 (Money Withdrawal Application)
In
AO No.29 of 2020
Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J.
Mr. I.P. Kohli, Advocate, for the 
appellant.
Mr. A.M. Saklani, Advocate, for the 
respondents.
This appeal has been filed by the 
appellant against the award dated 
06.11.2019 passed by the Motor Accident 
Claims Tribunal, Nainital in MACP No.200 
of 2018, whereby a compensation of 
Rs.14,95,816/- (Rupees Fourteen Lakh 
Ninety Five Thousand Eight Hundred 
Sixteen Only) has been awarded to the 
claimants. 
Learned counsel for the appellant has 
given a statement before this Court that the 
appellant has deposited the entire awarded 
amount before the concerned Tribunal.
Now a money withdrawal application 
has been filed by the claimants for 
withdrawal of the said amount.
Money withdrawal application is 
allowed.
Let an amount of Rs.10,00,000/-
(Rupees Ten Lakh Only) be released in 
favour of the claimants forthwith on their 
moving an appropriate application before 
the concerned Tribunal. The amount shall 
be given to each of the claimant in 
proportion to what has been fixed by the 
Motor Accident Claims Tribunal. 
List this matter after four weeks in the 
daily cause list. 
 (Sudhanshu Dhulia, J.)
