The candidature of the 
petitioner has been rejected for his 
appointment as a Police Constable.
It is the case of the petitioner 
that his rejection is not as per law. 
He belongs to the hill category; he 
meets all the physical requirement 
for recruitment.
On behalf of the respondents 
it is being stated that the petitioner 
did not place for perusal of the 
Public Service Commission (“the 
Commission”) his documents that 
he belongs to the hill category. 
But, the question is, whether 
the Commission ever asked for 
such documents? If so, whether 
the Commission ever made any 
communication in writing? 
Whether the Commission before 
rejection of candidature of the 
petitioner required him to produce 
such documents revealing the consequences in non-production of 
it? When questioned, learned 
counsel for the Commission seeks 
time. 
List on 06.06.2023, just after 
fresh cases.
