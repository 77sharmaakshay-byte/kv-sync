Heard learned counsel for the 
parties and perused the record.
Admit.
The petitioner applied for the 
position of Constable in 
Uttarakhand Police or PAC/IRB, 
etc. He cleared the examination, 
but finally his candidature was 
rejected on the ground that he did 
not produce his certificate that he 
belongs to the hill category. 
Learned counsel for the 
petitioner would submit that, in 
fact, the petitioner had the 
certificate, but he did not produce 
it on time and for that ground, he 
could have been given an opportunity to produce it and his 
candidature could not have been 
rejected. 
When the matter was taken 
up yesterday, the Court wanted to 
know, as the whether the 
Commission ever asked for such 
documents? If so, whether the 
Commission ever made any 
communication in writing? 
Whether the Commission before 
rejection of candidature of the 
petitioner required him to produce 
such documents revealing the 
consequences in non-production of 
it?
Learned counsel for the 
respondent no.4, Uttarakhand 
Public Service Commission today, 
under instruction, gives a 
statement that any such 
communication was not made. 
Having heard, this Court is of 
the view that this matter requires 
deliberation. 
Admit.

Heard on Interim Relief 
Application (IA) No.1 of 2023.
It is argued that the 
appointment letters have already 
been issued by the respondent 
authorities and the joining would 
be done from tomorrow onwards. It 
would be the process which were 
initiated by virtue of an 
advertisement dated 28.12.2021. 
Since challenge is made to a 
selection process, at this stage, the 
Court can only observe that the 
selection that has been done 
pursuant to the advertisement 
shall remain subject to the 
decision of this writ petition. 
Interim relief application 
stands disposed of accordingly.
