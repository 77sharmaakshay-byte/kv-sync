WPMS No.618 of 2008 
Hon’ble B.S.Verma,J
(Stay application No.1867/08) 
 
Sri Rajendra Dobhal, learned counsel 
for the petitioner. 
Sri K.C. Tiwari, learned brief holder 
for the State-respondents. 
Heard and perused the free hold deeds 
dated 27-11-2003 and 30-11-2004 executed 
by the respondent No.1 in favour of the 
petitioner. 
By the impugned order dated 16-10-
2007, the plots which were free hold in 
favour of the petitioner have been cancelled 
and the revision filed against the same have 
been dismissed vide order dated 21-2-2008. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner has 
vehemently contended that the sale deed/free 
hold deed only can be cancelled by civil 
court under Section 31 of the Specific Relief 
Act, 1963. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner has 
relied upon the Division Bench judgment 
dated 28-5-2007 passed in First Appeal No. 
31 of 2002 Kamleshwar Prasad and another 
Vs. Municipal Board, Mussorie wherein it 
was held that the Collector has no power to 
cancel the sale deed/deed of free hold. 
 The free hold deeds were duly 
registered with Sub-registrar, Pithoragarh. 
 I have perused the impugned orders 
and the deeds of free hold, which were duly 
registered. The order passed by the Collector 
as well as Additional Secretary seems prima 
facie without jurisdiction in view of the 
Division Bench judgment of this Court 
passed in First Appeal No. 31 of 2002 
Kamleshwar Prasad and another Vs. 
Municipal Board, Mussoorie. 
Learned counsel for the respondents 
prays for and is granted four weeks’ time to 
file counter affidavit. 
List thereafter. 
In the meantime, the operation of the 
impugned orders dated 16-10-2007 and 21-2-
2008 passed by the respondent Nos. 3 and 2 
shall remain stayed. 
Dated:-09-04-2008 (B.S. Verma,J) 
M.K.
