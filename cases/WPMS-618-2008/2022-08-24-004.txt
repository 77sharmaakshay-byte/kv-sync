Heard.
This is an application for restoration 
of the writ application dismissed for default 
on 12.10.2017. There is 941 days delay in 
filing the restoration application.
It is submitted that the learned 
counsel could not become aware of the 
listing of the case, hence, both the counsels
were absent. The fact of dismissal of the 
writ application remained unnoticed for a 
long time, for which, the petitioner could 
not file the restoration application in time
In that view of the matter, the Delay 
Codonation Application and the Application 
for Restoration of the petition are allowed. 
The writ application is restored to file.
