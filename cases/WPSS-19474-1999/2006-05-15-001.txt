2. By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has prayed 
for the following reliefs:- 
i. Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
certiorari quashing the order dated 28.01.1999 passed 
by the respondent no. 3 which was supplied to the 
petitioner on 10.02.1999 as annexure no. 4 to this writ 
petition, 
ii. Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding and directing the 
respondents to provide the appointment to the 
2006:UHC:3130petitioner in place of her husband on the basis of dying 
in harness Rules, 
iii. Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus, commanding and directing the 
respondents to provide family pension and other dues 
and benefits to the petitioner, for which she is entitled 
in accordance with law and rules, 
iv. Issue any other writ, order or direction of any nature as 
this Hon’ble Court may deem fit and proper in the 
interest of justice, 
v. Award the cost of the present petition to the petitioner. 
3. The respondents have filed a short counter affidavit. In 
para-6 of the counter affidavit, the respondents have stated that 
in May 15, 2003, the petitioner was engaged as Cook on daily 
basis and since then she is continuing in the Corporation. From 
June 19, 2005 her provident Fund is also being deducted and 
her Employees Provident Fund account no. is 3532. 
4. Keeping in view the aforesaid fact mentioned in the 
counter affidavit the petitioner is already engaged in the 
Department. Nothing remains in the writ petition to be 
adjudicated, as the grievances of the petitioner have already 
been settled by the Department, thus the writ petition has 
become infructuous.
