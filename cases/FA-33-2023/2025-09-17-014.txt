Hon'ble Ra kesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. V.K.Kaparuwan, learned counsel for the 
appellant.
2. Mr. Tarun Mohan, learned Brief Holder 
respondent no.2 and 3.
3. Mr. V.K.Kohli, learned Senior Advocate 
assisted by Mr. Kanti Ram Sharma and Mr. Tarun 
Lakhera, learned counsel for the private respondent.
4. The instant First Appeal is time barred by 271 
days. For the purposes of condoning of delay an 
application has been moved under Section 5 of 
Limitation Act by contending therein that against the
judgment dated 08.12.2017 passed by the learned 
Additional District Judge in L.A. Case no. 29 of 
2004. A Review Application No. 345 of 2018 was 
filed and the same was decided on 17.01.2022 and 
after obtaining the certified copy of the judgment 
passed in the review petition, the matter was 
forwarded to the Ministry of Law and Justice, New 
Delhi, wherein on 30.08.2022 the directions were 
issued to prefer an appeal. It is contended that the 
delay in preferring the instant appeal is bonafide and 
only because of the reason that the review petition 
was filed which was decided on 17.01.2022 and 
subsequently thereto the file was processed for 
seeking an appropriate opinion.
5. On the other side, Mr. V.K.Kohli, learned 
Senior Advocate for the respondent seriously 
objected on the delay.
6. On 02.11.2023 on an urgency application of 
the appellant and further taking into consideration 
that the appellant is ready to deposit the entire decretal amount the effect and operation of the 
impugned judgment and order dated 08.12.2017 and 
17.01.2022 were stayed with this stipulation that the 
appellant shall deposits the entire decretal amount 
before the Reference Court. 
7. It is also made clear in the order that the 
amount so deposited shall not be released without 
express leave of the Court.
8. On the previous dated i.e. 03.09.2025, the 
counsel for the respondent submits that the amount 
towards the compensation, at the rate as admitted to 
the appellant, in the review application be released 
and on such request the counsel for both the parties 
were directed to calculate the amount of 
compensation and inform to this Court.
9. Today, it has been apprise to this Court that 
the actual amount of compensation as determine by 
Special Land Acquisition Officer has already been 
released in favour of the claimants and the amount 
as increased by the Reference Court is subject 
matter of the instant First Appeal, therefore, the 
amount as enhanced by the Reference Court be not 
released.
10. I found forced on such submission since the 
amount as enhanced by the Reference Court is the
subject matter of the instant appeal, therefore, the 
amount which is subject matter of the instant appeal 
cannot be released.
11. Admittedly, the instant appeal is time barred 
and delay is sufficiently explained in the delay 
condonation application and furthermore it is a 
regular first appeal, therefore, this Court is of the 
view the delay in preferring the instant appeal is 
liable to be condoned.
12. Accordingly, the Delay Condonation
Application IA No. 01 of 2023 is allowed. The delay 
is condoned. Simultaneously, since the amount as 
increased by the Reference Court is subject matter 
of the instant appeal and as observed above, such 
amount cannot be released, consequently the Money 
Withdrawal Application IA No. 06 of 2024 moved 
by the respondent/claimant is also rejected. 13. Admit the appeal.
14. Summon the trial court record.
15. Registry is directed to send the requisition to 
get the record from the trial court and after receiving 
the record, the Registry is also directed to prepare 
the paper book immediately and supply the same to 
the learned counsel for the parties on payment of 
usual charges so that the appeal be decided finally 
on the date fixed.
16. Put up this case for final argument on 
15.10.2025.
