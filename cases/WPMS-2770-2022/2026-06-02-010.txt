4. Vide order dated 18.11.2022, learned Counsel 
appearing for respondent no.2 made a submission
before the Court that the cancelled plot could be 
restored if the petitioner pays Rs.6,81,775/- over and 
above the land premium along with interest up to 
01.11.2022. 
5. Today, learned Counsel for the petitioner has 
passed on to this Court the note-sheet of respondent 
no.3. The same is taken on record. At page no.3 of 
the said note-sheet dated 17.11.2022, it is stated that 
if management has considered the request of 
allottee/unit, subject to deposit of certain amount, 
restoration of plot would be done. Moreover, the 
advocate appearing for respondent no.2 was directed 
to be informed about the Re-conciliation Policy, who, 
in turn, was supposed to inform the Court about the 
same. 
6. It appears that only acting upon the aforesaid 
note-sheet, the submission was made on 18.11.2022 
before the Court by learned Counsel who appeared for 
respondent no.2. However, the fact of the matter is 
that till date, no decision has been taken, and rather, 
respondent no.2 is taking different stands by changing the lawyers before this Court. This fact is well 
discernible from the previous order-sheets.
7. In such view of the matter, this Court directs the 
Managing Director of respondent no.2- State 
Infrastructure & Industrial Development Corporation 
of Uttrakhand (SIDCUL) to appear before this Court, 
either in person or through virtual mode, on 
08.06.2026, to assist the Court for resolving the 
controversy once and for all.
8. Post this matter on 08.06.2026 under the 
caption ‘personal appearance’.
9. Interim order dated 18.11.2022 to continue till 
the next date of listing.
