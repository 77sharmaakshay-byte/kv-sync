By the Court:-
 Miscellaneous application (IA No.17070 of 2019) 
is allowed. Counter affidavit filed on behalf of State is taken on 
record. 
 
2. This appeal has been filed by the appellant 
against the judgment and order dated 18.05.2018 passed by 
the learned Sessions Judge, Pithoragarh in Session Trial No.03 
of 2017, whereby the appellant has been convicted under 
Sections 302 and 323 of IPC, and has been sentenced to 
undergo life imprisonment under Section 302 of IPC, and to 
undergo six months imprisonment under Section 323 of IPC, 
with default stipulation. The appellant/accused was not on bail 
during trial. 
3. Lower Court record has already been received. 
4. Learned counsel for the appellant has argued on 
the bail application of the appellant. 
5. Heard on the bail application. 2 
6. Considering the facts and circumstances of the 
case, this Court is not inclined to grant the bail to the 
appellant. 
7. The bail application moved on behalf of appellant 
stands dismissed. 
8. However, the appellant would be at liberty to 
move an appropriate application for expedite hearing of the 
case.
