1. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that petitioner participated in the recruitment held 
for the post of Constable in Uttarakhand 
Police/Provincial Armed Constabulary (PAC) in 
Uttarakhand.
2. The contention of learned Counsel for 
petitioner is that the petitioner belongs to the 
scheduled caste and he submitted his documents 
for verification to the respondent commission. The 
said documents were forwarded to the District 
Magistrate, Haridwar on 16.03.2026. As per 
petitioner, documents of petitioner were found to 
be valid but the petitioner’s name has not been 
recommended for appointment though his name 
found place in the select list.
3. Learned counsel for the respondents, per 
contra, submits that the petitioner has not 
produced his scheduled caste certificate on the 
relevant date i.e. 10.02.2026. However, it is admitted to the State Counsel that the said 
certificate was produced later on which was sent 
for the purpose of verification.
4. In such view of the matter, it will not make 
any difference as to whether the caste certificate 
submitted by the petitioner at the time of 
verification or subsequent thereto.
5. Learned counsel for the respondent￾Commission is directed to seek clear instructions 
in the matter within 7 days, and if possible, send 
the recommendation to the Government for 
appointment of petitioner.
