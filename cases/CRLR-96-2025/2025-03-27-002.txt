Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Sanjay Bhatt, Advocate for the revisionist.
2. Mr. Akshay Latwal, Brief Holder for the State.
3. Mr. V.K. Kaparuwan, Advocate for respondent 
no.2.
4. Learned counsel for the revisionist/accused would 
submit that this revision is filed against the order dated 
17.02.2025 passed by Special Sessions Judge, Pauri 
Garhwal in Sessions Trial No.23 of 2020, whereby the 
application filed by the revisionist/accused under 
Section 227 of Cr.P.C. has been rejected and 
consequently charges have been framed against the 
revisionist/accused for the offences punishable under 
Sections 307, 323, 384, 504, 506 and 34 of IPC.
5. Learned counsel for the revisionist/accused would 
submit that the charge u/s 307 of IPC has been wrongly 
framed against the revisionist/accused whereas there is 
no evidence, whatsoever, in the charge-sheet regarding 
any injury on the vital part of the body.
6. Learned State Counsel prays for some time to file 
counter affidavit, however, he does not dispute the 
above submission. 
7. List on 30.06.2025.
8. Till the next date of listing, further proceedings of 
Sessions Trial No.23 of 2020 “State vs. Rajeev Gaur 
and others”, pending in the court of Sessions Judge, 
Pauri Garhwal, shall remain stayed. 
(Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.)
