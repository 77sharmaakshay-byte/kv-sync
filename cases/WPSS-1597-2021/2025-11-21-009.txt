COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 1597 of 2021
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mrs. Monika Pant, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. I.P. Kohli, learned Standing Counsel assisted 
by Mr. Tarun Mohan, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Mr. Rajesh Sharma, learned counsel for respondent 
nos. 2 and 3.
4. Apparently the respondent illegally withhold the 
gratuity and the consequential benefits like third ACP 
and the leave encashment of the petitioner pursuant to 
the order impugned dated 28.12.2024. Apparently by the 
order impugned all the benefits have been withheld on 
the ground that the same be subject to the final outcome 
of Criminal Appeal No. 262 of 2019. The petitioner was 
placed under suspension but the disciplinary proceeding 
was never been initiated against him and by that time on 
31.12.2020 the petitioner retired from service. 
Surprisingly, just before two days of the retirement on 
28.12.2020 the Regional Manager passed order impugned, 
whereby, the petitioner was reinstated subject to this 
condition that the other benefits will be subject to the 
decision of the criminal appeal, which has been preferred 
by the petitioner against his conviction. Impugned order 
on the face of it appears to be illegal and dehors the rules,
since at no point of time the disciplinary proceeding was 
initiated against the petitioner.
5. Put up this matter on 26.11.2025.
6. On that day the Managing Director either to appear 
in person or to show cause why and under which 
provision of law the benefits have been withheld 
pursuant to the order impugned.
