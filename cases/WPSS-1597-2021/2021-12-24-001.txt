The petitioner in the present writ petition 
has put a challenge to the impugned order dated 
18.12.2020, which was passed by the respondent 
No.2, whereby the benefits of the 3rd ACP, which 
was granted to the petitioner, was later withdrawn.
Initially, when the writ petition was taken 
up, on account of certain typographical error, the 
petitioner sought a liberty to file an amendment 
application to make the necessary rectification in 
the pleadings made in the writ petition.
The said permission was granted by an order 
dated 06.12.2021.
Accordingly, the amendment application i.e.
(IA No.02 of 2021) has been filed by the petitioner.
After having gone through the contents of the 
amendment application, since the nature of the 
amendment is only by way of a correction of the 
typographical error, which has chanced in the writ 
petition, the same would stand allowed, and the 
necessary amendment as prayed for by adding 
paragraphs provided in paragraph Nos.5 and 6, of 
the amendment application i.e. permitted to be 
carried out by the petitioner in the memo of writ 
petition. The petitioner would undertake the 
exercise of supplying the amended memo of writ 
petition within a period of two weeks from today.
Therefore, three weeks’ time is granted to 
the respondents to file counter affidavit.
List thereafter. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
