Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
 Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate for the

 Mr. M.A. Khan, AGA for the State. 
 The challenge in this petition is made to 
the order dated 14.08.2023, passed in Misc. 
Case No.41 of 2018, by the court of First 
Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Dehradun, District Dehradun, by which, in 
Case Crime No.376 of 2017, under Sections 
420, 406, 506 IPC, Police Station Patel 
Nagar, District Dehradun re-investigation 
has been has been ordered. 
 Heard learned counsel for the petitioners 
and perused the record.
 The case is based on an FIR, lodged by 
the respondent no.3, according to which, 
there was an agreement between the 
respondent no.3 and the petitioner no.1, 
Rajesh Chopra with regard to Helicopter 
services. After the use of Helicopter, 
according to the FIR, money was also given 
to the petitioner no.1 and, thereafter, in 
violation of the condition of the agreement 
petitioner no.1 provided his Helicopter 
services to the State of Uttarakhand and 
pressurized the informant to make more 
payment. 
 In this FIR, after investigation the
Investigating Officer filed a Final Report 
stating that the dispute relates to an 
agreement and there is an arbitration 
clause. It is basis of the case, in which 
informant was heard and, thereafter, 
impugned order has been passed.  Learned counsel for the petitioners 
submits that:-
(i) The court may not order re￾investigation. At the most, further 
investigation could have been 
ordered and;
(ii) The dispute is purely civil in nature 
covered by the arbitration clause.
 
 Having considered, this Court is of the 
view that this matter requires deliberation.
 Admit.
 Issue notice to the respondent no.3, 
returnable within six weeks. Steps to be 
taken within a week.
 List on 06.01.2025.
 Till the next date of listing, the effect and 
operation of the impugned order shall 
remain in abeyance. 
 Interim Relief Application (IA) No.1 of 
2024, stands disposed of.
