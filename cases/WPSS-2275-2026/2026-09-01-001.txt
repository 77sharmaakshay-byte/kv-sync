3. Petitioner has challenged the 
advertisement dated 29.06.2026 
(Annexure No.3) issued by respondent 
No.2, whereby, applications were invited 
for the post of Anganwadi Karyakarti for 
Anganwadi Kendra, Bagela (Code: 
5065020604), Champawat Project, 
District Champawat, and other ancillary 
reliefs. 
4. The ground for challenging, as 
reflected from the writ petition, is that 
the petitioner was engaged as 
Anganwadi Sahayika in the aforesaid 
Anganwadi Centre, on 18.06.2025; the 
case of the petitioner is that as per the 
government order applicable, if 
Anganwadi Sahayika is working in the 
Anganwadi Centre for continuous one year in the absence of Angawadi 
Karyakarti, she has matured a right to 
be considered for Anganwadi Karyakarti 
in the centre and no advertisement can 
be issued.
5. Learned counsel for petitioner 
alleges that the post of Anganwadi 
Karyakarti is vacant even before the 
joining of petitioner.
6. Learned State Counsel is directed to 
seek instruction particularly with regard 
to the fact in writing as on which date 
the petitioner has joined as Anganwadi 
Sahayika in the aforesaid Anganwadi 
centre.
7. Put up as fresh on 07.09.2026.
8. Till then, no further action shall be 
taken by the respondent-State pursuant 
to the impugned advertisement.
