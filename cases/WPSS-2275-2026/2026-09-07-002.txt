Mr. Radha Krishna Aithani, learned 
counsel for petitioner.
2. Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Additional 
C.S.C. with Mr. Prem Prakash Bhatt, 
learned Brief Holder for the State of 
Uttarakhand/respondents.
3. Petitioner has challenged the 
advertisement dated 29.06.2026 (Annexure 
No.3) issued by respondent No.2, whereby, 
applications were invited for the post of 
Anganwadi Karyakatri for Anganwadi 
Kendra, Bagela (Code: 5065020604), 
Champawat Project, District Champawat, 
and other ancillary reliefs. 
4. Petitioner claimed in the writ petition, 
that she was engaged as Anganwadi 
Sahayika in the aforesaid Anganwadi 
Centre, on 18.06.2025 and since she has 
joined prior to one year of issuance of 
advertisement, therefore, she has matured 
a right to be considered for Anganwadi 
Karyakatri in the centre and no 
advertisement can be issued.
5. This Court vide order dated 01.09.2026 
directed learned State Counsel to seek 
instruction with regard to the fact in writing 
as on which date the petitioner has joined 
as Anganwadi Sahayika in the aforesaid Anganwadi centre.
6. Today, learned State Counsel passed 
on instruction dated 02.09.2026 and 
submitted that the date of joining of 
petitioner is 04.08.2025 and the 
advertisement was issued before 
completion of one year of the petitioner as 
Anganwadi Sahayika, therefore, petitioner 
has got no matured right to be considered 
for the post of Anganwadi Karyakatri in the 
aforesaid Anganwadi centre. The said 
instruction is taken on record.
7. Per contra, learned counsel for 
petitioner submits that petitioner was 
issued appointment on 18.06.2025, but due 
to Panchayat elections in the year 2025, 
she could not join and the fault is on the 
part of respondent-employer, not on the 
petitioner for her late joining.
8. Learned State Counsel is directed to 
file detailed counter affidavit within three 
weeks.
9. Two weeks thereafter is granted to 
petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
10. Put up on 26.10.2026.
11. Till then, no further action shall be 
taken by the respondent-State pursuant to 
impugned advertisement dated 29.06.2026.
