Petitioners complained that 
their result for the first year exam 
has not been declared. Time was 
granted but the respondent did not 
choose to file counter affidavit. It is 
being argued that the enrollment is to 
be done by tomorrow only and the 
exams are scheduled to have been 
conducted from 10th June, 2023. 
In this matter interim 
application has been pressed, which 
has not been granted earlier. It has 
been argued on behalf of the 
petitioners that the petitioners were 
admitted by respondent no.5/college 
in defiance to circular, which requires 
the candidates for admission, who 
have qualified NEET. 
Notices have yet not been issued 
to respondent no. 5. 
Let notices be issued to 
respondent no.5. Dasti notice be 
taken out. 
All the respondents shall file 
counter affidavits on 5th June, 2023. 
The Principal of the respondent no. 5/college shall join through Video 
Conferencing to assist the Court. 
Prior to that, he will insure that the 
counter affidavit is filed on his behalf. 
The matter shall be taken on 6th
June, at 2:00 P.M. 
 The Principal of the respondent 
no. 5/college shall join the Court 
through Video Conferencing to assist 
the Court. It may also help the Court 
to understand as to how without 
qualifying NEET, the petitioners were 
admitted by the college and if it is in 
defiance of the circular issued, as 
argued on behalf of the University, 
why the college be not asked to pay 
costs for any loss that may occasion 
to the petitioners. 
List this writ petition on 
06.06.2023.
