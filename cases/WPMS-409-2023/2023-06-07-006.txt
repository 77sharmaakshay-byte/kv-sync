Heard on Interim Relief Application 
No. 1 of 2023.
The petitioners are students of 2nd
Year BMS Course run by the respondent 
no.5, which is affiliated to the 
respondent no.2, Uttarakhand Ayurveda 
University (“the University”),
It is the case of the petitioners that 
they were permitted to appear in the 1st
Year Examination, but their result has 
not been declared and they were not 
allowed to write the 2nd Year 
Examination which begins from 
tomorrow.
Having considered, as an interim 
measure, the respondent no.2, University is directed to declare the 
result of 1st Year Examination of the 
petitioners by today evening and if they 
qualify, they may be permitted to write 
the 2nd Year Examination. This interim 
measure shall be subject to the final 
outcome of the writ petition.
Learned counsel for the respondent 
no.2/the University shall communicate 
the order of the Court to the concerned 
authorities for its compliance.
Interim relief application also 
stands disposed of accordingly.
Let a copy of this order be supplied 
to learned counsel for the parties, today 
itself, on payment of usual charges.
