4. The present writ petition has been filed 
challenging the judgment and order dated 
18.07.2026 passed by learned Commissioner, 
Garhwal Region, Camp Dehradun in Appeal 
No. 03 of 2026 under Section 6 of the Uttar 
Pradesh Control of Goondas Act, 1970 (as 
adopted by the State of Uttarakhand) (for 
short ‘Goondas Act’), whereby, the learned 
Commissioner has set-aside the judgment and 
order passed by the District Magistrate and 
have remanded back the matter to be heard 
after taking into consideration the directions 
given in the remand order.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that Section 6 of the ‘Goondas Act’
does not empower the Competent 
Authority/Commissioner to remand back thematter. It is further contended that the 
Commissioner may either confirm the order, 
with or without modification, or set it aside, 
and may, pending disposal of the appeal, stay 
the operation of the order subject to such 
terms, if any, as he thinks fit. On this basis, 
learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the ‘Goondas Act’ itself is amply 
clear that the power of remand is not available 
with the Commissioner and thus the said 
order is not sustainable in the eyes of law. In 
support of his contention, learned counsel for 
the petitioner has relied upon a judgment 
passed by Hon’ble High Court of Judicature at 
Allahabad in Criminal Misc. Writ Petition No. 
2700 of 2026, Anil Chaudhary Vs. State of 
Uttar Pradesh and Others, wherein, Hon’ble 
High Court of Judicature at Allahabad, in a 
similar matter, has held that the 
Commissioner has no power to remand back 
the matter while hearing the appeal under the 
‘Goondas Act’.
6. Per contra, learned counsel for 
respondent no. 2 submits that the Writ 
Petition itself is not maintainable, inasmuch 
as, if any person, who is aggrieved by the 
impugned order, is the State and the State 
has not challenged the order passed by the 
Commissioner.
7. Arguments, as advanced by learned 
counsel for respondent no. 2, cannot be 
accepted for the simple reason that the writ 
petitioner was the complainant and she has 
participated in the proceedings initiated 
before the District Magistrate as well as 
Appellate Court. As such, she is the aggrieved 
party and she has a right to appeal.
8. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.9. After hearing learned counsel for the 
parties and considering all the facts available 
on record, this Court prima facie is in 
conformity with the judgment passed by 
Hon’ble Allahabad High Court as Section 6 of 
the ‘Goonda Act’ only vests the appellate 
authority the jurisdiction to either confirm the 
order, with or without modification, or to set it 
aside but the appellate authority cannot 
remand the matter back to the District 
Magistrate for deciding it afresh on merits
under Section 3/4 of the ‘Goonda Act’. As 
such, as an interim measure, it is directed 
that till the next date of listing, the effect and 
operation of the judgment and order dated 
18.07.2026 passed by learned Commissioner, 
Garhwal Region, Camp Dehradun in Appeal 
No. 03 of 2026 shall remain stayed.
10. Learned counsel for the respondent no. 2 
prays for and is granted two weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit.
11. List this matter on 22.09.2026.
12. Interim Relief Application (IA No. 1 of 
2026) stands disposed of accordingly.
