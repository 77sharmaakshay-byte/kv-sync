Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Sanjay Bhatt, learned counsel with 
Mr. Devashish Rana, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Dinesh Kumar Bankoti, learned 
Brief Holder for the State.
3. The order impugned rejecting the claim 
of the petitioner for seeking appointment on 
compassionate ground cannot sustain, since, 
same is rejected merely on the ground that 
the petitioner’s mother is serving as “Shiksha 
Mitra”.
4. It is submitted by the learned counsel 
for the petitioner that the appointment of the 
petitioner’s mother firstly has nothing to do 
with the claim of the petitioner for seeking 
appointment on compassionate ground; 
secondly, the engagement of the petitioner’s 
mother is not a permanent assignment.
5. Prima facie, the order impugned is 
wholly unjust and improper and cannot 
sustain since the same has been rejected on 
the ground which is neither reasonable nor 
justifiable. In such view of the matter let the 
Authority may re-examine the issue and
apprise to this Court on the next date, failing 
which the concerned Authority shall join the 
proceeding through VC.
6. List this case on 05.11.2026.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
