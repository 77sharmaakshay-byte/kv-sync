21. It is a case of alleged sexual assault by real uncle on 
his niece. In the case of sexual assault, the sole testimony of 
prosecutrix may be basis for conviction provided it is fully reliable. 
In all cases, it is not insisted that the statement of prosecutrix 
should get corroboration, but it depends upon the credibility and 
the level of confidence which such statement inspires. In other 
cases, when the statement of victim is not totally reliable, the court 
seeks for corroboration.
9UKHC010151732024
2026:UHC:8474-DB
22. It is also settled law that in such cases of sexual 
assault, it is not expected that the FIR is always prompt. The 
family members at times take time to consider before lodging a 
report. The court also takes into notice the trauma which a victim 
of sexual assault generally undergoes. At times, it may not be 
possible for her to reveal the incident, particularly when she is 
under threat or for other variety of social reasons.
23. In the instant case, this Court proceed to examine the 
testimony of PW-1 the victim to appreciate as to whether it is fully 
reliable evidence and if not, does it find corroboration in material 
particulars, for example, from forensic or medical examination.
