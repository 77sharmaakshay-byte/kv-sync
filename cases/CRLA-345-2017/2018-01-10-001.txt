 Mr. S.S. Adhikari, A.G.A. with Mr.

 Heard on the bail application. 
 By the impugned judgment and 
order, the accused/appellant Amit Gupta 
@ Amit Vashrney has been convicted 
under Section 3(1) of U.P. Gangsters Act 
and has been sentenced to five years’ 
rigorous imprisonment along with fine of 
Rs.50,000/-. 
 This is the second bail application 
moved on behalf of the appellant. Vide 
order dated 14.12.2017, first bail 
application was disposed of by this Court, 
whereby the appellant was granted short 
term bail for a period of three weeks. 
 Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that the appellant was on bail 
during trial and he has never misused the 
liberty of bail granted to him. He also 
submits that the co-accused have been 
granted regular bail by this Court vide 
order dated 29.12.2017 and the appellant 
is entitled for bail on the ground of parity. 
 Having considered the submissions 
of learned counsel for the appellant, 
without expressing any opinion as to the 
final merits of the case, I am of the view 
that the appellant should be enlarged on 
bail during the pendency of present 
criminal appeal. 
 Accordingly, bail application is 
allowed.  Let the appellant be released on bail 
on his executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two sureties each of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of court 
concerned. 
 Deposition of 50% fine shall be 
condition precedent for appellant’s release 
on bail. 
 It is directed that the Magistrate, 
while taking personal bonds, shall obtain 
photographs of the appellant and his 
identity proof on the personal bonds. 
While taking sureties, the Magistrate shall 
ensure that the latest photographs of the 
sureties along with their proper identity 
proof be affixed. The Magistrate shall also 
obtain duplicate copy of the papers 
regarding personal bonds and sureties 
and shall send one set of the same to this 
Court within a period of 15 days so as to 
keep the same in the file of appeal. 
 
(Lok Pal Singh, J.)
