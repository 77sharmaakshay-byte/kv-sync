As against the partial decreeing of a 
Suit by the Court of Civil Judge (J.D.), 
Almora, by the judgment dated 25.04.2019 as 
rendered in Suit No. 42 of 2018 ‘Manoj Singh 
Kanwal and Others vs. Deewan Singh 
Dhaporal and Others’ the defendants to the 
Suit had preferred a First Appeal No. 6 of 
2019 ‘Deewan Singh and Others vs. Manoj 
Singh and Another’. 
The learned Court of District Judge, 
Almora, by virtue of the impugned judgment 
under challenge has set aside the judgment of 
the learned Trial Court and as a consequence 
thereto, had dismissed the Suit preferred by 
the plaintiffs (appellants herein) for 
declaration of the sale deed as to be null and 
void and also for the grant of decree of 
mandatory injunction. 
After having heard the learned counsel 
for the plaintiffs (appellants herein), this 
second appeal is admitted on substantial 
question no. 3 and amended substantial 
question no. 1, which reads as under: 
“(iii) Whether the learned appellate 
court committed error of law by 
not appreciating that the disputed 
property had been described with 
boundaries and which was 
identifiable between the parties 
and no question of non￾identification was ever raised by 
the respondents as well as no issue 
was framed. 
(i) Whether the first appellate court 
was justified to allow the appeal 
by rejecting the relief sought by 
the appellants/plaintiffs regarding 
cancellation of sale deed executed 
by respondents/ defendants 
pertaining to Khasra No. 2181 as well as relief of the permanent 
injunction while the appellants/ 
plaintiffs are having title and 
possession of same.” 
Summon the lower court record. 
In the meantime, till the next date of 
listing during the pendency of the second 
appeal the parties are restrained from dealing 
with the property in any manner, whatsoever.
