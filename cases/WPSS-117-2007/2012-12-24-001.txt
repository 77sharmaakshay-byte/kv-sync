This writ petition has been filed 
against the impugned order of rejection for 
compassionate appointment. 
The petitioner’s mother applied for 
petitioner’s appointment on death in 
harness category. The petitioner’s father 
died in harness indisputably. The order of 
rejections says that the petitioner’s case 
would not be considered because this case 
falls below 40 percent benchmark. On 
verification, it was found that benchmark 
could be 35% or 36%. 
Having gone through the counter 
affidavit, impugned order and other 
documents, it appears to me that this 
decision has been taken on the basis of 
vague idea. There is no basis to adjudge 
and assess the marks. On what basis, the 
benchmark could be arrived at is not spelt 
2012:UHC:7988out. No Rule has been shown before me to 
justify the rejection of the claim of the 
petitioner. The fact remains that the 
appointment in case of death in harness is 
allowable under Rules. Under what 
circumstances it is allowed, is not clear 
before me. 
Accordingly, the impugned order is 
set aside. The writ petition is allowed. 
The respondents are directed to 
reconsider the matter with Rules with 
published norms and guidelines. This shall 
be done within a period of three months 
from the date of communication of this 
order.
