COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPCRL No. 838 of 2025
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. for the 
State.
3. Mr. Arvind Vashistha, learned Senior 
counsel assisted by Mr. Rachit Mullick, learned 
counsel for NIA.
4. The petitioner, in compliance to the 
previous order, is present in Court. On the 
previous date, the petitioner was directed to 
handover his mobile with SIM to the S.S.P. 
concerned, but he has not complied with the 
order and this is the reason he was asked to 
appear in person. 
5. Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for the 
petitioner submit that since 2004, the 
petitioner is provided regular security by the 
State at his own expenses but in last five 
months he has no security and still there is a 
threat. In para-19 of the writ petition, a 
specific averment has been made that there is 
a continuous threat to the life of the petitioner 
and his family members, as the petitioner has 
received messages from a Pakistani number 
starting from +92 money has sought to be 
extorted from the petitioner. The petitioner 
reported to the higher police officials but no 
attempt was made to provide security to the petitioner. Certain WhatsApp messages have 
also been enclosed as Annexure No. -29 which 
is in Gurumukhi language, the translation of 
which is also enclosed at page no.99 which 
reads as under:-
 “ Rahul Bhatia had explained to you 
before that to become a man’s son, do 
what you are told to do! If you do not 
arrange 5 crores in the next 20 days and 
arrange your afterlife.” 
6. Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for the 
petitioner also submits that the petitioner has
some audio recording of the conversation
wherein the petitioner was abused and also 
given threatening. 
7. If the petitioner is receiving any regular 
threat from Pakistani number, then it is 
serious issue. However, at this juncture 
merely submitting a complaint is not 
sufficient. The petitioner at least should
handover his mobile to the Investigating 
Agencies but no such effort has been made 
and straightway the petitioner has filed this 
petition on 25.07.2025. 
8. Be that as it may, receiving threat from 
Pakistani national to an Indian citizen is really 
a very serious issue. Therefore, the petitioner 
who is present in Court is directed to 
handover his mobile set in which he has 
received the threat from Pakistani national to 
the S.S.P. concerned by tomorrow. After 
receiving the mobile set of the petitioner, the 
S.S.P. concerned shall immediately constitute 
a team of three officials with the experts of 
cyber crime and examine whether there is any 
threat from Pakistani national or not. In 
addition to this, the S.S.P. shall give the task 
to the committee to enquire about day to day 
activities of the petitioner and place the report before this Court after a month.
9. After receiving the mobile phone of the 
petitioner, the S.S.P. concerned may clone the 
device and return back the to the petitioner. 
Apart from this, the S.S.P. concerned shall 
also personally assess any threat perception 
to the petitioner from Pakistani national and if 
he finds that the petitioner has any threat 
perception, then he may take appropriate 
steps to secure the life and liberty of the 
petitioner and his family members. 
10. The matter is being kept pending. 
taking into consideration that the issue is very 
sensitive and is being clubbed with another 
petition i.e. WPCRL No. 737 of 2025 in which 
the State is also directed to review all the 
matter in which the security has been 
provided.
11. The S.S.P. shall place before this Court 
the status report in a sealed envelope on the 
next date fixed.
12. List along with WPCRL No. 737 of 2025.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
