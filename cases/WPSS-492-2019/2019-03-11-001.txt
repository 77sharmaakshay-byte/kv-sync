Heard. 
Issue notice to respondent No. 5 
returnable at an early date. 
According to the petitioner, he is 
serving as Class IV employee on daily wage 
basis in a Govt. aided educational institution, 
namely, Kanhaiya Lal Polytechnic, since 
27.11.2006. He is aggrieved by termination of 
his service vide order dated 31.01.2019. The 
only ground taken for terminating services of 
the petitioner is that State Government has 
issued instructions to engage employees 
through outsourcing only. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that respondent no. 4 has 
completely misinterpreted the Government 
Order dated 27.04.2018. He further submits 
that State has to act as a model employer; 
therefore, termination of service of the 
petitioner, after 13 years of service, under the 
pretext of Government Order is not justified. 
Learned counsels for the respondents 
pray for and are allowed four weeks’ time for 
filing counter affidavits. Thereafter, 
petitioner may file rejoinder affidavit(s), if 
any, within one week. 
List this matter on 29.04.2019. 
Till the next date of listing, operation of 
the order dated 31.01.2019 shall remain 
stayed. 
Interim Relief Application (IA No. 3075 
of 2019) stands disposed of.
