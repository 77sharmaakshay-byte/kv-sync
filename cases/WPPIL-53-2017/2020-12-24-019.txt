This petition is filed in public interest seeking for a 
writ, order or direction in the nature of a mandamus to 
direct the respondent no.1 to take appropriate action 
against the private respondents from carrying out the 
illegal construction activity at the property-in-question 
for demolition of illegal construction and consequential 
reliefs.
Notices were issued. Various counter affidavits 
have been filed.2
The plea of the petitioner is that an order was 
passed under Section 28 of the Uttar Pradesh Urban 
Planning and Development Act, 1973 for sealing of the 
property-in-question on the ground of putting up 
construction in violation of the building plan. Various 
other orders have also been issued subsequent to the 
order dated 10.09.2015. At that point of time the 
building was at the stage of RCC columns were being 
put in the basement floor. Showing disrespect to the 
orders, the private respondents have continued the 
construction and have virtually completed the entire 
building. 
It is the further case of the petitioner that political 
force was used in order to protect the respondents in 
putting up the construction.
Counter affidavits have been filed by the concerned 
respondents. The respondent no.2 through their counter 
affidavit, have narrated the facts. They have indicated 
that a sealing order had been issued on 10.09.2015. 
However, construction still continued. Yet another 
sealing order was passed on 11.04.2016 and a series of 
orders have been passed thereafter. All the orders have 3
been disobeyed by the respondents. They have not 
respected the orders of the authority.
The respondent nos.5 to 8 have also filed their 
counter affidavits. They have stated that they have 
made an application to the second respondent seeking 
to regularize the unauthorized construction before the 
second respondent. The same is pending consideration. 
Therefore, it is pleaded that the court refrain itself from 
proceeding further in the matter while directing the 
respondent no.2 to consider their case for regularization. 
Presently, the learned counsel for respondent no.2 
submits that such an application has been rejected in 
terms of the order dated 18.12.2020. Therefore, no 
proceedings are pending before the concerned 
respondents. No regularization has been ordered.
On the previous date, the learned counsel for 
respondent nos.5 and 6 submitted that he will file an 
affidavit that he will pull down the building by 
themselves. On the previous occasion substantial 
arguments were advanced. It primarily came on record 
that in spite of the sealing order issued in the year 
2015, the entire building has been put up. Therefore, 4
the counsel for respondent nos.5 and 6 submitted that
rather than the authority pulling down the building he 
may be given an opportunity to bring the building in 
accordance with the rules and by-laws. An affidavit has 
been filed by respondent no.5 on behalf of respondent 
no.6 and this is all he has to say in para 2 as under:-
“2. That the deponent hereby undertake to 
rectify the existing construction of this building as 
per the norms of H.R.D.A.”.
On considering the affidavit we do not find any 
reason to accept the same. The affidavit is not a bona 
fide affidavit. On the previous occasion, it was submitted 
that all the partners have a stake in the building. 
Therefore, the learned counsel for respondent nos.5 and 
6 submitted that an affidavit will be filed on behalf of all 
the partners or the entire firm. The same has not been 
done. It is not filed on behalf of all the partners, but only 
on behalf of respondent nos.5 and 6. The undertaking 
given is to rectify the existing construction of this
building as per the norms of the H.R.D.A. We put it to 
the learned counsel that by using different words he 
cannot save himself from the illegal acts committed by 
him. The illegal construction put up by him is ostensibly 5
admitted in view of the regularisation sought for. The 
regularisation application has also been dismissed which 
has been suppressed before this Court by the 
respondent nos.5 and 6. The information regarding the 
rejection is brought on notice only by the learned 
counsel appearing for respondent no.2.
Subsequent to the contentions being advanced on 
the previous dates, it was specifically informed to the 
private respondents that the building has to be brought 
in a position as it existed as on the date of sealing. This 
was so because once the property has been sealed, 
nothing further could be done regarding construction 
therein. Therefore, all such constructions, subsequent to 
the order of sealing, have been carried out not only in 
gross disobedience and in violation of the order of 
sealing, but by blatantly violating the law.
Therefore, there is nothing else that remains to 
be considered in this petition. The sealing order has 
been violated. There is scant disrespect to the orders of 
the authority. Forcible and unauthorised constructions 
have been put up not only in violation of the laws 
but also in violation of the sealing order. Therefore, 
all such constructions, subsequent to the order of 6
sealing, are illegal constructions. The illegality would 
have to be brought in consonance with law.
Even though the learned counsel for respondent 
nos.5 and 6 submitted that the same will be done and 
stated that they will file an affidavit in this regard, the 
same has not been done. What they have stated in the 
affidavit is to rectify the existing construction as per the 
norms of H.R.D.A. When the construction of the entire 
building is itself illegal, the question of bringing it within 
the norms of H.R.D.A. cannot be accepted. This is so 
because the sealing order was passed when the building 
was at the basement stage. However, at present, the 
building has a number of floors to it. Therefore, all 
constructions, subsequent to the sealing order, are 
illegal. Therefore, we have no other option but to direct 
respondent no.2 to demolish the structure and bring it 
to the position that existed on the date of the sealing 
order namely as on 10.09.2015. 
The respondent no.2 submits that he requires two 
weeks’ time to execute this order. Hence, respondent 
no.2 is granted two weeks’ time to comply with the 
directions in this order. At the request of respondent 
no.2, the District Magistrate and the concerned police 7
officials shall offer adequate protection to respondent 
no.2 to execute this order.
Call after two weeks to report compliance.
