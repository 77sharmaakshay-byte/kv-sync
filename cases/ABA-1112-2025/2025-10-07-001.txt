This Application has been filed for anticipatory bail 
in Case Crime No.415 of 2025, registered at Police Station 
Gangnahar, District Haridwar under Sections 120B, 420, 467, 
468, 471 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 and Sections 111, 
351 and Section 352 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.
2. According to the First Information Report dated
27.08.2025, lodged by Nand Kishor Bhatt, a forged power of 
attorney of the real owner Smt. Rekha was prepared by the 
applicant and other co-accused persons. On the basis of the 
said forged power of attorney, the co-accused Pankaj Astwal 
sold the properties of Smt. Rekha to several persons.
3. Heard Mr. Gaurav Singh, learned counsel for the 
applicant and Mr. Akshay Latwal, learned Assistant 
Government Advocate for the respondent.
4. Mr. Gaurav Singh, Advocate submitted that the 
applicant had not forged any such document. He was neither 2
the seller nor the witness. Ankit Kumar, the co-accused of 
the similar role, has been granted interim bail in Anticipatory 
Bail Application No.1027 of 2025.
5. Mr. Akshay Latwal, Assistant Government Advocate,
has sought time to file objection to the anticipatory bail 
application. 
6. Time is granted. 
7. Having heard the learned counsel for the parties and 
in the facts and circumstances of the case, as an ad-interim 
measure, it is directed that in the event of arrest of the 
applicant-Rajkumar, he shall be released on interim bail on 
executing a personal bond of Rs.30,000/- and two reliable 
sureties, each of the like amount, to the satisfaction of the 
Investigating Officer/Arresting Officer, subject to the following 
conditions:-
(i) Applicant shall cooperate with the Investigating 
Agency and he shall make himself available for 
interrogation by a police officer as and when required; 
(ii) Applicant shall not directly or indirectly make any 
inducement, threat or promise to any person 
acquainted with the facts of this case;
(iii) Applicant shall not leave the country without the 
previous permission of this Court. 
8. List on 16.10.2025 along with the record of 
Anticipatory Bail Application No.1027 of 2025 for hearing on 
the anticipatory bail application.
