The present Contempt Petition is listed 
on number of applications. The first 
application, which is listed today is 
miscellaneous application being MCC No. 
1365/2018, whereby, the applicant has sought 
recall of the order dated 13.09.2018 passed by 
the Coordinate Bench of this Court, by virtue of 
which the Contempt Petition has been disposed 
of. 
The reason for recall as pleaded in the 
application is that the manner in which the 
disposal of the Contempt Petition was made 
based on the compliance affidavit filed by the 
respondent, as a matter of fact, it was not a 
compliance because the judgment of the learned 
Single Judge, which has been affirmed by the 
Division Bench with the slight modification has 
not been complied with in its letter and spirit 
and, thus, a recall has been sought for of the 
judgment dated 13.09.2018. 
For the reason and purpose of seeking 
recall of the order, he submits that the 
advertisement referred to in the compliance 
affidavit, since has not included within it 
inviting applications of those candidates, who 
were having B.Ed. qualification and since the 
advertisement did not include the B.Ed. decree 
holders and was not an advertisement in 
relation to all the vacancies, which was the 
subject matter of controversy in the Writ Petition, the compliance affidavit ought not to 
have been taken as to be the compliance 
affidavit and the Contempt Petition deserved to 
be decided on merits. 
Considering the grounds taken in the 
recall application, this Court is satisfied that the 
Contempt Petition requires a consideration on 
its merit. Hence, recall application is allowed. 
The order dated 13.09.2018 is recalled and the 
Contempt Petition is restored to its original 
number. 
The delay condonation application filed 
in support of the compliance affidavit is 
allowed. Compliance affidavit is taken on 
record. 
Urgency Applications listed today would 
too stand disposed of with the direction that this 
Contempt Petition may be listed along with 
WPSS No. 182/2018 in the second week of 
January, 2019.
