IA 1 of 2022 (Delay Condonation Application)
3. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
on the application. 
4. As per the office report, there is a 
delay of 129 days in filing the present 
leave to appeal application. 
5. The State has challenged the 
judgment and order dated 14.06.2022 
passed by learned Third Additional 
Sessions Judge, Haridwar in S.T. No. 377 
of 2014 (Yusuf Vs. Vazir and others). 
6. A perusal of the affidavit filed in 
support of the delay condonation 
application reveals that the delay had 
occasioned in obtaining permission from 
the State Government. In our opinion, the 
delay appears to be procedural in nature, 
which has sufficiently been explained. 
Accordingly, for the reasons indicated in 
the affidavit, we are inclined to allow the 
application seeking condonation of delay. 
The same is allowed. Delay is condoned. 2
SPLA No. 256 of 2022
7. Having heard the learned State 
Counsel as well as learned counsel 
appearing for respondents-accused, we are 
satisfied that the matter requires hearing 
as certain evidence, led by the 
prosecution, requires to be re-assessed. In 
such view of the matter, the application 
seeking leave to appeal is allowed.
