COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 2364 of 2023
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Mahavir Kohli, Advocate for the 
petitioner.
Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, Additional C.S.C. 
for the State/respondent nos. 1 to 7.
The petitioner claims that he was retired 
on 31.01.2022 from Group-D post, but he has 
not been paid any of his retiral dues.
Learned State counsel would submit that 
there is some dispute with regard to the 
amount that is payable.
Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that in para 7 of its counter affidavit, 
the State has admitted that the gratuity is to be 
paid to the petitioner. He seeks time to file 
rejoinder affidavit seeking further direction to 
the respondents to release his gratuity.
List on 20.05.2025 for final hearing. 
Meanwhile, the respondents shall release 
the gratuity, which the respondents admits to 
be payable to the petitioner. It shall be so paid 
within four weeks from today. If it is not done, 
the Court would initiate stringent action 
against the respondent no.5 Executive 
Engineer, Provincial Division, Public Works 
Department, Pauri, District Pauri Garhwal. In 
case, the respondent no.5 fails, the Court shall 
consider it as his personal failure and an 
appropriate action shall be taken against him.
In case, the gratuity amount is not 
deposited in the account of the petitioner 
within three weeks, the petitioner shall be at 
liberty to mention the case before this Court.
Let a copy of this order be forward to the 
respondent no.5, Executive Engineer, Provincial 
Division, Public Works Department, Pauri, 
District Pauri Garhwal.
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
