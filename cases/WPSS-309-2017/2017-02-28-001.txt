WPSS No. 309 of 2017
Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate, present for 
the petitioner. 
 Mr. Tej Singh Bisht, Central Government 
Standing Counsel, present for the Union of 
India/respondent No.1. 
 The petitioner claims to have been working in 
Army Public School, Almora on contractual basis for 
the last 10 years. Now a fresh advertisement has been 
issued inter alia calling for application forms from 
eligible candidates for the same post on which the 
petitioner is working. These appointments are again to 
be made on contractual basis. 
 According to the petitioner, since he is 
serving for the last 10 years, his services are liable to 
be regularized and in order to deny the petitioner’s 
regularization, yet again the Army Public School, 
Almora resorted to filling up the vacancy by way of 
contractual employment, which is not fair. 
 As an interim measure, it is provided that the 
selection process may go but no appointment letters be 
issued for the post of Lab Attendant on which the 
petitioner is working, until further orders of this Court. 
Issue notices to respondent Nos. 2 to 5. 
 Steps to be taken within a week. 
 Three weeks’ time is granted to the 
respondents to file counter affidavit. 
 List this matter after four weeks in the daily 
cause list. 
 It is further made clear that until further 
orders of this Court petitioner’s services shall also 
continue. 
 Interim relief application (CLMA No.1642 of 
2017) stands disposed.
