 Heard on Interim Relief Application 
No.1 of 2025.
 The petitioner responded an 
advertisement for appointment on the post 
of Assistant Teacher, LT Grade. He was 
declared successful and was 
recommended for his posting in Kumaon 
Region. But, he has not been offered 
appointment by the respondent no.3, 
Additional Director, Elementary 
Education, Kumaon Mandal, Nainital on 
the ground that the petitioner has not 
brought the permanent resident certificate 
of State Authority. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that there was no such requirement of permanent resident 
certificate. He referred to Clause 13 of the 
advertisement as well as a communication 
dated 23.09.2024 of the Commission 
addressed to the Additional Director, 
Education, Kumaon Mandal. The 
recommendation has been made as the 
case of the petitioner was covered by the 
guidelines.
 Having considered, as an interim 
measure, till the next date of listing one 
post of Assistant Teacher, LT Grade, shall
be kept vacant. 
 Interim relief application stands 
disposed of accordingly.
