These are the cases pertaining to 
premature release of the convicts in accordance 
with the State Policy of 2022.
It is the grievance of the petitioners that 
despite being eligible for premature release, 
their cases have not been considered for 
premature release.
On 25.05.2026, when this matter was 
taken up, on behalf of the State Government, it 
was argued that committee considering the 
remission had held meeting on 22.05.2026 
and had recommended eight persons for 
premature release. On that date, it was 
assured that there are 40 other cases, 
including the cases of the petitioners which 
would be taken up in near future.
On 25.05.2026, under instructions, on 
behalf of the State, learned State counsel gave 
an assurance that cases of the petitioners for 
premature release shall be considered within two weeks.
Today, learned State counsel would 
submit that the names of the petitioners were 
considered, but only two names were 
recommended for premature release and 
names of Nihal Singh, Pawan and Sukhdev 
Singh have not been considered for premature 
release.
Learned State counsel tendered the 
instructions which he received. Let it be taken 
on record.
The proceeding records that in view of the 
report of the Superintendent of Police and the 
District Magistrate, the cases of Nihal Singh, 
Pawan and Sukhdev Singh were not 
recommended for premature release.
The Court wanted to know from learned 
State counsel, as to under what clause of the 
Policy of 2022, such recommendation has a 
final bearing on the premature release of the 
convicts.
Learned State counsel submits that it is 
not in the Policy.
The question is that the premature 
release is to be ensured in accordance with the 
Policy of 2022, which is not done in the case? 
Why? Who is responsible for it?
The Court contemplated to seek response 
from the Committee for such action, which 
appears to be dehors the Policy of 2022. But, 
then learned State counsel submits that he 
may be given 24 hours time to convey the 
correct legal position so that the cases may be 
considered in accordance with the Policy of 
2022 and the settled law.
