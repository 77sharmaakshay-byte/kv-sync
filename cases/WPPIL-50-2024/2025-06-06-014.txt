2. Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Deputy Advocate 
General for the State. 
3. Learned Deputy Advocate General has placed 
a compilation detailing the names of 140 prisoners 
who are eligible for consideration under the policy 
of the Government for premature release. Against 
the first three names in the compilation, it is stated 
that they have been eligible for consideration under 
the Government Order since 2019, 2020 and 2021. 
It is a travesty of justice that despite their eligibility 
to be considered as way back as in 2019 and 2020, 
i.e. for more than five years, they are languishing 
in jail. The lethargy cannot be appreciated. The 
National Legal Services Authority, the State Legal 
Authority and the District Legal Services Authorities 
have been repeatedly making efforts to make the 
process more effective and speed-up the releases, 
yet we find the State is lacking in efforts resulting 
in the persons eligible for premature release 
languishing in prisons for more that 05-06 years.4. In that view, we direct that the Competent 
Authority/ Board, for consideration of the prisoners 
for premature release, shall be constituted within 
two weeks from today and commence the process 
of consideration of the cases of the prisoners, more 
particularly, list of the prisoners placed before this 
Court today. In the event, the exercise is not 
started in right earnest, the same would be viewed 
seriously.
5. List on 20.06.2025.
