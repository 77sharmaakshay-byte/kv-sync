1. Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned Chief Standing
Counsel along with Mr. J.S. Virk, learned

respondents.

Nitin Sharma, Principal Secretary (L.R.),

examine the file keeping in view the

22.03.2024, at 10:30 A.M. 
5. This gesture of both the officers is being
appreciated by this Court that they will do 
such exercise by 05:00 P.M. today.
