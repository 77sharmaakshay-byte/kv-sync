By means of present writ petition, the 
petitioner seeks following reliefs:- 
To quash the order dated 17.08.2017 
passed by Deputy Director Consolidation/ 
Additional Collector, Haridwar in Revision 
No.117/2015-16, Sukhbir Singh and others vs. 
Balbeer Singh & others consolidated with 
Revision No.72/2014-15, Mahavir Singh & 
others vs. Balbeer Singh & others. 
Supplementary affidavit is taken on 
record. 
Miscellaneous application with 
supplementary affidavit (IA No.4403 of 2017) 
is, therefore, disposed of. 
As prayed, two weeks’ time is granted 
to the private respondent to file counter 
affidavit. 
After hearing learned counsel for the 
parties and having gone through the 
documents brought on record, it is provided as 
an interim measure that parties shall maintain 
status quo regarding 3rd chak in question. 1st
and 2nd chak are not disputed. 
Miscellaneous application (CLMA 
No.10952 of 2017) stands disposed of.
