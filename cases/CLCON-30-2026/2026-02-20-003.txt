Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The petitioner had filed a Writ Petition (S/S) 
No.920 of 2020 with the following prayers:-
“(i) Issue a writ, order or direction, quashing 
the impugned the suspension order dated 
23.08.2007 passed by the respondent no. 3 
(contained as Annexure No. 1 to this writ 
petition), the termination order dated 
05.11.2007 passed by the respondent no. 3 
(contained as Annexure No. 5 to this writ 
petition), inquiry report dated 04.11.2007 
(contained as Annexure No. 7 to this writ 
petition) and order/letter dated 11.01.2020 
passed by the respondent no. 3 (contained 
as Annexure No. 10 to this writ petition). 
(ii) Issue a writ order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing the 
respondent reinstate the petitioner on the 
post of Lab Technician B.Pharma along full 
back wages.”
2. The Writ Petition (WPSS No.920 of 2020) 
was disposed of on 06.10.2025. The Co-ordinate 
Bench passed the Order:-
“6. In view of the submission as advanced 
by the learned counsel for the parties, the 
instant writ petition is disposed of in terms of 
the judgment rendered in WPSB Nos. 27, 21 
and 76 of 2008.”
3. Heard Mr. Tapan Singh, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
4. Amended memo of parties is taken on 
record.5. Mr. Tapan Singh, Advocate, submitted that 
the respondents restored the services of the 
petitioner in the pay level – 5 (in the pay scale of 
Rs.29200-92300) with other applicable 
allowances, but order dated 06.10.2025 has not 
been deliberately fully complied with. The 
petitioner has not been granted full back wages.
6. Notice is being issued to the respondent 
no.4. 
7. The respondent no.4 is directed to appear 
before this Court through video conferencing on 
19.03.2026 at 10:30 a.m. and show cause why 
contempt proceedings should not be initiated 
against him.
8. Mr. Tapan Singh, Advocate, has sought three 
days’ time to take steps. 
9. List on 19.03.2026 at 10:30 a.m.
