JUDGMENT :(per Mr. G. Narendar, C.J.)
Delay Condonation Application (IA No.01 of 2024)
For the reasons stated in the Application seeking 
condonation of delay, the delay in preferring the aforementioned 
Special Appeal is condoned. Accordingly, the Application (IA 
No.01 and 2024) seeking condonation of delay, stands allowed.
2. So far as the above special appeal is concerned, it is
seen that the basis for termination of the petitioner’s/ 
appellant’s services, is the letter dated 25.05.2018, issued by 
the Chaudhary Charan Singh University, Meerut, which has 
stated that B.Ed. degree/ mark-sheet does not tally with the 
enrolment and confidential record maintained by the said 
University. In the absence of challenge to this foundational fact, 
we are unable to comprehend as to how any relief can be
granted to a consequential order of removal which, admittedly, 
is premised on the opinion of the University.
3. It is also not in dispute that the letter was issued

pursuant to the inquiry by the Special Investigation Team 
constituted for unearthing such unjustified/ illegal 
appointments.
4. In that view, learned counsel for the appellant prays
leave to withdraw the instant special appeal with liberty to file 
fresh petition calling in question the letter of the University 
dated 25.05.2018.
5. Reserving such liberty, the special appeal is dismissed 
as withdrawn.
6. There shall be no order as to costs. 
7. Pending application, if any, also stands disposed of 
accordingly.
