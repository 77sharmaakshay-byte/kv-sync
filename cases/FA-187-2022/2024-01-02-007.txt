FA No.187 of 2022
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate for the 
appellant.
2. Mr. D.S. Mehta, Advocate for respondent 
no.1.
3. Mr. Ravnit Malhotra, Advocate for 
respondent no.2 through V.C.
4. Learned counsel for the appellant would 
submit that on the last date of hearing i.e. on 
04.09.2023 this Court had recorded the 
undertaking given by respondent nos.1 and 2 that 
they will not prosecute the execution proceedings 
in Execution Case No.6/2023 till the next date of 
hearing and had fixed the case for 08.12.2023, 
however, on 08.12.2023 the Bench was not 
available; that, after 08.12.2023 the respondents 
have started pursuing the executing case in 
breach of their solemn undertaking given in the 
Court. 
5. Learned counsel for respondent nos.1 and 2 
would fairly admit the above submissions made 
by counsel for the appellant but at the same time 
would submit that by the same order i.e. 
04.09.2023 the appellant was also directed to 
submit the accounts of the total receipts and the 
profits made from the property from the date of 
MoU till 31.10.2023 by way of affidavit with 
advance copy to the counsel for the respondent 
nos.1 and 2 but when the appellant did not 
comply with this direction till 08.12.2023, the 
respondents were constrained to pursue the execution proceedings.
6. To this, learned counsel for the appellant 
would submit that due to some unavoidable 
circumstances the appellant could not submit the 
affidavit of accounts till 08.12.2023 and this fact 
could not be brought into the notice of the Bench 
because of non-availability of Bench on 
08.12.2023.
7. At this stage, learned counsel for 
respondent nos.1 and 2 would submit that the 
appellant is sitting in the property of respondent 
nos.1 and 2 and earning profits without 
complying with the direction given in order dated 
04.09.2023. They would, however, submit that
they are ready to give fresh undertaking that the 
respondent nos.1 and 2 will not pursue the 
Execution Case No.6/2023 till the next date of 
hearing. 
8. The undertaking given above is accepted. 
9. List on the date already fixed i.e. 
15.03.2024. 
10. The appellant is directed to file affidavit, as 
directed in para 8 of order dated 04.09.2023, 
within a week with advance copy to the 
respondents by registered post and email as well 
as to Mr. D.S. Mehta, counsel for respondent, if 
available in the Court. 
10. It is clarified that if the above affidavit is 
not filed by the appellant within the stipulated 
time, the respondents shall be discharged from 
the undertaking made in the Court today.
11. Urgency Application IA No.8/2023 is 
disposed of.
