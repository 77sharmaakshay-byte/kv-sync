WPSS No. 1173 of 2022
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J.
Mr. Sanjay Bhatt, Advocate for the 
petitioner.
Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, Advocate 
for the respondents.
Heard learned counsel for the 
parties.
Petitioner is a Group ‘D’ employee 
in G.B. Pant University of Agriculture 
and Technology, Pant Nagar. According 
to petitioner, her date of birth is 
10.12.1973, which has been duly 
recorded in her service book, however, 
respondent authorities have unilaterally 
changed her date of birth, as 
27.07.1962. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
points out that a letter was sent by 
Establishment Officer, G.B. Pant 
University of Agriculture and 
Technology, Pant Nagar to District 
Magistrate, Kushinagar, Uttar Pradesh 
on 13.08.2021, for getting petitioner’s 
date of birth verified from the 
concerned authorities and, without waiting for the report of the concerned 
authorities, the impugned order has 
been passed, changing her date of birth 
from 10.12.1973 to 27.07.1962.
It is settled position in law that 
date of birth cannot be changed in 
respect of government employee at the 
fag end of his career. In the present 
case, the respondent authorities have 
changed the date of birth of the 
petitioner at the fag end of her career, 
that too, without waiting for the report 
of District Magistrate, Kushinagar.
As prayed, four weeks’ time is 
granted to learned counsel for the 
respondents to file counter affidavit. 
List thereafter.
Considering the submission 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
parties, it is directed that, till the next 
date of listing, effect & operation of the 
impugned order dated 19.05.2022, 
shall remain stayed.
Stay application (I.A. No. 1 of 
2022) stands disposed of.
