Mr. Raman Kumar Shah, Advocate for the

Heard.
Admit the revision.
Issue notice to the respondent No. 1.
Steps to be taken within a period of 10 days from
today.
An issue, which has been raised by the learned 
counsel for the revisionist is to the effect that if there is 
a decree under Section 9 of the Hindu Marriage Act, 
1955, in favour of the revisionist and despite of the 
decree, if the respondent-wife is not discharging her 
matrimonial obligations, then whether she would be 
entitled for the grant of maintenance under Section 125 
Cr.PC or not. 
This ratio as sought to be pleaded and pressed by 
the learned counsel for the revisionist is supported by 
the judgement reported in 2010 (1) NCC, Ganesh 
Singh Nagarkoti v. State and other.
In view of the aforesaid ratio, the effect and 
operation of the impugned judgement dated 18.10.2019, 
as passed by the Court of Family Court, Kotdwar, Pauri 
Garhwal in Suit No. 101 of 2015, Seema v. Anil 
Nawani, determining the maintenance @ 2500/- per 
month would be continued to be remitted by the 
revisionist, subject to the condition that the award of the 
amount from the date of application till the date of the 
order, only that part would be kept in abeyance.
List this matter after the service is complete.
Stay Application stands disposed of.
 
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
