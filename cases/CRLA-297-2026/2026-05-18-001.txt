2. Mr. S.S. Chauhan, learned DAG with Mr. Vikash

3. Heard on the delay condonation application.
4. The delay is of 37 days’ in filing the present criminal 
appeal.
5. This Court finds ground for condonation of the delay is 
sufficient. For the reasons indicated in the delay condonation

Appellant was enlarged on bail in consideration that the 
relation between the victim and the Appellant was consensual.
7. Let objections to the bail application (IA No.2/2026) be 
filed within a period of two weeks.
8. List this matter on 02.07.2026.
