WPMS No. 2246 of 2019
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Heard Mr. B.S. Kathayat, Advocate, 
holding brief of Mr. Siddhartha Sah, 
Advocate for the petitioner.
Affidavit of service is taken on record. 
Misc. Application (IA No. 12044 of 2019) 
stands disposed of. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that notice issued by this Court vide 
order dated 05.08.2019 has been served. In 
support of his contention he has referred to 
the affidavit of service in which the 
respondent has acknowledged the receipt of 
notice. A perusal of the service report 
submitted by the Registry of this Court also 
indicates that as per the tracking report, the 
item has been delivered to the respondent.
In such view of the matter, this Court 
finds force in the contention made by 
learned counsel for the petitioner that writ 
petition is wrongly listed under Chapter 12 
Rule 4 of High Court Rules.
Since service is complete, list this 
petition for disposal on 27.11.2019.
In the meantime, it is provided that in 
case any application for adjournment of the 
case is moved by the petitioner, learned 
Court below shall consider the same for 
grant of adjournment only till 27.11.2019.
 (Manoj K. Tiwari, J.) Navin 22.10.2019
