 Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, Additional Chief

 It is the case of the petitioner that earlier 
when he was not paid retiral dues, he filed WPSS 
No.433 of 2025, which was decided by this Court 
on 08.04.2025 directing the respondents to pay 
all the retiral dues to the petitioner within a 
period of one month. But, it is submitted that 
now, the respondent authority has deducted 
Rs.4,20,821/- from the gratuity of the petitioner 
without informing him.
 When the Court posed this question to the 
learned State Counsel, as to how the recovery 
could be made from the gratuity amount? He 
seeks time to get instructions. 
 Let it be made clear that how such recovery 
may be made in view of the judgment passed by 
the Hon’ble Supreme Court in the case of State of 
Punjab and others Vs. Rafiq Masih, (2015)4 SCC 
334, that recovery from retired Government 
Servant may not be made, unless there are 
serious allegations of misrepresentation and/or 
fraud, etc? 
 Learned State counsel shall give a categorical 
statement on this aspect on 22.05.2025.
 List on 22.05.2025.
