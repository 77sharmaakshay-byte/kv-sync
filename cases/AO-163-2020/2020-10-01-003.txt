Heard learned counsel for the parties 
through video conferencing. 
Since a sum of ` 5,00,000/- has 
already been released in favour of the 
claimants in A.O. No. 167 of 2020, therefore, 
there is no good ground to allow this money 
withdrawal application.
Accordingly, money withdrawal 
application (CLMA No. 7940 of 2020) stands 
rejected. 
Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that the appellant has deposited the 
entire awarded amount before Employees’
Compensation Commissioner. He further 
submits that necessary direction be issued 
to invest the same in an interest bearing 
scheme. 
Since the said amount has not been 
invested in an interest bearing scheme, 
therefore, it is directed that the said amount 
forthwith be deposited in an interest bearing 
scheme of a nationalized bank.
