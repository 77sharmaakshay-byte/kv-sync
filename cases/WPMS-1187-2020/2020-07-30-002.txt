WPMS No. 1187 of 2020
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. U.K. Uniyal, Senior Advocate, assisted 
by Mr. Sandeep Kothari, Advocate for the 
petitioner.
Mr. Paresh Tripathi, Chief Standing 
Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand.
Heard learned counsel for the parties 
through video conferencing. 
As per office report, defect no. 1 has been 
cured by the petitioner. Regarding defect no. 2, 
learned Senior Counsel for the petitioner 
submits that he will positively file the affidavit 
and Vakalatnama within ten days from today.
In view of the undertaking given by 
learned Senior Counsel for the petitioner, the 
Exemption Application (CLMA No. 5417 of 
2020) is allowed.
Learned Senior Advocate for the petitioner 
submits that respondents cannot make any 
recovery from the petitioner in terms of the 
order dated 16.07.2020, as petitioner’s 
`1,19,51,147/-, as detailed in paragraph nos. 
42 & 49 of the writ petition is still lying with the 
respondents, therefore, there is no occasion for 
demanding a sum of `1,01,24,344/- by the 
impugned order from the petitioner.
As prayed, three weeks’ time is granted to 
learned Chief Standing Counsel to file counter 
affidavit. One week’s time thereafter is allowed 
for filing rejoinder affidavit.
List this matter on 07.09.2020.
Considering the submission advanced by 
learned counsel for the parties, it is directed 
that, till the next date of listing, no recovery 
shall be made from the petitioner pursuant to 
the impugned order.
Stay application (CLMA No. 5416 of 2020) 
stands disposed of. 
 (Manoj K. Tiwari, J.) Arpan 30.07.2020
