Heard.
This is an application for amendment.
It is submitted by Mr. K.P. Upadhyaya, 
the learned Senior Advocate that prayer 
against respondent no. 4 is no more relevant 
and has become infructuous, as the petitioner 
wants to delete the name of respondent no. 4 
and wants to make some consequent 
amendment in the prayer portion of the writ 
petition.
The application is allowed. 
There is no objection from the other side 
as it is in the interest of justice.
Let the petitioner file appropriate 
amended writ petition within seven days, 
hence. The respondent may file a short 
counter affidavit, if so advised, base on the 
amendment carried out today.
