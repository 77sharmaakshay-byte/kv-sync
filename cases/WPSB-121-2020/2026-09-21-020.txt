arguments, it was revealed that in a 
meeting held on 01.11.2019 between the 
Officers of the State and the Institute, a 
decision was taken that a revised merit list 
of the candidates shall be prepared.
These minutes have been enclosed 
along with the counter affidavit of the 
respondent no.1 dated 03.11.2021.
The Court wanted to know from 
learned counsel for the petitioner, learned 
State Counsel as well as learned counsel for 
the institute as to whether any revised
merit list has been prepared? None has any 
answer to it.Learned State Counsel seeks time to 
get instructions on this aspect.

time because earlier affidavit, which has 
been filed on behalf of the respondent no.2, 
he had some personal grudge against the 
then Registrar. Therefore, he wants to get 
more instructions on it.
