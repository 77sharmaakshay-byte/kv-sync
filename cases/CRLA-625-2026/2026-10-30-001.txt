Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that the appellant is a Child in 
Conflict with Law, who after preliminary 
assessment under Section 15 of the 
Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of 
Children) Act, 2015 (“the Act”), has been 
tried as an adult; he is entitled to get 
benefit under the provisions of the Act; the 
appellant is appearing in Class XII 
examination conducted by National 
Institute of Open Schooling, and the 
examination is underway. He submits that
directions may be issued that as and when he has to write the examination, he may be 
taken in police custody to the examination 
centre.
List this matter on 05.11.2026, for 
hearing on the bail application.
State may file objections including the 
social investigation report that has been 
filed at the court below and any additional 
material that is available with the State 
agency with regard to the antecedents, etc. 
of the appellant.
In the meantime, the Court also 
directs that as and when the appellant has 
to appear for his Class XII examination 
conducted by National Institute of Open 
Schooling, he shall be taken in police 
custody. 
The appellant shall inform a day prior 
to his examination to the jail authorities so 
that necessary arrangements may be made. 
Let a certified copy of this order be 
supplied to learned counsel for the parties, 
today itself, on payment of usual charges.
