Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Priyank Kharkwal, learned counsel for petitioner, 
through video conferencing.
2. Mr. N.S. Pundir, learned D.A.G. with Mr. Narayan 
Dutt, learned S.C. for the State/respondent nos.1 and 3.
3. Mr. Tilak Ram Sharma, learned counsel holding brief 
of Mr. Shailendra Nauriyal, learned counsel for respondent 
no.2. 
4. The writ petition has been filed by the petitioner 
challenging the order dated 16.05.2023, annexure no.7 to 
the writ petition, whereby the petitioner has been debarred 
from appearing in the competitive examination for coming 
five years on the ground that the petitioner was found guilty 
of mass copying by using unfair means.
5. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that a first information report has been lodged against the 
petitioner, therefore, the order of debarring the petitioner for 
coming five years in the competitive examination held by the 
Commission is very harsh. 
6. Learned counsel for respondent no.2 submits that the
first information report has been lodged by the Commission 
for mass copying against unknown persons and during the 
investigation, the name of many candidates including the 
petitioner have come up and for that reason only, he has 
been debarred by the Commission. 2
7. Be that as it may, this Court is of the opinion that at 
this stage only this can be said that the charges were 
against the petitioner, but at this stage, he cannot be said to 
have been found guilty of the aforesaid misconduct, 
therefore, the impugned order dated 16.05.2023 by which 
the petitioner is debarred appears to be harsh and pre￾mature. 
8. Let counter affidavit(s) be filed by the respondent(s) 
within six weeks. 
9. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the petitioner 
to file rejoinder affidavit. 
10. List this matter on 26.10.2026. 
11. Having considered the submissions of learned counsel 
for the parties, as an interim measure, the operation of the 
impugned order dated 16.05.2023 shall remain in abeyance 
till the next date of listing. 
12. The petitioner shall not be stopped from making any 
application in any of the advertisement issued by the 
respondent-Commission on the ground of his debarment 
vide impugned order. 
13. Stay application (IA No.1 of2023) stands disposed of 
accordingly.
