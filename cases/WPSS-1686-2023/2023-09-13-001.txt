2. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Standing

Commission.
4. It is the contention of the 
petitioner, who belongs to ST category, 
that he participated in the selection 
process of Assistant Teacher, L.T Grade 
(Hindi) conducted by the respondent￾Commission. In the said selection 
process, (in the written examination) the 
petitioner secured 57.75 marks. 
5. It is submitted by learned counsel 
for the petitioner that the cut-off marks 
of the open category candidates is 
66.50, who are private respondent nos. 
6 & 7 in the writ petition.
6. It is further submitted by learned 
counsel for the petitioner that the last 
selected candidate in ST category 
secured 66.75 marks but despite scoring
more marks than the last selected 
candidate in open category, he was not 
shifted to the open category and thereby
depriving the petitioner of his right of 
selection in the selection process.7. It is vehemently submitted by 
learned counsel for the petitioner that till 
date, no appointments have been made 
in Assistant Teacher, L.T Grade (Hindi); 
however the said fact is denied by 
learned counsel for the respondent￾Commission.
8. Learned counsel for the respondent 
–Commission prays for and is granted six 
weeks’ time to file counter affidavit. Two 
weeks’ time thereafter is granted for 
filing rejoinder affidavit.
9. List on 24.11.2023.
10. In the meantime, it is directed that 
one post of ST category in Assistant 
Teacher, L.T Grade (Hindi) shall be kept 
vacant during the pendency of the writ 
petition.
