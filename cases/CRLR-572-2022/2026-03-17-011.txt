3. Both the parties were married on
20.1.2016 and due to some differences 
in between them both are living 
separately since 2018 i.e. almost more 
than eight years. There is one issue 
from their marriage, the son who is 
living with the wife. As on date the total 
amount towards the arrears of 
maintenance is about Rs. 9,19,000/-.
The husband is getting monthly salary 
in hand about Rs. 1,10,000/-. 
4. In such an eventuality, the 
husband is directed to clear the entire 
arrears of maintenance and in this 
regard he shall pay Rs. 30,000/-
towards the arrears of maintenance
every month and in addition to this he 
shall also pay regular maintenance of 
Rs. 21,000/- in total i.e. Rs. 51,000/-
(30,000+21,000) in the first week of every month. 
5. In the meantime, since both the 
parties are mature and are having a 
son, therefore, this Court is of the view 
that they are the best person to resolve 
their matrimonial dispute in order to 
secure the future of son. Let both the 
parties may think over it sincerely, in 
order to settle their differences.
6. List this case on 20.04.2026.
