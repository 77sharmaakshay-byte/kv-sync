Mr. Vikas Kumar Guglani,

The revisionist proposes to 
challenge the order dated 06.08.2022, 
passed in Misc. Criminal Case No.59 
of 2018, Smt. Pragya Chaubey and 
another vs. Om Shiva Chaubey, by the 
court of Judge, Family Court, 
Khatima, District Udham Singh 
Nagar. By it, an application for interim 
maintenance filed by the revisionist 
no.1 has been allowed and the 
respondent has been directed to pay 
`21,100/- per month to the 
revisionists (`15,000/- per month to 
the revisionist no.1 and `6,000/- per 
month to the revisionist no.2).
The revision is delayed. A Delay 
Condonation Application No.1 of 2022 
has been filed. 
It is the case of the revisionists 
that the revisionist no.1 has to arrange 
the finance to prefer the revision and 
in that process delay occurred. 
Having considered the grounds, 
this Court is of the view that the delay 
in filing revision may be condoned. 
Accordingly, the Delay 
Condonation Application (IA) No.1 of 
2022 is allowed.
Delay in filing the criminal 
revision is condoned. Admit the revision.
The respondent is being 
represented. 
Call for the LCR. 
List this case on 29.04.2023.
