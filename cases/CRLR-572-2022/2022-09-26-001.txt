CRLR No.572 of 2022
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Vikas Kumar Guglani, Advocate 
for the revisionist.
The challenge in this revision is 
made to order dated 06.08.2022, passed 
in Criminal Case No.59 of 2018, Smt. 
Pragya Chaubey and Another Vs. Om Shiv
Chaubey, by the court of Family Judge, 
Khatima, Udham Singh Nagar, by which 
an application for maintenance filed by 
the respondent no.1 for herself and her 
son, the respondent no.2, has been 
allowed and the revisionist has been 
directed to pay Rs. 21,000/- per month as 
maintenance to them.
Heard.
Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would submit that the respondent no.2 
has been staying separate without any 
reasonable cause; she, without seeking 
divorce or obtaining divorce, has uploaded 
her profile on a matrimonial website, 
seeking alliance. It is argued by learned 
counsel for the revisionist that this fact 
has not been considered.
Perused the order and examination 
of the respondent no.1 and her mother, 
this Court is of the view that this matter 
definitely requires deliberations.
Admit.
Issue notices to the respondent nos. 
1 and 2, returnable within 4 weeks.
Steps to be taken within a week.
List this matter on 06.12.2022.
 (Ravindra Maithani J.) 26.09.2022
