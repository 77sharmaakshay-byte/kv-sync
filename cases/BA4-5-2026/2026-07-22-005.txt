1. Mr. Arvind Vashisht, learned Sr. Advocate 
assisted by Ms. Devanshi Joshi, learned counsel for the 
applicant. 
2. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned AGA for the
State. 
3. It is a fourth bail application. Against the 
rejection of third bail application, the applicant 
approached the Hon’ble Apex Court and the Hon’ble 
Apex Court on 17.11.2025 dismissed the appeal. Now, 
the instant fourth bail application has been moved 
wherein on 11.05.2026, counter affidavit was called 
and again on 29.05.2026, again time was granted to file 
counter affidavit and then again on 15.06.2026 further 
time was granted to the State to file counter affidavit 
but no counter affidavit has been filed, then 
subsequently, on 14.07.2026 I.O. was called. Now, the 
I.O. joined the proceedings through V.C. He submits 
that some part is being investigated particularly about 
some Crypto Account. 
4. Mr. Arvind Vashisht, learned Sr. Advocate for 
the applicant submits that now under the garb of
further investigation, the prosecution is taking plea of 
further investigation of Crypto Account, though as a 
matter of fact trial has already been commenced and 
uptil, February, 2026, five prosecution witnesses have 
been examined. He further submits that there is no 
question of further investigation, since the trial has already been commenced.
5. Be that as it may, counter affidavit is not on 
record though sufficient time has been granted to file 
counter affidavit. Today, I.O. submits that certain 
issues are under further investigation. 
6. Since no counter affidavit has been filed despite 
several opportunity, only ten days’ time is granted to 
the State to file counter affidavit positively giving 
status of the further investigation with regard to 
Crypto Account.
7. List on 04.08.2026.
