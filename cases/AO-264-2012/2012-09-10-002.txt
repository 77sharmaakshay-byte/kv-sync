Hon’ble B.S.Verma,J
(Money Withdrawal application No.10385 of 2012) 
Sri Bindesh Kumar Gupta, Advocate for the 
appellant. 
Sri Hari Mohan Bhatia, Advocate for the 
respondent Nos.1 to 6/claimants. 
This is money withdrawal application 
No.10385 of 2012 moved on behalf of 
claimant/respondent Nos. 1 to 6 praying for 
withdrawal of awarded amount, deposited by the 
appellant before the MACT concerned. 
Heard. 
This Money withdrawal application is 
allowed only to this extent that Claimant/ 
respondent Nos.1 to 5 are permitted to withdraw 
50% of the amount deposited by the appellant 
before MACT concerned, as per their shares. So far 
as the claimant/respondent No. 6 (minor) is 
concerned, his money withdrawal application is 
dismissed. Rest of the amount shall be kept in 
Fixed Deposit Interest Earning Scheme. 
Money withdrawal application stands 
disposed of. 
List this appeal in the week commencing 
29.10.2012 alongwith connected matters. 
 
 (B.S. Verma, J)
