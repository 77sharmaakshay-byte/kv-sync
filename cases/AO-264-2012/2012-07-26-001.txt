 Admit the appeal.

(Stay Application No.5792 of 2012) 
Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties, it is directed that till next date of listing, 
execution of the award dated 23.02.2012 passed 
by Motor Accident Claim Tribunal/District 
Judge, Pauri Garhwal in M.A.C.P. No.38 of 
2004, Smt. Sateshwari Devi and Others vs. Shiv 
Dayal Singh & Others shall remain stayed, 
provided appellant deposits `3,50,000/-of the 
awarded amount before the Motor Accident 
Claims Tribunal concerned within a period of six 
weeks from today. The amount already deposited 
under the statutory provisions shall be adjusted. 
  
Registry is directed to remit the entire sum, 
deposited under the statutory provision, to the 
Tribunal concerned within two weeks from 
today. The amount so remitted by this Court as 
well as the amount deposited by the appellant in 
compliance of this order, shall be invested by the 
Tribunal in a fixed deposit so that it could earn 
interest and would be subject to further orders of 
this Court. 
 Stay application stands disposed of.
