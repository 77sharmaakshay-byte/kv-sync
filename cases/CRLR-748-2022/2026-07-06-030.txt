5. The private respondents were acquitted by the 
Trial Court against which no appeal was preferred by 
the prosecution rather the present revisionist filed an 
appeal, which was registered as Miscellaneous Case 
No. 548 of 2022 and the same was dismissed on the 
ground of locus and now being aggrieved the instant 
revision has been preferred.
6. Mr. B.S. Adhikari though advanced his 
argument on the previous date questioning serious 
remark on the conduct of the prosecution by 
submitting that firstly the prosecution moved an 
application under section 321 of Cr.P.C. for 
withdrawal of the proceeding, which was rejected by 
the reasoned order on 17.09.2018 and thereafter on 
27.11.2018 charges were framed. He submits that while 
submitting the charge-sheet there were in total 28 
prosecution witnesses, but deliberately 18 prosecution 
witnesses were examined by the Trial Court though 
most of the prosecution witnesses are the employees of 
the police department and public servant. He further submits that deliberately in respect of the electronic 
evidence 65 B certificate was not produced and the 
finding to this extent was recorded by the trial court in 
its para-12, 14 and 15. He submits that in one side the 
Trial Court have not taken into consideration the 
electronic evidence produced by the prosecution in 
absence of 65B certificate but on the other side the Trial 
Court consider electronic evidence produced by the 
witnesses at the defence stage in absence of 65B 
certificate. He further submits the notification with 
regard to section 144 Cr.P.C. was deliberately not 
produced before the Trial Court in original. He also 
submits that PW1 to PW12 consistently supports the
case of the prosecution. He submits that though after 
thorough investigation the charge-sheet was filed and 
despite the fact that the Trial Court acquitted all the 
accused persons no appeal was filed and now raising 
an issue of locus of the present revisionist.
7. On the previous date the Trial Court record were 
summoned for perusal.
8. Mr. Virk, learned Additional Advocate General 
raise an issue of locus of the revisionist. 
Simultaneously, Ms. Manisha Bhandari also raising the 
same issue.
9. Let both of them may file their objection within 
two weeks.
10. List this matter on 16.07.2026
