CRLR No. 748 of 2022
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Hoshiyar Singh Bisht, revisionist, in person.
2. Mr. K.S. Bora, learned Assistant Government 
Advocate for the State.
3. Ms. Manisha Bhandari, learned counsel for the 
respondent no. 2. 
4. Mr. Ramji Srivastava, learned counsel for the 
respondent no. 4.
5. On the previous date, record of the trial court 
was summoned and is now received. 
6. Primary issue of locus standi has to be decided 
because this issue has been raised by the private 
respondents, herein, who have been acquitted by the 
trial court. 
7. Mr. K.S. Bora, learned AGA, who is presenting 
the prosecution has also raised the issue of locus standi 
of the present revisionist. 
8. Today, arguments are heard at length. 
9. On the previous date, the question arose whether 
present revisionist should be treated as victim in 
respect of the offence punishable under Section 147 
IPC. In response to this, Mr. Ramji Srivastava and Ms. 
Manisha Bhandari, learned counsel for the respondent 
no. 2 and 4, submit that in view of Section 372 Cr.P.C. 
present revisionist is not “victim” in respect of offence 
punishable under Section 147 IPC, therefore, revision is 
not maintainable. 
10. Since the trial court has been received, I have 
gone through with the post mortem report of the horse, 
which was prepared on 20.04.2016 at 05.00 p.m. and as 
per post-mortem report, the age of the horse was 13 
years 7 months. Two clauses of the post mortem report 
are necessary to be mentioned, which are being 
extracted hereinbelow: 
 “(i) Animal had suffered from a compound fracture 
of the left meta tarsal bone from its distal extremity on 
14.03.2016. (ii) Remark and etiology of death. - The opinion is 
given by four Veterinary Officers that based on the clinical 
history, the animal died due to sudden cardiac arrest due to 
myocardial infarction.”
11. Since the trial court record is received, Registry is 
directed to prepare the paper book and supply the 
same to the learned counsel for the parties. 
12. List on 03.12.2024 at 02.00 p.m. 
13. The matter will be heard on day-to-day basis.
