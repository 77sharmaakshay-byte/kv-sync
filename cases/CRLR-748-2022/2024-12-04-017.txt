reason it was not marked as an exhibit, and it was also not 
relied upon by the Trial Court.

the report of the Standing Committee consist of three

comments submitted by the concerned A.P.O. were examined. Mr. Virk, submits that the report of the Standing 
Committee, which was placed before this Court in the 
sealed envelope, is not a confidential document. The same 
is opened in the Court.
