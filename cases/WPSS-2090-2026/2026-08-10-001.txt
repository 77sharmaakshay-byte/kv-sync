3. By means of present writ petition, 
petitioner has challenged the order dated 
27.07.2026, whereby, petitioner was 
called upon to inform the Deputy 
Education Officer, Sitarganj, District 
Udham Singh Nagar, in writing by 
28.07.2026, as to whether the recovery
of ₹57,838/- be made from her either at
one go or in installments.
4. It is contended by learned counsel 
for petitioner that the said recovery is 
being made against the petitioner on the 
ground that some excess payment has 
been made to her in salary w.e.f. 
January 2024 to November 2025.
5. It is further contended that before 
passing such order, no opportunity of 
hearing was given to petitioner and the 
respondent No.5-Deputy Education 
Officer, Sitarganj, Udham Singh Nagar, has decided to recover the said amount 
under the garb of excess payment.
6. He further submitted that the 
petitioner has not been attributed any 
misrepresentation or fraud for the 
alleged excess payment having been 
made to her. In such view, being a 
Class-III employee, respondent￾authority cannot direct for recovery of 
the said amount
7. Learned State Counsel prays for and 
is granted four weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit.
8. Put up on 16.10.2026.
9. Till the next date of listing, no 
recovery shall be made from the 
petitioner pursuant to impugned order 
dated 27.07.2026.
10. Stay application (IA/1/2026) stands 
disposed of.
