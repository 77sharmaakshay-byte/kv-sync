 The challenge in this petition is made 
to the orders dated 27.04.2026 and 
18.05.2026, passed in Small Causes 
Court Execution Case No.1/2026, Ashok 
Kumar and others Vs. M/S Prism 
Payments Services Pvt. Ltd. and others, 
by the court of Civil Judge (Sr.Div.), 
Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar.
 Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
 Record reveals that the possession of 
the property has already been handed 
over by the decree holder, but execution 
has been filed for recovery of 
Rs.10,93,953/- as well and in recovery 
of it, on 27.04.2026 and on 18.05.2026, 
orders for attachment were passed, 
pursuant to which, five SBI ATMs have 
been attached. It has been impugned in 
the writ petition. Arguments on multiple 
points have been raised on behalf of the 
petitioner. 
 It may be noted, at the very outset, 
on 20.07.2026, this Court required the petitioner to deposit Rs.5,27,638/-
before the executing court till 
24.07.2026, in pursuant to which, the 
operation of order dated 24.07.2026, 
passed in the execution case was kept in 
abeyance. It is this order, by which, 
attachment of five ATMs was made. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that, in fact, after U.P. 
Amendment for issuance of attachment 
process, a notice ought to have been 
issued to the judgment debtor which has 
not been issued. 
 It is argued on behalf of the 
respondents that this point has never 
been raised. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner 
further submits that the petitioner may 
be permitted to deposit Rs.2.5 Lakhs 
more before the executing court and he 
may furnish bank guarantee to the 
remaining amount to this Court subject 
to final order of this Court. 
 Learned counsel for the respondent 
submits that the submission of bank 
guarantee should be limited by this 
Court. 
 Let the petitioner deposited Rs.2.5 
Lakhs before the executing court within 
a week and shall give bank guarantee for 
the remaining decreetal amount to this 
Court. It shall abide by the final decision 
of this petition. Subject to it, the ATMs, 
which have been attached pursuant to 
order dated 27.04.2026 and 18.05.2026, 
shall be released in favour of the 
petitioner.
 List on 06.11.2026.
