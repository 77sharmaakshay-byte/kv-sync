This matter is mentioned by learned 
counsel for the petitioner.
On 15.10.2026, in this matter, this Court 
had passed an order for releasing of the ATMs, 
which were attached subject to certain 
conditions. The condition was depositing of Rs. 
2.5 Lakhs before the executing court within a 
week since then, and also to produce a bank 
guarantee to the remaining amount to this Court.
Subsequent to it, on 06.11.2026, on time 
extension application, IA No.4 of 2026, this Court 
had extended time for two weeks so that the 
bank guarantee could be produced on time. 
Now, learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that another time extension application 
has been filed because due to some official 
correspondence, the bank guarantee could not be 
filed on time. It is two days late, and the Registry 
has denied to take it.
The petitioner is at liberty to file the bank 
guarantee within next three working days from 
today. 
The time is extended accordingly. 
List this matter on the date already fixed.
