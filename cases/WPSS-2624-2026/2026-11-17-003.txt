Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Anil Kumar Joshi, learned counsel for the
petitioners.
2. Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Addl. CSC for the State.
3. The grievance of the petitioners are that in terms
of Clause 3 of the MACP scheme dated 17.02.2017 they
are entitled to get Grade Pay of Rs. 4600/- on
completion of 20 years of their service, which is Grade
Pay of Sub Inspector the next promotional post.
Learned counsel for the petitioners further submits
that in the year 2023, a new post of ASI was created
but it will not curtail their entitlement of Grade Pay of
Rs. 4600/- in terms of financial upgradation under
MACP Scheme since the right to get next financial
upgradation under MACP Scheme was accrued in
their favour in the year of 2021 on completion of 20
years of service. 
4. On the other side, Mr. Dabral, submits that
submissions as advanced by Mr. Anil Kumar Joshi, for
claiming financial upgradation of Grade Pay of Rs.
4600 on completion of 20 years of service is totally
misconceived, due to enforcement of MACP scheme
with effect from 01.01.2017, since financial upgradation
as per MACP are based on pay matrix i.e. Pay Level
and MACP Scheme is passed in suppression of earlier
ACP scheme, which has no relevance, now as the same
came to an end on 31.12.2016.
5. Be that as it may, submission of Mr. Dabral,
learned Addl. CSC for the State, is without anysubstance. The MACP Scheme is nothing but
modification of the earlier ACP scheme. Clause 1, 2
and 3 of the said scheme are relevant to examine the
issue in hand which are being reproduced herein as
under:
“1. Under the Modified Assured Career Progression
Scheme, a maximum of three financial upgradations will be
granted to an employee who has not been promoted during his
entire service period, which will be counted on completion of 10,
20 and 30 years of regular and satisfactory service respectively
from the date of joining after substantive appointment to a direct
recruitment post. Financial upgradation under this scheme will
be admissible when a person has completed 10 years of regular
service in the same level in the pay matrix.
Promotions received between the first financial
upgradation to the third financial upgradation will be considered
as financial upgradation and the benefits of MACTS shall
accordingly be reduced to that extent. However, promotions
within the same level as MACPS will not be considered further
financial upgradation.
2. Under the Modified Assured Career Progression
Scheme, upgradation is to be given to the immediately next
higher Level in the sequence of Levels of the Pay Matrix given in
Annexure-1 of the Uttarakhand Government Servants Pay Rules,
2016 (as amended from time to time). In such a situation, the pay
Level obtained by an employee as a financial upgradation may in
some cases fall between the pay Levels of his promotion post. In
such cases, the higher Level attached to the next promotion post
in the hierarchy of the concerned cadre/organisation shall be
given only at the time of regular promotion.
3. At the time of financial upgrading under this scheme,
the pay fixation benefit provided at the time of regular promotion
will be admissible. In such a case, the pay will increase to the next
higher cell of the cell from which the pay was being drawn at the
level prior to such upgradation, resulting in the benefit of one
increment. No further pay fixation benefit will be granted at the
time of regular promotion if it is to the same level as provided
under MACPS. However, if the actual promotion is to a post at a
higher level than that available under MACPS, the pay fixation
will be done under Fundamental Rule 22(b) of the Financial
Handbook, Volume 2, Parts 2 to 4. For example, if a Government
servant enters service as a direct recruit in Level-1 with a pay of
Rs. 18,000/-, he will be granted financial upgradation to the
higher Level-2 on completion of 10 years of service and his pay
will be fixed at the cell of the same amount in Level-2 by granting
one increment in Level-1 or in the absence of the same amount, at
the next higher cell in Level-2. After getting financial
upgradation under MACPS, if the said Government servant gets
promoted to the next gradation in his cadre as per rules,
Which is Level(4) his pay on regular promotion will be fixed
as per Fundamental Rule 22(B) of Financial Hand Book Volume-2 Parts 2 to 4 which will be treated as next financial upgradation
under MACPS”
6. On plain reading of Clause 3 of MACP Scheme,
prima facie, it reveals that petitioners are entitled to get
next financial upgradation on completion of 20 years
of service the Grade Pay of Rs. 4600/- which is the
Grade Pay of next promotional post i.e. Sub Inspector.
7. Let Secretary, Finance, may file his personal
affidavit positively within ten days to explain why
benefit under the MACP scheme are not being given to
the petitioners, who were initially inducted as
Constables and now, claiming financial upgradation in
the Grade Pay of Rs. 4600/- which is a Grade Pay of
next promotional post of Sub Inspector.
8. List on 28.11.2016 immediately after fresh cases.
