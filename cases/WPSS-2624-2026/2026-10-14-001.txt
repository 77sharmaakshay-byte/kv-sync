Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Anil Kumar Joshi, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Addl. C.S.C. for the 
State.
3. The petitioners are claiming grade pay of Rs. 
4,600/- w.e.f. 11.10.2021 attached to the post of Sub 
Inspector, the next promotion avenue of the petitioner, 
in terms of the clarification as given by the 
Government Order dated 04.05.2018. It is submitted 
that the petitioner was initially inducted as a Constable 
and in the year of 2011 he was give first promotional 
pay scale of Head Constable and the next promotional 
avenue is Sub Inspector and as scuh he is entitled to 
get grade pay of Rs. 4,600/-, which is attached to the 
post of Sub Inspector.
4. On the other side, Mr. Anil Dabral, learned 
Addl. C.S.C. submits that next promotional post is 
A.S.I. and not the Sub Inspector, therefore, the claim as 
raised is misconceived, however, in response to this 
Mr. Anil Kumar Joshi submits that the next 
promotional post of A.S.I. came into existence in the 
year 2023 though prior to that there was only one 
promotional avenue i.e. Sub Inspector and the 
entitlement of grade pay of Rs. 4,600/- of Sub 
Inspector was accrued in favour of the petitioner on 
10.11.2021 prior to the creation of new promotional 
avenue of A.S.I.
5. Apart from this, he submits that petitioner is 
being denied the benefit only because of the reason that now as per the recommendation of 7th Pay 
Commission the new pay matrix has came into force 
which is completely misconceived in view of the 
clarification as given by the Government by 
Government Order dated 04.05.2018, wherein, it is 
clearly provided that where the promotional avenues 
are not available then the financial up-gradation will 
be based in terms of pay matrix and where the
promotional avenues are available then the financial 
up-gradation will be based on grade pay attached to 
the next promotional post.
6. Apart from this, he submits that Hon’ble the 
Chief Minister on 03.11.2021 has also announced for 
grant of grade pay of Rs. 4,600/- to the Constables.
7. Let on the next date the State may clarify the 
position if the promotion avenues are available to the 
petitioner then whether the financial up-gradation 
should be based on the grade pay attached to the 
promotional post or it will be based upon the pay 
matrix.
8. In addition to this, State may also address on the 
clarification as per the Government Order dated 
04.05.2018.
9. Put up this matter on 29.10.2026.
