JUDGMENT
1. According to the petitioner, he was engaged 
on contract as Junior Engineer in U.P. Cooperative 
Processing & Cold Storage Federation Ltd. w.e.f. 
01.07.1995; upon State reorganization, he was 
absorbed in the same capacity in Uttarakhand Pey Jal 
Nigam Ltd. w.e.f. 28.12.2004 and his services were 
ultimately regularized on 20.03.2017.
2. Grievance of the petitioner is that service 
benefits, which are available to regular employees of 
Uttarakhand Pey Jal Nigam, are not being paid to him 
and benefits of pay, promotion, pension etc. have also 
been withheld from him. 
3. By means of this writ petition, petitioner has 
sought the following relief:-
(i) Issue a writ order or direction in the nature of 
Mandamus commanding the respondents to 
grant the benefit of Pay Protection, Seniority, 
pension, promotion, ACP benefits on the post of 
Junior Engineer and all other admissible service 2
benefits to the petitioner from the date of his 
absorption/appointment in the respondent Nigam 
w.e.f. 28.12.2004/31.12.2004 along with all 
consequential benefits.
4. Mr. B.S. Bisht, learned counsel appearing for 
respondent nos. 2 to 5 assures the Court that in case 
petitioner makes a representation to the Competent 
Authority, decision thereupon will be taken 
expeditiously.
5. Accordingly, the writ petition is disposed of 
with liberty to petitioner to make representation to 
Managing Director, Uttarakhand Pey Jal Nigam Ltd. 
(respondent no. 2), within ten days from today. If such 
representation is made within stipulated time, 
respondent no. 2 shall look into the matter and take 
appropriate decision, as per law, as early as possible, 
preferably within six weeks from the date of 
presentation of representation with this order. Since 
this Court has not decided this writ petition on merit, 
therefore, petitioner shall be at liberty to approach this 
Court upon decision on his representation. 
(MANOJ KUMAR TIWARI, J.) Aswal
