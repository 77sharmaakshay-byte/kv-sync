There is a delay of 22 days in preferring the 
present appeal. 
Heard Mr. Rahul Adhikari, the learned counsel 
holding brief of Mr. Ganesh Kandpal, the learned 
counsel for the appellant and Mr. J.S. Virk, the learned 
Deputy Advocate General for the State of Uttarakhand. 
It is apparent from the records that the appellant 
is in judicial custody and he has been convicted for the 
offence under Section 302 of the Indian Penal Code, 
and sentenced to undergo life imprisonment. 
Since the appellant is in custody, we are inclined 
to condone the delay of 22 days in filing the appeal. 
Accordingly, the delay of 22 days in filing the present 
appeal is condoned. Delay Condonation Application (IA 
No. 01 of 2022) is, hereby, allowed. 
Heard. 
Admit.

An application (IA No. 02 of 2022) has been filed 
by the appellant for bail and suspension of sentence. 
Let the learned Deputy Advocate General file his 
objections to the same in the meantime. 
Let the matter be listed after preparation of the 
paper-book.
