Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Arvind Vashistha, learned Senior Advocate assisted 
by Ms. Devanshi Joshi, learned counsel holding brief of Mr. 
Hemant Singh Mahra, learned counsel for the revisionist.
2. Mr. G.C. Joshi, learned AGA along with Mr. Rakesh Negi, 
learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Mr. Yogesh Upadhyay, learned counsel for respondent 
no.2.
4. By means of this criminal revision, the revisionist has 
challenged the order dated 08.12.2025 passed by the learned 
Additional Sessions Judge/FTCSC, Rudrapur, District Udham 
Singh Nagar in Sessions Trial No. 188 of 2021, State of 
Uttarakhand vs. Deepak Sagar, whereby the learned trial 
court altered the charge against the accused by adding 
Sections 5/6 of the POCSO Act.
5. It is contended by the learned Senior Advocate appearing 
for the revisionist that, after investigation, the police 
submitted a charge-sheet under Sections 376(2) and 506 IPC. 
An application was moved by the victim herself on 
05.07.2024, wherein it was pleaded that at the time of the 
incident she was a minor and that her date of birth was 
03.07.1999; therefore, the charge ought to have also been 
framed under Sections 5/6 of the POCSO Act. The said 
application was allowed after hearing the revisionist-accused.
6. The gravamen of the argument advanced by the learned 
Senior Advocate is that such an application could not have 
been moved by the victim herself and ought to have been filed 2
by the DGC/Public Prosecutor.
7. In the opinion of this Court and in view of the present 
scenario and the manner in which the prosecution has 
proceeded with the case, the said argument appears to be 
hyper-technical.
8. The learned Senior Advocate further drew the attention of 
this Court to the evidence of the victim, wherein she stated 
that she was born on 03.07.1999; however, in her cross￾examination, she stated that her mother had died in the year 
1998. According to the learned Senior Advocate appearing for 
the revisionist, this statement in the victim’s evidence 
demolishes the prosecution case itself.
9. In such view of the matter, this Court is inclined to 
interfere in the matter at this stage by admitting the revision.
10. Admit the revision.
11. Respondents may file the counter affidavit within four 
weeks.
12. List on 17.02.2026.
13. Till the next date of listing, further proceedings of 
Sessions Trial No.188 of 2021, State of Uttarakhand vs. 
Deepak Sagar, pending before learned Additional Sessions 
Judge/FTCSC, Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar shall 
remain stayed.
14. Stay application (IA No.1/2026) stands disposed of 
accordingly.
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
