Hon’ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
 Mr. B.S Bisht, Advocate holding brief

 Ms. Prabha Naithani, Advocate for the

Heard. 
This is money withdrawal application 
No. 2409 of 2018 moved on behalf of the 
claimants/respondents for release of the 
sum deposited by the appellant in 
compliance of this Court’s order dated 
21.12.2016 in connection with Motor 
Accident Claim case no. 326 of 2011 
decided by Motor Accident Claim Tribunal/ 
Ist Addl. District Judge, Udham Singh 
Nagar on 24.09.2016. 
 Heard. 
 Having considered submissions of 
learned counsel for the claimants/ 
respondents, the money withdrawal 
application, is disposed of with the 
direction that the sum deposited by the 
appellant in compliance of this Court’s 
order dated 21.12.2016 shall be released 
by the tribunal in favour of the claimants/ 
respondents without any security. 
Statutory amount shall be remitted to the 
Tribunal concerned. 
List for hearing on 14th May 2018. 
 (Lok Pal Singh, J.)
