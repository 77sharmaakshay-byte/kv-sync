2. This Court, by order dated 12.06.2018, after finding that 
the explanation given by the two officers, namely, Mr. Arvind 
Kumar Saxena and Mr. Anand Swaroop of the appellant-Insurance 
Company not satisfactory had found that prima facie the finding 
which has been recorded in the earlier order of the coordinate Bench 
of this Court rendered on 14.05.2018 to be correct, had directed that 
an appropriate disciplinary proceedings may be drawn against the 
erring officials in furtherance of orders dated 14.05.2018 and 
30.05.2018. One of the officers, namely, Mr. Arvind Kumar Saxena 2
has filed the present Review Application (CLMA No. 10544 of 
2018). His contention in the review application is that the 
disciplinary proceedings as directed to be carried against him may 
not be appropriate, for the reason being that according to his 
submission, as he was transferred on 30.03.2012 to the Human 
Resource Department, New Delhi he ought not be harnessed with the 
responsibility of derelicting from performance of his duty. His 
contention in the Review Application is that the Amendment 
Application in the proceeding before the Court below was filed on 
30.01.2013 i.e. much after his transfer and the same was allowed by 
the learned Motor Accident Claims Tribunal on 19.03.2014, and 
hence he expresses his ignorance that since he was not in the helm of 
affairs at that point of time when the application was filed or at the 
time when it was considered and allowed. Hence, the order directing 
to conduct an inquiry against him by order dated 12.06.2018 requires 
to be reviewed. 
3. Recording the above submission, this Court is of the 
view that it would not be appropriate to review the judgment at this 
stage, however, it is clarified that when the Chairman proceeds with 
inquiry in compliance of the orders dated 14.05.2018 and 30.05.2018 
and the consequential order dated 12.06.2018, the he would first look 
into the aforesaid fact about the impact of his transfer on 13.03.2012 
to Human Resource Department, New Delhi and his involvement 
over the issue of filing of amendment and its allowing and thereafter 
only proceed with the disciplinary proceedings against Mr. Arvind 
Kumar Saxena, if at all required. 
4. Subject to the above observations, Review Application 
(CLMA No. 10544 of 2018) stands disposed of. As far as the Review 3
Application (CLMA No. 10543 of 2018) sought by Mr. Anand 
Swaroop is concerned, that would stand rejected. 
5. This order will simultaneously dispose of the Review 
Application (CLMA No. 10543 of 2018) filed in AO No. 688 of 
2016. Let the copy of this order be placed in the connected Appeal 
(AO No. 688 of 2018).
