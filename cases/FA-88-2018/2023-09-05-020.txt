2. Counsel for the appellant would submit that 
during pendency of the first appeal, it came to 
know that the property has been sold to one Shri 
Satprakash and proposed respondent Shri Kriti 
Sharma, therefore, an impleadment application 
(I.A. No. 15030 of 2022) was moved to implead 
Satprakash and Shri Kriti Sharma as party
respondent nos. 4 and 5 in the appeal.
However, during pendency of the said 
impleadment application it came in knowledge 
that Satprakash also died, therefore, a substitution 
application has been filed for impleading the legal 
heir of Late Shri Satprakash in the impleadment 
application (I.A. No. 15030 of 2022). A 
Substitution Application (IA 15032 of 2023) has 
already been allowed vide order 18.03.2023 
passed by the Coordinate Bench of this Court, 
whereby Mr. Sushil Kumar has been substituted 
as party respondent no.5 in the appeal as legal 
heir of Late Satprakash.
3. The proposed respondent Sushil Kumar i.e. 
legal heir of Late Shri Satprakash filed his 
reply/objections by filing Misc. Application (IA 
No. 15034 of 2023) stating therein that 
Satprakash had bequeathed the suit property 
purchased by him to his wife Tara Devi. The 
Misc. Application (IA No. 15034 of 2023) is 
hereby allowed.4. Consequently, a Delay Condonation 
Application (IA No. 15037 of 2023) along with 
Substitution Application (IA No. 15038 of 2023)
was moved for condoning the delay in moving 
the substitution application to substitute the legal 
heir i.e. Tara Devi wife of Late Shri Satprakash as 
respondent no. 4/2 in the appeal. 
5. Counsel for the proposed respondents Shri 
Kriti Sharma and Shri Sushil Kumar would 
submit at Bar that they have no objection to allow 
the application for substituting Smt. Tara Devi in 
the impleadment application as all the parties 
should be before the Court for substantial justice. 
In view of the above, the Delay
Condonation Application (IA No. 15037 of 2023)
is allowed. Consequently, the Substitution 
Application (IA No. 15038 of 2023) would stand 
allowed. 
6. Counsel for the respective parties would 
submit that the proposed respondent Shri Sushil 
Kumar has already been substituted in 
impleadment application (IA No. 15030 of 2022).
7. In view of the above, the Impleadment 
Application (IA No. 15030 of 2022) for 
impleading Shri Sushil Kumar, Shri Kriti Sharma 
as respondent nos. 4 and 5 respectively is 
allowed.
8. Counsel for the appellant is directed to file 
amended memo of parties within two weeks.
9. Issue notices to the newly impleaded 
respondent Smt. Tara Devi W/o Late Shri 
Satprakash through ordinary process, registered 
post acknowledgment due as well as through e￾mail address and WhatsApp contact, if available.
10. Steps to be taken within two weeks.
11. List this matter on 29.11.2023 for hearing.
