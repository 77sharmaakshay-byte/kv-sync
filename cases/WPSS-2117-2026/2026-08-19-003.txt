4. By means of the present writ petition, 
the petitioner has challenged the suspension 
order dated 01.04.2026, passed by the 
Manager-respondent no.5.
5. The main contention of the petitioner in 
the present writ petition is that the 
suspension order required to be approved by 
the Chief Education Officer of the district and 
since in the case in hand, the approval has
not been granted to the suspension order, 
therefore, it cannot be sustained.
6. This Court vide order dated 10.08.2026 
directed the learned State Counsel to get 
instructions to this effect as to the whether 
the approval was given to the suspension 
order or not. 7. It is submitted by the learned State 
Counsel on instruction that the approval has 
not been given to the impugned suspension 
order, rather the Chief Education Officer took 
a stand to get the investigation/inquiry be
conducted by the Block Education Officer 
prior to grant of approval and till further 
order, the status quo shall be maintained. 
The instructions supplied by learned State 
Counsel are taken on record.
8. Since the Chief Education Officer has 
not granted approval to the suspension 
order, rather he has directed inquiry to be
conducted by the Block Education Officer, in
this view of the matter, this Court is inclined 
to grant protection to the petitioner.
9. The impugned suspension order 
01.04.2026 shall remain stayed till next date 
of listing.
10. The respondents may file the counter 
affidavit within a period of four weeks.
11. List this case on 04.11.2026.
12. Stay application (IA/1/2026) stands 
disposed of.
