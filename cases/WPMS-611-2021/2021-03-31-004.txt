Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Kurban Ali, Advocate for the petitioner. 
Apart from the fact that the challenge given 
in the Writ Petition is to the judgment of 13th
November, 2019, to override the effect of having 
preferred the Writ Petition at a belated stage, the 
learned counsel for the petitioner has filed a 
supplementary affidavit taking shelter to the 
judgment of Hon’ble Apex Court dated 8th March, 
2021, as was rendered in Suo Moto Writ Petition 
No. 3 of 2020, where it has been provided that the 
period of limitation has to be liberally construed for 
the period, the limitation of which is expiring or 
expired, which has been prescribed therein 
commencing from 15th March, 2020 till 14th March, 
2021. 
This aspect would be dealt with as soon as 
the petitioner, who contends to raise the plea about 
the bar of maintainability of the proceedings under 
Section 15 of the Provincial Small Causes Courts 
Act, on the ground that the provisions of Act No. 13 
of 1972, would be applicable. 
He submits that before the learned Trial 
Court, in order to substantiate his argument 
regarding the age of construction, he has placed on 
record, paper No. 94-Ga, i.e. the assessment register 
pertaining to 1988-89, which he contends that, that 
has been wrongly read by the learned Trial Court,
as well as paper No. 96-Ga and 97-Ga, which are 
the receipts, which he contends that these papers 
were the rent receipts, which were issued in relation 
to the tenement, which he relied in order to show 
that it was in existence prior to the cut off provided 
under the Act No. 13 of 1972, in order to hold the 
proceedings of SCC Court, as to be not 
maintainable. The document filed in support thereto along 
with the Writ Petition, in fact, do not show that 
these documents, which are the receipts and the 
assessments were placed as exhibits, as these 
documents are not bearing any exhibit numbers. 
In order to read those documents in support 
of the argument which has been extended by the 
learned counsel for the petitioner, he is directed to 
place the certified document on records of the Writ 
Petition as referred in para 6 of the judgment 
rendered by the Judge, Small Causes Court, in order 
to substantiate that the documents relied were the 
exhibits before the Court below, to which, the 
learned counsel for the petitioner prays for and is 
granted ten days’ time. 
List thereafter. 
Having passed the above order, it may not be 
construed that this Court has entertained the Writ 
Petition on its own merit. 
 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
