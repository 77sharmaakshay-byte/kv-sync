Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Kurban Ali, Advocate for the petitioner. 
These Writ Petitions have been preferred 
against the Revisional Court’s judgment dated 13th
November, 2019, passed by the Judge Small Causes 
Court / Additional District Judge, Kashipur, in 
respective SCC Revisions. 
The affidavit filed in support of the 
Revisions, is shown to have been sworn on 24th
February, 2021. It suffers from defect of laches, 
which has not been explained. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner prays for 
and is granted a week’s time to file supplementary 
affidavit to explain the laches. 
Grant of permission to explain the laches, 
may not be construed by the Execution Court that 
this Court had entertained the Writ Petitions on its 
own merit. 
Put up these Writ Petitions immediately after 
one week.
