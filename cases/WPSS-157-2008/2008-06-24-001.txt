 This is application (No. 3562 of 2008), for condonation of delay in 
filing the counter affidavit, moved on behalf of the respondents. 
 The order dated 25.03.2008, passed in Special Appeal No. 26 of 2008, 
whereby this writ petition was restored, contained among other things 
following direction:- 
“………..The respondents shall file counter affidavit in two months. 
Rejoinder affidavit thereto be filed in one month thereafter. Time shall not 
be extended…………” 
 In view of the above direction of appellate court (Division Bench), 
this Court cannot condone the delay in filing the counter affidavit on behalf 
of the respondents. 
 Learned counsel for the respondents states that he would get the time 
extended from the appellate court (Division Bench). 
 List after one month.
