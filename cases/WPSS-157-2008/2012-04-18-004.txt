WPSS No. 157 of 2008 
Hon’ble Tarun Agarwala, J.
Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, Senior Advocate 
assisted by Mr. Anup Kumar Verma, Advocate 
for the petitioner. 
Mr. Arvind Vashisth, Astt. S.G. for the 
respondents. 
The petitioner’s mother, while working in 
the department of Telecommunication, died in 
harness on 26th June, 2002. The petitioner 
applied for appointment on compassionate 
ground on 14th August, 2002. The said 
application was rejected by an order dated 12th
July, 2004. The petitioner, being aggrieved, 
filed a writ petition which was disposed of by an 
order dated 7th August, 2007 directing the 
authority to reconsider the matter on the 
representation of the petitioner. Based on the 
said representation, the authority reconsidered 
the matter and rejected it again by an order dated 
19th November, 2007 on the basis of some new 
scheme framed by a letter dated 27th June, 2007. 
The petitioner, being aggrieved, has filed the 
present writ petition. 
Having heard Shri C.D. Bahuguna, the 
learned senior counsel for the petitioner and Shri 
Arvind Vashisth, the learned counsel for the 
respondents wherein much stress was laid that 
even as per the scheme dated 27th June, 2007, 
the petitioner was entitled to be considered for 
compassionate appointment. 
The court, however, finds that it is not 
necessary to consider the scheme of 27th June, 
2007, inasmuch as, the Court is of the opinion 
that the scheme which was prevalent at the time 
when the petitioner applied is required to be 
considered. The petitioner applied on 14th
August, 2007 upon the death of his mother on 
26th June, 2002 and, consequently, the scheme of 
2012:UHC:1783the respondents as existing on the date when the 
petitioner applied is required to be taken into 
consideration. Subsequent schemes issued by 
the respondents can only be prospective in 
nature and cannot be utilized or considered on 
the ground that the petitioner’s application was 
pending consideration. 
In the light of the aforesaid, the impugned 
order cannot be sustained and is quashed. The 
writ petition is allowed. The respondents are 
directed to reconsider the petitioner’s application 
for appointment on compassionate ground as per 
the existing scheme existing on the date of the 
petitioner’s application within three months 
from the date of the production of a certified 
copy of the order.
