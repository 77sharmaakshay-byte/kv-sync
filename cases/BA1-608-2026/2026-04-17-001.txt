Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Rishabh Bisht, learned counsel holding brief of Mr. Vikas 
Kumar Guglani, learned counsel for the Applicant. 
2. Mr. S.S. Chauhan, learned Deputy Advocate General assisted by 
Mr. Vikash Uniyal, learned A.G.A. for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. The Applicant is in judicial custody in connection with FIR No. 
168 of 2025, registered at Police Station Khatima, District Udham Singh 
Nagar, for the offences punishable under Sections 3(5), 61(2), 305(E), 
317(2), 317(4), 317(5), 324(3), and 324(5) of the B.N.S., 2023, as well as 
Section 3(2)(a) of the Prevention of Damage to Public Property Act and 
Sections 136 and 137 of the Electricity Act.
4. Learned counsel appearing for the Applicant submits that the 
present matter pertains to the alleged theft of electricity wire, which was 
recovered from the co-accused, who has already been enlarged on bail by 
the learned Trial Court. It is further submitted that the Applicant has been 
falsely implicated in the present case. 
5. Learned State Counsel, however, refutes the submissions so 
advanced by learned counsel for the Applicant, contending, firstly, that 
the Applicant has a criminal history, and secondly, that he was 
absconding and has active involvement in the theft in question. He seeks 
time to file objections to the bail application. 
6. List this case on 04.05.2026. 
7. In the meantime, objections/counter affidavit, if any, shall be filed 
by the State.
