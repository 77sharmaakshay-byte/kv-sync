4. A Coordinate Bench of this Court vide 
order dated 02.01.2024 has been pleased 
to stay the proceedings of the Criminal 
Case No.226 of 2023 State of Uttarakhand 
Vs. Trilok and Others, pending in the Court 
of learned Judicial Magistrate, Jaspur, 
District Udham Singh Nagar, on a 
condition that the maintenance should be 
paid to respondent No.2 (wife) regularly 
by the applicant No.1 (husband).
5. Learned counsel for respondent No.2 
raised an objection that the maintenance 
has not been paid by the applicant No.1 to 
the respondent No.2 and there are arrears 
of maintenance. This fact was disputed by 
learned counsel for the applicants, 
therefore, this Court has directed the 
applicants to file an affidavit stating 
therein conspicuously that he was not in 
arrears of amount of maintenance towards 
respondent No.2.
6. In sequel to order dated 10.03.2025, 
an affidavit has been filed by learned 
counsel for the applicants, in which, it has 
been stated that the applicant No.1-husband has paid a sum of Rs.44,000/- to 
the respondent No.2 towards the 
maintenance amount.
7. Today, the learned counsel for the 
applicants passed on to this Court an 
order dated 21.05.2025, which is taken on 
record. From perusal of the said order, it 
has been reflected that further a sum of 
Rs.25,000/- was also received by 
respondent No.2-Pooja. It is submitted by 
learned counsel for the applicants on the 
basis of the said order that in the case of 
Domestic Violence Act, there is no balance 
amount of maintenance to be paid to the 
respondent No.2-Pooja.
8. In this view of the matter, the Court is 
satisfied that the amount of maintenance 
has been paid to the respondent No.2-wife 
by the applicant No.1-husband, therefore, 
the interim order dated 02.01.2024 is 
extended till next date of listing.
9. Put up on 22.07.2025.
10. Meanwhile, learned counsel for 
respondent No.2 may file counter affidavit.
