Heard.
Petitioner is second wife of late Kalu 
Ram Ahirwar, who was serving as Principal 
in a Government Inter College and died 
after retirement on 06.03.2022. It is not in 
dispute that late Kalu Ram Ahirwar was 
earlier married to Smt. Kamlesh, and out of 
the said marriage, there were four children, 
namely, Ms. Aruna Bharti, Mr. Awadesh 
Kumar Bharti, Mr. Pravesh Kumar Bharti & 
Mr. Sarvesh Kumar. 
According to the petitioner, all the four 
children born out of the first wedlock are 
major and three are married and are well 
settled; while, the youngest son 
Mr. Sarvesh Kumar is yet to be married. It 
is further contended on behalf of the 
petitioner that all the children from first 
marriage of late Kalu Ram Ahirwar have 
given No Objection Certificate for grant of 
family pension to petitioner, yet the authorities of Education Department are not 
releasing family pension, even after eight 
months from the date of death of 
petitioner’s husband.
Learned State counsel prays for and is 
granted four weeks’ time for filing counter 
affidavit.
List this case on 07.03.2023.
