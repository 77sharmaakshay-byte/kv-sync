C528 No.1266 of 2025
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Amanjot Singh Chadha, learned counsel for the 
Applicant.
2. Mr. Akshay Latwal, learned AGA, for the State/1.
3. The present criminal miscellaneous application filed 
under C528 of BNSS, is with a prayer that the proceedings in 
criminal miscellaneous case no.174/2024, under section 12 of 
the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005, 
“Shrimati Monika Pal Vs. Ashutosh Kumar and others”, 
pending before the court of learned ACJM, Kashipur, District 
Udham Singh Nagar, be quashed.
4. The applicant has also made a prayer for interim relief 
vide paper no.46.
5. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the Applicant 
makes a statement with reference to paper no.44 and 45, 
which is an order of this Court, passed in C528 No.1012 of 
2025, that in a similar matter, wherein Respondent No.2 was 
the complainant and wherein the Court was pleased to grant 
an interim relief. Learned counsel makes a submission that in 
the said case as paper no.44 and 45, the next date fixed is 26th
August, 2025.
6. Admit.
7. Issue notice to the Respondent No.2. Steps to be taken 
within a week. Counter affidavit to be filed within a period of 
three weeks.
8. Till the next date of listing, the entire proceedings of
criminal miscellaneous case no.174/2024, under section 12 of 
the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005, “Shrimati Monika Pal Vs. Ashutosh Kumar and others”, 
pending before the court of learned ACJM, Kashipur, District 
Udham Singh Nagar, shall remain stayed.
9. List this matter along with C528 No.1012 of 2025.
10. Stay application (IA No.1/2025) stands disposed of 
accordingly.
