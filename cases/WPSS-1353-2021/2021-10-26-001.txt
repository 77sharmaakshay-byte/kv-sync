Mr. Shobhit Saharia, Advocate, for the

Mr. Prateek Tripathi, Advocate, 
holding brief of Mr. Vipul Sharma, Advocate, 
for the respondent No. 1.
The petitioners’ candidature for being 
appointed on compassionate grounds, has been 
rejected by the respondents, by the impugned 
order dated 27.02.2020, on the ground that the 
establishment of CCB i.e. (Coordination 
Committee of Bhawans) is not the part & parcel 
of the principal establishment of IIT Roorkee 
and hence, the heirs of the deceased employees
of CCB, who were serving in the said CCB, 
would not be entitled for consideration of 
appointment on compassionate grounds.
The matter requires scrutiny.
Respondent No. 1 is represented by 
Mr. Vipul Sharma, Advocate.
Let notices be issued to respondent 
Nos. 2 & 3. Steps would be taken by the 
petitioner within a period of 10 days from 
today.
List this writ petition after service of 
notices on the respondents or the counter 
affidavit is filed whichever is earlier.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
