COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 581 of 2025
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. S.K. Shandilya, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned 
Addl. C.S.C. for the State.
3. Mr. Ashish Joshi, learned counsel 
for respondent no. 2 through V.C.
4. Mr. Rajendra Arya, learned counsel 
holding the brief of Mr. Neeraj Garg, 
learned counsel for respondent no. 3.
5. By the instant petition, the 
petitioner is challenging the 
advertisement dated 13.09.2024, issued 
by respondent no. 2 whereby the post 
which has been occupied by the 
petitioner, i.e., Research Officer has 
been advertised.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the petitioner is working 
since last more than 13 years on the 
post of Research Officer which was 
subsequently sanctioned by the 
Government in the year 2016. He 
submits that the petitioner has a 
legitimate experience to get 
regularization of his services since he is 
working for last 13 years in view of the 
judgment passed by the Division Bench 
of this Court in PIL No. 116 of 2018 
decided on 12.11.2018 which was 
affirmed by the Hon’ble Apex Court in 
Civil Appeal Nos. 7181-7317 of 2021 by 
judgment and order dated 15.10.2024.7. He further submits that though age 
relaxation has been given to the 
incumbent who are already working but 
at present the petitioner is about 53 
years old and merely by giving benefit of 
age relaxation if the petitioner could not 
succeed in the ongoing selection process 
he will certainly be deprived from his 
right of livelihood.
8. The respondent has not disputed 
that the petitioner is working since last 
more than 13 years and the post against 
which the petitioner is working was 
subsequently sanctioned in the year 
2016.
9. Let respondents may file counter 
affidavit within four weeks. Two weeks 
thereafter is granted to the petitioner to 
file rejoinder affidavit.
10. After taking into consideration that 
the petitioner is working since last more 
than 13 years on the post of Research 
Officer and now at present he is 53 years 
old and further taking into consideration 
the judgment passed by the Division 
Bench of this Court in PIL No. 116 of 
2018 decided on 12.11.2018, the 
respondents are directed to proceed with 
the selection pursuant to the impugned 
advertisement, however, result for the 
post which the petitioner is occupying 
shall not be declared without leave of the 
Court and furthermore the petitioner 
shall be permitted to continue till the 
next date of listing.
11. List this matter on 17.06.2025.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
