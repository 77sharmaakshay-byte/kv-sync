In WPSS No. 1400 of 2024, learned 
counsel for the petitioner submits that the 
petitioner is deprived of appointment without 
any reasonable basis. He would submit that 
the Commission has conducted inquiry against 
11 persons, including one Sandeep Kumar S/o 
Rajpal, who has his experience certificate from 
Shivam Engineering Works Punjab, but 
subsequently, he was given appointment.
Learned counsel for the Commission 
submits that the Commission has merely 
conducted inquiry of 9 candidates and 
submitted a report to the State Government 
and it is the State Government, which has to 
take a final call.
Let State file a short affidavit as to what 
decision has been taken on the report of the 
Commission with regard to the verification of 
the experience certificate of 9 candidates, as 
also the State shall indicate that if the 
certificate of the candidate Sandeep Kumar S/o 
Rajpal was in dispute, how was he granted 
appointment? 
Let this affidavit be filed within three 
weeks.
