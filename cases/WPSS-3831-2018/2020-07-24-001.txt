HIGH COURT OF UTTARAKHAND AT NAINITAL
Writ Petition (S/S) No. 3831 of 2018
Anil Tripathi …Petitioner
Vs.
State of Uttarakhand and Others …Respondents
Advocate: Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate for the petitioner.
Mr. Anurag Bisaria, Standing Counsel with Mr. Sachin Mohan Singh 
Mehta, Brief Holder for the State of Uttarakhand
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
(Via Video Conferencing)
The cause raised by the petitioner in the present writ petition,
is on account of an inaction on part of the respondents of not 
considering his claim for being appointed on compassionate grounds,
despite there being directions issued by the competent authorities, no 
decision, as such, has been taken on the same till the date of filing of 
the writ petition.
2. The precise facts of the present case are that the petitioner has 
contended in the writ petition that his late Mr. Anand Ballabh
Tripathi, who was working as a Primary School Teacher, under the 
aegis of respondent No. 2, in the capacity of a confirmed Primary 
School Teacher. He met with the sad demise in harness on 30th June 
2014. The petitioner’s contention is that he is requisitely qualified as 
he is Graduate in BSc, having an additional certificate of an IT 
Course from Sikkim Manipal University and is unemployed would 
be eligible to be considered for the compassionate appointment; 
under the Harness Rules. 
3. Faced with the aforesaid domestic and financial contingencies 
on account of an untimely death of his father, the petitioner filed an 
application on 21st August 2014, before respondent No. 4, for 
considering his claim for the grant of compassionate appointment, as 
against Class-III post, for the reason that he fulfils all the parameters 
of educational qualifications for it. 2
4. The said application, as preferred by the petitioner, and also as 
would be apparent from the endorsements made therein, that the 
office of Block Education Officer, Dwarahat, District Almora,
forwarded the same before the Chief Education Officer, for its 
consideration. Despite of the aforesaid recommendation, having been 
made by the Block Education Officer to the Chief Education Officer, 
wherein he has pleaded the detailed facts of the family crisis, which 
the petitioner has contended necessitated the appointment on 
compassionate grounds; but the same was not granted to him.
5. The grievance of the petitioner initially was that respondent 
No. 3, without considering his application, and even without passing 
any order on the same had returned the original proposal of the 
petitioner, by a letter dated 3rd October 2014, and the reasons which 
were assigned therein the aforesaid correspondence, was that since 
one of the sons of late Mr. Anand Ballabh Tripathi i.e. Mr. Manoj 
Tripathi, is already employed in the Army, hence his claim for 
compassionate appointment cannot be considered. Consequently, by 
an order passed by the Additional Director, (Primary Education),
Kumaon Mandal, his claim was rejected on 30.10.2014.
6. Despite of the said communication, as made on 30th October 
2014, by the Additional Director, Primary Education, the petitioner 
still perused the matter through his mother, who filed a 
representation before respondent No. 1, and respondent No. 1, taking 
cognizance on the said representation of the mother of the petitioner, 
had called a detailed report to be submitted in the matter from the 
office of respondent No. 2 for reasons which attributed to the 
cancellation of claim for compassionate appointment.
7. The grievance of the petitioner in the present writ petition is 
that without undergoing that exercise of determining the facts and 
the contingencies which were being faced by the family, by his 
correspondence dated 9th July 2015, had rejected the same on an
identical ground as it has been taken earlier in the correspondence 3
dated 3rd October 2014. The petitioner even thereafter subsequently 
continued to raise the claim for grant of compassionate appointment 
by filing the representations on 12.08.2015 and 15.10.2015 and 
henceforth, contending thereof that the grounds taken for denying the 
compassionate appointment on the pretext that the brother of the 
petitioner is under employment in the Armed Forces, is absolutely 
untenable for the reason being that the brother of the petitioner was 
residing separately from the family and none of the family members 
of late Mr. Anand Vallabh Tripathi, were ever dependent upon him.
8. In continuation thereof, there had been yet another 
correspondence dated 15.10.2015, issued by respondent No. 2, who 
furnished a report to respondent No. 1, on the representation of the 
petitioner dated 12.08.2015, wherein the Minister for Secondary 
Education, was directed by the Chief Minister to consider the claim 
of the petitioner for the grant of compassionate appointment; but,
however since the controversy pertaining to one of the sons of late
Mr. Anand Vallabh Tripathi since was already serving in the Armed 
Forces and as to whether his serving in the Armed Forces would 
create any impediment in considering the claim of the petitioner for 
compassionate appointment; was an issue which was to be decided 
by the competent authority. On the basis of endorsements made by 
the superior State authorities, the District Magistrate, Almora, was 
requested by respondent No. 3, the Additional Director of Education 
to conduct an enquiry through the competent revenue official and 
submit a report, as to whether the fact that the elder son of late Mr.
Anand Vallabh Tripathi is residing separately or not may be 
ascertained from records, and report was called for.
9. The Tehsildar, Dwarahat, district Almora; in continuation to 
the said direction issued by respondent No. 3, on 8th April 2016,
which directed the Tehsildar to submit the report, who furnished it 
by way of his letter No. 83/MU15-16 dated 3rd May 2016. The 
Revenue Inspector; thereafter forwarded the said report to the 
District Magistrate, Almora, who in turn to submitted the same to4
respondent No. 3, with his covering letter on 20th July 2016, wherein 
in the said report, the Tahsildar has observed as under:-

10. In view of the aforesaid report of the Tahsildar dated 
03.05.2016, which was submitted before the District Magistrate, he 
has reported that the elder brother of the petitioner Mr. Manoj 
Tripathi even prior to the death of late Mr. Anand Vallabh Tripathi 
was residing separately after his marriage which was held on 28th
November 2013, and he had no relationship whatsoever now, nor the 
petitioner had any dependency upon them. The said fact in the report 
stood further affirmed by the details, which were supplied by the 
Regional Gram Panchayat Adhikari, Vikas Nagar, Dehradun.
11. After the receipt of the said report, it has come on record that 
respondent No. 3, has furnished the entire report to respondent No. 2 
vide his correspondence dated 29th July 2016, along with District 
Magistrate report, and despite of it, no action or decision was taken 
on it. Rather to the contrary, the petitioner’s argument is that the 
Director (Elementary Education), Uttarakhand, who is the competent 
authority to take the decision on the report, as submitted by the 
Tahsildar through District Magistrate but, despite of the said 
correspondence made to him on 29th July 2016, the Director, instead 
of taking his own decision, had remitted the matter to the State 
Government vide his correspondence letter dated 8th August 2016,
and no decision as such has been taken on the same as a result 5
thereto, the petitioner had filed an application before the respondent 
under the Right to Information Act, for seeking an appropriate reply 
as to what decision was taken on the basis of report submitted by the 
Tahsildar on 03.05.2016, and the same was answered to the 
petitioner on 22nd March 2018, by supplying the information that the 
decision in relation to the consideration of his claim for 
compassionate appointment was still pending consideration before 
the State Government, based on the letter of the respondent No. 2
dated 8th August 2016. 
12. The petitioner filed the present writ petition, and in support of 
his contention as to what would be the impact of the definition of 
“family”, given under the Rules, as to whether a married brother 
residing separately will fall to be within the definition of family as 
provided under Rule 5 of the Dying in Harness Rules? As to whether 
a married brother, who is residing separately could be held as to be a 
person on whom the applicant is dependent to deny him the 
compassionate appointment. But despite of all the efforts made by 
the petitioner, no decision as such, has been taken till date.
Consequently, the present writ petition for the following reliefs:-
“(i) To declare the action of the Respondents in not 
appointing the petitioner on compassionate ground, as 
arbitrary and illegal and also against the law 
propounded by this Hon’ble Court on the subject from 
time to time.
(ii) To issue a writ order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding the respondents to forthwith 
appoint the petitioner on suitable post on compassionate 
ground, along with all consequential benefits.
(iii) To issue any other suitable writ, order or direction 
which this Hon’ble Court may deem fit and proper in 
the circumstances of the case.
(iv) To award the cost of the writ petition in favour of the 
petitioner.”
13. After having given a thoughtful consideration, in the light of 
the material which has been brought on record, and particularly, the 6
report of the Tahsildar dated 03.05.2016, which has been submitted 
to the District Magistrate, which was in turn forwarded to the 
Director, it was the Director himself, who was supposed to take a 
decision; but the same has been unnecessarily and for no logical 
reason has been referred, to respondent No. 1, and the matter is being 
delayed. 
14. In that view of the matter and for the reasons aforesaid, since 
exclusively the decision has to be taken by respondent No. 2, to 
whom already sufficient detail have been furnished. This writ 
petition is hereby disposed of with the direction to the petitioner to 
file afresh representation, along with the judgements, which he has 
placed on record to be relied for consideration of his claim for 
compassionate appointment, and as soon as the petitioner submits his 
representation, the same would be considered by respondent No. 2 or
by any other competent respondent, having competent to pass an 
order of compassionate appointment and to take a decision on the 
same based on the materials already discussed above within the 
period of six weeks from the date of presentation of the certified 
copy of this judgement.
15. Subject to the aforesaid observations, the writ petition stands 
disposed of.
