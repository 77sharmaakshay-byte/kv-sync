COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CLCON No. 59 of 2026
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Bhupesh Kandpal, learned counsel
for the petitioners.
2. Mr. P.C. Bisht, learned C.S.C along with 
Mr. J.S. Bisht, learned Standing Counsel for the 
State.
3. Mr. P.C. Bisht, learned Chief Standing 
counsel, apprised to this Court that in the entire 
area regarding which the writ court passed an 
order, as on date no illegal mining activity is 
being carried out. If it is so, then the attempts 
made by the officials are really appreciable, 
however, the question is whether the illegal 
mining activities are completely stopped 
forever and in case those who were indulged in 
illegal mining activity and penalized for more 
than five times, and acquired assets then 
whether those assets are the proceeds of crime, 
and if it so then what action should be taken to 
such proceeds of crime. 
4. It has also been apprised that against 
various persons FIR has been lodged. Now, the 
question is if the FIR has been lodged, is it 
sufficient? If yes in what manner, the 
investigation is being carried out.
5. In the written instructions the references 
have been given about certain FIRs and 
therefore this Court wants to know in what 
manner the investigation is being conducted. 
6. Put up this case on 19.05.2026. 
7. The concerned investigating officers shall 
join the proceedings through V.C.8. Mr. P.C. Bisht also apprised to this Court 
that against those license holders who were 
found to be indulged in illegal mining activity,
recommendation has been made for cancelling 
their license as well as to blacklist them so that 
in future they may not get any mining license. 
The exercise as done by the officials after taking 
cognizance in this contempt petition is really 
appreciable and this Court hope and trust that 
in future they will take strict action against 
those who were indulged in illegal mining 
activity. 
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
