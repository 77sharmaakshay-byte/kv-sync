COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 1713 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. M.C. Pant, learned counsel for the petitioner, 
through video conferencing and Mr. D.S. Mehta, learned 
counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Sachin Mohan Singh Mehta, learned Brief 
Holder for the State.
3. It is the case of the petitioner that petitioner was 
substantively appointed as ‘Collection Ameen’ with the 
respondent-Department on 19.04.2005.
4. The respondent-Department vide orders dated 
12.09.2022 and 18.07.2023 has directed for regularization 
of services of various Collection Ameens (private 
respondents herein) w.e.f., 09.11.2000.
5. The contention of learned counsel for the petitioner 
is that by doing this, the petitioner, who has been 
substantively appointed, will come down in the seniority 
list of the Collection Ameen. His objection to this tentative 
seniority list has been rejected vide order dated 03.08.2023 
passed by the respondents.
6. Per-contra, learned counsel for the State submitted 
that Collection Ameen, who have been directed to be 
considered regularized w.e.f. 09.11.2000 were engaged 
with the respondent-Department, much prior to the 
petitioner and have been regularized by various orders and 
in order to bring uniformity, all the Collection Ameens
were deemed to have been regularized w.e.f., 09.11.2000.
7. But it appears that action of the respondent has 
created anomaly in the seniority list of the Collection 2
Ameens, who have been appointed subsequently. It is trite 
law that a person is regularized from the date of his 
regularization and not from the date of his engagement as 
Daily Wager or a Contractual Employee. It appears that the 
matter requires deliberation. 
8. To explain the position, learned counsel for the State 
prays for and is granted eight weeks’ time for filing counter 
affidavit.
9. Further two weeks’ time is granted to the learned 
counsel for the petitioner for filing rejoinder affidavit.
10. Issue notice to the private respondent.
11. Steps to be taken within a week.
12. List this case on 30.11.2023.
13. As an interim measure, the impugned order dated 
03.08.2023, (Annexure No.1 to the writ petition) passed by 
respondent-Authority is hereby stayed, till the next date of 
listing.
14. Interim Relief Application (IA No.1 of 2023) stands 
disposed of accordingly.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
