CLMA No. 5914 of 2019 (Application for Withdrawal) 
IN 
WPSS No. 2157 of 2018 
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
 
Mr. T.P.S. Takuli, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. Susheel Vasistha, Brief Holder for 
the State of Uttarakhand. 
Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
A withdrawal application has been filed 
by the petitioner seeking withdrawal of writ 
petition. 
In the withdrawal application, it is 
stated that, during the pendency of the writ 
petition, respondent no. 3 has given 
appointment to the petitioner, hence, the 
petitioner does not want to press the writ 
petition. 
 In view of the facts mentioned in the 
withdrawal application, withdrawal 
application is allowed. 
Consequently, the writ petition is 
dismissed as withdrawn.
