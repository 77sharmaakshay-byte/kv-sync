Hon'ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Kartikeya Naithani with Mr. Shivam Rana, 
learned Counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Narain Dutt, learned Counsel for respondent 
no.1.
3. Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned Counsel for 
respondent no.2.
4. Petitioner is aggrieved by order dated 26.04.2023 
passed by respondent no.2 whereby a recovery of 
Rs.71,728/- in 72 monthly installments from the 
petitioner has been directed to be made. 
5. Petitioner is working in the office of respondent 
no.2 as a Class IV employee in regular establishment 
since 01.03.2003. The impugned order has been 
passed by respondent no.2 on the premise that the 
benefit of Assured Career Progression (ACP) Scheme, 
which was granted to the petitioner calculating the 
period of service rendered by him as daily wager, was 
modified which resulted into reduction of salary of 
petitioner, as according to respondent no.2, petitioner 
was entitled to be given the benefit of First ACP w.e.f. 
01.03.2013 and not from 16.12.2002, and therefore, 
excess payment was directed to be recovered.
6. Learned Counsel for petitioner submits that 
there is no role of petitioner in grant of ACP to him which resulted into alleged excess payment in favour 
of petitioner. Neither any misrepresentation nor any 
fraud has been alleged on the part of petitioner in 
grant of ACP, therefore, it is submitted that the 
recovery which has been directed cannot be made 
from the petitioner. 
7. Per contra, learned Counsel for respondent no.2 
submits that the impugned order was passed on 
26.04.2023 and pursuant to that order, about 40 
installments have already been deducted; there is a 
delay of more than three years in challenging the 
impugned order.
8. This Court finds substance in the submissions 
made by learned Counsel for the petitioner. The 
petitioner is a Class IV employee and there is no 
misrepresentation of facts, fraud or concealment on 
his part. Thus, the submission that 40 installments 
have already been recovered, cannot find favour with 
the Court at this stage.
9. Let the respondents file counter affidavit in the 
matter within six weeks.
10. List on 25.09.2026 along with WPSS No.2199 of 
2023.
11. In the meantime, no further recovery shall be 
made from the petitioner pursuant to the impugned 
order dated 26.04.2023.
12. Stay Application (IA/1/2026) stands disposed of.
