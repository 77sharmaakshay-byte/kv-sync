It is the claim of the petitioner that 
his father was working as a Junior 
Foreman with the respondent 
no.2/Uttarakhand Road Transport 
Corporation Ltd. He died on 12.05.2010, 
while still in service. But, his retiral dues 
have yet not been paid. The petitioner 
seeks interim directions for payment of 
such dues.
Learned counsel for the respondent 
nos. 2 and 3 would submit that whatever 
was due to the deceased employee had 
already been paid to the petitioner.
To it, learned counsel for the 
petitioner would submit that leave 
encashment and funds have not been 
provided to the petitioner.Interim Relief Application No. 1 of 
2025 is decided with the direction to the 
respondnet nos. 2 and 3 that till the next 
date fixed, they will calculate as to what 
are the amounts, under different heads,
that was available to the deceased 
employee and if there is any amount, it 
shall be paid to the petitioner.
