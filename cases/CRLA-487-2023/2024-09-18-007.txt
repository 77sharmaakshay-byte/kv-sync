2. Mr. G.S. Sandhu, Additional Advocate

the State of Uttarakhand. 
Bail Application (IA No. 1 of 2023)
3. This Appeal under Section 374(2) CrPC 
has been preferred against the judgment and 
order dated 20.06.2023 passed by learned 
Special Sessions Judge (POCSO), Pithoragarh 
in Special Sessions Trial No. 14 of 2021, 
whereby appellant has been convicted under 
Sections 376(2)(3) IPC, Section 5/6 of POCSO 
Act and Section 3 (1) (xii) of Scheduled Caste 
and Scheduled Tribe (Prevention of Atrocities) 
Act and was awarded maximum punishment 
of 20 years rigorous imprisonment with fine of 
Rs. 25,000/-. The appellant has sought his 
release on bail. 
4. Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that, as per the statement given by 
the victim, she was in love with the appellant; 
the age of the appellant was 19 years at the 
time of incident. He submits that appellant is 
resident of Haldwani and he had visited the 
place of victim at Pithoragarh, on account of 
friendship between them. He further submits 
that testimony of the victim indicates that it 
was a consensual relationship; it is improbable 
that forcible sexual intercourse could happen 
in the same room, where brother and sister of the victim were also residing; the appellant 
has no criminal history and is languishing in 
jail for four years. 
5. Learned State Counsel, however, 
opposed the prayer for bail and submitted that 
the victim was about 15 years of age on the 
date of incident. He, however, fairly admits 
that the appellant was aged about 19 years at 
the time of occurrence and he was also a 
teenager.
6. Since the appeal was filed only in the 
year 2023 and it is not likely to come-up for 
final hearing in near future, therefore, without 
expressing any opinion as to final merits of 
the case, we are of the opinion that appellant 
deserves bail at this stage. Accordingly, bail 
application is allowed.
7. Let the appellant- Pradeep Rawat be 
released on bail during the pendency of 
Appeal, on his executing a personal bond of 
Rs. 40,000/- and furnishing two sureties, each 
in the like amount, to the satisfaction of Court 
concerned. The sentence shall remain 
suspended in the meanwhile. 
8. List in due course.
