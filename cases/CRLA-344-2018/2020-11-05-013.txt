Mr. M.S. Pal, Senior Advocate

Mrs. Pushpa Bhatt, Deputy

Holders for the State of Uttarakhand. 
This is the second bail application. 
The first bail application was rejected on 
18.01.2019 on merits. 
Learned counsel for the appellant 
would submit that the appellant has 
served almost half of the sentence i.e. 
three years four months. He further 
submits that that if the present appellant 
is granted bail from this Court he will 
fully cooperate with the appeal and will 
not misuse the same. 
Learned State Counsel opposed the 
bail application but admits the fact that 
the appellant has served almost half of 
the sentence i.e. three years four months. 
Having heard learned Counsel for 
the parties and considered the facts and 
circumstances of the case, I am of the 
opinion that the appellant deserves bail 
at this Stage. Hence, the bail application 
is allowed. 
Let the appellant be released on 
bail, on executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two reliable sureties, each of 
like amount, to the satisfaction of Court 
concerned. 
 (N.S. Dhanik, J.)
