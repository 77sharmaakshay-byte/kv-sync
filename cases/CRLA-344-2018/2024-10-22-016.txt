COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLA No. 344 of 2018
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. M.S. Pal, learned Senior 
Advocate assisted by Mr. Vikramaditya 
Shah, learned counsel for the appellant.
2. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned Dy. A.G. 
with Mr. Sandeep Sharma and Mr. Deepak 
Bhardwaj, learned Brief Holders for the 
State.
3. Ms. Medha Pande, learned counsel 
for respondent no. 2.
4. The present appeal has been 
preferred against the judgment passed by 
the Trial Court dated 12.10.2018 in S.S.T.
No. 36 of 2017, State vs. Pradeep Kumar 
whereby the present appellant has been 
convicted under Section 363, 376 IPC read 
with Section 3/4 of the POCSO Act. The 
appellant was enlarged on bail on 
07.11.2020, by the coordinate Bench of 
this Court by observing therein that at the 
time of the incident the prosecutrix was 17 
years of age and in her statement 
recorded under Section 161 and 164 
Cr.P.C. she has categorically stated that 
she went with the appellant at her own will 
and she married with the appellant. It has 
been further observed that the medical 
report also does not suggest commission 
of offence of rape. 
5. Now the compounding application 
has been moved with this contention that 
the appellant married with the 
respondent- victim on 18.01.2020 at 
village Kaligaon, Lohaghat District 
Champawat and the said marriage is also registered in the office of the Registrar, 
Compulsory Registration of Marriages, 
Champawat on 21.01.2020, copy of which 
is enclosed as Annexure 2 to the 
compounding application. It is also 
contended in the compounding application 
that from this marriage one female child is
also born on 19.10.2021.
6. Today, the appellant who is on bail 
as well as the victim/prosecutrix, who now 
married with the appellant on 18.01.2020 
are present before this Court along with 
minor child and this Court also interact 
with each of them particularly with the 
prosecutrix who submits that since she is 
now married with the appellant and from 
their marriage one female child is born 
and she is living happily with the 
appellant, therefore, the order of 
conviction be set aside and the appellant 
be acquitted otherwise it will spoil their 
married life.
7. Per contra, Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, 
learned Dy. Advocate General for the 
State seriously object on the compounding 
application by placing reliance on the 
judgment of Hon’ble Apex Court in Suo 
Motu Writ Petition (C) No. 3 of 2023 
decided on 20.08.2024.
8. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned Dy. 
Advocate General with Mr. Sandeep 
Sharma, learned Brief Holder prays for 
and is granted three weeks’ time to file 
objection. One week thereafter is granted 
to the learned counsel for the appellant to 
file response to the said objection.
9. List this case on 08.11.2024.
