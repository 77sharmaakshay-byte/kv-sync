CRLA 344/2018
Hon’ble N.S. Dhanik, J.
Mr. B.S. Adhikari, Advocate, for 
the appellant/applicant. 
Mr. J.S. Virk, AGA, for the State. 
Applicant has been convicted for 
the offences under Sections 363, 376 IPC 
and 3/4 of the Protection of Children from 
Sexual Offences Act. 
Learned Counsel for the appellant 
contends that the Court below has erred in 
convicting the appellant. 
Learned State Counsel opposed the 
bail application by contending that the 
present appellant has committed very 
heinous crime and the victim is a minor 
girl and after trial the appellant stood 
convicted and has been sentenced to 
rigorous imprisonment for seven years for 
the offences under the POCSO Act. 
Considering the facts and 
circumstances of the case and on perusal 
of the materials on the record and also 
considering the nature of offences, this 
Court is not inclined to grant bail to the 
accused applicant. 
Bail application (CRMA 
11087/2018) is rejected. 
Urgency application (IA 751/2019) 
stands disposed of. 
 
 (N.S. Dhanik, J.)
