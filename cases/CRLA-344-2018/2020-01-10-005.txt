CRMA No. 43/2020 (Short Term Bail Application)
IN
CRLA No. 344 of 2018
Hon’ble N.S. Dhanik, J.
Mr. B.S. Adhikari, Advocate for the 
appellant.
Mrs. Geeta Parihar, Brief Holder for the 
State of Uttarakhand.
Heard learned counsel for the parties.
Learned counsel for the appellant 
contends that the marriage of the appellant 
and prosecutrix is scheduled to be held on 
18.01.2020, therefore, he may be granted short 
term bail for a period of one month.
Learned State Counsel admits this fact 
that the marriage of appellant and proseuctrix 
is scheduled to be held on 18.01.2020.
Considering the facts and circumstances
of the case, this Court is of the view that the 
appellant should be granted short term bail 
for a period of one month.
Consequently, let the appellant be 
enlarged on short term bail only for a period 
of one month from the date of his release, on 
his executing personal bond with two reliable 
sureties each of the like amount to the 
satisfaction of the Court concerned. 
It is directed that, on expiry of one 
month, the appellant shall surrender before 
the Superintendent of the Jail concerned.
Short term bail application stands 
disposed of accordingly.
Let a certified copy of this order be 
issued to the learned counsel for the parties, 
today itself, on payment of usual charges.
 
 (N.S. Dhanik, J.)
