are present in person before this Court.
5. After interacting with the parties, and after hearing the 
learned counsel for the parties, this Court is of the opinion 
that still there may be some reasonable grounds that the 
parties may come into the terms of the compromise, and settle 
their dispute amicably. Husband – Kamal Upreti, at the outset 
makes a statement that there is unnecessary mending by the 
relatives of his wife, and the reasons of his anonymity is that 
wife left his house without any considerable reasons, whereas 
the wife – Poonam Upreti, in turn also makes a similar 
statement that on instigation of the in-laws, the husband does 
not behave as he should, and thus, this is the main reason of 
their differences.
6. In considerate opinion of this Court, the matter is not 
such that it cannot be resolved.
7. Since the Husband – Kamal Upreti, has come all the 
way from Delhi, and the Wife – Poonam Upreti, is also 
present in person before this Court, the matter is referred immediately to the Mediator, State Legal Services Authority, 
for mediation between the parties. Let a report in this regard 
be also filed.
