 In this appeal against an Award under the 
Motor Vehicles Act, 1988, an application under 
Order 41 Rule 27 of the Code of Civil Procedure, 
1908 has been filed by the appellant so as to bring 
on record the income tax return filed by the 
deceased prior to his death.
 Learned counsel for the appellants submits 
that, in fact, the Tribunal has wrongly assessed the 
income of the deceased by not taking into 
consideration the income tax return filed by the 
deceased. But, according to him, as such the 
income tax return was not filed before the Tribunal, 
but only its acknowledgement was filed.
 Income of the deceased is to be assessed. In 
the claim petition, the deceased was earning 
Rs. 25,000/- per month, whereas it is submitted 
that as per the income tax return of the deceased of 
the previous year, his annual income was 
Rs. 4,95,000/-. In view of the settled law, an average of 
income has to be taken. It is also settled law that 
income tax return filed post death of the deceased 
may also be taken into consideration while 
computing the income of the deceased. 
 In view of it, the income tax return that is 
proposed to be filed by the appellant may be taken 
into evidence, but before it is done, the respondents 
may be given an opportunity to file any document in 
rebuttal or to inform the Court as to whether they 
would like to adduce any evidence.
