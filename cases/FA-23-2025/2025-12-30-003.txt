Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. D.S. Negi, learned counsel for the 
appellants.
2. Mr. I.P.Kohli, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State.
3. The appellant no.2 as per the array of 
parties is the president of Society namely Shri 
Ram Mulakh Darbar, Divya Yog Mandir, 
Sanyas Road, Kankhal, District Haridwar, 
which is a registered society. Learned counsel 
for the appellants on 04.03.2025 submits that 
the appellants have no source of income to 
pay the Court fee and permitted the 
appellants to file the present First Appeal as 
indigent persons and on such submission, Mr. 
I.P.Kohli was sought time to inquire about the
means of the appellants. As per the report,
the appellant no.2 is residing in Gurgaon, but 
as per the array of parties the registered 
society is located at Sanyas Road, Kankhal, 
District Haridwar. The appeal is filed by a 
society through its president, therefore, there 
is no any question to seek a permission to file 
the appeal by an indigent person particularly 
when the appellants is a society.
4. Let the Collector of Haridwar may get 
the report about the society and submit its
report by 02.01.2026.
5. List this case on 02.01.2026.
6. Urgency Application IA No. 03 of 2025 
stands disposed of.
