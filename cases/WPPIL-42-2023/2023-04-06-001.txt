The petitioner has preferred the present writ 
petition in Public Interest raising an important issue 
with regard to disposal of bio-medical waste in the 
State, particularly in District Pithoragarh. 
The petitioner claims that bio-medical waste is 
being disposed of as municipal garbage, and is being 
burnt in open space, and is even being put in drains, 
rivers or gutters. The petitioner has placed on record 
photographs of, what is claimed to be, the illegal 
disposal of bio-medical waste. One photograph shows 
the condition of the machine installed for treating bio￾medical waste in Government Hospital in Pithoragarh 
City, which the petitioner claims is non-operational. 
The petitioner also submits that even in the 
Government Hospital for Women in Pithoragarh, there 
is no shredder machine installed for treating bio-medical waste. 
An inspection report of Dr. Madal Bonal, 
Additional Chief Medical Officer, Pithoragarh has been 
placed on record in relation to clinical establishments 
located in Pithoragarh district. According to this report, 
a large number of clinics and nursing homes in district 
Pithoragarh have not even registered their 
establishments with the Uttarakhand State Pollution 
Control Board. 
Mr. Singh, who appears on advance notice on 
behalf of the Uttarakhand State Pollution Control 
Board, states that there are only two bio-medical 
waste treatment plants operational, and two are under 
construction. The two operational plants are situated 
at district Haridwar and in district Udham Singh Nagar. 
There is no bio-medical waste treatment place 
established in the hilly areas. 
In our view, this is an alarming situation, which 
the State Government has to look at top priority, 
considering the fact that the Country is still going 
through the pandemic of COVID-19. We, therefore, 
direct the respondents to file their respective counter￾affidavits within three weeks. They should specifically 
state as to what steps they have already taken to 
comply with the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 
2016. 
We further direct the respondents-authorities to 
implement the provisions of the said Rules forthwith. 
The Uttarakhand State Pollution Control Board shall 
disclose in its affidavit what steps have been taken 
against the violators of the aforesaid Rules, and in 
accordance with the Rules. 
In the affidavit to be filed by respondent nos.3 and 4, they should specifically state as to what steps 
have been taken for allocation of land for 
establishment of bio-medical waste treatment plants in 
the State. The affidavits shall be filed on behalf of 
respondent nos.3 and 4, by the Secretary, Forest and 
Environment, and by the Secretary, Health 
themselves. 
List on 09.05.2023.
