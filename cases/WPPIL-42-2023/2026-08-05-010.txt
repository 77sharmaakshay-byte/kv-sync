7. In compliance of the previous order, respondent 
nos.7 & 8 have filed the affidavit of Scientific Officer, 
Regional Office Uttarakhand Pollution Control Board, 
Haldwani, District Nainital. According to it, inspection was 
conducted on 12.03.2024 at M/s B.D. Pandey District Male 
Hospital and M/s H.G. Pant District Female Hospital. The 
observations and findings of the inspection carried out at 
the aforesaid two hospitals reveals that none of them are 
fully compliant with the Bio-Medical Waste Management 
Rules, 2016. In M/s B.D. Pandey District Male Hospital, 
Pithoragarh, which is a 120 bedded district hospital, it was 
found that the medical waste was being mixed with 
domestic/ solid waste, and consequently discharged 
through the common sewage line, or as municipal waste. 
8. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the UKPCB states that this is happening because of lack of 
understanding of the staff between bio-medical waste and 
municipal waste. He states that the Scientific Officer, 
UKPCB is going to organize a training camp in the hospital 
to impart training to the nurses and other staff of the 
hospital on the said aspect. 
9. It is also stated in the affidavit that both the aforesaid 
two hospitals are disposing of their bio-medical waste in the 
deep burial pits in the hospital premises. In M/s B.D. 
Pandey District Male Hospital, the bio-medical waste was 
found lying openly within the hospital premises without 
proper segregation. Learned counsel for the UKPCB 
submits that the deep burial pits of M/s B.D. Pandey District 
Hospital were not found covered with wire mesh, as is 
stipulated under Schedule-II of Bio-Medical Waste 
Management Rules, 2016. He states that the said issue has 
been duly communicated to the Medical Superintendent of 
the hospital and he has assured to rectify the same. It is 
submitted that the UKPCB will make another inspection 
after two weeks to find out, whether the said issue has 
been addressed or not. 
10. In respect of M/s H.G. Pant District Female Hospital, 
it is stated that the Consent to Operate the medical facility 
was valid only upto 31.03.2026 and, thereafter, the
hospital had not submitted any application through online 
system of the Board for renewal. The same issue has also 
been flagged in respect of M/s B.D. Pandey District Male 
Hospital, Pithoragarh. 
11. The affidavit further states that there is only one 
common bio-medical waste facility, which is operational in 
Kumaun Region. It is located at Gadarpur Town, Udham 
Singh Nagar. It is stated that under the Rules, if bio￾medical facility is not located within 75 kms. of healthcare 
facilities, then it is permissible to dispose of medical waste in deep burial pits in rural and remote areas and, therefore, 
it is permissible for the hospitals to dispose of the medical 
waste in deep burial pits. It is further stated that one more 
common bio-medical waste treatment facility is being 
established in District Almora, and wherein Consent to 
Establish had already been granted. Another facility at 
Sitarganj Town, Udham Singh Nagar is also at final stages 
of operation. 
12. Learned counsel for UKPCB further submits that there 
are five more hospitals in District Pithoragarh, and 
inspection of the said hospitals had already been carried 
out. He states that the reports relating to these hospitals 
would be filed within two weeks. 
13. In view of the above, we issue the following 
directions:-
(a) The Medical Superintendent of both the 
hospitals will ensure that application for renewal of 
the Consent to Operate is filed without any further 
delay, and the steps taken in this regard shall be 
disclosed by them by filing their affidavits. 
(b) The Scientific Officer of the UKPCB shall, in 
consultation with the Medical Superintendent of both 
the government medical facilities, chalk out a 
timetable for imparting training on the relevant 
pollution laws to the nursing staff and other 
concerned employees of the hospital within four 
weeks. 
(c) The Medical Superintendent of M/s B.D. Pandey 
District Male Hospital, Pithoragarh shall ensure that 
the missing wire mesh and other shortcomings found 
during inspection are duly taken care of by the next 
date, and the same shall also be reported to this 
Court through the affidavit to be filed in compliance of 
the instant order. (d) The State Government shall explore, whether it 
would be feasible to establish an independent Bio￾Medical Waste Treatment Plant at District Pithoragarh
keeping in view the fact that there are eight major 
Government Hospitals and several other government 
and private health centres. The stand of the State 
Government, in respect of the same, shall also be 
brought on record through the affidavit of respondent 
no. 4.
14. List after four weeks.
