 Heard on Stay Application (IA) No.1 of 2025.
 The petitioner appeared for the post of 
Assistant Teacher (L.T.) Computer Education in 
response to the advertisement issued by the 
respondent no.2/Uttarakhand Subordinate 
Services Selection Commission (“the 
Commission”). They were to appear for 
documents verification on 10.06.2025, but 
according to the Commission, the petitioner did 
not appear. Therefore, her candidature was 
rejected by virtue of notification dated 
05.07.2025. It is impugned in this writ petition. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the petitioner is a Computer 
educated person. No notice was ever given to the 
petitioner to appear for document verification on 
10.06.2025. He would submit that the petitioner 
was continuously checking the website of the 
Commission. On 15.07.2025, till 05:54 morning, 
there was no notification uploaded and 
subsequently, the petitioner checked the website 
at 12:51 hours on that date when the notification 
was updated. It is also submitted that on 15.07.2025, at 05:54 AM, when the petitioner did 
not see any notification, she came to know that 
her candidature has been rejected. She 
immediately made a telephonic call to the 
Commission and, in fact, she wrote an e-mail on 
15.07.2025 at 08:31 AM, which is Annexure No.9 
to the writ petition, but she did not receive any 
information. 
 Learned counsel for the Commission would 
submit that, in fact, the notice was uploaded on 
the website on 05.06.2025, requiring the 
candidates to appear for documents verification 
on 10.06.2025. He would submit that, in fact, the 
website has gone for maintenance and up￾gradation, therefore, in the new website the data 
is being migrated and there may be some issues 
related to that. 
 Having considered, as an interim measure, till 
the next date of listing this Court directs that one 
position, on which, the petitioner applied, shall 
be kept vacant. 
 Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
