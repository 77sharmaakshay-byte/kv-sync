2. Petitioner retired from the post of Health 
Visitor (Group-D) from Department of Medical 
and Health on 31.03.2023. Her GPF has not been 
released so far, which has compelled her to file 
this writ petition.
3. Perusal of the record reveals that Incharge 
Medical Officer, Community Health Centre, 
Dwarahat, District Almora referred the matter of 
petitioner to Accountant General on 16.03.2026 
and in that letter, impression was given that 
petitioner is about to retire from service.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that when petitioner retired in the month of 
May, 2023, then there was no occasion for the 
Incharge Medical Officer to mention that 
petitioner is about to retire from service.
5. He submits that because of this mischief, the amount of GPF has not been released to her 
which is causing untold misery to the petitioner.
6. Let explanation be called from the 
Incharge Medical Officer, Community Health 
Centre, Dwarahat, District Almora within 10 
days.
7. List on 06.05.2026 as fresh.
8. Mr. Rajesh Sharma, learned counsel 
appearing for Accountant General shall take 
instructions regarding the progress made in the 
matter in the meantime. 
9. Registry is directed to communicate this 
order to Chief Medical Officer, Almora, 
electronically within 24 hours.
