COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRLR No. 237 of 2017
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Ms. Zeba Naaz, learned counsel holding the brief of 
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, learned counsel for the revisionists.
2. Mr. Tarun Prakash Singh Takuli, learned counsel for 
the respondent.
3. The revisionist joined in person before the counsellor, 
however, the respondent-husband joined the proceeding 
through V.C. since he was not aware about the date fixed.
4. I peruse the counsellor’s report from which it reveals 
that both the parties require further counselling along with 
their daughter who is pursuing her studies in B.Tech in 
Graffic Era. The revisionist is also serving in Government 
Primary School and she is also highly educated. The 
husband is serving at New Delhi.
5. After perusing the counsellor’s report this court is of 
the view that further counselling is also required though 
after further interaction with both of them they with a 
heavy heart submits that both of them will try to focus on 
the future of their daughter, who is studying in B.Tech and 
this court is also of the view that both the parties being the 
parents of the daughter, who is pursuing studies in B.Tech, 
should focus on the future of their daughter.
6. In such view of the matter, let both the parties may 
talk with each other and if possible enjoy the Holi festival 
together with their daughter at any place, since both of 
them are the best person to resolve their matrimonial 
dispute.
7. Both the parties are directed to exchange their mobile 
numbers today itself and they will open their whatsapp 24 
hours so that both the parties may talk to each other.
8. List this matter on 24.03.2026.
9. On that day the parties along with their daughter 
may join the proceeding through V.C.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
