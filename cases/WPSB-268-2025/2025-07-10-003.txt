COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSB No.268 of 2025
Hon’ble G. Narendar, C.J.
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Heard Mr. Harshit Sanwal, learned counsel for the 
petitioner and Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned C.S.C. along with 
Mr. S.S. Chaudhary, learned Standing Counsel and Mr. 
S.M.S. Mehta, learned Brief Holder for the State. 
2. Dr. K. K. Pandey, Regional Director, Medical 
Health, Kumaon Division and Dr. N.S. Rawat, 
Orthopaedic Surgeon, Government Hospital Nainital are 
present before the Court. 
3. The subject, which constrained this Court to seek 
the personal presence of the Doctors, was a Medical 
Certificate issued by Regional Medical Board at B.D. 
Pandey, District Hospital, Nainital, wherein the ailment of 
the Government Servant is recorded as under:-
“Elsewhere, managed case PIVD C5-C6 with nerve root 
compression. Patient needs regular follow up.”
4. We have queried Dr. K. K. Pandey and he would 
submit that the opinion has been formed, after examining 
the radiological reports issued by one Om Minakshi Health 
Care and the OPD chits issued by one Vivekanand 
Hospital and the District Government Hospital, Nainital. 
The MRI Film is not part of the file, which is produced by 
the Doctor present before the Court. 
5. We have specifically queried the Doctor, as to 
whether the medical condition is such that it would restrict 
physical activities of the subject candidate, to which the 
Doctor states that it depends on the body condition and it 
does not amount to or cannot be equated with the 
handicap. 6. Be that as it may, the cause for concern is the lack of 
elucidation. The provisions of Section 3 (d) of The 
Uttarakhand Annual Transfer for Public Servants Act of 
2017 reads as under:-
“(d) "Serious Patient" means the spouse and family 
(which includes the children of the age up to 18 years and 
parents) of any employee suffering from a serious diseases 
and it includes Cancer, Blood Cancer, AIDS/HIV 
(positive), Heart disease (Having Bypass surgery or 
Angioplasty done) Kidney disease (dependent on the 
dialyses due to failure of both the kidneys or kidney 
transplanted or one kidney removed), Tuberculosis (both 
the lungs infected or one lung fully damaged), SARS (third 
stage), epilepsy, mental disease and any such other 
disease for which State Medical Board doesn't recommend 
for posting in any particular place/area and the committee 
constituted under section 27 of the Act gives its approval 
thereon.”
7. Section 3(d) defines, who is a “serious patient” and 
it also mandates that the certificate ought to be issued by 
the State Medical Board and the State Medical Board 
must, in categorical terms, not recommend the posting of 
the employee in any particular area or place. Disability is 
defined under Section 3(e) and the competent authority to 
issue certificate is defined under Section 3 (f). 
8. On a reading of the above, it is apparent that the 
certificate issued by the Regional Medical Board does not 
meet the requirements nor in compliance with the mandate 
of Clause (d) of Section 3. 
9. The opinion is that the patient needs regular follow 
up. The opinion does not disclose as to whether the 
medical condition, either completely handicaps the person 
from discharging duty or that the medical condition is 
likely to get aggravated and hamper the performance of 
duty by the patient if posted in any particular place. 
10. The certificate is so ambiguous to the extent, that it 
merely states that the patient requires regular follow up which, in our opinion, has probably resulted in an 
interpretation which favours the Officer, which is 
impermissible and, in fact, completely against the spirit of 
the Clause (d) of Section 3. 
11. In that view of the matter, it is but necessary that a 
direction be issued to all the Medical Boards, more 
particularly, to the Medical Boards, other than State 
Medical Board, to examine as to whether they are 
competent to issue a certificate, in terms of Section 3 of 
the Act of 2017. 
12. We direct the State Medical Board to ensure that the 
certificates issued are in compliance with the provisions of 
Section 3 of the Act of 2017. We further direct the State 
Medical Board to ensure that all examinations and all 
certificates are accompanied by the necessary radiological 
reports and other diagnosis and such other medical records, 
which amply demonstrate the opinion of the State Medical 
Board. The State Medical Board and other Medical Boards
shall dispense with the system of issuing just a certificate
and shall ensure that all medical certificates are 
accompanied by a set of medical records, which have 
enabled the Medical Board to form an opinion. 
13. List this case on 14.07.2025. The presence of the 
Doctors, in future hearings, is dispensed with.
14. Copy of this order be circulated to the Chief 
Secretary for circulation amongst the concerned 
authorities. 
15. Copy of this order be also furnished to the Chief 
Standing Counsel.
