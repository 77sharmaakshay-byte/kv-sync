6. After hearing learned counsel for the parties, it 
reveals that selection process was initiated for supplying
73 vacancies on the post of Pharmacist in October, 2024 
and the selecting body has recommended only 64 
candidates for appointment against 73 vacancies. 
7. This Court has been informed that due to the 
interim order dated 10.01.2026, some of the candidates, 
who have been recommended for appointment could not 
be offered appointment.
8. In such view of the matter, the interim order dated 
10.01.2026 is modified, and it is provided that all 64 
candidates, recommended for appointment, shall be 
offered appointment and petitioners in these writ 
petitions may be permitted to continue on contract 
against the remaining unfilled vacancies.
