The present Application has been filed by the 
applicants seeking anticipatory bail in Case Crime No. 226 of 
2025, registered at Kotwali Roorkee, District Haridwar under 
Sections 318(4), 351(3), 352 and Section 61 of the Bharatiya 
Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. 
2. As per the First Information Report dated 24.06.2025, 
the applicants had taken Rs.40,00,000/- from the informant to 
deposit in the mutual fund. They had got his signature on the 
documents of the mutual fund. But, they did not deposit the 
money in the mutual fund. 
3. Heard Mr. Parikshit Saini, learned counsel for the 
applicants and Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. for the 
respondent. 
4. Mr. Parikshit Saini, Advocate, has submitted that the 
applicant no.1 had given Rs.30,00,000/- to the informant as a 
friendly loan. He returned only Rs.14,15,500/-. 2
5. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, A.G.A., has sought three weeks’ 
time to file objection to the anticipatory bail application. 
6. Time is granted. 
7. Having heard the learned counsel for the parties and 
in the facts and circumstances of the case, as an ad-interim 
measure, it is directed that in the event of arrest of the 
applicants Deepak Kumar Baweja, Gagan Baweja, Vikas Kumar 
Baweja and Chirag Baweja, they shall be released on interim bail 
on executing a personal bond of Rs.30,000/- and two reliable 
sureties, each of the like amount, by each one of them, to the 
satisfaction of the Investigating Officer/Arresting Officer, subject 
to the following conditions:-
(i) Applicants shall cooperate with the Investigating 
Agency and they shall make themselves available for 
interrogation by a police officer as and when required; 
(ii) Applicants shall not directly or indirectly make any 
inducement, threat or promise to any person 
acquainted with the facts of this case;
(iii) Applicants shall not leave the country without the 
previous permission of this Court. 
8. List on 07.08.2025 for hearing on the anticipatory bail 
application.
