Mr. V.D. Bisen, Additional C.S.C. for

Heard. 
The appellants sought permission to 
sell 50 per cent share of land in her name 
and in the name of her children Sameer 
Sharma and Sonali Sharma, under Sections 
7 and 8 of the Hindi Minority and 
Guardianship Act, 1956, and Sections 27 
and 29 of the Guardians and Wards Act, 
1890, on the ground that the son of the 
appellant Sameer Sharma is mentally 
retarded; he needs continuous caring; the 
property is big one. Therefore, the appellant
no.1, Shivani Sharma, wants to sell the 
property and purchase a small property so 
that her son Sameer Sharma can be looked 
after properly.
This application was rejected by the 
impugned judgment dated 19.04.2025, inter 
alia, on the ground that the appellant could 
not show as to how after the sale of the 
property, the interest of the son, Sameer
Sharma, would be protected.
In the application that was filed in the 
court below as well as in this appeal, Sonali 
Sharma, the another share holder of the 
property, is not a party. The property is joint 
and 50 per cent shares belongs to one 
Anupam Sharma. He is also not made the party.
The Court posted a question to learned 
counsel for the appellants as to whether the 
brother in law of the appellant no.1, Shivani 
Sharma, and Sonali Sharma are agreeable to 
this sale, as proposed by the appellants? 
Learned counsel for the appellants seeks time 
to file affidavits of those two persons.
Let it be so filed within a week. 
List this matter on 03.07.2026, at 
02:15 p.m.
The appellant, Ms. Shivani Sharma, her 
daughter Sonali Sharma as well as Anupam 
Sharma shall join the proceedings on that 
date through video conferencing. 
 (Ravindra Maithani J.)
