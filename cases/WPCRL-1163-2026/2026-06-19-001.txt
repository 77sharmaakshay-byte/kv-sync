Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Paritosh Dalakoti and Mr. Hemant 
Pant, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. V.S. Pal, learned A.G.A. along 
with Mr. Nikhil Bisht, learned Brief Holder 
for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution 
of India, the petitioner seeks issuance of a 
writ in the nature of certiorari for quashing 
the First Information Report/Case Crime 
No. 0343 of 2026 dated 06.06.2026, 
registered under Sections 115(2), 127(1), 
351(2) and 352 of B.N.S. at Police Station 
Patel Nagar, District Dehradun. The 
petitioner further prays for issuance of a 
writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus directing the respondent no. 2 
to transfer the investigation of the 
aforesaid case to a higher rank police 
officer and the petitioner shall not arrest in 
connection with the aforesaid F.I.R. 
4. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that petitioner no.1 is working as a Lecturer in Physical 
Education, petitioner no.2 is the Head of 
the Department of Basic Science and 
petitioner no.3 is the Head of the 
Department of Information Technology at 
Uttarakhand Polytechnic Institute, 
Dehradun; that, on 04.06.2026, the 
semester examinations of various courses 
were being conducted in the institution 
and the petitioners had been duly 
appointed as invigilators for the said 
examinations; that, the son of respondent 
no.4 was also appearing in one of the 
examinations; that, during the course of 
the examination, he was allegedly found 
using unfair means and was accordingly 
intercepted by the petitioners and sternly 
warned not to indulge in any misconduct.
5. Learned counsel would further submit 
that on the very next day, i.e., on 
05.06.2026, respondent no.4, accompanied 
by his son and several other persons, 
including one Mahesh Kandwal, Sub￾Inspector, unlawfully entered the premises 
of the institution and assaulted and 
manhandled the petitioners and other 
members of the staff; that, the entire 
incident was captured in the CCTV 
cameras installed in the college premises.
6. He would further submit that 
immediately after the incident, the 
petitioners approached the concerned 
Police Station and lodged a complaint; 
however, no action was taken thereon and 
their complaint was not registered; that, in 
the meantime, respondent no.4 lodged an 
F.I.R., which came to be promptly registered by the concerned Police Station; 
that, thereafter, upon an application 
submitted by the Principal of the 
institution to the Senior Superintendent of 
Police, Dehradun, the complaint of the 
petitioners was eventually registered on 
06.06.2026.
7. Learned counsel would submit that 
respondent no.4 is himself a police official 
and is presently posted at a Police Station 
in Dehradun, therefore, taking advantage 
of his official position, respondent no.4, 
along with other police personnel 
accompanying him, assaulted and 
intimidated the petitioners and other staff 
members within the institution premises. 
The CCTV footage depicting the entire 
occurrence has already been handed over 
to the Investigating Officer. In such 
circumstances, it is submitted that the 
petitioners have made out a prima facie 
case and deserve interim protection during 
the pendency of the present writ petition.
8. Issue notice to respondent no.4, 
returnable at an early date.
9. Steps to be taken within one week.
10. List this case after service report is 
received.
11. State shall file detailed counter 
affidavit in the matter. 
12. Having regard to the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
petitioner and considering the facts and 
circumstances of the case, it is provided, 
by way of an interim measure, that till the 
next date of listing, the petitioners shall not be arrested pursuant to F.I.R./Case 
Crime No. 0343 of 2026 dated 06.06.2026, 
registered at Police Station Patel Nagar, 
District Dehradun provided that the 
petitioners cooperate with the investigation 
in all respects and shall appear before the 
Investigating Officer as and when required.
13. Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
