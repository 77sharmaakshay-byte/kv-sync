Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate for

Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate for the

This revision has been filed, under 
Section 25 Provincial Small Causes 
Court Act, against the order dated 
27.04.2018 passed by learned Judge, 
Small Causes Court/Additional District 
Judge, Tehri Garhwal in S.C.C. Suit No. 
03 of 2016. 
Heard. 
Admit the revision. 
Lower court record be summoned. 
List this revision on 27.06.2018. 
As an interim measure, it is directed 
that till the next date of listing, execution 
of impugned order dated 27.04.2018 shall 
remain stayed subject to the condition that 
revisionist deposits the entire decretal 
amount before the Court below on or 
before 31.05.2018 and also continue to 
pay a sum of Rs. 1,000/- (Rupees one 
thousand) per month before the learned 
court below, for use of occupation of the 
premises in question. 
The said amount, so deposited by 
the revisionist shall be released in favour 
of the dependant/respondent on his 
application without prejudice the rights 
and contention of the revisionist. 
Consequently, stay application 
(CLMA No. 5982 of 2018) stands 
disposed of.
