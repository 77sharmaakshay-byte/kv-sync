Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Sunil Chandra, Advocate for the appellant.
This Second Appeal was preferred against as 
many as 11 respondents. The order sheet reflects that 
respondent Nos. 1 to 8 have been served and they are 
being represented by their counsel Mr. Rajat Mittal.
There is no office report pertaining to 
respondent Nos. 9 to 11, though they have been 
shown as to be proforma respondents, but as a 
measure of precaution and for better appreciation of 
the controversy, this Court is of the view that they are 
also required to be served.
Hence, the appellant would take steps for 
serving respondent Nos. 9 to 11 within a period of 
one week from today.
List this matter after the notices are served on 
respondent Nos. 9, 10 and 11.
The Urgency Application No. 10854/2019 
stands disposed of accordingly.
