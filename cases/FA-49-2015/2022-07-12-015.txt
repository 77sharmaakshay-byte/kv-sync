Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate,

The arguments in this First Appeal 
proceeded at a final stage, but during the 
course of hearing, it was found that 
respondent Nos. 9, 10 & 11, who have been 
shown as performa respondents, were 
rather contesting the proceedings before 
the learned trial Court by filing their 
independent written statement.
While admitting the First Appeal, 
notices were sent to the respondents and 
there is an office report dated 15.10.2019, 
that respondent Nos. 9 & 10, have been 
served with the notice; respondent No. 11 
has not yet been served with the notice;
with the report that he is not residing at the 
given address.
In that eventuality, since respondent 
Ns. 9, 10 & 11, are the real brothers of the 
present appellant, it would be in all fitness 
and in the interest of justice to make an 
effort for serving respondent Nos. 9, 10 & 
11, by way of dasti.
Learned counsel for the appellant 
would take necessary steps within a period of one week from today.
List this First Appeal under the same 
head as soon as the affidavit of service is 
filed by the learned counsel for the 
appellant.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
