WPSS No.1751 of 2005
Hon’ble Tarun Agarwala, J.
Mr. A. D. Tripathi, the learned counsel for the petitioner. 
Mr. Subhash Upadhyaya, the learned Brief Holder for the 
State of Uttarakhand. 
Heard the learned counsel for the parties. 
A perusal of the affidavit of the State of Uttarakhand filed in the 
review application indicates that the services of the petitioner was 
allocated to the State of U.P. The petitioner was suspended in the year 
1996. The State Government of U.P. has not been arrayed as party. 
Consequently, no direction can be issued to the State Government of 
U.P. Further this court is of the opinion that this court does not have 
any territorial jurisdiction to decide the matter. The writ petition fails 
and is dismissed. 
 (Tarun Agarwala, J.)
