Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Prabhat Bohra, learned counsel for petitioners. 
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned S.C. for the State.
3. Mr. A.K. Bisht, learned counsel for District 
Cooperative Bank /respondent nos.2 & 3.
4. The petitioners have challenged the order dated 
22.06.2024 issued by respondent no.2 (Annexure No.1 to the 
writ petitions) whereby the services of the petitioners have 
been dispensed with, and a further writ of mandamus has 
been sought commanding the respondents to permit the 
petitioners to perform their duties to the post on which they 
were appointed after due process of law. 
5. It is the case of the petitioners that they were 
participated after following due selection process vide order 
dated 23.03.2022 and after completion of the process they 
were selected and appointed as Assistant/Guard in the 
District Cooperative Bank, Pithoragarh. By the impugned 
order the service of the petitioners have been dispensed with.
6. It is contended by the learned counsel that petitioners 
have completed probation period of one year as required 
under the U.P. Cooperative Societies Employees Service 
Regulations 1975 (hereinafter to be referred to as 
‘Regulations of 1975) and according to learned counsel for 
the petitioners, once the petitioners have completed their 
probation period, they cannot be thrown out of service. 
7. It is further contended by learned counsel for the 
petitioners that a full fledge disciplinary inquiry is required 
under Regulations 84 and 85 of the Regulations of 1975. 
8. It is also contended that before passing the impugned 
order though, a show cause notice was issued to the 
petitioners, but, they have never been issued any charge 
sheet before passing such order nor any disciplinary inquiry 
was conducted. 
9. I have perused the impugned order as well as the 2
provisions of Regulation 1975 referred by the petitioners 
during arguments. 
10. Since petitioners are regular employees of 
respondents-District Cooperative Bank, they cannot be 
thrown out of service in this manner without adhering to the 
disciplinary proceedings as provided under Regulation 84 
and 85 of the Regulations 1975.
11. I find substance in the submission made by learned 
counsel for the petitioner. 
12. Respondents are directed to file their counter affidavits 
within six weeks.
13. The impugned order dated 22.06.2024, passed by 
respondent no.2 is hereby stayed qua the petitioners till the next 
date of listing.
14. List this matter on 30.09.2024.
15. Registry is directed to struck out the name of Mr. 
Pankaj Chaturvedi from the cause list and in his place, name 
of Mr. Anil Kumar Bisht, learned counsel for respondent
nos.2 & 3 be published henceforth.
16. Interim relief applications filed in these writ petitions, 
stand disposed of.
