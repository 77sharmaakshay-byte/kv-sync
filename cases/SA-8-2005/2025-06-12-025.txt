COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
I.A. No. 4 of 2025
In
SA No. 155 of 2022
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. J.K. Pandey, holding brief of Mr. Piyush Garg, 
learned counsel for the appellants.
2. Mr. R.P. Nautiyal, learned Sr. Advocate, assisted by 
Mr. Pavan Kumar Nath, learned counsel for the 
respondents.
3. There was the delay of 5749 days in filing the instant 
second appeal, which was condoned by this Court on 
12.03.2024. Against the order dated 12.03.2024, respondents 
filed a Special Leave to Appeal before the Hon’ble Supreme 
Court, which was disposed of by the Hon’ble Supreme 
Court on 18.11.2014 with the following directions:
 
 “While we are not inclined to interfere with the order 
passed by the High Court condoning the delay of 5749 
days in filing the second appeal, we direct the respondents 
to pay costs quantified at Rs. 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty 
Thousand Only) to the appellant herein within a period of 
three months from today.” 
4. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that in 
compliance of the Hon’ble Supreme Court’s order, a 
demand draft of Rs. 50,000/- was sent through post to the 
respondents but the same was refused and thereafter, 
original demand draft is placed on record by way of 
application (I.A. No. 4 of 2025).
5. Learned counsel for the respondents submits that the 
validity period of demand draft was 3 months, and as such, 
the same is expired now. 
6. In such an eventuality, appellant is directed to place a
fresh demand draft or get the demand draft renewed within 
a week. 
7. Registry is directed to return back the demand draft 
to the learned counsel for the appellants. 
8. List on 25.06.2025.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
