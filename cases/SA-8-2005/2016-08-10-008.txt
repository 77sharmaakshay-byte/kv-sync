SA No.08/05 
Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
 Mr. R.P. Nautiyal, Sr. Advocate, for 
the appellant. 
 Mr. Rajendra Dobhal, Sr. Adv. 
assisted by Mr. Prince Chauhan, 
Advocate, for respondent no.1. 
 Plaintiff Mr. Rajendra Singh and 
defendant/respondent no.1 Mangal 
Singh are the close relatives of each 
other. The dispute is regarding the 
cancellation of sale deed which was 
executed by Mr. Kripal Singh (father of 
Rajendra Singh) in favour of Mr. Mangal 
Singh on 22.8.1969. 
 It has been averred that the land 
was in the nature of Sirdari, so this sale 
deed could not have been executed. 
 The suit was perfectly decreed on 
19.11.2001, where-against the First 
Appeal No.10 of 2001 was preferred and 
it was allowed, in part, holding that Mr. 
Kripal Singh could have transferred only 
one-half of the questioned land which fell 
in his share. It may be added that the 
remaining half portion of land was owned 
by Smt. Tara Devi, the wife of Mr. Kesar 
Singh, who died intestate. 
 Feeling aggrieved, Mr. Rajendra 
Singh has come up in this second appeal. 
 In the backdrop of facts, as stated 
above, I am of the opinion, and wherefor 
learned Senior Counsel for the parties 
also do agree, that Mr. Rajendra Singh 
and Mr. Mangal Singh be called, in 
person, in the Court so that the matter 
may be referred for reconciliation in the 
Alternative Disputes Redressal Forum. 
 Ordered accordingly. 
 List on 30.8.2016 for the purpose. 
 
 
 (Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.)
