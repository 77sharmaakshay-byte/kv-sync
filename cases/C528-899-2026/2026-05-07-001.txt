COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
C528/899/2026
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Akshay Pradhan, learned 
counsel for the applicants.
2. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned Deputy 
A.G. along with Mr. S.C. Dumka, 
learned A.G.A. for the State.
3. Present C-528 application has 
been filed seeking quashing of the 
summoning order/notice dated 
10.01.2024 passed by the learned 
Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Laksar, District Haridwar in Complaint 
Case No. 04 of 2023, along with the 
entire proceedings of the aforesaid 
complaint case.
4. Learned counsel for the applicants 
would submit that the dispute in 
question arises out of matrimonial 
discord between the parties; that, the 
applicant no.1 is the husband of 
respondent no.2; applicant no.2 is the 
father-in-law; applicant no.3 is the 
mother-in-law; and applicant nos.4 to 6 
are the maternal aunt, maternal uncle 
and cousin brother-in-law of 
respondent no.2, respectively; that, due 
to matrimonial differences, respondent 
no.2 instituted proceedings under 
Sections 12, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21 and 22 
of the Protection of Women from 
Domestic Violence Act, 2005, by levelling false and baseless allegations 
against the applicants; that, the learned 
trial court, without proper application 
of judicial mind and in a mechanical 
manner, proceeded to take cognizance 
and issue summons against the 
applicants.
5. Learned counsel for the applicants 
would further submit that the 
applicants have falsely been implicated 
in the present proceedings; that, 
applicant nos.4 to 6 are residing 
separately and have no concern 
whatsoever with the day-to-day 
matrimonial affairs or domestic life of 
applicant nos.1 to 3 and respondent 
no.2/complainant, therefore, 
continuation of the proceedings against 
them amounts to abuse of the process 
of law and has been initiated only with 
an ulterior motive to harass the 
applicants. 
6. Having heard learned counsel for 
the applicants and upon consideration 
of the submissions advanced, this 
Court finds that applicant nos.4 to 6 
are stated to be residing separately and 
appear to have no direct concern with 
the day-to-day affairs and matrimonial 
life of applicant nos.1 to 3 and, further 
proceedings against applicant nos.4 to 
6 deserve to be stayed at this stage. 
7. Issue notice to respondent no.2, 
returnable at an early date. 
8. Steps shall be taken within one 
week. 9. List the matter after receipt of the 
service report. 
10. Considering the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
applicants, it is directed that, till the 
next date of listing, further proceedings 
of Complaint Case No.04 of 2023 
pending before the court of learned 
Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Laksar, District Haridwar shall remain 
stayed qua applicant nos.4 to 6 only. 
However, it is made clear that 
proceedings of the aforesaid complaint 
case shall continue in respect of 
applicant nos.1 to 3.
 (Alok Mahra, J.) 07.05.2026 Mamta
