Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Anil Anthwal, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. R.S. Bisht, learned Additional C.S.C. 
along with Mr. Bhupendra Singh Koranga, 
learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. By means of this writ petition, petitioner 
has sought a direction to the respondents to 
grant a high risk allowance to the petitioners as 
they are working in the Fire Fighter And 
Emergency Service in the State of Uttarakhand. 
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
petitioners that the basis of filing the writ 
petition lies in a recommendation which has 
been made by a high power committee to the 
Government to grant high risk allowance to the 
petitioners, in view of the risk factor in their 
services.
5. Learned State Counsel prays for and is 
granted ten days’ time to seek instructions in the 
matter as to what action has been taken on the 
recommendation made by the Committee.
