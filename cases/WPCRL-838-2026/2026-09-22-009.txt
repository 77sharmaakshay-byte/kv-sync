2. In sequel to the order dated 16.09.2026, 
petitioner no.1 and her parents are present before the 
Court today. Petitioner no.1 has been brought before 
the Court in the custody of L.C. No.184, Nikita, from 
Nari Niketan, Dehradun.
3. This Court interacted with petitioner no.1 and 
her parents in the Chamber. During the interaction, 
petitioner no.1 expressed her willingness to go with 
her parents. Her parents have also given an 
undertaking before this Court that they are willing to 
take petitioner no.1 with them and shall ensure that 
she is not harassed, ill-treated or victimized in any 
manner. Petitioner no.1 has, in turn, undertaken that 
she shall respect her parents and shall not indulge in 
any act or conduct which may cause annoyance or 
inconvenience to them.
4. Since there is a dispute regarding the age of 
petitioner no.1, inasmuch as, different dates of birth 
are reflected in her school leaving certificate, birth 
certificate, Parivar Register and Aadhaar Card, and a 
Coordinate Bench of this Court has earlier directed 
determination of her age, this Court considers it 
appropriate that an ossification test of petitioner no.1 
be conducted for the purpose of assisting in 
determination of her age.5. Accordingly, the parents of petitioner no.1 are 
directed to take petitioner no.1 to the office of the 
Chief Medical Officer, Haridwar, for undergoing the 
ossification test on 30.09.2026. The Station House 
Officer, Police Station Laksar, District Haridwar, shall 
depute a responsible police officer to accompany 
petitioner no.1 and her parents and ensure their safe 
transit and appearance before the concerned medical 
authority for conducting the aforesaid test.
6. The Chief Medical Officer, Haridwar, shall 
ensure that the ossification test is conducted on the 
date fixed and the report thereof is forwarded to this 
Court through the office of the Government Advocate, 
Uttarakhand, before the next date of listing.
7. In view of the statement and undertaking given 
by petitioner no.1 and her parents before the Court, 
the custody of petitioner no.1 is handed over to her 
parents, subject to their undertaking that petitioner 
no.1 shall not be harassed, ill-treated or victimized by 
them. The parents shall also ensure her safety, well￾being and proper care.
8. Learned State Counsel is directed to 
communicate this order forthwith to the Chief Medical 
Officer, Haridwar, as well as the Station House 
Officer, Police Station Laksar, District Haridwar, for 
necessary compliance.
9. List this matter on 13.10.2026.
