COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPCRL No. 838 of 2026 
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Rajveer Singh, learned counsel for 
the petitioners and Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned 
A.G.A. for the State. 
2. After post lunch session, Mr. Tumul 
Nainwal, learned A.G.A. for the State apprised 
to this Court that the birth certificate of the 
petitioner no.1 was sent for verification today 
itself to the concerned SHO and on verification, 
he has apprised that the documents furnished 
before the authority, the school leaving 
certificate wherein the date of birth of the 
petitioner no.1 is shown as 20.07.2007. He 
also apprised that concerned official also visited 
Govt. Girls Junior High School from where, it 
has been apprised that as per the original 
record, the date of birth of the petitioner no.1 is 
20.07.2010. 
3. Admittedly, there are some discrepancies 
in the date of birth of the petitioner no.1. Her 
parents, who are present in Court submits that 
they married in the year 2009, therefore, there 
is no question that the petitioner no.1 was born 
in 2007. If it is so, it certainly creates doubt 
about the age of petitioner no.1, therefore, to 
examine her correct age, she has to go with 
some medical tests. 
4. In such view of the matter, the petitioner 
no.1 is directed to be sent to Nari Niketan of the 
concerned district. The Police officials are 
directed to take her in protection and sent her to Nari Niketan. The Superintendent of Nari 
Niketan is also directed to immediately contact 
with the concerned Chief Medical Officer to 
make an arrangement for medical examination 
of the petitioner no.1 and submit his report 
within a week. 
5. The parents of the petitioner no.1 are also 
agree for medical examination and till then, she 
may be sent to the Nari Niketan. 
6. So far as the petitioner no.2 is concerned, 
Mr. Rajveer Singh, learned counsel for the 
petitioner undertakes that on the next date, the 
petitioner no.2 will remain present in Court. 
7. The In-charge Circle Officer, High Court is 
directed to send the petitioner no.1 with the 
assistance of some lady police officer under 
protection 
8. Put up on 08.07.2026. 
9. Copy of this order be supplied to Mr. 
Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. for the State so 
that he may send it to the concerned official for 
necessary compliance. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
