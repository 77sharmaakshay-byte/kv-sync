3. The instant writ petition is preferred for the 
following reliefs:-
“I. Issue writ, order or direction in the nature of 
Mandamus directing the Respondents to initiate the 
selection process for the post of Teaching and Non 
Teaching Staff in the Janta Inter College Chaid 
Chainpur, District – Pauri Garhwal expeditiously 
within the time frame of three months as provided 
under the Regulation framed under the School 
Education Act, 2006.”
4. The brief facts of the case are that initially an 
advertisement was issued on 29.07.2022 for the six 
posts of Assistant Teacher for the subjects Hindi, 
Mathematics, Physical Training and one single 
post of Junior Clerk, however, the permission was 
not accorded since there were some restrain order 
from the Higher Authorities. 
5. The main contention in the writ petition is 
that the total strength of the students in the present 
academic session of 2024-2025 is 110 and there is 
scarcity of teaching and non-teaching staff in the 
Institution. It is further contended that the 
identical issue was also came up before the 
coordinate Bench in WPMS No.2839 of 2025, 
wherein, the coordinate Bench quash the ban 
imposed on the regular recruitment of Teaching 
and Non-Teaching staff in the grant-in-aid 
Institution and directed the State Government to fill up the vacant post. It is contended that the 
Management sent a request letter to the Chief 
Education Officer, copy of which was also address 
to the Director General for seeking permission to 
initiate with the selection process, but no decision 
has been taken. The request letter is appended at 
page 24 of the writ petition. 
6. On the previous date, the Chief Education 
Officer and the Director General were asked to 
appear through VC to explain why they are 
sleeping over the matter and why the permissions 
are not being accorded. 
7. Subsequently, a communication was given 
to the learned Chief Standing Counsel that as per 
the instructions of the High Court the permission 
is accorded and this Court takes serious note on it 
since there was no such direction and only an 
explanation was called for that what steps has 
been taken on the request letter of the 
Management. 
8. Consequently, both the Officers were asked 
to join the proceedings and now they are present 
and submits that the communication as given to 
the learned CSC was in fact due to some 
misunderstanding the order of the Court since the 
Hon’ble Court only ask for the explanation. Both 
the Officers i.e. Director General as well as the 
Chief Education Officer tender apology undertake 
that they will examine the issue and take a 
decision on the request letter of the Management.
9. On such submissions, learned counsel for 
the petitioner submits that by that time now there 
are more existing vacancies which are also 
required to be filled up, therefore, they may also be 
given a liberty to send a fresh proposal with regard 
to the present position.
10. Let the Management may give a fresh 
proposal to the Chief Education Officer by sending 
the copy of the same to the Director General of the 
School Education, giving details of existing 
vacancies, and on such proposal the Director General shall examine the issue and give 
appropriate direction to the Chief Education 
Officer to decide the proposal of the Management 
in accordance with law.
11. Put up this matter on 04.11.2026.
12. Personal appearance are exempted.
