JUDGMENT : (per Sri G. Narendar, C.J.) 
So far as the above Special Appeal is concerned, it 
is seen that the basis for termination of the appellant-writ 
petitioner’s service is the communication dated 17th August, 
2016 issued by the Registrar, Education Regulatory Authority, 
Uttar Pradesh, Allahabad, wherein it is stated that Enrolment 
No. 828 of L.T. Examination, 1999 from Sakaldiha Training 
College, Sakaldiha, Varanasi does not belong to the petitioner, 
rather it belongs to one Rajvir Singh, son of Sri Padadhar 
Singh. In the absence of challenge to this foundational fact, 
we are unable to comprehend as to how any relief can be 
granted to a consequential order of removal, which 
admittedly, is premised on the opinion of the University. 2025:UHC:6748-DB

2. It is also not in dispute that the letter dated 
29.10.2020 was issued pursuant to an inquiry conducted by 
the SIT constituted for unearthing such unjustified / illegal 
appointments. 
3. In that view, learned counsel for the appellant prays 
leave to withdraw the instant Special Appeal with liberty to file 
fresh petition calling in question the letter of the Registrar, 
Education Regulatory Authority, Uttar Pradesh, Allahabad 
dated 17.08.2016. 
4. Reserving such liberty, the instant Special Appeal is 
dismissed as withdrawn.
5. There shall be no order as to costs. 
6. Pending application, if any, also stands dismissed.
