Mr. Amar Murti Shukla, learned counsel and 
Mr. B.M. Pingal, learned counsel for the Appellant.
2. Mr. B.S. Parihar, Addl. Chief Standing Counsel

3. Heard on Delay Condonation Application.
4. There is delay of 08 days in filing the recall 
application. The delay has been sufficiently 
explained and is hereby condoned. Accordingly, 
delay condonation application is allowed. 
5. The recall application is accordingly taken up 
for consideration. 
6. Learned counsel for the parties agreed that the 
matter is covered by the order passed today on the 
Recall Application No.04 of 2025 in SPA No.110 of 
2025. The operative part of the order passed 
thereon is as follows:-“The contention appears to have force as 
in case the appeal is not heard and decided on 
merits, the order of learned Single Judge,
dismissing the challenge to the order of 
termination of services of the appellant, would 
attain finality and any separate challenge 
made by the appellant, in pursuance of liberty 
granted by this Court before the learned Single 
Judge, would not serve any purpose. 
Accordingly, recall application is allowed. Order 
dated 31.07.2025 is hereby recalled. The 
special appeal shall be listed for hearing before 
the appropriate bench as per Roster.” 
7. Accordingly, recall application stands disposed 
of in terms of the order passed in the connected 
Special Appeal No.110 of 2025.
