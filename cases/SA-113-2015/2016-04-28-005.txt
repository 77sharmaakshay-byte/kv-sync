Heard. 
This second appeal is admitted 
for hearing on the following substantial 
questions of law: 
(a) Whether once plea of 
defendant no. 2 of acquiring title by 
efflux of time was declined and 
attained finality up till Hon’ble 
Supreme Court of India, plaintiff was 
entitled for decree even on the basis of 
title and defendant was liable to be 
evicted on the ground of denial of title 
of plaintiff? 
(b) Whether once plea of 
limitation raised by defendant in O.S. 
no. 346 of 1991 decided against him 
attained finality up till Hon’ble 
Supreme Court of India and operates 
resjudicata in view of section 11 
C.P.C., lower appellate court was 
justified in dismissing the suit of 
plaintiff/appellant on the ground of 
limitation? 
(c) Whether lower appellate 
court was justified in dismissing the 
suit of plaintiff with the finding that 
since rights of predecessor in interest 
of plaintiff already stood extinguished 
under section 27 of Limitation Act, 
plaintiff has no right to institute the 
suit, ignoring the fact that suit was 
instituted by plaintiff before regular 
civil court on account of involving 
question of title and was tried as regular civil suit based on sale deed 
dated 14.5.1984? 
(d) Whether judgment and 
decree passed by lower appellate court 
can be sustained for want of framing 
and deciding points for determination 
as per Order 41 Rule 31 C.P.C.? 
Lower court record has already 
been received.
