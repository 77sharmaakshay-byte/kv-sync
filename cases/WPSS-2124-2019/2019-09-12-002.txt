WPSS No.2124 of 2019 
Hon’ble Lok Pal Singh, J.
 
 Mr. Alok Mahra, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
 Mr. Sushil Vashishtha, Brief Holder for 
the State. 
 By means of present writ petition, 
petitioner has sought a writ in the nature of 
certiorari for quashing the impugned 
termination order dated 02.06.2016 passed 
by respondent no.4. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that a show cause notice/charge￾sheet was issued to the petitioner, a reply 
whereof was submitted by the petitioner 
denying the charges leveled against him, but 
thereafter without conducting any inquiry, 
impugned termination order has been passed 
against the petitioner, in violation of Rule 7 of 
Uttarakhand Government Servant (Discipline 
and Appeal) Rules, 2003, which provides that 
before imposing any major punishment on 
any government servant, an inquiry shall be 
conducted. 
 Having considered the submissions of 
learned counsel for the petitioner, this Court 
directs the respondent no.4-District 
Education Officer (Elementary Education), 
Udham Singh Nagar, to conduct inquiry in 
the matter, after affording reasonable 
opportunity of hearing to the petitioner, 
within a period of eight weeks from today, and 
to submit report before this Court thereafter. 
 List after inquiry report is received.
