Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Ms. Devika Tiwari, counsel for the

2. Mr. Bindesh Kumar Gupta, counsel for the 
respondent no.1/Insurance Company. 
3. Heard the learned counsel for the parties on 
the appeal arising out of the judgment of the 
Motor Accident Claim Tribunal, Rudrapur, 
Udham Singh Nagar whereby the claim petition 
of the appellant/claimant was rejected. 
4. Learned counsel for the 
respondent/Insurance Company would submit 
that in paragraph no. 6 of the claim petition, the 
appellant/claimant has stated his salary @
`4,000/- per month thereby Rs. 48,000/- per 
annum; that, as per the schedule applicable in the 
petition under Section 163A of the Motor Vehicle 
Act (the law as then was) the compensation can 
be awarded under this section only if the income 
of the deceased or permanently disabled claimant 
was less than `40,000/- per annum. 
5. Per contra, learned counsel for the 
appellant/claimant would submit that the 
appellant/claimant could not prove his income 
and the respondent/Insurance Company has not 
raised this point in the Tribunal concerned nor 
any issue was framed, therefore, this point cannot 
be raised in the present appeal at this stage; that, 
there are plethora of judgments of the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court which say that as the income of 
the appellant/claimant was not proved in the 
Tribunal, therefore, only the notional income can 
be taken which is always less than `40,000/- per 
annum. 
Counsel for the appellant/claimant prays
for time to place law in support of her contention.
6. Permission is granted. 7. Learned counsel for the parties are 
requested to exchange citations, on which they 
need to rely upon in support of their arguments to 
each other on every point of law and shall supply 
the copy of the said citations before the Court 
three days prior to the date of listing. 
8. List this matter on 18.02.2025.
