COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.497 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
1. Mr. Yogesh Pacholia, learned counsel for 
the petitioners.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hariya, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. Mr. B.D. Pandey, learned counsel for 
respondent no.2-Commission. 
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
5. By means of this writ petition, the 
petitioner has challenged the application of 
reservation issued by the Commission in their 
recruitment process for the post of Assistant 
Teacher (LT Grade) (Mathematics) pursuant to 
the advertisement dated 13.10.2020.
6. The contention of the petitioner is that 
petitioners belong to open category candidate 
and participated as such in the selection 
process. Under the orphan quota, two posts
each were reserved in both Garhwal Mandal as 
well as in Kumaon Mandal. According to the 
petitioner, no candidate is available against that 
quota. As per Government Order dated 
22.05.2020, the said posts of orphan quota 
should be reverted back to the general category 
but the respondent-Commission has not done 
so and by inaction on the part of the 
respondent-Commission, petitioner may be 
deprived of his selection on the post of 
Assistant Teacher (L.T. Grade) (Mathematics).
7. Several opportunities have been given to 
learned counsel for respondent-Commission to 
file counter affidavit but till date no counter 
affidavit has been filed.
8. Respondent-Commission is given further 
six weeks’ time to file counter affidavit. 
9. List on 19.09.2024.10. In the meantime, two posts of Assistant 
Teacher (L.T. Grade) (Mathematics) shall be 
kept vacant in Garhwal Mandal as well as in 
Kumaon Mandal.
