2. After hearing the learned counsel for the 
parties, the short question for consideration in the 
present Appeal is, whether out of the 
compensation taken by the appellants, the 
respondent has the share of 1/4th. It is not in 
dispute that the partition proceedings are going on 
before the Assistant Collector, First Class, Haridwar 
as Case No. 5 of 2018 under Section 176 of the 
Uttar Pradesh Zamindari Abolition and Land 
Reforms Act. 
3. As per the observation made in Paragraph-8
of the impugned order passed by the learned Single 
Judge dated 25.06.2024, respondent No. 5 was 
claiming 1/4th share in the land comprising in Khasra No. 280. 
4. Since respondent No. 5 is claiming 1/4th
share, the order dated 25th June, 2024 is being
modified that the appellants-writ petitioners will 
deposit only 1/4th compensation received by him on 
11.11.2021 and pursuant to the order dated 
25.06.2024, they have deposited the entire 
compensation before the Special Land Acquisition 
Officer, Haridwar. 
5. Keeping in view that the respondent No. 5 
has only deposited 1/4th amount, the order dated 
25th June, 2024 is being modified that the 
competent Authority, where the compensation was 
deposited by the appellants-writ petitioners, will 
retain 1/4th share of the amount and will release 
rest of the amount to the present appellants. 
6. The present Appeals are admitted to be listed 
for hearing after the partition suit is decided.
