2. The applicant has moved the present 
Miscellaneous Application (I.A. No.1 of 2026) seeking 
permission to travel to Messina, Italy, where he is 
pursuing his M.Tech. course in Engineering.
3. Learned State Counsel was directed to obtain 
instructions in the matter. Today, on instructions, 
learned State Counsel submits that charge-sheet has 
already been filed in the matter on 20.08.2026. He 
further submits that, at present, the personal 
presence of the applicant before the learned trial 
Court is not required and, if this Court deems it 
appropriate, the applicant may be permitted to travel 
to Messina, Italy for pursuing his higher studies.
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the record. It is not disputed that the 
applicant was pursuing his M.Tech. course even prior 
to the lodging of the F.I.R. It is also stated that the 
applicant has cooperated with the investigation and 
has appeared before the Investigating Officer as and 
when required. The applicant has further stated in 
the application that his parents are permanent 
residents of Dehradun and own property there. He 
has undertaken that, as and when his presence is 
required by the learned trial Court, he shall appear before the Court and shall not seek any unnecessary 
adjournment or otherwise cause delay in the 
proceedings.
5. Having regard to the aforesaid facts and 
circumstances, particularly the fact that the charge￾sheet has already been filed, the applicant has 
cooperated with the investigation, and his higher 
studies are being pursued at Messina, Italy, this 
Court is of the view that the applicant may be 
permitted to travel abroad for the aforesaid purpose. 
The permission, however, shall remain subject to his 
furnishing adequate security and undertaking to 
ensure his appearance before the learned trial Court 
as and when required.
6. Accordingly, the applicant is permitted to travel 
to Messina, Italy for pursuing his M.Tech. 
course/higher studies, subject to the following 
conditions:
(i) The applicant shall furnish two reliable sureties to 
the satisfaction of the learned trial Court.
(ii) The applicant shall furnish an undertaking before 
the learned trial Court that he shall appear before the 
Court as and when his personal presence is 
specifically required.
(iii) The applicant shall cooperate with the trial and 
shall not, either directly or indirectly, cause any 
unnecessary delay in the conclusion of the 
proceedings.
(iv) The applicant shall furnish his complete address, 
contact details and details of his course/institution in 
Italy before the learned trial Court and shall keep the 
Court informed of any change therein; and
(v) The applicant shall return to India and appear 
before the learned trial Court whenever so directed.
7. Subject to the aforesaid conditions, the present 
Miscellaneous Application (I.A. No.1 of 2026) stands 
disposed of.
8. List in due course.
