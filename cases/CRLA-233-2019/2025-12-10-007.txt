Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
The instant appeal is preferred against the 
judgment and order dated 09.05.2019 passed by learned 
Sessions Judge/Special Judge, POCSO, Almora in S.S.T. No. 
01 of 2019 ‘State Vs. Suraj Pal and Others’, whereby the 
appellant has been convicted and sentenced for the offence 
punishable under Sections 307 r/w 34 and 376(2) I.P.C.
2. The appellant seeks bail during pendency of the 
appeal.
3. Heard learned counsel for the parties on the bail 
application and perused the record.
4. This is second bail application. The 1st bail 
application has been dismissed on merit by the Division 
Bench of this Court vide order dated 19.03.2020.
5. Upon consideration of the submissions advanced 
and on perusal of the record, this Court finds no ground to 
grant bail to the appellant at this stage.
6. Accordingly, the second bail application is rejected.
7. List the criminal appeal along with connected 
appeal for final hearing on 17.03.2025.
