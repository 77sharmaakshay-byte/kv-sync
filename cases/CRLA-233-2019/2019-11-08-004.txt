Heard on short term bail application.
The applicant moves application for short term 
bail on the ground that he has only niece i.e. bhanji 
and her marriage is scheduled to be solemnized on 
21.11.2019. In the marriage, the applicant has to 
perform some matrimonial rituals. 
Learned counsel for the applicant would argue 
that the applicant has to perform certain rituals in the 
marriage, therefore, he may be granted short term bail. 
Having considered the matter, this court is of 
the view that short term bail application deserves to be 
allowed.
Short Term Bail application is allowed. Let the 
applicant Sohan Singh be released on short term bail 
for a period of 5 days i.e. 20.11.2019 to 25.11.2019, 
on his executing a personal bond and furnishing two 
reliable sureties, each of the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the Court concerned. It is made clear 
that one of the sureties should be of blood relation of 
the appellant/appellant. 
After completion of five days, the applicant will 
surrender before the Court concerned and information 
to this effect will be placed before this Court. 
Let the paper book be prepared and supplied to 
the learned counsel for the parties on payment of 
usual charges. 
List the appeals after two weeks.
