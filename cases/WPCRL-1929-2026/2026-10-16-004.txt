Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Sagar Kothari, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Ms. Sweta Badola Dobhal, learned 
Brief Holder for the State.
3. By means of present writ petition, 
petitioner has sought the following 
reliefs:-
“(i) Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of certiorari quashing the impugned First 
Information Report (Annexure No.1 pages no.22 
to 28), being FIR No. 117 of 2026 dated 
19.08.2026, registered under U/s 126(2), 221 
and 351(2) of Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 
2023, at P.S. Selaqui, District Dehradun.
(ii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing Respondents no. 4, 
5 and 6 to compensate the petitioner for 
damaging and destructing her property or restore 
status quo ante of the property as it existed prior 
to their illegal act;”
4. Learned counsel for the State prays 
for and is granted three weeks’ time to 
file counter affidavit.5. Having considered the submissions 
made by learned counsel for the 
petitioner and having perused the 
material available in the writ petition,
this Court is of the view that respondent 
cannot take such an action demolishing 
the gate of the petitioner’s property
without due process of law. The 
petitioner deserves some protection by 
this Court in view of the above facts and 
circumstances of the matter.
6. Accordingly, it is directed that till 
the next date of listing, no coercive 
action shall be taken against the 
petitioner, pursuant to aforesaid FIR No. 
117 of 2026, dated 19.08.2026, under 
Sections 126(2), 221 and 351(2) of 
BNS, 2023, registered at Police Station 
Selaqui, District Dehradun, and 
petitioner shall cooperate with the 
investigation. 
7. List this case on 20.11.2026.
8. Stay Application (IA/1/2026) 
stands disposed of.
