3. Applicant is under incarceration in connection with 
FIR No.89 of 2026, registered under Section 8/21 of the 
Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985, 
at Police Station Kichha, District Udham Singh Nagar, 
since 09.03.2026.
4. Applicant has moved a short-term bail application 
(IA No.2 of 2026) seeking his release for the purpose of 
appearing in the B.Com. 2nd Semester Examination, 
scheduled to be conducted from 10.06.2026 to 
03.07.2026. In the said application, applicant has 
submitted that he has already passed the B.Com. 1st
Semester Examination and now wants to appear in the 
B.Com. 2nd Semester Examination. The admit card for 
the 2nd Semester Examination has been annexed as 
Annexure-SA1 to the supplementary affidavit. In 
paragraph 3 of the supplementary affidavit, it has been 
stated that applicant's examinations are scheduled to be 
held on 10.06.2026, 12.06.2026, 16.06.2026, and 
03.07.2026. Accordingly, applicant has prayed for his 
release on short-term bail. 
5. Learned counsel appearing for the State submits 
that it is correct that the applicant has to appear in the 2
B.Com. 2nd Semester Examination. 
6. In view of the aforesaid facts and circumstances, the 
short-term bail application is allowed. 
7. The applicant-Mohd. Shahwaz, is directed to be 
released on short-term bail with effect from 09.06.2026 
till 03.07.2026, subject to his executing a personal bond 
and furnishing two reliable sureties of the like amount 
each to the satisfaction of the concerned Special Judge, 
NDPS. Applicant shall surrender before the concerned 
Special Judge, NDPS on 04.07.2026. 
8. Applicant shall utilize the period of short-term bail 
solely for the purpose for which it has been granted and 
shall maintain good conduct throughout the said period.
9. List on 14.07.2026.
10. Let certified copy of this order be issued to the 
learned counsel for the applicant today itself on payment 
of usual charges.
