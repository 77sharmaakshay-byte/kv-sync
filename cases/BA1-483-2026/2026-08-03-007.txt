3. Today, the matter is listed on the second short￾term bail application (IA No.4/2026). By means of the 
said short-term bail application, applicant seeks his 
release on short-term bail on account of suffering 
from jaundice.
4. The attention of this Court has been drawn by 
the learned State Counsel to page 17 of the second 
short-term bail application, wherein the doctor has 
opined that applicant is to have rest for a period of 
one month from 26.06.2026. Learned State Counsel 
submits that the said period has already expired, as 
the applicant has already undergone the said period 
inside the jail, during which he had been advised to 
take rest. Thus, according to him, no case is made 
out for releasing the applicant on short-term bail.
5. Having heard the learned counsel for the parties and upon perusal of the record, this Court is of the 
opinion that no good ground is made out to release 
the applicant on short-term bail. Accordingly, the 
second short-term bail application (IA No.4/2026) is 
hereby rejected.
