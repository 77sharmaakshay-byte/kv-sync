COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLA No.245 of 2026
“Ram Kishor Vs. State of Uttarakhand”
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Yogesh Chandra Brijwari, learned counsel holding 
brief of Ms. Shivangi Gangwar, learned counsel for the 
Appellant.
2. Mr. S.S. Chauhan, learned DAG with Mr. Vikash Uniyal, 
learned Brief Holder, for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. Learned counsel representing the Applicant submits that 
firstly the present matter relates to the contraband, namely, 
charas weighing 757 grams which is less than the stipulated 
commercial quantity as per the NDPS Act. Secondly, the 
mandatory and the directive provision of the NDPS Act, has not 
been followed especially during the course of seizure and 
sampling. The said contraband is alleged to have been recovered 
from the possession of the Applicant/Appellant in form of sticks 
and it is not clear as to from where the sample had been taken 
and sent for the FSL examination. Besides above, there are 
anomalies regarding the procedure as stipulated in the NDPS 
Act, and the face of it, it is apparent that the Appellant had been 
wrongly convicted.
4. Let the Trial Court Record be called for. After receipt of 
the Trial Court Record, let the paper-book be prepared and 
supplied to the learned counsel for the parties on the payment of 
usual charges.
5. List this matter on 18.06.2026.
6. In the meantime, objection, if any, be filed to the bail 
application (IA No.1/2026).
 (Ashish Naithani, J.)
