The challenge in this revision is 
made to order dated 15.12.2022, passed 
in Misc. Criminal Case No.57 of 2019, 
Smt. Rekha Devi Vs. Rakesh Singh Gusai, 
by the court of Family Judge, Kotdwar, 
District Pauri Garhwal. By it, the 
revisionist has been directed to pay Rs. 
10,000/- per month maintenance to the 
private respondent.
Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would submit that, in fact, the revisionist 
was in Army. Due to health condition, he 
was sent back from Army to the custody 
of his wife, the private respondent. The 
account was joint. Taking advantage of 
the ill health of the revisionist, it is 
submitted that the private respondent 
withdrew about Rs. 30 Lakhs from the 
joint account without notice and 
knowledge of the revisionist. In fact, it is 
argued that the revisionist was mentally 
not stable. Reference has been made to 
the statement of the private respondent 
recorded at Page 8 of the impugned 
judgment, wherein she has admitted that 
she withdrew 27 Lakhs from the joint 
account.
Having considered the arguments, 
under the fact and circumstances of the 
case, this Court is of the view that this 
matter definitely requires deliberations.
Issue notice to the respondent no.2 
returnable within four weeks.Steps to be taken within a week.
List this matter on 08.06.2023.
