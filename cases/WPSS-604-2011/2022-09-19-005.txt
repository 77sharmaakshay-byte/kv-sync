19.09.2022 WPSS No.604 of 2011
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
On 19.04.2018, present Writ 
Petition (S/S) No.604 of 2011 was 
disposed of. Against the said order dated 
19.04.2018, a Special Appeal (No.353 of 
2019) was filed. On 27.07.2022, the said 
Special Appeal has been disposed of,
and, by disposing the said appeal,
present matter has been remanded back 
to this Court.
Heard Mr. Sushil Vashisth, learned 
Standing Counsel with Mrs. Indu 
Sharma, learned Brief Holder for the 
State/respondents. 
None is present on behalf of the 
petitioner. 
The learned Standing Counsel 
appearing for the State/respondents 
submitted that Mr. Jagdish Singh Bisht, 
Advocate, who was earlier appearing for 
the petitioner, has been engaged as a 
Government Counsel and Mr. Kamlesh 
Lohani, Advocate, who was appearing for 
the petitioner, has been engaged as a 
Government Teacher. 
Issue notice to the petitioner. 
List this case on 06.12.2022.
The Registry is directed to remove 
the name of Mr. Jagdish Singh Bisht, 
Advocate and Mr. Kamlesh Lohani, 
Advocate on behalf of the petitioner in 
the next cause list.
