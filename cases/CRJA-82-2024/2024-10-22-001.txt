2. The present jail appeal has been preferred on 
behalf of the appellant Badal Singh, wherein, the 
judgment and order dated 10.09.2024 passed by 
FTC/ADJ/Spl. Judge POCSO, District Udham Singh 
Nagar, passed in SST No. 102 of 2023 arising out of 
case crime No. 340 of 2022, has been challenged, 
wherein the present appellant has been convicted for 
the offence punishable under Section 9/10 of the 
POCSO Act and sentenced to five months rigorous 
imprisonment with a fine of Rs. 10,000/- and in 
default of payment of fine he has to go further
additional three months simple imprisonment and 
also convicted for the offence punishable under 
Section 354 D of IPC with one year rigorous 
imprisonment with a fine of Rs. 5,000/- and in 
default of payment of fine the additional 15 days
simple imprisonment has been awarded.

supply the copy of the appeal to the learned Amicus 
Curiae.
