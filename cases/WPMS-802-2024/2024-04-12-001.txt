COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.802 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
1. Mr. Reituparna Joshi, learned counsel 
holding brief of Mr. Shivanand Bhatt, learned 
counsel for the petitioner/plaintiff. 
2. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner.
3. Present writ petition has been filed by the 
petitioner challenging the order dated 
16.11.2019 passed by Civil Judge (S.D.) 
Rishikesh Dehradun in Original Suit No.77 of 
2015 Vijay Singh & another vs. Budh Singh and 
others whereby the suit filed by the 
petitioner/plaintiff was dismissed for default; 
the order dated 27.03.2023 passed by learned 
Senior Civil Judge Rishikesh, Dehradun whereby 
the recall application thereagainst was rejected 
and order dated 11.03.2024 passed by 1st
Additional District Judge, Rishikesh Dehradun in 
Civil Revision No.14 of 2023 Vijay Singh & 
another vs. Budh Singh & others which was also 
dismissed.
4. It is submitted by the learned counsel for 
the petitioner that when the suit was dismissed 
for his default, on that day the wife of the 
petitioner/plaintiff was admitted in hospital due 
to paralysis of the lower part of her body.
5. Having considered the submission made 
by learned counsel for the petitioner/plaintiff, 
this Court is inclined to interfere in the matter.6. Issue notice to the respondents 
returnable within six weeks.
7. Steps to be taken within one week.
8. List this petition on 19.06.2024.
9. Respondents may file their counter 
affidavit in the meantime. 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
