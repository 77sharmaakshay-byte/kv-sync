Mr. Mohit Bhauriyal, learned counsel for

3. Mr. Shailendra Nauriyal, learned counsel
for respondent no.2-Selecting Body.
4. The petitioner participated in a
recruitment process initiated by respondent￾Selecting Body, by issuance of an advertisement 
dated 30.10.2024, for the post of Constable 
(Male) with the Uttarakhand Police Department
under Dependent of Freedom Fighter Category.
5. There is no dispute that petitioner was 
successful in the written as well as the physical 
examination, but, when he was called for 
document verification, instead of having a
Certificate of Dependent of Freedom Fighter,
under which category petitioner applied, he
produced Identity Card of the Dependent of
Freedom Fighter issued by the SDM Kanda, 
District Bageshwar. 
6. The respondent/Selecting Body did not 
found favour with the said documents and, 
therefore, the name of petitioner was not 
recommended for appointment to the State 
Government in the provisional Recommendation List dated 27.02.2026.
7. It is contended by the learned counsel for 
the petitioner that on the document verification 
the Identity Card of Dependent of Freedom 
Fighter was there and when, the objection was 
raised by the respondent/Selecting Body on 
that card, within four days, the petitioner had
produced the Certificate of Dependent of
Freedom Fighter issued by the Competent 
Authority-SDM Kanda, District Bageshwar,
before the Selecting Body, but that has not be 
considered and the petitioner was not 
recommended.
8. Learned counsel for the respondent/
Selecting Body submitted that the petitioner 
was not holding the said Certificate on the last 
date of submission of application form, and 
therefore, the submission of the said Certificate 
after document verification was not considered.
9. This appears to be a very hyper technical 
objection on behalf of the respondent/Selecting 
Body.
10. The respondents may file the counter 
affidavit(s) within two weeks.
11. Any appointment made, pursuant to the 
advertisement dated 30.10.2024 on the post of 
Constable (Male) under the category of 
dependant of Freedom Fighter, shall be subject 
to final decision of this Court.
12. List this case on 25.08.2026.
