4. Pursuant to the directions, the Secretary, 
Technical Education, is before this Court virtually. We 
have queried the learned Secretary as to the stand of 
the Government with regard to the application of a 
G.O. regarding applicability of the Old Pension Scheme 
to such of those government servants whose 
appointment was pursuant to a notification issued prior 
to 01.10.2005, though the appointment was post the 
cut-off date. A circular was issued and pursuant to the 
same, the Petitioners opted for the Old Pension 
Scheme, but subsequently, the same came to be 
rejected.
5. On a query, learned Secretary would submit that 
the rejection of the claim is by the Treasury 
Department, and the second letter issued is in usual 
course.6. Be that as it may, we direct the learned Secretary 
to file an affidavit clarifying the stand of the 
Government as to whether the employees of 
autonomous bodies, like the petitioners, are also 
entitled to the scheme or not.
7. The affidavit shall be placed before this Court 
before the next date of hearing.
8. List on 31.10.2025.
9. The appearance of the Secretary and other 
officers for further hearing is dispensed with.
