3. In this appeal a short issue is being raised that 
there are the two suits (1) SCC Suit No.24 of 2001, 
originally filed by Smt. Shanti Devi, against the State 
Authorities, the SSP Dehradun, wherein, the plaintiff 
sought for eviction of the defendants from the 
property bearing Property No.72, Rajpur Road, 
Dehradun, consisting of a land measuring 10.5 bigha
with the construction of two units over an area of 1616 
square meters, which was decreed ex parte on 
24.08.2002. Against the exparte -decree, the matter 
went up before the Hon’ble Apex Court and the 
Hon’ble Apex Court remanded the matter to the Trial 
Court to decide afresh by judgment and order dated 
12.02.2024. Another suit was also filed i.e. the regular 
suit bearing O.S. No.514 of 2004 by present appellant, 
wherein, the decree of possession was sought. 
4. Now, in the suit filed by the present appellant, 
the original plaintiff of SCC Suit No.24 of 2001 moved 
an impleadment application and the impleadment 
application was allowed in the year 2005 because the 
original plaintiff of SCC Suit No.24 of 2001 was 
claiming a right over the suit property of regular suit 
of 2004. Now after remand of SCC Suit No.24 of 2001, 
the plaintiff of regular suit of 2004 moved an 
application under Order I Rule X of CPC in SCC suit 
of 2001 on the ground that suit property of 2004 suit is 
now incorporated by way of an amendment in the 
schedule of property of SCC Suit of 2001, therefore, in 
order to avoid further conflict, he is necessary party to 
be impleaded in SCC Suit No.24 of 2001.
5. This application was rejected by the learned 
Trial Court by observing that the suit is SCC suit,wherein, the boundary dispute cannot be looked into 
and is beyond the scope of suit. 
6. It is submitted by learned counsel for the 
appellant Mr. Aditya Singh, that the order impugned 
passed by the Trial Court rejecting his application 
under Order I Rule X of CPC is illegal on two 
accounts. (1) In the regular suit filed by the appellant
in 2004, the plaintiff of SCC suit of 2001 sought for her
impleadment for claiming title over the suit property 
of 2004. (2) The plaintiff of SCC suit also sought 
amendment to the schedule of property by including 
the suit property of 2004 regular suit in SCC Suit of 
2001.
7. By referring these two points, he submits that 
the issue of title in fact was claimed by the plaintiff of 
SCC suit by seeking an amendment to the schedule of 
the property of SCC suit and then by moving an 
application for her impleadment in the regular suit 
filed by the plaintiff, therefore, the appellant, herein, 
who is plaintiff of regular suit of 2004 has no option 
except to sought his impleadment in SCC suit and this 
is the same property in which he is claiming title by 
virtue of the regular suit filed in 2004.
8. He submits that on the aforesaid two points, the 
application moved by the appellant in SCC Suit of 
2001 has to be allowed and in such an eventuality 
since the nature of SCC suit is changed then in terms 
of Section 23 of the Provincial Small Cause Courts Act, 
1887, plaint should be return and to be presented for a 
court of appropriate jurisdiction by treating it to be a 
regular suit.
9. Issue notice to the respondent nos.5 and 6. 
Learned counsel for the appellant serve to the 
respondent nos.5 and 6 through the counsel who are
appearing in the Trial Court.
10. Put up this matter on 08.09.2026 as fresh.
11. In the meanwhile, State Counsel may also get 
instructions whether the State still want to acquire the 
property under the Law Acquisition Act.
