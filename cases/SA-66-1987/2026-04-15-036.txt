Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Sunil Khera, learned Additional Advocate 
General with Mr. Yogesh Chandra Tiwari, learned 
Standing Counsel for the State.
2. Mr. D.S. Patni, learned senior counsel assisted by 
Mr. Dharmendra Barthwal and Mr. Karmanya Pandey, 
learned counsel for the respondents.
3. Today Mr. Sunil Khera, learned Additional 
Advocate General with Mr. Yogesh Chandra Tiwari, 
learned Standing Counsel argued on three writ 
petitions i.e. WPMS Nos. 7176 of 2001, 808 of 2002 and 
920 of 2001.
4. WPMS No. 7176 of 2001 was preferred by the 
State against the order of Board of Revenue dated 
22.01.1993 passed in a revision filed by the respondent, 
whereby, the Board of Revenue set aside the order of 
the Commissioner dated 16.03.1983 as well as the order 
of Collector dated 31.12.1981. The main ground of 
challenge is that the second revision is barred in terms
of section 219(2) of L.R. Act and, as such, the revision 
preferred by the private respondents before the Board 
of Revenue was not maintainable.
5. So far as WPMS No. 920 of 2001 is concerned, the 
same was preferred by the respondent against the 
order of Board of Revenue dated 20.12.1996, which is 
an order of the Full Bench of the Board of Revenue, 
wherein, the Full Bench make an observation about the 
order dated 22.01.1993 is illegal and without 
jurisdiction.
6. The third petition is WPMS No. 808 of 2002 
preferred by the State against the order of Board of 
Revenue dated 22.07.2002 on a revision preferred by 
the respondents against the order of Collector dated 
02.05.2001 as well as against the order of SLAO dated 
31.05.2001, whereby, the restrictions were imposed on 
sale and purchase of the land in question since the land 
was under the acquisition. The Board of Revenue allowed the revision of private respondents on 
22.07.2002 and both the orders of the Collector and the 
SLAO were also set aside and the restrictions as 
imposed on sale and purchase were also lifted.
7. During the pendency of the aforesaid three writ 
petitions two preferred by the State and one by Mr. 
Ravi Kumar a review petition was filed by the State 
against the order dated 22.01.1993, which was reviewed 
by the Board of Revenue by condoning the delay on 
21.10.2005 by confirming the order of Collector dated 
31.12.1981, which was never been challenged by 
respondent Ravi Kumar so far.
8. Put up this matter along with all connected 
matters for further arguments on 21.04.2026 
immediately after fresh at 3:00 PM.
9. By that time both the parties are directed to 
prepare two page synopsis in the form of date and 
events and also prepare a compilation.
