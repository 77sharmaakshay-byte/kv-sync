CLR No.107 of 2024
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J. 
The proposed revisionist – plaintiff 
had filed a suit for recovery of money 
against the respondent – defendant. In 
the said Original Suit (O.S. No.117 of 
2021), summons was issued to the 
defendant. Process server’s report 
received that the defendant has refused to 
take the summons. Therefore, the trial 
court directed the said suit to proceed ex￾parte. Ex-parte judgment was passed on 
20.02.2023. The respondent – defendant 
had filed an application under Order IX 
Rule 13 CPC along with a Delay 
Condonation Application to set aside the 
ex-parte decree dated 20.02.2023. While 
allowing the said applications vide 
impugned order dated 24.09.2024, 
learned Civil Judge (Senior Division), 
Haridwar observed that due to the Corona 
outbreak the work of the court was not 
being done in full swing.
2. Heard Mr. Siddhartha Singh, learned 
counsel and Mr. Nikhil Singhal, learned 
counsel for the proposed appellant.
3. Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate, has 
contended that the respondent –defendant had stated the incorrect facts in 
their applications, therefore, the 
respondent is not entitled for any relief. 
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate, has 
relied upon a judgment of the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court, passed in Pundlik Jalam 
Patil (D) by LRS vs. Exe. Eng. Jalgaon 
Medium Project and Another, (2008) 
17 SCC 448. As per the arguments of 
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate, a 
execution case has been filed by the 
revisionist against the respondent.
4. Notice is issued to the respondent
before admission.
5. Steps to be taken within a week.
6. List on 08.01.2025.
7. In the facts and circumstances of 
this case, the effect and operation of the 
impugned order dated 24.09.2024 and the 
further proceedings of Execution Case 
No.9 of 2023, pending before the court of 
Civil Judge (Senior Division), Haridwar are 
stayed till the next date of listing.
