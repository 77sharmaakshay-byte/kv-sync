Invariably in all these writ petitions, the 
petitioners who are aspirants to be considered for 
appointment on the post of Nursing Officer have 
agitated their grievance either by putting a challenge 
to Clause VII pertaining to the relaxation of the age,
which they are seeking to enable them to participate 
in this process of selection in pursuance to the 
advertisement dated 03.01.2023 or by putting a 
challenge to Clause IX relating to the amendments 
which were made in the qualification criteria, which 
was confined to be extended to those candidates who 
have completed their Intermediate or High School
from the State of Uttarakhand.
Similar issues have been dealt with in a 
number of writ petitions, as to in what manner the 
aforesaid restrictions could be imposed in enabling 
the candidates to participate in the process of selection and invariably in all these writ petitions, the 
interim orders have been passed and one of the 
interim orders would be those as rendered in Writ 
Petition No. 169 of 2021 as passed by the Division 
Bench on 16.04.2021, but this Court is of the view 
that at an interim stage there cannot be a positive 
mandamus so far it relates to the extension of benefit 
of determination of age, by extension of the benefit 
of the provisions contained under Uttarakhand Public 
Service (Relaxation of Age Limit for the 
Recruitments) Rules, 2003.
In that view of the matter, this Court is of the 
view that the present petitioners would be permitted 
to participate in the process of selection though 
provisionally subject to the condition, that so far as
the aspect of their age is concerned if it falls for 
consideration within the parameters of the provisions 
of the Rules of 2003. So far as the aspect pertaining 
to the qualification is concerned, the same would be 
governed by the interim protection granted by the 
Division Bench on 16.04.2021.
Learned counsel for respondent no. 2 prays for 
and is granted three weeks’ time to file counter 
affidavit.
