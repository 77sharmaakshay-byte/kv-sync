COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
26.06.2025 WPMB No. 433 of 2025
Hon’ble G. Narendar, C.J.
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
1. Mr. Akshay Pradhan, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. B.S. Parihar, learned Additional Chief 
Standing Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand/ 
respondent nos. 1 & 2. 
3. Mr. Davesh Bishnoi, learned counsel for 
respondent nos. 3 & 4. 
4. We have heard the learned counsel for the 
petitioner, the learned Standing Counsel for the Nagar 
Palika and the learned Standing Counsel for the State 
of Uttarakhand. 
5. The tenders have been invited for construction of 
drains. It is bemusing and surprising that the Palikas 
and the Nigams want to carry out construction 
activities during the torrential rains. It would not be 
out of place to recollect a news article, which was
reported in a national daily newspaper, wherein it was 
stated that the retaining/ safety wall constructed a 
month ago, to prevent flooding by river, has been 
washed away in the overnight rains. It is also in the 
news that the potholes that were filled up to facilitate 
the travel of Hon’ble His Excellency The Vice President 
of India during his visit to Nainital, had vanished 
overnight. It is widely believed that mere sand was 
filled into the potholes. 
6. The attempt to construct drains or construct such 
safety/ retaining walls etc., when the gush of water is so dense and furious, perhaps is to ensure vanishing of 
evidence of poor quality of work. In that view, we are 
of the opinion that the State owes an explanation, as to 
the necessity of carrying out construction works during 
the torrential monsoons. It is not in doubt, and in fact 
the weather forecast is above normal monsoons, and 
the persistent rains in the State are also a proof of the 
same, and the rains that had commenced a month ago, 
have not been sparse, but have become a daily 
feature. The State is required to take a conscious call, 
as to whether such conditions are conducive for 
executing construction works, and such contracts are 
not reduced to a mere eyewash, and a route to 
corruption. 
7. We request the learned Advocate General to 
assist the Court, and we also direct the Chief 
Secretary, Government of Uttarakhand to appear 
virtually tomorrow. 
8. List this case on 27.06.2025 on the top of the 
list.
9. A copy of this order be forwarded to the learned 
State Counsel. Registrar (Judicial) shall also forward a 
copy of this order to the Chief Secretary, Government 
of Uttarakhand by e-mail.
