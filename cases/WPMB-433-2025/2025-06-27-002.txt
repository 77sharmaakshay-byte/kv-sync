4. In response to our order dated 26.06.2025, the Chief 
Secretary, the Secretary, PWD, Engineer-in-Chief, PWD
and other officers are also present.
5. The concern of the Court was that the funds of the 
public exchequer should not go down the drain in the 
execution of the works, in view of the copious rains that 
the State receives every year.
6. In that direction, we have queried the Chief 
Secretary and the Chief Secretary submits that the State 
Government has already addressed this issue and, in fact, 
has issued advisory to all the departments not to issue 
work orders permitting the commencement of works
during the monsoon and departments have been advised to 
ensure that the monsoon season is utilized for paper work 
and preparing the DPRs etc. and ensure that the works
commence immediately after the monsoon.
7. We are of the view that further orders in this 
direction may not be necessary. We may direct the Chief 
Secretary to reiterate the advisory once again to all the 
concerned departments. Any work, which the administration is of the considered opinion, is required to 
be commenced, to meet any emergent situation or 
contingency, the Administration is at liberty to do so to 
meet the exigencies and contingencies that may arise.
8. It is needless to say that the administration may take 
a conscious decision of executing works that they are of 
the opinion are required to be commenced or continued
during the monsoon.
9. List this case on 22.07.2025, alongwith WPMB No.
385 of 2025.
