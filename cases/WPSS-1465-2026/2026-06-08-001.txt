Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, learned senior 
counsel assisted by Mr. Chandra Shekhar 
Dalakoti, learned counsel for petitioner.
2. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State of Uttarakhand/
respondents.
3. The present writ petition has been filed 
by petitioner challenging the impugned 
order dated 13.05.2026 issued by 
respondent No.3, whereby, petitioner was 
denied appointment.
4. Learned senior counsel for petitioner 
submits that the respondent No.3 invited 
application for the post of Assistant 
Teacher in Government Primary School.
Petitioner responded to the said 
advertisement and was called for 
counselling. She participated in the 
counselling, but, she was refused 
appointment initially on the ground that 
petitioner has not supplied the certified 
UDISE particulars by the Block Education 
Officer. Petitioner submitted the said 
certificate certified by the Block Education 
Officer, but still petitioner has not been 
given the appointment, rather the 
impugned order has been passed on a new 
ground saying that petitioner has obtained 
D.El.Ed. qualification despite having B.Ed. 
qualification.
5. He further contends that petitioner 2
before passing the T.E.T. examination, has 
already possessed qualification of D.El.Ed. 
course, therefore, objection made by the 
respondent No.3 is totally uncalled for. 
Petitioner applied for the post of Assistant 
Teacher, Government Primary School, on
the basis of her D.El.Ed. and T.E.T. 
qualification, which is essential qualification 
as required under advertisement.
6. Learned State Counsel submits that 
petitioner qualified T.E.T. examination on 
the basis of B.Ed. qualification while the 
petitioner has to pass the Bridge Course.
7. Learned State Counsel prays for and is 
granted six weeks’ time to file counter 
affidavit.
8. Put up on 11.08.2026.
9. In the meantime, one post of Assistant 
Teacher, Government Primary School, shall 
be kept vacant for the petitioner.
