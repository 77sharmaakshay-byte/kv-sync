WPSS No.1866 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Anil Anthwal, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Atul Bahuguna, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State.
3. By means of this writ petition, petitioner has 
challenged order dated 21.08.2023 (Annexure no.7 to 
the writ petition), whereby petitioner was declared 
‘unfit’ for promotion, on the post of Administrative 
Officer; and further a direction to the respondent to 
give promotion to the petitioner, on the post of 
Administrative Officer, from the date his juniors were 
promoted i.e., 21.08.2023.
4. It is contention of petitioner that he is a senior 
most, Senior Assistant in the Office of Deputy 
Education Officer, Jakhnidar, Tehri-Garhwal, his name
was not included in the list, dated 21.08.2023, which 
was prepared for the purpose of promotion of 
employees in Ministerial Cadre, through counseling. 
Consequently, promotions have been made, except that
of the petitioner.
5. From order impugned dated 21.08.2023, it is 
demonstrated that the Annual Confidential 
Report (ACR) of petitioner, for the year 2019-20, his 
services were found ‘satisfactory’ and for the year 
2021-22 “bad/unsatisfactory”. According to Rules of 2
2013, referred in the impugned order, out of five 
Annual Confidential Reports (ACR) minimum four
Annual Confidential Reports (ACR) should contain 
‘excellent’ or ‘higher category’.
6. Learned counsel for the respondents prays for 
and is granted six weeks’ time for filing counter 
affidavit.
7. Further two weeks’ time is granted to the 
learned counsel for the petitioner for filing rejoinder 
affidavit.
8. List this case on 14.12.2023.
