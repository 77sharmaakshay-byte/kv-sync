12.07.2024 WPMS NO. 1369 of 2024
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Sagar Kothari, counsel for the petitioners.
2. Mr. S.K. Jain, Senior Counsel assisted by Mr. 
Siddhartha Jain, counsel and Mr. Amar Murti Shukla, 
counsel for the caveator.
3. This petition under Article 227 of the 
Constitution of India has been filed by the petitioners
to set-aside the judgment and order dated 30.04.2024 
passed by learned 8th Additional District Judge, 
Dehradun in Rent Control Appeal No. 16 of 2022, 
whereby the said court dismissed the said appeal and 
affirmed the order dated 26.07.2022 passed by the 
Prescribed Authority/2nd Additional Civil Judge (S.D.), 
Dehradun.
4. Counsel for the petitioners/tenants would 
submit that the respondents/landlords filed a release 
application before the Prescribed Authority, Dehradun 
for release of their tenanted property; that, the said 
release application was allowed by the Prescribed 
Authority/2nd Additional Civil Judge (S.D.), Dehradun 
vide judgment and order dated 26.07.2022 directing 
the petitioners/tenants to vacate the property in 
question and to handover the peaceful possession of 
the same to the respondents/landlords within a period 
of 30 days; that, being aggrieved, the 
petitioners/tenants filed Rent Control Appeal No. 16 of 
2022 before the 8th Additional District Judge, 
Dehradun, which was dismissed by the said court by 
affirming the order dated 26.07.2022. Hence, this 
petition.
5. Counsel for the petitioners/tenants would further 
submit that the Prescribed Authority erred in law in 
passing the order dated 26.07.2022 as the 
respondents/landlords had filed the release application 
without giving mandatory notice of six months, as 
stipulated in the proviso to Section 21(1)(a) of the Act 
No. 13 of 1972; that, without any pleading, the 
respondents/landlords placed a letter dated 12.02.1990
written by the father of petitioner no.2/tenant to the previous owner/landlord of the tenanted property
stating thereby that the petitioners have been informed 
that the tenanted property has been sold to the present 
respondent/landlord, therefore, copy of the sale deed
be provided to him; that, the letter has been 
maliciously used by respondents/landlords and has 
been placed before the court when the same was not 
pleaded by the respondents/landlords.
He would further submit that in the absence of 
any pleading that any notice as per Section 21(1)(a) of 
the Act No. 13 of 1972 has been given to the 
petitioners/tenants, then the said letter could not have 
been admitted or relied upon as evidence before the 
Prescribed Authority, thus, both the courts below erred 
in law in passing the impugned orders and are
therefore unsustainable in the eyes of law and are 
liable to be set aside.
6. Per contra, counsel for the 
respondents/landlords would submit that in the bottom 
of the aforesaid letter, it was endorsed that the 
petitioners/tenants have received the copy of the sale 
deed in the year 1990, however, the release application 
was filed in the year 2015.
7. In view of the above submissions made by the 
respective counsel for the parties, this Court is of the 
considered view that the matter needs deliberation.
8. Admit the petition.
9. Lower Court Record be summoned.
10. Counsel for the respondents/landlords seeks and 
is granted time to file counter affidavit.
11. List this case on 04.12.2024.
12. Meanwhile, the effect and operation of the 
impugned judgment and order dated 30.04.2024 
passed by 8th Additional District Judge, Dehradun in 
Rent Control Appeal No. 16 of 2022 and impugned 
judgment and order dated 26.07.2022 passed by 
Prescribed Authority/2nd Additional Civil Judge (S.D.), 
Dehradun in P.A. Case No. 22 of 2015 shall remain 
stayed till further orders of this Court.
13. Stay application (IA 1 of 2024) stands disposed 
of accordingly.
