4. Petitioner, who is a scheduled caste 
candidate, applied for the post of 
Instructor (Electrical) in Government 
Industrial Training Institute (I.T.I.), 
pursuant to advertisement issued by the 
respondent-Selecting Body. Petitioner 
secured 54.50 marks in the written 
examination. The last one selected 
candidate in the Open category secured 
47.25 marks.
5. It is the case of petitioner that in view 
of the higher merit, petitioner should 
migrate to the open category and 
accordingly, he is entitled for appointment.
6. Learned counsel for respondent￾selecting body submits that the Selecting 
Body could not find candidates in general 
category for women, ex-servicemen and 
orphan children (horizontal reservation), 
therefore, vacancy has been filled up by 
the candidate from the open category.
7. In the opinion of this Court, application 
of reservation seems to be faulty inasmuch2
as the open category is opened for all 
irrespective of the social capacities i.e. 
S.C., S.T., O.B.C. and E.W.S. category. In 
such view of the matter, submission made 
by learned counsel for petitioner carries 
some force.
8. Issue notice to respondent No.4 
returnable within six weeks.
9. Steps to be taken within 03 days.
10. Respondents may file counter 
affidavit(s) within six weeks.
11. Put up on 11.08.2026.
12. One post of Instructor (Electrical) in 
Government Industrial Training Institute 
(I.T.I.), in the open category, shall be kept 
vacant for the petitioner pursuant to 
advertisement dated 16.02.2024, during 
the pendency of this writ petition.
13. Interim relief application (IA/1/2026) 
stands disposed of.
