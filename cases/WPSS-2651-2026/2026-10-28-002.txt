the deceased employee is placed before this 
Court by the learned Addl. CSC on perusal

employee since the date of initial induction 
was occupying the substantive post till he

dispute that he was occupying substantive

the petitioner is entitled to get 
compassionate appointment in view of Rule 
2(ka) (3) of Dying in Harness Rules.
4. In such view of the matter let the 
concerned Authority may consider the claim 
of the petitioner for compassionate 
appointment and apprise to this Court by 
the next date.
