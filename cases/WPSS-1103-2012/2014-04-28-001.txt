4. The petitioner is the daughter of a deceased 
employee who was working on daily rated basis in 
Uttarakhand Forest Corporation and died while on duty 
on 04.03.2003. Petitioner’s father was appointed only on 
contractual basis in the Forest Department. Petitioner 
claims regular appointment on compassionate ground. 
But the difficulty with the Department which is reflected 
in the counter affidavit is that the Rules which are 
applicable for such purpose i.e. the U.P. Recruitment of 
Dependants of Government Servants Dying in Harness 
Rules, 1974, provides that the appointment on 
compassionate ground is given only to the dependants of 
such “Government Servant” who are permanent in such 
employment or though temporary but had been regularly 
appointed in such employment or though not regularly 
appointed, had put in three years’ continuous service in 
regular vacancy in such employment. Rule 2 of the U.P.

Recruitment of Dependants of Government Servants 
Dying in Harness Rules, 1974, Rules reads as under:- 
“2.Definitions.- In these rules, unless the context 
otherwise requires- 
(a) “Government servant” means a 
Government servant employed in 
connection with the affairs of Uttar 
Pradesh who- 
(i) was permanent in such

 Or

Explanation.-“Regularly appointed” means

the case may be; 
(b)………………………………………………………
… 
(c)……………………………………………………….. 
(d)……………………………………………………….” 
5. The father of the petitioner, admittedly does not fall 
under any of the aforesaid categories, therefore, although 
the matter of regularization of the service of the father of 
petitioner was sent to the Government as he was working 
on daily rated basis since the year 1983 but he was not 
regularized at the time of death. Hence the aforesaid 
Rules are not applicable in the case of the petitioner. 
6. In view thereof, no relief can be granted to the 
petitioner. 
7. The writ petition is hereby dismissed. No order as to 
costs.
