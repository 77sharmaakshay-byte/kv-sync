Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. M.S. Bhandari, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. S.S. Chaudhary, learned Standing Counsel 
with Mr. Manish Bisht, learned Brief Holder for the 
State.
3. By means of the instant writ petition, petitioner 
has challenged the impugned Termination Order 
dated 06.05.2026 issued by respondent no.4, whereby 
services of the petitioner was terminated.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that petitioner was appointed as an 
Assistant Teacher (Primary) and at that time the 
petitioner possess Bachelor Degree in Arts and secured 
44.93% marks in graduation.
5. The services of the petitioner is now terminated
by the order impugned on the ground that the 
petitioner’s marks are below 45% in graduation.
6. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that in view of the notification of NCTE 
dated 13.11.2019, for the candidates who have taken 
admission in B.Ed. course prior to 29.07.2011, the 
condition of securing 45% marks in graduation is not 
applicable.
7. It is contended by learned counsel for petitioner 
that the petitioner did graduation and B.Ed. course 
prior to 2011 and thus, the condition of having 45% 
marks in graduation would not be attracted in case of 
the petitioner being a General Category Candidate. He 
further submits that in identical issue the Coordinate Bench already stayed the impugned order.
8. Having perused the writ petition and having 
appreciated the contention raised by learned counsel 
for the parties, this Court finds substance in the 
submission made by learned counsel for the petitioner.
9. In such view of the matter, in the peculiar facts 
and circumstances of the case, petitioner deserves 
protection from this Court.
10. In view of the above, it is directed that till the 
next date of listing, effect and operation of the 
impugned communication/order dated 06.05.2026 
passed by respondent no.4, shall remain stayed. 
11. The respondent(s) may file their counter 
affidavit(s) in the matter within six weeks.
12. List this case on 27.11.2026 along with WPSS No. 
2487 of 2026 and WPSS No. 2486 of 2026.
