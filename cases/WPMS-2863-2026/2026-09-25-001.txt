By means of the instant petition, the 
petitioner seeks directions to the Assistant 
Collector, First Class, Haridwar, to decide 
and dispose of the revenue proceedings 
bearing Case No. Nil of 2026, Smt. Seema 
Singh v. State of Uttarakhand, instituted 
under Section 41 of the UP. Land Revenue 
Act, 1901 (“the Act”), in respect of Khata 
No.795, Khasra No.878, measuring 0.1680 
hectare, situated at Village Salempur, 
Mahdood-II, Pargana Roorkee, Tehsil and 
District Haridwar, expeditiously. 
Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that under the statute, such 
applications under Section 41 of the Act are 
to be decided within a period of 3 months, 
but till date, it has not been decided. 
Learned State Counsel admits that the 
time for disposal of such applications is 
within a period of 3 months. The Court requests the concerned 
Assistant Collector, First Class, to look into 
the matter and make endeavour to decide 
such application within a statutory time 
period, and if it is not possible, then make all 
endeavours to decide it as early as possible. 
With this observation, the writ petition 
stands disposed of.
