Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. M.C. Pant, (through V.C.) learned counsel for the 
revisionist.
2. Mr. Piyush Garg, learned counsel for the CBI.
3. Mr. Aditya Singh and Mr. Tapan Singh, learned 
counsel for the respondent no. 2.
4. This revision is pending since 2016 and the instant 
criminal revision has been preferred against the order 
passed by Special Judge (Anti Corruption), CBI, Dehradun 
dated 01.03.2016 in Sessions Trial No. 36 of 2014 (State Vs. 
Basant Soni and others) whereby application moved by the 
revisionist under Section 319 Cr.P.C. has been rejected. 
5. Revisionist filed two applications under Section 319 
Cr.P.C. one for summoning Shamim Alam Khan and 
another for summoning Constable Naveen Chandra. 
Application moved for summoning Constable Naveen 
Chandra was allowed, however, application moved for 
summoning Shamim Alam Khan was rejected. 
6. On perusal of the order sheet, it reveals that instant 
revision has yet not been admitted though it was heard on 
various dates, even on the previous date i.e. 18.07.2025, the 
matter was heard at length. On perusal of the record, it also 
reveals that revisionist / husband of the deceased had also 
made a request on administrative for expediting the 
revision since he is old aged person.
7. Taking into consideration that this revision is old one 
and record has yet not been summoned.
8. Admit. 
9. Summon the record of Sessions Trial No. 36 of 2014 
(State Vs. Basant Soni and others) pending in the court of by 
Special Judge (Anti Corruption), CBI, Dehradun through 
special messenger within ten days. 
10. Registry is directed to send requisition for 
summoning the record by special messenger. 
11. List on 06.08.2025.
