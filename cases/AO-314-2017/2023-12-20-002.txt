Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Bharat Tewari, Advocate, for the

Mr. Pulak Agarwal and Mr. Raunak Pant, 
Advocates, for the respondents. 
This is a claimant’s Appeal from Order, 
seeking enhancement of the compensation, as it has 
been determined by the learned Motor Accident 
Claims Tribunal in MACP No. 153 of 2012, 
Kashmir Singh Vs. Chandra Prakash and others. 
The Appeal from Order is accompanied with 
the Delay Condonation Application No. 6047 of 
2017, seeking condonation of 140 days of delay. 
The respondents have put in appearance, but 
they have not filed any objection till date. 
In view of the reasons given in the Delay 
Condonation Application, the Court is satisfied, that 
there are justifiable reasons to condone the delay. 
Hence, the delay would stand condoned. 
With the consent of the parties, the Appeal is 
taken up for admission. 
Admit the Appeal. 
Summon the LCR. 
List as soon as records of the court below are 
received.
