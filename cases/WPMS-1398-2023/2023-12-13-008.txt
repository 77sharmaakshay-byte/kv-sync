The Court made the following: 
ORDER:(per Hon’ble Sri Rakesh Thapliyal)
 According to respondent nos.3 and 4, the term of 
Executive Body of the Uttarakhand Matri-Shishu Evam Parivar 
Kalyan Mahila Karmchari Association is already come to an 
end on 16.11.2023. Learned counsel for respondent nos.3 
and 4 submits that, though two years’ period has already 
been expired on 16.11.2023, but pursuant to Clause 10 of the 
Constitution, the period may be extended for a further period 
of one year by calling an emergent meeting. 
2. The clause of the Constitution, which the learned 
counsel for respondent nos.3 and 4 is referring, reads as 
under:- 
“संघ की कायŊकाįरणी का कायŊकाल 02 वषŊका होगा। िवशेष पįरİ̾थितयों
मŐŮाȶीय कायŊकाįरणी की आपातकालीन बैठक बुलाकर 01 वषŊ का कायŊकाल
बढ़ाया जा सकता है।”2 
3. Learned counsel for respondent nos.3 and 4 further 
submits that for the purposes of extension of tenure, an 
emergent meeting was called in the month of September, 
2023, wherein a decision was taken for extension of further 
one year period from 17.11.2023. 
4. On the other side, learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the Constitution, in which, respondent nos.3 and 
4 are relying, pursuant to which, the period was extended for 
one year in an emergent meeting, which was called in the 
month of September, 2023, is a fabricated document, and in 
reference to this, specific averment has been made in 
Paragraph No.9 of the writ petition. Paragraph No.9 of the 
writ petition reads as under:- 
“9. That when the members of the association including 
petitioner herein revolted against the action of the present 
office bearers, i.e. respondent no.3 & 4, they were shown a 
copy of the fabricated bye-laws of the association purporting 
it to be original bye-laws which is entirely contrary to the 
original bye-laws on the basis whereof the association was 
given recognition on 27.07.2007”. 
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner further submits 
that, as per the original bye-laws, the period of Executive 
Body is for two years, and there is no provision for further 
extension of tenure. 
6. On instructions, learned Standing Counsel placed 
before this Court the instructions issued from the Director 
General, Medical Health and Family Welfare, Uttarakhand, 3 
Dehradun, dated 02.12.2023, wherein the Constitution of 
Association is also enclosed. 
7. Learned Standing Counsel submits that the 
Constitution, which has been received through instructions, is 
the same, in which learned counsel for the petitioner is 
relying. He further submits that in this Constitution, which he 
has received through instructions, there is no provision for 
further extension of tenure. 
8. Put up this matter on 26.12.2023. 
9. By that time, the Director General, Medical Health 
and Family Welfare, Uttarakhand, Dehradun may scrutinize 
both the Constitutions:- (i) in which the petitioner placed 
reliance, and; (ii) in which respondent nos.3 and 4 placed 
reliance. 
10. In the meantime, since as per the Constitution, 
which the learned Standing Counsel has received through 
instructions, there is no provision for further extension of 
tenure, therefore, admittedly, the tenure of the Executive 
Body is already expired on 16.11.2023. 
11. On instructions, learned Standing Counsel submits 
that there is no provision for appointment of an 
Administrator. 4 
12. In view of this, since there is no provision under 
the Constitution of Association to appoint Administrator, in 
the interest of justice for an interim measure, the Director 
General, Medical Health and Family Welfare is directed to 
appoint any government gazetted official as an Administrator 
in place of the Executive Body of the Association, and the said 
Administrator will perform all the functions of the Association. 
13. The instructions received by the learned State 
Counsel have been placed before this Court, and the same 
are taken on record. 
(RAKESH THAPLIYAL, J.)
