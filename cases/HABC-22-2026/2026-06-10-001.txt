This is Habeas Corpus writ petition. 
After arguing for a while, learned counsel 
for the petitioner restricts the scope of this 
writ petition to the prayer no.4, by which it 
is sought that the respondent no.3, the 
Station House Officer, Police Station 
Bhagwanpur, be directed to preserve, 
secure and produce the CCTV footages from 
01.02.2026 to 04.02.2026. She submits 
that the police is duty bound to produce 
and supply the CCTV footages to the 
petitioner in view of the law, as laid down 
by the Hon’ble Supreme Court in the case 
of Paramvir Singh Saini v. Baljit Singh and 
Others, Special Leave Petition (Criminal)
No.3543 of 2020, and directions issued by 
the State Government.
Learned State Counsel seeks time to 
get instructions in the matter.
List this matter on 15.06.2026.
