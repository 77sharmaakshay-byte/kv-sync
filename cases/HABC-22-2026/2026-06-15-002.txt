Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Hon’ble Siddhartha Sah, J.
Ms. Prabha Naithani, Advocate for the

Mr. B.N. Molekhi, D.A.G. for the State.
This is a Habeas Corpus petition. On 
10.06.2026, when the matter was taken up, 
learned counsel for the petitioner restricted the 
scope of this writ petition to the prayer no.4, 
by which, it is sought that the respondent no.3, 
the Station House Officer, Police Station 
Bhagwanpur may be directed to preserve, 
secure and produce the CCTV footages from 
01.02.2026 to 04.02.2026.
Today, learned State counsel, under 
instructions, submits that the CCTV footages 
are not available as NVR had some fault, which 
was detected on 24.04.2026 and thereafter, on 
03.05.2026, new NVR was installed.
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the petitioner was taken by the 
police from his residence on 01.02.2026 and 
the petitioner was detained illegally in the 
police station till 04.02.2006, therefore, it is 
necessary to get the CCTV footages for that 
period. But, as stated, it is reported that the 
CCTV was not working at the relevant time.
During the course of hearing, learned 
counsel for the petitioner has also handed over 
the communication dated 19.01.2021 of the 
Director General of Police, Uttarakhand to all 
the Police Superintendents and others with 
regard to the installation of CCTV, pursuant to 
the direction of the Hon’ble Supreme Court 
passed in Special Leave Petition (Criminal) No. 
3543 of 2020, Paramvir Singh Saini Vs. Baljit 
Singh & others.
CCTVs are installed with a purpose. But, 
if CCTV footages are denied on the grounds as stated in the instant case, it would defeat the 
very purpose of installing CCTVs.
Let the Senior Superintendent of Police, 
Haridwar conduct an inquiry in this matter 
and submit his report on the following points:-
(i) What is the backup of the CCTV 
footages at the police station?
(ii) How it is ensured that the CCTV 
cameras are properly working?
(iii) Who is responsible for ensuring 
proper working of the CCTV cameras 
at the Police Station?
(iv) In the CCTV camera installed at 
Police Station Bhagwanpur, which 
are the last footages that are 
available and what is the cause of 
further non availability of data of 
Police Station Bhagwanpur? This 
should be clarified with the help of 
NIC. 
 The report should reach this Court on or 
before 20.07.2026.
 List on 20.07.2026.
