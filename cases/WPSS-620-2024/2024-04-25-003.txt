4. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
5. Issue notice to the respondent nos.2 & 3, 
returnable within six weeks.
6. It is submitted by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that petitioner was initially engaged 
as daily wager Sweeper with respondent nos.2 
& 3 on 01.01.2000 and he had been 
continuously discharging his duty as such. But, 
suddenly, the status of daily wager was 
changed to contractual and he was engaged 
through outsourcing agency i.e. respondent 
no.4-UPNL. He further submits that earlier, as a 
daily wager, petitioner was getting salary of 
Rs.18,323/- per month from respondent nos.2 
& 3, but now, suddenly, after the change of his 
status as contractual employee, he is being 
paid only Rs.5,000/- per month as wages since 
April, 2022.
7. Apparently, the treatment which has been 
meted out to the petitioner appears to be 2
arbitrary, therefore, the interim relief 
application will be considered immediately after 
filing of the counter affidavit by the 
respondents.
8. Respondents may file their counter 
affidavit within a period of six weeks.
9. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to 
the petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit.
