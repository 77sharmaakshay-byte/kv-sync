Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Zafar Ullah Siddiqui, Advocate, for

Mr. Lalit Belwal, Advocate, for the 
respondent.
After the appeal from order was 
dismissed for want of prosecution on 
28.02.2019, the lower court record of MACP 
No.25 of 2009, “Mohd. Shadab Vs. Naved 
Ahmad and others”, were sent back to the
concerned Motor Accident Claims Tribunal.
The said order has been recalled by this 
Court, but the lower court record has not been 
summoned. Hence, the Registry is directed to 
summon the lower court record of MACP 
No.25 of 2009, “Mohd. Shadab Vs. Naved 
Ahmad and others”.
Apart from it, the coordinate Bench of 
this Court vide its order dated 11.11.2021, had 
constituted the medical board, in order to access 
the percentage of disability, which was suffered 
by the claimant. 
The CMO, has submitted the medical 
report in a sealed envelope, which was opened 
during the proceedings of this appeal today,
which were held today, in presence of the 
learned Counsels of both the parties.The xerox copy of the said medical
report is supplied to each of the learned 
counsels for the parties to the appeal from 
order, but this Court is apprehensive to read the 
said report for the purposes of deciding this 
appeal from order, because it is deemed to be a 
document, which is not on record, to be read for
the purposes of deciding the appeal from order.
Learned counsel for the appellant prays 
that he may be granted four days’ time to file 
necessary affidavit to read the medical report 
submitted by the CMO, in pursuance to the 
order of this Court, for the purposes of deciding 
the appeal.
The Registry is directed to ensure that the 
lower court record is summoned and place 
before this Court within 48 hours.
Put up this appeal from order on 
27.06.2022.
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
