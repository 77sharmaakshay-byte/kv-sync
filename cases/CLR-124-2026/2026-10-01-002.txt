Mr. Neeraj Garg, learned Senior Counsel

3. Objections have been filed by the counsel for 
the respondents in CLR No.122 of 2026. The 
same are taken on record. Misc. Application, 
made therefor, stands disposed of.
4. Learned counsel for the revisionists submits 
that during the pendency of the present revision 
the possession of the property has been taken by 
the landlord. He further submits that execution 
proceedings, so far as it relates to the mesne
profit, are still pending. He submits that though 
the possession of the property has been taken, 
however, the respondents may be restrained from 
changing the nature of the property and the 
further execution proceedings in relation to the mesne profit may be stayed during the pendency 
of the present revision.
5. Admit.
6. Summon the LCR.
7. Till the next date of listing, the respondents 
are restrained from changing the nature of the 
property. The revisionists shall be at liberty to 
move an application before the execution court
seeking adjournment. In case such an application 
is filed, the learned Court shall consider the same
for granting an adjournment till the next date of 
listing.
8. List this case on 06.11.2026.
