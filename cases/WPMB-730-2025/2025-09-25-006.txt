Per: Sri G. NARENDAR, C. J.
1. Registrar (Judicial) has sought for extension of 
time to place on record the statistics regarding the 
number of petitions filed by petitioner challenging the 
decision of the Tender Evaluation Committee and award 
of tender to persons who are not L1. Request is 
accepted. Further four weeks’ time is granted to the 
Registrar (Judicial).
2. The documents placed today by the HDFC Bank 
clearly records that the Send Receive Date was 
104.08.2025 at 13:57 Hrs and the status recorded is 
received. The receipt has been further confirmed by the 
recipient bank i.e. ICICI by email dated 22.09.2025 in 
response to email sent by the petitioner’s banker namely 
HDFC Bank. Despite this, an audacious statement is 
made by the respondent, that the message regarding the 
purchase of bank guarantee has not been sent by the 
issuing Bank, in our opinion, would only reflect to the 
levels that the respondents are stooping to favour a 
particular tenderer. Prima facie, this is the only opinion 
that we are able to infer. 
3. That apart, the HDFC Bank has also placed a 
communication of the RBI dated 25.02.2016 wherein the 
RBI has intimated to all the banks regarding the approval 
of the use of SWIFT also for domestic financial 
transactions. The communication reads as under:-
“RBI approves SWIFT India Domestic Services 
Private Limited (SIDSPL) for Domestic Financial 
Messaging Services in India
The Reserve Bank of India has granted approval to 
SWIFT India Domestic Services Private Limited (SIDSPL) 
to provide messaging services for domestic financial 
transactions in India.
Background
2The decision to permit the use of SWIFT for domestic 
financial transactions was announced in the Monetary 
Policy Statement 2012-13. Currently, the messaging for 
inter-bank transactions in India is through Structured 
Financial Messaging System (SFMS). Messaging 
operations of SWIFT will support and strengthen the 
infrastructure for financial transactions in the country. 
Alpana Killawala
Principal Advisor” 
4. A reading of the above press release of the RBI 
would obviate any discussion on the aspect. It records 
messaging operations of SWIFT will support and 
strengthen the infrastructure for financial transactions in 
the country. The statement speaks for itself. 
5. That apart, the reply forwarded by the ICICI 
Bank i.e. the bankers of the respondent tendering 
authority to the HDFC Bank clearly shows that the 
intimation regarding purchase of bank guarantee by the 
petitioner favouring the official respondent has been 
communicated by the issuing/assuring bank i.e. the 
HDFC bank on 04.08.2025 and the same communication 
has been received at 13:57 Hrs and the status recorded 
is “received” which is further confirmed by the email 
dated 22.09.2025.
