5. Learned Advocate General submits that the work 
order for the construction of the road in Pithoragarh was 
issued in favour of respondent no.5 which was put to 
challenge in the present writ petition and the Hon’ble Court 
vide interim order dated 16.09.2025 stayed all further 
proceedings.
6. He submits that the road being located in the border 
area is of strategic importance and that due to the interim 
order granted by the Court the work of public importance is 
affected.
7. Per contra, learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that the interim order was passed in presence of the parties 
after hearing them at length, however, he fairly admits that 
the construction of road is of strategic importance.
8. Having heard learned counsel for the parties, we are 
inclined to modify the order dated 16.09.2025, subject to 
the condition that the successful bidder shall prepare a fix 
deposit of ₹2 Crore in the name of Registrar General, 
Uttarakhand High Court for a period of six months and shall 
furnish the same before the Court within two weeks from today so that, in case, the writ petition is allowed then the 
Court may consider to award appropriate damages to the 
petitioner.
9. In case the aforesaid FDR is deposited within two 
weeks from today the successful bidder would be permitted 
to carry on the construction work of the road. In default of 
compliance of this order, the interim order dated 
16.09.2025 shall revive against the respondents.
10. List this case after vacation.
 (Subhash Upadhyay, J.) (G. Narendar, C.J.)
