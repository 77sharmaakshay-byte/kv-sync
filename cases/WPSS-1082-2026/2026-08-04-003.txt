3. It is the case of petitioner that despite 
petitioner’s claim to be considered as Guest Lecturer 
(English) under reserved category in District 
Champawat, his case has not been considered, and 
rather rejected on frivolous grounds by respondent 
no.2-Director, Secondary Education saying that there 
is no vacancy on the post of Guest Lecturer (English) 
in District Champawat. 
4. Petitioner contends that he had filed a writ 
petition on previous occasion in which petitioner was 
given liberty to file representation; in that 
representation, it was shown that there were 
vacancies available in District Champawat, but still, 
the claim of petitioner to be considered on the post of 
Guest Lecturer was not considered and rather 
rejected.
5. Learned State Counsel submits that from the 
record, it transpires that for the vacancies, the 
requisition has been sent to the Uttarakhand Public Service Commission in the year 2025 and 
advertisement was issued on 30.12.2025. He further 
submits that the select list/merit list of Guest 
Lecturer was prepared on 19.11.2024, and therefore, 
in view of expiry of life of select list, petitioner cannot 
be offered appointment/engagement. 
6. The argument advanced by learned State 
Counsel is bereft of merit as the rule of expiry of life of
select list will not be attracted to the case of petitioner 
as it is not a regular appointment. The delay, 
whichever has occurred in offering appointment to the 
petitioner, is due to high handedness on the part of 
Director of Education Department. 
7. Learned State Counsel is directed to get complete 
instructions in the matter, within a week, from 
respondent no.2 as to how petitioner can be 
accommodated against the available vacancies of 
Guest Lecturer (English) in District Champawat till a 
regularly selected candidate joins the office. 
8. List on 12.08.2026.
