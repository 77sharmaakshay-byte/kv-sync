Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Vikas Bahuguna and Mr. Yash 
Bisht, learned counsel for the applicant.
2. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned Additional 
Advocate General along with Mr. S.C. 
Dumka, learned A.G.A. and Mr. Nikhil 
Bisht, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Mr. Ramji Srivastava and Mr. Gaurav 
Nagpal, learned counsel for the 
complainant.
4. By means of the present application, 
the applicant seeks anticipatory bail in 
connection with Case Crime/F.I.R. No. 
0223 of 2026, registered at Police Station 
Doiwala, District Dehradun, for the offence 
punishable under Section 80(2) of the 
Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (B.N.S.).
5. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would submit that the impugned F.I.R. has 
been lodged by the mother of the deceased, 
alleging that the marriage of the deceased 
was solemnized with the son of the present applicant on 29.11.2025, according to 
Hindu rites and rituals. It is alleged that, 
after the marriage, the deceased was 
subjected to harassment and victimisation 
by her in-laws in connection with the 
alleged demand of dowry and was also 
subjected to sarcastic and humiliating 
remarks. It is further alleged that, being 
aggrieved by such conduct, the deceased 
committed suicide on 28.07.2026. 
6. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would further submit that the applicant is 
innocent and has falsely been implicated 
on the basis of vague and unfounded 
allegations; that, the marriage between the 
deceased and the applicant's son was 
solemnized at Trijuginarayan Temple, 
District Rudraprayag, on 29.11.2025 and 
the reception was thereafter held at 
Dehradun on 02.12.2025. It is contended 
that neither the applicant nor any member 
of her family ever demanded dowry from 
the deceased or her parental family, nor 
was the deceased subjected to physical or 
mental cruelty by the applicant.
7. It is further submitted that, after the 
marriage, the deceased stayed at her 
matrimonial home at Dehradun for about 
fifteen days and thereafter returned to her 
place of posting, as she was serving as an 
Assistant Teacher (L.T.) at Government 
Inter College, Rigoli, Kirtinagar, District 
Tehri Garhwal; that, shus, owing to the 
nature of her employment, she ordinarily 
resided at her place of posting and was not 
continuously residing in the matrimonial home; that, the applicant's son, i.e. the 
husband of the deceased, is employed in 
the State Secretariat, Dehradun.
8. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would further submit that the deceased 
returned to the matrimonial home on or 
about 01.01.2026 during her winter 
vacation and the applicant's son and the 
deceased were scheduled to proceed on 
their honeymoon to the Andaman and 
Nicobar Islands on 04.01.2026, for which 
the requisite bookings had already been 
made by the applicant, however, the 
proposed trip could not take place as the 
applicant's husband suffered a serious 
deterioration in his health and remained 
admitted at Max Hospital, Dehradun, from 
02.01.2026 to 08.01.2026 and again from 
23.01.2026 to 06.02.2026; that, thereafter, 
he was referred to Medanta Hospital, Delhi, 
on 10.02.2026, where he remained under 
treatment and ultimately expired on 
26.07.2026.
9. He also submits that, throughout the 
aforesaid period, the applicant and her 
family members were preoccupied with the 
deteriorating health and treatment of the 
applicant's husband, who was suffering 
from Stage-IV brain cancer; that, upon 
receiving information regarding the demise 
of her father-in-law, the deceased came to 
the matrimonial home on 26.07.2026 along 
with her mother.
10. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would also submit that the applicant has 
never demanded dowry from the deceased or her family members and has never 
subjected the deceased to any form of 
physical or mental cruelty; that, the 
allegations against the applicant, according 
to learned counsel, have been levelled 
subsequently on account of the 
unfortunate death of the deceased; that, 
the deceased had been experiencing 
emotional and mental difficulties even 
prior to her marriage and that her 
emotional condition had allegedly 
deteriorated over a period of time, which 
aspect was within the knowledge of the 
applicant's son.
11. It is further submitted that the 
applicant is a senior citizen aged about 66 
years, has no criminal antecedents, is a 
permanent resident of District Dehradun 
and there is no likelihood of her 
absconding the process of law, therefore, 
the applicant is entitled for anticipatory 
bail.
12. Per contra, learned counsel for the 
complainant would vehemently oppose the 
prayer for anticipatory bail. He would 
submit that immediately prior to 
committing suicide, the deceased recorded 
a video, which subsequently became 
available on social media, wherein she 
stated that she had been subjected to 
harassment by her in-laws in connection 
with the demand for dowry. He would 
further submit that the contents of the 
said video constitute a material 
circumstance against the applicant and 
her family members. However, learned counsel for the complainant prays for and 
is granted four weeks' time to file a detailed 
counter affidavit.
13. Learned State Counsel has also 
sought time to obtain complete 
instructions and place the relevant 
material before the Court.
14. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties and having considered the nature 
of the allegations, the material presently 
available on record, the submissions 
advanced on behalf of the applicant, as 
well as the fact that the applicant is a 
woman aged about 66 years and has no 
criminal antecedents, this Court is of the 
prima facie view that the applicant has 
made out a case for grant of interim 
protection from arrest. However, without 
expressing any opinion on the merits of the 
case or the evidentiary value of the 
allegations, the matter requires further 
consideration after the respondents are 
afforded an opportunity to place their 
response on record.
15. Accordingly, it is directed that the 
investigation shall proceed independently, 
fairly and strictly in accordance with law 
and shall not be influenced by any 
observation made in this order. However, 
in the event of arrest of the applicant in 
connection with the aforesaid Case 
Crime/F.I.R., she shall be released on 
interim anticipatory bail till the next date 
of listing on his furnishing a personal bond 
along with two reliable sureties, each in 
the like amount, to the satisfaction of the Arresting Officer/Investigating Officer, 
subject to the following
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation and shall appear before the 
Investigating Officer as and when required;
(ii) The applicant shall not directly or 
indirectly make any inducement, threat or 
promise to any person acquainted with the 
facts of the case so as to dissuade such 
person from disclosing such facts to the 
Court or to the Investigating Agency;
(iii) The applicant shall not leave the territory 
of India without prior permission of the Court 
concerned;
(iv) The applicant shall not misuse the liberty 
granted by this order and shall maintain 
peace and good conduct during the period of 
interim protection;
(v) In case of breach of any of the aforesaid 
conditions, it shall be open to the 
State/prosecution to seek modification or 
cancellation of interim anticipatory bail in 
accordance with law.
 (Alok Mahra, J.) 20.08.2026 Mamta
