2. Mr. Vikas Pandey, learned Brief Holder, present for

3. Rejoinder affidavit filed today is taken on record. 
4. Father of the petitioner was a permanent Class III 
employee in the Agriculture Department of the State of 
Uttarakhand. He died while in harness on 07.10.2012. 
Petitioner being son, applied for compassionate 
appointment on a Class III post under Dying in harness 
Rules on 02.11.2012. This application of the petitioner 
has been rejected by the respondent no. 2 vide order dated 
14.12.2012 on the ground that brother of the petitioner is 
already in service in the Central Government, therefore, 
such appointment cannot be given. 
5. Aggrieved, the petitioner filed present writ petition 
before this Court. 
6. In the Counter affidavit which was called, same 
objections have been reasserted such as that 
compassionate appointment cannot be given to the 
petitioner as the elder brother of the petitioner is in the 
Central Service and, therefore, such appointment cannot 
be given. Rules which are applicable for such appointment 
in Uttarakhand are U.P. Recruitment of Dependents of

Government Servant Dying-in-Harness Rules, 1974. Rule 
5 of the said rules, which is in fact the main provision 
reads as under:- 
“5. Recruitment of a member of the family of 
the deceased. – (1) In case a Government 
servant dies in harness after the 
commencement of these rules and the spouse of 
the deceased Government servant is not already 
employed under the Central Government or a 
State Government or a Corporation owned or 
controlled by the Central Government or a State 
Government, one member of his family who is 
not already employed under the Central 
Government or a State Government or a 
Corporation owned or controlled by the Central 
Government or a State Government shall, on 
making an application for the purposes, be 
given a suitable employment in Government 
service on a post except the post which is 
within the purview of the Uttar Pradesh Public 
Service Commission, in relaxation of the normal 
recruitment rules if such person – 
(i) fulfils the educational qualifications 
prescribed for the post, 
(ii) is otherwise qualified for Government 
service, and 
(iii) makes the application for employment 
within five years from the date of the death of 
the Government servant : 
Provided that where the State Government is 
satisfied that the time-limit fixed for making the 
application for employment causes undue 
hardship in any particular case, it may 
dispense with or relax the requirement as it 
may consider necessary for dealing with the 
case in a just and equitable manner. 
(2) As far as possible, such an employment 
should be given in the same department in 
which the deceased Government servant was 
employed prior to his death.] 
[3) Each appointment under sub-rule (1) should 
be under the condition that the person 
appointed under sub-rule (1) shall upkeep 
those other family members of the deceased 
Government servant who are incapable for their 
own maintenance and were dependant of the

abovesaid deceased Government servant 
immediately before his death.]” 
7. When an employee dies in harness the only 
restriction is that the spouse of the deceased Government 
servant is not already employed under the Central 
Government or the State Government of a Corporation 
owned or controlled by the Central Government or State 
Government, then the compassionate appointment to any 
of the family members of the deceased shall not be given. 
There is no such restriction under the Rules that if one of 
the sons of the deceased employee is in service, the other 
family member cannot be given employment under the 
Dying in Harness Rules. Infact this issue has already been 
adjudication by this Court in the case of Ajay Kumar Vs. 
State of Uttarakhand & others, reported in 2009 (2) 
U.D. 556 (Annexure No.5 to the writ petition), which has 
been upheld by the Division Bench of this Court. 
8. In view thereof, the writ petition is allowed. Order 
dated 14.12.2012 and order dated 22.12.2012, passed by 
respondent no. 2 and respondent no.3 is quashed. Since 
the above referred ground is the only reason for denying 
appointment to the petitioner, which is incorrect, a 
mandamus is issued to the respondents to give 
compassionate appointment to the petitioner on available 
vacancy on which such appointment can be given, within 
a period of 12 weeks from the date of production of 
certified copy of this order. 
9. No order as to costs.
