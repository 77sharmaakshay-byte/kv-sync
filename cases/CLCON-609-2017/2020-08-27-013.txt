Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.

Mr. Chitrarth Kandpal, Advocate for the petitioner.
Mr. T. A. Khan, Senior Advocate assisted by 
Mr. Vinay Bhatt, Advocate for the respondent.
This matter is heard through video conferencing.
The petitioner in this present Contempt Petition is an 
appellant in Second Appeal No. 124 of 2017 Masroor 
Ahmed vs. Abdul Malik and another. The Second 
Appeal was admitted by the Co-ordinate Bench of 
this Court on 12.10.2017, an interim order was 
granted themselves directing the parties to maintain 
status quo qua possession. The appellant contending 
that there was an infringement of the said direction
dated 12.10.2017 had filed this present contempt 
petition on 09.11.2017. Notices were issued to the 
respondent no.3 and the matter is taken up today.
This Court has posed a question to the counsel for 
the petitioner as to how the Contempt Petition would 
be maintainable under Section 12 of the Contempt of 
Courts Act, of 1971, particularly, when the 
proceedings of the Second Appeal are governed by 
the provision under Section 108 of the CPC and its 
exclusively happens to be a self contained civil 
proceedings and for an infringement of any interim 
direction issued in a civil proceedings, the remedy 
available would be as per provisions contemplated 
under the CPC etc. that is Order 39 Rule 2 A of the 
CPC. In such an eventualities this Court is of the 
view that for drawing a contempt proceedings by
party to the proceedings in Second Appeal would not 
lie, hence, the Contempt Petition is dismissed with a 
liberty open to the petitioner to seek an appropriate 
remedy available to him in accordance with the 
provisions of the Civil Procedure Code before an 
appropriate Court.
Contempt petition is accordingly closed.
