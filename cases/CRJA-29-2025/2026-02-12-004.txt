IA No.1/2025 (Bail Application)
With 
CRJA No.29 of 2025
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Ravi Shankar Kandpal, learned legal aid counsel 
for the Appellant.
2. Mr. G.C. Joshi, learned AGA with Mr. J.P. Kandpal, 
learned Brief Holder, for the State of Uttarakhand. 
3. Appellant was convicted for offence punishable under 
Sections 342, 354, 354-B and 506 of IPC, vide judgment and 
order dated 11.04.2025, passed by the court of learned 
Additional Session Judge, Ranikhet, District Almora in 
Sessions Trial No.06 of 2024, “State vs. Vivek Joshi”. 
Challenging his conviction and sentence, Appellant has filed 
this appeal.
4. Heard on the bail application (IA No.1/2025).
5. Learned counsel for the Appellant presses the bail 
application in favour of the Appellant with a plea that 
Appellant has falsely been implicated by the Complainant in 
collusion with the villagers and the revenue police. He further 
submits that there are major contradictions in the testimony of 
the prosecution witnesses.
6. The bail application of the Appellant has been objected 
on behalf of the learned State Counsel mainly on the ground 
that considering the offence against an elderly woman and 
confining herself and subjected her to sexual assault does not 
form any ground for bail, and it ought to be rejected at the 
outset. 
7. After hearing learned counsel for the parties and on perusal of the record and without going into the merits of the 
case as it would affect the final outcome of the appeal, at this 
juncture, the grounds for bail are not found to be sufficient. 
Consequently, the bail application is rejected. 
8. List this matter for final hearing on 12.03.2026.
