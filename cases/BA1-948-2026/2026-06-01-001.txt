BA1 No. 948 of 2026
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Ms. Rajni Rangwal, learned counsel for the Applicant. 
2. Mr. Deepak Bisht, learned Deputy Advocate General for the State 
of Uttarakhand. 
3. The present Bail Application has been moved by the Applicant—
Kumari Sanjana alias Dolly, D/o Vinod Kumar, R/o Subhash Nagar, 
Ward No. 8, Police Station Bajpur, District Udham Singh Nagar, who is 
in judicial custody in connection with Case Crime No. 474 of 2023, 
registered at Police Station Bajpur, District Udham Singh Nagar, for 
offences punishable under Sections 302, 201 and 34 of the IPC.
4. Learned counsel for the Applicant, for consideration of bail, at the 
outset, submits that two co-accused persons have already been granted 
bail on the sole ground that the grounds of their arrest were not 
communicated to them. It is contended that the case of the present 
Applicant stands on the same footing, as the grounds of her arrest were 
also not communicated to her. Therefore, invoking the principle of parity, 
learned counsel prays that the Applicant be enlarged on bail.
5. Learned State Counsel, however, refutes the said submissions and 
contends that the co-accused who have been granted bail were not named 
in the FIR, whereas the present Applicant is specifically named therein. 
He, therefore, seeks time to file objections/counter affidavit.
6. Learned State Counsel is granted two weeks' time to file 
objections/counter affidavit.
7. List this case on 17.06.2026.
