Ms. Neetu Singh, learned counsel for the

5. This Court, vide order dated 20.07.2026, 
directed the Chief Medical Officer, Haridwar, to 
constitute a Medical Board of three doctors within 
one week from the date of the order. The Medical 
Board was directed to consist of one Neurologist, one 
Psychiatrist, and one other doctor of his choice, 
fixing the date and time for the examination of 
petitioner no.1 (Anjali Katariya) and informing 
petitioner nos. 2 to 4 of the same. The Medical Board 
was directed to examine petitioner no.1 and submit a 
report straightaway, fixing the case on 05.08.2026.
6. Today, the matter was called. There is a letter of 
the CMO, Haridwar, dated 30.07.2026, addressed to the Deputy Registrar (Judicial), whereby the CMO, 
Haridwar, has informed that in District Haridwar, 
there was no Neurologist posted, therefore, petitioner 
no.1 cannot be examined as per the order passed by 
this Court dated 20.07.2026. He advised in his letter 
that petitioner no.1 (Anjali Katariya) be medically 
examined either at Doon Medical College, Dehradun, 
or AIIMS, Rishikesh.
7. Having taken cognizance of this letter, this 
Court directs the Director, AIIMS, Rishikesh, to 
constitute a Medical Board of three members within 
one week from today, consisting of one Neurologist, 
one Psychiatrist, and one other doctor of the choice 
of the Director. The Medical Board, so constituted, 
shall fix the date and time for the examination of 
petitioner no.1 (Anjali Katariya) and inform petitioner 
nos.2 to 4 of the same. The Medical Board shall 
examine petitioner no.1 and submit the report to this 
Court straightaway.
8. List on 16.09.2026.
9. Registry is directed to send the copy of order to 
the Director, AIIMS, Rishikesh within 24 hours.
