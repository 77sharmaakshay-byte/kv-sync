5. One Mr. Bikram Pal retired from the post 
of Deputy Branch Manager from Union Bank of 
India on 31.12.2018 and thereafter he died on 
13.11.2021. Mr. Bikram Pal had married twice, 
initially with one Ms. Savita and after her death 
on 30.04.2004, he re-married with one Ms.
Kurmala (respondent no. 6) on 08.05.2005. 
The entire dues, including family pension was 
paid to the second wife (respondent no. 6). 
6. This writ petition is filed by the children 
born out from the first wife, challenging the 
pension payment order in favour of respondent 
no. 6, on 01.01.2019.
7. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
contends that the eldest daughter of the 
deceased employee Ms. Anjali Katariya suffers 
from Schizophrenia, therefore, as per the applicable Rules, she is also entitled to family 
pension. 
8. Learned counsel for the bank however, 
submits that in the absence of any certificate 
issued by a competent authority, the assertion 
made on behalf of the petitioner that Ms. Anjali 
Katariya is suffering from Schizophrenia,
cannot be accepted. 
9. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submitted that a Medical Board headed by 
Chief Medical Officer, Haridwar has certified 
that Ms. Anjali Katariya suffers from
Schizophrenia. 
10. Learned counsel for the bank, however, 
submits that as per the Rules applicable in the 
bank, the disability/ailment has to be examined 
by a doctor empanelled/approved by the bank 
and such certificate alone can be considered. 
11. Court had directed learned counsel for the 
bank to get instructions regarding the doctor, 
who is approved by the bank. Today, on 
instructions, he submits that one Dr. Kapil 
Arora, whose particulars are given below, has 
been approved by the bank: 
Dr. Kapil Arora,
H.No. 56/57, Madhubani, 
Uttarigaon, Near Kasiga School, 
Dehradun-248009
Ph. No. 7351300900
 7451900300. 
12. Therefore, Ms. Anjali Katariya can be 
produced before the said doctor for examination and if the said doctor certifies that 
she suffers from some disability, then bank will 
do the needful as per the Rules. 
13. In such view of the matter, let 
Ms. Anjali Katariya be examined by 
Dr. Kapil Arora, on 17.11.2025. The doctor 
concerned shall submit his report, including 
certificate, if any, in the office of Regional 
Manager, Union Bank of India, situate at 
Dehradun. The Regional Manager concerned 
shall ensure that all the documents submitted 
by the concerned doctor are transmitted to the 
counsel appearing for the bank within one 
week thereafter.
