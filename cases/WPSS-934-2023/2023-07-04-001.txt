Heard on Interim Relief Application IA 
No.1 of 2023.
It is the case of the petitioners that 
after the death of their father, the family 
pension has not been shared with them and 
the second wife of the father has been 
granted the entire family pension in 
defiance to the regulation 40 of the Union 
Bank of India (Employees) Pension 
Regulations, 1995, which has been quoted 
in Para 17 of the writ petition. The relevant 
portion reads as follows:-
Where the deceased employee or pensioner is survived by a widow but 
has left behind eligible child or 
children from another wife who is not 
alive, the eligible child or children 
shall be entitled to the share of 
family pension which the mother 
would have received if she had been 
alive at the time of the death of the 
employee pensioner:
Having considered, as an interim 
measure, till the next date of listing, no 
further family pension shall be paid to the 
respondent no.6
Interim relief application stands 
disposed of, accordingly.
