5. Vide order dated 12.11.2025, this Court had 
directed petitioner no.1 (Anjali Katariya) to be 
medically examined by Dr. Kapil Arora on 17.11.2025 
regarding the disease suffered by her i.e. 
Schizophrenia.
6. Today, learned Counsel appearing for respondent 
–Bank submits that petitioner no.1 could not be 
examined as Dr. Kapil Arora showed his reluctance to 
examine petitioner no.1 since he is a mere physician 
and was not an expert Psychiatrist. 
7. In such view of the matter, in order to get 
petitioner no.1 (Anjali Katariya) medically examined, 
this Court is of the view that she must be examined by 
a panel of doctors one of whom must be a 
Psychiatrist. 8. Accordingly, the Chief Medical Officer (CMO), 
Haridwar is directed to constitute a Medical Board of 
three doctors within one week from today; the Medical 
Board must be consisting of one Neurologist, one 
Psychiatrist and one other doctor of his choice; the 
C.M.O. Haridwar shall fix the date and time for 
examination of petitioner no.1 (Anjali Katariya) and 
inform petitioner nos.2 to 4 for the same; the Medical 
Board shall examine petitioner no.1; and submit its 
report to this Court straightway.
9. List this matter on 05.08.2026 along with the 
report.
10. Registry to send a copy of this order, along with 
complete memo of parties and their addresses, today 
itself, to the C.M.O. Haridwar for information and 
compliance.
