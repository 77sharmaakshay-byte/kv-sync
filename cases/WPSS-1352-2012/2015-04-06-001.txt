Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J. (Oral) 
1. The father of the petitioner was working on a 
regular post of Sweeper, who passed away on 27.07.2004 
while being an employee of Uttarakhand Jal Vidyut Nigam 
Limited which is a State Corporation. After the death of his 
father, elder brother of the petitioner was appointed on 
compassionate ground under dying in harness rules. His 
brother too, passed away on 06.06.2010. It is an admitted 
fact that the brother of the petitioner who passed away on 
06.06.2010 was unmarried. The petitioner, now being the 
younger brother of the deceased claims appointment on 
compassionate ground under dying in harness rules. Since 
this has not been given to the petitioner, he filed the 
present writ petition before this Court. 
2. The Rules which are applicable in the case of the 
petitioner are the U.P. State Electricity Board Dyeing in 
Harness Rules, 1975 which have been adopted by the 
Uttarakhand Jal Vidyut Nigam Limited as well. Admittedly, 
since 06.08.2002, the definition of “Family” means 
“husband” or “wife” and “son” which would include the 
“adopted son” and “unmarried and widow daughter”, but 
there is no provision of “brother” in the definition. 
2015:UHC:19802 
3. In view thereof, since the petitioner is not 
included in the definition of “Family” under the Dying in 
Harness Rules, 1975. He is not liable for appointment on 
compassionate ground. 
4. The writ petition therefore, fails and is hereby 
dismissed. 
5. No order as to costs.
