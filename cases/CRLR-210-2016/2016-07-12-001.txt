CRLR No. 210 of 2016 
Hon’ble U.C.Dhyani, J.
Mr. Deep Chandra Joshi, Advocate, present 
for the revisionist. 
By means of present criminal revision, the 
revisionist seeks to set aside the impugned order 
dated 12.04.2016, passed by the Family Court 
Judge, Nainital in Criminal Case No. 50 of 2014, 
Smt. Chanda & another vs. Zameel Ahmad) under 
Section 125 Cr.P.C.. 
Issue notices to the respondent. Steps be 
taken within a week. 
After hearing learned counsel for the 
revisionist and having perused the impugned 
order, it is hereby directed, as an interim measure, 
that till the next date of listing, the effect and 
operation of the impugned order dated 12.04.2016, 
passed by the Family Court Judge, Nainital in 
Criminal Case No. 50 of 2014, shall remain stayed 
(for the balance amount), provided the revisionist 
pays a sum of Rs. 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty 
Thousand only) to the respondent within three 
weeks from today and also continues to pay 
Rs.2500/- (Rupees Two Thousand Five Hundred 
only) per month to the respondent by 10th of each 
month. 
List this matter after the notice is served upon 
the respondent. 
Stay Application (CRMA No. 1038 of 2016) 
stands disposed of. 
(U.C.Dhyani, J.)
