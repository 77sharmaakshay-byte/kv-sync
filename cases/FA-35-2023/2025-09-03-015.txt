COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
FA Nos. 33, 34 and 35 of 2023
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
(Through V.C.)
1. Mr. V.K. Kaparwan, learned counsel for the 
appellant.
2. Mr. Tarun Mohan and Mr. P.S. Bisht, learned Brief 
Holders for the State. 
3. Mr. V.K. Kohli, learned Sr. Advocate assisted by Mr. 
Sudhir Kumar and Mr. Tarun Lakhera, learned counsel for 
the private respondent.
4. These three first appeals have been preferred against 
the order passed by learned District Judge in Reference 
preferred under Section 18 of the Land Acquisition Act. 
5. According to the learned counsel for the appellant, 
the rate as determined by the Reference Court are not 
correct. 
6. These appeals are not admitted as yet since the same 
are time barred. 
7. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that before
preferring these first appeals, appellant preferred review 
application but the subsequently the same were returned. 
Order passed by the Reference Court has already been 
stayed subject to deposit of entire decretal amount and in 
compliance thereof, entire decretal amount has been 
deposited before the Executing Court. 
8. Mr. V.K. Kohli, learned Sr. Advocate for the private 
respondents submits that the amount towards the 
compensation, at the rate as admitted to the appellant, in 
the review application preferred before the learned District 
Judge may be released. 
9. Let counsel for both the parties may calculate the 
amount of compensation at the rate admitted to the 
appellant and inform to this Court on the next date.
10. List on 17.09.2025.
