COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
10.02.2023 WPSS No. 193 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Heard Mr. B.S. Negi, learned 
counsel for the petitioners, Mr. P.C. Bisht, 
learned Additional Chief Standing Counsel 
with Mr. M.S. Bisht, learned Brief Holder 
for the State and Mr. Nishant Krishna 
Adhikari, Advocate holding brief of Mr. 
Ramji Srivastava, learned counsel for 
respondent no.2 
Admittedly, in a similar matter 
(WPSB No. 169 of 2021) on 16.04.2021, 
this Court has directed the respondents to 
entertain and consider the applications of 
the petitioners of the said Writ Petition 
and to permit them to participate in the 
selection process for the post of Staff 
Nurse provisionally.
Learned counsel appearing for 
respondent no.2 fairly conceded that the 
present matter and the matter of WPSB 
No. 169 of 2021, are similar in nature.
Learned counsel for the 
respondents requested four weeks’ time 
to file counter affidavit.
Having heard the learned counsel 
for the parties, the respondent no.2 is 
directed to allow the petitioners to submit 
their forms and to permit them to participate in the selection process for the 
post of Staff Nurse provisionally.
It is clarified that this order shall 
be subject to final outcome of the present 
Writ Petition, and/or subject to further 
order(s), which may be passed in this 
petition.
Interim Relief Application (No.02 
of 2023) is disposed of accordingly. 
List this case along with WPSS No. 
149 of 2023.
(Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
