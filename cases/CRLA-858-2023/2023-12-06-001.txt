(1) Mr. Prateek Tripathi, learned counsel 
for the appellant.
(2) Mr. Sandeep Sharma, learned Brief 
Holder for the State of Uttarakhand.
(3) This is an appeal against conviction 
filed through legal-aid-counsel under 
Section 374 of Cr.P.C. against the judgment 
and order(s) dated 14/15.07.2023 rendered 
by learned Special Judge (POCSO) /
Additional District Judge, Dehradun in 
Special Session Trial No. 02 of 2020, 
whereby appellant has been convicted 
under Section 363 of I.P.C. and Section 6 of 
Protection of Children from Sexual Offences
Act. He has been directed to undergo 
rigorous imprisonment for three years under 
Section 363 I.P.C. with fine of ₹5,000/- and, 
in default of payment of fine, he was 
sentenced to undergo further fifteen days 
simple imprisonment; he has also been 
sentenced to undergo rigorous 
imprisonment for twenty years under 
Section 6 of POCSO Act with fine of 
₹10,000/- and, in default of payment of 
fine, he was sentenced to undergo further 
one month simple imprisonment. It was 
directed that all the sentences shall run
concurrently.
(4) As per Office report, there is a delay of 
48 days in filing the appeal. 
(5) Learned State Counsel submits that he 
does not want to file any objection to the 
delay condonation application.(6) The delay in filing the appeal is hereby 
condoned. Accordingly, delay condonation 
application (IA No. 1 of 2023) is allowed. 
(7) Also heard learned counsel for the 
appellant on the exemption application. 
(8) Learned counsel for appellant seeks 
exemption for filing translated copy of 
judgment, affidavit in the bail application, 
affidavit in support of delay condonation, 
court fee and memo.
(9) In the interest of justice, exemption 
application (IA No. 3 of 2023) is allowed. 
(10) Admit. 
(8) Summon the lower court record.
(9) List this case on 12.03.2023.
(8) In the meantime, State Counsel may 
file objection to the bail application, if any.
