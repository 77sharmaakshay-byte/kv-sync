By means of present writ petition, the 
petitioner seeks to issue writ in the nature of 
certiorari quashing the impugned orders dated 
04.05.2012 and 23.05.2012 both passed by

District Magistrate, Haridwar. 
Issue notices to the respondents. Steps be 
taken within a week. 
List after the notices are served upon the 
respondents.

land in question till the next date of listing. 
Stay application no. 12442 of 2015 stands 
disposed of.
