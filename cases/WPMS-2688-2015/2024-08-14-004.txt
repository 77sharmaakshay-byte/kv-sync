4. A Restoration Application (MCC /14165
/2024) has been filed by the petitioner to set 
aside the order dated 11.07.2024, whereby the 
writ petition was dismissed for want of 
prosecution.
5. Learned counsel for respondent no.1 as 
well as counsel for respondent no.2/1 has no 
objection to the same.

Application is allowed subject to the cost of 
₹2,000/- to be deposited by the petitioners in 
the account of the High Court Legal Services 
Committee within a period of three weeks from 
today.
7. Hence, the writ petition is restored to its 
original number along with interim order dated 
29.10.2015.
