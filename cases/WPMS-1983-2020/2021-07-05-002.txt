Heard learned counsel for the parties 
through video conferencing.
According to the petitioner, Chief 
Education Officer, Almora vide order dated 
28.09.2020 has granted approval to the 
new Committee of Management elected in 
2018, although, that very election was 
cancelled by him vide order dated 
31.12.2018.
Perusal of the order dated 31.12.2018 
indicates that the election process for 
constituting Committee of Management of 
the institution in question was cancelled on 
various grounds. The said order was passed 
by Chief Education Officer when this Court 
directed him to take decision on 
representation submitted by a member of 
the society, namely, Sri Nand Kishore 
Upreti.Perusal of the impugned order dated 
28.09.2020 reveals that Chief Education 
Officer has cancelled his own order passed 
on 23.09.2020, wherein it was provided that 
the election process for constituting 
Committee of Management has to be 
started afresh as per Scheme of 
Administration.
Since the Chief Education Officer had 
cancelled the election process by a reasoned 
order dated 31.12.2018, which was passed 
in terms of order of this Court and he had 
also provided for holding fresh elections in 
his subsequent order dated 23.09.2020, 
therefore, he could not have granted 
approval to the new Committee of 
Management which was elected on 
01.12.2018. Thus, this Court is prima-facie 
satisfied that a Committee of Management, 
whose election stood cancelled, cannot be 
permitted to manage the institution. 
Learned counsel for respondent no. 5 
prays for and is granted further two weeks’ 
time to file counter affidavit. The 
respondent no. 4 may also file counter 
affidavit within the same time. 
List this matter in the first week of 
August, 2021.
Till the next date of listing, effect and 
operation of the impugned order dated28.09.2020 passed by respondent no. 3 
shall remain stayed. 
Stay Application (CLMA No. 8985 of 
2020) stands disposed of.
