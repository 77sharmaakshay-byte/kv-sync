At the very outset, learned 
counsel for the private respondent 
would submit that the petition has 
rendered infructuous because the 
term of Committee of Management 
is for one year, which has already 
expired long back. 
Learned counsel for the 
petitioner would submit that there 
are serious issues involved in it.
What the petitioner has sought 
by way of this petition is quashing of 
an order dated 28.09.2020, by 
which approval was granted to the 
election of Committee of 
Management held on 01.12.2018. 
The Court wanted to know 
from the learned counsel for the 
petitioner that, what is the tenure of 
Committee of Management? He very 
fairly concedes that it is for one 
year, which means the Committee of 
Management for which approval was 
accorded on 28.09.2020 had already 
become redundant due to afflux of 
time.Learned counsel for the 
petitioner would submit that many 
things are still surviving in the 
matter because the members were 
illegally inducted and if further 
election held, they may caste their 
votes, but that is not the issue in 
this case.
List for final hearing on 
26.09.2023.
Interim order, if any, is 
vacated.
