COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
C-528 No.313 of 2026
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Sagar Kothari, learned counsel for 
the applicant.
2. Present C-528 application has been 
filed by the applicant seeking quashing of 
the impugned order dated 12.02.2026 
passed by the learned 3rd Additional Chief 
Judicial Magistrate, Dehradun in 
Complaint Case No. 4029 of 2020, under 
Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments 
Act, 1881, whereby the application dated 
22.10.2024 moved by the applicant has 
been rejected.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would submit that the entire criminal 
proceedings are unsustainable in the eyes 
of law. It is contended that the applicant 
had moved a detailed application dated 
22.10.2024 before the learned trial court 
seeking examination of the cheque in 
question by the State Forensic Science 
Laboratory on the specific ground that the 
date mentioned on the cheque appears to 
have been tampered with and manipulated. 
It is further submitted that the applicant 
had placed on record a photocopy of the 
original cheque allegedly handed over to 
respondent, mentioning interpolation in 
the date of the cheque. However, the 
learned trial court, without properly 
appreciating the said contention and the 
necessity of forensic examination, rejected 
the application vide the impugned order 
dated 12.02.2026.4. Learned counsel would further submit 
that upon receipt of the statutory notice 
under Section 138 of the Negotiable 
Instruments Act, the applicant consistently 
took the stand that the cheque in question 
had been issued merely as a security 
cheque and that the same has 
subsequently been altered and misused by 
the complainant by interpolating the date, 
therefore, the entire criminal proceeding is 
bad in law and same is liable to be 
quashed. 
5. A comparative perusal of the 
photocopy of the cheque allegedly handed 
over by the applicant to the respondent 
and the cheque filed along with the 
complaint under Section 138 of the N.I. Act 
would prima facie reveal variation in the 
date mentioned therein, suggesting 
apparent interpolation. 
6. Issue notice to respondent, returnable 
within six weeks.
7. Steps to be taken within one week.
8. List after service of notice upon 
respondents.
9. Meanwhile, as an interim measure, 
further proceedings of Complaint Case No. 
4029 of 2020 under Section 138 of the 
Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881 pending 
in the court of learned 3rd Additional Chief 
Judicial Magistrate, Dehradun shall remain 
stayed till the next date of listing.
10. Stay application (IA 1 of 2026) stands 
disposed of accordingly.
11. Objections, if any, be filed by the 
respondents in the meantime.
