Mr. V.D. Bisen, Advocate for the 
applicant. 
2. Applicant is aggrieved by the orders 
dated 17.05.2025 and 23.03.2026, whereby 
his application seeking setting aside of the 
ex-parte order has been rejected, and the 
Criminal Revision preferred thereagainst has 
also been dismissed.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that no notice of the aforesaid 
proceedings was ever duly served upon the 
applicant. It is contended that this fact has 
been specifically averred in the application 
filed under Order IX Rule 13 C.P.C. 
However, the Revisional Court, while 
dismissing the revision, has erroneously 
observed that the applicant had not stated 
the said fact in his affidavit, despite the 
same having been clearly pleaded in the 
application under Order IX Rule 13 C.P.C. It 
is further submitted that in the event the 
impugned orders are not set aside and the 
applicant is not afforded an opportunity to 
cross-examine the respondent and to 
adduce evidence in support of his case, the 
applicant shall suffer irreparable loss and 
grave prejudice.
4. Issue notice to the respondent, 
returnable at an early date.
5. List the matter after receipt of the 
service report. 
6. Considering the submissions advanced 
by learned counsel for the applicant, it is 
directed that, till the next date of listing, 
further proceedings of Misc. Criminal Case 
No.67 of 2025, pending before the Court of learned Additional Principal Judge, Family 
Court, Haldwani, District Nainital, shall 
remain stayed.
