perusal of which, it reveals that before the 
Counsellor both of them were agreed for 
reunion of their relationship and to live 
together and to give due regard to each 
other. The written undertakings are also the

her mother.

interaction it reveals that both of them are 
now fighting only for minor issues though 
the husband is always ready for resolving the 
matrimonial dispute and want to live 
together. He also filed a petition for the 
restitution of conjugal rights under Section 9 
of Hindu Marriage Act in March 2025 but the 
respondent-wife chooses to approach the 
police authorities.7. The report further reveals that there is 
lot of interference of their family members 
and there is also a communication gap in 
between both of them and it is apprised that 
only for six weeks they lived together. 
8. Put up this matter on 24.08.2026.
9. Interim order is made absolute. 
10. In the meantime, the parties may think 
over it and then come up with the fresh 
proposal and it is also advised to the family 
members of both the parties not to interfere 
in between both of them so that both of them 
in the meantime think over it and still their 
family members want to intervene they only 
intervene to settle their matrimonial issues 
amicably.
