COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
SABA No. 53 of 2025
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Ms. Ambika Vats, learned counsel with 
Mr. Junaid Ahmad, learned counsel for the 
applicants.
2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. with 
Ms. Rangoli Purohit, learned Brief Holder for 
the State.
3. Learned counsel for the applicants is 
not pressing the instant application in 
respect of applicant no.1 since she has 
already been arrested and now the instant 
second anticipatory bail application is being 
pressed only for applicant no. 2 and 3. The 
first bail anticipatory bail application was
dismissed as withdrawn with liberty to file 
afresh. 
4. Learned counsel for the applicant 
argued that the applicant no. 2 and 3 are 
the daughters of applicant no.1, they are 
Hindu by conversion. She further argued 
that the applicant no. 2 and 3 have no 
concern with regard to the marriage of the 
co-accused namely Monish with the victim. 
She further argued that the Monish is 
already granted bail by the trial court. 
5. In para-5 it is also contended that the 
applicant no. 2 and 3 are the step-sisters of 
co-accused Monish @ Manish and both of 
them are unmarried and pursuing their 
studies in Bachelor of Physiotherapy at 
Graphic Era University, Dehradun and other 
one completed Bachelor of Sociology and 
preparing for competition. 
6. It is contended that the First 
Information Report has been lodged by the 
complainant on 05.09.2025 bearing FIR No. 0162 of 2025, Police Station Nanakmatta, 
District Udham Singh Nagar implicating also 
both of them though they have no concerned
with the marriage of Monish @ Manish with 
the victim and now there is an apprehension 
of arrest, therefore, they may be granted 
some interim protection. 
7. Taking into consideration that the 
applicant no. 2 and 3 are the step-sisters of 
Monish @ Manish who according to the FIR is 
the main accused of marriage with the victim 
and further taking into consideration that 
applicant no. 2 and 3 are the students and 
pursuing their studies, this Court is of the 
view that both the applicants i.e. applicant 
no. 2 and 3 deserve for interim protection.
8. Let respondents may file counter 
affidavit within four weeks. Two weeks 
thereafter is granted to the applicant to file 
rejoinder affidavit.
9. Having heard the arguments of learned 
counsel for the parties, for an interim 
measure, without expressing any opinion on 
the merits of the case, in an event of arrest 
during investigation, the applicants shall be 
released on interim anticipatory bail subject 
to furnishing of their personal bond with two 
reliable sureties, each of the like amount, to 
the satisfaction of the arresting Officer 
subject to the following conditions:-
(i) The applicants shall cooperate 
with the investigation; 
(ii) The applicants shall not approach 
any witness in any manner, 
whatsoever. 
10. List this case on 06.01.2026.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
