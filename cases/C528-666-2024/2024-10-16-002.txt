Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Davesh Bishnoi, counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. G. S. Sandhu, learned Additional Advocate 
General assisted by Ms. Mamta Joshi, learned Brief 
Holder for the State.
3. This criminal misc. application under Section 
528 of B.N.S.S. is filed by the petitioner/accused to 
quash the judgment and order dated 09.09.2024 
passed by First Additional District Judge, Kashipur, 
District Udham Singh Nagar in Criminal Revision 
Nos. 139 & 140 of 2024 in C-528 No. 666 of 2024 
and C-528 No. 667 of 2024 respectively under 
Section 438 of B.N.S.S., the judgment and order 
dated 21.08.2024 passed by Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar in 
Criminal Case No. 4837 of 2022 under Section 500 
of IPC registered at P.S. Kashipur, District Udham 
Singh Nagar as well as the Application dated 
09.01.2024 filed by the respondent no. 2 to issue 
summons calling Sh. Veer Singh, the then Circle 
Officer, Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar as 
witness.
4. Counsel for the petitioner would submit that 
respondent no. 2 in C-528 No. 666 of 2024 is Bhabhi 
of the petitioner and respondent no. 2 in C-528 No. 
667 of 2024 is the real brother of the petitioner; that,
there is civil litigation between the parties regarding 
properties and just to pressurise the petitioner, the 
respondent no. 2 filed a false complaint of sexual
harassment against the petitioner, for which, an 
inquiry was conducted by the Department of the 
petitioner that resulted into absolving the petitioner 
from charges. 
5. He would further submit that during the course of the investigation on the complaint of sexual
harassment, the petitioner had given statements to the 
concerned Inquiry Officer/Circle Officer and after 
obtaining the copy of the statements of the petitioner
from the police department under the Right to 
Information Act, the respondent no. 2 in both the 
petitions, filed their separate complaint cases under 
Section 500 of IPC in which they summoned the 
Circle Officer as a witness and the same has been 
allowed.
6. Counsel for the petitioner would further submit
that in view of the fact that those statements were 
made in departmental inquiry before the Inquiry 
Officer, therefore, no offence of the defamation can 
be made out against the petitioner when the copy of 
the statements of the petitioner is available on record, 
therefore, there is no need for summoning the Circle 
Officer, hence this petition.
7. In view of the above, this Court is of the 
considered view that the matter needs deliberation.
8. Admit the petition.
9. Issue notice to the respondent no. 2 through 
ordinary process and registered post, 
acknowledgement due as well as by email and 
WhatsApp, if available.
10. Steps to be taken within three weeks.
11. Counter affidavit, if any, be filed within four 
weeks thereafter. Rejoinder affidavit, if any, be filed 
within four weeks thereafter.
12. List this matter on 03.03.2025.
13. Till further orders, further proceedings of 
Criminal Case Nos. 4837 & 4838 of 2022 in C-528 
No. 666 of 2024 and C-528 No. 667 of 2024 
respectively registered at P.S. Kashipur, District 
Udham Singh Nagar shall remain stayed.
