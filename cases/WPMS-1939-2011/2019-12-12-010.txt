Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. Ajay Veer Pundir, Advocate for

Mr. Rajendra Negi, Advocate, holding 
brief of Mr. Pankaj Purohit, Advocate for 
Uttar Pradesh Avas Vikas Parishad.
Heard learned counsel for the 
parties.
Learned counsel for the petitioners
submits that Uttar Pradesh Avas Vikas 
Parishad is not executing sale deeds in 
favour of the petitioners under the pretext 
that petitioners have not deposited the 
development charges, as demanded by 
Uttar Pradesh Avas Vikas Parishad. He 
further submits that petitioners have 
already deposited the entire price of the 
land, after borrowing money from friends 
and financial institutions, in the year 
2011. He, therefore, submits that, in 
order to utilize the land in question, 
petitioners are ready to deposit the 
development charges, as demanded by 
Uttar Pradesh Avas Vikas Parishad, in the 
bank account of Uttar Pradesh Avas Vikas 
Parishad, without prejudice to their rights 
and contentions in the writ petitions and 
the deposit so made by the petitioners 
shall be subject to final outcome of these 
writ petitions. 
Learned counsel appearing for Uttar 
Pradesh Avas Vikas Parishad has no 
objection to the proposal made by learned 
counsel for the petitioners. 
In view of the offer so made by the 
learned counsel for the petitioners, it is 
provided that if petitioners deposit the entire amount of development charges in 
the bank account of Uttar Pradesh Avas 
Vikas Parishad on or before 30.12.2019, 
Uttar Pradesh Avas Vikas Parishad shall 
execute sale deeds in favour of petitioners 
on or before 20.01.2020. This, however, 
will be subject to final outcome of these
writ petitions.
List these petitions in the month of 
February, 2020.
 (Manoj K. Tiwari, J.) Arpan 12.12.2019
