2. The State proposes to file an appeal against 
the judgment and order dated 08.10.2025, passed 
in Special Sessions Trial No.44 of 2023, State vs.
Vikki, by the court of learned Special Sessions 
Judge, District Chamoli (Gopeshwar). By it, the 
respondent has been acquitted under Sections 363, 
366, 376 of IPC and Section 3/4 of Protection of 
Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012. There is 
a delay of 118 days in filing the appeal.
3. Notices were duly served upon the 
respondent, and on behalf of the respondent, a 
parcha has already been filed by the learned 
counsel.
4. Heard learned counsel for the 
State/appellant on the delay condonation 
application (IA No.01 of 2026).
5. Having considered the grounds taken in the 
affidavit enclosed with the delay condonation 
application, this Court is of the view that there is a 
good ground to condone the delay, therefore, the 
delay in preferring the appeal is condoned and the delay condonation application stands allowed 
accordingly.
6. Admit. 
7. Call for LCR. 
8. Once the LCR is received, let the paper 
book be prepared and provided to the learned 
counsel for the parties as per Rules
9. List for hearing on 23.09.2026.
