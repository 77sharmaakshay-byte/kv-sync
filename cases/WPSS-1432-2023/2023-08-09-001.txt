COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 1432 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. T.A. Khan, learned senior counsel
assisted by Mr. Vinay Bhatt, learned counsel
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, learned counsel 
for the respondents.
3. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
4. By means of this writ petition, 
petitioner has prayed for following reliefs:
(i) Issue a writ, order of direction in the 
nature of mandamus commanding the 
respondents to re-engage the petitioner 
as daily wager on the same post, on 
which he was performing the duties till 
21.08.2009, with all consequential 
benefits of the back wages.
(ii) Issue a writ, order of direction in the 
nature of mandamus commanding the 
respondents to regularize the services of 
the petitioner, after his re-engagement 
and he may also be given the promotion 
like his juniors.
(iii) Issue a writ, order of direction in the 
nature of certiorari quashing the order 
dated 16.03.2023, passed by the 
respondent no.4 (annexure no.1 to the 
writ petition).
5. It is the case of the petitioner that the 
first information report was lodged against 
him in the year 2009, for the offence of 
cheating with the respondents-University.
6. It is submitted by learned senior 
counsel for the petitioner that charge-sheet 
was submitted under Sections 419, 420, 
467, 468 and 471 of IPC and he faced the 
trial, wherein, in the criminal appeal no.193 
of 2015, he was finally acquitted by the 
learned court of 3rd Additional Sessions 
Judge, Rudrapur vide judgment and order 
dated 21.08.2021.7. It is further submitted by learned 
senior counsel for the petitioner that after 
the acquittal of the petitioner was recorded, 
he moved a representation/application to 
the respondents/University to re-engage 
him, which was rejected vide impugned 
order dated 16.03.2023 (Annexure No.1 to 
the writ petition).
8. Thus, feeling aggrieved by the 
aforesaid rejection of his prayer, the 
petitioner is before this Court by filing writ 
petition.
9. Learned counsel for the respondents/ 
University prays for and is granted eight
weeks’ time for filing counter affidavit. Two 
weeks’ thereafter is granted for filing 
rejoinder affidavit.
10. List on 30.10.2023.
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
