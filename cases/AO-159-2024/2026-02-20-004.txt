Service is sufficient on the respondent nos.

The appellant proposes to challenge the 
judgment and order dated 06.02.20232, passed 
by the Employees’ Compensation 
Commissioner/Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Bageshwar, in ECA Case No.04 of 2020, Smt. 
Tara Devi Vs. DLM Van Nigam Bageshwar and 
Others.
The appeal is delayed. The Delay 
Condonation Application, IA No.2 of 2024, has 
been filed. 
Heard on the delay condonation 
application. 
It is the case of the appellant that the 
department was lately informed about the award, 
and due to procedural formalities, delay 
occurred. 
Since, there is no objection filed on the 
delay condonation application, this Court is of 
the view that there are good grounds to condone 
the delay. 
The delay in preferring the appeal is 
condoned.
The delay condonation application is 
allowed. 
Let call for the LCR. 
List this matter for final hearing on
