3. The complaint of the petitioners is that there is no 
compliance with the mandate of the Transfer Act, which 
mandate that the eligibility list shall be prepared and, 
thereafter, options will be called for on the posts of the 
vacancy. The post appears to be that of the District Level 
Officer i.e. Designated Officer.
4. In that view of the matter, the number of post and 
vacancies being minimal, the said exercise can be 
completed by the 2nd respondent within a limited window 
period.
5. Hence, we direct the 2nd respondent to carry out the 
exercise of preparing the eligibility list and the existing 
vacancies and post the same on the department’s website 
by 30.06.2025 and the options shall be exercised by all the 
eligible candidates within one week thereafter i.e. on or 
before 07.07.2025 and the orders shall be passed by 
11.07.2025.
6. List this case on 14.07.2025.
