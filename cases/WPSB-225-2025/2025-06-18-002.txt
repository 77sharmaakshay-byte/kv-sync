Mr. Harshit Sanwal, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. B.S. Parihar, learned Additional Chief 
Standing Counsel for the State.
3. Mr. M.C. Pant, learned counsel for respondent 
no.4.
4. It is the case of the petitioner that he was 
promoted on the post of Chief Agriculture Officer 
vide order dated 26.07.2024, and he was posted at 
Dehradun, while respondent no.4 was promoted on 
the post of Chief Agriculture Officer and she was 
given posting at Nainital. The averment made by the 
petitioner is that respondent no.4 did not join on her 
promoted post at Nainital, and thereafter, vide order 
dated 26.11.2024, she was attached at the 
Directorate of Agriculture, Dehradun. Now, by the 
impugned transfer order, the petitioner has been 
transferred from Dehradun to Nainital and 
respondent no.4 has been transferred from Nainital 
to Dehradun.
5. The allegations made by the petitioner are 
that he has not completed the requisite minimum 
four years’ service in an accessible area, as defined 
under Section 7(a) of the Uttarakhand Annual Transfer of Public Servants Act, 2017, and therefore, 
this transfer order could not be sustained. He has 
further alleged that this transfer order has been 
passed just to accommodate respondent no.4 by 
posting her at Dehradun.
6. We find force in the averments made by the 
petitioner.
7. Accordingly, the impugned transfer order is 
stayed.
8. It is further submitted by learned State 
Counsel that the petitioner has been relieved ex 
parte vide order dated 11.06.2025.
9. Since the impugned transfer order is an ex 
parte order and the petitioner has not handed over 
the charge, we permit the petitioner to resume his 
duties on his place of posting at Dehradun.
10. Mr. Pant, learned counsel for respondent no.4 
prays a week’s time to file counter-affidavit.
11. List on 10.07.2025.
