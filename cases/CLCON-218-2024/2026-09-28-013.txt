3. The subject matter of the present 
contempt petition is the order passed by 
Writ Court dated 14.09.2015 passed in 
WPMS No. 1192 of 2015, which was 
affirmed by the Division Bench of this Court 
and also by the Hon’ble Apex Court. 
4. From perusal of the record, it 
transpires that the Modification Application 
has been moved, after dismissal of the SLP,
before the Division Bench of this Court, that 
too was dismissed by the Division Bench of 
this Court. The Special Leave Petition was 
filed against the order, by which the 
Modification Application was rejected. The 
said SLP was dismissed by the Hon’ble Apex 
Court on 07.09.2026.5. After dismissal of the SLP against the 
order passed by the Division Bench of this 
Court in the Modification Application, this 
Court vide order dated 09.09.2026 expected
the contemnors to comply with the order 
without any further delay within two weeks.
6. But today, instead of compliance of the 
order, learned counsel appearing 
for the respondents/contemnors seeks 
adjournment on the ground that the review 
against the order dated 07.09.2026 before 
Apex Court has been filed on 25.09.2026 
which was given the Diary No.59745 of 
2026. 
7. From looking at the history of the case,
it appears that the respondents/contemnors
are adamant not to comply with the order 
passed by the Writ Court. By hook and 
crook they want the matter to linger on. The 
review petition against the dismissal of the 
modification is no remedy in the eyes of law. 
This Court is of the view that this is attempt 
to violate and circumvent the order passed 
by Writ Court. It is also denial of justice to 
petitioner. 
8. In such view of the matter, the 
respondent/contemnor No.1 shall remain 
present physically before this Court on 
06.10.2026 or comply with the order by 
06.10.2026.
9. Put up on 06.10.2026.
