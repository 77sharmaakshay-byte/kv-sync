 Heard on Interim Relief Application (IA) 
No.1 of 2025.
 The petitioners run Homoeopathic Medical College and Hospital in District 
Udham Singh Nagar. The petitioners 
aggrieved by the action of the respondent 
no.4, by which, the results of the students 
admit for the Academic Session 2019-20 
and 2020-21 has not declaring the result.
 Learned Senior Counsel for the 
petitioners would submit that when in the 
Academic Session 2019-20 and 2020-21 
students are admitted in the BHMS 
Course. There were no need of NEET 
counselling. Earlier also, when for the 
Academic Session 2021-22, the 
respondent University has obstructed the 
process of admission; the petitioners were 
compelled to file a writ petition bearing 
WPMS No.822 of 2022 (“the first petition”), 
in which, on 22.04.2022, the Court has 
passed the following order:-
 In the interregnum, we pass the 
following order 
(i) Respondent no. 2 will carry on the 
counseling for admission to the 
undergraduate courses in the homeopathy 
stream qua those candidates who have 
qualified the NEET along with other 
candidates under the prevailing regime. 
(ii) In case any surplus seats are left, the 
concerned institutions will be permitted to 
provisionally admit students in the 
Homeopathy stream qua undergraduate 
courses vis-a-vis academic year 2021-2022 
(iii) It is made clear that in case admission 
is granted to the petitioner, by the 
concerned institutions, by adopting the 
route as delineated in clause (ii) above–
their admission being provisional, they 
would not be allowed to claim any equities 
in case they fail in the writ petition. 
We have passed this order keeping in view 
the observations made by the Hon’ble High 
Court of Karnatka in Writ Petition No. 
100650 of 2021 on 31.08.2021 and the 
Hon’ble High Court of Delhi in the case of 
Priyanshu Undaviya and others vs. Union of India and others, in WP(C) 451 of 2022 
on 25.02.2022. 
 It is argued that for the Academic 
Session 2019-20, the students have 
already cleared Second Year Examination 
and their Third Year Examination Result 
has been withheld, which may obstruct 
their career prospects. Similarly, it is 
argued that for the Academic Session
2021-22, the students have already been 
enrolled by the University, their First Year 
Result has, in fact, is withheld despite the 
fact that they were admitted pursuant to 
the interim order passed by this Court in 
the first petition. 
 By means of the interim relief 
application, petitioners seek directions to 
the respondent University to declare the 
Third Year Result of the students enrolled 
in the Academic Session 2019-20 and also 
directing the respondent University to 
declare the result of the students enrolled 
in the Academic Session 2020-21. 
 During the course of arguments, 
reference has been made to the 
Regulations 4(A) made under the 
Homeopathy Central Council Act, 1973, 
which it is argued, were stuck down by the 
Hon’ble Karnataka High Court in Writ 
Petition No. 100650/2021 EDN-NED), 
which it is argued was followed by the 
various High Courts including this High 
Court in the first petition.
 It is also argued that the students are
enrolled by the respondent University. The 
University did not object at any point of 
time earlier and suddenly, this order has 
been passed for not declaring the result of 
the students, who have already undergone 
substantive period of the course. They are 
already enrolled with the University.  Learned counsel for the University 
would submit that at the relevant time, 
the Academic Session 2019-20 and 2020-
21, the counselling was necessary. 
Without counselling the students were 
admitted. 
 Having considered, as an interim 
measure, the respondent University is 
directed to declare the result of the 
students, who were admitted in the 
Academic Session 2019-20 as well as in 
the Academic Session 2020-21 and not to 
obstruct their studies. The final result of 
these students shall be subject to the 
outcome of this writ petition. 
 Interim Relief Application (IA) No.1 of 
2025 is disposed of accordingly.
