COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
(Short Term Bail Appl. No.04 of 2026)
In
CRLA No.132 of 2024
Harjinder Singh Sandhu
Vs.
State of Uttarakhand
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Vikas Anand, learned counsel with Ms. 
Gyan Mati Kushwaha, learned counsel for the 
Appellant/Applicant. 
2. Mr. Pramod Tiwari, learned AGA assisted by 
Mr. Vikas Uniyal, learned Brief Holder for the 
State.
3. Mr. Abhishek Verma, learned counsel and 
Ms. Aishwariya Thapliyal, learned counsel for the 
Complainant. 
4. Today the matter is listed on short term bail 
application. 
5. This is a criminal appeal filed by the 
appellant against the judgment and order dated 
06.03.2024 passed by learned FTC/Additional 
District and Session Judge/Special Judge (POCSO), 
Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar in Special 
Session Trial No.436 of 2019. By the said judgment 
under Section 9/10 of POCSO Act appellant was 
sentenced to undergo five years rigorous 
imprisonment alongwith fine of Rs.10,000/- and in 
default stipulation three months additional simple 
imprisonment; under Section 354 of IPC he was 
sentenced to undergo three years rigorous 
imprisonment alongwith fine of Rs.10,000/- and in 
default stipulation three months additional simple 
imprisonment was imposed upon him. 
6. Learned counsel for the appellant submits 
that marriage of Sehran Sandhu, son of the 
appellant, is to be solemnized on 03.05.2026 and 
the appellant being the father is required to be 
present in the marriage ceremony to look after the 
affairs and traditions. It is stated that appellant is in judicial custody since more than two years and two 
months and he was on bail during trial and he had 
never misused the same. It is requested that short 
term bail may be granted to him for attending the 
marriage ceremony of his son. 
7. Learned State counsel has no objection to the 
short term bail application. 
8. Having considered the submission of learned 
counsel for the parties, this Court is of the view that 
the appellant should be released on short term bail. 
Let the appellant-Harjinder Singh Sandhu be 
released on short term bail only for a period of 
eleven days i.e. from 26.04.2026 to 06.05.2026 
from the date of his actual release on executing a 
personal bond and furnishing two reliable sureties 
each of the like amount to the satisfaction of the 
court concerned.
9. On expiry of a period of eleven days of short 
term bail, the appellant shall surrender before the 
Superintendent of the jail concerned.
10. Short term bail application no.04 of 2026 
stands disposed of. 
11. List this bail application on 08.05.2026. 
 (Ashish Naithani, J.) 22.04.2026
