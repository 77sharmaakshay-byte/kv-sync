C528 No.37 of 2025
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Rajeev Sharma, learned counsel for the applicants. 
2. Mr. Vipul Panuly, Brief Holder for the State. 
3. By means of this C528 application, applicants have
challenged the order dated 20.09.2024, passed by the 
learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Kotdwar, Pauri 
Garhwal, in Criminal Case No. 2721 of 2024, State vs. 
Mamta Devi and another, under Sections 323 and 506 IPC, 
pending in the Court of learned Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Kotdwar, Pauri Garhwal as well as the entire 
proceedings of the aforesaid criminal case.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the applicants 
that the applicant no. 1 Smt. Mamta is wife of Jay Wardhan 
Singh. There is a family discord between the husband and 
wife. The husband wants to sell land situated in village 
Padampur Patti Sukhro, Kotdwar, District Pauri Garhwal to 
respondent no. 3 and when the applicant no. 1 intervened in
selling the land by her husband, a false and fabricated case
was lodged against the applicants. Learned counsel for the 
applicants submitted that she has not committed any mar￾peet as alleged in the FIR. It is contended by learned counsel 
for the applicants that in the FIR, general allegations were 
made and even there is no date and time mentioned. 
5. Per contra, learned counsel for the State submits that 
after lodging the FIR, investigation was conducted and 
charge-sheet was submitted against both the applicants 
under Sections 323 and 506 IPC. 
6. Having heard learned counsel for the parties, this Court 
feels that matter needs some scrutiny, for which, counter 
affidavit is necessary. 
7. Learned counsel for the State is directed to file counter 
affidavit within six weeks. Two weeks’ time, thereafter, is 
granted to the applicants to file rejoinder affidavit.
