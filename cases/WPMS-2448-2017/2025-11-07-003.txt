COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.2448 of 2017
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. M.S. Tyagi, learned senior counsel 
assisted by Mr. Sunil Chandra and Mr. 
Kailash Chandra, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Sudhir Kumar Nailwal, learned 
Standing Counsel for the State of 
Uttarakhand/respondent No.1.
3. Mr. V.K. Kaparuwan, learned counsel 
for applicant in Impleadment Application 
(IA/14457/2025).
4. An impleadment application has been 
moved by applicant-Muntiyaz on the 
premise that land subject matter of the 
writ petition was allotted to him, and 
therefore, he wants himself to be 
impleaded in the present proceedings as 
respondent.
5. Learned senior counsel for the 
petitioner filed objection to the said 
impleadment application, which is taken 
on record. Misc. Application (IA/14458/ 
2025) made therefor, is allowed.
6. Learned senior counsel for the 
petitioner objected to implead the 
applicant as party respondent on the 
ground that the proceedings for land in 
dispute which is allotted to the applicant is 
still pending before learned Commissioner 
Garhwal Mandal, therefore, impleadment 
application cannot be allowed.7. Having considered rival submissions 
made by learned counsel for the parties, 
this Court is of the view that unless and 
until the land allotted to the applicant￾Muntiyaz is cancelled by the learned 
Competent Authority, he has got every 
interest to contest the claim and to make 
any impleadment application where the 
said land is involved.
8. Accordingly, impleadment application 
(IA/14457/2025) is allowed.
9. Petitioner is directed to implead the 
applicant-Muntiyaz S/o Ali Hassan R/o 
Village Khempur, Pargana Manglaure, 
Tehsil Roorkee, District Haridwar, as 
respondent No.3 in the array of parties.
10. Let the necessary amendment be 
incorporated within a period of a week, by 
supplying the amended memo of parties.
11. Learned counsel for the newly 
impleaded respondent No.3 may file 
counter affidavit within four weeks.
12. Put up on 12.12.2025.
13. Interim order dated 04.10.2017 is 
extended till then.
