5. There is a delay of 750 days in filing the review 
application (MCC No. 469 of 2020). 
6. Subsequent to the order dated 16.08.2018 
passed by the Division Bench, the review applicants 
received notices for demolition on 03.09.2020. Only 
thereafter, they came to know of passing of this 
order. Hence, there is a delay in filing the review 
application. 
7. On considering the reasons assigned, we are of 
the considered view that the same constitutes 
sufficient cause for condoning the delay. Hence, the 
application (CLMA No. 8171 of 2020) seeking to 
condone the delay in filing the review application is 
allowed and the delay in filing the review application 
is condoned. 2 
8. Application No. 469 of 2020 is filed seeking to 
review the order dated 16.08.2016 passed by the 
Division Bench, in terms whereof, the writ petition 
was disposed-off with certain directions based on the 
interim order dated 26.08.2013 passed in Writ 
Petition (PIL) No. 25 of 2013. 
9. It is presently brought to our notice that the 
said writ petition was finally dismissed by the order 
dated 28.05.2015. 
10. Therefore, we are of the considered view that, 
since the earlier writ petition had been dismissed, 
the subsequent writ petition cannot be allowed based 
on the interim order passed in the earlier writ 
petition since the interim order would stand merged 
in the final order. Since the earlier writ petition has 
been finally dismissed, the order dated 16.08.2016 
requires to be reviewed. 
11. For the aforesaid reasons, the application (MCC 
No. 469 of 2020) is allowed. The order dated 
16.08.2016 is, therefore, reviewed and recalled. The 
writ petition is restored to file.
