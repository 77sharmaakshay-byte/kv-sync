Mr. Davesh Bishnoi, Advocate, for the

Mr. M.S. Bisht, Brief Holder, for the 
State.
The petitioner had attained the age of 
superannuation on 31.03.2019. Certain 
controversies pertaining to processing of his 
documents for the purposes of grant of 
pensionary benefits was made as the subject 
matter to be considered by this Court.
This Court, on 22.03.2021, has passed 
certain directions to be complied with by the 
petitioner, by presenting himself before the 
Management of the respondent College.
The learned counsel for the petitioner
Mr. Davesh Bishnoi, Advocate submits that in 
compliance thereto, the petitioner had already 
presented himself before the Management of the 
respondent College and has produced the 
relevant documents before them. But since 
when the matter was taken up today in the 
revised call, respondent’s counsel was not 
present.
In that eventuality, the learned counsel 
for the petitioner is not in a position to make 
any statement, as to whether the management of 
the respondent College has further processed 
the documents and forwarded the same to the 
office of the District Education Officer or not, 
for the purposes of determination of the retiral 
benefits of the petitioner. 
In that eventuality, learned Brief 
Holder for the State is directed to complete his 
instructions from the office of respondent No. 3, 
as to whether they are in the receipt of any communication from respondent No. 4 and if 
so, what decision has been taken by them on it. 
The said information would be supplied to the 
Court, on the next date fixed i.e. 24.04.2021.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
