Nos. 4 and 5. 
The petitioner, to the Writ Petition, had 
principally claimed for the arrears of salary after the 
grant of increment, allegedly due to be paid to him,
which he claims that he would be entitled to, in 
pursuance to the decision of respondent No.4, 
which was taken on 23rd January, 2020. 
As far as payment of pensionary benefits is 
concerned in pursuance to the previous orders 
passed by this Court, it is admitted by the learned 
counsel for the petitioner, that the pensionary 
benefits have already been made payable to the 
petitioner and he has already received the same, 
which will be continued to be remitted based on the 
last salary drawn by him as on 31st March, 2019. 
As far as petitioner’s principal relief 
pertaining to the entitlement of the increment is 
concerned, which is alleged to be wrongfully 
withheld by the Committee of Management, which 
had ultimately resulted into a reduction of the 
determination of the pensionary benefits and other 
service benefits too, for that purpose only, the Writ 
Petition is ‘admitted’. 
The Writ Petition is directed to be listed in 
the 3rd week of February, 2022. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
