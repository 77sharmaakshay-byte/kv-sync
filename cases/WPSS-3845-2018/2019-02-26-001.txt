Misc. Application No. 1873 of 2019 
filed along with supplementary affidavit by 
the petitioner is allowed. Supplementary 
affidavit is taken on record. 
The Coordinate Bench of this Court on 
16th November, 2018, have granted three 
weeks’ time to file counter affidavit to 
respondent Nos. 4 and 5. Till date, no counter 
affidavit has been filed by respondent Nos. 4 
and 5 and the petitioner is on the verge of his 
retirement. 
The argument, as extended by the 
learned counsel for the respondents is that the 
records are not retrievable. Since the matter 
pertains to 2010, some more time is required. 
This cannot be an excuse for delayed filing of 
the counter affidavit. Being an employer, it is 
expected that respondent Nos. 4 and 5 are to 
be equipped with the records of the petitioner 
/ employee. 
Learned counsel for respondent Nos. 4 
and 5 prays for and is granted two weeks and 
no more time to file their counter affidavit. 
List on 18th March, 2019.
