The petitioner has filed Amendment 
Application, seeking amendment in the 
pleadings, grounds, as well as in the relief 
clause, on account of subsequent event, which 
has developed. 
The matter was taken up on previous 
date, i.e. 14th August, 2020, in order to enable 
the learned counsel for the respondents to 
decide as to whether he proposes to file 
objection or not. 
When the matter was taken up today, 
the learned counsel for the respondent Nos. 4 
and 5 submits that he does not propose to file 
any objection. 
Consequently, the Amendment 
Application is allowed. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner is 
directed to supply the amended memo of Writ 
Petition within a period of two weeks from 
today. 
The learned counsel for the respondents 
may file their counter affidavit to the amended 
Writ Petition within a period of three weeks 
thereafter. 
List this Writ Petition after five weeks.
