Prior to attaining the age of 
superannuation, the petitioner was called 
upon by the Principal Kisaan Intermediate 
College, Peerumadara, District Nainial, vide 
his communication on 18.12.2018 to fulfill 
the certain documentary requirements for 
the purposes of processing his pensionary 
papers. The petitioner contends that by 
virtue of the response of 07.05.2019, he 
has supplied those documents.
But further the counsel for the 
respondents submits that after attaining 
the age of retirement, the petitioner was 
yet again called upon by the respondents to 
complete the additional formalities, which 
were necessarily required to be fulfilled 
after attaining the age of retirement, which 
according to the respondents has not been 
fulfilled by the petitioner. There is no such 
document on record, as such, placed by the 
respondents to show that they have called 
upon the petitioner and he has not 
responded to it.
The counsel for the respondents is 
directed to place the said communication 
on record, in order to enable the Court to 
pass further orders for the purposes of 
processing his pensionary papers. The 
same would be complied with by the counsel for the respondents within a period 
of two weeks.
List this writ petition on 10.03.2021.
