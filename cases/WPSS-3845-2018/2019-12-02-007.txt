Mr. Sudhir Kumar, Advocate for private 
respondent(s).
The petitioner, who has now a superannuated teacher, 
has filed this writ petition, claiming for certain arrears of 
salary and hence, he has modulated his reliefs in the 
following manner:-
“i) Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing the respondents to 
grant the overdue claims of petitioner’s salary from 
the date when the same fell due; pay the arrears;
ii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing the respondents to 
calculate and pay the claims of petitioner’s salary 
from the date when the same fell due, as arrears, 
along with interest thereon;”
Apart from various factual aspects as involved 
adjudication, he has contended that when he was in service, 
he had raised a claim with regards to the payment of salary 
for the aforesaid defaulted period by filing a representation 
before the management, supported by the recommendations 
made by the Chief Education Officer, one of the reference, 
the same finds place in his representation dated 24.09.2018 
made before the Manager, where he has referred a 
communication, made by the Chief Education Officer dated 
13.08.2018.
Before venturing into considering the main relief, by 
way of a writ of mandamus, this Court is of the opinion that 
it wZxcould be apt to direct the petitioner to file a detailed 
representation with all the recommendation(s) and 
communication(s), as made by the Education Authority and 
submit the same before the Manager concerned within a 
period of two weeks from today. He can file a representation 
in relation to the salary in dispute apart from any other dues
payable to him. 
The Manager concerned is directed to take a decision 
on the same within a period of one month thereafter and 
place the said order on record of the present writ petition.
List this matter after six weeks.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
