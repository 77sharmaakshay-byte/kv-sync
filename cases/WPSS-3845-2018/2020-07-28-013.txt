Misc. Application No. 6669 of 2020, along 
with supplementary affidavit filed by the 
petitioner is taken on record. 
Misc. Application No. 6669 of 2020, 
accordingly stands disposed of. 
During the course of argument, what is 
revealed is that the petitioner has contended that 
despite of fulfilling all the formalities for 
remittance of the pensionary benefits, the same 
has been denied by the order dated 23rd January, 
2020, by the respondents on the pretext that the 
petitioner has not fulfilled the conditions. 
Since the petitioner’s argument is that he 
has fulfilled the conditions and the reason for 
rejection is erroneous, in such an eventuality, the 
petitioner would have to challenge the order dated 
23rd January, 2020, by seeking an appropriate 
amendment along with appropriate pleadings, for 
which, the learned counsel for the petitioner prays 
for that he may be granted a week’s time. 
The said prayer is granted. 
He may file an appropriate application as 
permissible under law. 
List this Writ Petition thereafter.
