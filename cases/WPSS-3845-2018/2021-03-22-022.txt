COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.3845 of 2018
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Davesh Bishnoi, Advocate, for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. M.S. Bisht, Brief Holder, with 
Mr. Gajendra Tripathi, Brief Holder, for the State 
of Uttarakhand/respondent nos.1 to 3.
Mr. Sudhir Kumar, Advocate, for the 
respondent nos.4 and 5.
This Court on the previous dates had sought 
certain queries, which is to be provided by the
respondents. 
Today, when the matter was taken up for 
final adjudication, the learned counsel for the 
respondent nos.4 and 5 Mr. Sudhir Kumar, had 
submitted that the Principal of the Intermediate 
College, in which, the petitioner was initially 
working and from where he has attained the age of 
superannuation and retired from service. On 
31.12.2019, the Principal of the Intermediate 
College has written to the petitioner to approach to 
his office to clarify certain defects, which the 
Principal was facing for the purpose of processing 
his application for grant of the pensionary 
benefits. 
Learned counsel for the respondents has 
submitted that the nature of observations made in 
the communication dated 15.11.2019, required the
verification, for which the personal appearance of 
the petitioner and his wife, were required to 
appear before the Management, in order to enable 
the Management to process the application of the 
petitioner for pensionary benefits. 
Mr. Sudhir Kumar, learned counsel for the 
College submits that instead of appearing in 
person before the Management, the petitioner did respond to the aforesaid letter vide his 
communication dated 06.12.2019, but that in itself
would not suffice, the purpose for which, the
Letter No.169 dated 15.11.2019, was issued by the 
Principal, because of the absence of the personal 
appearance of the petitioner and his wife, the 
documents cannot be processed, because other 
codel formalities are to be completed. 
To tied-away such peculiar circumstances 
and considering the fact that the petitioner has 
attained the age of superannuation and retired 
from service on 31.12.2019, and is claiming for 
the grant of pensionary benefits, an exception is 
carved out in the following manner:-
(1) That the petitioner would ensure to appear 
before the Management of the respondent 
College, along with his wife on 05.04.2021, 
to complete the formalities. 
(2) The respondent College and its 
Management is directed to fulfil all the 
formalities of processing the documents for 
pensionary benefits, including the 
verification of the petitioner and his wife, 
which is required for processing pensionary 
documents. 
(3) As soon as the said process is over, the 
Management would ensure to forward the 
papers/documents of the petitioner further 
to the superior authorities for the purpose of 
processing of pensionary benefits of the 
petitioner within a period of one week 
thereafter.
List this writ petition on 19.04.2021.
