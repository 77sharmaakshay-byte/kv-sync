Though earlier the time was already 
granted to the respondents’ Counsel to file a 
counter affidavit, to the amended Writ Petition, 
but the same has not yet been filed, since 
August, 2020. 
Today, the learned counsel for the 
respondents prays for that he may be granted 
two week’s further time, to file counter affidavit 
to the amended Writ Petition. 
The prayer as sought for is strongly 
opposed by the counsel for the petitioner, but, 
however, in the interest of justice, the time 
prayed for is granted to the respondents by way 
of a last opportunity. 
He may file the counter affidavit within a 
period of two weeks from today 
The Writ Petition is directed to be listed 
in the week commencing 14th December, 2020. 
Accordingly, the Urgency Application 
would stand disposed of.
