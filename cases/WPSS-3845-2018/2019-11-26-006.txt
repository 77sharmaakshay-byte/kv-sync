This Court is of the tentative view that 
pendency of the Writ Petition in relation to 
the reliefs claimed therein will not have any 
bearing with regard to the post retiral dues 
and other pensionary benefits, which 
otherwise the petitioner is entitled to receive 
after his attainment of age of superannuation 
on 31st March, 2019. 
In relation thereto, the learned counsel 
for the respondent Nos. 4 and 5 seeks three 
days’ time to seek instructions from the 
respondents as to what are the reasons behind 
it to curtail the post retiral dues. 
Put up this matter on 2nd December, 
2019.
