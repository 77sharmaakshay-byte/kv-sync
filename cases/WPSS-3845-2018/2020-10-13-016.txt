Today the Writ Petition is listed on the 
Correction Application No. 8361 of 2020, as 
filed by the petitioner, seeking to rectify 
certain typographical error, which has chanced 
in the amendment application, which already 
stood allowed by the order of this Court. 
There cannot be any correction as such 
in an amendment application, which already 
stood allowed. 
Hence, this application is being directed 
to be treated as an amendment application, in 
a prior Amendment Application No. 5959 of 
2020. 
The Application is allowed. 
The petitioner is directed to carry out 
the necessary amendment, in the amendment 
application itself, and thereafter, rectify the 
amendment, which he has already made in the 
Writ Petition. 
The entire exercise is directed to be 
completed within a period of two weeks from 
today.
