COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.3845 of 2018
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Davesh Bishnoi, Advocate, for the 
petitioner.
Mr. P.C. Bisht, Additional CSC, for the 
State of Uttarakhand. 
The petitioner had attained the age of 
superannuation from the services of the 
respondent on 31.03.2019. 
During the pendency of this writ petition, 
an issue was raised by the petitioner with 
regards to the non settlement of the retiral 
benefit, which was being deprived to him. But 
later on, the facts which has been placed on 
record by the respondent, were that they have 
submitted, that the Committee of Management
had already processed the documents of the 
petitioner for the remittance of the retiral 
benefits, including the pension, and the same 
has already been referred to the Chief Treasury 
Officer, but since the Chief Treasury Officer 
was sitting over it, and no decision has been 
taken, the matter was placed today with an 
observation made by this Court in the order 
dated 15.11.2021, directing the learned 
Additional CSC to complete his instructions; as 
to what action has followed for the purposes of 
the remittance of the retiral benefits to the petitioner, as it continued to be determined on 
the basis of the observations made by this Court 
in its order dated 19.04.2021, onwards. 
Learned Additional CSC has submitted 
that on the basis of the instructions, which he
has received from the office of the Chief 
Education Officer, Nainital by way of his 
communication No.8086, dated 17.11.2021.
The documents which was sent to the Chief 
Treasury Officer by the Management of 
Institution of petitioner for processing the 
retiral benefits of the petitioner. There were
certain objection, which were raised by the 
office of the Chief Treasury Officer vide its 
communication dated 30.10.2021. It is stated by 
the Chief Education Officer, that those defects 
had now been rectified and the documents of 
the petitioner for the purposes of the remittance
of the retiral benefits has been referred back to 
the office of Chief Treasury Officer, to be 
processed in accordance with law, but no 
decision as such has been taken so far after the 
rectification of the defects, which has been 
pointed by the communication of 30.10.2021.
In that eventuality, while keeping this 
writ petition pending, and considering the 
response received by the office of the Chief 
Standing Counsel on 17.11.2021, I am of the 
view, that in view of the rectification of the 
errors which was pointed by the Chief Treasury Officer, since they now stand rectified in fact 
there could not be any practical impediment for 
the Chief Treasury Officer, now to process the 
documents of the petitioner for the purposes of
remittance of the retiral benefits.
For the reasons aforesaid, it is hereby 
directed that the Chief Treasury Officer in 
continuation to the communication made by the 
Chief Education Officer on 30.10.2021, would 
ensure to complete the documentation and 
process the retiral benefits and pension of the 
petitioner within a period of ten days’ from 
today and inform the Court accordingly. 
Put up this writ petition after ten days. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
