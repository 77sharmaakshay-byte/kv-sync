Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
in compliance of the judgement, as passed by the 
Division Bench of this Court on 18.07.2018, he 
represented his claim before the respondent(s) for the 
payment of the benefit, as prayed for by him in the 
writ petition on 01.08.2018.
During the course of the argument, the learned 
counsel for the petitioner informs that on the basis of 
said representation, the respondents had issued an 
order dated 23.01.2020, assigning reasons for their 
inability to pay the amount.
This Court is of the view that the said document 
is to be brought on record, in order to judiciously 
scrutinise the reasons for denial, for which the learned 
counsel for the petitioner prays a week’s time to place 
the same document on record.
The same is granted.
Learned counsel for the petitioner to place the 
said document on record either by way of 
supplementary affidavit or by way of an amendment, 
challenging the same as the case may be.
List this matter after one week.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
