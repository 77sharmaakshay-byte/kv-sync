 Mr. S.S. Adhikari, A.G.A. with Mr. 
P.S. Uniyal, Brief Holder for the State. 
 Heard on the bail application. 
 It is the contention of learned 
counsel for the appellant that the 
appellant was on bail during trial and he 
has never misused the liberty of bail 
granted to him. 
 Having considered the submission 
of learned counsel for the appellant, 
without expressing any opinion as to the 
final merits of the case, I am of the view 
that the appellant should be enlarged on 
bail during the pendency of present 
criminal appeal. 
 Accordingly, bail application is 
allowed. 
 Let the appellant Himendra Singh 
Rautela be released on bail on his 
executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two sureties each of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of court 
concerned. 
 It is directed that the Magistrate, 
while taking personal bonds, shall 
obtain photographs of the appellant and 
his identity proof on the personal bonds. 
While taking sureties, the Magistrate 
shall ensure that the latest photographs 
of the sureties along with their proper 
identity proof be affixed. The Magistrate 
shall also obtain duplicate copy of the 
papers regarding personal bonds and 
sureties and shall send one set of the same to this Court within a period of 15 
days so as to keep the same in the file of 
appeal.
