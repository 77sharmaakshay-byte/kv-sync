In this appeal against conviction, the 
appellant has filed IA No.05 of 2025 for 
Miscellaneous Application for taking on 
record certain Government Orders, etc. 
State was required to file objections to 
it. But today, learned State Counsel 
submits that no objection is required to be 
filed because whatever documents are 
proposed to be filed, they are Government 
records only.
Heard on the miscellaneous 
application.
Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that, in fact, the date of incident, 
according to the prosecution in the instant 
case, is 24.07.2021, which was in the midst 
of COVID-19 pandemic, when curfew was 
clamped in most parts of the State of 
Uttarakhand. He submits that those 
documents are required to be perused so as 
to appreciate the evidence in the instant 
matter.
State has no objection in taking on 
record those documents. Therefore, the 
miscellaneous application is allowed.
In fact, the documents that are filed 
are not additional evidence. They are 
Government Orders. They may be referred 
to while hearing the appeal.List this matter on 28.04.2026, for 
final hearing.
