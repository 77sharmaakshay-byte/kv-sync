Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Ms. Soniya Chawla, learned counsel for the 
applicant. 
2. Present applicant is challenging the 
proceedings of Criminal Case No. 182 of 2025 
(Archana Gautam Vs. Shivam Sagar and others)
pending in the court of learned Judicial Magistrate, 
Kashipur, District – Udham Singh Nagar wherein 
the present applicant has been summoned to face 
the trial for the offences punishable under Sections
85, 115 (2), 353, 351 (2) BNS and under Section 3 / 
4 of the Dowry Prohibition Act. 
3. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
applicant that applicant and respondent married in 
the year 2021 and there is no issue from this 
marriage and both the parties are living separately. 
She further submits that applicant in order to 
restore his married life has filed a petition under 
Section 9 of the Hindu Marriage Act for restitution 
of their conjugal rights and he is ready to restore 
his matrimonial life. 
4. Issue notice to respondent by all modes 
including whatsapp and email. 
5. Steps be taken within a week.
6. List on 29.07.2026.
7. On the next date, both the parties shall 
remain present before this Court in order to settle their dispute amicably. 
8. Taking into consideration that dispute 
between the parties is matrimonial one and present 
applicant wants to restore his married life, this 
Court is of the view that efforts may be made for 
their amicable settlement, therefore, as an interim 
measure, further proceedings of Criminal Case 
No. 182 of 2025 (Archana Gautam Vs. Shivam 
Sagar) pending in the court of Judicial Magistrate, 
Kashipur, District – Udham Singh Nagar is stayed. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
