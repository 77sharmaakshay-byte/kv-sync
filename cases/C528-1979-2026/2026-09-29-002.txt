3. The instant application is preferred under 
Section 528 of BNSS, 2023 by the present applicant 
– Jaipal singh Palani, for challenging the order 
dated 18.10.2024, passed by the District Magistrate, 
Champawat in Criminal Case No.1 of 2023, by 
invoking Section 14 (1) of Uttar Pradesh Gangsters 
and Anti-Social Activities (Prevention) Act, 1986, 
whereby, the motor bike of the applicant registered 
as UK01 C 0592 (Royal Enfield Classic 350) is seized 
on the ground that the applicant could not disclose 
about the source of his income for depositing the 
monthly EMI of Rs.12696/-, and as such the 
amount deposited towards the EMI is the proceeds 
of crime. Being aggrieved with the order of the 
District Magistrate dated 18.10.2024, the applicant 
preferred an appeal i.e. Criminal Appeal No.45 of 
2024, which is also dismissed by the learned 
District Judge by upholding the order of District 
Magistrate.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant argued that 
the finding as recorded by the District Magistrate 
as well as by the learned District Judge, are
completely unreasonable and perverse because the 
applicant obtained a loan of Rs.1,15,000/- from 
“Indostar Capital Finance Company” on 24.08.2018 
and as per the loan agreement, the applicant has to repay the entire loan amount in between 15.09.2018 
to 20.07.2022 on monthly instalment of Rs.12,696/-
which is already paid finally in the year of 2019.
5. On perusal of the order of the District 
Magistrate what this Court observed that on 
absurd ground District Magistrate passed the order 
and it is further strange that even the learned 
District Judge have not examine this aspect that 
after getting loan from the Finance Company the 
applicant purchased the bike and make full and 
final payment to the Finance Company and how 
such an amount can be a proceed of crime. 
6. Admittedly, the applicant took a loan from a 
Finance Company to purchase motor bike, and EMI 
was also fixed and he has already paid entire loan 
amount and the last EMI was paid on 29.06.2019, 
despite this after more than four years, the District 
Magistrate pass the order impugned. At least the 
District Magistrate has to apply it’s mind before 
passing an order. The loan which was cleared in 
the year 2019 the District Magistrate passing the 
order after four years, this is really very surprising 
and now the learned District Judge also upheld the 
finding recorded by the District Magistrate. All 
these aspects need scrutiny. 
7. Learned State Counsel prays for and is 
granted four weeks’ time to file counter affidavit. 
Two weeks’ time is granted to the learned counsel 
for the applicant to file rejoinder affidavit.
8. List this matter in the week commencing 
23.11.2026.
9. In the meantime, the District Magistrate, 
Champawat, is directed to release the vehicle of the 
applicant forthwith.
