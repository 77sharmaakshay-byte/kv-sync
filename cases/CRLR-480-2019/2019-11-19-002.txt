 The revision is listed today on the 
delay condonation application and it is 
reported by the Registry that there is a delay 
of 20 days in filing the revision challenging 
the judgment dated 27.04.2019 as rendered 
by the learned Family Court while passing 
an order on an application under Section 125 
of Cr.P.C. 
After having considered the grounds 
taken in the delay condonation application 
and also considering the fact that the 
controversy arises out of a matrimonial 
dispute and also the fact that the respondent 
do not propose to seriously oppose the delay 
condonation application, the same would 
stand allowed. 
The revision is directed to be listed 
next week for hearing on admission.
