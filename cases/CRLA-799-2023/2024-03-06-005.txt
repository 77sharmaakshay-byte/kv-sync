COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRLA No. 799 of 2023
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J.
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
(1) Mr. Nagesh Agarwal, Advocate 
holding brief of Mr. Rajendra Singh Azad, 
learned counsel for the appellant.
(2) Mr. R.K. Joshi, learned Brief Holder
General for the State of Uttarakhand.
(3) This appeal is filed under Section 374 
of Cr.P.C. against the judgment dated 
01.11.2023 and sentence order dated 
03.11.2023 rendered by learned 2nd
Additional Sessions Judge, District 
Haridwar in Sessions Trial No. 29 of 2017, 
whereby appellant has been convicted 
under Sections 302/120-B of I.P.C. She 
has been directed to undergo 
imprisonment for life under Section 
302/120-B I.P.C. with fine of ₹5,000/-
and, in default of payment of fine, she was 
sentenced to undergo further two months
simple imprisonment. It was directed that 
all the sentences shall run concurrently.
(4) Heard learned counsel for the parties 
on the bail application (IA No. 1 of 2023) 
moved on behalf of appellant – Pooja and 
perused the record.
(5) On the last date, State Counsel was 
directed to file objection to the bail 
application of appellant – Pooja. Today, 
objection is on record. 
(6) It is submitted by learned counsel for 
the appellant that this is no case in itself 
and the appellant was only implicated in the aforesaid crime for the reason that she 
was in constant telephonic conversation 
with Mukaram (co-accused). 
(7) As per prosecution case, First 
Information Report was lodged by Sarafat 
- brother of co-accused (Mukaram) 
alleging therein that his brother -
Mukaram S/o Asraf left the village for his 
job in Roshnabad; on 19.09.2016 at 06:30 
pm, he was informed that dead body of 
Mukaram was found in Piran Kaliyar and 
the body was identified by him from his 
trouser & identity card and other 
documents. Subsequently, in the 
investigation it was found that Mukaram 
was alive and unclaimed dead body was of 
unknown person. 
(8) So far as the case of present 
appellant is concerned, there is no 
evidence on record to implicate her with 
the aforesaid crime and there was no 
motive against her to commit murder of 
an unknown person. 
(9) It is argued by learned counsel for 
the appellant that appellant is in custody 
since 01.11.223 and she is having three 
children, including a six months baby. 
(10) Per contra, learned State Counsel 
submits that the appellant was involved in 
the conspiracy to kill an unknown person 
and she was later on arrested in the 
company of Mukaram (co-accused). 
(11) Having considered the rival 
submission of the parties, especially on 
the ground that appellant is a lady, having 
three children, including a six months 
baby, without going into the merits of the 
case, we feel that appellant is entitled to 
be released on bail.(12) Accordingly, Bail Application (IA No. 
1 of 2023) is allowed. Let the appellant –
Pooja be released on bail during the 
pendency of this appeal on her executing 
a personal bond and furnishing two 
reliable sureties each of the like amount to 
the satisfaction of the Court concerned. 
(13) List this case in due course.
