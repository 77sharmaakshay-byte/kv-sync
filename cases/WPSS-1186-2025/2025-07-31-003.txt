Heard on interim relief application (IA 
No.1 of 2025).
It is the case of the petitioner that he 
retired as a Junior Engineer on 30.04.2024, 
but till date his pension has not been 
released.
Learned counsel for the respondent 
nos. 3 & 4 was required to get instructions.
He would submit that when the 
petitioner posted, in an electricity 
connection, some instruments were 
installed, which caused theft to the 
electricity and the matter is pending before the authority. He would submit that 
provisional gratuity and provisional pension 
would be released to the petitioner within 
two weeks from today.
The petitioner claims that he may be 
provided pensionary benefits forthwith. 
That would be granting final relief. That 
cannot be granted. But, the Court directs 
the respondent nos. 3 & 4 to release the 
provisional pension and provisional gratuity 
in favour of the petitioner within two weeks 
from today. In case, it is not paid within two 
weeks from today, the petitioner shall be at 
liberty to make a mention.
Interim relief application stands 
disposed of accordingly.
