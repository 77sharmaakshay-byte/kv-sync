In the present appeal, the plaintiff and the 
defendant are brothers and there is a dispute with 
regard to partition of the property. Before the 
Court proceed the matter on merit, let an 
opportunity be given to the party to reconcile the 
matter between themselves through mediation. 
Therefore, the Court directs the parties, namely, 
the appellant and the defendant to appear before 
the mediation and conciliation center on 5th July, 
2010 for possible settlement. 
Let the matter be listed after the report of 
the mediation / conciliation center.
