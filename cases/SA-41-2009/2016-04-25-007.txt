Having heard learned Sr. Counsel 
for the parties, it is evident that Mr. 
J.M. Singhal was the Counsel of the 
plaintiff in the trial court as well as 
before the first appellate court. First 
appeal was preferred by the plaintiff 
himself. Mr. Singhal had never filed his 
Vakalatnama on behalf of the 
defendant/respondent. Therefore, 
substantial question of law, formulated 
in this regard vide order dated 
29.7.2009, appears to have been framed 
under some misconception. Hence, the 
said substantial question of law is 
hereby recalled and, instead, following 
questions are framed for adjudication: 
(i) Whether once the registered 
document is not set aside by any 
competent court of law, the same should 
be read or relied as it is or not? 
(ii) Whether both the courts below 
erred in law in not accepting the 
concept of Joint Hindu Family regarding 
the joint property recorded in various 
records? 
If the need is felt at the time of 
arguments, additional substantial 
question(s) of law may be framed. 
Order dated 29.7.2009, passed by 
this Court, stands modified to the extent 
indicated above. The application moved 
therefor, on behalf of the respondent, 
stands disposed of accordingly.
