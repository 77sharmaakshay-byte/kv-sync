This is Modification Application No.
13515 of 2011 moved on behalf of the third
party applicant Vijendra Kumar Goyal for
modification of interim order dated
29.07.2009, passed by this court.
Heard. 
The applicant Vijendra Kumar Goyal is
not a party in the appeal. Perusal of the
interim order dated 29.07.2009, clearly
shows that it is directed that the respondent
(in the appeal) i.e. Ram Niwas Goyal, shall
not create any third party interest in respect
of 50 percent share in the property
purchased through the sale deed dated
23.12.1976.
The interim order, in the above
circumstances, does not bind the third party.
Accordingly, the Modification Application
No. 13515 of 2011 is disposed of with the
clarification that the interim order passed in
this appeal is not directed against the third
party/applicant Vijendra Kumar Goyal.
