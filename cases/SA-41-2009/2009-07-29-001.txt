SA No. 41 of 2009 
Hon'ble Prafulla C. Pant, J.
 
Heard Sri Sharad Sharma, Senior Advocate, assisted by Sri C.S. 
Rawat, learned counsel for the appellant and perused the impugned 
judgment and decree dated 30.04.2009, passed by the first appellate 
court (Additional District Judge / Fast Track Court V, Dehradun), in 
Civil Appeal No. 5 of 2008. 
Admit the appeal on following substantial question of law:- 
Whether the act on the part of the counsel (Sri J.M. Singhal) who 
appeared earlier on behalf of appellant (plaintiff) in the suit and 
thereafter the said counsel appeared on behalf of respondent at the 
stage of first appeal, vitiated the entire proceedings before the first 
appellate court? 
Issue notices to respondent-Ram Niwas Goel. 
Having heard learned counsel for the appellant and after going 
through the impugned judgment and decree passed by the courts 
below, as an interim measure it is directed that respondent shall not 
create third party interest in respect of the 50% share in the property 
purchased through registered sale deed dated 23.12.1976. (Stay 
Application No. 6450 of 2009 stands disposed of). 
Summon the Lower Court Record. 
List after Lower Court Record is received.
