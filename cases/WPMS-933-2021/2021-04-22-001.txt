Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
(Through Video Conferencing)
Mr. Navneet Kaushik, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. J.S. Bisht, Standing Counsel for the State 
of Uttarakhand. 
Mr. V.K. Kapurwan and Mr. Sanjay Bhatt, 
Advocate for the respondents. 
The precise grievance which has been raised 
by the petitioner in the present Writ Petition is that,
as against the respective quota of seats available to 
be filled in, in the petitioner’s institution, as against 
the State Quota seats in the BAMS course, there are 
certain seats, which are still unfilled. The petitioner 
proposes to fill in those seats from amongst those 
candidates, who have qualified their NEET, only as 
against the vacant seats, which are available against 
the State quota. 
Looking to the nature of controversy 
involved and, particularly, when it relates to the 
academics of the candidates, exclusively by way of 
a provisional measure, it is directed that the 
petitioner may tentatively fill in those vacant seats 
of State quota seats, which are still remaining 
unfilled, exclusively on the basis of the respective 
ranking of the candidates in their NEET 
qualification from All-India quota. 
In the meantime, the respondents may file 
their counter affidavit within a period of three 
weeks from today. 
List thereafter.
