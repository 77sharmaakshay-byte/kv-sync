HIGH COURT OF UTTARAKHAND 
AT NAINITAL
Writ Petition (M/S) No. 933 of 2021
Shivalik Institute of Ayurveda and Research … Petitioner
Vs.
State of Uttarakhand and others … Respondents
Advocates : Mr. Navneet Kaushik, Advocate, for the petitioner
Mr. T.S. Phartiyal, Addl. CSC, for the State of Uttarakhand
Mr. Munish Bhardwaj, Advocate, for the respondent University
Mr. Yogesh Pant, Advocate, holding brief of Mr. V.K. Kapruwan, 
Standing Counsel, for the Union of India/respondent No. 4 
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
The petitioner to the present writ petition had prayed for an 
appropriate direction to respondent Nos. 1 and 3, for permitting 
to hold an additional round of counselling in order to enable them 
to fill up the vacant seats, which were available with the 
petitioner institution in respect of B.A.M.S. Courses, which were 
left vacant as against the total seats which were made available to 
the petitioners institution to be filled under State quota seats 
available with them from amongst the NEET qualified students
based on their respective merits.
2. When the matter was taken up initially on 22nd April 2021,
the respondents and the University were represented through their 
respective counsels. Considering the principles laid down for 
filling up the seats which were left available, as against the State 
quota seats from amongst the NEET qualified candidates, as per 
the respective merit of qualification from All India quota basis,
this Court by an interim order had permitted that 15 intake of 
students, would be permitted to be admitted provisionally as 
against the unfilled seats of State quota, by the petitioner 
institution. But unfortunately, at that relevant point of time, when 
the interim order was passed i.e. on 22nd April 2021, no proper 2
assistance of any nature, whatsoever, was extended by the 
learned counsel for the University, on the pretext, that as per the 
guidelines, which were issued by the Ministry of Ayush, for the 
Academic Year 2020-21, there was a cut off provided for 
admission as to be31st March 2021.
3. Be that as it may. Apart from the fact, that there was no 
proper assistance provided by the learned counsel for the 
respondent University, even if it is presumed that the said 
guidelines issued by the Ayush Ministry, was creating any 
embargo in granting admission to the candidates as against the 
aforesaid vacant seats to be filled from the State quota and it was 
contrary to the guidelines, which were issued by the Ministry of 
Ayush then in that eventuality the respondent University, was 
expected to challenge the said interim order before the Division 
Bench, which was not done.
4. On the contrary, it has been argued by the counsel for the 
petitioner’s institution that, on the basis of an order dated 22nd 
April 2021, passed by this Court, the petitioners’ had already 
granted admission to 15 students, exclusively as against the 
vacant State quota seats, which were vacant and available in the 
institution for the B.A.M.S. course and also, that it was strictly
based on their placement in the merit of NEET examination. It 
was that only after the grant of admission, when the approval was 
sought of the admission made from the University, the University 
has declined to grant an approval to the aforesaid admissions, on 
the basis of correspondence dated 11th August 2021, which has 
been placed on record, along with the Miscellaneous Application 
which has been preferred by the petitioner on 29th September 
2021, wherein they have prayed for that 15 students, thus 
enrolled on the basis of the interim order dated 22nd April 2021, 3
they may be directed to be approved by the respondent 
University, for the purpose of enabling them to pursue their 
academics and the consequential examinations to be held 
thereafter in future.
5. When the said Miscellaneous Application was taken up, the 
respondent University was directed to complete their instructions 
and make their submission accordingly. In compliance of the 
order dated 4th October 2021, the changed counsel for the 
respondent University Mr. Munish Bharadwaj, Advocate, had 
placed before this Court a Communication No. 2153 dated 5th 
October 2021, written by the Registrar of the University, which 
was addressed to the counsel, that the approval to the said 
admission was granted in pursuance to the interim order passed 
by this Court, could not be granted on the basis of an embargo 
which has been created by the guidelines which were issued by 
the Ministry of Ayush, providing the cut off period for the 
purposes of admission as to be 31st March 2021, and since the 
admissions have been granted on 22nd April 2021, hence, the 
approval cannot be granted to the admission thus made.
6. In fact, the communication, which has now been placed 
before this Court, runs contrary to the own conduct of the 
respondent University, as they have derelicted in performing their 
professional responsibility in providing the proper assistance to 
the Court and then after the order dated 4th October 2021, they 
have rather not placed any document as such on record by way of 
an affidavit, to deny the approval and what has been placed 
before this Court is a privileged communicated which was made 
between the University and its counsel to which no cognizance 
could be taken by the Court on judicial side, in absence of the 
same being placed on record. But, considering the fact that the 4
admission to the students, as against the available vacant State 
quota seats was exclusively based on their placement on merit in 
the NEET examination, on All India selection, basis is an 
arrangement, which is permitted to be carried and coupled with 
the fact that the interim order dated 22nd April 2021, under the 
strength of which they were admitted continues to operate and it 
still exists in the eyes of law. The respondent cannot carve out a 
distinction by denying to grant of an approval to the admission 
thus made and thereby jeopardising the academics of 15 students 
who have already been admitted. 
7. Hence, University is directed to grant provisional approval 
to the admission given to the admission of 15 students, which has 
been granted by the petitioners in pursuance to the order dated 
22nd April 2021 and proceed accordingly and file an affidavit on 
the record of the writ petition to that effect.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
