The revisionist herein has filed the 
substitution application, bringing on record, the 
heirs of the deceased-respondent, who had met with 
the sad demise on 23.08.2019. The Registry has 
reported that the substitution application, is delayed 
by one day. An oral request has been made by the 
counsel for the petitioner for condonation of one 
day’s delay, which has chanced in filing the 
substitution application, which is not being 
seriously opposed by the respondent’s counsel. The 
same would stand accepted and condoned. 
Accordingly, the Substitution Application 
No.15761 of 2019, too which has been preferred by 
the revisionist would stand allowed. Petitioner is 
permitted to carry out the necessary amendment, in 
both the revisions, in the cause title of the revisions 
by bringing the heirs on record of the deceased￾respondent within a period of ten days from today. 
List this revision immediately after ten days.
