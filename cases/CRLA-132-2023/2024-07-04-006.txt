(3) Having been convicted for the 
offence under Section 302/34 IPC and 
sentenced to undergo rigorous 
imprisonment for life, vide judgment and 
order dated 28.1.2023, passed by the 5th
Additional Sessions Judge, Haridwar in 
Sessions Trial No. 168/2017, appellants 
are in jail. Appellants/applicants have 
preferred these bail applications 
(IA/1/2023) for releasing them on bail 
during pendency of the present appeals.
(4) There is direct evidence of two 
eyewitnesses, PW3 Anand Kumar and 
PW5 Sandeep, who saw all the 
appellants committing the murder of deceased Satish @ Mangeram Saini at 
about 12.00 hours on 27.4.2017. From 
perusal of the record, it is also revealed 
that there was some enmity between the 
deceased and one of the appellants with 
regard to purchase of land. 
(5) Since there is direct evidence of 
committing the crime, therefore, we do 
not find any reason to release the 
appellants on bail. Accordingly, bail 
applications are rejected.
