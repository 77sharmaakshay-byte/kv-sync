03.02.2023 WPSS No. 182 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Present Writ Petition has been filed 
under Article 226 of the Constitution of India.
Heard Mr. P.C. Petshali, learned counsel 
for the petitioner through Video 
Conferencing.
Mr. Ramji Srivastava, learned counsel 
for respondent no. 1.
Mr. V.S. Rawat, learned Brief Holder for 
the respondent no. 2.
During the arguments, Mr. P.C. 
Petshali, Advocate, fairly conceded that the 
case of the petitioner does not fall under 
Economically Weaker Section. The 
respondent nos. 3 & 4 have been selected 
under Economically Weaker Section. The 
marks of respondent no. 3 are less than the 
petitioner. He further submitted that the 
respondent nos. 3 & 4 cannot claim under 
Economically Weaker Section, since they 
were over age, and, the age relaxation 
cannot be granted to the respondent nos. 3 & 
4 as per the terms of the Advertisement 
(Annexure No. 4).
Learned counsel for the respondent 
nos. 1 & 2 requested two weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavit.
Issue notice to the respondent nos. 3 & 
4.
Steps to be taken within three days.
List on 25.02.2023.
Urgency Application (I.A. No. 2 of 
2023) stands disposed of accordingly.
