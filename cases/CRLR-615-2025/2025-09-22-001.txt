CRLR No.615 of 2025
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Pramod Kumar Bailwal, Advocate for the 
revisionist.
2. This criminal revision has been filed by the revisionist, 
whereby he has put to challenge the order dated 
05.07.2025, passed by learned Sessions Judge, Dehradun, 
in Criminal Misc. Application No.60 of 2025, Deepanshu 
Nautiyal Vs. State and others, whereby the application 
moved by the revisionist under Section 5 of the Limitation 
Act, 1963, has been rejected and consequently the criminal 
appeal has also come to an end due to default/non￾prosecution.
3. It is contended by counsel for the revisionist that the 
revisionist was convicted under Section 138 of N.I. Act, 
1881, by the trial court vide judgment and order dated 
03.12.2024, passed by learned IIIrd Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, in Complaint Case No.6508 of 2021, Smt. Sunita 
Gulati Vs. Deepanshu Nautiyal, and sentenced six months 
simple imprisonment and a fine of ₹4,00,000/-.
4. The said judgment and order dated 03.12.2024 was 
challenged by the revisionist by filing a delayed criminal 
appeal in the year 2025 along with an application under 
Section 5 of the Limitation Act, 1963 for condoning the 
delay in filing the criminal appeal, which was registered as 
Criminal Misc. Application No.60 of 2025, Deepanshu 
Nautiyal Vs. State and others.
5. The revisionist failed to appear before the learned 
appellate Court on 05.07.2025 and therefore the Criminal 2
Misc. Application No.60 of 2025 has been rejected by the 
learned Sessions Judge, Dehradun, for default/non￾prosecution.
6. It is contended by counsel for the revisionist that 
when the criminal appeal along with misc. application was 
fixed on 05.07.2025, due to heavy rain the petitioner could 
not reach to the Court which was beyond his control and 
therefore the order suffers from illegality and deserves 
interference.
7. He further submits that the outcome of the rejection of 
the application under Section 5 of the Limitation Act, 1963, 
resulted into dismissal of the criminal appeal automatically 
and therefore interference is also required.
8. This Court finds some substance in the submission 
made by the revisionist.
9. Issue notice to the respondent no.2, returnable within 
four weeks.
10. Steps to be taken within three days.
11. List this case on 25.11.2025.
12. In the meantime, no coercive action shall be taken 
against the revisionist pursuant to the judgment and order
dated 03.12.2024, passed by learned IIIrd Additional Chief 
Judicial Magistrate, in Complaint Case No.6508 of 2021, 
Smt. Sunita Gulati Vs. Deepanshu Nautiyal.
