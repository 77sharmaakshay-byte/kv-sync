4. The facts, in brief, are that the petitioners are 
students of respondent no.3-College; that, the admissions 
were forwarded to the University and the National 
Commission for Indian System of Medicine (NCISM) has 
approved the admissions; that, now, when the time for 
taking up examinations have arrived and hall tickets were 
sought, the University has stalled the process of 
consideration on the ground that the students have not 
participated in the counselling; that, the university does 
not dispute the fact that they had participated in the NEET 
examination and the University also does not dispute the 
fact that they have secured marks above the stipulated cut￾off marks of 50% or so, and that they are fully qualified to 
be admitted. 
5. The fact remains that the admissions are against 
seats that have not been filled up during the counselling. 
6. In view of the twin facts that the students have 
participated in the NEET examination and are part of the 
merit list prepared by the National Testing Agency (NTA), which conducts the NEET examination and, in view of the 
fact, that they possessed all the requisite qualification to be 
admitted into the course. We are of the considered opinion 
that technicality of non-approval of the University, which 
needs to be gone into after a full-fledged hearing, the 
academic career of the students should not be jeopardised 
and, hence, pending disposal of the writ petitions, we 
direct the University to issue hall tickets and permit the 
petitioners/ students to take up the examination, subject to 
the final orders in the writ petition. It is made clear that the 
result of the examination shall not be announced without 
the concurrence of this Court. 
7. Copy of this order be forwarded to the concerned 
Registrar of the University forthwith. 
8. List this case on 09.09.2025.
