Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Ramji Shrivastava, learned counsel 
along with Mr. Nishant Krishna Adhikari, learned 
counsel for the petitioners in WPSS Nos. 909/2024, 
911/2024 and 950/2024.
2. Mr. Rajendra Dobhal, learned Senior 
Counsel assisted by Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, learned 
counsel for the petitioners in WPSS Nos. 1945/2024 
and 1946/2024.
3. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Brief Holder 
for the State/respondent No. 1.
4. Mr. Saurabh Adhikari, learned Standing 
Counsel along with Mr. Manoj Kumar, learned 
Standing Counsel for the Union of India/respondent 
No. 2.
5. Mr. Arvind Vashisth, learned Senior 
Counsel, assisted by Mr. Y.C. Brijwasi and Mr. 
Abhishek Anthwal, learned counsels for 
NESTS/respondent No. 3.
6. Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned counsel for 
respondent Nos. 4 & 5.
7. In all these petitions issues are 
common. 
8. In Writ Petition (S/S) No. 920 of 2024, 
Writ Petition (S/S) No. 911 of 2024 and Writ 
Petition (S/S) No. 950 of 2024 the petitioners, who were engaged on contractual basis in different 
Institutions, challenging the order dated 
30.05.2024 whereby their contractual 
appointments were discontinued. 
9. The coordinate Bench of this Court, by 
order dated 12.06.2024 restrained the respondents 
from giving joining to any of the persons selected 
through open selection on the post, held by the 
petitioners in the Institution.
10. Mr. Ramji Srivastava, learned 
counsel for the petitioners submits that the 
petitioners are challenging the order impugned 
dated 30.05.2024 on the ground that, in fact, 
against the posts occupied by these contractual 
employees, the regular selection has taken place 
though, Mr. Arvind Vashisth, the learned Senior 
Counsel, appearing for the National Education 
Society for Tribal Students (NESTS) gave an 
undertaking before the Division Bench of this Court 
in Special Appeal No. 331 of 2023 which was 
decided on 26.09.2023 that the recruitment is 
being conducted in such a way as not to disturb the 
contractual teachers.
11. He also submits that by virtue of 
an undertaking, as given before the Division Bench 
of this Court, these petitioners, who were engaged 
on contractual basis, cannot be discontinued by virtue of the impugned order. 
12. In response to this, Mr. Arvind 
Vashisth, learned Senior Counsel submits that 
discontinuation of these contractual employees,
who are the petitioners in WPSS Nos. 909/2024, 
911/2024 and 950/2024 were not discontinued 
because of the regular selection process, rather 
they were discontinued by virtue of the terms and 
conditions of their contract and their contractual 
period was already expired. 
13. Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned counsel, 
who appears for respondent Nos. 4 & 5 submits 
that the petitioners of WPSS Nos. 909/2024, 
911/2024 and 950/2024 have no right to continue 
since they are contractual employees and they are 
bound by the terms and conditions of their 
contractual employment. Apart from this, he also 
submits that all the petitioners of these three 
petitions have also participated in the selection 
process, therefore, they cannot challenge the 
regular selection process.
14. In the meantime, two writ 
petitions have been filed i.e. WPSS No. 1945 of 
2024 and WPSS No. 1946 of 2024 by those who 
participated in the regular selection process,
initiated pursuant to an advertisement dated 
05.07.2023 and got selected and the appointment letters have been issued. In these two subsequent 
petitions, a relief has been sought that the 
direction be issued to the respondents to give 
joining to each of the petitioners in “Eklavya Model 
Residential Schools (EMRS). 
15. Mr. Rajendra Dobhal, learned 
Senior Counsel, submits that because of the interim 
order passed by the coordinate Bench of this Court 
in WPSS Nos. 909/2024, 911/2024 and 950/2024 
dated 12.06.2024, the respondents are not 
permitting them to join though they are regularly 
selected, who participated in the selection process 
and by virtue of interim order passed by the 
coordinate Bench of this Court, their legitimate 
right to hold the post pursuant to their selection 
has been taken away though they were not 
impleaded as party respondents in these petitions.
16. He also submits that the 
petitions, filed by the contractual employees i.e. 
WPSS Nos. 909/2024, 911/2024 and 950 of 2024 
are liable to be dismissed also on that ground that 
they have not been impleaded the selected 
candidate as party respondent.
17. Mr. Arvind Vashisth, learned 
Senior Counsel, who appears for the National 
Education Society for Tribal Students (NESTS), 
informs to this Court that there are 4 Institutions,in the State of Uttarakhand, and total sanctioning 
posts are 52 for each of the Institution, and today, 
there are total 13 petitioners before this Court in 
two petitions i.e. WPSS No. 1945 of 2024 and WPSS 
No. 1946 of 2024, who are regularly selected 
candidates and have been issued appointment 
letter. He also submits that there are in total 17 
numbers of contractual employees, who are before 
this Court in three writ petitions i.e. WPSS Nos. 
909/2024, 911/2024 and 950/2024, therefore, for 
an interim measure, the petitioners of writ 
petitions i.e. WPSS No. 1945 of 2024 and WPSS No. 
1946 of 2024 may be permitted to join. 
18. He further submits that since the 
matter is being posted for final disposal, therefore, 
till such time the matter is decided finally, the 
contractual employees, who are the petitioners in 
WPSS No. 909 of 2024, WPSS No. 911/2024 and 
WPSS No. 950 of 2024, may also be permitted to 
continue.
19. Admittedly, as on today, there 
are 17 contractual employees and 13 regular 
incumbents, who are before this Court. In such an 
eventuality, the respondents are directed to give 
joining to each of the petitioner of WPSS No. 1945 
of 2024 and WPSS No. 1946 of 2024 forthwith. 
Simultaneously, the respondents are also directed not to discontinue 17 contractual employees, who 
are before this Court in WPSS No. 909 of 2024, 911 
of 2024 and 950 of 2024 and be paid salary;
however, their continuation will be subject to the 
outcome of all these petitions.
20. Put up these matters on 
12.11.2024 as a first case along with WPSS No. 
1427 of 2023 and WPSS No. 1680 of 2023. 
21. On that day, the matter shall not 
be adjourned.
22. Mr. Arvind Vashisth, learned 
Senior Counsel for the NESTS as well as Mr. C.S. 
Rawat, learned counsel for respondent Nos. 4 & 5, 
shall communicate about this order to the 
respondents for necessary compliance 
telephonically today itself.
