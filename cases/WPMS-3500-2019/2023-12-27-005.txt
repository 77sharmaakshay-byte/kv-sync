Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Vinod Nautiyal, learned counsel, 
holding brief of Mr. Shailendra Nauriyal, 
learned counsel for the petitioner appears 
through V.C.
2. Mr. Anil Dabral, learned Additional 
Chief Standing Counsel, for the State of 
Uttarakhand.
3. The writ petition was dismissed in 
default. Now MCC No.3175 of 2023 (For 
Restoration Application) is moved for 
restoration of the writ petition
4. The reason, as given in the 
restoration application, is that due to 
some inadvertence, the clerk of the 
learned counsel for the petitioner could 
not mark the case in the cause-list. 
5. Mr. Tarun Lakhera, learned Brief 
Holder for the State, has not raised any 
objection.
6. The reasons, as given in the 
Restoration Application, are satisfactory.
7. The Restoration Application, MCC 
No.3175 of 2023, is allowed.
8. The writ petition is restored to its 
original number.
9. Mr. Vinod Nautiyal, learned counsel 
for the petitioner, also submits that 
identical matter is also subjudice in 
WPMS No.3508 of 2019.
10. List this writ petition, along with 
WPMS No.3508 of 2019, in the week 
commencing 19.02.2024.
