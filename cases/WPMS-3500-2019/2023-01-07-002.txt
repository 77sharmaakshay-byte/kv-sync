This is an application for amendment of 
the writ petition. The petitioner intends to 
add a prayer to the writ petition for 
challenging the Government Order dated 
08.03.2016 and to add the Secretary Home, 
State Government as a respondent no. 3. 
The application for amendment is 
formal one and is necessary for just and 
proper for adjudication of the writ petition. 
Hence, the application for amendment (IA 
No. 3174 of 2022) is allowed. 
Let the Secretary Home, State 
Government be impleaded as respondent no. 
3 to the writ petition. 
The petitioner is directed to amended
writ petition within seven days.
Mr. Suyash Pant, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State accepts notice on 
behalf of the newly respondent no. 3. 
Let the additional counter affidavit, if so 
advised, be filed within four weeks.
Misc. Applications (IA Nos. 3172 and 
3173) for taking counter affidavit and
rejoinder affidavit respectively on record are 
allowed. Counter and rejoinder affidavit are
