This is an application for short term bail 
filed by the applicant – Bala Devi on the 
ground that her daughter is suffering from 
various ailments including hepatitis.
It is also submitted by Mrs. Pushpa 
Joshi, the learned Senior Advocate appearing 
for the appellants that the appellant – Bala 
Devi was granted parole and in the 
meantime, she has already surrendered before 
the Jail Authorities concerned. There is no 
allegation against her that she misused the 
liberty granted by us to her.
We have perused the medical certificates 
and of the view that the applicant should be granted short term bail for a period of 30
days, hence this Court is inclined to allow the 
application for short term bail. 
Let the applicant –Bala Devi be released 
on short term bail for a period of 30 days,
which shall be reckoned from the date of her
release from jail on such suitable terms and 
conditions, as deems fit and proper by the 
learned 5th Additional Session Judge, District 
– Dehradun.
On expiry of 30 days, applicant – Bala 
Devi shall surrender before the trial Court,
who in turn, shall send him to jail. In case, he 
fails to surrender after expiry of 30 days, the 
learned 5th Additional Session Judge, District 
– Dehradun shall issue Non Bailable Warrant 
against the applicant – Bala Devi. 
In that view of the matter, the 
application for short term bail is allowed for 
a period of 30 days. 
List this matter on 14.02.2023 along 
with connected matter.
In the meantime, learned counsel for the 
State shall file objection to the application 
filed, under Section 389 of the Code of 
Criminal Procedure, by Chotu alias 
Shikander.
The Short Term Bail Application (IA 
No. 341 of 2022) stands disposed of.
