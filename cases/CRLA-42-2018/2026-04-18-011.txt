The instant appeal has been preferred against 
judgment and order dated 25.01.2018, passed in Sessions Trial 
No.151 of 2007, State Vs. Bala Devi and others, by the court of 
Fifth Additional Sessions Judge, District Dehradun. By it, the 
appellants have been convicted and sentenced under Sections 302 
and 201 IPC. 
2. Heard.
3. This appeal has already been admitted.
4. List in due course for final hearing. 
5. Heard on Third Bail Application (IA) No.346 of 
2018
6. Learned counsel for the appellants submits that 
co-convict, having similar role, has already been granted bail; the 
appellants have already undergone more than half of the sentence
imposed upon them; there are less chances of the appeal being 
heard in near future.
7. These facts are admitted by learned State Counsel.2
8. Having considered the period of incarceration and 
other attending factors, we are of the view that it is a case in 
which the execution of sentence should be suspended and the 
appellants be enlarged on bail.
9. The bail application is allowed.
10. The sentence appealed against is suspended 
during the pendency of the appeal.
11. The appellants- Bala Devi and Mohar Singh @ 
Mohan Singh be released on bail during the pendency of the 
appeal on their executing a personal bond and furnishing two 
reliable sureties, each of the like amount, by each one of them, to 
the satisfaction of the court concerned.
