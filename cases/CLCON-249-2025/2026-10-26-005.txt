4. Petitioners have filed these contempt 
petitions alleging that order dated 
03.04.2024 passed by Division Bench of 
this Court in Special Appeal No.101 of 2023 
Union of India and Others Vs. Ajay Singh 
and Others, and Batch of Special Appeals, 
has not been complied with rather 
respondents-contemnors informed 
petitioners that claim for appointment of 
petitioners as Constable (General Duties) 
was rejected. 5. It is contended by learned counsel for 
petitioners that such order is under the 
teeth of the order passed by the Division 
Bench of this Court and the respondents￾contemnors has committed deliberate 
contempt by passing the order rejecting the 
case of petitioners on the self-same ground 
which was already discussed and 
considered by the writ court as well as by 
the division bench of this court while 
deciding Special Appeals.
6. This Court finds substance in the 
submission made by learned counsel for 
petitioners. Respondents-contemnors 
cannot sit over the judgment passed by the 
High Court as an Appellate Authority and 
the manner in which petitioners’ claim for 
appointment on the post of Constable 
(General Duties) was rejected, it itself is 
contempt of order dated 03.04.2024 passed 
in aforesaid Special Appeals.
7. In such view of the matter, 
respondents-contemnors are directed to 
immediately comply with the order dated 
03.04.2024 passed by Division Bench, in its 
letter and spirit, ignoring the earlier 
rejection order passed by them.
