Short Term Bail Application (IA No. 3655 of 2023)
(3) Appellant has been convicted for 
offence punishable under Section 302 of 
I.P.C. and has been awarded rigorous 
imprisonment for life by learned Additional 
Sessions Judge, Haldwani, District Nainital. 
Presently, appellant is undergoing sentence at 
Central Jail, Sitarganj, District Udham Singh 
Nagar. 
(4) An application has been moved on behalf 
of appellant/applicant seeking short term bail 
for a period of three months, on the ground 
that he is suffering from coronary artery 
disease. In support of such contention, 
discharge summary issued by Dr. Susheela 
Tiwari Government Hospital, Haldwani is 
enclosed. Perusal of the same indicates that 
appellant was suffering from heart ailment.
(5) Learned State Counsel was asked to get 
instructions regarding medical condition of 
the appellant. Today, on instructions, learned 
State Counsel submits that earlier, appellant 
was admitted in Dr. Susheela Tiwari 
Government Hospital, and presently, he is 
being treated as an outdoor patient. Thus, it 
is admitted to learned State Counsel that appellant is suffering from heart problem.
(6) In such view of the matter, we are 
inclined to allow the prayer for short term bail 
made by the appellant.
(7) Accordingly, Short Term Bail Application 
is allowed. Let the appellant/applicant be 
enlarged on short term bail only for a period 
of three weeks on his executing personal 
bond with two reliable sureties, each of the 
like amount, to the satisfaction of the Court 
concerned. 
(8) Needless to say that immediately after 
expiry of three weeks, the applicant/ 
appellant shall surrender before the 
Superintendent of the Jail concerned.
