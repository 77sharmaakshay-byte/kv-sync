Mr. B.M. Pingal, learned counsel for the 
appellant.
2. Mr. V.S. Rawat, learned A.G.A. for the State.
3. Mr. Ajay Ganapati, Senior Superintendent of 
Police, Udham Singh Nagar joined the proceedings 
through video conferencing. 
3. The report submitted by Mr. Ajay Ganapati, Senior 
Superintendent of Police, Udham Singh Nagar is taken 
on record.
4. In fact, in the instant case, it was reported that the 
appellant had to undergo bypass surgery and was to be 
admitted in All India Institute of Medical Sciences, 
Rishikesh, but for want of police guard, he could not be 
sent on multiple occasions. This Court had required
Senior Superintendent of Police, Udham Singh Nagar to 
submit a report. Report confirms that on multiple 
occasions, police guard was not available, due to which 
the appellant could not be admitted at All India Institute of Medical Sciences, Rishikesh.
5. Admittedly, it is a serious lapse. A patient of 
bypass surgery was not forwarded due to want of police 
guard. 
6. Mr. Ajay Ganapati, Senior Superintendent of 
Police, Udham Singh Nagar, who joined the proceeding 
through VC assures that he would make a protocol for 
District Udham Singh Nagar that such incident do not 
re-occur in future. We appreciate his intention. 
7. Let such protocol be put in place so as to ensure 
that as and when requisition is received from jail for 
providing police guard for medical check-up of any 
inmate, without delay, the police guard is deputed.
8. This Court expects that Mr. Ajay Ganapati, Senior 
Superintendent of Police, Udham Singh Nagar to submit 
the copy of the protocol to this Court also, so that it may 
be circulated throughout the State, so that such situation 
may not recur anywhere in the State.
9. Mr. Ajay Ganapati, Senior Superintendent of 
Police, Udham Singh Nagar requests four weeks’ time to 
make this protocol. 
10. List on 15.09.2026.
