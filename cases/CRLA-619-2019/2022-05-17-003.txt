 This is a Bail Application under Section 389 of the 
Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (hereinafter referred 
to as “Code”.) for suspension of sentence and grant of 
bail upon appeal. The appellant has been convicted by 
the 1st Additional Sessions Judge, Haldwani, District 
Nainital in Sessions Trial No. 22 of 2018 for the offence 
under Section 302 of the Indian Penal Code, and has 
been sentenced to undergo life imprisonment and fine 
of Rs. 10,000/- for the said offence and in default of 
payment of the fine, he is required to undergo six 
months’ further simple imprisonment. 
 It is borne from the record that the appellant was not on bail in course of trial. P.W.1 is the person, who 
saw the accused standing near the dead-body of his 
father having stabbed injuries, and the appellant was 
holding blood-stained knife. The blood-stained knife 
has been recovered at the instance of the appellant. 
The same is an evidence under Section 27 of the Indian 
Evidence Act, 1872 and it is found to be stained with 
the DNA of the appellant 
 In that view of the matter, this Court finds no 
reason to suspend the sentence as there is no statable 
probability that the appeal will be allowed. Hence, the 
Application (IA No. 3653 of 2022) for suspension of 
sentence and the grant of bail upon appeal is rejected. 
 Let the matter be listed as and when directed. 
 
 (R.C. Khulbe, J.) (S.K. Mishra, ACJ)
