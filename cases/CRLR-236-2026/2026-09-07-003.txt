Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Saurabh Kumar Pandey, learned 
counsel for the revisionist through Video 
Confernecing.
2. Mr. Prabhat Kandpal, learned A.G.A. 
for the State.
3. Mr. D.S. Mehta, learned counsel for 
respondent no.2. 
4. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
submits that Miscellaneous Application (IA 
No. 3 of 2026) has been filed seeking 
cancellation of the non-bailable warrants 
issued against the revisionist and further 
seeking reasonable time to make payment 
of the remaining amount payable to 
respondent no.2.
5. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
further submits that a Coordinate Bench of 
this Court, vide order dated 07.04.2026, 
considering the undertaking furnished by 
the revisionist to pay the remaining 
amount of ₹13,00,000/- within a period of 
four months, had allowed the exemption application and exempted the revisionist 
from surrendering before the Court 
concerned. It is submitted that, owing to 
his ailment and continuous medical 
treatment, the revisionist could not 
arrange the aforesaid amount within the 
stipulated period. He would further submit
that this Court, vide order dated 
12.08.2026, directed the learned 
Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar, to 
issue non-bailable warrants against the 
revisionist and fixed the matter for 
16.09.2026.
6. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
submits that the revisionist has, in the 
meantime, made sincere efforts to arrange 
the requisite funds and has paid a sum of 
₹3,00,000/- to respondent no.2 during the 
month of August, 2026, through UPI mode. 
It is submitted that an amount of 
₹10,00,000/- still remains payable to 
respondent no.2. Learned counsel submits 
that the revisionist requires further time of 
two and a half months to arrange the 
remaining amount and undertakes to pay 
the entire outstanding amount within the 
time so granted by this Court.
7. Learned counsel appearing for 
respondent no.2 would submit that the 
revisionist has failed to comply with the 
undertaking earlier given before this Court 
and has not paid the entire amount within 
the stipulated period. However, considering 
the submissions made on behalf of the 
revisionist, learned counsel submits that 
the remaining amount of ₹10,00,000/-
may be directed to be paid in two 
instalments within a specified period.
8. Having considered the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
parties and taking into account the payment of ₹3,00,000/- already made by 
the revisionist, this Court is inclined to 
grant one final opportunity to the 
revisionist to clear the remaining amount 
of ₹10,00,000/- payable to respondent 
no.2.
9. Accordingly, the revisionist is directed 
to pay the aforesaid remaining amount of 
₹10,00,000/- to respondent no.2 in two 
equal installments of ₹5,00,000/- each. 
The first installment of ₹5,00,000/- shall 
be paid within a period of 45 days from 
today, and the remaining installment of 
₹5,00,000/- shall be paid within a further 
period of 45 days thereafter.
10. Subject to the revisionist duly 
complying with all the aforesaid conditions 
within the stipulated time, the non-bailable 
warrants issued against him shall remain 
cancelled.
11. It is made clear that the aforesaid 
indulgence is being granted to the 
revisionist as a final opportunity. In the 
event of failure on the part of the 
revisionist to comply with any of the
conditions stipulated hereinabove, or in 
the event of default in making payment of 
either of the instalments within the time 
prescribed, the benefit of cancellation of 
the non-bailable warrants granted by this 
order shall stand automatically vacated, 
and the order dated 12.08.2026, whereby 
non-bailable warrants were issued against 
the revisionist, shall revive and become 
operative without any further order of this 
Court.
12. List this matter on 30.10.2026.
