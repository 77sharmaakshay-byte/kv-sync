Mr. J.S. Virk, the learned Dy. Advocate

Heard the learned counsel for the parties 
on short term bail application.
The short term bail application has been 
filed by the applicant Bhura Ansari, who has
been convicted for the offence punishable 
under Sections 8/20(b)(11)(c) of the Narcotic 
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act and 
sentenced to twenty years rigorous 
imprisonment with fine of Rs. 2,00,000/- with 
default sentence by the court of learned Special 
Judge, NDPS Act, Dehradun, in Special Session
Trial No. 140 of 2017.
It is submitted by the learned Senior 
Counsel that the applicant’s mother is suffering 
from psychiatric disorder and the other son has 
passed away in the meantime and nobody is 
there to look after the welfare or treatment of 
his mother.
Mr. Shobhit Saharia, the learned counsel 
for the NCB would submit that he has 
instructions that mother of the applicant 
appeared before the Doon Medical College, 
Dehradun, as per the paper enclosed and she 
was given medicine for gastric and some 
vitamins, therefore, he would submit that 
applicant is not making out a case for short 
term bail.
However, we see that the applicant’s 
counsel has stated that the mother of the 
applicant is suffering from psychological disorder/ depression etc., and there is no 
refutal of the same by the NCB. The learned 
counsel appearing for the Board has also not 
stated that there is any reasonable possibility 
that he will abscond from the process of justice. 
It appears that as the appeal is being of the 
year 2020 and may not be taken for final 
disposal in the near future. 
In that view of the matter, we are inclined 
to grant a short term bail to the applicant for a 
period of 45 days. 
Let applicant-Bhura Ansari be released on 
short term bail for a period of 45 days, to be 
reckoned from the date of release, on suitable 
terms and conditions as deemed just and 
proper by the learned Special Judge, NDPS Act, 
Dehradun. On completion of bail period, from 
the date of release, he shall surrender before 
the learned trial court. On such an event, he 
shall be again sent back to the custody. In case 
he failed to do so, proper warrant shall be 
issued.
In addition to the condition imposed by 
the learned Special Judge, the following 
condition shall also be incorporated:-
(i) The applicant shall report before
the S.H.O. Police Station 
Manglore, District Haridwar once 
in a week for the period he is 
released on interim bail preferably 
on Monday in between 11:00 am 
to 12:30 pm and shall not misuse 
the liberty granted to him.
With such observations, the short term 
bail application stands disposed of.
Criminal appeal is ripe for hearing. List for 
final hearing in the first week of April 2023.
Grant certified copy as per Rules.
