This is an application for recall of the order 
dated 26.08.2022 thereby granting short term bail 
to the appellant. 
Learned Senior Counsel for the appellant 
would submit that there was error on his part in 
making submission that brother of appellant 
died in fact, his father had died. 
In that view of the matter, the appellant 
does not take advantage of the short term bail 
granted by us. Hence, by exercising powers 
conferred under Section 482 of the Code of 
Criminal Procedure, 1973, order dated 26.08.2022 
is hereby recalled.
Recall application stands disposed of. 
List this matter before the assigned Bench
on 29.11.2022 for final disposal.
