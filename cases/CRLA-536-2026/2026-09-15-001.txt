3. The instant appeal is preferred by the appellant 
‘Salman’ S/o Chota against the judgment and order 
passed by the learned Trial Court dated 24.08.2026 
passed in Session Trial No. 19 of 2025, whereby, the 
appellant herein is convicted under sections 74 and 
75(2) of BNS, 2023 with the maximum sentence of 
three years with the fine of Rs. 5,000/-.
4. As per the office report, the appeal is well within 
time.
5. Admit.
6. Summon the Trial Court record.
7. Heard on bail application.
8. It is contended by the learned counsel for the 
appellant that the Trial Court while convicting the 
appellant also enlarged him on interim bail. Since the 
maximum sentence as awarded by the Trial Court is 
three years and at present is on interim bail, therefore, 
the interim bail granted by the Trial Court while 
convicting the appellant is made absolute.
