AO No.142/15 
Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
 Mr. A.S. Rawat, Additional Advocate General, for the 
State/appellants. 
 In order to facilitate Lohari-Nagpala Hydel Scheme to be 
founded by National Thermal Power Corporation (NTPC), a road 
known as Helgu-Adit Motor road, 6 kilometers long, and another 
road namely Dabrani-Adit, length 0.75 kilometer, had to be 
constructed and for the purpose, the contract was granted to the 
respondent by the competent officer (Superintendent Engineer of 
the Road Construction Division, PWD, Uttarkashi) to the worth of 
Rs.19,93,64,000/-. The agreement was entered into on 14.06.2005 
and the work had to be completed by the contractor within twenty 
months viz. by 14.3.2007. The contractor did not complete the 
said work and sought extension of time for the same which was 
granted firstly by 31.3.2009 and second time, by 31.3.2011. The 
work could not be done even after granting the time extension, as 
afore-stated. Meanwhile, the NTPC, for the reasons, inter alia, 
wanting of road, foreclosed the project. 
 Learned Additional Advocate General of the State has argued 
before this Court that the contractor completed only one-kilometer 
road out of six kilometers for Helgo-Adit Motor road project and 
that too without construction of bridge, wherefor he was also 
granted the contract, whereas in another project, he completed 
road of 0.75 kilometer but without any culvert, for which the 
contract was also granted. 
 Nonetheless of above facts, the contractor agitated the matter 
before the Arbitrator for release of rest of the amount, and the said 
Arbitrator, on the basis of few misconceived letters, sanctioned 
Rs.7.00 crores to be paid to the contractor. Reference against the 
award of such Arbitrator has also been dismissed by learned 
District Judge, Uttarkashi. 
 Feeling aggrieved, the State Government has come up before 
this Court. 
 It is also pertinent to mention that the delay condonation 
application has also been filed seeking condonation of 10 days’ 
delay in filing this appeal. 
 Issue notice to the respondent for filing objection, if any, 
against the said application besides seeking his response by way of 
counter affidavit with regard to the facts, as have been highlighted 
above.  Meanwhile, the execution of Award made by the Arbitrator as 
well as the effect and operation of the impugned order dated 
29.11.2014, shall remain stayed. 
 Call for the L.C.R. 
 List thereafter for further hearing. 
 Stay application (CLMA 2490/2015) stands disposed of 
accordingly. 
 
 
(Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.)
