AO No. 142 of 2015
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. I.P. Kohli, Standing Counsel for the 
State/appellant.
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate for the 
respondents.
Heard learned counsel for the parties 
through video conferencing.
Learned counsel for the respondents 
submits that on the very first date, interim 
order was passed without notice to him, 
although there was delay in filing the 
appeal. He further submits that blanket stay 
has been granted, although as per the 
proviso to sub-section (2) of Section 36 of 
Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, 
award passed by Arbitrator has to be treated 
as money decree under CPC. 
Having regard to the aforesaid facts, 
the interim order dated 26.03.2015 is 
modified and it is provided that the 
execution of the award shall remain stayed 
provided appellants deposit a sum of `3 
crore with the Registry of this Court within a 
period of four weeks from today.
List this matter in the month of 
February, 2021.
Urgency application (IA No. 12281 of 
2020) stands disposed of.
