4. Petitioner has filed the present writ petition 
challenging the order dated 07.08.2026, passed by 
respondent no.2, whereby respondent no.2 has 
directed respondent no.3 to supply another person in 
place of petitioner.
5. The case of the petitioner as per averment made 
in the writ petition is that petitioner is working as 
Senior Manager-IT, with respondent no.2 through 
different outsourcing agencies from time to time
since 2011.
6. By the impugned order respondent no.2 has 
directed respondent no.3 to supply another set of 
outsourcing employees in place of petitioner. Some 
imputation has also been leveled against the 
petitioner.
7. Learned counsel for petitioner submits that no 
complaint, which is with respondent no.2, was forwarded to the petitioner or no show-cause notice 
was issued to petitioner. 
8. Learned counsel for respondent no.2 submits 
that there are complaints against petitioner, 
therefore, this impugned order has been passed by
respondent no.2 for replacing petitioner.
9. Learned counsel for respondent no.2 admits 
that as on today the veracity and genuineness of the 
complaint has not been examined and without 
examining the truthfulness of the complaints such a 
harsh decision should not be taken against a person 
who has been working for last so many years.
10. Respondent(s) may file counter affidavit(s) 
within four weeks.
11. Issue notice to respondent no.3 returnable 
within four weeks.
12. Steps to be taken within three days.
13. List this case on 20.11.2026 showing name of 
Mr. R.P. Singh, learned counsel for respondent no.2.
14. In the meantime, petitioner shall not be 
disturbed from discharging the duties of Senior 
Manager-IT and he shall be paid his admissible 
remuneration as paid to him earlier regularly.
