2. Heard learned counsel for the parties.2
3. Since the objection to the bail application has
already been filed by the State, the appellant would press 
the bail application today.
4. The prosecution was set in motion when a 
written complaint dated 07.01.2018 was filed by 
informant-Angrej Singh in P.S. Sitarganj stating therein 
that his father was murdered on 17.07.2015, in which he 
was informant; in the said case, the cross examination 
(Jirah) was left to be made; the accused persons of that 
incident were pressurizing upon him for compromise. 
Due to this enmity on 07.01.2018 at about 01:15 p.m., 
when his cousins-Daljeet Singh, Rajwinder Singh, S/o 
Gurmukh Singh and Kuldeep Singh, S/o Surjeet Singh 
and relatives of the informant; Harbans Singh, S/o 
Jageer Singh were participating in Nagar Kirtan, when 
they reached near Old Mandi Gate, Harjeet Singh @ Kala, 
S/o Manveer Singh, Prabhjot Singh @ Jota, S/o Sarjeet 
Singh, Gurpej Singh, S/o Jageer Singh, Baljeet Singh @ 
Jeeta S/o Manveer Singh, Tarsem Singh S/o Mukhtyar 
Singh, Jajpal Singh S/o Nirvair Singh, R/O Village 
Dehda, P.S. Sitarganj, District Udham Singh Nagar 
attacked the informant and his companions-Daljeet 
Singh, Rajwinder Singh, Kuldeep Singh and Harbans 
Singh with sharp-edged weapons, Lathi, Danda and 
Tamancha with common intention to kill them. In the 
said incident, co-accused-Harjeet Singh @ Kala inflicted 
a fatal blow on the neck of Daljeet Singh with sword and 
stabbed sword in the stomach of Harbans Singh; 
co-accused-Tarsem Singh inflicted on the head of 
Rajwinder Singh with Gandasa which landed at the right 
side of Rajwinder’ face and right hand. Co-accused￾Prabhjot Singh shot a fire from Tamancha (country made 3
pistol) and other above mentioned accused chased them 
with the weapons in their hands with the intention to kill 
them; in between Rajwinder Singh and Kuldeep Singh 
saving their lives fled from the spot. In this incident, 
Daljeet Singh and Harbans Singh died on the spot; 
injured-Rajwinder Singh was taken to Krishna Hospital, 
Haldwani by the informant-Angrej Singh and other 
people. 
5. After investigation, the charge-sheet was 
submitted and on conclusion of trial, the 
appellant/applicant-Jagveer Singh was convicted as 
stated above along with other co-accused. 
6. Learned Senior Advocate appearing for the 
appellant submits that appellant/applicant is not named 
in the FIR and no charge-sheet was filed against him. 
According to learned Senior Advocate, the name of the 
appellant/applicant came in evidence on examination of 
a CCTV footage of the incident by the prosecution 
witnesses and he was summoned to face the trial by 
invoking the provisions of Section 319 of the Cr.P.C.
7. It is vehemently argued by learned Senior 
Advocate appearing for the appellant/applicant that from 
CCTV footage only the presence of appellant/applicant is
seen on the spot and no overt action could be seen and 
attributed to him during the incident. He further submits 
that though the role of exhortation was given to the 
appellant/applicant, but he has been convicted by 
learned trial court under Sections 302/307 IPC with the 
aid of Section 149 IPC. He also submits that since no role 
has been assigned to the appellant/applicant and no 
overt action has been attributed to him; he cannot be 
convicted with the aid of Section 149 IPC.4
8. In order to buttress his argument, learned 
Senior Advocate appearing for the appellant/applicant 
relied upon the judgment of Hon’ble Apex Court in the 
case of Daya Kishan vs. State of Haryana; reported in 
(2010) 5 SCC 81 and submits that in order to convict a 
person with the aid of Section 149 IPC some overt action 
is needed. 
9. Per contra, learned Deputy Advocate General 
for the State submits that though the 
appellant/applicant was put to trial after invoking the 
provisions of Section 319 of the Cr.P.C. yet that cannot 
be a sole ground to release the appellant/applicant on 
bail. He vehemently argued that the presence of 
appellant/applicant-Jagveer Singh is evident from the 
CCTV footage wherein he was clearly seen and identified 
by the witnesses and from CCTV footage, he is seen in 
scuffle with Daljeet Singh and Rajwinder Singh (page 98 
of the paper book 13.17 min 29 sec.) that is sufficient to 
attract Section 149 IPC for convicting the 
appellant/applicant. He further relied upon the para of 
22 of the judgment of Hon’ble Apex Court in the case of 
Prabhu Dayal vs. State of Rajasthan; reported in 
(2018) 8 SCC 127 and submits that a member of 
unlawful assembly can be prosecuted for the criminal act
of the unlawful assembly and it need not to be proved 
that he had committed overt act. 
10. Apart from that, it is argued that by learned 
State Counsel that this is a double murder case and 
moreover, the murdered persons are the witnesses in the 
murder trial of father of informant-Angrej Singh (PW1). 
Co-accused Harjeet Singh @ Kala and Prabhjot Singh @ 
Jota are the accused in the murder trial of father of 5
informant-Angrej Singh. He also argued that the murder 
was committed by the appellant/applicant with other 
co-accused in the midst of crowd in Nagar Kirtan and in 
broad daylight at about 01:15 p.m.
11. Learned counsel appearing for the informant 
also addressed the Court and vehemently opposed the 
bail application on the same ground as argued by 
learned Deputy Advocate General for the State.
12. Having considered the rival submissions of 
learned counsel for the parties and having gone through 
the paper book of the appeal, since the gruesome murder 
was committed by a group of people in unlawful 
assembly of which the appellant/applicant was also a 
member, he is not entitled to be released on bail. 
Accordingly, bail application is hereby rejected. 
13. List in due course.
14. It is made clear that the observations made 
hereinabove are only for the purpose of deciding the 
present bail application and will not affect the final 
merits of the case.
