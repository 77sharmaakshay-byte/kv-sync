1. Mr. Shailendra Nauriyal, learned counsel for the
applicant.
2. The instant petition is moved under Section 528
of BNSS, 2023 by the applicants for challenging the
proceeding of Complaint Case No. 4755 of 2024
(Vandana Dadi Vs. Sagar Dadi and another) pending
in the court of 4
th Addl. CJM, Dehradun wherein
applicants have been summoned to face the trial for
the offence punishable under Section 498-A, 323, 504,
506 IPC and under Section 3 / 4 of the Dowry
Prohibition Act.
3. It is argued by the learned counsel for the
applicant that applicant no. 1 married with the
respondent no. 2 in the year 2021 and from the
wedlock, there is one female child, however, due to
some differences in between both of them, respondent
no. 2 started living separately since 2023. He further
submits that allegations, as alleged, are general in
nature and prior to lodging of the complaint, applicant
no. 1 filed a suit under Section 9 of the Hindu Marriage
Act for restitution of conjugal rights, which was
decreed ex parte on 07.06.2025 and as a counter blast to
this proceeding, the instant complaint has been filed.
He also submits that as on date, applicant is ready to
resolve all the issues with respondent no. 2 and ready
to live with her.
4. All these aspects require scrutiny.
