COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
SA No.81 of 2024
Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
Mr. Mahesh Bhatt, learned counsel for 
the appellant.
2. Mr. Vaibhav Mahtolia, learned counsel 
for the respondent.
3. Stay vacation application (IA/5/2025)
has been filed by the respondent to vacate the 
interim order dated 23.04.2025. 
4. In paragraph no.5 of accompanying 
affidavit to the stay vacation application, it 
has been stated that, in view of the law laid 
down by the Hon’ble Supreme Court in U. 
Sudheera & Others Vs. C. Yashodha & Others 
2005 LiveLaw(SC) 79, interim order cannot be 
passed in Second Appeal without framing 
substantial question of law.
5. Learned counsel for the respondent 
submits that there is delay of 210 days in 
filing this second appeal and without 
condoning the delay an interim order has 
been granted in the second appeal.
6. Learned counsel for the appellant seeks 
and is granted one week’s time to argue on 
the question as to whether any interim order 
can be granted in second appeal, more 
particularly, in view of the judgment passed 
by the Hon’ble Supreme Court in U. 
Sudheera & Others (supra).
7. List this case on 03.07.2025.
8. Urgency Application (IA/4/2025) stands 
disposed of accordingly.
(Subhash Upadhyay,J.)
