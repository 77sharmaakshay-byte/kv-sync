SA No.81 of 2024
Hon’ble Siddhartha Sah, J.
Mr. Mahesh Bhatt, Advocate for the 
appellant.
2. Mr. Vaibhav Mahtolia, Advocate for the 
respondent/plaintiff.
3. By means of this second appeal, the 
defendant/appellant has assailed the 
judgment and order dated 14.09.2023 
passed by the learned 1st Additional District 
Judge, Kotwali, District Nainital in Civil 
Appeal No.7 of 2023, whereby, the appeal 
filed by the plaintiff/respondent has been 
allowed and the judgment and order dated 
18.04.2023 passed by the trial court in Civil 
Suit No.72 of 2021, has been set aside. 
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
5. The plaintiff/respondent had filed the 
suit for mandatory injunction and the same 
was dismissed by the 2nd Additional Civil 
Judge, Haldwani, District Nainital vide ex 
parte judgment and order dated 18.04.2023.
In the said judgment, in para no.10, the trial 
court had categorically held that the plaintiff 
has failed to prove the contents of the sale 
deed and also Khatauni does not appear to 
be trustworthy. In addition, the trial court 
also held that the Halka Patwari was also 
produced only on the basis of oral evidence.
No person can be deemed owner of 
immovable property and thus proceeded to 
dismiss the suit. The matter was agitated in 
appeal by the plaintiff and vide the impugned 
judgment and order dated 14.09.2023, the Court of the 1st Additional District Judge, 
Haldwani, District Nanital, has allowed the 
said civil appeal and set aside the judgment 
dated 18.04.2023 passed by the trial court.
6. Perusal of the said judgment dated 
14.09.2023 would reveal that firstly, the first 
appellate court has failed to frame points for 
determination as envisaged under the 
provisions of Order 41 Rule 31 of CPC. 
Secondly, the first appellate court has relied 
upon the report of Halka Patwari and who has 
been summoned as a witness. Thus, on the 
basis of the aforesaid facts, the first appellate 
court came to the conclusion that plaintiff has 
succeeded to prove herself the owner of the 
property in question and the defendant has 
encroached upon 148 sq.ft. of the land and 
thus proceeded to allow the said civil appeal. 
Since the first appellate court has failed to 
frame any point of determination and simply 
relied upon the report of the Halka Patwari 
which does not clarify the demarcation of the 
respective properties of the parties.
7. Per contra, the counsel for the 
plaintiff/respondent has made a submission 
that the plaintiff is the owner of the property 
in question.
8. After having gone through the entire 
record and having heard the submissions of 
the counsel for the parties, the present 
second appeal deserves to be admitted on 
the following substantial questions of law:-
“(i) Whether it is incumbent upon the 
1st appellate court to frame points for 
determination as per the provisions of Order 
41 Rule 31 CPC?
(ii) Whether the suit of the 
plaintiff/respondent could have been decreed 
in the absence of clear identification of the 
properties of the respective parties by means 
of a commission?”9. Summon the Trial Court Record.
10. List this case on 30.04.2026.
11. Since the appeal has been admitted and 
after considering the entire facts and 
circumstances of the case, the effect and 
operation of the judgment and order dated 
14.09.2023 passed by the 1st Additional 
District Judge, Haldwani, District Nainital 
shall remain stayed.
