The appellants herein have filed an application 
wrongly heading it to be under Order 41 Rule 1,
though it ought to have been under Order 41 Rule 27. It 
would be treated to be an application under Order 41 
Rule 27, which is yet to be taken into consideration 
because the appeals are yet to be argued on admission 
stage.
The respondents are represented through Mr. 
Harshpal Sekhon, Advocate, who submits that he has 
received instructions from the respondents today itself,
and he will be filling his authority on their behalf. The 
said applications are taken on record subject to the 
objections if any, to be filed by the respondents, which 
is to be considered at the stage of final hearing of the 
second appeal, yet again subject to the condition that 
the appeals are admitted. 
The counsel for the respondents may file his 
objection to the application under Order 41 Rule 27 
C.P.C., within a period of two weeks.
List this second appeal after two weeks.
