The appeal was dismissed for non 
prosecution on 27.05.2013. Restoration 
application has been filed along with delay 
condonation application as the restoration 
application was filed belatedly. Notices were 
issued to the respondents, in spite of notices 
they have not filed any objection as yet. 
Although Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate is 
represented respondent No. 2. 
In view of the above, fact, the delay 
condonation application is allowed as the 
ground urged for condoning the delay is bona 
fide. 
Also heard on the restoration application. 
The grounds are sufficient to allow the 
restoration application. 
Accordingly, the restoration application is 
allowed. The order dated 27.05.2013 is hereby 
recalled. 
Let the appeal be restored to its original 
number. 
List this appeal for admission in the week 
commencing 25.11.2013.
