Mr. Akshay Pradhan, Advocate, for the 
applicant in IA No. 13946 of 2021. 
Out of the subsequent purchasers, one of 
whom happens to be Mrs. Deepti Singhal, is an 
applicant to the Application No. 13946 of 2021, 
which has been filed under the head of 
Impleadment Application, without referring to the 
provisions, under which, it has been filed. Though 
the pleadings in support thereto is based upon the 
conferment of right, on the proposed applicant to 
the Impleadment Application, by virtue of the 
subsequent sale deed executed in her favour. 
If a right is devolved on the basis of the 
subsequent purchase made during the pendency of 
the lease, the subsequent assignment made of the 
disputed property would be independently 
considered within the ambit of the provisions 
contained under Order 22 Rule 10 of the CPC. Mr. Akashay Pradhan, the learned counsel 
for the applicant to the Impleadment Application, 
seeks to file an additional supporting affidavit to the 
Impleadment Application, clarifying as to under 
which provisions of law, the said Impleadment 
Application has been filed. 
He may do so on or before the next date 
fixed. 
List these Second Appeals on 18th July, 2022. 
In the meantime, Mr. Piyush Garg, the 
learned counsel for the respondents, may also file 
his objection, to the application under Order 41 
Rule 27 of the CPC, in compliance of the earlier 
order.
