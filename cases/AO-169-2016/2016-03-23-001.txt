AO No.169 of 2016
Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
None 
 Mr. Pankaj Purohit, Advocate for the 
appellant/owner. 
 Learned counsel has submitted that the joint 
written statement filed by him along with his 
driver but unfortunately, no such averment, as 
regards the existence of valid registration 
certificate, driving licence, permit and fitness, was 
ever averred by their counsel in such statement. 
These all papers, though were valid, but were not 
available in the pleadings, which has resulted into 
passing of the impugned award against him. 
 Here, in the appeal, such papers have been 
produced along with an application under Order 41 
Rule 27 r/w Section 151 CPC requesting this Court 
that the same may be accepted on the record. 
 The Court will hear on such application 
after seeking the response of claimants/ 
respondents as well as of the insurance company 
but at the same time, is not inclined to permit the 
appellant/owner to go scot-free without depositing 
even a single penny. 
 The appeal is thus, admitted subject to 
deposit of a sum of rupees five lakh by the 
appellant, within three weeks, before the 
concerned Tribunal adjusting the statutory deposit 
made by him in this Court. 
 Summon the LCR. 
 Issue notices to the respondents. 
 Steps be taken by 28.3.2016. 
 It is, however, directed that if the amount is 
deposited, as directed by the Court hereinabove, 
then the execution of the impugned award shall 
remain stayed. 
 Stay application (CLMA 2681/16) stands 
disposed of accordingly. 
 Let a copy of this order be issued today 
itself to the appellant on payment of usual charges. 
 
 
 (Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.) 
 23.03.2016
Rdang
