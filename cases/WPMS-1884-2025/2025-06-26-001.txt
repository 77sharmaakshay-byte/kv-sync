Heard on Interim Relief Application No. 1 
of 2025.
It is the case of the petitioner that for the 
year 2024-2025, she alongwith respondent 
no.6 was allotted Indian made liquor shop at 
Landhaur, District Haridwar. This year both, 
the petitioner as well as the respondent no.6 
filed separate applications for allotment. The 
allotment was made in favour of the petitioner. 
She has started running her business. But 
suddenly, by an order dated 23.06.2025 of the
respondent no.1/ the Secretary, State of 
Uttarakhand it was observed that the 
petitioner was not eligible for allotment of the 
liquor shop and the respondent no.3, the 
District Magistrate, Haridwar was directed to 
proceed further, who in tearing hurry, on 
23.06.2025 itself cancelled the license allotted 
in favour of the petitioner and also passed 
certain other orders.
By means of interim relief application 
the petitioner seeks stay the effect and 
operation of the impugned orders.
In fact, the order passed by the 
respondent no.1/the Secretary, State of Uttarakhand on 23.06.2025 in the 
proceedings under Section 11 (2) of the U.P. 
Excise Act, 1910 has been tendered for the 
perusal of the Court, which is taken on record.
Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the respondent no.1/the 
Secretary, State of Uttarakhand had passed 
the impugned order without affording an 
opportunity of hearing to the petitioner. He 
would submit that the petitioner is already 
running a shop under the license. Her shop, 
in fact, was sealed in the night of 23.06.2025 
itself and now the respondents/authorities are 
proceeding to grant license of the same shop 
by way of resettling. Therefore, while staying 
the operation of the impugned order, the 
respondents/authorities may be directed not 
to proceed further for allotting the same shop 
or resettling it, in any manner, to any other 
person.
Perusal of the order dated 23.06.2025 
passed by the respondent no.1/the Secretary, 
State of Uttarakhand, does not reveal that the 
petitioner was afforded an opportunity of 
hearing before observing that she has not 
been eligible for allotment of license.
Therefore, as an interim measure, till the 
next date of listing, the 
respondents/authorities are directed not to 
proceed further of allotting the shop to any 
other person; the shop shall not be resettled 
till the next date of listing. 
A statement has been given that the 
shop has already been sealed. This order shall 
not be construed as an order, opening the 
shop.
The Court requests learned State 
counsel to communicate this order to the 
respondents/authorities today itself.
Interim relief application stands 
disposed of accordingly.
