Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Kurban Ali, Advocate, for the 
petitioner.
Mr. Shakti Singh, Advocate, for the 
respondent. 
This writ petition arises out of the 
concurrent judgments which had been rendered 
in the proceedings under Section 15 of the
Provincial Small Causes Courts Act. 
This Court while granting, the interim 
order on 14.07.2021 had directed the 
petitioner/tenant to deposit the entire decreetal 
amount, the same has been deposited in 
compliance thereto. The petitioner who is 74 
years of age has filed the IA No.2 of 2022 
(Money Withdrawal Application) praying, that 
the decreetal amount, which has been thus
directed to be deposited may be permitted to be 
withdrawn by her.
Considering the fact, that it is a monetary 
liability which has been settled in favour of the 
respondent/landlord, which already stand 
deposited. The decreetal amount thus deposited 
by the petitioner in compliance of the interim 
order dated 14.07.2021, is permitted to be 
withdrawn by the respondent/landlord.
Accordingly, the money withdrawal 
application would stands disposed of.
Interim order already granted would 
remain extended till the next date of listing. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
