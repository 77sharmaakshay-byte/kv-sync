Mr. Kurban Ali, learned counsel for the Petitioner 
through VC.
2. Mr. Govind Singh Latwal, learned counsel holding 
brief of Mr. Chandra Shekhar Joshi, learned counsel for the 
Respondent.
3. The present writ petitions under Article 227 of the 
Constitution of India relates to a tenancy issue regarding the 
tenancy of a shops, which have been under the Petitioners 
possession since 1985, which is owned by the father-in-law of 
the Respondent, namely, Amarnath and the shops were taken 
on a lease for a period of 18 years.
4. Learned counsel for the Petitioner appearing through 
VC online makes a statement that after late Amarnath, his 
daughter-in-law Smt. Beena Agarwal, was in possession of the shops. Learned counsel for the Petitioner further submits that 
Ms. Beena Agarwal, too has passed away.
5. This Court has been informed that Smt. Beena Agarwal 
W/o Rajendra Kumar Agarwal, passed away on 28.08.2023, 
and this fact was communicated to the Petitioners/tenants as 
per learned counsel for the Respondent. Whereas, learned 
counsel for the Petitioners makes a submission that it is not 
so, the information so received with a delay of about one year.
6. Substitution applications have been filed along with 
delay condonation applications. There is a delay in filing the 
substitution applications. Accordingly, the delay in filing 
substitution applications is condoned. Delay condonation 
application is allowed. For the reasons indicated, substitution 
applications are allowed. Let the necessary amendments be 
incorporated in the cause title of the writ petitions.
7. List this matter on 12th September, 2025.
 (Ashish Naithani, J.)
