Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Dharmendra Barthwal, learned counsel for 
the revisionist.
2. As per the office report there is a delay of 14 days 
in preferring the instant revision. The delay is 
sufficiently explained and as such delay condonation 
application is allowed and the delay is condoned.
3. Admit the revision.
4. The instant revision has been preferred by the 
revisionist against the order dated 16.07.2025 passed by 
the Principal Judge, Family Court, Nainital, in 
Miscellaneous Criminal Case No. 57 of 2022 ‘Smt. 
Sunita Bhatt vs. Girish Chandra Bhatt’, whereby, the 
application preferred under section 125 of Cr.P.C. was 
allowed and the revisionist was directed to pay a sum 
of Rs. 5,000/- per month as an interim maintenance.
5. Taking into consideration that both of them 
married in the year 2021 and admittedly the dispute is 
nothing but a matrimonial dispute, therefore, this court 
is of the view that the efforts may be made for their 
amicable settlement.
6. Issue notice to the respondent.
7. Steps within 48 hours.
8. Let both the parties shall remain present in court 
on 23.03.2026.
