Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. S.K. Shandilya, learned counsel for 
the applicant.
2. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned Addl. 
Advocate General along with Mr. S.C. 
Dumka, learned A.G.A. for the State.
3. The applicant seeks anticipatory bail in 
connection with FIR No. 0217 of 2022, 
registered at Police Station Kotwali 
Dehradun, District Dehradun, for the 
offences punishable under Sections 420, 
467, 468, 471, 120-B of I.P.C.
4. Learned counsel appearing for the 
applicant would submit that the applicant is 
innocent and has been falsely implicated in 
the present case; that, the applicant had 
earlier approached this Court by filing 
WPCRL No.971 of 2022, wherein, on the 
instructions received from the State that no 
forged document had, till then, been found 
during the course of investigation and that 
the investigation was still in progress, the 
Coordinate Bench of this Court, vide order dated 23.05.2026, granted interim 
protection to the applicant; that, pursuant 
to the said order, the applicant has 
remained protected during the course of 
investigation.
5. He would further submit that the 
investigation has now been concluded and 
the concerned Investigating Officer has 
submitted the charge-sheet before the court 
below; that, the applicant has duly 
cooperated with the investigation and there 
is no allegation that she has misused the 
protection granted by this Court or has 
obstructed the investigation in any manner; 
that the similarly situated co-accused has 
already been granted anticipatory bail; that, 
in view of the completion of investigation 
and submission of the charge-sheet, no 
useful purpose would be served by 
subjecting the applicant to custodial 
interrogation; that the applicant is ready 
and willing to appear before the court below 
and to cooperate with the proceedings; and 
that she undertakes to abide by all such 
terms and conditions as may be imposed by 
this Court while granting her protection 
from arrest.
6. Learned State Counsel would 
vehemently oppose the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
applicant. However, she seeks and is 
granted three weeks' time to file a detailed 
counter affidavit, bringing on record the 
relevant facts, the material collected during 
investigation and the specific role, if any, 
attributed to the applicant.
8. In the interest of justice, four weeks time is granted to learned State Counsel to 
file a counter affidavit. 
9. List thereafter.
10. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties and upon perusal of the material 
available on record, this Court finds that the 
matter requires consideration. However, 
considering the nature of the allegations, the 
material presently available on record, the 
specific submissions advanced on behalf of 
the applicant regarding his alleged role, and 
the fact that the State has sought time to 
place the relevant material on record, this 
Court is of the view that the applicant 
deserves protection by way of interim 
anticipatory bail till the matter is considered 
further.
11. Accordingly, as an interim measure, 
without expressing any opinion on the 
merits of the case, it is directed that, in the 
event of arrest of the applicant during the 
course of investigation, he shall be released 
on interim anticipatory bail on furnishing a 
personal bond and two reliable sureties, 
each in the like amount, to the satisfaction 
of the Arresting Officer, subject to the 
following conditions:
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation and shall appear before the 
Investigating Officer as and when 
required;
(ii) The applicant shall not, directly or 
indirectly, make any inducement, threat 
or promise to any person acquainted 
with the facts of the case, so as to 
dissuade such person from disclosing 
such facts to the Court or to any police 
officer;
(iii) The applicant shall not leave India without the prior permission of the 
concerned Court;
(iv) The applicant shall not tamper with 
the evidence or otherwise obstruct or 
hamper the investigation in any manner;
(v) The applicant shall furnish his
current mobile number/address to the 
Investigating Officer and shall keep the 
same operational during the course of 
investigation.
12. It is made clear that, in case the 
applicant fails to cooperate with the 
investigation or violates any of the aforesaid 
conditions, it shall be open to the State to 
bring the said fact to the notice of this Court 
and seek appropriate orders, including 
cancellation of the interim anticipatory bail.
