1. Mr. Piyush Garg, learned counsel and 
Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, learned counsel for the 
petitioners, Ms. Rajni Supyal, learned Brief Holder 
for the State, Mr. Shobhit Saharia, learned counsel 
for the High Court, Mr. Sanjeev Aggarwal, learned

Bhatia, learned counsel for respondent Nos. 4, 8, 
13 and 14 and Mr. Vipul Sharma, learned counsel 
for the applicant in Impleadment Applications.
2. The Amendment Application has been 
filed by the petitioners, seeking formal 
amendment in relief No. 2 of the main prayer. On 
this amendment, the objections have been filed by 
respondent No. 4, wherein in para 3, the reference 
of the judgment of the Hon’ble Apex Court in the 
case of Union of India Vs. Manpreet Singh 
Poonam, (2022) 6 SCC 105 as well as in the case of 
Bihar State Electricity Board & others Vs. 
Dharamdeo Das, Civil Appeal No. 6977 of 2015
decided on 23.07.2024 has been given, wherein it 
has been held that seniority can neither be 
reckoned from the date when the vacancy arises, 
nor it can be granted retrospectively unless the 
service rules specifically provide for such a situation.
3. We have considered the objections. 
Since the amendments as sough are formal in 
nature and consequential to the relief as sought 
earlier, therefore, the same is allowed. Let the 
necessary amendment be carried in the relief 
clause of the petition and file the amended copy.
4. The Impleadment Application Nos. 
IA/8/2024 & IA/9/2024 filed in WPSB No. 348 of 
2022, IA/8/2024 & IA/9/2024 filed in WPSB No. 
576 of 2021, as well as IA/2/2024 & IA/3/2024 
filed in WPSB No. 621 of 2022 have been moved by 
Mr. Vipul Sharma, learned counsel for impleading 
the persons as party respondent.
5. However, since the pleadings are 
already exchanged, therefore, at this stage, the 
persons on whose behalf the Impleadment 
Applications have been moved, are permitted to 
intervene in the matter and advance their 
arguments at the time of final hearing.
