Issue Rule Nisi.
Learned counsel for respondent nos. 1, 2 
and 13 have already been served with advance 
copies. They accept notices on behalf of the said 
respondents. 
Issue notice to rest of the respondents by 
registered post acknowledgment due, returnable 
within four weeks. 
Postal requisites be filed within seven days. 
At present, we are not fixing any date in 
this matter in view of the fact that another Writ 
Petition No. 576 (SB) of 2021 with the same 
issues and prayers is pending before this Court, 
which was taken up by the Bench comprising 
one of us – S.K. Mishra, J and Justice Shri 
Ravindra Maithani. 
Learned counsel for both the parties 
express their anxiety over the fact that two 
different coordinate Benches are hearing two 
different writ petitions involving same facts, 
issues, prayer and parties. 
We are of the opinion that this constitution 
of Bench is not made by design. Hence, it could 
always be rectified. Hence, Registrar (Judicial) is directed to take instructions from Hon’ble the 
Chief Justice for assigning both the writ petitions 
to one Bench.
