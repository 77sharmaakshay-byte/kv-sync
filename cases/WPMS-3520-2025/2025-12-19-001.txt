2. Mr. Sudhir Kumar Nailwal, learned Standing

3. This writ petition has been filed under Article 227 
of the Constitution of India challenging the order dated 
25.09.2025 (Annexure-5 to the writ petition), passed by 
the learned Settlement Officer (Consolidation), 
Rudrapur, Udham Singh Nagar in Appeal No.316 of 
2025 under Section 11(1) of the U.P. Consolidation of 
Holdings Act, Kushal Singh Bisht vs. State, whereby the 
parties were restrained from sale, mortgage, or transfer 
in any other manner.
4. It is contended by the learned Senior Advocate 
appearing for the petitioner that the appeal in question 
was filed against the judgment and order dated 
20.09.2011 passed by the Consolidation Officer, 
Kashipur, in Case No. 981, Ravi Nandan Sharma vs. 
Kala Singh, whereby the application filed under Section 
12 of the U.P. Consolidation of Holdings Act was 
allowed on 28.08.2025. He further contends that the 
appeal was highly belated by 14 years; therefore, 
without condoning the delay, no interim order could 
have been passed by the appellate court. He also 2
submits that no notice of the appeal was ever served 
upon the petitioner. The averment to this effect has 
been made in paragraph 7 of the writ petition.
5. I have gone through the impugned order and the 
record of the case. This Court is of the view that no 
interim order could have been passed in an appeal filed 
after a delay, accompanied by an application for 
condonation of delay, without first condoning the 
delay.
6. In such view of the matter, case is made out for 
interference. 
7. Issue notice to the respondent no.3, returnable 
within six weeks.
8. Steps to be taken a week.
9. List on 10.03.2026.
10. In the meantime, respondents may file counter 
affidavit.
11. Till the next date of listing, the effect and 
operation of the impugned order dated 25.09.2025 
(annexure-5 to the writ petition) shall remain stayed.
12. Stay application (IA No.1/2025) stands disposed 
of accordingly.
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
