2. Learned counsel for the State contends 
that the State possesses the authority to amend 
Service Rules, rendering such amendments 
immune from challenge by employees. To 
substantiate this position, reliance is placed on 
paragraph 10 of the Hon'ble Supreme Court's 
judgment in P.U. Joshi & Ors. Vs. Accountant 
General, Ahmedabad & Ors., reported in (2003) 
2 SCC 632.
3. In opposition, learned counsel for the 
petitioners asserts that vested rights accrued to 
the petitioners under the existing Service Rules 
cannot be divested by retrospective 
amendments thereto. This submission draws 
support from paragraphs 20 and 24 of the 
Hon'ble Supreme Court's decision in Chairman, 
Railway Board & Ors. Vs. C.R. Rangadhamaiah 
& Ors., reported in (1997) 6 SCC 623.
