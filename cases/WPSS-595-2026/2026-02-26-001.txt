Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J.
Mr. Sanjay Bhatt and Mr. Prem 
Prakash Bhatt, Advocates for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Atul Bhatt, Standing Counsel for 
the Union of India/respondents. 
3. Petitioner participated in a selection 
for appointment as Constable (GD) in a 
combined selection for Central Armed 
Police Forces. According to him, he was 
declared successful in the written and 
physical examination, however, he was 
declared medically unfit, by holding that 
he has ‘tied tongue’. Petitioner was 
thereafter advised to appear before 
Review Medical Board, which was held 
on 18.04.2024 at 23rd Battalion, ITBP, 
Dehradun.
4. According to the petitioner, he was 
found to be medically fit in the Review 
Medical Board and his name was 
included in the select list and 
recommendation was also made to the 
competent authority for issuing 
appointment order, however, on 
06.02.2025, Commandant, CISF, NTPC 
Unit, Dadri issued a communication to 
the petitioner informing him that his left 
thumb impression, as given in biometric 
system, is not matching and petitioner 
was advised to re-appear in the office of 
Commandant, CISF, Dadri, on 
11.02.2025. According to the petitioner, 
he did appear at the scheduled time, 
place and date, however, result thereof 
is not being declared. By means of this writ petition, petitioner has sought the 
following relief:-
“i-A writ, order or direction in the nature 
of mandamus commanding the Respondents to 
declare the result of biometric matching of 
finger print of Petitioner and consequently issue 
appointment letter in favour of Petitioner with 
effect from the date other similarly situate 
persons were appointed, pursuant to 
recommendation of appointment made by the 
selecting body and pay all the consequential 
seniority and other benefits arising out of such 
appointment.”
5. This Court finds merit in the 
submission made by learned counsel for 
the petitioner that inaction on the part of 
concerned authorities is wholly 
unwarranted. 
6. List on 23.03.2026.
7. Respondent no. 3 is directed to 
declare the result of biometric matching 
of finger print of petitioner, as held on 
11.02.2025, or to show cause on or 
before the next date of listing.
