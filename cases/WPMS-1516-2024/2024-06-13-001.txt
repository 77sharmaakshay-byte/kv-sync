(2) Petitioner is a builder, who 
constructed a housing project in 
Dehradun. Uttarakhand Pollution Control 
Board passed an order on 5.3.2024, in 
exercise of its statutory powers, whereby 
penalty of ₹83,71,200/- has been imposed 
upon petitioner. Reading of the said order 
reveals that petitioner did not obtain prior 
consent from the State Pollution Control 
Board for raising the construction of 220 
residential apartments. The show cause 
issued to petitioner reveals that the 
housing project built by petitioner was 
releasing untreated water to the tune of 
1.50 lakh litre on road as well as natural 
drain in Sahastradhara Road, Dehradun. 
(3) Learned Counsel for Uttarakhand 
Pollution Control Board submits that if the 
built up area of a housing project is more 
than 20,000/- sq. mt., then environmental 
clearance from the Ministry of 
Environment is also required to be taken, 
which was not taken by the petitioner. He 
further submits that due to these various 
violations of statutory provisions, penalty has been imposed on petitioner, after 
issuing show cause notice to him. 
(4) Per contra, learned Counsel for 
the petitioner submits that petitioner had 
earlier approached the Central 
Government for environmental clearance, 
however, petitioner was asked to approach 
the State Authorities and when petitioner 
applied for consent to establish before 
Uttarakhand Pollution Control Board, his 
prayer was turned down by informing him 
that he should have approached the State 
Government. He further submits that 
ultimately petitioner made application 
before the State Government, however, no 
decision has been taken on his application. 
(5) From the rival submissions made 
by learned Counsel for the parties, it can 
be inferred that petitioner does not have 
environmental clearance and consent to 
establish from the competent authority. 
(6) Let counter affidavit be filed 
within four weeks. Rejoinder affidavit, if 
any, be filed within one week thereafter. 
(7) List on 5.8.2024. 
(8) It is made clear that pendency of 
this writ petition will not preclude the 
respondent from recovering the amount 
indicated in the impugned order.
