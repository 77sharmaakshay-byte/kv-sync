Mr. Prabhat Bohra, learned counsel for the

2. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Standing Counsel

3. Mr. Ramji Shrivastava, learned counsel for the 
respondent nos.3&4-Selecting Body.
4. Present writ petition is filed by the petitioner 
challenging the order dated 12.02.2024 (Annexure 
No.4), whereby the result of the petitioner has not 
been declared by the respondent-Selecting Body on 
the ground that the petitioner was not participated in 
the earlier selection process in the year 2018, which 
was subsequently cancelled/withdrawn by the State 
Government itself. 
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
the batch of the writ petition has been decided by this 
Court vide judgment and order dated 21.11.2023, 
wherein the petitioner was one of the petitioner in
connected writ petition No.1030 of 2022 (S/S) Munni 
Adhikari & Others Vs. State of Uttarakhand & 
Others. This ground that the petitioner was not 
participated in the earlier selection, has been argued 
during the hearing of the earlier batch of writ 
petitions and the same was considered by this Court 
and the order was passed to grant relaxation of age to 
all the petitioners including the petitioner in the 
present writ petition.
6. It is submitted by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that the petitioner has moved a 
representation dated 14.02.2024 (Annexure No.6) 2
explaining all these things before the Selecting Body 
and the same is still pending consideration.
7. A request has been made by the learned counsel 
for the petitioner that the justice would be done, if a 
direction is made to the Selecting Body to decide the 
representation by speaking order in the light of the 
judgment and order rendered by this Court dated 
21.11.2023 in the batch of the writ petition No. 1030 
of 2022(S/S).
8. Learned counsel for the respondent-Selecting 
Body does not propose to have any objection, if such 
a direction is issued by this Court to the Selecting 
Body.
9. Accordingly, writ petition is finally disposed of 
with a direction to respondent-Selecting Body to 
decide the petitioner’s representation dated 
14.02.2024 (Annexure No.6 to the writ petition) 
within a period of 10 days from the date of 
production of certified copy of this order.
10. Pending application(s), if any, also stands 
disposed of.
