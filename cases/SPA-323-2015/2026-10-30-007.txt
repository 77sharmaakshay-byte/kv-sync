4. This intra-court appeal was dismissed vide 
judgment dated 10.10.2018. Today, the matter is 
listed on a Review Application (MCC/7734/2025).
5. For the reasons indicated, the prayer for 
condonation of delay is granted. Delay Condonation 
Application (IA/7735/2025) is allowed. 
6. It is contended by Review Applicant that the land 
in question is not recorded as water-body in the 
Revenue Records, and it is recorded as khaliyan (barn), 
while this Court has dismissed the appeal by treating 
the same to be water-body. It is further contended 
that, as many as 90 residential units were constructed
over the said land for weaker sections of society by 
Municipal Board, Landhora out of the funds provided by 
the State Government, and 90 families belonging to 
weaker sections of the society are residing in those 
residential units. 7. Since this Court, in the judgment, has recorded 
that the land is recorded as khaliyan (barn) in Revenue 
Records, therefore, the said land cannot be treated to 
be a water-body. Since the sole basis, for issuing 
directions to evict the unauthorised occupants, is that 
the land in question is water-body, which appears to be 
factually incorrect, therefore, we are inclined to review 
the order.
8. Accordingly, Review Application 
(MCC/7734/2025) is allowed. 
9. List this petition for final hearing on 04.11.2026.
