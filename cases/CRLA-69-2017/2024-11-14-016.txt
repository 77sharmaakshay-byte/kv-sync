COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
14.11.2024 CRLA No. 69 of 2017
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Shivanand Bhatt, learned counsel 
for the appellants and Mr. G.S. Sandhu, learned 
Additional Advocate General alongwith V.S. Pal, 
learned A.G.A. for the State.
2. Both the appellants have been 
convicted by the trial court for the offences
punishable under Sections 498-A r/w Section 34 
and 304-B r/w section 34 of IPC. The maximum 
sentence, which has been awarded by the trial 
court, is seven years rigorous imprisonment with a 
fine of Rs. 1000/-.
3. Now, learned counsel for the parties 
have informed to this Court that the appellants 
have completed their sentence as well as the 
additional sentence, which was awarded in default 
of payment of fine.
4. Mr. G.S. Sandhu, learned Additional 
Advocate General, on a written instructions, also 
informed to this Court that after completing the 
sentence, the appellants have been released and 
submits that instant appeal be dismissed as 
infructuous.
5. It is very strange that though the appeal was admitted, but, the appeal could not be 
heard. Even the appellants were not enlarged on 
bail. Though after completing the sentence, the 
appellants have been released, but, still the status
of both the appellants as on today, are of 
“convicts”.
6. Now, the question which is required to 
be considered is whether it amounts to great 
injustice with the appellants, whose statutory 
appeals were admitted, but, could not be heard 
within time and ultimately the appellants have 
completed the sentence.
7. Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, learned 
counsel is appointed as an amicus curiae in this 
case to assist this Court.
8. Registry is directed to supply the entire 
paper-book to Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, learned 
counsel within three days.
9. List on 05.12.2024, immediately after 
fresh.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
