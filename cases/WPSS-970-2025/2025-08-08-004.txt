Heard on Stay Application No. 1 of 
2025
It is the case of the petitioners that 
they have been working with the 
respondents/authorities for a long, but 
now, the respondents/authorities are 
orally directing the petitioners not to come 
for work and stopping them from work. 
They want to replace the petitioners from 
other set of workers.
Yesterday, learned State counsel was 
required to get instructions. He would 
submit that out of 19 petitioners, nine
petitioners are working with the 
department as a plantation worker, 
namely, Narayan Singh, Shamim, Gulsher, 
Krishan, Mithu Ram, Laxman Singh, 
Arvind Kumar, Hirden, and Satyam. 
It is the case of the petitioners that 
out of the above, the petitioner Shamim, 
Krishan and Arvind Kumar have been 
working for more than ten years.
Having considered, till the next date 
of listing, the services of the petitionersShamim, Krishan and Arvind Kumar shall 
not be discontinued by the 
respondents/authorities and the other 
petitioners shall not be replaced by other 
set of outsourced persons.
Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
