District Education Officer, Primary Education, 
Pithoragarh, before this Court.

interim order has not been extended, therefore, the 
respondent-contemnor is under duty to comply with the 
order passed by Writ Court.2
6. Respondent no.3-Mr. Tarun Pant, District Education 
Officer, Primary Education, Pithoragarh has stated before 
this Court that if the petitioner come she will be given 
joining and the order will be complied with subject to any 
order passed in SPA.

respondent no.3-Mr. Tarun Pant, District Education 
Officer, Primary Education, Pithoragarh is exempted.
