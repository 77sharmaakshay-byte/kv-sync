COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CLCON No. 102 of 2026
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Tapan Singh, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Devesh Ghildiyal, learned Standing 
Counsel for the respondent/contemnor.
3. Mr. Tarun Pant, District Education 
Officer, Pithoragarh is present in Court.
4. Apparently, the order of the Writ Court 
is not complied with whereby the writ petition 
was allowed and the impugned order was 
quashed and the respondent was directed to 
reinstate the petitioner forthwith with all 
consequential benefits which the petitioner is 
entitled as per law. The writ Court passed the 
order on 23.12.2025 and almost four months 
have been passed and during this period 
neither the judgment has been complied with 
nor assailed by way of filing special appeal 
though the limitation has also been expired.
5. In such view of the matter this Court 
has no option except to frame charge against 
the respondent, who is present in Court. This 
Court is framing the following charge against 
the respondent/contemnor:-
“The respondent/contemnor Mr. Tarun 
Pant, District Education Officer of Pithoragarh 
committed contempt of this Court by willfully 
disobeying the order of the Writ Court dated 
23.12.2025 passed in WPMS No. 3421 of 2018, 
despite having knowledge of the same.”
6. Since, charge has been framed in the 
presence of contemnor, let he may file 
response within a week for the aforesaid 
charge and to show cause why he may be punished for contempt of Court.
7. Put up this matter on 04.06.2026. On that 
day the respondent/contemnor shall remain 
present in Court.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
