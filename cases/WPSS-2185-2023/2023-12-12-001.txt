WPSS No. 2185 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Heard Mr. Sandeep Kothari, Advocate for the 
petitioner.
2. By means of this writ petition, the petitioner 
has challenged the order dated 26.09.2023 
(Annexure No. 12 to the Writ Petition), whereby, a 
representation of the petitioner for permitting her to 
join at the place of posting has been rejected 
pursuant to the order dated 20.05.2022.
3. In short, it is the submission of learned 
counsel for the petitioner that during the Pandemic 
Covid-19, on 20.05.2019, various teachers of the 
respondent department have been given promotion, 
but, due to the Pandemic Covid-19, the posting had
not been made. 
4. Subsequently, when Pandemic Covid-19 was 
over, the respondent department placed various 
teachers on their promotional posting by passing the 
several orders in the month of March, 2021, 
January, 2022 and even in the month of July, 2022.
5. When such order for posting of the petitioner 
was not passed, she filed a claim petition before the 
Tribunal, which is Case No. 127/DB/2023, Seema 
Malhotra Vs. State of Uttarakhand, wherein, the 
learned Tribunal while disposing the said claim 
petition directed the Competent Authority to take decision in the matter of petitioner and the 
petitioner was directed to move a fresh 
representation. The respondent authority vide 
impugned order has rejected the representation of 
the petitioner. 
6. According to the learned counsel for the 
petitioner, the rejection of the representation of the 
petitioner was on a very flimsy ground that due to 
Forgo Rules, 2020, the petitioner cannot be placed 
and posted pursuant to the promotion order dated 
20.05.2020.
7. The learned counsel for the petitioner 
submitted that the promotion order was passed on 
20.05.2020 while the Forgo Rules, 2020 came into 
existence on 07.08.2020 and the same would not be 
applicable and further the order in writing for 
application of the Forgo Rules should have been 
made under Rule 3 of the said Rule.
8. The learned State Counsel prays for and is 
granted four weeks’ time to file counter affidavit in 
the matter.
9. List this matter on 11.01.2024.
