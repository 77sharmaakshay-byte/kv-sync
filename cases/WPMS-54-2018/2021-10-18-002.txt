In Writ Petition No. 54 of 2018, the sole 
petitioner has met with the sad demise on 26th
January, 2021. Hence, the proposed heirs of the 
deceased/petitioner, had filed the Substitution 
Application No. 4229 of 2021, along with Delay 
Condonation Application No. 4230 of 2021, 
explaining the delay of 158 days’, which has 
chanced in filing the Substitution Application. 
Being satisfied with the reasons given in the 
Delay Condonation Application, coupled with the 
fact that, it is not being seriously opposed by the 
respondents, the delay condonation application 
would stand allowed. 
The delay in filing Substitution Application 
would stand condoned. Accordingly, the Substitution Application too 
would stand allowed. 
The learned counsel for the petitioner is 
directed to carry out the necessary amendment in 
the cause title of the Writ Petition, by bringing the 
heirs of the deceased/petitioner on record. 
In WPMS No. 2268 of 2017, the private 
respondents have filed the Impleadment 
Application No. 14343 of 2019, to which, the 
learned counsel for the petitioner, prays for and is 
granted two weeks’ time to file objection. 
Place the copy of this order in the connected 
Writ Petition. 
List thereafter.
