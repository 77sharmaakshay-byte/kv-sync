These are the two connected matters. In 
one of the writ petitions, being WPMS No.
2268/2017, an Impleadment Application 
(CLMA/14343/2019), has been filed by as many as 
eight persons who have sought themselves to be 
impleaded in the writ petition. The learned counsel 
for the petitioner was granted time to file objection 
to the said impleadment application which he could 
not do so within the time prescribed. As such, he 
makes a statement at the Bar that the Impleadment 
Application may be allowed.
Accordingly, the Impleadment 
Application would stand allowed.
The learned counsel for the petitioner of 
WPMS No. 2268/2017, would carry out the 
necessary amendment in the cause title of the writ 
petition by impleading the proposed applicants to 
the impleadment application as a party respondents.
List these writ petitions as soon as the 
amendment is carried, and during the intervening 
period, if so advised, the learned counsel for the 
petitioner may file his rejoinder affidavit to the 
counter affidavit filed by respondent Nos. 3 to 5, 
which has already been filed on record. 
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
