CLMA No. 11636 of 2020 (Money Withdrawal Appl.)
In 
A.O. No. 39 of 2020
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. M.K. Goyal, Advocate for the 
appellant.
Mr. Nalin Saun, Advocate for claimants/
respondent nos.1 to 4.
Heard learned counsel for the parties 
through video conferencing.
This money withdrawal application has 
been filed by claimants/respondent nos. 1 to 4 
for withdrawal of the amount of compensation 
deposited by the Insurance Company before 
learned Tribunal. 
Learned Tribunal had awarded a sum of
` 23,72,144/- as compensation to the 
claimants/respondent nos. 1 to 4.
Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that the appellant has deposited the 
entire awarded amount with the concerned 
tribunal in terms of the order passed by this 
Court.
Having regard to the facts and 
circumstances of the case, money withdrawal 
application is partly allowed. 
Learned Tribunal is directed to release a 
sum of ` 12,00,000/- of the deposited amount, 
in favour of claimants/respondent nos. 1 to 4 
proportionately, without asking any security. 
The remaining amount shall be invested in an 
interest bearing scheme of a nationalized 
bank.
 (Manoj K. Tiwari, J.)
