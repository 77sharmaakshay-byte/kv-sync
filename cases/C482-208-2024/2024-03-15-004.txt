COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C-482 No. 208 of 2024
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Abhishek Verma, counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr Saurabh Pande, A.G.A. for the State.
3. Counsel for the petitioner would submit that a contract 
between the petitioner/accused and the complainant to 
produce a documentary film on Lord Shiva, namely, Khoj has 
been held; that, said documentary film was completed and 
ready for production, the respondent no.2/complainant had to 
invest Rs.3.5 lacs for which the petitioner/accused sent a legal 
notice to the respondent no.2/complainant through Advocate 
on 24.08.2022 (Annexure No.4 to the petition).
He would further submit that instead of replying and 
making payment of Rs.3.5 lacs, the complainant/respondent 
lodged a false complaint as counterblast against the 
petitioner/accused under Sections 406, 504 & 506 I.P.C. at 
P.S. Muni-ki-Reti, District Tehri Garhwal.
4. State counsel would submit that there are some 
business deals between the petitioner and respondent 
no.2/complainant and also family relationship between them. 
However, he would submit that the proper facts of the case 
would come by filing the counter affidavit. Thus, State 
counsel seeks and is granted six weeks time to file counter 
affidavit.
5. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the 
material available on record.
6. Admit the petition.
7. Issue notices to the respondent no. 2 through ordinary 
process and registered post acknowledgment due as well as 
through e-mail and whatsapp, if available. 
8. Steps to be taken within two weeks.
9. List this case on 07.05.2024.
10. Till the next date of listing, further proceedings of 
Criminal Case No. 916 of 2023 under Section 406, 504 & 506 
I.P.C. Police Station Muni-ki-Reti, Tehri Garhwal pending 
the court of Judicial Magistrate, Narendra Nagar, Tehri 
Garhwal shall remain stayed.
