Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Raunak Pant, learned counsel 
holding brief of Mr. Naresh Pant, learned
counsel for the appellant. 
2. Mr. Tajhar Qayyum, learned counsel 
for respondent Nos.1 & 2/claimants. 
3. Recall Application (IA No. 5 of 2025) 
has been filed by the appellant seeking 
recall/modification of the order dated 
29.08.2025, to the extent it directs the 
appellant to deposit the entire awarded 
amount before the concerned Tribunal, 
excluding the statutory amount.
4. Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that in paragraph no.9 of the order 
dated 29.08.2025, it has been recorded that 
the appellant has deposited the entire 
awarded amount with the concerned Court; 
however, the correct position is that the 
appellant had undertaken to deposit the 
awarded amount within stipulated period 
before the concerned Court.
5. Considering the submissions and to 
correct the inadvertent error, the recall 
application is allowed. Accordingly, the order 
dated 29.08.2025 is modified to the extent 
that the appellant shall deposit the entire 
awarded amount before the concerned 
Court within a period of two weeks from 
today.
6. List this case on the date already 
fixed, i.e., 13.11.2025.
