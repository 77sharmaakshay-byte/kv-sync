 Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the record. 
 Respondent no.3 has filed compliance 
affidavit in which it is submitted that 
representation of the petitioner dated 
08.05.2012 has been rejected on 30.10.2012. By 
the said order, the application of the petitioner 
seeking compassionate appointment has been 
rejected. 
 In view of the fact that representation of 
the petitioner for compassionate appointment 
has been decided, the contempt petition has 
rendered infructuous and same is dismissed as 
such. 
 It is open for the petitioner to challenge 
the order of rejection before appropriate Court. 
 Notices issued against the respondents 
stand discharged.
