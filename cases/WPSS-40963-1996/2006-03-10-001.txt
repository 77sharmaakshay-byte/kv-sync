2. By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has prayed 
for the following relief:- 
i. to issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding and directing the 
respondents to allow the regular pay in the pay￾scale ofRs. 1100-1710 on the principle of equal pay 
for equal work to the Assistant Teacher appointed 
under dying-in harness Rules, similar to other 
Assistant Teacher of primary schools. 
ii. to issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus to the respondents to allow the Assistant 
Teacher appointed under dying-in-harness Rules, 
the annual increments in the pay and the 
consolidated fixed pay of Rs. 850/- may be 
2006:UHC:1399converted into regular pay-scale giving all the 
benefits, as are admissible to regular pay-scale. 
iii. issue writ or direction in the nature of mandamus 
treating the Assistant Teacher appointed as regular 
teacher and give benefit of regular pay-scale of Rs. 
1100-1710/- from their respective initial 
appointment. 
iv to issue any other writ, order or direction which this 
court may deem fit and proper under the facts and 
circumstances of the case and to which the 
petitioner is entitled in law. 
v. to award cost to the petitioner. 
3. The writ petition was filed in the year 1996, but the 
respondents did not file counter affidavit till date. 
4. The petitioner, who was appointed as Assistant Teacher 
on 08.05.1996 by the respondents under dying-in-harness 
Rules, has prayed that he may be paid regular salary as par with 
the teachers who are appointed on regular basis instead of 
paying Rs. 850/- per month, which is being paid to the 
petitioner. 
5. Counsel for the petitioner has submitted that the 
petitioner is still in job and is getting Rs. 850/- per month, he 
may be paid pay scale as Assistant Teacher as par with the 
untrained teachers who have been appointed as Assistant 
Teacher on regular basis, as the petitioner was appointed under 
dying-in-harness Rules. 
6. Since the appointment of the petitioner was made under 
dying-in-harness Rules, it is well settled law that appointment 
made under dying-in-harness Rules shall be treated as regular 
appointment and, as such, the incumbents are entitled to get 
salary as par with the regular employee. 
2006:UHC:13997. In the circumstance, it will be proper and justified to 
direct the respondents that in case the petitioner has 
completed the training which is required for trained teachers, 
he shall be paid pay-scale of the teachers who have been 
appointed on regular basis as trained teachers and in case, he 
has not completed the training, he shall be paid the pay scale of 
Untrained Teacher. 
8. With this direction, the writ petition is finally disposed of. 
No order as to costs.
