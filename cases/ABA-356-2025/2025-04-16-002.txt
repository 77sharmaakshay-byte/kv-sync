COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
ABA No.356 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. B.M. Pingal, learned counsel for the 
applicants.
2. Mr. Deepak Bhardwaj, learned Brief 
Holder for the State.
3. Applicants are seeking anticipatory bail 
in relation to FIR No.0155 of 2025, dated 
27.03.2025, wherein the applicants, who are 
the family members, have been implicated for 
the offences punishable under Section 420, 
467 and 506 of the Indian Penal Code (I.P.C),
registered at Police Station SIDCUL, District 
Haridwar.
4. Learned counsel for the applicants 
submits that the allegations, as alleged,
appears to be a civil dispute since there was 
some money transaction towards a property 
which was agreed to sell to the complainant. 
Apart from this, he submits that a figure of 
Rs. 85 lakhs has been shown in the FIR, 
which was alleged to be paid to the 
applicants in cash, but, in fact, no such 
amount in cash was paid and only an 
amount of Rupees Two crores and Five lakhs
paid through online and Rs. 20 lakhs in 
cash. It has been informed by learned 
counsel for the applicant that after filing of 
instant ABA, one of the bank account of the
applicant has been freezed. He also submits 
that the present applicant No.3 is under 
medical treatment in Ganga Ram Hospital, at 
Delhi.
5. In order to assist this Court, the 
concerned Investigating Officer-Narender 
Singh was directed to join the proceeding 
through V.C. and today, he joins the proceeding through V.C. and apprise to this 
Court that all the amount in other bank 
accounts has been withdrawn by the 
applicant and only one account was freezed
in which approximately an amount of Rs. 5 
lakh 37 thousand is the balance amount. 
Apart from this, he submits that the 
complainant, in his statement, submits that 
he has paid in cash Rs. 65 lakh to the 
present applicant. If it is so, then the 
question is that as to how the amount of Rs. 
65 lakh was paid in cash.
6. This aspect may be enquired by the 
concerned investigating officer from the 
complainant because under the provision of 
the I.T. Act, such a huge amount cannot be 
paid in cash. Apart from this, since the 
present applicants are in emergent need as 
one of the applicants i.e. applicant No.3
admitted in the Ganga Ram Hospital, New 
Delhi, the Investigating Officer concerned is 
directed to proceed by tomorrow i.e. on 
17.04.2025 for de-freezing the account which 
was freezed.
7. Apart from this in view of the statement 
as given in para No.35 of the application, the 
applicant is ready to pay the entire amount 
of Rupees Two crores and Five lakhs as well 
as the amount of Rupees Twenty lakhs 
received in cash from the complainant within 
fifteen days.
8. List this matter on 06.05.2025.
9. In the meantime, the respondents may 
file an affidavit giving the details of the status 
of the investigation, particularly in reference 
to the query with regard to the transfer of the 
amount in cash.
10. Having considered the submissions, as 
an ad interim measure, till the next date of 
listing, in the event of arrest, the applicants
shall be released on bail subject to theirfurnishing personal bonds with two sureties, 
each in the like amount, by each one of them,
to the satisfaction of the Arresting Officer 
(“AO”). In addition to it, the applicants shall 
also comply with the following conditions:-
(i) The applicants shall co-operate 
with the investigation.
(ii) They shall not approach any 
witness in any manner, whatsoever.
(iii) They shall not leave the country 
without prior permission of the court 
concerned.
(iv) The applicants shall also give an 
undertaking on (i), (ii) & (iii) above.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
