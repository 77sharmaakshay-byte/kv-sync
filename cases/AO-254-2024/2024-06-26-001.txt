26.06.2024 AO No.254 of 2024
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The proposed Appeal has been filed 
challenging the Award dated 23.03.2024, 
passed by learned Motor Accident Claims 
Tribunal/VIth Additional District Judge, 
Dehradun in Motor Accident Claim 
Petition No.72 of 2018, by which, the 
learned Tribunal has awarded a total 
sum of Rs.55,73,542/-(Rupees Fifty Five 
Lakh Seventy Three Thousand Five 
Hundred Forty Two) as compensation 
along with an interest at the rate of 9% 
per annum from the date of petition till 
its actual payment. 
2. Heard Mr. I.P. Kohli, learned 
counsel for proposed appellant. 
3. Mr. I.P. Kohli, Advocate, submitted 
that on the date of the accident, the 
driver of the bus was not having a valid 
and effective driving license and the 
owner of the bus was not having a valid 
permit and fitness certificate.
4. As per the award, driving license 
and permit were filed before the 
Tribunal. Therefore, the Original Record is summoned before Admission. 
5. List on 20.07.2024.
