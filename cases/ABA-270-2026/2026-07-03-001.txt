2. The present Anticipatory Bail Application has 
been filed by the applicant- Anuradha Singh, seeking 
anticipatory bail in connection with F.I.R. No. 219 of 
2026 dated 16.06.2026, registered at Police Station 
Kichha, District Udham Singh Nagar, for the offence 
punishable under Section 108 of the Bharatiya Nyaya 
Sanhita, 2023.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant submits that 
the applicant has been falsely implicated in the present 
case. It is further submitted that the co-accused, 
Rajeev Ahuja, has already been granted interim 
protection by this Court in a Criminal Writ Petition 
vide order dated 19.06.2026. It is, therefore, prayed 
that, the present applicant may also be granted ad￾interim anticipatory bail.
4. Learned State Counsel prays for and is granted 
four weeks' time to file a counter affidavit.
5. Having heard learned counsel for the parties and 
considering the submissions advanced on behalf of the 
applicant, this Court is of the view that the matter 
requires consideration.
6. Accordingly, as an interim measure, without 
expressing any opinion on the merits of the case, it is 
directed that, in the event of arrest during the 
pendency of the investigation, the applicant shall be released on ad-interim anticipatory bail on furnishing 
a personal bond with two reliable sureties of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the Arresting 
Officer/Investigating Officer, subject to the following 
conditions:
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation and shall make herself available 
for interrogation as and when required by the 
Investigating Officer;
(ii) The applicant shall not, directly or 
indirectly, induce, threaten or promise any 
person acquainted with the facts of the case so 
as to dissuade such person from disclosing such 
facts to the Court or to any police officer;
(iii) The applicant shall not tamper with the 
prosecution evidence or influence any witness in 
any manner whatsoever.
7. List this case on 12.08.2026.
