A complaint was submitted by the informant to the 
Station House Officer, Kotwali, Vikas Nagar, Dehradun. Mr. 
Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A., submitted on instruction that 
an FIR (Registration No.188 of 2025) has been registered 
against the present applicant under Sections 69, 351(2) and 
Section 352 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. 
2. As per the informant, the applicant under the 
pretext of marriage established physical relations with the 
informant. She became pregnant. 
3. Heard Mr. Sameer Rawat, learned counsel for 
applicant and Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. for 
respondent. 2
4. Mr. Sameer Rawat, Advocate, submitted that the 
applicant has neither made any promise for marriage nor did 
he establish physical relationship on the pretext of false 
promise of marriage. The said allegations are absolutely 
false, frivolous and baseless. 
5. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A., on 
instructions, has sought three weeks’ time to file objection 
to the Anticipatory Bail Application. 
6. Time is granted. 
7. Having heard the submissions of learned counsel 
for the parties and in the facts and circumstances of the 
case, as an ad-interim measure, it is directed that in the 
event of arrest of the applicant Vidit Panwar, he shall be 
released on interim bail on executing a personal bond of 
Rs.30,000/- and two reliable sureties, each of the like 
amount, to the satisfaction of the Investigating Officer/ 
Arresting Officer, subject to the following conditions:-
(i) Applicant shall cooperate with the Investigating 
Agency and he shall make himself available for 
interrogation by a police officer as and when 
required; 
(ii) Applicant shall not directly or indirectly make any 
inducement, threat or promise to any person 
acquainted with the facts of this case.3
(iii) Applicant shall not leave the country without the 
previous permission of this Court. 
8. List on 04.08.2025 for hearing on the Anticipatory 
Bail Application.
