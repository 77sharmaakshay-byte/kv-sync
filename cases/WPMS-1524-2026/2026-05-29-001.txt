WPMS No. 1524 of 2026
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Navnish Negi, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Petitioner moved an Election Petition under 
Section 131 (H) (10) of the Uttarakhand Panchayati 
Raj Act, 2016 questioning the election of 
respondent no. 5 – returned candidate on the 
ground that there is clear cut interpolation in the 
counting sheet of table no. 5 and the number of 
votes which were casted in favour of the petitioner 
were shifted to returned candidate. He further 
submits that the observer of the table no. 5 was 
examined and he specifically denied that he was 
observer in table no. 5, however, he has not 
disclosed, who was observer of table no. 5. 
3. Case of the petitioner before the Prescribed
Authority was that recounting be done but the 
same was rejected. Now, being with aggrieved, 
the petitioner has filed the instant petition. 
4. Mr. Ghanshayam Joshi, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State accepts notice on behalf of 
respondent no. 1.
5. Mr. Sanjay Bhatt, learned counsel accepts 
notice on behalf of respondents no. 2 to 4.
6. Issue notice to respondent no. 5. 
7. Steps be taken by 02.06.2026.
8. List on 16.06.2026 immediately after fresh.
