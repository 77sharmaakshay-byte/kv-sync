COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.1657 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, learned counsel
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Bhupendra Koranga, learned 
Brief Holder for the State of 
Uttarakhand/respondent No.1.
3. Mr. B.D. Pande, learned counsel for 
respondent No.2-Commission.
4. Petitioner has filed present writ 
petition challenging the Provisional 
Recommendation/Merit List of 
candidates dated 09.08.2024 (Annexure 
No.8), with regard to the private 
respondents and to indulge his name in 
the said Provisional Merit List in OBC 
category.
5. It is the case of the petitioner that 
pursuant to the advertisement dated 
16.01.2024 issued by the respondent￾Commission for the post of Amin in 
PWD Department of State of 
Uttarakhand, petitioner submitted his 
candidature under the OBC category.
He participated in the written 
examination and succeeded.
6. According to the petitioner,
thereafter, he was called for the 
document verification and He 
participated in the said document 
verification. But, during document 2
verification, it transpires that the OBC 
certificate which was annexed by him, is 
meant for the services of Government 
of India, therefore, he cannot be 
appointed/selected for the services of 
State of Uttarakhand. 
7. It is submitted by learned counsel 
for the petitioner that though, petitioner 
was not having OBC certificate issued 
by the Competent Authority/Tehsildar 
for getting benefit the services of the 
State, but the same was obtained by 
the petitioner on 04.07.2024, much 
after the last date of submission of 
application form. However, the
petitioner instead of submitting the said 
certificate dated 04.07.2024, submitted
a clarification certificate dated 
21.05.2024 given by Tehsildar 
Bhatwari, district Uttarkashi, after five 
days of document verification i.e. 
27.05.2024, wherein, it has been stated 
that the OBC certificate dated 
09.01.2023 issued to the petitioner was 
also valid for the service of State 
Government. But, this certificate 
appears to be contradictory to the 
certificate issued by the Competent 
Authority.
8. Since, the petitioner is in 
possession of the certificate as of now, 
therefore, he cannot be deprived of his 
selection in the selection process and 
the controversy involved in the writ 3
petition requires some serious 
deliberation as to whether the 
certificate issued after the last date of 
submission of the application form 
would come to the rescue of the 
petitioner or not.
9. Issue notice to respondent Nos.3
and 4, returnable within six weeks.
10. Steps to be taken within a week.
11. Respondents are directed to file 
counter affidavits within six weeks.
12. List on 28.11.2024.
13. As an interim measure, during the 
pendency of this writ petition, it is 
directed that one post of Amin in PWD 
department under OBC Category shall 
be kept vacant for the petitioner.
14. Interim relief application (IA/1/ 
2024) stands disposed of.
