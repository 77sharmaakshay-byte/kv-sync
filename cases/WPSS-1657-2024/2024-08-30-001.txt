4. It is the case of the petitioner that 
pursuant to the advertisement dated 
16.01.2024, petitioner participated in 
the selection process held for the post 
of Amin in PWD Department. He 
submitted his candidature as OBC 
candidate of Uttarakhand State. After 
document verification, final select list 
has been published by the respondent￾Commission on 09.08.2024, in which 
the name of the petitioner was not 
figured.
5. Petitioner secured 62.50 marks in 
the written examination and in the 
Select List the persons, who secured 
lesser marks than the petitioner have
found place, while the petitioner is 
segregated by the respondent￾Commission.2
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
further submits that during document 
verification process, petitioner was 
informed that his OBC certificate is 
meant for the services of Government 
of India, and, therefore, he is not 
qualified for the appointment. But, it is 
submitted by the petitioner that during 
the document verification process, OBC 
certificate, which is issued by the 
Tehsildar, Bhatwari, District Uttarkashi
for the purpose of services of the State 
of Uttarakhand, has also submitted, 
despite that, he has not been selected.
7. Learned counsel for the 
respondent-Commission prays for and is 
granted 03 days’ time to seek clear 
instruction in the matter.
