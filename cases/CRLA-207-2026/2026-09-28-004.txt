1. Ms. Aishwarya Thapliyal, learned counsel
holding the brief of Mr. Abhishek Verma, learned
counsel for the appellant.
2. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned AGA for the
State.
3. The instant criminal appeal has been preferred
by the appellant – “Asha, wife of Manoj,” against the
judgment and order dated 08.04.2026 passed by the
learned 4
th Addl. Session Judge, Haridwar in Sessions
Trial No. 32 of 2023 whereby appellant is convicted for
the offences punishable under Section 370/34, 363/34
of IPC with the sentence of 10 years rigorous
imprisonment and fine of Rs. 8,000/-.
4. The trial court record is received.
5. Admit.
6. Heard on bail application.
7. It is argued by the learned counsel for the
appellant that appellant was on bail during trial and
she never misused the same. Apart from this, she
submits that at the time of conviction, appellant was
the advance stage of her pregnancy and thereafter, she
recently gave birth to a female child on 12.08.2026
while languishing in jail.
8. On the previous date, State Counsel was directed
to get instruction in the matter whether appellant gave
birth to a child or not. Mr. Pandey, learned AGA on
instructions, apprised to this Court that appellant gave
birth to a female child recently. 9. After hearing the learned counsel for the parties
and taking into consideration that appellant was on
bail during trial and she never misused the same and
she gave birth to a female child on 12.08.2026 in jail,
therefore, this Court is taking humanitarian approach
to enlarge the appellant on bail.
10. Accordingly, without expressing any opinion on
the merit of the case, the bail application no. 1 of 2026
is allowed. 
11. Let the appellant “Asha, wife of Manoj” be
released on bail during the pendency of the instant
appeal, on executing personal bond and furnishing
two reliable sureties, each of like amount, to the
satisfaction of Court concerned. Fine imposed by the
trial court shall also remain suspended during the
pendency of this appeal. 
12. List this appeal in due course along with
Criminal Appeal No. 205 of 2026.
