The revisionist has filed this Revision 
on 17th February, 2018, contending thereof 
that there is delay in preferring the Revision. 
However, Registry has not pointed out any 
delay in preferring the Revision. 
Since Registry has not reported any 
delay in preferring the Revision, the Delay 
Condonation Application No. 265 of 2018, 
which has been preferred by the revisionist, 
has been wrongly filed. The same is 
accordingly stands disposed of. 
The Revision would be treated to be 
filed well within time. 
Mr. Ankur Sharma, Advocate has taken 
notice on behalf of respondent No. 2. He 
prays for and is granted three weeks’ time to 
file objection to the stay application. 
List thereafter.
