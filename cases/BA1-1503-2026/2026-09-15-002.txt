Mr. R.S. Sammal, learned Senior

2. Mr. Sandeep Sharma, learned AGA for

3. Mr. Lalit Sharma, learned counsel for

4. The short term bail application is 
moved on behalf of the applicant-Ajay 
Kulyal, S/o Jagat Singh Kulyal, who is 
implicated alongwith others in Case Crime 
No.030 of 2026 for the offence punishable 
under Section 109 of BNS, 2023, P.S. Bhimtal, 
District Nainital.
5. The investigation is still going on and 
some of the accused persons are still 
absconding and the condition of the injured 
is critical and as apprised by learned counsel 
for the complainant he is in coma. 
6. In this bail application, the present 
applicant is praying for short term bail on the 
ground that he is pursuing his study in BCA 
in Graphic Era Institute and 5th Semester 
examination are scheduled to be held from 
26th September followed by practical 
examination, therefore he may be granted short term bail. 
7. There is no objection from the other 
side since the applicant is praying for short 
term bail for the purposes to appear in the 
forth coming examinations. 
8. After considering the submissions as 
above and taking into consideration of forth
coming examination of 5th Semester of BCA, 
this Court is of the view that the applicant 
Ajay Kulyal, S/o Jagat Singh Kulyal deserves 
for short term bail for the period of six 
weeks.
9. Accordingly, I.A. No.01 of 2026 
whereby the applicant is seeking short term 
bail, is allowed. 
10. Let the applicant- Ajay Kulyal be 
released on short term bail for a period of 
six weeks to be reckoned from the date of 
his actual release on his executing a 
personal bond and two sureties of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the court 
concerned.
11. On expiry of the period of six weeks, 
the applicant shall surrender before the 
Superintendent of the jail concerned.
12. Put up this bail application on the date 
16.10.2026.
