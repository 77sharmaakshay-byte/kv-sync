2. Mr. Manoj Bhatt, learned Brief Holder for

4. Learned counsel for the applicant would 
submit that the Coordinate Bench of this 
Court, vide order dated 15.09.2026, granted 
short-term bail to the applicant, taking into 
consideration the fact that he is pursuing a 
B.C.A. course at Graphic Era Institute and 
that his forthcoming fifth-semester 
examination was scheduled to commence on 
26.09.2026.
5. Learned counsel would further submit 
that the present time extension application 
has been filed seeking extension of the short￾term bail for a further period of one month, as 
the applicant is required to appear in the Odd 
Mid-Semester Theory Examination, 2026, 
scheduled to be held from 16.11.2026 to 
23.11.2026, as well as the Odd Mid-Semester Practical Examinations, scheduled to be held 
from 25.11.2026 to 12.12.2026, as per the 
official examination schedule issued by the 
University.
6. Learned counsel appearing for the 
complainant would oppose the application, 
contending that the applicant is accused of 
having committed a heinous offence and, 
therefore, does not deserve any further 
extension of short-term bail. However, he 
would submit that, in the event the present 
time extension application is allowed, it may 
be made clear that no further application 
seeking extension of short-term bail shall be 
entertained by the Court.
7. Having considered the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the parties 
and having regard to the fact that the 
applicant is pursuing his studies and is 
required to appear in the aforesaid 
examinations, this Court is of the view that, in 
the interest of the applicant's academic 
future, the present application deserves to be 
allowed.
8. Accordingly, the time extension 
application is allowed, and the short-term bail 
granted to the applicant vide order dated 
15.09.2026 is extended up to 12.12.2026. The 
applicant shall surrender before the 
competent Court on 14.12.2026. It is, 
however, made clear that no further 
application seeking extension of the short￾term bail shall be entertained.
9. List in due course.
