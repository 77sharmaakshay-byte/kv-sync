5. Petitioner’s claim for salary has been 
rejected on the ground that reservation 
roster has not been properly followed by the 
Management of the concerned college. 
6. It is conceded by learned counsel for 
the petitioner, as well as counsel 
representing the Management, that selection 
was held as per the roster which was 
prepared by the State Government in 2001, 
and the State Government is now finding
fault with the selection on the ground that 
roster, which was prepared in 2018, was not 
followed. 
7. It is contended that since the selection 
process was initiated in 2016, therefore, the 
amended roster would not apply to ongoingselection in view of the law declared by Apex 
Court. Thus, it is contended that the 
objection raised by the State Government is 
not sustainable in the eyes of law, as 
reservation roster prepared in 2018, cannot 
be made applicable to the process of 
selection which was initiated in 2016. 
8. Learned State counsel shall get clear 
instruction on the aforesaid aspect,
especially, whether the objection of State 
Government is that reservation roster 
prepared in 2001 was not followed, or the 
modified reservation roster issued in 2018 
was not applied to the selection in question. 
9. List the matter on 09.12.2024, after 
fresh.
