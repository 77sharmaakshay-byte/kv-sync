This Application has been filed for anticipatory bail 
in the First Information Report No.422 of 2025, registered at 
Police Station Rishikesh, District Dehradun under Sections 
115(2), 351(3), 352, 85 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, 
Section 3 and Section 4 of the Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961.
2. Heard Mr. Shashi Kant Shandilya, learned counsel 
for the applicants and Mr. Akshay Latwal, learned Assistant 
Government Advocate for the respondent.
3. Mr. Shashi Kant Shandilya, Advocate submitted 
that all the allegations are false. Applicant No.3 is the 
husband of the informant’s daughter. Applicant No.1 is the 
father, applicant no.2 is the mother and applicant no.4 is the 
sister of the applicant no.3.
4. Mr. Akshay Latwal, Assistant Government Advocate,
has sought three weeks’ time to file objection to the 
anticipatory bail application. 2
5. Time is granted. 
6. Notice is being issued to the victim, the wife of the 
applicant no.3. Steps to be taken within a week. 
7. Having heard the learned counsel for the parties and 
in the facts and circumstances of the case, as an ad-interim 
measure, it is directed that in the event of arrest of the 
applicants Virender Sharma, Smt. Rajesh, Anuj Sharma and 
Swati Sharma, they shall be released on interim bail on 
executing a personal bond of Rs.30,000/- and two reliable 
sureties, each of the like amount, by each one of them, to the 
satisfaction of the Investigating Officer/Arresting Officer, 
subject to the following conditions:-
(i) Applicants shall cooperate with the Investigating 
Agency and they shall make themselves available for 
interrogation by a police officer as and when required; 
(ii) Applicants shall not directly or indirectly make any 
inducement, threat or promise to any person 
acquainted with the facts of this case;
(iii) Applicants shall not leave the country without the 
previous permission of this Court. 
8. The applicant no.3 and the victim are directed to 
appear in-person before this Court on 13.11.2025.
9. List on 13.11.2025 for hearing on the anticipatory 
bail application.
