Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned counsel for the

2. Mr. K.N. Joshi, learned D.A.G. for the State.
3. This writ petition has been filed by the 
petitioner feeling aggrieved by the recovery order 
which has been issued by respondent no.3 whereby 
excess payment made to the petitioner was directed 
to be recovered from his salary.
4. The contention of learned counsel for the 
petitioner is that earlier the petitioner was engaged 
as Daily Wager Employee with the respondent￾Department. Subsequently he was placed in the 
Work Charge Establishment and his salary was fixed 
on the bases of 7th Pay Commission by the 
respondents at ₹49,328/-. Subsequently the 
respondent-Department awoke from his deep 
slumber and found that the petitioner was being 
paid some higher salary and therefore his salary was 
reduced to ₹31,632/- vide order dated 06.05.2026. 
Consequently he was directed to refund the excess 
payment made to him. The respondent-Department 
has started deduction from salary of the petitioner 
w.e.f., June 2026.
5. It is contended by learned counsel for the UKHC010210822025
petitioner that the petitioner is nowhere party to the 
wrong fixation of the salary on the bases of 7th Pay 
Commission and therefore deduction cannot be 
made from the salary of the petitioner for the excess 
payment made to him.
6. He further submits that the petitioner is a 
Class III Employee and his case is covered by the 
judgment and order passed by the Apex Court in the 
case of State of Punjab and others Vs. Rafiq Masih 
(white washer) and others reported in (2015) 4 SCC 
334.
7. Per contra, learned counsel for the State 
submits that recovery has been made from the 
petitioner on the basis of judgment of Apex Court in 
Rafiq Masih (supra).
8. The contention raised by learned State counsel 
does not find favour with this Court as the law is 
clear on the subject that the recovery cannot be 
made from the Class III and IV Employees, if wrong 
fixation of salary was not made on the basis of some 
fraud or mis-representation by the 
petitioner/government servant.
9. The respondents may file their counter affidavit 
within six weeks.
10. List this case on 18.08.2026.
11. In the meantime, no recovery shall be made 
from salary of the petitioner pursuant to the 
impugned order.
