2. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned A.G.A. for the State of

3. The present Criminal Appeal has been filed against the 
judgment and order dated 04.06.2025 passed by the learned Special 
Sessions Judge/District Judge, Chamoli (Gopeshwar), in Special 
Sessions Trial No. 20 of 2023, titled “State vs. Kailash Khanduri”, 
whereby the Appellant has been convicted under Section 8/20 of the 
NDPS Act and sentenced to undergo ten years’ rigorous 
imprisonment along with a fine of Rs. 1,00,000/-, and in default of 
payment of fine, to further undergo six months’ simple 
imprisonment.
4. This is the second Bail Application moved on behalf of the 
Appellant. 
5. Learned counsel for the Appellant submits, for consideration 
of bail, firstly, that the Appellant has been in long incarceration 
since 05.02.2023, i.e., for almost three years and four months. 
Secondly, it is submitted that the arrest memo and sample seals 
contain the FIR number even before the FIR was allegedly lodged. 
Thirdly, it is contended that the contraband alleged to have been 
recovered from the possession of the Appellant, namely charas
weighing 1.5 kg, is of commercial quantity and was in the form of 
sticks (“batti”), however, there is inconsistency regarding the 
sampling process, as it is not clear from which batch or from which 
particular stick the samples were drawn for being sent to the FSL. 6. Objections, if any, be filed by the Respondents to the Bail 
Application.
