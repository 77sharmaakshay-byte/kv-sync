 The petitioner is a public servant. The reliefs 
sought in the Writ Petition relate to preponement of the 
date of promotion of the petitioner. This relief can be 
granted by the Uttarakhand Public Services Tribunal. 
We are, therefore, inclined to direct the Registry to 
transmit the complete record of the Writ Petition, since 
pleadings have been exchanged, before the Tribunal for 
its registration as a Claim Petition, and for its disposal 
by the Tribunal. 
 Learned Senior Counsel for the petitioner, 
however, submits that it would not be appropriate for 
this Court to relegate the petitioner to the Tribunal at 
this stage. In this regard, he wishes to cite certain 
case laws. 
 At his request, list on 22.11.2022.
