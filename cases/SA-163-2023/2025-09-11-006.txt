COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
SA No. 163 of 2023
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
(Through V.C.)
1. Mr. B.D. Pandey, learned counsel for 
the appellant.
2. Mr. Shakib Husain, learned counsel 
for the respondent.
3. Both the parties are claiming their 
rights over the property in question on the 
basis of the registered sale deeds. In both 
the sale deeds, description and location of 
the land are entirely different, and, as 
such, prima facie, it appears to be a case 
of demarcation.
4. Since, both the parties are claiming 
that they are in possession over the land 
which they purchased by virtue of 
registered sale deed, and prima facie, this 
appears to be a case of demarcation, in 
such an eventuality, this Court is of the 
view that the land of both the parties 
should be demarcated.
6. Consequently, both the parties are 
directed to approach the concerned Sub 
Divisional Magistrate within a week and 
furnish their sale deeds and after receiving 
their sale deeds the Sub Divisional 
Magistrate proceed with the demarcation. 
It is made clear that both the parties shall 
cooperate with the SDM concerned and 
after completion of demarcation 
proceedings the Sub Divisional Magistrate 
shall place the report in a sealed envelope 
before this Court within a month.
7. In the meantime, both the parties 
are directed to maintain status quo in respect of the property in question and will 
not create third party interest.
8. List this matter on 15.10.2025.
9. Let a copy of this order be supplied 
to Mr. I.P. Kohli, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State for necessary compliance.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
