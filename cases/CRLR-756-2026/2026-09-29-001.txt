Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Harshvardhan Dhanik, learned 
counsel for the revisionist/applicant.
2. Present criminal revision has been 
preferred against the judgment and order 
dated 18.09.2026 passed by learned 
Additional Sessions Judge, Khatima, 
District Udham Singh Nagar in Criminal 
Appeal No.05 of 2026, whereby the 
learned Appellate Court has dismissed 
the appeal and affirmed the judgment 
and order dated 22.01.2026 passed by 
learned Judicial Magistrate, Khatima in 
Complaint Case No.977 of 2023, whereby 
the revisionist has been convicted under 
Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments 
Act, 1881 and sentenced him to undergo 
simple imprisonment for a period of one 
year and to pay a fine of ₹9,10,000/-.
3. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would submit that both the courts below 
have failed to properly appreciate the oral 
as well as documentary evidence 
available on record and have recorded the 
conviction of the revisionist without properly considering the material brought 
on record by the defence. He would 
further submit that the cheque in 
question was not issued by the revisionist 
in favour of the complainant towards 
discharge of any legally enforceable debt 
or liability.
4. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would further submit that the revisionist 
has already deposited 20% of the cheque 
amount at the time of filing of the appeal 
and is ready and willing to deposit a 
further sum of ₹1,20,000/- to 
demonstrate his bona fides. He, therefore, 
prays that the revisionist may be 
exempted from surrendering before the 
court concerned during the pendency of 
the present criminal revision.
5. Admit the revision.
6. Issue notice to respondent, 
returnable at an early date.
7. Let the Lower Court Record be 
summoned.
8. Considering the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
revisionist and without expressing any 
opinion on the merits of the case, the 
application seeking exemption from 
surrender is allowed, subject to the 
revisionist depositing a sum of 
₹1,20,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Twenty 
Thousand only) before the court 
concerned within a period of four weeks 
from today.
9. Subject to compliance with the 
aforesaid condition, the sentence awarded 
to the revisionist shall remain suspended 
during the pendency of the present 
criminal revision.
10. Accordingly, the revisionist shall be released on bail during the pendency of 
the present criminal revision, on his 
furnishing a personal bond and two 
reliable sureties, each in the like amount, 
to the satisfaction of the court concerned, 
subject to such other conditions as the 
court concerned may deem fit and proper 
to impose.
11. The amount deposited pursuant to 
this order shall remain subject to the 
final outcome of the present criminal 
revision and shall be dealt with by the 
court concerned strictly in accordance 
with law.
12. It is made clear that in the event the 
revisionist fails to comply with the 
aforesaid condition within the stipulated 
period, respondent/complainant shall be 
at liberty to move an appropriate 
application before this Court for recall of 
the order granting bail, in accordance 
with law.
13. List this case in due course.
(Alok Mahra, J.)
