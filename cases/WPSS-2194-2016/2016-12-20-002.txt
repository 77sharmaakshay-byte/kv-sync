Km. Deepa Joshi ………Petitioner 
 Versus 
Director General Medical, Health & Welfare 
& another ……..Respondents 
Present:- Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate for the petitioner. 
Mr. Vikas Pandey, Brief Holder for the 
State/respondents. 
Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J. (Oral)
 The petitioner is a Senior Assistant working in 
the Office of Additional Chief Medical Officer, Nainital. 
The petitioner has primarily challenged the order dated 
17.10.2016, by which she has been transferred on 
promotion from the Office of Additional Chief Medical 
Officer, Nainital to the Office of Chief Medical Officer, 
Almora. 
2. The challenge of the petitioner is on the 
ground that she is an unmarried woman and has to take 
care of her 65 years old mother, who is physically 
challenged. Apart from this, it has further been 
contended that there is no one in her family to look after 
her mother except the petitioner. 
3. The petitioner submits that she was appointed 
on compassionate ground in the year 1999 and since 
then she has been working in District Nainital. It is for 
the first time she has been transferred to another district 
that too on promotion. 
4. No interference is liable to be made in the 
transfer order dated 17.10.2016. However, in case the

petitioner is not willing to join her new posting which is 
on her promotion, she must forego her promotion. There 
is no interference as to the transfer. All the same, 
considering the medical condition and old age of 
petitioner’s mother, a limited interference is being made, 
subject to the undertaking given on behalf of the 
petitioner that she would join her posting on or before 
01.03.2017. 
 
5. In view of the aforesaid, the petitioner shall not 
be disturbed from the present place of working and she 
will be allowed to continue at Nainital on the same post, 
provided she gives an undertaking that she will join her 
transfer place of posting at Almora on or before 
01.03.2017 before the authority concerned. It is, 
however, made clear that in case, the petitioner fails to 
join at Almora on or before 01.03.2017 and does not 
notify to the Director concerned of her joining, the 
Director shall initiate a disciplinary proceeding against 
the petitioner forthwith. 
6. It is further made clear that since the 
petitioner has been allowed to work at Nainital by the 
interim order of this Court, she shall be given her arrears 
of salary in time by the department. 
7. In view of the aforesaid, the writ petition 
stands disposed. 
8. The Registrar General of this Court is hereby 
directed to communicate this order to the Director, 
Medical Health and Family Welfare, Kumaon Mandal for 
onward compliance. 
 (Sudhanshu Dhulia, J.)
