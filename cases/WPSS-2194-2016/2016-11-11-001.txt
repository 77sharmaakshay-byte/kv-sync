IN THE HIGH COURT OF UTTARAKHAND AT NAINITAL 
Writ Petition (S/S) No. 2194 of 2016 
Km. Deepa Joshi … Petitioner 
Vs 
Director General Medical, Health & Welfare 
& another … Respondents 
 
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra and Ms. Janki Surya, Advocates, present for the petitioner. 
Mr. N.S. Pundir, Brief Holder for the State/respondents. 
Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J.
 The petitioner is a Senior Assistant working in the 
Office of Additional Chief Medical Officer, Nainital. She has 
been promoted to the post of Chief Assistant vide order dated 
17.10.2016 and has been transferred from Nainital to the 
Office of Chief Medical Officer, Almora. 
 The case of the petitioner is that since there are 
posts of Chief Assistant lying vacant in Nainital, Bhowali and 
Haldwani, therefore, she may be transferred against such post 
for the reasons that she has to take care of her 65 years old 
mother, who is also physically challenged. It has further been 
stated that there is no other person in her family to look after 
her mother. The petitioner is an unmarried woman and 
moreover, her father passed away in the year 1999 and after 
the death of her father, there is no one to take care of her 
mother. 
 In view of these genuine reasons raised by the 
petitioner, it is hereby directed that the petitioner shall move a 
representation before the Director General, Medical, Health & 
Family Welfare, Uttarakhand within a period of one week from 
today and the Director General, Medical, Health & Family 
Welfare shall take a decision on it within a period of two weeks 
thereafter. 
 It is also made clear that there is no direction to the 
Director General, Medical, Health & Family Welfare to adjust

 Let a decision be taken in accordance with law

 List this matter on 15.12.2016 in the daily cause list.

 However, it is further made clear that till the next
