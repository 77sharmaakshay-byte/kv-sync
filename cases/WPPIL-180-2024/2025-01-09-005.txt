4. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner 
Mr. Navnish Negi, and the learned Chief 
Standing Counsel Mr. C.S. Rawat, and the 
Director Ms. Nitika Khandelwal, who has 
submitted to the Court that steps have been 
taken and instructions issued to have the 
rag-pickers examined medically and that 
there are proposals to make it a calendar 
event to ensure the health of these 
marginalized class of citizens. That apart, 
the Director has also submitted that 
instructions have been issued to ensure that 
sanitation facilities are provided to the rag￾pickers, and that such instructions have been 
issued to the local authorities. The same are 
placed on record. 
5. The Amicus Curiae after a field visit and interaction with the rag-pickers has filed a 
detailed report. A copy of which is also 
furnished to the respondent Government. 
6. Having heard the Director, we are of the 
opinion that the submissions made and steps 
initiated by the Director would be better 
appreciated if it is placed before this Court in 
the form of an affidavit along with the 
enclosures. Along with such material, 
including communications, directions, 
instructions issued for ameliorating the lives 
of these marginalized citizens. 
7. Affidavit by tomorrow.
