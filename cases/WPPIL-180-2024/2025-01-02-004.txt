02.01.2025 WPPIL No. 180 of 2024
Hon'ble G. Narendar, C.J.
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J.
Mr. Navnish Negi, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Manoj Kumar, learned Standing Counsel for 
the Union of India- respondent no.1.
3. Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned Chief Standing Counsel 
for the State.
4. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner, learned 
Chief Standing Counsel for the State, and Ms. Nitika
Khandelwal, Director, Urban Development Department.
5. An action taken report dated 31.12.2024 has 
been filed in the Court along with the communication 
addressed to the Chief Standing Counsel and signed by 
the Director, Urban Development Department.
6. In Paragraph No.6 of the said report, it is stated 
that they have identified 1056 rag-pickers, and out of 
them, 40 rag-pickers were found to be homeless, and a 
total of 101 shelters/ Rain Basera are established by 
the ULBs’ and there are 48 permanent shelters/ Rain 
Baseras with a capacity to house and accommodate 
1268, and the Directorate has also setup 53 temporary 
shelters/ Rain Baseras with intake capacity of 935.
7. In Paragraph No.7, the number of homeless, who 
have been shifted to the Rain Baseras is set out. In Paragraph No.8, it is stated that 452 bonfire/ Alao are 
stated to have been provided. In Paragraph No.9, it is 
stated that adequate number of blankets have been 
distributed, and in Paragraph No.10, it is stated that a 
sum of Rs.50.00 lakhs has been set apart for the 
purpose of maintenance of night shelters. In Paragraph 
No.11, it is stated that 45 health camps have been 
organized for the rag-pickers and boots, safety jackets 
and helmets have been provided to 151, 165 and 26 
rag-pickers respectively.
8. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
he has not been furnished a copy of the report dated 
31.12.2024.
9. We have queried learned counsel for the 
petitioner whether it would be possible for him to 
personally interact with the rag-pickers and place a 
report before this Court, to which, he has prayed for a 
week’s time.
10. The action taken report dated 31.12.2024 is 
placed on record.
11. It is further directed that the Department shall 
depute one officer to accompany the petitioner’s 
counsel and show him the camps where the rag-pickers 
are housed.
12. List this matter on 09.01.2025, at the same 
stage. 
 (Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J) (G. Narendar, CJ)
