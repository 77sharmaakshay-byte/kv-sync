Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J.
Mr. Navnish Negi, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Manoj Kumar, learned Standing Counsel for 
the Union of India- respondent no.1.
3. Mr. Amarendra Pratap Singh, learned Additional 
Advocate General assisted by Mr. S.S. Chaudhary, 
learned Brief Holder for the State.
4. Heard learned counsel for the petitioner, learned 
counsel for the respondents, and Ms. Nitika
Khandelwal, Director, Urban Development Department.
5. Pursuant to the order of this Court dated 
26.12.2024, the Director, Urban Development 
Department was present virtually. She submits that 
there are various affordable housing schemes, and they 
have carried out a census of the number of rag-pickers, 
and about 740 rag-pickers have been identified and out 
of the same, about 549 rag-pickers have been issued 
identity cards and the Government would be taking 
further steps to make available to these identified 
persons the affordable housing schemes.
6. The fact remains that weather, in the coming 
couple of months, is in all likelihood going to be harsh according to the forecast and mere availability of 
affordable housing schemes would not be an immediate 
succour to the homeless people. In order to protect 
them from the adverse weather conditions, this Court 
has proposed to the Director, Urban Development to 
explore the possibility of creating temporary housing 
facilities with a view to mitigate the hardships that the 
shelterless rag-pickers would be forced to face.
7. The Court has suggested creation of temporary 
dormitory accommodations with tents and necessary 
infrastructure like bedding and clothing till they are 
able to tide over the cold weather.
8. The Director, Urban Development has sought a 
week’s time to devise a proposal and place it before 
this Court for approval and implementation. The same 
is placed on record.
9. List this matter on 02.01.2025, at the same 
stage. 
 (Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J) (G. Narendar, CJ)
