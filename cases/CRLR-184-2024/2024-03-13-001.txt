COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRLR No.184 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Siddhartha Sah, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. V.S Rawat, learned AGA with Ms. 
Rangoli Purohit, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. The revision is directed against the final order 
dated 15.02.2024, passed by learned Additional 
Judge, Family Court, Roorkee, District Haridwar, 
whereby the petition for claiming maintenance under 
Section 125 CrPC was partly allowed and the 
revisionist-husband was directed to pay a sum of 
₹42,000/- per month as maintenance allowance to the 
respondent-wife w.e.f., the date of filing of the 
petition i.e., 24.12.2020 and the said maintenance was 
made payable from 10th date of every month.
4. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
revisionist that the revisionist was working in a IT 
company and was getting around ₹1,50,000/- as 
salary per month when the petition was moved for
maintenance, but, subsequently due to recession in IT 
sector, the petitioner has tendered his resignation and 
now when this judgment was passed, the revisionist 
has no income.
5. I have perused the impugned judgment and 
order passed by the learned Additional Judge, Family 
Court, Roorkee, Haridwar. The learned Judge did not 
find favour with the theory put forward with the revisionist-husband of resignation due to recession 
rather it was observed that in order to deprive the 
wife from maintenance, revisionist-husband has 
deliberately resigned from service.
6. There is no denying of the fact that the 
respondent-wife is the legally wedded wife of the 
revisionist and the husband has an obligation to 
maintain the wife and it is further contended that the 
respondent is paying interim maintenance regularly.
7. Issue notice to respondent no.2, returnable 
within six weeks.
8. Steps to be taken within a week.
9. Call for T.C.R.
10. List this case on 20.05.2024.
11. In the meantime, it is provided that subject to 
the revisionist paying a sum of ₹35,000/- per month 
on tenth of every month to the respondent-wife, 
execution of the impugned judgment and order shall 
remain stayed.
12. It is further provided that the revisionist￾husband shall also pay 50% of the arrears of the 
maintenance @ ₹35,000/- adjusting the amount, 
which has already been paid within three months.
13. Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2024) stands 
disposed-off accordingly.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
