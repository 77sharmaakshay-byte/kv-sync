COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPSS No.2337 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Anil Anthwal, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. B.S. Koranga, learned Brief 
Holder for the State/respondent nos.1 & 
2.
3. Mr. Ramji Srivastava, learned 
counsel with Mr. Nishant Krishna 
Adhikari, learned counsel for respondent 
no.3. 
4. Petitioner is challenging the 
rejection of her candidature for the post 
of Nursing Officer (Female) under 
Diploma quota of Post Code 201.
Petitioner’s candidature has been 
rejected on the ground that she could 
not furnish the OBC certificate on the 
last date of submission of application 
form for which she applied under OBC 
category. 
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that infact, by birth petitioner is 
OBC and earlier the certificate was 
issued but due to statutory period the 
earlier certificate was automatically 
expired and she could not furnish the 
fresh OBC certificate on the last date of 
submission of form though she has 
already applied for issuance of fresh OBC 
certificate prior to the last date of 
submission of application form. 
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that since by birth petitioner belongs to OBC category, therefore, her 
candidature cannot be rejected merely 
on the ground that she could not furnish 
the OBC certificate on the last date of 
submission of form.
7. All these questions required 
deliberation for which respondents’ 
version are necessary.
8. Let the respondents may file 
counter affidavit within two weeks. 
9. For an interim measure, respondent 
no.3 is directed to consider the 
candidature of the petitioner for 
appointment to the post of Nursing 
Officer (Female), Post Code 201, 
provisionally, and the result be kept in 
the sealed cover and be placed before 
this Court on the next date.
10. List on 30.12.2024.
