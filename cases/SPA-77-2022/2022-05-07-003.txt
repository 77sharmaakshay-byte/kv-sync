This Court, vide order dated 
25.04.2022, had passed the following 
order: - 
“In the interregnum, it is 
directed that the classes of the 
students, who have already been 
admitted in the college, shall 
continue to be taken by the 
appellant-college till the next date 
of listing.” 
 Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that the University is going to hold 
examinations with effect from 09.05.2022, 
however, the students taken from ‘All India 
Quota’ by the College, for filling vacant 
‘State Quota’ seats, are not being permitted 
to appear in the examination. It is further 
contended on behalf of appellant that since 
very less number of candidates were 
available for admission against ‘State Quota’ 
seats, therefore, appellant had taken 
candidates from ‘All India Quota’ for filling 
those seats. He further submits that all the 
students,who have been given admission, 
are National Eligibility Cum Entrance Test 
(NEET) qualified. He further submits that 
the candidates from ‘All India Quota’ are more meritorious than the ‘State Quota’ 
candidates, and, in fact, all of them have 
secured more marks than the cut-off marks 
meant for ‘State Quota’. 
 Mr. Munish Bhardwaj, learned counsel, 
appearing for Ayurveda University, submits 
that if appellant deposits a sum of Rs.10.00 
lakh (ten lakh only) with the University, 
during the course of the day, for holding 
examination of the aforesaid candidates, 
then the University has no objection in 
permitting those students to appear in the 
examination. 
 Mr. Navneet Kaushik, learned counsel 
appearing for the appellant undertakes to 
abide by the aforesaid condition. 
 Let him do so. However, it is clarified 
that the result of those students shall not be 
declared without express leave of this 
Court. 
 List on the date already fixed. 
 Mr. Munish Bhardwaj, learned counsel 
appearing for respondent nos.2 and 3, 
undertakes to inform the University about 
this order during the course of the day.
