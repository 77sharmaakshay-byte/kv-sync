An impleadment application alongwith an 
application seeking interim relief has been filed by 
the proposed applicant.
Considering the contentions of learned 
counsel for the parties, we are of the view that it 
would be appropriate for the proposed applicant to 
file a fresh writ petition rather than to implead 
himself in the present writ petition. 
It is needless to say that the applicant has 
always a liberty to file a fresh writ petition in 
accordance with law. 
Under these circumstances, the impleadment 
application as well as the interim relief application 
stand rejected with the aforesaid observations. 
As requested, four weeks’ time is granted to 
the learned counsel for the respondents to file the 
counter affidavit. 
List alongwith connected writ petitions after 
four weeks.
