6. During the course of hearing, our attention has 
been drawn to the affidavit filed on behalf of 
respondent no. 5 by Shri S.P. Subudhi, the Member 
Secretary, SEIAA. As per this affidavit, SEIAA has not
received any application for environmental clearance, 
with regard to the project in question. It further states 
that violation of the notification dated 14.09.2006 falls 
within the jurisdiction of the State Pollution Control 
Board to look into.
7. Mr. Salwan, learned Senior Counsel has also 
drawn our attention to the affidavit of Mr. Gajendra Prakash Narwane, the Assistant Inspector General of 
Forests (AIGF), who states that as per the submission 
of the project proponent, the project proponent will not 
construct more than 20,000 Sq. Mtrs. Therefore, in 
view of the EIA notification, 2006 (as explained in 
paragraph no. 9 of the same affidavit), the project 
does not need prior environmental clearance. In this 
regard, we may refer to Clause 8(a) of the EIA 
notification, 2006, which reads as follows :-
“8(a); Building and Construction projects-≥20000 sq. mtrs 
and < 1,50,000 sq. mtrs. of built-up-area- (built up area 
for covered construction; in the case of facilities open to 
the sky, it will be the activity area).”
8. Learned counsel for respondent nos. 3 and 4 
have tendered in Court a plan, demarcating the area in 
red, whereon they wish to carry out the development 
work. It is submitted that the said area, which is 
marked in red, does not have forest like vegetation -
even as per the reports filed before this Court. The 
plan also contains the description of the Khasra 
Numbers, which is bounded by the area marked in red, 
which, according to respondent nos. 5 & 6, admeasures 
5.2 Hectares. 
9. Firstly, it would be necessary to require a 
competent authority to verify that the Khasra 
Numbers, which are enumerated in the tabulation 
contained in the said plan, corresponds with the actual 
Khasra Numbers, as seen in the plan. Secondly, to 
ensure that there is compliance of the assurance given 
to this Court - that development shall not be taken in 
an area of more than 20,000 Sq. Mtrs., it is necessary 
that respondent nos. 3 and 4, and all other entities 
owning lands within the same estate, file their 
respective affidavits by clearly stating the area, which they plan to develop, within the area bounded in red in 
the plan tendered in the Court today, and also by 
enclosing the plan of the area that they wish to 
develop. 
10. We direct the SDM, Dhari to examine the plan, 
which has been tendered in Court today by respondent 
nos. 3 & 4, and to send a report to this Court, whether 
Khasra Numbers tabulated therein, and the area of the 
said Khasra Numbers, corresponds with the Khasra 
Numbers in the area, which are demarcated in red in 
the said plan. The coloured copy of the said plan shall 
be made available by respondent nos. 3 & 4 to Mr. J.P. 
Joshi, learned Additional Advocate General during the 
course of the day for preparation of the said report by 
the SDM.
11. The affidavits, in terms of this order, be filed by 
respondent nos. 3 and 4, and other associated entities 
within a week. 
12. List on 09.08.2023 at 02:00 P.M.
13. A certified copy of this order be provided to the 
learned counsel for the parties by the end of the day.
