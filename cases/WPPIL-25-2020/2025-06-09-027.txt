09.06.2025 WPPIL No. 25 of 2020
Hon’ble G. Narendar, C.J.
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
1. Mr. Karthik Jayashankar, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. J.P. Joshi, learned Additional Advocate General

4. Mr. Manoj Kumar, learned Standing Counsel for the 
Union of India. 
5. Heard the learned counsel for the petitioner, learned 
counsel for the intervener, and the learned Additional 
Advocate General for the State. 
6. The controversy in the instant Writ Petition was one 
relating to the 2019 Government Order, whereby certain 
guidelines were framed by the State Government to enable 
the formulation of a definition of the word “forest”, and the 
same came to be questioned before this Court, and this 
Court, by order dated 28.02.2020, has been pleased to grant 
an interim order. Much water has flown since then. The 
issue of forest, or what constitutes a forest, has now been 
seized of by the Bench of Hon’ble the Chief Justice of India in 
Writ Petition(s) (Civil) No(s). 1164/2023, wherein the 
petitioner - a Retired Indian Forest Service Officer, has been 
pleased to question the amendment brought about to the 
Forest Conservation Act, more particularly to Section 2 of the 
Forest Conservation Act. An interim order came to be 
passed on an undertaking of the then ASG. The parties were relegated to a position, where it was made mandatory that 
any diversion of the forest land would be compensated by 
the State by way of a parcel of land, to enable compensatory 
afforestation for the extent of forest land that was lost on an
approved ratio 
7. That apart, by order dated 04.03.2025, the Bench of 
Hon’ble the Chief Justice of India and companion Judge, 
while reiterating the interim order granted earlier in 
paragraph no. 12, proceeded to observe and direct in 
paragraph nos. 7, 8, 9 as under :
“7. Ms. Aishwarya Bhati, learned Additional Solicitor General, 
submits that in pursuance to the directions issued by this Court, the 
Union of India has sought information from various State 
Governments/Union Territories. She relies on the additional affidavit 
dated 28.02.2025 filed on behalf of the Ministry of Environment, 
Forest and Climate Change. A perusal of the same would reveal that 
certain State Governments and Union Territories have not even 
appointed Experts Committees, as are required to be constituted as 
per the directions of this Court. The said affidavit would further show 
that most of the State Governments and Union Territories have not 
completed the work required to be undertaken in accordance with 
Rule 16(1) of the Rules of 2023. 
8. Sub-rule (1) of Rule 16 of the Rules of 2023 requires all the 
State Governments and the Union Territories to prepare a 
consolidated record of such lands including the forest like areas 
identified by the Experts Committee constituted for this purpose, 
unclassed forest lands or community forest lands on which the 
provisions of the Adhiniyam shall be made applicable. 
9. We find that once an exercise, as required to be done under 
sub-rule (1) of Rule 16 of the Rules of 2023 is complete, it will lead to 
resolution of many issues. It is therefore necessary that all the State 
Governments and the Union Territories should make an earnest effort 
to complete the said exercise within a stipulated period. While doing 
so, the State Governments/Union Territories would also be bound to 
follow the guidelines as issued by this Court in its order dated 6th 
July, 2011 in the case of Lafarge Umiam Mining Private Limited, 
(2011) 7 SCC 338, which reads thus:-
“(vii) Creation and regular updating of a GIS based decision support 
database, tentatively containing inter alia the district-wise details of 
the location and boundary of (i) each plot of land that may be defined 
as forest for the purpose of the Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980; (ii) 
the core, buffer and eco-sensitive zone of the protected areas 
constituted as per the provisions of the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 
1972; (iii) the important migratory corridors for wildlife; and (iv) the 
forest land diverted for non-forest purpose in the past in the district. 
The Survey of India toposheets in digital format, the forest cover 
maps prepared by the Forest Survey of India in preparation of the 
successive State of Forest Reports and the conditions stipulated in the 
approvals accorded under the Forest (Conservation) Act, 1980 for 
each case of diversion of forest land in the district will also be part of 
the proposed decision support database.””
8. After observing as above, the Hon’ble Apex Court has 
been pleased to direct in paragraph no. 10 as under :
“10. We, therefore, direct all the State Governments and the Union Territories, in which the Experts Committees have not yet been 
constituted, to constitute such committees within a period of one 
month from today. The said committees shall complete the exercise, 
as required under Rule 16(1) of the Rules of 2023 along with the 
directions issued by this Court in the case of Lafarge Umiam Mining 
Private Limited (supra), within a period of six months from today and 
submit a report to the Union of India.”
9. Further, in paragraph no. 13, the State Governments 
have been called upon to complete the exercise by 
09.09.2025, and the Hon’ble Apex Court, in paragraph no. 
15, has also put on notice the Chief Secretaries of various 
States and Union Territories, who would be held responsible 
if there is no compliance with the directions dated 
04.03.2025. 
10. The issue relating to forest and forest land, or forest 
like land, being subsisting before the Hon’ble Apex Court, 
and the Hon’ble Apex Court having called for reports of the 
Expert Committees, in terms of Rule 16(1), further 
consideration of the instant Writ Petition, in our considered 
opinion, would be in the teeth of the exercise now being 
carried out by the Hon’ble Apex Court. 
11. That apart, the interim order granted in the instant 
case, in our considered opinion, cannot be pointed out, or 
dallied about, as an excuse for the State not to constitute the 
Expert Committee, and proceed further and comply with the 
directions of the Hon’ble Apex Court. 
12. With these observations, the hearing is adjourned. 
The present Writ Petition shall be listed before this Court on 
12.09.2025.
