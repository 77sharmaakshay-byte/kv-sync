 The petitioner has challenged the legality 
of the Office Order dated 21.11.2019 and 
subsequently, the Office Order dated 
19.02.2020. 
 Mr. S.N. Babulkar, learned Advocate 
General for the State of Uttarakhand, is 
directed to bring the policy of the Government 
with regard to the afforestation and 
deforestation of the State on record. For, it is 
imperative that the conflicting interests of 
sustaining the environment, and developing
the State are balanced for the growth and 
progress of the State. 
 Mr. S.N. Babulkar, learned Advocate 
General, seeks four weeks’ time to bring the 
said policy on record.
