COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS 
12.12.2023 C-482 No. 2377 of 2023 
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Amanjot Singh Chadha, learned counsel, 
appears for the applicants. 
2. Mr. Saurabh Pandey, learned Brief Holder 
appears for the State, accepts notice on behalf of 
respondent No. 1. 
3. Issue Notice to respondent No. 2. 
4. Learned counsel for the applicant shall take 
steps, within a period of one week, to serve respondent 
No. 2 by registered post. 
5. By the instant application, preferred under 
Section 482 of Cr.P.C., the applicants are challenging
the proceeding of Criminal Complaint Case No. 2451 of 
2023 Disha Agrawal vs. Bipin Jindal and others for the 
offences punishable under Sections 323, 325, 504 and 
506 IPC in connection with FIR No. 395 of 2020, Police 
Station Kashipur, District-Udham Singh Nagar on the 
ground that the present applicants have been 
implicated pursuant to the FIR lodged by respondent 
No. 2 on 11.08.2020, on which after investigation, a 
final report was filed and on protest, directions were 
issued for further investigation and again after further 
investigation, a final report was submitted and again a 
protest was filed by respondent No. 2 and on protest, it 
was submitted that the police, in connivance with the 
present applicants, who were accused in the FIR lodged 
by respondent No. 2, submitted final reports twice and, 
therefore, her protest petition be treated as complaint case. Consequently, the Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, by order dated 05.08.2023, treated the 
protest petition as the complaint case and the same 
was registered as Criminal Case No. 250 of 2020 and 
after recording statements under Sections 200 and 202 
of Cr.P.C. of Nitin Sharma (CW1), Gaurav Agrawal 
(CW2) and Rakesh Kumar Agrawal (CW3), the 
Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Kashipur, took 
cognizance and issued summons against the applicants. 
6. Learned counsel for the applicants submits that 
treating the protest petition as the complaint case by 
the Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Kashipur is 
totally without application of mind and further without 
any material, though twice investigations were carried 
out on the FIR lodged by respondent No. 2 and the final 
reports were submitted. 
7. The most relevant aspect of the matter is that in 
respect of the incident which was happened on 
07.08.2020, a First Information Report was lodged by 
applicant No. 1 against Saurabh Agrawal, Gaurav 
Agrawal an Rakesh Kumar Agrawal and three to four 
unknown persons, wherein the allegations have been 
alleged that the daughter of applicant No. 1 was tried 
to be molested by the accused against whom the FIR 
has been lodged and his son was brutally beaten. This 
FIR was lodged on the same day on which date, the 
incident was happened, i.e. on 07.08.2020, and after 
thorough investigation, charge-sheet was filed against 
Nitin Sharma, Gaurav Agrawal, and Rakesh Agrawal for 
the offences punishable under Sections 147, 323, 354, 
504 and 506 of IPC, in which the Court has already 
taken cognizance on 11.09.2020 and all these three accused persons are on bail, but charges have not been 
framed against them. 
8. This is very surprising that the charge-sheet was 
filed on 11.09.2020 but up till date, charges have not 
been framed against these accused persons. 
9. Learned counsel for the applicants also placed 
before this Court the photographs of the CCTV footage, 
along with the CD of the CCTV footage, and this Court 
has also gone through the photographs, which have 
also been examined by Mr. Saurabh Pandey, learned 
Brief Holder for the State. 
10. Prima facie, after examining the records placed 
on record and further taking into consideration that in 
the FIR lodged by respondent No. 2 twice final reports 
were submitted and in respect of the incident which 
was happened on 07.08.2020, after more than four 
days, the FIR was lodged by respondent No.2, i.e. on 
11.08.2020, whereas in respect of the same incident, 
applicant No. 1 lodged the FIR on the same day, 
wherein the charge-sheet was filed. Implication of the 
present applicants appears to be malicious prosecution. 
11. I have also gone through the order taking 
cognizance on the complaint after recording statements 
under Sections 200 and 202 of Cr.P.C., whereby the 
present applicants have been summoned and it appears 
that the statements of those persons were taken into 
consideration while summoning the present applicants, 
who are in fact accused in the FIR lodged by the 
present applicants on 07.08.2020, wherein the charge￾sheet was filed and cognizance has been taken. 
12. Prima facie, this Court is of the view that taking 
cognizance on the complaint of respondent No.2 by the Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Kashipur, District￾Udham Singh Nagar is wholly without application of 
mind and furthermore I also examined the allegations 
as alleged in the FIR, which shows that it is a glaring 
example of malicious prosecution. 
13. Let counter-affidavits be filed by the respondents 
within a period of three weeks. 
14. One week’s time thereafter is granted to learned 
counsel for the applicant to file rejoinder-affidavit. 
15. For an interim measure, further proceeding of 
Complaint Case No. 2451 of 2023 Disha Agrawal vs. 
Bipin Jindal and others, which in fact was arising out of 
FIR No. 395 of 2020 lodged on 11.08.2020 at P.S. 
Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar, which was 
subsequently, after submission of final reports twice, 
was converted to complaint, are stayed till further 
orders. 
16. List this matter on 22.02.2024. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J)
