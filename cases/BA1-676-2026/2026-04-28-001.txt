Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Harshpal Sekhon, learned counsel for the Applicant. 
2. Mr. Vikash Uniyal, learned Brief Holder for the State of 
Uttarakhand. 
3. The present Bail Application has been moved by the 
Applicant, who is in judicial custody in connection with FIR No. 
188 of 2025, registered at Police Station Pantnagar, District Udham 
Singh Nagar, for the offences punishable under Sections 109 and 
3(5) of the B.N.S., 2023 and Section 3/25 of the Arms Act, 1959.
4. The primary contention advanced by learned counsel for the 
Applicant, for consideration of bail at this juncture, is that the 
prosecution story is wholly false and that the Applicant has been 
wrongly implicated in the present matter. It is alleged by the 
prosecution that at the time of the incident, during the course of 
patrolling duty, the Applicant opened fire upon police officials. 
However, learned counsel for the Applicant submits that the true 
version is that the police were pursuing the Applicant and attempted 
an encounter, during which the Applicant sustained a gunshot injury 
allegedly caused by the police officials.
5. Learned State Counsel is directed to file objections to the bail 
application.
6. List this case on 15.05.2026.
