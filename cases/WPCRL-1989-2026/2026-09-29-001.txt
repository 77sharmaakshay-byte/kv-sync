2. By means of the present Writ Petition, the 
petitioner has sought quashing of First 
Information Report dated 16.09.2026, being FIR 
No. 364 of 2026, registered at Police Station 
Gangnahar, District Haridwar, for the offences 
punishable under Sections 109(1), 352 and 
353(3) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that the petitioner has been falsely implicated 
in the present matter and no specific role has 
been assigned to him. It is further submitted 
that the petitioner neither fired any shot nor 
committed any other overt act and was merely 
present in the car of co-accused Ravi 
Chaudhary, having no concern with the 
incident. Furthermore, even if the averments 
made in the F.I.R. are taken to be true on their 
face value, no offence is made out against the 
petitioner.
4. Be that as it may.
5. Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the record.
6. This Court finds some substance in the 
submissions advanced by learned counsel for 
the petitioner. Having considered the 
submissions made and the material available 
on record, this Court is of the view that the 
matter requires consideration.
7. Let a counter affidavit be filed by the 
respondents within three weeks.
8. Issue notice to respondent no. 3. Steps be taken within one week.
9. List this case after receipt of the service 
report.
10. Till the next date of listing, as an interim 
measure, the petitioner shall not be arrested in 
connection with the aforesaid F.I.R., provided 
that he cooperates with the investigation.
11. The Stay Application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
