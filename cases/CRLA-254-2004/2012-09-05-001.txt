2. Mr. Vinay Sharma, Advocate submitted that he has been 
engaged very recently to argue the case on behalf of appellant Ashok 
in the appeal. He has further submitted that some senior has been 
engaged and, in the absence of the senior, he is unable to argue the 
case. It was submitted that the senior was on way to Nainital, but 
because he took ill, he could not reach Nainital. It was submitted that 
a short adjournment may be given. We, accordingly, directed that the 
matter be passed over for the week. No sooner we passed that order, a

submission was made that the matter be fixed for 19th September, 
2012, inasmuch as, the senior advocate, engaged by Mr. Vinay 
Sharma, will be at Mumbai during the next week. We refused to fix 
the matter on 19th September, 2012. We have got great doubt about 
the substance of the submissions those were made before us. We, 
accordingly, directed Mr. Vinay Sharma to argue the case. He failed 
to do so. 
3. We, accordingly, appoint Mr. J.S. Virk, Advocate as Amicus 
Curiae to argue the case on behalf of appellant Ashok in CRLA No. 
259 of 2004. Let a copy of the paper book in connection with the said 
appeal be handed over to the Amicus Curiae in course of this week. 
4. Since no one is appearing on behalf of the appellant in CRLA 
No. 254 of 2004, we also direct Mr. J.S. Virk, Advocate to act as 
Amicus Curiae in connection with the said appeal too. 
5. Mr. Arvind Vashisth, Advocate appears on behalf of the 
appellant in CRLA No. 258 of 2004. He is present in Court and, in 
his presence, this order is passed on the understanding that Mr. 
Vashisth is ready to argue the case on behalf of the appellant in CRLA 
No. 258 of 2004. 
6. Mr. Rajendra Singh, Advocate, who was appearing on behalf of 
appellant Ashok in CRLA No. 259 of 2004 did not make any 
submission in view of the submissions made by Mr. Vinay Sharma, 
Advocate and, on his prayer, we are permitting him to withdraw from 
the appeal. 
7. List these matters for hearing in the weekly cause list 
commencing from the week 10th September, 2012, along with the 
Government Appeal.
