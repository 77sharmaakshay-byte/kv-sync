It is the case of the petitioner that certain 
land was acquired, which was on lease to the 
petitioner on which he has raised a nursery. 
The compensation was fixed separately for land 
as well as for the nursery. The land owners 
have received the amount of compensation qua
land, but the amount computed for 
compensation of the nursery is still lying with 
the Collector, to which the petitioner is 
entitled.
Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the record.
During the course of hearing, the Court 
wanted to know as to how without impleading 
the land owners, whose land was acquired, the 
claim of the petitioner may be decided in this 
petition?
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that he would make them party. He 
would submit that all those land owners are 
party in the court of Additional District Judge, 
Tehri Garhwal, in LAR Case No. 1 of 2020. He 
submits that once they are made party, the 
petitioner be permitted to serve them through 
Collector, Tehri Garhwal.
Learned counsel for the petitioner seeks time to file application for adding those land 
owners within a week.
