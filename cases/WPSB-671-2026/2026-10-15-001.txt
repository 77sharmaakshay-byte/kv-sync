6. Since common facts are involved in this 
batch of writ petitions, therefore, facts of WPSB 
No. 671 of 2026 are being taken into 
consideration for the sake of brevity and 
convenience.
7. The petitioner was engaged as Assistant 
Professor (Pharmacology science) in Gurukul 
Kangri Vishwavidhyalaya, Haridwar in the year 
2010 for a term of two years. According to the petitioner, he has been serving continuously in 
the said capacity and, his term was extended 
from time to time. However, he has been 
disengaged from service w.e.f. 01.08.2026.
8. Mr. Ajay Veer Pundir, learned counsel 
appearing for the Vishwavidhyalaya, submits 
that petitioner did not make any representation 
for extension of his term. He assures that if 
petitioner makes such representation, the same 
shall be considered, as per law.
9. In view of above, we adjourn this matter to 
03.11.2026.
10. In the meantime, petitioner shall be at 
liberty to make representation to the competent 
authority in the Vishwavidhyalaya within two 
weeks. The decision taken on petitioner’s 
application shall be communicated to the 
petitioner within one week thereafter.
11. List on 03.11.2026 along with connected 
matters.
