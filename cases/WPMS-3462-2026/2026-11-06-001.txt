Hon’ble Siddhartha Sah, J.
Mr. Amish Tewari, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Devendra Pant, learned Standing 
Counsel with Mr. Hargovind Pant, learned Brief 
Holder for the State/respondent nos.1 to 3. 
3. By means of the present writ petition, the 
petitioner has sought the following relief:-
“(i) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of certiorari 
to quash/set aside the impugned letter dated 07.03.2026 
issued by the respondent no. 3 to the petitioner.
(ii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding the respondent no. 3 to give 
permission for construction of building in accordance with 
the G.O. dated 30.06.2021.”
4. Perusal of the sale deed of the petitioner 
would show that the property has been 
purchased as an industrial property and due 
stamp duty has been paid for the same as 
industrial property.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner has 
further drawn the attention of the Court to 
Notification issued by the Government of Uttarakhand dated 06.10.2018, wherein Section 
143A has been inserted in UP ZA and LR Act as 
applicable in the State of Uttarakhand.
6. Perusal of the said provision reveals that 
once land is purchased for industrial purpose 
under Section 154 shall be deemed to be declared 
automatically with the industrial estate under 
Section 143.
7. The grievance as raised by the petitioner by 
means of the present petition is that, in spite of 
the single window clearance application, which is 
an online application for building construction 
permit, the said application has not been 
processed with the objection of as CLU
(industrial) of the land is to be submitted, till then 
the application cannot be processed.
8. Learned counsel for the petitioner on 
instructions would submit that this CLU would 
stand for “change of land use”.
9. He would further submit that since the 
property in question has been purchased as an 
industrial property, therefore, by virtue of the 
notification of the State of Uttarakhand dated 
06.10.2018, once the property is purchased as 
industrial for industrial purposes, it shall be 
deemed to be automatically declared as industrial 
under Section 143 of the UPZA and LR Act. 
10. Per contra, learned State counsel would 
submit that while drawing the attention of the 
Court to explanation to Section 143A, that the 
word 'industrial purpose' mentioned in this 
section includes medical, health and academic 
purposes and also as per explanation to Section 154 (2), the land and the project of the petitioner 
are in Haridwar District, therefore, they would not 
be covered by the aforesaid Notification of the 
State of Uttarakhand. 
11. The matter requires deliberation. 
12. Admit.
13. Learned counsel for the respondent nos. 1, 
2 and 3 seeks and is granted three weeks’ time to 
file counter affidavit positively. 
14. List for further consideration on 
30.11.2026.
