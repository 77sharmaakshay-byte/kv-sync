CLMA No.5531 of 2020 (Money Withdrawal Appl.)
In 
A.O. No. 26 of 2020
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
The matter is taken up through video 
conferencing.
Heard Mr. I.P. Kohli, Advocate for the
appellant.
There is no representation for the 
respondents.
This money withdrawal application 
has been filed by claimant/ respondent 
no. 1 for withdrawal of the amount of 
compensation deposited by the Insurance 
Company before learned Tribunal. 
Learned Tribunal had awarded a sum 
of ` 7,10,000/- as compensation to the 
claimant/respondent no. 1(husband of the 
deceased).
 Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that the appellant has deposited 
the entire amount with the concerned 
tribunal in terms of the order passed by 
this Court.
 Having regard to the facts and 
circumstances of the case, money 
withdrawal application is allowed and it is 
provided that a sum of ` 4,00,000/- of the 
awarded amount shall be released in 
favour of claimant/ respondent no. 1. The 
remaining amount shall be invested in an 
interest bearing scheme of a nationalized 
bank.
 Accordingly, money withdrawal 
application also stands disposed of. 
 (Manoj K. Tiwari, J.) Arpan 07.08.2020
