4. The matter has been listed on several dates and was listed even 
yesterday. Yesterday, the proceedings of the Chairman, Municipal 
Corporation, Nainital was placed. In the order dated 16.10.2025, this 
Court observed that Room No. 6 is unoccupied. The observations are not 
controverted. 
6. In that view, pending disposal of the writ petition, we direct the 
respondent-Municipal Corporation, Nainital to allot Room No. 6 to the 
appellant. The keys shall be handed over to the appellant by the 
respondent-Municipal Corporation 20.10.2025. 
7. We pass the order in view of the twin facts that the petitioner, 
who was in occupation of the property has been ousted from it and is a 
senior citizen and; that right to shelter is now in law recognized. 
8. Registry to communicate this order to the respondent-Municipal 
Corporation through e-mail forthwith. 
9. List this matter on 30.10.2025.
