4. By means of the present writ petition, petitioner 
sought a direction to respondent no. 3 to re-examine and 
reconsider the petitioner’s objections regarding Question 
Nos.31, 42 & 46 through an expert body, giving an 
authentic conclusion on each of them and other ancillary 
reliefs.
5. It is contended by learned counsel for the petitioner 
that petitioner is working as Assistant Teacher, L.T. 
Grade (Agriculture), in Government Intermediate College, 
Thangdhar, Chamba, District Tehri Garhwal. Respondent 
no.2 invited applications for posting/arrangement in Atal 
Utkrisht Government Secondary Schools as Lecturer and 
Assistant Teacher, L.T. Grade, of the relevant subject 
who are working in Government Secondary Schools, vide 
order dated 07.12.2024. For the said purpose, 
applications were invited and it was decided that the 
screening examination would be conducted by the 
respondent/State. Petitioner participated in the 
Screening Examination, 2025, held by respondent no. 3.
6. According to the petitioner, there are two papers in 2
the screening examination, namely, (i) English and (ii) 
Subject Specified. Since petitioner is Assistant Teacher, 
L.T. Grade (Agriculture), therefore, he appeared in both 
the examinations, i.e., English as well as the Subject 
Specified (Agriculture). As per the terms and conditions 
of the examination, a candidate has to secure 12 marks 
out of 30 in each paper. Petitioner qualified the English 
paper by obtaining 20 marks out of 30, while in the 
Subject Specified, i.e., Agriculture, he could obtain 9 
marks out of 30.
6. Objections were invited by respondent no. 3 and 
petitioner filed objections regarding Question Nos. 31, 
37, 42, 46 & 55. The objections of the petitioner 
regarding Question Nos. 37 & 55 found favour with 
respondent no. 3 and the petitioner was granted bonus 
marks for Question Nos. 37 & 55. Thus, there was an 
increase of 2 marks in the total score of the petitioner, 
bringing his total score in the Subject Specified paper to 
11 out of 30.
7. So far as Question Nos. 31, 42 & 46 are concerned, 
the Commission found the answers given to these 
questions to be correct on the basis of the opinion given 
by the subject expert and, accordingly, the objections 
raised by the petitioner were disposed of. Feeling 
aggrieved, the petitioner approached this Court.
8. It is contended by the petitioner that, in respect of 
Question Nos. 31, 42 & 46, the answers given by him are 
also correct; therefore, he deserves 3 more marks. If 3 3
more marks are granted to the petitioner, his total score 
would become 14 out of 30, which would make him 
qualified to be posted as Assistant Teacher, L.T. Grade, 
in Atal Utkrisht Government Secondary Schools. The 
petitioner relied upon photocopies of certain books, i.e., 
Basic Fundamentals of Soil and Water Conservation, to 
suggest that the answers given by him are correct, and 
also relied upon certain documents to suggest that the 
answers given by him are correct.
9. Since the subject expert has opined against the 
petitioner in respect of Question Nos. 31, 42 & 46, the 
information given by the subject expert cannot be 
ignored while considering the writ petition. Accordingly, 
respondent no. 3 is directed to file a counter affidavit 
within four weeks, annexing thereto the opinion of the 
subject expert along with the documents/books relied 
upon by the subject expert.

11. However, it is provided that one post of Assistant 
Teacher, L.T. Grade (Agriculture) in Atal Utkrisht 
Government Secondary Schools in the Garhwal Region 
shall be kept vacant for the petitioner.
