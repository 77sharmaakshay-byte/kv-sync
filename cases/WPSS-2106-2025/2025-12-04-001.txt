Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Hirdiyangam Shah, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. Rajeev Singh Bisht, learned Additional

3. Mr. Virendra Singh Rawat, learned counsel 
for respondent no. 3.
4. Petitioner participated for selection to the 
post of Assistant Teacher in Atal Utkrisht 
Rajkiya Madhyamik Vidyalaya.
5. As per the contention of the petitioner,
after the declaration of the result, the 
provisional answer key was published by the 
Board. In same publication/declaration of result, 
the respondent no. 3, UBSE invited objections 
from the candidates. In this regard, the 
petitioner submitted five objections regarding 
discrepancies in the published answer key.
Petitioner gave objection in respect of five 
questions being Question nos. 31, 37, 42, 46 
and 55.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the Board considered the objection 
and found that the options given by the 
petitioner in respect of question nos. 37 and 55 
were correct and two marks were given to him, 
but, options regarding question no. 31, 42 and 
46 were rejected.
7. Petitioner has drawn the attention of this 
Court to question nos. 31, 42 and 46, which are 
as hereunder:-
“Question No. 31: Most drought tolerant crop is?
A. Chick pea
B. MustardC. Lentil
D. Barley
Answer by UBSE Answer by petitioner
D (Barley) C (Lentil)
Question No. 42: Khoa contain milk fat not less than?
A. 25%
B. 20%
C. 30%
D. 35%
Answer by UBSE Answer by petitioner
B (20%) C (30%)
Question No. 46: T & V system of a good example?
A. Extension approach
B. Cooperative approach
C. Training approach
D. Integrated Development approach”
Answer by UBSE Answer by petitioner
C (Training Approach) D (Integrated development 
approach)
8. In support of his contention, learned 
counsel for the petitioner would submit that the 
answer given by respondent no. 3 is wrong on 
the face of the record. In support of his 
contention, he has placed the Gazette of India 
and FSSAI report.
9. Learned counsel for respondent no. 3 has 
sought three days’ time to seek instructions 
regarding these questions.
