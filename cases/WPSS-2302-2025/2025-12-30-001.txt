It is the case of the petitioner that 
from his pension Rs.1,73,334/- has been 
deducted without there being any recovery 
order.
Learned State counsel submits that, 
in fact, it is the petitioner who himself has 
deducted the amount.
On it, learned counsel for the 
petitioner submits that it is a trick played 
by the Education Department. They asked 
the retiring person to fill the form so that 
the amount may be deposited.
Let learned State counsel place for 
the perusal of the Court the order by which 
the recovery has been ordered and the 
order by which the petitioner was required 
to deposit the amount in the Government 
exchequer.
List this matter on 07.01.2026.
