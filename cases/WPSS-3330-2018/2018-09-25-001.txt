WPSS No. 3330 of 2018 
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. N.P. Sah, Standing Counsel for the State 
of Uttarakhand/respondents. 
The argument of the learned counsel for the 
petitioner is that the issue involved in this writ 
petition for the relief claim is pending consideration 
before this Court in Writ Petition (S/S) No. 1427 of 
2018 as well as in a bunch of writ petitions with 
leading Writ Petition (S/S) No. 1502 of 2018 
Jyotendra Prasad Barthwal and others Vs. State of 
Uttarakhand and Others, in which this Court has 
granted the following interim order: 
“It is the contention of learned counsel for the 
petitioner that by impugned order dated 06.01.2018 
the benefits extended to the petitioner calculating his 
ad-hoc service for the purpose of grant of time scale 
has been taken away which has serious repercussions 
considering the fact that the benefit which was 
extended to the petitioner cannot be taken away by 
way of office order without affording opportunity of 
hearing to the petitioner. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner also placed 
reliance on the Hon’ble Apex Court’s judgment in 
case of Secretary, Minor Irrigation Department Vs. 
Narendra Kumar Tripathi. 
Having heard the learned counsel for the 
parties and after considering the documents brought 
on record, effect and operation of the order dated 
08.12.2017 and consequential orders dated 
13.12.2017, 16.12.2017 and 06.01.2018 shall remain 
stayed. 
Interim relief application stands disposed of.” 
Respondents may file their counter affidavit 
within three weeks. 
List this case immediately after three weeks.” 
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.) 
25.09.2018 Mahinder/
