2. Mr. B.C. Joshi, learned A.G.A. for the State.
3. By the instant petition, preferred under 
Section 528 of BNSS 2023, the present applicant is 
challenging the proceeding of Special Sessions 
Trial (Electricity Act) No. 957 of 2026, State vs. 
Babu Ram, whereby the applicant is facing the 
trial for the offence punishable under Section 135 
of the Electricity Act. 
4. It is submitted by the learned counsel for 
the applicant that firstly the offence punishable 
under Section 135 of the Electricity Act is 
compoundable and in reference of one of the 
notice dated 02.08.2022, the applicant requests to 
the Authorities to redress his grievance but the 
same has not been redressed, consequently, in 
that compelling circumstances he made online 
complaint in the C.M. Portal but no action has 
been taken thereof. Online complaint is annexed 
as annexure-5.
5. All these aspects require scrutiny.
6. The respondent is arrayed as party in 
official capacity. Let a copy of this petition be 
served upon the learned counsel for the 
Department.
7. The respondents may file counter affidavit within three weeks. One week thereafter is 
granted to the applicant to file rejoinder affidavit.
8. List this case on 15.09.2026.
9. In the meantime, further proceeding of 
Special Sessions Trial (Electricity Act) No. 957 of 
2026, State vs. Babu Ram, pending in the court of 
District & Sessions Judge, Haridwar shall remain 
stayed.
10. Stay application (IA No. 1 of 2026) stands 
disposed of.
