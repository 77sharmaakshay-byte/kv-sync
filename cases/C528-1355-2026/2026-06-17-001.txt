2. Mr. Zoheb Hussain, learned counsel through VC 
with Ms. Monika Pant, learned counsel for the 
respondent/ED.
3. The instant petition has been preferred by the 
applicant under Section 528 of BNSS, 2023 challenging 
the order dated 12.06.2026 passed by the learned 
Special Judge, PMLA, Dehradun in Special Session 
Trial No.28 of 2024, titled as “ED Vs. Parvinder Singh”, 
whereby the application moved on behalf of the 
applicant seeking release from judicial custody and 
opposing further remand was rejected.
4. It is argued by the learned senior counsel for the 
applicant that in fact earlier remand order was expired 
on 06.06.2026, and there was no application of
prosecution to seek further remand, despite this, 
learned Special Judge, by the order impugned hold that 
the remand cannot be presumed to be illegal in terms 
of Section 187 of BNSS, 2023 and the remand as such 
deemed to be justified under the deeming power even 
if the earlier cognizance order was set aside on a
technical ground. 
5. It is argued that there is no deeming provision to 
grant further remand and the order impugned is per se 
illegal. He further submits that Enforcement 
Directorate never asked for further remand, since 
investigation qua the applicant was already completed 
and the remaining bitcoins has already been traced out 
and the provisional attachment order was passed as 
reflected from the application of Enforcement 
Directorate, which is annexed as Annexure no.4. He 
further submits that since after 06.06.2026, there was no 
remand order, therefore, the detention of the applicant 
is illegal and as such he should be released forthwith.6. On the other side, learned counsel for the ED 
submits that the learned Special Court rightly passed 
the order impugned since the cognizance order was set 
aside by the Hon’ble Apex Court only on a technical 
ground and this power is recognized by the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court in the case of “Suresh Kumar 
Bikhamchand Jain Vs. State of Maharashtra” reported 
in 2013 (3) SCC 77, in paragraph 6, 7, 13 and 18.
7. A request has been made by the learned senior 
counsel for the applicant that the pending bail 
application is already fixed for 06.07.2026 and the same 
be prepone for the same date. Learned counsel for the 
ED has not object on this.
8. Put up for further arguments on 29.06.2026 along 
with BA 1st No.1825 of 2025. On that day, learned 
counsel for both the parties may place before this Court 
the complete order-sheet.
9. In the meantime, respondent may also file their 
objections.
