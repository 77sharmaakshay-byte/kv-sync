WPSS No. 702 of 2023
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Vinay Kumar, Advocate for the 
petitioners.
Mr. Puran Singh Bisht Additional 
C.S.C. for the State.
Mr. C.K. Sharma, Advocate, holding 
brief of Mr. Harsh Vardhan Dhanik, 
Advocate for the respondent no.5.
It is the case of the petitioners that the 
respondent no.5 conducted an examination 
for recruitment to the post of Foresters 
through one recruiting agency the National 
Stock Exchange for Information and 
Technology (“for short, the NSEIT”). 
Initially, when some persons came to know 
that the NSEIT is blacklisted in many 
States, they filed a Writ Petition (S/S) No. 
1165 of 2021 before this Court for change of 
recruitment agency. But, on behalf of 
respondent nos. 4 and 5, it was assured to 
the Court that respondent no.4 the NSEIT is 
not negligent anywhere and the examination 
would be conducted as per Rules and 
Regulations. It is argued that the entire 
examination process, which was conducted 
in eighteen phases, has been scraped by the 
respondent no.5 and they have advertised 
another schedule for the conducting 
examination, which is to begin from 
11.06.2023, this is Annexure 7 to the writ 
petition, which is questioned in this writ 
petition.
Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that the wrong doers, if any, 
may be identified by respondent no.5. The 
whole examination process which took place 
in eighteen phases, it need not be scraped
in toto. It is submitted that the examination 
was on online mode. The tracking would be 
quite convenient to track the wrong doers.Having considered, this Court is of the 
view that this matter requires deliberation.
Issue notice to the respondent no.4.
Learned counsel for the petitioners 
seeks permission to serve the respondent 
no.4 through electronic mode. In fact, for 
that purpose no permission as such is 
required. It is an accepted mode of service 
by this Court
Let such service be effected.
Since it is stated that the examination 
is to be held on 11.05.2023 and learned 
counsel for the respondent no.5 as well as 
the learned counsel for the State are 
agreeable to decide the case at the earliest.
Let counter affidavit be filed by the 
respondents within next 10 days. 2 days 
thereafter, rejoinder affidavit, if any, may be 
filed.
Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that no rejoinder affidavit is 
required to be filed.
List for final hearing on 22.05.2023 
just after fresh cases.
