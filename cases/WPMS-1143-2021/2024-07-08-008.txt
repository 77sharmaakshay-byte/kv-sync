2. Petitioner pursued M.B.B.S. Course 
on subsidized fee from a Government 
Medical College of Uttarakhand, after 
executing a bond that she will serve in 
hill areas of Uttarakhand for minimum 
period of 5 years. Petitioner has served 
only for about 7 months in terms of the 
bond and she has made a request to 
Director General, Medical, Health & 
Family Welfare to release her educational 
certificates, which are lying deposited 
with the Principal of the concerned 
Government College. Her request was 
turned down by Competent Authority. 
Thus, feeling aggrieved, petitioner has 
approached this Court.
3. In paragraph no.11 of the counter 
affidavit filed on behalf of respondent 
no.3, following averment is made:
“11. That the respondent no.03 sent a letter dated 
28.01.2021 to the petitioner informing that as per 
the G.O. 596/XXVIII(1)/2009-UO/03 dated 
07.09.2009, in order to get free of the bond, the will 
have to deposit Rs.29,16,473/- to the institute as 
per the terms and conditions of the bond signed at 
the time of admission in MBBS on state quota.”
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that petitioner is ready to 
deposit the amount indicated in paragraph no.11 of the counter affidavit
under protest, however, such deposit 
shall abide by outcome of this writ 
petition. 
5. In such view of the matter, 
petitioner shall deposit a sum of 
₹29,16,473/- with the Principal, 
Government Medical College, Srinagar, 
Pauri Garhwal, within 15 days from 
today. Subject to petitioner depositing 
the aforesaid amount under protest with 
the Principal of the concerned Medical 
College, her educational certificates shall 
be released. However, deposit of the 
aforesaid amount in lieu of release of 
educational certificates, shall abide by 
outcome of the writ petition.
6. Interim relief application (I.A. No.1 
of 2021) stands disposed of.
7. List for hearing on 23.08.2024.
