The instant jail appeal has been preferred against 
judgment and order dated 24.04.2023, passed in Special Sessions 
Trial No.57 of 2020, State Vs. Pratap, by the court of Additional 
Session Judge/FTSC, District Haridwar. By it, the appellant has 
been convicted under Sections 363, 376(3) IPC, and Section 
3(a)/4(2) of the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 
2012 and sentenced accordingly. The appellant has filed Short 
Term Bail Application, IA No.3 of 2026, on the ground that his 
sister is to marry on 22.06.2026.
2. Heard on Short Term Bail Application, IA No.3 of 
2026
3. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that the 
sister of the appellant is to marry on 22.06.2026; the appellant is 
the eldest brother; his presence is required. Therefore, purely on 
humanitarian grounds, short term bail may be granted for 
attending the marriage of his sister and for making necessary 
arrangements.2
4. Learned State Counsel, under instructions, 
confirms that the marriage of the sister of the appellant is 
scheduled on 22.06.2026.
5. Having considered, without adverting to the merits 
of the case, purely on the humanitarian ground, this Court is of 
the view that the appellant may be enlarged on short term bail for 
a period of two days from the date of his release.
6. Let the appellant be released on short term bail for 
a period of two days from the date of his actual release, on his 
executing a personal bond and furnishing two reliable sureties, 
each of the like amount, to the satisfaction of the court concerned.
7. It is further directed that on expiry of the period of 
short term bail, the appellant shall surrender before the court 
concerned and intimation to that effect shall also be given to this 
Court.
8. The short term bail application stands disposed of, 
accordingly. 
9. List in due course for final hearing.
