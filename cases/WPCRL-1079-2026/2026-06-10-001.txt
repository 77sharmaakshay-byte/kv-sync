Heard. 
Learned counsel for the petitioners 
submits that the petitioners are convicts. 
They are eligible for premature release even 
according to the Government’s Policy, but 
their case has not been recommended for 
premature release.
The Court wanted to know from 
learned State Counsel as to why it is done? 
He seeks time to get instructions.
List this matter on 17.06.2026.
On that date, learned State Counsel 
shall give a categorical statement as to what 
is the basis of not recommending the case 
of the petitioners for premature release if 
their case is covered under the policy for 
premature release, as laid down by the 
State Government itself.
