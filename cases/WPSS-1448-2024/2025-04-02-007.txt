COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 1448 of 2024
And connected matters
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, Mr. Vinay Kumar, Mr. Tapan 
Singh and Mr. M.C. Pant, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State.
3. Ms. Devika Tiwari, learned counsel for the 
respondent.
4. Chairman and the Secretary are also present in 
order to assist this court and they place before this 
court the guidelines.
5. Put up this matter on 04.04.2025 at 2:00 PM to 
examine the following issues:
(i) Whether the recruiting/selecting body has a 
right to reject the candidature of a candidate after 
permitting him to participate in the examination 
on the ground of the eligibility?
(ii) Whether the guidelines framed by the 
Commission in the year 2015 have a statutory 
force particularly in view of Section 15(2) of the 
Act when the guidelines apparently are
inconsistent with the Act and the Rules?
(iii) Whether the Commission/Selecting body was 
right in publishing the provisional merit list 
thrice in this case particularly in view of Section 
18 of the Uttarakhand Act No. 20 of 2014?
(iv) Whether while preparing the merit list the 
eligibility of a candidate has to be seen or only 
the marks obtained in the examination?
(v) Whether the eligibility of a candidate has to be 
scrutinized at once that too before the 
commencement of the written examination or till 
the conclusion of the selection?
(vi) Whether the Commission was right in 
entertaining the complaints of the candidates 
that too after the commencement of the written examination i.e. after issuance of the first 
provisional merit list dated 17.04.2023?
(vii) Whether the State Government was right in 
intervening in the mid of the selection on the 
request of the commission to depute their 
officials for examining the genuineness of the 
certificate of the candidates?
(viii) Whether selecting body was right in sending 
select list in part followed by their appointment
particularly when the eligibility of some of the 
candidates who have permitted to participate in 
the written examination were under scrutiny?
6. In the meantime, learned counsel for the 
selecting body shall prepare compilation of the Act, 
Rules and the Guidelines.
7. For the time being the personal appearance of the 
officers are exempted.
