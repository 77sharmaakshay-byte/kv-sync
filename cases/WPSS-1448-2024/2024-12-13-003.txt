7. On the previous date, learned State 
Counsel placed before this Court the Uttarakhand 
Act No.20 of 2014 i.e. The Uttarakhand 
Subordinate Service Selection Commission Act, 
2014 as well as the Rules framed by the 
Commission by invoking the powers conferred by 
Section 23 of Act No.20 of 2014 and this Court 
examined all the provisions of the Act as well as the Regulations and there is no such provision, 
which empowered the recruiting body to prepare 
a provisional list and to publish the same in the 
website. 
8. Section 18 of the Act clearly provides that 
after receiving the requisition, the recruiting body 
after scrutinizing the applications shall proceed
with the written examination or interview,
whatever it is and then prepare a select list and 
forward it to the Appointing Authority. There is 
no such provision for publication of merit list in 
the website. 
9. Surprisingly, in this particular case, thrice 
the provisional merit list was published in the 
website and thereafter certain news items were 
got viral on the social media on which the 
recruiting body referred the matter to vigilance 
establishment and also constituted a committee 
to examine the genuineness of the testimonials 
of some of the candidates. Thereafter, again 
recruiting body received some more complaints 
from different sources and thereafter result of 14 
candidates were put on hold. Thereafter, again a 
three members committee was constituted and 
the said committee submitted its report, which is 
reflected from the show cause notices issued to 
the candidates wherein it is mentioned that the 
Commission from its own level through enquiry 
committee carried physical verification of the 
concerned firms, but the Committee does not 
find the firms in the given address and local 
peoples also inform that they have no knowledge 
of firms from which it reveals that firm is not 
established in the given address.
10. It is very surprising and very shocking that 
in mid of selection process, the news relating to 
select list got viral in social media. It should not 
be happened because once Government sent a 
requisition to the recruiting body, then secrecy and transparency of selection has to be 
maintained.
11. Apart from this, what this Court observed 
that by publishing the provisional merit list in the 
website on different stages without forwarding it 
to the Appointing Authority inviting objections 
from the candidates, who participated in the 
selection process, wholly impermissible and 
unwarranted. Who are responsible for this? This 
Court will also go into this question. 
12. Now the question is whether in the mid of 
selection process, recruiting body can invite 
objections from candidates, particularly, when 
the selection is on the way and no select list was 
sent to the Government/Appointing Authority as 
per mandate of Section 18 of Act No.20 of 2014. 
13. After gone through the provisions of 
Uttarakhand Act No.20 of 2014, i.e. the 
Uttarakhand Subordinate Service Selection 
Commission Act, 2014, as well as the entire 
Rules framed by the Commission by invoking 
power, as conferred by Section 23, and further 
the procedure, as adopted by the respondent￾Commission, primarily, this Court is of the view 
that the Commission failed to maintain the 
secrecy of selection, which itself is reflected from 
Annexure-10, the press note dated 29.01.2024, 
wherein there is a reference that the news 
relating to selection, in question, got viral in 
social media. Therefore, this Court is taking very 
serious view in the matter, for which, this Court 
wants the assistance of learned Advocate 
General. Therefore, this Court is framing certain 
questions, so that the learned Advocate General 
may address on these issues.
i. Whether the Uttarakhand Subordinate Service 
Selection Commission was right in publishing 
provisional merit list in its website for the 
purpose of inviting objections and 
representations from the candidates, as it is reflected from the advertisement dated 
25.10.2023 (Annexure-6), advertisement dated 
10.11.2023 (Annexure-7), and advertisement 
dated 08.01.2024 (Annexure-8).
ii. Whether Section 18 of the Uttarakhand Act 
No.20 of 2014 empowers the Commission to 
publish provisional merit lists in its website, to 
forward the one select list to the Appointing 
Authority.
iii. Whether the Commission was right to 
entertain the complaints received from different 
sources (as reflected from press note dated 
29.01.2024) in mid of the selection process, 
particularly, when the select list was not 
forwarded to the Appointing Authority as per 
Section 18 of the Act.
iv. Whether the Commission was right in 
constituting a Committee for verifying the 
location of firm to ascertain the genuineness of 
certificates of the candidates, and based on the 
said report, selection of petitioners put on hold.
