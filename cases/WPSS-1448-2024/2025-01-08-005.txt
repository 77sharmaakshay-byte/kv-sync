1. Mr. M.C. Pant, Mr. Vinay Kumar, Mr. 
Pramod Bailwal and Mr. Tapan Singh, learned 
counsel for the petitioners, Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, 
learned Standing Counsel for the State and Mr. B.D. 
Pande, learned counsel for respondent no. 3.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing 
Counsel in order to assist this Court placed before 
this Court one of the Government Order dated 
13.03.2024, which clearly provides, at what stage 
the document verification has to be done.
3. On perusal of this Government Order, it 
reveals that the Recruiting Body are exceeding their 
powers and jurisdiction, due to which, there is no 
secrecy and transparency in the selection conducted by the recruiting body. This is a case in 
which a news item with regard to these selections 
got viral in the social media and thereafter so many 
complaints have been received and thereafter, the 
Recruiting Body constituted a Committee for the 
purposes of spot inspection and the members of 
the Committee inquired by visiting the site and get 
the statement of the local people and submit the 
report.
4. Since, the entire functioning of the 
Recruiting Bodies is under scrutiny, therefore, put 
up this matter on 09.01.2025 at 04:00 p.m.,
treating WPSS No. 1448 of 2024 as the leading one.
Copy of this order be placed in the file of each of 
the connected matters.
