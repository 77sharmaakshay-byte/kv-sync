COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 2239 of 2022
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. K. S. Agarwal, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Hari Mohan Bhatia, learned counsel for the 
respondent no. 2.
3. On 28.03.2025, this Court passed an order that 
amount of tax pursuant to the notice dated 30.12.2021 
(Annexure No. 6) be remitted by the Bank – respondent no. 
5 to the Tax Recovery Officer – respondent no. 2 within a 
week and the remittance of the said amount of tax shall be 
subject to the outcome of writ petition.
4. Mr. H.M. Bhatia, learned counsel for the respondent 
no. 2 submits that since there was no amount with Bank, 
therefore, PITCUL issued a bank draft in favour of the Tax 
Recovery Officer of an amount Rs. 2,93,27,493/- on 
30.04.2025, toward the amount of tax as demanded by notice 
dated 30.12.2021. He further submits that while issuing the 
bank draft, one email communication was also sent to the 
Tax Recovery Officer with the instruction that since there is 
no order of this Court to credit this amount, therefore, the 
Tax Recover Officer shall not deposit bank draft in the PD 
Account of the Department for encashment. Mr. H.M. 
Bhatia, learned counsel has supplied the photo copy of the 
bank draft, which is taken on record and submits that bank 
draft is already encashed and deposited in PD account.
5. Since tax is a public money and petitioner is already 
protected by this Court by earlier order dated 28.03.2025 
that the remittance of the amount of tax shall be subject to 
the outcome of the writ petition, therefore, rider imposed by 
the PITCUL not to deposit the bank draft in PD account of 
the Department with RBI is unreasonable. In the opinion of 
this Court the Tax Recovery Officer has rightly deposited 
the amount with PD account of the department in RBI. 
6. List for final hearing after four weeks. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
