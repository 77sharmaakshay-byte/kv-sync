Heard on Interim Relief Application, IA 
No.1 of 2025
It is the case of the petitioner that in 
the year 2004, recruitment was done for the 
position of Constables in PAC for both men 
and women. After recruitment was over, by 
an order of the respondent-authorities, it 
was directed that the date of appointment 
shall be 22.10.2005.
The grievance of the petitioner is that 
subsequent to it, the respondent￾authorities have prepared separate seniority 
list for men and women, which, it is argued, 
is against the provisions of law. 
It is submitted by learned counsel for 
the petitioners that both men and women 
Constables are working in the same duties; 
there is no restrictions of posting of either of them in any position; the petitioners have 
already made a representation, Annexure 
No.7 to the writ petition, to the department, 
which has yet not been decided by the 
respondents. 
By means of the interim relief 
application, the petitioners seek stay of the 
separate promotion and Seniority list for 
the male candidates.
Affected persons are not made parties. 
The promotions already made cannot be 
stayed at this stage under the facts and 
circumstances of the case. Therefore, at 
this stage, interim relief application may 
not be allowed. However, the petitioners 
have made a representation, which is 
Annexure No.7 to the writ petition, to the 
respondent no.2, which has yet not been 
decided. 
In order to better assist the Court, it 
would be purposeful if the respondent no.2, 
at this stage, is directed to decide the 
representation in the meanwhile given by 
the petitioners, Annexure No.7 to the writ 
petition. Therefore, the respondent no.2 is 
directed to decide the representation, 
Annexure No.7 to the writ petition, within 
four weeks. 
Interim relief application stands 
disposed of, accordingly.
