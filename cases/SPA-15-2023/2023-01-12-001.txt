Issue notices.
Learned counsels for the respondents
appear and accept notices.
Learned counsel for the appellant points 
out that the impugned judgment has been 
passed without granting an opportunity to the 
appellant or the other respondents to file
their respective counter affidavits.
The writ petition was listed before the 
learned Single Judge on 14.12.2022. On that 
day, the Court directed that copy of the 
petition be served on the counsel for the 
appellant. Matter was adjourned to 
19.12.2022. On 19.12.2022, without calling
for a counter affidavit of any of the 
respondents, petition has been allowed. 
It is further pointed out that there is no 
reason to be found in the impugned judgment
for allowing the same. 
Having been perused the record, we are 
inclined to stay the operation of the impugned 
judgment. 
List the appeal on 27.06.2023.
In the meantime, the respondent no. 2 
and 3 may file their counter affidavits. Since, 
no counter affidavit was called in the writ 2
