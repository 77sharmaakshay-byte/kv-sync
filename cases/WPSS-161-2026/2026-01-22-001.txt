Mr. Parikshit Saini, learned counsel for 
the petitioner through Video Conferencing.
2. Mr. Yogesh Tiwari, learned Addl. C.S.C. 
along with Mr. Gajendra Triptathi, P.C. Bisht 
and Mr. Naveen Tiwari, learned Brief Holder 
for the State.
3. Petitioner is presently serving on the 
post of Child Development Project Officer, 
Roorkee, District Haridwar. On 05.12.2025, a 
show cause notice was issued to the 
petitioner by respondent no.3, wherein it was 
stated that on 03.12.2025, a letter had been 
issued by respondent no.2 taking cognizance 
of a purported viral audio. In the said audio, 
allegations relating to irregularities in the 
nutrition scheme and illegal collection of 
money were levelled, wherein one person 
allegedly named “Varsha Sharma”, the 
petitioner herein. On the basis of the said 
viral audio, it was alleged that the petitioner 
had demanded illegal gratification during a 
meeting, and accordingly, an explanation 
was sought from her.
4. Pursuant thereto, the petitioner 
submitted a detailed reply to the show cause 
notice on 10.12.2025, specifically denying all 
the allegations levelled therein and asserting 
that the allegations were false, baseless, and 
unsupported by any cogent material.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that on the same set of 
circumstances, the petitioner had also lodged 
a complaint under the Sexual Harassment of 
Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition 
and Redressal) Act against respondent no.6, 
who is the Programme Officer and the 
immediate superior of the petitioner. Though 
the statement of the petitioner was recorded 
in the said complaint, no further proceedings 
were undertaken, nor was any action taken in accordance with law.
6. It is further submitted that instead of 
taking the complaint of the petitioner to its 
logical conclusion, the petitioner has been 
transferred on so-called administrative 
grounds, giving a colour of justification to the 
earlier show cause notice. Despite lapse of 
approximately one month after submission of 
the reply to the show cause notice, no 
progress in the proceedings related to the 
viral audio in the department. However, the 
impugned transfer order has been passed on 
the vague allegation that the petitioner may 
influence the inquiry. In absence of any 
pending departmental inquiry or adverse 
finding, the impugned transfer order dated 
15.01.2026 is arbitrary, unjustified, and 
unsustainable in the eyes of law.
7. Issue notice to respondent no.6, 
returnable within six weeks.
8. Steps shall be taken within two weeks.
9. List the case after service of notice upon 
respondent no.6.
10. Meanwhile, the respondents may file 
their counter affidavit.
11. As an interim measure, it is directed 
that the effect and operation of the impugned 
order dated 15.01.2026 shall remain stayed 
till the next date of listing.
12. Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2026)
stands disposed of.
13. Urgency Application (IA No.2 of 2026)
also stands disposed of.
