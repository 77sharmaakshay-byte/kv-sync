COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPCRL No. 737 of 2025
WPCRL No. 838 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Umanshu Singh Jolly, learned counsel 
holding the brief of Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. for the State.
3. In these two petitions the petitioners are praying 
for security since there is a regular threat to their life.
4. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. placed 
before this court the judgment rendered by the 
Division Bench of this court dated 10.11.2016 in 
WPCRL No. 1037 of 2016 and certain relevant extract of 
this judgment are relevant, which are also being 
reproduced herein as under:
“However, in the larger public interest, the State 
Government is directed to withdraw the security cover 
forthwith granted to the persons against whom criminal 
cases for heinous crimes under Sections 302, 304, 376, 392, 
395, 386 I.P.C., POCSO, N.D.P.S. and Arms Act are 
pending. 
There is a scarcity of police personnel in the State. The role 
of the police is to maintain law and order situation and to 
protect the honest citizens. It is not duty of the police to 
protect the criminals. Police protection has become status 
symbol rather necessity. Maintenance of law and order is a 
legal / sovereign function to be discharged by the State 
and the same cannot be permitted to be mis-used by the 
criminals.
In future, the Secretary Home, Government of 
Uttarakhand shall take the final decision on the 
recommendation made by the Committee comprising of 
Inspector General of Police / D.I.G. as well as Senior 
Superintendent of Police of the area concerned on a 
specific intelligence input to whom security cover is to be 
provided.”
5. As it appears from the order passed by the 
Division Bench the role of police is to maintain law and 
order situation and to protect the honest citizen and it 
is not the duty of the police to protect criminals and 
now a days police protection has become status symbol as necessity. Maintenance of law and order is legal 
sovereign function to be discharged by the State and 
the same cannot be permitted to be misused by the 
criminals.
This is one aspect of the matter that if a person 
have a criminal antecedent and are also involved in the 
criminal activity whether he deserves for any 
protection in view of this judgment. Admittedly, the 
police is not meant to protect the criminals.
The Division Bench of this court also directed 
that in future the Secretary Home of the State shall take 
final decision on the recommendation made by the 
committee comprising of Inspector General of Police / 
D.I.G. as well as Senior Superintendent of Police of the 
area concerned on a specific intelligence input to whom 
security cover is to be provided
6. Now, this court want to know from the State 
whether in the light of the view as expressed by the 
Division Bench State have reviewed all those matters in 
which the security has been provided to the persons 
who have criminal background or criminal antecedents 
or indulged in criminal activity.
7. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. apprise to 
this court that the committee in terms of the aforesaid 
order has already constituted. 
8. Be that as it may, still the question remains 
whether the State has reviewed all those matters 
strictly in the light of the Division Bench order, 
therefore, the Secretary Home is directed to file his 
personal affidavit within two weeks after reviewing all 
the cases in which the security has been provided.
9. It is made clear that if any person is involved in 
any criminal activity or have criminal antecedents does 
not deserve for any security in the light of the judgment 
of the Division Bench of this court, therefore, the 
Secretary Home while filing the affidavit should keep 
in mind the order of the Division Bench of this court.
10. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. apprise to 
this court that the petitioner of WPCRL No. 838 of 2025 
have a criminal antecedent of one case so in view of the 
order of Division Bench he does not deserve for any 
security.
11. So far as WPCRL No. 737 of 2025 is concerned, 
the Committee so constituted by the Division Bench will examine the case and then apprise to this court on 
the next date. Let the committee do this exercise.
12. In addition to this, the Secretary Home shall also 
apprise to this court how much fund the State is 
utilizing for this purpose.
13. List this matter on 17.06.2026 as a first case.
