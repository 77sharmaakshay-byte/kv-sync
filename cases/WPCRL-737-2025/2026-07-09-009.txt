4. IA 3/2026 (Clarification Application) in WPCRL

clarification application to be pending in the cause list.
5. The Division Bench of this court in WPCRL No. 
1037 of 2016 passed the following order on 10.11.2016 
which read as of under:
“The petitioners are permitted to move appropriate 
application seeking protection of their life and property 
before the Senior Superintendent of Police, Udham Singh 
Nagar within a period of one week from today. Senior 
Superintendent of Police, Udham Singh Nagar shall 
decide the same in accordance with law. 
However, in the larger public interest, the State 
Government is directed to withdraw the security cover 
forthwith granted to the persons against whom criminal 
cases for heinous crimes under Sections 302, 304, 376, 
392, 395, 386 I.P.C., POCSO, N.D.P.S. and Arms Act 
are pending. 
There is a scarcity of police personnel in the State. 
The role of the police is to maintain law and order 
situation and to protect the honest citizens. It is not duty 
of the police to protect the criminals. Police protection has become status symbol rather necessity. Maintenance of 
law and order is a legal / sovereign function to be 
discharged by the State and the same cannot be permitted 
to be mis-used by the criminals. 
In future, the Secretary Home, Government of 
Uttarakhand shall take the final decision on the 
recommendation made by the Committee comprising of 
Inspector General of Police / D.I.G. as well as Senior 
Superintendent of Police of the area concerned on a 
specific intelligence input to whom security cover is to be 
provided. 
The writ petition stands disposed of accordingly.”
6. Let the Secretary (Home) file his personal 
affidavit in compliance to the order of Division Bench 
dated 10.11.2020 passed in WPCRL No. 1037 of 2016.
7. Mr. Amit Bhatt, learned G.A. requests that the 
matter be posted after ten days.
8. List on 27.07.2026 along with the record of 
WPCRL No. 1037 of 2016 decided on 10.11.2016.
