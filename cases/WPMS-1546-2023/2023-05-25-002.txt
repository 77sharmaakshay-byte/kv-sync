COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.1546 of 2023
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Raman Kumar Shah, Advocate, for 
the petitioners.
The petitioner before this Court is an 
employer, who has put a challenge to the award 
dated 25.02.2023, as it has been rendered by the 
learned Industrial Tribunal/Labour Court, 
Dehradun, in Adjudication Case No.71 of 2008, 
“Jayanti Prasad Benjwal Vs. Secretary, 
G.M.O.U, Kotdwar”, by virtue of the impugned 
award, the employee has been directed to be 
reinstated and 25% of the arrears has been 
directed to be paid. The Presiding Officer by 
the award dated 25.02.2023, has held that the 
order of the termination of the services of the 
workman on 29.08.2006, was bad in the eyes of 
law.
Issue notices to the respondent. The steps 
to be taken by the petitioner within a period of 
one week from today.
List after service of the notice on the 
respondent.
The execution of the award dated 
25.02.2023, would be kept in abeyance, subject 
to the condition, that the petitioner/employer 
reinstate the respondent into the services forthwith, but so far as the payment of the 
arrears as determined by the Presiding 
Officer/Labour Court is concerned, that would 
be kept in abeyance, and would abide by the 
decision on the writ petition. 
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
