3. The instant petition is moved under 
Section 528 of BNSS, 2023 alongwith the 
compounding application with the affidavit 
of applicant for quashing the proceeding of 
Special Session Trial No.583 of 2024 pending 
in the court of FTC/Additional Sessions 
Judge/Special Judge (POCSO), Rudrapur, 
Udham Singh Nagar wherein the present 
applicant is facing trial for the offences 
punishable under Sections 363, 366-A of IPC 
read with Section 16/17 of POCSO Act. 
4. It is contended that the applicant infact 
married with the victim in Shiv Temple 
(Durga Colony), Kashipur on 18.07.2025 and 
a certificate is also issued by Pandit Indresh 
Sharma on 18.07.2025, which is enclosed as 
Annexure 2 and from their marriage a baby 
boy was also born on 16.06.2026 in support of 
which the birth certificate is also enclosed as 
Annexure 3. It is contended that since the 
applicant now married with the victim and a baby boy is also born from their wedlock, 
therefore, the proceeding be quashed. 
5. On the other side Ms. Meenakshi 
Sharma, learned AGA submits that victim at 
the time of marriage as well as, as on date 
still minor, therefore, as per law the offence 
cannot be compounded even if the applicant 
marry with the victim their marriage is itself 
void. 
6. Issue notice to respondent no.3
returnable at an early date.
7. Steps to be taken within one week.
8. Learned State counsel prays for and is 
granted four weeks time to file counter 
affidavit.
9. Two weeks time is granted to the 
applicant to file rejoinder affidavit.
10. After taking into consideration that 
applicant marry with the victim and a baby 
boy is also born from their wedlock, till the 
next date of listing further proceeding of
Special Session Trial No.583 of 2024 pending 
in the court of FTC/Additional Sessions 
Judge/Special Judge (POCSO), Rudrapur, 
Udham Singh Nagar is stayed. 
11. List in the week commencing 
26.10.2026.
12. Stay application stands disposed of.
