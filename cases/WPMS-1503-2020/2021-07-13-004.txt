Counter affidavit filed on behalf of

Substitution application (I.A. No. 
12037 of 2021) filed in WPMS No. 1503 
of 2020, being unopposed by learned 
counsel for the respondents, is allowed. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
to produce amended memo of parties in 
the Registry on or before 19.07.2021. 
This application (I.A. No. 12036 of 
2021) has been filed by Mr. Jogendra
Kumar Sahani with a prayer that he may 
be impleaded as petitioner no. 2 in 
WPMS No. 1503 of 2020. 
The ground taken in the 
impleadment application is that the sole 
petitioner, namely, Tarun Sahani has 
passed away on 24.05.2021. However, 
the fact of the matter is that an 
application for substitution of legal heirs of Tarun Sahani has also been filed, 
which has been allowed, therefore, this 
Court does not find any reason to allow 
this impleadment application.
It is not the case of the applicant 
that he had given any power of attorney 
in favour of Tarun Sahani or that Tarun 
Sahani was looking after the affairs of 
the land in question.
Accordingly, the impleadment 
application is dismissed. However, 
applicant shall be at liberty to file an 
individual writ petition for ventilation of 
his grievance, if any. 
List these matters after three 
weeks. 
Pleadings be exchanged in the 
meantime.
Interim order, if any, to continue till 
then.
