COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C-528 No. 582 of 2026
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Devesh Saini, learned counsel for 
the applicant (through V.C.).
2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned A.G.A. for the 
State.
3. The present criminal misc. application is 
filed with the prayer to set-aside and quash the
impugned order dated 13.02.2026 passed by 
learned 1st Additional Session Judge, Roorkee, 
District Haridwar in Criminal Revision No. 04 of 
2025, Smt. Mamta Vs. Haroon and another and 
also the impugned order dated 19.09.2023 
passed by learned 1st Judicial Magistrate, 
Roorkee, District Haridwar in Criminal Complaint 
Case No. 3003 of 2023, Haroon Vs. Sumit and 
Another, under Section 138 of Negotiable 
Instruments Act.
4. In this case, a complaint was filed by the 
respondent alleging therein that the cheques 
issued by the applicant, which were of the joint 
account of her husband and herself, were 
dishonoured. It is the case of the applicant that 
applicant is not the drawer of the cheque as she 
has not signed the cheques and only her 
husband has signed the cheques.
5. Learned counsel for the applicant in 
support of his case, has placed reliance upon a 
judgment passed by Hon’ble Supreme Court in 
the case of Aparna A. Shah Vs. M/s Sheth
Developers Pvt. Lt. and Another, reported in 
(2013) 4 SCC (Cri) 241.
6. This Court finds some substance in the 
submission made by learned counsel for the 
applicant and having heard the submission, under the facts and circumstances of the case, 
this Court is of the view that this matter 
definitely requires deliberation.
7. Issue notice to the respondent, returnable 
within three weeks. Steps to be taken within a 
week.
8. List after service report is received. 
9. In the meantime, counter-affidavit, if any, 
be filed by the respondent.
10. After perusing the records and having 
heard learned counsel for the parties and also 
considering the fact that the cheques alleged to 
have been dishonoured were signed only by the 
husband of the applicant and the applicant has 
not signed it, though the account was the joint 
account, this Court, prima facie, is of the 
opinion and is satisfied that in the interest of 
justice, further proceedings of Criminal 
Complaint Case No. 3003 of 2023, Haroon Vs. 
Sumit and Another, under Section 138 of 
Negotiable Instruments Act, pending in the 
court of learned 1st Judicial Magistrate, Roorkee, 
District Haridwar, shall remain stayed qua the 
applicant till the next date of listing.
 (Alok Mahra J.) 06.04.2026
