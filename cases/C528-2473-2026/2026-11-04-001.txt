2. This appears to be a rather peculiar 
case, inasmuch as, the complainant had 
instituted a complaint against the applicant
under Section 138 of the Negotiable 
Instruments Act, 1881, which was registered 
as Criminal Case No.3952 of 2019 (Old Case 
No.1600 of 2019). The said complaint came 
to be dismissed under Section 256 Cr.P.C. 
vide order dated 06.03.2025 passed by 
learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Roorkee, District Haridwar, on account of the 
complainant’s continuous absence from the 
proceedings. However, despite dismissal of the 
complaint, the proceedings in the said case 
appear to have continued before the Court of 
learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Roorkee. Surprisingly, the case was not 
consigned to the record room, and subsequent 
orders were repeatedly passed therein, 
pursuant to which bailable warrants and, 
subsequently, non-bailable warrants came to 
be issued against the applicant.3. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that when the applicant was arrested 
and produced before the Court on 
31.05.2026, he specifically apprised the Court 
of the fact that the complaint had already 
been dismissed vide order dated 06.03.2025. 
It is further submitted that this fact has also 
been specifically pleaded in paragraph no.5 of 
the bail application. In support of the said 
submission, an e-certified copy of the order 
dated 06.03.2025, whereby the complaint was 
dismissed, has also been placed on record.
4. Having regard to the peculiar facts and 
circumstances of the case, this Court 
considers it appropriate to direct the learned 
District Judge, Haridwar, to conduct an 
inquiry into the entire matter, including the 
circumstances under which the proceedings 
continued and coercive processes came to be 
issued against the applicant despite the 
dismissal of the complaint under Section 256 
Cr.P.C. Learned District Judge shall also 
examine the relevant judicial record and 
ascertain as to how subsequent orders were 
passed without taking due notice of the order 
dated 06.03.2025. A compliance report shall 
be submitted before this Court on or before 
16.11.2026.
5. List this matter on 16.11.2026, at the 
top of the board.
6. Registry is directed to communicate this 
order to learned District Judge, Haridwar, 
forthwith, for necessary compliance.
