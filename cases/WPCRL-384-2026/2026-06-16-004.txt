COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPCRL No.384 of 2026
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Ramji Srivastava, learned 
counsel for the petitioners.
2. Mr. S.S. Chauhan, learned Deputy 
A.G. along with Mr. Vikash Uaniyal, 
learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Mr. Bhupesh Kandpal, learned 
counsel for the caveator.
4. By means of the present criminal 
writ petition under Article 226 of the 
Constitution of India, the petitioners seek 
issuance of a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of certiorari for quashing the 
impugned First Information Report being 
F.I.R./Case Crime No. 0009 of 2026 dated 
09.02.2026, registered under Sections 
419, 420, 467, 468 and 471 of the I.P.C. 
at Police Station Buggawala, District 
Haridwar. The petitioners have also 
prayed for issuance of a writ, order or 
direction in the nature of mandamus 
commanding the respondents not to arrest 
them in pursuance of the aforesaid F.I.R.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
submits that on the previous date of 
hearing, the Coordinate Bench of this 
Court had directed the State authorities 
not to arrest the petitioners pursuant to 
the impugned F.I.R., and the said 
direction was duly communicated to the
concerned authorities. He further submits 
that the Coordinate Bench, vide order 
dated 20.03.2026, extended the interim 
protection granted to the petitioners.6. Per contra, learned counsel 
appearing for the respondent vehemently 
opposes the submissions advanced on 
behalf of the petitioners and contends that 
no written interim protection was granted 
by the Coordinate Bench and that only an 
oral observation had been made without 
passing any formal order restraining the 
arrest of the petitioners.
7. Learned State counsel, however, 
submits that the records maintained by 
the State reflect an endorsement regarding 
the grant of interim protection to the 
petitioners.
8. Having regard to the rival 
submissions advanced by learned counsel 
for the parties and considering the facts 
and circumstances of the case, it is 
provided, by way of an interim measure, 
that till the next date of listing, the 
petitioners shall not be arrested in 
connection with F.I.R./Case Crime No. 
0009 of 2026 dated 09.02.2026, registered 
at Police Station Buggawala, District 
Haridwar, subject to the condition that 
they shall cooperate with the investigation 
and shall appear before the Investigating 
Officer as and when required.
9. List this matter on 02.07.2026.
10. In the meantime, learned State 
counsel and learned counsel appearing for 
respondent no. 2 may file their objections, 
if any.
11. Urgency application stands disposed 
of accordingly.
 (Alok Mahra, J.)
