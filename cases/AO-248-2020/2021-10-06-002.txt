The appellant to the present Appeal 
from Order is reported to have met with the sad 
demise on 10.03.2021, hence the Substitution 
Application No. IA/10799/2021, has been 
preferred on 23.08.2021, to bring on record the 
heirs of the deceased sole appellant, the details 
of which had been provided by the applicant in 
the Substitution Application in its para ‘B’.
In fact, the heir of the deceased 
appellant, who has been sought to be 
substituted, is already impleaded as respondent 
No. 7 to the present Appeal from Order. But, 
since there is an office report that respondent 
Nos. 1 & 4, have not been served with the 
notices and that the respondent Nos. 2, 3 & 5 
have been served with the notices by affixation, 
before passing any order on the applicant’s 
application for substitution, let notices be issued 
to the unserved respondents and the steps would 
be taken by the heir of the appellant within a 
period of one week from today.
List this application for its 
consideration thereafter.
