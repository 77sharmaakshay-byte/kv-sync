COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.2307 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Lalit Samant, learned counsel for the petitioners.
2. Mr. Atul Bahuguna, learned Standing Counsel for 
the State.
3. By means of this writ petition, the petitioners have
challenged the order dated 30.11.2023, passed by 
respondent no.3 (Annexure no.6), whereby the Ist A.C.P. 
granted to the petitioner has been cancelled and withdrawn.
4. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioners 
that some of the petitioners were granted Ist A.C.P. in ten 
years and some in 16 years, after continuous and 
satisfactory service, but by the impugned order the said 
benefit which was extended to the petitioners have been
cancelled and withdrawn.
5. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioners 
that the said A.C.P was granted to the petitioners after 
intervention of this Court in various writ petitions filed by 
the petitioners and therefore the cancellation and withdrawal 
of the said benefit extended to the petitioner is illegal and 
cannot sustain.
6. Learned counsel for the State prays for and is granted 
six weeks’ time for filing counter affidavit.
7. Further two weeks’ time is granted to the learned 
counsel for the petitioners for filing rejoinder affidavit.
8. List this case on 28.03.2024.
9. In the meantime, it is open to the petitioners that if 
proceeding for recovery is initiated against them, petitioners 
are free to move before this Court by filing an application.
10. Interim Relief Application (IA No.1 of 2023) stands 
disposed-off accordingly. 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
