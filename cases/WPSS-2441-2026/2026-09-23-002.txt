Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Sandeep Kothari, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Anil K. Dabral, learned Addl. C.S.C. with Mr. P.P. 
Bhatt, learned B.H. for the State.
3. By means of this writ petition, petitioner has 
challenged order dated 29.07.2026 issued by respondent 
no.2, whereby objections preferred by petitioner to 
tentative seniority list dated 09.05.2024 and 14.11.2024 
has been rejected and further to quash placement of 
petitioner at Serial No.109 in the seniority list and he be 
placed at Serial No.71A (72) of the final seniority list so 
issued for the cadre of Excise Inspector.
4. It is case of petitioner that petitioner was initially 
appointed as an Urdu Translator-Ministerial Employee 
with Excise Department and subsequently on 27.09.2014 
he was promoted on the post of Excise Inspector. DPC for 
said purpose was initially convened on 09.06.2014, but
due to some administrative reasons it was adjourned and 
finally it was held on 26.09.2014 wherein petitioner was 
promoted vide order dated 27.09.2014.
5. Petitioner has challenged the impugned order 
whereby his objection to the seniority list dated 
09.05.2024 and 14.11.2024 was rejected, mainly on the 2
ground that after amendment in the year 2024 in Rule 
8(3) of The Uttarakhand Government Servants Seniority 
Rules, 2002 (hereinafter referred to as “Rules of 2002”), 
for the purpose of fixation of seniority Rota-Quota Rule 
would be applicable if incumbent is promoted on 
promotional post in the same selection year with direct 
recruits.
6. According to learned counsel for petitioner, petitioner 
is claiming seniority over direct recruits who were 
appointed on 12.09.2013 (Selection Year 2013-14) as 
petitioner’s selection year of promotion is also 2013-14. 
The reason being that petitioner’s DPC was convened on 
09.06.2014 for the first time and subsequently it was 
convened on 26.09.2014 and he was accordingly 
promoted on 27.09.2014.
7. Learned counsel for petitioner submits that selection 
year 2013-14 of petitioner is same as that of the direct 
recruits, therefore Rota-Quota Rule would be attracted 
wherein promotee will be given preference vis-à-vis to 
direct recruits.
8. Per contra, learned counsel for State vehemently 
submits that direct recruits were inducted in service 
through Combined Lower Subordinate Service Selection 
Examination-2010 for which amended requisition was 
sent on 26.03.2012 i.e., Selection Year-2011-12 and 
recommendation was made by Commission to State 
Government on 13.06.2013 and finally direct recruits 
were appointed on 12.09.2013.
9. Contrary to this, requisition of petitioner was sent on 3
05.02.2014 (Selection Year 2013-14) and he was promoted 
vide DPC recommendation dated 26.09.2014 vide 
promotional order dated 27.09.2014 (which according to 
learned State counsel comes to Selection Year 2014-15).
10. Accordingly there is no illegality in the impugned 
order.
11. Having heard learned counsel for parties, this Court 
is of the view that after amendment of Rule 8(3) of the 
Rules of 2002, in the year 2024 if appointment or 
promotion is made in same selection year principle of 
Rota-Quota Rule would be attracted.
12. In view of Rules of 2002 as amended requisition sent 
earlier for direct recruitment would not make any 
difference as appointment or promotion should have been 
made in same selection year to attract Rule 8(3) of the 
Rules of 2002.
13. Issue notice to respondent nos.4 to 11 returnable 
within four weeks.
14. Steps to be taken within three days.
15. List this case on 04.11.2026.
16. Respondent(s) may file detail counter affidavit(s) 
within four weeks.
17. In the meantime, respondent is restrained from 
making any promotion on the post of Assistant Excise 
Commissioner.
