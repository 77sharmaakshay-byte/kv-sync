4. Issue notice to respondent no.2-Equivalence Committee,

5. Steps to be taken within three days.
6. By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has 
challenged the report of Equivalence Committee dated 
23.03.2024 (annexure no.1 to the writ petition), whereby the 
qualification held by the petitioner in B.tech, Information 
Technology has not been found equivalent with the essential 
qualification required in the examination i.e., B.Sc in Computer 
Science as per the advertisement dated 31.07.2017.
7. It is case of the petitioner that the petitioner participated 
pursuant to the aforesaid advertisement in selection process held 
on the post of Additional Private Secretary Examination. A 
dispute regarding the qualification held by the petitioner 
cropped in saying that the qualification held by the petitioner in 
B.tech, Information Technology is not at par with the essential 
qualification in advertisement i.e., B.Sc in Computer Science
and his candidature was rejected.
8. The petitioner has filed a writ petition challenging 
rejection of his candidature before this Court by filing a Writ 
Petition (S/S) No.962 of 2020, Tarun Singh Karki Vs. State of 2
Uttarakhand. The writ petition was disposed-off, whereby State 
Government was directed to constitute Equivalence Committee 
and the matter regarding qualification of the petitioner was also 
sent to the said Committee.
9. It is the order of the Equivalence Committee dated 
23.03.2024 which is impugned in the present writ petition.
10. It is argued by learned counsel for the petitioner that one 
candidate namely, Shivani Dhasmana’s degree of B.tech in 
Information Technology from the same University has been 
found equivalent to the qualification required for the 
examination of the APS as per the advertisement with the B.Sc 
in Computer Science, but at the same time the petitioner was not 
found equivalent. Feeling aggrieved by the said decision, the 
petitioner is before this Court.
11. Learned counsel for the respondent(s) pray for and are 
granted six weeks’ time for filing counter affidavit(s).
12. Two weeks’ time thereafter is granted to learned counsel 
for the petitioner for filing rejoinder affidavit.
13. List this case on 09.12.2024.
14. As an interim measure, it is provided that one seat on the 
post of Additional Private Secretary (General Category) shall be 
kept vacant for the petitioner, during pendency of the writ 
petition.
