 Heard on Amendment Application (IA) 
No.4 of 2025.
 By means of this amendment application, 
the petitioner proposes to incorporate two 
paragraphs in the writ petition along with 
prayer.
 Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the 
petitioner would submit that in the counter 
affidavit filed by the respondent no.3 some 
orders have been enclosed, of which, copies 
have not been given by the respondent no.1
to the petitioner. Now, petitioner proposes to 
challenge those orders, as well. 
 It is not objected to by the learned State 
counsel. 
 Learned counsel for the respondent no.1 
would submit that in the proposed prayer 
clause what is sought to be quashed is an 
Annexure of the counter affidavit, whereas 
that orders should have been made part of the writ petition. 
 Heard.
 Having considered, this Court is of the 
view that the amendment application may be 
allowed. 
 The amendment application is allowed. 
 Let the amended petition be filed within a 
week. 
 Three weeks thereafter, the respondents 
may file their counter affidavits/additional 
counter affidavits. 
 A week thereafter, rejoinder affidavit, if 
any, may be filed.
 List for final hearing on 05.05.2025.
