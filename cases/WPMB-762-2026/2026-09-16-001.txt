3. The contention of learned counsel for the petitioner is 
that the present employment of the petitioner at AIIMS, 
Rishikesh is also a public service in an Institute under the 
Central Government and therefore the present service 
should also be taken into account for purposes of the bond. 
It is further contended that since the petitioner had done 
PG after taking No-Objection from the Government, 
therefore, the period of PG course i.e. two years is also 
liable to be added while counting the period of five years 
under the bond.
4. The matter requires consideration. 
5. Let counter affidavit be filed by the respondents 
within four weeks.
6. List thereafter.
7. Meanwhile, subject to the petitioner depositing 1/4th
of the amount demanded by the impugned order dated 
07.09.2026, the recovery of the remaining amount shall 
remain stayed.
