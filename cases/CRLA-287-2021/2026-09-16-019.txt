3. Instant appeal is preferred against the judgment and 
order dated 31.08.2021/02.09.2021, passed in Sessions Trial 
No.144 of 2018, State vs. Snehlata Chauhan & Anr., by the 
court of learned 4th Additional Sessions Judge, Dehradun. By 
it, the appellant has been convicted under Sections 302, 201 
and 120-B of IPC. 
4. The appellant, Snehlata Chauhan seeks short term bail 
on the ground that her two children are suffering from 
depression and the younger one has to be admitted in Class 
10th as he did not write his examination last year.
5. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that 
appellant's two children, Sarthak Chauhan and Shaurya 
Pratap Singh Chauhan, are staying with their grandmother 
and grandfather; they are suffering from depression. The 
Child Welfare Committee, District Dehradun on 12.06.2026 
had given a report to that effect. The children are passing 
through psychological trauma due to the loss of their father 
and mother being in jail. Therefore, it is argued that the 
appellant may be released on short-term bail for some time so that she could get her younger son admitted in Class 10th
and give them some company so that they could be 
emotionally a little strong to wade through their life.
6. Learned State counsel is required to get a report. 
Learned State Counsel submits that in fact the children of the 
appellant, Snehlata Chauhan, are staying with her parents.
There is no material on record to controvert the report of 
Child Welfare Committee, which is Annexure-2 to the short￾term bail application.
7. We have carefully gone through the CWC report, 
which records that the children of the appellant are suffering 
from depression and they are also going through emotional 
trauma. The report also records that if timely guidance is not 
given, the possibility of their coming into contact with anti￾social elements may not be ruled out.
8. Having considered, purely on humanitarian ground, 
the short term bail is granted to the appellant Snehlata 
Chauhan for a period of three weeks from the date of her
release, subject to her executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two reliable sureties, each of the like amount, to 
the satisfaction of the court concerned. 
9. It is further directed that on expiry of the duration of 
short term bail, the appellant shall surrender before the court 
concerned.

11. Let a copy of this order be immediately forwarded to

that after obtaining e-true copy of it, necessary action may be 
initiated so that the release of the appellant is not delayed.
