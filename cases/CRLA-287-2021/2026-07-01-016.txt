The instant appeal has been preferred against judgment 
and order dated 31.08.2021/02.09.2021, passed in Sessions Trial 
No.144 of 2018, State Vs. Snehlata Chauhan and Another, by the court 
of learned IVth Additional Sessions Judge, Dehradun. By it, the 
appellant has been convicted under Section 302 read with Section 
120B and Section 201 of IPC and sentenced accordingly. The appellant 
seeks bail during pendency of this appeal.
2. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the 
record.
3. First Bail Application (IA No. 01 of 2022) was rejected on

No. 5 of 2024).

Application. According, this Court is of the view that there is no 2
ground to enlarge the appellant on bail. Accordingly, the Second Bail 
Application deserves to be rejected.
5. The Second Bail Application (IA No.5 of 2024) is rejected.
