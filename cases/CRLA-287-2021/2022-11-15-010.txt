This is an application for extension of short 
term bail, granted by us on 21.09.2022, on the 
ground that the purpose for which he has been 
granted short term bail could not be completed. 
It is borne out from the record that the 
appellant-Snehlata Chauhan was granted short 
term bail by us so that she could secure legal 
heirs certificate for her children and also get the 
GPF and other death-cum-retirement benefits of 
her late husband in her favour and also to get 
Income Tax refund. It is submitted that though 
she succeeded in getting the Income Tax refund 
but the other two duties i.e. the legal heirs 
certificate and release of death-cum-retirement 
benefits could not be worked out. Hence, she is 
prayed for an extension of short term bail. 
We are inclined to grant extension of 
short term bail to the appellant for a period of
30 days on the same bail bond executed by him
on 21.09.2022. 
It is stated that the short term bail granted 
by us earlier comes to its logical conclusion 
today. Hence, the appellant is granted 
extension till 15th December, 2022. 
On 16.12.2022, she shall surrender before 
the learned trial court. On such an event, she will be remanded to the custody. If she does 
not surrender, proper warrant shall be issued 
against her.
The matter be listed on 28.02.2023 as 
fixed by us earlier. 
Let a certified copy of this order be issued 
to the learned counsel for the parties, today 
itself, on payment of usual charges. 
(Alok Kumar Verma, J.) (Sanjaya Kumar Mishra, J.)
