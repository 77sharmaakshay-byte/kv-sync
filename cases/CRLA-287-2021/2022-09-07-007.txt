This is a short term bail application 
filed by the appellant Amit Parley on the 
ground of medical illness. He has been 
convicted under Sections 302, 201 and 
120B IPC and sentenced for a term of life 
imprisonment.
On the last date, learned Deputy 
Advocate General was directed to take 
instructions. 
He has taken instructions and 
submits before the Court that the 
appellant was sustained a AC Joint Injury 
and he is recommended for treatment to 
surgical intervention. Copy of instructions 
is produced before us. 
The report of the Medical Officer, 
District Jail, Dehradun reveals that the 
patient is suffering from TINEA CRURES 
and Alopecia Aereata and that he is being 
treated regularly. 
In that view of the matter, we are 
inclined to grant short term bail of 45
days to the appellant-applicant. 
Let the applicant Amit Parley be 
released on short term bail for a period of 
45 days, which shall be reckoned from 
the date of his release from the jail, on 
such suitable terms and conditions, as the learned 4th Additional Sessions Judge, 
Dehradun deems fit and proper, in 
Sessions Trial No. 144 of 2018, (State vs. 
Sneh Lata Chauhan & another), decided 
on 31.08.2018 and 02.09.2019. 
On expiry of 45 days, the appellant 
shall surrender before the Court, in turn, 
shall send him to jail. In case, he fails to 
surrender after expiry of 45 days, the 
learned 4th Additional Sessions Judge, 
Dehradun, shall issue non bailable 
warrant against the appellant. 
Urgent copy of this order be supplied 
to the learned counsel for the parties, as 
per Rules. 
(Alok Kumar Verma, J.) (S.K.Mishra, J.)
