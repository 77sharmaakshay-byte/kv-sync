judgment and order dated 31.08.2021/02.09.2021,

Snehlata Chauhan & Anr., by the court of learned

201 and 120-B of IPC.

16.09.2026 for a period of three weeks on the 
ground that her children were suffering.

2026) has been filed on the ground that the children of the appellant are undergoing treatment, and

granted to the appellant is extended for further

extended short term bail, the applicant shall 
surrender before the court concerned and a 
certificate be produced before this Court.
7. Bail extension application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
