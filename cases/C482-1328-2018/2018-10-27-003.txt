C-482 No. 1328 of 2018
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate for applicants.
The applicants are accused in a Complaint 
Case No. 421 of 2014, which has been renumbered as 
Case No. 1340 of 2014, Ranjeet Singh Vs. Amar 
Singh and others under Sections 323, 504, 506, 392, 
120-B of IPC, which is pending consideration before 
the A.C.J.M-1st Dehradun, District Dehradun, in 
which the summoning orders have been issued 
against the present applicants on 17.03.2015. 
This Court had directed the applicants to serve 
the complainant/respondent through dasti vide its 
order dated 20.08.2018. 
The affidavit of service filed by the applicants
with the folio, it has been informed that the 
respondent is not residing at the given address, shown 
in the present C-482 Application. The landlady has 
also endorsed that she is not aware of the new and 
correct address of the respondent herein. 
Learned counsel for the applicants informs the 
Court that on one hand the respondent is avoiding 
service, while on the other hand, he is contesting the 
proceedings before the Court below, and is duly 
represented by his advocate, let notice be served on 
him through his advocate to representing him before 
the Court below.
Learned counsel for the applicants is directed 
to serve respondent through registered post to his 
address as shown to be that of Yamuna Nagar.
List this matter in the week commencing 
19.11.2018. 
During the intervening period only till the next 
date fixed before this Court, the bailable warrant 
would be kept in abeyance.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
27.10.2018 Mahinder/
