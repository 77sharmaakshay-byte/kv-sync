Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Akshay Joshi proxy counsel for Mr. 
Harshpal Sekhon, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. Chitrarth Kandpal, learned Brief 
Holder for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition, 
petitioners seek quashing of FIR/Case Crime 
No. 29 of 2026 dated 17.07.2026, under 
Sections 115(2), 296, 351(3), 85 of BNS, 
registered at Police Station-Tallital, District 
Nainital. The petitioners have further sought 
issuance of a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus commanding the 
respondent authorities not to arrest the 
petitioners pursuant to the aforesaid F.I.R.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that the present matter arises 
out of matrimonial discord between the 
parties; that, petitioner no.1 is the husband of 
respondent no.3/complainant, whereas 
petitioner nos.2 and 3 are her mother-in-law 
and father-in-law, respectively and [etitioner 
nos.4 to 9 are other relatives of petitioner 
no.1. It is contended that the impugned F.I.R. has been lodged by respondent 
no.3/complainant alleging, inter alia, demand 
of dowry and physical as well as mental 
harassment by the petitioners; that, the 
allegations made in the F.I.R. are omnibus, 
general and vague in nature and that no 
specific overt act has been attributed to each 
of the petitioners; that, despite the allegation 
of physical assault, the F.I.R. is not supported 
by any medical report, injury report or other 
contemporaneous material indicating that 
respondent no.3 was subjected to any 
physical violence at the hands of the 
petitioners.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would further submit that the impugned 
F.I.R. has been lodged with an ulterior motive 
to pressurize and harass petitioner no.1 and
his family members by implicating the entire 
family in the matrimonial dispute; that, even 
the married sister of petitioner no.1 and her 
husband have been unnecessarily arrayed as 
accused, despite the fact that they are 
residing separately and have no day-to-day 
involvement in the matrimonial affairs of 
petitioner no.1 and respondent no.3; that, the 
allegations against such relatives are wholly 
general in nature and do not disclose any 
specific incident, role or overt act attributable 
to them, therefore, the impugned F.I.R. is 
liable to be quashed.
6. Having heard learned counsel for the 
petitioners and upon perusal of the 
allegations contained in the impugned F.I.R., 
this Court finds that respondent 
no.3/complainant has arrayed, amongst 
others, the married sister of petitioner no.1 
and her husband as accused, notwithstanding 
the specific submission that they are residing separately and are not living with petitioner 
no.1 and respondent no.3. The allegations 
against them also appear, prima facie, to be 
general in nature without specifying their 
individual role or attributing any particular 
overt act to them. 
7. Issue notice to respondent no.3, 
returnable at an early date.
8. Steps to be taken within one week.
9. List after service report is received.
10. Meanwhile, counter-affidavit be filed by 
the respondents within four weeks.
11. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties, till the next date of listing, as an 
interim measure, the petitioner shall not be 
arrested in connection with the F.I.R. No.29 of 
2026 registered at Police Station Tallital, 
District Nainital, provided the petitioners 
cooperate with the investigation.
12. Stay Application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
