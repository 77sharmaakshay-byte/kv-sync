4. The instant bail application is moved on 
behalf of the applicant – Gaurav Kumar S/o Shri 
Durga Das, for seeking bail in connection with FIR 
dated 03.05.2026 bearing FIR No.0079 of 2026,
Police Station Rajpur, District Dehradun, which 
was lodged against unknown person for the 
offences punishable under Section 318(4) of BNS, 
2023. After investigation, the applicant is 
chargesheeted for the offences punishable under 
Sections 318(4) and 319 of BNS, 2023.
5. Learned counsel for the applicant argued 
that after filing of the chargesheet the applicant 
settle the dispute with the informant on the basis 
of which, the compounding application is filed for 
quashing of the entire proceedings arising out of 
the aforesaid FIR, which is also pending for 
consideration. He further submits that all the 
offences are triable by the Magistrate and are 
punishable upto 7 years, and the informant do not 
want to pursue with the trial court proceeding, 
therefore, the applicant be released on bail. About 
criminal antecedents, he submits in some of the 
cases, the applicant has been acquitted and in 
others he is enlarged on bail. 
6. On the other side, learned counsel for the complainant submits that he settled the dispute 
with the applicant and received the entire amount, 
and therefore, the informant do not want to 
proceed with the trial court proceedings.
7. On the other side, learned Brief Holder 
submits that though the applicant is a habitual 
offender and indulged in criminal activities in 
Punjab and Haryana but since now in the present 
case the informant do not want to pursue with the 
proceedings since he has received the entire
money, therefore, this aspect can be looked into 
while considering the bail application as well as 
the fact that in 528 petition, the compounding 
application is filed.
8. After hearing the submissions of the learned 
counsel for the parties, and taking into 
consideration that the offences as alleged are 
triable by the Magistrate, and furthermore the 
informant received the entire money from the 
applicant and filed the compounding application 
for closing the entire trial court proceedings and in 
the present case the applicant is languishing in jail 
since 07.05.2026, therefore, this Court is of the view 
that the applicant deserves for bail.
9. Accordingly without expressing any opinion 
on the merits of the case, the instant bail 
application is allowed. 
10. Let the applicant – “Gaurav Kumar”, be 
released on bail, on his executing a personal bond 
and furnishing two reliable sureties, each of the 
like amount to the satisfaction of the Court 
concerned subject to the condition that after being 
released on bail he shall give his contact details to 
the nearest Police Station so that he may be in a 
strict surveillance for six months.
