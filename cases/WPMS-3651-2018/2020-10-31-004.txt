Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate for the petitioners.
Mr. Neeraj Garg, Advocate for the respondents.
By virtue of the counter affidavit filed by the 
respondents, an averment was made therein that 
respondent nos.2, 8, 11 and 14 have met with the sad 
demise. Consequently, the counsel for the petitioner by 
an order dated 18.02.2020, was directed to do the 
needful; but till date no steps have been taken by the 
petitioner nor any substitution application, as 
permissible under the law has been preferred. 
The matter is listed today on an Abatement 
Application No.9229 of 2020; as well as Urgency 
Application No.10501 of 2020, which has been
preferred by the respondents for dismissing the writ 
petition as having abated, as well as on urgency 
application.
Owing to the fact that in spite of the sufficient 
time being granted since 18.02.2020, no substitution 
application has been filed. The writ petition would be 
treated to have been dismissed as abated qua the 
deceased respondent nos.2, 8, 11 and 14. Accordingly, 
the abatement application is disposed of. 
So far as the urgency application is concerned,
the writ petition is directed to be listed in the last week 
of November, 2020. 
Interim order already granted is extended till 
next date of listing. 
 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
