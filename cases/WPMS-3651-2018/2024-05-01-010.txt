Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Naitik Bhatt, proxy counsel for Mr. Piyush Garg, 
counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Neeraj Garg, counsel for respondent nos. 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 
7, 10, 12 & 13, 15, 16 & 17.
3. A Recall Application (IA 11243 of 2020) along with delay 
condonation application (IA 11244 of 2020) has been filed by 
the petitioner to recall the order dated 31.10.2020, whereby the 
present writ petition was dismissed as abated qua the deceased 
respondent nos. 2, 8, 11 & 14.
Counsel for the petitioner would submit that the 
respondent nos. 2,8 & 14 passed away prior to the filing of the 
writ petition; that, only respondent no.11 has expired on 
08.06.2019 during the pendency of the present petition, 
therefore, the order dated 31.10.2020 is liable to be recalled 
against the respondent nos. 2, 8 & 14. 
4. Counsel for the respondent has no objection if the Recall 
Application as well delay condonation application is allowed.
5. Recall application as well delay condonation application 
are accordingly allowed. Order dated 31.10.2020 is hereby 
recalled.
6. Re-issue notices to the proposed legal heirs of 
respondent nos. 2, 8 & 14 on the impleadment application (IA 
11268 of 2020) through ordinary process and registered post 
acknowledgment due as well as through e-mail and whatsapp, 
if available. 
7. Steps to be taken within two weeks.
8. List this case on 23.08.2024.
9 Urgency application (IA 18772 of 2024) stands 
disposed of accordingly.
