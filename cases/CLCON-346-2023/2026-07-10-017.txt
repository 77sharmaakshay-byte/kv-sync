In this petition, there are multiple 
allegations against the police officers with 
regard to manipulation of the records, 
malafide, forgery, illegal arrest etc. One of the 
allegations is that when illegally the resort was 
raided by the police officers, they took the DVR 
out and did not return it.
Now a supplementary affidavit dated 
02.03.2025 has been filed by the petitioners 
enclosing conversation between the Asif Khan, 
the Investigating Officer (“the IO”) with Hariom 
Sharma, who, it is stated is father of the 
petitioner no.2, which records that, in fact, on 
the date of conversation, the DVR was in the custody of the IO, Asif Khan.
Let IO, Asif Khan file a short affidavit 
stating as to where is that DVR? How he get 
custody of it? What was the occasion for him to 
speak to Hariom Sharma on 13.12.2013? Has 
the DVR been sent for forensic examination? If 
yes, when?
This affidavit shall be filed within a week.
List on 17.07.2026 after fresh cases.
This Court requests learned State counsel 
to ensure that this order is communicated to 
the IO, Asif Khan and he files his affidavit 
within time.
