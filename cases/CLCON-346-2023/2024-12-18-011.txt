A report is received from the Deputy 
Inspector General of Police, Kumaon Division.
 According to the petitioner, rooms in the 
resort were demanded by the sub-ordinate of 
for his guests of contemnor/respondent no.1. 
The report says that the contemnor/respondent 
no.1 did not make any request.
Whether, the Deputy Inspector General, 
Police made any inquiry, as to who made the 
‘WhatsApp chats’? What was the basis of 
those chats? How could a subordinate of the 
contemnor/respondent no.1 demand for a room 
in a resort in the name of the 
contemnor/respondent no.1? Were there any 
guests coming on those days? Had they come? 
Where did they stay? How the sub-ordinate 
could make a demand in the name the 
contemnor/respondent no.1 without the 
direction of the contemnor/respondent no.1? If 
such inquiry was not made, how it can be 
recorded that demand was not made by the 
contemnor/respondent no.1? Why the 
contemnor/respondent no.1 was posted in the 
same police station again where there are 
allegations that he had falsely implicated the 
resort owner and its employees and committed 
contempt of the Court?
When asked, learned Chief Standing 
Counsel seeks time.
List on 19.12.2024 at 2:00 PM along with 
connected matter.The Court requests the Deputy Inspector 
General of Police, Kumaon Division to also join 
the proceedings through video conferencing.
Let a copy of this order be sent to the 
Director General of Police as well as Deputy 
Inspector General of Police, Kumaon Division,
today itself, through e-mail/FAX for 
information and necessary action.
