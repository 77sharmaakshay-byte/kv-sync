On 18.12.2024, this Court had 
requested the Deputy Inspector General of 
Police, Kumaon Division, Nainital to assist 
the Court on certain aspects. On that date, 
the Court passed the following orders:- 
 “A report is received from the 
Deputy Inspector General of Police, 
Kumaon Division. 
 According to the petitioner, 
rooms in the resort were demanded by 
the sub-ordinate of for his guests of 
contemnor/respondent no. 1. The 
report says that the 
contemnor/respondent no. 1 did not 
make any request.
 Whether, the Deputy Inspector 
General, Police made any inquiry, as 
to who made the ‘WhatsApp chats’? 
What was the basis of those chats? 
How could a subordinate of the 
contemnor/respondent no. 1 demand 
for a room in a resort in the name of 
the guests coming on those days? Had 
they come? Where did they stay? How 
the sub-ordinate could make a demand in the name of the 
contemnor/respondent no. 1 without 
the direction of the 
contemnor/respondent no. 1? If such 
inquiry was not made, how it can be 
recorded that demand was not made 
by the contemnor/respondent no. 1? 
Why the contemnor/respondent no. 1 
was posted in the same police station 
again where there are allegations that 
he had falsely implicated the resort 
owner and its employees and 
committed contempt of the Court?
 When asked, learned Chief 
Standing Counsel seeks time.
 List on 19.12.2024 at 2:00 PM 
along with connected matter. 
 The Court requests the Deputy 
Inspector General of Police, Kumaon 
Division to also join the proceedings 
through video conferencing.
 Let a copy of this order be 
sent to the Director General of Police 
as well as Deputy Inspector General of 
Police, Kumaon Division, today itself, 
through e-mail/FAX for information 
and necessary action. ”
Dr. Yogendra Singh Rawat, Deputy 
Inspector General of Police, Kumaon
Division, Nainital assisted the Court. 
The Deputy Inspector General of 
Police submits that he would further 
examine this matter and would decide as to 
what is to be done.
Let a copy of the supplementary 
affidavit dated 15.12.2023 in connected 
WPCRL No. 1655 of 2022 be also supplied 
to the learned Chief Standing Counsel for 
its further transmission to the Deputy 
Inspector General of Police, Kumaon
Division, Nainital.
List on 16.02.2025.
