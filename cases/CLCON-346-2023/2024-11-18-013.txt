 On behalf of the respondent nos.1 and 2, 
time is sought for filing objections. 
 Let it be so filed.
 List on 18.12.2024, just after fresh 
cases.
 On 27.12.2023, this Court has made 
such an observation:-
“If there are such allegations against the 
police of which judicial notice has been 
taken by the Court in WPCRL No. 1655 
of 2023 and the instant petition, why 
should the same police officer be 
permitted to continue in the area where 
the resort falls? Could not it be an act of 
perpetuating those alleged wrong? Does 
not the concept of clean governance and 
the rule of law may require that such 
police officers may be transferred to 
some other police station quite at a 
distance from the place where the resort 
falls? Could not it be good for both, the 
contemnor as well as the petitioner? The 
Court leaves it to the wisdom of the 
senior police officers and would examine 
this issue, if occasion arises on 
11.01.2024.”
 The Court today wanted to know, as to 
what action has been taken in the matter? 
There are allegations of corruption against 
the police officers that they wanted two rooms for free and when it was not given, 
someone was arrested in defiance to the 
Rules. This is what is alleged.
 Learned State counsel is not in a 
position to respond that action, if any, has 
been taken in pursuance to the Court’s 
order dated 27.12.2023.
 Let a copy of this order along with the 
copy of order dated 27.12.2023 be sent to 
the Deputy Inspector General, Kumaon 
Division. He is requested to inform the 
Court, action, if any, taken in the matter 
pursuant to the Court’s order dated 
27.12.2023. 
 List on the date fixed. 
 
(Ravindra Maithani, J.)
