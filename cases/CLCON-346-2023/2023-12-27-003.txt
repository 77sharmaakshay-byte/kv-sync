CLCON No. 346 of 2023
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Dushyant Mainali, Advocate for 
the petitioner.
Mr. J.S. Bisht, Brief Holder for the 
State.
This matter was heard alongwith 
WPCRL No. 1655 of 2023.
According to the petitioner, it is case 
of the contemnor. The petitioner was 
arrested on false grounds in defiance to 
the directions of the Hon’ble Supreme 
Court laid down in the case of Arnesh 
Kumar Vs. State of Bihar, (2014) 8 SCC 
273 as well as the arrest of the petitioner 
was made in defiance to the circular No. 
315/UHC/Admin-B/V-a-7-2014 dated 
04.09.2023.
During the course of hearing 
learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the petitioner is Manager of 
the resort. The police wanted rooms free
of cost. On one occasion, it could not be 
provided because the rooms had already 
been booked for the marriage. Therefore, 
the police became annoyed and a false 
case has been lodged. The arrest was 
made in defiance of the guidelines given 
by the Hon’ble Supreme Court in the case 
of Arnesh Kumar (supra) as well as the 
circular of this Court dated 04.09.2023. 
It is also argued that the resort people are 
much scared of the Police. The SHO of 
the police station was suspended, but 
within three days he was reinstated and 
he is still there.The Court wanted to know about it,
from the learned State counsel. He would 
submit that he has no idea about it.
In the instant matter, the date is 
already fixed for 11.01.2024.
State has already been required to 
file objection.
If there are such allegations against 
the police of which judicial notice has 
been taken by the Court in WPCRL No. 
1655 of 2023 and the instant petition, 
why should the same police officer be 
permitted to continue in the area where 
the resort falls? Could not it be an act of 
perpetuating those alleged wrong? Does 
not the concept of clean governance and 
the rule of law may require that such 
police officers may be transferred to some 
other police station quite at a distance 
from the place where the resort falls? 
Could not it be good for both, the 
contemnor as well as the petitioner? The 
Court leaves it to the wisdom of the 
senior police officers and would examine 
this issue, if occasion arises on 
11.01.2024.
List this matter on the date already 
fixed.
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
