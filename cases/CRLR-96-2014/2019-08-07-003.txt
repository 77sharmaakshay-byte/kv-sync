This revision arises out of an Appellate 
Court’s order passed under Section 29 of the 
Domestic Violence Act. The revision is yet to be 
admitted. There is an interim order of 
16.05.2014, whereby, the effect and operation of 
the impugned order has been kept in abeyance. 
While allowing the urgency application 
fixing the matter for the week commencing 
26.08.2019 the interim order is modified to the 
extent that it would remain extended till the next 
date of listing.
