2. Mr. J.S. Bisht, learned Standing 
Counsel with Mr. Devesh Ghildiyal, 
learned Brief Holder for the State of 
Uttarakhand/respondents-contemnors.
3. Petitioners have filed these 
contempt petitions alleging the willful 
and deliberate non-compliance of the 
order passed by writ court, wherein, writ 
court has directed respondents￾contemnors to consider re-engagement 
of petitioners on the post of Junior 
Assistant/Data Entry Operator (Class-III 
Post). When the petitioners have not 
been accommodated, they filed these 
contempt petitions.
4. The claim of the petitioners for 
consideration on the aforesaid post was 
rejected by the department on the 
ground that no posts of Junior Assistant/
Data Entry Operator are available in the 
department.5. Petitioners filed information sought 
under the RTI Act in one of the contempt 
petitions, in which, it has been 
categorically stated that in the 
department, there are 66 vacancies on 
the post of Junior Assistant/Data Entry 
Operator.
6. The said contention was met by the 
respondents-contemnors by filing 
response affidavit in CLCON/628/2025, 
in which, it has been stated that these 
posts are meant for promotions made 
from Class-IV posts of the department.
7. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties, this Court is of the view that 
petitioners are seeking contractual 
appointment and not regular 
appointment against the vacancies 
available in department. Thus, this stand
of respondents-contemnors cannot hold 
any water. Respondents-contemnors 
may accommodate petitioners against 
these vacancies and as and when the 
promotional exercise would be 
undertaken for promoting Class-IV 
employees, the junior most may be 
disengaged accordingly.
8. Put up on 26.10.2026 giving one 
more opportunity to respondents￾contemnors to comply the order.
