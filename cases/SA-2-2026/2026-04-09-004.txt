The second appeal is preferred against 
the judgment and decree dated 30.10.2025 
passed by the learned District Judge, 
Rudraprayag in Civil Appeal No.1/2025, L.P. 
Bhatt and another vs. Jay Prakash, 
whereby, the appeal preferred against the 
judgment and decree dated 07.02.2025 
passed by the learned Civil Judge (S.D.), 
Rudraprayag, District Rudraprayag in 
Civil/Original Suit No.11/2023, Jay Prakash 
Vs. L.P. Bhatt and another was dismissed.
The learned counsel for the appellant 
has pointed out that the present appeal 
emanates out of a suit instituted by the 
plaintiff for perpetual injunction against 
encroaching upon his property as well as 
sought demolition of the construction that 
have been made unauthorizedly on the land 
of the plaintiff. The plaintiff has come out 
with the version that he is owner of certain 
khasra numbers and the defendant no.2 had 
purchased certain khasra numbers from the 
father of the plaintiff. The plaintiff has also 
pleaded that taking advantage of the Covid￾19 period, the defendants have encroached 
upon the land khasra nos.864 to 876 and 
sought the relief of perpetual injunction and 
demolition and possession. 
The suit was contested by the 
defendant no.1 and defendant no.2. The 
defendant no.2 has come out with the case 
that he has not made any unauthorized occupation of the land of the plaintiff and 
the plaintiff has filed the suit without due 
demarcation of his land. 
The trial court framed as many as six
issues, therein the Issue Nos.1 and 2 have 
been the key issues. So far as the Issue
no.1 is concerned, the trial court came to 
the conclusion that Khet Nos.864 to 876 are 
recorded in records in the name of the 
plaintiff. The Issue no.2 was decided to the 
effect that the defendant no.2 has made 
construction of a building over the disputed 
property without any legal right and thus 
the suit was decreed for permanent 
injunction and relief of removal of the 
permanent construction and demolition was 
also granted.
Aggrieved against the said judgment 
and order dated 07.02.2025, the defendants 
carried the matter in appeal. The first 
appellate court re-visited all the issues and 
concurred with the findings returned by the 
trial court and thus vide judgment and order 
dated 30.10.2025 dismissed the appeal of 
the defendants and affirmed the trial court’s 
judgment and decree.
Aggrieved against the aforesaid 
judgment and decree of the trial court and 
the first appellate court, the present second 
appeal has been filed.
The learned counsel for the defendant 
no.1/appellant has made the submissions 
that the plaintiff has not led evidence to 
prove his case. There is nothing on record 
to identify the disputed property. The 
reports of setting up hot mix plant are 
legally impermissible and cannot read in 
evidence and thus did not prove the 
plaintiff’s case.
Per contra, Mr. Pankaj Tangwan, 
Advocate appearing on behalf of the 
respondent/plaintiff has made the submissions that out of the land of 
defendant no.2 an area of 4 and 1/2 Nalis
land of defendant no.2 was acquired in the 
year 2017 for widening of national highway 
another 1 and 1/2 nails land was acquired 
earlier and thereafter, the defendant no.2 
was left only 4 nalis land and thus, the 
defendants have encroached upon the land 
of the plaintiff. The defendant no.2 also got 
compensation for the land acquired. There 
is a joint inspection report by the 
government employees wherein 24 nalis land 
khasra nos.864 to 876 has been reported to 
be used for establishment of a hot mix plant.
The defendant no.2 is left with only 4 nali 
land comprising in khasra nos.856 and 857
which is clear from report dated 06.12.2023.
After considering the entire facts and 
circumstances of the case and going through 
the record, the present second appeal 
deserves to be admitted on the following 
substantial question of law:-
(i) Whether the defendants have 
made unauthorized occupation 
over the plaintiff’s khasra 
nos.864-876 and have made 
unauthorized construction 
thereon?
Summon the Trial Court Record.
Since the appeal is admitted, the effect 
and operation of the impugned judgment 
and decree shall remain stayed till the next 
date of listing.
List this case on 04.06.2026.
