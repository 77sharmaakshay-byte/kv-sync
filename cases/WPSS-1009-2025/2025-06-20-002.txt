Heard on Stay Application, IA No.01 of 
2025
The challenge in this petition is made 
to the transfer order dated 05.06.2025, by 
which the petitioner has been transferred 
from Division Nainital to Division 
Bhikyasen.
By means of instant stay application, 
the petitioner seeks stay of the transfer 
order. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that the petitioner is a senior 
employee, as per The Uttarakhand Annual 
Transfer for Public Servants Act, 2017 (“the 
2017 Act”), and in view of Section 7(d)(i), he 
is exempted from compulsory transfer. He 
submits that at present, the petitioner is 
posted in an accessible area and he has been transferred to remote area. 
Learned State Counsel was required 
to get instructions on the point that if the
petitioner is exempted from compulsory 
transfer, how he may be transferred.
Today, learned State Counsel admits 
that as per Section 7(d)(i) of the 2017 Act, 
the petitioner is exempted from compulsory 
transfer. 
Having considered, as an interim 
measure, till the next date of listing, the 
effect and operation of the impugned order 
shall remain in abeyance. 
Stay application stands disposed of, 
accordingly.
