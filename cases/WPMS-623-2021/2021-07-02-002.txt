Miscellaneous application (IA No. 2 of 
2021) stands disposed of by taking counter 
affidavit filed on behalf of respondent no. 1
on record. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
prays for and is granted a week’s time to 
file rejoinder affidavit. 
Learned Additional C.S.C. for the 
State/petitioners submits that the order 
dated 28.06.2016 passed by Chairman, 
Scheduled Tribes Commission, Dehradun
also needs to be stayed, as there was a 
prayer made in the stay application and 
operation of the said order was also stayed, 
but due to mistake, the same has not been
transcribed in the order dated 16.03.2021.
Having regard to the submission made 
by learned counsel for the State/petitioners, 
the order dated 16.03.2021 is modified and it is provided that effect and operation of 
the impugned orders dated 28.06.2016, 
03.03.2020 & 14.07.2020 shall remain 
stayed till the next date of listing.
List this matter in the month of 
August, 2021.
In the meantime, learned counsel for 
respondent 2 may file counter affidavit, if 
any.
