COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C407 No.22 of 2023
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. T.P.S. Takuli, Advocate, for the 
applicant. 
Mrs. Lata Negi, Brief Holder, for the 
State of Uttarakhand.
The applicant has preferred this 
transfer application, seeking transfer of the 
proceedings under section 125 of CrPC, 
being Miscellaneous Criminal Case No.12 
of 2020, “Ashwika @ Ashwi Vs. Nipun 
Pratap Singh”, which are the proceedings 
pending being held under section 125 of 
CrPC, before the Family Court, Kashipur, 
District Udham Singh Nagar. The same is 
being sought to be transferred to any
competent court within the District Nainital. 
The transfer has been sought on the 
ground, that the applicant has lost trust over 
the Family Court, Kashipur, District Udham 
Singh Nagar, owing to the earlier dismissal 
of the proceedings as referred to in 
paragraph 7, of the transfer application.
This Court is of the view that prior to 
passing any final orders on the same, the 
respondent is required to be noticed.Issue notices to the respondent no.2. 
Steps to be taken within a week. List after 
service of the notice on the respondent. 
Till the next date of listing, the further 
proceedings of the Miscellaneous Criminal 
Case No.12 of 2020, “Ashwika @ Ashwi 
Vs. Nipun Pratap Singh”, pending 
consideration before the Family Court, 
Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar, 
would be kept in abeyance. 
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
