Hon'ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Gaurav Singh, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned Addl. 
Advocate General along with Mr. S.C. 
Dumka, learned A.G.A. and Mr. Nikhil 
Bisht, learned Brief Holder for the State
3. By means of the present writ petition 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution 
of India, the petitioners have prayed for 
issuance of a writ in the nature of 
certiorari for quashing the First 
Information Report/Case Crime No.70 of 
226 dated 09.04.2026 under Sections 
115(2), 121, 132, 221, 351(2) and 352 of 
B.N.S. registered at Police Station Prem 
Nagar, Dehradun. The petitioners have 
further sought issuance of a writ, order or 
direction in the nature of mandamus 
commanding the respondent authorities 
not to arrest the petitioner pursuant to the 
aforesaid F.I.R.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioners would submit that, according to the 
allegations contained in the First 
Information Report, the complainant, who 
was posted as a Sub-Inspector at Police 
Station Prem Nagar, District Dehradun, 
alleged that petitioner no.2, without any 
provocation, slapped the complainant and 
used abusive language. It is further alleged 
that petitioner no.1 also abused the 
complainant and assaulted a lady
constable by twisting her arms; that,
according to the prosecution case, both the 
petitioners further manhandled other 
police personnel present at the Police 
Station, while petitioner no.2, who was 
accompanying them, allegedly recorded a 
video of the incident and thereafter fled 
from the spot.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would further submit that the petitioners 
are innocent and have falsely been 
implicated in the present case; that, no 
incident, as alleged in the First Information 
Report, ever took place at Police Station 
Prem Nagar; that, on the contrary, it is 
submitted that the complainant herself 
initially slapped petitioner no.2, 
whereupon petitioner no.2, in a retaliatory 
reaction, slapped the complainant. He 
would further submit that thereafter 
petitioner no.2 was allegedly manhandled 
by the police personnel present at the 
Police Station and was pulled by her hair.
6. On the previous date, learned State 
counsel was directed to produce the CCTV 
footage as well as the relevant General 
Diary entries dated 08.04.2026 and 09.04.2026 of Police Station Prem Nagar, 
District Dehradun.
7. Learned State counsel submits that 
he has perused the video footage placed on 
record. He fairly submits that the CCTV 
footage does not fully corroborate the 
prosecution version regarding the incident 
in question as alleged in the F.I.R.. 
However, he seeks four weeks' time to file a 
counter affidavit.
8. In the interest of justice, four weeks' 
further time, as prayed for, is granted to 
learned State counsel to file the counter 
affidavit.
9. List thereafter. 
10. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the material available on 
record.
11. In view of the aforesaid facts and 
circumstances, and particularly in view of 
the submission made on behalf of the State 
that the CCTV footage does not fully 
corroborate the prosecution version of the 
incident, it is directed that further 
proceedings pursuant to First Information 
Report/Case Crime No. 70 of 2026, dated 
09.04.2026, registered at Police Station 
Prem Nagar, District Dehradun, shall 
remain stayed till the next date of listing.
12. Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
