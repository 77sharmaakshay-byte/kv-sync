 Defect pointed out by the Registry is formal

 Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate has filed 
caveat on behalf of candidates, who possess 
D.L.Ed. qualification. Since these candidates are 
not impleaded as party in the writ petition, thus, 
they have no right to be heard at this stage. 
 Heard. 
 By means of present writ petition, petitioners 
has sought a writ of certiorari quashing the 
impugned condition for having minimum 50% 
marks in graduation, stipulated in the impugned 
advertisement. 
 Counter affidavit to be filed within four 
weeks. 
 List thereafter. 
 Also heard on the interim relief application. 
 Having considered the submissions of 
learned counsel for the petitioners and upon 
perusal of the material brought on record, this 
Court is of the view that condition of 50% marks 
in graduation is against the Notification dated 
23.08.2010 issued by the N.C.T.E., as has been 
held by Hon’ble Apex Court in the case of Neeraj 
Kumar Rai and others vs. State of U.P. and 
others, (2017) 14 SCC 608. Accordingly, the 
respondent is directed to accept the offline 
application form of the petitioners and to permit 
them to provisionally participate in the on going 
selection process. However, the result of the 
petitioners shall not be declared without the 
express leave of the Court.  Interim relief application CLMA No.10407 of 
2020 is disposed of. 
 Let a certified copy of the order be issued to 
learned counsel for the parties, today itself, on 
payment of usual charges.
