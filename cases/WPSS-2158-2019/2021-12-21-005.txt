Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Akshay Kumar, Advocate holding brief 
of Mr. Navnish Negi, Advocate for the petitioner.
Mrs. Anjali Bhargawa, Addl. C.S.C. for the 
State of Uttarakhand.
The petitioner contends, that the date of his 
inductment into the services of respondents as daily 
wager as it has been shown in Annexure 3, is to be 
July 2009 is wrong, in fact, in accordance with the 
certificate issued by the competent authority, he 
was inducted in 2007, and hence, he ought to have 
been considered for regularization, which is being 
granted to the alleged juniors, according to the 
petitioner who were appointed in 2008. 
But, however there is no authentic material 
on record to show, as to what was the specific date 
of petitioner’s inductment as daily wager with the 
respondents. He submits that he will be filing the 
relevant documents within two weeks.
The prayer sought for is granted. 
He may file the required document within the 
aforesaid two weeks, giving specific date of his 
appointment as a daily wager with the respondents.
List this matter in the week commencing 
14.02.2022. 
Accordingly, the Urgency Application 
No.16315 of 2021 preferred by the petitioner would 
stand disposed of.
