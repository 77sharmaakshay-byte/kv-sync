4. The present Writ Petition under Article 226 of the 
Constitution of India has been filed with the following 
prayers:-
“(i) A writ order or direction in nature of certiorari 
quashing the impugned FIR No. 0137/2025 dated 
21.03.2025 under Sections 120-B, 467, 468, 471 IPC 
and Sections 115(2), 305(a), 351 (3) and 85 B.N.S., 
2023 P.S. SIDCUL, District Haridwar.
(ii) A Writ order or direction or an appropriate order to 
stay the investigation in FIR No. 0137 of 2025 
registered at P.S. SICUL, District Haridwar under 
Sections 120-B, 467, 468, 471 IPC and Sections 
115(2), 305(a), 351(3) and 85 B.N.S., 2023 during 
the pendency of the present writ petition. 
(iii) A Writ order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus directing the respondents not to take any 
coercive action against the petitioner pursuant to the 
FIR No. 0137 of 2025 dated 21.03.2025 under 
Sections 120-B, 467, 468, 471 IPC and Sections 
115(2), 305(a), 351(3) and 85 B.N.S., 2023 .”
5. Respondent No. 4- Smt. Santosh Singhal, is present in 
person before this Court.
6. Learned counsel for the Petitioner submits that before 
the matter proceeds further, an attempt at mediation may 
be made so that the parties may reconcile and sort out their 
issues.
7. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of Respondent 
No. 4- Smt. Santosh Singhal, objects to the same. After interacting with Respondent No. 4, this Court is of the view 
that mediation or any such attempt would be futile, as 
Respondent No. 4 outrightly refuses to participate and 
expresses apprehension of threats.
8. Learned counsel for the Petitioner presses the interim 
application; however, since the question of maintainability 
has arisen, the same must first be addressed.
9. List this case on 29.12.2025 for hearing on the issue 
of maintainability of the Writ Petition on the next date.
