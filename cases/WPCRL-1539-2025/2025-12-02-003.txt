COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPCRL No. 1539 of 2025
Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Bhupesh Kandpal, Mr. Nishant Anand and Ms. Gunjan 
Bansal, learned counsel for the Petitioner. 
2. Mr. J. S. Virk, learned D.A.G. for the State. 
3. Mr. Sanjay Abbot (through V.C.) along with Mr. Ramji 
Srivastava, learned counsel for the Respondent no.4. 
4. The present writ petition under Article 226 of the Constitution 
of India is filed by the Petitioner with the following prayers:-
(a) A writ, order or direction in the nature of certiorari quashing the 
impugned FIR No.0137 of 2025 dated 21.03.2025 under Sections 120-B, 
467, 468, 471 of IPC and Sections 115(2), 305(a), 351(3) and 85 of BNS, 
2023, Police Station SIDCUL, District Haridwar. 
(b) A writ, order or direction or an appropriate order to stay the 
investigation in FIR No.0137 of 2025 registered at Police Station SIDCUL, 
District Haridwar under Sections 120-B, 467, 468, 471 of IPC and Sections 
115(2), 305(a), 351(3) and 85 of BNS, 2023 during the pendency of the 
present writ petition.
(c) A writ, order or direction in the nature of mandamus directing the 
respondents not to take any coercive action against the petitioner pursuant 
to the FIR No.0137 of 2025 dated 21.03.2025 under Sections 120-B, 467, 
468, 471 of IPC and Sections 115(2), 305(a), 351(3) and 85 of BNS, 2023, 
Police Station SIDCUL, District Haridwar.
5. Learned counsel appearing for the Respondent no.4 makes a 
submission that the present matter is not maintainable solely on the 
ground that as an interim measure anticipatory bail application was 
earlier filed and was withdrawn and no recourse in this regard was 
taken thereafter; Petitioner neither approached the Sessions Court,
nor preferred any writ in this regard. 
6. Whereas the learned counsel for the Petitioner makes a statement that the present writ petition is maintainable and can be 
heard by this Court. It is said that the present petition is in the nature 
of a writ, direction or order for quashing the impugned FIR No.0137 
of 2025 dated 21.03.2025 under Sections 120-B, 467, 468, 471 of 
IPC and Sections 115(2), 305(a), 351(3) and 85 of BNS, 2023, 
Police Station SIDCUL, District Haridwar and also by way of a writ 
in the nature of a mandamus with the plea for directing the
respondents not to take any coercive action against the Petitioner 
pursuant to the abovementioned FIR. 
7. After hearing the learned counsel for the parties and perusal of 
the record at this juncture, this Court finds the question of 
maintainability sufficient.
8. Admit.
9. Objections, if any, to be filed by the Respondents within a 
period of three weeks.
10. List this matter on 10.01.2026.
11. In the interregnum, it is directed that the investigation shall 
proceed but the Petitioner shall not be arrested by the police in 
connection with the impugned FIR No.0137 of 2025 dated 
21.03.2025 under Sections 120-B, 467, 468, 471 of IPC and Sections 
115(2), 305(a), 351(3) and 85 of BNS, 2023, Police Station 
SIDCUL, District Haridwar. However, he will continue to cooperate 
with the Investigating Officer of the case and shall remain present, as 
and when required for production of documents and for recording of 
their statement. 
12. Interim Relief Application (IA No.01 of 2025) stands disposed 
of.
