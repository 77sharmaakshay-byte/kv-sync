List on 18.09.2021, to enable the 
Central Government Standing Counsel to get 
instructions in the matter. 
It is the case of the petitioner that 
having been successful in the recruitment for 
soldier general duty, they were called to 
report, so as to complete all the formalities as 
a Regimental Head Quarter at Lansdowne. 
But, when they reached there, they were not 
allowed to enter into the premises and they 
were not told, as to why, it was done by those 
Arms Force Authorities. 
This matter is taken up today for the 
first time. 
The Court wanted to know from the 
learned counsel appearing for the 
respondents authorities, as to why, the 
petitioners were not allowed to report as per 
call letters issued to them? And why no 
communication to that effect was made to the 
petitioners?
To it, learned counsel for respondents 
seeks time.
The detailed instructions may not be 
required at this stage. A statement may be 
given, as to what prompted those authorities 
to deny the petitioners to report as per the call 
letters.
List on 18.09.2021, just after fresh. 
Meanwhile, instructions be sought. On that date, the Court shall also 
consider the maintainability of this petition, 
in view of the provisions as contained in the 
Armed Forces Tribunal Act, 2007.
