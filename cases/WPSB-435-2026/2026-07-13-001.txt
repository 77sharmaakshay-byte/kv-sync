1. Sri Hari Mohan Bhatia, learned counsel for the petitioners. 
2. Sri B.S. Parihar, learned Additional Chief Standing Counsel 
for the State of Uttarakhand. 
3. The case of the petitioners is that they were promoted from 
the post of Deputy Commissioner, Sales Tax, to the post of Joint 
Commissioner, Grade-II in pursuance of recommendations made by 
DPC on 12.06.2025 and promotion orders were issued in their favour 
on 30.06.2025. They were given joining on the promoted post as 
Joint Commissioners, Grade-II in pursuance of order dated 
25.09.2025. They also joined on the promoted post and have since 
then worked as Joint Commissioner, Grade-II. Now, during the 
annual chain of transfers by a Notification dated 07.07.2026, 
impugned herein, they have been reverted back to their original post, 
i.e. Deputy Commissioner. The reason, given for doing so, is that 
this Court in WPSB No. 304 of 2025 titled ‘Thakur Ranveer Singh 
vs. State of Uttarakhand and others” by order dated 24.07.2025 has 
stayed the operation of the recommendations made by DPC on 
12.06.2025 and the order of promotion dated 30.06.2025. 
4. The case of the petitioners is that Thakur Ranveer Singh, who 
filed the said writ petition, was Joint Commissioner, Grade-II and he 
raised dispute regarding his promotion to Grade-I. Further case of the petitioners is that coincidently, the proceedings for promotion for 
the post of Grade-I was also held on the same date, i.e. 12.06.2025 
and the order of promotion is also of the same date, i.e. 30.06.2025. 
He submits that the DPC proceedings, though of the same date, are 
different in respect of promotion to Grade-I and so was the order of 
promotion dated 30.06.2025, but since the dates were same, the 
Department has misconstrued the interim order passed in the writ 
petition of Thakur Ranveer Singh. 
5. Learned Additional Chief Standing Counsel for the State of 
Uttarakhand has placed on record the instructions received from the 
Additional Secretary, Uttarakhand Government, dated 30.06.2026. 
Paragraph-5 thereof reads as follows:-

6. Thus, while it could not be shown by the learned Additional 
Chief Standing Counsel for the State that in the writ petition filed by 
Thakur Ranveer Singh, there was any controversy regarding 
promotion to Grade-II, or the DPC recommendations for promotion 
to Grade-II and the order of promotion of the petitioners to Grade-II,
but the stand taken is that by the impugned order, the petitioners have 
been permitted to continue at the same place, but they are only being 
treated as holders of the post of Deputy Commissioner. 
7. As we find that the controversy involved in the writ petition, 
filed by Thakur Ranveer Singh, related to the promotion to the post 
of Joint Commissioner, Grade-I and, therefore, apparently the State 
has misread the interim order passed in the said case. 
8. In view of it, we hereby stay the impugned order to the extent it relates to the petitioners and only to the extent it provides that the 
petitioners would be treated to be working on their original post of 
Deputy Commissioner, State Tax.
