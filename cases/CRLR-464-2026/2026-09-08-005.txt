2. Heard on the Delay Condonation 
Application (I.A. No. 01 of 2026).
3. The reasons stated in the application 
are found sufficient to condone the delay. 
Accordingly, the delay in filing the present 
Criminal Revision is hereby condoned. The 
Delay Condonation Application is, 
accordingly, allowed.
4. The present Criminal Revision has 
been filed against the judgment and order 
dated 08.04.2025, passed by the learned 
Additional Sessions Judge, Khatima, District 
Udham Singh Nagar in Criminal Appeal No. 
173 of 2024, preferred against the judgment 
and order dated 03.08.2024, passed by the 
learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Khatima, District Udham Singh Nagar in 
Criminal Case No. 1197 of 2019, under 
Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments 
Act, 1881, whereby the learned Appellate 
Court upheld the judgment of the learned 
Trial Court convicting the revisionist under 
Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments 
Act and sentenced him to undergo rigorous imprisonment for a period of one year along 
with a fine of ₹ 12,50,000/-. In default of 
payment of fine, the revisionist was directed 
to undergo further simple imprisonment for 
a period of four months.
5. Today, the wife of the revisionist is 
present before this Court and submits that 
she has brought a demand draft of ₹
1,00,000/-, drawn in favour of the 
complainant. The said demand draft has 
been handed over to learned counsel 
appearing for respondent no. 2, who has 
assured the Court that the same shall be 
handed over to respondent no. 2.
6. Today, a supplementary affidavit has 
also been filed on behalf of the revisionist, 
which is taken on record. In the said 
affidavit, an undertaking has been given on 
behalf of the revisionist by his wife that the 
revisionist shall pay the entire amount of 
fine of ₹ 12,50,000/-, as directed by the 
learned Trial Court. Out of the said amount, 
₹ 1,00,000/- has been paid today by way of 
demand draft, and the remaining amount 
shall be paid within a period of ten months.
It is further stated that the revisionist is 
presently facing financial hardship and is 
the sole bread earner of his family. It is, 
therefore, submitted that, considering the 
admitted liability and the undertaking 
furnished on his behalf, the revisionist may 
be enlarged on bail on humanitarian 
grounds.
7. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties and upon consideration of the facts 
and circumstances of the case, particularly 
the fact that the revisionist is stated to be 
the sole bread earner of his family, the 
payment of ₹ 1,00,000/- made today by way 
of demand draft, and the undertaking 
furnished on his behalf by his wife, this 
Court is of the opinion that sufficient 
grounds exist for granting bail to the 
revisionist at this stage.
8. It is made clear that the observations 
made hereinabove are confined to the consideration of the prayer for bail and shall 
not be construed as an expression of opinion 
on the merits of the case.
9. Accordingly, the revisionist shall be 
released on bail during the pendency of the 
present Criminal Revision, upon executing a 
personal bond and furnishing two reliable 
sureties, each in the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the learned Trial Court.
10. List this case on 12.10.2026.
11. The revisionist is directed to appear 
before this Court on the date fixed.
