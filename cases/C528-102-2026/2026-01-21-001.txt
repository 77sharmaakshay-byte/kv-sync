2. An F.I.R. was registered on the 
complaint of the respondent No. 2 (wife), 
alleging that her marriage with applicant 
No. 1 was solemnized on 05.03.2024 
according to Hindu rites and ceremonies. 
Post-marriage, the complainant resided 
in the matrimonial home, where the 
applicants allegedly began harassing her 
for dowry, specifically demanding a cash 
amount of ₹5,00,000/-. It is further 
alleged in the F.I.R. that applicant No. 2 
(father-in-law) cast lecherous eyes upon 
her and attempted to touch her 
inappropriately, compelling her to leave 
the matrimonial home within two months 
of the marriage. The complainant also 
lodged a complaint with the Women 
Helpline, Rudrapur. Thereafter, an 
amicable agreement was reached 
between the parties, whereby it was 
decided that the complainant and 
applicant no.1 would reside separately in 
a rented accommodation. Pursuant to 
this agreement, they began living 
separately in a rented premises. 
However, it is alleged that applicant no.1 
continued to harass the complainant for 
dowry even thereafter. On 03.11.2024, 
the complainant left the rented 
accommodation and returned to her 
parental home. Subsequently, the F.I.R. 
came to be lodged against all the 
applicants.3. Learned counsel for the applicants 
submits that there exist strong prospects 
of an amicable settlement between the 
parties, which could be explored through 
mediation. It is further submitted that all 
family members of applicant no.1, 
including his married sister and brother￾in-law, have been unnecessarily arrayed 
as accused despite lacking any specific 
role in the alleged incidents, thereby 
abusing the process of law.
4. Issue notice to respondent no.2, 
returnable at an early date. 
5. As prayed, three weeks’ time is 
granted to learned State Counsel to file 
counter affidavit. 
6. List after service report is received.
7. Considering the submission 
advanced by learned counsel for the

Udham Singh Nagar, shall remain 
stayed. 
8. Stay application (I.A. No.2 of 2026)

accordingly.
