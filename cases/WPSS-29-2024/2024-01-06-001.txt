WPSS No.29 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Amish Tewari, learned counsel for 
the petitioner. 
2. Mr. Rajiv Singh Bisht, learned Additional 
C.S.C. along with Mr. Bhupendra Singh 
Koranga, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Mr. Yashpal Arya, learned counsel 
holding brief of Mr. Neeraj Garg, learned 
counsel for respondent no.4.
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
5. By means of this writ petition, petitioner 
has sought the indulgence of this Court for 
quashing the order dated 30.12.2023, which 
was served upon petitioner through WhatsApp 
sent through Clerk of respondent no.3, 
whereby petitioner was asked not to join 
services as Class-IV employee with the 
respondent no.3.
6. It is the case of the petitioner that 
petitioner is working with the respondent no.3 
as Class-IV employee on the post of 
Mali/Gardner since June, 2015. He was initially 
given engagement as a contractual employee 
and according to the terms and conditions of 
the engagement letter dated 11.06.2015, it is 
stated that in case the work and conduct of 
the petitioner is not found satisfactory, the 2
appointment shall be brought to an end. 
7. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that there is never any complaint 
regarding petitioner’s work and conduct from 
the side of the respondent no.3; still the 
petitioner has been asked not to come to the 
campus of respondent no.3 w.e.f. 01.01.2024.
8. Issue notice to the respondent no.3, 
returnable within six weeks.
9. Respondent(s) may file their counter 
affidavit(s) within a period of six weeks. 
10. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to 
the petitioner to file rejoinder affidavit. 
11. List this matter on 15.05.2024.
12. In the meantime, respondents are 
directed to permit petitioner to continue as a 
Mali/Gardner in the campus of respondent 
no.3, in accordance with the terms and 
conditions of the engagement order dated 
11.06.2015. It is further directed that 
petitioner shall be paid his remuneration as 
admissible to him regularly. 
13. Interim relief application (IA No.1/2024) 
stands disposed of accordingly.
