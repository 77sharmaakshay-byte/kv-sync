2. By means of the present Criminal Revision, the 
revisionist has assailed the judgment and order dated 
15.09.2026 passed by the learned Additional District and 
Sessions Judge, Kotdwar, District Pauri Garhwal in 
Criminal Appeal No. 14 of 2026, titled as "Vineet 
Khurana vs Gajendra Bhatia and another", whereby the 
learned Additional District and Sessions Judge, Kotdwar 
has dismissed the appeal preferred by the revisionist and 
has affirmed the impugned judgment and order dated 
22.01.2026 passed by the learned Civil Judge (Junior 
Division)/Judicial Magistrate, Kotdwar, District Pauri
Garhwal in Criminal Case No. 1394 of 2022, titled as 
"Gajendra Bhatia vs Vineet Khurana", for the offence 
punishable Under Section 138 of Negotiable Instrument 
Act, 1881, whereby the learned Judicial Magistrate/Civil 
Judge (J.D.) has convicted him and sentenced him to eight months simple imprisonment along with fine of 
Rs. 2,70,000/- and in default of payment of fine, the 
revisionist shall further undergo one-month simple 
imprisonment additionally.
3. Assailing the impugned judgments and orders of 
the trial court as well as the appellate court, the learned 
counsel for the revisionist would submit that the 
revisionist has rebutted the presumption under Sections 
118 and 139 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, yet the 
trial court convicted the revisionist and the appellate 
court also erred by not allowing the appeal.
4. The learned counsel for the revisionist has also 
given an undertaking on behalf of the revisionist that the 
entire fine amount of ₹2,70,000 would be deposited 
before the Registry of this Court within two months. 
5. The matter requires deliberation.
6. Admit. 
7. Issue notice to the respondent. Steps to be taken 
within a week. 
8. Summon the trial court record. 
9. List on 04.11.2026. 
10. By means of the Exemption Application (IA No.1 
of 2026), the revisionist has sought exemption from 
surrendering in compliance of the impugned orders.
11. The learned counsel for the revisionist would 
submit that the revisionist was on bail during trial as 
well as during the pendency of appeal and he has never 
misused the bail, and has also stated in the exemption 
application that the revisionist is ready to deposit the amount as fixed by this Court and an undertaking has 
already been recorded hereinbefore that the revisionist 
shall deposit the entire amount of fine of ₹2,70,000 
within a period of two months before the Registry of 
this court.
12. In view of the aforesaid facts, the Exemption 
Application is allowed. 
13. The revisionist is exempted from the requirement 
of surrendering in compliance of the orders impugned.
