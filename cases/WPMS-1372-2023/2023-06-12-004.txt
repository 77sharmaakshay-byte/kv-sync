Heard. 
In fact, the Court had dictated the 
judgment in the open Court, but when the 
Court inquired, learned counsel for parties 
are agreeable that perhaps this matter may 
be decided by way of mediations.
It is a dispute with regard to the 
school, which was established by the father 
of the petitioner and the respondent no.3 
namely, Hansram Maithani. He constituted 
a society which was registered. The 
byelaws were made. The founder member 
Hans Ram Naithani was lifetime Manager 
of the society. After his death, the dispute 
arose. Both of his sons, the petitioner as 
well as the respondent no.3 started 
claiming the position of the Manager in the 
society.
In fact, during the course of 
arguments, it is stated that the wife of the 
respondent no.3 is appointed as Principal 
and she is intervening in the activities of 
the school as are being managed by the 
petitioner Chandra Kishore Naithani.
Before referring the matter to the 
Mediation Centre of this Court, it is 
suggested by the learned counsel for the 
parties, that the parties may be called 
before the Court.
