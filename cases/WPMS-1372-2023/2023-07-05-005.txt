On the last date, the Court was 
in the process of dictating judgment, 
when possibility was raised that 
perhaps the parties may settle the 
dispute amicably. The dispute has 
been summarized in the Court’s order 
dated 12.06.2023 as follows:-
“It is a dispute with regard to 
the school, which was established 
by the father of the petitioner and 
the respondent no.3 namely, 
Hansram Maithani. He constituted a 
society which was registered. The 
byelaws were made. The founder 
member Hans Ram Naithani was 
lifetime Manager of the society. After 
his death, the dispute arose. Both of 
his sons, the petitioner as well as 
the respondent no.3 started 
claiming the position of the Manager 
in the society.”
On the last date the Court had 
recorded that before referring the 
matter to the Mediation Centre of this 
Court it will be appropriate if the 
parties may be called before the Court. The petitioner and the 
respondent no.3 are before the Court, 
duly identified by their respective 
counsel. They are brothers.
After interacting with them, this 
Court is of the view that perhaps if
genuine efforts are made they may 
arrive at an amicable settlement and 
that will be better for both of them as 
well as the Institution, for which, they 
are before this Court. Therefore, the 
matter is referred to the Mediation 
Centre of this Court. The parties are 
directed to appear before the 
Medication Centre of this Court today 
itself. 
List on 12.09.2023 along with 
the report of Mediation Centre of this 
Court.
