In this petition on 12.06.2023, 
a possibility was explored, as to 
whether the parties may settle the 
dispute amicably? The matter was 
referred to mediation. A report dated 
05.07.2023 from Mediation Centre 
has also been received. According to 
the mediation report, the parties 
have settled the dispute amicably. 
Today, learned counsel 
appearing for the petitioner would 
submit that, in fact, the mediation 
does not resolve anything. A 
resolution has yet to be made. The 
dispute is with regard to the 
position of Manager. Since, the 
mediation report has not been 
accepted though, according to the 
report, it has been signed by the 
petitioner also, the matter shall be 
heard finally. 
List for final hearing on 
11.01.2024.
Learned counsel for the 
petitioner would submit that the petitioner is being obstructed by the 
respondents from entering in the 
school. 
On behalf of the private 
respondent, it is argued that the 
petitioner is interfering in the 
business of the school. 
The issue before this Court is 
an order passed by an authority, 
which is impugned. If any party has 
any grievance from adverse party, 
he is at liberty to seek such 
recourse, under the law, be it civil or 
criminal, as is permissible.
