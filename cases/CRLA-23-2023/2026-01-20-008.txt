COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
CRLA No.23 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Priyanshu Gairola, learned legal 
aid counsel for the appellant/applicant.
2. Mr. Vijay Khanduri, learned Brief 
Holders for the State.
3. The applicant is in judicial custody in 
connection with F.I.R. No. 26 of 2021, 
arising out of S.S.T. No. 3 of 2022, 
registered at Police Station Narendranagar, 
District Tehri Garhwal, for the offences 
punishable under Sections 376(3) I.P.C., 
Section 5(m)/6 of the Protection of Children 
from Sexual Offences Act, 2012, and 
Section 3(2)(v) of the Scheduled Castes and 
the Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of 
Atrocities) Act. The applicant has moved the 
present application seeking grant of short￾term bail.
4. Heard learned counsel for the 
appellant/applicant and learned State 
counsel on the Short Term Bail Application 
(I.A. No. 8 of 2026).
5. Learned counsel for the 
appellant/applicant would submit that the 
marriage of the applicant’s daughter is 
scheduled to be solemnized on 06.02.2026 
at Village Pooranpur, Post Jogirampuri, 
Tehsil Nagina, District Bijnor (U.P.). It is 
further submitted that as per Hindu rites 
and customs, the presence of the father is 
essential for performing various marriage 
ceremonies and rituals. On account of the 
said unavoidable family obligation, the 
present application has been filed seeking 
release of the applicant on short-term bail 
for a period of twenty days from the date of 
his release.6. Instructions were called from the 
learned State counsel. However, upon 
query, learned State counsel submits that 
no instructions have been received so far. 
nevertheless, he fairly admits that the 
marriage invitation card has been annexed 
with the short-term bail application.
7. Learned counsel for the 
appellant/applicant would further submit,
on instructions, that the factum of the 
proposed marriage of the applicant’s 
daughter has been verified by the 
concerned authority.
8. Having considered the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the parties 
and upon perusal of the record, this Court 
is of the view that, in the facts and 
circumstances of the case, the applicant 
deserves to be enlarged on short-term bail.
9. Accordingly, the Short-Term Bail 
Application (I.A. No. 8 of 2026) is allowed.
10. Let the appellant/applicant be 
released on short-term bail for a period of 
twenty days from the date of his release, on 
his executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two reliable sureties, each of the 
like amount, to the satisfaction of the Court 
concerned.
11. It is made clear that upon expiry of the 
period of short-term bail, the 
appellant/applicant shall surrender before 
the concerned authority/court forthwith.
