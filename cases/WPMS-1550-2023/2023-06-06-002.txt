The issue involved in the present 
writ petition almost happens to be akin to 
that as engages consideration in Writ 
Petition (M/S) No. 1546 of 2023.
By virtue of an award dated 
25.02.2023, as rendered in Adjudication 
Case No. 70 of 2008, Anil Chandola Vs. 
Secretary, G.M.O.U. Kotdwar, whereby the 
reference under Section 4A of the U.P. 
Industrial Disputes Act, was sought to be 
adjudicated as against the order of 
termination dated 07.09.2006. 
The said reference has been 
answered by the learned labour Court by 
the impugned award, by virtue of which the 
workman has been directed to be reinstated 
along with 25% of the back wages to be paid 
to him. 
In the connected writ petition, this 
Court has stayed the effect and operation of 
the award subject to the condition that the 
workman would be reinstated. So far as the payment of arrears of 25% of the back 
wages is concerned, that was directed to be 
kept in abeyance.
Hence, this writ petition would too 
carry a similar interim order as that passed 
on 25.05.2023 in Writ Petition (M/S) No. 
1546 of 2023.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
