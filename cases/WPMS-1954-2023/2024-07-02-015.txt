COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No. 1954 of 2023
Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J.
(1) Mr. Rajendra Dobhal, learned Senior 
Counsel assisted by Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, 
learned counsel for the petitioner.
(2) Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel 
for the respondent – Cantonment Board, 
Dehradun.
(3) Mr. Anil K. Dabral, learned Additional 
C.S.C. for the State of Uttarakhand.
(4) Petitioners constructed a house 
within limits of Cantonment Board, 
Dehradun. Cantonment Board passed a 
sealing order, in respect of construction 
raised by petitioners, on 27.06.2023.
Petitioners have challenged the said 
sealing order in this writ petition.
(5) Mr. Rajendra Dobhal, learned Senior 
Counsel appearing for the petitioners 
submits that construction was raised after 
obtaining sanction from the Competent 
Authority in the Cantonment Board, 
therefore, the sealing order is bad in law. 
(6) Learned counsel for Cantonment 
Board submits that petitioners have not 
submitted completion notice, as required 
under Section 242 of the Cantonments 
Act, 2006, therefore, construction raised 
by petitioner was sealed. 
(7) Learned Senior Counsel for the 
petitioners refers to Section 249 of the 
Cantonments Act, 2006 which empowers 
the Cantonment Board to seal unauthorised construction. Based on the 
said provision, it is contended on behalf of 
petitioners that the construction raised by 
petitioners cannot be termed as 
“unauthorised” merely because completion 
notice was not served by the petitioners.
(8) Per contra, Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, 
learned counsel for Cantonment Board 
submits that petitioners were given 
sanction by the Cantonment Board for 
constructing two floors of the building, 
however, petitioners are trying to 
construct third story in the said building, 
that too without submitting completion 
notice. 
(9) This Court is prima facie satisfied 
that Section 249 of the Cantonments Act, 
2006 would not be attracted merely 
because the completion notice was not 
given by the petitioners to Cantonment 
Board. 
(10) List this case on 04.09.2024 for final 
hearing. 
(11) Until further orders, sealing order 
shall remain stayed. Petitioners shall not 
make any alteration or deviation from the 
sanctioned map. Petitioners are also 
restrained from raising any construction 
on the third story.
(12) Interim relief application (IA No. 1 of 
2023) stands disposed of.
