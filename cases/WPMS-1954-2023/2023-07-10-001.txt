that vide the sanction plan (Annexure No.1) the 
petitioner was allowed to construct two storey 
building; that, due to Covid Pandemic the 
extension for one more year was granted vide 
letter dated 26.04.2022 (Annexure No.2) and the 
construction was completed within that period, 
whereafter the petitioner applied for construction 
of another floor on that two storey building vide 
sanction letter (Annexure No.4); that, however, it 
came in the knowledge as it was captured in the 
CCTV Cameras installed by the petitioner that on 
28.06.2023 this construction was sealed by the 
respondent nos. 1 and 2 without giving any notice 
to the petitioner.

construction of the ground floor and the first floor

sealed. He would further submit that a proposal
