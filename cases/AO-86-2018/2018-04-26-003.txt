 While filing the appeal, the 
appellant, under the impression, that 
there is delay in filing the appeal, filed 
the delay condonation application and 
also served copy of the same upon 
counsel for respondent no.1, whereas, 
there was no delay in filing the appeal. 
Against the delay condonation 
application, respondent no.1 also 
prepared objections. As there was no 
delay in the appeal and delay 
condonation application was also not 
entertained by the Court, hence, there is 
no requirement for respondent no.1 to 
file objections. 
 This is an admitted appeal. LCR has 
also been received. 
 List for final hearing in its turn.
