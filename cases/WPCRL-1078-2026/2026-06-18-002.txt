Kishan Singh and Another ......Petitioners
v.
State of Uttarakhand and Others .......Respondents
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Hon’ble Siddhartha Sah, J.
Ms. Manisha Bhandari, Advocate for the 
petitioners.
Mr. B.N. Molakhi, D.A.G. for the State.
The petitioners are convicts. They seek 
premature release on the ground of their illness.
According to the writ petition, petitioner 
no.1, Kishan Singh, is suffering with 
Adenocarcinoma Rectum (Cancer) and he 
requires continuous medical supervision. The 
petitioner no.2, Raees Ahmad, is a patient of 
paralysis. 
Heard.
In this matter, learned counsel for the 
petitioners submits that the condition of 
petitioner no.1, Kishan Singh, is deteriorating 
day by day. He is suffering with cancer. His 
condition is pathetic. According to the 
Uttarakhand State (for Sentence 
Pardon/Premature Release of Convicted 
Prisoners Punished with Life Imprisonment by 
court) Permanent Policy, 2022 (“the Policy”), the 
petitioner no.1, Kishan Singh, is entitled to be 
released prematurely because according to Para 
4 of the Policy, the disease falls under Incurable 
Malignancy, and the Jail Manual also provides 
for premature release of such convicts. 
Learned State Counsel, under instructions, 
submits that the petitioner no.1, Kishan Singh, is 
suffering with Carcinoma Rectum with Post 
Colostomy case.
Learned counsel for the petitioners also 
submits that the petitioner no.1, Kishan Singh, is 
operated case of cancer. He has to carry a bag for his natural discharge. 
Learned State Counsel very fairly concedes 
that the case of the petitioner no.1, Kishan 
Singh, may be considered by the authority for 
premature release. However, according to him, 
the condition of the petitioner no.2, Raees 
Ahmad, is not such, which may require 
premature release. Though, he submits that he 
may be given time to file counter affidavit.
Let State file counter affidavit within two 
weeks. 
List this matter on 13.07.2026, along with 
the connected matters. 
Learned counsel for the petitioners 
submits that as an interim measure, the 
petitioner no.1, Kishan Singh, may be released 
from jail subject to his furnishing a personal 
bond and if surety is to be taken, he may be 
given time because he is not in such a condition 
to easily arrange surety, as his condition is 
pathetic. She would refer to Para No.525 of the 
Jail Manual, which, according to her, empowers 
the Jail Superintendant to take bonds from a 
convict, who is released from custody. 
Having considered the condition of the 
petitioner no.1, Kishan Singh, as an interim 
measure, we are of the considered view that till 
the petition is decided, the petitioner no.1, 
Kishan Singh, be released from custody subject 
to furnishing a personal bond and a surety of Rs. 
50,000/- and a surety. The petitioner no.1, 
Kishan Singh, shall be at liberty to produce 
surety before the Superintendant of Jail within 
four weeks from his release.
Accordingly, we directed the 
Superintendant of the jail concerned to release 
the petitioner no.1, Kishan Singh.
(Siddhartha Sah, J.) (Ravindra Maithani J.)
