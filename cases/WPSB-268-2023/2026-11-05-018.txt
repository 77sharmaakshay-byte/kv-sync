5. Mr. D.S. Patni, learned Senior Counsel 
appearing for the Uttarakhand Power Corporation 
Limited has filed a supplementary counter-affidavit
in the Court today, which is taken on record. The 
said affidavit is filed by Mr. Sumit Singhal, Deputy 
Chief Personnel Officer (Human Resource), 
Uttarakhand Power Corporation Limited, Dehradun. 
In Para 3 of the said affidavit it is stated that petitioners in WPSB No. 268 of 2023 and WPSB No. 
319 of 2023 shall be considered for promotion to the 
promotional post of Executive Engineer subject to 
their fulfilling the prescribed eligibility criteria and 
other applicable conditions under the relevant 
service rules. Their promotion, however, shall be 
subject to final outcome of the writ petition. 
6. Mr. Patni, learned Senior Counsel reiterates 
what has been stated in Para 3 of the 
supplementary counter-affidavit and he submits that 
UPCL be permitted to hold promotion exercise for 
filling available vacancies on the post of Executive 
Engineer.
7. The interim order dated 07.08.2026 is 
modified and Uttarakhand Power Corporation 
Limited is permitted to hold promotion exercise in 
view of the undertaking given in Para 3 of the 
supplementary counter-affidavit dated 05.11.2026 
filed by Mr. Sumit Singhal, Deputy Chief Personnel 
Officer (Human Resource), UPCL, Dehradun.
8. However, promotion order shall be issued only 
with the leave of this Court.
9. List on 30.11.2026.
