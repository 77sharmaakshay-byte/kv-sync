Delay Condonation Application (IA/1/2026)
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
5. The appeal is delayed by 43 days.
6. Learned counsel for the respondents do not 
object to the delay being condoned.
7. Accordingly, the delay is condoned.
8. Office is directed to allot a regular number to 
the instant appeal.
9. The submission is that in the instant case the 
respondents were seeking to recover the annual 
increments stated to have been wrongly granted to 
the appellants from the date of their recruitment till 
01.01.2016, the date of implementation of the 
recommendation of the last Pay Commission. Thus,
it relates to a period much before the undertaking was furnished on 12.06.2023 just before the 
retirement on 30.06.2023.
10. It is submitted that the said undertaking was 
given on the eve of retirement, in a prescribed form,
to get pensionary benefits. It was also never 
intended to indemnify the respondents of the 
amounts paid by way of increments since initial
recruitment uptil the year 2016.
11. The matter requires consideration.
12. The appeal is admitted for hearing.
13. List after six weeks.
14. Meanwhile, counter affidavit, if any, be filed by 
the respondents.
