Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Vaibhav Singh Chauhan,
Advocate for the appellants.
Mr. S.S. Adhikari, D.A.G., for the 
State.
The appellants Vipin Kumar and 
Virendra have been convicted and 
sentenced under Section 307 read 
with 34 and 326 read with 34 IPC on 
23.09.2021, in Sessions Trial No. 62 of 
2016, by the Court of 2nd Additional 
Sessions Judge, Roorkee, District 
Haridwar.
Heard on Bail Application No. 2 of 
2021.
Learned counsel for the 
appellants would submit that the 
scribe of the FIR has not been 
examined. The person, who was 
informed about the incident by the 
victim, after the incident, has also not 
been examined. The FIR is delayed. 
Though, PW4 Ramesh Singh would 
submit that he had informed the family 
members of the victim and the Police 
as well on the date of incident itself. In 
addition, it is also argued that the 
medical examination does not support 
the oral evidence.
According to the FIR, in the late 
evening on 17.08.2015, the victim was 
attacked by co-convicts Sonu and 
other persons. The charge sheet was 
submitted against the appellants also. 
In evidence, the victim as PW2 has 
stated that he was attacked with a 
sharp edged weapon by the appellants 
also.
The victim has stated about the 
incident. There are eyewitnesses. PW3Dr. Tasdiq Khan and PW9 Dr. Arvind 
Verma have stated about the injuries,
which were found grievous in nature. 
In fact, there were fractures on the face 
itself. The evidence was found reliable 
by the court below.
Having considered the entirety of 
facts, this Court is of the view that it is 
not a fit case for bail.
The bail application is rejected.
Paper book is ready. Let it be 
provided to learned counsel for the 
appellant.
List for final hearing.
