Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Akshay Pradhan, learned counsel for the 
applicant.
2. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned A.G.A. for the 
State.
3. The instant appeal is an admitted appeal, 
admitted on 24.11.2021 and the Trial Court record is 
summoned and the paper book is also ready. The 
appellant herein namely Vipin Kumar is already 
enlarged on bail on 10.12.2024 and now the bail 
application is moved on behalf of appellant no. 2-
Virendra. The Trial Court convicted both the 
appellants for the offences punishable under sections 
307 read with 34 of IPC with the maximum sentence of 
7 years imprisonment with the fine of Rs. 10,000/-.
4. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the 
appellant on the basis of the jail custody certificate 
dated 18.04.2026 of District Jail, Haridwar, that the 
present appellant served more than half of the 
sentence since as on 18.04.2026 as he has served 5 years 
4 months 1 day without remission and 5 years 9 
months 13 days with remission.
5. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned A.G.A. have not 
raised any objection with regard to the long 
incarceration of the appellant and he also fairly 
submits on the basis of the custody certificate that the 
appellant no. 2 already served more than half of the 
sentence.6. After hearing the submission of learned counsel 
for the parties and taking into consideration the 
custody certificate dated 18.04.2026 of Jailor of District 
Jail, Haridwar, since the appellant no. 2 already served 
more than half of the sentence, this court is of the view 
that the appellant no. 2 deserves for bail.
7. Accordingly, without expressing any opinion on 
the merit of the case the bail application of appellant 
no. 2 is allowed.
8. Let the appellant no. 2 ‘Virendra’ be enlarged on 
bail on furnishing his personal bond and two reliable 
sureties each of the like amount to the satisfaction of 
the court concerned during the pendency of the instant 
criminal appeal.
9. List this appeal in the month of November, 2026
for final disposal.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
