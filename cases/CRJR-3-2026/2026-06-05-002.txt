Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Ms. Suraiya Naaz, learned Amicus 
Curiae for the revisionist.
2. Mr. Dinesh Chauhan, learned A.G.A. 
for the State.
3. By means of the present criminal jail 
revision, the revisionist has challenged the 
judgment and order dated 18.10.2023 
passed by the learned trial court, whereby 
he was convicted for the offence punishable 
under Section 138 of the Negotiable 
Instruments Act and sentenced to undergo 
three months' simple imprisonment along 
with a fine of ₹3,20,000/-. It was further 
directed that in default of payment of fine, 
the revisionist shall undergo an additional 
sentence of two months' simple 
imprisonment. The criminal appeal 
preferred by the revisionist against the 
aforesaid judgment and order was dismissed 
by the appellate court vide judgment and 
order dated 29.07.2025.
4. Learned Amicus Curiae appearing for 
the revisionist would submit that the 
revisionist remained in judicial custody from 
22.02.2026 till 07.05.2026 and has already 
undergone the substantive sentence of three 
months' simple imprisonment awarded by 
the trial court. It is further submitted that, 
on account of non-payment of the fine 
amount, the revisionist has been undergoing 
the default sentence since 08.05.2026. 
Learned counsel would contend that the 
revisionist has already remained 
incarcerated for more than four months and has thus undergone a substantial portion of 
the total sentence awarded to him.
5. Heard learned Amicus Curiae for the 
revisionist and learned State Counsel.
6. Admit.
7. Summon the lower court record.
8. Issue notice to respondent no.2, if any.
9. Considering the submissions advanced 
by learned counsel for the revisionist, the 
nature of the accusation, the period of 
incarceration already undergone by him, 
and without expressing any opinion on the 
merits of the case, it is directed that the 
revisionist shall be released on bail during 
the pendency of the present criminal 
revision, subject to furnishing a personal 
bond and two reliable sureties each in the 
like amount to the satisfaction of the court 
concerned.
