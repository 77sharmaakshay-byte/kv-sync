2. Today, the matter was mentioned before 
the Court during the morning session. 
Considering the nature of the grievance 
raised, the Court directed the presence of a 
responsible police officer from Police Station 
Mallital, District Nainital. Accordingly, the 
matter was taken up after lunch.
3. Pursuant to the aforesaid direction, Mr. 
Dinesh Joshi, Sub-Inspector, Police Station 
Mallital, is present before the Court. He has 
apprised the Court that the complaint 
submitted by the petitioner has been received 
and an inquiry thereon is presently underway. 
He has further assured the Court that 
appropriate action shall be taken, strictly in 
accordance with law, against any person 
found responsible upon completion of the 
inquiry. Mr. Dinesh Joshi has further 
informed the Court that there exists a dispute between two jamaat, namely, the Tabliki and 
Shura groups. He has assured the Court that 
a meeting of the Managing Committee of Jama 
Masjid, Nainital, along with representatives of 
both the aforesaid jamaat, shall be convened 
with a view to amicably resolving the dispute 
and ensuring that peace, harmony and 
communal amity are maintained. Mr. Dinesh 
Joshi, Sub-Inspector, has also assured the 
Court that the local police shall ensure that 
no person is unlawfully prevented or 
obstructed from offering namaz at Jama 
Masjid, Nainital, and that adequate measures 
shall be taken to maintain law and order.
4. List this matter on 25.08.2026 for 
submission of the progress report regarding 
the inquiry as well as the steps taken to 
resolve the dispute and maintain peace.
