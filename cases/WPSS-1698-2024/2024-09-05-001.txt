COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.1698 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Parikshit Saini, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Standing Counsel for the State.
3. Issue notice to respondent no.3, returnable within six weeks.
4. Steps to be taken within one week.
5. By means of this writ petition, the petitioner has challenged the 
order dated 18.05.2024 passed by respondent no.2, whereby the 
additional increment paid to the petitioner at the time of promotion in the 
year 2008 has been held to be given erroneously and a direction was 
made for recovering the excess payment made to the petitioner.
6. It is submitted by learned counsel for the petitioner that the 
increment has been paid by the respondent-Department to the petitioner 
without any misrepresentation and fraud having been played by the 
petitioner and it is also not the case of the respondent-State.
7. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the case is covered 
by the judgment of State of Punjab Vs. Rafiq Maish reported in 2015 (4) 
SCC 334 as petitioner is also a Class-III Employee and no recovery shall 
be made from the petitioner.
8. This Court finds substance in the submissions made by the
learned counsel for the petitioner prima facie.
9. No recovery shall be made from the petitioner. 
10. Learned counsel for the State may file counter affidavit within six 
weeks.
11. Two weeks’ time thereafter is granted to learned counsel for the 
petitioner for filing rejoinder affidavit.
12. List this case on 17.12.2024.
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
