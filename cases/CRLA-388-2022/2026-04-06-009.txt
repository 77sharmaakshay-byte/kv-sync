Instant appeal is preferred against the judgment and 
order dated 26/27.08.2022, passed in Special Sessions Trial No. 51 of 
2020, State of Uttarakhand Vs. Mohit, by the court of Special Judge 
POCSO/Additional Sessions Judge/FTC, Haldwani, District Nainital. 
By it, the appellant has been convicted under Section 376 (3) IPC and 
Section 5(m)/6 of the Protection of Children From Sexual Offences Act, 
2012 (“the Act”) and sentenced accordingly. He seeks bail during the 
pendency of the appeal.
2. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the 
record.
3. This is an admitted appeal.
4. LCR has already been received.
5. List 26.05.2026 for final hearing.
Heard on Bail Application No. 1 of 20232
6. According to the FIR, on 18.09.2020, the victim was called 
by the appellant on the false context. They were known to each other. 
The appellant bolted the door from the inside and did galat kaam with 
the victim. This is the case in the FIR.
7. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that it is not a 
case under Section 5 of the Act. It is not a case of penetration to any 
extent. He submits that even the victim in her statement has stated 
that the victim tried to penetrate his penis into her vagina, but he did 
not succeed. Learned counsel also referred to the statement of the 
victim, when she said that she had told it to the Investigating Officer 
that the appellant had touched his penis into her vagina, but she did 
not allow him to insert it inside. He submits that at the most it was a 
case of Section 7 of the Act. The appellant is in jail for about five years 
now.
8. Learned State counsel submits that the victim has 
supported the prosecution case. PW4 Dr. Pallavi Toliya had found 
injuries on vagina of the victim and found contusion and cut marks.
9. In order to commit penetrative sexual assault as per 
Section 3 of the Act, it is not necessary that the penetration should be 
complete. Penetration to any extent into the vagina amounts to offence 
of penetrative sexual assault. At this stage, much of the discussion is 
not expected of.
10. Suffice it to say that in her examination, the victim has 
stated that the appellant did kiss the victim, opened her pyjama and 
tried to insert his penis into her vagina and he tried it for about half an 
hour. PW4 Dr. Pallavi Toliya has stated that though there was no 3
redness in the vagina of the victim, but inside labia there was a small 
wound like contusion and a cut mark. Definitely, the argument which 
has been raised at this stage would find deliberation during hearing of 
the appeal as to whether the act falls under Section 5 of the Act?
11. Having considered, we do not see any reason to enlarge
the appellant on bail. Accordingly, bail application deserves to be 
rejected.
12. The bail application is rejected.
