Mr. Bhuwan Bhatt, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. Sanjay Katyal, learned counsel holding brief 
of Mr. Vikas Pande, learned counsel for the 
respondents. 
3. We have heard learned counsel for the petitioner 
and learned counsel for the respondents. 
4. It is the case of the petitioner that he is suffering 
from 50% locomotor disability and that his daughter is 
pursuing her education in 10th standard and is preparing 
to face the board examination and that he is a single 
parent and, in that view, the transfer to Coimbatore, 
several thousand kilometres away, would not only 
seriously impair the academic career of his daughter, 
but would also cause personal hardship to the petitioner.
5. We have queried the learned counsel for the 
respondents with regard to the claim of the petitioner 
being affected by locomotor disease and he, being a 
single parent, and his daughter is pursuing the 10th
standard course. Learned counsel for the respondents 
would affirm these facts. 
6. The order of transfer for reasons recorded is in the 
middle of the academic year and admitted fact is that he 
is a single parent and the petitioner is forced to shift his 
residence to Coimbatore, it can be presumed that the 
same would seriously impair the educational prospects 
of the minor child. 7. In that view, we have suggested to the 
respondents to consider placing him in any post nearby 
office, till the minor child completes her academic 
career and completes her board examinations. 
8. List this case on 18.08.2025.
