WPSB No.323 of 2025
HON’BLE G. NARENDAR, C.J.
HON’BLE ASHISH NAITHANI, J.
Mr. Bhuwan Bhatt, learned counsel for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. V.B.S. Negi, learned Senior Counsel assisted by 
Ms. Azmeen Wason, learned counsel for the respondents.
3. We have interacted with the petitioner. It is 
apparent that the petitioner cannot even stand on his
own. His legs are deformed and he requires support to 
stand. We have also observed his conduct. He does not 
come out as an aggressive person. It is not in dispute 
that his daughter is pursuing 10th standard. The 
demeanour and the physical deformity create a doubt 
regarding the truthfulness of the complaints made 
against the petitioner. 
4. On a query, the petitioner has submitted that he 
was put in-charge of regularizing biometrics attendance;
he would even submit that officers have grudge against 
him because he was strict in implementation of the same.
5. Be that as it may, we direct the petitioner to array 
all the complainants as party respondents in the instant 
petition.6. The nature of complaints causes some amount of 
disbelief in us.
7. In view of the fact that petitioner’s daughter is 
pursuing 10th standard, which is crucial in the academic 
career of a student, we are inclined to stay the order of 
his transfer. 
8. It is also brought to the notice of the Court that the 
petitioner has not been paid his salary for the last several 
months. 
9. It is also not in dispute that the petitioner is a 
single parent and the father of girl child, who is pursuing 
her 10th standards. 
10. We, accordingly, direct respondent no.2 to release 
the arrears of salary of petitioner within two weeks. Till 
the disposal of the writ petition, the petitioner shall be 
permitted to retain the post from which he was 
transferred. The petitioner shall report to his duty and the 
respondent no.2 shall issue appropriate proceedings 
enabling the petitioner to resume his duties in the 
erstwhile post. 
11. List this matter on 09.10.2025.
(ASHISH NAITHANI, J.) (G. NARENDAR, C.J.)
