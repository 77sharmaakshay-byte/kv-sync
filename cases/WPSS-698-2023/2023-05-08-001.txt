COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 698 of 2023
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Heard Dr. M.S.Pal, learned Senior
Counsel, assisted by Mr. Apoorv 
Chauhan, Mr. Sachin, Mr. Kunwar 
Vikramaditya Shah, Mr. S.C.Gautam,
and Mr. Manoj Bhatt learned counsel for 
the petitioner and Mr. P.C. Bisht, learned 
Additional Chief Standing Counsel 
assisted by Mr. Virendra Singh Rawat, 
Brief Holder for the State/respondents. 
2. By the instant petition, the petitioner 
is praying for out of turn promotion to 
the post of ‘Head Constable’ under the 
policy of 2008 notified on 13.08.2008 on 
the ground that the petitioner is entitled
to get out of turn promotion pursuant to 
said policy of 2008, however, his claim 
has been rejected vide order dated 
04.02.2023 on the basis that the 
petitioner is not entitled to get out of 
turn promotion, as per “the Uttarakhand 
Police Skilled Player Out of Turn 
Promotion Proceeding Rules, 2016”.
3. The petitioner in the petition has 
enclosed Anexure-5 which is an order 
issued on 13th July, 2019 in order to 
demonstrate that even after framing of 
the Rules of 2016, the out of turn 
promotions were given to other 
incumbents though from the post of
Head Constable to Sub Inspector. 4. The only issue which is required to 
be decided is that what would be the 
impact of Rules of 2016 in respect of 
claim of the petitioner for out of turn 
promotion to the post of Head Constable 
accrued prior to the framing of 2016
Rule, particularly, pursuant to the policy 
of 2008. 
5. Mr. P.C.Bisht, learned Additional 
Chief Standing Counsel prays for three 
weeks’ time to file counter affidavit on 
behalf of the respondents. 
6. The time so prayed is granted. He 
may file counter affidavit within a period 
of three weeks from today. In reply 
thereto, Learned counsel for the 
petitioner is granted a week’s time to file 
rejoinder affidavit. 
7. List immediately after expiry of four 
weeks. 
8. In the counter affidavit the 
respondents should give a specific reply 
that how many incumbents were given 
out of turn promotion pursuant to 2008
policy even after framing of Rules of 
2016.
