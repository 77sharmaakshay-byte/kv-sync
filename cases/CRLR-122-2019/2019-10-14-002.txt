Mr. D.C.S. Rawat, Advocate for

This is an admitted revision, which is 
listed today on an application filed by the 
respondent for seeking a direction to the effect 
that the revisionist may be directed to deposit 
the penalty amount as imposed by the judgment 
of conviction and pronounced against revisionist 
against whom the proceedings under Section 
138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, which 
has concurrently concluded by the courts below 
resulting into modification of the conviction by 
the Appellate Court’s judgment dated 
19.02.2019. 
When the revision was admitted, the bail 
was granted to the revisionist. Since none 
appears to press the revision coupled with the 
fact that this Court cannot proceed to dismiss 
the revision in default, in such an eventuality, 
the enlargement on bail of the revisionist is 
cancelled. 
He is directed to surrender forthwith and 
to serve the sentence as imposed by the 
impugned judgment dated 19.02.2019.
