In pursuance to the previous order 
passed by this Court in Writ Petition No. 610 
of 2015 (S/S), on 24.04.2018, whereby, the 
petitioner has raised claim for grant of family 
pension, as a consequence of the death of her 
husband, who was working as Toll Moharir. 
The petitioner submits that the 
stipulation provided in the judgment dated 
24.04.2018, to entitle her for the grant of 
family pension, she has complied the same, 
but still, the respondents by virtue of the 
impugned order under challenge, had denied 
grant of family pension. 
The matter requires scrutiny. 
Let counter affidavit be filed by the 
respondents within a period of two weeks
from today. 
List this Writ Petition immediately after 
two weeks.
