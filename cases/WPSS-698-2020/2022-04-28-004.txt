Heard learned counsel for the 
parties.
Petitioner’s husband was a member 
of Palika Centralized Services, who died 
on 14.12.2001. Since Family Pension 
has not been sanctioned so far, 
therefore, this writ petition has been 
filed.
Learned counsel for Nagar Palika 
Parishad has referred to Rule 7 of Palika 
Non-Centralized Service Retirement 
Benefit Rules, 1984 in support of his 
contention that since petitioner’s 
husband had not deposited ` 5,000/- at 
the relevant time for becoming member 
of the Family Pension, therefore, 
petitioner cannot be given benefit under 
the said Rules. However, learned 
counsel for the petitioner submits that a 
sum of ` 5,000/- has to be deposited 
upon sanction of the Family Pension, 
therefore, petitioner cannot be denied 
Family Pension because her husband did 
not deposit ` 5,000/-.
There appears to be some 
substance in the contention made on 
behalf of the petitioner. 
In such view of the matter, list this 
matter on 11.05.2022, on which day, Executive Officer, Nagar Palika Parishad, 
Pithoragarh shall remain present before 
this Court alongwith entire record.
