Heard. 
Admit. 
Six weeks’ time is granted to learned 
counsel for the respondents to file counter 
affidavit.
List thereafter alongwith WPSS No. 1033 
of 2020. 
Also heard on interim relief application. 
Petitioner is aggrieved by the impugned 
orders dated 02.09.2020, 03.09.2020 and 
05.09.2020, whereby the services of the petitioner 
from the post of Lecturer (Chemistry) has been 
terminated by the respondents authorities. 
It is contended that the petitioner is working 
against a regular post in the institution. It is 
further contended that the impugned order is 
unsustainable as the same has been passed 
without affording opportunity of hearing to the 
petitioner. 
Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties, as an interim measure, it is provided that 
the effect and operation of impugned orders dated 
02.09.2020, 03.09.2020 and 05.09.2020 shall 
remain stayed until further orders of this Court. 
Respondents/competent authority are directed to 
permit the petitioner to continue on the post of 
Lecturer (Chemistry). 
Stay application (CLMA 7197 of 2020) 
stands disposed of accordingly.
