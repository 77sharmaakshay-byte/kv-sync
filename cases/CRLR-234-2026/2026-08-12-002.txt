2. All these Criminal Revisions arise out 
of three different complaints filed under 
Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments 
Act, 1881. The learned Trial Court convicted 
the revisionist in all the complaints, and the 
said judgments of conviction were affirmed 
by the learned Appellate Court. In all the 
three complaints, the learned Trial Court 
sentenced the revisionist to undergo simple 
imprisonment for a period of three months 
and imposed a total fine of ₹25,00,000/- in 
all the complaints. Out of the said amount, 
the revisionist has paid a sum of ₹11.55 
lakh to the complainant. 
3. The revisionist appeared before a 
Coordinate Bench of this Court on 
07.04.2026. On the undertaking given by 
the learned counsel for the revisionist that
the revisionist was ready and willing to pay 
the remaining amount of ₹13,00,000/-
within a period of four months, Court 
allowed the exemption application filed by the revisionist and exempted him from 
surrendering before the court concerned. 
4. Today, learned counsel for the 
revisionist submitted that despite the lapse 
of the aforesaid period of four months, the 
revisionist has neither complied with the 
undertaking given before this Court nor paid 
the remaining amount of ₹13,00,000/- to 
the complainant. Learned counsel further 
submitted that despite his advice to the 
revisionist to honour the undertaking given 
before the Court, the revisionist has failed to 
do so. 
5. Considering the facts and 
circumstances of the case, the learned 
Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Kashipur, District Udham Singh Nagar, is 
directed to issue non-bailable warrants 
against the revisionist in Complaint Case 
Nos. 823 of 2017, 959 of 2017 and 16 of 
2017, “Deepa Bisht vs. Rahul Dhiman”, and 
ensure compliance of this order forthwith. 
6. List these Criminal Revisions on 
16.09.2026.
7. Registry is directed to communicate 
this order to the concerned Court forthwith.
