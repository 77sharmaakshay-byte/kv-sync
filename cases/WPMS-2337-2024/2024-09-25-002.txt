25.09.2024 Recall Application IA No.3/2024
In
WPMS No.2337 of 2024
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Mr. Arvind Kumar Sharma, Advocate for 
the petitioners.
2. Mr. Bilal Ahmed and Mr. Mohd. 
Alauddin, Advocates for the 
respondents/applicants.
3. Learned counsel for the respondent no.1 
would submit that he has moved the instant 
recall application seeking recall of stay order 
dated 30.08.2024 granted by this Court. 
4. He would further submit that the 
petitioners have not made the concerned Gram 
Sabha as party respondent in the present 
petition and has got mutation of 15 bighas of 
land on the basis of sale deed of 1.5 bighas of 
land and on the basis of same petitioners are 
creating third party interest over the said land. 
5. At this stage, learned counsel for the 
petitioners would submit that on his oral request 
he may be permitted to implead the concerned 
Gram Sabha as party respondent in the present 
petition, if it is desirable and necessary. He 
seeks and is granted one week time to file 
amended memo of parties.
6. Perusal of the Recall Application and the 
affidavit accompanying the same would reveal 
that the affidavit is filed by the learned counsel 
for the respondent no.1 and the affidavit of the 
deponent Abdul Gaffar (at page 85) does not mention that the averments made in what 
application are subscribed to the true and best 
knowledge by the deponent i.e. respondent 
no.1.
7. In the understanding of this Court, the 
affidavit should clearly mention the facts and 
should make a solemn declaration that the 
information contained therein is true and correct 
to the best of his personal knowledge and if the 
affidavit is being filed in support of any 
application then that application should also be 
mentioned in the affidavit.
8. Learned counsel for respondent no.1 prays 
for some time to place the Rules under which 
this practice is being followed that the 
application is filed with signatures of the 
Advocate and the signatures of the party are 
taken at the bottom of the application.
9. Learned counsel for the petitioners prays 
for and is granted four weeks time to file 
objections to the Recall application.
10. List on 05.11.2024.
