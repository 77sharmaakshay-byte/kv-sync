COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPCRL No. 686 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. V.K. Gemini, learned Deputy Advocate 
General with Mr. V.S. Pal, learned A.G.A. and Mr. 
Akshay Latwal, learned Brief Holder for the 
State/respondent nos. 1 and 2.
3. This is a case in which the present petitioner is 
praying for quashing of the First Information Report 
dated 07.03.2024 P.S. Gadarpur, District Udham Singh 
Nagar bearing FIR No. 0080 of 2024, whereby, the 
petitioner as well as others have been implicated for the 
offences punishable under Sections 323, 504 and 506 of 
IPC for an incident, which was alleged to be happened on 
05.01.2024 at 6:25 PM outside the house of Firasat Ali-the 
present petitioner.
4. The petitioner is challenging this impugned FIR on 
the ground that in respect of the same incident, which 
was happened on 05.01.2024, already a First Information 
Report was lodged by the petitioner on 17.01.2024 at the 
same police station i.e. P.S. Gadarpur bearing FIR No. 
0019 of 2024, wherein, the time of the incident is shown as 
6:30 PM and the place is within the house of complainant 
Firasat Ali-the present petitioner.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the
impugned FIR is nothing but counter blast to the FIR
lodged by the petitioner on 17.01.2024 at P.S. Gadarpur as 
the same pertains to the same incident regarding which 
the impugned FIR has been lodged.
6. Apart from this, learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that on the instructions of the Vice Chairman of 
the Minority Commission the Chief Medical Officer 
submitted a supplementary injury report of Jane Alam 
and Iqbal Ahmed, who is the complainant of the 
impugned FIR, and pursuant to the instruction of the 
Vice Chairman of the Minority Commission, the Chief 
Medical Officer submitted a supplementary inquiry
report to the Vice Chairman of the Minority Commission by letter dated 25.05.2024, which is enclosed as 
Annexure-8.
7. Apart from this, learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the brother and sister-in-law of the 
complainant of the impugned FIR Iqbal Ali are serving in 
the Police Department at Nainital, which is in district 
Nainital. He submits that on the interference of the 
Minority Commission and on the instigation of the 
brother and sister-in-law, who are serving in district 
Nainital, the investigation of the impugned FIR has been 
transferred to the District Nainital, which is reflected 
from page-34, and, thereafter, the petitioner submitted a 
representation to the Deputy Inspector General of Police 
of Kumaon Region on 20.05.2024, which is also enclosed 
as Annexure-6.
8. Learned counsel for the petitioner further submits 
that the investigation of the impugned FIR are being 
interfered by the Vice Chairman of the Minority 
Commission as well as the relatives of the complainant, 
who are serving in police department.
9. Learned A.G.A. Mr. V.S. Pal informs one important 
fact before this court that in respect of the FIR lodged by 
the petitioner on 17.01.2024 at P.S. Gadarpur, bearing FIR 
No. 0019 of 2024 already a charge-sheet has been filed 
against four persons on 26.04.2024.
10. Learned A.G.A. Mr. V.S. Pal prays for three days’
time to get appropriate instruction in the matter since on 
the next date he will address to the court why two FIRs 
has been registered in respect of one incident.
11. Put up this matter on 03.07.2024 as fresh.
12. A free copy of this order be supplied to the learned 
A.G.A. Mr. V.S. Pal in order to enable him to get complete 
instructions in the matter.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
