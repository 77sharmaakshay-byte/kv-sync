The appellant seeks leave to appeal 
against the order of acquittal recorded on 
11.10.2021 in Complaint Case No. 1806 
of 2017, Rajpal Singh Vs. Praveen Kumar.
Heard.
Learned counsel for the appellant 
would submit that the acquittal has been 
recorded mainly on the ground that the 
cheques were misused after having been 
stolen.
Having considered, this Court is of 
the view that in the instant case, leave is 
to be granted. Therefore, the leave to 
appeal IA No. 112 of 2021 is allowed.
Leave granted.
