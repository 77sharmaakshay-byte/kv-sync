Almost an identical issue pertaining to the 
same process of selection which was initiated in 
pursuance to the advertisement of 02.03.2019, has 
been a subject matter of challenge in a writ petition 
being Writ Petition (S/S) No.1325 of 2021, in 
which this Court has already interfered and passed a 
conditional interim order on 20.10.2021.
The petitioner in the present case, has 
submitted that in accordance with Clause 10 and as 
per the qualification, which was possessed by him, 
he ought to have been included in the select list, 
which has been published by the respondents on 
29.09.2021 because he contends that the year of 
qualification of the petitioner since being 2000, 
hence as per the amended Rule 14(5) his name 
ought to have been considered for appointment. But 
at this belated stage, there cannot be any interim 
order, as such because that will run contrary to the 
interim order already passed by me in the earlier 
writ petition.
Connect this writ petition with the pending 
Writ Petition (S/S) No.1325 of 2021 and in the 
meantime the respondents to the present writ 
petition may file their counter affidavit within a 
period of three weeks.
List immediately after three weeks. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.) 30.11.2021
