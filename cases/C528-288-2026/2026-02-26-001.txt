C-528 No. 288 of 2026
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Kailash Chandra, learned counsel for 
the applicants.
2. Mr. Prabhat Kandpal, learned Brief Holder 
for the State.
3. In this case, an FIR was lodged by 
respondent no. 2 under Sections 498-A, 323, 
504, 506 and Section 3/4 of Dowry Prohibition 
Act, 1961.
4. Learned counsel for the applicants would 
submit that marriage was solemnized between 
applicant no. 1 and respondent no. 2 on 
18.04.2024 and after only a month, applicant 
no. 2 left her matrimonial home and is living 
presently in Haldwani and pursuing her PhD 
course. Respondent no. 2 had earlier filed a 
criminal case No. 363 of 2025 on 29.01.2025 
under Sections 323, 504, 506, 498-A of IPC and 
Section 3/4 of Dowry Prohibition Act. The 
applicants have approached this Court and this 
Court vide its order dated 07.07.2025 had 
stayed the further proceedings of the case. It is 
further submitted that respondent no. 2 had 
also filed a case under Section 144 of BNSS 
before the court of Principal Judge, Almora, 
Camp Ranikhet. The matter is pending and the 
applicant no. 1 has put in his appearance and is 
pursuing his case. Respondent no. 2 again filed 
a Misc. Criminal Case No. 51 of 2025 under 
Section 12 of the Protection of Women from 
Domestic Violence Act, before the Court of 
Judicial Magistrate, Dwarahat. He submits that 
all these cases have been filed just to victimize 
and harass the applicants. Applicant no. 1 is 
husband, applicant no. 2 is father-in-law, applicant no. 3 is mother-in-law, applicant no. 4 
is brother-in-law and applicant no. 5 is sister-in￾law of respondent no. 2. All the family members 
have been implicated in the case.
5. Having heard the submission, this Court is 
of the view that this matter definitely requires 
deliberation.
6. Issue notice to respondent no. 2, 
returnable at an early date. Steps to be taken 
within a week.
7. List after service report is received.
8. In the meantime, counter-affidavit(s), if 
any, be filed by the respondents.
9. Having heard submission of learned 
counsel for the parties and also considering the 
fact that since misc. case is pending before the 
Court of Principal Judge, Family Court, Camp 
Ranikhet and applicant is pursuing this case,
this Court, prima facie, is of the opinion and is 
satisfied that in the interest of justice, further 
proceedings of Criminal Case No. 51 of 2025, 
Minakshi Vs. Girish Upreti and Others, under 
Section 12 of Protection of Women from 
Domestic Violence Act, pending in the court of 
learned Judicial Magistrate, Dwarahat, District 
Almora as well as further proceedings of 
Criminal Misc. Case No. 96 of 2024, pending in 
the court of Judge, Family Court, shall remain 
stayed qua the applicants till the next date of 
listing.
