4. By the instant petition, the petitioner is praying
for the following reliefs:
“i. Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of
certiorari quashing the impugned order dated 12.08.2026
(Contained as Annexure No.12, Page No. 31 of this writ
petition, Issued by the Respondent No.2) Executive Officer,
Nagar Palika Parisad Gopeshwar Chamoli.
ii. Issue a writ, order or direction in the nature of
mandamus commanding the respondents to pay the
petitioners minimum of pay scale as being paid to the
regular employee working on the same cadres.
iii. Issue writ rule or direction in the nature of
mandamus commanding and directing to the respondents to
regularize the services of the petitioners on Class IV in the
respondent department.”
5. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the
petitioner that the relief, as sought primarily, is on the
ground that the issue as raised in the instant petition,
has already been decided by the Division Bench of this
Court in bunch of Special Appeals, leading one SPA
No. 963 of 2017, decided on 18.12.2020, which was
affirmed by the Hon’ble Supreme Court, however, therepresentation of the petitioner has been rejected by
the respondent ignoring the judgments of Division
Bench as well as Hon’ble Supreme Court.
6. Let the State Counsel may get instructions in the
matter whether the petitioner is entitled for the relief
in terms of the order of the Division Bench or not.
