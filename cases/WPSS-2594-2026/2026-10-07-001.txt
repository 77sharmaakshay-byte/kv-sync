Mr. Sandeep Tiwari, learned counsel for 
petitioners.
2. Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned Brief Holder for the

3. Mr. Pankaj Miglani, learned counsel for 
respondent No.3-Commission.
4. By means of this writ petition, petitioners 
have challenged requisition/proposal dated 
30.07.2026 (Annexure No.4 to the writ petition), 
sent to respondent No.3-Public Service 
Commission for supplying vacancy on the post 
of Junior Engineers in respondent-P.W.D. 
department.
5. Learned counsel for petitioners contends 
that petitioners have been working as 
contractual Junior Engineers with respondent￾P.W.D. for the last more than ten years; when 
their services as contractual engagement were 
terminated, various writ petitions were filed 
before this Court challenging the illegal and 
arbitrary action on part of the State.
6. The said bunch of writ petition came to be 
decided by this Court vide judgment and order 
dated 10.08.2026 in WPSS No.321 of 2026, 
Prasoon Nautiyal Vs. State of Uttarakhand and 
others and batch, and those writ petitions were 
disposed of by directing respondents to 
undertake process of regularization of 
petitioners of those writ petitions ignoring earlier 
rejection of their claim for regularization.7. Now without protecting petitioners, State 
has send requisition against same vacancies on 
which petitioners are working as contractual 
Junior Engineers for the last more than ten 
years. If the vacancies would be supplied, 
petitioners would be thrown out of their job.
8. It is contended by learned counsel for 
petitioners that petitioners have been working 
against sanctioned post and they are holder of 
requisite qualification for Junior Engineer with 
P.W.D. and further they have gained experience 
of more than ten years which is no doubt 
beneficial for discharge of their duties with the 
respondent-Department.
9. Per contra, learned Sate Counsel submits
that requisition was issued for fresh 
appointment as petitioners’ case is not covered 
under the Daily Wage, Work-Charged, Contract, 
Fixed-Pay, Part-Time and Ad-hoc Employees 
Regularization (Amendment) Rules, 2025 
promulgated on 05.12.2025 as they have not
completed ten years of service on 04.12.2018.
10. He also submits that the order passed by 
this Court in the aforesaid writ petitions may be 
challenged by State by filing special appeals.
11. Respondent(s) may file counter affidavit(s) 
within four weeks.
12. Put up on 27.11.2026 along with WPSS 
No.2449 of 2026.
13. In the meantime, the impugned requisition 
dated 30.07.2026 shall remain stayed.
