Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
Mr. Vinay Kumar, learned counsel for 
the petitioners.
2. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State.
3. By the instant petition, the petitioners 
are praying for their re-designation from the 
post of Constable (M) to Assistant Sub 
Inspector (M) from the date of promotion to 
the post of Constable (M) on the ground that 
similar designation has been given to the 
direct recruitees pursuant to the Government 
Order dated 30.11.2025. In addition to this,
the petitioners are also praying for re-fixation 
of their salary on the basis of the pay scale of 
Rs.5200-20200/- with Grade pay of Rs.2800/-
attached to the post of ASI (M) from the date 
of their promotion to the post of Constable 
(M). 
4. The relief, as sought infact, is in the 
light of the judgment rendered by the 
Coordinate Bench dated 29.09.2021 passed in 
WPSS No.630 of 2019 in compliance thereof 
Government Order dated 09.11.2022, was 
also issued. 
5. On perusal of the record, it reveals that 
the petitioners are praying for their re-designation on the post of ASI (M) in terms 
of the order of the writ court dated 29.09.2021 
passed in WPSS No.630 of 2019 as well as in 
terms of the Government Order dated 
09.11.2022. 
6. The counter affidavit has been filed by 
the respondents and there is no denial of the 
order of the Coordinate Bench dated 
29.09.2021 and the Government Order dated 
09.11.2022. The statement, as made in the 
counter affidavit, infact has already been 
dealt with by the Coordinate Bench while 
rendering the judgment on 29.09.2021 passed 
in WPSS No.630 of 2019. The denial of the 
benefit, as prayed for in the instant petition is 
infact appears to be in flagrant violation of 
the order of the writ court dated 29.09.2021 
passed in WPSS No.630 of 2019, which infact 
has been complied while issuing the 
Government order dated 09.11.2022.
7. Let Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned 
Standing Counsel may get the instructions
from the respondent no.2 why the 
Government Order dated 09.11.2022 is not 
being implemented uniformly in letter and 
spirit.
8. List on 28.10.2026.
