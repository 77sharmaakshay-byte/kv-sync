5. In this writ petition, petitioners have made 
prayer for adding three persons as petitioner Nos. 
230, 231 and 232. This prayer, however, is opposed 
by learned counsel for the respondents who contend that no one can be added as petitioner after seven 
years of filing of the writ petition.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioners, however, 
submits that Impleadment Application was filed in 
2022.
7. Be that as it may, this Court is not inclined to 
allow the prayer for impleadment at this belated 
stage. The persons to whom petitioners want to be 
added in this writ petition may file separate writ 
petition, if so advised.
8. Impleadment Application (IA/13003/2022) is 
rejected.
