Mr. Nikhil Singhal, Advocate for

2. This money withdrawal application has 
been filed by claimants for withdrawal of the 
amount of compensation deposited by the 
Insurance Company before learned Tribunal. 
3. Learned Tribunal had awarded a sum of 
₹35,49,235/- as compensation to the claimants. 
Learned counsel for the appellant submits that 
the appellant has deposited 50% of the 
awarded amount with the concerned tribunal, in 
terms of the order passed by this Court.
4. Having regard to the facts and 
circumstances of the case, money withdrawal 
application is allowed. Learned Tribunal is 
directed to release 50% of the deposited 
amount in favour of claimants proportionately, 
without asking any security. The remaining 
amount shall be invested in an interest bearing 
scheme of a nationalized bank.
