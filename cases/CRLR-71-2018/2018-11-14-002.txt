This is an admitted Criminal Revision 
where the revisionist has challenged the 
impugned order dated 14.02.2018 passed by the 
Additional Sessions Judge in Appeal No. 38 of 
2017, whereby he has affirmed the order passed 
by the Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Laksar, Haridwar in Miscellaneous Case No. 301 
of 2012, Smt. Manju Vs. Veer Singh and another, 
by virtue of which the learned court below had 
awarded an interim maintenance to the 
respondent No. 2 under Section 23 (1) of the 
Protection of Women from Domestic Violence 
Act, 2005 at the rate of Rs. 2,000/- per month.
The revision was admitted on 16.03.2018 
subject to the condition that if revisionist by way 
of interim measure pays a sum of Rs. 1,000/- per 
month to the respondent no. 2 by 10th of each 
month. The effect and operation of the impugned 
order dated 01.02.2017 was directed to be kept in 
abeyance.
A statement has been made by the learned 
counsel for respondent no. 2, that in compliance 
of the interim order dated 16.03.2018, no amount 
has been remitted by the revisionist to respondent 
No. 2 thus she is suffering financial crises. In 
defense response to it, the learned counsel for the 
revisionist submits that the financial conditions of 
revisionist is not so sound to comply with the 
direction dated 16.03.2018 i.e. remittance of Rs. 
1,000/- per month as maintenance to the respondent No. 2. This will amount that the order 
dated 16.03.2018 stands not complied with. 
In that eventuality, the interim order dated 
16.03.2018 being a conditional interim order and 
since the condition itself has not been complied 
with, it will cease to subsist in the eyes of law, 
hence would stand vacated.
The Miscellaneous Application (IA No. 
9638 of 2018) as preferred by the revisionist for 
taking the accompanying affidavit on record is 
allowed and the same is taken on record.
List this matter on 03.12.2018 for final 
disposal.
