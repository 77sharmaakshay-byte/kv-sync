COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
C-528 No. 933 of 2026
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. B.D. Pande and Ms. Shweta Mehra, 
learned counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Prabhat Kandpal, learned A.G.A. for 
the State.
3. The present criminal misc. application is 
filed with a prayer to quash and set-aside the 
chargesheet, summoning order as well as the 
entire proceedings of Criminal Case No. 298 of 
2025, pending in the court of learned ACJM, 
Haldwani, District Nainital.
4. In this case, an FIR was lodged by the 
complainant alleging therein that power of 
attorney was executed by her father in favour 
of one Sumit Rawat, but, Sumit Rawat in 
connivance with the applicant sold extra land
to the applicant. Besides this, it is alleged that 
the possession of the land too has been given 
in the other plot instead of the plot in which 
power of attorney was granted.
5. Learned counsel for the applicant would 
submit that even if all allegations as 
mentioned in the FIR are taken on their face 
value, then also, it is purely a civil dispute. It 
is further submitted that applicant already 
filed a suit for injunction against the 
complainant and other persons and 
respondent no. 2 have filed suit for 
cancellation of the sale deed. It is also 
submitted that the sale deed was executed in 
the year 2019 and thereafter mutation have 
been effected in his name.
6. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the record.
7. This Court finds some substance in the 
submission made by learned counsel for the 
applicant and having heard the submission, 
under the facts and circumstances of the case, 
this Court is of the view that this matter 
definitely requires deliberation.
8. Issue notice to respondent no. 2, 
returnable within three weeks. Steps to be 
taken within a week.
9. List after service report is received.
10. In the meantime, counter-affidavit(s), if 
any, be filed by the respondents.
11. After perusing the records and having 
heard learned counsel for the parties, under 
the facts and circumstances of the case and 
also considering the fact that already civil 
suits are pending in regard to injunction and 
consideration of the sale deed and as such the 
dispute is purely civil in nature which has 
been given a criminal colour, this Court, prima 
facie, is of the opinion and is satisfied that in 
the interest of justice, further proceedings of 
Criminal Case No. 298 of 2025, pending in the 
court of learned ACJM, Haldwani, District 
Nainital, shall remain stayed qua the applicant 
till the next date of listing.
 (Alok Mahra J.) 07.05.2026
