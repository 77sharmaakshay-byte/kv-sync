Mr. Anil Bisht, S.C., for the State. 
Appellant has laid challenge to the 
judgment dated 25.7.2018 rendered by a 
learned Single Judge of this Court in WPSS 
No.1236 of 2017. 
Appellant’s father was a daily wager. 
Thus, his case was not covered under the 
definition of government employee as 
provided in U.P. Recruitment of Dependents 
of the Government Servants Dying in 
Harness Rules, 1974. 
Accordingly, there is no illegality or 
perversity in the judgment, under challenge. 
The appeal fails and the same is hereby 
dismissed. 
Pending application, if any, stands 
disposed of.
