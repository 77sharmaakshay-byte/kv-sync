Holder for the State of Uttarakhand. 
2. The present applicant is seeking 
anticipatory bail in connection with F.I.R. 
dated 17.09.2026, registered as Case Crime 
No. 154 of 2026, for the offences punishable 
under Sections 3, 5 and 11 of the 
Uttarakhand Protection of Cow Progeny Act, 
2007, at Police Station Jhabrera, District 
Haridwar.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that the applicant has been falsely 
implicated in the present case and has no 
concern with the alleged recovery or the 
commission of the alleged offence. It is 
further submitted that the applicant was not 
apprehended at the spot and has been 
implicated solely on the basis of information 
allegedly furnished by an informer 
(mukhbir), apart from which no other 
evidence is available against him. Learned 
counsel further submits that no 
independent witness was associated with 
the alleged recovery, which raises serious 
doubts regarding the prosecution version, 
particularly when the place of the alleged 
incident is situated in a densely populated 
area.4. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
5. This Court finds some substance in the 
submissions advanced by learned counsel 
for the applicant. Having regard to the facts 
and circumstances of the case, this Court is 
of the view that the aforesaid aspects require 
deeper scrutiny.
6. Let the respondent-State file its 
counter-affidavit within three weeks.
7. List thereafter.
8. In the meantime, as an interim 
measure and without expressing any 
opinion on the merits of the case, in the 
event of arrest, the applicant shall be 
released on interim anticipatory bail on 
furnishing a personal bond with two reliable 
sureties, each in the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the Arresting Officer/Trial 
Court, subject to the following conditions:
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation and make himself 
available for interrogation as and when 
required by the Investigating Officer.
(ii) The applicant shall not, directly or 
indirectly, approach, influence, threaten 
or induce any witness in any manner 
whatsoever.
