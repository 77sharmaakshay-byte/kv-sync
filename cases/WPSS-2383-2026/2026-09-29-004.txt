4. By means of present writ petition, 
petitioners have challenged the order dated 
21.05.2026 passed by Board of Revenue 
(Annexure No.5 to writ petition), whereby, 
District Magistrate, District Udham Singh Nagar, 
was directed to submit the seniority report 
contrary to the orders passed by learned Public 
Service Tribunal dated 10.03.2025.
5. The contention of the petitioners in short is 
that final seniority list of Lekhpal appointed in 
the District Udham Singh Nagar was 
promulgated in 2008. Subsequently, tentative 
seniority list was issued by the District 
Magistrate in the year 2019, against which some 
objections were filed by petitioners. But their 
objections were rejected and final seniority list 
was issued on 08.08.2022. The said seniority list 
was challenged by petitioners by filing a claim 
petition No.30/NB/DB/2026 Rajiv Kumar and 
Others Vs. State of Uttarakhand and Others.
6. The said claim petition was decided by the learned Tribunal vide judgment and order dated 
10.03.2025, whereby, the final seniority list 
dated 08.08.2022 was quashed and respondents 
were directed to redraw the seniority list of 
petitioners and private respondents keeping the 
seniority decided in 2008 intact, within three 
months from the date of presentation of certified 
copy of judgment.
7. Pursuant to aforesaid judgment, the 
seniority list was redrawn by the District 
Magistrate in May, 2025. Now, by the impugned 
order, Board of Revenue wants to disturb the 
seniority list, which has been redrawn after 
order passed by learned Tribunal.
8. It is contended by learned counsel for 
petitioners that said order passed by learned 
Tribunal has not been challenged and the order 
impugned is totally illegal and arbitrary and 
against the order passed by learned Tribunal.
9. At this juncture, learned State Counsel 
submitted that now the said judgment passed in 
claim petition is under challenge at the hands of 
private respondents before the Division Bench of 
this Court in WPSB No.608 of 2026 Mukesh 
Kumar and Others vs. State of Uttarakhand and 
others, and the same is listed today before the 
Division Bench of this Court.
10. In such view of the matter, put up as fresh 
on 03.11.2026.
11. Meanwhile, learned counsel for petitioners 
may file objection to the impleadment 
application (IA/2/2026).
