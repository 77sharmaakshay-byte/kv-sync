4. Earlier there was seniority list of 2008 
and the dispute was raised with regard to 
the inter se seniority before the Public 
Service Tribunal and the claim petition was 
allowed by the Tribunal on 10.03.2025. The 
operative portion of the said order is being 
reproduced herein as under:-
“The claim of the petition is hereby 
allowed. The impugned seniority list dated 
08.08.202 is hereby quashed. The 
respondents are directed to redraw the 
seniority list of the petitioners and the private 
respondents keeping the seniority decided in 
2008 intact, within three months from the 
date of presentation of certified copy of this 
judgment. No order as to costs.”
5. Pursuant to the order of the Tribunal , 
the senior list was redraw on 24.05.2025 
and forwarded to the Board of Revenue for 
promotion to the post of Kanoongo, however,
the Board of Revenue make certain comments on the seniority list and the 
entire promotion to the post of Kanoongo is 
put on hold.
6. Now, the question is if the seniority list 
was redraw pursuant to the order of Public 
Service Tribunal how the Board of Revenue
can put on hold the entire promotion 
exercise.
7. Let the learned State Counsel may get 
instructions from the Deputy Revenue 
Commissioner of the Board of Revenue.
8. List this matter on 05.11.2026. 
9. In the meantime, Mr. Kothari, learned 
counsel for the petitioner may file rejoinder 
affidavit or to address on this issue.
