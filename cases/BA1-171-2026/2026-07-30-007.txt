Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Lalit Sharma (through V.C.) and Mr. Bharat 
Chaudhary, learned counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Sandeep Sharma and Mrs. Rangoli Purohit, 
learned A.G.A. for the State.
3. Mr. Lalit Miglani, learned counsel for the 
complainant.
4. The applicant ‘Ayush Kumar @ Sikandar’ is 
praying for bail in reference to First Information 
Report dated 03.06.2025 P.S. Prem Nagar, District 
Dehradun lodged by one Abhishek Barthwal 
implicating one Azhar Tyagi and one unknown person 
for the offences punishable under sections 103(1) r/w 
section 3(5) of BNS, 2023. Arising out of this FIR the 
applicant is also implicated in Case Crime No. 177 of 
2025 under section 2/3 of U.P. Gangsters and Anti￾Social Activities (Prevention) Act, 1986.
After this incident in one another incident of FIR 
No. 443 of 2025 the applicant is implicated for the 
offences punishable under sections 109 and 3(5) of 
BNS, 2023, in which he has already been enlarged on 
bail.5. As per the prosecution, at the time of incident 
one Azhar Tyagi came in motorcycle along with 
unknown person caused fire due to which Rohit Negi 
died (deceased). The present applicant is pursuing 
study in BCA in DBIT and living in P.G. Azhar Tyagi 
is also living in the same P.G. where the present 
applicant was living and he is also pursuing study but 
in different institution.
6. As per learned counsel, both the applicant and 
Azhar Tyagi known to each other since both of them 
were studying in different institution but living in one 
P.G. As per the prosecution, before the incident there 
was some hot alteration in between Azhar Tyagi and 
one Ms Anya Khan in reference to the deceased Rohit 
Negi and thereafter the incident was occurred.
7. It is submitted that as per the prosecution 
including the FIR Azhar Tyagi is the main assailant 
and the applicant has been implicated since he was 
driving the motorcycle.
8. It is further argued that the applicant is not 
named in the FIR and one another person is shown as 
an unknown person, which itself shows that the 
complainant does know about the applicant who is he.
9. It is argued that the applicant has been 
implicated only because of the reason that he was 
driving motorcycle and behind him Azhar Tyagi was 
sitting and except that there is no any such evidence 
even with regard to establishing common intention 
and motive.
10. Twice the time was given to the learned State 
counsel to file objection but the same has not been 
filed.
11. As a last opportunity, seven days’ time is given 
to the State to file their objection.
12. Put up this matter on 11.08.2026.
13. In the counter affidavit the I.O. shall disclose 
what material he has collected for establishing the 
common intention and motive in reference to the 
present accused Ayush Kumar. In the counter affidavit 
the I.O. shall also enclose the site plan.
