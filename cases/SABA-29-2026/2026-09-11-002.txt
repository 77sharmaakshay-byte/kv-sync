4. The present second anticipatory bail 
application has been preferred by the 
applicant seeking anticipatory bail in 
connection with FIR No. 0153 of 2026, under 
Sections 137(2), 351(3) and Section 352 of 
BNS, Sections 64 and 87 of BNS and Sections
5 and 6 of POCSO Act, registered at Police 
Station-Kunda, District Udham Singh Nagar.
5. Learned counsel for the applicant would 
submit that during investigation, Sections 5 
and 6 of POCSO Act were added in the case;
that the applicant and the victim were in 
relationship for last more than three years; 
that the victim is major and her date of birth 
is 05.03.2008, as reflected from the Aadhar 
Card of the victim.6. State was directed to file objection to the 
anticipatory bail application. Today, on 
written instructions, learned State Counsel 
submits that the affidavit has been prepared 
and has been sent for swearing. As such, it 
could not be filed today, but, on the basis of 
the written instructions supplied by the 
Investigating Officer, learned State Counsel 
submits that the victim in her statement 
under Section 180 of BNSS, has submitted 
that she is major aged about 15 years.
7. On this basis, learned counsel for the 
applicant submits that the age as reflected in 
the Aadhar Card of victim is different from the 
age, as stated by the victim in her statement 
recorded under Section 180 of BNSS and also 
as prosecution has narrated in the FIR.
8. Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
9. The Court also went through the 
statement of the victim recorded under 
Section 180 of BNSS. Victim has further 
stated that she has gone alongwith the 
applicant on her own sweet will and there was 
no force or coercion. Hence, this Court finds 
some substance in the submission made by 
learned counsel for the applicant and having 
considered the submissions under the facts 
and circumstances of the case, this Court is of 
the view that all these aspects require deep 
scrutiny.
10. Let the respondent-State may file their 
counter affidavit within three weeks. Two 
weeks, thereafter, is granted to the applicant 
to file rejoinder affidavit. 
11. List thereafter.
12. In the meantime, as an interim measure, 
without expressing any opinion on the merit of the case, the applicant shall be released on 
an interim anticipatory bail, subject to 
furnishing a personal bond with two reliable 
sureties, each of the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the Arresting Officer/Trial 
Court subject to the following conditions:-
(i) The applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigation;
(ii) The applicant shall not approach any 
witness in any manner, whatsoever.
