COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
15.09.2023 ABA No.821 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Present Application has been filed 
under Section 438 of the Code of 
Criminal Procedure, 1973 for grant of 
Anticipatory Bail under Section 306 and 
Section 354 C of the Indian Penal Code, 
1860 in connection with the Case Crime 
No.29 of 2021, registered at police 
station Kotwali Pauri, District Pauri 
Garhwal.
2. Heard Mr. Navnish Negi, learned 
counsel for applicant and Mr. Rakesh 
Negi, learned Brief Holder for State. 
3. Mr. Navnish Negi, Advocate, 
contended that the applicant was not 
named in the First Information Report 
and the name of the applicant is not 
mentioned in the suicide note. 
4. Mr. Rakesh Negi, Brief Holder, has
conceded that the name of the applicant 
is not mentioned in the suicide note. 
However, he has sought three weeks’ 
time to file objection(s) to the 
Anticipatory Bail Application. 
5. List on 13.10.2023 for hearing on 
the Anticipatory Bail Application. 
6. In the facts and circumstances of 
the case, it is directed that in the event 
of arrest, applicant Anil Pant shall be
released on Interim Anticipatory Bail on 
furnishing his personal bond of Rs. 
30,000/- and two reliable sureties, each in the like amount, to the satisfaction of 
the Investigating Officer/Arresting Officer 
on the following conditions:- 
(i) Applicant shall not directly or 
indirectly make any inducement, 
threat or promise to any person, 
acquainted with the facts of this 
case; 
(ii) Applicant shall not leave the 
country without previous permission 
of this Court.
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
