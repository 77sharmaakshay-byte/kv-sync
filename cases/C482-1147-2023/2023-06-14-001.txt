complaint proceedings drawn by the 
complainant/respondent which was emanating from 
the matrimonial discord. Subsequent to the

exclusively a private in nature, there is always 
possibility of making an effort for mediation.

taken by the applicants within a week from today. 
List after service on notice on the 
respondents, to enable the parties to appear in the 
mediation proceedings.
