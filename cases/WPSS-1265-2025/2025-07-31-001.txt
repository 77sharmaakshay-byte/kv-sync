 Heard on Stay Application (IA) No.1 of 
2025.
 It is the claim of the petitioner that he 
applied for the post of Assistant Teacher (L.T.) 
Computer Education in response to the 
advertisement issued by the respondent 
no.2/Uttarakhand Subordinate Services 
Selection Commission, Dehradun (“the 
Commission”). He qualified the Written 
Examination. He was awaiting for the 
documents verification, but no notice was was 
ever published. Suddenly, he came to know 
that by the impugned Office Notification dated 
14.07.2025, his candidature has been rejected 
on the ground of his non-appearance. It is the 
case of the petitioner that the notice was never 
published. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that the notice inviting the candidates for documents verification was never uploaded 
on the website of the Commission prior to 
10.06.2025. 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner submits 
that in the similarly situated cases, this Court 
has been pleased to pass interim orders. 
 This fact is admitted to the learned counsel 
for the Commission that this Court under the 
similar circumstances has passed interim 
orders directing the respondents to keep one 
post vacant.
 Having considered, as an interim 
measure, till the next date of listing this Court 
directs that one post of the Assistant Teacher 
(L.T.) Computer Education, on which, the 
petitioner applied, shall be kept vacant. 
 Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
