Admit the Second Appeal on the following 
substantial questions of law :-
(i) Whether the findings of courts below on 
issue Nos. 1 & 3 are based on misreading 
and misinterpretation of documents?
(ii) Whether the courts below are justified in 
holding that lease deed dated 05.04.2007 of 
plot No.309/2 executed by Zila Panchayat, 
Udham Singh Nagar in favour of Suresh 
Verma-respondent No.2 is valid?
(iii)Whether the suit filed by the appellant 
for declaration is barred by the limitation, 
since the appellant came to know about the 
lease deed dated 05.04.2007 only on 
06.02.2008?
(iv)Whether the courts below are justified in 
holding that the suit filed on behalf of the
appellant is barred under Order 23 Rule 1 
(4) and Order 2 Rule 2 of the Code of Civil 
Procedure, 1908?
