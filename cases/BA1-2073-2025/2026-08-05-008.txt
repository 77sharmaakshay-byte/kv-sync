Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Ms. Divya Jain, learned counsel for the applicant.
2. Mrs. Rangoli Purohit, learned A.G.A. for the 
State.
3. Mr. Piyush Garg, learned counsel for CBI.
4. Correction application has been moved for 
seeking certain correction with regard to the penal 
provisions in the order dated 29.07.2026, whereby, the applicant was granted interim bail for one month. 
5. It is submitted that the composite order pertains 
to four bail applications including the bail application 
No. 2073 of 2025, wherein, the applicant is 
chargesheeted also for the offences punishable under 
section 120B, 420, 467, 471 of IPC r/w section 21(3) of 
the Banning of Unregulated Deposits Schemes Act, 
2019 and Section 3 of the Uttarakhand Protection of 
Interest of depositors (in financial establishment) Act, 
2005, which in fact has been left out to be incorporated 
in the order dated 29.07.2026.
6. Mr. Piyush Garg, learned counsel for CBI, have 
no objection on this and submits that this correction is 
also required particularly in reference to Bail 
Application No. 2073 of 2025.
7. In such view of the matter, the correction 
application is allowed and order dated 29.07.2026 
whereby the applicant is granted interim bail and as 
such in reference to BA1 No. 2073 of 2025 relating to 
the penal provision i.e. sections 120B, 420, 467, 471 of 
IPC r/w section 21(3) of the Banning of Unregulated 
Deposits Schemes Act, 2019 and Section 3 of the 
Uttarakhand Protection of Interest of depositors (in 
financial establishment) Act, 2005 as inserted in the 
order dated 29.07.2026 at the bottom of paragraph no. 
4. 
8. This order will be treated as part of the order 
dated 29.07.2026.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
