4. Present applicant Ramnath Gupta, S/o 
Late Shri Rajnarayan Gupta is implicated in 
four FIRs bearing FIR No.0049 of 2025, FIR 
No.0057 of 2024, FIR No.009 of 2025 and FIR 
No.0018 of 2025 in which present applicant has been charge sheeted for the offences 
punishable under Sections 111(2), 111(3), 
318(4), 336(3), 338, 340(2), 61(2) of BNS, 2023 
and Section 3 & 21(3) of The Banning of 
Unregulated Deposits Schemes Act 2019 and 
Section 3 of The Uttarakhand Protection of 
Interest of Depositors (in financial 
establishment) Act, 2005 and under Section 
111, 111(2)(b), 111(3), 318(4), 61(2) of BNS, 
2023 and Section 3 & 21(3) of The Banning of 
Unregulated Deposits Schemes Act 2019 and 
Section 3 of The Uttarakhand Protection of 
Interest of Depositors (in financial 
establishment) Act, 2005. 
5. It is argued by learned counsel for the 
applicant that the applicant infact was a 
salaried employee working as a Branch 
Manager in a Company, namely, Loni Urban 
Multistate Credit & Thrift Co-operative 
Society, Branch at Varanasi and he has 
nothing to do with day to day affairs of the 
company having branches in different parts
of the country. It is argued by learned 
counsel for the applicant that in all these FIRs 
the investigation was conducted by the State 
police and the charge sheet was filed but 
subsequent thereto by an order of High 
Court dated 17.09.2025, the investigation is 
transferred to the CBI and the CBI pursuant 
to that registered RC No.0072025E0007 on 
26.11.2025 and now pursuant to the said RC, 
the further investigation is going on. It is 
further argued that the applicant is 
languishing in jail since 22.05.2025 and infact 
the earlier investigating agency already filed 
the charge sheet.6. On the other side Mr. Piyush Garg, 
learned counsel, who appears for the CBI, 
submits that earlier in total 18 FIRs were 
registered and by clubbing all one R.C. is 
registered by the CBI for the purposes of 
further investigation and at about 55 persons 
are under the scrutiny including the present 
applicant and as on date 18 persons have 
been charge sheeted and against rest, further 
investigation is still going on, therefore, the 
applicant may not be released on bail since it 
may affect ongoing investigation. 
7. Taking into consideration the further 
investigation is going on against rest of the 
persons but charge sheet is filed by the 
earlier investigation agency against the 
applicant and applicant is languishing in jail 
since last more than one year i.e. w.e.f. 
22.05.2025, therefore, this Court is of the view 
at this juncture that the investigation against 
the applicant is already completed, therefore, 
the applicant deserves for interim bail since 
further investigation is going on. Hence,
applicant is admitted to interim bail for a 
period of one month. Let the applicant--
Ramnath Gupta be released on interim bail 
for a period of one month “to be computed 
from the date of his release” subject to his 
executing a personal bond and furnishing 
two reliable sureties of the like amount to the 
satisfaction of the court concerned subject to 
the following conditions:-
“i. Applicant after being released on 
interim bail shall cooperate with the 
ongoing further investigation in respect 
of the remaining accused and will not 
make any attempt to come in contact with the persons against whom the 
further investigation is going on.
ii. He will also not make any 
attempt to temper any evidence and 
surrender his passport, if any, to the 
Special Judge Buds Court, First 
Additional Sessions Judge, Dehradun 
within 48 hours after being released on 
bail
iii. The applicant shall provide his 
mobile number to the Investigating 
Officer so that as and when it requires 
for his cooperation in respect of 
ongoing further investigation, he may 
ensure his presence before the I.O.
8. However this regular bail is kept 
pending so that by the next date the CBI may 
file counter affidavit.
9. The interim bail granted to the present 
applicant will not be treated as a precedent in 
respect of the other co-accused. 
10. On expiry of the period of one month, 
the applicant shall surrender before the court 
concerned, failing which necessary warrant 
will be issued. 
11. List on 17.08.2026.
