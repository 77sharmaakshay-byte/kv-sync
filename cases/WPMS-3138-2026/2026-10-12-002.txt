5. It is the contention of the petitioner,
who is a practicing Advocate and member of 
the Bar Association of Bhagwanpur, that 
without there being construction of the 
chamber finalized, process of allotment of 
the chambers have started.6. This Court is of the view that the 
process of allotment of the chamber must be 
done when the chamber’s building is 
completed.
7. In such view of the matter, it is 
provided that any process of allotment of the 
chamber shall remain stayed until and 
unless the chamber building is completed 
and it is handed over to the Bar Association.
It is further directed that in the meantime, 
no process for allotment of the chamber 
shall be made by the respondent no.4-Bar 
Association, Bhagwanpur. 
8. The respondents may file counter 
affidavit within six weeks.
9. List this case on 08.12.2026.
