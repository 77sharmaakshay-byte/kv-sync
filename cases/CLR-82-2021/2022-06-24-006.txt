Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. T.A. Khan, Senior Advocate, assisted by

Mr. Rakesh Thapliyal, Senior Advocate, for

This is a Civil Revision, which has been 
preferred under Section 83 (9) of the Waqf Act, in 
which, delay was earlier condoned, subject to 
payment of cost. The cost in compliance of the 
order has been deposited. 
Learned counsel for the revisionist submits 
that the Coordinate Bench of this Court by an order 
dated 19th April, 2022, had permitted the revisionist 
to place on record certain additional documents, 
which he could not do so, in view of the aforesaid 
fact, that the matter remained pending and he was 
unable to procure the relevant documents, on 
which, he wanted to place reliance. 
Learned counsel for the revisionist prays for 
and is granted ten days’ time to place the said 
document on record in compliance of the order 
dated 19th April, 2022. 
List immediately after ten days.
