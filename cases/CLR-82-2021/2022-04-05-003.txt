COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
05.04.2022 CLR No.82 of 2021
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Heard Mr. T.A. Khan, learned Senior 
Advocate assisted by Ms. Divya Jaiswal, 
learned counsel for the applicants and Mr. 
Rakesh Thapliyal, learned Senior Advocate 
assisted by Mr. C.M. Issar, learned counsel for 
the respondent. 
The learned Senior Advocate appearing 
for the applicants submitted that the 
applicants do not want to proceed with the 
delay condonation application. Therefore, he 
requested to permit them to withdraw the 
delay condonation application.
The learned Senior Advocate appearing 
for the respondent opposed for cost. 
In the interest of justice, on the cost of 
Rs.2,000/- (Rupees two thousand) the Delay 
Condonation Application No.02 of 2021 is 
dismissed as withdrawn. 
The said cost shall be deposited by the 
applicants with the High Court Bar Association 
Welfare Fund, before the next date.
In view of the order of the Hon’ble 
Supreme Court, passed In Re: Cognizance for
Extension of limitation, the present proposed 
revision is within limitation. 
The learned counsel for the respondent 
requested one week’s time to file objection(s) 
to the proposed revision. 
At the request of the learned counsel for 
the parties, post this case on 19.04.2022 
after fresh cases, for arguments on 
admission. 
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
