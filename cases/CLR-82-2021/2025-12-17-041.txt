Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. T.A. Khan, Sr. Advocate assisted by Mr. Vinay 
Bhatt, Advocate for the revisionists.
2. Mr. Chandra Mohan Issar with Ms. Priyanka 
Aggarwal, Advocates for respondent.
3. The Amendment Application (IA No.13 of 2025) has 
been moved by the revisionists with the prayer to 
substitute the Chief Executive Officer, Adhoiwala, 
Dehradun of the Waqf Board in place of deceased 
Mutawalli Mohd. Asad Khan. 
4. Objection/supplementary counter affidavit by way of 
applications IA No.15/25 & 19/25, respectively, have been 
filed by respondent to the Amendment Application which 
are taken on record. The said applications are disposed of
accordingly.
5. Similarly, delay in filing the objection to the 
Amendment Application is condoned. Objection filed by 
the respondent is taken on record. Delay Condonation 
Application (IA No.14 of 2025) stands disposed of.
6. It is also argued by learned counsel for the 
revisionists that since the sole Mutawalli Mohd. Asad Khan 
has expired, therefore, the Chief Executive Officer is 
required to be impleaded in his place.
7. Learned counsel appearing for the respondent 
submits that since the CEO of the Waqf Board is already a 
party which is Uttarakhand Waqf Board through CEO 
before this Court, therefore, there is no need to move the 2
substitution/amendment application.
8. I found force in the submission of learned counsel for 
the respondent.
9. Accordingly, the Amendment Application (IA No.13 of 
2025) is rejected.
10. It is however, clarified that the rights of revisionist 
no.2 Waqf No.659 can well be taken care of by revisionist
No.1.
11. List this matter on 27.02.2026.
12. Interim order dated 25.04.2025 is extended till the 
next date of listing.
