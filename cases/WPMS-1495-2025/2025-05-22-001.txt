Heard on stay application (IA No. 1 of 
2025).
The challenge in this petition is made 
to the order dated 22.04.2025 passed by 
the appellate authority under Section 340 
of the Cantonments Act, 2006 as well as 
the notice issued by the Cantonment 
Board, Dehradun under Section 248 of the 
Cantonments Act, 2006.
Proceedings under the Cantonments 
Act, 2006 were initiated for demolition of 
certain property. Demolition order was 
passed, which was unsuccessfully assailed in the appeal. The property has already 
been sealed since 2019. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that the petitioner has all the 
chances to success in the matter, therefore, 
till the matter is finally heard, the operation 
of the impugned order may be kept in 
abeyance.
Learned counsel for the Cantonment 
Board would submit that status quo order 
may be passed and the property may 
remain sealed as such.
Having considered, till the next date 
of listing, as an interim order, it is directed 
that no demolition shall be done and the 
status quo shall be maintained with regard 
to the property in dispute. 
Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
