COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS 
WPPIL No.88 of 2022 
Shri Vipin Sanghi, C.J. 
Shri Ramesh Chandra Khulbe, J.
Mr. Nalin Saun and Mr. Shashank Saun, 
learned counsel for the petitioner. 
 The submission of learned counsel for 
the petitioner is that the private persons are 
unabashedly selling the lands, which have 
been declared surplus under the U.P. 
Imposition of Ceiling on Land Holdings Act, 
1960 in the district of Dehradun. The 
petitioner seeks a restraint against the sale 
or purchase of such lands. Reliance has 
been placed upon a decision of the Supreme 
Court in Kunwar Chander Bahadur Singh Vs. 
Addl. Distt. and Sessions Judge & Anr. dated 
24.10.1996. 
 Issue notices to the respondents. 
 Mr. Pradeep Joshi, learned Addl. Chief 
Sanding Counsel for the State, appears and 
accepts notices for all the respondents. 
 Counter affidavit be filed within four 
weeks, wherein, the respondents should 
also disclose the particulars of transactions, 
which have been undertaken in violation of 
the aforesaid judgment of the Supreme 
Court. 
 Rejoinder affidavit before the next date. 
 List on 12.09.2022. 
 Till the next date of hearing, we direct 
that the Sub-Registrar, Dehradun shall not 
register any sale deed or any instrument of 
transfer in respect of lands, which have 2
been declared surplus. 
 We further direct that in respect of the 
lands earmarked for ‘Tea Plantation’, no 
transaction shall be registered without prior 
permission of the State Government. 
 Stay Application (IA No.1 of 2022) 
stands disposed of. 
 
(R.C. Khulbe, J.) (Vipin Sanghi, C.J.)
