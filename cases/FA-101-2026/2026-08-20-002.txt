Instant appeal is preferred against the 
judgment and order dated 22.12.2025, passed 
in Original Civil Suit No. 229 of 2024, Siyaram 
Vs. Sri Khemkaran Lal and others, by the court 
of Civil Judge (Senior Division), Rudrapur, 
District Udham Singh Nagar. It is delayed by 
71 days.
Heard on Delay Condonation Application 
No. 1 of 2026.
According to the appellant, after 
impugned judgment and decree, he applied for 
the copy and sought the advice. Thereafter, 
winter vacations started, the appellant has to 
manage for the funds and in that process, 
delay occurred.
Learned counsel for the respondent nos. 
1, 2, 4 and 5 submits that the delay has not 
properly been explained.
Respondent no.3 has also been served 
with the notice personally, but he is not 
present.
Having heard, this Court is of the view 
that there are good grounds to condone the delay.
The delay condonation application is 
allowed. 
Delay in preferring the appeal is 
condoned.
In order to appreciate the arguments on 
admission, let call for the LCR.
List on 10.09.2026 for hearing on 
admission.
