WPSS No.933 of 2009
Hon’ble Tarun Agarwala, J.
Mr. M. C. Kandpal, the learned Senior Advocate assisted by Mr. 
S. S. Chaudhary, the learned counsel for the petitioners. 
Mr. Subhash Upadhyaya, the learned Brief Holder for the State. 
This Court by its last order dated 12th April, 2010 had directed the 
Additional Director, Horticulture and Food Processing Department, 
Ranikhet to appear in the court alongwith the record. The learned Brief 
Holder has stated that the Additional Director had to go out of country 
in relation to some conference and in his place the Director, 
Horticulture and Food Processing Department, Ranikhet is present. 
During the course of the hearing, the learned senior counsel for 
the petitioners pointed out that even though, no interim order was 
passed by this Court, nonetheless, the petitioners have been allowed to 
continue to work as Mallies in Raj Bhawan, Nainital, but they are being 
paid wages on daily rated basis. The learned senior counsel for the 
petitioners further submitted that the petitioners were allowed to retain 
their accommodation, but during the pendency of the writ petition and 
after the order of this court directing the Director of Horticulture & 
Food Processing Department to appear in person, the respondents 
forcibly evicted the petitioners from their quarters. Let these facts be 
brought on record by means of a supplementary affidavit within 24 
hours indicating as to whether the petitioners are being paid the 
minimum pay scale or are being paid wages on daily rated basis. 
The learned Brief Holder for the State will file an affidavit 
indicating as to why the steps have not been initiated to fill up the 
admitted vacancies existing on the post of Mallies and, if there is any 
impediment in filling up these posts, the affidavit should indicate the 
reasons for such impediment that is coming in their way. The learned 
Brief Holder will bring on record any regularization rules that has been 
issued by the State Government for regularization of Class IV 
employees. 
List this matter on 20th April, 2010 for further consideration. It 
would be open to the Director, Horticulture and Food Processing 
Department, Ranikhet to be present in the Court, if he so desire. 
 (Tarun Agarwala, J.)
