Hon’ble Tarun Agarwala J. 
 Mr. M.C.Kandpal, the learned Senior 
Counsel assisted by Mr. Rakesh Kumar Joshi, 
the learned counsel for the petitioner. 
 Mr. Subhash Upadhyay, the learned brief 
holder for the State. 
 Having heard the learned counsel for 
the parties at some length, the court is 
horrified on the action of the respondents in 
adopting unfair labour practice. The petitioners 
were earlier being paid the minimum wages, 
which was withdrawn on account of dismissal 
of their writ petition with regard to the 
regularization of their services. The petitioners 
are still working as Malis in the Raj Bhawan, 
Nainital, but, are being paid wages on daily 
rated basis. Admittedly, the petitioners have 
been working for more than 13 years. The 
respondents have neither advertised the post. 
Admittedly, the posts have not been filled as 
yet. The petitioners are not being paid the 
minimum pay scale, nor any steps are being 
taken to regularize their services. 
 In the light of these appalling facts, 
which is apparent on the basis of the record, 
the court directs the Director, Udhyan Evam 
Khadya Prasanskaran Uttarakhand, Udhyan 
Bhawan, Choubatia, Ranikhet, Almora, 
respondent no.2 to be present in the court on 
23rd March, 2010. 
 List this matter for further hearing on 
23rd March, 2010. 
 (Tarun Agarwala, J.)
