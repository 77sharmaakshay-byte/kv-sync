Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Ravindra S. Rawat, learned counsel for the 
applicant.
2. Mr. Bhaskar Chandra Joshi, learned A.G.A. for 
the State.
3. The instant bail application is moved by the 
present applicant ‘Vijay Pal’ S/o Shri Malha for 
seeking cancellation of bail of the respondent no. 2, 
who is facing trial arising out of Case Crime No. 14 of 
2025 that is registered on the basis of First Information 
Report dated 06.01.2025 at P.S. Kotwali Manglore, 
District Haridwar for the offences punishable under 
sections 65(1), 74, 351(2), 70(2) of BNS, 2023 r/w 
section 3(A)/4(II), 5(G)/6 of POCSO Act.
4. This court grant bail to the respondent no. 2 in
Case Crime No. 14 of 2025 by order dated 23.05.2025. It 
is contended that after being released on bail the 
respondent no. 2 along with five others namely Sagar 
S/o Sunil, Rani, W/o Padam, Kavita W/o Sunil, Ashu 
S/o Saamil and Vineet S/o Padam forcefully entered 
in the house of the informant-the present applicant and 
abuses all the family member and brutally beaten the 
mother of the informant regarding which a First 
Information Report is lodged at P.S. Manglore on 
27.07.2026 bearing FIR No. 0380 of 2026 implicating all 
these persons for the offences punishable under 
sections 115(2), 117(2), 190, 191(2), 191(3), 333, 351(2) 
and 352 of BNS, 2023. In support of the injury certain 
photographs are also enclosed along with the injury 
report, on perusal of which it reveals that the applicant’s mother got injury in most vital part of the 
body caused by hard and blunt object.
5. Prima-facie from the photographs and the 
medical report it reveals that the injury are grievious in 
nature and in most vital part and also danger to the 
life.
6. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
applicant that there is a regular threat to the present 
applicant and his family member from the respondent 
no. 2, who is facing trial for the heinious offence under
the POCSO Act and is misusing liberty granted by this 
court by order dated 23.05.2025, whereby, he has been 
granted bail, therefore, his bail should be cancelled 
forthwith.
7. I found force on the submission and on perusal 
of the First Information Report dated 27.07.2026, the 
photographs of the injured and the injury report it 
appears that the respondent no. 2 is misusing the
liberty of bail.
8. Mr. Joshi, learned A.G.A. accepts notice for 
respondent no. 1 and he shall inform through the 
S.H.O concerned to the respondent no. 2 to be remain 
present in court on the next date fixed and service is 
affected to the respondent no. 2 through the S.H.O. 
concerned because there is a regular threat to the 
applicant from respondent no. 2.
9. Put up this matter on 24.08.2026 on top of board.
