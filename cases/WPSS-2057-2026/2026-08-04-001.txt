4. Petitioners have filed the present writ petition 
challenging the order dated 11.12.2025, whereby 
representations submitted by petitioners seeking 
relaxation of the upper age limit were rejected by 
respondent no.2 of the Medical Health and Medical 
Education Department.
5. Petitioners have challenged the aforesaid order 
dated 11.12.2025 mainly on the ground that no reasons 
have been assigned for not extending the benefit of age 
relaxation to the petitioners and the reasons, if any, 
assigned are not correct.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioners submits that it 
is wrong on the part of the respondents to state that the 
posts of the Pharmacist cadre were created in the 
Medical Education Department on 31.03.2022, 
inasmuch as the document annexed at page 76 of the 
writ petition, issued by the Secretary of the same department dated 31.03.2022, reflects that 119 posts of 
the Pharmacist cadre were transferred to the Medical 
Education Department from the Medical Health and 
Family Welfare Department. He further submits that 
Rule 3 of the Rules known as the Uttarakhand Public 
Servants (Relaxation of Age Limit for Direct Recruitment) 
Rules, 2003 has not been taken note of, despite the 
order passed by a Co-ordinate Bench of this Court dated 
07.10.2025 in WPSS No. 2090 of 2022, Shailendra 
Prasad Nautiyal & Others vs. State of Uttarakhand & 
Others.
7. This Court finds substance in the submissions of 
learned counsel for the petitioners, inasmuch as, while 
directing petitioners to file a representation before the 
Competent Authority, petitioners were also directed to 
participate provisionally in the document verification. It 
was further directed that the results of the petitioners 
shall not be declared.
8. However, learned counsel for the respondent￾Selecting Body submitted that, since petitioners have not 
been granted any relaxation in the upper age limit by the 
State Government, they were not found eligible for 
selection, and the selection process has, at present, been 
completed.
9. Learned counsel for the respondent(s) may file 
counter affidavit(s) within a period of six weeks.
