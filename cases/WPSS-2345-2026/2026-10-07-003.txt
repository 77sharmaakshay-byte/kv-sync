Hon'ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Vinod Fulara, learned counsel for 
the petitioner through V.C.
2. Mr. K.N. Joshi, learned D.A.G. for the 
State of Uttarakhand/respondents.
3. Petitioner no.1 Krishna Nand Bhatt is 
working as Data Entry Operator w.e.f.
01.04.2015 and petitioner no.2 Harish Dutt 
Chaushali is working as Driver since 
16.10.2010, with the respondent￾department. 
4. The writ petition is filed by the 
petitioners for the reason that they have not 
received their remuneration for a period of 
16 months as per the detail given in para-19 
of the writ petition. The petitioners are 
working on the said post even on the date of 
the filing of the present writ petition.
5. Since petitioners are working with the 
respondent department as stated above, this 
Court asked the learned State Counsel to get 
instructions, as to why petitioners are not
being paid monthly remuneration as 
mentioned in para-19 of the writ petition.
6. On 18.09.2026, on instruction, it was 
stated by the learned State Counsel that 
matter regarding payment of remuneration 
of petitioners is under process and the same 
would be resolve recently.7. Today, on instructions dated 
05.10.2026, learned State Counsel submits 
that remuneration of month of September,
2026 only, has been sent to the Treasury,
for disbursement to petitioners on 
02.10.2026 and the remaining remuneration
would be given on availability of the budget.
The said instruction dated 05.10.2026 is 
taken on record.
8. Petitioners are low paid employee and 
this attitude of the respondents is highly 
despicable. The petitioners should have been 
paid remuneration on month-to-month basis
as salary being paid to the higher officers of 
the department. 
9. This Court take serious note of the fact 
that the interest of the Class-III and Class-IV 
employees are been neglected by the Senior 
Officers of the department.
10. In such view of the matter, the 
Divisional Forest Officer–respondent no.3, 
Mr. Deepak Singh, is directed to remain 
present before this Court on 12.10.2026 and 
to explain as to what action has been taken
by him regarding the payment of the arrears 
of remuneration to the petitioners or to pay 
the entire remuneration by 12.10.2026.
11. List this case on 12.10.2026, as fresh.
