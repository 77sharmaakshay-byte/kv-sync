Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. Vinod Nautiyal, Dy. A.G. for the 
State. 
Mr. Hrishek Lakhera, Advocate for 
the respondents/applicant. 
Heard on Money Withdrawal 
Application (CLMA No. 6695 of 2018). 
This application has been filed by the 
respondents in the appeal seeking 
permission to withdraw 50% of the total 
amount, which has been deposited by the 
appellant before Reference Court in 
compliance of order dated 07.11.2016 
passed by this Court. 
From perusal of the application, 
under Section 18 of Land Acquisition Act 
(Annexure No. 2 to the Stay application), it 
reveals that respondent no.1 is having 
25% share in the acquired land, while the 
share of respondent nos. 2, 3, 4 & 5 in the 
acquired land is 32.5%, 10%, 12.5% and 
20%, respectively. 
In such view of the matter, 50% of 
the amount, so deposited by the appellant, 
pursuant to the order of this Court dated 
07.11.2016 be released in favour of the 
respondents/applicants in proportion to 
their share in the acquired land. 
Consequently, money withdrawal 
application stands disposed of. 
List this appeal in the month of 
November, 2018 for final hearing 
alongwith connected matter. 
Let certified copy of this order be 
supplied to learned counsel for the parties 
within 24 hours on payment of usual 
charges. 
(Manoj K. Tiwari, J.) 30.05.2018
