These are land acquisition Appeals, 
which have been preferred under Section 54 
of the Land Acquisition Act, 1894 against an 
award dated 20.12.2013. 
The appeals stand admitted by this 
Court. 
Today the matter is listed on Urgency 
Application No. 8693 of 2019, preferred by 
the respondents praying for fixation of an 
early date. 
Considering the fact that since these are 
regular First Appeals under Section 54 of the 
Land Acquisition Act and in view of the 
provisions contained under Order 41 Rule 12, 
these appeals are directed to be posted for 
final hearing in the week commencing from 
29th July, 2019.
