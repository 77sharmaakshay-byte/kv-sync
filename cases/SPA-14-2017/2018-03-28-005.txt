Heard. 
In view of the grounds taken in the 
affidavit, filed in support of the dealy 
condonation application and in the

special appeal is hereby condoned. Delay 
condonation application is allowed. 
Heard. 
It is settled law that the Court has 
to take liberal view while dealing with the 
appeal filed by the State. 
The issue raised in appeal is after 
the agreement entered into between State 
of Himachal Pradesh and the State of 
Uttarakhand, the Dehradun-Paonta Sahib 
Road has not been notified. 
Accordingly, the special appeal is 
allowed. Judgment dated 02.12.2013, 
rendered by learned Single Judge is set￾aside. The State Government is directed 
to notify the Dehradun-Paonta Sahib 
road, within a period of eight weeks from 
today, as per the provisions of Motor 
Vehicles Act, 1988. 
Pending application, if any, also 
stands disposed of accordingly.
