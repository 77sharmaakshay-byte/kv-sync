Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
 Mr. Imran Ali Khan and Mr. Harsh 
Vardhan Dhanik, Advocates for the 
revisionist.
 The challenge in this revision is made 
to the judgment and order dated 
14.08.2024, passed in Misc. Criminal 
Case No.85 of 2020, Smt. Richa Sharma 
and another Vs. Shri Vipin Kumar, by the 
court of Judge, Family Court, Kashipur, 
District Udham Singh Nagar, under 
Section 125 of the Code of Criminal 
Procedure, 1973 (“the Code”). By it, 
the revisionist has been directed to 
pay `20,000/- per month to the 
respondent no.2, as interim 
maintenance.
 Heard.
 Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would submit that, in fact, the respondent 
no.1 is also earning. Therefore, the 
amount of maintenance that was awarded 
to the respondent no.2 should have been 
divided between the revisionist and the 
respondent no.1. 
 The perusal of the impugned order 
reveals that, in fact, what is the amount of 
maintenance required to the respondent 
no.2 is also not been assessed. Once it is 
done, in case of both the mother and 
father earning the amount should have 
been divided in proportion to their income. Therefore, this matter requires 
deliberation. 
 Admit.
 Issue notice to the respondent no.2 
through respondent no.1, returnable 
within six weeks. Steps to be taken within 
a week.
 List on 07.01.2025. 
 Till the next date of listing, the 
revisionist shall continue to pay `15,000/-
per month to the respondent no.2.
 Stay Application (IA) No.1 of 2024, 
stands disposed of accordingly.
