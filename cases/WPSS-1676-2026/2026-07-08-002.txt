Mr. Shailendra Nauriyal, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. Piyush Tiwari, learned counsel for the 
Union of India.
3. Petitioner has filed this writ petition seeking a 
direction to the respondent-Authorities to permit the 
petitioner to join on the post of Agniveer (General 
Duty) pursuant to joining list/letter uploaded by 
respondent no.2-Army Recruitment Office (ARO) of 
Agniveer through Commanding Officer, Garhwal 
Rifles Regimental Centre, Lansdowne on 29.06.2026, 
annexure no.5 to the writ petition.
4. Petitioner participated in the recruitment drive 
for Agniveer (General Duty) in which he was duly 
selected and his joining letter was uploaded by 
respondent no.2 on the official website of the 
respondent on 26.06.2026. His name is found place 
at Sl. No.354 Roll No.LUC_UTT_LAN_AVGD
_2025_109333 and was given date of joining on 
29.06.2026.
5. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that when the petitioner reached for 
joining in Lansdowne on 29.06.2026, he was not 
given joining. 
6. This Court vide order dated 06.07.2026 
directed the learned counsel for the Union of India to get instructions that as to why the petitioner was not 
allowed to join as Agniveer in the Army.
7. Today learned counsel for the Union of India, 
on instructions, which are taken on record, 
submitted that since an FIR was lodged against the 
petitioner under Sections 147, 323, 504 and 506 of 
IPC on 19.06.2021 in Police Station (Patwari Circle)-
Bulhad, Tehsil-Chakrata, District-Dehradun and he 
is facing trial for the said offences, therefore he was 
refused joining.
8. It is further submitted by learned counsel for 
the Union of India that since Section 147 of IPC is 
now Section 191(2) of the B.N.S. 2023, therefore the 
petitioner was not given joining.
9. It is contended by learned counsel for the 
petitioner that the FIR was lodged on 19.06.2021 
against the petitioner relating to an incident which 
happened on 11.06.2021 at which date the 
petitioner was 15 years, 9 months and 23 days old, a 
juvenile.
10. Since petitioner’s trial was undergoing with the 
adults in the court of learned Judicial Magistrate, 
therefore being a juvenile the trial of the petitioner 
was stayed by a Co-ordinate Bench of this Court in 
C528 No.570 of 2026, Sandeep Joshi Vs. State of 
Uttarakhand and another, on 02.04.2026.
11. Learned counsel further submitted that even 
Section 24 of the Juvenile Justice (Care and 
Protection of Children) Act, 2015 categorically stated 
that “a child who has committed an offence and has 
been dealt with under the Act shall not suffer any 
disqualification attached to a conviction under any other law.”, therefore at this juncture petitioner 
cannot be refused joining merely on an FIR lodged 
against him and he is facing trial.
12. This Court finds substance in the submission 
made by learned counsel for the petitioner.
13. Respondents may file their counter affidavits 
within four weeks.
14. List this case on 16.09.2026.
15. In the meantime, respondent no.2 is directed to 
give joining to the petitioner provisionally and the 
delay in joining may be ignored by respondent no.2 
in view of the facts and circumstances of the case.
