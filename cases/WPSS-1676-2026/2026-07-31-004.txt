Mr. Tilak Ram Sharma, learned counsel for the

2. Mr. Piyush Tiwari, learned CGSC for the Union of 
India/applicant.
3. Today, the matter is listed on recall application (IA 
No.2/2026) filed by Union of India/respondents to recall 
the order dated 08.07.2026 passed by this Court. 
4. Petitioner has filed the objection to the recall 
application.
5. Having perused the recall application as well as the 
objection, this Court is of the opinion that the order sought 
to be recalled was passed by this Court after hearing both 
the parties and after receiving instructions from the 
respondents/Union of India. Therefore, no good ground has 
been made out to recall the order dated 08.07.2026.
6. Accordingly, the recall application is rejected.
