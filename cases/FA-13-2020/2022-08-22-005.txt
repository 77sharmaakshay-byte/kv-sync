Mr. Pankaj Kumar, Advocate, holding

This is a defendant’s first appeal 
preferred against the impugned judgment and 
decree dated 13.08.2019, as rendered in Suit 
No.151 of 2013, seeking the decree of partition 
and permanent injunction.
The appeal was preferred by the 
appellant along with the delay condonation 
application CLMA No.1571 of 2020, seeking 
condonation of 49 days of delay, on the ground 
that there was a delayed information, which has 
imparted to the appellant by his counsel, who 
was conducting the case before the learned trial 
court.
The delay condonation application 
preferred by the defendant, the delay is being 
opposed by the respondent nos.1, 2 and 3, by 
filing objection through IA No.7255 of 2020.
Simultaneously, the respondents, who 
has filed the objection against the delay 
condonation application has also preferred a cross appeal under Order 41 Rule 22 of the 
CPC, through IA No.7259 of 2022.
After having heard the learned counsels 
for the parties, and particularly, considering the 
reasons for delay, which cannot be directly
attributed to the appellant causing delay in 
filing the delayed appeal, due to the fault of the 
counsel, who had given a delayed information. 
The delay of 49 days, which has chanced in 
filing the appeal, would stand condoned.
Accordingly CLMA No.1571 of 2020, would 
stands disposed of.
As a consequence of the order of 
allowing the delay condonation application, the 
objection filed to the delay condonation 
application by respondent no.1, through IA 
No.7255 of 2022, would too stand disposed of.
List this first appeal, as well as the cross 
appeal under Order 41 Rule 22 of CPC, for 
hearing at an admission stage next week. 
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
