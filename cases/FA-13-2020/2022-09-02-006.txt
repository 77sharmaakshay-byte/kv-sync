02.09.2022 FA No.13 of 2020
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Present proposed First Appeal has 
been filed against the judgment and 
decree dated 13.08.2019, passed by the 
Civil Judge (Senior Division), Dehadun in 
Original Suit No.151 of 2013, 
“Prabhatesh Pratap Singh vs. Prabhan 
Pratap Singh and Others”, whereby, the
Original Suit, filed for partition, 
mandatory and prohibitory perpetual 
injunction, has been allowed and a 
preliminary decree has been passed. 
Heard Mr. Piyush Pant, the learned 
counsel for the appellant-defendant and 
Mr. Yashpal Singh, learned counsel for 
the respondent no.1-plaintiff. 
None is present on behalf of the 
respondent nos.2 & 3.
Cross-objection has also been filed 
by the respondent no.1-plaintiff. 
Admit the appeal.
Summon the lower court records.
List this case on 24.11.2022. 
In the facts and circumstances of 
the case, the effect and operation of the 
impugned judgment and decree dated 
13.08.2019 is stayed till the next date of 
listing. 
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.) 02.09.2022
