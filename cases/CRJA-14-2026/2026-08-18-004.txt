Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mrs. Shruti Joshi, learned Amicus Curiae for the 
applicant.
2. Mr. Bhaskar Chandra Joshi, learned A.G.A. for 
the State.
3. The instant jail appeal is already admitted on 
20.04.2026 and the Trial Court record has been 
summoned and the paper book is also ready. The 
instant jail appeal is preferred through Jail on the 
recommendation of the Jailor of District Jail, Haridwar.
4. The appellant herein is convicted by the learned 
Addl. Session Judge, FTSC, Haridwar, District 
Haridwar by judgment and order dated 18.02.2026
passed in Special Session Trial No. 73 of 2021 for the 
offence punishable under section sections 363, 366A, 
377, 323 and 506 of IPC r/w section 5(tha)(da)/6 of 
POCSO Act with the maximum sentence of ten years 
R.I. and fine of Rs. 10,000/-.
5. Learned Amicus Curiae Mrs. Shruti Joshi 
appointed by this court and submits that the present 
appellant herein awarded the maximum sentence of 
ten years and he has already served more than half of 
the sentence, therefore, he may be released on bail 
since this jail appeal is preferred in the year 2026, 
therefore, there is no possibility that the appeal would 
be expedited soon.
6. Mr. Joshi, learned A.G.A. placed before this 
court the custody certificate dated 15.06.2026 issued by 
the Jailor of District Jail Haridwar; on perusal of which 
it also reveals that as on 15.06.2026 the appellant served 5 years 2 months without remission and 5 years 
2 months 18 days with remission.
7. After taking into consideration that the appellant 
herein already served more than half of the sentence 
and there is no possibility that the appeal be expedited 
soon, therefore, taking into consideration that the 
appellant already served more than half of the 
sentence, this court is of the view that the appellant 
deserves for bail.
8. Accordingly, without expressing any opinion on 
the merit of the case, the bail application is allowed.
9. Let the appellant ‘Raju Yadav’ be released on 
bail on furnishing his personal bond and two reliable 
sureties each of the like amount to the satisfaction of 
the court concerned during the pendency of criminal 
jail appeal. Fine shall also remain suspended.
10. List this appeal in the month of November, 2026.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
