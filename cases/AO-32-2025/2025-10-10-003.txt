Nainital in M.A.C.P.N No.18 of 2020, 
whereby, the Tribunal has awarded

of the claimants.
5. Lower Court record be summoned.
6. Heard on Money Withdrawal 
Application (IA 2 of 2025)
7. Respondent nos. 1 & 2/claimants 
have moved the present application 
stating therein that by order dated 
12.02.2025, the Coordinate Bench of this 
Court stayed execution of the impugned 
award subject to deposit of the 50% 
amount out of the total awarded sum by 
the appellant/Insurance Company before
the Tribunal; that, in compliance of the 
same, the 50% amount has been 
deposited by the appellant/Insurance 
Company before the concerned Tribunal. 
The applicants/claimants submit that 
they are in pressing need of funds to meet 
household expenses, medical treatment, and livelihood, and therefore seek release 
of the deposited amount.
8. For the reasons stated, the Money 
Withdrawal Application is allowed.
9. It is directed that out of the amount 
deposited by the Insurance Company 
before the Tribunal, the 50% amount be 
released in favour of respondent nos. 1 & 
2/claimants and the remaining amount 
shall be kept in an interest-bearing fixed 
deposit in a nationalised bank subject to 
further orders of this Court.
10. List this case in due course along 
with connected appeal.
