Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Tiwari, J
1. Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. N.K. Pipnoi, learned Brief Holder
for the State of Uttarakhand.
3. Mr. Bhuwan Bhatt, learned counsel for 
the respondent. 
4. Mr. Ravi Bisht, learned counsel for the 
caveator in WPSS No.1177 of 2025.
5. Petitioner was appointed as Junior 
Engineer through outsourcing in 2007. His 
engagement has been terminated by the 
Executive Officer, Nagar Panchayat, Pauri 
Garhwal, vide order dated 09.07.2025. The 
said order has been passed on the 
instructions of Additional Director, Urban 
Development, Directorate. The letter, issued 
by Additional Director, is on record as 
Annexure-2 to the writ petition, wherein 
certain allegations were made against the 
petitioner. 
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that Executive Officer is the 
Competent Authority to make appointment 
on the post of Junior Engineer and 
petitioner was appointed by the Executive 
Officer, therefore, Additional Director had 
no business to issue such direction. 
7. Mr. Bhuwan Bhatt, learned counsel for 
the respondent, however, submits that the 
post of Junior Engineer is not sanctioned as 
yet, therefore, Additional Director intervened by directing the Executive Officer 
to terminate the services of the petitioner, 
based on a complaint made by someone. 
8. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that Executive Officer has acted on 
the dictates of Additional Director and has 
abdicated his discretion. He further submits 
that letter issued by Additional Director 
indicates that the services of petitioner have 
been terminated by way of punishment as 
certain stigma has been cast on the 
petitioner.
9. Learned counsels for the respondents 
pray for and are granted four weeks time to 
file counter affidavits.
10. List these cases on 16.10.2025.
11. Till the next date of listing, impugned 
termination order(s) shall remain stayed. 
12. Stay application (IA No. 1 of 2025) 
stands disposed of.
