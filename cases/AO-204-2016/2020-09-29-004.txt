Heard learned counsel for the parties 
through video conferencing.
This money withdrawal application 
has been filed by claimants/respondent 
nos. 1 to 5 for withdrawal of the amount of 
compensation deposited by the Insurance 
Company before learned Tribunal. 
Learned Tribunal had awarded a sum 
of `24,31,750/- as compensation to the 
claimants/respondent nos. 1 to 5.
Learned Senior Counsel for the 
appellant submits that the appellant had
deposited a sum of `8,00,000/- with the 
concerned Tribunal in terms of the order 
of this Court. He further submits that out 
of the said amount `5,00,000/- only was 
released in favour of the claimants and 
remaining amount is still lying with the 
Tribunal. 
Mr. Lalit Belwal, learned counsel for 
the claimants submits that respondent no. 
3 – Ms. Ankita Gupta (daughter of the 
deceased) is the elder daughter and talks 
of marriage are going on, for which, Smt. 
Savita Gupta (mother of respondent no. 3) 
needs funds. He further submits that sons 
of the Smt. Savita Gupta also require 
money for higher studies. 
Having regard to the facts and 
circumstances of the case, money withdrawal application is allowed and it is 
provided that entire deposited amount,
including interest, if any, shall be released 
in favour of claimant/respondent nos. 1 to 
5 without asking any security. 
List this matter in the first week of 
November, 2020 along with connected 
matter.
 (Manoj K. Tiwari, J.) Aswal 29.09.2020
