COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
AO No. 204 of 2016
Shri S.K.Mishra, ACJ.
 The matter is taken up virtually.
Heard Mr. Lalit Belwal, learned 
counsel for the respondent nos. 1 to 
5/applicants, who were the claimants 
before the learned Tribunal and Mr. 
V.K.Kohli, learned Senior Counsel, assisted 
by Mr. I.P.Kohli, learned counsel for the
appellant/Insurance Company. 
The grievance of the appellant is that 
though this Court (a Coordinate Bench) by 
virtue of order dated 29.09.2020, has 
directed release of the rest of the awarded
amount deposited by the Insurance 
Company, the learned Judge, Presiding the 
Tribunal on a flimsy pretext is refusing to
release the money as there is no 
specification regarding the statutory 
deposits made by the appellant in this 
Court.
Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties, this Court is of the opinion that if 
at all such a fact is true, which appears to 
be true because of the fact that the 
petitioner has filed an affidavit annexed to
the application, then the matter is very 
serious. An Order passed by the High 
Court, which is clear and there is no 
ambiguity in it, cannot be refused to be 
implemented by a Subordinate Court. In 
fact, if a Presiding Officer of a Court subordinate to the High Court refuses to 
implement the order of the High Court, it 
may in appropriate cases invite a civil 
contempt. 
Therefore, we hope and trust that the 
Judge, Presiding the matter i.e. Motor 
Accident Claims Tribunal shall act 
appropriately instead of avoiding to 
discharge his/her duty on flimsy pretext.
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
undertakes that the petitioner shall 
produce a downloaded copy of this Order 
from the High Court website with an 
endorsement of correctness by Mr. Lalit 
Belwal, Advocate, before the Tribunal as 
early as possible. 
Urgent copy of the order be provided 
to the learned counsel for the 
applicant/respondent nos. 1 to 5 as per 
rules. 
 (S.K.Mishra, ACJ.)
