2. By means of the present writ petition, petitioner 
seeks quashing of F.I.R./Case Crime No.0219 of 
2026, under Section 108 of B.N.S., registered at
Police Station Kiccha, District Udham Singh Nagar.
3. As per the allegations contained in the 
impugned F.I.R., the same has been lodged by the 
wife of the deceased alleging that the petitioner, along 
with certain other persons, had been continuously 
harassing and threatening the deceased to sell his 
property to them, failing which they would eliminate 
his entire family. It is alleged that, owing to the 
continuous threats extended by the petitioner and the 
co-accused, the deceased remained under constant 
mental stress, fear and intimidation. On the evening 
of 05.06.2026, the deceased suddenly felt uneasy and 
was taken to a local hospital. At about 10:30 p.m., he 
disclosed to the attending doctor that he had 
consumed poison. As his condition deteriorated, he 
was referred to a hospital at Bareilly, where he 
ultimately succumbed to the poisoning during the 
intervening night of 05/06.06.2026 at about 12:00–
12:30 a.m.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that the deceased died on 05.06.2026, whereas the 
impugned F.I.R. came to be lodged only on 
16.06.2026, after an unexplained delay of more than 
eleven days. It is further submitted that a registered 
Agreement to Sell had already been executed between 
the petitioner and the deceased on 08.08.2025 for a 
total sale consideration of Rs.1.4 crore, out of which 
an amount of Rs.80,00,000/- had been paid to the 
deceased through cheques. The balance sale 
consideration was agreed to be paid at the time of 
execution of the sale deed, and the parties had 
stipulated that the sale deed could be executed on or 
before 20.12.2027. It is, therefore, contended that 
there was no occasion or necessity for the petitioner 
to pressurize or threaten the deceased. Learned 
counsel further submits that immediately after the 
incident, on 06.06.2026, the cousin sister of the 
deceased submitted an application before the Station 
House Officer, Police Station Kiccha, alleging that it 
was the complainant and her brother who had been 
harassing the deceased with the intention of grabbing 
his property. According to the petitioner, the said 
contemporaneous complaint completely demolishes 
the prosecution case and indicates that the impugned 
F.I.R. has been lodged as an afterthought.
5. Admit.
6. Issue notice to respondent no.4, returnable at 
an early date, by registered post, acknowledgment 
due.
7. List after service report is received alongwith 
WPCRL No.1179 of 2026. Counter affidavit, if any, be 
filed in the meantime.
8. Considering the submission advanced by 
learned counsel for the petitioner, it is directed that, 
till the next date of listing, investigation pursuant to 
the impugned F.I.R. may go on, however, petitioner 
shall not be arrested, provided he cooperates with the 
investigation.
9. Interim relief application (I.A. No.1 of 2026) 
stands disposed of.
