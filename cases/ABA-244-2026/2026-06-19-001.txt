2. Applicants are praying for anticipatory 
bail in reference to F.I.R./Case Crime No.0177 
of 2022, under Section 420 & 506 I.P.C., 
registered at Police Station Kankhal, District
Haridwar.
3. An F.I.R. came to be lodged by the 
complainant alleging that the father of the 
present applicants had received advance 
payment from the complainant-company for 
supply of certain goods/materials, but failed 
to deliver the same despite receipt of the 
consideration. Pursuant thereto, investigation 
was undertaken. During the course of 
investigation, it has surfaced that the advance 
amount paid by the complainant was allegedly 
transferred by the father of the applicants into 
the bank accounts of the applicants in the 
form of fixed deposits, while the contracted 
material was never supplied.
4. Learned counsel for the applicants submits that the applicants have been falsely 
implicated in the present case. It is contended 
that even if the allegations contained in the 
F.I.R. and the material collected during 
investigation are accepted in their entirety, no 
offence punishable under Section 420 I.P.C. is 
made out against the applicants. It is further 
submitted that the dispute arises out of a 
commercial transaction and pertains to an 
alleged breach of contractual obligations, for 
which the appropriate remedy lies before the 
competent Civil Court. Learned counsel 
further argues that the essential ingredients 
of the offence of cheating, namely fraudulent 
or dishonest intention at the inception of the 
transaction, are conspicuously absent from 
the record. It is also submitted that applicant 
no.2 delivered a child on 01.06.2026 and, 
owing to her recent childbirth, it would be 
difficult for her to undertake travel to 
Haridwar for furnishing personal bonds. 
Accordingly, it is prayed that, in the event 
anticipatory bail is granted, the personal bond 
furnished by applicant no.2 may be permitted 
to be verified through online mode.
5. As prayed, four weeks’ time is granted to 
learned State Counsel to file a counter 
affidavit.
6. List thereafter.
7. As an interim measure, and without 
expressing any opinion on the merits of the 
case, it is directed that in the event of arrest 
during the course of investigation, the 
applicants shall be released on interim 
anticipatory bail on furnishing a personal 
bond by each of them along with two reliable 
sureties each of the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the Arresting Officer, subject to 
the following conditions:
(i) The applicants shall extend full cooperation 
to the investigating agency and shall make 
themselves available for interrogation as and when required;
(ii) The applicants shall not directly or 
indirectly induce, threaten, influence, or 
contact any prosecution witness, nor shall 
they tamper with the evidence in any manner 
whatsoever;
(iii) The applicants shall not leave the country 
without prior permission of the Court 
concerned and shall furnish their mobile 
numbers and residential addresses to the 
Investigating Officer;
(iv) The personal bond to be furnished by 
applicant no.2 may be verified through online 
mode, considering the fact that she has 
recently delivered a child on 01.06.2026.
