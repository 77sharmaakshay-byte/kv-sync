2. A Correction Application (I.A. No. 02 of 2026) 
has been filed by the applicants seeking correction of 
the order dated 19.06.2026 on the ground that Section 
120-B of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 was 
inadvertently omitted and, therefore, deserves to be 
reflected along with Sections 420 and 506 of the 
Indian Penal Code, 1860.
3. The Correction Application is allowed.
4. Accordingly, the order dated 19.06.2026 shall 
stand corrected by mentioning Section 120-B of the 
Indian Penal Code, 1860 in addition to Sections 420 
and 506 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860. The order 
dated 19.06.2026 stands corrected accordingly.
