Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
Mr. Vinay Kumar, learned counsel for the 
petitioner in WPSS No.2593 of 2026.
2. Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, learned counsel for the 
petitioner in WPSS No.53 of 2025.
3. Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for the

4. Mr. P.S. Bisht, learned Chief Standing 
Counsel with Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Brief 
Holder for the State of Uttarakhand.
5. In all these matters serious issue has been 
raised on the process of transfer in the mid￾academic session. We are in the October, 2026 and 
the present matters pertains to process of transfer 
in large scale in to the Education Department, and 
such a process of transfer in mid-academic-session 
are neither in the interest of the Education 
Institutions nor in the interest of the students.
6. This Court is very surprised that in mid￾academic-session the State Authorities are 
proceeding to transfer in large scale on the request 
of the individuals. In some of the cases, Mr. Vinay 
Kumar and Mr. Navnish Negi, pointed out that
there is no transparency at all.7. Mr. Vinay Kumar, pointed out that about 
more than 200 persons were asked to appear 
before the Authorities to satisfy them whether 
their case is genuine or not? He submits that for 
transfer personal hearing is not at all required and 
it is nowhere provided under the Act.
8. Mr. Navnish Negi in WPSS No.2630 of 2026 
pointed out that there was a time schedule as 
notified by the Director General in terms of which
the schedule date, for inviting applications on 
request was 17.07.2026 to 30.07.2026, 10.08.2026 
was the date for uploading the list of options and 
details of the applications, 15.08.2026 to 24.08.2026 
was scheduled for meeting of the Transfer 
Committee and for recommendations of the
Competent Authority and 27.08.2026 is the 
deadline for issuing the transfer order. 
9. Mr. Negi pointed out that one person, 
namely, Mr. Vinay Kumar, who is the private 
respondents, herein, preferred a writ petition being 
WPSS No.2227 of 2026, on 17.08.2026, which is 
prior to the schedule deadline of meeting of 
Transfer Committee and the writ petition was 
disposed of finally on 19.08.2026, whereby the 
respondents were directed to constitute a Medical 
Board and take appropriate steps. 
10. Mr. Navnish Negi, brought to the notice of 
this Court Annexure No.13, which is the Circular 
of the Director dated 03.09.2026 along with the 
remark of Medical Officers, wherein, the name of 
this respondent is mentioned at S.No.6, and by the 
order impugned dated 28.09.2026, this incumbent 
was transferred though his claim was rejected on 
medical ground which itself is reflected from 
Annexure no.13. He submits that the Director has 
not applied its mind and by ignoring the list 
wherein, his claim was rejected on medical 
ground, he was transferred.Apart from this, he 
submits that petitioner’s claim was recommended 
by the Medical Board despite this he has not been 
transferred. 11. After examining the submissions as above 
by the learned counsel for the petitioners, this 
Court is of the view that there is no transparency at 
all in the process of transfer and even otherwise in 
the mid of the academic session, particularly in 
October, 2026 there was no justification for the 
Authorities to go with the transfer of employees in 
large scale, particularly when the Board 
Examinations are likely to be held in the month of 
February, 2027. It is not proper either in the 
interest of the Institute or in the interest of the 
student to transfer large number of Teachers in the 
mid of the academic session. 
12. On the previous occasion, this Court take
serious cognizance in WPSS No.533 of 2025 and 
WPSS No.53 of 2025, wherein, issue was raised 
whether the transfer on the basis of the 
classification of “Sugam” and “Durgam” is proper
and what is the basis and how the authorities have 
identified the “Sugam” and “Durgam” places,
particularly when, there is no reference of “Sugam”
and “Durgam” places in the Act itself. If there is no
clarity on “Sugam” and “Durgam” places, how the 
authorities can go with the transfer on the basis of 
such classification i.e. “Sugam” and “Durgam”, and
therefore, respondent - State was restrained to 
transfer on the basis of such classification of 
“Sugam” and “Durgam” but it has been apprised 
this order is not being complied with in a letter 
and spirit and in some of the Departments despite 
the Court’s order the Authorities are repeatedly 
transferring on the basis of the classification of 
“Sugam” and “Durgam”. This appears to be in a 
flagrant violation of the High Court’s order. On the 
request made by the State, subsequently this Court 
modified the order on 16.12.2025. The relevant 
extract of the order is being reproduced 
hereinasunder:-
“My earlier order dated 09.04.2025 is very clear 
whereby respondents were permitted to transfer 
in certain contingencies including the long tenure 
which the respondents can examine on the basis of the period which was rendered in the plain area 
like Dehradun, Haridwar, Haldwani, Rudrapur, 
Ramnagar and Kotdwar etc. Therefore, the 
respondents are free to transfer any incumbent 
from one place to another place but not on such 
classification of Sugam and Durgam.”
13. Despite the clear cut direction of this Court, 
it has been apprised that there are large number of 
the incumbent in every Departments, who are 
serving in these places as mentioned in my order 
since long back and no steps are being made by the 
Authorities to transfer them.
14. This Court is now taking a serious note on it 
since the direction issued earlier was not only 
confined to the particular Department, therefore, 
in such an eventuality the Chief Secretary Head of 
the State should explain why the order of this 
Court has not been complied with in letter and 
spirit and why those persons who are posted in the 
places like Dehradun, Haridwar, Haldwani, 
Rudrapur, Ramnagar and Kotdwar etc since long
back have not been transferred so far.
15. Let Mr. P.S. Bisht, learned Chief Standing 
Counsel may get the necessary instructions from 
the Chief Secretary in this regard.
