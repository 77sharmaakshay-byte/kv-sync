COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSSNo.533 of 2025
WPSS No. 53 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
(through V.C.)
1. Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, learned counsel for the 
petitioner in the connected writ petition.
3. Mr. P.S. Bisht, learned Additional C.S.C. for the 
State.
4. The Division Bench of this court by order dated 
01.09.2025 passed in WPPIL No. 168 of 2025 directed to 
expedite the matter since the zero transfer may affect 
people who are posted in hard areas to move to 
accessible areas and it is also a blessing in disguise to 
the people who are now posted in accessible areas and 
who are avoiding rendering duty in remote areas.
5. While hearing WPSS No. 533 and 53 of 2025 this 
court observed in its order dated 09.04.2025 on pointing 
by the Secretary Education that in implementing the 
Transfer Act particularly the transfer on the basis of the 
classification of Sugam and Durgam the department is 
facing so many problems in computation of the marts 
of the candidates and not only this even for 
computation of marks the man power are being 
engaged and this difficulty has been pointed out to the 
Secretary Karmik.
6. This court also further observed that the transfer 
is an exigency of service, therefore, in certain 
contingencies the transfer can be made except on the 
basis of the classification of Sugam and Durgam. There 
is no direction of this court to declare the present 
session as a zero transfer except that transfer should 
not be made on the basis of the classification of Sugam 
and Durgam.
7. Mr. P.S. Bisht, learned Additional Chief Standing 
Counsel apprise to this court that a committee of 
twelve members was constituted chaired by Hon’ble Education Minister and the matter has been referred to 
the Karmik and the decision of the Karmik is still 
awaited.
8. On the request of Mr. P.S. Bisht, the matter is 
posted on 08.10.2025 so that he may get the necessary 
instructions from the Karmik department.
9. It is made clear that the department is free to 
transfer the incumbent except on the basis of 
classification of Sugam and Durgam, which this court 
already observed in the order dated 09.04.2025.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
