COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 53 and 533 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
(through V.C.)
1. Mr. Bhagwat Mehra and Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, 
learned counsel for the petitioner. 
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing Counsel 
and Mr. Tarun Mohan, learned Brief Holder for the 
State.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner apprised to 
this Court that against the order dated 13.02.2025, the 
State went in a Special Appeal No. 383 of 2025. 
4. It is pointed out by the learned counsel for the 
petitioner that while filing the SPA No. 383 of 2025, 
subsequent order passed by this Court dated 12.09.2025 
was suppressed by by Mr. Ajay Kumar Nauriyal, 
Director, Elementary Education. 
5. On the other side, Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned 
Standing Counsel for the State, pointed out that in 
WPPIL No. 168 of 2025, the Division Bench of this 
Court on 01.09.2025 requested the learned Single Judge 
to expedite these petitions since zero transfer may 
affect people who are posted in hard areas to move to 
accessible areas and it is also a blessing in disguise to 
the people who are now posted in accessible areas and 
who are avoiding rendering duty in the remote areas. 
6. It is relevant to mention here that while passing 
the subsequent order dated 12.09.2025, this Court 
already took note of the order passed by the Division 
Bench dated 01.09.2025 in WPPIL 168 of 2025 and 
clearly observed that Department is free to transfer the 
employees except on the basis of classification of 
Sugam and Durgam.
7. Not only this, in paragraph 6 of the order it was 
also observed that there is no direction to declare the 
present session as a zero transfer. 
8. While passing the order on 12.09.2025, Mr. P.S. 
Bisht, learned Standing Counsel for the State also 
apprised to this Court that a Committee of 12 Members 
has been constituted headed by the Hon’ble Education 
Minister and now, the matter has been referred to the Karmik Department.
9. Now, Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing 
Counsel for the State, apprise to this Court that the 
Committee took a decision that classification of Sugam 
and Durgam depends upon the category of the 
institutions. Copy of the minutes is placed before this 
Court, which is taken on record.
10. List on 08.12.2025 for further arguments. 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
