ORDER : (per Mr. Manoj Kumar Gupta C. J.)
1. The Ministry of AYUSH, Government of India by 
letter dated 08.08.2025 extended the timeline for 
completing the admissions under the Central Nomination 
Quota by further period of 45 days from the date of 
issuance of the letter.
2. In the instant case, the petitioner filed application 
for being given admission under the Central Government 
Nomination Pool on 24.10.2025 i.e. much after the time line
prescribed by letter dated 08.08.2025 had expired.
3. Even the State Government initiated the process 
for inviting applications under the Central Government 
Nomination Pool for the first time on 25.10.2025 by issuing 
advertisement in newspapers. 
14. It is also noteworthy that, in case the process of 
nomination is not completed within the timeline prescribed 
by letter dated 08.08.2025, option was given to the donor 
college authorities to admit NEET qualified students under 
intimation to the Ministry.
5. The donor institute i.e. respondent no.4 in its 
communication to the National Commission for Indian 
System of Medicine has taken the stand that the 
nomination for admission under the Central Pool was 
received in the office of Principal for the first time on 
22.04.2026 which was after the expiry of the extended 
period for completing the process of nominations i.e. 
21.09.2025. Therefore, admission was not given to the 
petitioner.
6. There is no material on record to indicate 
whether the timeline prescribed by the Ministry of AYUSH 
for completing the process of nomination under the Central 
Pool was ever extended after the letter dated 08.08.2025.
7. Accordingly, we direct respondent no.6 to clarify 
whether the time line for completing the process of 
nomination under the Central Nomination Pool was ever 
extended after the letter dated 08.08.2025 and whether the 
seat in question was filled up by the donor institute or it 
continued to remain vacant.
8. As it is stated before us that last date for 
admission was extended upto 23.01.2026, on the basis of 
2the letter of Ministry of AYUSH dated 13.01.2026, 
therefore, we direct respondent no.6 to bring the said letter 
of the Ministry of AYUSH on record so that it could be 
ascertained whether thereunder the time frame for 
completing the process of nomination under the Central 
Pool was also extended or it was only for the purposes of 
filling up the vacant seats on the basis of spot counseling.
9. Learned counsel appearing on behalf of the 
Ministry of AYUSH also undertakes to clarify the said 
aspects by filing an affidavit.
10. List on 18.06.2026.
