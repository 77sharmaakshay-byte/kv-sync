1. Mr. Shariq Khurshid, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. J.C. Pandey, learned Standing Counsel for the 
State of Uttarakhand/ respondent nos.1 to 3.
3. Mr. Manoj Kumar, learned Standing Counsel for the 
Union of India/ respondent no.5.
4. Mr. Ramji Shrivastava, learned counsel along with 
Mr. Gaurav Nagpal, learned counsel for respondent no.6.
5. As per office report dated 28.04.2026 notice sent to
respondent no.4 by speed post has been duly delivered. 
However, none is present on behalf of the said respondent.
6. Shri Ramji Shrivastava, learned counsel appearing on 
behalf of respondent no.6 states that he has received 
instructions from respondent no.4, according to which, the 
timeline for giving admission was 15.05.2025 and it was 
extended by the Ministry of AYUSH by a further period of 
45 days i.e. upto 21.09.2025.
7. By that time, no nomination was received from the 
Government of Uttarakhand in respect of admission of the 
petitioner.
8. The communication about the nomination of the 
petitioner under the State Quota in the Central Pool was 
received in the office of Principal for the first time on 
22.04.2026 much after the expiry of the extended date i.e. 
21.09.2025 and it is for the said reason that the college 
has not given admission to the petitioner.
9. Shri Manoj Kumar, learned counsel appearing on behalf of respondent no.5 is directed to obtain instructions 
from the said respondent as to whether there could be any 
relaxation in the timeline in the facts of the instant case 
and also bring on record the notification by which timelines 
have been prescribed and the basis therefor.
10. List as fresh on 12.05.2026.
