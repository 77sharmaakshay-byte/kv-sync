(2) Mr. Bhuwan Bhatt, learned cousnel 
for respondent nos. 1/1 and 1/3.
(3) As per Office report, there is delay 
of four days in filing restoration 
application. Learned counsel for 
applicant submits that the order, 
restoration whereof has been sought,
was passed on 09.08.2023; applicant 
applied for certified copy of the said
order on 11.08.2023; certified copy was 
prepared on 16.08.2023 which was 
delivered on 22.08.2023 and restoration 
application was filed on 12.09.2023. 
Thus, he submits that six days period 
spent in preparing certified copy of the 
order has to be excluded while 
computing the period of limitation. He 
further submits that restoration 
application was filed on 34th day from 
which six days time spent in preparing 
certified copy has to be excluded, thus it 
is to be deemed that the application was 
filed within time. He further submits 
that limitation for filing restoration 
application is 30 days, therefore, 
restoration application was filed in time.
(4) This Court find substance in the 
contention made by learned counsel for 
the applicant and the objection raised by Registry is overruled. 
(5) For the reason indicated in the 
restoration applications, the same (MCC 
No. 9579 of 2023 and MCC No. 3433 of 
2023) are allowed. 
(6) The petitions are restored to their
original numbers, along with interim 
order(s), if any.
(7) List this case on 28.12.2023.
