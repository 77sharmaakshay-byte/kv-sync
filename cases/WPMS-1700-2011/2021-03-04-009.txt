In WPMS No. 1700 of 2011, respondent No. 
1/1 Mohd. Iliyas is reported to have died on 
09.01.2020, hence subsequent to filing of an 
application under Order 22 Rule 10A by the 
respondent, the petitioner has filed a Substitution 
Application (CLMA No. 3422/2020) along with the 
Delay Condonation Application (CLMA No. 
3424/2020).
Similarly, in WPMS No. 213 of 2007, the 
heirs of deceased respondent No. 1/1 Mohd. Iliyas
is being sought to be substituted under the same 
facts and circumstances. 
After having considered the reasons given 
therein, since there is no delay as such in filing the 
substitution (CLMA No. 3427/2020), the Delay 
Condonation Application (CLMA No. 3425/2020), 
no orders are required.
The Substitution Application preferred by the 
petitioner to bring the heirs of deceased respondent 
No. 1 on record would stand allowed. 
The learned counsel for the petitioner may 
file the amended memo of parties to the writ
petition within a period of 10 days from today.
Accordingly, all the applications, except the 
Stay Vacation Application stand disposed of.
List this matter in the week commencing 
30.03.2021.
