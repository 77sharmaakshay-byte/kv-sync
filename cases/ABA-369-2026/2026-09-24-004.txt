Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Ankur Sharma, learned counsel for 
the applicant.
2. Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned AGA with Mr. 
Nikhil Bisht, learned Brief Holder for the 
State.
3. The instant anticipatory bail application 
is moved by the applicant Harvinder Singh, 
S/o Late Gurudev Singh for seeking 
anticipatory bail in reference to FIR dated 
28.05.2025, bearing Case Crime No. 258 of 
2025 lodged by one Rajesh Agrawal wherein 
four persons, namely, Makan Singh Negi, 
Usman, Subodh Pokhariyal and Jagmohan 
Singh Chauhan have been implicated for the 
offences punishable under Sections 318(4) 
and 61(2) of BNS 2023.
4. It is argued by the learned counsel for 
the applicant that the applicant has nothing 
to do with the allegation as alleged in the FIR 
since he gave power of attorney in favour of 
Makan Singh Negi on 06.06.2024 and 
subsequently he revoked the same on 
31.08.2024, therefore, after revocation of the 
power of attorney Makan Singh Negi has no 
authority to sell the property. He further 
submits that proper communication of 
revocation was also given to Makan Singh 
despite this he without informing to the 
applicant executed the sale deed on 
23.12.2024. He submits that though the 
applicant is not named in the FIR and he has 
nothing to do with the allegation as alleged in 
the FIR since he already revoked the power of attorney despite this when he moved 
anticipatory bail before the Sessions Court the 
police submitted a report that the applicant is
also wanted in the present case but he is 
absconding. Apart from this, he submits that 
one of the person Jagmohan Singh who is 
named in the FIR is also granted interim bail
in ABA No. 752 of 2025. Apart from this, he 
submits that, in fact, on close scrutiny of the 
FIR the allegations are nothing but appears to 
be civil in nature.
5. Learned counsel for the State prays for 
and is granted four weeks’ time to file counter 
affidavit. Two weeks’ thereafter is granted to 
the applicant to file rejoinder affidavit.
6. Taking into consideration that other co￾accused namely Jagmohan Singh who is 
named in the FIR is already granted interim 
bail, this Court is of the view that the 
applicant is in better footing, since he is not 
named in the FIR, therefore, he also deserves 
same protection. 
7. List in the week commencing 02.11.2026 
along with ABA No. 752 of 2025.
8. In such view of the matter, for an 
interim measure, the investigation may go on, 
however , it is directed that in the event of 
arrest of the applicant, he shall be released on 
interim bail on executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two reliable sureties of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the investigating 
officer/arresting officer subject to the 
following condition:
(i) Applicant shall cooperate with the 
investigating agency and he shall make 
himself available for interrogation by 
investigating officer as and when required.
(ii) Applicant shall not directly or indirectly 
make any inducement, threat or promise to 
any person acquainted with the facts of the 
case.
(iii) Applicant shall not leave the country 
without prior permission of this Court.
