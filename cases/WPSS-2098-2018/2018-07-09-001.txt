Heard learned counsel for the 
parties. 
It is the submission of learned 
counsel for the petitioner that 
petitioner is the widow of Rifleman, 
who sacrificed his life for nation in the 
year 1985. It is also submitted that 
petitioner has already served 14 years 
in Durgam area. He further submitted 
that petitioner’s mother-in-law, which 
is aged about 83 years, is suffering 
from acute health problems and is also 
suffering from complete memory loss. 
He also submitted that petitioner’s 
mother-in-law is undergoing treatment 
in Sri Mahant Indresh Hospital, 
Dehradun. 
Admit. 
As prayed, six weeks time is 
granted to the respondent/State to file 
counter affidavit. 
List thereafter. 
Having considered the 
submission of learned counsel for the 
parties and after perusal of the 
material brought on record, as an 
interim measure, it is directed that 
during the pendency of present writ 
petition, effect and operation of the impugned transfer order dated 
25.6.2018 shall remain stayed qua the 
petitioner. 
Stay application (CLMA 
No.9627/2018) stands disposed of. 
Let a certified copy of this order 
be issued to the parties within 24 
hours.
