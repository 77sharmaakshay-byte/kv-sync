3. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
4. By means of this writ petition, petitioner 
has sought the indulgence of this Court for a 
direction to the respondent to grant seniority 
to the petitioner from the date of file sent for 
approval by the Committee of Management to 
the Chief Education Officer, District U.S. 
Nagar.
5. It is the case of the petitioner that 
petitioner participated in the selection process 
for the post of Assistant Teacher, L.T. Grade 
(Science), pursuant to the advertisement 
dated 22.05.2015 and the petitioner was 
selected and his file for approval was sent to 
the competent authority on 05.04.2017. The 
approval was granted to the petitioner on 
20.05.2022. 
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that delay in getting the approval 
from the competent authority was due to the 
reason that there was a ban in operation for 
appointment in the private schools. Now, 
petitioner has filed this writ petition for the 
seniority to be given from the date when file 
was sent to the approval after his selection.
7. It is strenuously argued by learned 2
counsel for the petitioner that the similarly 
situated persons, who have selected pursuant 
to the advertisement as referred hereinabove, 
were granted the seniority even during the 
period on which the ban was imposed upon 
the appointment. 
8. Issue notice to the respondent nos.4 & 
5.
9. Steps to be taken within a week.
10. Respondent(s) may file their counter 
affidavit(s) within a period of six weeks.
11. List this matter on 19.02.2024.
