The petitioner, who is a Registrar of 
Cooperative Societies, has put a challenge to 
the impugned award, as it was rendered by the 
learned Labour Court in Adjudication Case 
No.10 of 2020, “Rajendra Singh Kandiyal Vs. 
Secretary/ General Manager, District 
Cooperative Bank Ltd. & Another”.
A reference under Section 4K which was 
sought to be answered was as against the 
action of respondent-bank of discontinuance of 
service of respondent no.1 w.e.f.19.11.2018.
The learned Labour Court has answered 
the reference against the employer. But, 
however, while parting with the judgment, the 
Labour Court has exceeded beyond the 
question of reference, which was made to it 
about fixation of personal liability for recovering the amount from the salary of the responsible 
person i.e. employer no.2-the petitioner herein.
This part of the award rendered by the 
Tribunal on 04.01.2023 is beyond the scope of 
reference and beyond its competence also to
direct recovery of an amount from the salary of 
the employer. So far as this part is concerned,
that would be kept in abeyance.
The respondents, who are represented by 
their respective counsels, may file their counter 
affidavit within a period of three weeks.
List thereafter.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
