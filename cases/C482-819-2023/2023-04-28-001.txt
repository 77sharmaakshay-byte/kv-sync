The present C482 application arises out of 
a matrimonial dispute, wherein, the present 
applicants have been summoned to be tried for 
the offences under section 498A, 323 and 504 of 
IPC, and ¾ of the Dowry Prohibition Act. 
Though, it is stated at the bar, that there 
happens to be a corresponding proceedings for 
restitution of conjugal rights which is pending, in 
which the respondent/complainant has not put in 
appearance, despite of service of the notice.
This Court is of the view, that since it
being a private matrimonial dispute, an effort for 
mediation is required to be made in order to patch 
up the controversy/misunderstanding between the 
parties, and to reunite the family.
Issue administrative notice to the 
respondent no.2. The Registry is directed to 
ensure the service of the notice on the respondent 
no.2, and submit its report by the next date fixed.
Respondent no.2, would appear in person 
on the date to be so fixed by the Registry, so 
would be the applicants will also appear before 
this Court, in order to make an effort for mediation.
As a consequence thereto, till the next date 
fixed or till the efforts for mediation are made by 
this Court, the proceedings of the Criminal Case 
No.5865 of 2022, “State Vs. Sameer Ahmed and 
others”, pending consideration before the court of 
4th Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Dehradun, would not be given effect to. 
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
