COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
09.06.2023 C482 No. 819 of 2023
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Shorabh Sharma, Advocate, for 
the applicants.
Mr. Atul Kumar Shah, Deputy 
Advocate General, along with Mrs. Mamta 
Joshi, Brief Holder, for the State.
Mr. Syed Nadim, Advocate, for the 
respondent No. 2.
This Court, while issuing notices to 
the respondent No. 2, by an order dated 
28.04.2023, was initially of the opinion, that 
since the issue arises out of a matrimonial 
discord, there could be a possibility of 
settlement. The respondent No. 2 was 
noticed and she has put in appearance 
through Mr. Syed Nadim, Advocate.
This Court has interacted with her. 
She has expressly made a statement before 
this Court, that owing to the given set of 
circumstances, there is no possibility of 
settlement and there were various 
allegations which were levelled by her 
against the present applicants.
In that eventuality, there is no 
scope for referring the matter before the 
Mediator. The respondent who has now put 
in appearance, may file counter affidavit in 
furtherance of an order dated 28.04.2023, 
within a period of three weeks from today.
The interim order, already granted 
on 28.04.2023 would continue to operate till the next date of listing.
Looking to the misbehaviour and 
the un-parliamentary vulgar dialect used by 
the respondent in her addresses, in the 
Court proceedings, this Court draws a suo 
moto contempt proceeding against the 
respondent. She is called upon to file a reply 
within a period of three weeks and would 
appear in person for the purposes of 
framing of a charge, if the reply is not found 
satisfactory.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
