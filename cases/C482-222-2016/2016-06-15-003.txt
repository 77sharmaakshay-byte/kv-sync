Pursuant to the First Information Report, lodged on 
the direction of the learned Single Judge of this Court in 
WPMS No. 713 of 2015, criminal proceedings have been 
initiated against the applicants in which charge-sheet has 
been filed against all the applicants and they have been 
summoned by the court concerned. 
The order of the learned Single Judge of this Court 
is under challenged in special appeal before the Division 
Bench of this Court, which is still pending before this 
Court. Learned counsel for the applicants would argue 
that it would be in the interest of justice that till the 
disposal of the said special appeals, and during the 
proceedings in these petitions before this Court, further 
proceedings before the court below may be stayed. 
Considering all the facts and circumstances of the 
case and in the interest of justice further proceedings of 
Criminal Case no. 85 of 2016 pending in the court of 
learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, Nainital shall remain 
stayed till the next date of listing. 
List these matters after three weeks in the daily 
cause list. 
Meanwhile, the State shall file its counter affidavit 
in these matters.
