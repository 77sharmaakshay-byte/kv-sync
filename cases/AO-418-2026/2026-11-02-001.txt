Mr. Siddhartha Singh, learned counsel

2. Brief facts of the case are that the 
appellant herein is the plaintiff of original 
Suit No.272 of 2026, instituted for seeking 
permanent injunction against the respondent
/defendant. The appellant/plaintiff 
purchased two piece of land by virtue of sale 
deed dated 30.02.2025 Khasra No.2284 and 
Khasra No.2285 measuring 1550 sq. mtr. 
wherein he raised the construction with 
boundary wall.
3. Consequently, plaintiff/appellant 
instituted suit wherein he moved an 
application for seeking temporary injunction 
i.e. Application 6C(2), however the same has 
been rejected and being aggrieved, the 
instant appeal is preferred. 
4. It is argued by learned counsel for the 
appellant that the finding as recorded in para 
13 is based on presumption that the plaintiff 
purchased the land from earlier vendor in 
excess though there is no adverse finding 
with regard to the possession. He submits 
that while considering the application for 
grant of temporary injunction, the trial court should focus on the possession of the parties. 
5. On perusal of para 13, it reveals that 
there is no dispute on possession, therefore, 
this Court is of the view that the balance of 
convenience lies in favour of the 
plaintiff/appellant.
6. Issue notice to the respondents. 
7. Steps by 04.11.2026.
8. Let the respondent may file their 
objection within two weeks.
9. Put up this matter on 30.11.2026. 
10. In the meantime, the parties are 
directed to maintain status quo over the suit 
property as on date.
