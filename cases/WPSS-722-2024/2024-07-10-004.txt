COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.722 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Rajendra Arya, learned 
counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned 
Standing Counsel for the State.
3. Mr. B.D. Pandey, learned counsel 
for respondent No.4.
4. By the instant petition, the 
petitioner is praying for a direction that 
he may be permitted to participate in 
the process of document verification for 
the post of Assistant Teacher pursuant 
to the Advertisement dated 13.10.2020 
since he qualified the written 
examination.
5. It is submitted that a tentative 
provisional merit list of candidates for 
document verification was issued on
17.01.2024 and the name of the 
petitioner was found place at serial 
No.18 under physically handicapped 
category. Subsequently, another revised 
provisional merit list of candidates for 
document verification was issued on 
16.02.2024, however, the name of the 
petitioner is not found in the said 
provisional list.
6. It is also submitted by learned 
counsel for the petitioner that no 
communication was given to the 
petitioner about the earlier provisional
merit list for document verification 
which was issued on 17.01.2024 and 
even otherwise, he was not aware about the said provisional list and he could 
not appear before the Commission
(UKSSSC) for the purpose of document 
verification.
7. Mr. B.D. Pandey, learned counsel 
for the Commission submits that on 
three occasions, the opportunity was 
given to the selected candidates, who 
were found place in the provisional
merit list, but the petitioner has not 
turned up for document verification and 
that is the reason that in subsequent 
revised provisional merit list, the name 
of the petitioner does not place.
8. On instruction Mr. B.D. Pandey 
inform to this Court that now the final 
selected list of candidates has been sent 
to the Government and pursuant to the 
select list, the Government issued the 
appointment letters.
9. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned 
Standing Counsel for the State submits 
that he may be given 24 Hours to get 
instruction in the matter.
10. Put up this matter on 12.07.2024 
as fresh. By that time, learned Standing 
Counsel may get instruction in the 
matter, whether the appointment letter 
has been issued to the selected 
candidates pursuant to the selection list 
issued by the Recruiting Agency and 
whether still any vacancy is lying 
vacant.
