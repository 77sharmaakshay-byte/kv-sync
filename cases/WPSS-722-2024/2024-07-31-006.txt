COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.722 of 2024
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
1. Mr. Rajendra Arya, learned counsel for 
the petitioner.
2. Mr. S.M.S. Mehta, learned Brief Holder for 
the State of Uttarakhand.
3. Mr. B.D. Pande, learned counsel for 
respondent no.4-Commission.
4. It is the case of the petitioner that 
petitioner participated in the selection process 
pursuant to the advertisement dated 
13.10.2020 wherein he was declared 
successful. Since petitioner is resident of 
District Pithoragarh and lives in village Bhatari, 
Pithoragarh, a remote place, he could not get 
any information regarding the document 
verification to be conducted by the respondent￾Commission, therefore, he failed to appear in 
the document verification.
5. This Court vide previous order has called 
for instructions from respondent-Commission. 
6. On instructions, it is submitted by learned 
counsel for the Commission that individually the 
candidates are not informed about the 
document verification rather the information 
regarding document verification is published in 
the official website of the UKSSSC (Uttarakhand 
Subordinate Service Selection Commission) and 
the candidates are supposed to be vigilant 
about the date of document verification. 
7. Learned State Counsel on instructions, 
submits that since the name of petitioner has 
not been recommended for appointment, 
therefore, he has not been offered 
appointment.
8. In view of the fact that petitioner is a 
resident of remote village in District 
Pithoragarh, this matter requires deliberation.9. Learned counsel for Commission as well 
as learned State Counsel are directed to file 
their counter affidavits within a period of six 
weeks.
10. Two weeks’ time thereafter is granted to 
the counsel for the petitioner to file rejoinder 
affidavit.
11. List on 04.10.2024.
