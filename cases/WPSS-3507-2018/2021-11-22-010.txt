COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.3507 of 2018
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Ramji Srivastava, Advocate for the 
petitioner.
Mr. V.K. Kapruwan, Standing Counsel for 
Union of India.
Having heard the learned counsel for the 
parties at length, a very peculiar situation which has 
emerged for consideration in the writ petition, is 
that the appellate authority has even travelled 
beyond the scope of the dismissal order, by 
extracting an act of alleged mis-behaviour of the 
petitioner with the female employee i.e. Parineeta 
Singh, and as a consequence of which he was 
initially suspended on 25.08.2011, whether the then 
set of allegation was one of the subject matter of 
charge sheet, on which the inquiry was conducted 
or not, there is no material on record as such 
brought by either of the counsels, to show that the 
conduct alleged in the suspension of 25.08.2011, 
was also a subject matter of inquiry before the 
disciplinary authority, as well as the appellate 
authority, who has passed the impugned orders.
The respondents are directed to supply the 
copy of the charge sheet, on the basis of which the 
proceedings were held against the petitioner and the 
order of dismissal dated 31.08.2012 was passed. 
He may file the copy of the charge sheet within a 
period of two weeks from today.
List this writ petition in the week 
commencing 06.12.2021. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.) 22.11.2021
