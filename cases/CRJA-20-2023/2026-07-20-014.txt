The instant jail appeal has been preferred against 
judgment and order dated 13.12.2022/14.12.2022, passed in 
Special Sessions Trial No.09 of 2020, State v. Kundan Patwal, and 
Special Sessions Trial No.26 of 2020, State v. Deepak Patwal, by 
the court of Additional Sessions Judge/FTC/ Special Judge 
(POCSO), Haldwani, District Nainital. By it, the appellant Kundan 
Patwal has been convicted under Section 5(l)(m)/6 of the 
Protection of Children from Sexual Offences Act, 2012 (“the Act”), 
whereas, the appellant Deepak Patwal has been convicted under 
Section 5(m)/6 of the Act, and sentenced accordingly. The 
appellants seek bail during the pendency of the appeal. 
2. This appeal has already been admitted. 
3. List in due course for final hearing. 
4. Heard on Third Bail Application (IA) No.4 of 2026.
5. The victim lost her father in the month of 
February, 2019, in a road accident. She was staying with her 
mother. The mother of the victim was on some job. On 
16.01.2020, when the victim’s mother had a holiday, the victim’s 2
mother had gone to market along with her younger son. When at 
1:30 p.m., she returned, she found that the door is bolted from 
inside. She knocked at the door. It is the appellant Deepak Patwal, 
who opened the door. Upon questioning, he muffled the mouth of 
the mother of the victim and left that place. Subsequently, the 
mother of the victim revealed that the salwar of the victim was 
untied. The victim was 14 years of age. The victim revealed that 
the appellant Deepak Patwal had done galat kaam with her, and 
his father, the appellant Kundan Patwal, had also done galat 
kaam with her.
6. Learned counsel for the appellants submits that 
the entire case is false; in her statement, PW2, the victim, in Para 
No.27 has stated that her statement recorded under Section 164 
of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973, was given at the behest 
of the police; in Para Nos. 39, 40, 49 and 68 of her statement, 
PW2, the victim, has stated about their dispute with the family of 
the appellants; there was enmity; the appellants have been falsely 
implicated; there have been no injury on the person of the victim 
in the medical report; merely because hymen is absent, the 
appellants may not be kept behind bars. 
7. Learned State Counsel submits that the victim is a 
young girl; she has been consistent about the act that has been 
committed by the appellants. 
8. It is a stage of bail post conviction. Much of the 
discussion is not expected of. Arguments are being appreciated 
with the caveat that any observation made in this order shall have 
no bearing at any subsequent stage of the proceedings. 
9. It is true that the victim in her statement has 
stated that her statement under Section 164 of the Code was 
given by her as stated by the police, but in Para Nos. 50 and 51 of 
her statement, she has categorically stated that she is not telling a 3
lie, and it is wrong to say that the appellants have not done any 
galat kaam with her. The victim has been consistent as to how the 
incident took place. In fact, the informant had once seen the 
appellant Deepak Patwal coming out from her house. 
10. Having considered, this Court does not see any 
ground, which may entitle the appellants to bail. Accordingly, the 
bail application deserves to be rejected.
11. The bail application is rejected.
