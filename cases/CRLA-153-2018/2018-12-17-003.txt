CRMA No. 959/2018 (Bail Application)
IN
CRLA No. 153 of 2018
Hon’ble N.S. Dhanik, J.
Mr. Rajendra Kotiyal and Mr. Ravi 
Joshi, Advocates for the appellant.
Mr. P.S. Bohra, A.G.A. along with 
Mr. Siddhartha Bisht, Brief Holder for 
the State of Uttarakhand.
Heard learned counsel for the 
parties.
Learned counsel for the appellant 
submitted that there is no independent 
witness of the alleged recovery and 
quantity of charas allegedly recovered 
from the appellant is below the 
commercial quantity. He further 
submitted that, while arresting the 
appellant, mandatory provisions of the 
N.D.P.S. Act have not been complied 
with. He also submitted that appellant 
was on bail during trial and he never 
misused the same. 
Considering the facts and 
circumstances of the case and
submissions of learned counsel for the 
parties, the bail application is hereby 
allowed. Let the appellant-Sagar Thapa
be released on bail on the same terms 
and conditions on which he was 
granted bail during trial, subject to 
deposit of 50% of the fine i.e. 
Rs.50,000/- imposed by the trial Court.
Bail application stands disposed 
of.
 (N.S. Dhanik, J.)
