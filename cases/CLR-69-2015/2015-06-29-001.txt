CLR No. 69 of 2015
Hon’ble Alok Singh, J.
Mr. Dharmendra Barthwal, Advocate for 
the revisionist. 
Learned counsel for the revisionist 
submits that suit for eviction was decreed 
against the father of the defendant / respondent, 
herein, wherein he was licensee, thereafter, 
same property was taken on rent by the 
defendant – respondent, herein, from the 
revisionists admitting the revisionists as 
landlord. He further contends that finding of the 
trial court that relation of landlord – tenant is 
not established, on the face of it, is wrong. 
Issue notice to respondent by registered 
post AD in addition to normal mode of service, 
returnable within three weeks on steps being 
taken by the revisionist within a week. 
In view of the fact that certified copy of 
the impugned judgment has been placed in other 
connected revision i.e. CLR No. 68 of 2015, 
therefore, revisionist is exempted in filing the 
certified copy of the impugned judgment. 
CLMA No. 7204 of 2015 stands 
disposed of accordingly.
