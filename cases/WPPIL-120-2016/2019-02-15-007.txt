A recall application is filed by the Indian 
Medical Association seeking review of the order 
passed by a Division Bench of this Court in Writ 
Petition (PIL) No.120 of 2016 dated 14.08.2018. 
Mr. A.K. Bansal, learned counsel for the 
respondent-writ petitioner, would submit that an 
application was filed earlier seeking review of the said 
order (Review Application No.1240 of 2018 in Writ 
Petition (PIL) No.120 of 2018); and the Division 
Bench had dismissed the said review application by its 
order dated 14.09.2018. 
While Mr. Ajay Veer Pundir, learned counsel 
for the review petitioner, would submit that the earlier 
review petition was not filed by the Indian Medical 
Association but by someone else, this Court would not 
entertain a second review application, albeit at the 
instance of another party, when an earlier application, 
seeking review of the very same order, has been 
dismissed. Any grievance which the petitioner may 
have, regarding the order of the Division Bench of this 
Court in Writ Petition (PIL) No.120 of 2018 dated 
14.08.2018, cannot be adjudicated by way of a second 
recall/review petition. 
The application seeking leave to file the recall 
petition, and for review, is dismissed. 
As a result all other applications are also 
dismissed.
