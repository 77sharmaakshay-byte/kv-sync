(3) Petitioners were appointed against 
non-teaching posts in Gurukul Kangri 
Vishwavidyalaya, a deemed-to-be 
University, in courses run on Self Financed 
Scheme. According to petitioners, they are 
entitled to various allowances, which 
various other employees of University are 
getting, however the same is being denied 
to petitioners. By means of this writ 
petition, petitioners have sought the 
following reliefs:
“a. Issue a writ, order or direction in 
the nature of Mandamus directing the respondents 
to grant benefit of Transport Allowance, LTC, 
Children Education Allowance, Dearness 
Allowances, Medical Allowances￾Fixed/Reimbursement, Dress Allowances etc., and 
Arrear comprising Salary Fixation, Retirement 
benefits to the petitioners as per UGC/AICTE/PCI 
norms. 
b. Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of Mandamus directing the respondents to 
grant benefit of other service benefits as per 
UGC/AICTE/PCI norms for employees appointed 2026:UHC:59
on or before SFS Rules 2020, on the permanent 
basis and further issued direction to the 
respondents not to implement/change the service 
condition of the petitioners in future 
retrospectively.” 
(4) Since petitioners have sought a 
writ of mandamus and they have not made 
any representation so far to the competent 
authority, therefore, this Court thinks that 
ends of justice would be met if petitioners 
are permitted to make representation, 
highlighting their grievance, to the 
competent authority, who may be directed 
to take decision thereupon. 
(5) Writ petitions are, accordingly, 
disposed of granting liberty to petitioners to 
make representation to Registrar of the 
University. If petitioners make 
representation within two weeks from 
today, decision thereupon shall be taken, as 
per law, within ten weeks thereafter.
