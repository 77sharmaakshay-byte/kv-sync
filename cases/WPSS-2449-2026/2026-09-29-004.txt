UKHC010166052026
WPSS No.2449 of 2026
Saurabh Kumar and others ………Petitioners
Vs.
State of Uttarakhand and others ………Respondents
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Aditya Singh, learned counsel and Mr. P.S. Uniyal, 
learned counsel holding brief of Mr. A.M. Saklani, learned 
counsel for the petitioners.
2. Mr. N.S. Pundir, learned D.A.G. for the State.
3. Mr. Pankaj Miglani, learned counsel for respondent no.3.
4. By means of this writ petition, petitioners have 
challenged requisition dated 30.07.2026, annexure no.3 to the 
writ petition, sent to respondent-Public Service Commission 
for supplying vacancy on the post of Junior Engineers in 
respondent-P.W.D. department.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioners contends that 
petitioners have been working as contractual Junior Engineers 
with respondent-P.W.D. for the last more than ten years; when 
their services as contractual engagement were terminated,
various writ petitions were filed before this Court challenging 
the illegal and arbitrary action on part of the State.
6. The said bunch of writ petition came to be decided by 
this Court vide judgment and order dated 10.08.2026 in WPSS 
No.321 of 2026, Prasoon Nautiyal Vs. State of Uttarakhand and 
other and batch, and the writ petitions were disposed of by 
directing respondents to undertake process of regularization of 
petitioners of those writ petitions ignoring earlier rejection of 
their claim for regularization.
7. Now without protecting petitioners, State has send 
requisition against same vacancies on which petitioners are 2
working as contractual Junior Engineers for the last more 
than ten years. If the vacancies would be supplied, petitioners 
would be thrown out of their job.
8. It is contended by learned counsel for petitioners that 
petitioners have been working against sanctioned post and 
they are holder of requisite qualification for Junior Engineer 
with P.W.D. and further they have gained experience of more 
than ten years which is no doubt beneficial for discharge of 
their duties with the respondent-Department.
9. Per contra, learned counsel for the State submitted that 
requisition was issued for fresh appointment as petitioners’ 
case is not covered under the Daily Wage, Work-Charged, 
Contract, Fixed-Pay, Part-Time and Ad-hoc Employees 
Regularization (Amendment) Rules, 2025 promulgated on 
05.12.2025 as they have not completed ten years of service on 
04.12.2018.
10. He also submits that the order passed by this Court in 
the aforesaid writ petition may be challenged by State by filing 
special appeals.
11. Respondent(s) may file counter affidavit(s) within four 
weeks.
12. List this case on 27.11.2026.
13. In the meantime, the impugned requisition dated 
30.07.2026 shall remain stayed. 
 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
