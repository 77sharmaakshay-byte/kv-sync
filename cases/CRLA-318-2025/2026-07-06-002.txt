 Heard.
 The appellant proposes to challenge the 
instant appeal against the judgment and order 
dated 16.04.2024, passed in Sessions Trial 
No.175 of 2022, State of Uttarakhand Vs. Ram 
Prasad, by the court of Additional Sessions 
Judge/FTSC, Haridwar, by which, the 
respondent no.2 has been acquitted of the 
charge under Sections 376(2)(n), 323, 376 IPC 
read with Sections 511 and 506 IPC. There is 
delay of 336 days. 
 Learned counsel for the appellant submits 
that the husband of the appellant is a labour 
class person; due to paucity of funds, the 
appeal could not be filed on time. 
 Learned counsel for the respondent no.2 
submits that there is no ground to condone 
the delay; there is huge delay of 336 days.  Having considered, the grounds of delay 
are good enough to condone the delay. 
Therefore, delay in filing the appeal is 
condoned. 
 Delay condonation application is allowed.
 Lower court record is already on record.
 List on 10.08.2026 for hearing on 
admission.
