Issue notice to respondent no.1. 
List after four weeks to enable the respondents to 
file counter affidavit. 
Heard on interim relief application no. 01 of 
2021. 
Petitioner seeks that respondents be restrained to 
make promotion pursuant to the seniority list dated 
21.11.2020. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that as 
of now, there is no such proposal for promotion, 
therefore, this Court sees no reason to consider on 
this application, at this stage. 
However, as and when, the occasion arises, the 
petitioner is always free to move fresh application. 
Accordingly the interim relief application is 
rejected.
