7. The Court had required learned State counsel to 
inform this Court as to why despite specific direction of 
the Court, the order of release has not been passed. In 
fact, on the last occasion, it was stated that State 
authorities are in clear defiance of the law laid down by 
the Hon’ble Supreme Court; type casting the convicts 
and denying them the premature release.8. In fact, today, it is also brought to the notice of the 
Court that on 15.05.2026, the Hon’ble Supreme Court in 
the case of “Rohit Chaturvedi vs. State of Uttarakhand 
and others” Writ Petition (Criminal) No.446 of 2023
further reiterated that heinousness of the offences is no 
ground to deny premature release of a convict.
9. Today, learned State counsel gives a statement that 
the matter is still under consideration of the State 
Government. 
10. The Court wanted to know as to whether any 
meeting of the Committee has been held in between the 
last date of hearing and today. The answer is in 
negative. Who is deliberating upon it? When is the 
meeting of the Committee now scheduled so as to 
consider the cases of pre-mature release as per the law 
laid down by the Hon’ble Supreme Court and the 
directions issued by this Court in WPPIL No.50 of 
2024?
11. The State counsel seeks time to get instructions on 
that aspect.
12. List these matters on 25.05.2026 along with 
WPCRL No.150 of 2025.
