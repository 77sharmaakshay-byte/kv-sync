 Heard on Civil Misc. Recall Application 
No.165 of 2023.
 This appeal is filed by the 
appellant/defendant against the eviction, in 
which, on 04.01.2023 status quo order was 
passed. But subsequently, when the 
appellant did not appear, on 07.06.2023, the 
Court vacated the interim order dated 
04.01.2023. 
 Now, a recall application has been filed, 
on which, objections have been filed.
 Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that on the date of hearing i.e. on 
07.06.2023, the appellant could not appear 
and the interim order was vacated. 
 Learned counsel for the respondents 
would submit that after vacation of the 
interim order, the possession of the property 
has already been handed over by the 
appellant. He has filed the memorandum of 
obtaining possession. 
 In view of the fact that the order has already been executed, there is no question 
of recalling the order dated 07.06.2023, by 
which, the interim order was vacated. Hence, 
Civil Misc. Recall Application No.165 of 2023 
is rejected. 
 Stay Application (IA) No.176 of 2026 has 
also been filed by the appellant. 
 At the request of learned counsel for the 
respondents, list this matter on 03.09.2026, 
so as to enable him to file objections to the 
stay application.
