4. In compliance of our previous order, the original 
receipt register of Cantonment Board has been placed before 
us and from perusal whereof it transpires that the letter 
dated 23.04.2001 (at page no.179 of the writ petition) was 
duly received in the Cantonment Office on the same date. 
However, Shri Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel appearing for 
the Cantonment Board submits that the endorsement made 
on the said register to the effect: (Degree M.A. No.-
1300021) had been made subsequently. He submits that 
the clerk who had put his signatures acknowledging receipt 
of the said letter though retired is still alive and he is ready 
to testify that the endorsement made within brackets was 
added subsequently. 
5. We find that during course of disciplinary proceedings, 
the Board got the testimonials of the petitioner verified from 
Hemvati Nandan Bahuguna Garhwal University. The 
University, by a communication dated 04.03.2015, informed 
the Chief Executive Officer, Cantonment Board, Dehradun 
that there is no mention of award of any such degree of M.A. 
or B.Ed. to the petitioner in the records of the University. On 
account of the said reason, the University informed the Cantonment Board that it is not possible for it to verify the 
correctness of the testimonials. 
6. We observe that the University, while informing the 
Cantonment Board that there is no mention of the 
testimonials in its record, has not disclosed the records 
which were looked into. 
7. According to learned counsel appearing for the
