5. Pursuant to our previous order dated 11.08.2026,
Dr. K.H. Gupta, learned counsel for the university 
(Hemvati Nandan Bahuguna Garhwal University) has 
produced before us the original enrolment register of the 
year 1993, the year in which the appellant had allegedly 
taken admission in M.A. (Sociology). The register does 
not contain any application by the appellant for his

certificate.
6. As far as the register wherein the degree issued to 
the students is recorded, the same has not been 
produced before us. Dr. K.H. Gupta, learned counsel for 
the university states that the said register is not 
traceable.
7. Photocopy of marks tabulation register of M.A. Part￾I (Sociology) and M.A. Part-II (Sociology) of the relevant years have also been produced before us.
8. Dr. K.H. Gupta states that the original is in custody 
of C.B.I. and we find that on the first page there is also 
an endorsement to the said effect by the seizing officer. 
In the aforesaid two registers also the name of the 
appellant does not find place.
9. Learned counsel for the university has also 
produced before us the tabulation register of B.Ed. 
examination in which name of the appellant finds place.
10. All the registers were shown to Shri Sandeep 
Kothari, learned counsel for the appellant but he could 
not point out to any other material which is worth 
noticing or contrary to what has been recorded above. 
He, however, prays for and is granted two weeks’ time to 
file affidavit of the appellant and bring on record 
therewith the photocopies of the marksheets of different 
examinations alleged to have been passed by the 
appellant and on the basis of which he secured his 
appointment on the post of Junior Lecturer.
11. List thereafter.
12. The original registers have been returned to learned 
counsel for the appellant and he undertakes to place on 
record the relevant part of the endorsement on the 
tabulation register by the officer who carried out the 
seizure and the relevant part of the tabulation register of 
B.Ed. examination where the name of the appellant 
figures. As in the other registers, the name of the 
appellant does not find place, therefore, photocopies of 
the other registers need not be filed.
