4. Mr. U. K. Uniyal, learned senior counsel 
appearing for the appellant contends that the 
original testimonials were submitted by the 
appellant before the Cantonment Board along 
with letter dated 23.04.2001 (at page 179 of 
the paper book). In support of the said plea, 
he has placed reliance on paragraphs 4, 5 & 6 
of the affidavit along with which the said 
covering letter was filed. In reply to the 
affidavit, the Cantonment Board in para 9 has 
stated that annexure-2, the covering letter appears to be a manipulated and procured 
document. 
5. We observe that the covering letter 
dated 23.04.2001 bears a stamp of receipt of 
said letter by the Cantonment Board. It also 
bears Dispatch No. 290, dated 23.04.2001. 
6. The appellant has been dismissed from 
service primarily on the ground that 
testimonials on basis of which he procured the 
appointment appears to be fake documents as 
the original certificates have not been 
produced. Therefore, we direct the 
Cantonment Board to place before us the 
relevant Dispatch register so that it could be 
ascertained whether the original testimonials 
were submitted by the appellant before the 
Cantonment Board.

8. Learned counsel for the Cantonment 
Board undertakes to produce the relevant 
Dispatch and Receipt registers on the next
