Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Gopal K. Verma, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Sandeep Kumar, learned Standing Counsel for the 
State.
3. Substitution application (IA No.2/2024) is moved by 
the learned counsel for the petitioner to implead new 
incumbent i.e. Mr. Saveen Bansal as party respondent no.2-
contemnor in the contempt petition as the earlier incumbent 
has been transferred. 
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner prays that the 
substitution application may be treated as impleadment 
application. 
5. Accordingly, the same is treated as impleadement 
application. 
6. Since the impleadment is formal in nature, therefore, 
there is no objection from the side of learned State Counsel.
7. Accordingly, the impleadment application is allowed. 
Let necessary impleadment be incorporated by supplying 
amended memo of parties. 
8. Issue notice to the newly impleaded respondent no.2-
contemnor, returnable within four weeks, who may file his 
response to the contempt petition, in between.
9. Steps to be taken within three days.
10. List on 12.02.2025.
