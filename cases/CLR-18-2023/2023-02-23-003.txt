COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
23.02.2023 CLR No.18 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
Heard Mr. Neeraj Garg, learned 
counsel for the revisionist and Mr. Abhishek 
Grover, learned counsel appearing for the 
respondent no.1-plaintiff.
Mr. Neeraj Garg, Advocate, submitted 
that a decree was passed on 05.02.2019 in 
S.C.C. Suit No. 35 of 2015 on the basis of 
compromise. The revisionist had filed a 
Miscellaneous Case No. 7 of 2022 to set 
aside the compromise decree on the ground 
that the Small Causes Court had no inherent 
jurisdiction to pass the compromise decree. 
The said Miscellaneous Case No. 7 of 2022 
has been dismissed.
Learned counsel Mr. Abhishek Grover, 
Advocate, submitted that he has filed his 
Vakalatnama today itself and requested for 
01.03.2023 for arguments.
Mr. Neeraj Garg, further submitted 
that in the execution proceedings, date is 
fixed for tomorrow i.e. 24.02.2023.
List this case on 01.03.2023.
Meanwhile, further proceedings of 
Execution Case No. 9 of 2020, pending 
before Judge, Small Causes Court/IIIrd
Additional District Judge, Dehradun are stayed.
It is clarified that the stay order will 
not be extended on the next date only on 
the request of the revisionist.
