COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.53 of 2025
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. C.S. Rawat, learned Chief 
Standing Counsel along with Mr. 
Bhupendra Koranga, learned Brief Holder
for the State.
3. Learned Chief Standing Counsel, after 
examining the entire record, fairly submits 
that these two institutions, i.e Government 
Primary School, Kantadi and Government 
Higher Primary School, Kantadi, though are 
located in the same place, same village and 
same Gram Sabha, but have been wrongly 
categorized as “Sugam” and “Durgam”,
respectively.
4. On the previous date this Court 
granted time to the State to explain as to
what was the logic for classifying the areas 
as Sugam and Durgam for the purpose of 
transfer.
5. The concerned Secretary shall file his 
personal affidavit in compliance to order 
dated 10.01.2025 positively within two 
weeks giving justification of making such a 
classification.
6. The counsel for the petitioner shall 
implead the concerned Secretary as party 
respondent during the course of the day.
7. Till the next date of listing, the 
respondents are restrained from 
transferring any incumbent on the basis of 
such classification.
8. List this matter on 04.03.2025 on top 
of Board.
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
