COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
MCRC No.03 of 2024 for Time Extension Application
In
BA3 No.68 of 2024
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Lalit Miglani, Advocate for the 
applicant, through video conferencing.
Mr. V.S. Rawat, A.G.A. for the State.
Applicant was in judicial custody in 
Case Crime No.422 of 2021, under 
Sections 147, 148, 149, 302, 307, 323, 
504, 506 and 120-B IPC, Police Station 
Kotwali Laksar, District Haridwar. He had 
sought short term bail on the ground of 
his illness. Now a Time Extension 
Application, MCRC No.03 of 2024, has 
been filed.
Learned counsel for the applicant 
would submit that the applicant’s one 
finger is to be amputated; the sugar level 
of the applicant is much high; the 
applicant has heart ailments as well.
Yesterday, when the matter was 
taken up, learned State Counsel was 
required to get instructions on the 
medical prescriptions, as filed by the 
applicant. 
Today, learned State Counsel 
confirms that the applicant is unwell and 
he has to undergo surgical procedure. 
Having considered, without adverting 
to the merits, purely on humanitarian 
ground, the Court is of the view that the 
Time Extension Application deserves to be 
allowed.
The Time Extension Application is allowed. 
The short term bail of the applicant
is further extended to the period of six
weeks from the date of its expiry. It is 
further directed that on expiry of the 
duration of extended short term bail, the 
applicant shall surrender before the court 
concerned and a certificate be produced 
before this Court.
 (Ravindra Maithani J.)
