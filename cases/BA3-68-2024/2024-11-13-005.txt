Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J. (Oral)
Applicant Irshad is in judicial custody in Case 
Crime No. 422 of 2021, under Section 147, 148, 149, 302, 
307, 323, 504, 506, 120B IPC, Police Station Kotwali 
Laksar, District Haridwar. He has sought short term bail 
on the ground of illness. Initially bail application was filed 
by the applicant, but on 25.09.2024, based on a 
statement given by the learned counsel for the applicant, 
the bail application has been treated as a short term bail 
application.
2. Delay Condonation Application No. 2 of 2024 is 
allowed. Delay in filing the response affidavit is condoned. 
Response affidavit is taken on record.2
3. Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the record.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant would 
submit that the applicant is 61 years old person; he is 
suffering with various ailments including coronary artery 
disease, high diabetic with hypertension.
5. Response was sought from the State. State has 
filed its response. According to it, the applicant is a 
known case of insulin dependent diabetes with 
Hypertension with coronary artery disease with 
diminished vision both eyes. Annexure 1 to the response 
in point no.4 records “Need follow up in AIIMS 
RISHIKESH. Will be sent there on availability of police 
guard.” These lines are not encouraging. If a person needs 
medical assistance, the police guard should be ensured. 
The treatment should not be dependent on the availability 
of police guard. 
6. Learned State counsel would submit that the 
applicant is unwell.
7. Without adverting to the merits, purely on 
humanitarian ground, this Court is of the view that the 3
applicant may be enlarged on short term bail for a 
period of four weeks.
8. Let the applicant be released on short term 
bail for a period of four weeks from the date of his 
release, subject to his furnishing a personal bond and 
two reliable sureties of the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the court concerned.
9. After expiry of the period of short term bail, 
the applicant shall surrender before the court 
concerned and an information shall be forwarded to this 
Court.
10. Short term bail application is allowed
accordingly.
(Ravindra Maithani, J.)
