The applicant seeks short term bail on 
the ground of illness.

submit that the applicant’s general 
condition is normal, as per the report, but

submit that the applicant is a patient of 
some brain ailments also. He has a clot in 
his brain. He is heavily diabetic. 
As such, investigation reports have
not been placed by learned State Counsel. 
Let State file its response to the short 
term bail application, which is dated 
22.09.2024, enclosing therewith the 
investigation reports and other details of
ailment, if any, with which the applicant is 
suffering. 
Let it be done within two weeks. 
List this matter on 06.11.2024. 
In the meanwhile, the Incharge, Jail, 
is requested to ensure that adequate 
medical facilities are being provided to the 
applicant.
