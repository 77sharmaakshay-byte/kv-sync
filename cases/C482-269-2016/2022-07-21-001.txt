These applications have been preferred by the 
applicants to seek extension of the Interim Order dated 
15.06.2016, which was extended by this Court on 
28.09.2021, till the pendency of the instant Criminal 
Miscellaneous Applications. 
The submission of the learned Senior Counsels 
for the applicants is that, by passing a detailed order 
on 28.09.2021, this Court had confirmed the stay 
granted on 15.06.2016, by which the further proceedings, in Criminal Case No. 85 of 2016 pending 
in the Court of the learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Nainital, were stayed. 
The need to prefer the present applications, at 
this stage, has arisen on account of the fact that the 
Trial Court has issued summons against the applicants
on 08.07.2022 in the light of the judgment of the 
Supreme Court in Asian Resurfacing of Road 
Agency Pvt. Ltd. & Anr. v. Central Bureau of 
Investigation, in Miscellaneous Application No. 1577 
of 2020.
We have heard learned counsels for the 
applicants as well as Mr. J.S. Virk, the learned Deputy 
Advocate General for the State, and we are inclined to 
continue the interim stay, which was granted in favour 
of the applicants and confirmed, as aforesaid, vide 
order dated 28.09.2021.
List these petitions for hearing on 19.10.2022.
