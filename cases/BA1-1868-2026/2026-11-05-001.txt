Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Nishant Krishna Adhikari, learned 
counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Manoj Bhatt, learned Brief Holder 
for the State.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant
would submit that the present short-term 
bail application has been moved on 
medical grounds. He would submit that on 
30.09.2026, the applicant was taken from 
jail to Government Base Hospital, Almora, 
where he was admitted to the Medicine 
ICU; that, as per the medical records 
maintained by the Jail Medical Officer, the 
applicant was discharged from the hospital 
on 05.10.2026.
4. He would further submit that the 
doctors at the said hospital advised that 
the applicant be referred to a higher 
medical centre, namely, AIIMS, Rishikesh, 
for further examination and treatment of 
his ailment. In this regard, a request was made to the Senior Superintendent of 
Police, Almora, on 23.10.2026, for 
providing adequate police personnel to 
facilitate the applicant's medical 
examination and treatment at AIIMS, 
Rishikesh, however, despite the lapse of 
considerable time, the applicant has not 
yet been referred to the said hospital.
5. Having regard to the medical 
condition of the applicant and the 
recommendation of the Medical Officer, 
District Jail, Almora, the Senior 
Superintendent of Police, Almora, is 
directed to make necessary arrangements 
for providing adequate police personnel to 
ensure that the applicant is taken to 
AIIMS, Rishikesh, for medical examination 
and necessary treatment, as advised by the 
doctors.
6. The requisite arrangements shall be 
made expeditiously, preferably within three 
days from the date of production of a copy 
of this order, particularly as more than two 
weeks have already elapsed since the 
request dated 23.10.2026 was made.
