Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
Learned counsel for respondent no. 2 
make a statement at the bar that the Board 
of Directors has decided in its meeting held 
on 08.06.2021 that benefit of A.C.P. shall 
be given to all the employees. He points out 
that in the said meeting, it is further 
decided that firstly payment of arrears of 
A.C.P. will be made to dependents of 20 
deceased employees, thereafter to retired 
employees and then to serving employees, 
however, he could not indicate the deadline 
for release of the amount as A.C.P. to the 
dependants of deceased employees and also 
to retired employees.
In such view of the matter, respondent 
no. 2 is directed to file supplementary 
affidavit within a period of three weeks 
indicating the deadline for releasing the 
amount. In the supplementary affidavit, he 
shall also mention the time needed by 
Uttarakhand Seeds and Tarai Development 
Corporation Ltd. to clear all the dues of 
Dearness Allowance and arrears of ex￾gratia.
