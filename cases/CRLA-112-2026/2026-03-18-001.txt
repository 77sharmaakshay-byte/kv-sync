COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
CRLA No.112 of 2026
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Hon’ble Siddhartha Sah, J.
Mr. Sandeep Adhikari, learned Legal Aid Counsel 
for the appellants through V.C.
2. Mr. Pankaj Joshi, learned A.G.A for the State.
3. The appellants propose to file an appeal against 
the judgment and order dated 19.05.2025 and sentence 
order dated 20.05.2025, passed in Sessions Trial No.73 
of 2022 State vs. Vivek Verma and Others, by the court 
of learned 2nd Additional District & Sessions Judge, 
Udham Singh Nagar. By it, the appellants have been 
convicted under Sections 302 r/w Section 34, Section 
394 and Section 201 of IPC and sentenced accordingly. 
There is a delay of 236 days in filing the appeal.
4. Heard learned counsel for the parties on the delay 
condonation application (IA No.2 of 2026).
5. Learned counsel for the appellants submits that he 
has been appointed by the High Court Legal Services 
Committee to provide legal assistance to the appellants. 
The appellants had no resources to prefer the appeal, 
therefore, the delay has occurred.
6. It is not disputed by the learned State counsel.
7. There are good grounds to condone the delay in 
filing the appeal. The delay in filing the appeal is hereby condoned and the Delay Condonation Application (IA 
No.2 of 2026) is allowed.
8. Heard. 
9. Admit. 
10. Call for LCR.
11. Once the LCR is received, let the paper book be 
prepared and provided to the learned counsel for the 
parties, as per Rules. 
12. Heard the learned counsel for the parties on the 
Exemption Application (IA No.01 of 2026) also. 
13. Learned counsel for the appellant has filed an 
exemption application on the ground that he has been 
appointed to provide legal aid to the appellant; he may 
not be in a position to file an affidavit and court fees due 
to non-availability of any pairokar and his financial 
condition, therefore, he may be exempted to file an 
affidavit and court fees.
14. Accordingly, the Exemption Application is 
allowed. 
15. List this matter on 27.04.2026.
(Siddhartha Sah, J.) (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
