4. Petitioner has impugned order/show￾cause notice dated 25.06.2026 issued by 
Director General Assam Rifles (Annexure 
No.11 to the writ petition) along with 
notices dated 01.04.2026 and 20.04.2026 
(Annexure Nos.8 and 10 respectively) and 
other ancillary reliefs.
5. A preliminary objection has been raised 
by learned counsel appearing for Union of 
India that writ petition is directed against 
notices/show-cause notice, therefore, the 
same is not maintainable.
6. Learned counsel for petitioner submits 
that these notices were challenged by the 
petitioner on the ground of non-competence 
and on being without jurisdiction as for the purpose of withholding and withdrawal of 
pension, the President in the competent 
authority, therefore, the show-cause notice
issued by Director General Assam Rifles is 
without competence. She relied upon the 
Rule 8 of the Central Civil Services 
(Pension) Rules, 2021.
7. Learned counsel appearing for Union of 
India submits that show-cause notice was 
sent to petitioner under Rule 7 of the 
aforesaid Rules of 2021.
8. Having heard the rival submissions of 
learned counsel for parties, this Court is
prima-facie of the view that notices for 
withholding the pension can be issued 
under Rule 8 and not under Rule 7 of the 
aforesaid Rules.
9. Respondents may file counter 
affidavit(s), if any, within four weeks.
10. Put up on 06.11.2026.
11. In the meantime, respondents are 
restrained from proceeding further on the
basis of impugned show-cause notice/other 
notices.
