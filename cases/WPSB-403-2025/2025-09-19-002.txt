COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
19.09.2025 WPSB No.403 of 2025
Hon’ble G. Narendar, C.J.
Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
Mr. Harshpal Sekhon and Mr. Basant 
Singh, learned counsel for the petitioner; Mr. 
Gajendra Tripathi, learned Standing Counsel 
and Ms. Rajni Supyal, learned Brief Holder
for the State/respondent no.1 and Mr. 
Ashish Joshi, learned counsel for the 
respondent no.2. 
2. Petitioner has challenged the order 
dated 15.09.2025, by which, in 
contemplation of an inquiry, he has been 
placed under suspension. 
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
contends that the petitioner was appointed as 
an Accountant in the year 1997 and was
promoted as In-charge Assistant General 
Manager (Finance) in the year 2024 and has 
rendered 29 years of satisfactory services and 
is due to retire in 3 and ½ years; that a news 
item was published in a newspaper on 
07.09.2025 and on 13.09.2025 with regard to 
an incident, which took place on 06.09.2025, 
wherein, 04 passengers were found to be 
travelling without tickets by a checking team
in Bus No. UK07PA 4548; that it is alleged that the petitioner was also a passenger 
travelling in the same bus, in which, 04
passengers were travelling without ticket and
the petitioner is a regular traveller of the said 
bus and Video recording of the said incident 
was done and the petitioner objected to the 
recording of the Video; that the petitioner 
used to prevent the checking team from 
entering into the bus.
4. Counsel for the petitioner contends that 
the petitioner, who has 29 years of
unblemished service records, has been 
placed under suspension on the basis of the 
said news paper publication without any 
notice and without ascertaining as to 
whether the petitioner, in any manner, was 
responsible towards the act of the conductor,
against whom the allegation was levelled of 
not issuing the tickets to the travellers. 
5. Counsel for the respondent no.2 
submits on the basis of instructions that it 
was found during checking that the bus 
conductor had taken money from the 
passengers but had not issued the tickets to 
four passengers, which amounts to 
embezzlement of corporation revenue. The
petitioner pressurised the checking team to 
modify the report and endorse that only two 
passengers were travelling without tickets. He submits that the conductor had 
complained that he was working in the 
pressure of the petitioner and the checking 
team had also stated that the petitioner used 
to exert pressure on them for not checking 
the bus, in which, he was travelling.
6. Prima facie, the submissions made by 
learned counsel for the respondent no.2 does 
not inspire confidence and appears to be an 
afterthought as the order of the suspension 
does not disclose any of the aforesaid 
reasons. Merely, because the petitioner was 
travelling in a bus, in which, the tickets were 
not issued by the conductor would not ipso 
facto made him liable for the misconduct 
committed by an employee, who otherwise is 
responsible for the discharge of the said duty.
7. Counsel for the respondents pray for 
and are granted four weeks’ time to file the 
counter affidavit. 
8. In the meantime, the effect and 
operation of the impugned order dated 
15.09.2025 shall remain stayed. 
9. List on 20.11.2025.
