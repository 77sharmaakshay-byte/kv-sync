4. Present C482 application has been 
moved along with the compounding 
application, supported with the affidavit

5. Applicant no. 2 who is the real 
brother of applicant no. 1 is in jail in

S.S.T. No. 30 of 2023, State vs. Prakash

respective counsel.

504IPC and under Section 9 of Prohibition 
of Child Marriage Act.
7. It is stated in the compounding 
application that applicant no. 1 now 
married with respondent no. 2 on 
24.10.2023 and the marriage has been 
registered. It is further submitted by the 
learned counsel for the applicants that 
since applicant no. 1 and respondent no. 
2 now married and their marriage is also registered and if the proceedings are
permitted to continue it will spoil their 
entire married life. 
8. On interaction with the complainant/ 
victim she submitted that due to some 
misunderstanding the FIR has been 
lodged.
9. The State Counsel submits that the 
offences are non compoundable but the 
State Counsel fairly submits that since 
applicant no. 1 married with respondent 
no. 2, therefore continuation of this 
proceeding is nothing but a futile exercise
and the same will spoil their married life.
10. All these aspects are required to be 
scrutinized for which the State Counsel 
may file objection within two weeks.
10. List this case on 09.05.2024.
11. In the meantime, further 
proceedings of S.S.T. No. 30 of 2023 
State vs. Prakash Ram & anr., pending in

husband.
