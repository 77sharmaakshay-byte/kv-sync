WPSS No.193 of 2016
Hon’ble Rajiv Sharma, J.
Mr. C.S. Rawat, Advocate for the 
petitioners. 
Mr. N.P.Sah, Standing Counsel 
for the State. 
The petitioners were the 
employees of the State Government. 
Thereafter, their services were engaged 
through Contractor after 2002. The 
petitioners have approached this 
Court. This Court directed the State 
Government to frame a policy. 
The State Government, till date, 
has not framed any policy despite 
mandatory directions issued by this 
Court. 
Accordingly, the writ petition is 
disposed of with the direction to the 
respondents to consider to frame a 
Policy within a period of six weeks 
from today, which would govern the 
petitioners and similarly situate 
persons for their regularization by 
counting their services which they 
have rendered with the State 
Government for more than 10-15 
years. 
All pending applications stand 
disposed of.
