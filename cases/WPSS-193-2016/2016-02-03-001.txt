By means of present writ petition, the 
petitioner seeks following reliefs among others: 
(i) Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of certiorari to quash the impugned order 
dated 07.01.2016 passed by respondent no. 5 
(Contained as Annexure no. 3 to this writ 
petition). 
 (ii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing the respondents to 
regularized the services of the petitioners in the 
light of Regularization Rules framed by the State 
Government time to time or give parity to other 
similarly situated incumbents (Annexure no. 7 of 
the writ petition). 
(iii) Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus direct the respondents to pay 
the minimum pay alongwith dearness allowance 
to the petitioners as are being paid to regularly 
appointed employees in the State of Uttarakhand. 
(iv) Issue a writ, order or direction in the 
nature of mandamus directing the respondents to 
make payment of arrears of dearness allowance to 
the petitioners. As prayed, four weeks time is granted 
to the learned counsel for the respondents to 
file counter affidavit. 
List this writ petition after four weeks. 
Urgency application (IA no. 682/2016) 
as well as stay application (CLMA no. 
789/2016) stand disposed of.
