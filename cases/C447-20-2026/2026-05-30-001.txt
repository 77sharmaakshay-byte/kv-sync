COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
C-447 No.20 of 2026
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. D.C.S. Rawat and Mr. Jai 
Krishna Pandey, learned counsel for the 
applicant.
2. Present C-447 application has been 
filed to transfer the Criminal Case 
No.289 of 2021 under Section 125 
Cr.P.C. pending in the court of learned 
Addl. Principal Family Judge 1st,
Roorkee, District Haridwar to any other 
competent Court at Haridwar, District 
Haridwar.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would submit that the present matter 
arises out of a matrimonial dispute 
between the parties. The applicant and 
respondent no. 1 are husband and wife 
and, due to matrimonial differences, they 
are residing separately; that, respondent 
no. 1 has instituted proceedings under 
Section 125 Cr.P.C. before the learned 
Family Court, Roorkee, District 
Haridwar. Learned counsel would further 
submit that the sister of the applicant is 
a practicing Advocate at the Civil Courts, 
Roorkee, and certain proceedings arising 
out of the dispute between the parties 
are also pending before the courts at 
Roorkee.
4. He would further submit that 
whenever the matter is listed before the 
concerned court, respondent no. 1 allegedly creates disturbances within the 
court premises and engages in 
quarrelsome conduct in the presence of 
litigants, advocates, and court staff. It is 
further alleged that respondent no. 1 
frequently uses abusive and insulting 
language against the applicant's sister 
and attempts to provoke arguments and 
altercations with her, causing 
embarrassment and inconvenience to the 
applicant and his family members, 
therefore, on these grounds, the 
applicant has sought transfer of the 
proceedings from the court concerned at 
Roorkee to a competent court at 
Haridwar.
5. Issue notice to respondents, 
returnable at an early date.
6. Steps to be taken within one week.
7. List this matter after service report 
is received.
8. Counter affidavit, if any, be filed by 
the respondents.
9. Considering the submission 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
applicant, it is directed that, till the next 
date of listing, further proceedings of 
Criminal Case No.289 of 2021 under 
Section 125 Cr.P.C. pending in the court 
of learned Addl. Principal Family Judge 
1st, Roorkee, District Haridwar shall 
remain stayed.
10. Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
