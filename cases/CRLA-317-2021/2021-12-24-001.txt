CRLA No. 317 of 2021
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Vaibhav Singh Chauhan,
Advocate for the appellant.
Mr. S.S. Adhikari, D.A.G., for the 
State.
The appellant Sonu Kumar has 
been convicted and sentenced under
Section 307 read with 34 and 326 read 
with 34 IPC on 23.09.2021, in 
Sessions Trial No. 62 of 2016, by the 
Court of 2nd Additional Sessions Judge, 
Roorkee, District Haridwar.
Heard on Bail Application No. 1 of 
2021.
Learned counsel for the appellant 
would submit that the scribe of the FIR 
has not been examined. The person, 
who was informed about the incident 
by the victim, after the incident, has
also not been examined. The FIR is 
delayed. Though, PW4 Ramesh Singh 
would submit that he had informed the 
family members of the victim and the 
Police as well on the date of incident 
itself. In addition, it is also argued that 
the medical examination does not 
support the oral evidence.
According to the FIR, on 
17.08.2015, late in the evening, the 
appellant and others assaulted the 
victim with a sharp edged weapon and 
left him unattended in the forest, 
under the impression that the victim 
has died.
The victim has stated about the 
incident. There are eyewitnesses PW3
Dr. Tasdiq Khan and PW9 Dr. Arvind 
Verma have stated about the injuries,
which were found grievous in nature. 
In fact, there were fractures on the face itself. The evidence was found reliable 
by the court below.
Having considered the entirety of 
facts, this Court is of the view that it is 
not a fit case for bail.
The bail application is rejected.
Paper book is ready. Let it be 
provided to learned counsel for the 
appellant.
List for final hearing.
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
