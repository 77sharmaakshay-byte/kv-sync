IA No. 8 of 2024
In
CRLA No. 317 of 2021
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Ms. Divya Jain, learned counsel for 
the appellant through V.C.
2. Mr. G.S. Sandhu, learned Addl.
Government Advocate and Mr. Rakesh 
Negi, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Heard on the short term bail 
application being IA No. 8 of 2024.
4. A short term bail application has 
been moved on behalf of appellant who 
has been convicted for the offences 
punishable under Section 307, 326 read 
with Section 34 IPC by the court of 
learned 2nd Addl. Session Judge, Roorkee 
District Haridwar in Session Trial No. 62 
of 2016.
5. This is an admitted appeal. The first 
bail application of the applicant was 
rejected by the Coordinate Bench of this 
Court by order dated 24.12.2021.
6. A short term bail has been sought 
by the appellant on the ground that the 
marriage of his real brother is scheduled 
to be solemnized on 04.02.2024, in 
support of which marriage invitation card 
is also annexed with the short term bail 
application.
7. This matter was listed yesterday and 
on the request of the learned counsel for 
the State the matter is posted for today.8. Today, Mr. G.S. Sandhu, learned 
Addl. Government Advocate received 
instruction in the matter and also verified 
the fact that the marriage is scheduled to 
be solemnized on 04.02.2024, and fairly 
submits that since the marriage of real 
brother of the present appellant is 
scheduled to be solemnized on 
04.02.2024, therefore, taking into 
consideration this aspect, short term bail 
may be granted to the appellant.
9. In view of the above, the short term 
bail application is allowed. Let the 
appellant Sonu Kumar be released on 
short term bail for a period of 15 days to 
be reckoned from the date of his release, 
on his executing a personal bond, and 
furnishing two sureties each of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the court 
concerned. On completion of 15 days, to 
be reckoned from the date of release, he 
shall surrender before the learned trial 
court. On such an event he will be 
remanded to the custody. If he does not 
surrender, proper warrant shall be issued 
against him. 
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
