COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
12.04.2024 C482 No. 463 of 2024
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Siddhartha Sah, learned counsel 
holding brief of Mr. Sanjev Singh, learned counsel 
for the applicant and Mr. Saurabh Pandey, 
learned Brief Holder along with Ms. Meenakshi 
Sharma, learned Brief Holder for the State.
2. By the instant C482 Application, the 
present applicant is challenging the proceedings 
of Criminal Case No. 7120 of 2021, State Vs. Basu 
Nagar, arising out of FIR No. 560 of 2020, wherein 
the present applicant has been summoned for 
the offence punishable under Sections 498-A, 
323, 504, 506 of IPC read with Section 3/4 of 
Dowry Prohibition Act.
3. The present applicant married with 
respondent No. 2 on 25.05.2019. It is submitted 
by the learned counsel for the applicant that 
respondent No. 2 the complainant had not 
disclosed that she was earlier married to another 
person and is a divorcee and when this fact came 
to the knowledge of the applicant then he 
preferred a suit, bearing Suit No. 170 of 2020 in 
Family Court, Haridwar, seeking declaration to declare the marriage solemnized on 25.05.2019 
be null and void.
4. In this proceeding, the respondent 
complainant put her appearance and moved an 
application under Section 24 of the Hindu 
Marriage Act, but, thereafter she did not appear 
in this proceeding and ultimately, the Family 
Court proceeded on 05.01.2022 and passed a 
judgment and decree on 09.03.2023 and declared 
the marriage solemnised on 25.05.2019 as null 
and void.
5. The learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that the suit was filed by the applicant on 
24.06.2020, which was decreed and in 
counterblast to the suit filed in the month of 
June, 2020, on 03.12.2020, the complainant 
respondent lodged this FIR in which the 
chargesheet was filed on 02.07.2021 and the 
applicant has been summoned on 16.11.2021.
6. Learned counsel for the applicant 
submits that the initiation of the proceeding of 
Criminal Case No. 7120 of 2021 is totally an abuse 
of process of law and the same was instituted 
pursuant to the FIR lodged on 03.12.2020 after 
the suit filed by the applicant for seeking 
declaration that the marriage be declared as null and void. He submits that this proceeding is 
nothing but abuse of process of law and 
therefore, the same should be quashed. 
7. Mr. Saurabh Pandey, learned Brief 
Holder accepts notice for respondent No. 1. 
8. Issue notices to respondent No. 2.
9. Learned counsel for the applicant shall 
take steps to serve respondents on both the 
address, by dasti.
10. Respondents are granted four weeks’ 
time to file counter affidavit. Two weeks’ time, 
thereafter, is granted to the petitioner to file 
rejoinder affidavit. 
11. List this case on 05.06.2024.
12. In the meantime, the further 
proceedings of Criminal Case No. 7120 of 2021, 
State Vs. Basu Nagar, pending in the Court of 
Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, Kashipur, 
shall remain stayed.
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
