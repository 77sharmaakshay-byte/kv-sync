2. Mr. Dinesh Chauhan, learned A.G.A.

Brief Holder for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution 
of India, the petitioner has prayed for 
issuance of a writ in the nature of 
certiorari for quashing the First 
Information Report/Case Crime No. 247 of 
2026 dated 17.04.2026, registered under 
Sections 318(4), 336(3), 338, 340(2) and 
61(2) of B.N.S. at Police Station Patel 
Nagar, District Dehradun. The petitioner 
has further sought issuance of a writ, 
order or direction in the nature of 
mandamus commanding the respondent 
authorities not to arrest the petitioner 
pursuant to the aforesaid F.I.R.
4. Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that the petitioner is 
innocent and has falsely been implicated in the present case; that, according to the 
allegations contained in the F.I.R., the 
petitioner has been arrayed as an accused 
merely because he is a witness to a power 
of attorney executed in favour of one 
Babita w/o Tek Singh. It is further 
submitted that mutation proceedings in 
respect of the disputed property are 
presently pending before the Tehsildar, 
Sadar, Dehradun, wherein the said power 
of attorney has been produced and is 
under consideration. He would contend 
that, till date, no finding has been recorded 
by the competent civil/revenue court 
declaring the said power of attorney to be 
forged or fabricated, therefore, in the 
absence of same, the impugned F.I.R. is 
wholly unwarranted and is liable to be 
quashed.
5. Issue notice to respondent no.4, 
returnable at an early date.
6. List after receipt of service report.
7. Counter/objections be filed by the 
State/respondent.
8. Till the next date of listing, as an 
interim measure, petitioner shall not be 
arrested in connection with the 
Information Report/Case Crime No. 247 of
2026 dated 17.04.2026, registered at 
Police Station Patel Nagar, District 
Dehradun provided, the petitioner co￾operates with the investigation.
9. Interim relief application stands 
disposed of accordingly.
