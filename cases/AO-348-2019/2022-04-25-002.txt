COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
AO No. 348 of 2019 
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Zafar Siddique, Advocate, for the 
appellants. 
Mr. D.C.S. Rawat, Advocate, for the 
respondent No.1.
As arising out of the accident dated 12th
September, 2010, the claimants, herein, had
instituted the proceedings before the Workmen 
Compensation Act, by filing the same on 20th
October 2018. 
During its pendency, the claimants have 
simultaneously had also filed a Claim Petition 
under Sections 166 / 140 of the Motor Vehicles 
Act, by instituting the same before the Motor 
Accident Claim Tribunal on 20th November 2018,
which was registered as MACP Case No. 213 of 
2018. 
The Claim Petition thus instituted by the 
claimants on 20th November, 2018, under the 
provisions of the Motor Vehicles Act, has been 
dismissed by the impugned award dated 17th July,
2019, against which, the present Appeal from Order 
under Section 173 of the Motor Vehicles Act, has 
been instituted. 
The Appeal is yet to be admitted. 
The respondents had filed the objection 
raising question to the tenability of the appeal itself 
on the ground that because of the bar created by 
Section 167 of the Motor Vehicles Act, that there 
cannot be a simultaneous proceedings for raising 
claim under Section 4/4A of the Workmen 
Compensation Act, as well as under Section 166 of 
the Motor Vehicles Act, also. 
On the objection being raised by the learned 
counsel for the respondents, the learned counsel for 
the appellant, to whom the offer of opting out of the 
proceedings as contemplated under Section 167 of 
the Act, was extended by this Court and he had made a statement at the Bar, that he would be
opting out to pursue the proceedings under Section 
166/140 of the Act, and would be withdrawing the 
proceedings of Case No. 23 of 2018, drawn by him 
under Section 4/4-A of the Workmen 
Compensation Act, as instituted on 28th October,
2018. 
In that view of the statement made by the 
learned counsel for the appellant, since he is opting 
out to continue with the proceedings under Section 
166 of the Motor Vehicles Act, and would be 
withdrawing the proceedings under the Workmen 
Compensation Act, owing to the aforesaid 
statement, the Appeal from Order is admitted. 
Since the records have already been 
summoned, let notices be issued to the respondent 
No.2. 
Steps to be taken by the appellant within a 
period of one week from today. 
List this Appeal from Order after service of 
notice on the respondent No. 2.
