Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, learned counsel for the 
Applicant. 
2. Mr. G.C. Joshi, learned A.G.A. for the State of 
Uttarakhand.
3. The present Criminal Misc. Application under Section 
528 of the Bharatiya Nagrik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, has 
been filed by the Applicant with the prayer to quash the 
charge-sheet dated 31.01.2022, the cognizance/summoning 
order dated 02.02.2022, the framing charge order dated 
22.04.2025, under Sections 384, 420, 406 read with Section 
34 IPC and the entire proceeding of Criminal Case No. 66 of 
2022, “State vs. Jitendra Saini and Another”, pending before 
the Court of learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Roorkee, District Haridwar. 
4. The Applicant has also filed a Stay Application (IA No. 
01 of 2025, Paper No. 58), supported by an affidavit, with a 
prayer to stay the further proceedings of Criminal Case No. 
66 of 2022, “State vs. Jitendra Saini and Another”, pending 
before the Court of the learned Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Roorkee, District Haridwar.
5. Learned counsel for the Applicant submits that the 
present matter is purely of a civil nature, arising out of a 
land deal, which was later given the colour of a criminal 
trial.
6. Admit.
7. Issue notice to Respondent No. 2.
8. Steps to be taken within a week.
9. List this case on 07.10.2025.
10. In the meantime, objections, if any, be filed.
11. As an interim measure, it is directed that, till the next date of listing, the further proceedings of Criminal Case No. 
66 of 2022, “State vs. Jitendra Saini and Another”, pending 
in the Court of learned Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Roorkee, District Haridwar, shall remain stayed qua the 
present Applicant.
12. Stay Application stands disposed of accordingly.
