Hon’ble Ashish Naithani, J.
Mr. Susheel Kumar, learned counsel for the Applicant.
2. The present application under Section 528 of BNSS has 
been filed on behalf of the Applicant – Mr. Mitesh Vijay Kadam, 
S/o Shri Vijay Govind Kadam, against the Respondent, 
challenging the entire proceedings of Complaint Case No.1222 of 
2021, “International Hotels Corporation Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Intellistay 
Hotels Pvt. Ltd and others” under Section 138 of Negotiable 
Instrument Act, pending before the court of learned Addl. Chief 
Judicial Magistrate, Haridwar, be quashed.
3. Considering the submissions advanced by the learned 
counsel on behalf of the Applicant, an interference is being called 
for in the matter.
4. Admit.
5. Issue notice to the Respondent. Steps to be taken within a 
week. Three weeks’ time is granted to the Respondent to file 
counter affidavit
6. Till the next date of listing, the entire proceedings of 
Complaint Case No.1222 of 2021, “International Hotels 
Corporation Pvt. Ltd. Vs. Intellistay Hotels Pvt. Ltd and others” 
under Section 138 of Negotiable Instrument Act, pending before 
the court of learned Addl. Chief Judicial Magistrate, Haridwar, 
shall remain stayed.
7. List this matter on 18th September, 2025.
8. Stay application (IA No.1/2025) stands disposed of 
accordingly.
