COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
C447 No.16 of 2026
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Vivek Shukla, learned counsel 
for the applicant
2. Ms. Pushpa Bhatt, learned Deputy 
A.G. along with Mr. S.C. Dumka, learned 
A.G.A. for the State.
3. Present C-447 application has been 
filed seeking transfer of Case No. 400 of 
2025, under Section 144 of the B.N.S.S., 
which is presently pending before the 
learned Principal Judge, Family Court, 
Haridwar, to the Family Court at 
Roorkee, District Haridwar.
4. Learned counsel for the applicant 
would submit that the dispute arises out 
of matrimonial discord between the 
applicant and respondent no. 2, who are 
husband and wife; that, due to certain 
matrimonial differences, respondent no. 
2 is living separately along with minor 
child and has filed an application under 
Section 144 B.N.S.S. seeking 
maintenance from the applicant. 
5. It is further submitted that 
respondent no. 2 is a practicing Advocate 
at the District Court, Haridwar, and has 
her independent chamber in the District 
and Sessions Court premises at 
Roshnabad, Haridwar; that, owing to this 
reason, according to the applicant, no 
Advocate at Haridwar is willing to appear 
on his behalf, and therefore he is facing difficulty in engaging a counsel to 
contest the case; that, the residence of 
respondent no. 2 is situated near the 
District Court, Haridwar, and the family 
members of respondent no. 2 are also 
putting pressure upon the applicant, 
therefore, the applicant has sought 
transfer of the case to the Family Court 
at Roorkee. 
6. Issue notice to respondent no. 2, 
returnable within four weeks. 
7. Steps shall be taken within one 
week. 
8. List this case immediately after 
service of notice upon respondent no. 2. 
9. Objections, if any, may be filed by 
the State/respondents before the next 
date of listing. 
10. Meanwhile, as an interim measure, 
further proceedings of Case No. 400 of 
2025, under Section 144 B.N.S.S., 
pending before the learned Principal 
Judge, Family Court, Haridwar, shall 
remain stayed till the next date of listing. 
11. Interim relief application stands 
disposed of accordingly. 
 (Alok Mahra, J.) 08.04.2026 Mamta
