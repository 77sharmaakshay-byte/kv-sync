Mr. V.D. Bisen, Brief Holder for

It is the case of the petitioner 
that they are owner of the property 
which has been wrongly transferred 
in contravention to Section 154 of 
The U.P. Zamindari Abolition and 
Land Reforms Act, 1950 as 
amended by the Uttarakhand 
Zamindari Abolition and Land 
Reforms (Amendment) Act, 2016 (for 
short, “the Act”). When the 
petitioner came to know about this 
illegality, they moved an application 
for cancellation of those deeds in 
view of Section 154 of the Act.
It is argued that, in fact, 
similar order has been passed by 
the Collector. Reference has been 
made to Annexure No.8.
Learned counsel for the 
petitioner gives a statement that, in 
fact, such application under Section 
154 of the Act has been given in the 
month of November, 2022. 
The Court wanted to know 
from the learned State counsel, as 
to what action has been taken on 
his complaint. He seeks time.List on 23.06.2023, just after 
fresh cases.
