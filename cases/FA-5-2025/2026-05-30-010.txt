Mr. Siddhartha Singh, Advocate for the

The present appeal is preferred by 
third party along with the Leave to Appeal 
Application.
In the Leave to Appeal Application, it 
has been stated that the appellants are bona 
fide purchasers for value and were
completely unaware of any of such 
agreement in favour of the plaintiff/ 
respondent no.1. The sale deed was 
executed by respondent no. 2 in favour of 
the appellant on 07.08.2019 prior to the 
judgment dated 23.08.2019 passed in O.S.
No. 134 of 2018.
It is further contended that the present 
appeal is a third party appeal. The 
appellants are most necessary and proper
party. The respondent no.2 has already filed 
an execution application as against the 
appellant as well and serious rights of the 
appellants are involved in the matter in 
question.
Per contra, learned counsel for the 
respondents would contend that the 
appellant was well aware about the 
registered agreement to sale in favour of the 
plaintiff/respondent which amounts to 
public notice and they have got the sale deed 
executed in their favour and they cannot be 
termed as bona fide purchaser.
In reply, Mr. Piyush Garg, Advocate for 
the appellants would reply on the judgment of Hon’ble Supreme Court in the case of H. 
Anjanappa and others vs. A Prabhakar and 
others reported in 2025 SCC OnLine SC 183 
and drew the attention of this Court to para 
43 viii thereof and has contended that the 
Leave to Appeal is to be granted to persons 
though not parties to the proceedings who 
would be bound by the decree or judgment 
in that proceeding.
In view of the contentions raised by the 
learned counsel for the parties and in view of 
the settled position of law on the subject, the 
Leave to Appeal Application is hereby 
allowed.
The applicant is permitted to prefer the 
appeal against the impugned judgment and 
order dated 23.08.2019. Since the valuable 
rights of the parties have to be adjudicated, 
hence admit the appeal. 
Summon the Trial Court Record.
List this case on 31.07.2026.
Since the appeal has been admitted, 
hence the effect and operation of the 
impugned judgment and order dated 
23.08.2019 shall remain stayed till the next 
date of listing. 
 (Siddhartha Sah, J.)
