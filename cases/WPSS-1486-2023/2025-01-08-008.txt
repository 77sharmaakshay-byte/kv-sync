Mr. Lalit Samant, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. J.S. Bisht and Mr. Sandeep Kumar, learned 
Standing Counsels along with Mr. Devesh Ghildiyal, learned 
Brief Holder for the State.
3. Today, the matter is listed on clarification application 
(IA No.10/2024) for clarifying the order dated 18.04.2024, 
whereby the termination order dated 15.01.2024 of the 
petitioner was stayed and at the same time, respondents 
were directed to maintain status quo with regard to the 
service of the petitioner. 
4. If the order is read in the light of observation made in 
para 10 of the said order, there is no doubt that only 
termination order was stayed and the status quo is with 
regard to the service of the petitioner as it was prevailing 
before the matter was dismissed for default. The 
respondents are deliberately misinterpreting the order and
denying the benefit of the order to the petitioner. 
5. Accordingly, the clarification application is allowed. The 
order stands clarified as discussed above. 
6. List on 09.01.2025.
