The instant Second Appeal is 
accompanied with the Delay Condonation 
Application (IA/2/2021), wherein, the 
appellant has sought condonation of 1499 
days delay which has chanced in filing the 
Second Appeal. The justification for the same 
is that, in a proceeding of regular Suit, which 
was decided on 20.09.2007, the appellants
had preferred a First Appeal and the said First 
Appeal was dismissed on 14.03.2019. 
Against the dismissal of the First 
Appeal, under a wrong advice, given by the 
learned Senior Counsel, the appellants were 
directed to prefer a writ petition, which was 
filed by them within the period of limitation 
on 12.01.2021. But ultimately, the writ 
petition was dismissed, holding as to be not 
maintainable by an order dated 07.12.2020 
with the following observations:-
“In view thereof, the writ petition is
not maintainable and is hereby dismissed, 
with liberty to the petitioners to file a second appeal under Section 100 of Code of Civil 
Procedure in accordance with law.”
While dismissing the writ petition, the 
liberty was left open for the petitioners to file 
a Second Appeal. The appellants have 
preferred the Second Appeal on 12.01.2021, 
once again within the period of limitation from 
the date of High Court’s order. That means, 
the limitation has to be determined in the light 
of the provisions contained under Section 14 
of Limitation Act.
Since the Second Appeal was filed on 
12.01.2021, it was filed well within the time 
provided under the Limitation Act from the 
date of the High Court’s order. Thus, for the 
reason aforesaid, the delay, which has 
chanced in filing the Second Appeal, would 
stand condoned. Delay Condonation 
Application stands allowed accordingly.
List this Second Appeal in the week 
commencing 18.12.2023 for hearing at 
admission stage, showing the name of Mr. 
Harshit Sanwal, as a counsel for the
respondent.
