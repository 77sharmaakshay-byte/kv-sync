 Mr. M.C. Pant, Advocate for the

 Mr. Sandeep Tandon, Advocate for the 
CBI. 
 Heard learned counsel for the parties on 
impleadment application. 
 Impleadment application no. 11705 of 
2016 is allowed in the interest of justice. 
 Let Secretary, Ministry of Department of 
Personnel and Training (DOPT), Govt. of India, 
New Delhi and Director Central Bureau of 
Investigation, CBI Headquarter, New Delhi be 
impleaded as respondent no. 3 and 4 in the writ 
petition, as prayed. 
 Amended memo of parties be brought on 
record within two days. 
 Notice on behalf of respondent CBI has 
been accepted by Mr. Sandeep Tandon, 
Advocate. 
 Notice on behalf of respondent Union of 
India has been accepted by Mr. Rakesh 
Thapliyal, learned Assistant Solicitor General. 
 Learned counsel for the respondents pray 
for and are granted six weeks’ time to file 
counter affidavits(s). 
 Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submitted that the petitioner is a practicing 
Advocate at Dehradun. His wife was killed, 
investigation is faulty and, therefore, the 
prosecution evidence, which are going on 
before the trial court be deferred for the time 
being. 
 Considering the peculiar facts of the 
case, it is provided as an interim measure, that 
if an application for adjournment is moved on 
behalf of the petitioner before the court below, 
the same shall be considered favourably for a 
period of eight weeks. 
 [Interim relief application no. 11625 of 
2016 also stands disposed of.]
