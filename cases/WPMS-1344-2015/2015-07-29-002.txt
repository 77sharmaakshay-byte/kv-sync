In the present case, vide impugned order, 
learned Collector, Udham Singh Nagar has 
passed the order fixing the compensation for 
acquisition of the land under the National 
Highways Act. According to learned counsel 
for the petitioner, Collector had absolutely no 
jurisdiction to decide the compensation part 
while exercising the powers under Section 64 of 
the Right to Fair Compensation and 
Transparency in Land Acquisition, 
Rehabilitation and Resettlment Act, 2013. 
On the other hand, learned counsel for the 
respondent Nos. 1 to 3 submits that Collector 
has passed the impugned order / award while 
exercising the jurisdiction under Sub-section (5) 
of Section 3G of National Highways Act, 1956. 
Learned counsel for respondent Nos. 1 to 
3 seeks and is granted ten days time to place on 
record the order whereby Collector was 
appointed as Arbitrator under Sub-section (5) of 
Section 3G of the National Highways Act. 
List after ten days.
