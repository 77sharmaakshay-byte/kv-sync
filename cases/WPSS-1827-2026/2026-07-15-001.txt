who shall look into their claim and take appropriate 
decision, as per law, within six months from the date

(Annexure No.1 to the petition) that prior to decision

19.12.2023 where-upon the benefit of grade pay was 
paid to the petitioners of that writ petition. 
5. It is contended by learned Senior Counsel for the 
petitioner that petitioner has yet not been paid the 
Grade Pay of Rs.2800/-, while the said contention raised on being of petitioner is being disputed by 
learned State Counsel on the basis of order dated 
18.05.2026, wherein it is stated that the benefit of 
Grade Pay of Rs.2800/- has already been paid to the 
petitioners of all the writ petitions which have been 
decided by this Court and batch; and budget is also 
sought from the Chief Engineer (Finance Controller) 
Irrigation Department, Uttarakhand, Dehradun for 
payment of arrears to the petitioners; and as and 
when the budget shall be made available, payment of 
arrears shall be made to the petitioners. 
6. Learned State Counsel shall get specific order 
whereby petitioner Kirtan Pal was sanctioned the 
benefit of Grade Pay of Rs.2800/- pursuant to the 
decision of his writ petition.
