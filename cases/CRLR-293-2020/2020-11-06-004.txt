List this case after service report is

Also heard on bail application. 
It is stated by learned counsel 
appearing on behalf of the revisionists 
that the revisionists were on bail during 
the trial as well as during the appeal and 
they never misused the bail granted to 
them. It is also submitted that no specific 
allegation of demanding dowry of Rs. 
5,00,000/- in terms of time, place and 
person had been proved during the trial 
by the prosecution. Furthermore, it is 
submitted that no list of articles allegedly 
given in marriage were neither prepared 
nor handed over to the parties to 
matrimony as such the conviction of 
revisionist under Section 4 of Dowry 
Prohibition Act, 1985. 
Learned State Counsel orally 
opposed the bail application and would 
submit that there is cogent evidence 
against the revisionists. 
Having heard learned Counsel for 
the parties and considered the facts and circumstances of the case, I am of the 
opinion that the revisionists deserve bail 
at this Stage. Hence, bail application is 
allowed. 
Let the revisionists be released on 
bail on executing a personal bond by 
each one of them and furnishing two 
reliable sureties for each one of them, to 
the satisfaction of the Court concerned, 
subject to deposit of fine as imposed by 
the trial Court, if already not deposited. 
Let a certified copy of this order be 
issued to the learned counsel for the 
parties, today itself, on payment of usual 
charges. 
 (N.S. Dhanik, J.)
