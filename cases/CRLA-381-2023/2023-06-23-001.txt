2. Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Deputy Advocate 
General along with Mr. Rakesh Joshi and Mr. 
Pankaj Joshi, learned Brief Holders for the 
State.
3. This is a appeal under Section 374 of 
the Cr.P.C. against the conviction recorded 
by learned Second Additional Sessions 
Judge, Rudrapur, Udham Singh Nagar in 
Sessions Trial No.87 of 2018, whereby 
appellant has been convicted under Section 
147 IPC and sentenced to one year rigorous 
imprisonment; he has further been 
convicted under Section 148 IPC and 
sentenced to two years rigorous 
imprisonment along with fine of Rs.500/-, 
with default stipulation of one month; he 
has further been convicted under Section 
307 read with Section 149 IPC and 
sentenced to seven years along with fine of 
Rs.2,000/- with default stipulation of three 
months and he has further been convicted 
under Section 302 read with Section 149 and sentenced for life imprisonment along 
with fine of Rs.5,000/- with default 
stipulation of six months. It was directed 
that all the sentences shall run concurrently. 
4. There is delay of 36 days’ in filing this 
appeal.
5. Learned State Counsel submits that 
since appeals filed by other convicts arising 
out of the same judgments have been 
admitted, therefore, he does not propose to 
file any objection to the delay condonation 
application. Thus, he submits that delay be 
condoned. 
6. Having regard to the facts and 
reasons, we are inclined to condone the 
delay. Accordingly, the delay condonation 
application is allowed. Delay of 36 days’ in 
filing the appeal is condoned. 
7. Since LCR has already been received in 
other connected appeals, therefore, there is 
no need of summoning the LCR.
8. List this matter along with CRLA 
No.178 of 2023.
9. In the meantime, objections to the bail 
application, if any, be filed by the State.
