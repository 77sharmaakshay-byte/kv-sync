 Heard. 
 Present civil revision has been 
preferred against the part of judgment and 
order dated 31.07.2019 passed by Judge, 
S.C.C./2nd Additional District Judge, 
Haridwar in SCC Suit No.22 of 2013 Rahul 
Chaurasia v. Sriram, whereby the 
respondent/plaintiff’s suit has been partly 
decreed for a decree of eviction and mesne 
profit. 
 Admit. 
 Summon the lower court record. 
 List thereafter. 
 Having considered the submissions of 
learned counsel for the parties, it is 
directed that the effect and operation of 
impugned judgment and decree dated 
31.07.2019 shall remain stayed, provided 
the revisionist deposits the entire decretal 
amount, adjusting the amount already 
deposited, if any, before the Judge, S.C.C. 
within a period of eight weeks and also 
continues to pay the rent @ 2,000/- per 
month by the 7th of each month. 
 Stay application CLMA No.12243 of 
2019 is disposed of.
