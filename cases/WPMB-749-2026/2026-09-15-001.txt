Hon’ble Manoj Kumar Gupta, C.J.
Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
1. Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. B.P.S. Mer, learned Standing Counsel with Mr. 
M.S. Bisht, learned Brief Holder for the State.
3. Mr. Girish Chandra Joshi, learned counsel for the 
caveator- respondent no.4 through video conferencing.
4. The contention of learned counsel for the petitioner 
firm is that its financial bid has been declared as ‘non￾responsive’ on a wholly unsustainable ground. His further 
submission is that the petitioner’s bid was for 
Rs.3,03,73,246.60/-, which is almost 25% less than the bid 
of respondent no.4 of Rs.3,99,01,459.68/-, who has been 
declared as successful bidder.
5. The bid of the petitioner has been declared as ‘non￾responsive’ on the ground that the affidavit filed by the 
petitioner in respect of availability of the machinery for 
execution of the contract was of a date prior to the date, on 
which, the bids were invited. The submission is that the 
affidavit was sworn on the stamp paper which was valid 
upto February, 2027 and, consequently, the mere fact that 
the affidavit was of a prior date, would not make any 
difference, nor there was any such prohibition in the 
Standard Documents for Procurement of Works.6. The further submission is that in identical facts and 
circumstances, in Writ Petition (M/B) No.665 of 2025, “M/s 
Prince Construction Company vs. State of Uttarakhand & 
others”, filed by the petitioner firm, in respect of a different 
contract, this Court has already interfered and has stayed 
the further process.
7. Mr. B.P.S. Mer, learned Standing Counsel appearing 
for the State does not dispute the aforesaid facts and that 
the issue raised herein is identical to that in Writ Petition 
(M/B) No.665 of 2026, wherein an interim protection has 
already been granted to the petitioner.
8. Notice on behalf of respondent no.4 has been 
accepted by Mr. G.C. Joshi, learned counsel.
9. As prayed by learned counsel for the respondents, 
they are granted two weeks’ time to file counter-affidavits.
10. List in the week commencing 05.10.2026 along with 
Writ Petition (M/B) No.665 of 2026.
11. Meanwhile, status quo in respect of the contract in 
question, shall be maintained by the parties.
