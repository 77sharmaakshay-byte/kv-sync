WPCRL No. 707 of 2026
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mrs. Krishi Shukla Bhandari, learned counsel for 
the petitioners.
2. Mr. Bhaskar Chandra Joshi, learned A.G.A. with 
Mr. Vijay Khanduri and Mrs. Rangoli Purohit, learned 
Brief Holders for the State.
3. The petitioner nos. 1 and 3 are the sons of 
petitioner no. 2 and all of them are praying for 
quashing of First Information Report dated 12.04.2026 
bearing FIR No. 0114 of 2026 P.S. Gadarpur, District 
Udham Singh Nagar, wherein, they have been 
implicated for the offences punishable under sections 
115(2) and 333 of BNS, 2023. 
4. The complainant herein is the brother of wife of 
petitioner no. 1 and it appears that there is some 
matrimonial dispute in between the petitioner no. 1 
and his wife. It is surprising that instead of registering 
the FIR atleast the SHO concerned should call upon the 
petitioners and the respondent no. 3 and then after 
interaction should take appropriate steps, but, here 
straightway the FIR has been lodged. The petitioner no. 
1 is serving in Army.
5. It is argued that the allegations as alleged are 
absolutely false.
6. Learned A.G.A. accepts notice for respondent 
nos. 1 and 2.
7. Issue notice to respondent no. 3.
8. Steps be taken within a week.
9. List this matter on 11.05.2026.
10. On that day the petitioner no. 1 and the 
respondent-complainant no. 3 and the wife of the 
petitioner no. 1 shall remain present in court.
11. Till the next date of listing the investigation 
pursuant to the impugned FIR shall remain stayed.
