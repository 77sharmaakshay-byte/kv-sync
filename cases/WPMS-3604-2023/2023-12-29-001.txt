3. By means of this petition under Article 
227 of Constitution of India, the petitioner 
has challenged the order dated 29.09.2023 
passed in Second Appeal No. 65 of 2023-24
titled as “Aman Bartra Vs. State and others”
(Annexure No.6), whereby, the Second Appeal 
filed by the respondent (petitioner herein) 
was entertained for hearing.
4. It is the main contention of the learned 
counsel for the petitioner that the First 
Appeal, which was preferred by the petitioner 
under Section 331 sub-Section (3) of Uttar 
Pradesh Zamindari Abolishment and Land 
Reforms (UPZALR) Act, was allowed and the 
case was remanded by the learned Additional 
Commissioner, Kumaun Mandal, Nainital to 
the learned Collector, Nainital to take the 
evidence of the parties and to decide the said 
Case No.51/01/2020-21 State Vs. Aman 
Batra and others.
5. It is submitted by learned counsel for 
the petitioner that against the remand order, 
no Second Appeal lies and therefore, the 
impugned order is bad in law and cannot 
sustained.
6. I have perused the impugned order as 
well as the order passed by the First 
Appellate Court and there is no doubt that by 2
the said impugned order, the matter was 
remanded back to the Court of Collector, 
District Nainital.
