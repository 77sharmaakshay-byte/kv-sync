3. Petitioner participated in an exercise for 
promotion from the post of Deputy 
Commandant to the post of Commandant in 
Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB). Learned counsel 
for petitioner submits that as per Office 
Memorandum dated 08.02.2002, issued by 
Department of Personnel & Training (DoPT), 
Government of India, there cannot be any 
supersession in promotion, where promotion 
is to be made by selection. Clause 3.2 of the 
said Office Memorandum, on which heavy 
reliance is placed, is extracted below:-
“3.2 Benchmark’ for promotion
 The DPC shall determine the merit of 
those being assessed for promotion with 
reference to the prescribed bench-mark and 
accordingly grade the officers as ‘fit’ or ‘unfit’
only. Only those who are graded ‘fit’ (i.e. who 
meet the prescribed bench-mark) by the DPC 
shall be included and arranged in the select 
panel in order to their inter-se seniority in the 
feeder grade. Those officers who are graded 
‘unfit’ (in terms of the prescribed bench￾mark) by the DPC shall not be included in the 
select panel. Thus, there shall be no 
supersession in promotion among those who 
are graded ‘fit’ (in terms of the prescribed 
bench-mark) by the DPC.” 4. Learned Central Government Standing 
Counsel, however, submits that petitioner was 
suppressed on account of his not being in 
Shape-I, as per medical assessment made in 
2023.
5. Since only one of the persons 
recommended for promotion would be 
affected, if the writ petition is allowed, 
therefore, for the time being, we are issuing 
notice only to respondent no. 6 and not to 
respondent nos. 4 & 5.
6. Steps to be taken by 05.01.2024.
7. Learned Central Government Standing 
Counsel prays for and is granted six weeks’ 
time for filing counter affidavit.
8. List on 27.03.2024.
9. The promotion, made pursuant to the 
exercise in question, shall abide by outcome 
of this writ petition.
