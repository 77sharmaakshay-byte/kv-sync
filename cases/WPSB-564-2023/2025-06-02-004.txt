Mr. Naitik Bhatt, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
2. Mr. Rajesh Sharma, Standing Counsel 
for the Union of India/respondent nos. 1,3 & 
6. 
3. Petitioner is aggrieved by denial of 
promotion to the post of Commandant 
(Communication) in Shashastra Seema Bal
(SSB). 
4. According to petitioner, as per norms, 
any officer, who has got three “very good”
entries out of last five APAR entries is entitled
for promotion. He submits that petitioner was 
rated as very good for the reporting year
2019-20, 2020-21 and 2021-22 and he has 
only two ‘good’ entries i.e. for the year 2017-
18 and 2018-19, therefore, denial of 
promotion to the post of Commandant 
(Communication) to him, is without any valid 
reason. 
5. Learned Standing Counsel Mr. Rajesh 
Sharma has drawn attention of this Court 
towards para 22 of the Counter affidavit filed 
by Union of India. In that, assessment of the 
petitioner made by Initiating 
Officer/Reviewing Officer and Accepting 
Officer, for the years 2017-18, 2018-19, 
2019-20 and 2020-21 is mentioned. However, there is no averment regarding 
assessment of the petitioner for the reporting 
year 2021-22.
6. On the request of counsel for the 
respondent- Union of India, case is adjourned 
to enable him to get clear instructions, as to 
whether petitioner was assessed as “very
good” for the year 2021-22 as mentioned by 
petitioner in para 17 of the writ petition. 
7. Mr. Rajesh Sharma shall make 
statement regarding petitioner’s eligibility for 
promotion to the post of Commandant
(Communication) based on three “very good”
entries, on the next date of listing. 
8. List this matter on 09.06.2025.
