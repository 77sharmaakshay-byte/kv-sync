Heard on interim relief application (IA 
No. 1 of 2025).
The petitioners did respond to an 
advertisement dated 13.12.2024 published 
by the Uttarakhand Public Service 
Commission (“the Commission”) for 
recruitment to the under Combined State 
Civil/Lower Subordinate Service 
Examination-2024. They appeared in the 
preliminary examination, but they have not 
been declared successful.
It is the case of the petitioners that 
many questions that were asked had wrong 
answers, which resulted in non-qualifying 
of the petitioners for Mains Examinations. 
Mainly, arguments have been raised 
on Question Nos. 2 and 33. Question No. 2 is as follows:-
“Q. No. 2- Consider the following statements:
(1) Uniform Civil Code for the citizens is 
described under Article 44 of the Constitution 
of India.
(2) Uttarakhand became the first State of India to 
implement the Uniform Civil Code.
Select the correct answer from the code given 
below-
(a) Only (1) is correct
(b) Only (2) is correct
(c) Both (1) and (2) are correct.
(d) Neither (1) nor (2) is correct.”
According to the petitioners, the 
correct answer is (C), whereas the 
Commission has given right answer as (A), 
which is wrong.
Learned counsel for the petitioners
has raised objection with regard to 
Question No. 33, which reads as follows:-
“Q. No. 33- Who among the following is the 
author of the book ‘Customary Law of Kumaon’?
(a) Taradatt Gairola
(b) B.P. Saxena
(c) Panna Lal
(d)L.D. Joshi”
 It is the case of the petitioners that 
Panna Lal has written a book namely, 
Kumaon Local Customs; he has not written 
any book with the name of Customary Law 
of Kumaon; there is no answer to this 
question, therefore, the petitioners have 
objected to it; according to the Commission, 
the correct answer is (c), which according to 
the petitioners is wrong.
Learned counsel for the Commission 
submits that, in fact, Goa is the first State 
to implement Common Civil Code. He also 
submits that, in fact, Panna Lal has written 
the book, namely, Kumaon Local Customs 
and the Customary Law of Kumaon. But, learned counsel for the Commission,
despite having taken time, could not 
support this with any document.
Learned counsel for the petitioners
submits that the forms for Mains 
Examination are to be filled today, 
therefore, they may be permitted to fill up 
the form.
Having considered, this Court is of the 
view that this is a matter, which requires 
intervention at interim stage. Therefore, the 
respondents are directed to provisionally 
permit the petitioners to fill the forms for 
Mains Examination and permit them to 
appear in the Mains Examination. However, 
the result of the petitioners shall not be 
declared without prior approval of the 
Court.
Interim relief application stands 
disposed of accordingly.
Let a certified copy of this order be 
issued today itself, as per rules.
