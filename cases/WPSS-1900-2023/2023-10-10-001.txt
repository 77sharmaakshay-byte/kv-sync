COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.1900 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. I.P. Gairola, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Narayan Dutt, learned Standing Counsel for the 
State.
3. By means of this writ petition, petitioner has 
challenged office memorandum dated 29.08.2023 
(Annexure no.7 to the writ petition), whereby the petitioner 
was called upon to show-cause as to why service of 
petitioner be not dispensed with.
4. Learned counsel for petitioner submitted that 
petitioner was, initially, appointed as Technical Assistant in 
a Government Company-Teletronix Ltd., Bhimtal, Nainital, 
which was wound up on 31.03.1996 and petitioner was 
retrenched.
5. It is case of petitioner that petitioner joined 
Teletronix Ltd. Company on 06.11.1987. After winding up 
of the aforesaid Government Company and M/s Kumaon 
Television Ltd., a scheme was launched to re-engage the 
retrenched employees of the companies, in different 
departments of the State Government, consequently 
petitioner was absorbed/reappointed in the Directorate of 
Training and Employment with State Government on 
28.06.2005. 
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that the 
petitioner was posted as Instructor in Radio & T.V. in I.T.I. 
run by Directorate of Training and Employment and since 
then petitioner has been continuously working with the 
respondent-Department in the capacity of Instructor.
7. Learned counsel for the petitioner further submitted 
that petitioner was not paid Ist A.C.P., despite his 
entitlement, after completion of ten years of uninterrupted 
and satisfactory service, thereafter, he filed a writ petition 
before this Court bearing WPSS No.1617 of 2023, 
“Bhagwan Chandra Pant and another Vs. State of 
Uttarakhand and another”, wherein this Court vide order 
dated 04.09.2023, while finally disposing-off the writ 
petition directed petitioners of that writ petition to move a 2
fresh writ petition, detailing their grievance and respondent 
no.2 was directed to decide the representation submitted by 
the petitioners within a period of 15 days’ from date of 
receipt of certified copy of the order and respondent no.2 
was directed to decide the fresh representations submitted 
by the petitioners, within a period of six weeks’ from the 
date of receipt of such representations, strictly in 
accordance with law and government order issued by the 
State of Uttarakhand covering the field.
8. Learned counsel for petitioner on 04.09.2023 
submitted his representation to respondent no.2 on 
11.09.2023. It is contention of petitioner that even before
the order could have been passed in the writ petition, 
respondent was so infuriated by demand of Ist A.C.P. by the 
petitioner that vide order dated 29.08.2023, petitioner was 
threatened to be dismissed from service and the impugned 
show-cause notice was issued, which was received by 
petitioner on 08.09.2023.
9. It is the contention of learned counsel for petitioner 
that although this is a show-cause notice, which has been 
duly replied by petitioner, dated 04.10.2023, showing cause
as to why the petitioner cannot be dismissed from service.
But he is afraid that he would be dismissed by the 
respondent.
10. It is contended by learned counsel for petitioner that 
in the show-cause notice, the reason for calling upon the 
show-cause from petitioner by the respondent is that, in the 
year 2009, according to the respondent certain decisions 
have been taken to remove those employees, who were 
engaged in the earlier Government Company after 
01.10.1986.
11. It is further submitted by petitioner that more than 
191 employees from these two companies have been 
absorbed and reappointed by the respondent-State and are 
serving in different departments, but only petitioner was
segregated and threatened by the show-cause notice.
12. Per-contra, learned State counsel submitted that this 
is a show-cause notice against which the writ petition is not 
maintainable.
13. Be that as it may. Having considered the rival 
contention and averments made in the writ petition, it 
appears that show-cause notice has been issued by the 3
respondent without any basis. If the decision to remove an 
employee was taken in the year 2009, why the respondent 
waited for last 14 years to take such an action against the
petitioner.
14. In this view of the matter, the respondents are 
directed not to take any decision on the show-cause notice, 
till the next date of listing.
15. List this case on 14.12.2023.
16. Respondents prays for are granted six weeks’ time 
to file their counter affidavit.
17. Stay Application (I.A. No.1 of 2023) stands 
disposed-off accordingly.
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
