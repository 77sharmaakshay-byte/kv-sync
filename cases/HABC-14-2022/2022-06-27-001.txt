The Court wanted to know from 
learned Counsel for the petitioner as to why 
this petition is to be entertained? According 
to the petition itself, the wife of the 
petitioner had left in the year 2005 taking 
away a very young child with her.
Now, what is being pleaded is that 
the petitioner is not aware of the 
whereabouts of the child. He has not seen 
it for long. The petition also records that 
the child was studying in a school.
Let issue notice to the respondent 
no.4. 
She can join the proceedings through 
video conferencing. 
The Registry may make necessary 
arrangements and also in the notice itself 
that she may join the proceedings through 
video conferencing. 
List this matter on 26.07.2022, at 
2:00 PM.
