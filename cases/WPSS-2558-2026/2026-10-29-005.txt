18 (1) The Commission shall, as soon as

7. A specific question is asked from the learned 
counsel for the Commission under which provision of law 
the Commission published the select list in the 
Commission’s website but there is no answer to this.
8. On perusal of Regulation 2015 particularly 
Regulation 13, the Commission should follow the 
procedure as laid down in the Notification dated 
01.10.2008.
Now, as per Regulation 8 of the Notification dated 
01.10.2008, it reveals that the Commission has no power 
to publish select list in it’s official website and after 
selection process is complete, the Commission has to 
forward the select list to the appointing authority and in 
turn the appointing authority shall publish the list in the 
Uttarakhand website, i.e., www.ua.nic.in .
9. The question is how the Commission without 
forwarding the select list to the Appointing Authority 
published the select list in its official website. It is settled 
principle of law that no right is accrued in favour of any 
candidate until and unless the appointing authority 
accept and approve the recommendation made by the 
Commission. Therefore, for what purpose these lists were 
published.
10. In so many matters this Court has observed that the 
procedure as adopted by the Commission appears to be 
faulty and there is a complete deviation of the procedure 
as prescribed.

Court submit a complete compilation of the Act and
