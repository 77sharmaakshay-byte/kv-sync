1. Mr. Saurabh Kumar Pandey and Mr. B.S. 
Kathayat, learned counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Bhaskar Chandra Joshi, learned A.G.A. for 
the State.
3. By the instant petition preferred under section 
528 of BNSS, 2023, the present applicant ‘Dr. Deepak 
Mehra’ is challenging the proceeding of Criminal Case 
No. 450 of 2026 arising out of First Information Report 
dated 07.08.2025 bearing FIR No. 22 of 2025 P.S. 
Dhaulchheena District Almora, wherein, the present 
applicant is now facing the trial for the offences 
punishable under sections 75(3), 79, 351(2) and 352 of 
BNS, 2023.
4. It is argued by the learned counsel for the 
applicant that the respondent/complainant was 
serving as Veterinary Pharmacy Officer and lodged the 
FIR with malafide intent and she has actual grievance 
with one another person namely Anil Adhikari and the 
applicant being a Veterinary Officer report to the 
Higher Official due to which she was seriously 
prejudice with the applicant why he has informed to 
the Higher Official due to which she lodged the FIR. 
He also submits that the respondent/complainant 
anyhow want her transfer to another place of her own 
choice at Haldwani. He submits that earlier interim 
protection was granted in WPCRL No. 503 of 2026, 
wherein, the present applicant challenged the First 
Information Report and now the charge-sheet has been 
filed, therefore, the instant petition is moved for challenging the proceeding. He submits that the 
charge-sheet has been filed in a very mechanical 
manner and the Trial Court without gone through the 
case diary and other material took cognizance in a 
mechanical manner.
5. All these aspects require scrutiny.
6. Learned State counsel accepts notice for
respondent no. 1.
7. Issue notice to respondent no. 2.
8. Steps be taken within a week.
9. Let the respondent may file counter affidavit 
within three weeks.
10. One week thereafter is granted to the applicant 
to file rejoinder affidavit.
11. List this matter on 07.09.2026.
12. In the meantime, further proceeding of Criminal 
Case No. 450 of 2026 arising out of First Information 
Report dated 07.08.2025 bearing FIR No. 22 of 2025 P.S. 
Dhaulchheena District Almora, shall remain stayed.
