1. Mr. V.K. Kohli, learned Senior Counsel assisted by 
Mr. Kanti Ram and Ms. Gurbani Singh, learned counsel 
for the appellants.
2. Mr. Siddhant Tiwari, Advocate holding brief of Mr. 
Narendra Bali, learned counsel for the respondent no.1.
Amendment (IA No.7/2026)
3. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
4. The appellants, by means of instant application,
have prayed for permission to amend the grounds of 
appeal. 
5. The appellants have sought to introduce some legal 
pleas by the amendment sought. The first respondent 
has filed objection against the same and learned counsel 
for respondent no.1 contends that ICC report is only 
recommendary in nature and, therefore, there was no 
need of challenging the said report. 
6. The contentions on merits are not required to be 
considered at this stage.
7. The amendment application is allowed. Learned 
counsel for the appellant is permitted to add the grounds 
as sought through amendment in the memo of appeal. 
8. The arguments raised by learned counsel for 
respondent no.1 on merits would be considered while deciding the matter finally.
9. The amendments in the memo of appeal would be 
carried out within a week. 
10. List in the 2nd week of July, 2026.
