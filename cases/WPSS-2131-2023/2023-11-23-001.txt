COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.2131 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
1. Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, learned Senior Counsel 
assisted by Mr. Chandra Shekhar, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. B.S. Koranga, learned Brief Holder for 
the State/respondent nos.2 & 3.
3. Mr. C.K. Sharma, learned counsel for 
respondent no.4.
4. By means of this writ petition the petitioner 
has sought indulgence of this Court for a direction 
to respondent no.1 to allot or recommend the name 
of petitioner for the post of Stenographer/Personal 
Assistant in Dr. R.S. Tolia, Uttarakhand Academy 
of Administration, Nainital.
5. It is contended that pursuant to the 
advertisement dated 27.07.2020 petitioner has 
submitted his application for the post of 
Stenographer/Personal Assistant; petitioner was 
selected and according to the terms and conditions 
of the advertisement, petitioner has to make his 
preference amongst 28 departments, which were to 
be supplied for the post of Stenographer/Personal 
Assistant. Petitioner has given his preference to be 
appointed in Dr. R.S. Tolia, Uttarakhand Academy 
of Administration, Nainital but despite of this fact 
petitioner was given appointment in Directorate of Medical Education, Dehradun and private 
respondent no.4 was given appointment in Dr. R.S. 
Tolia, Uttarakhand Academy of Administration, 
Nainital.
6. Learned State counsel to seek instructions 
from the Commission as to whether the selection 
process is still continuing pursuant to the 
advertisement dated 27.07.2020 and whether one 
post is still lying vacant with the office of 
respondent no.3.
7. List this petition on 28.11.2023 as fresh. 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
