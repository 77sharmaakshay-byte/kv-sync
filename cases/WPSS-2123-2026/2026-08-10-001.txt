4. Present writ petition has been filed by petitioner 
seeking a writ of mandamus directing the respondents 
to deduct the provident fund and other statutory 
contributions from the salary of petitioner who is 
working with respondent no.2 as Helper. 
5. Learned Counsel for respondent nos.2 and 3 
submits that such deductions cannot be made from 
the salary of petitioner as petitioner has already been 
removed from the service vide order dated 22.06.2024;

before this Court by filing WPSS No.1087 of 2024 in 
which removal order was stayed and petitioner is 
working since then.
6. In such view of the matter, respondents are 
directed to file counter affidavit in the matter within 
six weeks.
