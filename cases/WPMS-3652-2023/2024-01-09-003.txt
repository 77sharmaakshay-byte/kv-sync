COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.3652 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Rajendra Dobhal, learned Senior 
Advocate assisted by Mr. Shubhang Dobhal, 
learned counsel for the petitioners-defendants.
2. Mr. Shobhit Saharia, learned counsel for the 
respondents-plaintiffs.
3. Heard learned counsel for the parties.
4. The present petition under Article 227 of the 
Constitution of India is filed by the petitioners￾defendants challenging the judgment and order 
dated 30.09.2023, annexure-20 to the writ petition 
passed by the Board of Revenue, Uttarakhand in 
Second Appeal No.16 of 2019-20, Smt. Revti 
Pandey & others vs. Smt. Prema Devi & others, 
whereby the appeal was allowed and the case was 
remanded to the trial court.
5. It is submitted by learned counsel for the 
petitioners-defendants that in the earlier round of 
litigation, the matter had come up before this 
Court and this Court while entertaining the petition
under Article 227 of the Constitution of India partly 
allowed the said petition and twice remanded the 
matter to the trial court and this time again, the 
learned Board of Revenue remanded the matter 
without any rhyme and reason. 
6. According to the learned Senior Counsel for 
the petitioners-defendants the fact of the matter, 
which is appreciated by this Court is that the 
earlier when the revenue suit for partition under 2
Section 176 of the U.P. Zamindari Abolition and 
Land Reforms Act, 1950 was filed in the year 
1955, the same was dismissed as abated and that 
abatement has never been set-aside, therefore,
fresh suit is barred in view of the provisions 
contained under Order 22 Rule 9 of CPC.
7. Having considered the rival submissions of 
learned counsel for the petitioners, the petition is 
admitted for hearing.
8. Let counter affidavit be filed by the 
respondents-plaintiffs within a period of six weeks.
9. Thereafter, two weeks’ time is granted to the 
petitioners to file rejoinder affidavit.
10. List this matter on 12.06.2024.
11. Till the next date of listing, the effect and 
operation of the order impugned dated 
30.09.2023, passed by Board of Revenue, 
Uttarakhand in Special Appeal No.16 of 2019-
20,Smt. Revti Pandey & others vs. Smt. Prema 
Devi & others, shall remain stayed qua the 
petitioners.
12. Stay application (IA No.1/2023) stands 
disposed of accordingly. 
 (Pankaj Purohit, J.)
