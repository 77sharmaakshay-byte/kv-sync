Though the petitioner had earlier approached 
the writ Court by filing a Writ Petition (S/S) 
No.177 of 2017; which was allowed on 05.04.2017,
thereby directing the respondents to regularize the 
services of the petitioner. The said order was not 
compiled with, then in that eventuality and in fact, 
the petitioner’s recourse available to him under law 
would have been to file a contempt petition, which 
was not done so and in order to over ride the legal 
embargo which is created by Section 20 of the 
Contempt of Courts Act, the present writ petition 
has been filed for almost an identical relief.
But, before venturing in the writ petition on 
its own merits leaving all the contentions open to be 
raised by the respondents, they are directed to file 
the counter affidavit within three weeks.
List this writ petition as soon as the counter 
affidavit is filed by the respondents.
