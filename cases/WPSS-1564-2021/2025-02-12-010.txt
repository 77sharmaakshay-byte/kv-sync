COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No. 1564 of 2021
WPSS No. 1755 of 2022
Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. T.A. Khan, learned senior counsel assisted by 
Mr. Mohd. Shafy, learned counsel for the petitioners.
2. Mr. Pradeep Hairiya, learned Standing Counsel 
for the State.
3. Mr. V.K. Kaparuwan, learned counsel for 
respondent nos. 2 and 3.
4. Mr. V.K. Kaparuwan, learned counsel for the 
respondent corporation informed to this court that 
though the vacancies are available but due to the rider 
pursuant to the letter of the Additional Chief Secretary 
dated 05.09.2016 these available vacancies cannot be 
filled up by way of absorption/regularization until and 
unless this rider is lifted by the Government.
5. On the other side, learned Standing Counsel for 
the State Mr. Pradeep Hairiya informed to this court 
that after the letter of the Additional Chief Secretary 
the M.D. of the Corporation wrote a letter on 31.07.2017
to the Government, wherein, he has requested that no 
vacancies are available for regularization, therefore, 
create a supernumerary posts.
6. Now, the respondents may get the instructions 
what decision has been taken by the Government on 
the letter of the M.D. dated 31.07.2017. Furthermore, if 
the vacancies are available with the Corporation then 
why these available vacancies are not being utilized by 
way of regularization of the petitioners, who are 
serving in the department since 1989.
7. Learned senior counsel for the petitioners Mr. 
T.A. Khan also informed to this court that in fact 800 
vacancies were available with the Corporation in the 
year 2022 out of which a requisition was sent to the 
selecting body to fill up 200 posts and still 600 
vacancies are available.
8. Since, learned counsel for the Corporation Mr. 
V.K. Kaparuwan submits that due to the rider of the letter of the Additional Secretary these posts cannot be 
utilized for their regularization until and unless this 
rider is lifted by the Government. To resolve this issue, 
let a fresh proposal be sent by the Corporation to the 
Government giving reference of the vacancies and 
details of employees who are working since 1989 and if 
such a proposal is sent by the Corporation to the 
Government within a week from today, then within 
two weeks after receiving the proposal the Government 
shall take a decision and apprise to this court by way of 
an affidavit .
9. If this is the fact then the Corporation while 
sending the proposal to the Government may also 
examine this aspect. 
10. List this matter on 11.03.2025 along with WPSS 
No. 1755 of 2022.
 
 (Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
