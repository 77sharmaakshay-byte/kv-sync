This Application has been filed by the applicants
seeking anticipatory bail in Case Crime No. 170 of 2024, 
registered at Police Station Transit Camp, District Udham Singh 
Nagar under Sections 420, 467, 468, 471 and Section 120B of 
the Indian Penal Code, 1860. 
2. According to the first information Report dated 
18.06.2024, the informant and other partners had set up a 
factory for Solvent Extraction Plant in which the applicants were 
partners. They generated false invoices. They transferred a sum 
of Rs.1.91 Crore in the name of setting up a plant. They prepared 
fake bills and documents and committed fraud of more than 
Rs.80 Lakh.
3. Heard Mr. Arvind Vashisth, learned Senior Advocate 
for the applicants, Mr. Pradeep Lohani, learned Brief Holder for 2
the respondent and Mr. Basant Singh, learned counsel for the 
informant. 
4. Mr. Arvind Vashisth, Senior Advocate, contended that 
the applicant no.1, aged about 81 years and the applicant no.2, 
aged about 55 years, have been falsely implicated in this matter. 
They were not given any money under any trust and even in case 
of dispute it is a case of accounting. Applicants do not have any 
criminal antecedents.
5. Mr. Basant Singh, Advocate, has sought three weeks’ 
time to file vakalatnama and objection to the anticipatory bail 
application. 
6. Mr. Pradeep Lohani, Brief Holder, has also sought 
three weeks’ time to file objection to the anticipatory bail 
application. 
7. Time is granted. 
8. Having heard the learned counsel for the parties and 
in the facts and circumstances of the case, as an ad-interim 
measure, it is directed that in the event of arrest of the 
applicants Pogula Narasimha Reddy and Pogula Ashok Vardhan 
Reddy, they shall be released on interim bail on executing a 
personal bond of Rs.30,000/- and two reliable sureties, each of 
the like amount, by each one of them, to the satisfaction of the 
Investigating Officer/Arresting Officer, subject to the following 
conditions:-
(i) Applicants shall cooperate with the Investigating 
Agency and they shall make themselves available for 
interrogation by a police officer as and when required; 
(ii) Applicants shall not directly or indirectly make any 
inducement, threat or promise to any person 
acquainted with the facts of this case;3
(iii) Applicants shall not leave the country without the 
previous permission of this Court. 
9. List on 13.08.2025 for hearing on the anticipatory bail 
application.
