COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPSS No.2065 of 2023
Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. I.P. Gairola, learned counsel for the petitioner.
2. Mr. Sushil Vashisth, learned Brief Holder for the 
State.
3. By means of this writ petition, petitioner has 
challenged office memorandum dated 29.08.2023 
(Annexure no.7 to the writ petition), whereby the petitioner 
was called upon to show-cause as to why service of 
petitioner be not dispensed with.
4. Learned counsel for petitioner submitted that 
petitioner was, initially, appointed as Technical Assistant in 
a Government Company-M/s Kumaon Television Ltd.,
Bhimtal, Nainital, which wounded up on 30.12.1995 and
petitioner was retrenched.
5. It is case of petitioner that petitioner joined M/s 
Kumaun Television Ltd. on 12.051990. After winding up 
of the aforesaid Government Company and M/s Teletronix 
Ltd., a scheme was launched to re-engage the retrenched 
employees of the companies, in different departments of 
the State Government, consequently petitioner was 
absorbed/reappointed in the Directorate of Training and 
Employment with State Government on 14.12.2005 and he 
has joined his duties on 24.12.2005. 
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner submitted that the 
petitioner was posted as Instructor in I.T.I. run by 
Directorate of Training and Employment and since then 
petitioner has been continuously working with the 
respondent-Department in the capacity of Instructor.
7. Learned counsel for the petitioner further submitted 
that petitioner was not paid 1st A.C.P., despite his 
entitlement, after completion of ten years of uninterrupted 
and satisfactory service, thereafter, WPSS No.1617 of 
2023, was filed by this Court by one Bhagwan Chandra 
Pant, wherein this Court vide order dated 04.09.2023, 
while finally disposing-off the writ petition directed 
petitioners of that writ petition to move a fresh 2
representation , detailing their grievance within a period of 
15 days’ from date of receipt of certified copy of the order 
and respondent no.2 was directed to decide the fresh 
representations submitted by the petitioners, within a 
period of six weeks’ from the date of receipt of such 
representations, strictly in accordance with law and 
government order issued by the State of Uttarakhand 
covering the field.
8. It is contention of petitioner that even before the
order could have been passed in the writ petition, 
respondent was so infuriated by demand of 1st A.C.P. by 
the petitioner that vide order dated 29.08.2023, petitioner 
was threatened to be dismissed from service and the
impugned show-cause notice was issued.
9. It is the contention of learned counsel for petitioner 
that although this is a show-cause notice, which has been 
duly replied by petitioner. But he is afraid that he would be 
dismissed by the respondent.
10. It is contended by learned counsel for petitioner that 
in the show-cause notice, the reason for calling upon the 
show-cause from petitioner by the respondent is that, in the 
year 2009, according to the respondent certain decisions 
have been taken to remove those employees, who were 
engaged in the earlier Government Company after 
01.10.1986.
11. It is further submitted by petitioner that more than 
191 employees from these two companies have been 
absorbed and reappointed by the respondent-State and are 
serving in different departments, but only petitioner was 
segregated and threatened by the show-cause notice.
12. Per-contra, learned State counsel submitted that this 
is a show-cause notice against which the writ petition is not 
maintainable.
13. Be that as it may, having considered the rival 
contention and averments made in the writ petition, it 
appears that show-cause notice has been issued by the 
respondent without any basis. If the decision to remove an 
employee was taken in the year 2009, why the respondent 
waited for last 14 years to take such an action against the
petitioner.
14. In this view of the matter, the respondents are 
directed not to take any decision on the show-cause notice, 3
till the next date of listing.
15. List this case on 21.02.2024.
16. Respondents prays for are granted six weeks’ time 
to file their counter affidavit.
17. Stay Application (I.A. No.1 of 2023) stands 
disposed-off accordingly.
