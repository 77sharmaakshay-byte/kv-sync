Supplementary affidavit is taken on 
record. IA No. 3 of 2022 stands disposed of 
accordingly.
Appellants Nasruddin and Farida 
have been convicted and sentenced under 
Section 304B and 498A IPC and Section 4 
of the Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 on 
29.09.2021, in Sessions Trial No.124 of 
2014, State Vs Nasruddin and another, by 
the court of 2nd Additional District and 
Sessions Judge, Nainital. They have 
preferred appeal. In the appeal, bail 
application has been filed.
Heard.
Learned counsel for the appellants
would submit that the deceased and her 
husband were staying separate. The 
appellants were not staying with them. 
They have nothing to do with their day-to￾day affairs. In the dying declaration, the 
deceased had accepted that she committed 
suicide and her husband is innocent. It is 
argued that in order to conceal her 
mistake, she has blamed the appellants. It 
is also argued that, in fact, in the FIR, it is 
the case that the husband of the deceased 
and other in-laws harassed her for the 
demand of dowry, but it is argued that this 
case become false in view of the dying 
declaration of the deceased.
On the other hand, learned State 
counsel would submit that there are 
specific allegations. The deceased has 
given dying declaration blaming the 
appellants for the torture which she had undergone. The witnesses have supported 
the prosecution case in the FIR and it is 
not a fit case for bail.
Admittedly, the marriage between the 
deceased and her husband was 
solemnized on 05.11.2012. The husband 
of the deceased was also an accused in the 
case, but he died during trial. The 
deceased died on 31.07.2014 other than 
under normal circumstances by burn 
injuries.
According to the FIR, on 31.07.2014, 
the deceased was found in flames when 
her hands and feet were tied with ropes 
and her mouth muffled. There is a witness 
PW4 Mohd. Akram, he has stated about it. 
According to the FIR, the deceased was 
killed by the appellants and others by 
pouring kerosene and setting her ablaze. 
It is true that in her dying 
declaration, the deceased has stated that 
her husband is innocent. But, before this 
sentence, the deceased has also stated 
that her mother-in-law and brother-in-law, 
who are appellant no.2 and appellant 1
respectively, had badly tortured her, due 
to which, she committed suicide.
Having considered these factors, this 
Court is of the view that it is not a fit case 
for bail. Therefore, the bail application 
deserves to be dismissed.
The bail application is dismissed.
Paper book is ready. Let it be 
provided to learned counsel for the parties, 
as per rules.
List this matter for final hearing in 
due course.
Urgency Application Nos. 4 and 5 of 
2022 also stand disposed of accordingly.
