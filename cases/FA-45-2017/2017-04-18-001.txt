Having heard the rival 
contentions of learned counsels of both 
the parties, this Court is not inclined to 
disclose the detailed reasons 
highlighting all the merits of the 
controverting proceedings between the 
parties for the last two decades, lest it 
may decide the destiny of this appeal 
forthwith. 
So, I admit this appeal. Other 
party has already appeared. 
Summon the lower court record. 
List this first appeal soon after 
the LCR is received for final arguments. 
Meanwhile, both the parties shall 
maintain the status quo on the property 
in question. Neither of them shall create 
any third party interest/encumbrance 
in any manner, whatsoever. They shall 
not change the nature of the property as 
well, in any manner during the 
pendency of this first appeal but, at the 
same time, the court expects that the 
appellant/plaintiff will not prolong the 
pendency of this first appeal 
unnecessarily in future.
