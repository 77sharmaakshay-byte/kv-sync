4. The petitioner, who is Principal-Grade-2, 
Government Industrial Training Institute, Kaladhungi, 
has filed the present writ petition being aggrieved by 
order dated 25.08.2026, passed by Director (Training), 
Directorate of Training and Employment, Uttarakhand, 
Haldwani,, District Nainital-respondent no.2, attaching 
the petitioner with immediate effect and until further 
orders at the office of Joint Director 
(Training/Apprenticeship), Kumaun Mandal, Haldwani, 
Nainital; divesting him of his administrative and 
financial powers; and conferring the same in favour of 
one Nitin Kumar Sharma, Principal, Government 
1UKHC010162022026
Industrial Training Institute, Haldwani. The order recites 
that a complaint was received from 15 employees of 
the institute regarding harassment, exploitation and 
misbehavior towards female employees by the 
petitioner. It further records that in view of the said 
complaint, an inquiry Committee was constituted by 
order dated 14.08.2026. The said inquiry committee 
submitted its report dated 20.08.2026 in which it found 
the charges to be prima facie proved against the 
petitioner and has recommended for his 
transfer/disciplinary action. Acting on the said inquiry 
report, the impugned order has been passed. 
5. The contention of learned counsel for the 
petitioner is that the petitioner has an unblemished 
service record of more than 30 years and now he is 59 
years of age, i.e., at the verge of retirement. The case 
of the petitioner is that the charges levelled against the 
petitioner were wholly vague, omnibus and incorrect 
and he has denied each and every charge. He was not 
served with any charge-sheet. Further without holding 
any regular inquiry as contemplated under the 
provisions of Uttarakhand Government Servant Conduct 
Rules, 2002, the inquiry was completed within a week 
and on basis of absolutely vague observations made 
2UKHC010162022026
therein, the impugned order has been passed. 
6. It is also the contention of learned counsel for 
the petitioner that there is no provision to attach the 
petitioner at another place where there is no vacant 
position or/and divest the petitioner of his financial and 
administrative powers. It is urged that it amounts to 
awarding a punishment.
7. We have gone through the compilation of 
documents placed by the learned State counsel and it 
indicates that no formal charge-sheet was ever served 
to the petitioner; that on 14.01.2026, he was intimated 
that he was required to remain present before the 
Inquiry Committee on 18.10.2025 at 11:00 A.M.; that 
certain questions were supplied to the petitioner and he 
was made to answer the same; that the complaint 
does not cite any specific instance but only contains 
general allegations have been made; that even in the 
inquiry report, no specific finding has been recorded 
except for making general observations with regard to 
harassment, exploitation etc. 
8. Learned State Counsel is unable to point out 
any specific provision under which, the Government has 
power to order attachment of the petitioner or/and 
cease his financial and administrative powers without 
3UKHC010162022026
holding any regular inquiry as per service rules. It is 
also not disputed that the petitioner would be retiring 
next year. 
9. The inquiry held by the respondents could at 
best treated to be a preliminary inquiry and in case 
there was prima facie evidence the course open to the 
respondents was to proceed against him as per service 
rules and hold regular inquiry but which admittedly, has 
not been done. Consequently, the order of attachment 
of the petitioner at a different place and 
simultaneously, ceasing his administrative and financial 
powers, prima facie, appears to be illegal. 
10. Let counter affidavit be filed within three 
weeks. 
10. List immediately thereafter. 
11. Meanwhile, the effect and operation of the 
order dated 25.08.26 shall remain in abeyance,
however, this is without prejudice to the right of the 
respondents to take any other action as may be 
permissible in law.
