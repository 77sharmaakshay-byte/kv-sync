2. An F.I.R. was lodged by respondent 
no.3 against the applicant alleging that 
the applicant was operating a kitty 
scheme, in which the complainant had 
deposited certain amounts. It was 
further alleged that, upon maturity of the 
said scheme, the applicant refused to 
return the deposited amount to the 
complainant. Upon completion of 
investigation, a charge sheet came to be 
submitted against the applicant.
Thereafter, the applicant preferred Writ 
Petition (Criminal) No.1843 of 2021, 
wherein it was stated that the applicant 
and the complainant had amicably 
settled their dispute. As per the terms of 
the compromise, the applicant agreed to 
pay a sum of ₹2,50,000/- to respondent 
no.3, and the manner and schedule of 
payment were duly recorded in the order 
dated 08.10.2021 passed by a 
Coordinate Bench of this Court. 
According to the applicant, in compliance 
of the aforesaid order, the entire agreed 
amount of ₹2,50,000/- has been paid to 
respondent no.3. However, despite 
receipt of the said amount, the 
complainant is still pursuing the present 
proceedings.3. Considering the fact that the parties 
have entered into a compromise and the 
applicant has made full and final 
payment to respondent no.3 in terms of 
the settlement as recorded in the order 
dated 08.10.2021, no useful purpose 
would be served in permitting the 
criminal proceedings to continue.
4. Issue notice to the respondent 
no.3, returnable at an early date.
5. List the matter after receipt of the 
service report. 
6. Considering the submissions 
advanced by learned counsel for the 
applicant, it is directed that, till the next 
date of listing, further proceedings of 
Criminal Case No.4095 of 2022, pending 
before the Court of learned Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Dehradun, shall remain 
stayed.
7. Stay application (I.A. No.1 of 2026) 
stands disposed of.
