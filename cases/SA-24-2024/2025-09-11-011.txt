Amendment Application No. 02 of 2024
In

Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
(Through V.C.)
1. Mr. Arvind Vashisht, learned Sr. Advocate 
assisted by Mr. Rachit Manglik and Mr. Vivek Pathak, 
learned counsel for the appellant.
2. Mr. S.K. Mandal, learned counsel for the 
respondent.
3. Amendment application has been moved by the 
appellant for substituting one more ground in the 
memo of appeal.
4. Mr. S.K. Mandal, learned counsel for the 
respondent has seriously opposed the amendment 
application by submitting that the appellant cannot 
substitute completely a new ground which was neither 
pleaded before the trial court nor before the First 
Appellate Court.
5. Be that as it may, substituting or adding one 
more ground in the memo of appeal is nothing to do 
with the admission of appeal, since as per mandate of 
Section 100, Second Appeal has to be admitted, if there 
is any substantial question of law involved in the 
matter. Admittedly, instant Second Appeal has yet not 
been admitted and as such, objection can be raised at 
the time of admission of appeal. Appellant is simply 
seeking amendment in the grounds of memo of appeal, 
therefore, at this juncture, there is no justification in not 
allowing the amendment application for substitution 
fresh ground. Accordingly, in such an eventuality, 
amendment application is allowed. 
6. Let the necessary amendment be carried out in 
the memo of appeal within a week. 
7. List on 23.09.2025 for admission.
