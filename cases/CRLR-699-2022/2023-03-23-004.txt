CRLR No.699 of 2022
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Sagar Kothari, Advocate for the 
revisionist.
Mr. S.S. Adhikari, Deputy Advocate 
General for the State.
The challenge is made to the order 
dated 20.10.2022, passed in Misc. Criminal 
Case No.95 of 2020, Smt. Anita Devi vs. 
Ramesh Chand, by the court of Judge, 
Family Court, Vikasnagar, District 
Dehradun, by which the maintenance has 
been denied to the revisionist on the 
ground that she is staying separate on the 
basis of compromise. 
Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
It is argued that, in fact, by virtue of 
a compromise, the revisionist was staying 
separate from her husband in her 
matrimonial home only. Thereafter, she 
had been expelled from her matrimonial 
house on 08.03.2020. Reference has been 
made to compromise also. In her 
application seeking maintenance at para 
11, the revisionist has categorically stated 
as to how she was expelled from her house 
on 08.03.2020. This is what she has stated 
in her evidence. 
Having considered, this Court is of 
the view that this matter requires 
deliberation.
Issue notice to the private 
respondent.
Steps to be taken within a week.
List this matter on 21.06.2023.
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
