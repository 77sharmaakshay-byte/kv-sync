Urmila Bisht and others --Applicants
 Versus
State Of Uttarakhand and another -Respondents
Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
Mr. Jagat Singh Mehra with Mr. 
Anupam Verma, learned counsel for the 
applicants. 
2. Mr. G.S. Sandhu, learned Additional 
Advocate General assisted by Mr. Deepak 
Bhardwaj, learned Brief Holder for the State. 
3. Mr. Piyush Garg, learned counsel for 
CBI.
4. The instant bail application is moved 
on behalf of the three applicants, namely,
Urmila Bisht, W/o Jagmohan Singh Bisht, 
Jagmohan Singh Bisht, S/o Late Shri 
Narayan Singh Bisht (husband of Urmila 
Bisht-applicant no.1) and Jitendra Singh 
Niranjan, S/o Late Ratan Singh Niranjan for 
seeking bail in reference to RC0072025E0007 
of 2025, ACB Branch CBI, Dehradun wherein 
they have been charge sheeted for the 
offences punishable under Section 420 of IPC 
read with Section 318(4) and 111(2) of BNS, 
2023 read with Section 3 with 21(1), 21(2) and 
21(3), Section 5/23 and 25 (1) read with 
Sections 3, 21(1), 21(2), and 21(3) of The 
Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes 
Act, 2019 and Section 3 of The Uttar Pradesh
Protection of Interest of Depositors Act, 2016.5. It is argued by learned counsel for the 
applicant that applicant no.1 was salaried 
employee working as a Branch Manager in 
the Company, namely, LUCC (Loni Urban 
Multi–State Credit and Thrift Cooperative 
Society Ltd.) and the applicant no.2, the 
husband of applicant no.1 was the agent and 
the applicant no.3 was the Chairman. He 
submits that various depositors deposit their 
amount but on maturity the amount were not 
refunded and the different depositors filed as 
many as 18 FIRs and the investigation was 
carried out by the State Police and out of 18 
FIRs in 10 FIRs the charge sheet was filed but 
subsequently pursuant to the order of the 
High Court dated 17.09.2025 the 
investigation was transferred to the CBI and 
thereafter the CBI registered the RC on 
26.11.2025. He submits that applicant nos.1 & 
2 were arrested on 28.10.2024 and applicant 
no.3 was taken into custody on 17.05.2025. 
He submits that since the applicants already 
suffered prolonged incarceration and, 
therefore, they may be released on bail.
6. On the other side Mr. Piyush Garg, 
learned counsel, who appears for the CBI,
submits that by clubbing 18 FIRs RC was 
registered by the CBI and in total 
approximately 55 persons are under the 
scrutiny including these applicants and as on 
date 18 persons have been charge sheeted 
including all these applicants and against 
rest, the investigation is still going on. 
7. The official of the CBI also present in 
the Court and submits that further 
investigation is at a crucial stage and if these 
applicants are released on bail then there may be possibility that they may affect the 
ongoing further investigation since the 
investigation relates to the siphoning of huge 
amount of approximately Rs.8.15 crores, 
therefore, till such time further investigation 
is going on against the remaining accused 
these applicants be not enlarged on bail. 
8. Mr. Piyush Garg, learned counsel for 
the CBI pointed out that some of the accused 
persons left this country and the attempts are 
being made to deport them for the purpose 
of investigation and the process is underway 
and investigating agencies are in touch with 
the concerned department. 
9. Admittedly the CBI filed the 
supplementary charge sheet, charge sheeting 
all these three persons but the fact remains 
the further investigation is going on against 
the remaining accused, who are 
approximately more than 37 in numbers and 
some of them are in abroad then in such an 
eventuality the apprehension of the CBI is 
justified that if these persons are released on 
bail, at this juncture, they may affect the 
ongoing investigation, to some extent. No 
doubt the charge sheet has been filed against 
these applicants by the CBI also and the 
applicant nos.1 & 2 are languishing in jail 
since 28.10.2024 and so far as applicant no.3 
is concerned he was taken into custody on 
17.05.2025, in such view of the matter at this 
juncture, what this Court has observed that 
since the further investigation is going on in 
respect of the remaining accused persons and 
some of them are the Kingpin of this fraud 
and are living in abroad, therefore, this 
Court, at this juncture is of the view that applicant no.1, who is a married lady, the 
wife of applicant no.2 and languishing in jail 
since 28.10.2024 deserves for interim bail for 
a limited period. 
10. So far as applicant nos.2 & 3 are 
concerned no doubt the charge sheet has 
been filed against both of them but taking 
into consideration that further investigation 
as carried out by the CBI is in a very crucial 
stage and some of the accused persons are in 
abroad, therefore, at this juncture for 
considering their regular bail two weeks time 
is granted to the CBI to file counter affidavit. 
11. Accordingly, applicant no.1 is 
admitted to interim bail for a period of one 
month. Let the applicant no.1-Urmila Bisht
be released on interim bail for one month to 
be computed from the date of her release 
subject to her executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two reliable sureties of the like 
amount to the satisfaction of the court 
concerned subject to the following 
conditions:-
“i. Applicant no.1 after being 
released on interim bail shall cooperate 
with the ongoing further investigation 
in respect of the remaining accused and 
will not make any attempt to come in 
contact with the persons against whom 
the further investigation is going on.
ii. She will also not make any 
attempt to temper any evidence and 
shall surrender her passport, if any, to 
the Special Judge Buds Court, First 
Additional Sessions Judge, Dehradun
within 48 hours after being released on 
bail.iii. The applicant no.1 shall provide 
her mobile number to the Investigating 
Officer so that as and when it requires
for her cooperation in respect of 
ongoing further investigation, she may 
ensure her presence before the I.O.
12. Instant bail application is being kept 
pending so that by the next date the CBI may 
file counter affidavit. 
13. The interim bail is granted to the 
applicant no.1 in a peculiar facts and 
circumstances of the case with a 
humanitarian approach since she is married 
woman having responsibility to look after 
her children and also deserves to get 
privilege under Section 480 of BNSS, 2023 
however it will not be treated as a precedent 
in respect of the other co-accused. 
14. On expiry of the period of one month, 
the applicant shall surrender before the court 
concerned, failing which necessary warrant 
be issued. 
15. List on 17.08.2026. 
(Rakesh Thapliyal, J.)
