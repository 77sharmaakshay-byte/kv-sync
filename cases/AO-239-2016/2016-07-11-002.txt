This appeal has been filed with 
the delay of only eleven days, which has 
not seriously been opposed. More so, 
where-against, no written objection has 
been filed, hence, such delay is hereby 
condoned. Delay condonation application 
(CLMA 4388/2016) is accordingly 
allowed. 
Appeal is admitted for hearing. 
Rupees eight lakh be reimbursed 
to the claimants/respondent nos.1 and 2 
without asking any surety or security 
from them in this regard. 
Rest of the amount be invested in 
the form of TDR for at least three years 
with some local hill bank. 
Money withdrawal application 
(CLMA 6777/2016) stands disposed of. 
List in due course for final 
hearing.
