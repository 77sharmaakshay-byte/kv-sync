Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned Counsel for 
respondent no.3. 
The complaint in this writ petition, filed in larger 
public interest by the Gram Pradhan, and a resident, of 
Papdev Gram Panchyat, relates to the alleged illegal 
acts of the Pithoragarh Nagar Palika Parishad in seeking 
to construct a cow-shelter in their village, which is 
located beyond the territorial limits of the Pithoragarh 
Nagar Palika Parishad. 
Mr. Siddhartha Sah, learned Counsel appearing 
on behalf of the petitioners, would submit that the 
earlier Gram Pradhan had, without seeking approval of 
the Gram Sabha or the Gram Panchyat, illegally 
conveyed his consent for the construction of the cow￾shed; now the Gram Panchyat, by an unanimous 
resolution, has conveyed its unwillingness for the 
construction of this cow-shelter within the limits of their 
Gram Panchayat; and the action of the respondents, in 
seeking to construct a cow-shelter not within their 
territorial limits but within the territorial limits of a 
neighboring Gram Panchayat, is arbitrary and illegal. 
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, learned Counsel appearing 
on behalf of the third-respondent, initially contended 
that the cow-shelter is located within the territorial limits of the Nagar Palika Parishad. When we asked him 
why, if the cow-shelter was within its limits, the Nagar 
Palika Parishad had sought approval of the Gram 
Panchyat, learned Counsel would seek time to file a 
detailed counter affidavit. 
While the Nagar Palika Parishad is, undoubtedly, 
entitled to construct a cow-shelter within its territorial 
limits, its authority, to construct a cow-shelter beyond 
its territorial limits and in an adjacent Gram Panchayat, 
needs to be ascertained. Since construction has already 
commenced, as is evident from the photographs filed 
along with writ petition, suffice it to direct the third￾respondent not to make any further construction of the 
cow-shelter, in the adjacent Gram Panchayat, beyond 
the territorial limit of the Pithoragarh Nagar Palika 
Parishad, until further order.
