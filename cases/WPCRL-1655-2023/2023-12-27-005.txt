WPCRL No.1655 of 2023
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. G.C. Lakhchaura, Advocate for 
the petitioners.
Mr. Pramod Tiwari, Brief Holder for 
the State.
The challenge in this petition is 
made to an FIR No. 512 of 2023, under 
Section 60 (1) (a) read with Section 21 of 
the Uttarakhand (U.P.) Excise Act, 1910
(“the Act”), Police Station Ramnagar, 
District Nainital.
According to the FIR, the police 
visited a resort and found that liquor was 
being served there. A case has been 
lodged as the liquor was served without 
any license.
Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that the FIR is malafide; It 
has been filed to wreak the vengeance by 
the respondent no.3, Prakash Chandra 
Pokhriyal, Sub Inspector, In-Charge, 
Garjiya Police Post, Police Station 
Ramnagar, District Nainital because he 
wanted two rooms in the resort on a 
particular day. The room could not be 
provided because the rooms had already 
been booked for a marriage party. It is 
also argued that, in fact, a contempt 
petition was filed against the respondent 
no.3, the informant for making illegal 
arrest which is CLCON No. 346 of 2023, 
pending in this Court.
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that offence under Section 
60 of the Act, is not made out in the 
instant case.Having considered, this Court is of 
the view that this matter requires 
deliberation.
Issue notice to the respondent no.3.
Counter affidavit may be filed within 
three weeks. Two weeks thereafter, 
rejoinder affidavit, if any, may be filed.
List this matter on 26.02.2024.
Heard on Stay Application No. 1 of 
2023.
It is argued that the petitioner no.1 
has been arrested and granted bail.
Having considered, as an interim 
measure, till the next date of listing, 
further proceedings in terms of 
investigation etc, pursuant to FIR No. 512 
of 2023, under Section 60 (1) (a) read 
with Section 21 of the Act, Police Station 
Ramnagar, District Nainital shall remain 
stayed qua the petitioner no.2.
Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
