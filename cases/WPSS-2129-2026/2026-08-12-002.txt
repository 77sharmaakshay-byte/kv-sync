Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. C.D. Bahuguna, learned Senior
Advocate assisted by Mr. Chandra Shekhar
Dalakoti, learned counsel for the petitioners.
2. Mr. Vinay Kumar, learned counsel for the
respondents.
3. This Court after hearing both the parties,
had passed the order on 10.08.2026 asking
learned counsel for the respondents to get
instructions as to whether the draft regulations
i.e. UJVN Ltd. Accounts (Officers) Service
Regulations, 2019 were followed to give
promotion to the post of Accountant Officers or
not. 
4. The learned counsel for the respondents on
instructions submitted that no promotion was
made on the basis of Draft Regulations of 2019,
as referred herein above, but the promotion was
given on the basis of the earlier Regulation of
1984 after amending Regulation 7 in the year
2022. Thus, the contention of learned Senior
Advocate for petitioners that in case of thepromotion to the post of Account Officer, the
Draft Rules of 2019 were taken into
consideration and the promotion was made
thereunder and in the case of the petitioners,
promotion is not being done in accordance with
the Draft Rules of 2020, is not correct.
5. Both the parties are ad idem that UJVN
Accounts Staff Service Regulations, 2020 (Draft),
is ready and waiting for approval from the State
Government. Since the approval has not been
given to those Draft Rules, the promotion cannot
be made on the basis of the UJVN Accounts Staff
Service Regulations, 2020 (Draft).
6. In such view of the matter, the controversy
would be settled, if approval is sought by the
respondents-UJVN to the Draft Rules and the
promotion would be made in accordance with
that. 
7. The petitioners may implead the State
Government as party to the proceedings in the
present writ petition, so that a direction can be
issued to the State Government to expedite the
approval on the Draft Rules of 2020.
8. List this case on 24.08.2026.
9. Till 24.08.2026 only, no promotional
exercise shall be conducted by the respondents
on the post of Assistant Accountant.
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
