3. In compliance to the directions of this Court, 
the DGP and the Secretary Home filed their 
respective affidavits. In paragraph 5, a reference 
has been made that the “Investigation Cell” of 150 
police officials has been formed and deployed 
across the State. The district wise deployment has 
also been mentioned in paragraph 5.
4. On instructions, Mr. Amit Bhatt, learned 
Government Advocate, submits that the strength of
police officials can be increased in the said 
establishment as per the future requirement. Apart 
from this, other several steps have also been taken 
in order to comply with the directions issued by the 
Hon’ble Apex Court time to time, particularly, in 
the case of “Joginder Kumar Vs. State of U.P.”
reported in 1994 (4) SCC 260 and “Lalita Kumari 
Vs. State of U.P.” reported in 2014 (2) SCC 1, 
wherein the Hon’ble Apex Court observed that the 
arrest generally should be avoided except in 
heinous crimes by referring the report of the 
“National Commission of Police”. 
5. It is submitted by the learned Government 
Advocate that the mandate of Joginder Kumar’sdecision has to be followed strictly in letter and 
spirit. 
6. In reference to the registration of the FIR, a 
specific averment has been made in the affidavit 
that the law declared by the Hon’ble Apex Court in 
the catena of judgments, particularly, in the case of 
Lalita Kumari (Supra), shall be followed strictly 
and the circulars have also been issued in this 
regard to all the officials, and time to time all the
pending investigation will also be reviewed. 
7. In paragraph 28, a reference has been made 
that the Director of Forensic Science Laboratory,
was instructed to submit a report involving the 
case of rape, POCSO and NDPS and all other 
heinous offences within the statutory period as 
prescribed for filing the chargesheet i.e. 90 days’ 
and 60 days’, as the case may be, and therefore, the 
Forensic Science Laboratory have to furnish their 
reports positively within 75 days and 45 days, as 
the case may be. In this regard, the letter was also 
issued to the Director of Forensic Science 
Laboratory, which is appended as Annexure no.5.
8. This Court takes serious note on the averment 
as made in paragraph 28, particularly when the 
scientific evidence is most vital evidence for the 
trial, therefore the Director of Forensic Science 
Laboratory, is under legal obligation to furnish 
report to the concerned Investigating Officer on or 
before the expiry of the stipulated period as 
provided under the Act for furnishing the 
chargesheet. In this regard, the DGP issued a 
circular to all the SSP(s) to instruct the 
Investigating Officer(s) to send the sample within 
one week to the Forensic Science Laboratory after 
collecting the sample. 
9. Apart from this, in the affidavit, it has also 
been apprised that the Scientific Officers have also 
been increased from 11 to 28 in the Forensic Science 
Laboratory, therefore, in such an eventuality, this 
Court is also hope and trust that the Forensic 
Science Laboratory will submit their sample report within the period and there should be no delay in 
this regard.
10. This Court also advised to the DGP to make 
an arrangement for regular training programmes in 
every city by taking the services of the Judicial 
Officer(s) and the practicing lawyers, who have 
vast experience to conduct the trial. 
11. Since now the Secretary, Home and the DGP 
are sincere about all these steps as taken and bring 
to the notice of this Court by way of an affidavit, 
the issue, at this juncture to this extent is closed 
with this hope and trust that in future they will 
take remedial steps as and when it requires.
