Considering the fact that the respondent-wife is 
already a recipient of maintenance @ Rs. 15,000/- in 
a proceeding under Section 24 of the Hindu Marriage 
Act, this Court is of the view that the interim 
arrangement of remittance of Rs. 15,000/- per month 
was appropriately determined at the interim stage and 
there is no typographical error as such as pleaded in 
the Modification Application.
Consequently, the Modification Application 
No. 2713/2019 stands rejected.
