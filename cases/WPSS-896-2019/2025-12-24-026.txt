Hon’ble Mr. Justice Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. There is a serious debate from both the sides with regard to 
the amendment carried out in 1988 by the Board of Directors in its 
Meeting No. 39 held on 15.03.1988 particularly in reference to 
Rule 39, which pertains to procedure for premature retirement of 
executives and under the head of “Inefficiency” there is a 
reference of another category i.e. C-. The similar category of C- is 
also mentioned under the head of “Decision” under this 
amendment. 
2. According to Mr. Rajendra Dobhal, learned senior counsel 
by virtue of the said amendment of 1988 the another category of 
‘C-‘ was inserted, which is completely independent to the 
category ‘C’. In support of this he also placed the original rules of 
1982, wherein, in hindi as well as in English there is reference of 2
category C (-). This rule which Mr. Dobhal supplied is in English 
and hindi and both are being reproduced herein as under:
“(i) INEFFICIENCY
Inefficiency would be evaluated on the basis of the appraisal 
reports. An employee who has secured consecutively C(-) for 
three years in his Appraisal Reports, may be deemed as a fit case 
for premature retirement.

3. Aforesaid rule also further refers the decision and under the 
heading of decision also there is a reference of the category C(-). 
He submits that since the petitioner herein was retired 
prematurely on the basis of his appraisal report, which does not 
contain any C- category rather the appraisal report only contains 
‘C’ category, therefore, the order of premature retirement is illegal 
as the same has been passed in complete violation of amendment 
of 1988. He further submits that in three consecutive years in his 
appraisal report there is only entry of ‘C’ category, therefore, there 
was no occasion to retire petitioner prematurely.
4. On the contrary, Mr. V.K. Kohli, learned senior counsel for 
the respondent, submits that there is no category of C- and the 
only category is C. He submits that by virtue of amendment it is 
absolutely incorrect to say that any further category of C- has been 
inserted.
5. On the previous date the respondents were directed to file 
the supplementary affidavit and the supplementary affidavit has 
been filed on 11.08.2025, wherein, the proceeding of Meeting No. 
39 dated 15.03.1988 of the Board of Directors is enclosed and 
under the head of inefficiency there appears to be some erasen of 3
minus though under the heading of decision there is a reference of 
C-.
6. Now, the question is whether by virtue of amendment of 
1988 any sub category of grading has been inserted i.e. C- or by 
virtue of an amendment the only category is C, which was already 
in the original rules.
7. Mr. Pankaj Tyagi, the Chief General Manager on the request 
of Mr. V.K. Kohli, learned senior counsel, joined the proceeding 
through V.C. and apprise to this court that ‘-‘ is shown in the 
amendment is nothing but a clerical error.
8. Let Mr. Pankaj Tyagi may file his affidavit in this regard on 
or before 06.01.2025.
9. Put up this matter on 07.01.2026 on top of Board.
