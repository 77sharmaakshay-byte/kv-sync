The Court was informed about the 
disobedience of the Court’s order. In fact, 
the Court had directed to take a decision on 
the question of regularization of the 
petitioners.
The matter was deferred, therefore, 
the respondent no.1 was requested to join 
the proceedings through video 
conferencing.
Today, the respondent no.1 joined the 
proceedings through video conferencing. He 
submits that the operation of the 
regularization rules of 2013 has been 
stayed by the Hon’ble Supreme Court,
which was finally decided sometimes in the 
year 2024. Thereafter, the rules were to be 
modified, which has recently been done on 
05.12.2025. He submits that now, they will 
undertake the process, in view of the 
Court’s directions. He seeks three months’ 
time. List this matter on 13.03.2026. 
On that date, a statement shall be 
given that the Court’s order has been 
complied with. The Court would not 
consider any further extension, on 
whatsoever ground.
