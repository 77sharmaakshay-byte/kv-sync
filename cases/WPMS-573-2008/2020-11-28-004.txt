An Intervention Application had been filed 
by one Amita Sharma being Intervention 
Application No. 10313/202. 
The said Intervention Application is 
objected by one of the respondents to the present 
writ petition, alleging thereof that Amita Sharma 
would not be the necessary party to the present 
writ petition, for the reason being that she was not 
a party to the proceedings, which were held under 
Section 48(3) before the Court of Deputy Director 
of Consolidation. 
Since, she not being a party to the 
proceedings before the Court below, she is not a 
necessary party; but however, taking a pragmatic 
view, the Intervention Application would stand 
allowed, subject to the condition that the intervener 
Amita Sharma would be impleaded as a party 
respondent, limited to the extent that she would be 
permitted to argue the matter at the stage of final 
hearing only. 
The Intervention Application No.
10313/2020 would stand disposed of accordingly 
subject to above terms.
