In pursuance of our order dated 03.08.2022, a 
counter-affidavit has been filed on behalf of 
respondent nos.2 to 4. The said counter-affidavit has 
been perused. 
The counter-affidavit, inter alia, discloses that 
from 04.06.2022 onwards the irrigation work is being 
done continuously. The treated water from the Sewage 
Treatment Plant is being sent through the canal to 
irrigate 102 hectares of land. 
It appears that the entire treated water is, 
however, not required for purpose of irrigation. The 
remaining water, which is not used for purpose of 
irrigation, is being discharged into the Ganga. The 
respondents have stated that samples are received 
from the STP and are being tested by the TPI at an 
interval of 15 days. Copies of the reports of the tests 
conducted have been placed on record. 
Let the petitioner examine the reports placed on 
record, and file a rejoinder affidavit within four weeks. So far as the action to be taken against the 
named officers, whose conduct has been found to be 
actionable, it is stated that enquiry has been initiated 
against them, which is in progress. The respondents 
should report the further progress made in that regard 
by way of filing further status report before the next 
date. 
It is also stated that certain works in respect of 
projects, in question, are still not complete. A further 
status report in that regard should also be filed before 
the next date. 
List this case on 11.01.2023. 
In the meantime, the petitioner shall take steps 
to serve respondent no.6.
