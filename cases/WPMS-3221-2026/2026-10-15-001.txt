It is the case of the petitioner that initially, 
the defendant filed a release application against 
the petitioner for vacating a shop under Section 
21 of the U.P. Urban Buildings (Regulation of 
Letting, Rent and Eviction) Act, 1972, which was 
decreed and possession has already been handed 
over. But, thereafter, without any rhyme or 
reason, the defendants filed Civil Suit No.25 of 
2023, in the court of Civil Judge, Senior Division,
Ramnagar, District Nainital (“the suit”) for 
eviction from a tin shed in the possession of the 
petitioner. 
It is argued that the petitioner was never 
informed about the suit. The tin shed was 
constructed by the petitioner on Nagarpalika land. The defendant has nothing to do with it. 
Somehow the suit was decreed ex parte, and 
when the petitioner came to know about it, he 
filed an application under Order 9 Rule 13 CPC 
for setting aside the ex parte decree, and had 
also filed an application for condonation of delay 
under Section 5 of the Limitation Act, 1963, and 
also filed a stay application for keeping the ex 
parte decree in abeyance, but that has not been 
decided and execution proceedings have been 
initiated, which may defeat the rights of the 
petitioner. 
Having heard, as an interim measure, till 
the next date of listing, the operation of the 
Execution proceedings in Civil Execution Case 
No.3 of 2024, Sarvesh Kumar Mittal v. Ram 
Singh, pending in the court of Civil Judge, 
Senior Division, Ramnagar, District Nainital, 
shall remain in abeyance. 
Stay application stands disposed of, 
accordingly.
