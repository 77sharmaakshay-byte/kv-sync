Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
 Mr. Lalit Belwal, Advocate for the appellant. 
 Admit the appeal. 
 Issue notices to the respondents. Steps within ten days. 
 Also, call for the L.C.R. 
 List thereafter. 
 Learned counsel for the appellant has argued that the 
accident occurred on account of dashing of two vehicles with 
each other. Though, the negligence was contributory, 
nonetheless, the liability of entire compensation has been 
fastened upon the insurance company of only one vehicle. He 
has further argued that in order to evaluate the compensation, 
the Grade Pay, Service Pay and Class Pay have been added 
twice, which ought not to have been done. It was next argued 
that the income tax required to be payable by the deceased 
has not been deducted from his income. 
 So, looking to the above factors, the Court directs that 
the recovery of awarded amount, as against the appellant￾National Insurance Company, shall remain stayed subject to 
the deposit of Rs.15.00 lakh (Rupees Fifteen Lakh Only) by it, 
including the compulsory statutory amount, before the 
concerned Tribunal, within three weeks from today,. 
 Stay application (CLMA 3787 of 2015) stands disposed of 
accordingly.
