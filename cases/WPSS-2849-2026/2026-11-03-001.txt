apparently in complete defiance of the order of the 
writ court dated 25.08.2025 passed in bunch of 
petitions, the leading one is WPSS No. 483 of 2024, as 
well as another judgment of the Coordinate 
Bench dated 22.09.2025 passed in bunch of petitions, 
the leading one is WPSS No. 494 of 2024.
5. Both these two judgments relates to the 
advertisement in which the petitioner participate. 
Surprisingly, the Director by the impugned order by 
taking cognizance of another judgment which has 
nothing to do with the advertisement rejected the 
representation of the petitioner and refused to grant 
relaxation.
6. In such view of the matter, post this matter on 
05.11.2026.
7. The Director shall join the proceeding through 
V.C. and justify his stand in rejecting representation 
which appears be in complete defiance of the order of 
the writ court.
