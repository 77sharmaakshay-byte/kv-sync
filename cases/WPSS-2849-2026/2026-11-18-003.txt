4. Admittedly the order impugned whereby 
the claim of the petitioner for seeking age 
relaxation has been rejected without gone through 
with the judgment of the writ court dated 
25.08.2025 passed in bunch of writ petitions 
leading one is WPSS No.483 of 2024 “Abhilasha 
Saxena and others Vs. State of Uttarakhand and 
others”.
5. It appears that the official respondent who 
pass the order impugned have not applied their
mind. At this juncture, the operative part of the 
order of the writ court dated 25.08.2025, is being 
extracted hereinasunder:-
“26. All the writ petitions are allowed. Pursuant to 
the advertisement dated 11.03.2024 of the Board, the 
cut off date for determining the age of the petitioners 
shall be 01.07.2020. By the interim orders of this 
Court, all the petitioners were permitted to participate 
in the recruitment process, but it was directed to the 
Board that their result shall not be declared. Now, in 
view of this judgment, the Board shall declare the 
result of the petitioners.”
6. As per the order of the writ court, 01.07.2020
was fixed as cut off date for determination of the 
age. Taking the parity of this judgment, the 
petitioner also filed a writ petition, but the same was dismissed, and then he filed the special appeal 
and ultimately the special appeal was disposed of
finally and liberty was granted to the petitioner to 
move a representation for age relaxation and 
pursuant to such a liberty, he made a 
representation to the Director, but the 
representation has been rejected by taking note of
an another order of the writ court which has 
nothing to do with this selection in which the 
petitioner participate, which shows that the 
Director has not applied its mind at all and 
completely overlooked the judicial verdict of the 
writ court dated 25.08.2025 passed in bunch of writ 
petitions. The official respondent has no 
authority to bye-pass a judicial verdict and they 
have to obey it in letter and spirit. 
7. The Director joins the proceeding through 
VC and apologise for the mistake which he has 
committed while rejecting representation and 
undertakes that he will take a decision afresh. Let 
he may take a fresh decision positively within a 
week, and then apprise to the Court on the next 
date.
8. Put up this matter on 30.11.2026.
9. The personal appearance of the Official is 
exempted for the time being.
