5. Order impugned has been passed in complete
defiance of the order dated 25.08.2025 passed by the
Coordinate Bench in WPSS No. 483 of 2024, the
operative portion of which is being reproduced as
under:
“26. All the writ petitions are allowed. Pursuant to
the advertisement dated 11.03.2024 of the Board, the cut off
date for determining the age of the petitioners shall be
01.07.2020. By the interim orders of this Court, all the
petitioners were permitted to participate in the recruitment
process, but it was directed to the Board that their result
shall not be declared. Now, in view of this judgment, the
Board shall declare the result of the petitioners.”
6. It reveals that the impugned order has been
passed by the Director taking note of the another order
passed by the Writ Court in WPSS No. 2126 of 2025
which has nothing to do with the selection, in
question. Now, the question, how the Director has
passed the impugned order in defiance of the orderpassed by the writ Court. It appears that the action of
the Director while issuing impugned order appears to
be contemptuous, therefore, this Court has no option
except to invoke powers under Article 215 of the
Constitution of India. However, before taking steps
under Article 215 of the Constitution of India, ten days
time is granted to the Director to file his response.
