Mr. Narayan Dutt, Brief Holder for

The challenge in this petition is 
made to the office order dated 13.01.2023 
(Annexure No.1) passed by the respondent 
no.3 in respect of disbursement/release of 
General Provident Fund amount of Late 
Sri Surjeet Singh Bisht in favour of the 
respondent no.4.
Heard learned counsel for the parties 
and perused the record.
It is argued by learned counsel for 
the petitioners that, in fact, while passing 
the impugned order, the respondent no.3 
did not take into consideration the 
definition of Family, as defined under 
Uttarakhand General Provident Fund 
Rules, 2006 (“the GPF Rules”). It is argued 
that in order to consider a mother as a 
family member, it has to be established 
that the mother was dependent on the 
deceased. It is also argued that the 
impugned order does not record that the 
respondent no.4, the mother of the 
deceased employee, was dependent on 
him. Reference has been made to Rule 3 
sub-Rule 3, as amended in the year 2017 
of the GPF Rules.
Admit.
Issue notice to the private 
respondents returnable within four weeks.
Steps to be taken within a week.
State may file counter affidavit, on or 
before 28.03.2023. Thereafter, the petitioners may file 
rejoinder affidavit, if any, till 11.04.2023.
List this matter for final hearing on 
12.04.2023.
Till then, the operation of the 
impugned order shall remain in abeyance.
