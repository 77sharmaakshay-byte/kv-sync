Mr. Lalit Belwal, Advocate present for

Heard. 
 This petition was dismissed for non￾prosecution, vide this Court’s order dated 
06.03.2014. 
Learned counsel for the petitioner has 
moved Delay condonation application 
alongwith Restoration application and has 
prayed that delay in filing restoration 
application may be condoned. 
Grounds mentioned in the affidavit are 
bonafide & sufficient to condone the delay in 
filing the restoration application. The Delay 
condonation application is allowed. Delay is 
condoned. 
Heard on the restoration application. 
The restoration application is allowed 
on the ground urged. Order dated 
06.03.2014, passed by this Court, is hereby 
recalled. The petition is restored to its 
original number. 
Issue notice to Official Liquidator. 
List this petition after three weeks in 
daily cause list.
