Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Anil Kumar Joshi, Advocate for the

Mr. Pradeep Kumar Chauhan, Advocate for

Mr. Rakesh Kumar Joshi, Advocate for M/s 
Asset Reconstruction Company (India) Ltd. 
In pursuance to the permission granted by the 
Coordinate Bench of this Court, permitting the 
secured creditors represented through Mr. Anil 
Kumar Joshi, to sell the assets under Section 14 of 
the SARFAESI Act, he had been consistently 
directed by the orders passed by the Court on 
19.02.2020, 03.09.2020 and 27.10.2021, to deposit 
the sale proceeds before the Official Liquidator as 
the petitioners’ company of which the ARCIL was 
the secured creditor. 
The sale proceeds have not yet been 
deposited before the Official Liquidator, as a 
consequence thereto settlement of claim of the 
Workman and other creditors are at stake. 
The Official Liquidator has submitted a 
report and had invited the claims as would be 
apparent from Annexure B to the writ petition 
submitted by him. 
Looking to the risk factor, which the 
Workman or the other similar creditors are required 
to be satisfied or met with from the sale proceeds 
received, by M/s Asset Reconstruction Company 
(India) Ltd., hence the assets of the Secured 
Creditor who was permitted to sell the secured 
assets, their property would remain attached by the 
Official Liquidator. They are restrained further to 
deal with their assets by transfer, mortgage in any 
manner, except with the prior permission of this 
Court. In the meantime, if so advised, the secured 
creditors and the representative of Workman may 
also file their counter affidavit(s) to the report 
submitted by the Official Liquidator. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.) 20.12.2021
