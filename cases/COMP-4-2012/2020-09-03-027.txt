COMP No. 4 of 2012
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
The matter is taken up through video 
conferencing.
There is no representation for the 
petitioner.
Mr. Anil Kumar Joshi, learned counsel 
for the Official Liquidator.
Mr. P.K. Chauhan, Advocate for 
Workmen.
Mr. A.K. Joshi, learned counsel 
appearing for Official Liquidator submits 
that M/s Asset Reconstruction Company
India Ltd. (applicant in application No. 
11548 of 2017) was handed over possession 
of the assets of the Company under 
liquidation by the Official Liquidator within 
the time stipulated in the order dated 
04.04.2019. He further submits that, as per 
his instructions, assets of the Company in 
liquidation have been disposed off through 
auction; but, M/s Asset Reconstruction 
Company India Ltd. has not furnished the 
details about the sale proceeds of the 
Company under liquidation.
Having regard to the submission made 
by Mr. A.K. Joshi, M/s Asset Reconstruction 
Company India Ltd. is directed to furnish 
details of the sale proceeds of the Company 
under liquidation to the Official Liquidator 
within four weeks from today. 
List this matter after four weeks. 
 (Manoj K. Tiwari, J.)
