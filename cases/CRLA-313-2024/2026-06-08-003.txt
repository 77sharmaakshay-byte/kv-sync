Instant appeal is preferred against the judgment and order 
dated 2011.2023, passed in Special Sessions Trial No.102 of 2017, 
State vs. Abhinav and Another, by the court of learned Additional 
District & Sessions Judge/FTC (POCSO), Dehradun. By it, the 
appellant has been convicted under Sections 328 read with 120B of 
IPC and sentenced accordingly. The appellant seeks bail during 
pendency of this appeal.
2. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the 
record.
3. Learned Legal Aid Counsel for the appellant would submit 
that the appellant Shubham has been in jail for more than three years. 
She would further submit that even the prosecution has not come up 
with the case as to which substance was administered to the victim. In 
her cross-examination, the victim has stated that she has named the 2
appellant Shubham merely because he had accompanied co-convict 
Abhinav. 
4. Learned State counsel does not dispute this averment in so 
far as the appellant Shubham is concerned. 
5. Having considered, this Court is of the view that it is a 
case in which the execution of sentence should be suspended and the 
appellant be enlarged on bail. 
6. The bail application is allowed. 
7. The execution of sentence appealed against is suspended 
during the pendency of the appeal. 
8. The appellant – Shubham be released on bail, during the 
pendency of the appeal, on his executing a personal bond and 
furnishing two reliable sureties, each of the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the court concerned.
9. List the appeal in due course for final hearing along with 
connected matter.
