Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Mr. Niranjan Bhatt, learned counsel for the 
applicant. 
2. Ms. Sweta Badola Dobhal, learned Brief Holder 
for the State. 
3. The applicant is seeking bail on the ground of 
parity of other co-accused Shravan Kumar, son of late 
Sri Om Prakash, who has been granted bail by the 
Coordinate Bench on 12.05.2026. 
4. Admittedly, the contraband, which is shown to 
be recovered is commercial i.e. 2 kg 09 gram of charas, 
therefore, in such an eventuality, rider of Section 37 of 
the NDPS Act will come into way. However, on 
perusal of the order dated 12.05.2026 granting bail to 
other co-accused, it reveals that Section 37 of the NDPS 
Act has not been dealt by the Coordinate Bench. It is 
settled law that while deciding the bail application in 
reference to commercial quantity of contraband, twin 
condition as stipulated under Section 37 of the NDPS 
has to be dealt with. Order dated 12.05.2026 granting 
bail to other co-accused does not reveal that Section 37 
of the NDPS Act has been dealt with. 
5. Let the State may get instructions or may file 
objection within ten days. 
6. List on 23.07.2025 along with record of BA1 No. 
44 of 2026 decided on 12.05.2026.
