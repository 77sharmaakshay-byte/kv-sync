Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Ms. Swaleha Hussain, learned counsel for the 
applicant.
2. The instant petition is moved by the present 
applicant ‘Manoj Kumar’, wherein, he is challenging 
the proceeding of Complaint Case No. 04 of 2025
‘Minikshi vs. Manoj Kumar and Another’, in which the 
learned Civil Judge (S.D.)/Addl. Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Bageshwar, summoned the applicant to 
face the trial for the offences punishable under 
sections 85, 115, 351 and 352 of BNS, 2023, r/w section 
¾ of Dowry Prohibition Act.
3. It is submitted by the learned counsel for the 
applicant that all the allegations as alleged by the 
respondent/wife are false and frivolous and infact 
there are certain issues in between both of them, which 
are very minor and the applicant being husband make 
all possible efforts to resolve the issue but neither the 
respondent no. 2 nor her parents turn up to resolve 
their matrimonial dispute. 
4. She submits that the applicant married with 
respondent no. 2 on 29.04.2024 and after few months 
of marriage she left and now residing at her 
matrimonial house at Bageshwar though there is no 
child from their wedlock. Apart from this, she submits 
that there is no demand of dowry and all the 
allegations are false, however, the applicant want to 
resolve matrimonial dispute with her wife (the 
respondent no. 2). She submits that the dispute is only 
for the minor issues, which can be resolved due to the intervention of the counsellor, therefore, both the 
parties may be called for the purposes of their 
amicable settlement.
5. Admittedly, the dispute appears to be 
matrimonial one in between the applicant and the 
respondent no. 2, therefore, this court is also of the 
view that the efforts may be made for their amicable 
settlement.
6. Mr. Sain accepts notice for respondent no. 1.
7. Issue notice to respondent no. 2.
8. Steps within a week through all modes as well as 
through the counsel appearing in the Trial Court.
9. On 07.10.2026 at 11:00 AM both the parties shall 
appear before the counsellor and thereafter the matter 
be placed before the court on 12.10.2026 so that the 
court may examine the counsellor’s report.
10. For an interim measure, the further proceeding 
of Complaint Case No. 04 of 2025 ‘Minakshi vs. Manoj 
Kumar and Another’ pending in the court of learned 
Civil Judge (S.D.)/Addl. Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Bageshwar, shall remain stayed.
