Mr. Pooran Singh Rawat and Mr. 
Abhishek Singh Rawat, learned counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. Vipul Painuali, learned A.G.A. along 
with Mr. Chitrarth Kandpal, learned Brief 
Holder for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition filed 
under Article 226 of the Constitution of India, 
the petitioners seek issuance of a writ in the 
nature of certiorari for quashing the 
F.I.R./Case Crime No. 259 of 2026 dated 
15.07.2023, registered at Police Station Kichha, 
District Udham Singh Nagar, for the offences 
punishable under Section 103 (1) B.N.S. The 
petitioner has further sought issuance of a writ, 
order or direction in the nature of mandamus
commanding the respondent authorities not to 
arrest the petitioner pursuant to the aforesaid 
F.I.R.
4. In the present case, the impugned First 
Information Report has been lodged by the 
complainant alleging, inter alia, that petitioner 
no.1, who is the wife of the deceased, and 
petitioner no.2, who is the brother of petitioner 
no.1, had been continuously harassing and 
victimising the deceased and were pressurising 
him to pay them the sale consideration 
allegedly received by him pursuant to an 
agreement to sell. It is further alleged that they 
extended threats of dire consequences in the event of non-payment and on the basis of the 
aforesaid allegations, the complainant has 
sought to attribute the unfortunate death of the 
deceased, who was aged about 36 years, and 
who expired on 05.06.2026 while undergoing 
treatment in the hospital, to the alleged acts of 
the petitioners.
5. Learned counsel for the petitioners would 
submit that the petitioners are innocent and 
have falsely been implicated in the present 
case; that, prior to the registration of the 
impugned F.I.R., petitioner no.1 herself had 
lodged F.I.R. No.219 of 2026 against the 
complainant and other accused persons, 
namely, Rajeev Ahuja, Ashish Nagpal and 
Anuradha (respondent no.3 herein), alleging 
that they had been criminally intimidating her 
husband by threatening to forcibly encroach 
upon his entire property and to eliminate his 
family. It was specifically alleged therein that 
due to the continuous threats and harassment 
by the aforesaid persons, the husband of 
petitioner no.1 into severe mental distress, 
ultimately resulting in the unfortunate 
incident; that, respondent no.3 had preferred 
an application seeking anticipatory bail before 
the learned court below in respect of the 
aforesaid F.I.R., which was rejected; that, 
thereafter, the accused persons in the said 
F.I.R., including the present complainant,
approached this Court, where they were 
granted interim protection.
6. Learned counsel would further submit 
that the impugned F.I.R. is nothing but the 
same has been lodged as a counterblast to 
F.I.R. No.219 of 2026 instituted by petitioner 
no.1; that, the allegations levelled in the 
impugned F.I.R. are wholly false, concocted and 
devoid of any factual foundation; that, the medical record itself demonstrates that the 
deceased was admitted to the hospital after 
allegedly consuming poison, whereas the 
complainant has attempted to project a 
different and inconsistent version regarding the 
cause of his death. According to the learned 
counsel, the contradictory stand taken by the 
complainant clearly indicates that the 
prosecution story is an afterthought, only to
falsely implicate the petitioners in retaliation to 
the earlier F.I.R. lodged against the 
complainant and her associates.
7. Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the record. 
8. This Court finds some substance in the 
submission made by learned counsel for the 
petitioners and having considered the 
submissions, this Court is of the view that this 
matter definitely requires deliberation.
9. Issue notice to respondent no.4, returnable 
within four weeks.
10. Steps to be taken within a week.
11. List this case along with ABA No.270 of 
2026.
12. Meanwhile, counter-affidavit be filed by 
the respondents.
13. Till the next date of listing, as an interim 
measure, petitioners shall not be arrested in 
connection with the F.I.R./Case Crime No. 259 
of 2026 dated 15.07.2023, registered at Police 
Station Kichha, District Udham Singh Nagar, 
provided, they co-operate with the 
investigation.
14. Interim relief application stands disposed 
of accordingly.
