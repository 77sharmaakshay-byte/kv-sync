 This is Misc. application CLMA 
No.4545 of 2018 moved by the appellant 
whereby a prayer has been made either to 
hear the stay application or to pass an order 
of status quo. 
 This appeal is not admitted yet. 
 Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that by order dated 2.4.2018 the 
executing court has passed the order for 
demolition, ignoring the fact that 
construction was already existing on the 
spot before passing of the decree and that 
he did not raise any construction. He 
further submits that revisional court has 
passed the order dated 2.4.2018 on the 
pretext that the judgment debtor has raised 
construction after decree and thus issued a 
direction for demolition. 
 Unless, this Court admits the appeal, it 
cannot pass any interim order. In my 
considered view, appropriate remedy 
available to the petitioner would be to file 
revision before the competent court of 
jurisdiction. 
 At this stage, learned counsel for the 
appellant seeks permission to withdraw the 
misc. application with liberty to avail 
appropriate remedy before appropriate 
forum. 
 Permission is granted. 
 Misc. application is dismissed as 
withdrawn with liberty to the appellant to 
avail appropriate remedy in accordance with 
law.  Certified copy of order dated 
02.04.2018 shall be returned to the learned 
counsel for the appellant after keeping copy 
of the same on file. 
 Let certified copy of the order be issued 
to the learned counsel for the today itself, on 
payment of usual charges.
