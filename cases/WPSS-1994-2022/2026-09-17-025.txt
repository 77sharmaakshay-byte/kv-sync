Heard on Miscellaneous Application, IA 
No.13 of 2026, filed in WPSS No.1929 of 2022. 
During the course of final arguments, the 
petitioners moved the Miscellaneous Application, 
IA No.13 of 2026, in WPSS No.1929 of 2022, for 
production of certain records by the respondents. 
The prayer is as follows:-
“(i) Call upon the Respondents to 
produce the relevant files/papers concerning the appointments of the 168 
regularized employees whose names can 
be found in the Enquiry Committee 
Report dated 20.09.2022 filed as 
Annexure No.17 @ Pages 434 to 442 
[@Pg.99-107, Convenience 
Compilation filed on behalf of 
Petitioners],
(ii) disclose details pertaining to how 
many of the 168 employees, who were 
regularized, are close relatives of the 
competent authority/prominent political 
leaders,
(iii) pass such other/further order or 
direction which this Hon’ble Court deem 
fit and proper under the facts and 
circumstances of the case.”
Heard learned counsel for the parties and
perused the record. 
Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the 
petitioners submits that the respondent-Vidhan 
Sabha has not been uniform in exercise of their 
jurisdiction in terminating the services of the 
employees. They have chosen only the 
appointments that were made post 2016. 
Though, there were 168 appointments which 
were made since inception of the Uttarakhand 
Vidhan Sabha, and all those appointments were 
ad hoc appointments. Therefore, the record of all 
those 168 appointments, if produced before the 
Court, would help this Court to adjudicate this 
matter effectively. 
The respondent-Vidhan Sabha has filed 
objections to it by filing Miscellaneous 
Application, IA No.14 of 2026.
Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the 
respondent-Vidhan Sabha submits the 
petitioners cannot claim negative parity; there 
has been no pick and choose done by the 
respondent-Vidhan Sabha; the appointments, 
which were regularized, were not touched, and 
whichever appointments post 2016 was ad hoc, 
they were scrutinized. He submits that no ad hoc 
employee post 2016 has been left over by the 
respondent-Vidhan Sabha. 
He further submits that calling the records 
of 168 appointees prior to 2016 would not help 
this Court to adjudicate the instant matter. The case is at the final arguments stage. 
The case of the petitioners is to be 
examined on the basis of averments that are 
being raised. If petitioners have some information 
with regard to some other appointments, they are 
free to file such material by way of 
supplementary affidavit. But, for the purpose of 
adjudication of the instant matter, this Court is 
of the view that the record of earlier appointees 
prior to 2016 is not necessary at least at this 
stage. 
Learned Senior Counsel for the petitioners 
submits that he would like to file a 
supplementary affidavit bringing those facts on 
record.
Be it as it may, this Court does not see any 
reason to direct the respondent to produce the 
records concerning the 168 appointments that 
were regularized prior to 2016. Therefore, there is 
no reason to allow the Miscellaneous Application, 
IA No.13 of 2026. It is rejected, accordingly. 
The petitioners may file supplementary 
affidavit within two weeks. A week, thereafter, 
supplementary counter affidavit to it may be filed 
by the respondents. 
List this matter on 15.10.2026, for final 
hearing, at 02:15 p.m.
