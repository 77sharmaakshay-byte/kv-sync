 There is delay of 245 days in filing 
the appeal. The reason assigned is that 
there is no one to do pairvi on behalf of 
the appellant or to bear the expenses of 
the instant appeal. In view of the same, 
he, thereafter, approached the District 
Legal Services Authority, Udham Singh 
Naga. Thereafter, the request was made 
to the High Court Legal Services 
Committee to provide free legal aid to 
him. Thereafter, the appeal was filed. In 
the said process, there is delay of 245 
days. 
 On considering the reasons 
assigned, we are of the view that the 
appellant has made out sufficient cause. 
Therefore, the delay in filing the appeal is 
condoned. 
 Admit. 
 Call for the records. 
 Paper books to be prepared. 
 Learned counsel for the appellant 
submits that he will submit arguments 
only after receipt of the paper book. 
 In view of the same, post the bail 
application after paper books are given to 
the appellant’s counsel.
