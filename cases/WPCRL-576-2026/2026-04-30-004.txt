6. In compliance of the previous order dated 
24.02.2026 Mr. H.M. Bhatia, learned counsel for the 
Income Tax Department get the income tax details of 
Kulwinder Singh and Somlal Goyal, the respondent in 
WPCRL No. 576 of 2026 and submits that though there 
is a huge transaction in between both of them but the 
same does not reflect from the ITR. The additional 
affidavit has been filed by the respondent no. 3 Mr. 
Somlal. In paragraph 2 and 3 he has given the reference 
of the amount of Rs. 9,60,000/-, however, the learned 
counsel makes a statement that it is a typographical 
mistake and infact the actual amount is Rupees Nine 
crore Sixty lakhs, the copy of which has been supplied 
to Mr. Bhatia in order to enable him to get further 
instructions from the Income Tax officials and to verify 
the amount.
7. Prima-facie it appears that both the parties are 
indulged in transaction of the huge amount but 
deliberately they are not disclosing all those 
transactions in their income tax return and, therefore, 
in view of the judgment of the Hon’ble Apex Court in 
the case of The Correspondence, RBANMS 
Educational Institution vs. B. Gunashekar and 
Another 2025 INSC 490 let the Income Tax Department
may verify and submit their report.
8. Both the parties are agreed to transfer the land. Ifthey want they can proceed.
9. Put up this matter on 13.05.2026.
10. In the meantime, no coercive action shall be taken 
against the applicants/petitioners provided they 
cooperate with the investigation as well as cooperate 
with the income tax officials of the Income Tax 
Department.
