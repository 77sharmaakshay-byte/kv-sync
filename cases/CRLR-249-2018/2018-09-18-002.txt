Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. Rajendra Singh Negi, Advocate

Mr. A.K. Sah, A.G.A. for the State of 
Uttarakhand. 
Against the order passed by the 
Judicial Magistrate on 1st June, 2017 in a 
proceedings under Section 12 read with 
Section 23 of the Domestic Violence Act, the 
revisionist was directed to pay a sum of 
Rs.3,000/- p.m. by the judgment dated 26th
May, 2018. Relevant portion of the 
judgment reads as under :- 
“11- The appellant admitted that he 
has 5 acre agriculture land and he is 
labouring to maintain himself. Considering 
the fact that the wages of a labourer is 
minimum Rs.250/- per day now-a-days i.e. 
Rs.7500/- per month, therefore, Rs.3,000/- 
per month out of the appellant’s income 
would be just and proper to be awarded as 
maintenance to the respondent, therefore, the 
learned Magistrate rightly awarded interim 
maintenance of Rs.3,000/- per month to the 
respondent. 
12. In the result, I am of the view that 
no infirmity can be said to be occasioned by 
the order passed by the learned Magistrate. 
The appeal has no force. Therefore, this 
appeal is liable to be dismissed.” 
When this revision was admitted, this 
Court vide order dated 16th August, 2018 
directed revisionist to make the payment at 
the rate of Rs.2,500/-. Relevant portion of 
the order reads as under:- 
“Having heard learned counsel 
for the parties, it is directed that on 
payment of Rs.2,500/- per month as 
interim maintenance to respondent no. 2, the effect and operation of the order 
dated 01.06.2017 passed by Judicial 
Magistrate / IInd Additional Civil 
Judge, Kashipur, Udham Singh Nagar 
in criminal case no. 28 of 2016 shall 
remain stayed.” 
The revisionist instead of complying 
the said order, the revisionist had not been 
diligently participating in the proceedings 
before the Court below and had been seeking 
adjournments. By the order, which is being 
sought to be challenged in the present 
revision, is the order dated 18th August, 
2018, by virtue of which, his application for 
adjournment has been rejected on the ground 
that the revisionist has been deliberately 
absenting himself before the Court and had 
not been complying the order dated 16th
August, 2018. This Court does not find any 
reason to pass any order on the Misc. 
Application No. 7378 of 2018. 
Accordingly, the Misc. Application 
No. 7378 of 2018 is rejected. 
Learned counsel for the respondents is 
granted two weeks’ time to file counter 
affidavit. 
 (Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
