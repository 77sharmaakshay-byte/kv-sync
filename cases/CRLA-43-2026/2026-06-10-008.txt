In the instant case, on 09.06.2026, 
bail was granted to the appellants Angrej 
Singh alias Rinku, Sukhdev Singh alias 
Sukkha and Gurcharan Singh alias Bunty.
Now, correction applications have 
been filed.
Heard.
Learned counsel for the appellants 
submits that, in fact, the appellants 
Sukhdev Singh alias Sukkha was also 
convicted by a common judgment, 
including Sessions Trial No. 165 of 2018, 
State v. Sukhdev Singh alias Sukkha, but it 
could not be transcribed in the bail order. 
In addition, it is argued that in the 
bail order, “Sessions Trial No. 164 of 2018” 
has inadvertently been mentioned as 
“Sessions Trial No. 164 of 2016”. Hence, it 
may be corrected.
The impugned judgment reveals that 
the appellant Sukhdev Singh alias Sukkha 
was also convicted under Section 25 (1B) (a) 
of the Arms Act, 1959 in Sessions Trial No. 
165 of 2018, State v. Sukhdev Singh alias 
Sukkha, which is not mentioned in the bail 
order, though he has been granted bail in 
Sessions Trial No. 164 of 2018. 
Accordingly, correction applications are allowed.
The necessary corrections are carried 
out in the bail order dated 09.06.2026.
Let a copy of this order be supplied to 
the learned counsel for the parties, as per 
rules.
