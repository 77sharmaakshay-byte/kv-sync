Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J. (Oral)
Both the appeals arise from one common judgment and 
order dated 17.12.2025/22.12.2025, passed in Special Sessions Trial 
No.164 of 2016, State vs. Angrej Singh and Others and in Special 2
Sessions Trial No.166 of 2018, State vs. Gurcharan Singh alias Bunty, 
by the court of learned 3rd Additional Sessions Judge, Rudrapur, 
District Udham Singh Nagar. By it, the appellants have been convicted 
under Section 302 read with Section 34, 120B and 307 read with 
Section 34 of IPC and 25 (1Kha) (Ka) of the Arms Act, 1959 and 
sentenced accordingly. The appellants seek bail during the pendency of 
appeals.
2. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the 
record.
3. According to the prosecution case, on 03.05.2018 morning 
at 10:20 when deceased Samir along with PW1 Khalid, the informant 
and PW3 Shakil were waiting for their friends Sandeep Arora to come 
outside the office of Harpal Kakkar at Kiccha bypass, suddenly two 
motorcycles borne assailants who had masked their face and 4-5 
unknown persons opened indiscriminate firing on them. 3-4 bullets 
hit deceased Samir, PW1 the informant Mohd. Khalid and PW3 Shakil 
Ahmed somehow saved their lives; deceased Samir was taken to the 
hospital where doctor declared him brought dead. FIR is named 
against some persons other than the appellants. 
4. Learned counsel for the appellants would submit that the 
entire prosecution case is false, prosecution has relied on the 
testimony of PW2 Aslam, who claims to have seen the assailants 
masking their face and opening fire but it is argued that his testimony 
is not reliable because as per the Investigating Officer this witness PW2 
Aslam was called by the Investigating Officer on 04.05.2018 when he 
did not reveal anything and subsequently on 12.05.2018, he claims to 
have seen the incident. 3
5. It is argued according to PW2 Aslam, one of the assailants 
had put his hands around the neck of the deceased Samir and then 
opened fire, which it is argued against the case set up in the FIR. 
6. In addition to it, it is also argued that in fact PW2 Aslam is 
not some stranger, it is he who had entered into an agreement with 
Samir with regard to the property in Delhi. Therefore, he is interested 
and planted witness. 
7. Learned counsel for the appellants also submits that in 
fact PW4 and PW5, before whom it is claimed that extra judicial 
confession is made, has not supported the prosecution case; they were 
declared hostile.
8. Learned State Counsel would submit that PW2 Aslam has 
witness the incident; recovery of fire arms was made from the 
appellants and he had also identified the applicants in the court; he 
also submits that recovery of fire arm was made from the appellants 
and the cartridge case, which was recovered from the place of incident, 
has been found to be fired through the arms recovered from the 
appellants. 
9. It is also argued that in fact there were transactions
between the appellant and the co-accused
10. Learned counsel for the informant submits that there was 
money transaction also; the appellants are hired shooters, there is 
motive also, which is land dispute going between the parties. 
11. It is a stage of bail post conviction. Much of the discussion, 
at this stage, is not expected of. Arguments are being appreciated with 4
the caveat that any observation made in this order shall have no 
bearing at any subsequent stage of the proceedings and in any other 
matter. 
12. According to the FIR, two motorcycles borne assailants as 
well as 4-5 other persons, who had masked their face opened 
indiscriminate fire on PW1 Khalid, deceased Samir and PW3 Shakil, in 
which deceased died whereas now the case has been little different put 
up by PW2 Aslam. PW20, the Investigating Officer has stated that in 
fact on 04.05.2018, he had called PW2 Aslam also that nothing could 
be revealed from him. Thereafter, on 12.05.2018, PW2 Aslam revealed 
the incident. How did it happen, if PW2 Aslam has no knowledge of 
the incident on 04.05.2018 ? How come he know about the incident 
on 12.05.2018 ? This may require deliberation during hearing of the 
appeal. 
13. According to the FIR, it is a case of indiscriminate firing 
but PW2 Aslam at one stage has in fact stated that one of the 
assailants had put his hand around the neck of the deceased Samir 
and then opened fire. There is a variation in the FIR. PW2 Aslam 
claims that he has seen the incident from Max station. 
14. The Court wanted to know from the learned State counsel, 
learned State counsel to show as to where is the site plan of this Max 
station and presence of PW2 Aslam, he could not indicate the same. 
15. Having considered, this Court is of the view that it is a 
case in which the execution of sentence should be suspended and the 
appellants be enlarged on bail. 
16. The bail applications are allowed. 5
17. The execution of sentence appealed against is suspended 
during the pendency of the appeal. 
18. The appellants – Angrej Singh alias Rinku, Sukhdev Singh 
alias Sukkha and Gurcharan Singh alias Bunty be released on bail, 
during the pendency of the appeal, on their executing a personal bond 
and furnishing two reliable sureties, each of the like amount, by each 
one of them, to the satisfaction of the court concerned.
19. List the appeals in due course for final hearing along with 
connected matters.
