Bail Application No.29/14 
IN 
CRLA No.10 of 2014 
Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
Mrs. Pushpa Joshi, Senior Advocate for the 
applicant/appellant Shamsher. 
 Mr. M.A. Khan, AGA for the State. 
 Having heard the learned senior counsel, it appears that 
Shamsher –convict was granted bail by this Court on 2.4.2013 
after more than three months of his arrest initially in the case. 
Along with four others, he was convicted by learned trial court 
on 24.8.2013 and since then, he is in gaol. As per 
prosecution, Km. Shakur was kidnapped by him and forced to 
marry with self. 
 Learned senior counsel has argued that the cousin 
brother of Km. Shakur is wedded with the sister of accused 
Shamsher. So in that way, they are closely related to each 
other. Besides, the allegation of Section 395 IPC also does not 
appear to have worth because the lodger of the FIR is not an 
affluent person. Moreover, remaining four accused have 
already been granted bail by another Judge of this Court. 
 Thus, looking to the above circumstances, the applicant 
Shamsher is also granted bail on his executing a personal 
bond and furnishing two sureties, each of the like amount, to 
the satisfaction of the Magistrate concerned. 
 Bail application no.29 of 2014 stands allowed 
accordingly. 
(Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.)
