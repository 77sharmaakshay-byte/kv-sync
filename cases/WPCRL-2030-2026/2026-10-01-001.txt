Mr. Ravi Joshi and Mr. Gaurav Kandpal,

2. Mr. Pradeep Lohani, A.G.A. for the State.
3. By means of the present writ petition, 
the petitioner seeks protection of his life, 
personal liberty, and dignity, as well as that of 
his wife and children.
4. It is pleaded in the writ petition that on 
the intervening night of 22/23.09.2026, at 
about 02:00 AM, six to seven police personnel 
forcefully entered the petitioner's residential
house situated at Flat No. A-8/905, Windlass 
River Valley, Harrawala, Kuawala, Dehradun. 
The petitioner alleges that they forcibly took 
him to Police Station Doiwala, and 
subsequently to the Laltappar Forest Area, 
where he was assaulted and tortured by the 
police personnel and pressurized to delete his 
social media account. Thereafter, the police 
personnel abandoned the petitioner at the 
spot. The petitioner's wife informed a friend 
about the incident, who tracked the petitioner 
and rescued him. The petitioner was medically 
examined on 24.09.2026 at Government Doon Medical College and Hospital, Dehradun. 
Medical report of the petitioner is also 
enclosed as Annexure-4 to this writ petition. 
The petitioner has also enclosed a pen-drive 
containing CCTV footage of the incident 
obtained from the Windlass River Valley 
residential society.
5. The allegations levelled against the police 
personnel are highly serious, if found to be 
true.
6. List this matter on 08.10.2026, as fresh.
7. Meanwhile, State to seek instructions in 
the matter.
8. Petitioner has also prayed that 
meanwhile CCTV footage pertaining to 
22.09.2026 and 23.09.2026 of Windlass River 
Valley be preserved.
9. Station House Officer, Doiwala is 
directed to preserve the CCTV footage
pertaining to the aforesaid dates of Windlass 
River Valley Residential Society.
