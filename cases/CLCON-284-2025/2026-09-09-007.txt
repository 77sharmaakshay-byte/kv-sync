passed by writ court as the order passed

never been set aside by the superior 
court.

the assurance given by learned C.S.C.

adjourned for a week, so that learned 
C.S.C. would apprise this Court about 
the progress and the compliance of the 
order passed by writ court to avoid the 
unpleasant situation.
