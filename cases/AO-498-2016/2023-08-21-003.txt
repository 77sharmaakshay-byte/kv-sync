2. Counsel for the appellant would submit that 
the relief has been sought against the respondent 
no. 1/Insurance Company and not against 
respondent no. 2/claimant in this appeal and she 
has been made proforma respondent as she was
the claimant in the claim petition; that, the appeal 
is only on one point that the appellant/Employer 
being insured by the respondent no. 1/Insurance 
Company, therefore, the liability should be upon 
respondent no. 1/Insurance Company to pay the 
compensation.
3. Per contra, counsel for the respondent no. 
1/Insurance Company opposes the same on the 
pretext that the Insurance Company is not a 
statutory party rather there was a contract 
between the appellant and the respondent no. 
1/Insurance Company, therefore, it is not the 
question of vicarious liability on the respondent 
no. 1/Insurance Company. Thus, he seeks and is 
granted time to place the law on the said point 
before the Court.
