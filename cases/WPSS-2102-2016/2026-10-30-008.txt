Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
1. Mr. T.A. Khan, learned Senior Counsel assisted 
by Mr. Mohd. Shafy, learned counsel for the 
petitioner.
2. Mr. S.S. Chaudhary, learned Standing Counsel 
with Mr. S.M.S. Mehta, learned Brief Holder for the 
State. 
3. On 03.11.2023, a Co-ordinate Bench of this 
Court had passed an order wherein the respondents 
were directed to bring on record any evidence to 
show that the petitioner was intimated about the 
factum of modification of the transfer order. Although, 
an averment to the said fact was made in the 
supplementary affidavit, however, no evidence was 
brought on record to supplement the said averment.
4. This Court vide order dated 16.10.2026, granted 
an opportunity to the Chief Education Officer, District 
Udham Singh Nagar, to file an affidavit towards 
compliance with the order dated 03.11.2023. No 
affidavit has been filed to the said effect, and today, 
the matter is listed, on the personal appearance of 
Chief Education Officer.
5. Mr. Harendra Kumar Mishra, Chief Education 
Officer, District Udham Singh Nagar appeared in-person before this Court and made a statement that 
there is no evidence available with the Department to 
prove that the petitioner was intimated about the 
factum of the modification of the transfer order. The 
said statement given by Mr. Harendra Kumar Mishra, 
Chief Education Officer, District Udham Singh Nagar,
is placed on record.
6. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
in Writ Petition (S/S) No. 588 of 2007, a Co-ordinate 
Bench passed an order on 24.05.2007 directing the 
Joint Director of Education, Kumaon Mandal, Nainital 
to take a decision on the representation of the 
petitioner for payment of salary after conducting an 
enquiry on the question whether petitioner had not 
joined at new place of posting willingly or he was not 
permitted to join by the authority concerned.
7. A supplementary affidavit has been filed by the 
petitioner on 14.10.2026, wherein it is stated that on 
the application filed by the petitioner on 14.08.1996, 
an endorsement was made by the then Principal of 
the Government Inter College, Kichha, that there is 
no vacant post of Senior Clerk as such, the petitioner 
cannot be permitted to join the duties. It is further 
stated in the supplementary affidavit that, on an 
application filed by the petitioner, on 17.08.1996 the 
Additional Director Education (Uttarakhand Camp 
Office, Lucknow) passed an order on 26.08.1996 
directing the Deputy Director of Education, Nainital to 
permit the petitioner to join at the transferred place 
and it was directed that the Senior Clerk may be 
transferred / adjusted at some other place.8. Learned counsel for the petitioner, in view of the 
averments made in supplementary affidavit and the
documents enclosed therein thus contends that the 
authority had not permitted the petitioner to join at 
the transferred place. 
9. Learned State Counsel submits that the said 
averments have been made in the supplementary 
affidavit, filed by the petitioner on 14.10.2026 and, 
as such, an opportunity may be given to rebut the 
said contentions.
10. In the interest of justice, two weeks time is 
granted to the learned State Counsel to file a reply to 
the supplementary affidavit, filed by the petitioner. 
The learned State Counsel shall also specify whether 
any enquiry, as directed by this Court vide order 
14.05.2007, was conducted to ascertain whether it 
was the petitioner who did not join at the new place 
of posting or he was not permitted to join by the 
authorities concerned.
11. List this case on 20.11.2026, after fresh cases.
12. Personal appearance of Mr. Harendra Kumar 
Mishra, Chief Education Officer, Udham Singh Nagar 
is exempted.
 (Subhash Upadhyay, J.)
