Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
Mr. T.A. Khan, Senior Advocate, assisted by 
Mr. Vinay Bhatt, Advocate, for the petitioner. 
Mrs. Anjali Bhargava, Addl. C.S.C. for the 
State of Uttarakhand. 
The petitioner has filed Restoration 
Application No. 11159 of 2021, seeking recall of 
the order dated 25th October, 2021, wherein, the 
Writ Petition was dismissed for want of 
prosecution.
The reason for absence, which has been 
explained in the Restoration Application is that at 
the relevant time the petitioner’s counsel was on his 
legs before another Court, hence he could not make 
himself available when the matter was called in the 
revised call. 
Having considered the reason given, and 
being satisfied with the reasons for absence on the 
date fixed, the Restoration Application preferred by 
the petitioner, would stand allowed and the order 
dated 25th October, 2021, would stand recalled and 
the Writ Petition is restored to its original number. 
List in the second week of January 2022
