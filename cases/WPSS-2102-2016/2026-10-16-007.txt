Hon’ble Subhash Upadhyay, J.
1. Mr. T.A. Khan, learned Senior Counsel 
assisted by Mr. Ravi Shankar Kandpal, Advocate 
holding brief of Mr. Vinay Bhatt, learned counsel 
for the petitioner.
2. Mr. S.S. Chaudhary and Mr. Gajendra 
Tripathi, learned Standing Counsel for the State.
3. A supplementary counter affidavit was filed 
by Chief Education Officer, Udham Singh Nagar on 
02.12.2021. In para-3 of the said affidavit, it was 
stated that Additional Director of Education, 
Kumaun Mandal, Nainital, vide its letter dated 
22.08.1996 adjusted/posted the petitioner in the 
adjacent school at Government Inter College 
Sitarganj, U.S. Nagar vide its order dated 
27.08.1996 and the information was also sent in 
his home address which was available in the 
records. It has further been stated that the 
petitioner neither worked from the month of 
August, 1996 to 19.12.2003 in the education 
department nor submitted his joining in any office 
of the department and therefore the petitioner is 
not entitled for salary and other allowances for the aforesaid period. 
4. A Coordinate Bench on 03.11.2023 
considered the supplementary counter affidavit 
and found that the mode of giving such intimation
has not been indicated in the affidavit and no 
evidence has been brought on record to show that 
petitioner was intimated about the factum of 
modification of transfer order. Accordingly, the 
Coordinate Bench directed the State to get 
necessary instructions in the matter and to 
produce evidence, if any, regarding such 
intimation by the next date fixed. However, the 
instructions or the evidence, if any, as required by 
the Court vide its order dated 03.11.2023 has not 
been filed till date. 
5. Learned State Counsel submits that the said 
affidavit shall be filed positively by the next date. 
6. Let such an affidavit be filed by the State on 
or before 29.10.2026, failing which, Chief 
Education Officer, Udham Singh Nagar, shall 
appear in person on 30.10.2026.
7. List on 30.10.2026 along with connected 
matter.
