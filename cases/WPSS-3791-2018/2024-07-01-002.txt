5. List on 01.08.2024. 
(Delay Condonation Appl. No.17411 of 2024
6. During the pendency of the writ petition, the 
petitioner-Mohan Singh expired on 04.02.2023 
leaving behind his wife, namely, Smt. Nirmala 
Devi and his son Jitendra Singh (deponent). 
Unfortunately, in the intervening period wife of 
deceased government servant also expired on 
21.02.2024, who has already filed substitution, 
which was subsequently not pressed by the 
deponent. 
7. Present Substitution Application No.17410 
of 2024 is moved by the son of deceased-petitioner 
Mohan Singh alongwith Delay Condonation 
Application No.17411 of 2024 as there is delay of 
342 days in filing the substitution application. Vide 
order dated 13.05.2024 respondents were directed 
to file objection to the delay condonation
application as well as substitution application. But 
till date no objection to the same has been filed by the respondent/State.
8. Learned counsel for the State as well as 
learned counsel for Central Government submitted 
at bar that they do not wish to file any objection to 
the delay condonation as well as substitution 
application as the same is a mere formality for 
bringing the legal heirs of deceased petitioner on 
record. Accordingly, delay condonation application 
is allowed. Delay of 342 days in filing the 
substitution application is hereby condoned on the 
ground, indicated in the affidavit filed in support of 
delay condonation application. 
Substitution Application No.17410 of 2024
 
9. This substitution application is moved by the 
(deponent) son of petitioner for bringing on record 
legal heir of the petitioner, namely, Jitendra Singh, 
as petitioner no.1/1; petitioner had expired during 
pendency of the present writ petition. 
10. There is no objection from the opposite party 
on the substitution application. 
11. The substitution application is, accordingly 
allowed for the reason, indicated in the affidavit, 
filed in support of substitution application. 
12. Let necessary amendment be incorporated 
within a period of one week from today by taking
