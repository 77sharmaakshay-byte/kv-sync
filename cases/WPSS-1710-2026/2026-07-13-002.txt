Mr. Sandeep Tiwari, learned Counsel for the 
petitioners.
2. Mr. N.S. Pundir, learned Dy.A.G. for the State.
3. This Court, vide order dated 07.07.2026, had 
made a specific query from the State Counsel 
regarding the averments made in paragraph nos.5,6 
and 7 of the said order, and instructions were sought. 
4. Today, the instructions are shown to this Court 
by learned State Counsel and except replying the 
query raised by this Court, many things have been 
stated. Attention of the Court was also drawn to the 
Government Order dated 01.06.2026, wherein, in 
exercise of powers under Section 27 of theUttarakhand Annual Transfer for Public Servants Act, 
2017 (hereinafter to be referred to as “the Act”), certain 
provisions have been incorporated in the government 
order. It is contended by learned Dy.A.G. appearing 
for the State that in view of aforesaid government 
order, transfers have been effected. 
5. This Court is of the view that the Committee 
contemplated under the provisions of the Act, is 
required to submit its recommendation for the 
approval of Chief Minister regarding the difficulties 
arising in application of this Act, and thereafter, the 
State Government may make rules, as required. The 
said provision has been made under Section 27(2) of 
the Act. 
6. Issuance of a government order, in gross 
violation of provision of Section 27, along with 
aforesaid explanation put forth by learned State 
Counsel, cannot save the State Government to pass
the impugned transfer orders which are in teeth of 
provisions of Transfer Act, 2017, particularly, Section 
6 of the Act. 
7. On the request made by learned State Counsel, 
post these matters on 15.07.2026 as fresh. 
8. Interim orders, granted earlier, shall continue to 
operate till then.
(Pankaj Purohit, J.)
