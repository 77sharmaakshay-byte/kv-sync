Hon’ble Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.
Mr. Rajendra Dobhal, Sr. 
Advocate, assisted by Mr. G.D. Joshi, 
Advocate for the defendant/appellant. 
Mr. V.K. Kohli, Sr. Advocate, 
assisted by Mr. I.P. Kohli, Advocate for 
the respondent no. 1/plaintiff. 
Having heard learned Sr. 
Counsels for the parties, it transpires 
that the defendant/appellant purchased 
a property on 14.3.2002 from the true 
owner vide a sale deed. Respondent no. 
1/plaintiff Smt. Malti Devi was 
admittedly in possession of one room of 
such property. She instituted a suit for 
cancellation of the sale deed. The said 
suit was dismissed. First appeal also 
failed, but a direction was made therein 
that Smt. Malti Devi shall not be evicted 
from the room, which is under her 
occupation. This part of the judgment 
has been challenged by the 
appellant/defendant Lakhi Ram Joshi. 
Now, a miscellaneous application 
(CLMA 1705/2016) has been moved 
seeking ad interim injunction order 
against the respondent no. 1/plaintiff not 
to interfere in possession of the appellant 
over the property except the room in 
which the respondent no. 1/plaintiff was 
found in possession by the lower 
appellate court. This application has been 
opposed by learned Sr. Counsel of the 
plaintiff/respondent no. 1 on the premise 
that under the garb of the orders of first 
appellate court, Smt. Malti Devi is being 
resisted by the appellant/defendant from 
using even the land and the passage 
appurtenant to such room. 
Learned Sr. Counsel prays for 
and is granted two weeks’ time to file objections against the said miscellaneous 
application. Along with the objections, 
the respondent no. 1/plaintiff shall also 
file the photographs, taken from all 
dimensions, of the room under her 
occupation. 
List after two weeks. 
Meanwhile, status quo shall be 
maintained by both the parties. 
Let the certified copy of this order 
be supplied to learned Counsel for 
respondent no. 1 today itself on his 
expenses. 
 
 (Servesh Kumar Gupta, J.)
