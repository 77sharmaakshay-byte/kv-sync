Having heard the learned Senior
Counsels for the parties, it transpires
that at the time of admission of this
second appeal on 23.08.2013, ad interim
injunction was passed to maintain the
status-quo at the spot and parties were
directed not to change the nature of the
property.
Miscellaneous application (CLMA
1705/2016) moved by the defendant￾appellant on 1st March, 2016 seeking
further an injunction directing the
respondent no.1/plaintiff not to interfere
in his peaceful possession over the
property except the room, in which,
respondent no.1 was found in possession
by the Lower Appellate Court and
direction was issued to the appellant not
to evict the respondent no.1 from the said
room except in accordance with law. 
Having received the copy of this
added injunction application, cross￾objections (CLMA 2245/2016) were filed
on 14th March, 2016 under Order 41 Rule
22 CPC by the respondent no.1/plaintiff
with the delay condonation application
(CLMA 2242/2016) seeking to condone
the delay of 879 days. 
Plaintiff-respondent no.1 has filed
several photographs of the spot, which
suggest that she is in occupation of theland, in controversy, by means of a large
tin shed structure, which is more than 
one and all these tin shades would
certainly have the Kitchen, Wash
Room/Toilets, without them, comfortable
living of any family is not possible. The
photographs also suggest that this old
lady Smt. Malti Devi has grown up
daughters/children, who are living in this
tin shed structure. 
So, mentioning of “dwelling room”
in the operative portion of the Appellate
Court’s judgment, prima facie, it appears
not confined to small single room,
therefore, delay, which has caused in
filing the cross-objections, seems to be on
account of filing fresh application (CLMA
1705/2016) by the defendant-appellant,
is condonable, so, I condone the delay of
879 days. Delay condonation application
(CLMA 2242/2016) is accordingly allowed
and cross-objections are taken on record
and the same shall be finally adjudicated
at the time of final hearing of this second
appeal. 
LCR has been received. 
List in the second week of
November, 2016 for final hearing.
