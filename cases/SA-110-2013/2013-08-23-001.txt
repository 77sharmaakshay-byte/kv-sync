Heard.
(2) This second appeal is directed against the judgment
and decree dated 08.07.2013, passed by Vth Additional
District Judge, Dehradun, in Civil Appeal No. 70 of 2011
whereby said court has partly allowed the appeal. 
(3) Learned counsel for the appellant submitted that a
suit for cancellation of sale deed and declaration and
injunction was dismissed by the trial court, but the lower
appellate court without disturbing the findings recorded by
the trial court has passed a perpetual injunction directing
the defendants not to evict her from the room occupied by
her without due process of law. 
(4) Admit the second appeal on following substantial
question of law:-
Whether the lower appellate court has erred in law in
not appreciating that Shri Dome S/o Gayadeen had no
authority to execute a Will on 14.05.1971, in favour of the
plaintiff after Shri Dome had already executed registered
sale deed on 27.11.1968 in favour of Rajendra Singh3
(predecessor in title of defendant) ?
(5) Issue notice to respondents.
(6) Summon the lower court record.
(7) Having heard learned counsel for the appellant, and
after going through the impugned judgment and decree
passed by the lower appellate court, as an interim measure,
it is directed that respondent No.1 shall not change the
nature of the property until further orders of this court.
(Stay Application No. 8767 of 2013 stands disposed of).
(8) List after service of notice on the respondents.
