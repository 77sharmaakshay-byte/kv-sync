Hon’ble R.C. Khulbe, J.
 Mr. Aaqib Ahmed, learned counsel for the 
revisionist. 
 Mr. A.K. Sah, learned DAG for the State. 
 Mr. Rajat Mittal along with Mr. Neeraj Garg, 
learned counsel for the respondent-wife. 
 It is submitted by learned counsel for the 
respondent that in spite of the Court order, the 
revisionist has failed to pay the amount of 
maintenance to the respondent-wife. 
 From a perusal of the order dated 11.12.2019, 
the Co-ordinate Bench of this Court ordered the 
revisionist to pay Rs.15,000/- per month as 
maintenance instead of Rs.21,000/- as awarded by 
the Court below; but till date, no maintenance 
amount was paid by the revisionist to the 
respondent-wife. 
 Accordingly, the order dated 11.12.2019 is 
hereby vacated. 
 List the matter on 11.01.2022.
