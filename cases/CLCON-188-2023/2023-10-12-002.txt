CLCON No. 188 of 2023
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Mr. Kamlesh Kumar Mishra, 
Advocate for the petitioners through video 
conferencing.
The petitioners have brought to the 
notice of the Court the judgment and 
order dated 13.09.2019, passed by this 
Court in WPMS No. 891 of 2016, Nawab 
Khan Vs. Union of India and others (“the 
first petition”) has been wilfully 
disobeyed by the respondents.
On 30.06.2023, when the matter 
was taken up, none appeared on behalf of 
the petitioners. On that date, the Court 
had noted that there may be an issue of 
limitation because the petitioners have 
brought to the notice of the Court an 
order dated 13.09.2023.
Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that, in fact, the petitioners 
were not party in the first petition, in 
which the judgment and order dated 
13.09.2019 has been passed. He would 
submit that the respondents in the first 
petition did prefer an SLP against the 
judgment and order dated 13.09.2019 
before the Hon’ble Supreme Court and it 
is at that stage, the petitioners got 
themselves impleaded. Thereafter, the 
SLP was decided on 25.07.2022. A review 
application was dismissed on 
01.11.2022. Thereafter, the petitioners 
filed a contempt petition before the 
Hon’ble Supreme Court, which was 
withdrawn by them on 05.04.2023 with a 
view to proceed in a contempt before this 
Court.
Does it mean that a limitation, so 
far as the petitioners are concerned may be counted from 25.07.2022? The Court 
keeps this issue open.
Learned counsel for the petitioners 
would submit that respondent no.1 is the 
Chairman and Managing Director. He is 
the person responsible to comply with the 
judgment and order of the Court.
Let issue notice to the respondent 
no.1 at the first instance.
List this matter on 21.12.2023.
 (Ravindra Maithani, J.)
