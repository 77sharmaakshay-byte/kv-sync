30.06.2023 CLCON No. 188 of 2023
Hon’ble Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.
The matter is taken up in the 
revised call. None appears for the parties to 
press the instant contempt petition.
An active issue, because they are 
apprehending an hurdle, which might be 
created in view of the provisions contained 
under Section 20 of Contempt of Courts Act,
as the contempt is being sought of the 
judgment dated 13.09.2019, as it was 
rendered in a bunch of Writ Petitions, with 
leading writ petition, being Writ Petition 
(M/S) No. 891 of 2016, Nawab Khan Vs. 
Union of India and Others, which was 
decided by the coordinate Bench of this 
Court on 13.09.2019.
Since it seems that the petitioners 
are not interested to pursue the matter, the 
contempt petition is directed to be listed in 
its due course.
(Sharad Kumar Sharma, J.)
