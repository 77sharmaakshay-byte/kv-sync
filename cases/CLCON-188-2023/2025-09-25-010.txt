The instant contempt petition was 
listed for hearing on 08.08.2025. On that 
day, it was dismissed in non-prosecution.
Now, a restoration application has 
been filed.
Heard.
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
submits that the case could not be reflected 
in the cause list, due to which it could not 
be marked.
Having heard, this Court is of the view 
that good grounds have been shown for 
non-appearance. Accordingly, the 
restoration application deserves to be 
allowed.
The restoration application is allowed. 
The order dated 08.08.2025 is recalled. The 
contempt petition is restored to its number.
List on 13.11.2025.
