The appellant of the present Second 
Appeal after its admission on 7th November,
2012 and after having ventured to address the 
Appeal on merits have moved the present 
Misc Application No. 16367 of 2019 for 
formulation of additional substantial questions 
of law, apart from which already constituted 
to have been framed in the body of the memo 
of appeal. 
In such an eventuality, while exercising 
my powers under Sub-section (4) of Section 
100 of the CPC and looking to the nature of 
the controversy involved, apart from the 
substantial questions of law, on which, the 
Appeal has already admitted, the additional 
substantial questions of law “B”, “D” and “H” 
are framed as additional substantial questions 
of law, which are quoted hereunder :-
“(B) As to whether the suit for 
cancellation of registered instrument is 
only maintainable in civil court in 
absence of any provision in Schedule II 
of U.P.Z.A. & L.R. Act ?
(D) As to whether both the courts below 
had committed an error of law affecting 
the rights of the plaintiff by dismissing 
the suit for cancellation of sale deed on 
the ground of jurisdiction of civil court 
instead of returning the plaint for 
presentation before Revenue Court ?
(H) As to whether the judgment of First 
Appellate Court is vitiated being against the mandate of Order 41 Rule 
31 CPC as held by Hon’be Supreme 
Court in case of 2011 (2) R.D. 127, 
B.V. Nagesh & another Vs. H.V. 
Sreenivasa Murthy and further held in 
2011 (4) SCC 240, H. Siddiqui (Dead) 
by LRs. Vs. A. Ramalingam ? ”
Misc. Application stands disposed of 
accordingly. 
List this Second Appeal in the week 
commencing 6th January, 2020. 
Interim order to continue till the next 
date of listing.
