Admit.

List this matter after lower court’s 
record is received. 
Learned counsel for the revisionist 
submitted that the revisionist was on bail 
during trial and has never misused his 
bail. 
I have perused the judgments passed 
by the Courts below and have considered 
the submission of learned counsel for the 
revisionist. By considering all that I direct 
that the revisionist shall be released on 
bail on same terms and conditions on 
which he was granted bail by the trial 
Court. 
Bail application stands disposed of.
