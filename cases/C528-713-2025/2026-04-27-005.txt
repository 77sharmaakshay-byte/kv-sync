2. Learned counsel for the applicant submits 
that an earlier charge-sheet was filed on 
18.04.2022, upon which cognizance was taken 
by the learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Dehradun. However, the said charge-sheet was 
subsequently cancelled on 21.03.2023 by the 
S.S.P., Dehradun, and thereafter a fresh 
charge-sheet dated 11.04.2024 was submitted, 
on which cognizance has again been taken.
3. Learned counsel for the applicant has 
raised a preliminary objection, contending that 
once cognizance had already been taken by the 
competent Court on the earlier charge-sheet, 
the S.S.P. had no authority or jurisdiction to 
cancel the same. 
4. Despite repeated directions, learned State 
Counsel has failed to file a counter affidavit till 
date. 
5. As a last opportunity, time is granted to 
the learned State Counsel till 07.05.2026 to file 
a counter affidavit.
