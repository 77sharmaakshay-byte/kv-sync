Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J. 
1. Mr. Lalit Sharma and Ms. Suraiya Naaz, learned 
counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned A.G.A. with Mrs. 
Sweta Badola Dobhal, learned Brief Holder for the 
State.
3. Mr. Anchit Khokher, learned counsel for the 
respondent.
4. The Trial Court submitted its detailed report in 
compliance of the previous order, wherein, it is 
reported that the two accused persons namely 
Ramkumar Tyagi and Rajkumar Tyagi are avoiding 
the Trial Court proceeding, therefore, warrant have 
been issued against them for securing their presence 
and the next date fixed before the Trial Court is 
29.10.2026. The other accused persons are Ramnaresh 
Tyagi, Avneet Tyagi and Rakesh Tyagi are the 
applicants in C528 No. 713 of 2025 and Karan Singh, 
Vijay Kumar and Deepak Kumar, who are the 
applicants of C528 No. 1563 of 2025 are directed to 
ensure their presence before the Trial Court since as 
apprised to this court the charges has yet not been framed though there is no order regarding stay of the 
proceeding. In such a situation when there is no stay 
on the ongoing proceeding avoiding the Trial Court 
proceeding is unwarranted and, in such an 
eventuality, all the accused persons should ensure 
their presence before the Trial Court. The Trial Court 
also submitted earlier report and after perusing the 
same it reveals that the accused persons are 
deliberately avoiding the Trial Court proceeding. 
5. It has also been apprised that one of the accused 
person Ramkumar Tyagi is at present 90 years old
then, in such an eventuality, he has a liberty to move
an exemption application to appear through the 
counsel at the time of framing of charge by ensuring 
his presence through V.C. but in any manner the Trial 
Court proceeding cannot avoided.
6. C528 No. 1010 of 2026 is moved only for 
expediting the Trial Court proceeding since the Trial 
Court taking all efforts to expedite the trial, therefore, 
there is no useful purpose to keep this matter pending. 
Accordingly, the proceeding of C528 No. 1010 of 2026
is closed at this stage.
7. Since, the C528 No. 1010 of 2026 is closed, 
therefore, the Registry is directed to tag both the 
reports of Trial Court dated 10.09.2026 and 30.09.2026 
with C528 No. 713 of 2025.
8. In reference to the two chargesheets wherein the 
Trial Court took cognizance Mr. Tumul Nainwal, 
learned A.G.A., on instructions submits that in the first 
chargesheet there was no cognizance since without 
placing the chargesheet before the Trial Court the 
S.S.P. concerned ordered for further investigation and 
due to some inadvertence there is a seal of the 
concerned court in the first chargesheet, however, for 
clarifying this position the prosecution will move an 
appropriate application before the Trial Court within 
ten days so that there may not be doubt on this. He
also submits that the Trial Court proceeding is going 
on only in the subsequent chargesheet dated 
11.04.2023.
9. In C528 No. 713 of 2025 pleadings are 
exchanged. In C528 NO. 1563 of 2025 the counter 
affidavit has not been filed. Respondents are directed 
to file their counter affidavit within three weeks.
10. One week thereafter is granted to the applicant 
to file rejoinder affidavit.
11. List this matter in the week commencing 07.12.2026.
12. It is made clear that since the Trial Court 
proceedings are not stayed the Trial Court may 
proceed with the matter since the trial court 
proceeding are pending since 2024 but charges has not 
been framed as yet. All the accused persons are 
directed to ensure their presence before the trial court 
on the next date fixed.
