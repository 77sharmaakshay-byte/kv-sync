5. In C528 No. 713 of 2025 the two chargesheets are 
enclosed; one at page 145 and other one at page 177. The 
charge-sheet, which is enclosed at page 145 reflects that 
there is an endorsement of learned Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Dehradun, for taking cognizance on this charge-sheet for the offences punishable under sections 
420, 406 and 120 B of IPC and the last page of this 
charge-sheet reveals that it was cancelled on 21.03.2023.
Page 170 also reveals that the charge-sheet was 
forwarded by the S.H.O. at P.S. Prem Nagar on 
18.04.2022.
6. The another charge-sheet is at page 177 and it was 
forwarded on 11.04.2023, wherein, learned Judicial 
Magistrate, Dehradun, took cognizance for the offences 
punishable under sections 406, 420, 467, 468, 471 and 
120B of IPC.
7. Now, in this petition the proceeding is being 
challenged that from these two charge-sheets it reveals 
that the subsequent charge-sheet is the outcome of re￾investigation, though as per the Rules the re￾investigation is not permissible and the second charge￾sheet does not reveal that it is a supplementary charge￾sheet.
8. It is submitted that once the charge-sheet is filed 
there is no any question to file another charge-sheet 
particularly when in the first charge-sheet the learned 
Chief Judicial Magistrate already took cognizance. 
9. Apart from this, in C528 No. 1010 of 2026 the Trial 
Court submitted a detail report that the persons, who are 
chargesheeted are not joining the Trial Court proceeding 
and though the charge-sheet was filed in the year 2024 
but uptill date the charges has not been framed. In 
connected C528 petition the Trial Court proceeding is 
not stayed, therefore, in such an eventuality avoiding the 
Trial Court proceeding by the accused persons on one 
pretext to another is wholly unwarranted. It has been 
apprised that before the Trial Court 30.09.2026 is fixed, 
therefore, the applicants of the connected C528 petition 
shall ensure their presence on the date fixed before the 
Trial Court so that the Trial Court may proceed.
10. The Registry may get the report from the 
concerned Trial Court about these two charge-sheets, 
which are enclosed at page 145 and 177.
11. In addition to this, Mr. Tumul Nainwal, learned 
A.G.A. may also get the instructions from the concerned 
Investigating Officer about these two charge-sheets.
12. Put up this matter on 30.09.2026.
