Hon’ble Pankaj Purohit, J.
Mr. Gaurav Kandpal, learned counsel for the 
contempt petitioner.
2. This petition has been filed alleging that the 
respondent-contemnor has committed willful and 
deliberate non-compliance of the order passed by this 
Court on 08.08.2025 in WPSS No.218 of 2025, Neha 
Chauhan vs. State of Uttarakhand & others, which was 
disposed of on the statement made by the learned 
counsel appearing for the respondent-Commission, Mr. 
Shailendra Nauriyal, that the name of the petitioner 
could not be recommended due to a technical error 
identified by the Commission and the name of the 
petitioner would be recommended by the Commission 
shortly.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioner submits that 
though the order was passed in the presence of the 
counsel for the respondent-Commission and despite 
supply of the certified copy of the order to the 
respondent-contemnor on 08.12.2025, petitioner’s name 
has still not been recommended.
4. I have perused the order and it is evidently clear 
that the petition was disposed of on the statement made 
by the learned counsel appearing for the Commission. 
The respondent-contemnor has not recommended the 
name of the petitioner for the last ten months, prima 
facie, a case of contempt is made out.2
5. Issue notice to the respondent, returnable within 
two weeks.
6. Steps shall be taken within 24 hours.
7. List on 01.07.2026.
8. Respondent-contemnor shall file his response to the 
contempt petition or to comply with the order by 
recommending the name of the petitioner to the 
Government for appointment to the post of Assistant 
Teacher, L.T. Grade (Home Science).
