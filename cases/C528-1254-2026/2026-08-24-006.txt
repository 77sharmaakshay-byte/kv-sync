Hon’ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
Mr. Mukul Talwar, learned senior counsel 
through VC assisted by Mr. Dushyant Mainali, learned 
counsel for the applicant.
2. Mr. Tumul K Nainwal, learned AGA for the 
State of Uttarakhand.
3. Mr. Anmol Sandhu, Mr. Vikas Kumar Guglani, 
learned counsel for the complainant.
4. This matter was heard at length on the previous 
date and on the request of learned State Counsel posts
this matter for today i.e. for 24.08.2026 for considering 
the pending bail application of the present applicant –
Dhananjay Giri.
5. Mr. Nainwal, learned AGA submits that the 
present applicant is indulged in criminal activities 
since long back and cheated larger number of people 
and sell out those properties, which are mortgaged 
with the Bank. 
6. On the other side, Mr. Talwar, learned senior 
counsel submits that there is no any such material that 
the present applicant is running a syndicate and even 
the prosecution has not disclosed the name of any 
person in association with whom, the present 
applicant is running a syndicate. He also submits that 
the applicant was taken on remand under Section 111 
of BNS, 2023, only on the instance of some of the 
individuals, who lodged the FIR.
7. Mr. Talwar, learned senior counsel submits that 
the applicant has been detained in jail without any 
substance and material there are certain pending 
proceedings pending in different Forums, therefore, the applicant be enlarged on bail.
8. On this, learned AGA submits that instead of 
granting regular bail, the applicant may be released on 
short term bail with some stringent conditions since 
the issue of remand is sub judice in C528 Application 
No.1254 of 2026.
9. Let the respondents may file their counter 
affidavit in C528 No.1254 of 2026 within two weeks.
10. Put up these matters on 21.09.2026.
11. In the meantime, the present applicant –
Dhananjay Giri, is admitted to short term bail only for 
the period of three weeks, which is to be computed 
from the date of his actual release, on his executing a 
personal bond and furnishing two reliable sureties, 
each of the like amount to the satisfaction of the Court 
concerned in connection with the FIR dated 24.07.2025, 
bearing FIR No.94 of 2025, Police Station Kathgodam, 
District Nainital subject to the following conditions:-
“(A) The applicant after being released on 
short term bail is permitted to pursue the 
pending proceedings pending in different 
Forums.
(B) The applicant after being released on 
short term bail for the aforesaid period 
shall not leave the territorial jurisdiction 
of the trial court without prior permission 
of this Court.
(C) The applicant during the period of 
short term bail shall be in strict 
surveillance of investigating agency.
