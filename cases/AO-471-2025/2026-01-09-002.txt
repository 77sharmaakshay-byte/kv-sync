COURT’S OR JUDGE’S ORDERS
A.O. No.471 of 2025
Hon’ble Alok Mahra, J.
Mr. Siddhartha Singh, learned counsel 
for the appellant.
2. Mr. Pawan Mishra, learned counsel for 
respondents.
3. Learned counsel for the appellant would 
submit that respondent no. 9, Mr. Ravindra 
Singh Rawat, has executed a sale deed in 
favour of one Smt. Renu Thakur in respect of 
land measuring 502 square meters. It is 
further submitted that, since the total land 
falling to the share of respondent no. 9 was 
only 763.33 square meters, he was legally 
entitled to alienate only 261.51 square meters 
in excess thereof. However, respondent no. 9 
sold land measuring 348.5 square meters to 
the appellant, which is in excess by 88 square 
meters. On this premise, the learned trial court 
held that respondent no. 9 could not have sold 
land beyond his permissible share and, 
accordingly, refused to grant injunction in 
favour of the appellant. 
4. It is, however, noted that the sale deed 
alleged to have been executed by respondent 
no. 9 in favour of Smt. Renu Thakur has not 
been placed on record.
5. Learned counsel for the respondent, 
therefore, seeks time to file a copy of the said 
sale deed, which is relevant to the present 
case, along with his objections to the appeal.
6. List the matter on 10.02.2026.
7. In the meantime, learned counsel for the 
respondent shall file on record a copy of the 
sale deed executed by respondent no. 9 in 
favour of Smt. Renu Thakur, pertaining to the 
property in question.
8. Till the next date of listing, i.e., 
10.02.2026, both parties are directed not to 
change the nature and possession of the 
property in question.
