WPSS No. 604 of 2019 
Hon’ble Manoj K. Tiwari, J.
Mr. Kurban Ali, Advocate for the 
petitioner. 
Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Advocate for 
respondents. 
Heard learned counsel for the parties. 
According to the petitioner, he was 
appointed as Junior Engineer on contract in 
Cantonment Board, Dehradun in February 
2015. He is aggrieved by the advertisement 
dated 01.02.2019 whereby fresh applications 
have been invited for engagement of Junior 
Engineer on contract. 
According to learned counsel for the 
petitioner, one adhoc/temporary/ contract 
employee cannot be replaced by another. 
Learned counsel for the respondents 
prays for and is granted four week’s time for 
filing counter affidavit. One week’s thereafter 
is granted to the petitioner to file rejoinder 
affidavit. 
List this matter on 06.05.2019. 
Till the next date of listing effect and 
operation of advertisement dated 01.02.2019 
shall remain stayed. 
Interim Relief Application (CLMA No. 
3617 of 2019) stands disposed of.
