6. Since all these petitions raise 
identical issues concerning the 
dispute relating to admission to the 
B.A.M.S. course for academic sessions 
2018-2019, 2019-2020, 2020-2021, they are being heard together.
7. Learned counsel for the 
petitioners would submit that the 
present writ petitions have been filed 
seeking a direction to respondent no. 
2 to enroll the students admitted by 
the petitioners’ college and to permit 
them to appear in their practical as 
well as semester examinations in the 
B.A.M.S. course.
8. Learned counsel would further 
submit that admission to the B.A.M.S. 
course was made subject to 
qualification in NEET pursuant to the 
Central Government 
Notification/Regulation dated 
07.12.2018, whereby NEET was 
prescribed as a mandatory eligibility 
criterion for admission to the said 
course. It is submitted that NEET was 
introduced for the first time in the 
academic session 2018–2019, which 
resulted in the filing of several writ 
petitions across the country 
challenging the requirement of NEET 
as an essential qualification for 
admission to the B.A.M.S. course; 
that, the issue ultimately reached the 
Hon’ble Supreme Court, which, in
Association of Managements of 
Homeopathic Medical Colleges of 
Maharashtra v. Union of India & Ors.
reported in (2019) 20 SCC 511, held 
that for the academic session 2018–
2019, qualification in NEET was not 
an essential requirement for 
admission to the B.A.M.S. course. 
Thereafter, in subsequent civil 
appeals, the Hon’ble Apex Court 
relaxed the condition of mandatory NEET qualification and permitted 
students who were otherwise eligible 
to continue their studies for the 
academic session 2019–2020 and 
2020-2021 . It is submitted that most 
of such students have already 
completed their course, while a few 
are still pursuing the same.
9. Learned counsel submits that the 
students concerned in the present writ 
petitions were earlier permitted to 
appear in the examinations pursuant 
to interim orders passed by this 
Hon’ble Court. It is further submitted 
that the next practical and semester 
examinations are scheduled to be held 
on 27.01.2026. Therefore, it is prayed 
that the petitioners be permitted to 
appear in the examination scheduled 
on 27.01.2026, subject to the final 
outcome of the present writ petitions.
10. In view of the foregoing facts and 
purely in the interest of justice, the 
students who were admitted in the 
Academic Sessions 2018-2019, 2019–
2020 and 2020-2021 shall be 
provisionally permitted to appear in 
the examination scheduled to be held 
on 27.01.2026.
11. List the case in due course.
