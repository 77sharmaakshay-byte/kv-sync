2. By means of the present Writ Petition, the 
petitioners seek quashing of the F.I.R. dated 
19.08.2026, being F.I.R. No. 0018 of 2026, 
registered under Sections 142, 85 and 86 of the 
Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 and Sections 
3/4 of the Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961, at 
Police Station Lamgoan, District Tehri Garhwal.
3. Learned counsel for the petitioners 
submits that the marriage of petitioner no. 2 
with the daughter of respondent no. 3 was 
solemnized in the year 2023 and that there was 
no marital discord between petitioner no. 2 and 
his wife. It is submitted that petitioner no. 2 is 
working in Dubai and had returned to India on 
01.08.2026. Thereafter, his wife went missing 
in the afternoon of 06.08.2026. Despite efforts 
made by petitioner no. 2 along with other 
villagers to trace her, she could not be found. 
Consequently, petitioner no. 2 informed the 
police station regarding the missing of his wife. 
Subsequently, the impugned F.I.R. came to be 
lodged.
4. It is further contended that, till date, 
petitioner no. 2's wife has not been traced and 
that, prior to registration of the impugned 
F.I.R., no complaint had been lodged by the 
informant or any member of the family, 
including the wife of petitioner no. 2. It is also 
submitted that almost a month has elapsed 
since the wife of petitioner no. 2 went missing and her whereabouts are still not known.
5. This Court finds it appropriate to direct 
respondent no. 1 to apprise the Court, on the 
next date of listing, with regard to the 
investigation conducted by the local police for 
tracing the wife of petitioner no. 2. The Senior 
Superintendent of Police, Tehri Garhwal, shall 
also submit a report, within a week, regarding 
the investigation conducted by the local police 
pursuant to the missing report lodged by 
petitioner no. 2.
6. Upon a bare perusal of the allegations 
contained in the impugned F.I.R. and the 
material placed on record, this Court is of the 
view that the matter requires deliberation.
7. Issue notice to respondent no. 3. Steps be 
taken within a week.
8. Counter affidavit, if any, be filed by the 
respondents.
9. List this case on 21.09.2026.
10. Having heard learned counsel for the 
parties and considering the facts and 
circumstances of the case, as an interim 
measure, it is directed that, till the next date of 
listing, the petitioners shall not be arrested in 
connection with the aforesaid F.I.R., provided 
they cooperate with the investigation.
11. The Stay Application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
