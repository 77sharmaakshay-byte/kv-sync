02.09.2024 CRLR No. 603 of 2024
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The proposed Revision has been filed 
challenging the judgment dated 
18.10.2023, passed by learned Judge, 
Family Court, Dehradun in Criminal Case 
No. 331 of 2020, by which, the learned 
Judge has directed the proposed revisionist 
to pay Rs. 10,000/-(Rupees ten thousand)
per month for the maintenance of his wife, 
the respondent, under Section 125 of the 
Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973.
2. Heard Mr. Vishwast Kandpal, learned 
counsel for the proposed revisionist. 
3. The proposed Revision has been filed 
along with an Application under Section 5 of 
the Limitation Act, 1963 to condone the 
delay of 225 days.
4. The proposed revisionist is directed to 
file a copy of the petition, filed by him for 
Haq-e-zaujiyat.
5. List on 15.10.2024.
6. It is clarified that this Court has not 
passed any interim order/stay order in the 
present matter.
