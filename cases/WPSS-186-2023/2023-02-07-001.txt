COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
07.02.2023 WPSS No.186 of 2023
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J.
The present Writ Petition has been 
filed under Article 226 of the Constitution of 
India.
Mr. Devendra Singh Bohra, learned 
counsel for the petitioner and Mr. V.S.
Rawat, learned Brief Holder for the State.
Mr. Ashish Aggarwal, Advocate holding 
brief of Mr. Ramji Shrivastava, learned 
counsel for the respondent no.3 through 
video conferencing.
Admittedly, in a similar matter (WPSB 
No. 169 of 2021), this Court has directed 
the respondents on 16.04.2021 to entertain 
and consider the applications of the 
petitioner nos. 1 to 5, 8 and 9 of the said 
writ petition and to permit them to 
participate in the selection process for the 
post of Staff Nurse, provisionally.
The State was also directed to 
consider and to grant the age relaxation to 
the petitioner nos. 6 and 7 of the said writ 
petition in the light of the Uttarakhand 
Public Service (Relaxation of Age Limit for 
the Recruitment) Rules, 2003. However, the 
result of the petitioner shall be subject to 
the outcome of the writ petition.
In the present similar matter, subject 
to the directions and conditions, imposed by 
the Court on 16.04.2021, the respondents 
are directed to permit the petitioner 
namely, Mukesh Kumar Tyagi to participate 
in the selection process for the post of Staff 
Nurse, provisionally. It is clarified again that 
the result of the petitioner shall be subject to the outcome of this writ petition.
Learned counsel for the State 
requested four weeks’ time to file counter 
affidavit.
Connect the present Writ Petition with 
the Writ Petition (S/S) No. 149 of 2023.
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
