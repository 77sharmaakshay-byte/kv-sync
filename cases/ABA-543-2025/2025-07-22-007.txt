This Application has been filed by the applicant 
seeking anticipatory bail in Case Crime No.102 of 2025, 
registered at Police Station Kaliyar Sharif, District Haridwar 
under Sections 120B, 420, 467, 468, 471, 504 and Section 
506 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860. 
2. As per the First Information Report dated 
23.03.2025, three documents of agreement to sell dated 
23.12.2022 were executed between the applicant, co￾accused Yugendra Kumar Singh, co-accused Smt. Brajrani 
and the informant. The informant paid them 
Rs.2,50,00,000/-(Rupees Two Crore Fifty Lakh) in advance. 
The sale-deed to be executed by 23.12.2026. The informant 2
later came to know that action had been taken by the 
Enforcement Directorate against the property-in-question. 
3. Heard Mr. Tapan Singh, learned counsel for 
applicant, Mr. Pradeep Lohani, learned Brief Holder for 
respondent and Mr. Shashi Kant Shandilya, learned counsel 
for informant. 
4. Mr. Tapan Singh, Advocate, submitted that a sum 
of Rs.20,00,000/-(Rupees Twenty Lakh) has already been 
returned by the present applicant and the co-accused 
Yugendra Kumar Singh to the informant. The applicant 
undertakes that he shall return the rest of the amount of his 
share to the informant and his associates on or before 
31.12.2025. 
5. Mr. Shashi Kant Shandilya, Advocate, submitted 
that the informant is ready to give time till 31.12.2025 to 
the applicant to return the principal amount along with 
proper bank interest. 
6. The parties have requested time for arguments on 
merit. 
7. Having heard the submissions of learned counsel 
for the parties and in the facts and circumstances of the 
case, as an ad-interim measure, it is directed that in the 
event of arrest of the applicant Kamlendra Kumar Singh, he 
shall be released on interim bail on executing a personal 
bond of Rs.30,000/- and two reliable sureties, each of the 3
like amount, to the satisfaction of the Investigating Officer/ 
Arresting Officer, subject to the following conditions:-
(i) Applicant shall cooperate with the Investigating 
Agency and he shall make himself available for 
interrogation by a police officer as and when 
required; 
(ii) Applicant shall not directly or indirectly make any 
inducement, threat or promise to any person 
acquainted with the facts of this case.
(iii) Applicant shall not leave the country without the 
previous permission of this Court. 
8. List on 26.08.2025 for hearing on the Anticipatory 
Bail Application.
