COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
WPMS No.2156 of 2023
Hon’ble Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.
Present Mr. Mohd. Matlub, counsel for 
the petitioner. 
2. Present Mr. Ashish Joshi, counsel for 
the respondents. 
3. The present writ petition is preferred 
against the impugned Award dated 
15.04.2023 passed by Labour Court, 
Dehradun in Award Case No.01 of 2017, 
whereby the Labour Court has upheld the 
penalty imposed by the Labour 
Commissioner on workman Ram Mehar 
Singh. 
4. Counsel for the petitioner would 
submit that in para 18 of the impugned 
judgment it has been observed by the Labour 
Court that the domestic inquiry conducted 
against the workman is against the principle 
of natural justice, therefore, no penalty could 
have been imposed when the domestic
inquiry itself is vitiated for want of the 
interest of the natural justice, hence this writ 
petition. 
5. Counsel for the respondents would 
submit that the reference was made after 15 
to 16 years and there was no appeal by the 
workman against the domestic inquiry, 
therefore, the order of the Labour 
Commissioner could not be questioned at 
belated stage. 
6. In considered view of the Court, the matter needs deliberation. 
7. Admit. 
8. LCR be summoned. 
9. Put up on 12.04.2024.
(Vivek Bharti Sharma, J.)
