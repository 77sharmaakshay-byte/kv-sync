Hon’ble Mr. Justice Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. The petitioner has approached this court under Article 226 
of the Constitution of India challenging the impugned order, 
whereby, her appointment as an Assistant Teacher in Primary 
School under Scheduled Caste (SC) category was cancelled.
2. Briefly stated, the petitioner originally belongs to District 
Mujaffarnagar of State of U.P. and belongs to Koeri Sub-Caste, 
which is notified as Scheduled caste in the State of U.P. married 
to a person, who is the resident of State of Uttarakhand, who 
also belongs to Koeri Sub Caste, which is notified as Scheduled 
Caste also in the State of Uttarakhand.
The petitioner participate for appointment to the Primary 
School pursuant to an advertisement, got selected and then 
1UKHC010114452026
appointed and also joined, however, by the order impugned her 
appointment has been cancelled on the ground that she is not 
entitled to get the benefit of reservation in public employment 
in the State of Uttarakhand since she belongs to the State of U.P.
3. Mr. Patni, learned senior counsel, submits that since the 
Koeri Sub Caste notified as Scheduled Caste in both the States 
i.e. State of U.P. as well as in the State of Uttarakhand, therefore, 
she cannot be deprived from the benefit of reservation in the 
State of Uttarakhand simply for the reason that she belongs to 
the State of U.P. though infact now after marriage she is the 
resident of State of Uttarakhand.
In support of his submission, he placed reliance to a 
judgment of Division Bench dated 13.10.2014 passed in Special 
Appeal No. 281 of 2013 ‘State of Uttarakhand & Others vs. 
Neetu Singh’, wherein, the Division Bench dealt with the similar 
issue in para-6 onwards to para-8, which read as under:
“6. The learned counsel for the appellant Mr. P.C. Bisht admits 
that such a Government Order (G.O dated 02.04.2013) does 
exist and wherein such a benefit can be given to the petitioner. 
However, he nevertheless strongly contends that merely 
because the petitioner is to be granted the status of a 
“permanent resident”, it would not mean that she would also 
be given the benefit of the reservation on this aspect again in 
the State of Uttarakhand as well. We do not agree with this 
submission for the following factors which are in favour of the 
petitioner. 
(a) She was born in an OBC family and is of a caste 
called “Koeri”.
(b) Koeri is a notified as OBC in U.P. as well as in 
Uttarakhand. 4 
(c) When the above caste was notified as OBC it was for 
the whole of U.P. i.e. the undivided U.P. as it existed prior to 
the reorganization. 
(d) She has married to an OBC belonging to Koeri caste. 
2UKHC010114452026
(e) Government Order dated 02.04.2013 grants her 
status of permanent residence. 
7. Further we are not inclined to interfere in the present matter 
for another reason which is that the O.B.C. caste certificate has 
already been issued in favour of the petitioner (present 
respondent) by the appropriate authority in Uttarakhand. It is 
a valid caste certificate, which has not been cancelled as yet. 
Moreover, since the petitioner has already been given the 
status of “permanent resident” in the State of Uttarakhand, 
which is not denied by the appellant/State, we fail to 
understand as to why the benefit of reservation will not be 
given to her. 
8. In view of the admitted fact that the petitioner was born in 
an O.B.C. community in the erstwhile State of Uttar Pradesh, 
when the State of Uttar Pradesh was the undivided State and 
the declaration that “Koeri” is an O.B.C. caste was for the 
entire State and not just for the present State of Uttar Pradesh 
only, at the given time in the year 1994 U.P. Public Service 
(S.C. S.T., O.B.C.) was in existence. Moreover, “Koeri” is also 
declared as an O.B.C. community in the State of Uttarakhand 
as well. Moreover, this is again an admitted fact that petitioner 
married to a person who also belongs to “Koeri” community, 
who is a permanent resident of Uttarakhand. On the basis of 
these admitted facts, as above, we are of the considered view 
that in this given situation, the denial of caste 5 certificate to 
the petitioner and the denial of reservation benefit to the 
petitioner would be unjustified.”
4. On the other side, Mr. R.C. Joshi, learned Brief Holder for 
the State, placed reliance to another judgment of the Coordinate 
Bench dated 12.11.2025 passed in WPSS No. 462 of 2025 and 
submits that the similar issue was came up before the 
Coordinate Bench and the Coordinate Bench dismissed the 
petition in the light of the judgment of Division Bench dated 
29.08.20211 passed in WPSB No. 297 of 2007, which was later on 
affirmed by the Hon’ble Apex Court. Learned State counsel 
placed reliance to para-14 of the aforesaid judgment, which 
read as under:
“14. From perusal of the judgments referred to above, it is 
apparent that benefit of reservation can be availed of by a 
person only in the State to which he/she originally belonged 
and upon migration to some other State, for whatever reason, 
3UKHC010114452026
one would not be entitled to reservation in State Services of 
State to which he/she migrates. This view is supported by the 
judgment dated 29.08.2011 rendered by Division Bench of this 
court in WPSB No. 297 of 2007, which was affirmed by Hon’ble 
Supreme Court in Civil Appeal No. 8425 of 2013, vide 
judgment dated 02.11.2018.”
5. On perusal of both the judgments, what this court has 
observed that the issue as raised in both the judgments are 
slightly different. The judgment as relied upon by the State 
counsel relates to an issue of “migration”. Now the question is 
“whether the relocation of a petitioner from the State of U.P. to 
the State of Uttarakhand, by virtue of her marriage to a resident 
of Uttarakhand falls under the category of “migration”?
6. Since, the State counsel have not gone through with the 
judgment as relied upon by Mr. Patni, let he may go through 
with the same and advance his argument on the next date.
7. Put up this matter on 02.11.2026 along with the other 
connected matters.
