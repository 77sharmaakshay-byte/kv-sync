Arguments heard, but not concluded.
A report is submitted by the District 
Magistrate, Haridwar pursuant to order 
dated 26.07.2024 of this Court, but it has 
been heavily disputed by the petitioners.
On 16.03.2022, this Court had passed 
an order that no mining activity shall take 
place in the area in question. The Court has once issued a 
Commission also, the report of which 
reveals that stone crushers are working in 
defiance to the Court’s order and mining 
activities are being done by automatic 
machines. 
Now, it appears that the reports, 
which are being submitted by the State 
Authorities are hiding more than revealing. 
The Court requested the learned counsel for 
the Central Pollution Control Board and he
agreed that the representative of the 
Central Pollution Control Board would visit 
the area and submit a report with regard to 
mining activities that are being undertaken 
in the area in question, particularly the 
mechanized or automatic machines are 
being used or not, and also the location of 
the stone crushers and their working. 
The representatives of Central 
Pollution Control Board may be assisted by 
the petitioner, the representative of the 
Regional Office of the Uttarakhand State 
Pollution Control Board as well as by the 
District Magistrate, Haridwar.
The report by the Central Pollution 
Control be filed on or before the next date of 
listing i.e. 21.02.2025.
This Court is of the opinion that 
somewhere an officer of the administration 
should be held accountable.
Today, the petitioner in WPPIL No. 15 
of 2022 has placed for perusal of the Court 
some coloured photographs showing 
indiscriminate mining by mechanized or 
automatic machines, trucks. 
On 16.03.2022, this Court had 
stopped mining in the area in question. The 
Court directs the Senior Superintendent of 
Police, Haridwar to ensure the compliance 
of the Court’s order. Any defiance shall be 
considered as a disobedience by the Senior Superintendent of Police, Haridwar. He may 
use such force as is necessary to ensure 
compliance of the Court’s order. 
List this matter on 21.02.2025 at 
02:00 p.m.
On that date, the District Magistrate, 
Haridwar and the Senior Superintendent of 
Police, Haridwar are requested to join the 
proceedings through video conferencing.
