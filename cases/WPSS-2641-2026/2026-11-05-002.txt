1. Mr. Sandeep Kothari, learned counsel for the
petitioner.
2. Mr. S.S. Chaudhary, learned Standing Counsel
for the State.
3. In compliance of the previous order, Engineer in
Chief joined the proceeding through V.C. He apprised
to this Court that proceeding has been completed after
following the Government Order.
4. It is very surprising that instead of following the
statutory procedure as laid down under Section 47 of
the Persons with Disabilities (Equal Opportunities,
Protection of Rights and Full Participation) Act, 1995.
5. The official - respondent proceeded under the
Government Order, which is wholly unwarranted.
Statutory procedure cannot be bypassed by the
authority, therefore, the order impugned is apparently
in contravention of Section 47 of the Persons with
Disabilities (Equal Opportunities, Protection of Rights
and Full Participation) Act, 1995.
6. In such view of the matter, let the Engineer-in￾Chief, who is present through V.C. may re-examine the
entire issue and take a fresh decision, in terms of
Section 47 of the Persons with Disabilities (Equal
Opportunities, Protection of Rights and Full
Participation) Act, 1995 and apprise to this Court on
the next date. 
7. List on 30.11.2026.
