COURT’S OR JUDGES’S ORDERS
05.01.2023 AO No. 468 of 2022 
Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma, J. 
 The present Appeal has been filed 
under Section 173 of the Motor Vehicles 
Act, 1988, against the Award dated 
28.09.2022, passed by learned Motor 
Accident Claims Tribunal/ IInd Additional 
District Judge, Nainital in Claim Petition 
No. 171 of 2017, “Smt. Gopa Dutta 
Chatterjee and Another vs. Shri Rakesh
Kumar Sahu and Others”, by which, the 
learned Tribunal has awarded a total sum 
of Rs. 33,16,432/- (Rupees Thirty Three 
Lakhs Sixteen Thousand Four Hundred 
Thirty Two) along with an interest @ 7.5 % 
per annum from the date of the petition till 
its actual payment. Learned Tribunal 
further directed that the said amount 
would be payable jointly and severally by 
the defendant no.4 (present appellant) and 
the defendant no. 5 (National Insurance 
Company Ltd.). 
Heard Mr. V.K.Kohli, learned Senior 
Advocate, assisted by Mr. I.P.Kohli, learned 
Advocate for the appellant. 
Learned Senior Advocate submitted 
that the present appeal has been filed on 
the ground, inter alia, that the Income Tax
has not been deducted. 
Admit. 
Issue notice to the respondent no.1, 
and respondent nos. 3 to 7. Steps be taken 
within a week. 
List on 25th April, 2023. 
Heard on the Stay Application. 
In the facts and circumstances of the 
case, on depositing a sum of Rs. 
10,00,000/- (Rupees Ten Lakhs) within four weeks from today before the Tribunal 
concerned, the effect and operation of the 
impugned Award dated 28.09.2022 are 
stayed. 
Stay Application (IA No. 1 of 2022) is 
disposed of accordingly.
 (Alok Kumar Verma, J.)
