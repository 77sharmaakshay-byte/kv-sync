(1) Ms. Pushpa Joshi, learned Senior Counsel

for the appellant.
(2) Mr. J.S. Virk, learned Deputy Advocate 
General for the State of Uttarakhand.
(3) Mr. D.K. Sharma, learned counsel for the 
complainant.
(4) Appellant has moved short-term bail 
application on the ground that he has to 
perform SHARAD of his late father (Shri 
Radhaballabh Kandpal), who died in 2018.
(5) Learned State Counsel points out that 
appellant’s brother is available, who can 
perform the SHARAD of his father. 
(6) Having regard to the ground taken for 
short-term bail, we are not inclined to grant 
short-term bail to the appellant.
(7) Accordingly, short-term bail application 
(IA No. 2 of 2023) is rejected. 
(8) Learned counsel for the complainant 
prays for and is granted a week’s time to file 
objection to the bail application.
(9) List this case on 18.09.2023.
