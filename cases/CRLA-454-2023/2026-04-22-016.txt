Instant appeal is preferred against the judgment and order 
dated 15.06.2023, passed in Session Trial No.04 of 2019, State vs. 
Devendra Singh @ Bau & Others, by the court of learned Sessions 
Judge, Nainital. By it, the appellant has been convicted under Sections 
302/120-B of IPC and sentenced accordingly. The appellant seeks bail 
in this appeal.
2. Heard learned counsel for the parties and perused the 
record.
3. According to the prosecution case on 01.09.2018, the co￾convict, Devendra Singh @ Bau killed Virendra Manral by firing at him 
and he fled away in a car, which was being driven by one Darshan 
Singh, in which the appellant was also an occupant. 2
4. Learned counsel for the appellant submits that the driver 
of the car, Darshan Singh has already been acquitted by this Court 
and the case of the appellant stands on a better footing. It is further 
submitted that he was not named in the FIR; his name was allegedly
revealed during investigation and that no Test Identification Parade 
was conducted in the matter. 
5. Learned State counsel does not dispute these facts.
6. Since the driver of the car, Darshan Singh, in which 
allegedly the main shooter Devendra Singh @ Bau ran away from the
place of incident, has already been acquitted by this Court, the 
appellant, who was an occupant of the car, is also entitled to bail. 
7. The bail application is allowed. 
8. The execution of sentence appealed against is suspended 
during the pendency of the appeal. 
9. The appellant – Gurpreet @ Gopi be released on bail, 
during the pendency of the appeal, on his executing a personal bond 
and furnishing two reliable sureties, each of the like amount, to the 
satisfaction of the court concerned.
10. List in due course for final hearing along with connected 
matters.
