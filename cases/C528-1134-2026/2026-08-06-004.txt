Hon'ble Rakesh Thapliyal, J.
1. Ms. Sangeeta Patni, learned counsel for the
applicant.
2. Mr. Pratiroop Pandey, learned AGA for the
State.
3. Ms. Sheetal Selwal, learned counsel for the
respondent nos. 2 and 3. 
4. The instant petition under Section 528 of BNS,
2023 is preferred on behalf of Gopal Singh Bisht, son of
Mohan Singh, Govind Singh Bisht, son of Bhuvan
Singh Bisht, and Nav Prabhat Taragi, son of Sunder
Singh Taragi along with the compounding application
with the affidavit of applicants and respondent no. 2
and 3. 
5. Applicant no. 1 joined the proceedings through
V.C. and applicants no. 2 and 3 and respondent no. 2
and 3 are present in the Court and all are duly
identified by their respective counsel.
6. By the instant petition, applicants are praying for
quashing of entire proceedings of Special Sessions
Trial No. 05 of 2025, arising out of FIR No. 58 of 2024,
wherein applicants are facing the trial for the offences
punishable under Section 3, 4, 5(l), 5(n) and 6 of the
POCSO Act and under Sections 351 (2), 351 (3), and 65
(1) of the BNS, 2023.
7. It is contended in the compounding application
that applicant no. 3 is ready to marry with the victim,
as and when, she attains the age of majority and at
present, she is aged about 17 ½ years old, for whichvictim and informant (mother of the victim) are also
agreeable, therefore, entire proceedings be quashed.
8. On the other side, learned State Counsel submits
that firstly, offences are not compoundable and
secondly, applicant nos. 1 and 2 are also facing trial for
the said offences who are completely stranger to
applicant no. 3 and even they have no relation with the
family of applicant no. 3 and as such, this aspect may
also be considered that whether in view of the
settlement arrived at in between the parties, entire
proceedings can be quashed particularly qua the
applicant no. 1 and 2.
9. Let the State may file their counter affidavit
within three weeks. One week, thereafter, is granted to
the learned counsel for the applicants to file rejoinder
affidavit. 
10. List on 08.09.2026.
11. In the meantime, further proceedings of Special
Sessions Trial No. 05 of 2025, arising out of FIR No. 58
of 2024, pending in the court of learned Special Judge,
POCSO Act, Champawat, qua the applicant no. 3 shall
remain stayed.
