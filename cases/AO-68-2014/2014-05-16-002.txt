Hon’ble Alok Singh, J.
 Mr. Anil Kumar Joshi, Additional 
Chief Standing Counsel for the State/ 
appellants. 
 Mr. V.K. Kohli, Senior Advocate 
assisted by Mr. Kanti Ram, Advocate 
for the respondent. 
 Mr. A.K. Joshi, learned 
Additional Chief Standing Counsel 
contends that present appeal was 
preferred against the rejection of the 
application filed under Section 34 of 
the Arbitration and Conciliation Act; 
As per Section 36 of the Act, award 
cannot be enforced during the 
pendency of the application moved 
under Section 34 of the Act; Further 
contends that appeal is a continuation 
of the suit/application, therefore, 
award cannot be enforced during the 
pendency of the appeal as well; 
Further contends that there is no 
requirement to deposit the decreetal 
amount along with the appeal filed 
under Section 37 of the Act in view of 
the fact that award shall be executed 
as decree of civil court only when 
application under Section 34 of the Act 
is rejected finally upto the last stage. 
 Mr. A.K. Joshi, learned 
Additional Chief Standing Counsel 
further submits that order dated 
6.3.2014 directing the appellant to 
deposit the entire awarded amount, on 
the face of it, is wrong, which requires 
modification. He further submits that 
he shall be moving an application for 
deleting the direction, whereby appellants were directed to deposit the 
awarded amount. 
 On his request, list in the next 
week.
