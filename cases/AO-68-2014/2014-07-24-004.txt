Having heard learned Counsel for 
the parties, it transpires that in 
pursuance of the order dated 6.3.2014, 
passed by this Court, entire awarded 
amount has been deposited by the 
State Government. 
Considering the facts and 
circumstances of the case, the 
miscellaneous application (CLMA 
7926/2014) is allowed. M/s Hydel 
Construction Pvt. Ltd. is permitted to 
withdraw fifty per cent amount out of 
the amount deposited as a result of the 
arbitral award. Rest of the amount i.e. 
remaining fifty percent shall be 
invested in the form of T.D.R. in the 
State Bank of India, where the money 
is lying at present. Payment of that 
money along with the interest will be 
subject to final adjudication of the 
appeal.
