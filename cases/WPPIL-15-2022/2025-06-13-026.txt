In fact, yesterday, it was argued that 
48 stone crushers had been sealed 
pursuant to the order passed in WPPIL No. 
62 of 2016. This matter is heard today on this 
aspect. In fact, the entire issue relating to 
closure of stone crushers initiated by the 
directions dated 06.12.2016, passed by the 
Central Pollution Control Board (“CPCB) 
based on which subsequent directions 
were issued by this Court. WPPIL No. 62 of 
2016 was decided on 05.09.2018. The 
Court had finally directed the State and 
other agencies to comply with the order
dated 06.12.2016 of CPCB. The question 
still remains that how could it be de-sealed 
by the authorities on 16.12.2018. This 
office memorandum has been handed over 
to the Court by the learned C.S.C.
Learned counsel for the petitioner 
would submit that, in fact, before de￾sealing, the Administrative Department 
has sought opinion from Legal Department 
and the Legal Department had then opined 
that de-sealing cannot be done because it 
is pursuant to the direction dated 
06.12.2016 of the CPCB and if the State 
Government is aggrieved by the direction of 
the CPCB dated 06.12.2016, that direction 
is to be challenged. He seeks time to place 
for perusal of the Court the opinion of the 
Legal Department.
