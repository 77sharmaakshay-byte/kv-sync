6. Heard.
7. During the course of arguments, the Court 
Commissioners’ report was also discussed which reveals 
that the mining activities are still going on in that area. 
8. On 16.03.2022, this Court had passed the 
following order:-
“7. Since at paragraph 12 of the counter affidavit, the 
District Magistrate has admitted that the mining activity is 
undertaken by Uttarakhand Forest Corporation, we consider 
it appropriate to stay the further mining activity in that area. 
The State Government shall file its response within two 
weeks.”
9. The sense of this Court’s order dated 16.03.2022 
is that no mining activity should take place in that area.
10. Let the District Magistrate, Haridwar submit a 
detailed report as to why and under what 
circumstances, despite this Court’s order dated 
16.03.2022, mining activities are still being carried out 
in that area. The District Magistrate, Haridwar is 
directed to ensure compliance of this Court’s order. 2
11. The affidavit should be filed on or before the next 
date of hearing.
