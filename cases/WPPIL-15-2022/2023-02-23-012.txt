The Court made the following: 
ORDER: (per Hon’ble The Chief Justice Sri Vipin Sanghi) 
 An affidavit has been filed by the 
respondent No. 10-NMCG in pursuance of our order dated  2
15.12.2022. Along with the said affidavit, communication 
issued by the Secretary, Department of Water Resources, 
River Development and Ganga Rejuvenation dated 
31.01.2022, addressed to the Secretary, Ministry of 
Environment, Forests and Climate Change, has been 
enclosed, which requests that the issue of sand mining on 
river Ganga in Kumbh zone be reviewed keeping into 
consideration holistic status of mining in the said 
stretches, i.e., the stretch between Raiwala to Bhogpur, 
keeping in view its impact on river morphology and 
ecology. 
2. It appears that no further review has taken 
place at the end of the Ministry of Environment, Forests 
and Climate Change. At least, no further decision of the 
said Ministry has been placed before us. 
3. We direct the Ministry of Environment, Forests 
and Climate Change to place before us their stand in the 
light of the letter dated 31.01.2022 aforesaid. 
4. The response should be filed within the next ten 
days keeping in view the urgency in the matter. 
5. Copy of the affidavit filed by the NMCG shall be 
provided to the counsel for respondent No. 1 during the  3
course of the day by the learned counsel for the 
petitioner, along with its annexures. 
6. List on 17.03.2023, along with connected 
matter. 
7. Interim orders to continue till the next date of 
listing. 
8. This order be also communicated to the Ministry 
of Environment, Forests and Climate Change, Government 
of India, by the Registrar General of this Court, within 24 
hours for compliance.
