Today, during the course of hearing, 
Colonel Praful Thapliyal from 127 Infantry 
Battalion, (TA) Eco, Garhwal Rifles, joined 
the proceedings. He would submit that he 
would be more than happy to assist the 
Court in the matter and rejuvenate the 
banks of river Ganga. He submits that if a

would protect it and rejuvenate it. He 
submits that some modalities is to be 
completed for undertaking this task. 
The Court appreciates the statement 
given by Colonel Praful Thapliyal.

Thapliyal need not join the proceedings. 
Learned counsel for the parties

their written arguments.
