ORDER
This writ petition has been filed by a private 
limited company, which was denied registration as 
Contractor by respondent nos. 2 & 3 on the ground 
that it is not domiciled in Uttarakhand. Petitioner 
earlier approached this Court by filing Writ Petition 
(M/S) No. 2237 of 2021, which was allowed and the 
distinction made by Nagar Palika Parishad between 
contractors based on their domicile/residence was held 
to be bad and respondents were directed to reconsider 
petitioner’s application for registration, in accordance 
with law.
2. Now, petitioner has been registered by 
Nagar Palika Parishad, Mangloure, in a new category, 
namely, A+, which is meant for Contractors from 
outside the State and who are permitted to bid only, in 
respect of civil contracts above the value of ` 5 crores.
The Nagar Palika Parishad has issued a Notice Inviting 
Tender on 26.11.2021, in which, only Contractors 
registered in Class-A are permitted to participate. 
Since petitioner has been registered in A+ category, 2
therefore, petitioner apprehends that his bid will not 
be considered, on the ground that petitioner is not 
registered as Class-A Contractor. 
3. In the judgment rendered in Writ Petition 
(M/S) No. 2237 of 2021, the classification between 
Contractors made on the basis of domicile status has 
been held to be arbitrary. Even otherwise also, the 
bye-laws which has been relied upon by the Nagar 
Palika Parishad have not been notified in the official 
Gazette. In such view of the matter, a case is made 
out for grant of interim order. 
4. Learned counsel for the respondent prays 
for and is granted four weeks’ time for filing counter￾affidavit and two weeks’ thereafter is granted to the 
learned counsel for the petitioner for filing rejoinder￾affidavit. List this case after six weeks. 
5. In the meantime, petitioners bid submitted,
pursuant to Notice Inviting Tender dated 26.11.2021
shall not be rejected only on the ground that petitioner 
is not registered in ‘A’ category.
 
(MANOJ KUMAR TIWARI, J.) Shubham
