This criminal revision is directed 
against the order dated 11.10.2018 
passed by learned Judge, Family Court 
Kotdwar, District Pauri Garhwal in misc. 
Criminal No. 114 of 2014 “Smt. 
Dhaneshwari vs. Anil”, whereby the said 
Court directed the revisionist to pay 
maintenance @ Rs. 3,000/- per month to 
his wife Smt. Dhaneshwari (respondent) 
under Section 125 of Cr. P.C. 
The learned counsel for the 
revisionist submitted that revisionist is a 
poor person. He is not able to pay Rs. 
3,000/- per month. He was giving Rs. 
2,000/- and ready to pay that amount 
during the pendency of this criminal 
revision. 
Having heard the learned counsel 
for the revisionist and after going through 
the impugned order, as an interim 
measure, it is directed that, if the 
revisionist-Anil pays to his wife￾Dhaneshwari for maintenance @ Rs. 
2,000/- per month instead of Rs. 3,000/- as directed by the trial court or deposits 
in her favour before the trial court in 
terms of the impugned order. The 
recovery, in respect of remaining amount 
Rs. 1,000/- per month shall remain 
stayed. In case, default in payment of the 
maintenance @ Rs. 2,000/- per month, 
directed by this Court, this interim order 
shall, automatically, stand vacated after 
a period of two months.
