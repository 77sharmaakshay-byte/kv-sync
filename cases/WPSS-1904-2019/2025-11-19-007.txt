2. Mr. T.S. Bisht, learned Deputy Advocate General

3. A recall application (IA No. 14155/2025) has been 
filed by petitioner no. 2/the recall applicant seeking 
recall of the order dated 13.02.2024, whereby the writ 
petition was dismissed as withdrawn by petitioner 
no. 2/the recall applicant.
4. Learned counsel for petitioner no. 2/the recall 
applicant contends that the writ petition was 
withdrawn on the assurance that he would be engaged 
in some other department. However, when the said 
assurance did not materialize, he filed the present 
recall application.
5. Although there is a delay in filing the recall 
application, a delay condonation application (IA No. 
14154/2025) has also been submitted. The said 
application is not seriously opposed by learned State 
Counsel. Accordingly, the delay condonation 
application is allowed, and the delay in filing the recall 
application is condoned.
6. The reasons stated in the recall application are 
sufficiently convincing to allow it. Accordingly, the 
recall application is allowed. The order dated 2
13.02.2024 is hereby recalled. The writ petition is 
restored to its original number insofar as it relates to 
petitioner no. 2.
