IN THE HIGH COURT OF UTTARAKHAND AT NAINITAL
Writ Petition (S/S) No. 1794 of 2011
Km. Mamta Kaira ...Petitioner 
Versus 
Uttarakhand Transport Corporation 
and others …Respondents 
Present: Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Counsel for the petitioner. 
Mr. B. P. S. Mer, Counsel for the respondents. 
Hon’ble Sudhanshu Dhulia, J. (Oral)
1. Heard Mr. Bhagwat Mehra, Counsel for the 
petitioner and Mr. B. P. S. Mer, Counsel for the 
respondents. 
2. The father of the petitioner was an employee of 
Uttarakhand Transport Corporation (hereinafter referred 
to as “Corporation”), who died while in harness on 
11.9.2007. Earlier the mother of the petitioner applied for 
appointment on compassionate ground praying that her 
elder daughter Yamuna Kaira be appointed. However, 
appointment was not given to her. Meanwhile her elder 
daughter got married and the petitioner being the 
younger daughter of the deceased applied for 
compassionate appointment which was denied to her.
Consequently, petitioner was constrained to file the 
present writ petition before this Court. 
3. The respondents Corporation had shown a totally 
callous attitude in the matter, as the application of the 
petitioner remained pending for a considerable period of 
time inspite of there being specific regulation governing 
the appointment namely Uttar Pradesh Transport 
Corporation Employees (other than officers) Service, 
Regulation 1981 particularly regulation 77, which is 
2013:UHC:9912
presently in force in the State of Uttarakhand. Regulation 
77 reads as under:- 
“77. Employment to the dependant of 
an employee dying or incapacitated during 
service.- In the event of death or permanent 
disability of an employee while in service his 
son or any other member of his family 
dependant on him may be appointed on a post 
in the service for which he possesses the 
prescribed qualification. The procedure for 
recruitment contained in these Regulations 
will not be applicable in his case. If necessary 
the age limit and academic qualifications may 
also be relaxed.”
4. On the earlier occasion, the writ petition of the 
petitioner was disposed of on 2.8.2011 with a direction to 
the General Manager (Karmik), Uttarakhand Transport 
Corporation, Head Office, Dehradun to consider the case 
of the petitioner and pass appropriate order within four 
weeks from the date a certified copy of this order is 
produced before him. Consequent to the order dated 
2.8.2011, an order has been passed by the Corporation 
although admitting that there is a provision namely 
Regulation 77 of Uttar Pradesh Road Transport 
Corporation Employees (other than officers) Service, 
Regulation, 1981 to give appointment on compassionate 
ground, yet appointment cannot be given to the petitioner 
for the reason that the Corporation between 2004 to 
2011 has incurred financial loss of `8,571.89/- Lakhs. 
5. This cannot be a ground for rejecting the claim of 
the petitioner for appointment on compassionate ground.
It is clear that the respondents have no answer to the 
legitimate claim of the petitioner for appointment on 
compassionate ground. The reason they have assigned 
for refusing the claim of the petitioner is infact no reason 
2013:UHC:9913
in the eyes of law. As long as the Regulation 77 is 
applicable in the case of the petitioner, the petitioner was 
liable to be considered under the said Regulation.
Impugned order dated 15.11.2011 (Annexure No. 7 to the 
writ petition) is hereby quashed. It is therefore clear that 
the respondents have no just grounds for rejecting the 
claim of the petitioner. 
6. The writ petition therefore succeeds. A mandamus 
is being issued to the respondents to give compassionate 
appointment to the petitioner within 45 days from the 
date a certified copy of this order is produced before them 
on a post which can be filled by way of compassionate 
appointment, which can be a Class III or Class IV post, 
considering the qualification of petitioner in mind. 
7. No order as to costs.
