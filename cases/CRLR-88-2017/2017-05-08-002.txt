Hon’ble V.K. Bist, J.
Mr. Deep Chandra Joshi, Advocate 
for the revisionist. 
There is no representation for the 
respondent, in spite of sufficient service. 
Heard on the delay condonation 
application. 
There is delay of some 77 days in 
filing the present revision. 
Reason shown for delay is that the 
revisionist is 30% disable and unable to 
maintain himself, therefore, he could not 
arrange money to challenge the order on 
time. 
Reason seems to be bonafide. 
Accordingly, delay condonation application 
is allowed. Delay in filing the revision is 
condoned. 
Admit the revision. 
Also heard on the stay application. 
Having considered the submissions 
of learned counsel for the revisionist, as 
an interim measure, it is provided that if 
the revisionist pays monthly maintenance 
allowance @ Rs.2,000/- (Rupees Two 
Thousand) to the respondent, by the 10th
of each month, the effect and operation of 
the impugned judgments and orders dated 
20.12.2013 and 20.10.2016 passed by the 
Judge, Family Court, Camp Haldwani, 
shall remain stayed. (Stay application is 
disposed of.) 
 (V.K. Bist, J.)
