Hon’ble Ravi Malimath, A.C.J.
Hon’ble Ravindra Maithani, J.
Shri J.S. Virk, learned amicus curiae, for the petitioner. 
Shri K.N. Joshi, learned deputy advocate general for the 
State. 
Shri K.N. Joshi, learned deputy advocate general appearing 
for the State, on instructions, submits that the Committee 
formed in order to look into these issues, at large, will hold its 
meeting on 21.10.2020. He further submits that the Home 
Secretary, Government of Uttarakhand is on leave and, in his 
place, the Additional Chief Secretary, will be placed. It is his 
further submission that the Committee will hold its 
deliberations in terms of the Government Order dated 
21.06.2017 and all the cases, where a request has been made 
from those who are in custody, will be considered. 
To this, we direct the Committee to consider all the 274 
cases wherein the persons are in custody beyond the period of 
14 years. The Committee need not wait for a written request 
from any of the convicts. The Committee is at liberty to consider 
the case of each one of these persons on the basis of the 
Government Order dated 21.06.2017, in its meeting to be held 
on 21.10.2020. 
Shri K.N. Joshi, learned deputy advocate general, 
undertakes to furnish the entire case papers including the list of 
274 persons and all the documents which the Committee 
requires. 2 
Registry is directed to furnish a certified copy of this order 
to the learned counsels for the parties, today itself, on payment 
of the prescribed charges.
