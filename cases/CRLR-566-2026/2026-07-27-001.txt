2. Present criminal revision has been 
preferred against the order dated 
13.12.2024 passed by the learned 
Additional Principal Judge, Family Court, 
Haldwani, District Nainital in Misc. 
Criminal Case No. 506 of 2024, whereby 
the learned Family Court proceeded ex 
parte against the revisionist and fixed the 
matter for ex parte evidence/hearing, and 
the order dated 25.06.2026, whereby the 
application filed by the revisionist under 
Order IX Rule 7 C.P.C. seeking recall of the 
ex parte order was rejected.
3. Learned counsel for the revisionist 
would submit that the court below has 
committed a manifest error of law in 
proceeding ex parte against the revisionist 
and in rejecting his application for setting 
aside the ex parte order; that, the learned 
Family Court erroneously held that the 
notice had been duly served upon the 
revisionist, although, in fact, no summons 
was ever served upon him; that, immediately upon coming to know of the 
pendency of the proceedings, the 
revisionist entered appearance before the 
court below and filed an application for 
setting aside the ex parte order, specifically 
pleading that he had never received any 
summons and, therefore, could not appear 
before the Court; that, the absence of the 
revisionist was bona fide and neither 
intentional nor deliberate.
4. He would further submit that the 
learned Family Court failed to consider the 
grounds urged in the application and 
rejected the same without affording the 
revisionist an effective opportunity of 
hearing; that, if the proceedings before the 
Family Court are permitted to continue 
and culminate in an ex parte decision 
during the pendency of the present 
revision, the revision itself would become 
infructuous, causing irreparable prejudice 
to the revisionist.
5. Issue notice to the respondent 
through her natural guardian, returnable 
at an early date.
6. Steps shall be taken within one week.
7. List the matter after the service report 
is received.
8. Meanwhile, further proceedings of 
Misc. Criminal Case No. 506 of 2024 
pending before the Court of the learned 
Additional Principal Judge, Family Court, 
Haldwani, District Nainital, shall remain 
stayed till the next date of listing.
9. Stay application stands disposed of 
accordingly.
