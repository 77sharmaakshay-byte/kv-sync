Present Second Appeal has been preferred 
against the defendants-appellants being aggrieved 
against the judgment and decree dated 22.04.2014 
passed by III Additional District Judge, Rudrapur, 
Udham Singh Nagar dismissing the Civil Appeal 
No. 27/2011 and confirming the judgment and 
decree dated 26.05.2011 passed by Civil Judge 
(SD) Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar in 
Civil Suit No. 09 of 2008. 
Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the documents brought on record. 
Admit the second appeal on the 
following substantial questions of law: 
(i)Whether it was not open for the 
courts below to refuse decree of haq￾shufa to the plaintiff/appellant though 
there was utter failure on the part of 
the defendants to plead non-compliance 
of the pre-requisite of haq-shufa in 
their written statement? 
(ii)Whether the plaintiff/appellant 
being co-owner of the property in 
question had rightly exercised the right 
of haq-shufa and was entitled to a 
decree of pre-emption under the 
principle of Shifae-Shariq? (iii)Whether the first appellate court was right 
in concluding that there was of mis-description 
of the property in dispute as the 
plaintiff/appellant has not mentioned in built up 
construction in the description of the property 
in the plaint whereas the plaintiff had described 
the property as mentioned in the sale deed in 
question?
