Present Second Appeal has been preferred 
by the defendants-appellants being aggrieved 
against the judgment and decree dated 22.04.2014 
passed by III Additional District Judge, Rudrapur, 
Udham Singh Nagar dismissing the Civil Appeal 
No. 27/2011 and confirming the judgment and 
decree dated 26.05.2011 passed by Civil Judge 
(SD) Rudrapur, District Udham Singh Nagar in 
Civil Suit No. 09 of 2008. 
Heard learned counsel for the parties and 
perused the documents brought on record. 
Admit the second appeal on the following 
substantial questions of law: 
(i)Whether in absence of the evidence 
on part of the respondent / plaintiff 
that the construction has been done 
only over the area 150.63 meter of land 
of Plot No. 309/2 lease out to him, the 
judgment and decree of Courts below 
are justified? 
(ii)Whether the Courts below are 
justified in allowing the suit of 
respondent / plaintiff on weakness of 
the defendants / appellants, when the 
respondent / plaintiff has failed to adduce the oral and documentary 
evidence to prove his case? 
 (iii) Whether the respondent / plaintiff 
has not proved the easement of necessity 
in accordance with law on the basis of 
any documentary evidence or oral 
evidence, but the Trial Court as well as 
Appellate Court decided in favour of the 
respondent / plaintiff ? 
Summon the Lower Court Record. 
List after the Lower Court Record is received. 
Also heard learned counsel for the parties on 
CLMA Nos. 7128/2015, 9741/2015 and objections 
thereon. 
It is provided, as an interim measure, that the 
parties shall maintain status quo over the suit property 
till further orders. It is clarified that this Court has not 
given any direction to the State Government while 
passing such an order. 
Both the applications stand disposed of. 
 (U. C. Dhyani, J.)
