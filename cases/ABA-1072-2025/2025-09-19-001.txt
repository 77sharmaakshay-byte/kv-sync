Hon’ble Alok Kumar Verma,J.
The present Application has been filed by the 
applicant Kaushal Kumar Sahay alias Sunny seeking 
anticipatory bail in Case Crime No.415 of 2025, registered at
Police Station Gangnahar, District Haridwar under Sections 
120B, 420, 467, 468, 471 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860,
Sections 111, 351 and Section 352 of the Bharatiya Nyaya 
Sanhita, 2023. 
2. The First Information Report was lodged by Mr. 
Nand Kishor Bhatt, the inspector. According to the First 
Information Report dated 27.08.2025, a forged power of 
attorney of the real owner Smt. Rekha was prepared by the 
co-accused persons. On the basis of the said forged power of 
attorney, the co-accused Pankaj Astwal sold the properties 
of Smt. Rekha to several persons. 2
3. Heard Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, learned counsel for 
the applicant and Mr. Chitrarth Kandpal, learned Brief Holder 
for the respondent. 
4. Mr. Aditya Pratap Singh, Advocate, contended that 
the applicant is not named in the First Information Report. 
He was not involved in the said offence. He is a permanent 
resident of District Haridwar, therefore, there is no 
possibility of his absconding, and, Ankit Kumar, co-accused,
has already been granted interim bail by this Court. 
5. Mr. Chitrarth Kandpal, learned Brief Holder, on 
instructions, has sought two weeks’ time to file objection to 
the Anticipatory Bail Application. 
6. Time is granted. 
7. Having heard the submissions of learned counsel 
for the parties and in the facts and circumstances of the 
case, as an ad-interim measure, it is directed that in the 
event of arrest of the applicant Kaushal Kumar Sahay alias 
Sunny, he shall be released on interim bail on executing a 
personal bond of Rs.30,000/- and two reliable sureties, each 
of the like amount, to the satisfaction of the Investigating 
Officer/ Arresting Officer, subject to the following 
conditions:-
(i) Applicant shall cooperate with the Investigating 
Agency and he shall make himself available for 
interrogation by a police officer as and when 
required; 3
(ii) Applicant shall not directly or indirectly make any 
inducement, threat or promise to any person 
acquainted with the facts of this case.
(iii) Applicant shall not leave the country without the 
previous permission of this Court. 
8. List on 16.10.2025 with the record of ABA 
No.1027 of 2025 for hearing on the Anticipatory Bail 
Application.
