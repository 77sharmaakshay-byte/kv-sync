9. Heard on Interim Relief Application (IA No. 1 
of 2026).
10. Learned Senior Counsel appearing for the 
petitioners submits that the petitioners, who are 
Lecturers, who have been paid one increment at a 
particular stage pursuant to the Government Order 
dated 06.09.2019 and multiple orders have been 
passed by this Court in various writ petitions on that 
subject. But, it is argued that, by a Government 
Order dated 18.12.2025 (Annexure No.15 to the 
writ petition), now though pursuant to the Court’s 
order, recovery of excess amount has been stayed, 
but the department has been directed to re-determine 
the salary. It is argued that it is a kind of undoing 
what the Court has done by its order passed in 
different writ petitions, which is not permissible in the eyes of law. He submits that this is not 
specifically prayed in the interim relief application, 
but what is prayed is that status quo be directed to 
be maintained. 
11. Having considered, till the next date of listing, 
the effect and operation of the impugned 
Government Order dated 18.12.2025, shall remain 
in abeyance. 
12. The interim relief application stands disposed 
of accordingly.
