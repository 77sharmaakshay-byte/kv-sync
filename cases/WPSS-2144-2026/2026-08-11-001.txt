5. Petitioners submitted their application 
forms pursuant to the advertisement dated 
14.10.2023 issued by Uttarakhand Public 
Service Commission for appearing in Combined 
Junior Engineer Service Exam 2023. Petitioners
applied for the post of Junior Engineer (Civil). 
They claimed the benefit of vertical reservation 
available to candidates belonging to Other 
Backward Class (OBC) as well as the horizontal 
reservation available for differently able 
candidates suffering from locomotor disability. 
6. Petitioners appeared in the written 
examination and declared qualified for 
participating in the document verification for 
the post of Junior Engineer (Civil). Thereafter, 
they appeared in the interview. On 
10.07.2024, respondent No.5-Selecting Body 
has declared the final result, wherein, the 
name of petitioners was not found.7. Learned counsel for petitioners submits 
that for the post of Junior Engineer (Civil) in 
the Public Works Department, 20 vacancies 
were earmarked for differently able persons of 
different categories of disability, but, in the 
Final Result, 09 vacancies have been carried 
forward due to non-availability of the suitable 
candidates. He further submits that the 
petitioners secured more marks than 
respondent No.6, who was selected against the 
vacancy earmarked for PwD.
8. It is contended by learned counsel for 
petitioners that petitioner No.2 and other 
candidates with disability submitted 
representation dated 09.06.2026 and 
16.06.2026 to respondent-Selecting Body, to 
the said fact, but the same was rejected by the 
respondent-Selecting Body vide impugned 
office memorandum dated 29.06.2026. 
9. Having heard the learned counsel for 
petitioners and having gone through the entire 
material available on record, this Court thinks 
that the present matter requires some 
deliberation.
10. Respondents are directed to file counter 
affidavit(s) within four weeks.
11. Put up on 22.09.2026.
12. In the meantime, 02 posts of Junior 
Engineer (Civil) of Persons with Benchmark 
Disabilities under the OL category in Public 
Works Department shall be kept vacant.
