2. The applicant instituted a complaint 
under Section 138 of the Negotiable 
Instruments Act against respondent no.2. 
During the course of trial, at the stage of 
recording of statement under Section 313 
Cr.P.C., respondent no.2 produced a 
document, which was taken on record by 
the learned trial Court. Subsequently, 
applicant moved an application under 
Section 311 Cr.P.C. seeking recall of the 
accused for the purpose of cross￾examination with respect to the said 
document, on the ground that the same is 
essential for a just and proper adjudication 
of the case. However, the learned Court 
below, without properly appreciating the 
necessity and relevance of such cross￾examination, rejected the said application.
Being aggrieved by the impugned order, 
which has resulted in serious prejudice to 
the applicant and has curtailed his valuable 
right to effectively challenge the defence 
evidence, the present criminal 
miscellaneous application has been filed.
3. As prayed, three weeks’ time is 
granted to learned counsel for the 
respondent(s) to file counter affidavit(s). 
4. List on 01.06.2026.
5. Considering the submissions advanced 
by learned counsel for the applicant, it is 
directed that, till the next date of listing, further proceedings of Criminal Complaint 
Case No.2453 of 2021, pending before the 
Court of learned Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Haldwani, District Nainital, shall 
remain stayed.
6. Stay application (I.A. No.1 of 2026) 
stands disposed of.
