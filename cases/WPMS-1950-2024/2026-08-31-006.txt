5. For the reasons indicated, Intervention 
Application (IA/2/2024), is allowed.6. Mr. Ravi Shankar Joshi, Intervener, who is 
appearing in-person, submits that the process 
for regularisation of unauthorized occupation 
over Category-4 land, including the land in 
question was initiated in the year 1989 and 
District Magistrate, Nainital ultimately passed 
an order on 23.04.1991 rejecting the claim of 
unauthorised occupants for regularisation and 
District Magistrate further directed Tehsildar, 
Haldwani to reclaim possession of the land in 
question. It is further submitted by the 
intervener that some relevant documents, 
including the report submitted by Tehsildar in 
2016, which was ignored by District Magistrate 
while regularising possession, are not traceable
in the relevant files and there are various other 
documents, which indicate complicity of officers 
of district administration in the matter, are 
missing, which indicate that there are forces at 
play, which do not want correct picture to come 
before this Court. He submits that to prevent 
further loss of relevant documents, District 
Magistrate be asked to produce all relevant 
documents regarding land in question since 
1980, in a sealed envelope.
7. Having regard to the seriousness of the 
allegations made by intervener, District 
Magistrate, Nainital is directed to have an 
inquiry done to ascertain whether any relevant document, which weakens the case of the 
petitioners, went missing from the record, by an 
officer not below the rank of Additional District 
Magistrate, within two weeks. The District 
Magistrate shall also have all documents 
pertaining to the land in question, which may 
have some relevance to the issues raised in these 
petitions, produced before this Court in a sealed 
envelope within three weeks.
8. List on 05.10.2026. 
9. In case, intervener apprehends any threat 
to his life or liberty, then he shall be at liberty to 
approach the Senior Superintendent of Police,
Nainital, who shall provide necessary protection 
after assessing his threat perception. 
10. Mr. Avneesh Upadhyay, Advocate, holding
brief of Mr. Karthik Jayashankar, Advocate for 
the intervener submits that he may be permitted 
to withdraw the Vakalatnama filed on behalf of 
the intervener for which he has also moved an 
application. 
11. Necessary orders on that application shall 
be passed as and when the application is listed 
before this Court.
