 On 24.02.2025, this Court passed the 
following order:-
 “Meanwhile, the respondents shall 
release the gratuity, which the respondents 
admits to be payable to the petitioner. It 
shall be so paid within four weeks from 
today. If it is not done, the Court would 
initiate stringent action against the 
respondent no.5 Executive Engineer, 
Provincial Division, Public Works 
Department, Pauri, District Pauri Garhwal. 
In case, the respondent no.5 fails, the Court 
shall consider it as his personal failure and 
an appropriate action shall be taken against 
him.”
 Now, a Modification Application has 
been filed. 
 Learned State Counsel would submit 
that, in fact, gratuity has not been 
admitted. 
 Let State file a short affidavit giving 
response to the following:-
(i) Whether gratuity is payable to the 
petitioner? If so, what is the 
amount admitted to the State?
(ii) Why it has not been paid up till 
now? Let a short affidavit be filed within a 
week.
 List on 18.06.2025, just after fresh 
cases.
