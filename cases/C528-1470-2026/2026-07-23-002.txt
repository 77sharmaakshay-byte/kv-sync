1. Mr. Gaurav Singh, learned counsel for the

3. Mr. Bhavya Singh, learned counsel for 
respondent no.2/complainant through V.C.
4. Applicant no.1-Amandeep Singh Saluja is 
present through V.C and respondent no.2 Smt. 
Pawanjyot Kaur is also present in Court.
5. The applicant no.1 and respondent 
no.2/complainant married in the year of 2016 
and due to the matrimonial discord they were 
living separately and thereafter the husband filed 
a divorce petition on the ground of cruelty and 
the same was decreed by the Family Court on 
19.12.2025 against which a regular first appeal
has been preferred which is pending for 
adjudication.
6. By this petition the applicants are 
challenging the order of Ist Additional District 
and Sessions Judge Haldwani, District Nainital in 
Criminal Revision No. 12 of 2026 Amandeep 
Singh Saluja and others vs. State of Uttarakhand 
and others as well as the order dated 24.02.2026 
passed by learned Additional Chief Judicial 
Magistrate, Haldwani, District Nainital in Complaint Case No. 4022 of 2023 wherein the 
applicants have been summoned before the trial 
court to face the proceeding for the offence 
punishable under Section 406 of IPC.
7. It is argued by learned counsel for the 
applicants that the Family Court after hearing all 
these issues particularly with regard to the 
cruelty grant divorce decree which is now under 
challenge. About present proceeding he submits 
that the respondent have not taken any such plea 
in the divorce petition though she contest the 
divorce petition. 
8. All these aspects require scrutiny.
9. Let the respondents may file counter 
affidavit within two weeks. One week thereafter 
is granted to the applicants to file rejoinder 
affidavit.
10. List this case on 17.08.2026.
11. In the meantime, further proceedings of 
Crimanal Case No. 4022 of 2023 pending in the 
Court of Additional Chief Judicial Magistrate, 
Haldwani, District Nainital, shall remain stayed.
