Heard.
Issue notice to respondent nos.2 & 3.

Summon the lower court record. 
In a complaint case proceedings under Section 138 
of the Negotiable Instr[uments Act, which was 
registered as Complaint Case No.795 of 2011, M/s
Amar Shaheed Gajendra Singh Vs. Meghraj Kumar, the 
revisionist was initially convicted by the judgment 
dated 25.02.2013, as against the judgment of conviction 
an appeal was filed being Criminal Appeal No.36 of 
2013, Meghraj vs. State of Uttarakhand and another. 
The said appeal was allowed and the judgment of 
conviction was set aside by the appellate court on 
13.09.2013, against which the complainant preferred a 
Criminal Revision No.258 of 2013 before this Court 
and the Coordinate Bench of this Court has allowed the 
Criminal Revision and has remitted the matter back to 
the appellate court for its fresh decision. As a 
consequence thereto, the present impugned order dated 
12.09.2019 has been rendered whereby the judgment of 
conviction dated 25.02.2013 as passed by the learned 
trial court has been affirmed. 
In such an eventuality, as a consequence thereto, 
and as the result of affirmation of conviction, the 
revisionist has been sentenced to undergo simple 
imprisonment of one year and penalty of Rs.1,70,000/-
has been imposed upon him.
What is reflected from the record is that during the
pendency of the complaint case, he was on bail. In such 
an eventuality subject to the condition that the 
revisionist deposits a sum of Rs.50,000/- before the 
Magistrate concerned within two weeks from today he 
would be released on bail subject to furnishing of 
personal bond as well as two sureties of the like amount 
to the satisfaction of the Magistrate concerned.
