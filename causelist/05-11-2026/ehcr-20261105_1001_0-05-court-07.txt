 High Court Legal Service Committee, High Court Of Uttarakhand at Nainital 
NOTICE 
For National Lok Adalat on 12.12.2026 (Second Saturday)
1. This is for the information of the members of the Bar association that a National Lok 
Adalat is scheduled to be held on 12.12.2026 (Second Saturday) at 11:00 A.M. in the 
premises of Hon’ble High Court of Uttarakhand, Nainital. 
2. Pre Lok Adalat meetings are scheduled to be held on 19.11.2026 (Thursday) and 
04.12.2026 (Friday) at 04:00 P.M. in the hall of the Hon’ble Chief Justice Block of the 
Hon’ble High Court of Uttarakhand, Nainital. 
 In the National Lok Adalat following categories of cases will be taken up:- 
 (1) Criminal compoundable offences. 
 (2) Cases under section 138 of N.I. act. 
 (3) Money recovery cases. 
 (4) MACT cases. 
 (5) Labour and Employment dispute cases. 
 (6) Electricity, water bills and other bill payment cases (excluding non- 
 Compoundable). 
 (7) Matrimonial disputes (except divorce). 
 (8) Land acquisition cases (pending before Civil Courts/Tribunals). 
 (9) Service matters relating to pay and allowances and Retiral Benefits. 
 (10) Revenue cases (pending in High Courts only). 
(11) Other civil cases (rent, easmentary rights, injunction suits, specific 
performance suits) etc. 
3. Separate Cause Lists for the Lok Adalat will be issued for information of all the concerned. 
For convenience of litigants and advocates, Cause List will be issued in advance, and in 
phases. The Final Cause List will be issued and published on 11.12.2026. 
4. The members of the Bar association are requested to give list of their cases to the 
concerned sections of the Registry for listing of the cases before the National Lok Adalat. 
The members of the Bar are also requested to intimate their clients to appear in the Lok 
Adalat. 
5. The Lok Adalat will be organized through physical mode as well as virtual mode. 
6. For any other information regarding the National Lok Adalat and for listing of the cases 
before the National Lok Adalat, Advocates/Parties may contact office of the High Court 
Legal Services Committee situated in ADR Building in High Court premises. 
7. Advocates/Parties may also contact following officials of the High Court Legal Services 
Committee for any other information:-
