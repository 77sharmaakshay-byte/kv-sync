 This writ petition has been filed for issue a writ order
or direction in the nature of certiorari quashing the impugned
transfer order dated 30-4-2008 (Annexure No. 1) so far it relates to
the petitioner. Prayer for mandamus directing the respondents to
allow the petitioner to continue at the office of Superintendent of
Police Dehradun till the pendency of the writ petition has been
made. By the impugned order, the petitioner has been transferred
from Dehradun to district Chamoli.

 The grievance of the petitioner is that before passing
the impugned order of transfer, the petitioner filed representation
with the medical certificate before the Department for not
transferring him from Dehradun as per Transfer Policy dated 27-6-
2006.
 In the course of arguments, learned counsel for the
petitioner has submitted that the petitioner had already made a

2
representation to the Inspector General of Police, but the same has
not so far been decided. A copy of the representation has been
annexed as Annexure No. 5 (Page 29) to the writ petition. This
representation was made on compassionate ground.
 In the circumstances of the case, the respondent no. 2-
Inspector General of Police, Garhwal Region, Dehradun, is directed
to decide the representation of the petitioner (Annexure No. 5 to the
writ petition) by a speaking order within a period of two weeks
from the date of production of certified copy of this order.
 With the above direction, the writ petition is disposed
of finally.
